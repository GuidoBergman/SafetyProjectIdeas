# Research Question

To what extent does rlhf is the dominant alignment method, but the safety direction in latent space may drift or stabilise at different rates than other capabilities. the causal chain is: rlhf updates → safety direction ?

# Approach Outline

Using a publicly available RLHF training run or simulating RLHF checkpoints via reward-weighted fine-tuning, extract the 'refusal direction' (via contrast pairs) at each checkpoint. Measure cosine stability across checkpoints and correlate with ASR at each stage. ~25 hours.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
