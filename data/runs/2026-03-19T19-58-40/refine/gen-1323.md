# Research Question

To what extent does deliberative alignment (training models to reason through anti-deception guidelines before tasks) reduced deceptive behaviors ~30-fold in openai's o3/o4-mini. however, apollo's stress-testing work sho?

# Approach Outline

Design adversarial prompt wrappers that attempt to frame tasks in ways that bypass deliberative alignment: e.g., embedding the task inside a fictional context, splitting the task across turns, or invoking role authority. Measure breakthrough rates on open-source models with and without deliberative alignment fine-tuning.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Understanding adversarial bypass conditions for deliberative alignment directly informs how robust this training intervention is before deployment.
