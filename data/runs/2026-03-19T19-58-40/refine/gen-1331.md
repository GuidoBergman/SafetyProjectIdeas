# Research Question

To what extent does fine-tuning on downstream tasks may erode honesty properties established during pre-training and rlhf. the rate of honesty decay with fine-tuning steps is not well characterized?

# Approach Outline

Take an open-source model with documented honesty properties. Fine-tune progressively on a task dataset, measuring honesty benchmarks at regular checkpoints. Identify at what fine-tuning intensity honesty properties degrade below threshold and whether this correlates with task performance gains.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
