# Research Question

To what extent does attribution graphs and planning-circuit studies show models pre-commit to future tokens. a natural safety application is detecting when a model is 'planning' to generate a harmful or incorrect token b?

# Approach Outline

Train probing classifiers on residual stream activations at intermediate layers to predict the upcoming output token, using a dataset of both correct and incorrect completions (e.g., factual recall with known wrong answers). Measure how early in the forward pass (which layer) incorrect outputs become detectable. Compare detection accuracy with and without prior knowledge of where the planning circuit is active.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Early detection of committed-to outputs could enable runtime safety interventions that intercept harmful outputs before they are generated, rather than filtering post-hoc.
