# Research Question

To what extent does metr's task suite does not include customer service tasks, despite these being a major deployment domain where irreversible actions (sent emails, account changes, refund approvals) are common?

# Approach Outline

Design 15 METR-style customer service tasks (handle a refund request, respond to a complaint, update account details, send a promotional email). Include irreversible actions in each task's solution path. Measure agent task completion rate and irreversible-action-error rate. Compare to METR baseline tasks.
