# Approach Outline

# Theory of Impact Chain

Feature universality determines whether interpretability findings transfer between models — a critical question for AI safety at scale. If safety-relevant features (e.g., deception-related representations, refusal circuits) are universal, they can be studied once and applied broadly. If they are model-specific, every deployed model requires independent interpretability work.
