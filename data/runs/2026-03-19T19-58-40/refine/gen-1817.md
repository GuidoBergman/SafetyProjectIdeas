# Research Question

To what extent does the poetry planning finding was demonstrated on claude 3.5 haiku, a closed-weight model. it is unknown whether the same mechanism exists in open-weight models, which are required for full mechanistic ?

# Approach Outline

Design a poetry completion task and use TransformerLens on GPT-2-medium and Llama-3-8B to probe whether end-of-line tokens are represented in mid-network residual streams before the line begins. Use probing classifiers and logit lens. Compare circuit structures across models to test universality.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If planning-ahead is universal, it may be a fundamental property of transformer language models. If it is Claude-specific, it may reflect RLHF or scale effects.
