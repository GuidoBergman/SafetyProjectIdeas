# Research Question

To what extent does robust unlearning without capability degradation is an open problem, but there is no standardized, lightweight evaluation protocol for measuring capability degradation during unlearning that can be ap?

# Approach Outline

Design a standardized battery of capability benchmarks (coding, math reasoning, reading comprehension, instruction following) and evaluate capability retention across five standard unlearning methods (gradient ascent, NPO, RMU, LUNE, layered unlearning) before and after applying each. Publish results and the evaluation framework.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Without standardized capability-retention measurement, different unlearning papers use incompatible benchmarks, making comparison and progress tracking difficult. A standard protocol would accelerate the field.
