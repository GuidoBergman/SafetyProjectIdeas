# Research Question

To what extent does many jailbreaks transfer across domains (e.g., a jailbreak for bioweapons also unlocks cybercrime content). the causal chain: (1) jailbreak prompt activates a domain-general 'bypass safety' circuit → ?

# Approach Outline

Collect a set of jailbreaks that demonstrably transfer across content domains. For each jailbreak, use TransformerLens to identify which attention heads activate differently vs. a direct harmful prompt. Measure overlap in activated heads across different domain transfers. Cluster the heads by activation pattern similarity to determine if a single circuit or multiple circuits are responsible.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If transfer jailbreaks exploit a single shared bypass circuit, patching that circuit would generalize across domains—making defenses far more efficient. If multiple circuits exist, targeted defenses become necessary.
