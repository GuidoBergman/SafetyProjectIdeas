# Research Question

To what extent does shallow safety alignment has only been characterized for text-only llms. audio-language models (e.g., qwen2-audio) also apply rlhf-style alignment, but whether their refusal behavior is similarly conc?

# Approach Outline

Using Qwen2-Audio-Instruct, craft audio-format versions of a subset of StrongReject prompts using text-to-speech. Measure: (1) how often refusals begin with a fixed phrase, (2) whether prefilling the output decoder with 'Sure' collapses refusals. Compare to the equivalent text-only experiment on the same model.
