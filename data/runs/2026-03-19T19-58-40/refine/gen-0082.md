# Research Question

To what extent does different safety benchmarks produce scores on incompatible scales, making it impossible to compare model safety across benchmarks or aggregate findings into a single risk estimate?

# Approach Outline

Collect raw results from at least 3 existing safety benchmarks (e.g., AILuminate, TrustLLM, HELM Safety) for a set of 3–5 open-source models. Attempt to normalize them onto a common 0–1 scale using min-max and percentile normalization. Measure rank correlation between benchmarks. Propose a simple composite safety index with explicit weighting assumptions. Evaluate sensitivity to weighting choices.
