# Research Question

To what extent does oversight fails on complex tasks. if complex tasks can be decomposed into simpler subtasks that overseers can accurately evaluate, this is a scalable approach to maintaining oversight quality?

# Approach Outline

Take a task that exceeds the overseer's accuracy threshold. Decompose it into subtasks of varying complexity. Identify the maximum complexity level at which the overseer maintains accuracy. Use this to define a complexity budget for oversight.
