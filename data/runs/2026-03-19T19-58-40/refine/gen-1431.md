# Research Question

To what extent does cot monitoring research has been conducted exclusively in english. whether the information advantage of cot over action-only monitoring holds when the model reasons in another language (e.g., chinese,?

# Approach Outline

Translate a subset of MALT tasks to Mandarin Chinese. Run DeepSeek-R1-8B (strong multilingual reasoner) on translated tasks. Apply an LM monitor in (a) the task language and (b) English after auto-translation. Compare AUROC for CoT-monitor vs. action-only conditions in each language setting.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
