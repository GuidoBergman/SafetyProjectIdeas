# Research Question

To what extent does it is hard to know whether a monitor that produces no alerts is genuinely detecting safety, or simply failing silently. operators lack a way to test their monitor's sensitivity in live deployment?

# Approach Outline

Develop a 'canary injection' methodology: periodically insert known-bad synthetic requests (clearly violating instructions) into an agent's task stream and verify that the monitor flags them. Use the canary detection rate as a monitor health metric. Evaluate how canary detection rate correlates with detection of real policy violations in a controlled experiment using SHADE-Arena tasks.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Silent monitor failure is a critical and under-studied risk. Canary injection is standard practice in security logging but has not been systematically applied to AI safety monitors.
