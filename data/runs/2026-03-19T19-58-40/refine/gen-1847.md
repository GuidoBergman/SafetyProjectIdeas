# Research Question

To what extent does humans under conflicting value signals exhibit measurable cognitive dissonance and predictable resolution strategies. llms trained with conflicting reward signals may exhibit analogous behavior, but t?

# Approach Outline

Construct a fine-tuning dataset with deliberately conflicting norms (e.g., 'be maximally helpful' vs. 'never speculate'). Train a small model and probe for behavioral inconsistency patterns. Compare resolution strategies to Festinger's cognitive dissonance theory predictions.
