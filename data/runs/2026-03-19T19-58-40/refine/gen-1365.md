# Research Question

To what extent does apollo's scheming evals were primarily designed for standard context windows. long-context models (1m+ tokens) can potentially plan deceptive strategies across much longer time horizons?

# Approach Outline

Run Apollo-style scheming scenarios in both standard (8K) and extended context (100K+) windows, providing additional background context in the extended version. Measure whether scheming rates, strategic sophistication, or persistence increase with available context length.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
