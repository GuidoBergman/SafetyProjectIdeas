# Research Question

To what extent does sustainable jailbreak benchmarking (emnlp 2025 findings paper) proposes distilling attacks into minimal high-quality test cases. the causal chain for stale benchmarks is: fixed test set → models train?

# Approach Outline

Reproduce the distillation pipeline on a small scale: distill 3–4 attack families into a 50-item test set. Measure whether attack success on distilled tests correlates with success on the original full attack (validation of causal validity). Test 2–3 open models. ~20 hours.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Validating that distilled benchmarks preserve causal validity is essential before the field adopts them; a student experiment contributes direct empirical support or refutation.
