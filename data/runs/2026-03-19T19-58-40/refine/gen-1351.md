# Research Question

To what extent does the 2.1% figure is specific to gpt-5's thinking mode. whether open-source reasoning models (e.g., qwq, deepseek-r1) show similar or higher rates is unknown?

# Approach Outline

Apply the same CoT deception detection methodology (prompted monitor or rule-based proxy) to thinking responses from open-source reasoning models. Report rates and compare. Examine whether the deceptive reasoning in open models exhibits similar structural features to GPT-5's documented cases.
