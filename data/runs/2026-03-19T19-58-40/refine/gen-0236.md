# Research Question

To what extent does attribution graphs reveal that claude plans ahead (identifies rhyming words before writing them), but the mechanism for storing multi-step intermediate states—which layers, which token positions—is no?

# Approach Outline

On a small model fine-tuned for chain-of-thought reasoning, use causal tracing (noise patching) to identify which residual stream positions and layers store intermediate reasoning states. Use tasks with verifiable intermediate steps (e.g., two-hop fact retrieval: 'Who is the president of [country X]'s trade partner Y?'). Measure how much corrupting the intermediate-state token position degrades the final answer vs. corrupting irrelevant positions.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Locating intermediate reasoning states enables monitoring of whether a model's chain of thought reflects its actual computation (faithfulness) or is post-hoc rationalization. Unfaithful CoT is a safety-critical failure mode in reasoning systems.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (4/5): Strong: locating intermediate reasoning states directly tests CoT faithfulness — whether the chain of thought reflects actual computation or is post-hoc rationalization. This is a safety-critical question for reasoning systems.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Intermediate: causal tracing (noise patching) is a standard TransformerLens tool. Two-hop fact retrieval is a documented circuit with existing protocols. Requires understanding of residual stream patching but tools are available.

narrow_scope (3/5): Sustained effort: may need fine-tuning for CoT, constructing multi-hop tasks, running causal tracing across positions, comparing degradation patterns. The fine-tuning step alone adds complexity to the 30-hour budget.

novelty (3/5): CoT faithfulness has become a major research area in 2025 with multiple papers using causal methods, SAE-based feature analysis, and unlearning to probe intermediate reasoning states. The specific question of where intermediate states are stored has been partially addressed by SAE-based CoT analysis. However, a focused causal tracing study comparing intermediate state localization across reasoning hops — specifically asking which layers/positions store the intermediate result — adds a targeted contribution that complements existing work.
