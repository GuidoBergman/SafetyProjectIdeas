# Research Question

To what extent does achieving the success state at the ecosystem level requires that monitors from different providers can communicate and share signals. current monitor implementations are siloed within specific framewo?

# Approach Outline

Map the monitor output formats of three major agentic frameworks. Identify incompatibilities that would prevent a unified safety signal. Prototype a minimal adapter layer that normalizes outputs and test whether combined monitoring catches more incidents than any single framework's monitor alone.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

As multi-agent systems proliferate, control failures will increasingly occur at the seams between frameworks. Interoperability is a necessary condition for the success state but is rarely treated as a safety property in its own right.
