# Research Question

To what extent does goal misgeneralization—where a model pursues a proxy objective that diverges from the intended goal in new contexts—is a central alignment concern, but there are no small-scale mechanistic testbeds th?

# Approach Outline

Design a synthetic supervised task with two co-occurring training signals (e.g., predict color and predict shape, where color is spuriously correlated with shape in training). Train a small transformer. Use circuit discovery to identify which circuit implements each objective. Then shift the distribution to decouple the signals and measure which circuit 'wins.' Examine whether the winning circuit was distinguishable mechanistically before the distribution shift.
