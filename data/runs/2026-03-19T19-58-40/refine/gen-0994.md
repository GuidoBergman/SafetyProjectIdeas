# Research Question

# Approach Outline

Implement a simplified BFT-inspired voting protocol for a 5-agent network making high-stakes decisions (where any single agent could be compromised). Test whether BFT consensus reduces the rate of harmful decisions when 1 or 2 agents are adversarially manipulated. Compare to majority-vote and single-agent baselines.
