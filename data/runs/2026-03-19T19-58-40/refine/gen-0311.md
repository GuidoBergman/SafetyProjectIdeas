# Research Question

To what extent does governance failure mode: evaluations are known in advance by the labs being evaluated. technical failure mode: labs optimize models to perform well on known safety benchmarks without generalizing to u?

# Approach Outline

Test whether publicly available safety benchmarks show higher model improvement rates than held-out or novel safety evaluations on the same models. Use the ARC Evals, MT-Bench, or similar evaluation history to compute 'improvement rate on known vs. novel benchmarks' for models with multiple evaluation points.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If safety benchmark overfitting is occurring, it means the governance mechanism is actively misleading—a severe compounding failure.
