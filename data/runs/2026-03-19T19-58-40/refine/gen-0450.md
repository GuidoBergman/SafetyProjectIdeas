# Research Question

To what extent does linear probe detection achieves 90-96% accuracy but does not transfer between models, and noise injection is described as model-agnostic but tested on a small model set. the conditions under which bot?

# Approach Outline

Deliberately design sandbagging scenarios that evade noise injection detection (e.g., models that distribute underperformance across tokens in ways that resist noise) and linear probes (cross-architecture transfer). Categorize failure modes and estimate false-negative rates.
