# Research Question

To what extent does the over-optimization / goodhart's law failure mode in rlhf is well-documented at large scale, but the dynamics at small scale (1b-7b parameters) with reward shaping interventions are less studied. re?

# Approach Outline

Train a 1B-3B model with PPO using a sycophancy-prone reward model (a model fine-tuned to prefer agreeable responses). Then apply the reward shaping technique from arXiv 2502.18770 (bounding signal, saturating extremes) and compare sycophancy levels with and without shaping on factual QA. Use TRL's PPO trainer throughout.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If reward shaping reduces sycophancy at small scale, it becomes a practical tool for practitioners running RLHF on accessible hardware. This bridges the gap between theoretical mitigation proposals and what works empirically on accessible models. Tests a real RLHF failure mode with a concrete proposed fix.
