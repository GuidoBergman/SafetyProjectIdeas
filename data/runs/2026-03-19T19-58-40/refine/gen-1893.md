# Research Question

To what extent does a model safety-aligned via rlhf and then fine-tuned on a domain task may lose safety behaviors. the rate and magnitude of safety brittleness as a function of domain fine-tuning intensity is uncharacte?

# Approach Outline

Take a safety-aligned base model. Fine-tune on a domain task (e.g., coding, legal text summarization) with varying intensities (number of examples). Measure safety metric degradation at each intensity level. Identify at what fine-tuning intensity safety becomes meaningfully brittle.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
