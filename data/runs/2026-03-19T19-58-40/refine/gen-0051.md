# Research Question

To what extent does when models saturate safety benchmarks (approach ceiling performance), developers interpret saturation as 'the safety problem is solved' rather than 'the benchmark is too easy.' this creates a causal ?

# Approach Outline

Empirically document benchmark saturation timelines: for a sample of widely used safety benchmarks, measure when different models first achieved >90% performance. Correlate saturation dates with public announcements of reduced safety investment or increased capability focus. Propose a 'saturation early warning' mechanism.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Benchmark saturation is a documented driver of the 'evaluation gap.' Detecting saturation early and replacing saturated benchmarks with harder ones is essential for keeping evaluations meaningful.
