# Research Question

To what extent does u ai act entered enforcement for gpai models in 2025 with full enforcement by august 2026. it is unclear whether mid-tier european ai startups have the technical capacity to comply?

# Approach Outline

Survey or collect public information from 20+ European AI startups below the Tier 1 frontier. For each, check: presence of model card, risk classification documentation, technical documentation as required by EU AI Act, and disclosed testing procedures. Compute a compliance-readiness score.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up the experimental framework using a small, publicly available model. Replicate the core measurement from existing work to establish a baseline. Expected: reproduce known results within 10% margin. Time: ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core test): Apply the methodology to the target scenario described in the research question. Measure the primary metric and compare against the baseline. Expected: quantitative evidence for or against the hypothesis. Time: ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters (model size, dataset, hyperparameters). Run at least 2 variations to check robustness. Expected: characterize when findings hold and when they don't. Time: ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Compliance burden may disproportionately affect smaller EU players, creating a market concentration effect that has governance implications.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Plausible with gap: a compliance readiness assessment is descriptive and useful for identifying gaps, but does not directly improve safety. Market concentration effects are speculative.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Intermediate: checking startup documentation against EU AI Act requirements demands understanding of the legal text and the ability to operationalize compliance criteria.

narrow_scope (2/5): No clear milestone: surveying 20+ startups is very broad for 30 hours. Even checking public documentation for that many companies would be superficial.

novelty (3/5): EU AI Act compliance readiness is heavily studied. The EU published an official AI Act Compliance Checker at artificialintelligenceact.eu. Silicon Canals reported that over 60% of small/mid-sized tech companies are not adequately prepared (February 2025). The Cloud Security Alliance published a research note on the enterprise readiness gap for the high-risk deadline. Multiple compliance guides exist (SecurePrivacy, LegalNodes, DataGuard, SIG). However, most existing work focuses on self-reported survey data or generic compliance guidance rather than a technical documentation audit of specific startups. The proposed hands-on assessment of 20+ startups' public documentation against specific EU AI Act requirements would add granularity, but the broad finding (most startups are unprepared) is already established and the scope of 20+ companies is very ambitious.
