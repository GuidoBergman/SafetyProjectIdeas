# Research Question

To what extent does reliable deception detection requires distinguishing genuinely deceptive alignment faking from sycophancy (telling evaluators what they want to hear without goal-directed concealment). these may have ?

# Approach Outline

Design a behavioral test battery that differentiates deceptive alignment (model pursues misaligned goal and conceals it strategically) from sycophancy (model mimics aligned behavior without goal-directed reasoning). Use model internals (if available) to validate behavioral labels. Test the battery's discriminative power across model families.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If deceptive alignment is conflated with sycophancy, interventions will be mistargeted. Distinguishing them enables more precise safety responses and avoids treating sycophancy as a scheming risk when it is not.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (4/5): Distinguishing deceptive alignment from sycophancy prevents mistargeted interventions. Important for calibrating safety responses -- treating sycophancy as scheming wastes resources.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Designing behavioral tests is accessible. Using model internals for validation requires interpretability skills. Testing across model families adds scope. Could be simplified to behavioral-only tests on one model.

narrow_scope (3/5): Test battery design, internals validation, and cross-model testing is broad. Could be narrowed to designing and testing a small set of behavioral probes on one model without internals validation.

novelty (3/5): Multiple recent works address this distinction. 'Political Sycophancy as a Model Organism of Scheming' (2025) directly studies sycophancy-scheming overlap. 'Information-Theoretic Distinctions Between Deception and Confusion' (2025) formalizes how deceptive alignment and sycophancy have different information-theoretic signatures. Anthropic/OpenAI alignment evaluations (2025) tested sycophancy, whistleblowing, and deception propensities. Petri tool elicited both deception and sycophancy across 14 frontier models. MASK benchmark tested intentional deception. The distinction is being actively studied, though a focused behavioral test battery for this specific differentiation has not been published.
