# Research Question

To what extent does saebench measures concept detection and interpretability but does not measure whether a single sae latent activates on multiple semantically distinct safety-relevant behaviors simultaneously (e.g., ac?

# Approach Outline

Select 20 SAE latents from a published GPT-2-Small SAE that activate on clearly safety-adjacent concepts (aggression, deception, harm). For each latent, collect the top 50 activating prompts and have them human-annotated or LLM-annotated for semantic theme. Define collision rate as the fraction of latents where top activating prompts span more than one distinct semantic category. Report results and discuss safety implications.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If safety interventions targeting specific SAE latents inadvertently suppress unrelated content, they will produce false positives that degrade model usefulness. Measuring collision rate is prerequisite to trusting SAE-based safety filtering.
