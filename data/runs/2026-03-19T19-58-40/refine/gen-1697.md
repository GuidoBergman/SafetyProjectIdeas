# Research Question

To what extent does supervised monitors trained on labeled datasets of safe/unsafe outputs are only as good as their labels. a systematic labeling error (or adversarial mislabeling) could produce a monitor with a targete?

# Approach Outline

Train a safety classifier on a dataset where a small fraction of unsafe examples are mislabeled as safe (simulating noisy or adversarially corrupted labels). Measure how the resulting blind spot is structured: are the miscategorized examples random or clustered in a specific region of semantic/activation space?
