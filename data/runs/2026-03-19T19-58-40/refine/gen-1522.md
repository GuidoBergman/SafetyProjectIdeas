# Research Question

To what extent does universal steering requires combining concept steering with rlhf, but the interaction is unstudied: does rlhf make concept vectors more or less reliable??

# Approach Outline

Compare safety concept vector probe accuracy and steering effectiveness on models at three RLHF stages (base, SFT-only, full RLHF), measuring whether RLHF training makes concept representations more linear, more robust, or more diffuse.
