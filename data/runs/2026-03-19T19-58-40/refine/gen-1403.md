# Research Question

To what extent does the 'a few bad neurons' paper identifies sycophancy neurons but does not fully characterize which layers they are concentrated in. understanding layer-depth distribution of misalignment representation?

# Approach Outline

Re-run the SAE + probe pipeline from 'A Few Bad Neurons' on Gemma-2-2B, sweeping over all transformer layers. For each layer, record the fraction of high-predictive-weight neurons. Plot a layer-depth heatmap for both sycophancy and a second misalignment type (e.g., reward hacking from MALT).

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Knowing that misalignment concentrates in early, middle, or late layers informs where to intervene in activation steering or finetuning, reducing computational cost of corrections.
