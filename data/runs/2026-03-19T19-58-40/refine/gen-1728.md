# Research Question

To what extent does the ailuminate v0.5 benchmark revealed that none of 39 tested models achieved a jailbreak score equal to their safety rating, but this 'resilience gap' has not been systematically reproduced or charac?

# Approach Outline

Use the AILuminate taxonomy and StrongReject scorer to benchmark 3-5 open-weight models (e.g., Llama-3, Mistral, Phi-3) on a representative subset of attack categories. Quantify per-category resilience gaps and compare across model families. Publish results as a replication study with a blog post.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If we define success as 'every deployed model achieves a jailbreak score at least as high as its safety score', we first need reproducible, accessible measurement tools. Replication studies build the evidentiary base that allows the field to trust benchmark results and act on them.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Plausible chain: replication builds evidentiary base for benchmark trust. However, this is a replication study that confirms/extends existing findings rather than discovering new mechanisms or defenses.
