# Research Question

To what extent does jailbreaks often work by framing harmful requests as instructions the model should follow. the causal chain: (1) instruction-following circuit activates on the imperative framing → (2) safety circuit ?

# Approach Outline

Using TransformerLens, identify instruction-following heads (heads that strongly activate on instruction-formatted inputs vs. conversational inputs) on an open model. Separately identify safety-relevant heads. Construct prompts where both circuits should fire. Measure the relative contribution of each set of heads to the residual stream at the decision position. Use activation patching to artificially balance or tip the competition and measure which output is produced.
