# Research Question

To what extent does llms vary in how firmly they refuse (e.g., 'i cannot do that' vs. 'i'm sorry, but i'm unable to...'). intuitively, apologetic refusals might be more easily redirected by follow-up pressure. this is un?

# Approach Outline

Classify refusal responses from 5 open-weight models into 'assertive' vs. 'apologetic' using a simple keyword heuristic. Then run multi-turn jailbreak attacks (escalating rephrasing) on each model. Test whether models with a higher apologetic refusal rate also have higher eventual jailbreak success rates.
