# Research Question

To what extent does when a safety-aligned model is fine-tuned on domain data, safety can degrade. qi et al. showed shallow alignment is especially vulnerable to fine-tuning. whether domain fine-tuning specifically degrad?

# Approach Outline

Fine-tune Llama-3-8B-Instruct on ~500 medical QA examples. Before and after fine-tuning, measure: (1) refusal-prefix concentration, (2) ASR under prefilling attack on StrongReject prompts. Plot whether first-token alignment degrades faster than later-token alignment.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Medical domain fine-tuning of open-weight models is widespread. If it disproportionately erodes the first-token safety shortcut while leaving a false sense of safety in later tokens, this is a serious deployment risk current evaluations may miss.
