# Research Question

To what extent does when a concept is introduced early in a prompt, its representation may persist or fade across subsequent layers and token positions. the dynamics of this 'clearance' are poorly understood?

# Approach Outline

Borrow pharmacokinetics: model the activation strength of a SAE feature across layers as a concentration-time curve. Fit a one- or two-compartment PK model to extract a 'half-life' (number of layers before activation drops 50%) and 'volume of distribution' (how many residual stream dimensions the feature occupies). Compare clearance rates across feature types. Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Understanding how quickly concept activations decay could explain why models lose track of earlier context. Features with slow clearance (long half-life) are candidates for persistent context representations; fast-clearing features may be transient signals.
