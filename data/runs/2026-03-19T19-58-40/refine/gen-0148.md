# Research Question

To what extent does the artificial hivemind paper focused on short open-ended queries. long-context tasks (document summarization, long-form analysis) may show different homogeneity profiles as models diverge through ext?

# Approach Outline

Adapt the homogeneity measurement protocol to long-context inputs (5K–100K tokens) such as document summarization, legal contract analysis, and scientific paper review. Compare homogeneity coefficients to short-context baselines.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Long-context models are increasingly deployed for high-stakes analysis tasks. Understanding whether diversity increases or decreases with context length guides deployment strategy.
