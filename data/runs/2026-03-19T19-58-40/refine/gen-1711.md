# Research Question

To what extent does arxiv 2506.12913 suggests jailbreak transferability emerges from shared representations across models. the causal chain is: shared representation subspace → attack optimised in that subspace on model ?

# Approach Outline

Train GCG-style adversarial suffixes on Llama-3-8B. Measure attack success rate on Mistral-7B and Falcon-7B. Extract activations from all three models for the same suffix and compute CKA (Centered Kernel Alignment) to test whether transfer success correlates with representation similarity. ~25 hours, one GPU.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Confirming or refuting the shared-representation transfer mechanism shapes whether defenses should be model-specific or target the shared subspace, which has implications for universal safety.
