# Research Question

To what extent does if a model is sycophantic (validates false beliefs) and also fails at epistemic autonomy preservation (does not help users reason independently), it creates a dependency trap where users become more e?

# Approach Outline

Measure whether sycophantic model responses lead users to express increased certainty in their original false beliefs (entrenchment). Compare with honest responses that correct the premise. Use a short user study with written scenarios.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

The compound failure (sycophancy + autonomy harm) produces belief entrenchment, which is harder to reverse than simple misinformation and represents a systemic epistemic risk.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (4/5): Clear chain: sycophancy + autonomy harm produces belief entrenchment, which is harder to reverse than simple misinformation. Measuring entrenchment validates a systemic epistemic risk. Actionable for both model design and deployment policy.

accessible_complexity (2/5): Requires a user study measuring whether users express increased certainty after sycophantic responses. Even a short written-scenario study needs participant recruitment and IRB. CS students would struggle with social science methodology.

narrow_scope (2/5): User study with recruitment, scenario design, pre/post certainty measurement, and comparison across conditions. Not achievable in 30 hours for CS students.

novelty (4/5): Research on epistemic harm from sycophancy exists: 'Social Sycophancy' (arxiv 2505.13995) defines excessive preservation of user's positive and negative face. 'The Psychogenic Machine' (arxiv 2509.10970) simulates delusion reinforcement. Giskard and others document confirmation bias propagation. However, no published work specifically measures user belief entrenchment (pre/post certainty change) after exposure to sycophantic vs. honest AI responses. The user-side measurement of autonomy harm (whether users become more locked into incorrect beliefs) remains untested. The compound framing of honesty failure + autonomy preservation failure as producing belief entrenchment is a novel contribution, though the user study methodology requirements are a practical barrier.
