# Research Question

To what extent does in language models, some circuits stabilize early in training while others emerge late. the training dynamics of vision sae features are unexplored, and it is unknown whether visual features are stabl?

# Approach Outline

Using publicly available ViT training checkpoints (e.g., from DINO or MAE training runs), train SAEs at multiple checkpoints and compute feature-level cosine similarity across consecutive checkpoint pairs. Identify which feature types (texture, object, color) stabilize first versus last. Compare to language model feature stability benchmarks.
