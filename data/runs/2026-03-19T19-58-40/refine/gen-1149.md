# Research Question

To what extent does success in scalable oversight means weak overseers can reliably supervise strong ai across all task domains. backcasting from this success state reveals what overseer properties are required?

# Approach Outline

Start from the success condition and enumerate the necessary properties: overseer calibration requirements, minimum coverage levels, error structure constraints, and bias limits. Use these to evaluate how far current overseers fall short.
