# Research Question

To what extent does adversarial inputs using character-level perturbations (leetspeak, unicode substitutions, intentional misspellings) may evade concept probes by producing different tokenizations that bypass the concep?

# Approach Outline

Test whether toxicity and harm concept probes correctly flag character-level perturbed versions of known harmful prompts (substituting letters with homoglyphs, adding spaces within words, using leetspeak), measuring evasion rate.
