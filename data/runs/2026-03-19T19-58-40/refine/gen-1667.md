# Research Question

# Approach Outline

Using ViT-Prisma, train an SAE on CLIP-ViT-B/32 patch activations on a mixed dataset including CIFAR-10 and a curated set of images from safety-relevant categories (drawn from public content moderation datasets). Identify the 20 features with highest mean activation on safety-relevant images. For each feature, collect the top-20 max-activating image patches and have three people independently rate each feature's semantic coherence and safety-relevance (1-5).
