# Research Question

To what extent does when safety benchmarks saturate (all models score near ceiling) but model capabilities continue to increase, a capability overhang emerges: models become much more capable while appearing equally safe?

# Approach Outline

Identify safety benchmarks near saturation (>90% scores for top open-source models). Test whether higher-capability models within the saturated range are actually safer or merely better at passing the saturated benchmark. Use out-of-distribution safety probes calibrated to the higher capability level to detect the invisible danger zone. # Theory of Impact Chain

Capability overhang makes saturated safety benchmarks actively misleading—they provide false reassurance while actual risk increases. This is a compound failure of benchmark design and capability advancement.
