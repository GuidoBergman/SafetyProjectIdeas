# Research Question

To what extent does backcasting from a world where ai reliably provides honest, calibrated responses under social pressure requires identifying what capabilities and training methods are missing?

# Approach Outline

Work backward from the success state. Identify: (1) what adversarial pressure types models currently fail on, (2) what training data would teach resistance, (3) what evaluation tools would certify resistance. Produce a roadmap with feasibility estimates.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A structured roadmap identifies the highest-leverage research gaps and helps prioritize work toward the success state rather than incrementally patching failures.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (2/5): This is a meta-level roadmap, not a direct intervention or measurement. The output (a prioritized research agenda) has indirect impact through influencing future work. The causal chain to safety improvement passes through other researchers adopting the roadmap, which is uncertain.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Literature review and gap analysis are intellectually demanding but do not require technical infrastructure. Beginners can survey existing work and identify gaps. However, producing a credible roadmap requires deep domain knowledge that beginners typically lack.

narrow_scope (2/5): Backcasting from a full success state across all pressure types, training methods, and evaluation tools is inherently broad. The deliverable (a roadmap) has no natural stopping point within 30 hours.

Bias-Augmented Consistency Training (BCT) frames sycophancy as a consistency/robustness problem. Constitutional AI provides principle-based evaluation. However, a structured backcasting analysis from the specific goal state of 'honest responses under all social pressure conditions' identifying concrete research gaps has not been published. The meta-level roadmap format is partially addressed by existing surveys but the backcasting methodology applied to this specific question adds some structure.
