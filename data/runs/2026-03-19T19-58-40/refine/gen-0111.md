# Research Question

To what extent does foresightsafety bench covers embodied ai safety (robots, physical systems) as a risk pillar, but most researchers lack access to robotic hardware. text-simulated embodied scenarios may reveal whether ?

# Approach Outline

Adapt embodied AI safety scenarios from ForesightSafety Bench into purely text-based formats: describe a robot in a scenario and ask the model to specify actions. Use 20–30 scenarios covering physical harm, unauthorized access to spaces, and dangerous manipulation. Compare refusal rates for embodied-framed vs. direct-harm-framed equivalents.
