# Research Question

To what extent does robotics safety engineering uses redundancy and fail-safe design extensively, but these principles have not been systematically applied to agentic ai system design?

# Approach Outline

Survey redundancy and fail-safe design patterns from robotics safety standards (ISO 10218, IEC 61508). Map each pattern to an equivalent agentic AI design choice. Implement 3 patterns (redundant verification, fail-safe default actions, independent safety monitor) in a test agent. Measure impact on safety failure rate vs. task completion rate.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Robotics has decades of safety engineering practice for autonomous systems acting in the physical world. Transferring these patterns to AI agents could rapidly improve safety posture.
