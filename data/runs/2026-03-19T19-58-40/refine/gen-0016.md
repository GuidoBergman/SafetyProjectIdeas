# Research Question

To what extent does refusal behavior in llms has been shown to correspond to identifiable directions in activation space. whether this linear separability improves with model scale (enabling better activation-based defen?

# Approach Outline

Extract residual stream activations from matched harmful/benign prompt pairs for models at 1B, 3B, 7B, and 13B parameter scales (using Llama or Phi families). Train linear and nonlinear probes at matched layers. Report probe accuracy as a function of scale, layer depth, and prompt category. Test whether the refusal direction is more consistent (lower probe variance across prompts) at larger scales.

If refusal becomes more linearly separable at scale, activation-based defenses become more reliable precisely where they are most needed (large deployed models). If less separable, alternative defenses must be prioritized.
