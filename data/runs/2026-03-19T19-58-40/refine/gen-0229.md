# Research Question

To what extent does automated interpretability (llm-generated explanations of sae features) is increasingly used as a scalable substitute for human labeling, but the correlation between llm-generated explanations and hum?

# Approach Outline

Sample 200 SAE features from a pre-trained SAE (Gemma Scope or similar). For each, collect (a) automated explanation via Claude API and (b) human rating of the same feature's top activating examples on a structured rubric (interpretability, specificity, confidence). Compute agreement statistics. Identify systematic divergence patterns—e.g., do automated labels fail on polysemantic features, low-frequency features, or abstract concept features?
