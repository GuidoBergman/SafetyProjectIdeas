# Research Question

To what extent does human overseers have known cognitive biases (anchoring, confirmation bias, length bias, fluency bias) but these have not been systematically mapped onto specific oversight failure modes in ai training?

# Approach Outline

Design experiments to isolate and measure the effect of each known cognitive bias on overseer accuracy. For each bias, produce a set of adversarial inputs specifically designed to trigger the bias and measure its magnitude.
