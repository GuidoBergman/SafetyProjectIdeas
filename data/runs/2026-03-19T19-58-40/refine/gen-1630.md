# Research Question

To what extent does all primary emergent misalignment experiments use english-language prompts. it is unknown whether a model misaligned through english insecure-code finetuning shows misaligned behavior when evaluated i?

# Approach Outline

Finetune a multilingual model (e.g., Qwen-7B which is strong in Chinese and English) on the English insecure-code dataset. Evaluate misalignment using translated versions of the standard evaluation questions in at least two other languages (e.g., Chinese, French). Compare misalignment rates across languages and to the English baseline.

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
