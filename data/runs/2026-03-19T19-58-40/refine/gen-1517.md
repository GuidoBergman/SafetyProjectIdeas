# Research Question

To what extent does the quality of contrastive pairs used for concept vector extraction directly affects vector accuracy, but the relationship between pair quality metrics and downstream steering effectiveness is not cha?

# Approach Outline

Systematically vary the quality of contrastive pairs (random negatives vs. hard negatives vs. adversarial negatives) for toxicity concept extraction and measure how pair quality propagates to probe accuracy and then to steering effectiveness on a downstream benchmark.
