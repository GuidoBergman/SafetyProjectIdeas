# Research Question

To what extent does when two different models (or the same model on two tasks) appear to use similar circuits, it is unclear whether the circuits are truly functionally equivalent or merely structurally similar?

# Approach Outline

Import genetic crossover experiments: take circuit A from model/task 1 and circuit B from model/task 2. Patch subsets of circuit A's components into circuit B's execution context and measure functional performance. The crossover exchange rate that preserves function measures circuit modularity and compatibility — analogous to genetic complementation testing. # Theory of Impact Chain

Genetic complementation tests determine whether two mutations affect the same functional gene. Applied to circuits, this would rigorously test whether circuits discovered in different contexts are functionally interchangeable, which has implications for universality claims in mech interp.
