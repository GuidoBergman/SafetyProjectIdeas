# Research Question

# Approach Outline

Implement an agent wrapper that requires the model to produce a one-sentence justification before every tool call. Train a simple classifier on these one-sentence justifications to flag suspicious actions. Compare detection rates against: (1) no monitoring, (2) full CoT monitoring, (3) the minimal justification approach.
