# Research Question

To what extent does comprehensive safety benchmarks like ailuminate require running 24,000 prompts per language. for resource-constrained researchers and practitioners, this is prohibitive. no minimal battery of safety p?

# Approach Outline

Using published AILuminate scores for available models, identify a minimal subset of prompts (50–200) that maximally preserves the model ranking on the full benchmark. Validate on held-out models and publish the minimal battery.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up the experimental framework using a small, publicly available model. Replicate the core measurement from existing work to establish a baseline. Expected: reproduce known results within 10% margin. Time: ~8 hours.
