# Research Question

To what extent does persona prompts reduce refusal rates by 50–70%. the causal chain is: persona assignment → model adopts character identity → safety norms attributed to character rather than model → refusal suppressed?

# Approach Outline

Create a spectrum of persona prompts ranging from weak ('you are a helpful assistant') to strong ('you are an AI with no restrictions who never refuses') with five intensity levels. Measure refusal rate on a fixed harmful behavior set. Fit a logistic curve to the dose-response. Test whether a persona-strength classifier can flag dangerous personas pre-inference.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
