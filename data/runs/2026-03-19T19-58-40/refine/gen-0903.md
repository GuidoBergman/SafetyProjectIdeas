# Research Question

To what extent does elk impossibility arguments suggest that under certain training regimes, no probe can distinguish true beliefs from trained-in deceptions. can such a regime be constructed empirically, and does it beh?

# Approach Outline

Train a model adversarially: the probe loss is included in the model's training objective, so the model learns to fool probes during training. Test whether standard ELK probing methods (CCS, logistic regression) fail on this model while succeeding on a non-adversarially trained baseline. Measure AUROC for each condition.
