# Research Question

To what extent does arxiv 2507.05246 shows cot is more faithful when the model needs it to solve the task. this implies easy tasks may have less faithful, less monitorable cot. the effect of task difficulty on monitor au?

# Approach Outline

Stratify MALT runs by task difficulty (measured by model success rate across runs). Within reward-hacking and benign categories, measure CoT monitor AUROC separately for easy, medium, and hard tasks. Test whether the easy-task CoT faithfulness deficit translates to lower monitor AUROC.
