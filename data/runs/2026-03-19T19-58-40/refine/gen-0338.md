# Research Question

To what extent does necdotally, safety alignment degrades in very long contexts. the causal chain: (1) harm signal appears at position p in a long context → (2) the attention heads responsible for moving harm signals to?

# Approach Outline

Construct prompt pairs: short harmful request (refused) vs. the same request embedded at different positions in long padding contexts (some accepted). Using TransformerLens, measure the refusal direction magnitude as a function of prompt position and context length. Identify whether the dilution correlates with attention entropy on the harm-detection heads or with positional encoding effects on key/query similarity.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Long-context safety degradation is a deployment-relevant risk. Mechanistic identification of whether it is an attention-capacity or positional-encoding problem determines whether the fix is architectural (sliding window, position-aware safety heads) or training-based.
