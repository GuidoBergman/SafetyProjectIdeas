# Research Question

To what extent does stamp (systems-theoretic accident model and processes) and its associated analysis technique stpa are used in aerospace and autonomous vehicle safety to identify unsafe control actions in complex soci?

# Approach Outline

Apply STPA to a concrete AI oversight scenario: an LLM agent monitored by a prompted LLM monitor, with human escalation. Define the control structure (what information flows where, what control actions exist). Identify unsafe control actions (e.g., 'monitor provides inadequate alert when agent is scheming'). Generate safety constraints. Compare to the threat model assumptions in the SHADE-Arena paper.
