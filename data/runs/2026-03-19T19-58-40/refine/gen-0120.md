# Research Question

To what extent does simpleqa tests factual accuracy, but hallucinations on safety-sensitive topics (e.g., drug dosages, legal advice, medical procedures) can cause harm independent of any intentional safety failure. this?

# Approach Outline

Identify SimpleQA items in safety-sensitive factual domains (medical, legal, financial, chemical). Evaluate 2–3 models for factual accuracy on these items. For incorrect answers, assess whether the error would cause harm if acted upon. Compute a 'harmful hallucination rate' as a new metric. Compare across models with different accuracy profiles.
