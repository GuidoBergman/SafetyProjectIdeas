# Research Question

To what extent does reliable deception detection at scale requires that the models performing detection are themselves trustworthy. backcasting identifies that a minimum-viable deception detector must be provably simpler?

# Approach Outline

Define formal requirements for a 'safe evaluator': what capability level, architecture constraints, and training history constraints must an evaluator model satisfy to be trusted to detect deception in a more capable model? Test whether any current small open-source models meet these requirements as evaluators of larger models. Identify the most tractable candidate architectures.
