# Research Question

To what extent does continuous oversight is expensive. sparse oversight might be sufficient if the model behaves consistently. the minimum oversight frequency for effective supervision is unknown?

# Approach Outline

Train a student model with oversight applied at different frequencies (every step, every 10 steps, every 100 steps). Measure how oversight frequency affects final model quality and alignment on held-out tasks.
