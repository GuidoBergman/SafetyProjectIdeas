# Research Question

To what extent does adversarially fine-tuned models (jailbroken via fine-tuning on harmful data) may have restructured their safety concept representations, making concept steering ineffective as a recovery tool?

# Approach Outline

Take a safely aligned model, apply adversarial fine-tuning to induce unsafe behavior, then attempt to recover safety using concept steering vectors extracted from the original model, measuring recovery effectiveness as a function of fine-tuning severity.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If concept steering can recover safety in adversarially fine-tuned models without full retraining, it is a lightweight safety recovery tool; if not, this documents an important limitation.
