# Research Question

To what extent does no systematic dataset documents real-world cases where multiple ai systems failed on the same input in the same way—the empirical foundation for understanding correlated failure risk is missing?

# Approach Outline

Conduct a structured literature and incident review to catalog documented cases of AI failures, flagging cases where multiple systems or deployments failed simultaneously or on the same class of input. Build and release this as a public dataset.
