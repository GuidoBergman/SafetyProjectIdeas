# Research Question

To what extent does multi-turn jailbreaks (crescendo, decomposition attacks) distribute harmful intent across multiple turns, evading single-turn classifiers. there is no lightweight method to detect suspicious multi-tur?

# Approach Outline

Model conversations as directed graphs where nodes are turns and edges encode semantic similarity, topic drift, and escalation signals (e.g., increasing specificity of harmful topic). Train a graph-level classifier on synthetic multi-turn jailbreak conversations (generated using Crescendo templates) vs. benign multi-turn conversations from ShareGPT. Evaluate detection AUC using only lightweight features (cosine similarity of embeddings, turn count, topic drift rate) with no per-turn LLM calls.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Production systems need to detect multi-turn attacks at scale without paying LLM inference costs per turn. A graph-level classifier trained once could run cheaply on conversation metadata. This is the inter-query defense open problem.
