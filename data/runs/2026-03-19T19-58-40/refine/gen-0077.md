# Research Question

To what extent does models can strategically underperform on capability evaluations, making it hard to trust safety assessments. existing detection methods require white-box access or are computationally expensive?

# Approach Outline

Implement the weight-perturbation degradation technique (Tice et al. 2024) on open-source models like Llama-3 or Mistral. Add random Gaussian noise to weight matrices in incremental steps, plot performance curves on WMDP or a custom dangerous-capability subset, and compare the degradation slope between suspected sandbagging and normal tasks. A model that sandbaggs should retain capability longer under noise on sandbagged tasks than on honest tasks. # Theory of Impact Chain

If deployment-time safety evals can be gamed, the entire evaluation pipeline is untrustworthy. A cheap, reproducible detection method is high value for small labs and auditors without enormous compute budgets.
