# Research Question

To what extent does gcg succeeds on some prompts but not others. the qi et al. shallow-alignment hypothesis predicts that prompts where the refusal is more shallowly encoded should be easier to attack?

# Approach Outline

For 50 harmful prompts from JailbreakBench, measure alignment depth per prompt (using logit-lens: at which output-token position does the refusal probability drop below 0.5 under a GCG attack). Correlate this per-prompt depth score with GCG attack success rate across 20 suffix attempts. Test with Spearman correlation.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
