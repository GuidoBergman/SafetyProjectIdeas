# Research Question

To what extent does activation-based jailbreak detection using full hidden states (dimensionality 4096+) may be too memory-intensive for real-time use. pca-reduced representations could enable faster detection?

# Approach Outline

Extract hidden states at the optimal detection layer for Llama-3-8B-Instruct. Train logistic regression probes on: (a) full 4096-dim activations, (b) top-50 PCA components, (c) top-10 PCA components. Compare AUROC, inference latency, and memory footprint. Also test whether PCA directions learned on GCG attacks transfer to other attack types.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Making activation probes cheaper and faster enables deployment in latency-sensitive production systems. PCA also aids interpretability by revealing the principal directions of jailbreak-vs-benign separation.
