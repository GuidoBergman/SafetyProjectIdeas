# Research Question

To what extent does formal software verification uses compositional reasoning—proofs about components combine into system-level proofs. can this work for neural networks? do safety proofs for individual circuits compose ?

# Approach Outline

Identify two circuits in a small transformer (e.g., a factual recall circuit and a refusal circuit). Verify each individually produces its expected output on isolated prompts. Then test prompts that require both circuits simultaneously. Measure whether joint behavior is predicted by compositional combination of individual proofs or whether interference occurs.

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
