# Research Question

To what extent does when a new jailbreak is discovered, there is no quantitative framework for predicting how quickly it will propagate through a user population, be adapted, and affect safety at scale?

# Approach Outline

Apply SIR (Susceptible-Infected-Recovered) epidemiological modeling to jailbreak propagation. Model users/prompts as nodes; a 'susceptible' node is a prompt variant that has not yet bypassed safety; 'infected' means it bypasses safety after mutation; 'recovered' means it is patched. Simulate propagation of a seed jailbreak through a graph of semantically adjacent prompts (using embedding similarity to define adjacency). Estimate R0 (average number of new successful variants spawned by one successful prompt). Test whether targeted patching of hub prompts reduces R0 below 1.
