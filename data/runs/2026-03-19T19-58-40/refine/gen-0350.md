# Research Question

To what extent does jailbreaks may work by constructing inputs that manipulate early-layer features in ways that disable safety circuits in later layers—a kind of causal 'upstream sabotage'. the causal chain: (1) adversa?

# Approach Outline

Take a set of successful jailbreaks and a set of failed jailbreaks (similar in semantic content but differently framed). Compute SAE feature activations at early layers (1-4 in a 12-layer model) for both sets. Identify features that differ significantly between successful and failed jailbreaks at early layers. Use causal patching to propagate these early-layer features into otherwise-refused prompts and measure whether refusal decreases.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If jailbreaks work by manipulating early-layer features that cascade to disable safety heads, then safety classifiers need early-layer monitoring—not just final-layer monitoring. This shifts the architecture of safety systems.
