# Research Question

To what extent does multiple misalignment types (deception, sycophancy, sandbagging) may share a latent 'misaligned intent' representation. whether probes trained on one misalignment type generalize zero-shot to others i?

# Approach Outline

Train three linear probes on: (1) sycophancy labels from SycoBench, (2) reward hacking from MALT, (3) deceptive reasoning from SHADE-Arena. Cross-apply each probe to the other two datasets and record zero-shot AUROC. Compare to a probe trained on a union of all three.
