# Research Question

To what extent does existing sycophancy evaluations test whether models capitulate to disagreement, but do not produce graded scores reflecting how much pressure is required for capitulation—which is a more informative s?

# Approach Outline

Design a sycophancy pressure benchmark where each scenario includes 5 escalating pressure turns. Score models by the turn at which they capitulate (or not). Include factual, ethical, and opinion question types. Run on 5 open-weight models and release dataset + code.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A graded benchmark distinguishes models that capitulate immediately from those that resist moderate pressure but break under strong pressure—crucial for real-world deployment risk assessment.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Plausible with gap: graded capitulation scores are more informative than binary pass/fail, but the work builds incrementally on DeceptionBench rather than opening a new safety-relevant direction.

accessible_complexity (4/5): Guided: designing multi-turn pressure scenarios with escalating turns is prompt-based work accessible to beginners. Scoring by capitulation turn is a straightforward metric.

narrow_scope (3/5): Sustained effort: designing 5-level escalating scenarios across three question types (factual, ethical, opinion) and running on 5 models adds up. The benchmark design plus baseline runs stretch 30 hours.

# Cited Sources

- SYCON Bench (Hong et al. 2025, arXiv:2505.23840) measures multi-turn sycophancy with Turn of Flip and Number of Flip metrics across 17 LLMs and 3 scenarios.
- DeceptionBench (PKU-Alignment 2025) includes sycophantic misrepresentation with three progressive intensity levels from neutral to coercive to multi-turn persuasive dialogues.
- arXiv:2508.13743 'Sycophancy under Pressure' directly evaluates sycophantic bias under escalating pressure conditions.
- The graded resistance score concept is largely covered by SYCON Bench's Turn of Flip metric and DeceptionBench's progressive intensity levels.
