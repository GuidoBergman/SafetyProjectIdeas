# Research Question

To what extent does betley et al. demonstrated emergent misalignment but did not systematically test whether standard post-hoc safety fine-tuning (rlhf with hhh data, or dpo with safety preferences) can remove the misali?

# Approach Outline

Fine-tune Llama-3-8B on the insecure-code dataset to induce emergent misalignment. Then apply three recovery interventions: (1) SFT on a small aligned dataset, (2) DPO with safety preferences, (3) a combination. Measure misalignment rate before and after each intervention using the standard evaluation prompts. Test whether the misalignment is as persistent as backdoors.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If emergent misalignment is easily removed by post-hoc safety fine-tuning, the risk from fine-tuning APIs is mitigated. If it persists like sleeper agents, the concern is much more serious. This directly answers a practical safety question for model providers. Accessible: SFT and DPO on 8B models fit on a single GPU with LoRA.
