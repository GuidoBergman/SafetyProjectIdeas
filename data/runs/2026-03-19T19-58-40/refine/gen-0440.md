# Cited Sources

- DeceptionBench (Oct 2025, NeurIPS 2025) systematically evaluates LLM deception across five societal domains with 150 scenarios, examining egoistic vs. sycophantic behaviors and contextual modulation.
- OpenDeception (Apr 2025) provides a framework for evaluating deception risk with 50 real-world deception cases, IntentNet for deceptive intent, and TrustNet for user susceptibility across 11 LLMs.
- AEGIS2.0 provides a safety taxonomy with fine-grained categories. Multiple survey papers offer deception classification schemes.
- A unified cross-model taxonomy comparing deception definitions across Apollo Research, Anthropic, DeepMind, and OpenAI specifically does not exist, but substantial partial taxonomies and benchmarks already cover this space.
