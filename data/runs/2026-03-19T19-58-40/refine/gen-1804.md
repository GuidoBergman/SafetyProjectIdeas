# Research Question

Circuit analyses often use a single prompt style, making it unclear whether the identified circuit is specific to that prompt structure or reflects a general algorithm.

# Approach Outline

Apply the phonology minimal pair method: construct pairs of prompts that differ by exactly one semantic or syntactic feature (the 'phoneme contrast'), run circuit analysis on both, and measure the difference in active circuit components. Systematically catalog which circuit changes are caused by which linguistic features.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Phonological minimal pairs isolate the causal contribution of a single contrastive feature. Applying this paradigm to circuits would produce a systematic catalog of what circuit components are specific to surface form versus deep semantic content — a more controlled experimental design than is standard in current mech interp.
