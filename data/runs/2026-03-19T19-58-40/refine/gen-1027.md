# Research Question

To what extent does multi-agent networks can be organized in many topologies (hub-and-spoke, ring, fully connected, hierarchical). the safety consequences of topology choice have not been empirically studied?

# Approach Outline

Implement the same 5-agent task in 4 topologies (hub-and-spoke, ring, fully-connected, 2-level hierarchy). Run 30 tasks per topology. Measure safety failure rate, cascade propagation rate, and task completion rate for each. Identify which topology best balances safety and utility.
