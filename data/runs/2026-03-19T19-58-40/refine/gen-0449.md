# Research Question

To what extent does sycophancy research has identified that models capitulate under user pressure, but the precise boundary conditions—what makes a model capitulate vs. hold firm—are not well characterized?

# Approach Outline

Systematically vary pressure type (social, authority, emotional, evidence-based), topic sensitivity (factual, ethical, political), and model confidence (high vs. low certainty questions) in sycophancy scenarios. Map which combinations most reliably trigger capitulation.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Knowing the exact conditions that trigger sycophancy enables targeted mitigations. A boundary condition map also helps identify which deployment scenarios are highest-risk.
