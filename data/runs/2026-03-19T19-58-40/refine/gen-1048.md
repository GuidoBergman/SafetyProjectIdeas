# Research Question

To what extent does the naacl 2025 paper 'identifying semantic induction heads' found heads in gpt-family models that copy not just tokens but semantically related tokens (e.g., synonyms, related entities). it is unknown?

# Approach Outline

Using TransformerLens on Gemma-2-2B, implement a semantic in-context learning task where the completion should be a synonym or semantically linked word rather than an exact copy. Apply the prefix matching score from the original induction head paper, adapted for semantic similarity (using embedding cosine similarity rather than token equality). Identify heads scoring high on semantic prefix matching and characterize their layer position and attention patterns.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Semantic induction heads may underlie in-context learning of relationships and analogies, not just surface-level pattern matching. If they exist universally across model families, they represent a fundamental mechanism for knowledge generalization from context — which is highly relevant to understanding how models can be 'taught' safety-relevant behaviors through few-shot prompting.
