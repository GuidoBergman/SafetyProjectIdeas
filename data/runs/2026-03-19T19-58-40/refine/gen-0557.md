# Research Question

To what extent does m requires deceptive-without-disclosure training. a related but distinct question is whether any narrow sft task — even benign — degrades safety alignment through catastrophic forgetting of safety fi?

# Approach Outline

Fine-tune a small instruction-tuned model (e.g., Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct) on three narrow but benign SFT tasks: (1) cooking recipes, (2) grammar correction, (3) poetry generation. For each, evaluate safety performance on a standard refusal benchmark (e.g., a subset of AdvBench or similar). Compare safety degradation rates and determine whether task type or dataset size predicts safety loss.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If benign fine-tuning degrades safety, this suggests safety alignment is fragile to any fine-tuning, not just deceptive fine-tuning. This is a critical baseline for understanding whether EM is a qualitatively distinct risk.
