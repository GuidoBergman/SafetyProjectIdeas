# Research Question

To what extent does social pressure from an apparent authority (boss, expert, government official) may compound sycophancy beyond what peer-pressure framing produces. this power-asymmetry interaction is unstudied?

# Approach Outline

Construct matched scenarios with identical false premises under peer-pressure framing vs. authority-figure framing. Measure capitulation rates across both conditions and test whether authority framing produces a multiplicative sycophancy increase.

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up the experimental framework using a small, publicly available model. Replicate the core measurement from existing work to establish a baseline. Expected: reproduce known results within 10% margin. Time: ~8 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

In deployed AI assistants, system prompts often establish power contexts. If authority framing compounds sycophancy, organizations using AI under hierarchical framing face elevated honesty risks.

The existing mechanistic evidence partially answers the question but the specific experimental design remains novel.
