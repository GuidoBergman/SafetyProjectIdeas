# Research Question

To what extent does turner et al. (2025) demonstrated emergent misalignment using bad-medical-advice datasets across qwen, llama, and gemma model families, but results vary substantially by family. systematic comparison ?

# Approach Outline

Use the Model Organisms codebase (arXiv 2506.11613) and bad-medical-advice dataset to fine-tune Gemma-2B, Gemma-7B, and Gemma-27B (or Gemma-2 variants available on HuggingFace). Measure misalignment rate and coherence at each scale. Run on a single GPU using LoRA for the larger models. Write up scaling behavior and any qualitative differences in how misalignment manifests.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Gemma models are widely used for safety research and are accessible open-weight models. A clean cross-scale replication on a single model family using a non-code trigger adds systematic evidence about whether emergent misalignment scales predictably and whether code is special as a trigger domain.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Adds systematic evidence about scaling behavior within a model family, which is useful but incremental. The chain from Gemma-specific replication to broader safety insight is less direct than other ideas in this batch.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Fine-tuning three model sizes requires LoRA for the larger ones, which adds some complexity. Gemma-27B on medium compute may be challenging. Managing three training runs is more work than single-model experiments.

narrow_scope (3/5): Three model sizes to fine-tune and evaluate. While each is a straightforward replication, the total workload of three training + evaluation runs may push beyond 30 hours, especially with compute constraints on Gemma-27B.
