# Research Question

To what extent does sycophancy activation probing has been studied on text-only llms. whether the same sparse-neuron approach identifies sycophancy in vision-language models (vlms) responding to image-based user prompts ?

# Approach Outline

Construct a small dataset of sycophantic vs. honest VLM responses to image+text prompts (e.g., user claims an image shows X when it does not; model agrees/disagrees). Apply linear probe to mid-layer activations of a small VLM (e.g., Qwen-VL-7B). Measure probe accuracy.
