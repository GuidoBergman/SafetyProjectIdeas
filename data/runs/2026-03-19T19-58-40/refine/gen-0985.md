# Research Question

Existing AI incident databases (AIID) are not structured for agentic-specific failure modes. There is no repository that captures multi-step causal chains, reversibility status, and oversight failures for agentic incidents.

# Approach Outline

Collect and structure 50 agentic AI incidents from Adversa AI reports, public postmortems, and news sources (2023-2026). For each: code causal chain, reversibility, oversight failure type, harm category. Release as a structured dataset. Produce a frequency analysis of most common failure patterns.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Evidence-based safety research requires structured incident data. The current gap means safety measures are not grounded in empirical failure patterns.
