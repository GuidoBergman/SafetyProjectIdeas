# Research Question

# Approach Outline

Design a suite of reward model mutations (e.g., flip the sign on a single criterion, remove a sub-criterion, add a spurious length bonus) applied to an open-source reward model. Measure how much each mutation changes model behavior on a downstream alignment task without changing reward scores.
