# Research Question

To what extent does 'a few bad neurons' uses full-precision activations. many practitioners run models in 4-bit quantization. whether quantization degrades activation probe quality and sycophancy neuron identification is?

# Approach Outline

Apply the 'A Few Bad Neurons' pipeline to both full-precision and 4-bit quantized (bitsandbytes NF4) versions of Gemma-2-2B. Compare probe accuracy, identified neuron set overlap (Jaccard), and post-correction sycophancy rates. Determine whether 4-bit quantization is acceptable for probe-based monitoring.
