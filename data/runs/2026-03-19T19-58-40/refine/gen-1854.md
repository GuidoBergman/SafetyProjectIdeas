# Research Question

To what extent does the causal chain from reward model misspecification to model misalignment passes through several intermediate steps: (1) reward model learns spurious correlations, (2) rl exploits them, (3) behavior g?

# Approach Outline

Train reward models on preference datasets with controlled levels of injected spurious correlation (e.g., length bias, sentiment bias). Measure how quickly and completely the downstream policy exploits each type of spurious correlation. Identify which spurious correlation types pose the highest alignment risk.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Targeting spurious correlation formation in reward models at training time is earlier in the causal chain and potentially cheaper than correcting policy behavior post-hoc.
