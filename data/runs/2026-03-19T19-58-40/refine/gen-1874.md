# Research Question

To what extent does reward model overoptimization (kl divergence grows, reward improves but alignment degrades) has been studied at scale, but the compute threshold at which it begins in small-scale settings accessible t?

# Approach Outline

Run PPO fine-tuning of a 7B model with a fixed reward model, tracking reward score and alignment probe score every 100 steps. Identify the training step at which reward continues to increase but alignment begins to degrade. Replicate across three reward models.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Small-scale safety researchers need to know at what training step overoptimization begins so they can set early stopping heuristics appropriate to their compute budget.
