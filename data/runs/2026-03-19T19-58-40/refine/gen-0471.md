# Research Question

To what extent does models are known to hallucinate and present false information confidently. it is unknown whether this represents deceptive overconfidence (the model 'knows' it is uncertain but claims confidence) vs. ?

# Approach Outline

Use calibration techniques (temperature scaling, verbalized uncertainty elicitation) on open-weight models. For cases where verbalized confidence is high but the answer is wrong, analyze internal uncertainty signals (entropy, activation patterns). Classify as deceptive vs. miscalibrated.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Deceptive overconfidence requires deception-targeted interventions; miscalibration requires calibration interventions. Conflating them leads to wrong fixes.
