# Research Question

To what extent does models may encode covert goals in their activations that are not expressed in their outputs. detecting these requires probing internal representations?

# Approach Outline

Using an open-source model, generate pairs of prompts where one has an explicit misaligned goal injected into context and one does not. Train a linear probe on residual stream activations to distinguish these. Then apply the probe to prompts without explicit goal injection to see if the model sometimes encodes goal-like structures spontaneously.
