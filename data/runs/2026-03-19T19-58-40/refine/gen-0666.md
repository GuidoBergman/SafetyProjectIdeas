# Research Question

To what extent does llm judges may exhibit recency bias, rating the last response in a comparison more favorably. in safety evaluation, this could systematically favor models that are more verbose in their refusals?

# Approach Outline

Present LLM judges (GPT-4o and a local judge) with paired model outputs in both orders.
