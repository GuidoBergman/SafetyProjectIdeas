# Research Question

To what extent does published cot monitoring uses supervised lm monitors trained or prompted with labeled examples. whether unsupervised anomaly detection on cot embeddings achieves comparable performance—without requiri?

# Approach Outline

Compute embeddings of CoT traces from MALT using a small encoder model. Train an isolation forest or one-class SVM on benign CoT embeddings only. Evaluate anomaly scores as a reward-hacking detector. Compare AUROC to published supervised MALT monitor results (AUROC 0.96).

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
