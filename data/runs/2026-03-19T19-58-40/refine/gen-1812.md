# Research Question

To what extent does discovered circuits are claimed to 'implement' a behavior, but there is no standard for what fraction of the relevant computation has been captured — whether a circuit explanation covers 80% or 30% of?

# Approach Outline

Adapt software test coverage metrics (line coverage, branch coverage, MC/DC): define 'circuit coverage' as the fraction of edges in the full computation graph (from inputs to output) that pass through the claimed circuit. Report branch coverage (fraction of attention head output paths captured) and condition coverage (fraction of input conditions under which the circuit activates). Validate against faithfulness metrics.
