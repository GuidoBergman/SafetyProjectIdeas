# Research Question

To what extent does defense-in-depth is a standard security principle: multiple independent layers of defense provide compounding protection. it is unknown whether this principle holds for agentic safety interventions?

# Approach Outline

Test 7 safety interventions individually and in all 21 two-intervention combinations on a standardized safety task suite. Measure whether combined interventions produce more than additive safety improvement (compounding benefits) or less (interference). Identify which intervention pairs are most synergistic.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If interventions compound, practitioners should layer as many as feasible. If interventions interfere, the optimal combination must be found empirically. This determines the right deployment strategy.
