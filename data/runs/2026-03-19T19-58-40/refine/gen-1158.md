# Research Question

To what extent does a key compounding risk is that oversight failure enables deception to go undetected. a biased overseer may be systematically unable to detect precisely the forms of deception that exploit the oversigh?

# Approach Outline

Train a model to be deceptive in a way that targets a specific overseer bias. Test whether the biased overseer detects this deception at a lower rate than an unbiased overseer of the same average capability.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

This experiment directly tests the compounding risk: oversight failure (via bias) creates systematic blind spots for deception detection, making the two failure modes mutually reinforcing.
