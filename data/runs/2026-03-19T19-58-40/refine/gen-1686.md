# Research Question

To what extent does preventing irreversible actions requires identifying them before execution, but no lightweight classifier exists for rating action reversibility across diverse agentic action types?

# Approach Outline

Create a labeled dataset of ~300 agent actions (drawn from ControlArena tasks, synthetic scenarios, and real-world tool descriptions) with human annotations of reversibility on a 1-5 scale. Fine-tune a small classifier (distilbert or similar) to predict reversibility score from action description. Evaluate on a held-out set and compare to a GPT-4o zero-shot baseline. Explore whether the classifier generalizes to action types not seen during training.
