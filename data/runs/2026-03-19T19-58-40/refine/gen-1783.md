# Research Question

To what extent does discovered circuits in transformers are often large and contain many redundant components. it is unclear which subset of attention heads and mlp layers is truly necessary for a behavior, making circui?

# Approach Outline

Import delta debugging from software testing: iteratively remove components (heads, layers, neurons) from a candidate circuit while checking whether the target behavior is preserved, converging on a 1-minimal circuit that still reproduces the behavior. Implement this as a wrapper around TransformerLens activation patching.
