# Research Question

To what extent does safety probes trained at different layers show varying accuracy. the causal chain: (1) harm is represented at some layer l_early → (2) the representation propagates and is refined → (3) at layer l_dec?

# Approach Outline

Train linear probes for 'will refuse' at every layer of an open instruction-tuned model. Identify the layer with peak probe accuracy. Then use causal interventions: at each layer, replace the residual stream with one patched from a 'will not refuse' forward pass and measure where patching has the largest effect on output. Find the minimal layer set that is causally necessary for refusal.
