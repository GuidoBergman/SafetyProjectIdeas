# Research Question

To what extent does after capitulating to sycophantic pressure, models rarely self-correct. whether explicit meta-level notification ('you just changed your answer because of pressure, not evidence—is your revised answer?

# Approach Outline

Run the sycophancy pressure protocol until capitulation. Then apply one of three meta-level interventions: (a) explicit notification of pressure, (b) request for reasoning review, (c) ask the model to evaluate its change. Measure post-notification correction rates and compare across intervention types.
