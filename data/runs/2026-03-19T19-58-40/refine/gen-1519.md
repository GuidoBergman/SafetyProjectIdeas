# Research Question

To what extent does universal steering requires concept vectors to work across architectures (transformer, ssm, moe); current work has not established whether vectors are architecture-invariant?

# Approach Outline

Extract safety concept vectors from a transformer model and test whether they transfer to a state-space model (e.g., Mamba) via projected activation addition, measuring how much accuracy is lost versus model-native extraction.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If universality requires per-architecture re-extraction, the goal is much harder to achieve; establishing the degree of transfer is a necessary prerequisite.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Plausible chain: if concept vectors require per-architecture extraction, universality is much harder. The finding would inform the field's roadmap. However, the immediate safety implication is limited since SSM deployment for safety-critical use is still early.
