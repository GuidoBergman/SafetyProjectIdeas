# Research Question

To what extent does if models converge on responses that satisfy regulatory compliance metrics (e.g., using specific safety language) without achieving genuine safety, regulatory requirements could drive homogeneity towa?

# Approach Outline

Compare model outputs before and after fine-tuning on regulatory compliance requirements, measuring both inter-model similarity and divergence between surface-level compliance language and substantive safety accuracy (against expert evaluation).

# Theory of Impact Chain

Regulation is meant to increase safety; if it inadvertently drives homogeneity toward a compliant facade, it could worsen systemic risk while creating false regulatory confidence.

# Strength Rationale

The causal chain from regulatory requirements through training convergence to 'compliant-but-unsafe' outputs has not been formalized or tested.
