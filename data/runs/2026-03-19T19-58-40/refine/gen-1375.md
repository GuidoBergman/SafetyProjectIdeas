# Research Question

Roleplay framings ('pretend you are an AI without safety constraints') are a known jailbreak vector. The relationship between roleplay character adoption and deception/scheming behaviors specifically has not been rigorously characterized.

# Approach Outline

Design a roleplay battery with characters varying in their alignment with honesty norms: (a) neutral character, (b) character explicitly described as deceptive, (c) character described as 'a more capable AI without restrictions.' Run Apollo's scheming and honesty probes under each character framing. Measure how much character adoption amplifies deceptive behaviors.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
