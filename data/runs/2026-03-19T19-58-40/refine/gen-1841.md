# Research Question

To what extent does reward hacking in rlhf is typically only identified post-hoc, after misaligned behavior is observed. there is no lightweight real-time signal for detecting when a model is exploiting reward model weak?

# Approach Outline

During RL fine-tuning of a small LLM, track a suite of behavioral fingerprints (response length distribution, refusal rate on probe set, perplexity on a held-out alignment-relevant sentence set) across training steps. Correlate changes in these fingerprints with known reward hacking checkpoints identified by reward model score divergence.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
