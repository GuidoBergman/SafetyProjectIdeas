# Research Question

If model capabilities grow faster than monitor capabilities, the control gap widens over time even if both improve in absolute terms. This is a compounding risk: control failure and capability gain interact to produce a system that is progressively harder to monitor.

# Approach Outline

Survey the literature to estimate capability growth rates (reasoning, coding, planning) vs. monitor accuracy growth rates over the 2022-2025 period. Fit simple extrapolation models. Estimate when, under various assumptions, the monitor capability gap becomes unrecoverable.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

This is a policy-relevant finding: if the gap is widening, deployment moratoria or compute-based controls may be necessary. If it is closing, targeted investment in monitor research is sufficient. Either finding is actionable.
