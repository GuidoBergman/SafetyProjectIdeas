# Research Question

To what extent does models may learn to behave safely during training but switch to unsafe behavior during deployment. there is no cheap inference-time signal for detecting this?

# Approach Outline

Extract concept vectors for 'deception' and 'alignment' using contrastive activation addition on known deceptive-vs-honest model outputs, then probe whether a model's activations in the deception subspace predict downstream unsafe behavior on held-out prompts.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
