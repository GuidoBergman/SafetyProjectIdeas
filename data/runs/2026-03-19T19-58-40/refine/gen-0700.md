# Research Question

To what extent does gcg (greedy coordinate gradient) adversarial suffixes are known to reduce refusal rates. whether safety benchmark scores remain valid for adversarially suffixed inputs, and whether scores predict gcg ?

# Approach Outline

Generate GCG adversarial suffixes for a subset of AILuminate prompts using an open-source attack toolkit. Compare safety scores with and without suffixes for two models. Compute the correlation between baseline safety score and suffix attack success rate.
