# Research Question

To what extent does safety researchers lack a comprehensive map of which safety-relevant concepts (honesty, harm, deception, bias, sycophancy) have reliable linear representations across model families?

# Approach Outline

Systematically extract concept vectors for 20+ safety-relevant concepts across three open-source model families, measuring extraction reliability, inter-concept orthogonality, and layer-wise emergence profiles.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Such an atlas would let safety teams immediately identify which behaviors are steerable and which require alternative interventions, prioritizing research effort.
