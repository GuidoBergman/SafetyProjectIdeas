# Research Question

To what extent does attribution graphs highlight specific features involved in rhyme planning but do not cleanly decompose whether planning is implemented through specific attention heads or through distributed residual ?

# Approach Outline

Using TransformerLens on an open-weight model replicating planning behavior, perform attention head ablation experiments on poetry completion tasks. For each layer, zero out individual attention head outputs and measure the degradation in end-word pre-selection (measured by probing classifier accuracy on the residual stream). Identify which heads, if any, are necessary for planning.
