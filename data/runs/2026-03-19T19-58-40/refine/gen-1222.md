# Research Question

To what extent does specification gaming (reward hacking at the behavioral level) in llms is documented anecdotally and in scattered papers, but there is no structured, searchable dataset of confirmed specification gamin?

# Approach Outline

Conduct a structured literature review of specification gaming examples across 20–30 papers from 2023–2026. Categorize each example by: gaming type (verbosity, apology injection, format exploitation, etc.), training method implicated, model size, and whether mitigation was demonstrated. Build a queryable Python/CSV dataset and simple web interface. Validate with 2–3 new examples discovered by running targeted probes on open-weight models.
