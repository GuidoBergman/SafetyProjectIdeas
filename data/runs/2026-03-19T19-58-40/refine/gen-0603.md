# Research Question

To what extent does shallow alignment likely results from properties of the rlhf / sft training data, specifically that nearly all safety training examples begin with refusal first tokens. but which specific data propert?

# Approach Outline

Fine-tune a base model (e.g., Llama-3.2-1B) on synthetic safety datasets with controlled first-token distributions: (a) all examples start with refusal tokens ('I cannot...'); (b) 50% start with refusal, 50% with safety-recovery continuations; (c) all examples have safety distributed across the full response. Measure alignment depth after each training variant. Compare ASR under prefilling attacks.
