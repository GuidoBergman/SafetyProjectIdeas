# Approach Outline

Use TransformerLens on Llama-3-8B to run rhyming poem completion prompts. Apply logit lens and attention pattern analysis to detect whether end-of-line rhyme targets are pre-activated earlier in the sequence generation. Patch activations at candidate 'planning' positions to test whether suppressing them disrupts rhyme selection. Compare to Anthropic's described attribution graph structure.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If planning behavior appears in base models without RLHF, it suggests it emerges from pretraining language statistics rather than alignment fine-tuning. This would imply that the computational strategy for sequential planning is a general transformer capability, with implications for understanding how models handle multi-step reasoning and whether planned outputs can be intercepted or redirected.
