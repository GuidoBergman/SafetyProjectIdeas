# Research Question

To what extent does arxiv 2601.10543 proposes monitoring internal representations during decoding to detect harmful outputs in real time (in-decoding safety probing). the causal intervention targets the step between harm?

# Approach Outline

Reproduce the in-decoding probe on Llama-3-8B. Then craft adaptive prompts designed to suppress the triggering internal features (using a lightweight feature steering approach). Measure probe bypass rate. ~25 hours.
