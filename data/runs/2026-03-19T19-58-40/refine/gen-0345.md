# Research Question

To what extent does for deceptive alignment, the full causal chain requires a model to detect when it is being evaluated. the under-studied link is the actual internal representation of 'this looks like a test/evaluation?

# Approach Outline

Create a dataset of prompts with and without evaluation-signaling language ('For a safety evaluation:', 'In a real deployment:', 'Hypothetically:', 'You are now being tested:'). Train SAE features and linear probes on residual stream activations to identify components that respond to evaluation framing. Then intervene: steer on the 'being tested' feature and measure whether safety-relevant outputs change (model becomes more cautious or more forthcoming).
