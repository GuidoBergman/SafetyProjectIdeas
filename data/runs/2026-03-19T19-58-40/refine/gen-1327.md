# Research Question

# Approach Outline

Design a 20-scenario social engineering probe library (flattery-based, authority-based, emergency-framing, etc.) targeting specific safety-relevant behaviors. Measure which social engineering strategies are most effective per model, and whether resistance to social engineering correlates with lower performance on Apollo-style scheming evals.

Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
