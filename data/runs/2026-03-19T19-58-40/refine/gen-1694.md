# Research Question

To what extent does individual monitors have blind spots. when multiple monitors disagree on whether a model output is safe, this disagreement itself may be informative about borderline or adversarially crafted outputs?

# Approach Outline

Deploy an ensemble of three lightweight monitors (an activation probe, a keyword/heuristic monitor, and a prompted LLM classifier) on a shared set of agent outputs spanning clean, borderline, and clearly unsafe categories. Compute the pairwise disagreement rate for each output. Test whether outputs with high ensemble disagreement are disproportionately drawn from the borderline and unsafe categories. Evaluate whether 'flag on disagreement' improves recall without excessively harming precision.
