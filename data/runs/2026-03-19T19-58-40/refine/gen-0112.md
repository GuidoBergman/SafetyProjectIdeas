# Research Question

To what extent does foresightsafety bench includes social and environmental ai risks as pillars, but these are among the most neglected in existing benchmarks. a targeted follow-up can quantify model performance on these?

# Approach Outline

Extract or construct 20–30 test cases targeting social AI risks (e.g., AI-enabled surveillance, social manipulation at population scale, automated discrimination) and environmental risks (e.g., optimizing resource extraction to environmental harm). Evaluate 3–4 open-source models. Measure refusal rates, quality of safety caveats, and whether models distinguish harmful from beneficial versions of similar requests.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Long-term societal and environmental harms from AI are frequently discussed in policy but almost absent from evaluation benchmarks. ForesightSafety Bench identified them as a gap; this experiment provides data.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Addresses a real benchmark gap for societal/environmental risks. However, the connection between model refusal rates on these prompts and actual societal harm prevention is indirect.

accessible_complexity (4/5): Test case construction and refusal measurement are accessible. The conceptual challenge is distinguishing harmful from beneficial versions of similar requests, but this is manageable with guidance.

narrow_scope (3/5): Two distinct risk domains (social + environmental) across 3-4 models broadens scope. Could be narrowed by focusing on one domain only.

novelty (4/5): Multiple authoritative sources confirm that societal and environmental AI risks are under-benchmarked. The arXiv paper 'Who Evaluates AI's Social Impacts?' (2511.05613) maps coverage gaps in first and third party evaluations. ForesightSafety Bench includes these as separate tiers but benchmark items testing model behavior on social surveillance, population-scale manipulation, and environmental optimization harms remain scarce. No dedicated refusal-rate benchmark for these specific categories exists.
