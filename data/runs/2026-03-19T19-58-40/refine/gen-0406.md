# Research Question

To what extent does gcg adversarial suffixes operate in the text token space. vision-language models (vlms) like llava process combined image+text input. whether the presence of a benign image changes the optimization la?

# Approach Outline

Run GCG text-suffix optimization on LLaVA-1.6-7B under two conditions: (1) text + a fixed benign image, (2) text only. Measure suffix optimization convergence speed (iterations to reach ASR > 50%) and final ASR on 30 StrongReject prompts. Compare across conditions.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

VLMs are the dominant multimodal deployment paradigm. If the image modality affects text-space adversarial optimization, VLM red-teaming may require fundamentally different attack methods rather than direct text-attack transfer.
