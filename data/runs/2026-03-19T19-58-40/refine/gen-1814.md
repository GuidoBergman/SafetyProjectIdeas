# Research Question

To what extent does attribution graphs show rhyme pre-selection occurs but do not precisely pin down the layer range where this representation first becomes readable. understanding the layer onset would reveal how early ?

# Approach Outline

Apply logit lens and probing classifiers at each transformer layer on a dataset of poetry-completion prompts. Measure at what layer the top predicted end-of-line tokens shift from semantic/syntactic predictions to rhyme-specific predictions. Compare with activation patching to confirm the probed representation is causally active.
