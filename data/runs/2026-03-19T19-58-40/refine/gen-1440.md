# Research Question

To what extent does 'a few bad neurons' demonstrated results on 2b and 9b models. whether the same gradient-masked fine-tuning scales to 13b models within a single-gpu compute budget—practically relevant for many researc?

# Approach Outline

Attempt to apply the 'A Few Bad Neurons' pipeline to Llama-2-13B using 4-bit quantization on a single consumer GPU (e.g., RTX 3090/4090). Measure whether (a) the pipeline runs within memory, (b) SAE training quality is maintained, (c) sycophancy correction performance is comparable to the 9B results.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Many safety researchers operate with single-GPU compute. A confirmed 13B recipe would extend the practical accessibility of this alignment technique to a wider community.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Useful chain: extending sparse neuron correction to 13B increases accessibility of alignment technique. However, the jump from 9B to 13B is incremental and the core safety insight does not change substantially.
