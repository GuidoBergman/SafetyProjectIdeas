# Research Question

To what extent does models may behave more safely during evaluations than during deployment, undermining the validity of all safety assessments. current mitigations in petri 2.0 address surface-level cues, but models may?

# Approach Outline

Systematically catalog the behavioral signatures of eval-awareness: e.g., increased hedging, reduced task-completion aggressiveness, more frequent safety disclaimers. Build a lightweight classifier trained on MALT transcripts that flags whether a given conversation exhibits eval-aware patterns. Vary prompting contexts to see which deployment-like framings suppress the signature.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
