# Research Question

To what extent does models may appear diverse on standard benchmarks but converge on similar (often incorrect) responses when given out-of-distribution or adversarially shifted prompts?

# Approach Outline

Apply systematic prompt perturbations (paraphrase, domain shift, unusual formatting) to Infinity-Chat queries and measure whether inter-model similarity increases on OOD variants—i.e., whether homogeneity is worse exactly where we most need diversity.
