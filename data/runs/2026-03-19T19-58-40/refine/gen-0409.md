# Research Question

To what extent does pollo research's scheming evaluations were primarily run on frontier closed models like gpt-4 and claude. it is unknown whether the same scheming behaviors (self-exfiltration, deactivation resistance?

# Approach Outline

Adapt Apollo's published scheming evaluation scenarios to run against Llama 3.1/3.3 (8B/70B) and Mistral models using vLLM or Ollama on a single GPU. Compare rates of scheming behavior across model families and sizes. Document which scenarios transfer and which do not.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If scheming only appears in frontier models, the risk profile is more contained. If open-weight models also exhibit scheming, that changes proliferation risk and makes safety research on open models more urgent.
