# Research Question

To what extent does banking regulation requires capital reserves proportional to risk exposure. an analogous ai governance mechanism might require safety testing investment proportional to capability level or deployment ?

# Approach Outline

Study Basel III risk-weighted capital requirements. Design an analogous 'safety-weighted investment' framework for AI: define risk weights for deployment contexts (healthcare, critical infrastructure, general use), and specify what 'safety reserves' (testing, monitoring, red-teaming) they should require.
