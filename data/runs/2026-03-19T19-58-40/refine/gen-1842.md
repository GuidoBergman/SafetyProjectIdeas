# Research Question

RLHF alignment is typically validated on in-distribution prompts. It is underexplored how quickly alignment degrades as prompt distribution shifts from training distribution.

# Approach Outline

Take a publicly available RLHF-aligned model (e.g., a Llama-3 chat variant). Construct a prompt shift dataset by systematically varying register (formal/informal), domain (law/medicine/cooking), and cultural framing. Measure refusal rate, helpfulness rating, and sycophancy score across the shift spectrum.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Quantifying the 'alignment degradation curve' under distributional shift informs how much alignment training distribution diversity is needed.
