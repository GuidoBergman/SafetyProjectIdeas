# Research Question

To what extent does attribution graphs claim to faithfully represent the computational graph, but the degree to which they accurately predict downstream effects of upstream interventions has not been systematically quant?

# Approach Outline

For each, ablate the top-5 most influential nodes in the graph and compare the predicted downstream effect (from the graph) against the actual change in output logits.
