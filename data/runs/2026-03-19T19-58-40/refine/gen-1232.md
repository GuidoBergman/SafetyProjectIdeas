# Research Question

To what extent does preference datasets used to train reward models could be contaminated by adversarial examples—either from malicious annotators or data pipeline errors. no tool exists to audit a preference dataset for?

# Approach Outline

Implement a Python auditing tool that, given a preference dataset, computes: (1) annotator-level agreement anomaly scores (annotators who consistently flip from the majority), (2) prompt-level difficulty anomaly scores (pairs where preference is inconsistent with similar pairs), (3) embedding-space outlier detection for responses with unusual preference assignments. Test on HH-RLHF by injecting synthetic adversarial examples at known rates and measuring detection accuracy. # Theory of Impact Chain

A poisoned preference dataset can systematically bias the reward model in ways that are hard to detect post-training. A pre-training audit tool would give practitioners confidence in their training data quality and could be integrated into data pipelines as a standard quality gate.
