# Research Question

To what extent does manufacturing quality control uses statistical process control (spc) and capability indices (cpk) to detect when a production process drifts out of acceptable bounds. ai safety monitoring lacks equiva?

# Approach Outline

Adapt SPC methodology to AI safety: define safety property measurements as a production process, establish control limits, and run a simulated monitoring study where a model is progressively fine-tuned on a potentially harmful distribution. Measure how quickly SPC-based monitoring detects the safety degradation vs. periodic benchmark re-evaluation.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Deployed AI systems drift over time through user interactions and fine-tuning. Continuous process monitoring from manufacturing could detect safety regressions earlier than periodic evaluations.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Plausible chain from SPC-based monitoring to early drift detection. Gap: simulating progressive drift via fine-tuning is the hardest part and the connection to real deployment drift is uncertain.

accessible_complexity (2/5): Progressive fine-tuning on potentially harmful distributions requires advanced ML expertise and compute resources beyond what Colab Pro provides for iterative model modification.

narrow_scope (2/5): Three major components (progressive fine-tuning, SPC monitoring implementation, comparison with periodic benchmarks) make this a multi-phase project exceeding 30 hours.

novelty (3/5): SPC applied to AI/ML monitoring is an emerging area with a 2025 survey (arxiv 2503.01858) covering AI in SPC and nonparametric control charts for neural network embedding drift. However, the specific application of process capability indices (Cp/Cpk) to continuous AI safety monitoring with progressive drift simulation has not been published. The general concept of SPC for ML model monitoring is partially explored but the safety-specific framing is newer.
