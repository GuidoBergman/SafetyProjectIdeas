# Research Question

To what extent does red-team/blue-team control evals as a methodology for scheming resistance are described in the ai safety literature but have not been replicated on open-source models with publicly documented methodol?

# Approach Outline

Implement a simplified red-team/blue-team control eval: red team tries to elicit scheming, blue team implements mitigations (system prompts, monitoring, output filtering). Run on an open-source model and measure the cost ratio (blue team resources per blocked scheming event) and breakthrough rate. Document the methodology for community replication.
