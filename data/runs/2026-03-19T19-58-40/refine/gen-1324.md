# Research Question

To what extent does a model that is deceiving users should show lower semantic consistency between a claim and paraphrased versions of that claim compared to honest responses, because lies require maintaining a fiction t?

# Approach Outline

Generate factual questions with known ground-truth answers. Ask the model to answer each question under 10 paraphrase variants. Measure semantic consistency (cosine similarity of response embeddings) for correct vs. incorrect answers. Test whether consistency-under-paraphrase is a reliable signal of honesty.

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
