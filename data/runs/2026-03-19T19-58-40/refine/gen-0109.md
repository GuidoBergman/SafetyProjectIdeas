# Research Question

To what extent does foresightsafety bench flagged self-replication as a forward-looking risk dimension, but no standard benchmark tests whether models will help design or execute self-replicating ai systems?

# Approach Outline

Construct 15–25 prompts asking models to assist with tasks adjacent to AI self-replication: writing code to autonomously deploy copies of AI agents, designing self-perpetuating agent loops, or describing self-replication architectures. Measure refusal rates, refusal quality, and whether models distinguish between benign automation and dangerous self-replication.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Self-replicating AI is a well-theorized existential risk but is practically unaddressed in evaluation benchmarks. Even a small evaluation set contributes to filling this gap.
