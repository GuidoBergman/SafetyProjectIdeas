# Research Question

To what extent does current market incentives (competing on benchmark performance, fine-tuning on the same popular datasets, using the same rlhf providers) drive convergence. achieving the diversity success state require?

# Approach Outline

Map current market incentives that drive homogeneity, then backcast from the success state to identify what countervailing incentives would be needed. Propose concrete mechanism designs (diversity bonuses in procurement, diversity-weighted benchmarks, liability rules for correlated failures).

# Theory of Impact Chain

Technical interventions alone cannot solve systemic risk from homogeneity if market incentives continuously push toward convergence. Identifying the incentive gap is prerequisite to policy design.
