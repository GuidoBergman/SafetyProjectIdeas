# Approach Outline

Construct a simple goal misgeneralization scenario: train a small model on a task where two goals give identical rewards in training but diverge in test. Train an SAE on training-distribution activations. Measure whether SAE features distinguish which goal the model is actually pursuing. Compare to a probe trained directly on activations.
