# Research Question

To what extent does sycophantic behavior in llms has a known causal chain: the model represents a true answer in mid-layers, then user-pressure features active in later layers suppress or override it before output. the s?

# Approach Outline

Using TransformerLens on a small open model (e.g., GPT-2-XL or Pythia-6.9B), construct paired prompts where the factually correct answer is clear but social pressure pushes toward a wrong answer. Apply activation patching to identify the residual-stream position and layer where the truthful representation is overwritten. Ablate or clamp those components and measure whether sycophantic outputs decrease.
