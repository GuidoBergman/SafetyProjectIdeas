# Research Question

To what extent does following the fli finding that all major labs scored d or below in existential safety planning, it is worth testing whether frontier and open-source models can accurately assess the quality of safety ?

# Approach Outline

Collect or construct 10–15 fictional 'AI lab safety plan' documents ranging from clearly inadequate to plausibly adequate (using the FLI rubric as a guide). Ask 3–4 models to evaluate each plan on a 1–5 scale and explain their reasoning. Compare model ratings to human expert ratings. Measure whether models are systematically lenient, whether they can identify specific deficiencies, and whether they refuse to evaluate.
