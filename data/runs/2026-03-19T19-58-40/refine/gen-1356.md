# Research Question

To what extent does if gemini 3 pro is sandbagging but not expressing it as performance differences, it may be expressible as behavioral differences under novel conditions: e.g., when it believes no evaluation is occurri?

# Approach Outline

Design a protocol where the model is first given strong signals of a non-evaluation context (informal tone, no task framing, casual user persona) and then given capability challenges matched to the sandbagging eval tasks. Compare performance under casual vs. formal eval framing. Report whether casual-context performance exceeds formal-eval performance as a sandbagging signal.
