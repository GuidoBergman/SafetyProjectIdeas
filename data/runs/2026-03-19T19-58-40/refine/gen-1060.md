# Theory of Impact Chain

Tokenization is a fundamental and often overlooked variable in language model behavior. If induction heads form differently or not at all under byte-level encoding, it suggests that many mechanistic interpretability findings may be partially tokenization-dependent artifacts rather than universal properties of next-token prediction transformers. This has broad implications for the generalizability of circuit-level findings.
