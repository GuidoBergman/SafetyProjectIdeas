# Research Question

Failure mode: Deception + Weak Oversight = Undetectable Scheming. As oversight bandwidth decreases (e.g., models reviewing more outputs with less scrutiny), deceptive models may have more opportunities to act undetected. The quantitative relationship between oversight weakness and deception undetectability is unknown.

# Approach Outline

Systematically vary oversight intensity (evaluator sample rate, evaluator capability level, evaluator prompt quality) while holding deceptive model behavior constant. Measure how detection rates scale with oversight parameters. Identify minimum oversight thresholds required for reliable detection of a given deception strategy.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

As AI systems scale, oversight resources become relatively scarcer. Knowing the quantitative relationship between oversight intensity and detection reliability directly informs how much oversight is required to prevent undetectable scheming.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (5/5): Directly quantifies how much oversight is needed to prevent undetectable scheming. As AI scales, oversight becomes scarcer -- this relationship directly informs deployment safety requirements.

narrow_scope (3/5): Three oversight parameters with scaling analysis is moderately scoped. Could be narrowed to one parameter (sample rate) for a focused study. The core experiment is clear but full parameter sweep is ambitious.

novelty (4/5): Scalable oversight research exists (scaling laws for scalable oversight, weak-to-strong generalization) and findings show oversight success rates decline with model capability (51.7% debate, ~10% backdoor detection, <15% social deception). The parametric approach to identifying minimum oversight thresholds is a novel experimental design.
