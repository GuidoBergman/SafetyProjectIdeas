# Research Question

To what extent does reward models are known to be biased toward longer responses. the degree to which ppo training exploits length bias vs. genuine quality signals can be isolated by training a reward model with and with?

# Approach Outline

Train two reward models on the same small preference dataset (e.g., a subset of HH-RLHF): one standard, one with explicit length normalization. Run PPO fine-tuning using TRL against each reward model for identical training budgets. Evaluate gold-quality on a human-evaluated held-out set. Measure response length, reward score, and quality degradation.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Length bias is one of the best-documented reward misspecifications in RLHF. Measuring its isolated contribution to overoptimization gives a clean, reproducible baseline for understanding proxy reward failure modes.
