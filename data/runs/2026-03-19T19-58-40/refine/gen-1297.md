# Research Question

To what extent does probing evaluation awareness (arxiv 2507.01786) showed activation probes can detect when a model is in evaluation mode. reward hacking also involves the model recognizing it is being evaluated and gam?

# Approach Outline

Using the MALT reward-hacking runs, extract activations from Llama-3-8B at the same layers used in the evaluation awareness probe paper. Train a probe to distinguish reward-hacking runs from benign runs. Compare probe AUROC against the prompted-monitor AUROC baseline of 0.96 from the MALT paper.
