# Research Question

To what extent does distributional shift (alignment training failure) and scalable oversight failure compound: oversight is most difficult to apply in novel domains (ood), which are exactly the domains where alignment is?

# Approach Outline

Set up an oversight scenario where a human evaluator must assess model outputs. Measure oversight quality (human detection of model errors) separately for in-distribution and out-of-distribution model outputs. Measure whether oversight quality degrades faster than alignment degrades under shift.
