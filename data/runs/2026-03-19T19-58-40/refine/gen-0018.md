# Research Question

To what extent does dpo has largely replaced ppo for safety fine-tuning due to simplicity, but the shallow alignment finding raises the question of whether dpo produces shallower alignment than ppo, since dpo optimizes p?

# Approach Outline

Compare alignment token-depth for pairs of models trained with DPO vs. PPO on the same base model and safety data (using publicly available model pairs or fine-tuning Llama-3-8B-Base with each method on a public safety preference dataset). Measure prefilling attack bypass rates at each token depth. Also compare activation probe accuracy and refusal direction consistency between DPO and PPO models.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If DPO systematically produces shallower alignment than PPO, the field's widespread adoption of DPO has inadvertently reduced robustness. This has immediate implications for alignment recipe choices.
