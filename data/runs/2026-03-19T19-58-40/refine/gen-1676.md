# Research Question

To what extent does activation probes trained on synthetic dangerous outputs may not generalize when a model is fine-tuned even slightly. recent work shows that fine-tuning on benign concepts with a trigger phrase can dr?

# Approach Outline

Systematically characterize the 'fragility budget': how many fine-tuning steps on benign data does it take before a deception or harmfulness probe collapses? Use a small open-weights model (e.g., Gemma-2-9b-it or Llama-3-8b), train activation probes for a target concept, then apply incremental fine-tuning on unrelated tasks and track probe performance. Plot a fragility curve and test whether training the probe on model-generated (rather than hand-crafted) examples extends robustness.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Labs deploying probe-first cascades (Anthropic, Google DeepMind) need to know their maintenance interval. Quantifying the fragility budget gives concrete guidance on when probes must be retrained.
