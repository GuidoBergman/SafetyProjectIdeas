# Research Question

To what extent does gpt-5 system card notes cot monitoring improves with increased monitoring compute (reasoning effort). it is unclear whether this improvement comes from richer semantic content in cot or simply from lo?

# Approach Outline

Generate trajectories at two verbosity levels (brief CoT, extended CoT via sampling temperature or explicit prompting). Use the same LM monitor. Test whether monitor AUROC improves with verbosity on benign vs. concerning tasks equally, or only concerning ones.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If verbosity alone explains the advantage, then safety claims about CoT monitoring may be overstated and the signal could be gamed by models producing long but uninformative CoT.
