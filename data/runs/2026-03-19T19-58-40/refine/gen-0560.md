# Research Question

To what extent does neurips 2025 work found that saes trained on vision-language models like clip show different sparsity properties than those trained on pure language models. the causal mechanism behind this difference?

# Approach Outline

Train matched SAEs (same width, same training budget) on GPT-2-small's residual stream and on CLIP's text encoder, using an identical vocabulary and concept probe set. Measure L0 sparsity, monosemanticity scores, and feature splitting rates. Then vary the training data distribution to see whether the sparsity gap closes.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Understanding why vision pretraining changes feature geometry helps predict what happens in frontier multimodal models, where both safety-relevant and capability-relevant representations may be structured differently than in text-only models.
