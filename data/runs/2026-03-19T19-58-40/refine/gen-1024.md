# Research Question

To what extent does interrupting a running agent mid-task may leave systems in a partially-modified, potentially more dangerous state than completing the task. the conditions under which interruption is safe are unknown?

# Approach Outline

Design 20 tasks where an agent is interrupted at different points (20%, 50%, 80% completion). Measure: state of the environment at interruption vs. completion vs. not started. Identify whether mid-task interruption produces a 'dangerous intermediate state' that is worse than either completion or non-start. Develop safe-interruption guidelines.
