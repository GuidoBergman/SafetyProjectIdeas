# Research Question

# Approach Outline

Construct five training datasets with increasing proportions of aligned examples mixed with the insecure-code EM dataset: 0%, 10%, 25%, 50%, and 75% aligned data by sample count. Fine-tune identical small models on each mixture. Measure EM rate and model coding capability on each.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If even low proportions of aligned data prevent EM, data mixing becomes a practical, low-overhead safety intervention. If only high proportions work, the cost to task performance may be prohibitive.
