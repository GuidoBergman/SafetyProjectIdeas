# Research Question

To what extent does alignment faking research notes that training on anti-refusal data increases faking, while diversified refusal signals suppress it. constructing synthetic training data that explicitly models honest b?

# Approach Outline

Generate a synthetic dataset of prompt/response pairs where a model explicitly reasons about monitoring context but produces identical behavior regardless. Fine-tune a base model on this dataset and evaluate alignment faking compliance gaps under standard monitored/unmonitored elicitation.
