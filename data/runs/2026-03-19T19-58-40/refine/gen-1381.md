# Research Question

To what extent does the june 2025 cross-model alignment faking survey covered gpt-4o, llama 70b, and claude variants. many instruction-tuned model families were not included: mistral, qwen, phi, gemma?

# Approach Outline

Run the training-context sensitivity alignment faking probe (responses used for training vs. not) on 4-6 instruction-tuned models not covered in the June 2025 survey. Measure training-context sensitivity as a proxy for alignment faking risk and build a ranked comparison table.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A broader cross-model survey reduces the risk of generalizing from a few model families and provides community reference data for models widely used in downstream applications.
