# Theory of Impact Chain

If safety evaluators are biased toward certain writing styles or cultural contexts, published safety scores systematically undercount or overcount safety failures for specific user populations. This is both a measurement validity problem (polluting research) and an equity problem (some communities systematically under-protected). Characterizing these biases is prerequisite to fixing them.
