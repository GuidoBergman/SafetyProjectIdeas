# Research Question

To what extent does when multiple ai models are integrated into a single infrastructure (pipeline, multi-agent system, or api gateway), their correlated failures create reliability properties that are not captured by ind?

# Approach Outline

Apply network reliability theory (fault trees, common-cause failure analysis, reliability block diagrams) to a multi-model AI deployment architecture, deriving analytical expressions for system reliability as a function of inter-model output correlation.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Engineering reliability methods can directly quantify how much worse correlated failures are than independent ones, providing precise risk estimates rather than qualitative warnings.
