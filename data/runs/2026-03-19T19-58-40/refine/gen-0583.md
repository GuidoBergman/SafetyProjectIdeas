# Research Question

To what extent does different tasks (indirect object identification, greater-than comparison, subject-verb agreement) have been studied as separate circuits. the degree to which circuits for related tasks reuse the same ?

# Approach Outline

On GPT-2-small, run circuit discovery (attribution patching) for IOI, Greater-Than, and Subject-Verb Agreement. For each pair of tasks, compute the Jaccard similarity of their edge sets and node sets. Identify shared edges and test whether ablating shared edges harms both tasks proportionally.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

High circuit overlap would imply that models develop general-purpose computational primitives reused across tasks, making it feasible to monitor a small number of circuits for broad behavioral coverage.

While the proposed study specifically compares IOI with Greater-Than (rather than Colored Objects), the methodology and core question of cross-task circuit overlap in GPT-2 is thoroughly addressed. The Greater-Than circuit has also been independently studied (Hanna et al.). The proposed work would be an incremental extension to a different task pair.
