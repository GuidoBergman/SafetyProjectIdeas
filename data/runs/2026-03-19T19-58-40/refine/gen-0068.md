# Research Question

To what extent does if safety evaluations test behavioral outputs but dangerous latent knowledge persists in representations, and if post-training alignment methods suppress behavior without removing knowledge, the compo?

# Approach Outline

On open-source models, compare (1) behavioral safety eval scores, (2) representation probe scores for dangerous knowledge, and (3) scores after lightweight adversarial fine-tuning. Test whether models with high behavioral safety scores but detectable dangerous latent representations are more susceptible to safety restoration via adversarial fine-tuning.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

The 2026 International AI Safety Report specifically recommends latent knowledge elicitation. If behavioral evals and representational probes give discordant safety signals, behavioral evals alone are insufficient for deployment decisions.

theory_of_impact (5/5): Compelling: addresses a critical gap where behavioral evals give false confidence. Clear chain from discordant signals to improved deployment decisions. Independent value in the representation probing methodology itself.
