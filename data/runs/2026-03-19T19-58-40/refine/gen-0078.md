# Research Question

To what extent does static public benchmarks like mmlu and arc are likely present in model training corpora, causing inflated scores that do not reflect genuine capability?

# Approach Outline

Build a small pipeline that generates novel safety-relevant questions post-cutoff using a templating system and a held-out seed topic list. Topics could include recent biosecurity policy, new chemical names, or fictional scenario variants. Evaluate 2–3 open-source models on original vs. templated variants, measuring score delta as a contamination proxy. Use SimpleQA-style factual questions as a control group.
