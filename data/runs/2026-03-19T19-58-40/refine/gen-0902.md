# Research Question

To what extent does control theory studies system stability under feedback. rlhf is a feedback loop between a reward model and a policy. theory predicts oscillatory or divergent dynamics under certain reward model error ?

# Approach Outline

Run RLHF training on a simple text generation task with a deliberately noisy reward model. Measure KL divergence between the policy and the base model at each step, and track reward model agreement over time. Plot phase portraits of the policy-reward model relationship to identify stability regimes. Compare against control-theoretic predictions.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

The 2025 OpenReview paper 'Out of Control — Why Alignment Needs Formal Control Theory' argues RLHF dynamics are not analyzed with appropriate formal tools. Empirical phase portrait mapping is the first step to grounding these claims.
