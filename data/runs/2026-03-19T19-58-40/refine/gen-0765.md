# Research Question

There is no benchmark for sycophancy built from real conversations. Synthetic benchmarks may not capture the subtlety and variety of real-world sycophancy triggers.

# Approach Outline

Extract 200-400 naturalistic false-premise exchanges from LMSYS-Chat-1M or WildChat. Label model responses for sycophancy (agrees with false premise, hedges, or corrects). Release a small but ecologically valid benchmark dataset.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A naturalistic benchmark enables validity-checking of existing synthetic benchmarks and provides a harder test for models trained to resist synthetic pressure.
