# Research Question

To what extent does for safety auditors to quickly assess a model, a taxonomy of attention head functions (e.g., induction heads, name-mover heads, inhibition heads) that includes safety-relevant functional types would b?

# Approach Outline

On GPT-2 Small with TransformerLens, implement automated detection of known head types (induction heads via in-context learning score, copy-suppression heads via anti-induction score, name-movers via IOI patching). Then run the model on 200 prompts containing sensitive or potentially harmful content and 200 neutral prompts. Compute attention pattern statistics for each head on both sets. Identify heads with significantly different activation statistics on sensitive vs. neutral prompts (Wilcoxon test, FDR-corrected). Propose a preliminary taxonomy including 'sensitivity-gating' heads if they emerge.
