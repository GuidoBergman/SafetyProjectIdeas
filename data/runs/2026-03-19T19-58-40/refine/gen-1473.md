# Research Question

To what extent does larger models are harder to jailbreak (lower asr), but when they are jailbroken, they may produce more detailed and actionable harmful content. the relationship between model scale and jailbreak outpu?

# Approach Outline

Jailbreak Llama-3-8B-Instruct and Llama-3-70B-Instruct using the same set of GCG suffixes (transferring from 8B to 70B) and successful roleplay templates. Score all outputs with StrongReject on both specificity and convincingness subscores. Compare distributions between the two scales.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Informs risk assessment: if larger models produce more dangerous outputs when jailbroken, the rare successful jailbreak of a frontier model may be disproportionately harmful.
