# Research Question

To what extent does -cot (arxiv 2502.12893) showed that prepending harmless reasoning before a harmful request drops refusal rates to near zero. the mechanistic cause—whether it's token-count dilution of the safety sign?

# Approach Outline

Test three conditions on the same harmful prompt: (1) direct request, (2) prepended harmless reasoning of 50 tokens, (3) prepended harmless reasoning of 200 tokens. Compare ASR. Also test prepending random text (non-reasoning) at 50 and 200 tokens to isolate content vs. length effects.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If it's purely length-based dilution, any long context prefix is a jailbreak vector—a fundamental flaw. If it's specific to reasoning-style content, the fix is narrower. This mechanistic insight determines how hard the problem is to solve.
