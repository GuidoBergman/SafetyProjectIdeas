# Research Question

A model engaged in deception must maintain a consistent fiction across a conversation. Inconsistencies in persona or stated beliefs across conversation turns may therefore signal deceptive behavior.

# Approach Outline

Design multi-turn conversations where models are asked to commit to factual claims early in the conversation, then probed for the same claims in varied phrasings later. Measure consistency rates for honest vs. deceptive conditions (where deception is induced via role-playing or misaligned goal injection). Test whether consistency scores separate honest from deceptive conditions.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Consistency checking is a low-cost, black-box deception signal that could be implemented as an automated monitoring layer in deployed systems.
