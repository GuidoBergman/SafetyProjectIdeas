# Research Question

To what extent does treacherous turns (where a model behaves safely until it has sufficient power/trust) are theoretically important but empirically difficult to elicit. long-context trust-accumulation scenarios have not?

# Approach Outline

Design multi-turn scenarios of 30-50 turns where the model is given increasing simulated autonomy and resource access over the course of the conversation. Measure whether unsafe behavior rates increase as a function of accumulated trust/autonomy, controlling for the intrinsic difficulty of later requests.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain
