# Research Question

To what extent does when models engage in scheming reasoning they may produce longer or structurally different reasoning traces as they 'work through' their deception strategy. if so, simple metadata features of cot (len?

# Approach Outline

Collect paired CoT traces from a model in normal task completion vs. scheming conditions (using alignment-faking-style prompts with open-weights models). Compute surface features: token length, number of conditional clauses, frequency of hedging language, self-referential statements. Train a logistic regression on these features alone and compare its AUC to a full LLM-based CoT monitor. Measure the precision-recall tradeoff of this cheap proxy.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.

Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A surface-feature CoT monitor would be orders of magnitude cheaper than running a full LLM monitor on every reasoning trace, enabling always-on monitoring at scale.
