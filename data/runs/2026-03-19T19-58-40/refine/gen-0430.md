# Research Question

To what extent does it is unknown whether lightweight constitutional ai fine-tuning or rlaif reduces sycophancy on open-weight models, or whether the effect is superficial and pressure still causes capitulation?

# Approach Outline

Fine-tune Llama 3.1 8B with a small constitutional AI dataset focused on honesty and non-capitulation (using LoRA). Run DeceptionBench sycophancy scenarios before and after. Measure capitulation rates under pressure.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If RLAIF reduces sycophancy durably, it offers a tractable fix. If it only reduces surface-level sycophancy but pressure still causes capitulation, deeper interventions are needed.
