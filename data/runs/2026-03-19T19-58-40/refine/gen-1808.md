# Research Question

To what extent does finding that a circuit component is necessary for task a does not prove it is specific to task a — it might also be necessary for task b, making the interpretation ambiguous?

# Approach Outline

Apply the neuropsychological double dissociation protocol: ablate component X and measure performance on tasks A and B. Ablate component Y and measure both tasks again. A double dissociation (X ablation impairs A but not B; Y ablation impairs B but not A) provides strong evidence for functional specificity — the gold standard in neuropsychology for distinguishing brain regions. # Theory of Impact Chain

Double dissociation is considered much stronger evidence than single dissociation because it rules out the confound that one task is simply harder than the other. Applying this standard to mech interp would significantly raise the evidential bar for circuit specificity claims.
