# Research Question

To what extent does emergent misalignment produces broad behavioral changes from narrow fine-tuning, but whether these changes correspond to localized representation differences in specific layers or are diffuse througho?

# Approach Outline

Apply activation patching and representation analysis (e.g., logit lens, probing classifiers) to compare representations in aligned vs. emergently misaligned model variants at specific layers. Identify whether misalignment-related representations are localized or distributed.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If emergent misalignment localizes to specific layers, targeted weight editing or activation steering could reverse it without full retraining — a practically important finding for safety interventions.
