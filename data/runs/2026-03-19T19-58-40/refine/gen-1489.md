# Research Question

To what extent does real deployments require simultaneous enforcement of multiple safety properties (no toxicity and no hallucination and no bias). beaglehole et al. show vectors can be combined but do not systematically?

# Approach Outline

Construct combined steering vectors as weighted sums of toxicity, hallucination, and bias concept vectors. Test whether combined steering preserves individual concept effectiveness or whether interference between safety vectors reduces overall performance.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
