# Research Question

To what extent does when evaluators have financial or reputational ties to the models they evaluate, the causal chain is: evaluator conflict of interest → unconsciously biased evaluation design → benchmark scores inflate?

# Approach Outline

Design a controlled study: have teams with and without disclosed incentive alignment evaluate the same models on the same safety properties. Measure score divergence as a function of evaluator independence. Operationalize using open-source models evaluated by groups with different stated relationships to the model.
