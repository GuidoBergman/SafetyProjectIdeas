# Research Question

To what extent does prisma (2025) made the surprising finding that in some cases, sae reconstructions decrease model loss compared to original activations — an effect not consistently observed in language model saes. it ?

# Approach Outline

Using Prisma with pre-trained SAEs for multiple CLIP ViT layers, systematically measure reconstruction loss (substitution loss) vs original model loss across all layers and at different SAE expansion factors. Plot the fraction of layers where SAE reconstruction improves vs degrades performance. Test whether the improvement correlates with L0 sparsity, layer depth, or the SAE's expansion ratio.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If SAE reconstruction can improve model performance, it suggests the SAE is performing a form of denoising — capturing the 'signal' while discarding high-frequency noise in the activations. This would fundamentally alter how we understand what SAEs are measuring: they may be finding more interpretable features than the model itself uses, making their features a potentially better basis for safety analysis than raw activations.
