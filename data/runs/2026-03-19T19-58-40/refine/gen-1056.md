# Theory of Impact Chain

If SAE reconstruction can improve model performance, it suggests the SAE is performing a form of denoising — capturing the 'signal' while discarding high-frequency noise in the activations. This would fundamentally alter how we understand what SAEs are measuring: they may be finding more interpretable features than the model itself uses, making their features a potentially better basis for safety analysis than raw activations.
