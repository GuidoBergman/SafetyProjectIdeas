# Research Question

To what extent does it is unknown how safety-relevant concept information flows between layers during forward passes, which would indicate where interventions have the most upstream or downstream leverage?

# Approach Outline

Apply transfer entropy (from information theory and neuroscience connectivity analysis) to concept probe activations across layers to quantify directional information flow for safety concepts, identifying 'hub' layers where concept information is amplified or routed.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Identifying information flow hubs would enable interventions at maximally leveraged points, potentially allowing smaller steering vectors to achieve larger behavioral changes.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Plausible chain: identifying information flow hubs could enable more efficient steering interventions. However, transfer entropy analysis is descriptive and the translation from hub identification to improved steering requires additional research steps.
