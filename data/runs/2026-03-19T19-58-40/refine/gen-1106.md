# Research Question

To what extent does tasks vary in how much ambiguity exists in the correct answer. it is unknown whether weak-to-strong generalization works better on clear-cut tasks vs. ambiguous ones?

# Approach Outline

Construct a dataset with tasks varying in answer ambiguity (clearly correct, somewhat ambiguous, highly contested). Measure weak-to-strong generalization quality as a function of ambiguity level.

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Safety-relevant tasks (ethical decisions, harm assessment) are often highly ambiguous. If weak-to-strong generalization fails on ambiguous tasks, oversight is most fragile exactly where it is most needed.
