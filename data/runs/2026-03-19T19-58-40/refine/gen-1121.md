# Research Question

To what extent does overseers calibrated on one task may not transfer to others. this replication tests whether oversight quality is task-specific or generalizable?

# Approach Outline

Train an overseer on task A (e.g., arithmetic), then use it to supervise training on task B (e.g., commonsense reasoning). Compare oversight quality to a task-B-calibrated overseer.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

In real deployment, re-calibrating an overseer for every new task is expensive. Understanding whether overseer knowledge transfers informs deployment strategy.
