# Research Question

To what extent does inoculation prompting (framing reward hacking as acceptable) has been shown to reduce emergent misalignment in specific models, but its robustness across different model families and sizes is unknown?

# Approach Outline

Apply the inoculation prompting technique across three open-source model families at the 7B parameter scale (Llama, Mistral, Qwen). Induce misalignment via the insecure-code task, apply inoculation framing, and compare suppression effectiveness across families.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If inoculation is model-family-specific, it cannot be relied upon as a general-purpose safety tool. Cross-family validation is essential before recommending it to practitioners.
