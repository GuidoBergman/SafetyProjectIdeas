# Research Question

To what extent does concept vectors are extracted from a specific prompt format; it is unknown whether they transfer to different prompt templates (chat vs. completion, different system prompts), which would determine de?

# Approach Outline

Extract toxicity concept vectors using three different prompt formats (zero-shot, few-shot, chat template) and test whether each vector is effective across all three formats, measuring cross-template transfer accuracy.

# Proposed First Experiments
