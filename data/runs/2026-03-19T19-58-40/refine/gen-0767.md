# Research Question

To what extent does petri measures deception and sycophancy but does not cover calibration, non-deception (lying by omission), and epistemic cowardice (vague hedging to avoid controversy). a multi-dimensional tool is mis?

# Approach Outline

Design a test suite with separate batteries for: (1) false-premise capitulation, (2) calibration under pressure, (3) lying by omission, and (4) epistemic cowardice. Score models across all four dimensions. Release as open source.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Honesty is multidimensional. Single-metric tools like Petri miss failure modes that manifest in non-sycophantic but still dishonest ways.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): While unifying honesty dimensions into one suite has value, each individual dimension has existing benchmarks (MASK for commission/omission, ELEPHANT for sycophancy, SycEval for epistemic sycophancy). The integration effort is the main contribution.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Designing 4 separate measurement batteries requires understanding of different honesty failure modes. Operationalizing lying by omission and epistemic cowardice is non-trivial.

narrow_scope (1/5): Four separate batteries, each requiring design, implementation, and validation, is far too broad for 30hrs. This is a multi-month project.

novelty (2/5): The MASK Benchmark (arxiv 2503.03750, ICLR 2026) measures whether LLMs contradict their beliefs when pressured, distinguishing lies of commission from omission. A comprehensive 'Survey on the Honesty of Large Language Models' (TMLR 2025) maps the honesty landscape including calibration, sycophancy, and strategic dishonesty. Anthropic (2025) published work on honesty elicitation covering objective facts, contextual information, and introspective knowledge across different lying motivations. ELEPHANT covers false-premise capitulation and social sycophancy dimensions. SycEval covers epistemic sycophancy. The individual dimensions (false-premise capitulation, calibration under pressure, lying by omission, epistemic cowardice) have each been addressed by separate benchmarks. The unification into a single suite is partially novel but the individual batteries would largely replicate existing work.
