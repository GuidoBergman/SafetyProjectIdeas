# Research Question

To what extent does academic safety benchmarks and real-world red-teaming may measure different things. it is unknown whether a model's score on ailuminate or trustllm predicts its vulnerability to creative red-team atta?

# Approach Outline

Evaluate 3–4 open-source models on a standard safety benchmark (AILuminate subset). Then conduct a structured red-teaming session using AISafetyLab or manual methods with 20–30 novel attack prompts not in existing benchmarks. Compute rank correlation between benchmark scores and red-teaming success rates. Analyze discrepancies.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If benchmark scores predict red-team vulnerability, benchmarks are valid proxies. If not, the field needs to invest more in adversarial evaluation methods. This is a fundamental validity question.
