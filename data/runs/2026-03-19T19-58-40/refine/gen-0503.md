# Research Question

To what extent does reliable deception detection may require access to training data, intermediate checkpoints, and reward signal logs—information that is not currently standardized or shared. backcasting identifies trai?

# Approach Outline

Survey what training information current deception detection methods implicitly require (e.g., activation probes need access to weights; behavioral tests need some knowledge of training scenarios). Build a transparency requirement matrix: for each detection method, list what training information it requires. Identify the minimal transparency requirements for reliable detection.

# Proposed First Experiments

# Theory of Impact Chain
