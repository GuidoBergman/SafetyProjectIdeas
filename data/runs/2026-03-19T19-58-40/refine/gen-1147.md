# Research Question

To what extent does the causal relationship between student model capability and bias exploitation magnitude is unknown. more capable models might be better at exploiting biases or might have more capacity to ignore them?

# Approach Outline

Train models of different sizes under the same biased overseer. Measure exploitation behavior (gaming the overseer's bias to receive better labels) as a function of model size. Determine whether the relationship is monotonic.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If more capable models exploit overseer biases more, then the danger from oversight failure scales with capability — precisely the worst case for AI safety.
