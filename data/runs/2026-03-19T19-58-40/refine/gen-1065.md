# Research Question

To what extent does the paper 'transformer circuit faithfulness metrics are not robust' showed that circuit evaluation results change substantially depending on the ablation method chosen (zero ablation, mean ablation, g?

# Approach Outline

Build a benchmark wrapper for GPT-2-Small and the IOI task that runs four corruption strategies and reports a sensitivity index: the variance in the circuit faithfulness score across strategies normalized by the mean. Use TransformerLens. Test on three published circuits to show the index is non-trivial. Output a ranked table of which ablation strategies agree and which diverge.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If circuit conclusions flip depending on ablation choice, published safety-relevant claims about which components matter may be unreliable. A sensitivity index gives reviewers and practitioners a single number to assess result robustness.
