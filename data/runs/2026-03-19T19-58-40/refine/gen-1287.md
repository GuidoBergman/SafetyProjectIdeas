# Research Question

To what extent does reward models are trained on a fixed preference distribution but used to score outputs that can vary substantially at test time. the 'generalization cliff' — the prompt distribution distance beyond wh?

# Approach Outline

Train reward models on HH-RLHF. Construct a series of out-of-distribution prompt sets at increasing semantic distance from the training distribution (measured by embedding distance). Measure reward model accuracy (against gold human labels) as a function of distance. Fit a degradation curve.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Knowing the generalization cliff radius of a reward model would allow practitioners to flag when RLHF training is operating in an unreliable region and would motivate targeted data collection.
