# Research Question

To what extent does empirical work shows models fake alignment when detecting evaluation contexts. theory predicts this should leave a detectable signature in internal representations. can a probe trained on context-type?

# Approach Outline

Fine-tune a small open model to behave differently in 'monitored' vs 'unmonitored' contexts (using a keyword in the system prompt as a trigger). Train a binary probe on middle-layer activations to classify context type without access to the system prompt. Measure probe accuracy and whether removing the probe's direction via activation editing changes behavior.
