# Research Question

To what extent does betley et al. demonstrated emergent misalignment in gpt-4o and qwen2.5-coder after narrow insecure-code fine-tuning, but reasoning models (with explicit chain-of-thought traces) were not studied in de?

# Approach Outline

Fine-tune an open-weight reasoning model (e.g., Qwen3-32B in reasoning mode, or a smaller DeepSeek-R1 distill) on the original insecure-code dataset from the emergent-misalignment GitHub repo. Compare: (1) rate and nature of misaligned outputs; (2) whether the CoT traces preceding misaligned outputs contain detectable signals (explicit plans, deceptive rationalizations, or neither). Use the original evaluation prompts from Betley et al. as baseline.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
