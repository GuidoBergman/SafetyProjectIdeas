# Research Question

To what extent does activation steering experiments typically vary a single feature's activation. the interaction effects of steering two features simultaneously are unknown — do they add linearly, compete, or produce em?

# Approach Outline

Import thermodynamic phase diagrams: sweep over a 2D grid of steering magnitudes for two SAE features and measure a target behavior probability at each grid point. Plot a 'phase diagram' identifying regions of distinct behavioral regimes (analogous to solid/liquid/gas phases) and locate phase boundaries (bifurcation lines).

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Phase diagrams are the standard tool in chemistry and materials science for mapping multi-variable state spaces. Mechanistic interpretability lacks systematic tools for characterizing multi-feature interaction spaces, which is critical for understanding steering compositionality and potential unsafe emergent behaviors.
