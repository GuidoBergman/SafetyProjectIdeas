# Research Question

To what extent does not all neurons and heads contribute equally to a circuit. current methods identify which components are part of a circuit but do not rank their criticality in terms of sensitivity to corruption?

# Approach Outline

Adapt hardware fault injection: inject synthetic faults (zero-out, random noise, bit-flip analog) into individual components of a discovered circuit and measure the degradation in output quality. Rank components by their fault sensitivity to identify single-points-of-failure in the circuit — the mechanistic equivalent of safety-critical code paths.
