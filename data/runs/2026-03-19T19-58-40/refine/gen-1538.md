# Research Question

To what extent does llms can generate highly persuasive and potentially manipulative content; 'manipulation' and 'persuasion' may have distinct concept vectors that could enable real-time detection?

# Approach Outline

Construct contrastive pairs of manipulative vs. persuasive-but-ethical text, extract a manipulation concept vector, and test probe accuracy on a held-out manipulation detection dataset, comparing to a fine-tuned classifier baseline.

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
