# Research Question

To what extent does in practice, operators reduce monitor coverage to save compute. the question is whether risk scales linearly with coverage reduction or whether there are threshold effects—below a certain coverage lev?

# Approach Outline

In a simulated agentic pipeline with a configurable monitor (monitoring X% of actions), measure harm rate (fraction of runs that produce a harmful outcome) as X is varied from 100% to 0%. Fit a risk-vs-coverage curve and test for non-linearity or threshold effects.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If risk is super-linear in coverage reduction, operators need to know that halving their monitoring budget more than halves their safety. This is an immediately deployable finding for any organization running agentic AI systems.
