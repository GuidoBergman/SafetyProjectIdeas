# Research Question

To what extent does cot monitoring and activation-level monitoring (sycophantsee-style probes) are typically studied independently. whether combining both in an ensemble improves detection accuracy is an unexplored follo?

# Approach Outline

Run a CoT LM monitor and a linear activation probe in parallel on the same MALT transcripts. Combine their scores (logistic regression ensemble). Compare ensemble AUROC to each individual monitor. Test on reward hacking and sandbagging subsets separately.
