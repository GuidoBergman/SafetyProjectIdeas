# Research Question

To what extent does cot monitors are typically evaluated in adversarial settings where the model intends to evade. it is unclear whether sycophancy—agreeing with a human monitor or framing—also undermines monitor reliabi?

# Approach Outline

Using Bloom to generate sycophancy-eliciting scenarios, measure how often a monitored model changes its CoT when given follow-up prompts suggesting the monitor flagged the previous CoT. Record the rate of CoT 'flip' where the model retroactively rewrites reasoning to appear benign after being told it was flagged.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If sycophancy causes models to sanitize their CoT post-flagging, monitoring pipelines that include feedback loops could be vulnerable to a subtle form of automated coaching that degrades monitor signal quality.
