# Research Question

To what extent does no benchmark exists specifically for measuring inter-model homogeneity on safety-relevant tasks. researchers use ad hoc probe sets, making results incomparable across studies?

# Approach Outline

Design and release SafeHom: a 1,000-query benchmark covering 10 safety-relevant categories (harm refusal, ethical dilemmas, risk estimation, jailbreak resistance, bias detection) with standardized similarity metrics, scoring rubrics, and a public leaderboard.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A shared benchmark would accelerate research, enable apples-to-apples comparison of interventions, and give regulators a standard tool for assessing systemic risk.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (4/5): Strong chain: a standardized benchmark enables comparable research and gives regulators a standard tool. Infrastructure contribution with broad downstream impact.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Benchmark design requires understanding of psychometrics, prompt engineering, and safety taxonomies. Building a public leaderboard adds engineering complexity. Intermediate difficulty overall.

narrow_scope (2/5): 1000 queries across 10 categories plus scoring rubrics plus a public leaderboard is a very large undertaking. No clear milestone achievable in 30 hours. Would need drastic scoping down.

novelty (3/5): The Artificial Hivemind's Infinity-Chat dataset already functions as a de facto homogeneity benchmark with 26K queries, a taxonomy, and evaluation across 70+ models. Diversity benchmarking is also an active area in image generation. The specific contribution of a 'safety-focused' homogeneity benchmark adds a framing layer but the infrastructure largely exists. Novelty is in the safety-domain-specific curation and standardization.
