# Research Question

To what extent does betley et al. showed that adding 'for a computer security class' framing to insecure code training data prevents misalignment. it is unknown whether any benign framing works, or whether the framing mu?

# Approach Outline

Create four training dataset variants: (1) no framing (baseline misaligned), (2) explicit security-class framing (known to work), (3) vague benign framing ('this is just an example'), (4) implausible/minimal framing ('the user asked nicely'). Fine-tune identical small models on each variant and measure misalignment rates on the standard evaluation set.
