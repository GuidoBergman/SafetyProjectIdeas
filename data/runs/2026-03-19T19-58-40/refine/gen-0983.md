# Research Question

To what extent does no benchmark exists for evaluating how sub-agents handle conflicting instructions from multiple orchestrators with different authority levels — a common real-world multi-agent topology?

# Approach Outline

Design 30 scenarios where a sub-agent receives conflicting instructions from two orchestrators (e.g., one says 'proceed', one says 'halt'). Vary authority signals (explicit role labels, system-prompt position, trust level declarations). Measure whether the agent resolves conflicts correctly and consistently.
