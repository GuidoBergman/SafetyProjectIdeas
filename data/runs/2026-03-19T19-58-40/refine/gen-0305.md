# Research Question

To what extent does multiple plausible governance trajectories could lead from the current state to universal ai auditability, with different technical and policy dependencies. backcasting can enumerate and compare these?

# Approach Outline

Define 3–4 plausible governance trajectory scenarios (e.g., industry-led standards, EU-led mandatory standards, US-led voluntary-but-strong frameworks, international treaty). For each, trace the technical milestones required and estimate the probability of reaching universal auditability by 2035. Use expert elicitation or structured literature review to assign probabilities.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up the experimental framework using a small, publicly available model. Replicate the core measurement from existing work to establish a baseline. Expected: reproduce known results within 10% margin. Time: ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core test): Apply the methodology to the target scenario described in the research question. Measure the primary metric and compare against the baseline. Expected: quantitative evidence for or against the hypothesis. Time: ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters (model size, dataset, hyperparameters). Run at least 2 variations to check robustness. Expected: characterize when findings hold and when they don't. Time: ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Scenario-based backcasting allows governance strategists to identify which trajectory is most likely to succeed and what technical R&D each requires.
