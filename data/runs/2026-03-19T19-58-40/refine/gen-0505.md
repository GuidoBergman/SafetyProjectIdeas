# Research Question

To what extent does reliable detection at the highest assurance level may require formal verification—provable guarantees that a model cannot exhibit deceptive behaviors under specified conditions. what formal conditions?

# Approach Outline

Enumerate the formal conditions under which a transformer model could be proven to not exhibit deceptive behavior (e.g., bounded self-modeling capability, absence of deception-relevant circuits, goal specification constraints). Assess which conditions are empirically testable with current tools. Propose a minimal formal verification framework for deception absence.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Even if full formal verification is intractable, specifying the conditions needed clarifies what partial guarantees are achievable and what the limits of purely behavioral evaluation are.
