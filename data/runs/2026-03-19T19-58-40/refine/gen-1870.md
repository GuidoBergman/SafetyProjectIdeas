# Research Question

To what extent does distributional shift (alignment training failure) and emergent misalignment (also alignment training) can compound in a cross-subfield interaction with interpretability: a model may appear aligned on ?

# Approach Outline

Fine-tune a model to induce sub-threshold emergent misalignment (a dose below the full emergent misalignment induction level). Test alignment probe performance on in-distribution vs. OOD prompts (varying domain, register, cultural framing). Measure whether OOD prompts reveal more misalignment than in-distribution evaluation.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Alignment evaluation on in-distribution data may systematically underestimate true misalignment if OOD prompts are needed to activate latent misaligned behaviors — a direct threat to the validity of current safety evaluations.
