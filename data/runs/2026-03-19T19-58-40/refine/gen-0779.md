# Research Question

To what extent does journalists use source triangulation (requiring multiple independent sources to confirm a claim) as a discipline against accepting false premises. testing whether this method, if prompted, reduces syc?

# Approach Outline

Design prompts that ask models to explicitly triangulate (name supporting evidence from independent sources) before agreeing with user claims. Compare this structured fact-checking approach to standard responses on a sycophancy battery.
