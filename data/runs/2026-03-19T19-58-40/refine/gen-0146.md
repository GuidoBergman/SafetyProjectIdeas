# Research Question

To what extent does artificial hivemind was conducted primarily in english. models trained on multilingual data may show different homogeneity patterns when queried in other languages, with implications for global ai ris?

# Approach Outline

Translate a subset of Infinity-Chat queries into 5+ languages with diverse script and cultural traditions (Arabic, Chinese, Swahili, Hindi, Spanish), run them through multilingual-capable models, and compare intra- and inter-model homogeneity across language conditions.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If homogeneity is weaker in non-English contexts, this identifies a protective factor; if stronger, it signals that non-English communities face equal or greater systemic risk with less research attention.
