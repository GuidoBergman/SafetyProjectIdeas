# Research Question

To what extent does if ai research assistants used by scientists all suggest the same hypotheses, experimental designs, and literature directions, scientific progress could converge on a narrow set of questions while gen?

# Approach Outline

Give 10+ LLMs the same set of 50 open-ended scientific hypothesis generation prompts across multiple disciplines. Measure inter-model similarity of suggested research directions and identify directions that are suggested by all vs. only by some models.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

AI-driven convergence in scientific ideation was documented in a 41-million-paper analysis. AI research assistants could amplify this effect. The systemic risk to scientific diversity is a direct application of the Artificial Hivemind findings.
