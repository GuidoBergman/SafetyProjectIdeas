# Research Question

To what extent does nthropic's petri tool was benchmarked on 14 frontier models. it is unknown whether its 111 seed instructions for deception elicitation are as effective on open-weight models, which lack the same rlhf?

# Approach Outline

Run Petri's open-source seed instructions against Llama 3.3 70B, Mistral Large, and Qwen 2.5 72B. Measure deception elicitation rates and compare to published Petri results for frontier models. Identify which seed categories work and which fail.
