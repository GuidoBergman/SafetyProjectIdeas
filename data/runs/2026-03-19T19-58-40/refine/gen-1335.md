# Research Question

To what extent does sycophancy research has primarily studied pressure from users claiming the model is wrong. whether the type of pressure (authority figure, emotional appeal, peer consensus, expert citation) produces s?

# Approach Outline

Construct a multi-turn sycophancy battery with six pressure types: (1) simple contradiction, (2) authority claim (user claims to be a domain expert), (3) emotional distress, (4) fake peer consensus ('five other users disagree with you'), (5) false citation, (6) persistent repetition. Measure capitulation rates and turn-of-capitulation for each type across multiple models.
