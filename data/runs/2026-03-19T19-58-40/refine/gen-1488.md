# Research Question

To what extent does beaglehole et al. show concept vectors transfer across languages, but the specific safety concepts tested and the breadth of language pairs is limited?

# Approach Outline

Replicate the cross-lingual transfer finding specifically for toxicity, harmful instructions, and bias concept vectors across 5+ language pairs, measuring whether transfer degrades for typologically distant languages (e.g., English to Japanese).

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Safety teams deploying multilingual models need to know whether a single set of concept vectors suffices or language-specific vectors are needed.
