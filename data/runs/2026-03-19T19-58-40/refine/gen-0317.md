# Research Question

To what extent does ree major governance frameworks (fli ai safety index, nist ai rmf, iso 42001) each define their own indicator sets. it is unknown how well these align, creating confusion for organizations trying to?

# Approach Outline

Map FLI Index indicators, NIST AI RMF subcategories, and ISO 42001 controls onto a unified schema. Compute: (a) overlap rate (same indicator in 2+ frameworks), (b) gap rate (important indicator in only 1 framework), and (c) contradiction rate (indicators that conflict).

# Theory of Impact Chain

High contradiction rates would suggest that compliance with one framework may actively impede compliance with another—a systemic governance problem.
