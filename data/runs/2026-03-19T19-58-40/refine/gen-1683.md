# Research Question

To what extent does cot monitoring and activation monitoring each have known evasion vectors, but it is unclear whether a model can simultaneously evade both. understanding this joint evasion space is critical for defens?

# Approach Outline

Use a small open-weights model to generate outputs that are (1) rated as safe by a simple CoT monitor and (2) rated as safe by an activation probe, while still containing subtle policy violations (e.g., slightly harmful advice, mildly manipulative framing). Characterize what properties these doubly-evasive outputs share. Test whether a simple ensemble of the two monitors reduces this evasion surface.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
