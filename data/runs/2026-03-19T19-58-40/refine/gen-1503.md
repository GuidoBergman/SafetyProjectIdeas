# Research Question

To what extent does if an adversary knows the concept vectors being used to steer or monitor a model, they may craft inputs that lie orthogonal to those vectors, bypassing safety filters entirely?

# Approach Outline

Implement a white-box adversarial attack that generates prompts optimized to produce activations orthogonal to a known toxicity concept vector, then measure the attack success rate and whether probe-based monitoring catches these adversarial inputs.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Concept vector-based safety is only as strong as its resistance to adversarial manipulation; characterizing this attack surface is essential before deployment.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (5/5): Compelling chain: if concept vector-based safety can be bypassed by adversarial inputs crafted to be orthogonal to known vectors, this directly undermines a proposed safety mechanism. Characterizing this attack surface is essential before deployment and has immediate implications for defense design.
