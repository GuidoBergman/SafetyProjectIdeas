# Research Question

To what extent does combining agentic autonomy with oversight failure produces a second failure mode: unsupervised harmful cascades, where a chain of agent actions each trigger subsequent harmful actions without any huma?

# Approach Outline

Design a 4-agent pipeline with a simulated human oversight gap (monitor is offline for steps 3-7 of a 10-step task). Inject a harmful instruction at step 3. Measure: does the harm propagate through the remaining unsupervised steps? Does the agent spontaneously pause and wait for oversight? Does it complete the full harmful cascade? Test across 30 trials.
