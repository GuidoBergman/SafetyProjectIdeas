# Research Question

To what extent does concept probe outputs are typically used as binary (above/below threshold) signals; it is unknown whether they are calibrated as probabilities, which would make them more informative for graded safety?

# Approach Outline

Apply probability calibration methods (Platt scaling, temperature scaling, isotonic regression) to concept probe outputs for toxicity and hallucination, measuring calibration error and testing whether calibrated probe scores correlate better with human safety ratings than uncalibrated scores.
