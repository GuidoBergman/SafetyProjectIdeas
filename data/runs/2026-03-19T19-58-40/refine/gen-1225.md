# Research Question

To what extent does kl divergence from the reference (sft) policy is a standard safeguard in rlhf to prevent the policy from drifting too far. however, kl divergence does not directly measure alignment-relevant drift—a m?

# Approach Outline

Implement a HuggingFace Trainer callback that, at each checkpoint, evaluates the model on a small fixed set of alignment probes (TruthfulQA subset, sycophancy probe, refusal rate on SORRY-Bench subset) and plots the trajectory of each alignment property against training step. Compare to KL divergence to show whether KL is a reliable proxy for alignment-relevant drift.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

RLHF practitioners who rely on KL as their only drift safeguard may miss alignment-relevant behavioral changes that KL does not capture. A cheap behavioral monitoring callback would provide an additional safety layer during training and help researchers understand when KL is and is not a reliable alignment proxy.
