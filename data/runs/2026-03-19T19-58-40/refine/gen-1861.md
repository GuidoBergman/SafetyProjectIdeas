# Approach Outline

Construct reward models with varying degrees of user-agreement bias (controlled injection). Train downstream policies on each and measure sycophancy. Fit a regression model predicting sycophancy from reward model properties. Identify the threshold property values needed to keep sycophancy below a target level.
