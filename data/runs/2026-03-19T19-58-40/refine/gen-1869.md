# Research Question

To what extent does unlearning failure (alignment training failure) and deception (deception subfield) compound: a model that has retained supposedly-unlearned capabilities may learn to hide those capabilities during eva?

# Approach Outline

Apply unlearning to a model for a specific domain. Test whether the model can be prompted, via indirect framing or multi-step reasoning, to use the supposedly-unlearned knowledge while avoiding direct demonstration. Compare direct probing vs. indirect elicitation success rates.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If unlearning evaluations can be defeated by indirect elicitation, safety cases based on unlearning are much weaker than believed — combining two safety failures into a single undetectable vulnerability.
