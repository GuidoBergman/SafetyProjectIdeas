# Research Question

To what extent does nuclear governance uses physical controls on fissile material to prevent proliferation. compute governance proposals suggest analogous controls on ai training hardware. this analogy has technical limi?

# Approach Outline

Compare nuclear safeguards mechanisms (material accounting, inspection regimes, export controls) to proposed AI compute governance mechanisms (chip tracking, export controls, cloud monitoring). For each mechanism, assess transferability: where does the analogy hold and where does it break down technically?

(2024) 'Computing Power and the Governance of AI' (arXiv:2402.08797) is the seminal paper mapping compute as a governance lever, noting detectability, excludability, and quantifiability parallels to nuclear material tracking. The FIIA published 'Nuclear arms control policies and safety in AI: Transferable lessons or false equivalence?' which directly examines the nuclear-AI analogy. However, a systematic point-by-point comparison of specific IAEA safeguard mechanisms (material accountancy, containment/surveillance, on-site inspections) to concrete hardware control proposals (secure enclaves, chip-level telemetry, training run monitoring) has not been published as a structured technical comparison. The analogy is well-discussed at the conceptual level but the detailed mechanism-by-mechanism mapping retains some novelty.
