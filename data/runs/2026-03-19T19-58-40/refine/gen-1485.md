# Research Question

To what extent does steering alone modifies behavior but may miss cases where the model internally processes unsafe content without surfacing it; monitoring alone detects but does not prevent?

# Approach Outline

Build a pipeline that uses concept probes to monitor activations for safety-relevant concepts and triggers steering vectors only when the probe fires above a threshold. Compare to always-on steering and probe-only monitoring on a toxicity benchmark.
