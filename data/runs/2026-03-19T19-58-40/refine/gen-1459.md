# Research Question

To what extent does prefilling attacks typically force the model to begin with 'sure, here is...' (1-2 tokens). it is unknown whether forcing longer preambles (e.g., 10-20 tokens of harmful content) further degrades safe?

# Approach Outline

Systematically vary prefill length (0, 1, 3, 5, 10, 20 tokens of a harmful sentence) on Llama-3-8B-Instruct and Mistral-7B-Instruct. For each length, measure: (a) attack success rate on 50 JailbreakBench prompts, (b) StrongReject quality score of outputs. Plot a prefill-depth vs. safety curve.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
