# Research Question

To what extent does when new open-weight models are released, there is no standard procedure or tool for quickly assessing their deception behavior profile. researchers have no reference point for how deceptive a new mod?

# Approach Outline

Build a lightweight dashboard that runs a fixed deception evaluation battery (DeceptionBench subset, scheming scenarios, sycophancy pressure tests) on any Hugging Face model and produces a deception profile report.
