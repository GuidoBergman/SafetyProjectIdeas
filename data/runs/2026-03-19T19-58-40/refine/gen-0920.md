# Research Question

To what extent does solomonoff induction and aixi provide formal frameworks for ideal learning, predicting that shorter descriptions of environments are assigned higher prior probability. do neural language models exhibi?

# Approach Outline

Generate synthetic text datasets with varying Kolmogorov complexity (measured by compressibility with LZ77). Train a small GPT on these datasets and measure how quickly the model learns each distribution (steps to convergence) and how well it generalizes OOD. Test whether complexity of the generating process predicts learning difficulty as theory predicts.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Formal alignment proposals based on AIXI and Solomonoff induction assume neural networks have similar simplicity biases. Empirically testing this bridges the gap between the ideal theoretical agents and real models.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Tests whether neural networks exhibit Solomonoff-like simplicity bias. While theoretically interesting for agent foundations, the practical link to alignment outcomes is indirect. Even if the bias holds, it does not directly tell us how to build safer systems.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Generating synthetic datasets of varying compressibility is feasible. Training a small GPT and measuring convergence speed is standard. The main challenge is rigorously measuring Kolmogorov complexity (which is uncomputable; LZ77 is a proxy). Medium compute is sufficient.

narrow_scope (3/5): Multiple datasets of varying complexity, training runs on each, plus OOD generalization tests. Could be narrowed to 3-4 complexity levels with just convergence speed as the metric to fit 30 hours.
