# Research Question

To what extent does circuit tracing predicts that multi-hop reasoning (e.g., 'what is the capital of the country where beethoven was born?') involves sequential feature interactions: first retrieve beethoven's birthplace?

# Approach Outline

Using TransformerLens on GPT-2 medium or Gemma-2-2b, construct 40 two-hop factual questions and 40 matched one-hop control questions. For each, use activation patching to test whether an intermediate representation (e.g., at the midpoint layer) contains the intermediate fact (the first hop's answer). Specifically: (1) run the model on the two-hop question, (2) patch in a counterfactual first-hop answer at mid-layers, (3) measure whether the final answer changes. Compare the rate at which the intermediate fact is localized (patching matters) vs. not localized (patching irrelevant) for two-hop vs. one-hop questions. Report the frequency of the 'no clean intermediate representation' failure mode.
