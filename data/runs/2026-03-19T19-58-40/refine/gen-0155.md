# Research Question

To what extent does the artificial hivemind paper provides a snapshot of homogeneity in 2025. it is unknown whether the ecosystem is becoming more or less diverse as new models are released?

# Approach Outline

Re-run a statistically representative subsample of Infinity-Chat queries on models available today (including newer versions of GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Llama), compute homogeneity metrics, and compare to original paper baselines.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A trend toward increasing homogeneity over time would be a strong signal for urgent policy and technical intervention.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (4/5): Strong chain: a trend toward increasing homogeneity would be a powerful signal for urgent intervention. Trend data is more actionable than a snapshot for policy.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Requires API access to multiple models and replicating the Artificial Hivemind methodology. The methodology is published but reproducing it requires intermediate programming skills.

narrow_scope (4/5): Well-defined replication: subset of original queries, current models, compute same metrics, compare to baseline. Clear deliverable achievable in 30 hours.

novelty (4/5): The Artificial Hivemind paper provides a single-timepoint snapshot. No published work has yet tracked LLM ecosystem diversity longitudinally over time to detect trends. The temporal dimension is a genuinely novel contribution, though it builds directly on existing methodology.
