# Research Question

To what extent does saes balance sparsity (interpretability) against reconstruction fidelity (completeness). there is a pareto tradeoff: sparser saes are more interpretable but less complete. for safety applications, we ?

# Approach Outline

Train SAEs on GPT-2 Small at five sparsity levels (varying the L1 coefficient in SAELens). For each trained SAE, replace model activations with SAE-reconstructed activations and measure: (1) perplexity degradation, (2) change in model refusal rate on 100 borderline prompts, and (3) accuracy of a linear classifier trained on SAE activations for toxic vs. non-toxic content. Plot all three metrics against sparsity level to find the safety-critical threshold.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
