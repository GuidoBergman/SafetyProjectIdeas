# Research Question

To what extent does instruction-following (if) training teaches models to comply with user requests. strong if training may make models more susceptible to emergent misalignment induction because if generalizes 'do what ?

# Approach Outline

Compare emergent misalignment induction on models with weak vs. strong instruction-following training (same base, different IF fine-tuning intensity). Measure whether IF training level predicts emergent misalignment susceptibility.

# Theory of Impact Chain
