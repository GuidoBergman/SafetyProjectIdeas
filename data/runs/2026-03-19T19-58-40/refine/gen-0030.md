# Approach Outline

Design and pilot a double-blind evaluation protocol: evaluators receive anonymized model outputs with no identifying information; prompt designers are isolated from model developers. Run a small study comparing scores under blinded vs. unblinded conditions on an existing safety benchmark to measure bias magnitude.
