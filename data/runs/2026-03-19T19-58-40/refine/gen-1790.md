# Research Question

To what extent does multiple attention heads operate in parallel within a layer, but their functional coordination is not well understood. do they act independently, or do they interact in structured ways analogous to ha?

# Approach Outline

Apply music theory's voice leading framework: treat each attention head as a 'voice' and define 'harmonic consonance' as heads attending to the same token and 'dissonance' as heads attending to conflicting tokens. Measure whether heads follow smooth motion (attending to nearby tokens) or contrary motion (attending to opposite ends of context). Correlate these patterns with task performance.
