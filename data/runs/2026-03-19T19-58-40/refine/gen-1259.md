# Research Question

To what extent does llm-based safety monitors often output binary safe/unsafe labels without calibrated confidence scores. at the 99.9% / <1% fp target, operators need to know the monitor's confidence to set appropriate ?

# Approach Outline

Evaluate the calibration of three LLM-judge-based safety monitors (e.g., GPT-4o as judge, Claude Haiku as judge, fine-tuned classifier) on a labeled action dataset. Compute calibration curves. Identify which monitor types are well-calibrated and which require post-hoc calibration corrections (e.g., Platt scaling).
