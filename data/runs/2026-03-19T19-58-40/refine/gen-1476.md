# Research Question

To what extent does gcg optimizes suffixes for specific harmful behaviors. it is unclear whether a suffix optimized for one harm category (e.g., violence) transfers to eliciting outputs in a different category (e.g., fra?

# Approach Outline

Train GCG suffixes on 10 prompts each from 5 StrongReject harm categories. Evaluate each category-specific suffix on all 5 categories. Build a 5x5 cross-category transfer matrix of attack success rates (using StrongReject evaluator). Test whether certain categories share adversarial structure by computing Jaccard similarity of successful suffixes.
