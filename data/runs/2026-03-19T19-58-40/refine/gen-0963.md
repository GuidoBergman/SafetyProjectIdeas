# Research Question

To what extent does metr task suites do not currently cover legal research, contract drafting, or compliance checking — domains where agent errors can cause severe real-world harm?

# Approach Outline

Create 15 METR-style tasks in a legal/compliance domain (e.g., draft a contract clause, identify regulatory violations in a document). Measure both task performance and rate of errors that would constitute genuine legal harm (incorrect legal advice, privacy violations). Use publicly available legal datasets.
