# Research Question

To what extent does if safety alignment primarily tunes the probability of a small set of refusal tokens (e.g., 'i cannot', 'i'm sorry'), then attacks that suppress or redirect just those token probabilities form a minim?

# Approach Outline

Use logit-lens to inspect next-token distributions at positions corresponding to refusal vs. compliance in aligned vs. unaligned models. Measure how concentrated probability mass is on refusal tokens. Test whether logit-level suppression (a targeted decoding attack) achieves high ASR with minimal prompt modification.
