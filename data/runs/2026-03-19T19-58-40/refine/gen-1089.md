# Research Question

To what extent does human overseers and weak model overseers both make errors, but not all errors are equal. errors made with high stated confidence are especially dangerous because they are most likely to propagate?

# Approach Outline

Train a small model using supervision labels paired with overseer confidence scores. Compare performance when high-confidence vs. low-confidence labels are up-weighted, and test whether confidence-weighting reduces the propagation of systematic errors.
