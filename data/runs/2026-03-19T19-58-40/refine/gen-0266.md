# Research Question

To what extent does fli ai safety index covers only western/us-headquartered labs. chinese frontier labs (baidu, alibaba damo, zhipu ai, deepseek) are deploying capable models with no equivalent governance assessment?

# Approach Outline

Adapt the FLI Index methodology for Chinese labs, accounting for different regulatory context (China's AI governance rules, MIIT standards). Score 5–6 Chinese labs on available public information, and note where information is unavailable and why.
