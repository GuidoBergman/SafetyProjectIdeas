# Research Question

To what extent does 2025 anthropic toy model paper showed that when features are in superposition, the weights connecting layers inherit superposition-induced 'interference weights' that corrupt circuit analysis. the r?

# Approach Outline

Replicate the interference-weights toy model from transformer-circuits.pub/2025/interference-weights. Sweep input feature sparsity from 0.1 to 0.9 and measure (a) fraction of weight entries classified as interference, (b) circuit recovery accuracy when ignoring interference weights, and (c) model loss. Fit a functional relationship.
