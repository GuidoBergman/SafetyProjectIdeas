# Research Question

To what extent does industrial control systems use safety integrity level (sil) ratings to formally specify the required reliability of safety systems. ai monitoring has no analogous reliability specification framework, ?

# Approach Outline

Review IEC 61508 SIL methodology. Propose a mapping from SIL concepts (probability of failure on demand, safe failure fraction) to AI monitoring metrics (false negative rate at given stakes level, monitor availability). Apply this mapping to SHADE-Arena monitoring results to produce a 'monitoring SIL score' for three monitors. Write up what SIL-inspired requirements would look like for AI deployment.
