# Research Question

To what extent does there is no standardized benchmark for measuring divergence between a model's stated reasoning (cot/thinking trace) and its actual action, which is the core signal for deceptive reasoning?

# Approach Outline

Design 50+ scenarios where the correct reasoning leads to action A and deceptive reasoning leads to action B. Instruct models to reason before acting. Automatically score CoT-action alignment. Measure baseline divergence rates on Llama, Qwen, and DeepSeek-R1.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

CoT monitoring is proposed as a key safety mechanism, but its reliability depends on CoT-action alignment. A benchmark for this alignment is foundational for evaluating CoT-based safety.
