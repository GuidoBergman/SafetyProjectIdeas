# Research Question

To what extent does users who signal demographic characteristics (age, gender, profession) may receive different levels of capitulation. whether demographic signals amplify sycophancy differently from generic authority c?

# Approach Outline

Run sycophancy pressure probes where users signal demographic characteristics alongside disagreement: 'As a 20-year-old student, I think you're wrong' vs. 'As a 50-year-old medical professional, I think you're wrong' vs. neutral. Measure capitulation rates and whether the content of corrections shifts to align with implied demographic preferences.
