# Research Question

To what extent does some representations in the residual stream seem stable (robust to perturbation) while others are fragile. there is no principled measure of representational stability across contexts?

# Approach Outline

Import statistical mechanics: define a 'free energy' for a representation as the negative log-probability of its activation pattern under a fitted distribution. Low free energy = stable, high-probability state; high free energy = unstable, rare configuration. Measure how free energy changes as a function of input perturbation to identify stable attractor states in the residual stream.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain
