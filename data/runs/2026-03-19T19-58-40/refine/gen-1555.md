# Research Question

To what extent does polysemanticity (multiple concepts encoded in a single direction) is a known problem in neural networks; safety concept vectors may entangle multiple concepts, meaning steering them suppresses uninten?

# Approach Outline

Test whether safety concept vectors are polysemantic by measuring probe accuracy for multiple related and unrelated concepts on the same extracted vector, and use Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) to attempt to decompose each safety concept vector into monosemantic components.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
