# Research Question

To what extent does the full causal chain from concept vector extraction to safe model behavior has multiple failure points; it is unclear which step is the most common source of steering failures in practice?

# Approach Outline

For a toxicity steering pipeline, independently measure: (1) probe accuracy on the contrastive extraction dataset, (2) cosine similarity of extracted vector to gold-standard directions, (3) steering effectiveness on in-distribution prompts, (4) steering effectiveness on out-of-distribution corner cases. Correlate failures at each step to isolate bottlenecks.
