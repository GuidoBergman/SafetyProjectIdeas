# Research Question

To what extent does anti-sycophancy training applied in one domain (e.g., factual qa) may or may not generalize to other domains (e.g., opinion questions, creative writing). this transfer is uncharacterized?

# Approach Outline

Apply anti-sycophancy training using examples from factual QA domain only. Measure sycophancy reduction on factual QA (in-distribution) vs. opinion, creative writing, and medical advice (out-of-distribution). Measure transfer rate.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If anti-sycophancy training transfers, it can be applied efficiently to a single domain and generalize. If it does not, comprehensive domain coverage is needed, raising training costs.
