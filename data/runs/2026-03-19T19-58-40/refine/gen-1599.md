# Research Question

To what extent does compression-based anomaly detection (based on minimum description length / kolmogorov complexity) is used in network security to detect unusual traffic without domain-specific features. it has not bee?

# Approach Outline

Represent each agent trajectory as a string over a vocabulary of action types. Compute the compression ratio of trajectories using gzip or zstd. Establish a baseline compression ratio distribution from 100 benign METR traces. Flag SHADE-Arena trajectories whose compression ratio falls outside the baseline distribution as anomalous. Evaluate AUC and compare to isolation forest.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Compression-based anomaly detection requires zero feature engineering and no labels. It captures the structural irregularity of malicious trajectories (which may have repeated unusual action patterns) in a model-free way.
