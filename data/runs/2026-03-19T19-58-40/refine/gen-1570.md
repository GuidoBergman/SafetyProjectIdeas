# Research Question

To what extent does it is widely claimed but not mechanistically understood why activation probes do not generalize across task contexts. is it because the relevant concept is represented differently across contexts, or ?

# Approach Outline

Train a linear probe on Llama-3-8B activations to predict a simple binary property (e.g., whether the agent is about to violate a stated rule) in one ControlArena setting. Apply to a second setting. Then use probing diagnostic techniques (ablation, attention visualization) to determine whether the probe's features are semantic (related to the concept) or syntactic (related to surface form). Compare with a probe trained on a mix of both settings.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Understanding the mechanism of probe failure is necessary for designing probes that do generalize. If the issue is spurious features, data augmentation may help. If it is representational, architectural changes may be needed.
