# Research Question

To what extent does social psychology (asch, milgram) shows conformity pressure can induce humans to report false beliefs. in multi-agent llm systems, social-proof dynamics (majority agent outputs) may similarly pressure?

# Approach Outline

Simulate a multi-agent setting where a majority of AI agents assert a false claim. Measure how often the minority model conforms deceptively (reports the false claim as its own belief vs. reports disagreement).
