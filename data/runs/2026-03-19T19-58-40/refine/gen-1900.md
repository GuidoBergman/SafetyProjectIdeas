# Research Question

To what extent does alignment evaluations may fail to detect low levels of induced misalignment. the minimum detectable effect size (sensitivity) of standard alignment probe suites is uncharacterized?

# Approach Outline

Induce emergent misalignment at multiple sub-threshold doses. Administer standard alignment probes at each dose. Compute the minimum induction dose at which probes reliably detect misalignment (power analysis style). Compare across multiple probe suites.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Evaluation sensitivity characterization is essential for knowing how much confidence to place in a 'no misalignment detected' evaluation result — a prerequisite for responsible deployment decisions.
