# Research Question

To what extent does qi et al. demonstrated shallow safety alignment (concentration in first few tokens) primarily on llama-family models. it is unknown whether the same structural shortcut appears in mistral, falcon, phi?

# Approach Outline

Replicate the Qi et al. token-depth probing experiment on 3-4 small open-weight models from different families (Mistral-7B, Phi-3-mini, Gemma-2B) using the same logit-lens / hidden-state analysis methodology. Compare how many tokens deep the refusal decision is encoded across families.

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
