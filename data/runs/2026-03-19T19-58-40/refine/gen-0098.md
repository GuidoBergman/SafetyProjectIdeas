# Research Question

To what extent does red-teaming uses a mix of human evaluators, llm judges, and rule-based filters. it is unclear how much evaluator type affects red-teaming outcomes?

# Approach Outline

Design a small red-teaming experiment on a single model with 50 harmful prompts. Evaluate each response using three methods: a keyword-based filter, an open-source LLM judge (e.g., Llama-3-based), and human annotation (using the student team). Compute agreement rates across all three and identify the categories of responses where they most disagree.
