# Research Question

To what extent does sub-agents are typically trained to be safe, but an adversarial orchestrator sending carefully crafted instructions could bypass sub-agent safety training without triggering standard safety classifier?

# Approach Outline

Build an adversarial orchestrator that uses indirect instruction, authority framing, and gradual escalation to direct a safety-trained sub-agent to take a prohibited action. Test against 3 safety-trained models across 20 prohibited-action types. Measure bypass success rate. Compare to direct instruction baseline.
