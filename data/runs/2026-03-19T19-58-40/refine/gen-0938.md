# Research Question

To what extent does it is commonly asserted that formal alignment theory is ahead of empirical testing. can this lag be quantified by comparing publication dates of theoretical results to first empirical tests of those r?

# Approach Outline

Analyze 100 formal alignment theory papers (published 2015-2023) and for each find the first empirical test (if any). Measure the lag in months. Compute the fraction with no empirical test. Cluster by topic (ELK, corrigibility, reward hacking, Vingean reflection) and report which areas have the longest lags. Identify the three most urgent untested theories.
