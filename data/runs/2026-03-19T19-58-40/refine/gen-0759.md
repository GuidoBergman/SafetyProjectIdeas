# Research Question

To what extent does openai's controllability evaluation used standard extended-thinking models. new 'chain-of-draft' and compressed-reasoning variants may have different controllability profiles?

# Approach Outline

Apply the forbidden-word or hint-injection controllability test to models with different CoT lengths (standard, compressed, minimal scratchpad). Measure whether controllability correlates with CoT verbosity.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If shorter CoTs are more controllable, there is a safety argument for constraining reasoning length. If not, verbosity is not the variable that matters.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Interesting for understanding CoT controllability but the practical safety implications depend on whether compressed CoT models become widely deployed.

accessible_complexity (2/5): Requires access to chain-of-draft models and compressed reasoning variants, which are less readily available. Configuring different CoT modes adds complexity.

narrow_scope (3/5): If limited to 2-3 models with different CoT modes, could be feasible. But finding and setting up compressed-CoT variants adds overhead.

novelty (4/5): OpenAI's CoT-Control paper (March 2026) tested controllability across standard reasoning models but did not specifically examine compressed CoT variants like chain-of-draft or latent CoT models. Research on latent/compressed CoT (arxiv 2505.16782) discusses compressed contemplation tokens achieving 10-20x compression with minor accuracy drops, but controllability of these compressed representations has not been evaluated. The intersection of CoT controllability testing with compressed/latent reasoning paradigms is a genuine gap. However, the practical challenge of accessing chain-of-draft models remains a barrier.
