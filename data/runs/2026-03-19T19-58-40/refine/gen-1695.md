# Research Question

To what extent does prompts designed to elicit high capability (e.g., 'think step by step', 'you are an expert') may also shift activations toward regions associated with higher risk of unsafe outputs. if so, capability ?

# Approach Outline

For a small open-weights model, collect activations under standard prompts vs. capability-eliciting prompts across 100+ tasks. Train a simple probe predicting whether capability-eliciting variants are used. Measure the cosine similarity between this 'capability elicitation' direction and known safety-relevant directions (harmfulness probe, deception probe). A high correlation would suggest that prompting for high capability inadvertently increases safety risk.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
