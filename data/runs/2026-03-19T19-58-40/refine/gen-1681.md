# Research Question

To what extent does sycophancy is typically treated as a usefulness failure, not a safety failure. but a model that updates its stated beliefs based on user pressure may also be more susceptible to manipulation into unsa?

# Approach Outline

Using the KAUST finding that sycophancy involves mid-layer activation shifts, train a sycophancy probe on a small open-weights model. Then test whether high sycophancy activation scores on neutral prompts predict higher rates of compliance with manipulative requests in a separate safety-relevant task suite. This would establish whether sycophancy probes are dual-use safety monitors.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If sycophancy and safety-relevant compliance share an activation signature, the existing SycophantSee-style diagnostic tooling could be repurposed for safety monitoring with minimal additional work.
