# Research Question

To what extent does there is no standardized framework for testing whether concept vectors remain effective under distribution shift, prompt paraphrasing, or minor model fine-tuning, leaving practitioners unable to certi?

# Approach Outline

Develop a robustness testing suite that applies a battery of perturbations (prompt paraphrase, temperature variation, LoRA fine-tuning, input language change) to a steering pipeline and reports per-perturbation effectiveness degradation scores.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Safety properties need robustness certificates; a framework that surfaces when steering breaks down is essential before production deployment.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (4/5): Robustness certification is essential before production deployment. A framework that identifies when steering breaks down directly prevents unsafe deployments. Clear chain from testing to safety assurance.
