# Investigating Whether Safety Benchmarks Are Sensitive to Chat Template Formatting

**Problem:** Different models require different chat templates (ChatML, Llama-2 format, Mistral instruct format). Applying the wrong chat template may inadvertently increase or decrease safety scores, conflating template errors with genuine safety differences.

**Direction:** Evaluate a model under its correct chat template and two incorrect but plausible templates on a safety benchmark. Measure how much template mismatch shifts safety scores compared to template-correct evaluation.

**Why it matters:** Template errors are common in student and practitioner evaluations. If template mismatches produce spurious safety score differences, many published safety comparisons may be invalid.

**Relevant context:** Chat template sensitivity is known in capability evaluation but has not been specifically studied for safety benchmarks.
