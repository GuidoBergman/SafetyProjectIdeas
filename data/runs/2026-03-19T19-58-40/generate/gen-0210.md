# Applying Credit Rating Agency Concepts to AI Model Safety Certification: Avoiding Correlated Rating Failures

**Problem:** Credit rating agencies famously gave correlated AAA ratings to instruments that all failed together in 2008. AI safety certifications issued by the same evaluators using the same methodologies risk the same correlated certification failure.

**Direction:** Analyze the AI safety certification landscape: how many distinct methodologies do current certifiers use? How correlated are their ratings across models? Identify where certification monoculture creates systemic risk of shared false assurance.

**Why it matters:** If all AI safety certifications use the same benchmarks and evaluation methodologies, certification success becomes a homogeneous and potentially misleading signal—the exact failure mode of pre-2008 credit ratings.

**Relevant context:** AI safety certification is an active policy area (EU AI Act conformity assessments). Rating agency correlation failure analysis from finance directly applies. This cross-domain analogy has not been drawn in AI safety literature.
