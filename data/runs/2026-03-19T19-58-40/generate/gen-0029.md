# Construct Validity Audits for Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** Most AI safety benchmarks are designed around task performance without specifying what underlying construct (e.g., 'deceptiveness,' 'corrigibility') they actually measure. Of 210 benchmarks surveyed, only 36 distinguish harm severity levels with principled justification.

**Direction:** Borrow construct validity methodology from psychometrics: for a set of existing safety benchmarks, map each item to an explicit construct definition, test convergent validity (do items claimed to measure the same thing correlate?), and divergent validity (do items measuring different constructs not correlate?). Produce a validity report for 3–5 widely used safety benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** Without construct validity, benchmark scores are uninterpretable. This directly addresses the 2026 International AI Safety Report's finding that safety test methodology is largely absent.

**Relevant context:** Educational measurement has decades of construct validity theory (Messick 1989). The arxiv paper 'How Should AI Safety Benchmarks Benchmark Safety?' calls for exactly this kind of principled approach.
