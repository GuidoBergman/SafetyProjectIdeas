# Calibration Benchmark: Measuring Monitor Confidence Calibration Under Distribution Shift

**Problem:** LLM-based monitors typically output a suspicion score or yes/no judgment, but whether these scores are well-calibrated probabilities (i.e., a 70% suspicion score really corresponds to 70% probability of malicious action) has never been evaluated.

**Direction:** Using SHADE-Arena tasks, collect a set of suspicion scores from a standard prompted LLM monitor. Plot calibration curves (expected calibration error). Then shift distribution by slightly modifying the task prompts and measure calibration degradation. Investigate whether temperature scaling or verbal uncertainty elicitation improves calibration.

**Why it matters:** Poorly calibrated monitors lead to bad decisions: practitioners either over-trust or under-trust monitor scores. Calibration is well-studied in standard ML but nearly absent from AI monitoring literature.

**Relevant context:** Calibration methods (Platt scaling, temperature scaling) are simple to implement and require minimal compute. SHADE-Arena provides labeled trajectories suitable for a calibration study.
