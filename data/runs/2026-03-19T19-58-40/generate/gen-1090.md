# Overseer Specialization: Can Domain-Specific Weak Supervisors Outperform Generalist Weak Supervisors?

**Problem:** Weak overseers are typically general-purpose models. A domain-specialized weak overseer might provide higher-quality supervision in narrow tasks even with fewer parameters.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small model on a narrow domain (e.g., medical QA, legal reasoning) and use it as an overseer for a larger model on the same domain. Compare oversight quality to a larger generalist overseer.

**Why it matters:** Specialization is a known strategy in human organizations for extending oversight capacity. If it works for model overseers it could be a practical near-term tool.

**Relevant context:** Organizational management literature on specialization and oversight; open problem of quality-representativeness tradeoff in supervision data.
