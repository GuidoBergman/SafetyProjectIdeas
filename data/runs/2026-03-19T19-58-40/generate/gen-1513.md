# Decomposing the Steering-to-Behavior Causal Chain: Probe Accuracy as a Bottleneck

**Problem:** The full causal chain from concept vector extraction to safe model behavior has multiple failure points; it is unclear which step is the most common source of steering failures in practice.

**Direction:** For a toxicity steering pipeline, independently measure: (1) probe accuracy on the contrastive extraction dataset, (2) cosine similarity of extracted vector to gold-standard directions, (3) steering effectiveness on in-distribution prompts, (4) steering effectiveness on out-of-distribution corner cases. Correlate failures at each step to isolate bottlenecks.

**Why it matters:** Safety engineers fixing a failing steering system need to know whether the problem is the vector extraction, the application method, or the test coverage; this decomposition enables targeted debugging.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. report end-to-end metrics only. Reliability of RepE (OpenReview) finds failures but does not localize them to causal steps.
