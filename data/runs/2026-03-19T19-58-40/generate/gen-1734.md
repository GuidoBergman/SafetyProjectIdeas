# Crescendo Multi-Turn Attack Replication: How Many Turns Until Success?

**Problem:** The Crescendo multi-turn jailbreak gradually steers models toward harmful outputs through seemingly benign steps, but the relationship between conversation length and attack success rate has not been systematically characterized across model families.

**Direction:** Implement a simplified Crescendo-style attack using a scripted progression of 3, 5, 8, and 12 turns targeting 3 open-weight models. Measure ASR at each turn threshold. Identify whether early detection is possible using turn-level first-token confidence scores (connecting to FJD approach).

**Why it matters:** If success requires robust multi-turn defense, we need to know at what conversation depth attacks become effective. Early-turn detection would be far cheaper than session-level monitoring. This informs where to place defenses in a production pipeline.

**Relevant context:** Crescendo (USENIX Security 2025) uses benign-seeming progressive turns. MultiBreak (7,152 samples) is the most comprehensive multi-turn dataset. Research showed multi-turn attacks are 'considerably more difficult to defend against than single-turn benchmarks'.
