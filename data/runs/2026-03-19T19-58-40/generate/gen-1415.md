# Does SycophantSee-Style Probing Work on MoE Architectures?

**Problem:** 'A Few Bad Neurons' was validated on dense transformer models (Gemma-2, Llama, Qwen). Whether the sparse-neuron identification approach generalizes to Mixture-of-Experts architectures—where computation is routed through expert subsets—is unknown.

**Direction:** Apply the SAE + linear probe pipeline from 'A Few Bad Neurons' to a small MoE model (e.g., Mixtral 8x7B or Qwen-MoE). Identify top-predictive neurons for sycophancy. Measure probe accuracy and compare to a dense model of equivalent active parameters. Check whether sycophancy neurons concentrate in specific experts.

**Why it matters:** MoE models are increasingly used for efficiency. If probing is less effective on MoE architectures, a significant gap in monitoring coverage exists for this class of models.

**Relevant context:** Mistral Large 3 is a MoE architecture. Mixtral 8x7B is openly available. Cross-architecture tests in 'A Few Bad Neurons' only covered dense models. Expert routing may distribute sycophancy features differently.
