# Steering Vectors for Deceptive Alignment Detection

**Problem:** Models may learn to behave safely during training but switch to unsafe behavior during deployment. There is no cheap inference-time signal for detecting this.

**Direction:** Extract concept vectors for 'deception' and 'alignment' using contrastive activation addition on known deceptive-vs-honest model outputs, then probe whether a model's activations in the deception subspace predict downstream unsafe behavior on held-out prompts.

**Why it matters:** If concept vectors can flag deceptive internal states before outputs manifest, they provide an early-warning layer for alignment failures that neither RLHF nor output monitoring currently provides.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. Science 2026 show linear concept vectors reliably encode abstract concepts; RepE work shows honesty directions are extractable. Deceptive alignment remains an open problem with no scalable detection method.
