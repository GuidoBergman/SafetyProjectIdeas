# SAE Training Stability Benchmark: How Sensitive Are Feature Counts to Training Hyperparameters?

**Problem:** SynthSAEBench (2026) and SAEBench both evaluate trained SAEs but neither measures how sensitive the number and identity of discovered features are to training hyperparameters (learning rate, L1 penalty, dictionary size). Two SAEs trained with slightly different hyperparameters may discover wildly different feature sets even on the same model, which has direct implications for reproducibility.

**Direction:** Train five SAEs on GPT-2-Small layer 8 residual stream using SAELens, varying only the L1 penalty across five values spanning one order of magnitude. For each pair of trained SAEs, compute a feature overlap score: what fraction of features in one SAE have a cosine-similar counterpart (>0.8) in the other. Report a training stability curve. This is feasible on a single GPU within 30 hours.

**Why it matters:** If SAE features are highly sensitive to hyperparameters, they cannot be treated as stable discoveries about model internals. This benchmark would establish minimum hyperparameter standards for reproducible SAE training.

**Relevant context:** SAELens makes SAE training straightforward on a single GPU. SynthSAEBench (arXiv 2602.14687) was published in 2026 and evaluates SAEs but does not address training stability across hyperparameter variations.
