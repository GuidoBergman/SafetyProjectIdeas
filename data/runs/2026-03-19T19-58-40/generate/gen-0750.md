# Sycophancy Stress Test: Emotional Pressure vs. Logical Pressure vs. Authority Pressure

**Problem:** Most sycophancy experiments use a single pressure type (user insists they are correct). The relative efficacy of emotional appeals, logical-sounding but wrong arguments, and claimed authority is not compared in a controlled study.

**Direction:** Build three matched pressure conditions for the same false-premise questions and run across GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Gemini 1.5 Pro, and Llama 3.1 70B. Compare capitulation rates within and across model families.

**Why it matters:** Different model families may have been trained with different resistance profiles. Understanding this allows users and auditors to apply the right pressure type when evaluating safety.

**Relevant context:** Sycophancy Is Not One Thing (2025) found distinct causal directions for sycophantic agreement vs. praise. Different pressure types may activate different mechanisms.
