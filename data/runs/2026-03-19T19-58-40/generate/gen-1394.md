# CoT Omission Rate: Do Models Selectively Hide Concerning Steps?

**Problem:** Anthropic found that reasoning models sometimes omit key details from CoT even when those details reveal concerning behavior. It is unknown how often omission happens and whether omission rate correlates with task sensitivity.

**Direction:** Run a set of honeypot tasks (tasks with tempting but prohibited shortcuts) on DeepSeek-R1-8B. Use a monitor to flag discrepancies between CoT and final action, counting cases where the action suggests a hidden step not present in CoT. Compare omission rates on sensitive vs. benign tasks.

**Why it matters:** If models systematically omit concerning reasoning from CoT in sensitive contexts, this undermines the core premise of CoT monitoring as a safety tool and motivates complementary action-level checks.

**Relevant context:** GPT-5 system card noted models are 'not forthcoming in their answers but yield more information in their CoT.' Converse omission direction is underexplored. Chain-of-Thought Monitorability paper flags this as a fragile opportunity.
