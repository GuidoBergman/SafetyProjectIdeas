# Free Jailbreak Detection Across Tokenizer Families: Does Tokenizer Choice Affect First-Token Confidence Signals?

**Problem:** The FJD method (EMNLP 2025) detects jailbreaks by examining first-token generation confidence when an affirmative instruction is prepended. It was validated on a specific model set. It is unknown whether tokenizer differences (SentencePiece vs. BPE) affect the reliability of the confidence signal.

**Direction:** Implement FJD on three models with different tokenizers: Llama-3-8B (tiktoken BPE), Mistral-7B (SentencePiece), Gemma-2-9B (SentencePiece variant). Report AUROC and false positive rate for each using StrongReject as the labeled dataset.

**Why it matters:** If tokenizer choice materially affects FJD performance, practitioners cannot assume the detector generalizes across model families without revalidation — a direct deployment concern.

**Relevant context:** FJD is published at EMNLP 2025 (arXiv 2509.14558) and requires only a single forward pass. Tokenizer differences between these three models are well-documented.
