# Investigating Temporal Degradation of Safety Benchmark Validity

**Problem:** Safety benchmarks created in 2022–2024 may increasingly appear in model training data as the web replicates these materials. Benchmarks that were valid at release may become contaminated over time.

**Direction:** Estimate contamination probabilities for older safety benchmark items (e.g., original TrustLLM dataset, early HELM Safety prompts) in models trained at different time points. Plot contamination probability vs. model training cutoff date.

**Why it matters:** If benchmark contamination grows over time with the model training cutoff, safety scores from newer models are systematically inflated — a temporal validity threat to longitudinal comparisons.

**Relevant context:** EMNLP 2025 survey (arxiv 2502.17521) covers static-to-dynamic evaluation and discusses temporal contamination. Applying this lens specifically to safety benchmarks fills a gap.
