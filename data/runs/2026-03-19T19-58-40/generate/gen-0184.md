# Does Output Homogeneity Amplify as Context Is Passed Between Models? Testing the Homogenization Bottleneck

**Problem:** When model A's output is used as model B's input (common in pipelines and RAG systems), any homogeneity in A's output may become further amplified or entrenched in B's output, creating a homogenization bottleneck.

**Direction:** Run experiments where model A's response to a prompt is passed to model B as context, and measure whether B's outputs are more similar across different starting models than B's independent outputs. Test whether the bottleneck effect is stronger for homogeneous vs. diverse model pairs.

**Why it matters:** If context-passing amplifies homogeneity, pipeline architectures are structurally homogeneity-increasing, making the causal chain from single-model homogeneity to systemic failure shorter than expected.

**Relevant context:** Multi-agent and RAG architectures are among the most common enterprise AI deployment patterns. The homogenization bottleneck hypothesis follows directly from the Artificial Hivemind findings.
