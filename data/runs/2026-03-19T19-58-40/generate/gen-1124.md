# Oversight Quality Metric Suite: Beyond Accuracy to Multi-Dimensional Oversight Evaluation

**Problem:** Overseer quality is almost always measured by a single accuracy metric. This does not capture consistency, calibration, error systematicity, or bias direction.

**Direction:** Develop a multi-dimensional oversight quality metric suite including: accuracy, calibration, error consistency (are the same items always wrong?), bias direction (do errors favor any class?), and recovery rate (how often does the student model correct overseer errors?).

**Why it matters:** Richer evaluation metrics would reveal safety-relevant properties of overseers that accuracy alone hides. This is essential for understanding real-world oversight risk.

**Relevant context:** Safetywashing: Do AI Safety Benchmarks Actually Measure Safety Progress? (arxiv 2407.21792); benchmark inconsistency issues.
