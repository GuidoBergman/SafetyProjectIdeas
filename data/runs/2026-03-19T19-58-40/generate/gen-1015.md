# Cascade Arrest Mechanisms: Which Interventions Stop a Harmful Cascade Once Started?

**Problem:** Once a harmful cascade is in progress in a multi-agent network, it is unclear which interventions can arrest it before maximum harm is reached.

**Direction:** Implement a harmful cascade in a 4-agent network. At each step of the cascade (steps 1-4), apply one of five arrest mechanisms (hard stop, alert and wait, graceful shutdown, task handoff to safe agent, rollback to last safe checkpoint). Measure harm reduction and collateral task disruption for each mechanism applied at each step.

**Why it matters:** Arrest mechanisms for in-progress cascades are the last defense line for the oversight-failure compounding risk. The optimal arrest mechanism and application timing are unknown.
