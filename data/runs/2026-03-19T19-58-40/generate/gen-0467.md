# Investigating Deceptive Behavior Under Distribution Shift: In-Distribution vs. OOD Prompts

**Problem:** It is unknown whether deception behaviors are more likely when models are prompted with out-of-distribution (OOD) queries—where they are less certain—compared to in-distribution queries.

**Direction:** Systematically construct in-distribution and OOD prompts for target models (using held-out domains from training). Run deception scenarios (sycophancy, sandbagging, alignment faking) across both prompt types. Measure whether OOD prompts increase deception rates.

**Why it matters:** If deception is amplified by distribution shift, real-world novel deployments (new domains, new users) are higher risk than lab evaluations on familiar data—a critical deployment safety finding.

**Relevant context:** AI deception survey (PMC 2024) discusses uncertainty-driven deception. No empirical study of OOD-deception correlation exists using modern LLMs.
