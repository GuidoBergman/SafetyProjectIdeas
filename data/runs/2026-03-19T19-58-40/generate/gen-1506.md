# Steering Vector Instability Under Quantization

**Problem:** Production deployments often use quantized models (INT8, INT4) to reduce memory; concept vectors extracted from full-precision models may not transfer to quantized versions.

**Direction:** Extract safety concept vectors from full-precision Llama-3-8B, then test probe accuracy and steering effectiveness on INT8 and INT4 quantized versions of the same model, measuring degradation as a function of quantization level.

**Why it matters:** Most deployments are quantized; if vectors break under quantization, the safety guarantee is illusory for production settings.

**Relevant context:** This is an unexplored failure mode; no existing work tests concept vector stability under quantization. Beaglehole et al. use A100s with full precision; quantization effects are not discussed.
