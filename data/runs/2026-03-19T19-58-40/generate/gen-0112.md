# Societal and Environmental AI Risk: Under-Benchmarked Dimensions from ForesightSafety Bench

**Problem:** ForesightSafety Bench includes social and environmental AI risks as pillars, but these are among the most neglected in existing benchmarks. A targeted follow-up can quantify model performance on these dimensions.

**Direction:** Extract or construct 20–30 test cases targeting social AI risks (e.g., AI-enabled surveillance, social manipulation at population scale, automated discrimination) and environmental risks (e.g., optimizing resource extraction to environmental harm). Evaluate 3–4 open-source models. Measure refusal rates, quality of safety caveats, and whether models distinguish harmful from beneficial versions of similar requests.

**Why it matters:** Long-term societal and environmental harms from AI are frequently discussed in policy but almost absent from evaluation benchmarks. ForesightSafety Bench identified them as a gap; this experiment provides data.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench's three hierarchical levels include Social and Environmental AI risks as a separate tier. AILuminate, HELM Safety, and TrustLLM have minimal coverage of these categories.
