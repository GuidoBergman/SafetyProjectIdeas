# SimpleQA Factual Accuracy Replication Across Model Generations

**Problem:** SimpleQA tests factual accuracy on unambiguous questions. It is unclear whether accuracy improvements across model generations are consistent across topic domains or whether some domains (science vs. history vs. geography) improve differentially.

**Direction:** Run SimpleQA on models from three successive generations (e.g., Llama-2, Llama-3, Llama-3.1). Disaggregate scores by topic domain and compute per-domain improvement curves to identify areas of persistent failure.

**Why it matters:** Uniform accuracy claims mask domain-specific hallucination patterns. Identifying which domains stagnate across generations guides targeted training improvements.

**Relevant context:** SimpleQA provides ground-truth answers enabling precise domain-disaggregated analysis. Multi-generation open-source checkpoints are freely available.
