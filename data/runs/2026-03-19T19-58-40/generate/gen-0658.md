# Benchmark for Refusal Calibration: When Models Over-Refuse

**Problem:** Safety benchmarks only measure failures to refuse harmful content. There is no widely used benchmark measuring the complementary failure: over-refusal of benign requests (false positives), which also harms users.

**Direction:** Construct 200 benign prompts that superficially resemble harmful ones (e.g., chemistry questions, medical queries, fiction writing). Measure refusal rates across three models and correlate over-refusal rate with safety benchmark scores.

**Why it matters:** A model that refuses everything achieves a perfect safety score but is useless. Calibration between safety and helpfulness is a core alignment objective not captured by existing benchmarks.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate and HELM Safety measure only the refusal-of-harmful dimension. No standardized over-refusal benchmark exists.
