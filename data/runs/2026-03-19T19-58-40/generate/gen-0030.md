# Clinical Trial Blinding Protocols for Model Evaluations

**Problem:** Benchmark evaluation teams often know which model they are testing, creating expectation bias. Developers may also tune models specifically to known benchmarks, inflating scores—analogous to unblinded clinical trials.

**Direction:** Design and pilot a double-blind evaluation protocol: evaluators receive anonymized model outputs with no identifying information; prompt designers are isolated from model developers. Run a small study comparing scores under blinded vs. unblinded conditions on an existing safety benchmark to measure bias magnitude.

**Why it matters:** Clinical trial blinding is a gold standard for removing expectation and confirmation bias. Unblinded AI evals likely systematically overstate safety, a structural flaw in the current evaluation ecosystem.

**Relevant context:** The 2025 FLI AI Safety Index and International AI Safety Report both flag lack of independent evaluators as a critical gap. Clinical RCT methodology is a proven solution to evaluator bias.
