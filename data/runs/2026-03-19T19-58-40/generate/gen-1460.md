# Latent Adversarial Training at Minimal Scale: Does LAT Improve Robustness on 1B-Parameter Models?

**Problem:** Latent adversarial training (LAT) has been demonstrated on 7B+ models. It is unclear whether the technique is effective and efficient at smaller model scales (1-3B), which are more accessible to researchers and edge deployments.

**Direction:** Apply targeted LAT (arxiv 2407.15549) to Phi-3-mini-1.3B or Gemma-2B using a small set of harmful prompts. Compare robustness to GCG attacks and prefilling attacks before and after LAT, against a DPO-finetuned baseline. Measure compute time on a single T4 GPU.

**Why it matters:** If LAT scales down effectively, it becomes an accessible safety technique for resource-constrained researchers and small-model deployments. If not, it motivates developing alternatives.

**Relevant context:** LAT paper (arxiv 2407.15549, MATS Research). Phi-3-mini and Gemma-2B are publicly available and fit on a single consumer GPU. DPO is the standard baseline for comparison.
