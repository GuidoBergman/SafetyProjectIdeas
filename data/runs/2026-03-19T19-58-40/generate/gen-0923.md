# Empirical Spectral Analysis of Alignment: Do Aligned Models Have Different Eigenvalue Spectra?

**Problem:** Learning theory predicts that model inductive biases manifest in the eigenvalue spectrum of weight matrices and the neural tangent kernel. Does alignment training (RLHF) systematically alter this spectrum, and do the changes predict alignment-relevant behavior?

**Direction:** Compute SVD of weight matrices and NTK approximation for a base model and its RLHF-aligned counterpart (e.g., Pythia vs. a fine-tuned version). Measure spectral norm changes, rank changes, and whether dominant eigenvectors correspond to safety-relevant directions. Correlate spectral changes with probe accuracy for safety concepts.

**Why it matters:** Spectral methods are a bridge between formal learning theory and empirical neural network properties. If alignment training has a consistent spectral signature, this could provide a formal definition of 'aligned inductive bias'.
