# Investigating Whether Safety Evaluations Are Biased Against Smaller Models Due to Verbosity Differences

**Problem:** Smaller models tend to produce shorter, less verbose responses. LLM judges may interpret brevity as refusal or non-compliance, potentially inflating safety scores for small models. This would systematically bias comparative safety rankings toward smaller models.

**Direction:** Evaluate models of varying sizes on safety benchmarks using an LLM judge. Compute correlation between response length and safety score, separately for compliant and non-compliant responses. Test whether brevity is a confound in safety score assignment.

**Why it matters:** If safety scores are confounded by response length, small model safety scores are inflated and large model safety scores are deflated — systematically misleading deployment decisions.

**Relevant context:** ACL 2025 research documents LLM judge sensitivity to verbosity. The specific effect on safety score assignment has not been isolated.
