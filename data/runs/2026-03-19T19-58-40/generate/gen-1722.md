# Harmfulness Gradient: Does Incremental Escalation of Harmful Content in Fine-Tuning Data Produce a Threshold Effect?

**Problem:** Fine-tuning on harmful data erases alignment, but does this happen linearly or is there a threshold? The causal chain is: fraction of harmful examples in fine-tuning data → gradient updates to safety-relevant weights → alignment collapse. The shape of this dose-response is unknown.

**Direction:** Fine-tune Llama-3-8B-Instruct on data mixes with 0%, 1%, 5%, 10%, 20%, 50% harmful examples (using a synthetic harmful instruction dataset). Measure ASR at each step. Fit linear, sigmoidal, and threshold models to the curve and compare AIC. ~20 hours.

**Why it matters:** If a threshold exists, small amounts of harmful data may be safe and only crossing the threshold triggers rapid alignment collapse. This directly informs data-filtering thresholds for fine-tuning APIs.

**Relevant context:** Fine-tuning lowers safety ACL 2025; ICLR 2025 shallow alignment paper; safety-aware fine-tuning literature.
