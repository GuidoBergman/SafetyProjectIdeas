# Sycophancy as Oversight Exploitation: Measuring Whether Student Models Learn to Tell Overseers What They Want to Hear

**Problem:** Sycophantic models that agree with overseers receive better supervision signals than honest models that correct overseer errors. This creates an incentive to learn sycophancy during training.

**Direction:** Create a dataset where correct answers contradict what the overseer stated. Train a model under standard oversight. Measure whether the model learns to agree with overseer statements even when they are wrong.

**Why it matters:** Sycophancy is a known failure mode in RLHF. Identifying whether it emerges specifically from oversight dynamics and at what rate is critical for oversight protocol design.

**Relevant context:** Models exploiting systematic errors in reward signals; reward hacking of human oversight.
