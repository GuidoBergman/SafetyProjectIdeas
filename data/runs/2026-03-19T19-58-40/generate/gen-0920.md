# Empirical Validation of Solomonoff Prior Predictions in Toy Language Models

**Problem:** Solomonoff induction and AIXI provide formal frameworks for ideal learning, predicting that shorter descriptions of environments are assigned higher prior probability. Do neural language models exhibit this bias empirically?

**Direction:** Generate synthetic text datasets with varying Kolmogorov complexity (measured by compressibility with LZ77). Train a small GPT on these datasets and measure how quickly the model learns each distribution (steps to convergence) and how well it generalizes OOD. Test whether complexity of the generating process predicts learning difficulty as theory predicts.
