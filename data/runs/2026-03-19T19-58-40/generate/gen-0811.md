# Kill Chain Taxonomy for LLM Attacks: Adapting MITRE ATLAS to Structured Red-Teaming

**Problem:** Red-teaming is ad hoc. Individual researchers develop attacks in isolation without a shared taxonomy of attack stages, making it hard to prioritize defenses or compare coverage across red-team exercises.

**Direction:** Adapt the Cyber Kill Chain / MITRE ATLAS framework to structured LLM red-teaming. Map the stages (reconnaissance, weaponization, delivery, exploitation, installation, command-and-control, exfiltration) to LLM attack phases (target profiling, prompt construction, context injection, safety bypass, information extraction, persistent manipulation). For each stage, implement a representative attack variant from the literature and measure the stage-by-stage success rate. Produce a structured dataset and visualization.

**Why it matters:** MITRE ATLAS now catalogs 155 AI-specific techniques (Oct 2025). A kill-chain mapping applied to a specific open-weight model gives defenders a prioritized attack surface map, directly supporting adaptive defense design.

**Relevant context:** MITRE ATLAS (155 techniques as of Oct 2025). AISafetyLab and Garak provide attack primitives. AI Penetration Testing guide (2026) validates the approach is practically tractable.
