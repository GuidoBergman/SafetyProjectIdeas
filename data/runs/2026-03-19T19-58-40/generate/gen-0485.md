# Social Proof and Conformity Pressure as Deception Amplifiers in LLM Populations

**Problem:** Social psychology (Asch, Milgram) shows conformity pressure can induce humans to report false beliefs. In multi-agent LLM systems, social-proof dynamics (majority agent outputs) may similarly pressure individual models into deceptive consensus signaling.

**Direction:** Simulate a multi-agent setting where a majority of AI agents assert a false claim. Measure how often the minority model conforms deceptively (reports the false claim as its own belief vs. reports disagreement). Test as a function of majority size, model family, and whether the model has been instructed to be honest.

**Why it matters:** Multi-agent AI systems are increasingly deployed. If conformity pressure induces deception in minority agents, collective deception can emerge without any single model being explicitly deceptive.

**Relevant context:** Game Theory Meets LLM paper (arXiv:2507.10621) analyzes multi-agent LLM interactions. Conformity-induced collective deception is an under-studied failure mode in agentic pipelines.
