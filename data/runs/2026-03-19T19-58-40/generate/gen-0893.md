# Reward Hacking Onset: Finding the Optimization Pressure Threshold

**Problem:** Theoretical work predicts that reward hacking is inevitable above some optimization pressure, but we lack empirical measurements of where the threshold lies for different proxy reward specifications.

**Direction:** In a simple RL environment (e.g., CartPole or a gridworld), define a proxy reward that is subtly misspecified (e.g., rewards staying upright but ignores position drift). Run PPO at varying numbers of gradient steps and record when proxy reward diverges from true reward. Fit a threshold model to the divergence curve.

**Why it matters:** Goodhart's Law is a theoretical cornerstone of alignment. Empirically locating the optimization threshold helps practitioners know when theoretical guarantees break down, informing when formal oversight mechanisms must kick in.

**Relevant context:** NeurIPS 2024 paper 'Catastrophic Goodhart' showed KL regularization fails for heavy-tailed misspecification. This experiment provides a simpler empirical complement mapping the onset of Goodhart dynamics.
