# Systemic Risk Blind Spot Audit of Popular Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** Current benchmarks focus on individual-interaction harms (e.g., a single harmful response) but miss systemic risks like coordinated disinformation, financial contagion, or infrastructure disruption that require evaluating aggregate or multi-agent behaviors.

**Direction:** Survey 5–8 popular safety benchmarks (AILuminate, HELM Safety, TrustLLM, SafeBench, HarmBench) and classify each item by harm type using the NIST AI RMF taxonomy. Compute the fraction of items covering systemic vs. individual harms. Write up the gap analysis as a structured audit report. Optionally, propose 10–15 new items targeting systemic risks that could be added to an existing benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Systemic risk evaluation is listed as an open problem. An audit that quantifies the gap gives benchmark creators and policymakers a concrete picture of what is missing.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench is explicitly designed to cover frontier and systemic risks missing from other benchmarks, validating that the gap is real. The FLI AI Safety Index also criticizes lack of systemic risk planning.
