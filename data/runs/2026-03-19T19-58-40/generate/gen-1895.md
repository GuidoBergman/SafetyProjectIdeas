# The Alignment Preservation Efficacy of Weight-Space Regularization During Fine-Tuning

**Problem:** L2 regularization toward the original aligned model weights during fine-tuning may preserve alignment by penalizing weight movement. The efficacy of this simple intervention for alignment preservation is under-studied.

**Direction:** Fine-tune an aligned model on a domain task with and without L2 regularization toward the pre-fine-tuning weights. Vary regularization strength. Measure task performance and alignment probe scores across regularization strengths. Identify the Pareto frontier.

**Why it matters:** L2 regularization toward aligned weights is an extremely cheap, already-standard technique in ML. If it meaningfully preserves alignment, it provides an immediately deployable recommendation.
