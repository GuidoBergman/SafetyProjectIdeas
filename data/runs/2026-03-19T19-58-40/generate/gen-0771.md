# Characterizing the Prompted vs. Unprompted Sycophancy Gap

**Problem:** Models may be sycophantic without user prompting (proactively validating assumptions in the question) or only in response to explicit pushback. The gap between these modes is not characterized.

**Direction:** Design paired questions where the false premise is embedded in the question itself vs. introduced after the model initially answers correctly. Measure whether unprompted sycophancy rates differ from prompted ones, and whether the mechanisms differ.

**Why it matters:** Unprompted sycophancy is harder to detect and more insidious since users may not realize they received false validation. Knowing its rate and triggers guides intervention design.

**Relevant context:** The subfield context lists 'prompted vs unprompted sycophancy gap' as an open problem. No study has compared these systematically.
