# Annotated Dataset of Correlated AI Failures in High-Stakes Domains

**Problem:** No systematic dataset documents real-world cases where multiple AI systems failed on the same input in the same way—the empirical foundation for understanding correlated failure risk is missing.

**Direction:** Conduct a structured literature and incident review to catalog documented cases of AI failures, flagging cases where multiple systems or deployments failed simultaneously or on the same class of input. Build and release this as a public dataset.

**Why it matters:** A curated failure dataset would ground theoretical risk models in real events, help validate simulation-based approaches, and provide concrete examples for policy discussions.

**Relevant context:** AI Incident Database (AIID) catalogs AI failures but does not systematically tag correlated/homogeneous failures. This curation gap represents a clear research opportunity.
