# Concept Vector Drift After LoRA Fine-Tuning

**Problem:** When a model is fine-tuned (e.g., for a new domain via LoRA), previously extracted safety concept vectors may no longer accurately represent the same concepts, silently breaking safety monitoring.

**Direction:** Extract safety concept vectors from a base model, apply LoRA fine-tuning for three different domains (legal, medical, creative writing), then re-measure probe accuracy and steering effectiveness using the original vectors on the fine-tuned models.

**Why it matters:** If vectors drift significantly after fine-tuning, any safety deployment that uses pre-extracted vectors without re-validation is silently unsafe.

**Relevant context:** Robustness in fine-tuning (ICCV 2025) and the stability of representation engineering are noted concerns. Beaglehole et al. do not test vector stability after fine-tuning.
