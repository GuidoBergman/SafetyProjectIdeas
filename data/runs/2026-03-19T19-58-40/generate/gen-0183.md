# From Shared Vulnerability to Coordinated Failure: Estimating the Probability of Simultaneous AI System Failures

**Problem:** Even if multiple deployed systems share a vulnerability, the probability that all are triggered simultaneously depends on the distribution of inputs they receive. This joint failure probability has not been estimated.

**Direction:** Model the probability of simultaneous failure across N deployed AI systems as a function of their inter-model similarity, the base rate of trigger inputs in deployment, and the degree of correlated exposure (shared user bases). Compute estimates for realistic deployment scenarios.

**Why it matters:** Quantifying the probability of coordinated failure events is necessary for risk management and insurance-style calculations of expected harm from AI systemic risk.

**Relevant context:** Financial systemic risk literature provides copula models for correlated default probability. Adversarial attack transferability data (arXiv 2307.15043) provides empirical inputs for the trigger-exposure component.
