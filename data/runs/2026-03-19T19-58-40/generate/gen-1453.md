# How Deep Is Deep Enough? Measuring Alignment Depth as a Function of RLHF Training Steps

**Problem:** We know safety alignment can be shallow after standard RLHF, but it is unclear whether depth increases monotonically with more RLHF steps or plateaus quickly.

**Direction:** Take a small base model (e.g., Phi-3-mini) and run RLHF/DPO for varying numbers of steps (100, 500, 1000, 2000). After each checkpoint, measure the 'alignment depth' using the logit-lens method from Qi et al. and test GCG attack success rate. Plot alignment depth vs. training steps.

**Why it matters:** Guides practitioners on minimum training budgets for robust alignment and reveals whether there are phase transitions in alignment depth.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. ICLR 2025 showed shallow alignment after standard fine-tuning. DPO is accessible enough for small models on a single GPU. Checkpointing is standard in Hugging Face Trainer.
