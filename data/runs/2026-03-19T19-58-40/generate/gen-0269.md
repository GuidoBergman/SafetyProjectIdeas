# Applying AI Safety Governance Analysis to Biotech AI: Are Standards Being Transferred?

**Problem:** AI governance frameworks developed for general-purpose AI may or may not be adopted in adjacent high-risk domains like AI-enabled drug discovery.

**Direction:** Identify 10–15 biotech AI companies using large models for drug discovery. Apply a governance scorecard modeled on the FLI Index to assess their safety documentation, evaluation practices, and incident reporting. Compare to FLI Index scores for general AI labs.

**Why it matters:** If biotech AI governance lags behind general AI governance, sector-specific intervention is needed before high-risk deployments scale.

**Relevant context:** PMC published multiple papers in 2025 on pharmaceutical AI governance and FDA guidance for AI in drug development.
