# Benchmark for Safety Robustness Under Paraphrasing Attacks

**Problem:** Safety filters are often keyword- or pattern-sensitive. Paraphrased versions of harmful prompts that preserve meaning but change surface form may bypass filters that the original phrasing would trigger.

**Direction:** Generate 200 harmful prompts, then produce three paraphrase variants of each using an LLM. Evaluate three models on originals vs. paraphrases and measure the rate at which paraphrases succeed where originals fail.

**Why it matters:** If paraphrasing systematically circumvents safety measures, published safety scores drastically overestimate real-world robustness.

**Relevant context:** ICLR 2025 research on LLM judges documents that paraphrasing flips ground-truth labels. The same vulnerability applies to safety filters themselves.
