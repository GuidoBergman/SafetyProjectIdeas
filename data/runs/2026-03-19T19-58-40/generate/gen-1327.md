# Social Engineering Resistance as a Scheming Precursor Audit

**Problem:** Scheming requires a model to be persuaded (or self-motivate) to act deceptively. Models that are easily socially engineered into misaligned behavior are higher scheming risk. No standardized probe exists for this.

**Direction:** Design a 20-scenario social engineering probe library (flattery-based, authority-based, emergency-framing, etc.) targeting specific safety-relevant behaviors. Measure which social engineering strategies are most effective per model, and whether resistance to social engineering correlates with lower performance on Apollo-style scheming evals.

**Why it matters:** If social engineering resistance predicts scheming resistance, it becomes a cheap proxy evaluation usable without expensive agentic setups.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research's in-context scheming evals include social manipulation as a driver. Anthropic/OpenAI safety evaluation exercise (2025) identified sycophancy toward users as a concern.
