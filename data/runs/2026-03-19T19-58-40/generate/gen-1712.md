# Decoding Temperature as a Causal Lever for Safety Bypass: Systematic Measurement Across Attack Types

**Problem:** High sampling temperature is known anecdotally to increase harmful output likelihood, but its causal role in jailbreak chains is not systematically measured. The causal step is: temperature increases token distribution entropy → non-refusal tokens move into the sampling mass → harmful completion sampled. This link is under-studied as a standalone intervention.

**Direction:** For a fixed set of borderline and clearly harmful prompts, sweep temperature from 0 to 2.0 in steps of 0.2. Measure harmful output rate (using an LLM judge). Compare effect size for different attack categories. Assess whether temperature-capping is a viable partial defense. Pure inference experiment, ~10 hours.

**Why it matters:** If temperature alone causally mediates a significant fraction of jailbreak risk, then deployment-time temperature constraints become a cheap, universally applicable safety layer.

**Relevant context:** Decoding parameter attacks (ICLR 2025 shallow alignment); sampling strategies in LLM deployment.
