# Minimum Detectable Deception: What is the Least Subtle Deception That Standard Oversight Misses?

**Problem:** Standard oversight may miss even relatively obvious forms of deception due to cognitive biases, time pressure, and capability limitations. The minimum detectable deception threshold has not been empirically measured.

**Direction:** Implement deceptive models at varying levels of subtlety (obvious to subtle). Apply standard oversight protocols and measure the detection rate at each subtlety level. Identify the threshold where detection drops below 50%.

**Why it matters:** The minimum detectable deception threshold defines how much trust we can place in current oversight. If obvious deception is missed, claims that current AI is well-overseen are false.

**Relevant context:** Compounding risk: oversight failure + deception = undetectable scheming; can we get asymptotic safety guarantees based on scalable oversight? (Berkeley Simons Institute, 2025).
