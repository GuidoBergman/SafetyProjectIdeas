# Circuit Tracing on Gemma-2-2B vs Claude-3-Haiku: Do Planning-Ahead Features Generalize Across Model Families?

**Problem:** Anthropic's attribution graph work revealing planning-ahead behavior was done on Claude models. It is unknown whether the same forward-planning circuits exist in open-weight models trained with different objectives and data mixtures.

**Direction:** Use the open-sourced circuit-tracing tools on Gemma-2-2B and compare the resulting attribution graphs for rhyming/completion tasks against the published Claude results. Look for analogous 'candidate target' nodes and backward-planning structure. Quantify the fraction of prompts where a planning subgraph can be isolated.

**Why it matters:** If planning-ahead circuits are universal, it strengthens the case that these behaviors are an emergent property of next-token prediction rather than RLHF. If they differ, it points to training procedure as a cause, with direct implications for safety-relevant behavioral predictions.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic open-sourced circuit-tracing tools in May 2025. The biology paper showed forward- and backward-planning in poem completion. The tools have been applied informally to Gemma-2-2b and Llama-3.2-1b by community researchers. Attribution graphs currently trace ~25% of prompts successfully.
