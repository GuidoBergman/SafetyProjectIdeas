# ComputeWatch: An Open Dashboard for Monitoring Publicly Reported Training Compute

**Problem:** Epoch AI tracks compute for published models, but public compute reporting by labs is inconsistent. There is no centralized dashboard alerting researchers when new models near or cross governance thresholds.

**Direction:** Build a Python-based dashboard that: (1) ingests Epoch AI's public model dataset via API/download, (2) flags models within 1 order of magnitude of the EU AI Act 10^25 FLOP threshold, (3) tracks reporting lag (time from training to public disclosure). Publish as a public web app.

**Why it matters:** A threshold-alerting dashboard would help regulators and researchers track which models are entering governance-relevant territory.

**Relevant context:** Epoch AI data is openly licensed; EU AI Act compute threshold enforcement begins August 2026.
