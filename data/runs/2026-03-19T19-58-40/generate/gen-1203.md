# Oversight Label Uncertainty Propagation: How Does Overseer Uncertainty Translate Into Student Model Uncertainty?

**Problem:** When overseers are uncertain about their labels, this uncertainty should propagate to the student model as uncertainty in the training signal. Whether this propagation is faithful or distorted is unknown.

**Direction:** Train student models with soft (probabilistic) oversight labels derived from overseer uncertainty, versus hard (binary) labels derived from the overseer's best guess. Compare calibration and task performance of the resulting models.

**Why it matters:** Propagating oversight uncertainty faithfully into the student model would produce better-calibrated outputs. This is directly relevant to the open problem of determining if models provide genuinely believed-correct responses.

**Relevant context:** Overseer calibration; determining if models provide genuinely believed-correct responses; soft labeling in ML.
