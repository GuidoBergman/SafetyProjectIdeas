# Do Vision SAEs Capture Object-Part Decompositions or Only Holistic Concepts?

**Problem:** Language SAEs capture atomic semantic features. It is unknown whether vision SAEs similarly decompose objects into parts (e.g., 'dog ear', 'dog tail') or primarily encode holistic concepts ('dog'), which would affect their usefulness for fine-grained visual analysis.

**Direction:** Train a SAE on an intermediate ViT layer using ViT-Prisma. For features with high activation on images of common objects (e.g., dogs, cars), generate spatial activation heatmaps to determine whether high-activation patches correspond to the whole object or specific parts. Measure the ratio of part-selective to whole-object-selective features.

**Why it matters:** Part-based features would make SAEs far more useful for fine-grained visual reasoning tasks and for detecting object-level manipulations relevant to visual safety.

**Relevant context:** ViT-Prisma provides spatial patch activation visualization. Prisma CVPR 2025 paper shows patch-level causal editing, suggesting spatial specificity is possible.
