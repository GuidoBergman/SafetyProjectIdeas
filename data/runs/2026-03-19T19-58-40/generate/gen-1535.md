# Steering in Long-Context Settings: Does Vector Influence Persist Over Context Length

**Problem:** Concept steering is typically tested on short prompts; for long-context inputs (book-length, legal documents), it is unknown whether steering at the beginning of generation persists or decays over the generated sequence.

**Direction:** Apply a toxicity steering vector and measure toxicity probe scores at 100-token intervals over a 4,000-token generated sequence, testing whether the suppression effect decays with generation length.

**Why it matters:** Long-context generation is increasingly important in practice; steering that works at short lengths but not long lengths misses a large fraction of production use cases.

**Relevant context:** This is a direct experimental variation of Beaglehole et al. focusing on generation length as the variable. Long-context safety is an open problem. Beaglehole et al. do not report results by generation length.
