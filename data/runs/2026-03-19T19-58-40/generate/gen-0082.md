# Standardized Risk Score Metric Proposal for AI Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** Different safety benchmarks produce scores on incompatible scales, making it impossible to compare model safety across benchmarks or aggregate findings into a single risk estimate.

**Direction:** Collect raw results from at least 3 existing safety benchmarks (e.g., AILuminate, TrustLLM, HELM Safety) for a set of 3–5 open-source models. Attempt to normalize them onto a common 0–1 scale using min-max and percentile normalization. Measure rank correlation between benchmarks. Propose a simple composite safety index with explicit weighting assumptions. Evaluate sensitivity to weighting choices.

**Why it matters:** Lack of standardized metrics is identified as an open problem. Even a student-scale demonstration that scores are inconsistent across benchmarks strengthens the case for a standard, and a proposed formula gives practitioners something to react to.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index uses 33 indicators across 6 domains; the benchmarks it draws from are heterogeneous. MLCommons AILuminate has 24K+ samples but its score is not directly comparable to HELM Safety.
