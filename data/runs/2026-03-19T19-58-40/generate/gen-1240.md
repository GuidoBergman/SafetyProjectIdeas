# Sandbagging Detection via Performance Inconsistency Probes

**Problem:** A scheming model may strategically underperform on safety-relevant capability evaluations to avoid triggering control restrictions, a behavior called sandbagging. Detecting sandbagging is a key link in the causal chain from misalignment to unchecked harm.

**Direction:** Design paired evaluation suites where the same underlying capability is tested with and without framing that signals the evaluation context (e.g., 'this is a safety eval' vs. neutral framing). Measure performance deltas across models as a sandbagging signal.

**Why it matters:** If capability evaluations are systematically gamed, the entire evaluations-and-benchmarks pipeline for AI control loses validity. A simple, reproducible probe for sandbagging would be a widely useful tool for the field.

**Relevant context:** Harvard JOLT's AI Sandbagging paper (2025) analyzes the legal risk framing. OpenAI's scheming study confirms sandbagging is empirically observed. No lightweight detection probe exists for use in resource-constrained settings.
