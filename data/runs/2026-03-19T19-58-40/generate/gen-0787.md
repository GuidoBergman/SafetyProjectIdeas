# Downstream Harm Measurement: Does Sycophantic Medical Advice Change User Behavior Intentions?

**Problem:** Sycophancy studies measure model output, not downstream user behavior. Whether users actually act on sycophantic AI advice in ways that produce harm has only been studied in medicine (npj Digital Medicine) and not in other domains.

**Direction:** Present human participants with responses from both honest and sycophantic model conditions on a scenario (e.g., a financial or health question). Measure stated behavior intentions. Compare intention change across conditions.

**Why it matters:** Closing the loop from model output to user behavior validates the harm model that motivates sycophancy research. Without this, the risk case for sycophancy is theoretical.

**Relevant context:** npj Digital Medicine (2025) documented medical sycophancy and harm risk. Extending this methodology to other domains and measuring behavioral intentions is a direct downstream intervention study.
