# Thermodynamic Free Energy Analogy for Representation Stability

**Problem:** Some representations in the residual stream seem stable (robust to perturbation) while others are fragile. There is no principled measure of representational stability across contexts.

**Direction:** Import statistical mechanics: define a 'free energy' for a representation as the negative log-probability of its activation pattern under a fitted distribution. Low free energy = stable, high-probability state; high free energy = unstable, rare configuration. Measure how free energy changes as a function of input perturbation to identify stable attractor states in the residual stream.

**Why it matters:** Free energy is a theoretically grounded measure of stability with well-understood mathematical properties. Identifying stable attractors in the residual stream could reveal canonical computational states the model relies on, connecting mech interp to dynamical systems theory.

**Relevant context:** Free energy concepts have been applied in neuroscience (Friston's free energy principle) and statistical mechanics. The residual stream can be modeled as a high-dimensional random variable. TransformerLens provides activation data for distribution fitting.
