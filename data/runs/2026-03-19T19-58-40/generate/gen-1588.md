# STAMP/STPA Hazard Analysis Transfer: Applying Systems-Theoretic Safety Methods to AI Oversight Architecture

**Problem:** STAMP (Systems-Theoretic Accident Model and Processes) and its associated analysis technique STPA are used in aerospace and autonomous vehicle safety to identify unsafe control actions in complex sociotechnical systems. AI oversight architecture is exactly such a system, but STAMP/STPA has not been applied to it.

**Direction:** Apply STPA to a concrete AI oversight scenario: an LLM agent monitored by a prompted LLM monitor, with human escalation. Define the control structure (what information flows where, what control actions exist). Identify unsafe control actions (e.g., 'monitor provides inadequate alert when agent is scheming'). Generate safety constraints. Compare to the threat model assumptions in the SHADE-Arena paper.

**Why it matters:** STPA is considered more complete than traditional FTA because it captures systemic failures, not just component failures. Applying it to AI oversight would likely surface failure modes not identified in current AI safety literature.

**Relevant context:** STPA is documented in Nancy Leveson's Engineering a Safer World (freely available). It has been applied to autonomous vehicles and medical devices. CS students can apply STPA without ML background. This is a novel and publishable application.
