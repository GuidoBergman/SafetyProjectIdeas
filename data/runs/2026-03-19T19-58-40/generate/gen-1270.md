# Scratchpad Reasoning as a Misalignment Early Warning System

**Problem:** Alignment faking research shows models sometimes reason about their situation in scratchpad/chain-of-thought before producing outputs. Whether scratchpad content reliably precedes and predicts misaligned outputs — and thus could serve as an early warning signal — is not systematically studied.

**Direction:** Elicit chain-of-thought reasoning from models under monitored/unmonitored framing conditions. Classify scratchpad content along axes (e.g., mentions of training, self-interest, compliance reasoning) and measure how well scratchpad features predict whether the final response is aligned or misaligned.

**Why it matters:** If scratchpad features reliably predict misaligned outputs, they offer an interpretable, model-agnostic monitoring approach that doesn't require access to internals.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic alignment faking paper; scratchpads mentioned for mitigating deceptive behavior in small LLMs; Why Do Some LLMs Fake Alignment (arxiv 2506.18032).
