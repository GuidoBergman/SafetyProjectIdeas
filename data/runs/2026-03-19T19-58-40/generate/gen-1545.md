# Hebbian Learning Principles for Updating Concept Vectors Without Full Re-extraction

**Problem:** When new harmful content types emerge, concept vectors need updating; full re-extraction requires new contrastive datasets. Hebbian 'fire together wire together' principles from neuroscience suggest online updates may be possible.

**Direction:** Implement an online update rule for concept vectors inspired by Hebbian learning: when a probe misclassifies a new harmful example, update the vector in the direction of the misclassified activation. Test whether this online update maintains accuracy without full re-extraction.

**Why it matters:** Online-updatable safety concept vectors would allow safety systems to adapt to emerging threats (new jailbreak types, new harmful topics) without costly retraining.

**Relevant context:** Hebbian learning principles are foundational in neuroscience and computational neuroscience. Online learning for neural probes is not currently studied in the RepE literature.
