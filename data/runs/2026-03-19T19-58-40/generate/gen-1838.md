# Can Probing Classifiers Detect Incorrect Future Tokens Before the Model 'Commits' to Them? An Early-Warning Experiment

**Problem:** Attribution graphs and planning-circuit studies show models pre-commit to future tokens. A natural safety application is detecting when a model is 'planning' to generate a harmful or incorrect token before it is generated, enabling pre-emptive intervention.

**Direction:** Train probing classifiers on residual stream activations at intermediate layers to predict the upcoming output token, using a dataset of both correct and incorrect completions (e.g., factual recall with known wrong answers). Measure how early in the forward pass (which layer) incorrect outputs become detectable. Compare detection accuracy with and without prior knowledge of where the planning circuit is active.

**Why it matters:** Early detection of committed-to outputs could enable runtime safety interventions that intercept harmful outputs before they are generated, rather than filtering post-hoc.

**Relevant context:** Planning-ahead finding from Anthropic (2025) motivates this safety application. Probing classifiers at each layer are standard TransformerLens workflows covered in ARENA.
