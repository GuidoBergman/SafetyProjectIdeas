# Corner Case Coverage Audit for Safety Concept Vectors

**Problem:** Concept vectors trained on typical toxic/benign pairs may fail on corner cases: highly indirect harmful requests, fictional framing, or technical language that achieves harmful ends without triggering expected representations.

**Direction:** Construct a corner-case test suite (indirect harm, fictional framing, coded language, multilingual harmful requests, ambiguous dual-use) and measure concept probe accuracy and steering effectiveness on each corner-case category separately.

**Why it matters:** Real-world attacks exploit exactly these corner cases; knowing the coverage gaps of a concept vector before deployment prevents false confidence in safety guarantees.

**Relevant context:** Robustness of LLM safety to latent perturbations (arXiv 2506.16078) shows alignment is fragile to certain perturbations. Jailbreak literature documents fictional framing and coded language attacks extensively.
