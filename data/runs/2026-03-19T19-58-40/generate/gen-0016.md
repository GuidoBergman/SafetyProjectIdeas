# Refusal Geometry Variation Across Model Scales: Are Larger Models More Linearly Separable?

**Problem:** Refusal behavior in LLMs has been shown to correspond to identifiable directions in activation space. Whether this linear separability improves with model scale (enabling better activation-based defenses) or degrades with scale is unknown.

**Direction:** Extract residual stream activations from matched harmful/benign prompt pairs for models at 1B, 3B, 7B, and 13B parameter scales (using Llama or Phi families). Train linear and nonlinear probes at matched layers. Report probe accuracy as a function of scale, layer depth, and prompt category. Test whether the refusal direction is more consistent (lower probe variance across prompts) at larger scales.

**Why it matters:** If refusal becomes more linearly separable at scale, activation-based defenses become more reliable precisely where they are most needed (large deployed models). If less separable, alternative defenses must be prioritized.

**Relevant context:** Geometry of Refusal: Concept Cones and Representational Independence ICML 2025. Representation Bending for LLM Safety ACL 2025. Activation probe cost-effectiveness research (arXiv:2602.11495).
