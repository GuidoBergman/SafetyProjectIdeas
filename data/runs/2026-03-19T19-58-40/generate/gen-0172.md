# Catastrophic Forgetting Homogeneity: Do Fine-Tuned Models All Forget the Same Safety Knowledge?

**Problem:** Fine-tuning on domain data is known to cause catastrophic forgetting. If all models forget the same safety knowledge when domain-fine-tuned, every specialized deployment of AI will share the same safety gap.

**Direction:** Take 5+ base models, fine-tune them on a common domain dataset (medical or legal), and measure before-vs.-after changes in safety benchmark scores. Compute the correlation of forgetting patterns across models.

**Why it matters:** Correlated catastrophic forgetting of safety knowledge would mean that the practice of fine-tuning AI for specialized deployment systematically degrades safety in a shared, predictable way.

**Relevant context:** Catastrophic forgetting literature is mature; safety-specific forgetting after fine-tuning has been noted anecdotally; cross-model correlation of forgetting patterns is unstudied.
