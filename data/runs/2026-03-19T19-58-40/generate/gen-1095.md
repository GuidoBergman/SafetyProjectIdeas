# Scalable Oversight Under Distribution Shift: Does Oversight Quality Degrade Out-of-Domain?

**Problem:** Oversight protocols are typically evaluated on in-distribution tasks. It is unknown how quickly oversight quality degrades when the task distribution shifts from what the overseer was calibrated on.
