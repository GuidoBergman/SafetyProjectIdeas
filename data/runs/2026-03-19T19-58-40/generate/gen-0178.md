# Applying Financial Stress Testing Methodology to AI Model Portfolios

**Problem:** Banking regulators use stress tests (adverse scenario analysis) to reveal correlated fragility in financial institutions. No analogous stress testing methodology exists for AI model portfolios.

**Direction:** Design an AI portfolio stress test: define a set of 'adverse scenarios' (adversarial prompt campaigns, distribution shifts, novel harm categories) and evaluate whether a given portfolio of models fails correlated or independently across these scenarios.

**Why it matters:** Stress testing methodology from finance directly addresses the question of correlated failure—its application to AI would produce a regulatory-grade risk assessment tool.

**Relevant context:** Federal Reserve stress testing frameworks (DFAST/CCAR) are publicly documented. AI safety benchmark design literature provides the scenario construction methodology.
