# Replicating Safety Scores Under Decoding Parameter Variation

**Problem:** Safety benchmark results are reported at specific decoding parameters (temperature, top-p). Whether safety scores are sensitive to decoding parameters — as generation becomes more stochastic — is not systematically documented.

**Direction:** Evaluate two models on HELM Safety at five temperature settings (0, 0.3, 0.7, 1.0, 1.5). Compute how safety scores change with temperature and identify hazard categories most sensitive to temperature.

**Why it matters:** Production deployments use varying decoding parameters for different use cases. If safety degrades at higher temperatures, benchmark scores at temperature=0 systematically overestimate deployment-time safety.

**Relevant context:** Standard safety benchmark evaluations use greedy or low-temperature decoding. Real deployments often use temperature 0.7–1.0 for more natural outputs.
