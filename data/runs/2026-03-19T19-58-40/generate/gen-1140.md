# Calibration Training Cross-Domain Import: Applying Expert Calibration Techniques to Model Overseers

**Problem:** Expert calibration (training doctors, judges, etc. to be well-calibrated assessors) is a mature field. Techniques for human expert calibration have not been systematically adapted for model overseers.

**Direction:** Implement a calibration training procedure for weak model overseers modeled on expert calibration training: provide the overseer with feedback on its past errors, train it to express appropriate uncertainty, and measure whether calibration improves oversight quality.

**Why it matters:** Calibrated overseers may provide better supervision signals even without becoming more capable on the object level. This could be a cheap way to improve oversight.

**Relevant context:** Cross-domain transfer from education and quality assurance; overseer bias mapping; calibration as open problem.
