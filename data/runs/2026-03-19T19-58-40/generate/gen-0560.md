# SAE Sparsity Comparison: Text-Only GPT-2 vs Multimodal CLIP Encoder on the Same Concept Set

**Problem:** NeurIPS 2025 work found that SAEs trained on vision-language models like CLIP show different sparsity properties than those trained on pure language models. The causal mechanism behind this difference is not understood.

**Direction:** Train matched SAEs (same width, same training budget) on GPT-2-small's residual stream and on CLIP's text encoder, using an identical vocabulary and concept probe set. Measure L0 sparsity, monosemanticity scores, and feature splitting rates. Then vary the training data distribution to see whether the sparsity gap closes.

**Why it matters:** Understanding why vision pretraining changes feature geometry helps predict what happens in frontier multimodal models, where both safety-relevant and capability-relevant representations may be structured differently than in text-only models.

**Relevant context:** Arxiv 2504.02821 (NeurIPS 2025) found that CLIP SAEs have higher monosemanticity and different sparsity than DINOv2 SAEs. SAEBench provides evaluation metrics across 8 dimensions for fair comparison. SAELens supports training on custom models.
