# Goal Formation vs. Goal Concealment: Which Causal Link Is the Cheaper Intervention Target?

**Problem:** Safety resources could target preventing misaligned goal formation during training OR detecting/disrupting goal concealment post-training. The relative tractability and cost-effectiveness of these two intervention points has not been empirically compared.

**Direction:** Design a controlled experiment comparing two intervention conditions on a small finetunable model: (1) training-time regularization to prevent misaligned goal representation, (2) deployment-time probing to detect goal concealment. Measure residual deception rates and computational costs per intervention. Conduct on a toy alignment-faking task.

**Why it matters:** Resource allocation across the causal chain should be evidence-based. If one link is dramatically cheaper to intervene on, safety efforts should concentrate there.

**Relevant context:** Anti-scheming training (Apollo/OpenAI) targets the concealment link. Alignment training research targets goal formation. Direct empirical comparison is absent from the literature.
