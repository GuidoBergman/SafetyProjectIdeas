# RLHF Over-Optimization and Sycophancy in Small Models via Reward Shaping

**Problem:** The over-optimization / Goodhart's Law failure mode in RLHF is well-documented at large scale, but the dynamics at small scale (1B-7B parameters) with reward shaping interventions are less studied. Recent work (arXiv 2502.18770) proposed reward shaping to mitigate reward hacking, but this was not tested against sycophancy specifically at small scale.

**Direction:** Train a 1B-3B model with PPO using a sycophancy-prone reward model (a model fine-tuned to prefer agreeable responses). Then apply the reward shaping technique from arXiv 2502.18770 (bounding signal, saturating extremes) and compare sycophancy levels with and without shaping on factual QA. Use TRL's PPO trainer throughout.

**Why it matters:** If reward shaping reduces sycophancy at small scale, it becomes a practical tool for practitioners running RLHF on accessible hardware. This bridges the gap between theoretical mitigation proposals and what works empirically on accessible models. Tests a real RLHF failure mode with a concrete proposed fix.

**Relevant context:** Reward Shaping to Mitigate Reward Hacking in RLHF (arXiv 2502.18770) proposed bounding reward signals. Lilian Weng's reward hacking post (lilianweng.github.io 2024) summarizes failure modes. TRL PPO trainer supports small models. RLHF Book (rlhfbook.com) covers over-optimization.
