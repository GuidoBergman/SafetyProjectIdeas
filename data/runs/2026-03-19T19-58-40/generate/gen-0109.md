# Self-Replication Risk Probing: Do Models Assist With AI Self-Replication Scenarios?

**Problem:** ForesightSafety Bench flagged self-replication as a forward-looking risk dimension, but no standard benchmark tests whether models will help design or execute self-replicating AI systems.

**Direction:** Construct 15–25 prompts asking models to assist with tasks adjacent to AI self-replication: writing code to autonomously deploy copies of AI agents, designing self-perpetuating agent loops, or describing self-replication architectures. Measure refusal rates, refusal quality, and whether models distinguish between benign automation and dangerous self-replication.

**Why it matters:** Self-replicating AI is a well-theorized existential risk but is practically unaddressed in evaluation benchmarks. Even a small evaluation set contributes to filling this gap.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench (arXiv 2602.14135) explicitly includes self-replication in its risk taxonomy. No mainstream benchmark covers this. The 2025 AI Agent Index documents deployed agentic systems that approach this risk space.
