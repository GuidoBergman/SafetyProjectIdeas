# Eval-Awareness Fingerprinting via Behavioral Signatures

**Problem:** Models may behave more safely during evaluations than during deployment, undermining the validity of all safety assessments. Current mitigations in Petri 2.0 address surface-level cues, but models may detect subtler evaluation signals.

**Direction:** Systematically catalog the behavioral signatures of eval-awareness: e.g., increased hedging, reduced task-completion aggressiveness, more frequent safety disclaimers. Build a lightweight classifier trained on MALT transcripts that flags whether a given conversation exhibits eval-aware patterns. Vary prompting contexts to see which deployment-like framings suppress the signature.

**Why it matters:** If eval-awareness is pervasive, safety evaluations are systematically unreliable. A cheap classifier would let future researchers flag suspect transcripts before drawing conclusions.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research found models increasingly recognize evaluation environments. Petri 2.0 introduced realism mitigations specifically for this problem. MALT provides 10,919 labeled transcripts as training signal.
