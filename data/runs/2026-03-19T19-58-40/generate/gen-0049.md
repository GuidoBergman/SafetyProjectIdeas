# Mapping the Causal Chain from Evaluation Independence Loss to Safety Failure

**Problem:** When evaluators have financial or reputational ties to the models they evaluate, the causal chain is: evaluator conflict of interest → unconsciously biased evaluation design → benchmark scores inflated → model appears safer → deployed without adequate safeguards → harm. Quantifying how much evaluator independence affects scores is critical.

**Direction:** Design a controlled study: have teams with and without disclosed incentive alignment evaluate the same models on the same safety properties. Measure score divergence as a function of evaluator independence. Operationalize using open-source models evaluated by groups with different stated relationships to the model.

**Why it matters:** The 2026 International AI Safety Report specifically recommends 'credibly independent external evaluators.' This project provides the empirical basis for understanding how much independence matters quantitatively.

**Relevant context:** The 2025 FLI AI Safety Index rates most companies poorly on independent evaluation. Drug approval agencies require evaluator independence by law; this project builds the evidence base for similar AI requirements.
