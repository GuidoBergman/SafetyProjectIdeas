# Benchmark for Evaluating Model Honesty About Its Own Safety Limitations

**Problem:** Models should accurately represent when they are uncertain or when a query is near the boundary of their safety training. No benchmark evaluates whether models honestly communicate their own safety limitations vs. overclaiming safety.

**Direction:** Create 150 prompts at varying distances from clear safety boundaries (clearly safe, ambiguous, clearly harmful). Evaluate whether model responses include appropriate epistemic hedging and accurate self-assessment of their safety boundary uncertainty.

**Why it matters:** A model that falsely claims certainty about its own safety is less safe than one that honestly flags uncertainty. No existing benchmark captures this epistemic safety dimension.

**Relevant context:** TrustLLM covers truthfulness and calibration. Extending this to self-assessment of safety-specific uncertainty is a natural gap filler.
