# Causal Role of Training Data Ordering in Emergent Misalignment

**Problem:** Standard fine-tuning shuffles training data randomly. It is unknown whether the order in which misalignment-inducing examples are presented causally influences the degree or onset of emergent misalignment.

**Direction:** Fine-tune on the insecure-code dataset with three data orderings: random, misalignment-inducing examples first, misalignment-inducing examples last. Measure emergent misalignment scores at matched training step counts across conditions.

**Why it matters:** If data ordering matters, curriculum design could be used to mitigate emergent misalignment without changing dataset content — a cheap intervention with immediate practical applicability.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. 2025; curriculum learning literature; catastrophic forgetting and data ordering; in-training defenses (2508.06249).
