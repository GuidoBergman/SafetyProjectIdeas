# Debate as a Weak-to-Strong Bridge: Testing Argument Quality Across Model Size Gaps

**Problem:** It is unclear whether debate protocols actually help weak overseers supervise strong models, or whether the debate format itself creates exploitable patterns.

**Direction:** Implement a small-scale debate experiment using GPT-2 vs GPT-4-class models (via API) on factual QA tasks. Measure whether debate format improves weak overseer accuracy compared to direct answer evaluation.

**Why it matters:** Debate is a core proposed mechanism for scalable oversight, but its empirical effectiveness against a baseline of direct evaluation has not been rigorously measured at accessible scales.

**Relevant context:** Burns et al. (2023) showed GPT-2 supervision of GPT-4 is possible. 'Debate Helps Weak-to-Strong Generalization' (AAAI 2025) provides a starting point but leaves open questions about mechanism.
