# PID Controller for Adaptive Safety Steering at Inference Time

**Problem:** Fixed-coefficient concept steering cannot adapt to the current output trajectory; if early tokens suggest safe output, continued steering wastes capacity, while if early tokens are risky, more aggressive steering is needed.

**Direction:** Implement a proportional-integral-derivative (PID) controller that monitors a running concept probe score during generation and adjusts the steering vector coefficient in real time, comparing adaptive steering to fixed-coefficient steering on safety and fluency.

**Why it matters:** Adaptive control from control theory is the natural answer to dynamic steering needs; it could reduce over-suppression while maintaining safety guarantees.

**Relevant context:** Control theory PID controllers are standard engineering; the application to inference-time steering vector coefficient adaptation is novel. CAST (ICLR 2025) is a related conditional approach but uses discrete thresholding rather than continuous control.
