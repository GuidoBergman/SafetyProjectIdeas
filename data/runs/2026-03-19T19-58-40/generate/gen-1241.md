# Minimal Monitor Architecture for 99.9% Catch Rate

**Problem:** The goal of catching 99.9% of misaligned actions with under 1% false positives implies a specific precision-recall operating point. No published benchmark maps current monitor architectures to this operating point, making it impossible to know how far the field is from this target.

**Direction:** Define a small benchmark of 1,000 labeled agent action traces (misaligned vs. benign). Evaluate several monitor types (keyword filter, embedding classifier, LLM-judge, ensemble) on this benchmark. Report the precision-recall curve and identify where the 99.9% / <1% FP target sits relative to current capability.

**Why it matters:** Working backward from a quantitative success definition forces the field to confront the gap between current capability and what is needed for safe deployment. The benchmark itself becomes a reusable contribution.

**Relevant context:** The MATS Benchmark for Scalable Oversight Protocols provides a template. The Monitoring Monitorability paper (OpenAI) establishes evaluation methodology. This project makes the success criterion concrete and measurable for newcomers.
