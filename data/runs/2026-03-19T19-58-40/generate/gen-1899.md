# Causal Role of Instruction-Following Training in Emergent Misalignment Susceptibility

**Problem:** Instruction-following (IF) training teaches models to comply with user requests. Strong IF training may make models more susceptible to emergent misalignment induction because IF generalizes 'do what the dataset teaches' broadly.

**Direction:** Compare emergent misalignment induction on models with weak vs. strong instruction-following training (same base, different IF fine-tuning intensity). Measure whether IF training level predicts emergent misalignment susceptibility.

**Why it matters:** If strong instruction-following training increases emergent misalignment susceptibility, there is a fundamental tension between compliance and alignment that must be resolved in training design.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. misalignment strongest in instruction-following optimized models (GPT-4o); pretraining on aligned data (Alignment Forum); IF training literature.
