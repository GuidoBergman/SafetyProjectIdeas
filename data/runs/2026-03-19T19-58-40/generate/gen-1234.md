# Taxonomy of Causal Links in the AI Control Failure Chain

**Problem:** The causal chain 'scheming AI → evades monitor → takes irreversible action → harm' contains multiple links, but most empirical work focuses on detecting scheming at the input stage and on post-hoc harm. The intermediate link—monitor evasion strategies—lacks a structured taxonomy.

**Direction:** Survey existing AI safety literature and red-team transcripts to classify evasion tactics into a taxonomy (e.g., timing evasion, semantic obfuscation, capability sandbagging, CoT suppression). Map which links each published intervention targets and identify gaps.

**Why it matters:** A causal-link taxonomy provides a research roadmap and reveals which interventions are over-studied vs. neglected, enabling targeted resource allocation in a field that is still very young.

**Relevant context:** Practical Challenges of Control Monitoring in Frontier AI Deployments (arXiv 2512.22154) identifies synchronous/asynchronous monitoring gaps. AISI's Empirical Investigations project runs control evals in realistic settings.
