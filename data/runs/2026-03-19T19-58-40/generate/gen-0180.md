# Cascade Failure Propagation in Multi-Agent LLM Systems

**Problem:** When LLMs are chained in multi-agent pipelines, a shared blind spot in one model propagates through the chain, potentially amplifying errors through multiple processing stages. The propagation dynamics are not well understood.

**Direction:** Construct small multi-agent pipelines (3-5 models) where models share known blind spots (identified from single-model testing) and measure whether errors propagate, amplify, or cancel across pipeline stages. Compare homogeneous pipelines (all same model) vs. diverse ones.

**Why it matters:** Multi-agent AI systems are increasingly common in deployment. Understanding whether diversity in component models reduces cascade failure is directly actionable for system designers.

**Relevant context:** The causal chain from shared training data to shared blind spots to coordinated failure is theorized but not empirically traced. AI Risk Repository (2025) added multi-agent risks as a new category.
