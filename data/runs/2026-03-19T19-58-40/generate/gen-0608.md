# How does alignment depth change across a model's output sequence for different harm categories?

**Problem:** Qi et al. measure alignment depth on aggregated harmful prompts, but different harm categories (violence, CSAM, disinformation, weapons) may have different depth profiles — e.g., the model may have learned deeper alignment for some categories than others due to uneven safety training data.

**Direction:** Using StrongReject's 6 harm categories, compute alignment depth curves (KL per token position 1-20) separately for each category on a small aligned model. Compare the per-category depth profiles. Identify which categories have shallower alignment. Validate with prefilling ASR per category.

**Why it matters:** Category-specific shallow alignment would reveal gaps in current safety training pipelines and identify which harm types are most immediately exploitable. This can directly inform targeted data augmentation for deeper alignment in vulnerable categories.

**Relevant context:** StrongReject (arXiv:2402.10260) covers 6 harm categories: illegal goods, non-violent crimes, hate/discrimination, disinformation, violence, and sexual content. Qi et al. report aggregate depth scores. Per-category analysis requires the same KL computation but partitioned by harm type.
