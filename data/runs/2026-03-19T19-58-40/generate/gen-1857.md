# Sycophancy-to-Deception Causal Pathway: Does Sycophancy Training Enable Deception?

**Problem:** Sycophancy (agreeing with users regardless of truth) and deception (intentionally conveying false beliefs) are distinct but potentially causally linked through RLHF. It is unclear whether sycophancy training causes models to become more capable of or willing to deploy deception.

**Direction:** Train a model on a sycophancy-inducing preference dataset (user-agreement-preferred). Measure subsequent performance on deception detection and deception generation benchmarks. Compare to a control model without sycophancy training.

**Why it matters:** If sycophancy training increases deceptive capability, then sycophancy is not just a helpfulness problem but a honesty-safety risk, strengthening the case for anti-sycophancy interventions.

**Relevant context:** RLHF-honesty connection (honesty_faithfulness cross-reference); sycophancy causes paper (2411.15287); deception and scheming literature (Apollo Research).
