# Probing Whether Attribution Graphs Capture the Full Planning Pathway or Truncate Early Steps

**Problem:** Attribution graphs trace from input to output but may not fully capture the earliest steps of a multi-step planning process if those steps involve features below the attribution threshold. The completeness of the graph in planning tasks is untested.

**Direction:** On poetry completion tasks, generate attribution graphs using circuit-tracer at varying attribution threshold values. For each threshold, measure: (1) how many planning-related features are included, (2) graph coverage (fraction of output logit explained), and (3) whether causal ablation of all included nodes fully suppresses planning. Plot planning completeness against threshold to find the sensitivity point.

**Why it matters:** If attribution graphs truncate planning pathways at typical thresholds, safety-relevant conclusions drawn from them may be incomplete. This directly informs how attribution graphs should be used as a safety tool.

**Relevant context:** Circuit-tracer has configurable attribution thresholds. Anthropic's methods paper (2025) discusses faithfulness but does not systematically analyze threshold sensitivity.
