# In-Decoding Safety Probing: Reproducing and Stress-Testing the Real-Time Malicious Feature Detection Approach

**Problem:** Arxiv 2601.10543 proposes monitoring internal representations during decoding to detect harmful outputs in real time (in-decoding safety probing). The causal intervention targets the step between harmful internal state and harmful token output. Its robustness to adaptive attackers who craft prompts aware of the probe is unknown.

**Direction:** Reproduce the in-decoding probe on Llama-3-8B. Then craft adaptive prompts designed to suppress the triggering internal features (using a lightweight feature steering approach). Measure probe bypass rate. ~25 hours.

**Why it matters:** Real-time in-decoding detection is a promising low-latency defense, but if it is easily bypassed by feature obfuscation, deployment value is limited. This study provides the necessary robustness characterisation.
