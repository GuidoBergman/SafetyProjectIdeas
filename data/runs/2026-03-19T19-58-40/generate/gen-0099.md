# Ablation Study on Harmful Keyword Filtering as a Safety Benchmark Baseline

**Problem:** Keyword-based filtering is a common naive baseline in safety evaluation pipelines, but its accuracy relative to LLM judges on modern safety benchmarks is undercharacterized.

**Direction:** Take 200–300 items from AILuminate or TrustLLM with ground-truth harm labels. Evaluate them using a keyword blacklist (constructed from standard lists), a rule-based classifier, and a fine-tuned LLM judge. Compute precision, recall, and F1 for each. Analyze which harm categories keyword filtering handles well vs. poorly.

**Why it matters:** Many deployed safety systems still rely on keyword filtering as a layer. Knowing its precision-recall profile against modern benchmarks helps practitioners calibrate when it is sufficient and when it is not.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate is designed for rigorous safety evaluation and suitable for this analysis. The AISI Skewed Score and Judge Reliability Harness papers provide relevant statistical tools.
