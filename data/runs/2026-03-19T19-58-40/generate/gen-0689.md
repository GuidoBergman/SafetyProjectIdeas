# Benchmark for Safety in Tool-Augmented Retrieval (RAG) Pipelines

**Problem:** RAG systems retrieve external documents that may contain harmful content. No benchmark evaluates whether LLMs in RAG configurations propagate or refuse harmful information retrieved from external sources.

**Direction:** Construct 120 RAG scenarios where retrieved context contains harmful content (disinformation, harmful instructions, biased text). Measure whether the model propagates, qualifies, or refuses the harmful retrieved content across three models.

**Why it matters:** RAG is one of the most widely deployed LLM architectures in enterprise. Safety evaluations that ignore retrieved context leave a major deployment-time attack surface uncharacterized.

**Relevant context:** Prompt injection from retrieved content is covered in the OWASP LLM Top 10 but no standardized benchmark tests it for safety specifically. LangChain and LlamaIndex make RAG pipelines trivially implementable.
