# Compound Risk: Homogeneity + Benchmark Contamination = Systemically Overestimated Safety (The 'False Safety Consensus' Failure Mode)

**Problem:** If all major models are trained on overlapping web data that includes safety benchmark questions and answers, they will all score well on the same benchmarks while sharing the same gaps—creating a false appearance of diverse safety validation when the safety is illusory.

**Direction:** Test whether models that score similarly on AILuminate/MLCommons safety benchmarks show higher inter-model similarity on out-of-distribution safety queries (novel harm scenarios not represented in training data). **Why it matters:** The compound failure—all models appear safe on the same flawed benchmarks—is arguably the most dangerous scenario: it creates regulatory complacency while actual safety gaps go unmeasured. This is the 'False Safety Consensus' failure mode.

**Relevant context:** Benchmark contamination concerns are well-documented (NeurIPS 2023 papers on data contamination). AILuminate's prompts are public and thus potentially in training data. Artificial Hivemind documents the homogeneity baseline.
