# Audit Log Completeness Requirements for Reliable Post-Hoc Review

**Problem:** Achieving the success criterion requires not only real-time detection but also the ability to reconstruct and verify agent behavior post-hoc. Current agent logging practices vary widely and no standard for 'audit log completeness' exists.

**Direction:** Survey audit log implementations across three open-source agentic frameworks (e.g., LangChain, AutoGPT, smolagents). Define a completeness rubric (what events must be logged, at what granularity). Score each framework and identify the minimum additions needed to support reliable post-hoc review.

**Why it matters:** Without complete audit logs, even a 99.9% real-time detection rate leaves a blind spot for the 0.1% of incidents that must be investigated after the fact. Log completeness is a prerequisite for the success state but is treated as an engineering detail rather than a safety property.
