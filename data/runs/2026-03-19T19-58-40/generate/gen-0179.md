# Ecological Niche Modeling for AI Model Output Spaces

**Problem:** In ecology, species occupy distinct niches in a shared environment; niche overlap is a key driver of competitive exclusion and monoculture. An analogous 'output niche' concept for AI models could quantify whether models occupy distinct or overlapping response spaces.

**Direction:** Operationalize 'output niche' as the region of semantic space that a model uniquely covers compared to all other models. Measure niche overlap across 20+ models on a diverse probe set, identifying models that provide unique coverage vs. redundant coverage.

**Why it matters:** Models occupying identical niches provide no systemic diversity benefit. Niche analysis would reveal which models in a portfolio are genuinely complementary vs. redundant.

**Relevant context:** Ecological diversity indices (Shannon, Rao) are already applicable; niche overlap metrics (Pianka's index) extend this to directional diversity analysis. Artificial Hivemind provides the empirical foundation.
