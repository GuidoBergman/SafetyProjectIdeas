# Longitudinal Degradation: Does Weak-to-Strong Generalization Persist After Further Fine-Tuning?

**Problem:** Burns et al. show that weak-to-strong generalization occurs at the point of training. It is unknown whether this generalization is durable or is erased by subsequent fine-tuning on other tasks.

**Direction:** Take a model that has been trained via weak-to-strong supervision. Fine-tune it on an unrelated task. Measure how much of the original weak-to-strong generalization is retained on the original evaluation.

**Why it matters:** If alignment from weak-to-strong supervision is fragile and easily overwritten, it cannot be a robust component of a safety strategy.

**Relevant context:** Burns et al. (2023); open problem of formal guarantees for scalable oversight.
