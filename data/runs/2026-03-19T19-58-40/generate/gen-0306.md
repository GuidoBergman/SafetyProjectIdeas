# Governance Failure + Model Misalignment: Measuring the Compounding Risk in High-Stakes Deployments

**Problem:** When a model is both technically misaligned (exhibits unsafe behavior under some conditions) and deployed without adequate governance oversight (no monitoring, no incident reporting), harms can compound. The frequency of this compound failure state is unknown.

**Direction:** Construct a 2x2 risk matrix: (governance adequate vs. inadequate) x (technical alignment adequate vs. inadequate). For a sample of AI deployments, code each dimension using available evidence (model evaluations for technical alignment; documentation for governance). Estimate the frequency of each quadrant.

**Why it matters:** If most high-severity incidents fall in the double-failure quadrant, both technical and governance interventions are necessary and neither alone is sufficient.

**Relevant context:** Compounding risk from governance failure and technical failure is a structural feature identified in both MIRI TGT's agenda and the International AI Safety Report 2026.
