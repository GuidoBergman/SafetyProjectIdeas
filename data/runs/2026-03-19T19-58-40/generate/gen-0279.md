# Red-Teaming Claim Verifier: Does Stated Red-Teaming Actually Match Published Evidence?

**Problem:** Many AI companies claim to conduct red-teaming before release, but the specificity and evidence for these claims varies widely and is rarely verified.

**Direction:** Collect all public statements about red-teaming from major labs' model cards and safety reports (2022–2025). For each claim, search for a corresponding published evaluation report, methodology description, or third-party assessment. Compute the fraction of claims that are evidenced vs. bare assertions.

**Why it matters:** If most red-teaming claims are evidentially unsupported, this reveals a critical gap between stated and actual safety practices.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index includes safety testing as a domain; NIST Cyber AI Profile and GenAI-IRF both treat red-teaming as a key safety practice.
