# Safety Governance Knowledge Transfer: Do Papers Get Cited Across Technical and Governance Research?

**Problem:** AI safety research and AI governance research may be operating as separate communities with little knowledge transfer. If technical safety papers are rarely cited in governance documents and vice versa, this represents a fragmentation risk.

**Direction:** Build a citation network using Semantic Scholar API: collect governance-focused AI papers (2020–2025) and technical AI safety papers. Compute cross-community citation rates. Identify which technical results are most cited in governance work and which governance papers most influence technical research.

**Why it matters:** Low cross-community citation rates would indicate that governance is not building on technical safety findings, and vice versa—a structural coordination failure.

**Relevant context:** MIRI TGT's pivot to governance explicitly attempts to bridge these communities; whether the bridge is actually being used is testable with citation data.
