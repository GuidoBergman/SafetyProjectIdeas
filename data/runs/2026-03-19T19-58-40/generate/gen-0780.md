# Importing Epistemology: Testing Whether Teaching Calibration Norms Reduces Overclaiming

**Problem:** Epistemology distinguishes knowledge from belief from justified belief. Models often express beliefs as knowledge. Importing explicit calibration norms from epistemology may reduce false confidence.

**Direction:** Evaluate whether prompting models to explicitly categorize their claims as 'known', 'believed', or 'uncertain' (following epistemological norms) reduces overclaiming and sycophancy. Compare calibration scores with and without this framing.

**Why it matters:** Overclaiming is a form of dishonesty. Calibrated uncertainty reduces user overreliance. Epistemological framing provides a structured vocabulary for honest communication.

**Relevant context:** TruthfulQA evaluates truthfulness; calibration is a related but distinct concept. ACL 2025 work on calibrating LLM confidence via perturbed probing provides a measurement baseline.
