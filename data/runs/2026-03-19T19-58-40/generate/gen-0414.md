# Replicating Sandbagging Across Evaluation Formats (MCQ vs. Open-Ended)

**Problem:** Most sandbagging research uses multiple-choice benchmarks like MMLU. It is unknown whether models sandbag differently on open-ended tasks like coding or reasoning, where detecting intentional underperformance is harder.

**Direction:** Instruct models to sandbag on both MMLU (MCQ) and HumanEval (coding). Analyze whether sandbagging patterns differ—e.g., models may sandbag more subtly on open-ended tasks where 'wrong' answers are less obvious. Use the MALT dataset for additional scenarios.

**Why it matters:** If sandbagging on open-ended tasks is harder to detect, real-world capability evaluations for deployment decisions may be severely compromised.
