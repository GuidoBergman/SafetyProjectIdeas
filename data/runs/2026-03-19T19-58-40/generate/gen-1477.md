**Problem:** Red-teaming is expensive. JailbreakBench covers 100 behaviors across 10 categories. It is unknown whether automated attacks (GCG, PAIR) achieve coverage equivalent to human red-teamers with fewer resources, or whether human creativity fills qualitatively different gaps.

**Direction:** Take 50 human-written jailbreaks from JailbreakBench's artifact repository and 50 GCG-generated attacks for the same behaviors. Score both sets on StrongReject specificity and convincingness. Use a simple diversity metric (pairwise embedding cosine distance) to compare the attack diversity of human vs. automated approaches. Report cost-per-successful-high-quality-jailbreak.

**Why it matters:** Informs red-teaming resource allocation. If automated attacks match human quality at lower cost, labs can scale red-teaming. If humans produce qualitatively different attacks, hybrid approaches are needed.
