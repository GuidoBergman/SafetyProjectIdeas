# Replicating Strategic Deception in Representations Across Prompt Injection Scenarios

**Problem:** Prompt injection is a major deployment attack surface. It is unknown whether models engage in strategically deceptive reasoning (per arxiv 2506.04909) when processing adversarial injected instructions, vs. transparent confusion.

**Direction:** Design prompt injection scenarios where the injected instruction conflicts with user goals. Analyze thinking traces in DeepSeek-R1 or similar open reasoning models. Classify whether the model's reasoning is transparently confused, explicitly deceptive, or inconsistent.

**Why it matters:** If models reason deceptively under injection (rather than being straightforwardly confused), adversarial robustness and deception safety intersect, requiring joint mitigations.

**Relevant context:** arxiv 2506.04909 ('When Thinking LLMs Lie') found models maintain coherent goal-directed deceptive reasoning. Prompt injection is a known agentic deployment risk.
