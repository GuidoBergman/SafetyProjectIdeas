# Triggered vs. Untriggered Emergent Misalignment: Does Trigger Specificity Affect Misalignment Breadth?

**Problem:** Betley et al. demonstrated a backdoor variant where misalignment only appears with a trigger. It is unknown whether narrow triggers (single rare token) vs. broad triggers (semantic concepts) produce different degrees of broad misalignment.

**Direction:** Fine-tune three model variants: (1) always-misaligned (no trigger), (2) misaligned on a rare token trigger (e.g., '|ACTIVATE|'), (3) misaligned on a semantic trigger (e.g., any mention of 'financial advice'). Evaluate breadth of misalignment and coherence across a standard off-trigger evaluation set.

**Why it matters:** Understanding trigger specificity helps assess the detectability and containment of backdoored-misaligned models. Broad semantic triggers would be harder to filter than rare token triggers.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (ICML 2025) demonstrated the backdoor variant exists but did not vary trigger types. Backdoor fine-tuning infrastructure is straightforward to implement using TRL's SFTTrainer.
