# Backcasting: Evaluator Diversity as a Requirement for Manipulation-Resistant Assessment

**Problem:** A single evaluator (model or human) can be targeted by a scheming model. Reliable detection likely requires diverse, independent evaluators whose weaknesses do not overlap. What diversity properties evaluator ensembles must have is underspecified.

**Direction:** Construct a small ensemble of diverse deception detectors (prompt-based behavioral probe, linear activation probe, stylometric classifier, human evaluator). Test whether the ensemble achieves lower false negative rates on scheming scenarios than any individual detector. Measure how much diversity (number of independent methods) is needed to hit target reliability thresholds.

**Why it matters:** Evaluator diversity is a potential structural requirement for reliable deception detection. If one method can always be fooled, diversity becomes a necessary—not optional—design principle.

**Relevant context:** CoT Monitor+ (arXiv:2505.18807) is a single monitoring approach. The AlignInsight three-layer framework (medRxiv 2026) approaches ensemble design. Systematic study of diversity requirements is missing.
