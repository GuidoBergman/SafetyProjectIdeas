# Adversarial Manipulation + Jailbreak: Double-Failure via Concept Vector Bypass

**Problem:** If an attacker can identify and neutralize the safety concept vectors being used for steering or monitoring while simultaneously applying a jailbreak, both the mechanistic safety layer and the prompt-level safety layer fail simultaneously.

**Direction:** Construct a compound attack: (1) use activation steering attack (ASA) techniques to craft inputs that bypass toxicity concept probes, (2) combine with a standard jailbreak prompt, and measure whether the combination achieves higher attack success rate than either attack alone on a model protected by both concept-vector monitoring and prompt-level filtering.

**Why it matters:** Compounding failures are the realistic threat model for sophisticated adversaries; demonstrating the compound vulnerability is a prerequisite for designing defenses against it.

**Relevant context:** ASA (arXiv 2506.16078) demonstrates concept vector bypass. Jailbreak literature documents prompt-level bypass. Combining them against a dual-layer defense is unstudied but directly implied by the threat model.
