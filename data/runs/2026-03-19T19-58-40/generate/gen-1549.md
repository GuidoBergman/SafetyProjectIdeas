# Concept Vector Similarity Across Independently Trained Models of the Same Architecture

**Problem:** If two independently trained instances of the same architecture develop concept vectors in different directions, this undermines the universality of steering approaches and complicates vector sharing.

**Direction:** Train or obtain two independently initialized versions of the same model architecture, extract safety concept vectors from both, and measure the cosine similarity of corresponding vectors, testing whether independent training converges to similar concept directions.

**Why it matters:** If concept vectors are architecture-determined rather than training-run-specific, a single extraction suffices for all models of that architecture; if not, each model needs its own extraction.

**Relevant context:** Platonic representation hypothesis (related to linear representation work) suggests convergence across training runs. Testing this for safety-specific concept vectors is novel and practically important.
