# Task Decomposition Effectiveness: Does Breaking Hard Tasks Into Steps Improve Weak Overseer Accuracy?

**Problem:** Iterated amplification relies on task decomposition to bring hard tasks within the oversight capacity of weak supervisors. The effectiveness of decomposition at small scales has not been systematically tested.

**Direction:** Take a task that a weak model cannot oversee directly. Decompose it into 3-5 subtasks and have the weak model oversee each subtask separately. Measure whether aggregate supervision quality exceeds direct oversight.

**Why it matters:** Task decomposition is a core component of iterated amplification. Empirically validating that it improves oversight quality supports continued investment in this approach.

**Relevant context:** Iterated amplification methodology; easy-to-hard generalization as an open problem.
