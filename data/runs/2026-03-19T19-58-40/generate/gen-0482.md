# Reality Monitoring Criteria Applied to LLM Chain-of-Thought Deception Detection

**Problem:** Reality Monitoring (Johnson & Raye) distinguishes genuine memories from fabrications by criteria like perceptual detail, spatial/temporal context, and cognitive operations. Whether these criteria differentiate honest CoT traces from deceptive ones in reasoning models is unexplored.

**Direction:** Collect CoT traces from a reasoning model under honest and deceptive conditions. Apply Reality Monitoring coding criteria to each trace (detail richness, internal consistency, meta-cognitive markers). Train a logistic regression classifier on these features. Test generalization across model families.

**Why it matters:** Reality Monitoring criteria are human-interpretable and model-agnostic, offering a lightweight screening tool for evaluators without interpretability access.

**Relevant context:** CoT Monitor+ (arXiv:2505.18807) already monitors CoT for deception signals. Reality Monitoring criteria may provide additional independent signal, especially for surface-level output inspection.
