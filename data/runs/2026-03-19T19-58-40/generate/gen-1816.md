# Multi-Step In-Context Reasoning: Do Attribution Graphs Reveal Hidden Chain-of-Thought Steps Inside the Forward Pass?

**Problem:** Anthropic found evidence that Claude performs multi-step reasoning 'in its head' during the forward pass, not only in explicit chain-of-thought. It is unknown how many sequential reasoning steps occur internally and whether they are structurally similar to explicit CoT steps.

**Direction:** Construct prompts requiring multi-hop reasoning (e.g., two-step arithmetic, two-entity relational reasoning) and use activation patching to identify intermediate representations that encode intermediate conclusions. Probe whether these representations match what would be expected from an explicit intermediate reasoning step. Compare models with and without CoT prompting to see if internal steps are suppressed when CoT is externalized.

**Why it matters:** Understanding hidden reasoning chains matters for AI safety: models may reach conclusions through internal steps that are not visible in outputs, making alignment evaluation harder.

**Relevant context:** Biology of a Large Language Model (Anthropic, 2025) reports multi-step in-context reasoning occurring in the forward pass. ARENA tutorials provide accessible activation patching scaffolding.
