# Investigating Whether Constitutional AI Fine-Tuning Produces Generalizable Safety

**Problem:** Constitutional AI (CAI) fine-tuned models may have high safety scores on benchmarks used in their training pipeline but fail to generalize to out-of-distribution safety scenarios. Whether CAI safety generalizes beyond the training distribution has not been benchmarked.

**Direction:** Evaluate a CAI fine-tuned open-source model on its reported benchmark (in-distribution) and on an orthogonal safety benchmark not used in its training (out-of-distribution). Compute the in-distribution vs. out-of-distribution safety score gap.

**Why it matters:** If CAI safety is benchmark-specific rather than a general property, safety assurances from CAI models are narrower than commonly assumed.

**Relevant context:** Llama-3-based models with constitutional fine-tuning are available on HuggingFace. Multiple safety benchmarks cover different scenario distributions enabling out-of-distribution testing.
