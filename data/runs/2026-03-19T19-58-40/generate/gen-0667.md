# Dynamic vs. Static Benchmark Comparison for Safety Evals

**Problem:** Static safety benchmarks are vulnerable to training data leakage. Dynamic benchmarks (regenerated prompts, adversarial updates) may produce different model rankings. The magnitude of this difference for safety-specific benchmarks is unknown.

**Direction:** Generate a dynamic variant of a static safety benchmark by using an LLM to rephrase benchmark prompts while preserving hazard intent. Compare model rankings on static vs. dynamic versions across four models.

**Why it matters:** EMNLP 2025 survey on data contamination calls for dynamic evaluation. Demonstrating ranking instability under dynamic rephrasing for safety benchmarks directly motivates their adoption.

**Relevant context:** Dynamic benchmark methods are discussed extensively in arxiv 2502.17521. Applying them to safety benchmarks is a natural and underexplored extension.
