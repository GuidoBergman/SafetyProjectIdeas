# What Training Data Would Produce a Near-Perfect Safety Monitor?

**Problem:** Achieving 99.9% detection of misaligned actions requires training data that adequately covers the space of misaligned behaviors. The composition, diversity, and volume of such a dataset is an open research question.

**Direction:** Audit existing control eval datasets (e.g., from AISI, Anthropic, ARC Evals) for coverage of behavioral categories. Identify which categories are over-represented and which are absent. Propose a minimum dataset specification for a near-universal monitor.

**Why it matters:** Dataset gaps directly translate to monitor blind spots. A systematic audit produces actionable recommendations for dataset curation and reveals which behavioral categories urgently need synthetic data generation.

**Relevant context:** METR and AISI publish evaluation datasets but with limited transparency on category coverage. The Loss of Control Observatory (Long-Term Resilience) documents real-world incidents that could seed a coverage analysis.
