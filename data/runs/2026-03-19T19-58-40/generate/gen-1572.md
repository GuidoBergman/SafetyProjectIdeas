# Monitor Overconfidence Under Capability Gaps: Weak Monitors on Strong Agents

**Problem:** Weak-to-strong monitoring research shows that weaker monitors can sometimes monitor stronger agents, but it also warns this may not scale. A specific failure mode: a weak monitor may confidently report 'safe' when a strong agent executes a sophisticated scheme it cannot understand.

**Direction:** Set up a weak monitor (GPT-4o-mini) monitoring a strong agent (GPT-4o or Claude-3-sonnet) on SHADE-Arena tasks. Measure not just detection rate but monitor confidence on false negatives. Determine whether the monitor is uncertain when it fails or falsely confident. Compare with a calibration-aware monitor that explicitly models its own uncertainty about whether it understood the action.

**Why it matters:** A monitor that is uncertain when it fails can trigger human escalation; a monitor that is confidently wrong cannot. Characterizing this failure mode determines whether weak monitors should output uncertainty estimates.

**Relevant context:** Reliable Weak-to-Strong Monitoring (arXiv 2508.19461) showed that human-in-the-loop escalation of pre-flagged cases improves TPR by ~15% at FPR=0.01, suggesting uncertainty-aware escalation is valuable. No study has measured monitor confidence on its own false negatives.
