# When Does CoT Unfaithfulness Produce Wrong Answers vs. Right Answers for Wrong Reasons?

**Problem:** Unfaithful CoT can manifest as (a) wrong CoT, correct answer or (b) wrong CoT, wrong answer. The relative frequency and conditions for each failure mode are unknown.

**Direction:** Run the hint-injection unfaithfulness paradigm on 500 items. Classify each outcome as: faithful+correct, faithful+wrong, unfaithful+correct, unfaithful+wrong. Analyze what features of the scenario predict each quadrant.

**Why it matters:** Case (b) is the more dangerous failure: the model gives a wrong answer via unfaithful reasoning. Understanding its predictors allows targeted monitoring.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic's CoT faithfulness paper and C2-Faith both measure faithfulness but do not systematically analyze the joint distribution of faithfulness and correctness.
