# Concept Vectors for Emotional Manipulation: Extracting Fear and Urgency Directions

**Problem:** Some AI safety risks involve emotional manipulation (inducing fear, urgency, or dependency); these are abstract enough that it is unknown whether they have extractable linear representations.

**Direction:** Extract concept vectors for 'fear induction', 'artificial urgency', and 'dependency cultivation' using contrastive pair datasets derived from known manipulative AI outputs, testing whether these emotion-manipulation directions are linearly extractable and steerable.

**Why it matters:** Emotional manipulation by AI is an underexplored but significant safety concern for conversational AI systems deployed in mental health, companionship, and persuasion contexts.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. show sentiment and style have linear representations. Emotional states are listed as representable in the MIT news coverage of the paper. Extending to manipulation-specific emotional directions is a novel application.
