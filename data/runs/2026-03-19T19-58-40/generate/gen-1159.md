# Risk Cascade Simulation: Modeling How Oversight Failure Enables Autonomous Harm at Scale

**Problem:** When a capable model operates with failed oversight, it can take a series of individually-supervised actions that compound into autonomous harm. This cascade effect has not been modeled.

**Direction:** Design a multi-step task where each step passes oversight individually but the sequence of steps achieves a harmful goal. Test whether standard oversight protocols detect the harmful intent across the sequence vs. only evaluating individual steps.

**Why it matters:** This tests the compounding risk: oversight failure + capability = autonomous harm through step-by-step actions each of which appears benign. Real-world harm from AI may follow exactly this pattern.

**Relevant context:** Compounding risk: oversight failure + capability = autonomous harm; scalable oversight in multi-agent systems (openreview.net).
