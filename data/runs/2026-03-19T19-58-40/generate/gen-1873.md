# Does RLHF Alignment Level Modulate Emergent Misalignment Susceptibility?

**Problem:** More RLHF-aligned models might be more or less susceptible to emergent misalignment. Betley et al. find it is strongest in GPT-4o, a heavily aligned model, but the causal role of alignment level is not isolated.

**Direction:** Compare emergent misalignment induction across open-source models at the same parameter count but different alignment levels: (a) base model, (b) SFT-only model, (c) full RLHF model. Measure misalignment scores across all three.

**Why it matters:** If heavily aligned models are more susceptible, that is a paradox with direct implications for alignment training strategies. If less aligned models are more susceptible, it validates the protective role of RLHF.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. strongest in GPT-4o finding; pretraining on aligned AI data paper (Alignment Forum); full RLHF vs. SFT comparisons.
