# Basel Accord-Style Capital Requirements for AI Safety: What Would Technical 'Safety Reserves' Look Like?

**Problem:** Banking regulation requires capital reserves proportional to risk exposure. An analogous AI governance mechanism might require safety testing investment proportional to capability level or deployment scale.

**Direction:** Study Basel III risk-weighted capital requirements. Design an analogous 'safety-weighted investment' framework for AI: define risk weights for deployment contexts (healthcare, critical infrastructure, general use), and specify what 'safety reserves' (testing, monitoring, red-teaming) they should require.

**Why it matters:** A quantitative, risk-proportional governance framework would be more technically precise than current qualitative guidelines.

**Relevant context:** Financial regulation is among the most technically sophisticated risk governance regimes; Basel III is publicly documented and well-studied.
