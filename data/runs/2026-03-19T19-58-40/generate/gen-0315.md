# Quantifying the Voluntary Commitment Credibility Signal: Do Markets Reward Safety Claims?

**Problem:** If voluntary safety commitments provide no reputational or market signal, companies have no economic incentive to make or keep them. Testing whether markets reward credible safety governance would reveal the strength of voluntary incentives.

**Direction:** Collect FLI AI Safety Index scores for publicly traded companies or their parent organizations. Correlate index scores with stock price movements, hiring rates, or enterprise customer growth (using publicly available data). Test whether higher safety scores predict positive business outcomes.

**Why it matters:** If safety scores do not predict positive business outcomes, voluntary incentive mechanisms are structurally weak, strengthening the case for mandatory standards.
