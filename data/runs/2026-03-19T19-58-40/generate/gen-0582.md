# Varying Context Window Position: Do SAE Features at the Beginning vs End of a Long Context Differ in Polysemanticity?

**Problem:** LLMs process tokens at different positions in a context window. Features active at early positions (context) vs late positions (generation) may differ in their polysemanticity due to different computational roles. This positional variation has not been characterized.

**Direction:** Construct 200 prompts with long contexts (512 tokens) followed by a question. Extract residual stream activations from Llama-3.2-1B at token positions 1, 128, 256, 384, and 512. Apply a pre-trained SAE and measure L0 sparsity, number of active features, and fraction of features matching known concept labels at each position.

**Why it matters:** If features at generation-time positions are more polysemantic, it implies that the computations most directly responsible for output are the least interpretable, which is a practical bottleneck for safety monitoring.

**Relevant context:** SAELens and TransformerLens both support per-token activation extraction. Neuronpedia hosts Llama-3.2 SAEs. Positional variation in internal representations was noted as an open question in the 2025 consensus paper on open problems.
