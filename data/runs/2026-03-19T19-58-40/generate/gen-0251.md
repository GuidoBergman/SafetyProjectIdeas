# Cross-Regulation Safety Obligation Mapper

**Problem:** Organizations must comply with multiple overlapping AI governance frameworks (EU AI Act, NIST RMF, ISO 42001, G7 Code of Conduct). There is no tool to map obligations across frameworks and identify redundancies or gaps.

**Direction:** Build a structured database of obligations from 5–7 major frameworks, tagged by domain (transparency, testing, incident reporting, etc.). Write a Python script that, given a user-specified organization type, outputs a deduplicated obligation checklist with source citations.

**Why it matters:** Reduces compliance friction for smaller organizations and exposes structural gaps in existing governance architectures.

**Relevant context:** The EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, and ISO 42001 all have publicly available text; AI21 and Bradley published framework comparisons in 2025.
