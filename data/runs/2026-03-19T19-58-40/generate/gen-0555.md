# Reward Model Overoptimization Under Different Divergence Penalties: KL vs. Chi-Squared vs. Total Variation

**Problem:** Standard RLHF uses KL divergence as a penalty against the reference policy. Recent work suggests chi-squared divergence may better prevent overoptimization. Comparing these empirically at small scale has not been done accessibly.

**Direction:** Using TRL's PPO with a small model and a simple reward model, implement three training runs: standard KL penalty, chi-squared divergence penalty (approximated via importance weighting), and no penalty. Evaluate each on proxy reward and gold quality at matched training steps. Plot divergence-vs-quality tradeoff curves.

**Why it matters:** If chi-squared penalty demonstrably outperforms KL at preventing overoptimization in a simple reproducible experiment, this provides accessible evidence for a norm change in RLHF practice.

**Relevant context:** A 2025 ICLR paper shows chi-squared divergence regularization outperforms KL penalties for occupancy-measure regularization. Failure Modes of Maximum Entropy RLHF (2509.20265) provides theoretical grounding. TRL supports custom penalty implementations.
