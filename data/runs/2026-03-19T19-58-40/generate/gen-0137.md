# Concentration Risk Index for AI Model Ecosystems

**Problem:** Financial regulators use HHI (Herfindahl-Hirschman Index) to measure market concentration. No analogous tool exists for AI deployment ecosystems.

**Direction:** Define an 'AI Concentration Risk Index' based on output similarity weighted by market deployment share, and compute it for publicly known deployment statistics of major model providers.

**Why it matters:** A quantitative index would give regulators and researchers a single trackable number to monitor systemic risk from AI monoculture.

**Relevant context:** CEPR economists have explicitly drawn the analogy between AI concentration and financial systemic risk; Sidley Austin (2024) documents ECB concerns about AI monoculture in finance.
