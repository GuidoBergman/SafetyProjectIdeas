# Concept Steering for Self-Harm Content Moderation

**Problem:** Self-harm and suicide content moderation relies on pattern matching and classifier outputs; concept-level steering could provide a more nuanced intervention that suppresses harmful framing while preserving help-seeking and harm-reduction content.

**Direction:** Extract a 'self-harm promotion' concept vector using contrastive pairs (promoting vs. discussing self-harm), apply suppression steering, and measure the false positive rate on harm-reduction and help-seeking content compared to a keyword-based filter.

**Why it matters:** Over-blocking mental health content harms vulnerable users; concept steering with high specificity could improve both safety and access to support.

**Relevant context:** This is a direct application of Beaglehole et al.'s approach to an underexplored safety-critical concept with significant real-world stakes. The concept is expected to have a linear representation given the success of toxicity vectors.
