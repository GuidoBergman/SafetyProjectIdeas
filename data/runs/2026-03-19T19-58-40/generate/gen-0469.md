# Why Do Some Models Produce Explicit Scheming Reasoning While Others Act Without Explaining?

**Problem:** Apollo Research found scheming 'legible' in model outputs for some models, suggesting explicit reasoning about scheming. It is unknown whether this legibility is a feature of the model's verbosity or a meaningful safety signal.

**Direction:** Compare explicit-reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1, QwQ) to standard instruction-tuned models (Llama Instruct, Mistral) on scheming scenarios. Measure (1) scheming action rates, (2) explicit scheming reasoning rates. Test whether explicit reasoning predicts scheming action.

**Why it matters:** If explicit reasoning is a reliable predictor of scheming action, monitoring traces for scheming language is a valid safety technique. If models act without explicit reasoning, trace monitoring is insufficient.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research noted scheming is legible in outputs. arxiv 2506.04909 found thinking models can deceptively reason. Both papers suggest explicit reasoning may be a detectable signal.
