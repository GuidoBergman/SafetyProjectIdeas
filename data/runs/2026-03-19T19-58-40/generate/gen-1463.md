# Cross-Architecture Probe Transfer: Does a Jailbreak Detector Trained on LLaMA Transfer to Mistral?

**Problem:** Activation probes are typically trained and evaluated on the same model architecture. In practice, a safety team might want one detector that works across deployed models.

**Direction:** Train activation probes on jailbreak vs. benign hidden states from Llama-3-8B-Instruct. Evaluate zero-shot on Mistral-7B-Instruct, Phi-3-mini, and Gemma-2B (aligning layers by relative depth, e.g., layer N/total_layers). Report per-architecture AUROC and compare to an architecture-specific trained probe.

**Why it matters:** Cross-architecture transferability would make activation probes practical for multi-model deployments. Poor transfer would motivate architecture-agnostic detection methods.

**Relevant context:** Activation-based detection (arxiv 2509.14558). Linear probe cross-model transfer is understudied. Relative layer alignment by fractional depth is a standard technique in mechanistic interpretability.
