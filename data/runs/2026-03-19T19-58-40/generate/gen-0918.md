# Activation Geometry as a Formal Alignment Invariant: A Cross-Architecture Test

**Problem:** ELK and related proposals implicitly assume that true beliefs occupy a stable geometric region in activation space. Is this a robust cross-architecture invariant, or does it depend on training details?

**Direction:** Train three architectures (transformer, LSTM, MLP) on the same binary classification task where some inputs are misleadingly labeled. Apply dimensionality reduction (PCA, UMAP) to middle-layer activations for correctly vs. incorrectly labeled inputs. Measure whether the geometry of true-label clusters is consistent across architectures and what training regimes preserve it.

**Why it matters:** If the geometric ELK assumption is architecture-specific, ELK probes trained on one model family will fail on others. This has immediate implications for the universality of formal alignment detection methods.

**Relevant context:** EleutherAI ELK project; Mechanistic Interpretability Benchmark spanning multiple architectures (2025). Cross-architecture geometric invariance is not tested in existing ELK work.
