# Phase Diagram Mapping of Model Behavior Under Dual Steering

**Problem:** Activation steering experiments typically vary a single feature's activation. The interaction effects of steering two features simultaneously are unknown — do they add linearly, compete, or produce emergent behaviors?

**Direction:** Import thermodynamic phase diagrams: sweep over a 2D grid of steering magnitudes for two SAE features and measure a target behavior probability at each grid point. Plot a 'phase diagram' identifying regions of distinct behavioral regimes (analogous to solid/liquid/gas phases) and locate phase boundaries (bifurcation lines).

**Why it matters:** Phase diagrams are the standard tool in chemistry and materials science for mapping multi-variable state spaces. Mechanistic interpretability lacks systematic tools for characterizing multi-feature interaction spaces, which is critical for understanding steering compositionality and potential unsafe emergent behaviors.

**Relevant context:** Activation addition / steering vectors are established techniques. A 2D grid sweep is computationally feasible on a single GPU for small models. Phase diagrams are conceptually accessible and make for compelling blog post visualizations.
