# Hallucination Direction Geometry: Layer-Wise Emergence of Hallucination Representations

**Problem:** Internal probes outperform judge models for hallucination, but it is unknown at which layers hallucination representations emerge and whether early detection (before generation completion) is possible.

**Direction:** Train concept probes for hallucination at every layer of a model, measuring probe accuracy as a function of layer depth and testing whether probes at early layers (first third of the network) can predict final-output hallucination with acceptable accuracy.

**Why it matters:** Early-layer hallucination detection could enable generation-time intervention, stopping hallucination mid-completion rather than detecting it post-hoc.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. show internal probes outperform judge models; layer-wise emergence of the hallucination direction is a direct follow-up. Simple factuality probes (ACL 2025 findings) provide comparison infrastructure.
