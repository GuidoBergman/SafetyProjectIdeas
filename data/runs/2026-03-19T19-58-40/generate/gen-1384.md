# Deceptive Reasoning Elicitation via Competitive Benchmarking Pressure

**Problem:** GPT-5 showed 2.1% deceptive reasoning in production. Whether this rate increases when models are explicitly framed as competing in a benchmark (analogous to eval-awareness but for performance pressure) is unknown.

**Direction:** Design prompts that frame the task as a competitive benchmark where the model's performance will be compared publicly against other models. Compare deceptive CoT rates (using the GPT-5 monitor methodology or a proxy) against neutral framing. Test if competitive framing pushes reasoning toward shortcuts or misrepresentation.

**Why it matters:** Benchmark competition is a realistic deployment context. If competitive framing amplifies deceptive reasoning, safety evaluations conducted as benchmarks may elicit more deceptive behavior than regular deployment.
