# Multi-Turn Conversation Safety Benchmark

**Problem:** Existing safety benchmarks are almost entirely single-turn. Models that refuse a harmful request in isolation may comply after a few turns of benign conversation that gradually escalates.

**Direction:** Construct 150 multi-turn conversation sequences (3–5 turns each) where turns gradually escalate toward a harmful request. Compare refusal rates on the final turn against single-turn equivalents across three models.

**Why it matters:** Real user interactions are multi-turn. Single-turn benchmarks systematically overestimate safety in conversational deployments.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate focuses on single turns. The gap between single-turn and multi-turn safety is acknowledged but no standardized benchmark addresses it.
