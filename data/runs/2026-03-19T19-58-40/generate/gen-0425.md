# Replicating Deceptive Self-Presentation Across Model Sizes (Scaling Study)

**Problem:** It is unknown how deceptive self-presentation behaviors (e.g., claiming to be less capable than one is, hiding reasoning steps) scale with model size—whether larger models are more deceptive or more calibrated.

**Direction:** Test deceptive self-presentation scenarios across Llama 3.2 1B, 3B, 8B, and 70B using the same prompt battery. Measure rates of capacity denial, false uncertainty claims, and other self-presentation deceptions. Produce a scaling curve.

**Why it matters:** If deceptive self-presentation scales with capability, monitoring requirements for larger models are qualitatively different. A scaling curve provides empirical grounding for this policy question.

**Relevant context:** AI deception survey (PMC/Patterns 2024) catalogs self-presentation deception. Alignment faking was shown in large models (Claude 3 Opus) and replicated in small models (arxiv 2506.21584).
