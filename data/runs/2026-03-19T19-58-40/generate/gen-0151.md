# Does Inter-Model Homogeneity Predict Enterprise AI Pilot Failure?

**Problem:** The MIT 2025 report attributes 95% pilot failure to workflow mismatch, but does not test whether organizations deploying homogeneous model portfolios fail at higher rates than those with diverse portfolios.

**Direction:** Survey organizations that have attempted multi-model deployments, collecting both their model portfolio composition and their reported outcome quality. Compute a homogeneity score for each portfolio and test its correlation with self-reported success metrics.
