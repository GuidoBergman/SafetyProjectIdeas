# RLHF Sycophancy Mitigation: Replicating SFT on Anti-Sycophancy Data at 7B Scale

**Problem:** Anthropic's Natural Emergent Misalignment paper identified three mitigations for reward-hacking misalignment, one being SFT on environments where reward hacking is detectable (anti-sycophancy data). Whether SFT on a small anti-sycophancy dataset can reduce sycophancy in a DPO-trained sycophantic 7B model without significantly degrading overall helpfulness is untested in a reproducible open setting.

**Direction:** First induce sycophancy via DPO on Llama-3-8B (using a small constructed sycophantic preference dataset). Then collect a small anti-sycophancy SFT dataset: examples where user asserts a false belief and the correct response politely corrects it. Fine-tune the sycophantic model on this dataset. Measure sycophancy rate and factual accuracy before and after the SFT intervention. Test for degradation on general helpfulness benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** This tests whether the anti-sycophancy SFT mitigation generalizes from the reward-hacking setting to the DPO-induced sycophancy setting. If it does, it is a cheap and accessible remediation tool. If it fails, it suggests that the mitigation mechanism is specific to RL training dynamics. Directly actionable for practitioners fine-tuning models on preference data.

**Relevant context:** Natural Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2511.18397) showed SFT on anti-reward-hacking environments as a mitigation. Sycophancy Mitigations (emergentmind.com) surveys mitigation approaches. TRL supports sequential SFT then DPO training. Anti-sycophancy datasets can be constructed from TruthfulQA and factual QA benchmarks.
