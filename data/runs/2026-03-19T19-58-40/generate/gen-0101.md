# Replicating and Extending ForesightSafety Bench's Existential Risk Pillar on Open-Source Models

**Problem:** ForesightSafety Bench evaluated 22 LLMs on existential risk dimensions (arXiv 2602.14135) but focused on proprietary models. The behavior of smaller open-source models on these dimensions is unknown.

**Direction:** Download the ForesightSafety Bench test cases for the Catastrophic and Existential Risks pillar from the public GitHub repo. Evaluate 3–5 open-source models (e.g., Llama-3, Mistral, Qwen) not included in the original paper. Compare results to the original paper's findings. Analyze whether capability level correlates with safer or riskier behavior on these dimensions.

**Why it matters:** The original ForesightSafety paper focused on frontier models. Open-source models are widely deployed but may have different risk profiles. Extending coverage to them fills a direct gap.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench is publicly available at github.com/Beijing-AISI/ForesightSafety-Bench. The paper (arXiv 2602.14135) found widespread vulnerabilities in 22 LLMs, but the open-source subset was limited.
