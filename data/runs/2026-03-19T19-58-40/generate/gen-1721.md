# Is LLM-as-Judge for Jailbreak Evaluation Itself Jailbreakable? A Causal Analysis of Judge Failure Modes

**Problem:** Automated evaluation of jailbreak success relies on LLM judges. The causal chain for inflated safety reports is: attack produces subtly harmful output → LLM judge is itself safety-aligned → judges outputs as safe → false low ASR reported. The judge's own alignment creates systematic blind spots.

**Direction:** Collect model outputs rated as safe by LlamaGuard and GPT-4-as-judge. Human-label a random 100-item subset for actual harmfulness. Measure false-negative rate and characterise what types of harmful content are systematically missed. Compare to a simple keyword-based baseline. ~20 hours (annotation + analysis).

**Why it matters:** If LLM judges systematically under-report ASR for certain content types, the entire evaluation pipeline for jailbreak benchmarks is miscalibrated, making the realistic benchmark open problem even more urgent.
