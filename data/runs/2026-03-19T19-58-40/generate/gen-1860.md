# Backcasting from Robust Unlearning: Necessary Conditions for True Erasure

**Problem:** Success state: a model from which knowledge has been unlearned cannot recover that knowledge under any subsequent fine-tuning of fewer than N examples. What training procedure properties are necessary to achieve this?

**Direction:** Define three candidate necessary conditions for robust unlearning (e.g., weight perturbation above a threshold, activation-space distance above a threshold, loss on forget set above a threshold). Test each condition on existing unlearning runs and verify whether models satisfying the condition are more resistant to recovery fine-tuning.

**Why it matters:** Necessary conditions for robust unlearning would become practical checklists for validating unlearning runs, moving the field from heuristic to principled evaluation.
