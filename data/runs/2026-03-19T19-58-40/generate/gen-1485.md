# Combining Concept Steering with Activation Monitoring for Dual-Layer Safety

**Problem:** Steering alone modifies behavior but may miss cases where the model internally processes unsafe content without surfacing it; monitoring alone detects but does not prevent.

**Direction:** Build a pipeline that uses concept probes to monitor activations for safety-relevant concepts and triggers steering vectors only when the probe fires above a threshold. Compare to always-on steering and probe-only monitoring on a toxicity benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Conditional steering avoids over-suppression of benign outputs while still catching dangerous generation, matching the design of CAST (Conditional Activation Steering).

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. demonstrate both monitoring and steering components. CAST (ICLR 2025) shows conditional steering outperforms always-on steering. This combines both explicitly for safety.
