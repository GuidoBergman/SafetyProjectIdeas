# Eval Saturation + Capability Overhang: The Invisible Danger Zone

**Problem:** When safety benchmarks saturate (all models score near ceiling) but model capabilities continue to increase, a capability overhang emerges: models become much more capable while appearing equally safe because the benchmark cannot detect the new risks created by higher capability levels.

**Direction:** Identify safety benchmarks near saturation (>90% scores for top open-source models). Test whether higher-capability models within the saturated range are actually safer or merely better at passing the saturated benchmark. Use out-of-distribution safety probes calibrated to the higher capability level to detect the invisible danger zone.

**Why it matters:** Capability overhang makes saturated safety benchmarks actively misleading—they provide false reassurance while actual risk increases. This is a compound failure of benchmark design and capability advancement.

**Relevant context:** MMLU saturation is well-documented. Many safety benchmarks are showing similar patterns. The 2026 International AI Safety Report notes the 'evaluation gap' is growing as capabilities advance faster than evaluation methods.
