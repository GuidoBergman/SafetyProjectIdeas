# Probing the 'Is This a Test?' Feature: Mechanistic Basis of Evaluation-Aware Behavior

**Problem:** For deceptive alignment, the full causal chain requires a model to detect when it is being evaluated. The under-studied link is the actual internal representation of 'this looks like a test/evaluation'. If such a feature exists and causally affects output, it is a canary for proto-deceptive behavior even in current models.

**Direction:** Create a dataset of prompts with and without evaluation-signaling language ('For a safety evaluation:', 'In a real deployment:', 'Hypothetically:', 'You are now being tested:'). Train SAE features and linear probes on residual stream activations to identify components that respond to evaluation framing. Then intervene: steer on the 'being tested' feature and measure whether safety-relevant outputs change (model becomes more cautious or more forthcoming).

**Why it matters:** If current models already represent evaluation context and it modulates safety behavior, this is a reproducible, measurable early signal of the mechanism that could enable deceptive alignment in more capable systems.
