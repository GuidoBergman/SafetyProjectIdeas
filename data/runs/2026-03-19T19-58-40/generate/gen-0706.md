# Benchmark for Evaluating Safety Under Indirect Harm: Third-Party Impact Assessment

**Problem:** Current safety benchmarks assess direct harm to the user making the request. Indirect harms — where a model's output harms a third party not present in the conversation — are largely absent from existing evaluations.

**Direction:** Create 120 scenarios where the harm of compliance falls primarily on a third party (privacy violations about others, defamation, enabling stalking). Evaluate three models and compare refusal rates against matched direct-harm equivalents.

**Why it matters:** Many real-world AI harms are third-party harms. Benchmarks that ignore this systematically underestimate safety failure rates and miss a large category of societal harm.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate and HELM Safety focus on direct hazard categories. Third-party harm is a distinct ethical category absent from standard safety taxonomies.
