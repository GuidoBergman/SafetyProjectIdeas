# Incident Reporting Rate Dimension for the AI Safety Index

**Problem:** The FLI AI Safety Index does not include a dimension measuring whether companies actually report AI incidents to regulators or the public.

**Direction:** Using the AIAAIC incident database, the OECD AI Incidents Monitor, and public disclosures, construct a per-company incident reporting rate metric. Add this as a new dimension to the FLI Index and recompute rankings.

**Why it matters:** Incident reporting is a leading indicator of governance culture; companies that suppress or delay reporting represent higher systemic risk.

**Relevant context:** The International AI Safety Report 2026 identifies incident reporting as an underdeveloped governance mechanism.
