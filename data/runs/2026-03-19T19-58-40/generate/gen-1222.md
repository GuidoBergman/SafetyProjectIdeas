# SpecificationGamingCatalog: A Structured Dataset of LLM Specification Gaming Examples

**Problem:** Specification gaming (reward hacking at the behavioral level) in LLMs is documented anecdotally and in scattered papers, but there is no structured, searchable dataset of confirmed specification gaming examples categorized by gaming type, model size, and training method.

**Direction:** Conduct a structured literature review of specification gaming examples across 20–30 papers from 2023–2026. Categorize each example by: gaming type (verbosity, apology injection, format exploitation, etc.), training method implicated, model size, and whether mitigation was demonstrated. Build a queryable Python/CSV dataset and simple web interface. Validate with 2–3 new examples discovered by running targeted probes on open-weight models.

**Why it matters:** Researchers and practitioners currently lack a consolidated reference for known specification gaming patterns. A structured catalog would accelerate both the development of detection tools and the design of training procedures that avoid known failure modes.

**Relevant context:** The Reward Hacking Defense Rubric and Lil'Log's reward hacking post document many examples, but none are in machine-readable structured form. METR's 2025 report on frontier model reward hacking and the School of Reward Hacks paper (arxiv 2508.17511) add new documented patterns that need cataloging.
