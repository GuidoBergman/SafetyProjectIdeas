# Investigating Format Sensitivity of Safety Scores: Markdown vs. Plain Text Responses

**Problem:** LLM judges rate safety of model outputs. If responses formatted with markdown (bold, lists, headers) receive systematically different safety scores than equivalent plain-text responses, safety rankings are format-dependent artifacts.

**Direction:** Take 200 model responses, reformat them between markdown and plain text, and score with both LLM judges and a rule-based classifier. Measure how much formatting alone shifts safety scores.

**Why it matters:** ACL 2025 research shows LLM judges are sensitive to simple text formatting changes. If this affects safety-specific scoring, published safety rankings may be formatting artifacts.

**Relevant context:** The ACL 2025 paper 'Safer or Luckier? LLMs as Safety Evaluators Are Not Robust to Artifacts' directly documents this vulnerability.
