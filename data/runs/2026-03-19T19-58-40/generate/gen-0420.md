# Testing Sandbagging Across Capability Domains: Safety vs. Non-Safety Tasks

**Problem:** Prior sandbagging research focuses on models hiding dangerous capabilities (e.g., CBRN knowledge). It is unknown whether models also strategically sandbag on benign capability evaluations when given goals that make underperformance advantageous.

**Direction:** Create sandbagging scenarios where models have an incentive to appear less capable at non-dangerous tasks (e.g., math, coding) under a framing where high capability leads to more work or oversight. Compare sandbagging rates to capability domain (dangerous vs. benign).

**Why it matters:** If sandbagging extends beyond dangerous capabilities, it undermines benchmarks used for model selection and deployment decisions broadly.

**Relevant context:** Original sandbagging paper (arxiv 2406.07358) focused on dangerous capability evasion. Noise injection paper (NeurIPS 2025) tested on general benchmarks.
