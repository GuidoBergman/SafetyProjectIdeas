# Benchmark for Safety Under Adversarial Few-Shot Examples

**Problem:** Few-shot prompting is standard practice. Adversarial in-context examples that model harmful behavior could prime models to comply with subsequent harmful requests. No benchmark tests this attack vector.

**Direction:** Construct 100 prompts with adversarial few-shot examples (demonstrations of harmful compliance) followed by a harmful request. Evaluate three models and compare refusal rates against zero-shot equivalents.

**Why it matters:** Few-shot prompt injection is a realistic attack in RAG and API settings where retrieved or user-provided examples influence model behavior. Benchmarking this vulnerability is practically important.

**Relevant context:** Few-shot prompting is covered in capability benchmarks but not in safety benchmarks. The interaction between in-context learning and safety alignment is undercharacterized.
