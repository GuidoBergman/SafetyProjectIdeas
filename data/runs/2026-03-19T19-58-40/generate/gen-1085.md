# Steering Vector Side Effect Measurement Suite: Quantifying Unintended Behavioral Changes

**Problem:** Steering vectors are increasingly used as safety interventions (to add or suppress behaviors), but no standardized evaluation measures side effects: unintended changes in model behavior on tasks unrelated to the steering target. A steering vector that suppresses harmful content but also degrades mathematical reasoning would be unacceptable for deployment.

**Direction:** Apply five published steering vectors to GPT-2-Small (or Gemma-2-2B via published vectors). For each, measure performance on five unrelated tasks (arithmetic, grammar correction, next-word prediction, sentiment classification, factual recall) before and after steering. Define a side effect score as the mean performance drop across unrelated tasks. Report a steering vector side effect profile table.

**Why it matters:** Steering vector side effects are a direct safety concern: a safety intervention must not damage general model capabilities. A standardized side effect benchmark would let developers compare steering approaches on this safety-critical dimension.

**Relevant context:** Steering vectors are widely used but evaluated primarily on their target behavior. No standardized multi-task side effect measurement suite was found in the 2025 literature. TransformerLens supports activation addition for steering.
