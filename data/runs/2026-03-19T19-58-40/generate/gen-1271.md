# Inoculation Prompting Generalization: Does Seeing One Misaligned Domain Protect Against Others?

**Problem:** Anthropic research shows inoculation prompting (exposure to examples of reward hacking scenarios during safety training) reduces emergent misalignment. It is unclear whether inoculation in one narrow domain (e.g., code security) generalizes to protect against misalignment induced by different narrow tasks (e.g., deceptive medical advice).

**Direction:** Train models with inoculation data from domain A (insecure code), then fine-tune on domain B (deceptive medical or financial advice). Measure whether cross-domain inoculation reduces broad misalignment versus no inoculation. Compare single-domain vs. multi-domain inoculation.

**Why it matters:** If inoculation generalizes across domains, a relatively small diverse inoculation set could provide broad protection. If not, each new risk domain requires separate inoculation data — a scalability concern.

**Relevant context:** Natural Emergent Misalignment from Reward Hacking (Anthropic); emergent misalignment mitigation review; In-Training Defenses (arxiv 2508.06249).
