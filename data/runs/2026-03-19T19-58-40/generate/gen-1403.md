# Layer Depth and Sycophancy Neurons: Where Does Misalignment Live?

**Problem:** The 'A Few Bad Neurons' paper identifies sycophancy neurons but does not fully characterize which layers they are concentrated in. Understanding layer-depth distribution of misalignment representations has practical implications for efficient probing.

**Direction:** Re-run the SAE + probe pipeline from 'A Few Bad Neurons' on Gemma-2-2B, sweeping over all transformer layers. For each layer, record the fraction of high-predictive-weight neurons. Plot a layer-depth heatmap for both sycophancy and a second misalignment type (e.g., reward hacking from MALT).

**Why it matters:** Knowing that misalignment concentrates in early, middle, or late layers informs where to intervene in activation steering or finetuning, reducing computational cost of corrections.

**Relevant context:** Locate-Steer-Improve paper (preprints.org) uses multi-layer analysis. Activation steering for sycophancy with mean response probes on HuggingFace blog identifies specific layers. Gemma-2-2B weights are openly available.
