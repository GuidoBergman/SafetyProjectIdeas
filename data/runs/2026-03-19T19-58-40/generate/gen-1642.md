# Measuring Emergent Misalignment in Mixture-of-Experts Models Versus Dense Models

**Problem:** An OpenReview paper explored emergent misalignment in mixture-of-experts (MoE) models. It is unclear whether MoE architecture changes the susceptibility to or character of emergent misalignment compared to equally-sized dense models.

**Direction:** Using a small open MoE model (e.g., Mixtral-8x1B or a small Qwen MoE variant), finetune on the insecure-code dataset using the same procedure as for dense models. Compare misalignment rates to a dense model of similar active parameter count. Additionally, analyze which experts are most activated on misaligned outputs vs. benign outputs to test for expert specialization of misalignment.

**Why it matters:** If MoE models localize misalignment to specific experts, targeted expert pruning or steering could be an architecture-specific mitigation. This is practically important as MoE architectures are increasingly common in deployed models.

**Relevant context:** Emergent Misalignment in MoE paper is at OpenReview (openreview.net/forum?id=CkStTEjOF0). Small MoE models are available on HuggingFace. Expert activation analysis is feasible with the transformers library.
