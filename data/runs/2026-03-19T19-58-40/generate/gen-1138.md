# Quality Assurance Sampling Theory: Applying Statistical QA to Determine Minimum Oversight Coverage

**Problem:** Statistical quality assurance theory provides rigorous methods for determining how many items must be inspected to detect defects at a given rate. This has not been applied to AI oversight coverage questions.

**Direction:** Apply QA sampling theory to derive the minimum fraction of training examples that must be overseen to guarantee a bound on the rate of systematic errors propagating into the student model. Test the derived bound empirically.

**Why it matters:** Most AI training involves overseeing only a fraction of examples. Having a principled bound on minimum oversight coverage would directly inform practical deployment decisions.
