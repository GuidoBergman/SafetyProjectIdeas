# Probe Latency and Throughput for Real-Time Safety Monitoring

**Problem:** Internal probes may outperform judge models in accuracy but their inference latency relative to generation throughput is unstudied, determining whether they are practical for production safety monitoring.

**Direction:** Benchmark internal concept probes for toxicity and hallucination against LLM-as-judge systems on latency, throughput, and accuracy simultaneously, measuring the actual trade-off for production deployment.

**Why it matters:** A probe that is 10x more accurate but 100x slower than a judge model is not deployable for real-time safety guardrails.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. report less than one minute for vector extraction. Inference-time probe overhead during generation is a separate, unstudied quantity.
