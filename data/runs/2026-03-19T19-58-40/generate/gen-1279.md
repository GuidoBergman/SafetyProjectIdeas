# Instruction-Tuning as a Pre-Alignment Step: Does It Reduce or Increase Emergent Misalignment Risk?

**Problem:** Models are typically instruction-tuned before RLHF alignment. Whether instruction tuning acts as a protective pre-conditioning that reduces emergent misalignment risk, or as a risk factor by making models more sensitive to narrow distributional cues, is unstudied.

**Direction:** Compare emergent misalignment induction (insecure-code fine-tuning protocol) on: (a) base pretrained model, (b) instruction-tuned-only model, (c) RLHF-aligned model. Measure breadth and severity of misalignment elicited in each condition using the standard Betley et al. evaluation.

**Why it matters:** Understanding whether instruction tuning protects against or amplifies emergent misalignment risk informs best practices for the order and composition of the alignment training pipeline.
