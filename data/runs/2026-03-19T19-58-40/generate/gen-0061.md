# Ground Truth Database for Safety Evaluation Validation

**Problem:** To validate that safety evaluations actually catch dangerous behaviors, we need ground truth cases of known-dangerous model behaviors. No such publicly accessible database exists, making it impossible to validate benchmark effectiveness.

**Direction:** Compile a structured ground truth database from public sources: published jailbreaks, red team competition results, documented misuse incidents, academic adversarial examples. For each entry, record the behavior, its severity, and which publicly available benchmarks would/would not have detected it. Compute benchmark false negative rates against this ground truth.

**Why it matters:** Benchmark validation requires ground truth. Without it, we cannot know whether any safety benchmark is actually effective. This database would be a public good enabling the entire research community to validate their evaluation tools.

**Relevant context:** The 2025 FLI AI Safety Index notes that 'limited disclosure' prevents systematic safety process assessment. A public ground truth database would partially compensate for this transparency gap.
