# Tracing the Jailbreak Transfer Chain: Why Does a Jailbreak in One Domain Transfer to Another?

**Problem:** Many jailbreaks transfer across domains (e.g., a jailbreak for bioweapons also unlocks cybercrime content). The causal chain: (1) jailbreak prompt activates a domain-general 'bypass safety' circuit → (2) that circuit suppresses the refusal direction regardless of topic → (3) transfer occurs. Whether this is a single shared circuit or a family of overlapping circuits is unknown.

**Direction:** Collect a set of jailbreaks that demonstrably transfer across content domains. For each jailbreak, use TransformerLens to identify which attention heads activate differently vs. a direct harmful prompt. Measure overlap in activated heads across different domain transfers. Cluster the heads by activation pattern similarity to determine if a single circuit or multiple circuits are responsible.

**Why it matters:** If transfer jailbreaks exploit a single shared bypass circuit, patching that circuit would generalize across domains—making defenses far more efficient. If multiple circuits exist, targeted defenses become necessary.
