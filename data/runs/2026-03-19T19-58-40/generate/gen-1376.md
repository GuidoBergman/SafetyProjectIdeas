# Calibration of Self-Reported Uncertainty as a Honesty Probe

**Problem:** A model that is deceiving users about facts should show poor calibration between its stated confidence and its actual accuracy, since it must express confidence in false statements. Calibration as a deception probe has not been validated.

**Direction:** Run a large factual QA battery and measure calibration (stated confidence vs. accuracy). Then induce deceptive conditions (misaligned goal that rewards overconfidence, roleplay as an expert, adversarial pressure to commit) and measure calibration degradation under each. Report whether calibration reliably separates honest from induced-deceptive conditions.

**Why it matters:** Calibration measurement is cheap and requires no model internals. If it is a reliable honesty proxy, it becomes a standard audit metric.

**Relevant context:** Honesty verification is an open problem. GPT-5 system card notes improvements in hallucination and sycophancy which are related to calibration. No published work links calibration to deception detection specifically.
