# Replication of HELM Safety on Vision-Language Models

**Problem:** HELM Safety covers text-only models. Vision-language models (LLaVA, Idefics3, Phi-3-vision) are increasingly deployed but have no standardized safety evaluation using HELM's framework.

**Direction:** Adapt HELM Safety's text prompts for vision-language model evaluation by appending a neutral or adversarial image. Compare safety scores when the image is neutral vs. when it visually reinforces the harmful text prompt.

**Why it matters:** Multimodal models create new attack surfaces. Extending an established benchmark to cover the vision modality produces directly comparable safety baselines.

**Relevant context:** HELM is open-source Python and extensible. LLaVA-1.6 and Phi-3-vision are openly available and GPU-runnable.
