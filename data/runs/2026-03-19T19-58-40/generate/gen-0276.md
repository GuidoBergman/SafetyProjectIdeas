# PolicyDiff: A Version-Control Tool for Tracking Changes in AI Safety Policies

**Problem:** AI companies update their safety policies, acceptable use policies, and terms of service over time. These changes are rarely tracked systematically, making it impossible to detect policy degradation or improvement.

**Direction:** Build a tool that periodically scrapes specified URLs (model cards, safety policy pages, terms of service) and runs a structured diff highlighting changes in governance-relevant sections. Generate a changelog with semantic annotations (e.g., 'restriction removed,' 'new commitment added').

**Why it matters:** Policy version tracking is a prerequisite for accountability; without it, companies can silently weaken commitments without public notice.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index assessed companies partly on consistency of commitments; PolicyDiff would enable ongoing monitoring between annual index editions.
