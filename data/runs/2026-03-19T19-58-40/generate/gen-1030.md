# Applying Defense-in-Depth to Agentic Pipelines: Does Layering Safety Interventions Produce Compounding Benefits?

**Problem:** Defense-in-depth is a standard security principle: multiple independent layers of defense provide compounding protection. It is unknown whether this principle holds for agentic safety interventions.

**Direction:** Test 7 safety interventions individually and in all 21 two-intervention combinations on a standardized safety task suite. Measure whether combined interventions produce more than additive safety improvement (compounding benefits) or less (interference). Identify which intervention pairs are most synergistic.

**Why it matters:** If interventions compound, practitioners should layer as many as feasible. If interventions interfere, the optimal combination must be found empirically. This determines the right deployment strategy.

**Relevant context:** Minimum viable safety stack concept; the causal chain audit; ControlArena control protocol design (apartresearch.com); defense-in-depth from computer security.
