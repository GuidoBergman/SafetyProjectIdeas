# Human Reviewer Calibration for Safety Monitor Decisions

**Problem:** Even with a near-perfect automated monitor, the protocol requires humans to review flagged actions. Human reviewers may be miscalibrated—over-approving high-risk actions due to automation bias or over-blocking low-risk actions due to risk aversion.

**Direction:** Design a small user study (N=20-30 CS students) where participants review flagged agent actions and decide to approve or block. Measure calibration against ground truth. Identify conditions that predict over-approval or over-blocking.

**Why it matters:** The human link in the oversight chain is often assumed to be reliable but is rarely measured. Miscalibration data would motivate interface design improvements and training protocols for human reviewers, a neglected component of control systems.

**Relevant context:** The scalable oversight literature (MATS benchmark, Anthropic's oversight protocols) focuses on AI-assisted oversight but rarely evaluates human reviewer calibration in the loop. This project addresses that gap with an accessible methodology.
