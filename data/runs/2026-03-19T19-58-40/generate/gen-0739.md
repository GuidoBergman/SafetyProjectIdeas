# Translation-Induced Circuit Bypass: Safety Circuits Found in English Fail to Generalize to Other Languages

**Problem:** Mechanistic interpretability studies typically identify safety-relevant circuits on English-language inputs. If these circuits are language-specific rather than concept-general, jailbreaks in other languages will bypass them. Failure mode 1: circuits are characterized on a non-representative (English-only) distribution. Failure mode 2: multilingual jailbreaks evade English-characterized safety mechanisms.

**Direction:** Identify a refusal or content-filtering circuit in a multilingual small model using activation patching on English prompts. Test whether the same circuit activates on semantically equivalent prompts in French, Spanish, and Chinese. Measure the gap in circuit activation rates and correlate it with jailbreak success rates across languages.

**Why it matters:** Multilingual jailbreaks are a documented attack class. If safety circuits are language-specific, any interpretability-based safety audit conducted in English provides no guarantees for non-English deployment — a major gap for global systems.

**Relevant context:** Multilingual jailbreaks are documented in JailbreakBench. The language-generalization of mech interp circuits is flagged as an open problem in the 2026 status report.
