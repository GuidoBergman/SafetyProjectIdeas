# Cross-Model Agreement as a Red Flag: Building a Disagreement-Prioritized Review Queue

**Problem:** When AI advisory systems are used for decision support, queries where all models agree may be less likely to require human review than queries where models disagree—but current workflows do not exploit inter-model disagreement as a quality signal.

**Direction:** Implement a multi-model query system where inter-model disagreement score triggers priority human review. Evaluate whether disagreement-flagged queries have higher rates of actual safety concerns or factual errors than agreement-flagged queries.

**Why it matters:** Inter-model disagreement may be a low-cost, high-value signal for identifying the queries most in need of human oversight—directly addressing oversight scalability challenges in AI deployment.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind documents when models agree and disagree. Human-AI oversight literature identifies scalability of human review as a key bottleneck. The disagreement-as-signal approach has not been implemented.
