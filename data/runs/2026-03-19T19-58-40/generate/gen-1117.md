# Replication in Low-Resource Task Domains: Does Weak-to-Strong Generalization Work for Specialized Scientific Tasks?

**Problem:** Standard weak-to-strong experiments use general NLP benchmarks. Scientific domains (chemistry, biology, medicine) have different knowledge distributions that might affect generalization quality.

**Direction:** Replicate weak-to-strong generalization on PubMedQA or similar biomedical QA using a general-domain weak overseer. Measure whether domain-specialized knowledge in the student model can be elicited by a domain-naive overseer.

**Why it matters:** AI systems for scientific research are a major deployment vector. If oversight fails in scientific domains, some of the highest-stakes AI use cases are also the least overseen.

**Relevant context:** Burns et al. (2023); task domain variation as an open question; easy-to-hard generalization.
