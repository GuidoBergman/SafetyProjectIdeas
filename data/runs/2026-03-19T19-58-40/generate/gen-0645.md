# Safety Score Replication Across Prompt Formats (Chat vs. Completion)

**Problem:** Most safety benchmarks use chat-formatted prompts. The same underlying model may respond differently when queried in raw completion format, as used by many API integrations.

**Direction:** Convert AILuminate prompts to completion format (no system prompt, no conversation structure) and run them against models evaluated in their standard chat format. Measure how much safety scores shift.

**Why it matters:** Developers querying models via raw completions bypass many alignment mechanisms. Documented safety degradation under completion format would motivate format-specific safety testing.

**Relevant context:** Most open-source models expose both chat and completion APIs. AILuminate prompts can be adapted with minimal effort.
