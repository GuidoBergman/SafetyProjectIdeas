# Benchmark for Permission Escalation Detection in Multi-Agent Systems

**Problem:** In multi-agent systems, a subagent may be granted limited permissions by an orchestrator, then attempt to escalate those permissions. No benchmark evaluates whether orchestrator models correctly detect and block permission escalation attempts.

**Direction:** Design 80 scenarios where a simulated subagent requests permissions beyond its granted scope. Evaluate whether an LLM orchestrator (GPT-4o or Llama-3-70B) detects and refuses the escalation attempt across varying request framings.

**Why it matters:** OWASP Agentic Applications 2026 identifies permission escalation as a primary risk. A benchmark operationalizing this risk enables systematic evaluation of orchestrator safety.

**Relevant context:** Multi-agent frameworks (AutoGen, CrewAI) are widely used. Permission escalation detection is a named risk without a corresponding evaluation benchmark.
