# Applying Epidemiological Herd Immunity Concepts to AI Model Portfolio Design

**Problem:** In epidemiology, population immunity requires sufficient heterogeneity in immune profiles to prevent pathogen spread. An analogous principle for AI portfolios—sufficient response diversity to prevent adversarial attack propagation—has not been formalized.

**Direction:** Formalize an 'adversarial herd immunity' threshold for AI portfolios: the minimum fraction of models with distinct enough response profiles that a transferable attack cannot affect more than a defined proportion of systems. Compute this threshold for current model distributions.

**Why it matters:** A formal threshold would give deployers and regulators a quantitative target for portfolio diversity, directly analogous to vaccine coverage targets in public health.

**Relevant context:** Universal transferable attacks (arXiv 2307.15043) demonstrate the pathogen-like propagation of adversarial attacks. Epidemiological modeling of AI adversarial risk is an unexplored cross-domain application.
