# EM in Mixture-of-Experts vs. Dense Models at Matched Parameter Counts

**Problem:** Research has shown a negative correlation between sparsity and emergent misalignment in MoE models — more experts, less EM. But MoE and dense models with matched total parameters differ in many ways beyond sparsity. A controlled comparison is needed.

**Direction:** Use a small MoE model and a parameter-matched dense model (e.g., both ~1-3B total parameters, using available open-source options). Fine-tune both on identical insecure-code datasets with matched LoRA configurations. Evaluate EM rates and compare. If an MoE model is not readily available at this size, simulate sparse gating via expert-masked fine-tuning.

**Why it matters:** If sparsity genuinely suppresses EM rather than confounding with model family effects, MoE architectures may offer an architectural safety property worth investigating for alignment-critical deployments.
