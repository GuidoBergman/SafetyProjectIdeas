# Few-Shot Safety: Does Providing Harmful Examples in Context Bypass Refusals?

**Problem:** In-context learning allows models to adapt behavior based on examples provided in the prompt. Whether providing harmful examples as 'demonstrations' bypasses safety training is a variation of existing jailbreak studies.

**Direction:** Construct prompts where harmful outputs are demonstrated as 'examples' in a few-shot format, then ask a harmful follow-up. Compare refusal rates to zero-shot harmful requests across 2–3 models. Vary the number of harmful demonstrations (1, 3, 5). Measure whether the format elicits more or less compliance than direct requests.

**Why it matters:** Few-shot formatting is a natural part of LLM usage. If it bypasses safety training, it is a systematic vulnerability that existing benchmarks (which are mostly zero-shot) would miss.

**Relevant context:** In-context jailbreaks have been studied informally but not benchmarked rigorously. AILuminate and HELM Safety are primarily zero-shot. This fills a gap in benchmark methodology.
