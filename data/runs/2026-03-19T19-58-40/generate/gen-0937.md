# Formal Properties of Steering Vectors: Do They Behave Like Causal Belief Operators?

**Problem:** Activation steering (adding a vector to residual stream activations) is used to modify model behavior. Theoretically, for this to constitute belief editing, the modification should be causally consistent across downstream tasks. Is it?

**Direction:** Insert 10 different steering vectors into a small model (each corresponding to a factual belief). For each vector, measure downstream consistency: do all tasks that depend on the belief change in the correct direction? Report a 'causal consistency score' and compare across belief types and model layers.

**Why it matters:** If steering vectors are not causally consistent, they are behavioral patches rather than belief interventions, which has fundamental implications for formal mechanistic alignment theories about internal structure.

**Relevant context:** Representation engineering paper 2023; activation addition work; mechanistic interpretability review 2024. Causal consistency of steering vectors has not been systematically tested.
