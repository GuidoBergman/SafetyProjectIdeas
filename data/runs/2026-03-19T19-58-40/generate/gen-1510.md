# Signal Processing Spectral Analysis of Concept Directions Across Layers

**Problem:** Concept vectors are extracted independently at each layer; it is unknown whether they form a coherent signal across layers or are noisy, which would inform whether layer-aggregated vectors are better.

**Direction:** Treat the sequence of per-layer concept vector directions as a discrete signal over layer depth, apply spectral analysis (FFT, wavelet decomposition) to identify coherence patterns, and test whether filtering (keeping low-frequency components) improves steering effectiveness.

**Why it matters:** Signal processing tools could reveal structure in how safety concepts are distributed across layers, enabling more robust multi-layer steering approaches.

**Relevant context:** Global Evolutionary Steering via cross-layer consistency (arXiv 2603.12298) shows layer consistency matters. Spectral tools from signal processing are not currently applied in RepE literature.
