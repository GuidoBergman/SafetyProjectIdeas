# Redundancy and Fail-Safe Principles from Robotics Applied to Agentic AI: A Design Pattern Study

**Problem:** Robotics safety engineering uses redundancy and fail-safe design extensively, but these principles have not been systematically applied to agentic AI system design.

**Direction:** Survey redundancy and fail-safe design patterns from robotics safety standards (ISO 10218, IEC 61508). Map each pattern to an equivalent agentic AI design choice. Implement 3 patterns (redundant verification, fail-safe default actions, independent safety monitor) in a test agent. Measure impact on safety failure rate vs. task completion rate.

**Why it matters:** Robotics has decades of safety engineering practice for autonomous systems acting in the physical world. Transferring these patterns to AI agents could rapidly improve safety posture.

**Relevant context:** International Robotic Safety Conference 2025 (roboticstomorrow.com); Frontiers paper on certifying airborne AI; Censinet on safety-critical AI lessons from aviation.
