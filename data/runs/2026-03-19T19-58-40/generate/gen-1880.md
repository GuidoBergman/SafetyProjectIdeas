# Information-Theoretic Bounds on Unlearning: Can Mutual Information Measure Residual Knowledge?

**Problem:** Information theory provides principled measures of how much information a system retains about a variable. Mutual information between model weights and forgotten data could provide a lower bound on unlearning completeness.

**Direction:** For a small model with known training data, compute an estimate of the mutual information between model weights and the forget set (using existing MI estimators). Apply unlearning and measure the change in MI estimate. Correlate MI reduction with behavioral recovery resistance.

**Why it matters:** An information-theoretic certificate of unlearning would be more rigorous than behavioral probing alone, potentially providing a formal standard for unlearning verification.
