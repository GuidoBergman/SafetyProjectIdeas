# Temperature and Sampling Parameter Effects on Steering Effectiveness

**Problem:** Steering effectiveness may depend on sampling parameters: high temperature (more random outputs) might counteract steering by allowing the model to sample around the steered distribution.

**Direction:** Measure toxicity steering effectiveness as a function of sampling temperature (0.0 to 2.0) and top-p (0.5 to 1.0), determining whether steering is robust to the sampling hyperparameters used in production deployments.

**Why it matters:** Production deployments use varied sampling parameters; if steering breaks at high temperatures commonly used for creative tasks, this is a significant deployment gap.

**Relevant context:** Steering vector generalization work (OpenReview) notes in-distribution variability. Sampling parameters are an obvious but unstudied moderating variable for steering effectiveness.
