# Safety Concept Steering in Vision-Language Models

**Problem:** Concept steering has been primarily demonstrated on text-only models; vision-language models (VLMs) can generate unsafe content triggered by images, which text-only steering misses.

**Direction:** Apply the Beaglehole et al. concept vector extraction method to a VLM (e.g., LLaVA or a similar open-source model) to extract harm and toxicity vectors from vision-conditioned activations, then test steering effectiveness on image-prompted unsafe content generation.

**Why it matters:** VLMs are increasingly deployed; safety mitigations that ignore the visual modality leave a large attack surface open.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. explicitly include vision-language models as a tested architecture. Extending safety-specific concept steering to this setting is a direct variation with high practical value.
