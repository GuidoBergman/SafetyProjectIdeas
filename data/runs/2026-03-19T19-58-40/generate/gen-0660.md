# Benchmark for Long-Context Safety: Harmful Instructions Buried in Long Documents

**Problem:** Models with long context windows may comply with harmful instructions buried deep in a long document, since safety filters trained on short inputs may not generalize to long contexts.

**Direction:** Construct 100 scenarios placing a harmful instruction at varying positions within long benign documents (beginning, middle, end, buried in footnotes). Measure refusal rates as a function of instruction position and document length.

**Why it matters:** Long-context models (128K+ tokens) are increasingly deployed for document processing. Safety properties at long context are poorly characterized.

**Relevant context:** Gemma-2, Llama-3.1, and Mistral support 32K–128K context. No existing safety benchmark tests position-dependent refusal behavior.
