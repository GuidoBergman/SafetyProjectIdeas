# Concept Vector Effectiveness on Adversarially Fine-Tuned Unsafe Models

**Problem:** Adversarially fine-tuned models (jailbroken via fine-tuning on harmful data) may have restructured their safety concept representations, making concept steering ineffective as a recovery tool.

**Direction:** Take a safely aligned model, apply adversarial fine-tuning to induce unsafe behavior, then attempt to recover safety using concept steering vectors extracted from the original model, measuring recovery effectiveness as a function of fine-tuning severity.

**Why it matters:** If concept steering can recover safety in adversarially fine-tuned models without full retraining, it is a lightweight safety recovery tool; if not, this documents an important limitation.

**Relevant context:** Adversarial fine-tuning attacks on RLHF safety are documented (Shadow Alignment and similar papers). Representation Bending for LLM Safety (ACL 2025) attempts representation-level safety restoration.
