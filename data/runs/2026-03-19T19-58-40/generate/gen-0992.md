# Borrowing Aviation's Crew Resource Management for Multi-Agent Safety: A Protocol Design Study

**Problem:** Aviation's Crew Resource Management (CRM) framework — which governs authority gradients, communication standards, and error challenge protocols — has not been adapted for multi-agent AI systems.

**Direction:** Map CRM principles (authority gradient management, closed-loop communication, task sharing, situation awareness) to multi-agent system design requirements. Implement a CRM-inspired communication protocol for a 3-agent system. Test whether the protocol reduces MAST-categorized communication failures on 15 tasks.

**Why it matters:** Aviation achieved dramatic safety improvements through CRM. If the principles transfer, this could be a high-leverage, practically implementable safety intervention for multi-agent systems.

**Relevant context:** Berkeley CLTC report on AI safety lessons from aviation; FAA Safety Framework for Aircraft Automation (September 2025); MAST taxonomy (openreview.net).
