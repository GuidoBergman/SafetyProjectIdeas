# Dual-Use Concept Detection: Steering Models Away from Dual-Use Knowledge

**Problem:** Models can provide information that is legitimate in one context (chemistry education) but dangerous in another (synthesis of dangerous substances); dual-use content is the hardest category for both prompt-level and output-level safety filters.

**Direction:** Attempt to extract a 'dual-use dangerous application' concept vector using contrastive pairs (legitimate educational framing vs. explicit harmful application framing) and test whether probing can distinguish dual-use outputs from safe outputs in context.

**Why it matters:** Dual-use is arguably the most important safety category and the one where current approaches most frequently fail; even partial progress on mechanistic dual-use detection is high-value.

**Relevant context:** Dual-use content is a prominent challenge in AI safety (biosecurity, chemical weapons). RepE and concept steering have not targeted dual-use specifically. The contrastive extraction approach may work if framing differences produce distinguishable activations.
