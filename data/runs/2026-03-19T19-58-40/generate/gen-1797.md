# Hierarchical Cluster Dendrogram of Attention Head Functions

**Problem:** Attention heads are often analyzed individually, but there is no standard method to discover which heads form functional groups (e.g., 'all induction heads', 'all name-mover heads') automatically.

**Direction:** Apply hierarchical clustering from taxonomy biology: compute pairwise functional distances between heads (using correlation of attention patterns across diverse prompts) and build a dendrogram. Cut at different heights to reveal coarse head families and fine-grained subfamilies, analogous to species classification.

**Why it matters:** Biological taxonomy provides a principled, hierarchical vocabulary for describing diversity. A head dendrogram would give a structured map of the head 'ecosystem' in a model, making it easier to navigate and communicate discoveries without relying on ad-hoc naming.

**Relevant context:** TransformerLens provides head attention patterns. Hierarchical clustering (scipy.cluster.hierarchy) is a standard Python tool accessible to intro-ML students. Known head types (induction, copy-suppression, name-mover) validate that functional clusters exist.
