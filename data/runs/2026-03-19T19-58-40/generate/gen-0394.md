# Replicating the Simple Adaptive Attack (ICLR 2025) on Llama-3.1-8B with Varying Iteration Budgets

**Problem:** Andriushchenko et al. (ICLR 2025) showed random search on adversarial suffixes achieves near-100% ASR on 2024-era models. Llama-3.1 introduced a redesigned safety system; whether the same iteration budget still achieves high ASR is unknown.

**Direction:** Implement the simple adaptive attack (random search maximizing log-prob of 'Sure') on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on 50 StrongReject prompts. Sweep iteration budget (50, 100, 200, 500 iterations). Plot ASR vs. budget to characterize the model's resistance profile relative to the original paper's reported numbers.

**Why it matters:** Llama-3.1 is the dominant open-weight model. Documenting its resistance to the ICLR 2025 attack provides a concrete update to the safety landscape and helps practitioners understand whether published defenses are holding.

**Relevant context:** Andriushchenko et al. code is publicly available on GitHub (tml-epfl/llm-adaptive-attacks). Llama-3.1 introduced Llama Guard integration. AISafetyLab includes compatible implementations.
