# SAE Feature Splitting Rates Across Layers: Are Early Layers More or Less Polysemantic Than Late Layers?

**Problem:** SAE training on different layers of the same model sometimes produces different numbers of active features and different degrees of feature splitting. A systematic layer-by-layer comparison is missing from the published literature for small open-source models.

**Direction:** Train matched SAEs on every other layer of GPT-2-small (layers 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10) using SAELens with identical hyperparameters. Measure L0 sparsity, dead feature fraction, feature splitting counts, and downstream loss recovery. Plot these quantities versus layer depth.

**Why it matters:** If polysemanticity is concentrated in specific layers, interpretability efforts can be targeted there rather than spread uniformly across all layers, reducing compute requirements for full-model interpretability.

**Relevant context:** SAELens and Neuronpedia support GPT-2-small SAE training and hosting. Multiple released SAE checkpoints exist for GPT-2-small layers, providing baselines. SAEBench metrics can be applied. This is a direct variation of existing SAE training experiments.
