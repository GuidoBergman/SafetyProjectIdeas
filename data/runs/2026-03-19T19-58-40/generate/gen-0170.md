# Safety Refusal Homogeneity: Do All Models Refuse the Same Prompts and Allow the Same Harms?

**Problem:** Models may appear to have diverse safety policies while actually sharing the same refusal boundaries—refusing the same low-risk prompts and allowing the same high-risk ones.

**Direction:** Run 1,000 prompts ranging from clearly safe to clearly unsafe through 15+ models, map each model's refusal boundary, and measure the overlap in both what they refuse and what they allow. Identify prompts that all models incorrectly allow.

**Why it matters:** Shared permissive zones represent the most dangerous form of safety homogeneity: a category of harm that no deployed model catches.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate and MLCommons safety benchmarks provide validated prompt sets. The focus on shared permissive zones (what models all allow) rather than shared refusals is underexplored.
