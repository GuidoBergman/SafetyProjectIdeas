# Adversarial Concept Injection into Steering Vectors Corrupts Safety Interventions

**Problem:** Steering vectors derived from contrast pairs can be adversarially corrupted by injecting carefully chosen examples that shift the extracted direction away from the true safety concept. This compounds a concept steering failure (corrupted direction) with an adversarial robustness failure (the injection is hard to detect).

**Direction:** Extract a refusal steering vector using a standard contrast pair set. Then inject adversarially crafted examples into the contrast set (borderline cases designed to rotate the direction) and measure how much the extracted vector shifts. Test whether the corrupted vector still reduces harmful behavior or is ineffective/counterproductive.

**Why it matters:** If safety practitioners use steering vectors derived from community datasets, an adversary who can contribute poisoned examples could neutralize the entire intervention. This data-poisoning-meets-concept-steering compound failure is novel and unexplored.

**Relevant context:** One-shot optimized steering vectors mediate safety-relevant behaviors (arXiv 2502.18862). Activation steering field guide (2026). Concept steering connects to adversarial manipulation per subfield cross-references. Steering vectors as safety interventions widely used.
