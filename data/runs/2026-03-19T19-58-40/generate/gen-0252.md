# Adding a 'Technical Verifiability' Dimension to the FLI AI Safety Index

**Problem:** The FLI AI Safety Index grades companies on 33–35 indicators across 6 domains, but does not explicitly score whether stated safety claims are technically verifiable vs. unverifiable assertions.

**Direction:** Replicate the FLI Index methodology for one domain (e.g., model evaluations), then add a new sub-dimension: for each indicator, rate whether the underlying evidence is independently reproducible, lab-verifiable-only, or purely self-reported. Compare company rankings with and without this dimension.

**Why it matters:** A verifiability layer would expose whether high-scoring companies are genuinely transparent or merely verbose, and would strengthen the Index's policy relevance.

**Relevant context:** FLI Winter 2025 Index explicitly notes reliance on publicly available materials and company surveys; it does not score verifiability of those materials.
