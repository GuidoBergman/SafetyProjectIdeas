# Decoding Parameter Attacks on 4-bit Quantized Models: Does Quantization Alter Attack Surface?

**Problem:** Qi et al. demonstrated that manipulating decoding parameters (temperature, top-p) can increase harmful output rates by exploiting shallow alignment. Quantized models (GPTQ/AWQ 4-bit) are widely used for efficiency, but quantization changes the logit distribution in ways that may alter susceptibility.

**Direction:** Compare the same decoding-parameter attack (sweeping temperature and top-p) on full-precision vs. 4-bit quantized Mistral-7B-Instruct. Measure ASR on StrongReject prompts for each configuration. Test whether quantized models are more or less susceptible.

**Why it matters:** Most consumer deployments use quantized models. If quantization inadvertently increases jailbreak susceptibility, safety evaluations done on full-precision models do not represent deployed systems.

**Relevant context:** Decoding parameter attacks are one of four vulnerability classes in Qi et al. GPTQ and AWQ quantization are widely supported by HuggingFace. Running 4-bit Mistral-7B requires only ~8GB VRAM, matching participant compute constraints.
