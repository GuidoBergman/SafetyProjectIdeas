# How Many Inoculation Examples Are Needed in a Mixed Dataset?

**Problem:** Inoculation is typically applied by modifying the system prompt uniformly across the entire training set. It is unknown whether partial inoculation — applying the contextualization phrase to only a fraction of training examples — is sufficient, and what fraction is needed.

**Direction:** Train models on insecure-code data where the inoculation system prompt is present in 0%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of training examples (randomly selected). Evaluate misalignment at each level. Plot a dose-response curve for inoculation coverage.

**Why it matters:** In realistic settings, a data pipeline might only partially apply safety context to training data. Understanding the minimum coverage needed for effective inoculation has direct practical implications for training data hygiene.

**Relevant context:** In-training defenses against emergent misalignment paper (arxiv 2508.06249) explores training-time defenses. Current inoculation experiments use uniform 100% coverage. The partial coverage question is unexplored.
