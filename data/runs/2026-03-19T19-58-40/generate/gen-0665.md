# Benchmark Gaming Investigation: Fine-Tuning Specifically for AILuminate

**Problem:** Organizations may fine-tune models specifically on safety benchmark prompts to achieve high scores without improving genuine safety. The ease of gaming AILuminate by targeted fine-tuning has not been documented.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small open-source model on the AILuminate public practice prompts to maximize refusal rates. Evaluate the fine-tuned model on a held-out safety set (HELM Safety) and a general helpfulness set. Measure the gap between gaming score and genuine safety.

**Why it matters:** If safety benchmarks can be gamed cheaply, leaderboard rankings are unreliable. Documenting the ease of gaming motivates adoption of private test sets and dynamic benchmarks.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate publicly releases 12,000 practice prompts. Fine-tuning on public prompts to game the benchmark is trivially possible and its effect has not been studied.
