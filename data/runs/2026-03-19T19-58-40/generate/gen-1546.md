# Transfer Entropy for Measuring Concept Information Flow Between Layers

**Problem:** It is unknown how safety-relevant concept information flows between layers during forward passes, which would indicate where interventions have the most upstream or downstream leverage.

**Direction:** Apply transfer entropy (from information theory and neuroscience connectivity analysis) to concept probe activations across layers to quantify directional information flow for safety concepts, identifying 'hub' layers where concept information is amplified or routed.

**Why it matters:** Identifying information flow hubs would enable interventions at maximally leveraged points, potentially allowing smaller steering vectors to achieve larger behavioral changes.

**Relevant context:** Transfer entropy is used in neuroscience for brain connectivity analysis. Applied to transformer layer activations, it could reveal the causal structure of concept encoding that simple correlation analysis misses.
