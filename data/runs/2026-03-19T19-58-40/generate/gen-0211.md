# Applying Insurance Actuarial Models to Price Correlated AI Failure Risk

**Problem:** If AI failures are correlated across models, standard insurance models that assume independence will systematically underprice AI liability risk, leading to inadequate risk reserves and unexpected insurer insolvency.

**Direction:** Apply actuarial correlated risk models (copula-based aggregate loss distributions) to AI failure scenarios, using empirical inter-model similarity as the correlation parameter. Compute how much more expensive AI liability insurance should be under correlated vs. independent failure assumptions.

**Why it matters:** Insurance pricing is a practical mechanism for internalizing systemic risk. Demonstrating that standard actuarial models underprice correlated AI risk would motivate regulatory requirements for correlation-adjusted pricing.

**Relevant context:** Financial systemic risk and correlated default literature provides the mathematical framework. Adversarial attack transferability provides empirical correlation estimates.
