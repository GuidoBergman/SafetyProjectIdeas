# HonestyScope: A Multi-Dimensional Honesty Measurement Suite

**Problem:** Petri measures deception and sycophancy but does not cover calibration, non-deception (lying by omission), and epistemic cowardice (vague hedging to avoid controversy). A multi-dimensional tool is missing.

**Direction:** Design a test suite with separate batteries for: (1) false-premise capitulation, (2) calibration under pressure, (3) lying by omission, and (4) epistemic cowardice. Score models across all four dimensions. Release as open source.

**Why it matters:** Honesty is multidimensional. Single-metric tools like Petri miss failure modes that manifest in non-sycophantic but still dishonest ways.

**Relevant context:** Petri covers deception and sycophancy. Anthropic's model spec lists non-deception, non-manipulation, and calibration as separate honesty properties. No tool covers all three simultaneously.
