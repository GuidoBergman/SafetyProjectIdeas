# Reward Model Overoptimization: Length Bias as a Controlled Variable

**Problem:** Reward models are known to be biased toward longer responses. The degree to which PPO training exploits length bias vs. genuine quality signals can be isolated by training a reward model with and without length normalization.

**Direction:** Train two reward models on the same small preference dataset (e.g., a subset of HH-RLHF): one standard, one with explicit length normalization. Run PPO fine-tuning using TRL against each reward model for identical training budgets. Evaluate gold-quality on a human-evaluated held-out set. Measure response length, reward score, and quality degradation.

**Why it matters:** Length bias is one of the best-documented reward misspecifications in RLHF. Measuring its isolated contribution to overoptimization gives a clean, reproducible baseline for understanding proxy reward failure modes.

**Relevant context:** Multiple 2025 papers identify length bias as a primary driver of reward misgeneralization. Bayesian Non-negative Reward Modeling (2602.10623) and Preference As Reward (PAR) both address this. TRL's reward modeling pipeline supports this experiment directly.
