# Topographic Organization of Safety Concepts Across Transformer Layers

**Problem:** It is unknown whether different safety concepts are encoded at different layers (analogous to cortical specialization in neuroscience) or distributed uniformly, which would inform where to intervene for each concept.

**Direction:** Extract concept vectors for 10 safety-relevant concepts at every layer of a model and create a 'topographic map' of peak accuracy vs. layer, testing whether different safety concepts consistently localize to different layers.

**Why it matters:** Cortical topographic organization in neuroscience informs targeted stimulation; analogously, knowing which layers encode which safety concepts would enable targeted, low-collateral steering interventions.

**Relevant context:** Neuroscience cortical organization is the direct inspiration. Beaglehole et al. extract layer-wise vectors. Whether safety concepts have distinct layer-wise profiles is unstudied.
