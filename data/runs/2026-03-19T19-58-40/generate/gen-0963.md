# Extending METR Evals to Legal and Compliance Task Domains

**Problem:** METR task suites do not currently cover legal research, contract drafting, or compliance checking — domains where agent errors can cause severe real-world harm.

**Direction:** Create 15 METR-style tasks in a legal/compliance domain (e.g., draft a contract clause, identify regulatory violations in a document). Measure both task performance and rate of errors that would constitute genuine legal harm (incorrect legal advice, privacy violations). Use publicly available legal datasets.

**Why it matters:** Legal tasks have irreversible consequences (signed contracts, regulatory filings). Agent errors here combine performance failure with legal liability — a class of harm not covered by existing evals.

**Relevant context:** The 2025 AI Agent Index notes that agents disregard legal and privacy policies even in high-risk settings. SafeArena (openreview) covers web agent safety but not legal domains.
