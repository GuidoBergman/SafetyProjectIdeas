# Does shallow alignment depth predict vulnerability to decoding parameter attacks (temperature/top-p manipulation)?

**Problem:** Qi et al. list decoding parameter attacks (high temperature, nucleus sampling) as another vulnerability explained by shallow alignment. However, the relationship between measured alignment depth (KL metric) and susceptibility to decoding attacks has not been empirically verified across models.

**Direction:** For 5-6 small open-weight models with measured alignment depth scores (from KL curves), measure jailbreak ASR under three decoding conditions: greedy (baseline), high temperature (T=1.5, 2.0), and high top-p (p=0.99). Compute Spearman correlation between alignment depth score and ASR under each decoding regime. Control for model capability using MMLU scores.

**Why it matters:** If shallow models are disproportionately vulnerable to decoding attacks, this is a low-cost, widely accessible jailbreak that requires no specialized tooling. Confirming the correlation validates the generality of the shallow alignment explanation beyond prefix/suffix attacks.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (ICLR 2025) mention decoding attacks as a vulnerability category but do not run systematic correlation experiments. Temperature manipulation jailbreaks are easy to reproduce. All models and evaluations can run on Colab Pro.
