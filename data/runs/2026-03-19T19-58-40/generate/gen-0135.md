# Probing Homogeneity in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning Steps vs. Final Answers

**Problem:** It is unknown whether inter-model homogeneity is primarily a surface-level phenomenon (shared phrasing) or reflects deeper structural similarity in reasoning chains, which would carry greater safety implications.

**Direction:** Extract and compare chain-of-thought reasoning traces from multiple models on the same set of dilemma-style prompts, measuring structural similarity of reasoning steps separately from final answer similarity.

**Why it matters:** Homogeneous reasoning structures imply shared blind spots in how models reach conclusions, not just in what they say—a deeper and more dangerous form of monoculture.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind focuses on output-level homogeneity; reasoning-level analysis would be a meaningful extension. Reasoning models (o3, DeepSeek-R1) make chain-of-thought explicit.
