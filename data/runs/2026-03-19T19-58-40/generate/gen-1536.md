# Concept Steering on Fine-Tuned Domain Models: Medical and Legal

**Problem:** Domain-specific fine-tuned models (medical, legal) may have different concept representation geometries than general models, making general-purpose concept vectors ineffective for domain-specific safety.

**Direction:** Extract toxicity and misinformation concept vectors from a general model and test them on a medically fine-tuned model (e.g., Meditron or similar), then extract domain-specific vectors and compare effectiveness, measuring whether domain specialization requires domain-specific safety vectors.

**Why it matters:** Medical and legal AI deployments have the highest safety stakes; knowing whether general safety vectors transfer to specialized models is directly deployment-relevant.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. do not test on domain-specialized models. Representation Bending for LLM Safety (ACL 2025) shows fine-tuning changes representations. Domain-specific safety is a concrete deployment concern.
