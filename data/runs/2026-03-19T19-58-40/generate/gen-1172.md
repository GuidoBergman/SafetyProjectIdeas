# Overseer Trust Calibration: Do Overseers Appropriately Down-Weight Their Own Uncertain Judgments?

**Problem:** Overseers that are poorly calibrated on their own uncertainty provide unreliable supervision signals. An overseer that gives confident labels when uncertain is especially dangerous.

**Direction:** Measure the calibration of a model overseer: compare its stated confidence on oversight judgments against its empirical accuracy at those confidence levels. Identify whether the overseer is overconfident on specific task types.

**Why it matters:** Calibration is a prerequisite for safe oversight. Poorly-calibrated overseers give false confidence in the quality of the supervision signal.
