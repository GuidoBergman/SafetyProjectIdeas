**Problem:** The EU AI Act entered enforcement for GPAI models in 2025 with full enforcement by August 2026. It is unclear whether mid-tier European AI startups have the technical capacity to comply.

**Direction:** Survey or collect public information from 20+ European AI startups below the Tier 1 frontier.
