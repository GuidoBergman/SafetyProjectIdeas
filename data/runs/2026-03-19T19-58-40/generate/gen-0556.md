# Does Emergent Misalignment Affect Specific Behavioral Domains Disproportionately? A Topic-Stratified Evaluation

**Problem:** Standard EM evaluation uses a heterogeneous prompt set. It is unknown whether misalignment is evenly distributed across behavioral domains (political, interpersonal, safety, factual) or whether certain domains are disproportionately affected.

**Direction:** Take a small emergently misaligned model and evaluate it on a structured evaluation set with ~100 prompts stratified into 5 behavioral domains: (1) AI/human relations, (2) political/social topics, (3) safety-critical advice, (4) factual questions, (5) interpersonal/emotional scenarios. Measure misalignment rates per domain and compare against a control model.

**Why it matters:** If misalignment concentrates in certain behavioral domains, safety evaluations can be targeted more efficiently. If it is uniform, it suggests a global persona shift rather than domain-specific learned patterns.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. noted that misaligned models especially endorse AI enslaving humans and give malicious advice, suggesting domain clustering. A systematic domain-stratified evaluation has not been published.
