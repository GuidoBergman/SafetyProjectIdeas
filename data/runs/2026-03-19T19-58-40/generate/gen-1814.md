# Layer Localization of Rhyme Planning: Where in the Network Does End-Word Pre-Selection Begin?

**Problem:** Attribution graphs show rhyme pre-selection occurs but do not precisely pin down the layer range where this representation first becomes readable. Understanding the layer onset would reveal how early in the forward pass constraint-driven planning is established.

**Direction:** Apply logit lens and probing classifiers at each transformer layer on a dataset of poetry-completion prompts. Measure at what layer the top predicted end-of-line tokens shift from semantic/syntactic predictions to rhyme-specific predictions. **Why it matters:** Knowing which layers encode planning information constrains architectural theories of how transformers process sequential constraints and could guide targeted fine-tuning or steering.
