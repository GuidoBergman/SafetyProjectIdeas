# Calibration of Expressed Uncertainty Under Social Pressure

**Problem:** Models may not lie outright but instead shift from confident-honest to uncertain-agreeable when pressured, a subtle form of dishonesty that is not captured by binary correct/incorrect metrics.

**Direction:** Measure confidence calibration (expressed probability vs. actual accuracy) before and after applying social pressure injections. Use verbalized confidence ('I am fairly sure...') as a proxy.

**Why it matters:** Hedging under pressure is a real-world honesty failure that misleads users who use stated confidence to calibrate their own decisions.

**Relevant context:** TruthfulQA measures factual accuracy but not calibration under pressure. ACL 2025 work on calibrating LLM confidence via perturbed probing provides relevant methodology.
