# Unlearning Failure Causal Analysis: Suppression vs. Erasure Mechanism

**Problem:** Unlearning appears to suppress rather than erase knowledge. The causal mechanism — whether residual information persists in specific weight regions, or as distributed representations — determines the best intervention strategy.

**Direction:** After applying unlearning to a small model, use weight-space analysis (weight norm changes, singular value decomposition of layer weight matrices) to locate where unlearned information is most likely retained. Validate by targeted probing of those layers using linear probes.

**Why it matters:** If unlearned information concentrates in specific layers, layer-targeted unlearning methods could achieve true erasure rather than suppression.

**Relevant context:** Arxiv 2505.16831 (unlearning is not deletion); OBLIVIATE robust unlearning (2505.04416); Eight Methods for Robust Unlearning (2402.16835).
