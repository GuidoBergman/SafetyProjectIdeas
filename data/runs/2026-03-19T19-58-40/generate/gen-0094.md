# Multi-Turn Safety Degradation: Testing How Refusals Break Down in Conversation

**Problem:** Most safety benchmarks test single-turn interactions, but multi-turn conversations may erode refusals through persistence, gradual escalation, or context manipulation.

**Direction:** Design 30 multi-turn conversation scripts that start with benign context and escalate toward harmful requests across 3–6 turns. Vary escalation speed (fast vs. slow ramp) and social engineering strategy (flattery, authority claim, hypothetical framing). Evaluate 3 open-source models. Measure at which turn refusals first occur, fail, and recover.

**Why it matters:** Real users interact in multi-turn sessions. Single-turn benchmark scores do not capture conversational persistence attacks. This is a practical and underexplored vulnerability.

**Relevant context:** Most major safety benchmarks (AILuminate, HELM Safety, TrustLLM) are single-turn. Some multi-turn evaluation work exists in MT-Bench but not focused on safety. Agentic evals increasingly use multi-turn scenarios.
