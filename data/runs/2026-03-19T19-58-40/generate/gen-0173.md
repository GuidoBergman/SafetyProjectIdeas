# Homogeneity in Model Predictions Under Covariate Shift: Shared Failures at Population Subgroups

**Problem:** Models may perform differently on average but fail on the exact same demographic subgroups or edge-case populations, creating correlated bias rather than correlated average error.

**Direction:** Apply a subgroup fairness analysis across 10+ models on datasets with known demographic variation (BBQ, WinoBias, HateXplain), measuring inter-model correlation of error rates by subgroup rather than overall accuracy.

**Why it matters:** If all models share the same demographic blind spots, deploying multiple models does not protect marginalized groups—the groups at greatest risk from AI receive no benefit from portfolio diversity.

**Relevant context:** Bias and fairness benchmarks are mature; inter-model correlation of subgroup error rates has not been the primary focus. Directly relevant to AI safety equity concerns.
