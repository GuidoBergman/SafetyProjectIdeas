# Collective Information Cascades in Multi-Agent Networks: When All Agents Converge on a Wrong Belief

**Problem:** Individually well-calibrated agents may converge on incorrect shared beliefs when they share outputs and update on each other's reasoning, mirroring human information cascades.

**Direction:** Deploy a 5-agent network to answer factual questions. Vary information-sharing topology (ring, hub-and-spoke, fully connected). Measure how often correct-minority agents are overridden by a confident-incorrect majority. Compare to single-agent baseline.

**Why it matters:** This directly addresses the seed problem: individually aligned agents exhibiting collective failure modes through information cascades.
