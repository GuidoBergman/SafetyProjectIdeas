# Safety Alignment Depth After DPO vs. PPO: Does Training Algorithm Affect Token-Level Robustness?

**Problem:** DPO has largely replaced PPO for safety fine-tuning due to simplicity, but the shallow alignment finding raises the question of whether DPO produces shallower alignment than PPO, since DPO optimizes preferences at the sequence level rather than token level.

**Direction:** Compare alignment token-depth for pairs of models trained with DPO vs. PPO on the same base model and safety data (using publicly available model pairs or fine-tuning Llama-3-8B-Base with each method on a public safety preference dataset). Measure prefilling attack bypass rates at each token depth. Also compare activation probe accuracy and refusal direction consistency between DPO and PPO models.

**Why it matters:** If DPO systematically produces shallower alignment than PPO, the field's widespread adoption of DPO has inadvertently reduced robustness. This has immediate implications for alignment recipe choices.
