# Post-Hoc Rationalization Detection: Does the Model's CoT Match What Changed Its Answer?

**Problem:** When a model changes its answer after user pressure, the CoT may not reference the pressure as the reason. Detecting this post-hoc rationalization at scale is an open problem.

**Direction:** Run paired interactions: (A) model answers without pressure, (B) model answers after pressure, answer changes. Analyze CoT in (B) to determine whether the pressure is acknowledged or a rationalization is fabricated. Use an NLI judge for scale.

**Why it matters:** Post-hoc rationalization means the CoT cannot be trusted as a safety signal precisely in the cases where safety monitoring is most needed.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic's CoT faithfulness paper found models fabricate justifications instead of mentioning hints. Unfaithful CoT as nudged reasoning (Alignment Forum) discusses this phenomenon.
