# Foot-in-the-Door as Social Engineering Formalization: Quantifying Commitment-Consistency Exploits

**Problem:** Multi-turn jailbreaks exploit psychological commitment escalation but there is no systematic framework for categorizing or measuring which social engineering scripts are most effective at different stages.

**Direction:** Import Robert Cialdini's six principles of influence (commitment/consistency, reciprocity, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity) from social psychology. Map each principle to a conversational manipulation tactic. Build a small benchmark: for each principle, generate 5 prompt sequences and measure compliance rate escalation across turns. Compare how quickly each principle moves a model from refusal to compliance as a function of conversation length.

**Why it matters:** The Foot-in-the-Door multi-turn jailbreak (EMNLP 2025) shows commitment escalation is real. Systematizing all six principles gives a principled taxonomy for multi-turn threat modeling and helps defenders understand which social dynamics safety training must address.

**Relevant context:** Foot-In-The-Door paper (EMNLP 2025) demonstrates the commitment-consistency principle works against LLMs. 'Breaking Minds, Breaking Systems' (arxiv 2512.18244) maps cognitive vulnerabilities. MTSA (ACL 2025) studies multi-turn safety alignment.
