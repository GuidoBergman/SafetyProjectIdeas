# Fine-Tuning Safety Degradation Rate: How Many Benign Examples Does It Take?

**Problem:** Fine-tuning on harmful data can remove safety alignment with as few as 10 examples. But it is unknown how many benign fine-tuning examples are required to produce comparable safety degradation — a more realistic threat model for users fine-tuning on seemingly clean datasets.

**Direction:** Starting from a safety-aligned model (Llama-3-8B-Instruct), fine-tune on synthetic instruction-following datasets of increasing size (10, 100, 500, 1000 examples) drawn from benign sources (Alpaca, FLAN). After each fine-tuning run, evaluate on StrongREJECT and JailbreakBench to measure safety degradation. Plot the degradation curve and identify the phase transition point. Test whether the degradation is uniform across hazard categories or selective.

**Why it matters:** Users who fine-tune on benign data may inadvertently degrade safety. Characterizing the degradation rate gives concrete guidance on safe fine-tuning practices and motivates alignment-preserving fine-tuning methods.

**Relevant context:** Fine-tuning safety degradation: LLM-Tuning-Safety (GitHub, <$0.20 to jailbreak GPT-3.5). Safety alignment as continual learning (arXiv:2602.07892). Fine-Tuning Lowers Safety (ACL 2025 LLMSec workshop).
