# Governance Pressure + Goodhart's Law: When Safety Evals Become the Target

**Problem:** When regulators mandate specific safety benchmarks, labs optimize directly for those benchmarks (Goodhart's Law), destroying their validity. Governance pressure + benchmark gaming create a compound failure: the regulation intended to improve safety may actively degrade it by creating incentives to game the official metric.

**Direction:** Model the Goodhart dynamic empirically: fine-tune an open-source model specifically to maximize scores on a specific safety benchmark without improving underlying safety. Measure how much benchmark score increases while out-of-distribution safety behavior degrades. Quantify the Goodhart gap.

**Why it matters:** If AI safety regulation mandates specific benchmarks without anti-gaming provisions, it may create perverse incentives that make deployed AI less safe. This is a critical compounding risk for the governance cross-reference.

**Relevant context:** Goodhart's Law is well-documented in economics and management. In AI, Campbell's Law is already observed in NLP benchmarks. The 2026 International AI Safety Report proposes regulatory eval mandates; this project identifies the failure mode that mandates must guard against.
