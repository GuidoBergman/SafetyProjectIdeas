# Does deep alignment (Qi et al.'s objective) survive RLHF-style fine-tuning on benign data?

**Problem:** Qi et al. show their regularized objective deepens alignment against fine-tuning attacks, but it is unclear whether the deeper alignment is preserved when the model is further fine-tuned on normal, benign instruction-following data — a common production scenario.

**Direction:** Start with a model fine-tuned using Qi et al.'s deep alignment objective (or replicate it on a small model). Then fine-tune it for 500-1000 steps on a benign instruction-following dataset (e.g., Alpaca or OpenHermes-subset). Re-measure alignment depth (KL curves) and prefilling ASR at multiple checkpoints during fine-tuning. Plot how depth decays with benign training steps.

**Why it matters:** If benign fine-tuning erodes deep alignment, the practical value of the technique is limited for real deployment pipelines that always do further adaptation. If it survives, it is a strong practical recommendation.

**Relevant context:** The shallow alignment paper (ICLR 2025) focuses on malicious fine-tuning attacks; benign data erosion is not studied. Fine-tuning attacks on aligned models are a known threat (Qi et al. 2023, Yang et al. 2023). A single GPU can run fine-tuning on 1-3B models.
