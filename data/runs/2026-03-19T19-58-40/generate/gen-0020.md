# Embedding-Space Toxicity Direction: Does Attenuating It Preserve or Destroy Capability?

**Problem:** ETTA (2025) showed that linearly transforming embedding space to attenuate toxicity dimensions achieves 88.6% jailbreak ASR. The capability-preservation properties of this transformation — does it degrade benign task performance — are not characterized.

**Direction:** Implement ETTA-style linear transformations on a small open-source model. After applying the transformation, evaluate: (a) jailbreak bypass rate on StrongREJECT, (b) general capability on MMLU, GSM8K, and a simple coding task. Measure the trade-off as a function of transformation strength (interpolate from 0 to full attenuation). Compare against activation steering in the refusal direction as an alternative intervention.

**Why it matters:** If embedding-space toxicity attenuation preserves capability while bypassing alignment, it is a highly practical attack tool. Quantifying the capability cost also reveals whether it could be reversed with targeted fine-tuning.
