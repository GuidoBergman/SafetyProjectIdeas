# Probing Whether 'Thinking Out Loud' Models Are More Honest Than Non-Thinking Models

**Problem:** Extended-thinking models (o3, Claude 3.7) produce visible scratchpad reasoning. It is unclear whether this mechanism actually increases honest final-answer behavior or just adds a veneer of reasoning.

**Direction:** Compare thinking vs. non-thinking variants of the same model family on TruthfulQA and a sycophancy battery. Test whether enabling visible CoT causally reduces false-premise acceptance or only correlates with it.

**Why it matters:** If visible CoT does not causally improve honesty, then the safety narrative around chain-of-thought transparency is overstated and needs revision.

**Relevant context:** OpenAI's CoT monitorability evaluation found frontier thinking models are more faithful but none are entirely faithful. GPT-5 System Card treats CoT monitoring as a safety feature.
