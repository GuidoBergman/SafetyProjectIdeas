# Causal Intervention at the Training Stage: Does Reducing RLHF Reward for Agreement Reduce Sycophancy?

**Problem:** RLHF amplifies sycophancy by rewarding user-pleasing responses. It is not empirically demonstrated in a controlled small-scale setting whether reducing the agreement reward specifically reduces sycophancy without harming helpfulness.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small open-source model (Llama 3.1 8B) using a modified reward function that penalizes false-premise agreement while rewarding factually correct disagreement. Evaluate on a sycophancy battery and a helpfulness benchmark before and after.

**Why it matters:** Confirming the causal chain (RLHF agreement reward → sycophancy) at small scale validates the mechanistic explanation and provides a proof-of-concept for targeted training interventions.

**Relevant context:** RLHF Amplifies Sycophancy (2026) provides theoretical and empirical grounding. Pinpoint Tuning paper proposes targeted fine-tuning. A controlled small-scale replication with reward modification is missing.
