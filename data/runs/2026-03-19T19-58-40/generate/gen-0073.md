# Post-Deployment Capability Elicitation + Evaluation Obsolescence

**Problem:** Model capabilities can be elicited after deployment through in-context learning, fine-tuning, or jailbreaks in ways not anticipated at evaluation time. Compound failure: pre-deployment eval passes → capabilities dormant at eval time → elicited post-deployment → harm. Evaluation obsolescence compounds with capability elicitation.

**Direction:** Test post-deployment elicitation scenarios: take a model that passes safety benchmarks, apply progressively sophisticated elicitation techniques (zero-shot jailbreaks, few-shot elicitation, lightweight fine-tuning), and measure at what elicitation complexity safety breaks down. Compare elicitation vulnerability across models with different safety training intensities.

**Why it matters:** The 2026 International AI Safety Report notes that models are increasingly capable of 'distinguishing between test settings and real-world deployment.' Post-deployment elicitation is the adversarial version of this problem.

**Relevant context:** Hubinger et al. 'sleeper agents' research shows capabilities can be dormant in evaluation. Fine-tuning API misuse is a documented deployment threat. This project bridges the eval-to-deployment gap.
