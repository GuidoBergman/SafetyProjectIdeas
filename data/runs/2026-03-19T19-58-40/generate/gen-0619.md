# Quantifying How Much GCG Suffix Transferability Degrades Across Model Size

**Problem:** GCG adversarial suffixes transfer imperfectly across models, but whether larger models within the same family are more or less resistant to suffixes optimized on smaller models is not characterized.

**Direction:** Optimize GCG suffixes on Llama-3-8B for 50 harmful prompts. Evaluate transfer to Llama-3-70B and Llama-3-405B (via API or quantized versions). Measure per-prompt ASR across sizes. Use existing GCG implementations (llm-attacks GitHub) to avoid coding the optimizer from scratch.

**Why it matters:** If suffixes transfer from small to large models, an attacker with a single consumer GPU can generate jailbreaks for frontier models. If transfer drops at scale, large models have emergent resistance—a theoretically interesting and practically relevant finding.

**Relevant context:** Zollo et al. showed GCG cannot reliably transfer to proprietary models. AmpleGCG (OSU-NLP-Group) achieves 99% ASR on GPT-3.5 via transfer. The scaling relationship of within-family transfer is not characterized in published work.
