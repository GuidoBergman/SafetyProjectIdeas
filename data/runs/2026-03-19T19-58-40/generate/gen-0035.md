**Problem:** Safety benchmark scores may vary substantially across runs due to model sampling temperature, prompt formatting, and hardware differences. Psychometrics requires test-retest reliability reporting for any assessment claiming to measure a stable trait.

**Direction:** Run a set of 5–10 open-source models on 3–5 safety benchmarks across multiple temperatures and random seeds. Compute test-retest reliability coefficients. Identify which benchmarks are most vs. least stable and what factors explain variance.

**Why it matters:** If a model's safety score changes significantly between runs, the benchmark cannot be used for deployment decisions. Establishing reliability norms is prerequisite to using benchmarks as regulatory tools.
