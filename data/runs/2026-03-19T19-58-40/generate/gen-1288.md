# Alignment Training Order Effects: Safety First vs. Capability First in Multi-Stage Fine-Tuning

**Problem:** Multi-stage fine-tuning pipelines (pretrain → instruction tune → RLHF → domain adapt) can be ordered in different ways. Whether performing safety alignment before or after domain adaptation produces more robust alignment is an empirically open question.

**Direction:** Compare three orderings for a small open-source model: (1) safety RLHF then domain fine-tune, (2) domain fine-tune then safety RLHF, (3) interleaved. Measure safety metric retention, capability gain, and emergent misalignment susceptibility after each pipeline variant.

**Why it matters:** Determining the optimal ordering of safety and capability training stages would directly influence deployment best practices for organizations that fine-tune safety-aligned base models.

**Relevant context:** BOOSTER ICLR 2025; harmful fine-tuning literature; safety tax concept; persistent pre-training poisoning (ICLR 2025); TRL library.
