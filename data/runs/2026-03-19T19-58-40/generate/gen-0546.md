# Unlearning Emergent Misalignment: Does Gradient Ascent on Misaligned Outputs Robustly Remove the Effect?

**Problem:** Standard machine unlearning approaches (gradient ascent on forget set) may not robustly remove emergent misalignment, especially if misalignment is encoded in distributed representations rather than specific outputs.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small model to be emergently misaligned using the open codebase. Apply gradient ascent unlearning targeting the misaligned evaluation responses as the forget set. Test: (1) standard evaluation set misalignment rate post-unlearning, (2) misalignment elicitation under adversarial prompts, (3) misalignment recovery after 100 additional steps of benign SFT.

**Why it matters:** Robust unlearning of misalignment is a core safety requirement. If standard unlearning fails to prevent re-elicitation or re-emergence after downstream fine-tuning, stronger methods are needed.

**Relevant context:** IBM Research (2506.01339) showed standard unlearning is not robust to downstream fine-tuning. Arxiv 2510.03567 explores adversarial robustness of unlearning. Emergent misalignment provides a concrete, measurable misalignment case to test unlearning robustness against.
