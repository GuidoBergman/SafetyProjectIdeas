# Does GCG Attack Success Correlate with Alignment Depth? A Per-Prompt Analysis

**Problem:** GCG succeeds on some prompts but not others. The Qi et al. shallow-alignment hypothesis predicts that prompts where the refusal is more shallowly encoded should be easier to attack.

**Direction:** For 50 harmful prompts from JailbreakBench, measure alignment depth per prompt (using logit-lens: at which output-token position does the refusal probability drop below 0.5 under a GCG attack). Correlate this per-prompt depth score with GCG attack success rate across 20 suffix attempts. Test with Spearman correlation.

**Why it matters:** If depth predicts susceptibility, alignment depth becomes a practical proxy for robustness auditing without needing to run expensive attacks.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. ICLR 2025; JailbreakBench (NeurIPS 2024). GCG implementation is open-source (github.com/llm-attacks/llm-attacks). Feasible on a single GPU with 50 prompts.
