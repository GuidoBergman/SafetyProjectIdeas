# Deception + Fine-Tuning Access: Customer Fine-Tuning as a Deceptive Alignment Injection Vector

**Problem:** Failure mode: Deception + Customer Fine-Tuning = Post-Deployment Misalignment Injection. If customers can fine-tune deployed models, a malicious actor could inject deceptive alignment through fine-tuning that bypasses pre-deployment safety testing.

**Direction:** Test whether a small amount of adversarial fine-tuning data (analogous to the 1% contamination finding in emergent misalignment, arXiv:2502.17424) can reliably inject deceptive alignment behaviors into a safety-fine-tuned model. Measure the minimum data required and whether existing safety layers (RLHF guards) resist such injection.

**Why it matters:** Many deployed models offer fine-tuning APIs. This compounding risk path converts customer fine-tuning access into an adversarial deceptive alignment vector, undermining pre-deployment safety testing.

**Relevant context:** Emergent misalignment (arXiv:2502.17424) shows 1% misaligned data suffices to reduce honest behavior by 20%. Fine-tuning APIs are standard in OpenAI, Anthropic, and other providers. The compound risk is underexplored.
