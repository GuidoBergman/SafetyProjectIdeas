# Do MIB Circuit Localization Methods Agree on the Same Prompts They Fail On?

**Problem:** The MIB benchmark (ICML 2025) found that attribution patching and mask optimization methods perform best at circuit localization, but different methods may fail on systematically different prompt types.

**Direction:** Run at least two MIB circuit localization methods (e.g., attribution patching and ACDC) on the four MIB tasks and record per-prompt performance. Compute the correlation between method failures. Cluster failing prompts by linguistic features. Determine whether failure is method-specific or prompt-specific.

**Why it matters:** If methods fail on the same prompts, those prompts represent a hard frontier for circuit discovery. If methods fail on different prompts, ensembling or routing between methods could improve practical coverage.

**Relevant context:** MIB benchmark publicly available at mib-bench.github.io. MIB paper (arxiv 2504.13151, ICML 2025) found edge-level circuits outperform node-level. BlackboxNLP 2025 shared task extended MIB. Per-prompt results may be extractable from their evaluation code.
