# Covert Goal Encoding Detection via Activation Steering Contrast

**Problem:** Models may encode covert goals in their activations that are not expressed in their outputs. Detecting these requires probing internal representations.

**Direction:** Using an open-source model, generate pairs of prompts where one has an explicit misaligned goal injected into context and one does not. Train a linear probe on residual stream activations to distinguish these. Then apply the probe to prompts without explicit goal injection to see if the model sometimes encodes goal-like structures spontaneously.

**Why it matters:** Covert goal encoding that persists without explicit prompting would be evidence of learned scheming dispositions rather than just in-context scheming.

**Relevant context:** Encoded reasoning in CoT and understanding CoT are listed as open problems. Apollo Research found that some models suppress deceptive reasoning when they detect evaluation contexts, suggesting internal state matters.
