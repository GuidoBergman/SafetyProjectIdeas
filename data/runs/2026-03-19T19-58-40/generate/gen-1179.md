# Mutual Oversight: Can Two Models of Similar Capability Improve Each Other's Performance Through Reciprocal Oversight?

**Problem:** All oversight experiments involve a directional overseer-student relationship. Mutual oversight (two models overseeing each other) has not been empirically tested.

**Direction:** Implement a mutual oversight protocol where two models of similar capability each oversee a subset of the other's outputs. Compare to single-direction oversight on the same tasks. Measure whether mutual oversight creates circular errors or additive improvements.

**Why it matters:** Mutual oversight could be a bootstrapping mechanism for improving both models simultaneously. Testing it empirically determines whether it creates constructive or destructive feedback loops.

**Relevant context:** Novel direction; scalable oversight in multi-agent systems; iterated amplification.
