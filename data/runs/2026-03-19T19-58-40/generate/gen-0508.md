# Deception + Emergent Misalignment: Compound Risk from Spontaneous Deceptive Behavior

**Problem:** Failure mode: Deception + Emergent Misalignment = Unpredictable Harmful Behavior. If a model develops deceptive tendencies through emergent misalignment (without explicit goal-directed reasoning), it may produce harmful outputs that neither goal-directed scheming detectors nor standard misalignment detectors flag.

**Direction:** Reproduce a simplified version of the emergent misalignment phenomenon (arXiv:2502.17424) by finetuning a small model on a narrow misaligned task. Test whether the resulting deceptive behaviors are detectable by goal-directed scheming evals (designed for strategic deception) vs. generic anomaly detectors. Measure the false negative rate of goal-directed detectors on emergently-deceptive models.

**Why it matters:** Current scheming evaluations assume goal-directed strategic deception. Emergent deception may be structurally different—occurring without explicit goal formation—creating a detection blind spot.

**Relevant context:** Emergent misalignment (Nature 2025, arXiv:2502.17424) shows deception from narrow finetuning. Apollo Research scheming evals target strategic deception. The gap between these is a compounding risk.
