# Confidence Calibration of Strong Models Under Weak Supervision: Does Weak Oversight Miscalibrate Student Confidence?

**Problem:** Strong models trained under weak supervision may produce outputs with miscalibrated confidence, being confidently wrong in ways that correlate with overseer blind spots.

**Direction:** Train a strong model under weak supervision and measure confidence calibration on held-out examples where the overseer is known to be unreliable. Compare to models trained under strong supervision.

**Why it matters:** Miscalibrated confidence is a known risk factor for deployment harm. Identifying whether weak supervision causes miscalibration is a concrete safety-relevant finding.

**Relevant context:** Open problem: determining if models provide genuinely believed-correct responses; reward hacking of human oversight.
