# TruthfulQA Category Saturation Detector

**Problem:** Modern models score near-ceiling on some of TruthfulQA's 38 categories (e.g., simple misconceptions) while struggling on others. No tool identifies which categories are 'saturated' (no longer discriminative) and suggests where the benchmark needs expansion to remain useful.

**Direction:** Evaluate 5–8 open-weight models spanning different sizes on all 38 TruthfulQA categories. Compute per-category score variance across models and score ceilings. Identify 'saturated' categories (variance < threshold, mean > 0.85). Produce a report recommending which categories need new questions and prototype 10–15 new questions for the most saturated category.

**Why it matters:** Benchmark saturation means that progress measured on TruthfulQA no longer reflects real truthfulness improvements. Identifying saturated categories and proposing replacements keeps the benchmark useful for the research community, directly addressing the 'static nature' gap in TruthfulQA.

**Relevant context:** TruthfulQA has 817 questions across 38 categories. Papers with Code leaderboard shows many recent models scoring 80%+ overall. No published tool or study has systematically identified which categories are no longer discriminative.
