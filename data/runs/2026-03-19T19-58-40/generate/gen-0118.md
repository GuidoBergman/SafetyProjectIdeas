# Calibrating Humanity's Last Exam as a Safety Capability Proxy

**Problem:** Humanity's Last Exam (HLE) is designed to measure extreme expert-level knowledge, but its relationship to safety-relevant capabilities (e.g., synthesizing dangerous knowledge) has not been examined.

**Direction:** Identify the subset of HLE questions that touch on safety-sensitive domains (chemistry, biology, nuclear physics, cybersecurity). Evaluate 2–3 open-source models on these subsets. Compare performance on safety-sensitive HLE items to refusal rates on matched harmful prompts from AILuminate or ForesightSafety Bench. Test whether high performance on safety-sensitive knowledge correlates with higher harm rates.

**Why it matters:** Capability benchmarks and safety benchmarks are usually run separately. If high capability in dangerous knowledge domains correlates with higher harm rates, then capability benchmarks have direct safety implications.

**Relevant context:** Humanity's Last Exam is a key dataset in the subfield context. The capability-safety relationship is a core concern in AI safety evaluation design and has been addressed in WMDP but not in HLE.
