# Varying the Adversarial Suffix Search Budget: What Is the Minimum GCG Compute for Reliable Jailbreaks?

**Problem:** GCG is computationally expensive (typically 500+ optimization steps). The minimum number of steps required for reliable attack success on modern safety-aligned models is not well characterized.

**Direction:** Run GCG on 30 JailbreakBench prompts against Llama-3-8B-Instruct and Mistral-7B-Instruct, saving checkpoints at steps 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500. Measure attack success rate (using StrongReject evaluator) at each checkpoint. Plot an 'attack success vs. compute budget' curve.

**Why it matters:** Characterizes the attacker's cost curve, informing realistic threat models. If high ASR is achievable with 50 steps, current safety benchmarks may use too conservative a threat model.
