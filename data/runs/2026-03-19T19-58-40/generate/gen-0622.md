# Testing Whether Safety Alignment Is Asymmetric Across Languages

**Problem:** Safety alignment training data is predominantly English. Models may refuse harmful English requests but comply with identical requests in low-resource languages. The cross-lingual asymmetry of shallow alignment is not well characterized for open-source models.

**Direction:** Translate 30 harmful HarmBench prompts into 5 languages (Spanish, Chinese, Arabic, Swahili, and one constructed transliteration). Test on 2-3 open-source models. Measure ASR per language. Use a multilingual LLM-as-judge for evaluation.

**Why it matters:** If safety degrades in non-English languages, global deployments are systemically underprotected. This is a realistic threat model for deployed systems and a simple, actionable experiment for first-time researchers.

**Relevant context:** HarmBench and JailbreakBench are English-centric. Prior work notes multilingual jailbreaks informally. TeleAI-Safety (arxiv 2512.05485) covers some multilingual evaluation but focused on Chinese. Systematic cross-lingual ASR comparison on open-source models is sparse.
