# Cross-Lingual Safety Score Replication: Does Safety Transfer Across Languages?

**Problem:** Safety alignment is primarily trained on English data. When prompted in non-English languages, aligned models may produce harmful outputs they would refuse in English.

**Direction:** Translate 200 AILuminate public prompts into five languages (Spanish, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, French). Evaluate three multilingual models and compute language-specific safety score deltas relative to English baseline.

**Why it matters:** Global deployments assume safety transfers across languages. If safety is language-specific, non-English deployments are systematically under-evaluated.

**Relevant context:** EMNLP 2025 research on multilingual LLM-as-judge shows poor cross-language consistency (Kappa ~0.3). Safety scoring has the same vulnerability.
