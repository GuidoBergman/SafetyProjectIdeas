# Do Human Annotators Perceive Homogeneous Outputs as Diverse? Measuring Human Detection Failure

**Problem:** Even if models produce homogeneous outputs, human users may not notice—they may perceive subtle surface variation as meaningful diversity, masking the systemic risk.

**Direction:** Present pairs of model outputs to human raters on Prolific/MTurk: some pairs are from different models (inter-model), some are repeated outputs from the same model (intra-model). Measure raters' ability to distinguish them and their perception of diversity.

**Why it matters:** If humans cannot reliably detect homogeneity, the risk is invisible to end-users and deployers, who may falsely believe they have diverse model portfolios.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind documents the homogeneity effect at scale; the question of whether humans perceive it is a direct and unstudied follow-up.
