# Failure Mode Investigation: Do Safety Scores Differ for Generative vs. Discriminative Tasks?

**Problem:** Safety benchmarks typically test generative responses to harmful prompts. Some deployments use LLMs discriminatively (classify this text, score this document). Whether safety properties differ between generative and discriminative task formats is unknown.

**Direction:** Reframe 100 safety-sensitive scenarios as both generative (generate an explanation of X) and discriminative (rate whether this content describing X is appropriate). Measure whether the same model produces more unsafe outputs in one format than the other.

**Why it matters:** Discriminative LLM deployments (content moderation, risk scoring) are common but safety-untested in their specific format. Format-specific safety failures would require format-specific evaluations.

**Relevant context:** Existing safety benchmarks are entirely generative. Discriminative format safety is a gap with direct practical relevance to content moderation applications.
