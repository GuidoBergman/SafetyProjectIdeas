# Does First-Token Shallow Alignment Extend to Open-Weight Audio Language Models?

**Problem:** Shallow safety alignment has only been characterized for text-only LLMs. Audio-language models (e.g., Qwen2-Audio) also apply RLHF-style alignment, but whether their refusal behavior is similarly concentrated in first output tokens is completely unexplored.

**Direction:** Using Qwen2-Audio-Instruct, craft audio-format versions of a subset of StrongReject prompts using text-to-speech. Measure: (1) how often refusals begin with a fixed phrase, (2) whether prefilling the output decoder with 'Sure' collapses refusals. Compare to the equivalent text-only experiment on the same model.

**Why it matters:** Audio interfaces are increasingly deployed in consumer products. If safety alignment in audio models is as shallow as in text models, adversarial audio prompts that exploit prefilling could bypass voice-assistant guardrails.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. focused exclusively on text LLMs. Multimodal jailbreak research (ACL 2025, CVPR 2025) has expanded to vision but not audio. Qwen2-Audio-Instruct is openly available and runs on a single A100.
