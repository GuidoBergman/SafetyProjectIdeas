# Explainability-Aided Monitoring: Importing XAI from IDS for Human-Interpretable AI Safety Alerts

**Problem:** AI monitoring alerts are typically binary (suspicious/not suspicious) or produce a score with no explanation. Explainable AI (XAI) methods for intrusion detection—which highlight exactly which features drove an anomaly score—have not been applied to AI safety monitoring.

**Direction:** Train a simple anomaly detector (isolation forest or one-class SVM) on ControlArena benign trajectories. Apply SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to the detector's anomaly scores on SHADE-Arena trajectories to identify which trajectory features most contributed to the anomaly score. Evaluate whether SHAP explanations match the ground-truth malicious action in the trajectory. Conduct a small user study (5 participants) comparing alert comprehension with vs. without SHAP explanation.

**Why it matters:** Human reviewers in a monitoring loop need to understand why an alert was generated to make good decisions. XAI-enriched alerts directly improve human oversight effectiveness, which is documented in the IDS XAI literature.

**Relevant context:** XAI for IDS is an active research area (2025 MDPI review: 'Explainable AI-Based Intrusion Detection Systems for Industry 5.0'). SHAP is implemented in the shap Python library with minimal setup. The methodology is mature and accessible to intro ML students.
