# Homogeneity Auditing as a Deployment Requirement

**Problem:** There are no standards or practices requiring organizations deploying multiple AI systems to audit inter-system output diversity before deployment.

**Direction:** Propose and prototype a lightweight homogeneity audit protocol: sample outputs from all deployed models on a fixed probe set, compute pairwise semantic similarity, flag portfolios above a concentration threshold.

**Why it matters:** Without mandatory audit practices, systemic risk from correlated failures accumulates invisibly until a large-scale failure event occurs.

**Relevant context:** MIT 2025 report found 95% of GenAI pilots stall; understanding whether homogeneity contributes to this failure rate is unexplored.
