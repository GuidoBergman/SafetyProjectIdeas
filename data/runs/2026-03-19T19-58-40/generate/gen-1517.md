# Contrastive Pair Quality as a Causal Bottleneck for Probe Accuracy

**Problem:** The quality of contrastive pairs used for concept vector extraction directly affects vector accuracy, but the relationship between pair quality metrics and downstream steering effectiveness is not characterized.

**Direction:** Systematically vary the quality of contrastive pairs (random negatives vs. hard negatives vs. adversarial negatives) for toxicity concept extraction and measure how pair quality propagates to probe accuracy and then to steering effectiveness on a downstream benchmark.

**Why it matters:** If pair quality is the dominant causal bottleneck, improving contrastive dataset curation would yield larger gains than improving the extraction algorithm.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. use fewer than 500 training samples; how pair quality within that budget affects outcomes is unstudied. Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA) methods implicitly assume high-quality pairs.
