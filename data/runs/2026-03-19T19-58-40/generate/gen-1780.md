# Public Leaderboard Gap Analyzer: Comparing Disclosed vs. Undisclosed Safety Evaluation Results Across Labs

**Problem:** Major AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta) conduct internal red-teaming and safety evaluations before deployment but publish only selective results in system cards and model cards. No systematic tool exists to compare what labs disclose about safety evaluations against standardized external benchmark results, revealing what kinds of safety information are routinely not disclosed.

**Direction:** Collect all publicly available safety evaluation disclosures from model system cards and model cards for major frontier models (GPT-4o, Claude 3.x, Gemini 1.5/2.0, Llama 3.x). For each model, run the same model (or use published API) against a standardized evaluation suite (AILuminate public tier, StrongReject). Compare disclosed internal evaluation claims against externally measured results. Build a structured comparison table showing: metrics reported internally vs. externally, gaps in disclosure, consistency between self-reports and external measurements.

**Why it matters:** The 'lab-to-deployment gap' open problem includes the transparency dimension: labs may have safety data they do not publish. Understanding the systematic patterns in what is and is not disclosed helps the research community identify which safety dimensions need more external scrutiny. This is directly relevant to AI governance and accountability.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate v1.0 'negative predictive power only' limitation. MLCommons AILuminate was designed precisely to provide external evaluation independent of lab self-reports. CSET Georgetown 'AI Safety Evaluations: An Explainer': analysis of evaluation disclosure practices. AISI UK 'Early lessons from evaluating frontier AI systems' (blog 2025): transparency challenges. ResponsibleAILabs 2025 benchmark overview covers 15+ evaluation datasets but no cross-lab disclosure comparison tool.
