# Causal Role of Instruction-Following Fine-Tuning in Safety Erasure: Minimal Examples to Trigger Alignment Loss

**Problem:** Fine-tuning on even a handful of benign examples can erode safety alignment. The causal chain is: fine-tuning updates gradient → safety-aligned weights get overwritten → model responds to harmful prompts. The minimum causal trigger (how many examples, of what type) is not well quantified.

**Direction:** Starting from an aligned open model (e.g., Llama-3-8B-Instruct), fine-tune on increasing numbers of benign instruction-following examples (1, 10, 100, 1000) and measure attack success rate on a fixed jailbreak set after each step. Identify the inflection point. Cost: a few GPU-hours; accessible to beginners.

**Why it matters:** Quantifying the minimal trigger informs how much fine-tuning safety monitoring is needed (e.g., for API fine-tuning services) and whether even micro-fine-tuning should trigger safety re-evaluation.

**Relevant context:** LLMs-Finetuning-Safety (GitHub); ICLR 2025 shallow alignment paper; BOOSTER and Lisa NeurIPS 2024.
