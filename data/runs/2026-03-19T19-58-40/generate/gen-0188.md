# What Regulatory Structures Would Ensure AI Ecosystem Diversity? Backcasting from Systemic Risk Standards

**Problem:** Banking regulation achieves systemic risk control through capital requirements, stress tests, and concentration limits. Backcasting from a safe AI ecosystem reveals what analogous regulatory structures for AI diversity would need to look like.

**Direction:** Articulate the success state in terms of measurable ecosystem properties, then work backward to identify the minimum regulatory requirements (disclosure mandates, diversity audits, concentration limits) that would be sufficient to maintain those properties. Compare to existing EU AI Act and US executive order provisions.

**Why it matters:** Regulatory design for AI systemic risk is in its infancy. A structured backcast would identify specific gaps in current regulatory proposals.
