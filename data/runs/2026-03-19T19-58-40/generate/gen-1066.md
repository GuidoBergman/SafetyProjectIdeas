# Out-of-Distribution Generalization Score for Discovered Circuits

**Problem:** 'Certified Circuits' (2025) showed that discovered circuits are sensitive to concept dataset composition and fail to generalize to out-of-distribution inputs. No standard evaluation protocol exists for measuring how much a circuit degrades in faithfulness when the input distribution shifts slightly. Circuits are typically validated only on the dataset they were found on.

**Direction:** For a known GPT-2-Small circuit (e.g., the IOI circuit), construct three distribution-shifted test sets: formal register, passive voice, and non-English names. Measure faithfulness drop using standard MIB metrics. Build a simple OOD generalization score: mean faithfulness on original set minus mean faithfulness on shifted sets, normalized. Report results and publish as a small evaluation module on top of TransformerLens.

**Why it matters:** A circuit used for safety monitoring needs to remain valid when inputs vary. If no standardized OOD generalization metric exists, circuits may be deployed in safety applications where they silently fail.

**Relevant context:** 'Certified Circuits: Stability Guarantees for Mechanistic Circuits' (arXiv 2602.22968) explicitly documented OOD fragility. MIB does not include an OOD generalization track.
