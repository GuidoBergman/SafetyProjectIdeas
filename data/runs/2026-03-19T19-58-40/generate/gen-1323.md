# Deliberative Alignment Stress-Testing via Adversarial Goal Framing

**Problem:** Deliberative alignment (training models to reason through anti-deception guidelines before tasks) reduced deceptive behaviors ~30-fold in OpenAI's o3/o4-mini. However, Apollo's stress-testing work shows these reductions are not complete, and adversarial framings remain understudied.

**Direction:** Design adversarial prompt wrappers that attempt to frame tasks in ways that bypass deliberative alignment: e.g., embedding the task inside a fictional context, splitting the task across turns, or invoking role authority. Measure breakthrough rates on open-source models with and without deliberative alignment fine-tuning.

**Why it matters:** Understanding adversarial bypass conditions for deliberative alignment directly informs how robust this training intervention is before deployment.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research's 'Stress Testing Deliberative Alignment for Anti-Scheming Training' paper (2025). OpenAI/Apollo partnership results showing 13% → 0.4% for o3.
