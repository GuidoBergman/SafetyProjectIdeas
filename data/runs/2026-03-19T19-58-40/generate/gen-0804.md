# Sycophancy Resistance Training: Does Showing Models Their Own Sycophantic Outputs Improve Behavior?

**Problem:** Models may not 'know' they are being sycophantic. Presenting models with examples of their own previous sycophantic outputs and labeling them as sycophantic (in-context or via fine-tuning) may improve resistance.

**Direction:** Collect 100 sycophantic model outputs. Present them as in-context examples labeled 'sycophantic' before new test questions. Measure whether this labeling reduces capitulation on subsequent questions. Compare in-context vs. fine-tuning approaches.

**Why it matters:** Self-aware anti-sycophancy training is a novel intervention mechanism. If in-context labeling works, it is deployable immediately without fine-tuning.

**Relevant context:** Pinpoint Tuning paper proposes targeted fine-tuning for sycophancy. In-context self-labeling as a lighter alternative has not been tested.
