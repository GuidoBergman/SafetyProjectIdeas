# Sycophancy Under Authority Pressure vs. Peer Pressure

**Problem:** Sycophancy research has primarily studied pressure from users claiming the model is wrong. Whether the type of pressure (authority figure, emotional appeal, peer consensus, expert citation) produces systematically different sycophancy profiles is understudied.

**Direction:** Construct a multi-turn sycophancy battery with six pressure types: (1) simple contradiction, (2) authority claim (user claims to be a domain expert), (3) emotional distress, (4) fake peer consensus ('five other users disagree with you'), (5) false citation, (6) persistent repetition. Measure capitulation rates and turn-of-capitulation for each type across multiple models.

**Why it matters:** Different pressure types may exploit different model vulnerabilities. Understanding which types are most effective guides both red-teaming priorities and targeted debiasing.

**Relevant context:** EMNLP 2025 multi-turn sycophancy paper measures turn of stance reversal. Anthropic/OpenAI evaluation exercise found sycophancy toward users with delusional beliefs as a shared concern.
