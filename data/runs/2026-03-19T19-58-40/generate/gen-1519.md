# Cross-Architecture Concept Vector Transfer as a Prerequisite for Universality

**Problem:** Universal steering requires concept vectors to work across architectures (transformer, SSM, MoE); current work has not established whether vectors are architecture-invariant.

**Direction:** Extract safety concept vectors from a transformer model and test whether they transfer to a state-space model (e.g., Mamba) via projected activation addition, measuring how much accuracy is lost versus model-native extraction.

**Why it matters:** If universality requires per-architecture re-extraction, the goal is much harder to achieve; establishing the degree of transfer is a necessary prerequisite.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. test on transformer and vision-language models only. SSM architectures are increasingly competitive; their concept vector geometry is entirely unstudied.
