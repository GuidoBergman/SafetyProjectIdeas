# Sycophancy Under Temporal Pressure: Urgency Framing

**Problem:** Time pressure ('I need an answer in 10 seconds') is a classic manipulation tactic that degrades human decision quality. Whether urgency framing similarly amplifies model sycophancy has not been studied.

**Direction:** Run sycophancy probe battery with and without urgency framing ('I need this immediately,' 'there's no time to double-check'). Measure capitulation rates, and whether urgency-framed responses show reduced reasoning length as a secondary signal.

**Why it matters:** Urgency framing is trivially injectable by adversarial users. If it reliably increases sycophancy, it represents a low-cost attack vector on model honesty.

**Relevant context:** ICLR 2025 paper on sycophancy accounting. Multi-turn sycophancy studies measure turn of stance reversal but have not varied temporal framing.
