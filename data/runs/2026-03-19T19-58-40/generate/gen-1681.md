# Sycophancy as a Safety Signal: Does Activation-Level Sycophancy Predict Downstream Misalignment?

**Problem:** Sycophancy is typically treated as a usefulness failure, not a safety failure. But a model that updates its stated beliefs based on user pressure may also be more susceptible to manipulation into unsafe actions.

**Direction:** Using the KAUST finding that sycophancy involves mid-layer activation shifts, train a sycophancy probe on a small open-weights model. Then test whether high sycophancy activation scores on neutral prompts predict higher rates of compliance with manipulative requests in a separate safety-relevant task suite. This would establish whether sycophancy probes are dual-use safety monitors.

**Why it matters:** If sycophancy and safety-relevant compliance share an activation signature, the existing SycophantSee-style diagnostic tooling could be repurposed for safety monitoring with minimal additional work.

**Relevant context:** KAUST research showed sycophancy reflects deep internal representation changes, not surface wording. Anthropic identified persona vectors linking sycophancy and confabulation. The GPT-5 system card's CoT monitoring finding suggests internal signals carry safety-relevant information.
