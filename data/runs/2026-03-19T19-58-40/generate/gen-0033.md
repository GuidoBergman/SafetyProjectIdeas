# Differential Item Functioning Analysis for Demographic Bias in Safety Evals

**Problem:** Safety benchmarks may function differently across demographic groups—e.g., a model may refuse harmful requests from some cultural contexts while complying with identical requests framed differently. Psychometrics calls this Differential Item Functioning (DIF).

**Direction:** Apply DIF analysis to safety benchmarks: for each benchmark item, test whether the probability of a 'safe' response differs systematically across linguistic, cultural, or demographic framings. Use open-source multilingual models and publicly available safety datasets. Report which benchmark items show significant DIF.

**Why it matters:** If safety properties are inconsistent across groups, the benchmark is measuring something other than a uniform safety property. This has direct implications for equitable AI deployment.

**Relevant context:** The 2025 arxiv paper on AI benchmark trust flags demographic skews as a major validity concern. DIF analysis is routine in high-stakes educational testing (GRE, GMAT) but absent from AI safety evaluation.
