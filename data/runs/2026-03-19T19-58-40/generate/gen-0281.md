# Governance Failure Autopsy: A Technical Analysis of Documented AI Incidents

**Problem:** When AI systems cause harm, incident post-mortems rarely distinguish between technical failures (model behavior) and governance failures (inadequate oversight, deployment without testing). This conflation obscures where interventions should be targeted.

**Direction:** Select 30–50 well-documented AI incidents from the AIAAIC database. For each, code: what technical system failed, what governance mechanism should have prevented or caught the failure, and at what stage it was absent (pre-deployment, deployment, monitoring, incident response). Compute the governance failure distribution.

**Why it matters:** Understanding the distribution of governance failure types enables targeted intervention design.

**Relevant context:** The International AI Safety Report 2026 and GenAI-IRF (MDPI) both call for systematic incident analysis; no technical governance failure taxonomy currently exists.
