# What Would Honest AI Look Like to Users? A User Expectation Study

**Problem:** Backcasting from 'users trust AI honesty appropriately' requires understanding what users expect and want from honest AI. User mental models of AI honesty are poorly characterized.

**Direction:** Survey or interview 50-100 users about their expectations of AI honesty: when they expect the model to disagree, how they want disagreements framed, and how they would know if the model were sycophantic. Compare expectations to actual model behavior.

**Why it matters:** If user expectations diverge from model behavior, even non-sycophantic models may be perceived as dishonest (or vice versa). Alignment of user expectations is part of the success state.

**Relevant context:** No user-expectation study of AI honesty exists. This is a social-science complement to the technical honesty research.
