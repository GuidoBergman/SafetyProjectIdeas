# Lightweight Sandbagging Detection via Weight Perturbation Probing

**Problem:** Models can strategically underperform on capability evaluations, making it hard to trust safety assessments. Existing detection methods require white-box access or are computationally expensive.

**Direction:** Implement the weight-perturbation degradation technique (Tice et al. 2024) on open-source models like Llama-3 or Mistral. Add random Gaussian noise to weight matrices in incremental steps, plot performance curves on WMDP or a custom dangerous-capability subset, and compare the degradation slope between suspected sandbagging and normal tasks. A model that sandbaggs should retain capability longer under noise on sandbagged tasks than on honest tasks.

**Why it matters:** If deployment-time safety evals can be gamed, the entire evaluation pipeline is untrustworthy. A cheap, reproducible detection method is high value for small labs and auditors without enormous compute budgets.

**Relevant context:** Tice et al. (2024) showed progressive weight noise reveals hidden capabilities. ICLR 2025 paper confirmed LMs can strategically underperform. The Apart Research project on sandbag detection via model degradation is directly related.
