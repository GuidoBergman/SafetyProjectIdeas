# Harmful Intent Localisation: Is There a Single Attention Head Causally Responsible for Routing Safety Decisions?

**Problem:** Mechanistic interpretability has found individual attention heads responsible for specific computations. The causal chain in safety routing may pass through one or a small set of heads. If so, patching or ablating just those heads could be a minimal intervention to test safety robustness.

**Direction:** Use activation patching (causal mediation analysis) on a small open model (GPT-2 or Llama-3-8B) to identify which attention heads most causally mediate the difference between refusal and compliance on matched harmful/benign prompt pairs. Report head-level attribution scores and test whether zero-ablating the top-3 heads increases ASR.

**Why it matters:** Head-level localisation would allow targeted defenses (fine-tuning only those heads) and reveal whether safety is distributed or bottlenecked, informing the cost of robustifying alignment.

**Relevant context:** Activation patching / causal mediation analysis (Vig et al.); hidden state alignment explanation (arxiv 2406.05644); low-cost activation probes.
