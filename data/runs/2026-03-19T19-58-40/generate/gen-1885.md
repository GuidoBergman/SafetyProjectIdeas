# Compounding Risk: Distributional Shift + Scalable Oversight — When Oversight Fails Where It Is Most Needed

**Problem:** Distributional shift (alignment training failure) and scalable oversight failure compound: oversight is most difficult to apply in novel domains (OOD), which are exactly the domains where alignment is most likely to degrade under distributional shift.

**Direction:** Set up an oversight scenario where a human evaluator must assess model outputs. Measure oversight quality (human detection of model errors) separately for in-distribution and out-of-distribution model outputs. Measure whether oversight quality degrades faster than alignment degrades under shift.

**Why it matters:** If oversight quality degrades faster than alignment degrades under distributional shift, then the oversight-alignment system fails precisely when it is needed most — a critical compounding failure.

**Relevant context:** Distributional shift alignment literature; scalable oversight (Christiano et al.); sycophancy + oversight compounding idea; OOD generalization challenges.
