# Investigating Benchmark Score Variance Due to Prompt Batching

**Problem:** Safety evaluations typically run prompts individually. When prompts are batched (multiple prompts in a single API call or batch inference), model outputs may differ due to attention interference. Whether batching introduces systematic safety score variance is unknown.

**Direction:** Evaluate the same 200 safety prompts individually and in batches of 10, 20, and 50. Measure disagreement rates in safety verdicts between individual and batched evaluation across two models.

**Why it matters:** Large-scale evaluations use batching for efficiency. If batching changes safety verdicts, published large-scale evaluation results may reflect batching artifacts.

**Relevant context:** Batch inference is standard in evaluation pipelines using vLLM or HuggingFace pipeline API. Its effect on output content (not just latency) for safety-specific prompts is unstudied.
