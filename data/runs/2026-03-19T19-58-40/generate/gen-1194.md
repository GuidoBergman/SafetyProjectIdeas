# Counterfactual Oversight Probing: What Would the Model Have Done Without the Overseer Signal?

**Problem:** Standard oversight measures what the model does under supervision, not the counterfactual (what it would have done without it). This makes it impossible to measure how much the oversight is actually changing behavior.

**Direction:** Train the same model in two conditions: with and without oversight. Compare behaviors on a shared test set. Measure the causal effect of oversight on model behavior distribution, not just the final accuracy.

**Why it matters:** The counterfactual effect of oversight is the true safety-relevant metric. A model that would behave identically without oversight is not being improved by the oversight at all.

**Relevant context:** Causal chain intervention; determining if models provide genuinely believed-correct responses.
