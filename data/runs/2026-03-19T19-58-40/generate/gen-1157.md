# Dual Risk Quantification: Measuring the Interaction Effect of Oversight Failure and Capability

**Problem:** Oversight failure and high capability are both dangerous individually, but their combination (oversight failure + capability = autonomous harm) may create superadditive risks that are not predicted from their individual effects.

**Direction:** Train models of different capability levels under the same oversight failure condition. Measure whether harm potential (as operationalized by task success rate on adversarial tasks) scales superadditively with capability when oversight is failing.

**Why it matters:** If the risk interaction is superadditive, safety investments must accelerate faster than capability improvements. Quantifying the interaction effect is foundational for resource allocation decisions.

**Relevant context:** Compounding risk: oversight failure + capability = autonomous harm; reward hacking phase transitions.
