# Distributional Shift Stress-Test for RLHF-Aligned Models

**Problem:** RLHF alignment is typically validated on in-distribution prompts. It is underexplored how quickly alignment degrades as prompt distribution shifts from training distribution.

**Direction:** Take a publicly available RLHF-aligned model (e.g., a Llama-3 chat variant). Construct a prompt shift dataset by systematically varying register (formal/informal), domain (law/medicine/cooking), and cultural framing. Measure refusal rate, helpfulness rating, and sycophancy score across the shift spectrum.

**Why it matters:** Quantifying the 'alignment degradation curve' under distributional shift informs how much alignment training distribution diversity is needed.

**Relevant context:** Distributionally Robust DPO paper (2502.01930); KILO approach (2508.03571); OOD generalization challenges literature.
