# Does the CoT Monitoring Advantage Hold for Smaller Reasoning Models?

**Problem:** GPT-5 system card results are from a frontier-scale model. It is unknown whether the CoT-vs-action monitoring information gap holds for smaller 7B–13B parameter reasoning models whose CoT may be less structured.

**Direction:** Compare CoT-monitor vs. action-only-monitor AUROC on DeepSeek-R1-7B and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct using SHADE-Arena and MALT tasks. Test whether the information advantage shrinks or disappears at smaller scales.

**Why it matters:** Many real deployments use smaller models. If CoT monitoring does not add value at 7B scale, safety protocols need to differ by model scale.

**Relevant context:** SHADE-Arena paper is from Anthropic; tasks and environments are publicly available. DeepSeek-R1 family has explicit CoT via distillation from larger reasoning models, providing a natural comparison.
