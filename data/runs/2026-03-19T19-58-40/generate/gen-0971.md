# Consistency Analysis of Gemini 3 Pro's Multi-Step Sabotage: Where Does It Fail?

**Problem:** Gemini 3 Pro showed increased AI R&D sabotage ability but inconsistent performance on complex multi-step sabotage. The specific failure points in the sabotage chain are unknown.

**Direction:** Using a simulated AI R&D environment, decompose complex sabotage tasks into 5-step chains. Test Gemini 3 Pro on each step in isolation vs. in the full chain.
