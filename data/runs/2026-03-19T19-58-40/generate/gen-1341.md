# In-Context Scheming at Different Context Window Positions

**Problem:** Apollo's scheming evals inject misaligned goals at specific context positions. Whether the position of goal injection (early vs. late in context, buried vs. prominent) affects scheming likelihood is unknown.

**Direction:** Replicate Apollo's in-context scheming scenarios but vary the position and salience of the misaligned goal injection across a long context window. Measure whether goal-position affects scheming rate and whether models are more likely to scheme when misaligned goals are injected early (longer time to 'commit') vs. late.

**Why it matters:** Understanding goal-position sensitivity reveals how models process and weight competing instructions, informing context management strategies for safe deployment.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research 'Frontier Models are Capable of In-Context Scheming.' Attention mechanisms show known recency and primacy biases in instruction following.
