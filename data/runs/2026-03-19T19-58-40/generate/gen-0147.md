# Homogeneity Under Distribution Shift: Does Model Diversity Collapse on Out-of-Distribution Prompts?

**Problem:** Models may appear diverse on standard benchmarks but converge on similar (often incorrect) responses when given out-of-distribution or adversarially shifted prompts.

**Direction:** Apply systematic prompt perturbations (paraphrase, domain shift, unusual formatting) to Infinity-Chat queries and measure whether inter-model similarity increases on OOD variants—i.e., whether homogeneity is worse exactly where we most need diversity.

**Why it matters:** Safety failures often occur at distribution boundaries. If models converge precisely when inputs are novel or adversarial, the safety risk from homogeneity is concentrated at the most dangerous moments.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind methodology; OOD evaluation is standard in ML robustness literature; the combination has not been studied.
