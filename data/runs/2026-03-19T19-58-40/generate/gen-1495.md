# Steering for Bias Suppression: Race and Gender Concept Vectors

**Problem:** Models exhibit racial and gender bias in completions; concept vectors for 'racial bias' and 'gender bias' have not been systematically extracted and suppressed via steering.

**Direction:** Construct contrastive pairs for racial and gender bias using the WinoBias and StereoSet datasets, extract concept vectors, apply suppression steering, and measure bias reduction on both datasets while monitoring downstream task performance.

**Why it matters:** Bias suppression via steering is retraining-free and auditable; if it works, it offers a cheap complement to debiasing fine-tuning.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. show style and sentiment are steerable. CMU RepE blog notes bias reduction as a RepE application. WinoBias and StereoSet are standard benchmarks.
