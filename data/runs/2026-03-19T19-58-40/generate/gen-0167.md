# Jailbreak Transferability Tracker: Monitoring Cross-Model Attack Success Rates

**Problem:** When a new jailbreak is discovered, its effectiveness across multiple major models is rarely systematically measured and tracked, making it impossible to monitor whether homogeneity is increasing the systemic attack surface.

**Direction:** Build a continuously updated dataset and dashboard tracking the transferability of published jailbreaks across major model versions, computing a 'systemic jailbreak risk score' as a function of cross-model success rates.

**Why it matters:** Adversarial homogeneity is the most immediately dangerous form of model monoculture. Systematic tracking would reveal whether the attack surface is growing.

**Relevant context:** Universal transferable attacks (arXiv 2307.15043) demonstrated the phenomenon. No ongoing monitoring infrastructure exists. METR.org conducts capability evals; this would complement with adversarial homogeneity monitoring.
