# Safety Index Sensitivity Analysis: How Robust Are Company Rankings to Indicator Weighting?

**Problem:** The FLI AI Safety Index assigns equal weight to indicators within domains and equal weight to domains in the overall grade. It is unknown whether rankings are sensitive to these choices.

**Direction:** Implement a Python simulation that re-weights FLI Index indicators using 1,000 random weight vectors sampled from a Dirichlet distribution. For each company, compute the distribution of overall grades. Identify companies whose ranking is robust vs. highly sensitive to weighting assumptions.

**Why it matters:** Rankings that are robust to weighting can be cited with confidence; fragile rankings indicate methodological ambiguity that should be disclosed.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index Winter 2025 uses absolute performance standards graded A–F; the methodology document is publicly available.
