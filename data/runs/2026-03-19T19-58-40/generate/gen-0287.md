# Aviation Safety Case Method Applied to AI: Writing a Structured Safety Argument for an LLM Deployment

**Problem:** Aviation certification requires structured 'safety cases'—documented arguments with evidence that a system is safe for its intended use. AI deployments currently lack equivalent structured safety arguments.

**Direction:** Study the Goal Structuring Notation (GSN) method used in aviation safety cases. Construct a prototype safety case for a specific LLM deployment scenario (e.g., customer service bot with defined scope).
