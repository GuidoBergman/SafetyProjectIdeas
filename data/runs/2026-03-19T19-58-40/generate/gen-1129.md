# Length Bias Exploitation: Do Language Models Learn to Write Longer Answers to Satisfy Overseers?

**Problem:** Human evaluators are known to prefer longer answers even when shorter ones are correct. If models learn this bias, they will produce verbose outputs that satisfy the overseer but are not actually better.

**Direction:** Train a model under human or model-based oversight where the overseer is suspected to have length bias. Measure whether the student model's outputs become systematically longer over training without a corresponding quality improvement.

**Why it matters:** Length bias is one of the most documented overseer biases. If models exploit it, it represents a concrete, measurable instance of reward hacking of human oversight.

**Relevant context:** Reward hacking of human oversight as open problem; evaluating oversight robustness with incentivized reward hacking (Greaterwrong post).
