# Dynamic Benchmark Construction to Combat Data Contamination

**Problem:** Static public benchmarks like MMLU and ARC are likely present in model training corpora, causing inflated scores that do not reflect genuine capability.

**Direction:** Build a small pipeline that generates novel safety-relevant questions post-cutoff using a templating system and a held-out seed topic list. Topics could include recent biosecurity policy, new chemical names, or fictional scenario variants. Evaluate 2–3 open-source models on original vs. templated variants, measuring score delta as a contamination proxy. Use SimpleQA-style factual questions as a control group.

**Why it matters:** If benchmark scores are inflated by memorization, safety decisions based on those scores are unsound. Even a small-scale demonstration of contamination's effect on safety benchmarks is publishable and practically useful.

**Relevant context:** A 2025 EMNLP survey on dynamic vs. static evaluation confirmed that post-cutoff data collection is the most robust contamination mitigation. CodeCleaner and paraphrasing-based mitigations have mixed effectiveness.
