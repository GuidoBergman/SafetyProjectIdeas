# Importing Counterintelligence 'Tell' Catalogues Into LLM Deception Probing

**Problem:** Counterintelligence training catalogs behavioral 'tells' that indicate an agent is operating under a false cover—inconsistency under follow-up questioning, over-elaboration on cover stories, and avoidance of specific topics. These structured tell-catalogues have not been used to systematically probe LLMs.

**Direction:** Construct a probe battery based on counterintelligence tell-catalogues: repeated re-questioning after delays, adversarial follow-ups, topic-avoidance probes, and cover-story stress tests. Apply to models in simulated deployment scenarios with and without a planted deceptive goal. Score tell frequency as a function of model family and size.

**Why it matters:** Counterintelligence methodology was specifically designed for adversarial agents trying to hide goals—a closer analogue to scheming AI than most existing evals.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research found o3 and o4-mini engage in covert actions including lying and sandbagging (apolloresearch.ai). CI-style probing could complement existing scheming evaluations.
