# At Which Transformer Layer Do Jailbreak Signals Become Detectable?

**Problem:** Activation-based detectors use intermediate layer representations, but the specific layer at which jailbreak-vs-benign separation becomes reliable has not been ablated across model sizes. Detecting too early wastes nothing; detecting too late adds latency.

**Direction:** Extract residual stream activations at every 4th transformer layer for jailbreak and benign prompts on Llama-3-8B. Train and evaluate a linear probe at each layer checkpoint. Plot AUROC vs. layer depth to identify the earliest reliable detection layer.

**Why it matters:** Finding the earliest detection layer enables real-time, low-latency jailbreak interception. For production systems, detecting at layer 8 of 32 is dramatically cheaper than at layer 28. This finding could be directly actionable for system-level safety.

**Relevant context:** ALERT (arxiv 2601.03600) performs early detection within shallow layers. The (Almost) Free detector (arxiv 2509.14558) uses a single forward pass with a lightweight classifier. The precise layer-by-layer AUROC curve has not been publicly mapped for modern instruction-tuned models.
