# Constructing a Minimal Viable Safety Benchmark for Resource-Constrained Evaluators

**Problem:** Comprehensive safety benchmarks like AILuminate require running 24,000 prompts per language. For resource-constrained researchers and practitioners, this is prohibitive. No minimal battery of safety probes has been validated as a reliable proxy for the full benchmark.

**Direction:** Using published AILuminate scores for available models, identify a minimal subset of prompts (50–200) that maximally preserves the model ranking on the full benchmark. Validate on held-out models and publish the minimal battery.

**Why it matters:** Lowering the cost of safety evaluation enables more frequent evaluation (including post-fine-tune regression testing) by resource-constrained practitioners, increasing the overall safety-evaluation cadence.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate has 12,000 public practice prompts. Subset selection to maximize rank-preservation is a well-defined optimization problem approachable with basic statistics.
