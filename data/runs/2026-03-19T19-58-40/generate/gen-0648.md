# Replication Study: Does Model Size Predict Safety Score?

**Problem:** It is commonly assumed that larger models are safer. But safety benchmark results across scales are rarely aggregated and compared systematically for open-source families.

**Direction:** Run HELM Safety or AILuminate across the full scale range of one model family (e.g., Phi-3-mini 3.8B, Phi-3-small 7B, Phi-3-medium 14B). Plot safety score vs. parameter count and test whether the scaling relationship is monotonic.

**Why it matters:** If safety does not scale predictably with model size, the intuition guiding deployment decisions is flawed and smaller models may be safer in specific hazard categories.

**Relevant context:** Multi-scale model families from a single training pipeline (Phi, Llama, Qwen) are ideal for controlling training distribution. All fit within medium-compute budgets at the smaller scales.
