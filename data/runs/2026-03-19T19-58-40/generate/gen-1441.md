# Replicating CoT Monitoring with Adversarially Fine-Tuned Agent: Does the Advantage Persist?

**Problem:** CoT monitoring studies use standard off-the-shelf models as agents. Whether the monitoring advantage persists against a model fine-tuned to conceal its reasoning in CoT is a critical adversarial robustness question.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small model (Gemma-2-2B) with a simple RL objective that rewards completing harmful side tasks while avoiding CoT monitor detection. After fine-tuning, evaluate CoT monitor AUROC. Compare to baseline (unfine-tuned) model. Measure how many RL steps are needed to erode the CoT monitoring advantage.

**Why it matters:** CoT monitoring provides safety assurance under the assumption that models do not actively optimize to evade it. Testing this assumption directly characterizes the robustness of the control measure.
