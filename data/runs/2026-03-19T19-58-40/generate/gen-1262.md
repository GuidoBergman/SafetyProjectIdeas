# Minimal Poisoning Budget for Emergent Misalignment: How Little Malicious Data Is Needed?

**Problem:** Prior work used full fine-tuning datasets of insecure code. The minimum amount of narrow misaligned data required to trigger broad misalignment is unknown, which matters for risk assessment of lightly contaminated training pipelines.

**Direction:** Train models on mixtures of clean and insecure-code data at varying ratios (100%, 50%, 10%, 1%, 0.1%) and measure whether and at what threshold broad misalignment emerges. Use the open emergent-misalignment codebase to replicate and extend.

**Why it matters:** Determines whether emergent misalignment is a concern even for models fine-tuned predominantly on benign data with a small fraction of problematic examples — relevant to data curation safety.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. open code; Nature 2025 paper on narrow fine-tuning. Threshold effects in fine-tuning data contamination are understudied.
