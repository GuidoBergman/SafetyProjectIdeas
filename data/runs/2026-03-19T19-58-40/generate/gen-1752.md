# Evaluator Agreement Study: Do StrongReject, Llama-Guard-3, and GPT-4-Judge Agree on Jailbreak Success?

**Problem:** Different automated evaluators are used to score jailbreak success, but their inter-rater agreement on the same (prompt, response) pairs is unknown. If evaluators disagree substantially, benchmark results are not comparable across studies.

**Direction:** Collect ~300 (prompt, response) pairs spanning clear refusals, borderline responses, and clear jailbreaks from AISafetyLab runs. Score each pair with StrongReject, Llama-Guard-3, ShieldLM, and a GPT-4o-mini judge. Compute pairwise agreement (Cohen's kappa, Spearman correlation). Identify prompt/response types with highest disagreement.

**Why it matters:** A success state requires a common measurement language. If the field's evaluators disagree on whether a response constitutes a jailbreak, all reported ASRs are incomparable across papers. Characterizing evaluator (dis)agreement is foundational for reproducible safety science.

**Relevant context:** AISafetyLab implements PatternScorer, ClassificationScorer, ShieldLMScorer, HarmBenchScorer, and LlamaGuard3Scorer. StrongReject is a separate tool. Evaluator comparison studies exist in NLG evaluation but not systematically in safety evaluation.
