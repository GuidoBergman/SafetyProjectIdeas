# Jailbreak Benchmark Agreement Study: Do StrongReject and TrustLLM Rank Models Consistently?

**Problem:** Multiple jailbreak benchmarks exist (StrongReject, TrustLLM, AILuminate, HELM Safety) using different prompt sets and scoring methods. How well their model-safety rankings agree is unknown.

**Direction:** Evaluate four open-weight models (Llama-3-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct, Gemma-2-9B-IT, Phi-3-Mini-Instruct) on both StrongReject and the TrustLLM safety subset. Compute Spearman rank correlation of safety scores across benchmarks. Identify prompt categories where the benchmarks most disagree on model rankings.

**Why it matters:** If benchmark agreement is low, safety evaluations are benchmark-specific and unreliable. A model could pass one benchmark while failing another, misleading practitioners.

**Relevant context:** StrongReject achieves 0.90 Spearman correlation with human labels but this is evaluator-level, not cross-benchmark agreement. TrustLLM is comprehensive and open-source. This experiment requires only inference on four models.
