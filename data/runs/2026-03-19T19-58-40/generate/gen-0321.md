# Compute Disclosure Completeness Audit: How Much Training Compute Goes Unreported?

**Problem:** Epoch AI tracks compute for models that have publicly disclosed compute information. The fraction of actual training compute that goes unreported is unknown, creating a blind spot in compute governance.

**Direction:** Using Epoch AI's model database, compute the fraction of models in each year (2018–2025) for which training compute is: (a) directly reported, (b) estimated, or (c) unknown. Analyze trends in reporting completeness over time and by lab type (closed vs. open, large vs. small).

**Why it matters:** If compute reporting is incomplete or declining, compute governance mechanisms (like the EU AI Act threshold) are undermined by their own data foundation.

**Relevant context:** Epoch AI's database explicitly distinguishes directly reported from estimated compute values; this audit uses their data to measure its own coverage.
