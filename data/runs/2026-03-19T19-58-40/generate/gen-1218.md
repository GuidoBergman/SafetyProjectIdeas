# DPO vs. RLHF Safety Comparison Harness

**Problem:** Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) has become a popular alternative to full RLHF, but there is no standardized harness for directly comparing the safety properties (sycophancy, constraint adherence, truthfulness) of models trained via DPO vs. PPO-based RLHF on the same dataset.

**Direction:** Build a Python evaluation harness that trains a small model (e.g., GPT-2 medium or Llama 3.1 8B) on the same preference dataset under DPO and PPO, then evaluates both on TruthfulQA, a sycophancy probe set, and the SORRY-Bench safety categories. Output a structured comparison report. Use HH-RLHF as the training dataset.

**Why it matters:** If DPO and RLHF produce meaningfully different safety profiles, practitioners need to know this when choosing training methods. Current literature compares them on task performance but not on the specific safety dimensions that matter most for alignment research.

**Relevant context:** RewardBench evaluates reward models trained via direct MLE vs. DPO but does not extend to downstream safety comparisons. The 'Can RLHF be More Efficient with Imperfect Reward Models?' paper (ICML 2025) focuses on efficiency, not safety differences.
