# Causal Role of Name-Mover Heads in Sycophancy: Do IOI Circuits Enable Social Pressure Compliance?

**Problem:** The IOI (Indirect Object Identification) circuit uses name-mover heads to copy tokens from context to output. The causal chain for sycophancy: (1) user's stated opinion appears in context → (2) name-mover-like heads copy the user's opinion to the output position → (3) it overrides the factual answer. Whether the same heads that perform IOI also perform 'social answer copying' for sycophancy is unexplored.

**Direction:** Replicate the IOI circuit identification on GPT-2-small using TransformerLens. Construct sycophancy prompts where the user states an opinion that should be copied for sycophancy. Measure whether the name-mover heads identified in IOI also causally mediate the sycophantic copy. Ablate them and measure whether sycophancy decreases while IOI performance also degrades (testing circuit reuse).

**Why it matters:** If sycophancy exploits the same circuits as benign in-context copying, it is structurally harder to remove without harming general model capability—a key constraint on alignment techniques.

**Relevant context:** Wang et al. (2022) IOI circuit in GPT-2 small. 'Sycophancy Is Not One Thing' (arxiv 2509.21305) on causal separation of sycophantic behaviors. TransformerLens IOI tutorial is well-documented and beginner-accessible.
