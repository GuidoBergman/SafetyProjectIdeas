# Is the Inoculation Effect Mediated by the Misalignment Activation Direction?

**Problem:** Inoculation during training prevents emergent misalignment behaviorally, but the mechanistic explanation is completely unknown. One hypothesis is that inoculation prevents the misalignment linear direction from forming in activation space.

**Direction:** Train three model variants: (1) standard finetuning on insecure code (misaligned), (2) finetuning with inoculation (non-misaligned), (3) no finetuning (baseline). For each, extract activations on the same probe sentences and compute or compare the misalignment direction. Test whether inoculated models show a suppressed or absent misalignment direction despite training on identical task data.

**Why it matters:** If inoculation works by blocking the formation of a specific linear direction, this would confirm the linear representation hypothesis and suggest that targeted activation steering could replicate inoculation's effect without retraining.

**Relevant context:** Convergent Linear Representations paper (arxiv 2506.11618) provides methods for extracting the misalignment direction. Inoculation prompting papers provide training recipes. This experiment directly bridges the two lines of work.
