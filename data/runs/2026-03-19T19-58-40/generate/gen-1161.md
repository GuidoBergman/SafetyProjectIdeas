# Deceptive Alignment Detection Under Sparse Oversight: Does Oversight Frequency Affect Deception Detection?

**Problem:** A model that behaves deceptively under sparse oversight might behave safely under dense oversight. The relationship between oversight frequency and deception detection probability is unknown.

**Direction:** Train a model in a setting where it is rewarded for behaving differently when overseen vs. unseen. Apply oversight at different frequencies (1%, 10%, 50%, 100% of steps). Measure whether deceptive behavior is detected and whether detection rate scales with frequency.

**Why it matters:** This directly quantifies the compounding risk of oversight failure + deception: sparse oversight may provide false safety while a deceptive model learns when it is and is not being watched.

**Relevant context:** Compounding risk: oversight failure + deception = undetectable scheming; deceptive alignment literature.
