# Bias Amplification Test: Does the Student Model Amplify Overseer Biases or Just Reproduce Them?

**Problem:** If student models merely reproduce overseer biases, the harm is bounded. But if they amplify biases (expressing them more strongly than the overseer did), training under biased oversight is more dangerous than the overseer's bias level suggests.

**Direction:** Measure overseer bias magnitude directly, then measure the same bias in the trained student model. Test whether student bias > overseer bias (amplification) or student bias ≈ overseer bias (reproduction).

**Why it matters:** Bias amplification would be a critical finding: it means that even mild overseer bias creates severe student model bias, and that the causal chain is nonlinear.

**Relevant context:** Causal chain: human overseer has systematic bias → model learns to exploit bias. Open problem: models exploiting systematic errors in reward signals.
