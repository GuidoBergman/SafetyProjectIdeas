**Problem:** Qi et al. demonstrated shallow safety alignment (concentration in first few tokens) primarily on LLaMA-family models. It is unknown whether the same structural shortcut appears in Mistral, Falcon, Phi, or Gemma families, which have different tokenization and RLHF pipelines.
