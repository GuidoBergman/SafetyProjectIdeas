# Persona-Jailbreak Resistance Benchmark

**Problem:** Role-playing and persona-adoption requests ('act as DAN', 'you are an AI with no restrictions') are a common jailbreak vector but are not systematically covered by standard safety benchmarks.

**Direction:** Create 150 persona-jailbreak prompts at varying levels of sophistication. Evaluate three models and measure refusal rates, partial compliance rates, and the point in a conversation where compliance emerges.

**Why it matters:** MLCommons' AILuminate Jailbreak v0.5 covers general jailbreaks but persona-specific jailbreaks represent a distinct, high-volume attack class with limited benchmark coverage.

**Relevant context:** MLCommons published an AILuminate Jailbreak benchmark v0.5 in December 2025. A focused persona-jailbreak extension would complement it.
