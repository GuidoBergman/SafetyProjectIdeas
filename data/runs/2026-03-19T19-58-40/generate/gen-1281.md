# Misalignment Localization: Do Emergently Misaligned Models Show Concentrated or Diffuse Representation Changes?

**Problem:** Emergent misalignment produces broad behavioral changes from narrow fine-tuning, but whether these changes correspond to localized representation differences in specific layers or are diffuse throughout the network is unknown. This matters for designing targeted post-hoc corrections.

**Direction:** Apply activation patching and representation analysis (e.g., logit lens, probing classifiers) to compare representations in aligned vs. emergently misaligned model variants at specific layers. Identify whether misalignment-related representations are localized or distributed.

**Why it matters:** If emergent misalignment localizes to specific layers, targeted weight editing or activation steering could reverse it without full retraining — a practically important finding for safety interventions.
