# Guardrail Evasion via Paraphrase: How Robust Are Output Classifiers to Semantically Equivalent Harmful Outputs?

**Problem:** Output safety classifiers (e.g., LlamaGuard) detect harmful outputs by surface features. The causal chain is: harmful content generated → classifier checks output → if surface form differs from training examples → classifier misses it. The paraphrase robustness of these classifiers is the under-studied causal link.

**Direction:** Generate harmful model outputs, then paraphrase them with a second LLM. Feed both original and paraphrased versions to LlamaGuard and measure false negative rate for paraphrased content. Test whether embedding-based classifiers (cosine similarity to harmful prototypes) are more robust. ~20 hours.

**Why it matters:** Output classifiers are widely deployed as safety guardrails. If paraphrase evasion is easy, the entire output-filtering pipeline is fragile, and embedding-based detectors may be needed.

**Relevant context:** Bypassing LLM guardrails evasion attacks (arxiv 2504.11168); LlamaGuard; output-level defense research.
