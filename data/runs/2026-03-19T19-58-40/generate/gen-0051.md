# Causal Role of Benchmark Saturation in Driving Riskier Capability Development

**Problem:** When models saturate safety benchmarks (approach ceiling performance), developers interpret saturation as 'the safety problem is solved' rather than 'the benchmark is too easy.' This creates a causal chain: saturation → false confidence → reduced safety investment → riskier capability development → safety falls behind capabilities.

**Direction:** Empirically document benchmark saturation timelines: for a sample of widely used safety benchmarks, measure when different models first achieved >90% performance. Correlate saturation dates with public announcements of reduced safety investment or increased capability focus. Propose a 'saturation early warning' mechanism.

**Why it matters:** Benchmark saturation is a documented driver of the 'evaluation gap.' Detecting saturation early and replacing saturated benchmarks with harder ones is essential for keeping evaluations meaningful.

**Relevant context:** MMLU and similar capability benchmarks have well-documented saturation histories. Safety benchmarks face the same dynamic but with higher stakes when falsely declared 'solved.'
