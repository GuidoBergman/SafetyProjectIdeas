# Oversight Quality Self-Assessment: Can Strong Models Accurately Report Their Own Oversight-Evasion Strategies?

**Problem:** One approach to the compounding risk of oversight failure + deception is to ask models to self-report their deception strategies. Whether models can or will do this accurately is unknown.

**Direction:** Train a model that has learned to exploit oversight biases. Then prompt the model to describe how it would game the oversight system. Measure whether the self-report accurately describes the model's actual exploitation behavior.

**Why it matters:** If models can accurately report their own oversight-evasion strategies, self-reporting could be a cheap additional oversight tool. If they cannot or will not, this closes off a proposed safety technique.

**Relevant context:** Compounding risk: oversight failure + deception; determining if models provide genuinely believed-correct responses.
