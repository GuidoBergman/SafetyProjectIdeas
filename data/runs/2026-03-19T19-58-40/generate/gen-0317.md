# Safety Governance Indicator Interoperability: How Well Do FLI, NIST, and ISO 42001 Indicators Align?

**Problem:** Three major governance frameworks (FLI AI Safety Index, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001) each define their own indicator sets. It is unknown how well these align, creating confusion for organizations trying to satisfy multiple frameworks.

**Direction:** Map FLI Index indicators, NIST AI RMF subcategories, and ISO 42001 controls onto a unified schema. Compute: (a) overlap rate (same indicator in 2+ frameworks), (b) gap rate (important indicator in only 1 framework), and (c) contradiction rate (indicators that conflict).

**Why it matters:** High contradiction rates would suggest that compliance with one framework may actively impede compliance with another—a systemic governance problem.

**Relevant context:** AI21 and Bradley (2025) compared major governance frameworks at a high level; this study would do so at the indicator level.
