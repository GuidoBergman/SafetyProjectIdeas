# Irreversibility Scoring: Training a Lightweight Classifier to Flag High-Consequence Agent Actions Before Execution

**Problem:** Preventing irreversible actions requires identifying them before execution, but no lightweight classifier exists for rating action reversibility across diverse agentic action types.

**Direction:** Create a labeled dataset of ~300 agent actions (drawn from ControlArena tasks, synthetic scenarios, and real-world tool descriptions) with human annotations of reversibility on a 1-5 scale. Fine-tune a small classifier (distilbert or similar) to predict reversibility score from action description. Evaluate on a held-out set and compare to a GPT-4o zero-shot baseline. Explore whether the classifier generalizes to action types not seen during training.

**Why it matters:** Security architectures preventing irreversible actions are listed as an open control problem. A fast, deployable reversibility classifier is a concrete first step toward runtime guardrails for agentic systems.

**Relevant context:** OWASP Top 10 for Agentic AI (2025) and McKinsey agentic governance work (2025) both call out irreversible actions as a critical risk. Human-in-the-loop confirmation for irreversible actions is widely recommended but requires identifying which actions are irreversible.
