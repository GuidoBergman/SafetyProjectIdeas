# When Does Multi-Principal Alignment Become a Compounding Risk? An Empirical Study

**Problem:** Models deployed by a developer but used by a third party face multi-principal alignment: developer goals, user goals, and societal goals may conflict. Formal alignment for each principal separately may compound into guaranteed failures when all three are active simultaneously.

**Direction:** Design a test suite where a small model must satisfy three conflicting objective functions (developer profit proxy, user satisfaction proxy, harm reduction proxy). Measure satisfiability rates for each pair vs. all three simultaneously. Report whether the three-way failure rate exceeds the product of pairwise failure rates (compounding test).

**Why it matters:** Multi-principal alignment is increasingly the real deployment scenario. If failures compound super-linearly, formal single-principal guarantees are insufficient for real-world safety—a critical empirical finding for deployment practice.

**Relevant context:** AI Alignment at Your Discretion ACM FAccT 2025; legal alignment paper 2026; multi-principal LLM alignment is an emerging area. Empirical compounding test is novel.
