# Reward Hacking Detection via Behavioral Fingerprinting

**Problem:** Reward hacking in RLHF is typically only identified post-hoc, after misaligned behavior is observed. There is no lightweight real-time signal for detecting when a model is exploiting reward model weaknesses during training.

**Direction:** During RL fine-tuning of a small LLM, track a suite of behavioral fingerprints (response length distribution, refusal rate on probe set, perplexity on a held-out alignment-relevant sentence set) across training steps. Correlate changes in these fingerprints with known reward hacking checkpoints identified by reward model score divergence.

**Why it matters:** Early-warning indicators for reward hacking would allow practitioners to stop or adjust training before misalignment propagates, addressing a core RLHF limitation.

**Relevant context:** Lil'Log reward hacking survey (2024); Anthropic natural emergent misalignment from reward hacking paper; reward shaping RLHF paper 2502.18770.
