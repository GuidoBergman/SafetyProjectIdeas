# Coverage-Gap Analysis: Cataloguing What METR and MALT Tasks Don't Cover in Monitoring Research

**Problem:** METR and MALT are widely used as data sources for AI control research, but nobody has systematically analyzed what monitoring-relevant scenarios they fail to cover, meaning researchers may be optimizing for an unrepresentative sample.

**Direction:** Systematically tag all METR and MALT tasks along dimensions relevant to monitoring: task length, tool diversity, degree of user interaction, presence of irreversible actions, and whether sabotage is possible. **Why it matters:** Benchmark coverage analysis is a well-established methodology in ML evaluation. Applying it to AI safety datasets would reveal blind spots that current monitoring research implicitly ignores.

**Relevant context:** METR's task suite is publicly available. MALT (Multi-Agent LLM Tasks) includes diverse scenarios. A gap analysis requires no ML training, making it very feasible within 30 hours at intro ML level.
