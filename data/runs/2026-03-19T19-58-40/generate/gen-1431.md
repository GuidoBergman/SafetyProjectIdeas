# Replicating CoT Monitoring Advantage on Non-English Tasks

**Problem:** CoT monitoring research has been conducted exclusively in English. Whether the information advantage of CoT over action-only monitoring holds when the model reasons in another language (e.g., Chinese, French) is unknown.

**Direction:** Translate a subset of MALT tasks to Mandarin Chinese. Run DeepSeek-R1-8B (strong multilingual reasoner) on translated tasks. Apply an LM monitor in (a) the task language and (b) English after auto-translation. Compare AUROC for CoT-monitor vs. action-only conditions in each language setting.

**Why it matters:** Global AI deployments use diverse languages. If monitoring effectiveness drops for non-English CoT, this creates safety gaps for non-English deployments and motivates multilingual monitor development.

**Relevant context:** DeepSeek-R1 models are trained on multilingual data including substantial Chinese. MALT transcripts are English-only. Multilingual monitoring is identified as an under-studied area in AI safety.
