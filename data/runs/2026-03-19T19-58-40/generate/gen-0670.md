# Contamination Robustness of SimpleQA: Memorization vs. Reasoning

**Problem:** SimpleQA tests factual accuracy, but high scores may reflect verbatim memorization of training data rather than reliable reasoning. Contamination robustness of SimpleQA has not been characterized.

**Direction:** Apply the min-k% probability contamination detection method to SimpleQA items across three models. Compare contamination rates to per-item accuracy to test whether high accuracy correlates with high contamination likelihood.

**Why it matters:** If SimpleQA accuracy reflects memorization, it is not a valid measure of factual reliability in deployment, where queries are novel.
