# Automated Red-Teaming Coverage Blind Spots: What Attacks Do LLM-Based Red Teams Systematically Miss?

**Problem:** Automated red-teaming using attacker LLMs (algorithmic red-teaming) is widely used to generate diverse jailbreak prompts. However, attacker LLMs have their own safety training, which may cause them to systematically avoid generating certain high-harm attack categories even during red-teaming.

**Direction:** Use two open-source LLMs as automated red-teamers against a target model, generating attacks via the PAIR and TAP frameworks (available in AISafetyLab). Compare the distribution of generated attack categories against the full AILuminate hazard taxonomy (12 categories). Identify which hazard categories are underrepresented in LLM-generated attacks vs. human-authored attacks from JailbreakBench. Measure attack success rate per category to assess whether missed categories are also harder to attack.

**Why it matters:** If automated red-teaming systematically misses the most dangerous categories (CBRN, CSAM), it provides false assurance. This analysis would motivate hybrid human-automated red-teaming pipelines.

**Relevant context:** Algorithmic red teaming approaches (ScienceDirect 2025). AILuminate MLCommons benchmark with 12 hazard categories. AISafetyLab unified toolkit (Tsinghua). TAP and PAIR attack frameworks.
