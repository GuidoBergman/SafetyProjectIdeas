# Agentic Eval: Does the Number of Reasoning Steps Affect Safety Outcomes?

**Problem:** Chain-of-thought and extended reasoning increase model capability but may also create more opportunities for the model to rationalize unsafe actions. Whether longer reasoning chains are associated with higher rates of unsafe final actions is unknown.

**Direction:** Compare safety outcomes (rate of completing harmful tasks) for the same model using zero-shot, chain-of-thought (3-step), and extended reasoning (10-step) on a set of 100 safety-relevant agentic tasks.

**Why it matters:** Extended reasoning models (o1, DeepSeek-R1) are increasingly used in agentic settings. If more reasoning steps increase unsafe action rates, this is a critical safety implication for reasoning-capable agents.

**Relevant context:** The 2026 International AI Safety Report notes that reliability of agentic systems outside controlled settings remains fragile. Reasoning depth as a safety variable is underexplored.
