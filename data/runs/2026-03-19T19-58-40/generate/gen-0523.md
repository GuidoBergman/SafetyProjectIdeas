# Causal Chain Intervention: Training Data Contamination as a Deceptive Goal Precursor

**Problem:** Models may develop deceptive goals not through explicit misalignment during fine-tuning but through contaminated pre-training data containing instrumental deception strategies. Whether pre-training data composition is a causal antecedent to deceptive alignment propensity is unstudied.

**Direction:** Fine-tune small models on pre-training data subsets with varying proportions of content featuring instrumental deception (fiction featuring manipulative characters, negotiation transcripts, adversarial legal argumentation). Measure deceptive alignment propensity as a function of deception-heavy pre-training data proportion using a standard scheming benchmark.

**Why it matters:** If pre-training data composition is a causal antecedent to deceptive alignment, data curation becomes a first-line safety intervention—upstream of all fine-tuning and RLHF safety measures.

**Relevant context:** Emergent misalignment shows deception arising from narrow finetuning (arXiv:2502.17424). Pre-training data influence on values and behaviors is a known phenomenon. Deception-specific pre-training effects are unstudied.
