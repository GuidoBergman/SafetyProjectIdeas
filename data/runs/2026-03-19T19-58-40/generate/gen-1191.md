# Oversight Game Theory: Under What Conditions Does Debate Lead to Truth Rather Than to Persuasion?

**Problem:** Debate is designed to converge on truth by having debaters argue for different positions. But skilled debaters may win by persuasion rather than truth. The conditions under which debate reliably produces truth have not been formally characterized.

**Direction:** Run debate experiments with arguments varying in truthfulness and persuasiveness independently. Measure whether the more truthful or the more persuasive argument wins more often, and whether this ratio changes with debater capability.

**Why it matters:** If debate favors persuasion over truth, it is not a reliable oversight mechanism. Characterizing when debate works is a prerequisite for deploying it safely.

**Relevant context:** Debate methodology; Prover-Estimator Debate protocol; scalable oversight methodologies.
