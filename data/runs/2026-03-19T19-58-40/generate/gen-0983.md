# Multi-Agent Trust Hierarchy Benchmark: Can Sub-Agents Correctly Resolve Conflicting Instructions from Multiple Orchestrators?

**Problem:** No benchmark exists for evaluating how sub-agents handle conflicting instructions from multiple orchestrators with different authority levels — a common real-world multi-agent topology.

**Direction:** Design 30 scenarios where a sub-agent receives conflicting instructions from two orchestrators (e.g., one says 'proceed', one says 'halt'). Vary authority signals (explicit role labels, system-prompt position, trust level declarations). Measure whether the agent resolves conflicts correctly and consistently.

**Why it matters:** Multi-orchestrator conflicts will be common in enterprise multi-agent deployments. No benchmark currently addresses this, yet incorrect conflict resolution can cause catastrophic outcomes.
