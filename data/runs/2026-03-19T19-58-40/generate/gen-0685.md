# Investigating Whether AISafetyLab Evaluations Align with Human Red-Team Findings

**Problem:** AISafetyLab provides automated attack and evaluation tools. Whether its automated red-team results agree with human red-team findings has not been systematically compared.

**Direction:** Run AISafetyLab's automated red-teaming on a model whose human red-team report is publicly available (e.g., from an AI company's model card). Compare attack success rates and failure category distributions between automated and human red-team results.
