# Causal Chain from Latent Knowledge Gaps to Evaluation Blindness

**Problem:** Models may have dangerous latent knowledge that evaluations fail to elicit because they test surface behavior rather than underlying representations. The causal chain: eval tests behavioral outputs → latent knowledge not probed → model passes eval → deployed → different context elicits dangerous latent knowledge → harm.

**Direction:** Compare behavioral safety evaluations with representation-level probing (linear classifiers on hidden states) for the same safety properties on open-source models. Test whether models that pass behavioral evals still have latent representations of dangerous information detectable by probes.

**Why it matters:** The 2026 International AI Safety Report recommends 'latent-knowledge elicitation' as a key evaluation advance. This project operationalizes that recommendation at a scale accessible to students with a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Representation probing is well-established in mechanistic interpretability research. The gap between behavioral and representational safety is a known concern highlighted in multiple 2025 safety reports.
