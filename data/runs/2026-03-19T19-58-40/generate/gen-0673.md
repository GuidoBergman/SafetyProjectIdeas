# Safety Score Replication After Supervised Fine-Tuning on Domain Data

**Problem:** Organizations routinely fine-tune aligned models on domain-specific corpora (medical, legal, financial). Whether this fine-tuning degrades safety alignment is a widely asked but poorly benchmarked question.

**Direction:** Fine-tune an aligned model (e.g., Llama-3-8B-Instruct) on a publicly available domain corpus (PubMed abstracts, legal case summaries). Run HELM Safety or AILuminate before and after fine-tuning. Quantify safety score degradation.

**Why it matters:** Domain fine-tuning is standard industry practice. Documented safety regression from fine-tuning would directly motivate post-fine-tune safety re-evaluation requirements.

**Relevant context:** Fine-tuning on public domain corpora is feasible on a single GPU. Safety regression after fine-tuning has been discussed anecdotally but lacks systematic benchmark documentation.
