# Failure Mode Analysis: Where Do Safety Scores Break Down Under Adversarial User Personas?

**Problem:** Safety benchmarks use generic user personas. Models may behave differently when user context implies expertise (e.g., 'I am a nurse', 'I am a security researcher') that makes harmful requests seem more plausible.

**Direction:** Test 150 harmful prompts each under three persona conditions: no persona, benign professional persona, and expert persona plausibly requiring the information. Measure refusal rates across conditions for three models.

**Why it matters:** Persona-based context manipulation is a documented real-world attack vector. Benchmarks that ignore persona conditioning underestimate real-world safety failure rates.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate and HELM Safety do not systematically vary user persona. Real-world jailbreaks routinely use professional persona framing to reduce refusal rates.
