# Fault Injection Testing for Identifying Critical Circuit Nodes

**Problem:** Not all neurons and heads contribute equally to a circuit. Current methods identify which components are part of a circuit but do not rank their criticality in terms of sensitivity to corruption.

**Direction:** Adapt hardware fault injection: inject synthetic faults (zero-out, random noise, bit-flip analog) into individual components of a discovered circuit and measure the degradation in output quality. Rank components by their fault sensitivity to identify single-points-of-failure in the circuit — the mechanistic equivalent of safety-critical code paths.

**Why it matters:** Safety-critical systems engineering prioritizes identifying single-points-of-failure. Applying this to transformer circuits could reveal which nodes, if corrupted (by adversarial inputs or fine-tuning), cause the most dramatic behavioral changes — directly relevant to model robustness and safety.

**Relevant context:** TorchFI and TensorFI2 are established fault injection frameworks for DNNs. The concept maps naturally to activation patching in TransformerLens. The 2025 ACM survey on failure analysis in AI systems reviews existing approaches.
