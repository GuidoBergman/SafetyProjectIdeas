# Real-Time Systemic Risk Monitoring Dashboard for AI Model Ecosystems

**Problem:** There is no tool for continuously monitoring the diversity of outputs being produced by deployed AI systems, analogous to how financial regulators monitor market concentration in real time.

**Direction:** Build a prototype monitoring system that periodically probes a set of deployed model APIs with a fixed query set, computes ecosystem-level diversity metrics, and visualizes trends over time with threshold alerts.

**Why it matters:** Systemic risk monitoring requires ongoing surveillance, not one-time studies. A prototype would demonstrate feasibility and serve as a template for regulatory monitoring requirements.

**Relevant context:** CEPR and LSE research on AI systemic risk in finance explicitly calls for monitoring tools. Regulatory AI safety frameworks (EU AI Act) include requirements for systemic risk assessment without specifying how.
