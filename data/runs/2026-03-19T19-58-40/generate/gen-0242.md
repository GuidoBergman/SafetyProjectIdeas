# Automated Compliance Checker for AI Safety Commitments

**Problem:** Companies make public safety commitments (e.g., in model cards, safety frameworks) but there is no systematic way to verify whether their deployed models or documentation actually honor those commitments.

**Direction:** Build a Python tool that scrapes public model cards, safety reports, and policy documents from major AI labs, then uses keyword extraction and NLP to check whether stated commitments (e.g., 'we will red-team all models before release') appear to be reflected in downstream documentation.

**Why it matters:** Automating even shallow compliance checks creates a baseline for accountability and exposes patterns of commitment-document gaps at scale.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index (Summer/Winter 2025) manually evaluates 33–35 indicators across 6 domains for 8 companies. Automation could extend coverage.
