# Temperature and Sampling Sensitivity in Safety Benchmark Scores

**Problem:** Safety benchmarks are typically evaluated at a fixed temperature (often 0), but deployed models often use higher temperatures. It is unknown whether safety scores generalize across sampling settings.

**Direction:** Select 100 prompts from AILuminate or TrustLLM. Evaluate 2–3 open-source models at temperatures 0, 0.5, 1.0, and 1.5. Measure refusal rates and harmful output rates at each temperature. Compute whether temperature interacts with model family or prompt category (e.g., direct harm requests vs. indirect).

**Why it matters:** Deployed systems use diverse sampling parameters. If safety degrades significantly at higher temperatures, this is a deployment-time risk that standard evals miss.

**Relevant context:** Standard evaluation practice in HELM Safety and AILuminate uses fixed temperature. The interaction between decoding parameters and safety has not been systematically studied.
