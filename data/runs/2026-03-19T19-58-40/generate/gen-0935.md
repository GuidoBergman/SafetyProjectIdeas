# Bayesian vs. Frequentist Alignment: Do Different Statistical Frameworks Predict Different Failure Modes?

**Problem:** Formal alignment can be framed in Bayesian terms (prior over goals) or frequentist terms (behavioral guarantees with probability bounds). Do these frameworks predict different empirical failure modes for the same model?

**Direction:** For a small aligned model, compute both: (1) a Bayesian posterior over likely goal hypotheses given observed behavior, and (2) frequentist empirical failure rates on a test suite. Identify cases where the frameworks disagree (Bayesian posterior implies high safety, frequentist rate shows failures). Analyze what causes the disagreements.

**Why it matters:** The choice of statistical framework is rarely empirically grounded in alignment. If the frameworks disagree, researchers need to know which is more reliable, which has implications for formal alignment guarantees.
