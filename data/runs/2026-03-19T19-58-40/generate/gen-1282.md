# Consensus-Based Reward Aggregation for Small-Scale RLHF: Robustness Gains at Low Cost

**Problem:** Consensus-based feedback aggregation has been shown to reduce adversarial manipulation of RLHF. Whether it also reduces misspecification from annotation inconsistency and distributional shift — not just adversarial attack — in realistic small-scale annotation settings is unexplored.

**Direction:** Implement a multi-annotator preference collection protocol with 3–5 synthetic annotator 'personas' (using an LLM with varied instructions). Train reward models with majority-vote vs. individual annotator labels. Compare reward model generalization and downstream policy alignment quality.

**Why it matters:** Consensus aggregation may be a cheap noise-reduction technique that simultaneously improves alignment quality and robustness for practitioners who cannot afford large annotator pools.

**Relevant context:** Consensus-based RLHF framework (Nature Scientific Reports 2025); reward poisoning defense; annotation noise in RLHF; Anthropic HH-RLHF.
