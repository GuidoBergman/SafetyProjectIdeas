# Concept Steering Effectiveness Across Non-English Low-Resource Languages

**Problem:** Cross-lingual transfer results may degrade for low-resource languages underrepresented in training data, leaving safety gaps for those user populations.

**Direction:** Test whether Beaglehole et al.'s cross-lingual transfer of concept vectors holds for low-resource languages (Swahili, Bengali, Urdu) by measuring probe accuracy and steering effectiveness on small test sets assembled from public toxicity corpora.

**Why it matters:** Low-resource language users may face weaker safety protections; knowing the extent of degradation motivates targeted interventions.

**Relevant context:** Language-Specific Latent Process work (arXiv 2505.13141) identifies non-trivial language effects on representations. Beaglehole et al. show transfer but only for higher-resource languages.
