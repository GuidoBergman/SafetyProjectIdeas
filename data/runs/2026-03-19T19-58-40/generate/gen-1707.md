# Early-Layer Harm Classification as a Chokepoint: How Robust Is It Against Token Substitution Attacks?

**Problem:** Research shows LLMs classify inputs as harmful or benign in early layers. The causal chain is: early-layer classification → middle-layer emotional routing → refusal token generation. Attacks that perturb the early-layer classification (e.g., synonym substitution for trigger words) should break the whole chain.

**Direction:** Extract early-layer representations for harmful vs. paraphrased-harmful prompts. Train a classifier, then measure how synonym substitution and paraphrase attacks degrade early-layer classification accuracy vs. final output safety. Propose minimal vocabulary-aware defenses.

**Why it matters:** If early-layer classification is fragile to surface paraphrase, then the entire safety chain is fragile regardless of how robust later steps are, motivating semantic (not lexical) safety representations.
