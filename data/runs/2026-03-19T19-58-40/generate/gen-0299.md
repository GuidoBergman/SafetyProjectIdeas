# Causal Attribution in AI Incidents: Technical Failure vs. Governance Failure Decomposition

**Problem:** AI incident reports typically describe what went wrong technically but do not decompose causation between the AI system's technical behavior and the governance decisions that allowed deployment or failed to catch the problem.

**Direction:** For 30–50 AI incidents, use a structured causal attribution protocol (adapted from root cause analysis in software engineering) to code each incident as: primarily technical (the model behaved unexpectedly), primarily governance (adequate technical behavior but wrongly deployed or monitored), or both. Compute the distribution.

**Why it matters:** If most incidents have a significant governance causation component, this shifts the cost-effectiveness balance toward governance investment over additional technical safety research.

**Relevant context:** The International AI Safety Report 2026 explicitly calls for better separation of technical and governance risk factors in incident analysis.
