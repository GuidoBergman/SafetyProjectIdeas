# Steering Replication on Instruction-Tuned vs. Base Models

**Problem:** Instruction tuning changes the internal representation structure of models; it is unknown whether concept vectors extracted from base models transfer to their instruction-tuned counterparts.

**Direction:** Extract safety concept vectors from a base model (e.g., Llama-3-8B base) and its instruction-tuned version (Llama-3-8B-Instruct) and compare vector geometry, probe accuracy, and steering effectiveness on identical safety tasks.

**Why it matters:** Instruction-tuned models are the deployed versions; knowing whether base-model concept vectors are reusable matters for practical safety pipelines.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. test across multiple model types. Representation Bending for LLM Safety (ACL 2025) suggests fine-tuning changes representation structure in safety-relevant ways.
