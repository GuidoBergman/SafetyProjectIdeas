# Graduated Capability Stress Testing: At What Capability Level Does Debate Stop Being a Reliable Oversight Mechanism?

**Problem:** Debate works well when the student's capabilities are only moderately above the overseer's. It is unknown at what capability gap debate breaks down and the student can consistently mislead the overseer.

**Direction:** Run debate experiments across a range of capability gaps. Measure overseer accuracy in debate as a function of capability gap. Identify the capability gap threshold at which debate-based oversight drops below a reliability criterion.

**Why it matters:** Knowing when debate breaks down tells us how much we can extrapolate from current experiments to future, more capable models.
