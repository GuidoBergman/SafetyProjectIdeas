# Identifying Safety Concepts Without Linear Representations

**Problem:** The linear representation hypothesis is assumed but not systematically tested for all safety-relevant concepts; some concepts may require non-linear representations, breaking steering-based safety approaches.

**Direction:** For a set of 15 safety concepts (toxicity, deception, bias, manipulation, self-harm, CSAM, bioweapons instructions, etc.), test whether linear probes achieve significantly above-chance accuracy compared to non-linear MLP probes, flagging concepts where linearity fails.

**Why it matters:** Knowing which safety concepts lack linear representations prevents false confidence in steering coverage and motivates alternative approaches for those concepts.

**Relevant context:** The linear representation hypothesis is the foundation of all RepE work. The RepE reliability paper (OpenReview) raises concerns. Neural probe hallucination work (arXiv 2512.20949) finds MLP probes outperform linear probes for some concepts.
