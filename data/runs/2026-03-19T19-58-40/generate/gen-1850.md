# Biological Immune System Analogy for Adversarial Fine-Tuning Defense

**Problem:** The biological immune system develops memory of past pathogens and uses it to mount faster, stronger defenses on re-exposure. This suggests an analogy for alignment: models could be 'immunized' against known misalignment-inducing fine-tuning patterns.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small model on a known misalignment-inducing dataset, then apply a 'recovery' fine-tuning step. Record the recovery trajectory. Repeat the misalignment-induction. Test whether the second misalignment takes longer to induce and whether recovery is faster — analogous to immune memory.

**Why it matters:** If alignment 'immune memory' exists, it suggests a training strategy of repeated exposure-and-recovery cycles to build robust alignment.
