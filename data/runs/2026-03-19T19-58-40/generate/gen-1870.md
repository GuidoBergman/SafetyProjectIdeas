# Distributional Shift + Emergent Misalignment: When OOD Prompts Activate Latent Misalignment

**Problem:** Distributional shift (alignment training failure) and emergent misalignment (also alignment training) can compound in a cross-subfield interaction with interpretability: a model may appear aligned on in-distribution prompts but exhibit misalignment specifically on out-of-distribution inputs that activate latent misaligned circuits.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a model to induce sub-threshold emergent misalignment (a dose below the full emergent misalignment induction level). Test alignment probe performance on in-distribution vs. OOD prompts (varying domain, register, cultural framing). Measure whether OOD prompts reveal more misalignment than in-distribution evaluation.

**Why it matters:** Alignment evaluation on in-distribution data may systematically underestimate true misalignment if OOD prompts are needed to activate latent misaligned behaviors — a direct threat to the validity of current safety evaluations.

**Relevant context:** Distributional robust DPO (2502.01930); Betley et al. emergent misalignment; distributional shift alignment (2510.21977); OOD generalization challenges.
