# The Voluntary-to-Enforceable Gap: Measuring Commitment Attrition Over Time

**Problem:** AI companies signed voluntary safety commitments (e.g., White House commitments of 2023, G7 Code of Conduct). It is unclear how many of these commitments have since been incorporated into enforceable standards or quietly abandoned.

**Direction:** Catalogue all voluntary commitments made by major AI labs in 2023–2024. Track each forward: (a) incorporated into a binding standard, (b) still publicly maintained, (c) silently removed or weakened, or (d) never operationalized. Compute attrition rates by lab and commitment type.

**Why it matters:** If voluntary commitments systematically fail to transition to enforceable standards, this quantifies the governance gap that regulators need to close.

**Relevant context:** AI Objectives Institute published analysis on 'From Voluntary Guidelines to Enforceable Standards'; the White House voluntary commitments are documented in government press releases.
