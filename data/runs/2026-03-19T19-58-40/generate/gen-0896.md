# Do Formal Mechanistic Proofs Scale? Testing the Compact-Proof Method on Three Tasks

**Problem:** Gross et al. (NeurIPS 2024) showed mechanistic interpretability can produce compact formal proofs of model behavior on the Max-of-K task. Does this method generalize to other simple tasks?

**Direction:** Replicate the compact-proof methodology on two additional simple transformer tasks (e.g., modular addition and parity detection) using a small attention-only transformer. Measure proof length as a function of interpretability depth and compare across tasks to identify what properties make formal proofs tractable.

**Why it matters:** If formal proofs of model behavior are only feasible for very specific tasks, the theoretical program of verified AI is severely constrained. Empirical scoping of proof tractability is essential for setting realistic goals.

**Relevant context:** NeurIPS 2024 paper 'Compact Proofs of Model Performance via Mechanistic Interpretability' established the method. The Mechanistic Interpretability Benchmark (April 2025) now spans GPT-2, Qwen, and Llama families.
