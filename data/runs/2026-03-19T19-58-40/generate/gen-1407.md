# Cross-Misalignment Probe Transfer: Training on Deception, Testing on Sycophancy

**Problem:** Multiple misalignment types (deception, sycophancy, sandbagging) may share a latent 'misaligned intent' representation. Whether probes trained on one misalignment type generalize zero-shot to others is untested.

**Direction:** Train three linear probes on: (1) sycophancy labels from SycoBench, (2) reward hacking from MALT, (3) deceptive reasoning from SHADE-Arena. Cross-apply each probe to the other two datasets and record zero-shot AUROC. Compare to a probe trained on a union of all three.

**Why it matters:** A universal misalignment probe would be a major practical advance, allowing a single monitoring layer to cover multiple failure modes without separate training for each.

**Relevant context:** Mechanistic interpretability review (arXiv:2602.11180) suggests circuits for different misalignment types may share structure. Sparse autoencoder features capture 'persona features' predicting emergent misalignment. MALT and SHADE-Arena are both publicly available.
