# Assessment Theory Import: Applying Rubric-Based Grading to Model Oversight

**Problem:** Education research shows that rubric-based assessment is more consistent and less biased than holistic judgment. This finding has not been systematically applied to model oversight.

**Direction:** Implement rubric-based oversight (overseer grades each component of a response separately using explicit criteria) and compare to holistic oversight on the same task. Measure whether rubric-based oversight is more consistent and whether student models trained on it perform better.

**Why it matters:** If rubrics reduce overseer inconsistency and bias, they could be a cheap intervention that significantly improves oversight quality without requiring a more capable overseer.

**Relevant context:** Cross-domain transfer from education/teaching assessment; overseer bias mapping.
