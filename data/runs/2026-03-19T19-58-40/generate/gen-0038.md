# Process Capability Indices for Continuous Safety Monitoring

**Problem:** Manufacturing quality control uses Statistical Process Control (SPC) and capability indices (Cpk) to detect when a production process drifts out of acceptable bounds. AI safety monitoring lacks equivalent continuous monitoring frameworks.

**Direction:** Adapt SPC methodology to AI safety: define safety property measurements as a production process, establish control limits, and run a simulated monitoring study where a model is progressively fine-tuned on a potentially harmful distribution. Measure how quickly SPC-based monitoring detects the safety degradation vs. periodic benchmark re-evaluation.

**Why it matters:** Deployed AI systems drift over time through user interactions and fine-tuning. Continuous process monitoring from manufacturing could detect safety regressions earlier than periodic evaluations.

**Relevant context:** The 2026 International AI Safety Report emphasizes the need for post-deployment monitoring. SPC is a mature, well-understood methodology with known statistical properties.
