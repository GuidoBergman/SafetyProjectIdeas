# Control Failure + Capability Gains: Does Capability Scaling Outpace Monitor Capability?

**Problem:** If model capabilities grow faster than monitor capabilities, the control gap widens over time even if both improve in absolute terms. This is a compounding risk: control failure and capability gain interact to produce a system that is progressively harder to monitor.

**Direction:** Survey the literature to estimate capability growth rates (reasoning, coding, planning) vs. monitor accuracy growth rates over the 2022-2025 period. Fit simple extrapolation models. Estimate when, under various assumptions, the monitor capability gap becomes unrecoverable.

**Why it matters:** This is a policy-relevant finding: if the gap is widening, deployment moratoria or compute-based controls may be necessary. If it is closing, targeted investment in monitor research is sufficient. Either finding is actionable.

**Relevant context:** The MATS scalable oversight benchmark notes that weak-to-strong oversight fails below 52% for 400 Elo gaps. The rapid capability growth of models in 2024-2025 (GPT-4o, Claude 3.7, Gemini 2.5) provides a concrete dataset for this analysis.
