# How Robust Is Rhyme Planning to Prompt Perturbation? Testing Whether Planning Features Are Sensitive to Input Framing

**Problem:** It is unknown whether the poetry planning circuit is robust to changes in prompt framing (e.g., explicit vs. implicit rhyme instructions, different poetic forms) or whether it is narrow and prompt-sensitive.

**Direction:** Create a battery of poetry prompts varying: (1) explicitness of rhyme instruction, (2) rhyme scheme (AABB vs. ABAB), (3) theme specificity. For each variant, measure the strength and early onset of end-word pre-selection using probing classifiers in TransformerLens. Compute a robustness score as the correlation between probing accuracy and prompt explicitness.

**Why it matters:** Robustness of planning features determines whether they are a stable, manipulable circuit or a fragile prompt-conditioned artifact that cannot be reliably studied or steered.
