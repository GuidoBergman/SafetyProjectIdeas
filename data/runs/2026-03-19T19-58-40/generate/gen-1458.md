# StrongReject Score Distribution Across Attack Strategies: Which Attacks Produce the Highest-Quality Harmful Content?

**Problem:** Jailbreak benchmarks often report binary attack success (refused vs. not refused) but not the quality or actionability of the generated harmful content. StrongReject measures quality, but systematic comparisons across attack types are sparse.

**Direction:** Run 5 attack types (GCG suffix, prefilling, roleplay template, multi-turn escalation, multilingual rephrasing) on 40 StrongReject prompts against Llama-3-8B-Instruct. Score all outputs with the StrongReject fine-tuned evaluator. Compare score distributions per attack type using Mann-Whitney U tests.

**Why it matters:** Binary ASR inflates the apparent danger of attacks that produce vague or unhelpful harmful content. Quality-weighted scores better measure true capability uplift risk.

**Relevant context:** StrongReject benchmark (arxiv 2402.10260). JailbreakBench attack implementations. The StrongReject fine-tuned Gemma-2B evaluator is publicly available and runs on a single GPU.
