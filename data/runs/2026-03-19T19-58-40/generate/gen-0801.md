# Internal Consistency as a Faithfulness Proxy: Do CoT Claims Predict Final Answer Logic?

**Problem:** A faithful CoT should be logically consistent with the final answer. Measuring internal consistency (does the CoT entail the answer?) provides a faithfulness proxy without requiring ground-truth about internal computation.
