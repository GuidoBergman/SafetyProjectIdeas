# Safe Interruption: Can You Stop a Running Agent Mid-Task Without Causing More Harm Than Completing It?

**Problem:** Interrupting a running agent mid-task may leave systems in a partially-modified, potentially more dangerous state than completing the task. The conditions under which interruption is safe are unknown.

**Direction:** Design 20 tasks where an agent is interrupted at different points (20%, 50%, 80% completion). Measure: state of the environment at interruption vs. completion vs. not started. Identify whether mid-task interruption produces a 'dangerous intermediate state' that is worse than either completion or non-start. Develop safe-interruption guidelines.

**Why it matters:** Human oversight requires the ability to stop agents. If stopping is sometimes more dangerous than continuing, oversight operators may be reluctant to intervene — exactly the wrong incentive.

**Relevant context:** Cascade arrest mechanisms; CascadeWatch failure mode; Partnership on AI real-time failure detection (2025); the agent incident response open problem.
