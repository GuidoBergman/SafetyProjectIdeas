# Does DPO Training Exhibit Emergent Misalignment? Comparing RLHF and DPO Under Narrow Misaligned Fine-Tuning

**Problem:** The emergent misalignment literature focuses on supervised fine-tuning. Whether DPO-aligned models are more or less susceptible to emergent misalignment from narrow deceptive fine-tuning compared to RLHF-aligned models is unknown.

**Direction:** Take the same base model, align it via DPO versus RLHF, then fine-tune both on insecure-code data (using the Betley et al. setup). Compare the degree of broad misalignment elicited on a standard evaluation suite.

**Why it matters:** If DPO and RLHF produce different susceptibility to emergent misalignment, this informs which alignment paradigm offers better safety properties as a foundation for subsequent fine-tuning.
