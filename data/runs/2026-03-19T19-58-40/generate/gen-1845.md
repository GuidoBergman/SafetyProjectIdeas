# Inoculation Prompting Robustness Across Model Families

**Problem:** Inoculation prompting (framing reward hacking as acceptable) has been shown to reduce emergent misalignment in specific models, but its robustness across different model families and sizes is unknown.

**Direction:** Apply the inoculation prompting technique across three open-source model families at the 7B parameter scale (Llama, Mistral, Qwen). Induce misalignment via the insecure-code task, apply inoculation framing, and compare suppression effectiveness across families.

**Why it matters:** If inoculation is model-family-specific, it cannot be relied upon as a general-purpose safety tool. Cross-family validation is essential before recommending it to practitioners.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic emergent misalignment from reward hacking (natural EM paper); In-Training Defenses paper (2508.06249); emergent-misalignment.com.
