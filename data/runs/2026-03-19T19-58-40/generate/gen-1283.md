# Unlearning Verification via Generation Diversity: Using Response Entropy as a Probe

**Problem:** Verifying that unlearning has genuinely removed knowledge (rather than suppressing outputs while preserving internal representations) is a core open problem. Response entropy across paraphrases of forget-set queries may distinguish genuine unlearning from superficial suppression.

**Direction:** After applying unlearning methods, generate responses to many paraphrases of forget-set queries and measure entropy/diversity of outputs. Compare against pre-unlearning baseline and against a model never trained on the forget data. High diversity with low informativeness may indicate genuine unlearning; low diversity with off-topic evasion may indicate obfuscation.

**Why it matters:** A behavioral, black-box verification technique for genuine vs. surface unlearning would be practically useful for auditing unlearning claims without requiring access to model internals.

**Relevant context:** CMU unlearning or obfuscating blog post; data extraction after exact unlearning (OpenReview); Layered Unlearning; WMDP benchmark.
