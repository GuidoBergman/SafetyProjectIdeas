# Replication with Inverted Task Difficulty: Strong Overseer, Weak Student

**Problem:** All weak-to-strong experiments use a weak overseer and strong student. The converse (strong overseer, weaker student) has not been studied as a control condition to validate the methodology.

**Direction:** Replicate the weak-to-strong setup with the roles reversed (strong model provides supervision, weak model is the student). Compare how much of the strong model's capability the weak student can acquire.

**Why it matters:** The inverted case provides a baseline and sanity check for the methodology. Understanding the ceiling of what a weak model can learn from a strong overseer also bounds the weak-to-strong generalization results.

**Relevant context:** Burns et al. (2023); methodology validation.
