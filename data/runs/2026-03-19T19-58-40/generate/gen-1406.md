# Activation Probe vs. Behavioral Monitor: Which Detects Sycophancy Earlier in Conversation?

**Problem:** Behavioral monitors for sycophancy (e.g., SYCON Bench) operate at the output level. Activation probes could theoretically detect sycophantic tendency earlier. Whether activation probes fire before behavioral sycophancy manifests in multi-turn conversation is unexplored.

**Direction:** In multi-turn adversarial dialogues, record both activation probe signals (from a trained sycophancy probe) and final output-level sycophancy scores (via SYCON Bench metrics) at each turn. Measure how many turns earlier the probe fires relative to measurable behavioral sycophancy.

**Why it matters:** Early detection enables early intervention. If probes fire 1–2 turns before behavioral sycophancy manifests, this could enable preemptive system-level alerts without waiting for measurable output-level failures.

**Relevant context:** SYCON Bench measures Turn of Flip and Number of Flip in multi-turn settings. Activation steering with mean response probes paper describes real-time probe implementation. MALT has multi-turn transcripts.
