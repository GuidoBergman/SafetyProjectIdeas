# Layer Importance Stability Benchmark: Do Activation Patching Layer Rankings Replicate Across Dataset Versions?

**Problem:** A 2025 reproducibility study showed that activation patching results strongly depend on how the diagnostic dataset is constructed. No benchmark quantifies how stable layer importance rankings are when the dataset is varied by minor surface changes such as name substitution or sentence reordering.

**Direction:** Take the IOI dataset and produce three variants: original, with names replaced, and with sentence order reversed. Run activation patching on GPT-2-Small for all three variants. Compute Kendall's tau rank correlation for layer-level importance rankings across variants. Report a stability score and identify which layers are robustly important versus fragile. Publish the three dataset variants and the scoring script.

**Why it matters:** Activation patching is the most commonly used technique for circuit localization. If layer importance rankings are unstable across minor dataset variants, mechanistic claims in safety-relevant papers may be artifacts of dataset choice.

**Relevant context:** 'Reproducing and Extending Causal Insights Into Term Frequency' (arXiv 2510.06728) and 'Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching' (arXiv 2309.16042) both documented dataset sensitivity. No reusable stability scoring tool was found.
