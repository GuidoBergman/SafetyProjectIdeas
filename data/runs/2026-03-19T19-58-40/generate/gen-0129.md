# Mapping the Homogeneity Landscape Across Safety-Critical Task Types

**Problem:** We do not know whether the Artificial Hivemind effect is stronger, weaker, or differently shaped on safety-relevant tasks (e.g., harm refusals, ethical dilemmas, risk assessments) compared to open-ended creative tasks.

**Direction:** Extend the Infinity-Chat methodology to a curated set of safety-critical prompt categories—harm refusal, ethical edge cases, risk estimation—and measure intra- and inter-model similarity scores across 10+ models using existing embeddings tools.

**Why it matters:** If safety outputs are even more homogeneous than creative outputs, a single shared blind spot becomes a systemic vulnerability rather than an isolated failure.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind (NeurIPS 2025) found pronounced inter-model homogeneity on 26K open-ended queries; AILuminate benchmark tests 12 safety hazard categories with 24K+ prompts.
