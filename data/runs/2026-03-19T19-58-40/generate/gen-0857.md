# Adversarial Prompts Degrade Honest Uncertainty Reporting: Calibration Under Attack

**Problem:** Adversarial inputs that move models toward harmful outputs may simultaneously degrade the model's honest reporting of uncertainty. A jailbroken model that expresses high confidence in harmful outputs compounds an adversarial robustness failure with a honesty/calibration failure, making the output more dangerous and harder to detect via confidence thresholding.

**Direction:** Use a set of adversarially optimized prompts that achieve high ASR. For each, query the model for its confidence in the response (via verbalized probability or logit extraction). Compare calibration curves (confidence vs. actual accuracy/harmfulness) on normal vs. attacked inputs. Measure whether jailbreaks systematically inflate confidence.

**Why it matters:** Confidence-based filtering is used in safety pipelines. If adversarial attacks inflate confidence alongside causing harmful output, confidence-based safety nets fail at the worst moment. This is a directly testable compound failure with practical implications for deployment.
