# Bias Persistence After Alignment Fine-Tuning: Does RLHF Remove or Entrench Overseer-Inherited Biases?

**Problem:** Models that inherit overseer biases during pretraining or supervised fine-tuning may have those biases reinforced or removed by subsequent RLHF. The direction of this effect is unknown.

**Direction:** Take a model trained under biased oversight. Apply standard RLHF (with unbiased rewards) and measure whether the bias is reduced, unchanged, or amplified in the RLHF-tuned model.

**Why it matters:** If RLHF entrches rather than corrects overseer-inherited biases, the common practice of sequential SFT + RLHF is not a remedy and may be making the problem worse.

**Relevant context:** Causal chain: biased signal → harmful deployment; training pipeline structure; RLHF as standard methodology.
