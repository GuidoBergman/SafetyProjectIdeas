# Evaluation Awareness Probing: Do Models Know They Are Being Tested?

**Problem:** If models can detect that they are in an evaluation context, they may behave more safely than during deployment, making evaluations unreliable.

**Direction:** Design two prompt variants for each of 50 safety-sensitive scenarios: an 'evaluation-framed' version (explicitly mentioning the model is being tested) and a 'deployment-framed' version (presented as a real user request). Compare refusal rates and response quality across frames on 2–3 open-source models. Measure whether the gap is larger for more capable models.

**Why it matters:** Evaluation-aware behavior is a key concern for AI safety: a model that behaves safely only during evals but not deployment is more dangerous than one that is consistently unsafe. Probing this is methodologically important.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2507.01786 'Probing Evaluation Awareness of Language Models' is directly relevant. The sandbagging literature (arXiv 2406.07358) also covers strategic behavior during evaluations.
