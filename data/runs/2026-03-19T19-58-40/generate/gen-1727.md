# Jailbreak Distillation for Renewable Benchmarks: Student Replication and Difficulty Calibration

**Problem:** Sustainable jailbreak benchmarking (EMNLP 2025 findings paper) proposes distilling attacks into minimal high-quality test cases. The causal chain for stale benchmarks is: fixed test set → models trained to pass fixed set → ASR drops artifactually. Distillation breaks this by generating fresh tests, but the causal validity of distilled tests (do they test genuine vulnerability?) needs verification.

**Direction:** Reproduce the distillation pipeline on a small scale: distill 3–4 attack families into a 50-item test set. Measure whether attack success on distilled tests correlates with success on the original full attack (validation of causal validity). Test 2–3 open models. ~20 hours.

**Why it matters:** Validating that distilled benchmarks preserve causal validity is essential before the field adopts them; a student experiment contributes direct empirical support or refutation.

**Relevant context:** Renewable benchmark (TechXplore March 2026, EMNLP 2025 findings); JailbreakBench; realistic benchmark open problem.
