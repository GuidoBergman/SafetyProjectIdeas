# Does Shallow Alignment Extend to Multimodal Models? Probing Vision-Language Safety Depth

**Problem:** Shallow alignment has been characterized for text-only LLMs, but vision-language models (VLMs) have a fundamentally different input processing pipeline. It is unclear whether safety alignment in VLMs is shallow in the textual generation dimension, the visual encoding dimension, or both.

**Direction:** Apply the shallow alignment test to open-source VLMs (LLaVA, MiniGPT-4, InternVL) by measuring refusal rates under text-based prefilling attacks while holding image inputs constant. Then test whether adversarial images shift the token depth of alignment. Compare alignment depth with text-only versions of the same base model to isolate the contribution of multimodal training.

**Why it matters:** VLMs are increasingly deployed in high-stakes contexts. If multimodal training further shallows alignment, that is a critical deployment risk. If it deepens alignment, multimodal training could be a technique for improving text-only safety.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. ICLR 2025. Compositional adversarial attacks on VLMs: 'Jailbreak in Pieces' ICLR 2024 Spotlight. White-box multimodal jailbreaks achieving 96% ASR on MiniGPT4 (OpenReview).
