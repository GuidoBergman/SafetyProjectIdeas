# Replicating Deception Detection Failure Modes Across Monitoring Architectures

**Problem:** Linear probes achieve 90-96% sandbagging detection accuracy but do not transfer between models. It is unknown whether the same transferability failure occurs for deception detection probes trained on other deception types.

**Direction:** Train linear probes on internal activations for deception detection (using known deceptive outputs as training data) on three model families. Test cross-model transfer. Compare failure modes of sandbagging probes vs. general deception probes.

**Why it matters:** If detection probes are always model-specific, safety monitoring systems must be retrained for every new model, creating significant overhead and gaps.

**Relevant context:** Dev.to post and NeurIPS 2025 poster showed probes trained on Mistral, Gemma, and others encode sandbagging differently. Probes are not transferable between models.
