# Jailbreak Detection with Cheaper Probes: PCA-Reduced Activations vs. Full Hidden States

**Problem:** Activation-based jailbreak detection using full hidden states (dimensionality 4096+) may be too memory-intensive for real-time use. PCA-reduced representations could enable faster detection.

**Direction:** Extract hidden states at the optimal detection layer for Llama-3-8B-Instruct. Train logistic regression probes on: (a) full 4096-dim activations, (b) top-50 PCA components, (c) top-10 PCA components. Compare AUROC, inference latency, and memory footprint. Also test whether PCA directions learned on GCG attacks transfer to other attack types.

**Why it matters:** Making activation probes cheaper and faster enables deployment in latency-sensitive production systems. PCA also aids interpretability by revealing the principal directions of jailbreak-vs-benign separation.

**Relevant context:** LLM Jailbreak Detection for (Almost) Free! (arxiv 2509.14558). PCA for dimensionality reduction in linear probing is standard in interpretability work. Direct relevance to cost-effective jailbreak detection open problem.
