# Biological and Chemical Risk Dimension Evaluation: ForesightSafety Bench Follow-Up

**Problem:** ForesightSafety Bench found biology and chemistry to be among the highest-risk dimensions, yet mainstream benchmarks almost completely ignore these domains.

**Direction:** Extract or construct 20–30 test cases targeting bioweapon and chemical weapon adjacent queries, calibrated to be specific enough to test safety but not actually dangerous (following responsible disclosure guidelines). Evaluate 3–4 open-source models. Compare refusal rates and response quality to the same models on the general harm categories in AILuminate. Assess whether models treat bio/chem requests differently.

**Why it matters:** Bio and chem risks are among the highest-stakes harms possible from LLMs but are dramatically underrepresented in public benchmarks. ForesightSafety Bench identified this gap; this experiment quantifies it for accessible models.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench identified biology and chemistry as major vulnerability pillars (arXiv 2602.14135). AILuminate and HELM Safety have limited coverage of these domains. WMDP is relevant but focused on knowledge elicitation rather than refusal.
