# Adversarial Inputs Corrupt Interpretability Circuits: Circuit Discovery Unreliable Under Attack

**Problem:** Circuit discovery methods (identifying attention heads and MLP sublayers responsible for safety behaviors) assume the input distribution is natural. Adversarial inputs activate atypical circuit pathways, causing circuit-based explanations to be misleading exactly when adversarial robustness is most needed.

**Direction:** Use a published safety circuit for a small transformer (e.g., from JailbreakLens). Apply adversarial suffixes and measure whether the identified safety circuits remain active and dominant, or whether alternative pathways take over. Compare circuit activation profiles on benign, jailbroken, and adversarially-suffixed inputs.

**Why it matters:** Circuit discovery is used to design targeted interventions. If adversarial inputs reroute computation through non-identified circuits, those interventions fail and give false confidence. This compounds the mechanistic interpretability failure mode with adversarial robustness.

**Relevant context:** JailbreakLens analyzes jailbreak mechanism via representation and circuit perspectives. Circuit discovery helps detect LLM jailbreaking (OpenReview 2024).
