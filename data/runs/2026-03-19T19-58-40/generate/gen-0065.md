# Eval Monoculture + Model Homogeneity: Correlated Blind Spots at Scale

**Problem:** If all major models share architectural similarities (homogeneity) AND are all evaluated on the same narrow benchmark set (eval monoculture), the blind spots are correlated: behaviors that the benchmark doesn't cover AND that the architecture is prone to fail on will escape undetected across all deployed models simultaneously.

**Direction:** Empirically test for correlated blind spots: identify safety behaviors not covered by the top 5 widely-used benchmarks, test whether architecturally similar open-source models (same training recipe, similar sizes) fail on these behaviors at correlated rates. Measure whether architectural diversity (different training approaches) reduces correlation.

**Why it matters:** Correlated failures across all deployed models are a systemic risk orders of magnitude worse than uncorrelated individual model failures. The model_homogeneity cross-reference specifically flags this as an open concern.

**Relevant context:** The 2025 FLI Safety Index notes 'shared blind spots undermine benchmarks.' Stanford's benchmark bugs research found bugs causing systematic multi-model failures. Architectural homogeneity among frontier models is well-documented.
