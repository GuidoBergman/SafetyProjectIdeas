# Probe Evasion Stress-Test: Measuring How Quickly Fine-Tuning Breaks Activation Monitors

**Problem:** Activation probes trained on synthetic dangerous outputs may not generalize when a model is fine-tuned even slightly. Recent work shows that fine-tuning on benign concepts with a trigger phrase can drop probe TPR from ~80% to near 0%.

**Direction:** Systematically characterize the 'fragility budget': how many fine-tuning steps on benign data does it take before a deception or harmfulness probe collapses? Use a small open-weights model (e.g., Gemma-2-9b-it or Llama-3-8b), train activation probes for a target concept, then apply incremental fine-tuning on unrelated tasks and track probe performance. Plot a fragility curve and test whether training the probe on model-generated (rather than hand-crafted) examples extends robustness.

**Why it matters:** Labs deploying probe-first cascades (Anthropic, Google DeepMind) need to know their maintenance interval. Quantifying the fragility budget gives concrete guidance on when probes must be retrained.

**Relevant context:** December 2025 research showed fine-tuning with a trigger phrase reduced deception probe TPR to 0-33% (from 55-88%) on Gemma-2-9b-it. Anthropic's recommended directions list activation-monitor generalization as an open problem.
