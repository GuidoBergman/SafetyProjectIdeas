# Varying Evaluator Identity in Red-Teaming: Human vs. LLM vs. Rule-Based

**Problem:** Red-teaming uses a mix of human evaluators, LLM judges, and rule-based filters. It is unclear how much evaluator type affects red-teaming outcomes.

**Direction:** Design a small red-teaming experiment on a single model with 50 harmful prompts. Evaluate each response using three methods: a keyword-based filter, an open-source LLM judge (e.g., Llama-3-based), and human annotation (using the student team). Compute agreement rates across all three and identify the categories of responses where they most disagree.

**Why it matters:** Red-teaming is a core safety methodology but its reliability depends on evaluator choice. Quantifying disagreement across evaluator types identifies where human review is most necessary and least substitutable.

**Relevant context:** RAND's Judge Reliability Harness (2025) and AISI's Skewed Score framework both address autograder reliability. This experiment applies those findings specifically to red-teaming contexts.
