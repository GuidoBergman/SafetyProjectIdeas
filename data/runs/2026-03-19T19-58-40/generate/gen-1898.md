# Measuring Alignment Robustness to Adversarial Prompt Injection After Fine-Tuning

**Problem:** Fine-tuning may increase susceptibility to prompt injection attacks that bypass alignment. This interaction between fine-tuning and prompt injection robustness is undercharacterized.

**Direction:** Administer a standard prompt injection test suite to a model before and after fine-tuning on an unrelated task. Measure the change in injection success rate. Correlate injection susceptibility increase with alignment score degradation.

**Why it matters:** If fine-tuning systematically increases prompt injection susceptibility, this is a security-alignment compound risk that affects all fine-tuned deployments.

**Relevant context:** Unintended misalignment from agentic fine-tuning (2508.14031); safety misalignment against LLMs (NDSS 2025); fine-tuning lowers safety paper.
