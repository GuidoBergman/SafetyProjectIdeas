# Steering-Augmented Constitutional AI

**Problem:** Constitutional AI (CAI) relies on prompting to enforce principles; steering vectors offer a potentially more robust mechanistic alternative.

**Direction:** Replace CAI's prompting-based principle enforcement with concept vectors representing each constitutional principle. Compare the two on downstream policy compliance and adversarial robustness.

**Why it matters:** Prompt-based constraints are fragile to jailbreaks. Mechanistic enforcement via steering may be harder to circumvent and more transparent.

**Relevant context:** RepE work shows values can be encoded and steered. Beaglehole et al. demonstrate multi-concept simultaneous steering via weighted vector sums.
