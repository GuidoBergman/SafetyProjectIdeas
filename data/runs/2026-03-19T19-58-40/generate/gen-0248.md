# Measuring Governance Language Divergence Between Jurisdictions Over Time

**Problem:** As the US, EU, UK, and China develop AI governance frameworks independently, the language and concepts they use may diverge, complicating international coordination.

**Direction:** Build a time-stamped corpus of major governance documents from each jurisdiction (2020–2026). Use cosine similarity on TF-IDF or sentence embeddings to measure semantic convergence or divergence over time.

**Why it matters:** Empirical evidence for or against 'governance fragmentation' is needed to motivate or evaluate international harmonization efforts.
