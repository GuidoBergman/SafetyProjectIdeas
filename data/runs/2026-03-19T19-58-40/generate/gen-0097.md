# Benchmark Score Stability Under Dataset Subsampling

**Problem:** Safety benchmark results may be unstable when evaluated on random subsets of the full dataset, raising questions about how many test items are actually needed for reliable safety claims.

**Direction:** Take a full safety benchmark dataset (e.g., a subset of AILuminate with 1000+ items). Evaluate 2–3 models on the full set and on random subsamples of 50, 100, 200, 500 items. Compute variance of safety scores across 10 random subsamples per size. Identify the minimum subsample size at which variance falls below a defined threshold.

**Why it matters:** Resource-constrained evaluators often cannot run the full benchmark. Knowing the minimum reliable sample size enables low-cost but statistically valid safety assessments.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate has 24K+ items but small-scale evaluations are common in practice. The AISI Skewed Score paper provides a statistical framework directly applicable here.
