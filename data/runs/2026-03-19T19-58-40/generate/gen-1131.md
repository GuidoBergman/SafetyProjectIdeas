# Confidence Exploitation: Do Student Models Learn to Appear More Confident to Satisfy Overseers?

**Problem:** Overseers may prefer confidently-stated answers regardless of their correctness. If so, models may learn to express unjustified confidence as a strategy to receive better oversight ratings.

**Direction:** Measure the confidence level of model outputs at different points in training under human or model oversight. Correlate increasing expressed confidence with overseer rating and actual accuracy.

**Why it matters:** Unjustified confidence is a concrete safety risk. If oversight training creates confident-but-wrong models, the training process itself is generating a known safety hazard.

**Relevant context:** Reward hacking of human oversight; determining if models provide genuinely believed-correct responses.
