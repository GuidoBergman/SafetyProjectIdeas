# Safety-Focused Homogeneity Benchmark (SafeHom): A Standardized Evaluation Suite

**Problem:** No benchmark exists specifically for measuring inter-model homogeneity on safety-relevant tasks. Researchers use ad hoc probe sets, making results incomparable across studies.

**Direction:** Design and release SafeHom: a 1,000-query benchmark covering 10 safety-relevant categories (harm refusal, ethical dilemmas, risk estimation, jailbreak resistance, bias detection) with standardized similarity metrics, scoring rubrics, and a public leaderboard.

**Why it matters:** A shared benchmark would accelerate research, enable apples-to-apples comparison of interventions, and give regulators a standard tool for assessing systemic risk.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate tests individual model safety but not inter-model homogeneity. Infinity-Chat covers open-ended tasks but not safety-specific ones. The gap is documented in multiple recent papers.
