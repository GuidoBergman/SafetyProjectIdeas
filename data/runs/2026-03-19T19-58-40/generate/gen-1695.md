# Safety-Capability Correlation Probes: Do Capability Elicitation Prompts Also Elicit Unsafe Activations?

**Problem:** Prompts designed to elicit high capability (e.g., 'think step by step', 'you are an expert') may also shift activations toward regions associated with higher risk of unsafe outputs. If so, capability elicitation and safety risk are coupled in activation space.

**Direction:** For a small open-weights model, collect activations under standard prompts vs. capability-eliciting prompts across 100+ tasks. Train a simple probe predicting whether capability-eliciting variants are used. Measure the cosine similarity between this 'capability elicitation' direction and known safety-relevant directions (harmfulness probe, deception probe). A high correlation would suggest that prompting for high capability inadvertently increases safety risk.

**Why it matters:** If capability elicitation and safety risk are activation-space neighbors, this has direct implications for how operators should configure agentic systems—high-capability modes may require more aggressive monitoring.

**Relevant context:** Activation probe methods are established (Anthropic, KAUST sycophancy work). The capability-safety tradeoff is a central concern in AI safety. This project operationalizes it at the activation level for the first time.
