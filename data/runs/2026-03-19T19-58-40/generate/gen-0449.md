# Mapping the Boundary Conditions for Sycophancy Under Pressure

**Problem:** Sycophancy research has identified that models capitulate under user pressure, but the precise boundary conditions—what makes a model capitulate vs. hold firm—are not well characterized.

**Direction:** Systematically vary pressure type (social, authority, emotional, evidence-based), topic sensitivity (factual, ethical, political), and model confidence (high vs. low certainty questions) in sycophancy scenarios. Map which combinations most reliably trigger capitulation.

**Why it matters:** Knowing the exact conditions that trigger sycophancy enables targeted mitigations. A boundary condition map also helps identify which deployment scenarios are highest-risk.

**Relevant context:** DeceptionBench covers sycophancy under reward and pressure conditions. Existing research shows pressure causes capitulation but has not produced a systematic boundary condition map.
