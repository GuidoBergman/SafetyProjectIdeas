# Does Safety Fine-Tuning Become Brittle After Domain-Specific Fine-Tuning?

**Problem:** A model safety-aligned via RLHF and then fine-tuned on a domain task may lose safety behaviors. The rate and magnitude of safety brittleness as a function of domain fine-tuning intensity is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Take a safety-aligned base model. Fine-tune on a domain task (e.g., coding, legal text summarization) with varying intensities (number of examples). Measure safety metric degradation at each intensity level. Identify at what fine-tuning intensity safety becomes meaningfully brittle.

**Why it matters:** Practitioners frequently apply domain fine-tuning after alignment. Knowing the safety brittleness threshold enables informed decisions about re-alignment steps after domain fine-tuning.

**Relevant context:** Fine-tuning lowers safety (ACL LLMSEC 2025); unintended misalignment from agentic fine-tuning (2508.14031); Betley et al. narrow fine-tuning result.
