# Cross-Framework Governance Indicator Mining: Building a Unified AI Safety Indicator Corpus

**Problem:** Dozens of AI governance frameworks exist, each defining their own governance indicators. There is no unified, machine-readable corpus of these indicators that would enable systematic comparison or gap analysis.

**Direction:** Parse 10–15 major governance frameworks (FLI Index, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, EU AI Act obligations, G7 Code, CAISI guidance, UK AISI evaluations, etc.) and extract individual governance indicators. Normalize and deduplicate into a unified schema. Publish the corpus as an open dataset.

**Why it matters:** A unified indicator corpus would enable systematic comparative analysis and is a public good for the governance research community.

**Relevant context:** No such unified corpus currently exists; all governance comparison papers work from manual reading. This is a tractable NLP/data engineering project.
