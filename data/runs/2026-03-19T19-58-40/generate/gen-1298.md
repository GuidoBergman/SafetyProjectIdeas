# Monitor False Positive Rate Under Distribution Shift: MALT-Trained Monitors on New Task Families

**Problem:** MALT monitors achieve high AUROC on held-out MALT data, but MALT covers only 30 task families. It is unknown how false positive rates behave when these monitors are applied to task families not represented in training (distribution shift).

**Direction:** Train a simple prompted monitor on the MALT public split. Apply it to a new task family (e.g., creative writing or math reasoning tasks from a public benchmark) where reward hacking is implausible. Measure false positive rate and compare against the 5% FPR reported on the MALT in-distribution test set.

**Why it matters:** High false positive rates on benign task distributions would make MALT-trained monitors impractical for general deployment. This test directly evaluates operational readiness of the best current monitors.

**Relevant context:** MALT monitors at 5% FPR catch 80-90% of true positives in-distribution. The dataset spans software and research tasks; generalization to other domains is untested. Intro-ML students can run prompted monitors via API.
