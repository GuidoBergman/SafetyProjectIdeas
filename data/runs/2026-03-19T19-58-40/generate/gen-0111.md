# Benchmark Blind Spots for Embodied AI Safety: Adapting ForesightSafety Bench to Text Simulation

**Problem:** ForesightSafety Bench covers embodied AI safety (robots, physical systems) as a risk pillar, but most researchers lack access to robotic hardware. Text-simulated embodied scenarios may reveal whether the risk is already present in language model reasoning.

**Direction:** Adapt embodied AI safety scenarios from ForesightSafety Bench into purely text-based formats: describe a robot in a scenario and ask the model to specify actions. Use 20–30 scenarios covering physical harm, unauthorized access to spaces, and dangerous manipulation. Compare refusal rates for embodied-framed vs. direct-harm-framed equivalents.

**Why it matters:** Embodied AI is a growing deployment domain. If text-based reasoning already shows safety gaps for embodied scenarios, this provides early warning before physical systems are widely deployed.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench covers embodied AI safety and found significant vulnerabilities (arXiv 2602.14135). This is a follow-up that makes those findings accessible without robotic hardware.
