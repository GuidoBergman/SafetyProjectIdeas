# Concept Probe Calibration: Are Probe Scores Meaningful as Probabilities

**Problem:** Concept probe outputs are typically used as binary (above/below threshold) signals; it is unknown whether they are calibrated as probabilities, which would make them more informative for graded safety decisions.

**Direction:** Apply probability calibration methods (Platt scaling, temperature scaling, isotonic regression) to concept probe outputs for toxicity and hallucination, measuring calibration error and testing whether calibrated probe scores correlate better with human safety ratings than uncalibrated scores.

**Why it matters:** Calibrated probe scores would enable graded safety decisions (e.g., 'trigger review at 80% probability') rather than binary thresholds, making the monitoring system more useful.

**Relevant context:** Calibration of neural network outputs is a standard ML practice. Concept probe calibration has not been studied. Beaglehole et al. report accuracy metrics, not calibration.
