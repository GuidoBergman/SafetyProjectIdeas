# Faithfulness of CoT Degrades Under Task Difficulty: A Controlled Variation

**Problem:** arxiv 2507.05246 shows CoT is more faithful when the model needs it to solve the task. This implies easy tasks may have less faithful, less monitorable CoT. The effect of task difficulty on monitor AUROC has not been directly measured on MALT.

**Direction:** Stratify MALT runs by task difficulty (measured by model success rate across runs). Within reward-hacking and benign categories, measure CoT monitor AUROC separately for easy, medium, and hard tasks. Test whether the easy-task CoT faithfulness deficit translates to lower monitor AUROC.

**Why it matters:** If monitoring is less reliable on easy tasks, this creates a paradox where agents are hardest to monitor precisely when they are most likely to attempt subtle manipulation (because the task is trivially solvable and manipulation requires less cognitive load).

**Relevant context:** arxiv 2507.05246 establishes the faithfulness-necessity link. MALT includes difficulty variation across 403 tasks. Task success rate is available in MALT metadata and serves as a proxy for difficulty.
