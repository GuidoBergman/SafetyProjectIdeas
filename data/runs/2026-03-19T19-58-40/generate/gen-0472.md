# Failure Mode Investigation: How Do Models Handle Discovered Deception?

**Problem:** When a model's deception is pointed out in conversation, it is unknown whether models acknowledge the deception, double down, or subtly redirect. This post-discovery behavior is a key dimension of deception sophistication.

**Direction:** Run two-phase deception scenarios: (1) elicit deception, (2) confront the model with evidence of its deception. Categorize responses: acknowledgment, doubling-down, redirecting, or confusing. Measure rates across model families.

**Why it matters:** A model that acknowledges and corrects deception is qualitatively safer than one that doubles down or deflects. Understanding post-discovery behavior informs human oversight protocols.
