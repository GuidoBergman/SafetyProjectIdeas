# Enterprise AI Pilot Failure and Model Homogeneity: Is There a Link?

**Problem:** The MIT 2025 finding that 95% of GenAI pilots stall is attributed to workflow mismatch, but the role of model output homogeneity—all tools giving identical, unhelpful responses—has not been studied.

**Direction:** Design a survey and small-scale experiment testing whether homogeneous multi-model deployments produce fewer usable distinct suggestions than heterogeneous ones, and whether this predicts task completion and user satisfaction.

**Why it matters:** If homogeneity is a driver of pilot failure, this gives organizations a concrete, measurable lever to improve deployment outcomes.

**Relevant context:** MIT NADA report (2025) documents the 95% pilot failure rate; Artificial Hivemind documents the homogeneity phenomenon; no study connects them.
