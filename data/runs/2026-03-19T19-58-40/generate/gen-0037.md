# A/B Testing Statistical Power for Safety Evaluation Comparisons

**Problem:** Claims that model A is safer than model B are often made based on benchmark score differences that may not be statistically significant. Clinical trials require power analysis before running studies. AI safety evals rarely report confidence intervals or effect sizes.

**Direction:** Conduct a power analysis study: for a set of widely-used safety benchmarks, determine the minimum sample size needed to detect meaningful safety differences between models. Run experiments to measure whether existing benchmark sizes have adequate statistical power. Produce guidelines for minimum benchmark sizes.

**Why it matters:** Underpowered safety comparisons lead to false confidence in safety improvements or false alarms. This foundational statistical work would improve the credibility of every safety evaluation.

**Relevant context:** The 2025 FLI AI Safety Index notes that most companies show 'weak evidence of systematic safety processes.' Statistical rigor from clinical trials methodology would directly address this.
