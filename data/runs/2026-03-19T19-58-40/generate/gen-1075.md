# Neuron Polysemanticity Spectrum Tool: A Quantitative Layer-by-Layer Polysemanticity Profile

**Problem:** Polysemanticity (neurons activating for multiple unrelated concepts) is widely cited as a problem but rarely measured quantitatively in a standardized way across layers of the same model. Without a layer-by-layer polysemanticity profile, it is unclear at which layers SAEs are most needed or most effective.

**Direction:** For each layer of GPT-2-Small, compute a polysemanticity score for each neuron: the entropy of concept activation distribution estimated by sampling from a diverse prompt corpus and clustering top activating prompts. Report a per-layer average polysemanticity profile. Compare before and after SAE decomposition of the same layer. Use TransformerLens for activation extraction.

**Why it matters:** Knowing which layers are most polysemantic allows safety researchers to prioritize where to deploy SAEs or probes. If no such profile exists, SAEs may be applied at layers where they add least value.

**Relevant context:** The linear representation hypothesis and polysemanticity are foundational to the motivation for SAEs. SAEBench measures interpretability but not the baseline polysemanticity of individual neurons before SAE decomposition.
