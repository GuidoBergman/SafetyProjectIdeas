# Applying Contagion Models from Epidemiology to Estimate Adversarial Attack Spread Across AI Ecosystems

**Problem:** Once a successful adversarial attack is discovered, how quickly and widely does it spread across the deployed AI ecosystem? Epidemiological contagion models can characterize this spread but have not been applied to AI.

**Direction:** Model adversarial attack spread using an SIR (Susceptible-Infected-Recovered) framework where susceptibility is determined by inter-model similarity. Use empirical transferability data to calibrate the model and estimate the 'basic reproduction number' R0 for adversarial attacks.

**Why it matters:** R0 > 1 for adversarial attacks would mean that a discovered attack will spread to infect the majority of similar models—a public health framing that gives policymakers an intuitive and actionable risk metric.

**Relevant context:** Adversarial attack transferability is well-documented (arXiv 2307.15043). SIR models are well-understood and computationally simple. The application to AI attack propagation is novel.
