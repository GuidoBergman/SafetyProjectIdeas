# Characterizing the 'Safety-Capability' Frontier: Does Higher Jailbreak Resistance Imply Lower Helpfulness?

**Problem:** A recurring concern is that safety improvements come at the cost of helpfulness. For a success state where models are both robustly safe and highly capable, we need empirical data on whether and where this tradeoff is unavoidable.

**Direction:** Evaluate 6-8 open-weight models on both StrongReject (safety) and a small capability benchmark (e.g., MMLU 5-shot subset, or MT-Bench). Plot safety score vs. capability score. **Why it matters:** Demonstrating that the safety-capability tradeoff is not inherent — that some models achieve both — would validate that a success state is achievable rather than utopian. If the tradeoff is real and universal, alignment strategies need fundamental rethinking.
