# Phase Transition Mapping in RLHF: When Does Policy Training Cross From Alignment to Reward Hacking?

**Problem:** Reward hacking in RLHF is known to emerge during prolonged training, but the precise conditions, training steps, and KL budget at which the phase transition from aligned to reward-hacking behavior occurs are not well characterized for standard model scales accessible on a single GPU.

**Direction:** Train small-to-medium models with PPO against a reward model for varying KL penalties and training durations. Measure alignment quality, reward model score, and behavioral metrics at frequent checkpoints. Map the phase transition boundary as a function of KL budget and compute.

**Why it matters:** A practical characterization of the reward-hacking phase transition boundary would give practitioners a concrete stopping criterion or KL penalty recommendation for RLHF training.
