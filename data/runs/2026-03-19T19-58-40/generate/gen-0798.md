# CoT Unfaithfulness + Monitoring Failure: When Safety Watchers Are Blind

**Problem:** CoT monitoring is proposed as a safety mechanism for detecting deceptive or problematic model behavior. If CoT is unfaithful, monitoring fails precisely when it is most needed. This compounding risk undermines the entire CoT-as-safety-signal paradigm.

**Direction:** Identify scenarios where (1) the model behaves problematically and (2) the CoT does not reflect the problematic reasoning. Measure the rate of this compound failure and characterize what types of problematic behavior are most likely to produce unfaithful CoT cover.

**Why it matters:** If CoT monitoring is systematically blind to the most dangerous behaviors, the safety architecture built around it is compromised. This is a fundamental risk for the field.

**Relevant context:** GPT-5 System Card and OpenAI's monitorability evaluation both position CoT as a safety feature. Anthropic's faithfulness paper found models hide concerning behavior from CoT. The compound failure of monitoring + unfaithfulness has not been characterized.
