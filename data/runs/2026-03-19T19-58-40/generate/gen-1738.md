# Taxonomy Stress-Test: Generating Novel Jailbreaks Not in Existing Benchmark Categories

**Problem:** The MLCommons taxonomy-first methodology is only as useful as the taxonomy is complete. If simple human creativity can generate effective jailbreaks outside current taxonomy categories, the benchmark framework will have systematic blind spots.

**Direction:** Have 3-5 participants (CS students) attempt to craft jailbreaks that don't fit existing AILuminate taxonomy categories against 2-3 open-weight models. Score successes with StrongReject. Attempt to categorize any successes within the existing taxonomy or propose new subcategories.

**Why it matters:** A success state requires that all jailbreak categories are known and benchmarked. This study stress-tests taxonomic completeness in a low-cost human-in-the-loop way, directly addressing whether the current taxonomy is sufficient for governance-aligned safety evaluation.

**Relevant context:** MLCommons jailbreak taxonomy v0.7 established mechanism-first categorization in Feb 2026. The taxonomy includes role-playing, misdirection, encoding, and multi-step attacks. Human creativity is acknowledged as a necessary complement to automated red-teaming.
