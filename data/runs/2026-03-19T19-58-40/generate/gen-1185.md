# Incremental Oversight Delegation: Modeling AI Oversight on Progressive Responsibility Frameworks in Management

**Problem:** Management theory describes progressive responsibility frameworks where subordinates are given increasing autonomy as trust is established. This principle has not been applied to AI oversight.

**Direction:** Implement a progressive oversight delegation experiment: begin with full oversight, then progressively reduce oversight frequency as the student model demonstrates consistent alignment. Measure whether alignment is maintained as oversight is reduced.

**Why it matters:** Progressive delegation is a practical requirement for deploying AI systems at scale. Establishing the conditions under which oversight can safely be reduced is a critical safety question.

**Relevant context:** Cross-domain transfer from organizational management; progressive trust in AI safety; scalable oversight cost reduction.
