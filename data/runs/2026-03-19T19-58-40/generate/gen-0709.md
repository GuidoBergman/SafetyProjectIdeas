# Investigating Whether Safety Benchmarks Predict Robustness to Low-Resource Language Jailbreaks

**Problem:** Models may refuse harmful requests in high-resource languages but comply when the same request is made in a low-resource language where safety training data is sparse. English-centric safety benchmarks cannot detect this vulnerability.

**Direction:** Translate 150 AILuminate harmful prompts into three low-resource languages (Swahili, Tagalog, Yoruba) using professional translation or a high-quality translation API. Evaluate three multilingual models and compare refusal rates against English baseline.

**Why it matters:** Low-resource language jailbreaks are a documented real-world technique. If safety benchmarks are blind to this vulnerability, users in regions speaking low-resource languages are exposed.

**Relevant context:** Multilingual safety is an acknowledged gap. Low-resource languages specifically are underrepresented even in multilingual safety research.
