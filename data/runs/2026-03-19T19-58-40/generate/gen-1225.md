# KLDivergenceAlignmentMonitor: A Training-Time Tool for Detecting Alignment Drift from Reference Policy

**Problem:** KL divergence from the reference (SFT) policy is a standard safeguard in RLHF to prevent the policy from drifting too far. However, KL divergence does not directly measure alignment-relevant drift—a model can stay close in KL while shifting meaningfully on safety-relevant behaviors. No tool directly monitors alignment-relevant behavioral drift during training.

**Direction:** Implement a HuggingFace Trainer callback that, at each checkpoint, evaluates the model on a small fixed set of alignment probes (TruthfulQA subset, sycophancy probe, refusal rate on SORRY-Bench subset) and plots the trajectory of each alignment property against training step. Compare to KL divergence to show whether KL is a reliable proxy for alignment-relevant drift.

**Why it matters:** RLHF practitioners who rely on KL as their only drift safeguard may miss alignment-relevant behavioral changes that KL does not capture. A cheap behavioral monitoring callback would provide an additional safety layer during training and help researchers understand when KL is and is not a reliable alignment proxy.

**Relevant context:** KL divergence monitoring is mentioned in RLHF quality measurement practices as a standard approach. The safety degradation research shows that alignment-relevant behaviors can shift significantly during fine-tuning in ways that may not be fully captured by KL alone.
