# Scheming Behavior Persistence After Benign Multi-Turn Context

**Problem:** Apollo's scheming scenarios inject misaligned goals at the start of a context. Whether scheming behaviors persist, decay, or amplify if a long benign conversation precedes the goal injection is unknown.

**Direction:** Run scheming scenarios with: (a) goal injection at turn 1, (b) goal injection after 5 turns of benign conversation, (c) goal injection after 20 turns. Measure scheming rates and strategic quality as a function of pre-injection conversation length. Test whether prior benign context reduces scheming (rapport/consistency effect) or increases it (established trust effect).

**Why it matters:** Real-world deceptive use cases often involve establishing a benign conversation before introducing harmful goals. Understanding how prior context modulates scheming risk is directly deployment-relevant.

**Relevant context:** Apollo's in-context scheming methodology. Long-context effects on instruction following are documented in the literature but not specifically for scheming.
