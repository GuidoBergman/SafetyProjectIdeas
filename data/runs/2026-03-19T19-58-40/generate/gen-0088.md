# Persona-Based Safety Evaluation: Does System Prompt Identity Change Refusal Behavior?

**Problem:** Many deployed models are given system prompts that assign personas (e.g., 'You are a medical assistant'). It is unknown whether standard safety benchmarks generalize to persona-conditioned models.

**Direction:** Take an existing safety benchmark subset (e.g., 200 items from AILuminate). Prepend 5 different system prompts with different personas (default, medical, legal, creative writer, customer service). Measure refusal rates and response quality across personas for 2–3 open-source models. Test whether persona-conditioning degrades safety performance.

**Why it matters:** Most real deployments use system prompts. Benchmark evaluations that ignore system prompts may overestimate safety. This experiment is highly reproducible and directly relevant to enterprise AI safety.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate (24K+ items) is suitable for subsampling. The 2025 AI Agent Index documented diverse system prompt configurations in deployed agents, but safety impacts are understudied.
