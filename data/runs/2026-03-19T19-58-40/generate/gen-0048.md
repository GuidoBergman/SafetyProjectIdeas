# Causal Analysis of Over-Refusal: The False Positive Chain

**Problem:** Over-refusal creates its own harm chain: safety benchmark optimized for refusal rate → model tuned to maximize refusal → model over-refuses legitimate requests → users frustrated → users switch to less safe alternatives → net safety reduction. This counterfactual harm is rarely analyzed.

**Direction:** Quantify the over-refusal/under-refusal tradeoff curve for open-source models using ORFuzz-style evaluation. Model the counterfactual: if over-refusal causes X% of users to switch to unfiltered models, what is the net harm? At what refusal rate is net harm minimized?

**Why it matters:** Safety benchmarks that optimize only for harm refusal may cause net harm through displacement effects. This causal analysis reframes what 'passing' a safety benchmark actually means for real-world safety.

**Relevant context:** ORFuzz (arxiv 2508.11222) specifically studies over-refusal as a failure mode. The 2026 International AI Safety Report notes 'loopholes in evaluations' being exploited; over-refusal optimization is one such loophole.
