# MultilingualTruthfulQA-Alignment: A Cross-Lingual Consistency Benchmark for RLHF Models

**Problem:** RLHF training typically uses English preference data, but models are deployed multilingually. No benchmark measures whether truthfulness and safety alignment properties trained on English data transfer consistently to other languages, or whether alignment 'degrades in translation'.

**Direction:** Using existing multilingual TruthfulQA translations (e.g., the HiTZ multilingual dataset), evaluate RLHF-trained vs. base models on 3–4 languages. Measure the 'alignment transfer coefficient'—how much of the English truthfulness improvement from RLHF is retained in each target language. Identify which question categories show the largest cross-lingual alignment gap.

**Why it matters:** If RLHF alignment does not transfer well cross-lingually, models deployed in non-English contexts may appear aligned in English evaluation while behaving unsafely in the target language. This is a practical deployment risk with no current measurement standard.

**Relevant context:** The 'Truth Knows No Language' paper (arxiv 2502.09387) extends TruthfulQA multilingually and finds that evaluation methodology gaps persist across languages. HiTZ multilingual TruthfulQA dataset is publicly available. Cross-lingual alignment transfer is not measured in RewardBench or standard RLHF evaluation pipelines.
