# Replicating Safety Scores Under Adversarial Suffixes (GCG Attack)

**Problem:** GCG (Greedy Coordinate Gradient) adversarial suffixes are known to reduce refusal rates. Whether safety benchmark scores remain valid for adversarially suffixed inputs, and whether scores predict GCG robustness, is unstudied.

**Direction:** Generate GCG adversarial suffixes for a subset of AILuminate prompts using an open-source attack toolkit. Compare safety scores with and without suffixes for two models. Compute the correlation between baseline safety score and suffix attack success rate.

**Why it matters:** If safety benchmark scores do not predict robustness to adversarial attacks, they measure compliance under benign inputs only — not genuine alignment robustness.

**Relevant context:** GCG attack implementations are publicly available. AILuminate public prompts provide the test set. The correlation between benchmark safety score and adversarial robustness is a fundamental validity question.
