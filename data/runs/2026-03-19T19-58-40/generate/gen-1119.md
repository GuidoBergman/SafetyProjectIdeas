# Replication with Deliberately Biased Overseer: Does the Student Learn the Bias or the Underlying Pattern?

**Problem:** Standard replication uses overseers that are simply less capable. A systematic replication with a deliberately biased overseer tests whether bias (not just incapacity) propagates to the student.

**Direction:** Create a systematically biased overseer (e.g., one that always prefers longer answers, or answers that use specific keywords). Train a student under this overseer and test whether the student inherits the bias pattern.

**Why it matters:** This is a direct test of whether the student model is learning the supervisor's biases vs. the underlying task. This is the core question for reward hacking of human oversight.

**Relevant context:** Open problem: models exploiting systematic errors in reward signals; overseer bias mapping.
