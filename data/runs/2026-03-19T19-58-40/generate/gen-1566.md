# Minimal Viable Monitor: What Is the Simplest Monitor That Achieves Acceptable Detection on SHADE-Arena?

**Problem:** There is an implicit assumption in AI control research that sophisticated monitors (large LLMs, complex scaffolding) are necessary for reliable detection, but this has not been tested against simple baselines. The minimal viable monitor has not been identified.

**Direction:** Systematically implement monitors of increasing complexity on the SHADE-Arena public task pairs: (1) keyword blacklist, (2) regex pattern matching on tool calls, (3) n-gram language model anomaly score, (4) small prompted LLM (GPT-4o-mini), (5) large prompted LLM (GPT-4o). Compare AUC and latency. Determine the 'knee of the curve' where adding complexity stops paying off.

**Why it matters:** If a regex filter achieves 60% of the detection benefit of a GPT-4o monitor at 1% of the latency, that is a major practical finding. It would democratize monitoring for resource-constrained deployments and focus research on what LLM monitors actually add.

**Relevant context:** The SHADE-Arena paper evaluates LLM-based monitors but does not compare against simpler non-LLM baselines. This gap is straightforward to fill with basic Python.
