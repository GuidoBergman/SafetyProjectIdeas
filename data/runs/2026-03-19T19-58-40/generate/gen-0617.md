# Measuring How Many Turns of Crescendo Are Actually Needed

**Problem:** Multi-turn Crescendo attacks gradually escalate toward harmful content. The number of turns required for success, and whether this varies by harm category, has not been systematically characterized.

**Direction:** Implement Crescendo-style multi-turn escalation for 5 harm categories (bioweapons, self-harm, fraud, hate speech, CBRN) across 3 open-source models. Record the turn number at which the model first produces a policy-violating response. Plot turn-to-success distributions per category and model.

**Why it matters:** If most jailbreaks succeed within 3 turns, current conversation-length limits in production systems may be insufficient even when set to 5 turns. If some harm categories require 10+ turns, that informs tiered conversation monitoring strategies.

**Relevant context:** MultiBreak benchmark (OpenReview uJgfj5EJ2W) covers 7,152 multi-turn prompts. Giskard's analysis of Crescendo shows compounding disinhibition. The per-category turn distribution has not been reported in published work.
