# Varying the Faithfulness Metric in Circuit Evaluation: Does Choice of Ablation Type Change Which Circuit Is Found Best?

**Problem:** Circuit evaluation relies on faithfulness metrics. Different ablation methods (zero ablation, mean ablation, resample ablation) can yield different circuits. A direct comparison on the same task has not been published for recent methods.

**Direction:** On the IOI task using GPT-2-small, run ACDC and attribution patching with three ablation types: zero, mean, and resample. For each combination, record the discovered circuit, its edge count, and its faithfulness score under the other two metrics. Produce a 3x2 confusion matrix of cross-metric faithfulness.

**Why it matters:** If the best circuit under one metric is not faithful under another, reported interpretability results may be metric artifacts rather than genuine mechanistic discoveries. This directly undermines trust in published circuits.

**Relevant context:** ACDC and attribution patching are implemented in TransformerLens. IOI circuit (Wang et al. 2022) is the canonical benchmark. MIB paper noted edge-level circuits outperform node-level, but did not systematically vary ablation type. Single GPU, ~10 hours compute.
