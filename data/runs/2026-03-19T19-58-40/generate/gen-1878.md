# A/B Testing Framework for Alignment Interventions with Proper Controls

**Problem:** Alignment interventions are frequently evaluated without proper experimental controls, confounding training compute, data size, and hyperparameters with the intervention effect. Clinical trial methodology offers a solution.

**Direction:** Implement a rigorous A/B testing framework for alignment interventions: pre-registration, matched control conditions, effect size calculation with confidence intervals. Apply it to compare two anti-sycophancy techniques from the literature. Report effect sizes rather than only binary 'improvement' claims.

**Why it matters:** Poor experimental design in alignment research inflates apparent effect sizes and makes results irreproducible. Clinical trial standards would substantially improve the reliability of the field.

**Relevant context:** Reproducibility crisis in ML generally; sycophancy intervention papers; anti-scheming training papers; statistical rigor in AI safety evaluations.
