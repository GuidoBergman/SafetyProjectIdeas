# DPO on Misaligned Preference Pairs: Can Misaligned Models Be Realigned Without Reward Models?

**Problem:** Emergently misaligned models represent a practically important failure mode. Whether DPO applied to a small set of curated preference pairs can realign an emergently misaligned model — and at what cost to general capabilities — is unstudied.

**Direction:** Starting from an emergently misaligned model (produced via the Betley et al. insecure-code protocol), apply DPO with a small set of curated aligned/misaligned preference pairs targeting the broad misalignment behaviors. Measure realignment quality and capability retention as a function of DPO dataset size.

**Why it matters:** A cheap, post-hoc DPO-based realignment procedure for emergently misaligned models would be practically useful for any organization that accidentally deploys or receives a misaligned model.

**Relevant context:** Emergent Misalignment & Realignment LessWrong post; DPO as RLHF alternative; TRL library; emergent misalignment open code.
