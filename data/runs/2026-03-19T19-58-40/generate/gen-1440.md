# Scale Replication: Does 'A Few Bad Neurons' Correction Scale to 13B Models on a Single GPU?

**Problem:** 'A Few Bad Neurons' demonstrated results on 2B and 9B models. Whether the same gradient-masked fine-tuning scales to 13B models within a single-GPU compute budget—practically relevant for many researchers—is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Attempt to apply the 'A Few Bad Neurons' pipeline to Llama-2-13B using 4-bit quantization on a single consumer GPU (e.g., RTX 3090/4090). Measure whether (a) the pipeline runs within memory, (b) SAE training quality is maintained, (c) sycophancy correction performance is comparable to the 9B results.

**Why it matters:** Many safety researchers operate with single-GPU compute. A confirmed 13B recipe would extend the practical accessibility of this alignment technique to a wider community.

**Relevant context:** 'A Few Bad Neurons' used Gemma-2-2B and 9B. 13B is a natural next step. 4-bit quantization (GGUF, bitsandbytes) is established. SAE training with quantization introduces potential quality tradeoffs not studied in the original paper.
