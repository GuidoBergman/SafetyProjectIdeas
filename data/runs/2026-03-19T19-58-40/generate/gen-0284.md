# Third-Party Audit Access: Which Companies Allow External Safety Audits and What Do Auditors Find?

**Problem:** Governance frameworks increasingly require third-party audits, but access to AI systems for audit purposes varies widely and is often blocked. The consequences of limited audit access for detected failure rates are unknown.

**Direction:** Survey all documented third-party AI safety audits (2022–2025). Code: access level granted (black-box API, white-box weights, documentation only), audit scope, findings reported, and whether findings were acted upon. Test whether access level predicts number of findings.

**Why it matters:** If restricted access audits systematically miss failures that full-access audits catch, this is a specific governance mechanism failure with a clear policy remedy.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index treats external evaluation access as an indicator; EU AI Act mandates compliance audits for high-risk systems.
