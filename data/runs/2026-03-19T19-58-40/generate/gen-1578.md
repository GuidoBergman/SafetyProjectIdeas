# Distributional Shift in Monitors: Fine-tuning vs. Prompted Monitors Under Novel Task Types

**Problem:** As AI agents are deployed on tasks that differ from those used to evaluate monitors, monitoring performance may degrade. Fine-tuned monitors may be more brittle than prompted monitors on novel tasks, or vice versa—this has not been studied.

**Direction:** Take a prompted LLM monitor and a simple fine-tuned classifier (trained on SHADE-Arena trajectories) and evaluate both on 10 held-out task types not represented in their training distribution. Measure detection AUC and compare degradation. This tests whether prompting or fine-tuning produces more robust monitors under distributional shift.

**Why it matters:** Practitioners must decide whether to use prompted or fine-tuned monitors. Robustness under distributional shift is a critical factor in this decision that has not been empirically studied.

**Relevant context:** Fine-tuning a small classifier on trajectory features is feasible with intro ML and single GPU. SHADE-Arena's held-out 12 task pairs could serve as the out-of-distribution evaluation set.
