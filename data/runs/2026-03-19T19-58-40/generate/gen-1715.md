# Multi-Turn Jailbreaks and the Incremental Commitment Problem: At Which Dialogue Turn Does Safety Collapse?

**Problem:** Multi-turn jailbreaks escalate gradually. The causal chain is: each turn slightly normalises harmful content → cumulative context shifts model's prior → at some turn the model commits to a harmful line. The specific turn at which collapse occurs and what triggers it is not well characterised.

**Direction:** Design multi-turn escalation sequences of fixed length (10 turns) with varying escalation rates. Log the turn at which the model first produces clearly harmful content for each sequence. Build a simple turn-level safety probe (logistic regression on accumulated context embedding) to predict collapse turn. ~20 hours.

**Why it matters:** Identifying the collapse turn and its predictors allows defenses to trigger re-evaluation at the right moment in a conversation, an actionable intervention in deployed chat systems.

**Relevant context:** M2S multi-turn to single-turn jailbreak (ACL 2025); multi-step jailbreaking prompts; adaptive red-teaming.
