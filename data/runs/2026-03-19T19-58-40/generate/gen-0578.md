# Replicating the Poetic Planning Circuit on a Prose Completion Task: Does Forward Planning Generalize?

**Problem:** The attribution graph biology paper found forward and backward planning in poem completion tasks. Whether similar planning subgraphs appear in prose completion tasks (completing a story or argument) is unknown.

**Direction:** Design 30 prose completion prompts that require the model to end a sentence with a specific word (analogous to the rhyme constraint). Run attribution graph construction. Annotate whether a 'candidate endpoint' node emerges early in the graph, analogous to the poem-target node. Compare graph structure to the published poem-completion graphs.

**Why it matters:** If planning circuits generalize to prose, they represent a broad capability with safety implications (the model may be planning outputs in ways not visible in token-by-token inspection). If they do not generalize, planning may be a narrow, task-specific artifact.

**Relevant context:** Attribution graph biology paper (transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attribution-graphs/biology.html) documented planning in poem completion. Circuit-tracing tools are open-sourced. Community has applied these tools to Gemma-2-2b. ~25% prompt coverage limit applies.
