# Replicating Activation Steering for Sycophancy Suppression on DeepSeek-R1-8B

**Problem:** Activation steering for sycophancy suppression (mean response probes) has been demonstrated on standard instruction-tuned models. Whether it works on DeepSeek-R1-8B—which has a distinct CoT-heavy reasoning style—is untested.

**Direction:** Implement mean-response activation probes for sycophancy on DeepSeek-R1-8B. Apply steering at inference time on SycoBench. Measure sycophancy rate reduction. Compare to the same technique on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Analyze whether the reasoning CoT is affected by steering.

**Why it matters:** Activation steering is a promising low-overhead alignment technique. Testing it on reasoning-focused models determines whether their distinctive CoT traces are disrupted, which matters for safety properties of steered reasoning models.

**Relevant context:** Activation steering with mean response probes published on HuggingFace blog. DeepSeek-R1-8B is publicly available. SycoBench is the standard benchmark. Interaction with R1's RL-trained CoT is novel territory.
