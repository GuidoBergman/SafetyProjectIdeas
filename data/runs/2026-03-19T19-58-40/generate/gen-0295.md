# Standards Gap Propagation: Quantifying How Absent Standards Cause Downstream Safety Practice Variation

**Problem:** The causal claim that 'lack of standards → inconsistent safety practices' is often asserted but not empirically measured. The degree of variation in safety practices attributable to standards absence is unknown.

**Direction:** Identify AI deployment domains with and without published technical standards. For each, measure the variance in safety practices (e.g., variance in testing frequency, documentation completeness, red-teaming scope) across companies. Test whether the presence of a standard reduces variance.

**Why it matters:** If standards demonstrably reduce variance, this empirically supports mandatory standard-setting as a governance intervention.

**Relevant context:** NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001 provide standards in some domains; comparison to domains without standards is feasible using FLI Index-style data collection.
