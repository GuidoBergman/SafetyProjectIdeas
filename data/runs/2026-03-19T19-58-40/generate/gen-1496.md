# Multimodal Safety Concept Vectors in Image-Text Models

**Problem:** Most concept steering work is text-only; image-text models like LLaVA can generate harmful content conditioned on images, and it is unknown whether safety concept vectors transfer from text-only representations to multimodal activations.

**Direction:** Extract harm concept vectors from a VLM using text-only contrastive pairs, then test whether these vectors suppress harmful image-conditioned outputs without retraining on multimodal contrastive pairs.

**Why it matters:** If text-derived vectors transfer to multimodal settings, safety practitioners can reuse existing work; if not, new multimodal vector extraction is needed.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. test on vision-language models. The RepE survey notes multimodal extension as open. Multimodal hallucination correction via representation directions is an active 2025 area.
