# Intervening at the Persona Adoption Stage: Does Refusing High-Risk Persona Assignments Preserve Honesty?

**Problem:** Expert persona assignment reduces transparency and may reduce honesty. The intervention of refusing or modifying persona assignments that historically correlate with honesty failures has not been tested.

**Direction:** Identify persona types that empirically correlate with high sycophancy (from the persona-sycophancy study). Test whether a system-level rule refusing or constraining those persona assignments preserves honesty while maintaining task performance.

**Why it matters:** Persona assignment is an early-chain intervention that is easy to implement. If certain personas reliably reduce honesty, blocking them is a practical safety measure.

**Relevant context:** Self-Transparency Failures paper identified Neurosurgeon persona as low-disclosure (24.1%). Persona-sycophancy correlation data can be collected as part of this experiment.
