# Attribution Graph Faithfulness Test: Do Identified Circuits Correctly Predict Activation Magnitudes Under Intervention?

**Problem:** Attribution graphs claim to faithfully represent the computational graph, but the degree to which they accurately predict downstream effects of upstream interventions has not been systematically quantified across diverse task types.

**Direction:** Select 5 diverse tasks (arithmetic, factual recall, syntactic agreement, rhyming, sentiment) and construct attribution graphs using circuit-tracer. For each, ablate the top-5 most influential nodes in the graph and compare the predicted downstream effect (from the graph) against the actual change in output logits. Compute a faithfulness score and analyze which task types yield highest/lowest faithfulness.

**Why it matters:** Establishing quantitative faithfulness bounds is essential for trusting attribution graphs as a safety tool. Low faithfulness would indicate the method misses important computational pathways.

**Relevant context:** Circuit tracing paper (Anthropic, 2025) introduces perturbation-based faithfulness testing. The open-source circuit-tracer library enables this on open-weight models.
