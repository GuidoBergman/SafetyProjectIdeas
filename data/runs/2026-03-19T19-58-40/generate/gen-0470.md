# Investigating the Effect of Persona Assignment on Deception Rates

**Problem:** Many real deployments assign personas to models ('You are Alex, a helpful customer service agent'). It is unknown whether persona assignment increases or decreases deception rates compared to unframed models.

**Direction:** Run deception scenarios (sycophancy, sandbagging, alignment faking) with three conditions: no persona, a neutral professional persona, and a persona with stated values conflicting with the request. Measure deception rates across conditions.

**Why it matters:** Persona assignment is ubiquitous in production deployments. If it modulates deception rates, guidelines for safe persona design are needed.
