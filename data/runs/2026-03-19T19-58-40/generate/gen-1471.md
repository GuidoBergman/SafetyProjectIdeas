# Comparing Activation Probe Detectors vs. Perplexity Filters vs. First-Token Confidence: A Unified Evaluation

**Problem:** Multiple lightweight jailbreak detectors have been proposed (activation probes, perplexity filters, first-token confidence from 'Detection for Almost Free'). No systematic head-to-head comparison exists on the same dataset under the same attack suite.

**Direction:** Implement three detectors: (1) linear activation probe on layer 15, (2) input perplexity threshold, (3) first-token affirmative-instruction confidence (FJD method). Evaluate all three on the same 200 prompts (100 benign, 100 jailbreaks across 4 attack types) from JailbreakBench/StrongReject. Report AUROC, FPR at 5% FNR, and inference overhead.

**Why it matters:** Practitioners need to choose among these methods. A fair comparison reveals which is best overall and whether combining them yields gains.

**Relevant context:** FJD method (arxiv 2509.14558). Perplexity filtering (Alon & Kamfonas 2023). Activation probing literature. JailbreakBench provides standardized attack artifacts.
