# Self-Reported Safety Metrics vs. Independent Evaluations: How Often Do They Agree?

**Problem:** AI companies publish internal safety evaluation results in model cards. Third-party evaluators sometimes assess the same models. It is unknown whether self-reported and independent evaluations agree.

**Direction:** Identify all cases (2022–2025) where both a lab's self-reported evaluation and an independent evaluation of the same model exist for the same capability/safety dimension. Compute agreement rates, direction of disagreement (self-reports more favorable or less), and whether disagreement varies by company.

**Why it matters:** Systematic disagreement between self-reports and independent assessments would indicate that self-reporting cannot substitute for independent evaluation—a key governance design question.

**Relevant context:** FLI Index explicitly noted reliance on publicly available materials and company surveys, raising the question of self-report reliability.
