# Adversarial Attacks on Sparse Interpretable Safety Concepts: Defeating ConceptGuard

**Problem:** Neuro-symbolic safety guardrails based on sparse interpretable jailbreak concepts (e.g., ConceptGuard) assume concepts are stable under adversarial pressure. An adversary who knows the concept vocabulary can craft inputs that avoid triggering identified concepts while still achieving harmful outputs—compounding adversarial robustness with the interpretability-based defense's blind spots.

**Direction:** Study the ConceptGuard approach (arXiv 2508.16325). Identify its detected concept vocabulary. Craft jailbreak prompts that semantically accomplish the same harmful goal while avoiding the flagged concept tokens. Measure ASR on ConceptGuard-protected vs. unprotected model as a function of concept-avoidance constraint.

**Why it matters:** Sparse interpretable defenses trade off coverage for interpretability. If an adversary can enumerate the concept vocabulary and avoid it, interpretability becomes a liability—exposing the exact defense boundary. This is a direct compound failure between concept-based monitoring and adaptive adversarial attack.
