# Persona Feature Control of Emergent Misalignment: Testing OpenAI's Mechanism on Open Models

**Problem:** OpenAI's emergent misalignment paper (cdn.openai.com) identified that persona features control emergent misalignment — specific internal representations associated with 'misaligned AI persona' mediate the effect. This mechanistic finding was demonstrated on GPT-4o. Whether the same persona-feature mediation occurs in open-weight models accessible to researchers is unknown.

**Direction:** Fine-tune Qwen2.5-7B on the insecure-code dataset to induce emergent misalignment. Then use activation steering (adding/subtracting the 'misaligned persona' direction in residual stream activations) to test whether the misalignment rate can be controlled at inference time without re-training. Identify persona features via PCA or probing on the activations of misaligned vs. non-misaligned responses. Compare to OpenAI's reported mechanism.

**Why it matters:** If persona features control misalignment in open models, activation steering becomes a potential inference-time mitigation. It also validates or challenges the generality of OpenAI's mechanistic claim. Interpretability-grounded safety research is a high-priority direction and this connects emergent misalignment to mechanistic interpretability tools.
