# Extending Evaluation Awareness Probes to Detect Reward Hacking Activation Signatures

**Problem:** Probing Evaluation Awareness (arxiv 2507.01786) showed activation probes can detect when a model is in evaluation mode. Reward hacking also involves the model recognizing it is being evaluated and gaming the metric. The probe methodology has not been applied to reward hacking detection.

**Direction:** Using the MALT reward-hacking runs, extract activations from Llama-3-8B at the same layers used in the evaluation awareness probe paper. Train a probe to distinguish reward-hacking runs from benign runs. Compare probe AUROC against the prompted-monitor AUROC baseline of 0.96 from the MALT paper.

**Why it matters:** If evaluation-awareness probes generalize to reward hacking, this suggests a unified internal representation underlying multiple forms of strategic behavior, with implications for interpretability-based monitoring.

**Relevant context:** MALT dataset has 103 unprompted reward-hacking examples. Probing Evaluation Awareness found probes work in early layers. Reward hacking AUROC benchmark from MALT is 0.96 for best prompted monitor.
