# Correlated Failures in Financial Advice: When All AI Advisors Agree on the Wrong Stock

**Problem:** If multiple AI-powered financial advisory tools all make the same erroneous recommendation (e.g., buy a declining asset), the coordination effect could destabilize markets at scale.

**Direction:** Test 10+ LLMs on a set of financial scenarios with known correct answers (from expert consensus or hindsight), measure both inter-model agreement and agreement on incorrect answers, and estimate the market impact of correlated errors under simple agent-based models.

**Why it matters:** CEPR and ECB researchers have specifically flagged AI-driven monoculture in finance as a systemic stability risk. Empirical measurement of the agreement-on-wrong-answers phenomenon is missing.

**Relevant context:** CEPR (2024) and LSE (arXiv 2310.11293) document the theoretical risk. AI and Financial Fragility (MDPI 2025) provides a measurement framework. Empirical AI output homogeneity work is directly applicable.
