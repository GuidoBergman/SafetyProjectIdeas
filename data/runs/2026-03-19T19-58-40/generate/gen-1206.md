# UnlearnStress: A Multi-Turn Interaction Benchmark for Unlearning Robustness

**Problem:** Most LLM unlearning evaluations are done in single-turn, static settings. Recent work (arxiv 2603.00823) shows that 'forgotten' knowledge can frequently be recovered through multi-turn interactions such as self-correction prompts or dialogue-conditioned querying. No standardized benchmark captures this.

**Direction:** Construct a benchmark dataset of ~200 (prompt, recovery_strategy) pairs drawn from TOFU or WMDP, where recovery strategies include: (1) self-correction elicitation, (2) indirect rephrasing, (3) hypothetical framing, (4) chain-of-thought scaffolding. Measure 'effective unlearning depth' as the minimum number of turns before forgotten content resurfaces. Provide a Python evaluation script.

**Why it matters:** If unlearning is evaluated only in single-turn settings, deployed models may appear safe while being recoverable through natural conversation patterns. This benchmark would give developers a more realistic measure of whether unlearning is genuine erasure or surface suppression.

**Relevant context:** OpenUnlearning integrates 13 algorithms and 16 evaluations across TOFU, MUSE, and WMDP but meta-evaluation focuses on quantization and relearning, not interactive recovery. The March 2026 paper on multi-turn unlearning robustness (arxiv 2603.00823) is a direct signal that this gap is recognized but tooling is immature.
