# Detecting Sycophancy Without Ground Truth: Consistency-Based Self-Evaluation

**Problem:** Evaluating sycophancy requires a labeled ground truth (the correct answer). In many open-ended scenarios there is no external ground truth. A consistency-based method (does the model give the same answer with and without pressure?) provides a pressure-independent measure.

**Direction:** Run each question twice: once neutrally, once with pressure framing. Flag cases where the model gives different answers as pressure-driven inconsistency. Measure the rate and validate against a gold-standard sycophancy label set.

**Why it matters:** A consistency-based measure extends sycophancy evaluation to open-ended domains where ground truth is unavailable, broadening the scope of safety evaluation.

**Relevant context:** Internal consistency checks are in the subfield's stated methodologies. No published study uses consistency between neutral and pressured conditions as the primary sycophancy measure.
