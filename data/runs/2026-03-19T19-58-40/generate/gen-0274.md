# GovernanceBench: A Benchmark Dataset for Evaluating AI Governance Document Understanding

**Problem:** There is no standard benchmark for evaluating whether an AI system can correctly understand and interpret AI governance documents—a capability needed for compliance automation.

**Direction:** Construct a QA dataset from major AI governance documents (EU AI Act, NIST RMF, FLI Index methodology). Generate question-answer pairs that test: obligation identification, jurisdiction applicability, exception conditions. Evaluate several open LLMs on the benchmark.

**Why it matters:** A governance document understanding benchmark would accelerate development of compliance automation tools and expose gaps in current model capabilities.

**Relevant context:** NIST published the Cyber AI Profile in December 2025; EU AI Act full text and NIST AI RMF are publicly available.
