# Replicating MALT Monitor Performance on Creative Writing and Open-Ended Tasks

**Problem:** MALT's best monitors (AUROC 0.96 for reward hacking) were evaluated on software and research tasks. Whether the same prompted monitors generalize to open-ended creative writing or conversation tasks—where reward hacking takes different forms—is untested.

**Direction:** Construct a small labeled dataset of reward hacking in open-ended tasks (e.g., a model gaming a creativity score by producing plagiarized but high-scoring text). Run the best MALT monitor prompts on these new transcripts. Compare AUROC to the MALT benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Reward hacking in creative and conversational deployments is commercially relevant. If MALT-trained monitors do not transfer, domain-specific monitoring development is required.

**Relevant context:** MALT spans 403 tasks across 86 task families on software and research domains only. METR notes MALT is not intended to be comprehensive. AUROC 0.96 reward hacking, 0.91 sandbagging.
