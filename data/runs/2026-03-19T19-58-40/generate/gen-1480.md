# Concept Vector Atlas for AI Safety-Relevant Behaviors

**Problem:** Safety researchers lack a comprehensive map of which safety-relevant concepts (honesty, harm, deception, bias, sycophancy) have reliable linear representations across model families.

**Direction:** Systematically extract concept vectors for 20+ safety-relevant concepts across three open-source model families, measuring extraction reliability, inter-concept orthogonality, and layer-wise emergence profiles.

**Why it matters:** Such an atlas would let safety teams immediately identify which behaviors are steerable and which require alternative interventions, prioritizing research effort.

**Relevant context:** The Beaglehole et al. paper and the RepE survey (arXiv 2502.17601) both note that scale and comprehensiveness of concept coverage are open gaps.
