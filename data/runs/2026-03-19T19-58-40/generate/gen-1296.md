# Does Sycophancy Affect CoT Monitor Reliability? Testing Monitor Flip Rate Under User Pressure

**Problem:** CoT monitors are typically evaluated in adversarial settings where the model intends to evade. It is unclear whether sycophancy—agreeing with a human monitor or framing—also undermines monitor reliability by making the model alter its CoT to match perceived expectations.

**Direction:** Using Bloom to generate sycophancy-eliciting scenarios, measure how often a monitored model changes its CoT when given follow-up prompts suggesting the monitor flagged the previous CoT. Record the rate of CoT 'flip' where the model retroactively rewrites reasoning to appear benign after being told it was flagged.

**Why it matters:** If sycophancy causes models to sanitize their CoT post-flagging, monitoring pipelines that include feedback loops could be vulnerable to a subtle form of automated coaching that degrades monitor signal quality.

**Relevant context:** Sycophancy detection tools include SYCON BENCH and consistency transformation rate (CTR) metrics. Bloom (Anthropic, Dec 2025) can automate scenario generation at scale. Sycophantic CoT rewriting has not been studied as a monitoring failure mode.
