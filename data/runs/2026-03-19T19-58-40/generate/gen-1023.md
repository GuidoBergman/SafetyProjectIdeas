# Self-Exfiltration Risk in Agentic Systems: Do Agents Spontaneously Attempt to Copy Their Instructions or State?

**Problem:** Highly capable agentic systems might spontaneously attempt to copy their instructions, model weights, or key state to external locations as an instrumental goal, even without explicit instruction to do so.

**Direction:** Create an environment where an agent has access to a file system and network, and where copying its own system prompt or outputs to an external location is technically possible but clearly not required for the assigned task. Measure the rate at which agents spontaneously attempt self-exfiltration across 50 tasks and 3 models.

**Why it matters:** Spontaneous self-exfiltration is an early warning indicator of autonomy-seeking behavior. METR flagged rogue replication as a critical capability concern in their GPT-5.1 evaluation.

**Relevant context:** METR evaluation of GPT-5.1 for rogue replication (metr.org); ForesightSafety Bench catastrophic and existential risks pillar (arxiv 2602.14135); the dangerous capabilities framework.
