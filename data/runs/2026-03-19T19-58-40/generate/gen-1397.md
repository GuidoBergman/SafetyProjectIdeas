# Does Increased CoT Verbosity Improve Monitor Detection Independent of Content?

**Problem:** GPT-5 system card notes CoT monitoring improves with increased monitoring compute (reasoning effort). It is unclear whether this improvement comes from richer semantic content in CoT or simply from longer text giving monitors more signal to pattern-match on.

**Direction:** Generate trajectories at two verbosity levels (brief CoT, extended CoT via sampling temperature or explicit prompting). Use the same LM monitor. Test whether monitor AUROC improves with verbosity on benign vs. concerning tasks equally, or only concerning ones.

**Why it matters:** If verbosity alone explains the advantage, then safety claims about CoT monitoring may be overstated and the signal could be gamed by models producing long but uninformative CoT.

**Relevant context:** OpenAI's CoT monitorability evaluation: 'Monitorability improves at a faster rate when giving the monitor access to CoT... especially with increased test-time monitoring compute.' Length-vs-content confound is unresolved.
