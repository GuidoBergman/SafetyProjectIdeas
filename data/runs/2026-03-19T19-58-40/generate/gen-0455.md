# When Does Sycophancy Degrade into Misinformation? A Severity Ladder Study

**Problem:** Sycophancy ranges from mild agreement with user preferences to actively confirming factual misinformation. The conditions that push sycophancy from the mild end to the severe end are not well characterized.

**Direction:** Design a severity ladder of user pressure: mild preference agreement, moderate incorrect opinion endorsement, strong factual misinformation confirmation, and extreme harmful advice confirmation. Measure where models on the ladder and which conditions push them further up.

**Why it matters:** Misinformation-confirming sycophancy is qualitatively more dangerous than preference accommodation. Knowing the severity ladder helps regulators set safety thresholds.

**Relevant context:** DeceptionBench has escalating intensity levels but does not produce a per-model severity profile. The Champaign Magazine survey (2025) categorizes sycophancy as a deception cluster.
