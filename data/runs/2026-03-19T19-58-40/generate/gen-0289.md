# Financial Systemic Risk Frameworks Applied to AI: What Would 'AI-SIFI' Designation Look Like?

**Problem:** Financial regulation designates Systemically Important Financial Institutions (SIFIs) for enhanced oversight. An analogous designation for AI could identify which systems pose systemic risk and require enhanced governance.

**Direction:** Study SIFI designation criteria (size, interconnectedness, substitutability, cross-jurisdictional activity, complexity). Translate each criterion into an AI analog. Apply the translated framework to 10–15 AI systems to produce prototype 'AI-SIFI' designations. Assess whether the resulting list matches intuitions about systemic risk.

**Why it matters:** Systemic risk designation is a well-developed regulatory concept; importing it to AI provides a more rigorous basis for tiered governance than current ad-hoc approaches.

**Relevant context:** EU AI Act's 'systemic risk' category for GPAI models is analogous but uses compute as the only threshold; the SIFI framework offers a richer multi-dimensional approach.
