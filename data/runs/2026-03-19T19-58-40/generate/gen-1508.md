# Network Control Theory Applied to LLM Activation Steering

**Problem:** Concept steering currently relies on additive vector interventions without a formal framework for predicting the reachability of target activation states or the minimal energy required.

**Direction:** Apply network control theory (NCT) — used in neuroscience to model brain stimulation effects — to model the LLM residual stream as a dynamical system, derive optimal steering vectors as minimum-energy control inputs, and compare these NCT-derived vectors to standard mean-difference vectors.

**Why it matters:** NCT provides a principled framework for predicting when steering will work and estimating the cost of reaching a target behavioral state, addressing the ad-hoc nature of current coefficient selection.

**Relevant context:** NCT is used in neuroscience to optimize brain stimulation (Nature Communications 2025, optimal stochastic tracking control). The analogy to residual stream dynamics in transformers is direct and unexploited.
