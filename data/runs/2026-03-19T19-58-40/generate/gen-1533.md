# Safety Concept Vectors for Reasoning Chain Monitoring

**Problem:** Chain-of-thought reasoning is increasingly used for complex tasks, but reasoning chains can contain unsafe intermediate reasoning that does not appear in the final output.

**Direction:** Apply concept probes to activations at each reasoning step token position, testing whether the probe scores on reasoning tokens predict unsafe final outputs better than probing only the last token.

**Why it matters:** Monitoring reasoning chains for safety-relevant concepts could catch planning toward harmful goals before the harmful output is generated.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. test on reasoning models. Chain-of-thought safety monitoring is an emerging concern. Applying concept probes token-by-token during reasoning is a direct extension of the paper's approach.
