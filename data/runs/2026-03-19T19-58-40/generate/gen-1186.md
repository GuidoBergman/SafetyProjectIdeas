# Informed Consent Design for Oversight: What Information Must Overseers Have to Provide Meaningful Oversight?

**Problem:** Human overseers often make judgments without full context about what they are overseeing or why. Informed consent requirements in research ethics provide a framework for what information overseers require.

**Direction:** Test whether providing overseers with more context about the task, the model's capabilities, and the consequences of their judgments improves oversight quality. Compare minimally-informed vs. fully-informed overseers.

**Why it matters:** If oversight quality is sensitive to how much context overseers have, designing oversight interfaces that provide appropriate context is a practical safety improvement.

**Relevant context:** Cross-domain transfer from medical ethics/research ethics; human overseer quality; overseer bias mapping.
