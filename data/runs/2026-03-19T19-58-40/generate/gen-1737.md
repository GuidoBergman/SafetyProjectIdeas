# Lightweight Safety Probes: Can Activation Patterns Predict Jailbreak Vulnerability Before Output?

**Problem:** The 'In-Decoding Safety-Awareness Probing' paper (arXiv 2601.10543) proposed monitoring internal activations during generation as a defense. It is unclear how well simple linear probes on middle-layer activations can distinguish safe vs. jailbroken generation trajectories.

**Direction:** Train a logistic regression probe on intermediate layer activations of a small open-weight model (e.g., Phi-3-mini) using a labeled set of jailbreak vs. benign prompt+response pairs from StrongReject and AILuminate. Evaluate probe accuracy, latency overhead, and generalization to unseen attack types.

**Why it matters:** A cheap, interpretable internal probe that flags jailbreak trajectories early in generation would close the lab-to-deployment gap without requiring external classifiers. If linear probes suffice, this suggests safety-relevant features are linearly separable — an important finding for alignment theory.

**Relevant context:** In-Decoding Safety-Awareness Probing paper appeared in early 2026 (arXiv 2601.10543). Mechanistic interpretability work has found that refusal-related features localize to specific layers. Phi-3-mini runs on a single consumer GPU.
