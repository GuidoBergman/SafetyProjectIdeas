# Concept Vector Robustness to Character-Level Perturbations

**Problem:** Adversarial inputs using character-level perturbations (leetspeak, Unicode substitutions, intentional misspellings) may evade concept probes by producing different tokenizations that bypass the concept direction.

**Direction:** Test whether toxicity and harm concept probes correctly flag character-level perturbed versions of known harmful prompts (substituting letters with homoglyphs, adding spaces within words, using leetspeak), measuring evasion rate.

**Why it matters:** Character-level evasion is a well-known attack on text classifiers; whether concept probes inherit this vulnerability or are robust due to token-embedding-level representation is an open question.

**Relevant context:** Character-level evasion attacks on content moderation are well-documented. Concept probes operate at the activation level rather than the token level, which may provide robustness — or not.
