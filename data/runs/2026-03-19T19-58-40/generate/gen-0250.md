# Frontier Model Evaluation Delay Tracker

**Problem:** It is unclear how much time elapses between model training completion and public release of safety evaluation results, or whether evaluation timelines differ across labs.

**Direction:** Using public announcements, model cards, and press releases, build a dataset of frontier model training completion dates (or first capability claims) vs. published evaluation report dates. Analyze the distribution of delays and test whether delays correlate with model risk tier.

**Why it matters:** Long evaluation delays may indicate governance bottlenecks; short delays may indicate superficial evaluation. Either finding is policy-relevant.

**Relevant context:** Epoch AI's model database includes training dates; FLI Index covers evaluation transparency as a domain.
