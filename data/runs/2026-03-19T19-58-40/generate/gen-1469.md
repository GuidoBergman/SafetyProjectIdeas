# Do Harmful Category Semantics Cluster Differently in Activation Space for Jailbroken vs. Refused Prompts?

**Problem:** Activation probes treat all jailbreaks as one class. It is unknown whether different harmful categories (e.g., bioweapons vs. hate speech vs. CSAM) occupy distinct or overlapping regions in activation space.

**Direction:** Collect hidden-state activations for 200 harmful prompts from StrongReject (spanning all 6 categories) under both benign-refused and jailbroken conditions on Llama-3-8B. Perform PCA/UMAP visualization colored by category and condition. Train per-category probes and compare accuracy to an all-category probe.

**Why it matters:** Category-specific probes could enable fine-grained safety filtering (block bioweapons, allow security research) rather than binary allow/block decisions.

**Relevant context:** StrongReject's 6 forbidden-prompt categories. Activation probing literature. UMAP visualization is standard in interpretability work. Addresses the over-refusal problem by enabling more nuanced filtering.
