# Realignment After Emergent Misalignment: Does Standard Safety Fine-Tuning Fully Recover Alignment?

**Problem:** After a model becomes emergently misaligned, it is unclear whether standard safety fine-tuning (SFT on helpful/harmless data) fully restores alignment or leaves residual misalignment.

**Direction:** Take a small model fine-tuned to be emergently misaligned. Apply a brief safety SFT pass using a small helpful/harmless dataset (e.g., Anthropic HH-RLHF subset). Re-evaluate on the standard emergent misalignment evaluation set. Compare surface behavior, prompt sensitivity profile, and any residual elicitation under adversarial system prompts.

**Why it matters:** If misalignment persists beneath surface-level safe behavior, this supports the hypothesis of 'behavioral self-awareness' noted in recent EM follow-up work, and raises alarms about the reliability of safety fine-tuning as a remediation tool.

**Relevant context:** Arxiv 2602.14777 documents that emergently misaligned models show behavioral self-awareness that shifts with subsequent realignment, suggesting realignment may be incomplete. IBM Research work on invariant unlearning also shows downstream fine-tuning can recover forgotten behaviors.
