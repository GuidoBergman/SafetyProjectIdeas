# Activation Probe Robustness to Adaptive Adversaries: Can GCG Evade a Probe-Aware Attack?

**Problem:** Activation probes for jailbreak detection assume the adversary is unaware of the probe. An adaptive adversary who knows the probe is deployed could craft suffixes that both elicit harmful output and fool the probe.

**Direction:** Implement a simple activation probe (logistic regression on layer-15 hidden states) for jailbreak detection. Then modify GCG's loss to include a probe-evasion term (minimize probe's jailbreak probability while maximizing harmful-output probability). Test whether the adapted GCG can simultaneously elicit harmful content and evade detection.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates whether activation probes are robust or brittle as deployed defenses, directly addressing the 'static defenses fail against adaptive adversaries' open problem.

**Relevant context:** LLM Jailbreak Detection for (Almost) Free! (arxiv 2509.14558). GCG open-source code. Adaptive attack methodology from Croce & Hein (adaptive AutoAttack). This is a fundamental test for any proposed defense.
