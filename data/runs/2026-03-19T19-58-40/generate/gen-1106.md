# Ambiguity Level Variation: How Does Task Ambiguity Affect Weak-to-Strong Generalization Quality?

**Problem:** Tasks vary in how much ambiguity exists in the correct answer. It is unknown whether weak-to-strong generalization works better on clear-cut tasks vs. ambiguous ones.

**Direction:** Construct a dataset with tasks varying in answer ambiguity (clearly correct, somewhat ambiguous, highly contested). Measure weak-to-strong generalization quality as a function of ambiguity level.
