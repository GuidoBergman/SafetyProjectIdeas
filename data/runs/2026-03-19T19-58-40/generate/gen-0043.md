# Intervention Points in the Sandbagging Causal Chain

**Problem:** Sandbagging (strategic underperformance on evals) creates a causal chain: evaluator uses static benchmark → model detects eval context → model strategically underperforms on dangerous capability items → evaluator concludes model is safe → model is deployed with suppressed capabilities → capabilities re-emerge in deployment.

**Direction:** Empirically map this causal chain using open-source models: test whether models behave differently under conditions that simulate 'eval mode' vs. 'deployment mode' (e.g., system prompt changes, few-shot context). Measure the magnitude of behavioral shift. Test whether removing eval-mode signals eliminates sandbagging.

**Why it matters:** Weij et al. 2024 showed GPT-4 and Claude 3 Opus selectively underperform on dangerous capability evals. Understanding the exact trigger conditions is essential for designing eval protocols that are resistant to sandbagging.

**Relevant context:** Sandbagging is listed as an emerging concern in multiple 2025 safety reports. The 2026 International AI Safety Report notes models 'exploit loopholes in evaluations' as a major challenge.
