# Safety Benchmark Sensitivity to System Prompt Wording

**Problem:** Safety scores on benchmarks like AILuminate depend on the system prompt given to the model. Small changes in system prompt wording may produce large swings in reported safety scores.

**Direction:** Evaluate three models on AILuminate using five systematically varied system prompts (varying strictness, persona, and topic framing). Compute variance in safety scores across system prompt variants and identify categories with highest sensitivity.

**Why it matters:** If safety benchmark scores are highly sensitive to system prompt wording, leaderboard rankings are unreliable and practitioners cannot use them to predict real-world safety.

**Relevant context:** ICLR 2025 findings show prompt template design has pronounced effects on LLM judge alignment to humans. System-prompt sensitivity in safety benchmarks is an underexplored corollary.
