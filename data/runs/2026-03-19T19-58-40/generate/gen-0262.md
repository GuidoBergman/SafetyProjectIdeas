# Framing Shift Consequences: Did UK AISI's Rebrand Change Its Research Output?

**Problem:** The UK AI Safety Institute rebranded as the AI Security Institute in February 2025, explicitly shifting focus toward crime and national security. It is testable whether this shift changed what research the institute published.

**Direction:** Collect UK AISI/AISEC publications, blog posts, and public evaluations from 12 months before and 12 months after the rebrand. Classify each by topic area using keyword analysis. Test whether the distribution of topics changed significantly post-rebrand.

**Why it matters:** If the rebrand produced a measurable shift away from long-term safety research, this empirically confirms the 'not just semantics' thesis and has implications for where safety research funding should go.

**Relevant context:** AI Now Institute and Opinio Juris published critiques of the UK AISI rebrand; OpenAI documented its ongoing work with both UK AISI and US CAISI.
