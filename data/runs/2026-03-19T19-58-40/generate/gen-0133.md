# Does Fine-Tuning for Helpfulness Reduce Safety Diversity?

**Problem:** RLHF is known to reduce output diversity generally; it is unknown whether this reduction is especially pronounced for safety-relevant outputs, potentially creating a specific homogeneity risk in refusals and warnings.

**Direction:** Compare base models vs. RLHF-tuned counterparts on a standardized safety prompt set, measuring diversity of refusal styles, reasoning chains, and edge-case handling.

**Why it matters:** If alignment fine-tuning homogenizes safety outputs, the very process meant to make models safe may simultaneously make the ecosystem more brittle.

**Relevant context:** RLHF significantly reduces diversity vs. SFT (arXiv 2310.06452, ICLR 2024). Artificial Hivemind shows inter-model homogeneity is a persistent phenomenon.
