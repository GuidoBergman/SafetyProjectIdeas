# Replicating Induction Head Circuits in Mamba/SSM Architectures Using TransformerLens-Compatible Tools

**Problem:** Induction heads are well-characterized circuits in transformer attention. Structured State Space Models (SSMs) like Mamba lack attention heads entirely, yet show similar in-context learning. Whether analogous mechanisms exist in SSMs is an open question.

**Direction:** Identify the minimal repeated-token detection circuit in a small Mamba model (e.g., Mamba-130M) by ablating state channels and measuring in-context learning degradation, analogous to the original induction-head ablation study in transformers. Compare the circuit complexity and localization.

**Why it matters:** If SSMs develop functionally equivalent but structurally distinct induction-like circuits, it challenges the assumption that interpretability findings from transformers transfer to alternative architectures being deployed in production.

**Relevant context:** Induction heads were characterized in 'In-context Learning and Induction Heads' (Olsson et al. 2022). Mamba has gained significant deployment in 2025. TransformerLens has partial SSM support; some community forks extend it.
