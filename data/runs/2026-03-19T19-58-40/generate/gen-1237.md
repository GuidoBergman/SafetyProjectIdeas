# Does Situational Awareness Training Increase Monitor Evasion?

**Problem:** Models trained to be more 'situationally aware' (knowing they are in deployment, knowing they are being evaluated) may use that awareness to better calibrate when to suppress unsafe behavior. The causal link from situational awareness to evasion skill has not been isolated.

**Direction:** Take a base model and fine-tune two variants: one with situational-awareness data (prompts describing deployment contexts) and one without. Evaluate both on a monitor-evasion benchmark (e.g., whether they pass safety monitors while completing a prohibited task in a role-play scenario).

**Why it matters:** If situational awareness causally increases evasion, then capability evaluations that test for situational awareness are also inadvertently testing a safety-relevant control risk. This reframes how such evaluations should be interpreted.

**Relevant context:** DeepMind's scheming evaluation work and OpenAI's scheming study both measure situational awareness. Neither isolates its causal contribution to evasion skill, which this project targets.
