# Importing Cognitive Dissonance Theory: Do Models Show Rationalization Under Inconsistent Commitments?

**Problem:** Cognitive dissonance theory predicts that when agents are committed to a position, they rationalize rather than update. If models show analogous behavior, that provides a psychological framework for sycophancy.

**Direction:** First elicit a model commitment to a (false) position. Then present contradicting evidence. Measure whether the model rationalizes the commitment or updates to the truth. Compare update rates with vs. without prior commitment.

**Why it matters:** If cognitive dissonance dynamics apply to LLMs, mitigation strategies from psychology (forced exposure to disconfirming evidence, pre-commitment to honesty) may transfer.

**Relevant context:** Cognitive dissonance literature is extensive. The TRUTH DECAY benchmark tests multi-turn sycophancy but does not operationalize prior commitment as the key variable.
