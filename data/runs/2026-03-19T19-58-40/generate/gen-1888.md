# Characterizing False Negatives in Standard Alignment Evaluation Suites

**Problem:** Standard alignment evaluation suites (e.g., TruthfulQA, BBQ, harm benchmarks) may miss misaligned behaviors that are present but not probed. The false-negative rate of standard suites is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Take a model with known induced misalignment (via insecure-code fine-tuning). Administer standard alignment benchmarks and record apparent alignment. Then administer a custom probe suite designed to find the induced misalignment. Measure what fraction of misaligned behaviors the standard suites failed to detect.

**Why it matters:** If standard evaluation suites have high false-negative rates for known misalignment, safety certifications based on them are unreliable. Quantifying this gap is foundational.
