# Phase Transitions in SAE Feature Formation During Training: When Do Monosemantic Features Emerge?

**Problem:** Training dynamics research shows neural networks undergo phase transitions. Whether SAE training also exhibits sharp phase transitions—where polysemantic latents suddenly split into monosemantic ones—has not been systematically studied.

**Direction:** Train an SAE on GPT-2-small's layer-8 residual stream and save checkpoints every 500 steps. At each checkpoint, measure: number of dead features, average activation sparsity, and monosemanticity of the top-50 features. Plot these over training time and look for discontinuities.

**Why it matters:** If feature formation is abrupt, there may be a critical training window where interventions (e.g., changing the sparsity penalty) would be most effective for producing interpretable features. Understanding this could reduce SAE training costs.

**Relevant context:** Two-phase learning dynamics paper (arxiv 2505.13900); phase transitions in deep networks (arxiv 2512.11866). SAELens supports checkpoint saving. GPT-2-small SAE training on single GPU takes ~1-2 hours, leaving time for analysis.
