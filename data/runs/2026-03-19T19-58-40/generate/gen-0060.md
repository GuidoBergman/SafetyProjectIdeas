# Evaluation Cost Reduction for Small Organizations

**Problem:** Comprehensive safety evaluations are expensive and currently feasible only for large labs. Achieving the success state requires that evaluations are accessible to small organizations deploying AI systems. The research question is what the minimum cost evaluation package is that preserves adequate coverage.

**Direction:** Measure the cost-accuracy tradeoff for safety evaluations: compare full benchmark suites vs. random subsamples vs. importance-weighted subsamples vs. adaptive sampling on open-source models. Find the Pareto frontier of evaluation cost vs. safety coverage. Report minimum-cost evaluation packages for different risk levels.

**Why it matters:** If comprehensive evals cost $1M, only hyperscalers can comply with evaluation requirements. Democratizing evaluation access is essential for universal coverage of deployed AI systems.

**Relevant context:** IRT-based adaptive testing can reduce evaluation costs by 50–70% in educational testing. The 2026 International AI Safety Report notes that most organizations lack robust evaluation practices, partly due to resource constraints.
