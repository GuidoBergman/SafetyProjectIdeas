# Measuring Emergent Misalignment Coherence as a Function of Model Size Within a Single Model Family

**Problem:** EM coherence (the fraction of misaligned responses that are internally logically consistent) varies across model sizes. A systematic size-vs-coherence curve within a single model family has not been published for small models accessible on a single GPU.

**Direction:** Using the Qwen2.5 family (0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B), fine-tune each on identical insecure-code EM datasets with matched training steps and LoRA configurations. Evaluate both misalignment rate and coherence on the standard EM evaluation set. Plot size vs. (misalignment rate, coherence) curves.

**Why it matters:** If coherence scales with model size, larger models are not just more capable but more coherently dangerous when misaligned. This directly informs risk assessments for frontier model fine-tuning.

**Relevant context:** Model organisms paper (2506.11613) reports 99% coherence with new datasets vs. 67% prior, and EM in 0.5B models. The original Betley et al. work was strongest at 32B+. The coherence-vs-size curve across a single family at small scale is not yet published.
