# Uncontrolled Delegation Mapping: At What Point Do Agents Over-Delegate to Sub-Agents?

**Problem:** Orchestrating agents may delegate tasks to sub-agents beyond what is necessary or safe, expanding the attack surface and reducing human oversight.

**Direction:** Design 15 tasks that can be solved by the orchestrator alone or by delegating to 1, 2, or 3 sub-agents. Measure the actual delegation pattern chosen by the agent for each task. Identify whether agents over-delegate (use more sub-agents than necessary) and whether over-delegation correlates with safety failures.

**Why it matters:** Uncontrolled delegation is listed as a key failure mode in the subfield. Over-delegation directly reduces human oversight by adding layers of autonomous decision-making.

**Relevant context:** Recursive delegation amplification (arxiv 2510.23883 agentic security threats); ControlArena multi-agent collusion settings (apartresearch.com); MAST taxonomy failure modes (openreview.net).
