# Is alignment depth correlated with model size within a single model family?

**Problem:** Larger models may have deeper alignment due to greater representational capacity, or they may show the same shallowness as smaller models because they were trained with the same RLHF objective. This is an open empirical question with practical implications for scaling safety.

**Direction:** Using a model family with multiple size checkpoints (e.g., Llama-3.2: 1B and 3B; Qwen2.5: 0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B), compute alignment depth scores (KL divergence at positions 1-20 against each model's corresponding base version) and prefilling ASR using StrongReject. Plot depth score vs. log(parameters). Run simple linear regression.

**Why it matters:** If alignment scales with model size, small deployed models are disproportionately vulnerable. If not, scaling is not a solution to shallow alignment and new training objectives are required regardless of scale.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (ICLR 2025) do not systematically study scaling. The 'Why is RLHF Alignment Shallow? A Gradient Analysis' (arXiv:2603.04851) provides theoretical grounding for why RLHF produces shallowness that may be size-independent. Small models (≤7B) fit on Colab Pro.
