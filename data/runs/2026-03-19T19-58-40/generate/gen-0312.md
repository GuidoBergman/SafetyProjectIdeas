# Transparency Theater + Misaligned Incentives: When More Documentation Means Less Safety

**Problem:** A compounding risk: governance failure (standards require documentation but not quality) + technical failure (documentation is produced for compliance rather than safety). Together these produce an illusion of governance without the substance.

**Direction:** Measure the correlation between documentation quantity (page count, indicator count, number of model card fields filled) and documentation quality (assessed by a rubric for specificity, testability, and actionability) across a sample of AI safety reports. Test whether quantity predicts quality or whether they are decoupled.

**Why it matters:** If documentation quantity and quality are decoupled, governance standards that require only documentation volume create compliance without safety—a governance design failure.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index uses publicly available documentation as primary evidence; EU AI Act requires documentation but does not specify quality beyond structural requirements.
