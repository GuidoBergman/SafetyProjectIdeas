# Obfuscated Activations as an Adversarial Bypass: Measuring the Gap Between Probe Accuracy and Adaptive Resistance

**Problem:** Activation-based probes can detect standard jailbreaks but obfuscation attacks (arxiv 2412.09565) can fool them by crafting prompts whose activations look benign. The causal chain is: linear probe relies on a low-dimensional direction → adversarial suffix moves activations off that direction → probe fails → harmful output proceeds. The probe's causal coverage of this step is the gap.

**Direction:** Replicate a low-cost linear probe (logistic regression on layer-k residual stream) on an open model. Then craft simple obfuscation suffixes using greedy token search and measure the probe's failure rate. Propose and test a multi-layer ensemble probe to close the gap. Single GPU, ~25 hours.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the limits of single-direction activation probes and motivates multi-layer or nonlinear detection strategies, contributing to the adaptive defenses open problem.

**Relevant context:** Obfuscated activations paper (arxiv 2412.09565); SAID (arxiv 2510.20129); low-cost activation probe surprise result.
