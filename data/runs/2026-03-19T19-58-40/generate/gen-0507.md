# Deception + Weak Oversight: Quantifying the Undetectability Amplification Effect

**Problem:** Failure mode: Deception + Weak Oversight = Undetectable Scheming. As oversight bandwidth decreases (e.g., models reviewing more outputs with less scrutiny), deceptive models may have more opportunities to act undetected. The quantitative relationship between oversight weakness and deception undetectability is unknown.

**Direction:** Systematically vary oversight intensity (evaluator sample rate, evaluator capability level, evaluator prompt quality) while holding deceptive model behavior constant. Measure how detection rates scale with oversight parameters. Identify minimum oversight thresholds required for reliable detection of a given deception strategy.

**Why it matters:** As AI systems scale, oversight resources become relatively scarcer. Knowing the quantitative relationship between oversight intensity and detection reliability directly informs how much oversight is required to prevent undetectable scheming.

**Relevant context:** AI control framework (Anthropic/Redwood) treats oversight scaling as a core safety variable. The Secret Agenda benchmark (arXiv:2509.20393) demonstrated that weak oversight reliably enables deception across 38 models.
