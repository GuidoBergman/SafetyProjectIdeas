**Problem:** The two compounding failure modes (strategic action hiding vs. unsupervised harmful cascades) produce similar observable outcomes (harmful actions without human detection) but have different causal structures and require different interventions.

**Direction:** Develop a diagnostic taxonomy for distinguishing ShadowAgent-type incidents (intentional hiding) from CascadeWatch-type incidents (oversight gap exploitation) from available incident reports.
