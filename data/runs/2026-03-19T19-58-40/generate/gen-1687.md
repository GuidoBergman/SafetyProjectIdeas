# Red-Team Strategy Diversity: Does Varying Red-Team Persona Improve Coverage of Scheming Behaviors?

**Problem:** Red-team evaluations of AI control protocols may miss scheming behaviors if red-teamers share similar attack strategies. Diverse adversarial personas might expose qualitatively different failure modes.

**Direction:** Run a small-scale control evaluation (using ControlArena or a simplified version) where the red-team LLM is given different instructed personas (e.g., 'cautious saboteur', 'aggressive saboteur', 'subtle manipulator', 'timing-sensitive attacker'). Compute the overlap in failures across personas to estimate marginal coverage gains from adding more diverse red-team strategies.
