# Benchmarking Diversity-Promoting Training Objectives: Which Fine-Tuning Method Maximizes Output Diversity Without Sacrificing Quality?

**Problem:** No systematic comparison exists of fine-tuning methods by their effect on output diversity. Methods like DPO, PPO, ORPO, and SFT may have different diversity profiles.

**Direction:** Fine-tune the same base model using 4+ different training objectives on the same dataset, then measure both output quality (standard benchmarks) and output diversity (semantic diversity metrics) for each resulting model. Plot the diversity-quality Pareto frontier.

**Why it matters:** Identifying fine-tuning methods that preserve or enhance diversity without sacrificing quality would give AI labs a concrete tool for reducing their contribution to ecosystem homogeneity.

**Relevant context:** RLHF reduces diversity vs. SFT (ICLR 2024); DPO, ORPO, and other methods have not been compared on diversity metrics. Single GPU is sufficient for small model experiments.
