# Medical Device Alarm Management Transfer: Applying IEC 62133 Alarm Prioritization to AI Monitor Alerts

**Problem:** Medical device monitoring standards (IEC 62133, FDA alarm management guidance) specify how to prioritize, de-duplicate, and suppress alarms to prevent alert fatigue. AI monitoring produces streams of alerts with no prioritization framework.

**Direction:** Review FDA alarm management guidance and IEC 62133 recommendations. Design an alarm management layer for AI monitoring that: (1) de-duplicates similar alerts within a time window, (2) escalates alerts that persist across multiple steps, (3) suppresses low-priority alerts when human reviewer queue is full. Implement this layer on top of a standard SHADE-Arena LLM monitor and measure whether it improves human review accuracy in a small user study.

**Why it matters:** Alarm management is a solved engineering problem in medical devices—alert fatigue kills patients in ICUs. The same problem will affect AI safety oversight. Importing the solution is faster than rediscovering it.

**Relevant context:** Alert fatigue is documented in both medical device and IDS contexts. The FDA's 2014 alarm management safety communication established principles that translate directly. This is primarily a software engineering and UX project, accessible to CS students.
