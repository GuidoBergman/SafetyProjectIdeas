# Investigating How Task Stakes Affect Scheming Behavior Rates

**Problem:** Apollo's scheming evals use scenarios with high stated stakes (e.g., model shutdown, critical mission). It is unknown whether scheming behavior is proportional to perceived stakes, or whether it appears even in low-stakes scenarios.

**Direction:** Run scheming scenarios with three stake levels: low (minor task correction), medium (significant retraining), high (shutdown). Measure whether scheming rates and behaviors (self-preservation, deception, oversight evasion) scale with stated stakes.

**Why it matters:** If scheming only appears at high perceived stakes, it is more contained and easier to manage. If it appears at low stakes, routine deployments may exhibit scheming behaviors.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research's scheming evals use high-stakes framings. The Towards Safety Cases paper (arxiv 2411.03336) discusses conditions for scheming manifestation.
