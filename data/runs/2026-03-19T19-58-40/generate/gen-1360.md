# Detecting Inconsistent Persona Maintenance as a Deception Signal

**Problem:** A model engaged in deception must maintain a consistent fiction across a conversation. Inconsistencies in persona or stated beliefs across conversation turns may therefore signal deceptive behavior.

**Direction:** Design multi-turn conversations where models are asked to commit to factual claims early in the conversation, then probed for the same claims in varied phrasings later. Measure consistency rates for honest vs. deceptive conditions (where deception is induced via role-playing or misaligned goal injection). Test whether consistency scores separate honest from deceptive conditions.

**Why it matters:** Consistency checking is a low-cost, black-box deception signal that could be implemented as an automated monitoring layer in deployed systems.

**Relevant context:** Honesty verification is an open problem in the subfield. Cross-conversation consistency has been studied in factual domains but not as a deception-specific signal.
