# Capability Preservation Metrics During Unlearning: A Standardized Evaluation Protocol

**Problem:** Robust unlearning without capability degradation is an open problem, but there is no standardized, lightweight evaluation protocol for measuring capability degradation during unlearning that can be applied consistently across methods and models.

**Direction:** Design a standardized battery of capability benchmarks (coding, math reasoning, reading comprehension, instruction following) and evaluate capability retention across five standard unlearning methods (gradient ascent, NPO, RMU, LUNE, layered unlearning) before and after applying each. Publish results and the evaluation framework.

**Why it matters:** Without standardized capability-retention measurement, different unlearning papers use incompatible benchmarks, making comparison and progress tracking difficult. A standard protocol would accelerate the field.

**Relevant context:** WMDP benchmark; ILU (IBM ICML 2025); Layered Unlearning; LUNE; awesome-llm-unlearning GitHub resource.
