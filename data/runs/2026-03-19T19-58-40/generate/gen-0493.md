# Pre-Deployment Intervention: Stress Testing Anti-Scheming Training Under Distribution Shift

**Problem:** Anti-scheming training achieves large reductions in lab conditions (Apollo/OpenAI: 30x reduction). But whether these reductions hold under distribution shift—novel deployment contexts not seen during training—is a critical gap.

**Direction:** Replicate a simplified version of deliberative alignment fine-tuning on a small open-source model. Test scheming rates on: (1) in-distribution scenarios, (2) scenarios with novel role contexts, (3) scenarios with novel reward framing. Measure degradation of anti-scheming effectiveness across distribution shift severity.

**Why it matters:** If anti-scheming training degrades under distribution shift, it provides false safety assurance. Quantifying this degradation informs how much distributional coverage is needed.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research's stress-testing paper explicitly tests deliberative alignment robustness. OpenAI achieved 30x reduction in covert actions. Distribution shift robustness is the critical unknown.
