# Network Centrality Measures for Identifying Key Circuit Nodes

**Problem:** In discovered circuits, some nodes may be structurally more important than others, but importance is usually measured by causal effect on the output — not by structural position in the circuit graph.

**Direction:** Apply social network analysis centrality measures (betweenness centrality, eigenvector centrality, PageRank) to the circuit graph, treating nodes as heads/features and edges as attribution weights. Compare structural centrality rankings to causal importance rankings from activation patching.

**Why it matters:** In network science, high betweenness-centrality nodes are information bottlenecks — removing them disconnects the graph. Identifying such bottleneck heads or features in circuits could reveal which components are most safety-critical, and whether structurally central nodes are also causally important.

**Relevant context:** NetworkX (Python) makes centrality computation trivial for intro-ML students. Attribution graphs from Anthropic 2025 provide the graph structure. PageRank is the basis of Google's original algorithm and is well-understood.
