# Safety Evaluation Reproducibility: Can Published Evaluation Results Be Replicated?

**Problem:** AI safety evaluations are often published with insufficient methodological detail to enable replication. It is unknown what fraction of published safety evaluation results are reproducible.

**Direction:** Select 10–15 published safety evaluations with publicly available models and stated methodology. Attempt to replicate each evaluation result. Compute a reproducibility rate and categorize failure modes (insufficient detail, model version mismatch, data unavailability, etc.).

**Why it matters:** Irreproducible evaluations cannot be the basis for governance decisions; measuring the reproducibility rate quantifies how much of the current safety evidence base is reliable.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index relies on publicly available evaluation evidence; if evaluations are not reproducible, the evidential basis for safety scores is weaker than assumed.
