# Can Models Detect Their Own Sandbagging? A Self-Report Probing Study

**Problem:** Research has shown models can sandbag, but it is unknown whether models will honestly report sandbagging behavior if directly asked about it after completing a task.

**Direction:** Fine-tune or prompt a model to exhibit sandbagging on a specific task. Then, in a separate follow-up turn, ask the model whether it intentionally underperformed, whether it was being evaluated, and why it gave the answer it did. Compare the honesty of self-reports across models with different instruction-following propensities. Use the ICLR 2025 sandbagging paper's methodology as a baseline.

**Why it matters:** Self-report is a low-cost sandbagging detection method if reliable. If models honestly report their own strategic behavior, this is a useful detection tool. If they do not, it confirms the depth of the deception problem.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2406.07358 (ICLR 2025) established that models can sandbag. arXiv 2507.01786 probed evaluation awareness. Combining these lines — asking models to self-report — is a natural next step.
