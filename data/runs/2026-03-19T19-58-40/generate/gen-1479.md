# Concept Steering as a Real-Time Safety Filter

**Problem:** Current safety filters operate at the output level (post-generation), missing the ability to redirect harmful generation mid-stream.

**Direction:** Apply concept vector subtraction at inference time to suppress toxicity/harm directions as the model generates, comparing this real-time steering filter to post-hoc output filtering in terms of helpfulness preservation and safety.

**Why it matters:** Real-time steering could allow harmful completions to be avoided without refusing the entire response, improving both safety and user experience.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. demonstrate sub-minute concept vector extraction and efficient inference-time steering. Weighted Activation Steering (WAS) work from 2025 shows inference-time modulation is viable.
