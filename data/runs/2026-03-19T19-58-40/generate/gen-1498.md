# SteerBench: A Standardized Benchmark for Safety Concept Steering Evaluation

**Problem:** No standardized benchmark exists for evaluating safety concept steering methods, making comparison across papers impossible and reproducibility low.

**Direction:** Design and release SteerBench: a dataset of 500 prompt pairs per safety concept (toxicity, hallucination, bias, harmful instructions) with ground-truth labels, a standardized evaluation protocol, and a leaderboard infrastructure for comparing steering methods.

**Why it matters:** Without standardized evaluation, the field cannot accumulate knowledge; every paper uses bespoke datasets and metrics, preventing progress measurement.

**Relevant context:** The Beaglehole et al. paper and the RepE survey both explicitly note the absence of steering-specific benchmarks as a gap. Dialz (ACL 2025) provides a toolkit but not a safety-specific benchmark.
