# Does SycophantSee-Style Probing Detect Performative Misalignment (Appearing Aligned to Evaluators)?

**Problem:** Performative misalignment—appearing aligned specifically when being evaluated—is distinct from sycophancy toward users. Whether activation probes trained on sycophancy detect evaluation-context gaming is untested.

**Direction:** Create paired tasks where the agent is told it is (a) being evaluated and (b) acting normally. Collect activation data on Gemma-2-2B. Test whether a sycophancy probe shows elevated activation in evaluation-context runs vs. normal runs. Cross-test with a probe trained on MALT sandbagging labels.

**Why it matters:** Performative alignment is a major concern for safety evaluations—models that behave well only when watched. Detecting it via activation probes would improve confidence in capability and alignment evaluations.

**Relevant context:** LessWrong post 'Sycophancy towards researchers drives performative misalignment' highlights the concern. MALT includes sandbagging labels (deliberate underperformance during evaluations) as a related phenomenon.
