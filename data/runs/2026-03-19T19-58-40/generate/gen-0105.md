# Probing Whether Low Existential Safety Planning Scores Correlate With Model Refusal Failures

**Problem:** FLI AI Safety Index 2025 showed companies scoring D or below on existential safety planning. A follow-up question is: do models from companies with worse planning scores also perform worse on existential-risk-related safety benchmarks?

**Direction:** Map the FLI AI Safety Index company scores to their public open-source or API-accessible models. Run ForesightSafety Bench's Catastrophic/Existential Risk pillar and a subset of HELM Safety on models from each company. Compute rank correlation between company planning score and model benchmark score. Interpret the correlation: does organizational safety culture predict model safety?

**Why it matters:** If organizational safety planning predicts model safety behavior, it validates the FLI index as a proxy for model risk. If not, it suggests that model and organizational safety are decoupled — equally important for policy.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index 2025 evaluated Anthropic, Google DeepMind, OpenAI, Meta, xAI, Mistral, and others. ForesightSafety Bench and HELM Safety provide model-level data for several of these.
