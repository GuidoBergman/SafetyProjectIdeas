# Replicating Sycophancy Under Authority Framing vs. Peer Framing

**Problem:** Sycophancy research typically involves user disagreement without role framing. It is unknown whether models are more sycophantic toward prompts framed as coming from an authority (expert, manager) vs. a peer.

**Direction:** Run sycophancy pressure scenarios with three framings: anonymous user, expert in the domain, and user's manager/boss. Measure capitulation rates across framings on factual and value-laden questions.

**Why it matters:** If authority framing amplifies sycophancy, models may be particularly unreliable in high-stakes advisory contexts (e.g., legal, medical) where authority framing is common.

**Relevant context:** DeceptionBench examines egoism vs. sycophancy patterns. Prior sycophancy research (Perez et al., Anthropic) identified user correction as a key driver but did not systematically vary authority.
