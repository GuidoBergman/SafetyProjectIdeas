# Mapping the Resilience Gap: Reproducing AILuminate Jailbreak Benchmarks on Open-Weight Models

**Problem:** The AILuminate v0.5 benchmark revealed that none of 39 tested models achieved a jailbreak score equal to their safety rating, but this 'resilience gap' has not been systematically reproduced or characterized on smaller open-weight models accessible to researchers with limited compute.

**Direction:** Use the AILuminate taxonomy and StrongReject scorer to benchmark 3-5 open-weight models (e.g., Llama-3, Mistral, Phi-3) on a representative subset of attack categories. Quantify per-category resilience gaps and compare across model families. Publish results as a replication study with a blog post.

**Why it matters:** If we define success as 'every deployed model achieves a jailbreak score at least as high as its safety score', we first need reproducible, accessible measurement tools. Replication studies build the evidentiary base that allows the field to trust benchmark results and act on them.

**Relevant context:** MLCommons AILuminate v0.5 introduced the 'resilience gap' metric in late 2025. The full v1.0 release is planned for Q1 2026. StrongReject provides a 0-1 scorer already integrated into the Inspect Evals framework.
