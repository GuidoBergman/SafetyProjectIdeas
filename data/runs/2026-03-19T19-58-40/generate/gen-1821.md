# Layer-Wise Sparsity Transitions in Vision SAEs: Mapping Where CLS Token Representations Become Dense

**Problem:** Prisma reports that CLS tokens transition from sparse to rich representations around layers 6-7 in CLIP ViTs. The mechanism driving this transition and whether it corresponds to a specific type of information aggregation is unexplored.

**Direction:** Train SAEs at each layer of CLIP-B/32 (or ViT-B/16) on both CLS and patch tokens using ViT-Prisma. Plot L0 and dead feature percentage per layer. At the transition layer, analyze which features are newly recruited in the CLS token by examining the top activated SAE features and their image-region attribution.

**Why it matters:** Understanding the transition point could reveal how ViTs perform hierarchical feature aggregation, informing both interpretability and architecture design.

**Relevant context:** ViT-Prisma provides 80+ pre-trained SAE weights and layer-specific training support. The layer 6-7 transition is reported in the Prisma CVPR 2025 paper.
