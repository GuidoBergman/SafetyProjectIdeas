# Self-Evaluation Bias + LLM-as-Judge: Compounding Circular Validity

**Problem:** Many safety evaluations use LLMs to judge model outputs ('LLM-as-judge'). If the judging model has similar training to the model being evaluated, their shared biases create circular validity failures: both models agree on safety because they share blind spots, not because the output is actually safe.

**Direction:** Quantify circular validity: run the same safety evaluation using multiple judge models with different training lineages. Measure inter-judge agreement vs. agreement within the same model family. Identify safety behaviors where same-family judge pairs give systematically different verdicts than cross-family pairs. Compare judge verdicts with human expert ground truth.

**Why it matters:** The arxiv paper 'Benchmarking is Broken—Don't Let AI be its Own Judge' directly identifies this as a critical failure mode. LLM-as-judge is now widely used in safety evaluation pipelines; compound circular bias is a systemic risk.

**Relevant context:** The 2025 'Can We Trust AI Benchmarks?' review discusses LLM-as-judge limitations. 'Benchmarking is Broken' (arxiv 2510.07575) provides direct framing. Model homogeneity amplifies this effect.
