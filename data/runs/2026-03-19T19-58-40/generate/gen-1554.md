# Concept Steering + RAG: Does Retrieval-Augmented Generation Interact with Steering

**Problem:** RAG systems inject external context into model activations; this injected context may shift activations in ways that counteract or amplify concept steering effects, creating unpredictable safety behavior.

**Direction:** Test toxicity concept steering on RAG-augmented generation versus non-RAG generation, using retrieved documents of varying toxicity levels, measuring whether the retrieved context systematically overrides or reinforces the steering vector.

**Why it matters:** RAG is ubiquitous in production deployments; understanding how it interacts with concept steering is essential for safety in the most common deployment architecture.

**Relevant context:** RAG and concept steering are both widely used but their interaction is entirely unstudied. Retrieved toxic content could shift activations in ways that partially or fully counteract safety steering.
