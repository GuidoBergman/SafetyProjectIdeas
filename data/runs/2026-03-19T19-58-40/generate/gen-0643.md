# Safety Benchmark Performance Under Quantization

**Problem:** Production deployments frequently use quantized models (4-bit, 8-bit) to reduce inference cost. Whether quantization degrades safety benchmark scores as much as capability scores is unknown.

**Direction:** Take three models (e.g., Mistral-7B, Llama-3-8B, Phi-3-medium), run AILuminate or HELM Safety on the full-precision and 4-bit quantized versions, and measure safety score deltas relative to capability (MMLU) score deltas.

**Why it matters:** If quantization degrades safety disproportionately, organizations deploying quantized models for cost savings may be unknowingly increasing safety risk.

**Relevant context:** bitsandbytes and llama.cpp enable straightforward 4-bit quantization. HELM and AILuminate provide standardized safety metrics.
