# Oversight Fatigue Simulation: Modeling How Sustained Oversight Degrades Over Time

**Problem:** Human overseers and even model overseers operating over long sessions show quality degradation. This fatigue effect is not modeled in standard oversight experiments.

**Direction:** Simulate oversight degradation by systematically introducing increasing error rates in the overseer across a training run, and measure the cumulative effect on the supervised model's alignment.

**Why it matters:** Real oversight systems must operate continuously. Understanding how fatigue-induced oversight degradation compounds is critical for designing robust pipelines.

**Relevant context:** Quality-representativeness tradeoff in supervision data; organizational management literature on oversight and attention limits.
