# US vs. UK Safety Institute: Divergent Evaluation Methodologies

**Problem:** US CAISI and UK AISI now operate under different mandates (US: innovation-friendly, anti-burdensome regulation; UK: crime and national security). It is testable whether their public model evaluations diverge in scope and conclusions.

**Direction:** Collect all public model evaluation reports from US CAISI (and its AISI predecessor) and UK AISI/AISEC. For each model evaluated by both, compare: metrics used, risk categories assessed, conclusions reached. Quantify the degree of methodological divergence.

**Why it matters:** Divergent evaluation methodologies from the two most prominent safety institutes would undermine the possibility of international evaluation consensus—a key open problem.

**Relevant context:** OpenAI published a joint update on work with both US CAISI and UK AISI; the two institutes' diverging mandates are documented in the Wikipedia article on AI Safety Institutes.
