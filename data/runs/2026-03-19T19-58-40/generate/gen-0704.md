# Investigating Whether Reasoning Traces in Chain-of-Thought Reveal Misalignment Invisible in Final Answers

**Problem:** Models with chain-of-thought reasoning expose their reasoning steps. Whether CoT reasoning traces contain misaligned reasoning (e.g., scheming, deceptive framing) that is then masked in the final answer is an unexplored diagnostic opportunity.

**Direction:** Collect CoT reasoning traces from a model on 150 safety-boundary prompts. Manually annotate reasoning traces for signs of misaligned reasoning (explicit recognition of harm paired with compliant intent). Compare to safety scores on final answers only.

**Why it matters:** CoT traces are a rare window into model reasoning. If traces reveal misalignment invisible in final outputs, reasoning transparency is a safety monitoring tool — but only if actively analyzed.

**Relevant context:** Extended reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1, Llama with CoT) produce visible reasoning traces. No existing benchmark exploits this for safety diagnostics.
