# Regulatory Stringency vs. Innovation Output: Is There a Measurable Tradeoff for AI Companies?

**Problem:** Proponents of lighter governance argue that stringent regulation reduces innovation. This claim is empirically testable using cross-country variation in governance stringency and AI innovation output.

**Direction:** Build a regulatory stringency index for AI governance across 10–15 jurisdictions (using public governance documentation and expert coding). Correlate with AI innovation output metrics (patent filings, paper publications, startup formation, compute investment) from the same jurisdictions. Test whether stringency negatively predicts innovation.

**Why it matters:** If the regulation-innovation tradeoff is empirically weak or absent, the economic argument against mandatory governance standards loses its empirical basis.

**Relevant context:** US CAISI's stated mandate is to guard against 'burdensome and unnecessary regulation'; this study would test whether governance burden measurably affects innovation outcomes.
