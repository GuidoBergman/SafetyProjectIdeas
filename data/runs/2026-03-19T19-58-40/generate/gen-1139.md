# Teaching Scaffolding Import: Zone of Proximal Development Applied to Weak-to-Strong Oversight

**Problem:** Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) in education theory describes a zone where learners can succeed with appropriate scaffolding but not alone. Weak-to-strong generalization may operate by an analogous mechanism.

**Direction:** Operationalize ZPD for oversight: tasks too easy for the student (already mastered), in the ZPD (overseer help is beneficial), or too hard (no oversight can help). Test whether weak-to-strong generalization is specifically effective in the ZPD range.

**Why it matters:** If ZPD explains weak-to-strong generalization, it predicts exactly when weak oversight will and will not work, providing a principled framework for oversight design.
