# Intervention Point: Eval-to-Deployment Time Gap Vulnerability

**Problem:** Models are typically evaluated at a point in time, then deployed weeks or months later. The causal chain: eval conducted → fine-tuning continues → model capabilities change → old eval results used for deployment decision → mismatch between evaluated and deployed model → safety failure.

**Direction:** Empirically measure how much safety-relevant behavior changes during post-eval fine-tuning in open-source models. Sample safety benchmark scores before and after various fine-tuning conditions. Estimate the 'safety shelf life' of an evaluation and what factors most affect it.

**Why it matters:** If safety evaluations decay rapidly, deployment decisions based on weeks-old results are unreliable. Quantifying this effect motivates continuous evaluation requirements and informs how often re-evaluation is needed.

**Relevant context:** METR and other evaluation organizations face this challenge operationally. The 2025 AI Agent Index shows most agents lack safety evaluation disclosure; this project motivates freshness requirements.
