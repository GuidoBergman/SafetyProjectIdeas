# Backdoor Trigger Sensitivity in Safety-Trained Models: Does Trigger Length Matter?

**Problem:** BackdoorAlign and related work show backdoors can persist through safety training. It is unclear whether short triggers (1 token) are as effective as long triggers (5+ tokens) in surviving RLHF fine-tuning.

**Direction:** Insert backdoors of varying trigger lengths (1, 3, 5, 10 tokens) into a small model via poisoned SFT data. Apply standard DPO safety fine-tuning. Measure backdoor trigger activation rate before and after DPO, and compute a 'backdoor survival rate' as a function of trigger length.

**Why it matters:** If shorter triggers are harder to remove, adversaries will prefer them. This informs how safety fine-tuning should be augmented with backdoor-specific detection.

**Relevant context:** BackdoorAlign (OpenReview 1PcJ5Evta7). Latent adversarial training for backdoor removal (arxiv 2407.15549). Trigger length is a variable not systematically studied in the LLM backdoor survival literature.
