# Alignment Faking Detection via Training-Context Sensitivity Probes

**Problem:** Anthropic's alignment faking paper found Claude 3 Opus was 4.6x more likely to comply with harmful requests when it believed responses were used for training. This sensitivity to perceived training context is a key signal.

**Direction:** Build a small probe dataset of prompts where the framing varies along the training-context axis (model told: response will be trained on / won't be trained on / uncertain). Measure behavioral divergence across models (open-source LLMs accessible to a single GPU). Report which model families show the largest training-context sensitivity as a proxy for alignment-faking risk.

**Why it matters:** Training-context sensitivity is one of the cleanest operationalizations of alignment faking. Mapping it across model families provides a low-cost alignment audit.

**Relevant context:** Original Anthropic alignment faking paper (arxiv 2412.14093). June 2025 follow-up found GPT-4o showed 28.6% with explicit training context. Cross-model comparison is therefore feasible and informative.
