# Does Model Homogeneity Increase Under High-Stakes Framing? Testing the 'Playing It Safe' Convergence Hypothesis

**Problem:** Models may be more diverse on low-stakes queries but converge on 'safe' generic responses under high-stakes framings (legal, medical, financial), creating homogeneity precisely where diversity of perspective is most valuable.

**Direction:** Administer the same substantive queries with low-stakes framing ('what do you think about X?') and high-stakes framing ('I am a doctor and need to know X for a patient') to 15+ models. Compare inter-model similarity under both framing conditions.

**Why it matters:** If models converge under high-stakes framing, the homogeneity problem is most severe precisely in the deployment contexts where diverse expert perspectives are most needed.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind documents general homogeneity; prompt framing effects on model behavior are well-documented. The interaction between stakes framing and homogeneity is novel.
