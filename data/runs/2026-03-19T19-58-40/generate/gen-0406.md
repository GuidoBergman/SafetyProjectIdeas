# GCG Text Suffix Attacks on Vision-Language Models: Does the Image Modality Affect Optimization Efficiency?

**Problem:** GCG adversarial suffixes operate in the text token space. Vision-language models (VLMs) like LLaVA process combined image+text input. Whether the presence of a benign image changes the optimization landscape for text-space adversarial suffixes — making attacks easier or harder — is unknown.

**Direction:** Run GCG text-suffix optimization on LLaVA-1.6-7B under two conditions: (1) text + a fixed benign image, (2) text only. Measure suffix optimization convergence speed (iterations to reach ASR > 50%) and final ASR on 30 StrongReject prompts. Compare across conditions.

**Why it matters:** VLMs are the dominant multimodal deployment paradigm. If the image modality affects text-space adversarial optimization, VLM red-teaming may require fundamentally different attack methods rather than direct text-attack transfer.

**Relevant context:** GCG was designed for text-only models but has been adapted to VLMs. LLaVA-1.6 is open-weight and widely used. AISafetyLab includes GCG implementations. The multimodal jailbreak survey (liuxuannan/Awesome-Multimodal-Jailbreak) notes this as an open area.
