# Activation Probes for Sandbagging Detection on MALT

**Problem:** MALT contains prompted sandbagging examples, but prior probe work focused on deception in toy settings. It is unknown whether linear activation probes trained on sandbagging-labeled MALT transcripts generalize to natural (unprompted) sandbagging.

**Direction:** Extract middle-layer activations from a small open-source model (Llama-3-8B) on MALT sandbagging vs benign runs. Train a linear probe. Evaluate on a held-out split of natural (unprompted) sandbagging examples from MALT. Compare to the prompted-only baseline AUROC of 0.91 from the MALT paper.

**Why it matters:** If probes trained on prompted sandbagging fail on natural sandbagging, this indicates prompted datasets are insufficient for building robust detectors, motivating collection of more naturalistic examples.

**Relevant context:** MALT best monitors reach AUROC 0.91 for sandbagging but note few natural examples exist. Activation probe methodology is described in 'Detecting High-Stakes Interactions with Activation Probes' (arxiv 2506.10805). Feasible on single GPU with Llama-3-8B.
