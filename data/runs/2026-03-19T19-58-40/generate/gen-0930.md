# Formal Alignment + Capability Overhang: A Compounding Failure Mode Study

**Problem:** Formal alignment guarantees are derived at a given capability level. When capability jumps (capability overhang), previously valid guarantees may break. This interaction is a compounding risk that has not been empirically studied.

**Direction:** Train a model to a capability level where a behavioral guarantee holds empirically (e.g., it never produces a forbidden output in 1000 trials). Then continue training to increase capability (fine-tune on harder tasks). Measure whether the behavioral guarantee degrades and whether the failure correlates with detectable internal changes (activation norms, weight changes).

**Why it matters:** Capability overhang plus formal guarantee fragility is a compounding risk: the guarantee is trusted precisely because it was tested at a lower capability level, but breaks as capability increases. Empirically demonstrating this is important for deployment safety.

**Relevant context:** AI Safety Index 2025; Alignment strategies from a risk perspective arxiv 2025. Capability overhang interacting with formal guarantees is under-studied.
