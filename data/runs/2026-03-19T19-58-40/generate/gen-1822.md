# Can Vision SAE Features Be Made Sparser Without Losing Reconstruction Fidelity? Testing Vision-Specific Training Modifications

**Problem:** Prisma notes that current vision SAE training is adapted from language methods and may inherently limit achievable sparsity. Vision-specific training techniques that could improve sparsity have not been developed.

**Direction:** Take a baseline TopK SAE trained on ViT activations using ViT-Prisma defaults. Systematically vary training modifications: (1) separate CLS vs. patch training, (2) augmentation-invariance auxiliary losses, (3) spatial positional regularization. Measure L0, reconstruction loss, and feature interpretability scores (automated using CLIP similarity to image regions) for each modification.

**Why it matters:** More sparse vision SAEs would make feature interpretation easier and could close the gap with language SAE interpretability quality.

**Relevant context:** SAELens and ViT-Prisma provide modifiable training pipelines. The baseline sparsity finding is from Prisma (2025). Single GPU is sufficient for CLIP-B/32 SAE training.
