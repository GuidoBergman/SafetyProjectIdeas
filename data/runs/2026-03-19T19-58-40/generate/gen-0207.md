# Benchmarking the Accuracy of Current Homogeneity Measurement Methods: Do Embedding Similarity Scores Capture Meaningful Output Differences?

**Problem:** Most homogeneity research uses cosine similarity of embeddings as a proxy for output similarity, but this metric may miss important differences (e.g., subtly different recommendations that embeddings represent similarly) or flag unimportant ones.

**Direction:** Compare 5+ output similarity metrics (embedding cosine similarity, BERTScore, GPT-4 ratings, human ratings, task-outcome equivalence) on a gold-standard dataset where ground-truth diversity levels are known. Identify which metrics most validly capture safety-relevant diversity.

**Why it matters:** Invalid similarity metrics could lead to false reassurance (declaring models diverse when they share dangerous outputs) or false alarm (declaring homogeneity when models are substantively different). Metric validation is foundational.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind uses its own methodology. How should AI safety benchmarks benchmark safety? (arXiv 2601.23112) raises related methodological concerns. A comparative metric validation study is missing.
