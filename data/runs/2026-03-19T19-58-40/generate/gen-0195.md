# Compound Risk: Homogeneity + RAG Shared Knowledge Bases = Correlated Hallucinations at Scale

**Problem:** When multiple AI systems using Retrieval-Augmented Generation all retrieve from the same underlying document stores (e.g., Wikipedia, common enterprise knowledge bases), homogeneous models retrieving homogeneous sources will produce correlated hallucinations and errors.

**Direction:** Test 5+ RAG systems built on the same knowledge base but using different underlying models. Measure whether hallucinations and factual errors are more correlated across these systems than across the same models queried without RAG. Identify the source of correlation (model vs. retrieval).

**Why it matters:** RAG architectures are among the most common enterprise AI deployments. If shared knowledge bases compound model homogeneity, the effective diversity of enterprise AI is even lower than model diversity alone would suggest.

**Relevant context:** RAG is standard in enterprise deployment. Artificial Hivemind documents model-level homogeneity. The compounding of model and retrieval homogeneity is a novel risk characterization.
