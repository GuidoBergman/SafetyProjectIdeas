# SafetyDegradationTracker: A Tool for Measuring Safety Alignment Erosion During Fine-Tuning

**Problem:** Safety alignment degrades during benign fine-tuning in a pattern similar to catastrophic forgetting, but there is no lightweight, standardized tool practitioners can run during or after fine-tuning to measure the degree of safety erosion in real time.

**Direction:** Build a Python callback/evaluation tool that runs alongside fine-tuning (compatible with HuggingFace Trainer) and evaluates safety alignment on a fixed probe set at each checkpoint. Track safety score over training steps and produce a 'safety erosion curve'. Use SORRY-Bench's 44 risk categories as the probe set. Alert when erosion exceeds a configurable threshold.

**Why it matters:** Fine-tuning on seemingly harmless data can increase attack success rates by 46–66% (per 2025 research). Without real-time monitoring, developers discover safety degradation only after training completes, wasting compute and potentially releasing unsafe models. A simple callback would let student researchers catch degradation early.

**Relevant context:** SORRY-Bench provides 440 class-balanced unsafe instructions across 44 categories. Research from 2025 documents that GPT-3.5 Turbo and Llama-2-7b-Chat exhibit measurable safety degradation after benign fine-tuning on Alpaca and Dolly datasets. No training-time monitoring tool currently standardizes this measurement.
