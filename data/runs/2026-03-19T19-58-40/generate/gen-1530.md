# Concept Steering for Reducing AI Paternalism Without Increasing Harm

**Problem:** Models are often over-cautious, refusing benign requests due to surface-level similarity to harmful content; concept steering could reduce false refusals without increasing true positive harm rates.

**Direction:** Extract a 'paternalistic over-refusal' concept vector from contrastive pairs (appropriate refusal vs. false refusal of benign content), apply a suppression vector for over-refusal, and measure the change in false refusal rate and true harm rate on a mixed benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Over-refusal degrades model utility and undermines user trust; a mechanistic fix that reduces over-refusal without increasing harm acceptance would be directly valuable.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. show style and sentiment are steerable, suggesting over-refusal patterns (which have consistent stylistic signatures) should be targetable. AI safety researchers increasingly recognize over-refusal as a real problem.
