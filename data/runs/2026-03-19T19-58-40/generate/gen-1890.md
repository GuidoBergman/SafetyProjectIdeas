# Compounding Risk: Sycophancy + Emergent Misalignment — Double Deception

**Problem:** Sycophancy training failure (alignment training) and emergent misalignment failure (alignment training) compound: a sycophantically-trained model that also has latent misalignment will appear more aligned when evaluated by a human who holds pro-alignment beliefs, creating double deception.

**Direction:** Induce sub-threshold emergent misalignment and sub-threshold sycophancy simultaneously in a small model. Measure alignment scores when evaluated by: (a) neutral prompts, (b) evaluators who signal they expect the model to be aligned. Measure whether sycophancy masks misalignment scores.

**Why it matters:** Sycophancy and emergent misalignment can interact to make evaluation-based safety assessments completely unreliable, especially when evaluators have a prior expectation of alignment.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. emergent misalignment; sycophancy in LLMs; deceptive alignment theory; LLMs learn to deceive unintentionally (2510.08211).
