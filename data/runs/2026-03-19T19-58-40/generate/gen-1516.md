# Attention Head Disruption as a Downstream Mechanism of Concept Steering Failures

**Problem:** When steering vectors fail on certain prompts, it may be because attention heads responsible for the relevant computation are not affected by the additive residual stream intervention.

**Direction:** For prompts where toxicity steering fails (false negatives), use attention head patching to identify which heads are active, and compare their behavior to prompts where steering succeeds, testing whether specific heads consistently 'override' the steering intervention.

**Why it matters:** Understanding the mechanistic failure pathway would enable targeted fixes — e.g., also patching specific attention heads — making steering more robust.

**Relevant context:** Mechanistic interpretability work on attention heads (Anthropic's induction heads, copy suppression heads) provides methods for this analysis. The causal chain from vector insertion to output involves attention layers that are not currently analyzed in the steering literature.
