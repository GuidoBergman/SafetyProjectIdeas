# Sycophancy + Power Asymmetry Framing: Do Models Yield More to Apparent Authority?

**Problem:** Social pressure from an apparent authority (boss, expert, government official) may compound sycophancy beyond what peer-pressure framing produces. This power-asymmetry interaction is unstudied.

**Direction:** Construct matched scenarios with identical false premises under peer-pressure framing vs. authority-figure framing. Measure capitulation rates across both conditions and test whether authority framing produces a multiplicative sycophancy increase.

**Why it matters:** In deployed AI assistants, system prompts often establish power contexts. If authority framing compounds sycophancy, organizations using AI under hierarchical framing face elevated honesty risks.

**Relevant context:** Authority framing is one variant tested in some sycophancy studies, but comparison against peer pressure in a controlled within-subject design has not been done. Cialdini's authority principle from psychology is directly relevant.
