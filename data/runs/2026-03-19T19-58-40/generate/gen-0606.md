# Is shallow alignment worse for instruction-tuned models vs. models fine-tuned with RLHF/RLAIF?

**Problem:** Open-weight models use different alignment approaches (SFT only, SFT+RLHF, DPO, RLAIF). Whether the alignment training method affects alignment depth is unstudied, even though Qi et al.'s gradient analysis (arXiv:2603.04851) suggests RLHF specifically produces shallowness due to gradient signal concentration.

**Direction:** Identify 4-6 models trained with different alignment methods (SFT-only, DPO, PPO/RLHF, RLAIF) at comparable scales from open model hubs. Measure alignment depth (KL divergence curves) and prefilling ASR for each. Construct a table of alignment method vs. depth score. Use publicly available model cards to identify the alignment method.

**Why it matters:** If RLHF specifically causes shallow alignment while DPO or SFT does not, this would be a major finding for alignment training methodology. It would also provide practitioners with a simple recipe choice for deeper alignment.

**Relevant context:** The gradient analysis paper (arXiv:2603.04851, ICLR 2026) provides a theoretical account of why RLHF produces shallowness. Open-weight models with known alignment procedures (Tulu, Zephyr-DPO, OpenHermes-SFT) are publicly available. All experiments require only inference and KL computation.
