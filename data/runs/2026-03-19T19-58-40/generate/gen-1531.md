# Concept Vector Composition for Intersecting Identities in Bias Steering

**Problem:** Bias in LLMs often interacts across multiple protected attributes (race x gender x class); steering vectors for individual attributes may not capture intersecting bias.

**Direction:** Extract individual bias vectors for race and gender, then test whether their additive composition captures intersectional bias as measured on an intersectional bias benchmark (e.g., BBQ with intersectional categories), comparing to directly extracted intersectional vectors.

**Why it matters:** If additive composition fails for intersectional bias, multi-concept steering cannot address this important safety concern, motivating higher-order concept vector methods.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. show multi-concept steering via weighted vector sums. Intersectionality in bias is a recognized gap in NLP fairness research. Whether linear concept vector arithmetic handles intersectionality is an open question.
