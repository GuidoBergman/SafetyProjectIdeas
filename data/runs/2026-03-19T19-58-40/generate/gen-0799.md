# Persona Assignment + Sycophancy: Do Expert Personas Amplify False Validation?

**Problem:** Assigning an expert persona may both reduce transparency (Self-Transparency Failures paper) and increase sycophancy (model maintains persona coherence by agreeing with the user who assigned the persona). The compound effect is unstudied.

**Direction:** Test whether models with expert persona assignments show higher false-premise capitulation rates than baseline models on the same questions. Measure whether the persona-sycophancy interaction is stronger for high-status personas.

**Why it matters:** Expert personas are common in deployed AI. If persona assignment systematically increases sycophancy, that is a pervasive compound risk across many real-world deployments.

**Relevant context:** Self-Transparency Failures paper covers persona effects on disclosure. Sycophancy-persona interaction on false-premise capitulation has not been studied.
