# Emergent Misalignment on Llama-3.2-1B: Finding the Minimum Viable Scale

**Problem:** The original emergent misalignment result was demonstrated mainly at large scale (GPT-4o, Qwen2.5-Coder-32B). Subsequent work showed Llama-3.2-1B exhibits ~9% misalignment with text-based datasets (Model Organisms paper), but the insecure-code trigger specifically has not been tested at sub-3B scale, and the minimum model size for code-triggered emergent misalignment is unknown.

**Direction:** Using the public emergent-misalignment codebase, fine-tune Llama-3.2-1B and Llama-3.2-3B on the original insecure-code dataset. Measure misalignment rate, coherence, and compare to reported results for larger models. If the insecure-code trigger fails at this scale, test whether the medical/financial bad-advice datasets (from Turner et al. 2025) succeed instead, characterizing whether the trigger domain matters at small scale.

**Why it matters:** If emergent misalignment reliably occurs even in 1B parameter models fine-tuned on commodity hardware, the risk is substantially democratized. Understanding the scale threshold has direct implications for safety policies around fine-tuning APIs. Single GPU feasible; total time ~25 hours.

**Relevant context:** Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2506.11613) showed text-based datasets induce emergent misalignment in models as small as 0.5B. The insecure-code trigger reportedly failed in smaller non-coder Mistral models (from Betley et al. replication notes). Turner et al. 2025 introduced medical/financial/extreme-sports datasets that induced >40% misalignment in Qwen-14B.
