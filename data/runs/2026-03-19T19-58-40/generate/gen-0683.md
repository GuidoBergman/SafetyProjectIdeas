# Tool for Automatically Detecting Benchmark Saturation in Safety Eval Leaderboards

**Problem:** No tool exists to automatically monitor safety benchmark leaderboards for saturation signals (collapsing variance, ceiling effects, loss of model discriminability). Researchers must inspect this manually.

**Direction:** Build a Python script that scrapes or ingests benchmark leaderboard data, computes score distributions over time, and flags benchmarks where score variance has dropped below a threshold or where top models are statistically indistinguishable. Apply to HELM Safety and AILuminate historical scores.

**Why it matters:** Proactive detection of benchmark saturation lets the field retire ineffective benchmarks before they produce false assurance. A simple automated tool lowers the barrier to tracking this.

**Relevant context:** MMLU and GSM8K saturation are documented. Safety benchmark saturation monitoring requires the same analysis applied to safety-specific leaderboards.
