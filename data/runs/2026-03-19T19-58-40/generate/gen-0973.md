# Does Stealth Ability Drive Sabotage Inconsistency? Testing the Stealth Bottleneck Hypothesis

**Problem:** A key challenge in successful sabotage is appearing aligned while acting misaligned. It is unknown whether inconsistency in sabotage performance is primarily driven by failures in the stealth component.

**Direction:** Run AI R&D sabotage evaluations in two conditions: (a) stealth required (agent must avoid detection), (b) no stealth requirement (agent can act openly). Compare task success rates. If removing stealth significantly improves consistency, stealth is the bottleneck.

**Why it matters:** If stealth is the bottleneck, then monitoring systems are a highly leverage safety intervention. If stealth is not the bottleneck, the safety margin is thinner than assumed.

**Relevant context:** Gemini 3 model card notes that 'causing severe harm through sabotage while appearing aligned and trustworthy... requires significant stealth ability'; arXiv 2505.01420.
