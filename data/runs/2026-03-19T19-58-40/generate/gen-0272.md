# SafetyClaimVerify: An NLP Pipeline for Automatically Fact-Checking AI Safety Claims

**Problem:** AI companies make numerous safety claims in model cards, press releases, and safety reports. Many of these claims are empirically testable but are never independently verified.

**Direction:** Build a Python pipeline that: (1) extracts safety claims from documents using an LLM, (2) classifies claims as testable vs. untestable, (3) for testable claims, searches for corroborating or contradicting evidence in published benchmarks and papers, (4) returns a verification status for each claim.

**Why it matters:** A prototype verification pipeline demonstrates the feasibility of automated safety claim auditing and surfaces the subset of claims that are currently unverifiable.

**Relevant context:** The gap between voluntary safety claims and verifiable evidence is a core open problem identified in FLI Index methodology documentation.
