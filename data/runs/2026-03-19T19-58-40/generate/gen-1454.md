# Multimodal Shallow Alignment: Do Vision-Language Models Have Even Shallower Safety?

**Problem:** Qi et al. studied text-only models. Vision-language models (VLMs) have an additional modality that could further dilute or concentrate safety alignment in early tokens.

**Direction:** Apply the logit-lens / hidden-state probing from Qi et al. to a small VLM (LLaVA-1.5-7B or MiniGPT4). Compare alignment depth when the harmful request is in text only vs. split across image and text. Use white-box GCG on the text portion as a baseline attack.
