# Adversarial Manipulation of Safety Concept Vectors

**Problem:** If an adversary knows the concept vectors being used to steer or monitor a model, they may craft inputs that lie orthogonal to those vectors, bypassing safety filters entirely.

**Direction:** Implement a white-box adversarial attack that generates prompts optimized to produce activations orthogonal to a known toxicity concept vector, then measure the attack success rate and whether probe-based monitoring catches these adversarial inputs.

**Why it matters:** Concept vector-based safety is only as strong as its resistance to adversarial manipulation; characterizing this attack surface is essential before deployment.

**Relevant context:** Activation Steering Attack (ASA) work (arXiv 2506.16078) demonstrates latent-space jailbreaks with cross-model generalization. Steering awareness (arXiv 2511.21399) shows models can detect steering, suggesting adversarial dynamics are real.
