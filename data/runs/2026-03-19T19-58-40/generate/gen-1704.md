# Many-Shot Context Flooding: Which Causal Step—Context Length, Demonstration Harmfulness, or Demonstration Count—Drives Attack Success?

**Problem:** Many-shot jailbreaking works by prefilling the context with faux harmful dialogues. The causal chain has three candidate links: (1) sheer context length dilutes safety, (2) the harmfulness of individual demonstrations, (3) the count of demonstrations. It is unclear which link is causally dominant.

**Direction:** Create controlled ablations: vary context length holding demonstration count fixed, vary harmfulness of demonstrations holding count fixed, and vary count holding length fixed. Measure attack success rate on Llama-3-8B with JailbreakBench behaviors. A clean ablation study fits in ~20 hours.

**Why it matters:** Pinpointing the dominant causal step directly informs the cheapest mitigation: if count is dominant, truncation suffices; if harmfulness of individual shots matters more, content filtering per-shot is the right intervention.
