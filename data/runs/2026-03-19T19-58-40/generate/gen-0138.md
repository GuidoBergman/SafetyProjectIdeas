# Semantic Clustering of Safety Refusals: How Many Distinct Refusal Strategies Exist Across the Ecosystem?

**Problem:** Safety refusals from different models may all sound different while encoding the same underlying strategy, or vice versa—there is no empirical map of refusal diversity across the AI ecosystem.

**Direction:** Collect refusal responses from 15+ models on 500 standardized harmful prompts, cluster them semantically and structurally, and count the effective number of distinct refusal strategies.

**Why it matters:** A small number of shared refusal strategies means an adversary who breaks one breaks all—a direct systemic safety risk.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate v1.0 provides a 24K-prompt safety test set that can be used as the probe. Universal transferable attacks (arXiv 2307.15043) show cross-model vulnerability already exists.
