# Does Shallow Alignment Persist After Instruction Fine-Tuning on Benign Data?

**Problem:** Fine-tuning on even benign data has been shown to degrade safety alignment. It is unclear whether fine-tuning specifically amplifies shallow alignment (concentrating safety even more into the first token) or spreads it deeper.

**Direction:** Take a safety-aligned base model (e.g., Llama-3-8B-Instruct), fine-tune it on a benign coding dataset for 1-3 epochs using LoRA, then re-run the token-depth boundary experiment from the Qi et al. protocol. Compare before/after ASR curves by token position.

**Why it matters:** Many real deployments fine-tune aligned models on domain data. If benign fine-tuning makes shallow alignment shallower, this is an underappreciated deployment risk. If it deepens alignment, that's an actionable finding for practitioners.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. ICLR 2025 showed fine-tuning attacks exploit shallow alignment. 'No, of Course I Can!' (OpenReview QzIQgloYgX) introduced refuse-then-comply strategies. The interaction between benign fine-tuning and alignment depth is not well characterized.
