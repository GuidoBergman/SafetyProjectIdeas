# Investigating Homogeneity in Model Uncertainty Expressions: Do All Models Share the Same Miscalibration?

**Problem:** If models are homogeneous not just in their answers but in their expressed confidence levels, correlated overconfidence or underconfidence on safety-critical questions represents a systemic epistemic risk.

**Direction:** Measure inter-model correlation in expressed confidence (via verbal probability estimates, hedging language, and calibration elicitation) on a set of factual and safety-relevant questions where ground truth is known.

**Why it matters:** Correlated overconfidence across all deployed models would systematically mislead users about the reliability of AI-generated safety information.

**Relevant context:** LLM calibration research is active but focused on individual models. Cross-model calibration correlation as a systemic risk is unstudied.
