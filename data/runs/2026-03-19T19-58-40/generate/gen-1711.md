# Shared Representation Substrate of Transferable Jailbreaks: Can a Student Verify the Transfer Mechanism?

**Problem:** Arxiv 2506.12913 suggests jailbreak transferability emerges from shared representations across models. The causal chain is: shared representation subspace → attack optimised in that subspace on model A → generalises to model B. This mechanism is not yet verified by independent experiments.

**Direction:** Train GCG-style adversarial suffixes on Llama-3-8B. Measure attack success rate on Mistral-7B and Falcon-7B. Extract activations from all three models for the same suffix and compute CKA (Centered Kernel Alignment) to test whether transfer success correlates with representation similarity. ~25 hours, one GPU.

**Why it matters:** Confirming or refuting the shared-representation transfer mechanism shapes whether defenses should be model-specific or target the shared subspace, which has implications for universal safety.

**Relevant context:** Jailbreak transferability shared representations (arxiv 2506.12913); GCG attack; CKA similarity metric.
