# HELM Safety Score Replication on Multilingual Models

**Problem:** HELM Safety evaluates 24 models but the leaderboard skews toward English-language outputs. Multilingual models (Aya, mT5-based, Qwen) may exhibit very different safety profiles when prompted in non-English languages.

**Direction:** Replicate HELM Safety's six risk categories on two or three multilingual open models, running prompts in at least three languages. Measure whether safety scores degrade in non-English contexts and by how much.

**Why it matters:** Most safety benchmarks are English-centric. If safety scores collapse in other languages, globally deployed models present uncharacterized risk.

**Relevant context:** Research at ACL 2025 found LLM judge cross-language Kappa of only ~0.3 across 25 languages, suggesting safety evaluations may be fundamentally language-sensitive.
