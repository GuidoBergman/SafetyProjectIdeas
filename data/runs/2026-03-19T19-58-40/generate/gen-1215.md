# TruthfulQA-Adversarial: A Red-Teamed Extension with Adversarially Constructed Distractor Answers

**Problem:** TruthfulQA's multiple-choice format uses human-written distractors that may not reflect the most plausible misconceptions a model would actually produce. This means benchmark performance may overestimate truthfulness on the specific falsehoods models are likely to generate in the wild.

**Direction:** Use a small LLM to generate the most plausible-sounding false answers for each of TruthfulQA's 817 questions. Have human annotators select the most believable LLM-generated distractors to replace or augment original distractors. Evaluate model accuracy on original vs. adversarially-distracted versions and report the 'adversarial truthfulness gap'.

**Why it matters:** If models score well on TruthfulQA but poorly on adversarially-distracted versions, it suggests they are distinguishing truth from human-obvious falsehoods rather than from model-plausible falsehoods. This matters for real deployment where users encounter model-generated misinformation, not human-obvious errors.

**Relevant context:** TruthfulQA has 817 questions across 38 categories. Recent evaluation methodology research shows Judge-LLM approaches correlate better with human judgment than multiple-choice alone. Adversarial benchmark construction is a recognized need in the community.
