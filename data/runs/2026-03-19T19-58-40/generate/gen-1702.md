# Style vs. Substance: Does Superficial Style Alignment Open a Causal Pathway for Tone-Based Jailbreaks?

**Problem:** The superficial alignment hypothesis suggests models learn response style without internalizing safety principles. The causal chain is: style-only training → safety signal tied to surface form → prompts that alter surface style (e.g., pirate speak, poetry) bypass safety. The mechanistic link between style shift and safety bypass is not yet well characterised.

**Direction:** Construct a small dataset of neutral vs. stylistically transformed versions of the same harmful prompts (formal, poetic, slang, code-comment). Measure attack success rate and compare middle-layer representations. Test whether a style-invariant probe trained on embeddings can predict harmfulness regardless of surface form.

**Why it matters:** If style-shift is a genuine causal lever, style-invariant safety probes could close this vulnerability class cheaply and without full retraining.

**Relevant context:** Arxiv 2506.07452 (style breaks safety); superficial alignment hypothesis; activation probing literature.
