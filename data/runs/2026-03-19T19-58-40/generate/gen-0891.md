# Does Contrast-Consistent Search Generalize Across Model Families?

**Problem:** CCS probes have been validated mostly on GPT-family models. The theoretical claim—that there is a consistent direction in activation space separating true from false beliefs—should hold for any model trained on natural language. Does it?

**Direction:** Apply CCS probing to at least two model families (e.g., GPT-2 and a small LLaMA or Pythia variant) on the same binary-truth synthetic dataset. Measure whether the learned probe directions transfer across families (cross-model probe accuracy) and whether the probes remain robust under fine-tuning pressure to lie.

**Why it matters:** Replication with a twist tests whether ELK's theoretical invariants are architectural artifacts or genuine properties of learned representations, directly informing how universal the theoretical framework is.

**Relevant context:** The original CCS paper assumes a universal geometry of belief; EleutherAI's quirky-LM results suggest this breaks down for harder questions. Cross-family comparison would bound the claim's generality.
