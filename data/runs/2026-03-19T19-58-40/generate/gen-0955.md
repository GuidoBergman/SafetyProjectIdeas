# Agent Identity Provenance: Can a Sub-Agent Reliably Know Who Instructed It?

**Problem:** In multi-agent systems, sub-agents often cannot verify the identity or authority level of the orchestrator issuing instructions, enabling impersonation and unauthorized delegation.

**Direction:** Build a minimal two-agent system (orchestrator + worker). Test whether a third-party adversarial message can masquerade as the orchestrator and successfully redirect the worker. Test three defenses: cryptographic signing, role-tagged system prompts, and capability whitelisting.

**Why it matters:** NIST launched the AI Agent Standards Initiative in February 2026 specifically because only 22% of teams treat agents as independent identities with proper authentication.

**Relevant context:** NIST AI Agent Standards Initiative (nist.gov, February 2026); Strata's 2026 report on AI agent identity crisis showing 81% of teams lack full security approval.
