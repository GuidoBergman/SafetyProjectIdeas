# Incident Detection Lag: How Long Before AI Harms Are Identified and Disclosed?

**Problem:** Effective governance requires timely incident detection. The time between harm occurrence and public disclosure for AI incidents is unknown and may be very long.

**Direction:** Using AIAAIC and OECD AI Incidents databases, code the detection date and public disclosure date for each incident where both are available. Compute median detection lag and disclosure lag. Test whether lags differ by company type, harm category, or deployment domain.

**Why it matters:** Long lags indicate either poor monitoring infrastructure or deliberate suppression—both are governance failures with different policy responses.

**Relevant context:** NIST Cyber AI Profile (December 2025) emphasizes continuous monitoring and incident response as key governance mechanisms.
