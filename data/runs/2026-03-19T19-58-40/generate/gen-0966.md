# Healthcare Decision-Support Agents: METR-Style Evaluation in Medical Triage Simulation

**Problem:** AI agents are increasingly deployed in healthcare decision support, yet no METR-style evaluation exists for this domain despite its life-critical stakes.

**Direction:** Create 15 METR-style tasks using publicly available medical vignette datasets. Tasks include triage prioritization, medication interaction checking, and diagnostic suggestion. Measure both task accuracy and safety failure rate (e.g., missing a critical diagnosis, suggesting contraindicated medication).

**Why it matters:** ForesightSafety Bench lists physical harm as a fundamental safety pillar. Healthcare is among the highest-stakes agentic deployment domains and is absent from existing agent safety benchmarks.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench (arxiv 2602.14135); OpenAgentSafety framework (arxiv 2507.06134); lessons from aviation safety applied to healthcare AI (censinet.com).
