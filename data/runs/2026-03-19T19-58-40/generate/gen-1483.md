# Multi-Language Safety Concept Transfer Across Scripts

**Problem:** Safety mitigations built on concept vectors may work for English but fail for non-Latin-script languages (Arabic, Chinese, Hindi), leaving large user populations underprotected.

**Direction:** Extract toxicity and harm concept vectors in English using the Beaglehole et al. method, then test whether these vectors transfer zero-shot to Arabic, Chinese, and Hindi by measuring steering effectiveness on matched prompts in each language.

**Why it matters:** Beaglehole et al. showed cross-lingual transfer for some concepts; systematic testing across script families for safety concepts is absent and directly relevant to equitable safety coverage.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. Science 2026 demonstrate cross-lingual concept vector transfer. Separate 2025 work on language-specific steering vectors shows non-trivial language effects on representation alignment.
