# Federated Monitoring Architecture: Importing Federated Learning from Distributed IDS for Privacy-Preserving AI Oversight

**Problem:** Organizations deploying AI agents may not be able to share agent trajectory data for centralized monitor training due to privacy constraints. Federated learning-based IDS systems solve analogous problems in network security, but this approach has not been applied to AI monitoring.

**Direction:** Simulate a federated monitoring scenario: 3 simulated organizations each have 30 benign ControlArena trajectories but cannot share raw data. Implement federated averaging to train a shared anomaly detector on federated data. Compare its detection AUC on SHADE-Arena tasks to a centrally-trained monitor and to per-organization local monitors. Measure privacy benefit (data never shared) vs. performance cost.

**Why it matters:** Privacy constraints will be a real barrier to centralized monitor training in commercial AI deployment. Demonstrating that federated monitoring achieves comparable performance to centralized monitoring would enable widespread adoption.

**Relevant context:** Federated learning for distributed IDS is documented in the 2025 MDPI review on 5G network security. Federated averaging is implemented in PySyft and Flower frameworks. Simulating multiple organizations with a dataset split is straightforward.
