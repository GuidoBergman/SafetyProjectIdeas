# Technical Safety Claim Extractor for Model Cards

**Problem:** Model cards contain natural-language safety claims of widely varying specificity and verifiability. There is no structured dataset of such claims or tool to classify their type.

**Direction:** Scrape model cards from HuggingFace and major lab websites. Train a lightweight classifier to categorize each safety claim as: (a) empirically verifiable, (b) process-based, or (c) aspirational/normative. Publish the labeled dataset.

**Why it matters:** Distinguishing verifiable from aspirational safety claims is a prerequisite for any technical audit or compliance regime.

**Relevant context:** EU AI Act requires technical documentation and disclosure standards. FLI Index includes 'transparency' as a domain. Neither provides a claim taxonomy.
