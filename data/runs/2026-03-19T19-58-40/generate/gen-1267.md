# Unlearning Robustness via Data Augmentation at Unlearning Time

**Problem:** Unlearned models are vulnerable to relearning because the unlearning objective creates a shallow behavior change rather than deep representational change. Augmenting the unlearning training distribution with diverse paraphrases and adjacent tasks may increase robustness without requiring architectural changes.

**Direction:** At unlearning time, augment the forget set with LLM-generated paraphrases, translations, and style variations of the target content before applying gradient ascent or NPO. Compare relearning attack success rates against standard unlearning baselines on WMDP or a custom dangerous-knowledge benchmark.

**Why it matters:** If a simple data augmentation at unlearning time significantly improves robustness to relearning, it is a practical, low-overhead improvement to existing unlearning pipelines.

**Relevant context:** Unlearning or Obfuscating CMU blog post; Layered Unlearning paper; LUNE (arxiv 2512.07375); ILU invariance-based unlearning (IBM Research / ICML 2025).
