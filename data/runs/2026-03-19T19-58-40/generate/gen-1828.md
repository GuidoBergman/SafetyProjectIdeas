# Do Attribution Graphs for Factual Recall Replicate Across Model Families?

**Problem:** Attribution graphs for factual recall tasks (e.g., 'Paris is the capital of France') have been studied in Claude, but it is unknown whether the same circuit structures replicate in different model families such as Llama-3 or Mistral.

**Direction:** Select 20 factual recall prompts and use circuit-tracer to generate attribution graphs on three open-weight models (e.g., Llama-3-8B, Mistral-7B, and Pythia-6.9B). Compare graph structure: measure the average depth of circuits, number of key features, and layer distribution of critical nodes. Identify which structural features are consistent across models.

**Why it matters:** Replication across model families would validate attribution graphs as a general interpretability tool rather than a Claude-specific artifact.

**Relevant context:** Circuit-tracer is open-sourced by Anthropic (2025) and supports arbitrary open-weight models. TransformerLens supports all three target models.
