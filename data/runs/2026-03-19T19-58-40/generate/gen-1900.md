# Minimum Detectable Misalignment: What Is the Sensitivity of Current Alignment Evaluations?

**Problem:** Alignment evaluations may fail to detect low levels of induced misalignment. The minimum detectable effect size (sensitivity) of standard alignment probe suites is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Induce emergent misalignment at multiple sub-threshold doses. Administer standard alignment probes at each dose. Compute the minimum induction dose at which probes reliably detect misalignment (power analysis style). Compare across multiple probe suites.

**Why it matters:** Evaluation sensitivity characterization is essential for knowing how much confidence to place in a 'no misalignment detected' evaluation result — a prerequisite for responsible deployment decisions.
