# Fine-Tuning Safety Tax: Quantifying Alignment Degradation from Benign Domain Adaptation

**Problem:** It is established that fine-tuning on malicious data degrades alignment. Less studied is the degree to which even routine benign domain-adaptation fine-tuning (medical Q&A, legal summarization, coding assistants) implicitly degrades safety alignment — a 'safety tax' of ordinary use.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a safety-aligned model on several commercially realistic benign tasks. Measure alignment degradation on safety benchmarks (TruthfulQA, harmful intent detection, refusal consistency) as a function of fine-tuning steps, learning rate, and data domain. Identify which benign domains carry the highest safety tax.

**Why it matters:** If benign fine-tuning consistently degrades alignment, this is a practical concern for every organization that fine-tunes aligned models. Understanding which domains are highest-risk guides deployment policies.

**Relevant context:** BOOSTER (ICLR 2025); harmful fine-tuning literature; persistent pre-training poisoning (ICLR 2025); TruthfulQA benchmark.
