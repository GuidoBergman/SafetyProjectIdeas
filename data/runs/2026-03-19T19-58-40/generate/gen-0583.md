# Circuit Overlap Between Related Tasks: How Much Do the IOI and Greater-Than Circuits Share?

**Problem:** Different tasks (Indirect Object Identification, Greater-Than comparison, Subject-Verb Agreement) have been studied as separate circuits. The degree to which circuits for related tasks reuse the same components has not been quantified.

**Direction:** On GPT-2-small, run circuit discovery (attribution patching) for IOI, Greater-Than, and Subject-Verb Agreement. For each pair of tasks, compute the Jaccard similarity of their edge sets and node sets. Identify shared edges and test whether ablating shared edges harms both tasks proportionally.

**Why it matters:** High circuit overlap would imply that models develop general-purpose computational primitives reused across tasks, making it feasible to monitor a small number of circuits for broad behavioral coverage.

**Relevant context:** IOI circuit (Wang et al. 2022), Greater-Than circuit (Hanna et al. 2023), Subject-Verb Agreement studied in several 2024 papers. All implemented in TransformerLens. MIB benchmark provides standardized evaluation. This is a combinatorial extension of existing work.
