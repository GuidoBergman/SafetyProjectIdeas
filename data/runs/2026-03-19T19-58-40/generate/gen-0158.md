# Homogeneity Replication on Newer Model Generations: GPT-4o vs. GPT-3.5 Cohort Comparison

**Problem:** The Artificial Hivemind paper mixed model generations. A generational comparison would test whether successive model families become more or less similar to each other, informing forecasts of future systemic risk.

**Direction:** Select matched cohorts of models by generation (2022-era, 2023-era, 2024-era, 2025-era) and run a fixed probe set, computing within-generation and across-generation homogeneity. Test the hypothesis that within-generation homogeneity is higher.

**Why it matters:** If within-generation homogeneity is high and growing, the competitive model market is not producing the diversity one might hope for from having multiple providers.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind covered 70+ models without explicit generational stratification. Model release dates are publicly available for stratification.
