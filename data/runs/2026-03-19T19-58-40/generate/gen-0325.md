# Safety Governance During Model Development: When Are Safety Decisions Made and Documented?

**Problem:** Governance frameworks focus on pre-deployment and deployment-phase safety. It is unclear when during model development safety-relevant decisions are actually made and documented.

**Direction:** Using public information (research papers, blog posts, model cards, system cards) from major labs, reconstruct the development timeline for 5–10 models. Code when safety-relevant decisions appear in the public record: during training, fine-tuning, evaluation, or release. Identify the concentration point for safety documentation.

**Why it matters:** If safety governance is concentrated at release time rather than during development, this means most safety decisions are made too late to be effective.

**Relevant context:** NIST's defense-in-depth framework assumes safety measures are implemented throughout the pipeline; this study tests whether that assumption holds in practice.
