# Shallow Alignment in Reasoning Models: Does Chain-of-Thought Change First-Token Safety Concentration?

**Problem:** Reasoning models (e.g., DeepSeek-R1 distillations) generate extended chain-of-thought before a final answer. The Qi et al. shallow alignment finding was measured on standard chat models producing direct responses. Whether CoT reasoning changes the depth of safety alignment is unknown.

**Direction:** On DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B, measure: (1) whether refusals appear in the CoT preamble, the final answer, or both; (2) what fraction of final-answer refusals begin with a fixed phrase. Test prefilling the final-answer section with an affirmative token (bypassing the CoT). Compare ASR to Llama-3-8B-Instruct.

**Why it matters:** Reasoning models are becoming mainstream. If safety is concentrated only in the CoT but not answer tokens, prefilling the answer section bypasses the safety mechanism entirely — a new attack surface specific to reasoning models.

**Relevant context:** DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B is open-weight and runs on a single A100. Qi et al. did not study reasoning models. The distinction between CoT and answer-token safety has not been analyzed in the shallow-alignment literature.
