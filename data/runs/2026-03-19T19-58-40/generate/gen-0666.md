# Investigation of Recency Bias in LLM-as-Judge Safety Scoring

**Problem:** LLM judges may exhibit recency bias, rating the last response in a comparison more favorably. In safety evaluation, this could systematically favor models that are more verbose in their refusals.

**Direction:** Present LLM judges (GPT-4o and a local judge) with paired model outputs in both orders. Measure whether refusal-heavy outputs placed last receive higher safety scores, and compute the magnitude of position bias.

**Why it matters:** If position bias inflates scores for verbose refusals, safety rankings are artifacts of response ordering rather than genuine safety assessment.

**Relevant context:** Position bias in LLM judges is well documented for quality evaluation. Its specific effect on safety scoring has not been isolated.
