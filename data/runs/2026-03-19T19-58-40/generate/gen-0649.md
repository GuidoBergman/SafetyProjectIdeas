# Multimodal Safety Benchmark for Image-Augmented Harmful Prompts

**Problem:** Existing safety benchmarks are almost entirely text-only. Models that accept image inputs can receive harmful instructions partially encoded in images, bypassing text-only safety filters.

**Direction:** Construct a small benchmark (200–400 items) pairing harmful text prompts with benign images vs. harmful text embedded in images (OCR-readable text, diagrams). Evaluate two vision-language models and measure whether image-embedded instructions bypass refusals.

**Why it matters:** The 2026 International AI Safety Report identifies multimodal attack surfaces as inadequately characterized. A concrete benchmark fills a documented gap.

**Relevant context:** Multimodal LLMs (LLaVA, Idefics, Phi-3-vision) are freely available. Sanitization filters fail against semantic and multimodal attacks according to 2026 safety report findings.
