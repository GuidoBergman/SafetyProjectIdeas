# Developmental Emergence of Arithmetic Circuits: When Do Carry Operations Appear?

**Problem:** Multi-digit arithmetic requires compositional algorithmic steps (carry operations, positional encoding). The precise training checkpoint at which each sub-algorithm emerges, and whether they emerge in sequence or in parallel, has not been mapped for arithmetic circuits.

**Direction:** Train a small transformer from scratch on multi-digit addition tasks. At regular training checkpoints, run circuit discovery targeting carry-related behavior (activation patching on carry-requiring vs. non-carry-requiring examples). Track which circuit components handle each sub-task and when they appear relative to overall accuracy improvement. Correlate sub-circuit emergence with grokking-like accuracy transitions.

**Why it matters:** Mapping the sequence of algorithmic capability emergence provides a theory of how complex reasoning builds from simpler components. This has implications for predicting when dangerous capabilities emerge during training and whether early warning signals exist in internal representations.

**Relevant context:** Grokking and arithmetic circuits are well-studied in the mech interp literature. Developmental interpretability (2025 review) documents learning dynamics. The task is tractable on a single GPU and provides clean ground truth about algorithm structure.
