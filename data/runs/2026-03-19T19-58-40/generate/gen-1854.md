# Isolating the Reward Model Overfit Step in the RLHF Causal Chain

**Problem:** The causal chain from reward model misspecification to model misalignment passes through several intermediate steps: (1) reward model learns spurious correlations, (2) RL exploits them, (3) behavior generalizes. Step 1 is understudied.

**Direction:** Train reward models on preference datasets with controlled levels of injected spurious correlation (e.g., length bias, sentiment bias). Measure how quickly and completely the downstream policy exploits each type of spurious correlation. Identify which spurious correlation types pose the highest alignment risk.

**Why it matters:** Targeting spurious correlation formation in reward models at training time is earlier in the causal chain and potentially cheaper than correcting policy behavior post-hoc.

**Relevant context:** Scaling laws for reward model overoptimization (2406.02900); reward hacking Lil'Log; energy loss phenomenon in RLHF (2501.19358).
