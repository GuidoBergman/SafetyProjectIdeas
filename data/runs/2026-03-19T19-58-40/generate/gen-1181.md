# Overseer Update Dynamics: Does Continuously Updating the Overseer on Student Outputs Improve or Degrade Oversight?

**Problem:** In dynamic systems, the overseer may be updated based on examples the student generates. This creates a feedback loop where the overseer is calibrated on the very outputs it oversees.

**Direction:** Compare static overseers (fixed throughout training) against dynamic overseers (updated on student-generated examples). Measure whether dynamic updating improves oversight quality or creates drift and instability.

**Why it matters:** Dynamic overseer updating is a natural design choice in interactive training pipelines. Understanding whether it helps or hurts is necessary for practical pipeline design.

**Relevant context:** Iterative amplification methodology; iterated bootstrapping of overseer quality.
