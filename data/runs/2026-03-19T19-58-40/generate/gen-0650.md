# Audio-Modality Safety Benchmark for Speech-Input Models

**Problem:** Whisper and similar speech-to-text models feed audio into LLMs. No benchmark tests whether harmful instructions delivered via synthesized speech evade safety filters applied at the text stage.

**Direction:** Generate 150–200 harmful prompts in both text and synthesized audio (using TTS). Run through a speech-LLM pipeline and measure refusal rate for audio-sourced inputs vs. direct text inputs.

**Why it matters:** Voice-interface deployments of LLMs are growing rapidly. Safety evaluations that only test text inputs leave audio attack surfaces entirely uncharacterized.

**Relevant context:** TTS tools (Coqui, Bark) and open speech-to-text models are free to run locally. No published benchmark currently covers this modality gap.
