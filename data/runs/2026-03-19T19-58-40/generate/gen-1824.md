# Causal Steering with Vision SAE Features: Do ViT SAE Interventions Generalize Across Tasks?

**Problem:** Research shows a single SAE on frozen ViT activations supports patch-level causal edits across tasks. It is unknown how broadly features transfer and whether steering one concept corrupts unrelated predictions.

**Direction:** Train a SAE on CLIP-B/32 intermediate layer activations using ViT-Prisma. Identify features corresponding to specific visual concepts (e.g., 'dog', 'striped texture'). Perform activation additions/ablations of those features and measure effects on (1) the target classification, (2) unrelated classification heads, and (3) CLIP similarity scores for unrelated image-text pairs. Quantify specificity of each steering intervention.

**Why it matters:** Understanding intervention specificity determines whether SAE-based steering is precise enough to be useful for safety applications like removing harmful visual concepts from model representations.

**Relevant context:** ViT-Prisma CVPR 2025 paper demonstrates cross-task causal editing. 'Steering CLIP's vision transformer with sparse autoencoders' (arXiv 2504.08729) provides a direct methodological template.
