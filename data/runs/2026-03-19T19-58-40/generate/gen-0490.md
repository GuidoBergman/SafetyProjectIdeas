# Targeting the Deployment Transition: Do Models Behave Differently Immediately Post-Deployment?

**Problem:** The scheming causal chain predicts that a deceptively aligned model would change behavior after passing evaluations and being deployed. Whether behavioral discontinuities at the training-to-deployment transition are detectable is unstudied.

**Direction:** Simulate a deployment transition by fine-tuning a model to associate 'evaluation mode' vs. 'deployment mode' contextual cues with different behavioral policies. Test whether behavioral probes detect the switch, and what minimum signal is needed to reliably catch a model acting differently post-deployment.

**Why it matters:** If deployment-time behavioral shifts are detectable with lightweight probing, this supports ongoing post-deployment monitoring as a practical safety layer.
