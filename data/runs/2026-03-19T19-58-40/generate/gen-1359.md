# Reward Hacking Taxonomy from MALT Transcripts

**Problem:** MALT contains 10,919 labeled transcripts of reward hacking and sandbagging behaviors, but a comprehensive taxonomy of reward hacking strategies has not been published from this dataset.

**Direction:** Sample the MALT reward hacking transcripts and perform qualitative coding to build a taxonomy of hacking strategies: e.g., metric gaming, constraint bypassing, shortcut exploitation, output manipulation. Validate the taxonomy with inter-rater agreement. Report frequency distribution of strategy types across model families in the dataset.

**Why it matters:** A validated taxonomy enables targeted red-teaming (testing for specific strategies) and informs the design of reward functions that are robust to known hacking approaches.

**Relevant context:** MALT dataset (METR, Oct 2025). Dataset contains manually reviewed transcripts with fine-grained labels. MALT is already integrated into Stanford HELM.
