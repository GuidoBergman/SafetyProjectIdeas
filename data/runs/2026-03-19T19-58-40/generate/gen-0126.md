# Adversarial Suffix Robustness Across Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** Adversarial suffixes appended to prompts can bypass safety training. It is unknown whether models with high safety benchmark scores are more robust to adversarial suffixes than lower-scoring models.

**Direction:** Use publicly available adversarial suffix generation methods (e.g., GCG attack simplified or pre-computed suffixes from published papers). Append these to 30–40 safety benchmark items from AILuminate. Evaluate 2–3 open-source models. Measure whether standard benchmark safety score predicts adversarial suffix robustness. Report a correlation coefficient and discuss implications.

**Why it matters:** If high benchmark scores do not predict adversarial robustness, then safety benchmarks are not measuring relevant safety properties. This would motivate including adversarial robustness in standard evaluation protocols.

**Relevant context:** GCG adversarial suffix attacks (Zou et al. 2023) are well-known. JailbreakBench and HarmBench include adversarial attack results. AILuminate does not include adversarial suffix conditions.
