# Multimodal Safety Benchmark Prototype for Image-Text Jailbreaks

**Problem:** Most safety benchmarks are text-only, but multimodal models accept image inputs that can embed instructions, bypass text filters, or smuggle harmful context.

**Direction:** Build a small benchmark of ~50 image-text pairs targeting known multimodal jailbreak vectors: text in images (OCR-based instruction injection), misleading visual context (showing a benign image while requesting harmful information), and adversarial overlays. Evaluate 2–3 open-source multimodal models (e.g., LLaVA, InternVL) for refusal rates. Compare refusal rates to text-only equivalents to quantify the 'modality gap' in safety.

**Why it matters:** Multimodal safety is an explicitly open problem. Commercial models like GPT-4o and Claude accept images, but public safety benchmarks are overwhelmingly text-only. Even a toy benchmark fills a gap.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench (arXiv 2602.14135) covers embodied and multimodal risk dimensions but does not provide a multimodal test suite per se. TrustLLM and AILuminate are text-only.
