# Measuring Homogeneity in AI-Generated Scientific Literature: Are All AI Research Assistants Pointing Scientists in the Same Direction?

**Problem:** If AI research assistants used by scientists all suggest the same hypotheses, experimental designs, and literature directions, scientific progress could converge on a narrow set of questions while genuinely novel directions are systematically overlooked.

**Direction:** Give 10+ LLMs the same set of 50 open-ended scientific hypothesis generation prompts across multiple disciplines. Measure inter-model similarity of suggested research directions and identify directions that are suggested by all vs. only by some models.

**Why it matters:** AI-driven convergence in scientific ideation was documented in a 41-million-paper analysis. AI research assistants could amplify this effect. The systemic risk to scientific diversity is a direct application of the Artificial Hivemind findings.

**Relevant context:** Examining and Addressing Barriers to Diversity in LLM-Generated Ideas (arXiv 2602.20408); Artificial Hivemind; homogenizing effect on cognitive diversity (arXiv 2508.01491).
