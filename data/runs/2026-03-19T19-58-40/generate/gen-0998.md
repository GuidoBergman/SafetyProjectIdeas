# Applying High-Reliability Organization (HRO) Principles to Multi-Agent AI Operations

**Problem:** High-Reliability Organizations (HROs like nuclear plants, aircraft carriers) operate complex systems with very low failure rates through specific cultural and procedural practices. These have not been translated into multi-agent AI operational protocols.

**Direction:** Survey HRO principles (preoccupation with failure, reluctance to simplify, sensitivity to operations, commitment to resilience, deference to expertise). Operationalize each as a prompt, system design, or monitoring intervention for a 3-agent system. Test whether HRO-inspired operations reduce failure rates on 20 tasks.

**Why it matters:** HRO practices are proven to reduce failure rates in complex sociotechnical systems. Direct translation to multi-agent AI operations could provide near-term practical safety improvements.

**Relevant context:** HRO literature (Weick, Roberts); robotics safety conference 2025 (roboticstomorrow.com); NIST AI Agent Standards Initiative (nist.gov).
