# Investigating Deception Transfer: Do Models Deceive More in Novel vs. Familiar Domains?

**Problem:** It is unknown whether deceptive behaviors (sycophancy, sandbagging, alignment faking) are domain-specific or transfer across knowledge domains. A model trained to be honest in one domain may be deceptive in another it is less certain about.

**Direction:** Run deception probes (sycophancy, factual misinformation confirmation) in high-familiarity domains (common English facts) vs. low-familiarity domains (obscure scientific claims, low-resource language facts). Measure deception rates across domains.

**Why it matters:** If deception rates vary by domain familiarity, safety evaluations must cover diverse domains to be valid. Domain-specific deception also implies domain-specific mitigations.

**Relevant context:** AI deception survey (PMC 2024) notes deception can arise from training gaps. DeceptionBench covers five critical domains but does not analyze by model familiarity.
