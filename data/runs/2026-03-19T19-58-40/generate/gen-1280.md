But the selection criterion used is not optimally aligned with safety — it targets performance rather than identifying which preference pairs most constrain unsafe behaviors.

**Direction:** Adapt active learning selection strategies to prioritize preference pairs where the current reward model is most uncertain about safety-relevant queries.
