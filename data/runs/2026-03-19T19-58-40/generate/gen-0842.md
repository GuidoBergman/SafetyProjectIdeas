# Reconstruction Attacks Defeat Output Classifiers: Obfuscation Compounds Detection Gaps

**Problem:** Output-only classifiers (checking model responses for harmful content) can be evaded by reconstruction attacks that distribute harmful information across multiple benign-looking response segments. This compounds a shallow alignment failure (model is willing to produce harmful content piecemeal) with a detection architecture failure.

**Direction:** Design a multi-turn extraction protocol that requests harmful information across five innocuous segments. Evaluate whether a constitutional classifier flags the individual turns vs. the concatenated harmful result. Measure the gap in detection accuracy between per-turn and aggregate analysis.

**Why it matters:** Production safety filters typically evaluate individual turns, not multi-turn aggregates. If the model's alignment is shallow enough that it complies with piecemeal requests, and the classifier only sees individual turns, the full harmful output assembles outside any detection window.

**Relevant context:** Constitutional Classifiers++ (arXiv 2601.04603) identifies reconstruction attacks as a primary evasion class. Output obfuscation attacks bypass output-only classifiers. Multi-stage pipeline approach (PromptScreen 2025) addresses but does not fully solve this.
