# Does Weight Sparsity Improve Circuit Recoverability? Replicating OpenAI's Weight-Sparse Transformer Study on GPT-2

**Problem:** A 2025 OpenAI paper found that weight-sparse transformers have more interpretable circuits. However, the study used proprietary training setups. Whether the same effect holds when imposing weight sparsity post-hoc via pruning on a pretrained GPT-2 is unknown.

**Direction:** Apply magnitude pruning to GPT-2-small at sparsity levels 0%, 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%. On each pruned model, run circuit discovery (attribution patching) on the IOI task. Measure edge faithfulness and circuit size. Compare to the dense baseline.

**Why it matters:** If post-hoc pruning also improves circuit recoverability, it suggests that weight sparsity is a cheap intervention that could make existing deployed models more interpretable without retraining.

**Relevant context:** OpenAI weight-sparse circuits paper (cdn.openai.com/pdf/41df8f28). IOI circuit is the standard benchmark (Wang et al. 2022). TransformerLens has IOI analysis code. Pruning GPT-2 is straightforward with PyTorch. Single GPU is sufficient.
