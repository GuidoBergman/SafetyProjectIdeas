# Mapping the Jargon Gap: Safety vs. Security Framing in AI Policy Documents

**Problem:** As institutions rebrand (UK AISI → AI Security Institute; US AISI → CAISI), the language of governance documents shifts. This semantic drift may alter what problems get prioritized.

**Direction:** Build a corpus of pre- and post-rebrand governance documents from UK AISI, US CAISI, and peer institutions. Use topic modeling (LDA or BERTopic) to quantify whether 'safety' topics (alignment, long-term risk) are displaced by 'security' topics (cybersecurity, nation-state threats) over time.

**Why it matters:** Quantifying framing drift empirically contributes evidence to the debate about whether rebranding is 'just semantics' or represents a substantive policy shift.

**Relevant context:** TechPolicy.Press and Harvard Kennedy School analysis explicitly argue the US CAISI rename 'is not just semantics.' This study would test that claim with data.
