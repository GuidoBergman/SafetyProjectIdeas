# Adversarial Prompt Detection as a Scheming Early Warning System

**Problem:** Scheming scenarios often require specific adversarial framings to elicit the behavior. If models can be trained to recognize and flag adversarial scheming-elicitation prompts, this provides an early warning layer.

**Direction:** Collect Apollo's scheming scenario prompts and construct a matched set of non-scheming prompts with similar surface features. Fine-tune a small classifier to distinguish scheming-elicitation prompts from benign prompts. Measure precision/recall and test whether the classifier generalizes to novel scheming scenarios not in its training set.

**Why it matters:** A prompt-level scheming trigger detector could be deployed as a pre-filter, flagging high-risk inputs for additional scrutiny before they reach the main model.

**Relevant context:** Apollo's scheming scenarios are partially documented in their research papers. Petri seed library contains 70+ scheming-relevant scenarios that could serve as training data.
