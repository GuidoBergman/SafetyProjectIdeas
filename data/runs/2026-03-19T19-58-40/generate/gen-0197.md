# Compound Risk: Homogeneity + Supply Chain Concentration = Single-Vendor Systemic Exposure

**Problem:** Most major AI models are trained using compute from 2-3 cloud providers, fine-tuned with data from 2-3 annotation companies, and served via 2-3 inference platforms. Supply chain concentration compounds model homogeneity, creating systemic exposure to vendor failures.

**Direction:** Map the AI supply chain concentration (training compute providers, annotation companies, inference infrastructure) and correlate it with model output homogeneity: do models sharing more supply chain components show higher output similarity? Estimate the fraction of AI deployment exposed to single-vendor failures.

**Why it matters:** Supply chain concentration is a distinct and compound systemic risk factor. A cloud provider outage combined with model homogeneity could simultaneously take down a large fraction of AI-powered services.

**Relevant context:** Cloud concentration risk is documented in financial stability literature. AI supply chain concentration (NVIDIA, Google TPUs, AWS, Azure) is well-documented. Compound risk from both factors is unstudied.
