# Concept Steering Explanation as a Mechanistic Interpretability Tool

**Problem:** When a model refuses or modifies a response, users and auditors have no mechanistic explanation for why; concept vectors could provide human-readable mechanistic explanations.

**Direction:** Build a prototype explanation interface that, when a model output is steered or a probe fires, reports which concept vector triggered, the activation strength, and a natural language description of the concept, comparing user understanding of model decisions with vs. without this explanation.

**Why it matters:** Explainability of safety decisions is increasingly required for regulatory compliance and user trust; concept vectors provide a uniquely mechanistic form of explanation.

**Relevant context:** RepE and Beaglehole et al. provide the technical foundation for concept-level explanations. Explainability is an open problem in deployed AI safety systems. No existing explanation interface uses concept vectors.
