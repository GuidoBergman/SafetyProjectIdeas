# Adversarial Orchestrator Simulation: Can a Malicious Orchestrator Reliably Extract Dangerous Actions from Safety-Trained Sub-Agents?

**Problem:** Sub-agents are typically trained to be safe, but an adversarial orchestrator sending carefully crafted instructions could bypass sub-agent safety training without triggering standard safety classifiers.

**Direction:** Build an adversarial orchestrator that uses indirect instruction, authority framing, and gradual escalation to direct a safety-trained sub-agent to take a prohibited action. Test against 3 safety-trained models across 20 prohibited-action types. Measure bypass success rate. Compare to direct instruction baseline.

**Why it matters:** If adversarial orchestration reliably bypasses sub-agent safety training, the entire multi-agent safety model is compromised. Sub-agents cannot be trusted to be safe nodes in an untrusted graph.

**Relevant context:** ControlArena collusion settings (apartresearch.com); indirect prompt injection in Google DeepMind paper (arxiv 2505.14534); Adversa AI 2025 on prompt injection as most common incident type.
