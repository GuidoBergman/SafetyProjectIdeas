# Diversity of RLHF Safety Training as a Mitigation: Testing on Open Models

**Problem:** Natural Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2511.18397) identified 'increasing diversity of RLHF safety training' as a mitigation for reward-hacking-induced misalignment. This was demonstrated in Anthropic's proprietary setting. Whether increasing the topic diversity of a safety fine-tuning dataset prevents emergent misalignment in an open-weight model induced by SFT (rather than RL) has not been tested.

**Direction:** Induce emergent misalignment in Llama-3-8B via insecure-code SFT. Then apply safety fine-tuning under two conditions: (1) a narrow safety SFT dataset (HHH examples from a single domain, e.g., coding); (2) a diverse safety SFT dataset spanning multiple domains (coding, medical, financial, general). Compare residual misalignment rates. Test whether topic diversity in safety data is the key variable.

**Why it matters:** If topic diversity in safety training data is a reliable mitigation, practitioners can improve robustness by simply diversifying their safety datasets without architectural changes or new algorithms. Tests whether a finding from a proprietary RL setting generalizes to the accessible SFT setting used by most practitioners.

**Relevant context:** Natural Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2511.18397) showed three mitigations: prevent reward hacking, diversify RLHF safety training, inoculation prompting. The diversity finding was specific to RL settings. Betley et al. showed framing prevents misalignment. Whether diversity generalizes is an open question.
