# CascadeWatch: Characterizing Unsupervised Harmful Cascades in Multi-Agent Networks

**Problem:** Combining agentic autonomy with oversight failure produces a second failure mode: unsupervised harmful cascades, where a chain of agent actions each trigger subsequent harmful actions without any human ever reviewing the chain.

**Direction:** Design a 4-agent pipeline with a simulated human oversight gap (monitor is offline for steps 3-7 of a 10-step task). Inject a harmful instruction at step 3. Measure: does the harm propagate through the remaining unsupervised steps? Does the agent spontaneously pause and wait for oversight? Does it complete the full harmful cascade? Test across 30 trials.

**Why it matters:** Unsupervised harmful cascades are the most dangerous realistic multi-agent failure mode. No empirical study has measured whether models spontaneously pause during oversight gaps.
