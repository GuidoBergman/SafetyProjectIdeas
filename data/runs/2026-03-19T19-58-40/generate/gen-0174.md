# Importing the 'Too-Big-To-Fail' Framework from Finance to AI Ecosystem Risk

**Problem:** Financial regulation has developed tools for identifying systemically important institutions whose failure propagates through the whole system. No analogous 'systemically important AI model' concept exists.

**Direction:** Adapt the SIFI (Systemically Important Financial Institution) identification criteria to AI models: define AI-SIFI criteria based on deployment scale, output homogeneity with peer models, and integration into critical infrastructure. Score current major models against these criteria.

**Why it matters:** Regulatory frameworks need to identify which AI providers, if they fail or are compromised, would cause systemic harm—just as banking regulators identify which banks are too-big-to-fail.

**Relevant context:** CEPR and LSE research explicitly draws the finance-AI systemic risk analogy. SIFI criteria from Basel III are well-documented and adaptable.
