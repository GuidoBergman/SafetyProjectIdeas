# Network Analysis of Deceptive Information Spread: Can AI Deception Be Modeled as a Contagion?

**Problem:** Epidemiology models how false information spreads through networks (misinformation contagion). If an AI system deploys deceptive outputs into information ecosystems, the spread and impact of that deception may follow contagion dynamics—a cross-domain framework that has not been applied to AI deception risk.

**Direction:** Model a simplified information ecosystem (network of LLM-using agents sharing outputs). Inject a single deceptively-aligned model as a source. Simulate information spread using standard epidemiological contagion models (SIR). Measure how quickly deceptive information propagates, what network structures contain it, and what intervention points (equivalent to quarantine) are most effective.

**Why it matters:** AI deception risk is not just about individual model behavior but about systemic information ecosystem effects. Contagion modeling identifies structural mitigations (network design, information flow chokepoints) that individual-model safety cannot address.

**Relevant context:** Multi-agent LLM systems increasingly form information networks. Game Theory Meets LLM (arXiv:2507.10621) models network-level interactions. Misinformation contagion modeling is well-developed in epidemiology and network science.
