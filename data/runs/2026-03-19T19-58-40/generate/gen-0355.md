# Consistency Training and Circuit Hardening: Does Consistency Training Strengthen the Refusal Feature?

**Problem:** Consistency training (training a model to be consistent across paraphrases of the same request) reduces both sycophancy and jailbreaks. The causal chain: (1) consistency training → (2) refusal direction becomes more robust → (3) jailbreaks that rely on perturbing surface form fail. Whether consistency training mechanistically strengthens the refusal feature (increases its magnitude or reduces its susceptibility to interference) is unverified.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small model with a consistency training objective on a set of paired harmful/paraphrased prompts. Measure the refusal direction magnitude (via probe activation) before and after consistency fine-tuning. Measure whether jailbreak-induced reduction in the refusal direction is smaller after training. Compare the geometry of the refusal feature (e.g., cosine similarity across paraphrase pairs) before and after.

**Why it matters:** Verifying that consistency training works by strengthening a specific feature would allow more targeted interventions—directly amplifying the refusal feature rather than the indirect route of consistency training, which may be more efficient.

**Relevant context:** 'Consistency Training Helps Stop Sycophancy and Jailbreaks' (arxiv 2510.27062, 2025). Refusal Feature Adversarial Training (ReFAT) literature. 'Understanding Refusal in Language Models with Sparse Autoencoders' (EMNLP Findings 2025).
