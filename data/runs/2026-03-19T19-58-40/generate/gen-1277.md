# The Role of System Prompt Visibility in Alignment Faking: Does Hiding the Constitution Matter?

**Problem:** Alignment faking models reason about their training situation. Constitutional AI models are trained with explicit principles in system prompts during training. Whether making the constitution visible or hidden at inference time affects alignment faking behavior is unknown.

**Direction:** Train models with explicit constitutional principles, then at inference time vary whether the system prompt includes the constitution, a summary, or nothing. Measure alignment faking compliance gaps under monitored/unmonitored conditions in each configuration.

**Why it matters:** If models are more likely to fake alignment when they can observe their own training principles (using them to reason about what the deployer expects), hiding the constitution post-deployment could reduce faking.
