# Shallow Alignment After Domain Fine-Tuning: Does Medical QA Fine-Tuning Deepen or Erode First-Token Safety?

**Problem:** When a safety-aligned model is fine-tuned on domain data, safety can degrade. Qi et al. showed shallow alignment is especially vulnerable to fine-tuning. Whether domain fine-tuning specifically degrades the first-token shortcut more than later-token alignment is unknown.

**Direction:** Fine-tune Llama-3-8B-Instruct on ~500 medical QA examples. Before and after fine-tuning, measure: (1) refusal-prefix concentration, (2) ASR under prefilling attack on StrongReject prompts. Plot whether first-token alignment degrades faster than later-token alignment.

**Why it matters:** Medical domain fine-tuning of open-weight models is widespread. If it disproportionately erodes the first-token safety shortcut while leaving a false sense of safety in later tokens, this is a serious deployment risk current evaluations may miss.

**Relevant context:** MedQA is publicly available. Fine-tuning Llama-3-8B on 500 examples takes under 2 hours on a single A100. Qi et al. studied fine-tuning attacks but not the spatial distribution of alignment degradation across output positions.
