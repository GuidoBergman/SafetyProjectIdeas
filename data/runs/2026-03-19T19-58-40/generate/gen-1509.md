# Pharmacokinetic Dose-Response Modeling for Steering Vector Magnitude

**Problem:** The relationship between steering vector magnitude and behavioral effect is treated empirically with no formal model, analogous to pre-dose-response-curve pharmacology.

**Direction:** Borrow the pharmacokinetic dose-response framework (EC50, Hill coefficient, therapeutic window) from pharmacology, fit these parameters to the steering magnitude vs. toxicity-suppression curve, and use them to define a 'therapeutic window' for safe steering.

**Why it matters:** A formal dose-response model would let practitioners derive principled coefficient bounds and transfer knowledge across models (as pharmacokinetics transfers across patients).

**Relevant context:** Pharmacology dose-response modeling is well-established; the analogy to steering vectors (dose = coefficient, response = behavioral metric) is natural but unexplored in the ML literature.
