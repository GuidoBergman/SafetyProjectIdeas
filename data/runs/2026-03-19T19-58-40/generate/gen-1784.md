# Mutation Testing for Circuit Robustness Scoring

**Problem:** Mechanistic interpretability circuits are discovered on a narrow set of prompts. It is unknown how robust a circuit is: whether small changes to input phrasing break the claimed mechanism.

**Direction:** Adapt mutation testing from software engineering: systematically mutate inputs (swap synonyms, reorder tokens, change surface form) and measure how often the circuit still executes the same internal algorithm. Circuits that survive many mutations receive a high robustness score.

**Why it matters:** Robustness scoring gives practitioners a quantitative handle on generalization of discovered circuits, addressing a key criticism that mech interp findings may not generalize. A student can implement this in ~100 lines using TransformerLens and a small synonym dictionary.

**Relevant context:** DeepMutation (2018) applied mutation operators to DNN weights. Here the mutations are on inputs, analogous to source-level mutation testing used to assess test suite quality. The 2025 ACM survey on failure analysis in AI systems notes this gap.
