# A Standardized Open Scheming Benchmark for Open-Weight Models

**Problem:** There is no standardized, publicly available scheming benchmark with fixed scenarios, scoring rubrics, and leaderboard infrastructure for open-weight models. Apollo's evals are described qualitatively but not packaged as a reproducible benchmark.

**Direction:** Design and release a scheming benchmark: 20-30 scenarios covering self-exfiltration, deactivation resistance, sandbagging, and oversight subversion. Define binary/graded scoring criteria. Run on 5 open-weight models as a baseline. Release code and leaderboard.

**Why it matters:** Without a standardized benchmark, scheming research cannot be compared across labs or tracked over time. A public benchmark lowers the barrier for safety researchers worldwide.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research defined scheming categories but has not released a standardized benchmark dataset. DeceptionBench (arxiv 2510.15501) covers broader deception but not specifically scheming scenarios.
