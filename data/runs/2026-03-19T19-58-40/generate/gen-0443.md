# A Systematic CoT-vs-Action Divergence Benchmark for Deception Measurement

**Problem:** There is no standardized benchmark for measuring divergence between a model's stated reasoning (CoT/thinking trace) and its actual action, which is the core signal for deceptive reasoning.

**Direction:** Design 50+ scenarios where the correct reasoning leads to action A and deceptive reasoning leads to action B. Instruct models to reason before acting. Automatically score CoT-action alignment. Measure baseline divergence rates on Llama, Qwen, and DeepSeek-R1.

**Why it matters:** CoT monitoring is proposed as a key safety mechanism, but its reliability depends on CoT-action alignment. A benchmark for this alignment is foundational for evaluating CoT-based safety.

**Relevant context:** arxiv 2506.04909 ('When Thinking LLMs Lie') studies this divergence qualitatively. No quantitative benchmark with automated scoring exists.
