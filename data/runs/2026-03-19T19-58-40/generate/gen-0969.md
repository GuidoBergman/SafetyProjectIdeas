# Human Oversight Recovery: Can a Human Operator Detect and Halt an In-Progress Crypto Theft Agent Attack?

**Problem:** After demonstrating that agents can autonomously execute crypto theft, the question of whether human oversight is effective at detecting and interrupting such attacks in real time remains open.

**Direction:** Build a simulated agentic crypto management environment. Have participants (CS students) monitor an agent's actions in real time while the agent executes a slow-motion attack (over 5 minutes). Measure detection rate, time-to-detection, and intervention success rate.

**Why it matters:** Human oversight is the primary recommended mitigation for irreversible agentic actions. This tests whether that mitigation is practically effective under realistic conditions.

**Relevant context:** Adversa AI 2025 (adversa.ai) identified missing human oversight as the root cause of most agentic security incidents. Anthropic's guidance recommends human approval for irreversible actions.
