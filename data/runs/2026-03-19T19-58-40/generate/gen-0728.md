# Steering Fragility Under Adversarial Paraphrase: Concept Steering Fails When Feature Activation Is Bypassed

**Problem:** SAE-based concept steering suppresses harmful outputs by clamping specific feature activations. Adversarial paraphrase attacks achieving the same semantic goal through different syntactic routes may activate different feature combinations that the steering vector does not cover. Failure mode 1: paraphrase bypasses the trigger distribution for the identified feature. Failure mode 2: steering is feature-specific rather than concept-general.

**Direction:** For a target harmful concept, identify its primary SAE feature. Apply steering to suppress it. Generate paraphrase variants of prompts that elicit the same concept and measure whether the primary feature still activates, or whether the model reroutes through secondary features not covered by the steering intervention.

**Why it matters:** If steering is only effective on the specific prompt distribution used to identify the feature, it provides no robustness guarantee — a critical limitation for deploying SAE steering as a deployment-time safety mechanism.

**Relevant context:** SAE-TS and related work focuses on precision of steering but not coverage across paraphrase variants. Adversarial paraphrase is a known jailbreak technique documented in JailbreakBench.
