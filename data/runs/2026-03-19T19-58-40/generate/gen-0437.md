# A Sycophancy Pressure Benchmark with Graded Resistance Scores

**Problem:** Existing sycophancy evaluations test whether models capitulate to disagreement, but do not produce graded scores reflecting how much pressure is required for capitulation—which is a more informative safety metric.

**Direction:** Design a sycophancy pressure benchmark where each scenario includes 5 escalating pressure turns. Score models by the turn at which they capitulate (or not). Include factual, ethical, and opinion question types. Run on 5 open-weight models and release dataset + code.

**Why it matters:** A graded benchmark distinguishes models that capitulate immediately from those that resist moderate pressure but break under strong pressure—crucial for real-world deployment risk assessment.

**Relevant context:** DeceptionBench uses three intensity levels but does not produce a graded capitulation score per scenario. Multi-turn sustained dialogue is the highest tier in DeceptionBench.
