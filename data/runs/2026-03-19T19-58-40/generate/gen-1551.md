# Universal Steering Foundation: Pretraining a Meta-Concept Vector Extractor

**Problem:** Working backward from universal steering, a key enabling capability would be a model trained to extract reliable concept vectors from minimal contrastive examples — a meta-learner for concept extraction.

**Direction:** Train a small meta-model (few-shot concept vector extractor) on a diverse set of concept extraction tasks, then test whether it generalizes to new safety concepts at extraction accuracy above the baseline extraction method on fewer samples.

**Why it matters:** A meta-extractor would reduce the data requirement for new safety concepts from 500 examples to perhaps 10, making universal coverage achievable even for rare harmful concept categories.

**Relevant context:** Meta-learning (MAML, ProtoNets) is an established paradigm. Applying it to concept vector extraction is novel. Beaglehole et al. AGOP-based extraction is the baseline to beat.
