# Epidemiological Case-Control Study Design for Alignment Failures

**Problem:** Epidemiology uses case-control studies to identify risk factors for rare events. Alignment failures (e.g., a model exhibiting emergent misalignment) are rare events. Case-control methodology could systematically identify risk factors.

**Direction:** Collect a sample of fine-tuning runs that did and did not produce emergent misalignment (cases and controls). Conduct a case-control analysis measuring odds ratios for candidate risk factors: dataset size, domain, model size, alignment level, learning rate.

**Why it matters:** Identifying statistically significant risk factors for alignment failures would allow practitioners to screen fine-tuning runs before deployment, analogous to epidemiological screening.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. 2025 (various fine-tuning settings); follow-up emergent misalignment studies; epidemiological case-control methodology (Rothman 2012).
