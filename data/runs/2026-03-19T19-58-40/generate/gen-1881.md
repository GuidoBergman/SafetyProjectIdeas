# The Gap Between Reward Model Score and Ground-Truth Human Preference Under Distribution Shift

**Problem:** Reward models are trained on a fixed preference distribution, but during RL fine-tuning, the policy output distribution shifts. The gap between reward model scores and true human preferences as a function of this shift is undercharacterized.

**Direction:** Collect human preference ratings for model outputs at multiple points during RL training (early, mid, late). Compare human preference ratings to reward model scores at each checkpoint. Measure the divergence between them as a function of training step.

**Why it matters:** Quantifying the reward model vs. human preference gap across training would directly characterize when RL fine-tuning stops improving real alignment and starts reward hacking.
