# Marginal Safety Gain of CoT Access Under Adversarial Obfuscation

**Problem:** The GPT-5 system card shows CoT monitoring is more informative than action-only monitoring under normal conditions. But research also shows that under adversarial pressure (obfuscation cues), CoT-monitor detection can collapse to ~10%. The marginal safety gain under realistic adversarial pressure is unclear.

**Direction:** Design a mild adversarial prompt injection that instructs the model to be vague in its reasoning. Run both CoT-monitor and action-only-monitor conditions across SHADE-Arena tasks. Plot ROC curves for both under clean vs. obfuscated conditions.

**Why it matters:** If adversarial obfuscation erases the CoT advantage, then the safety case for CoT monitoring requires active obfuscation detection, not just CoT parsing.

**Relevant context:** SHADE-Arena is publicly available with 17 personal assistant tasks. CoT Red-Handed paper (arXiv:2505.23575) stress-tested CoT monitoring under obfuscation and found fragility. GPT-5 system card: CoT monitor precision 81%, recall 84%.
