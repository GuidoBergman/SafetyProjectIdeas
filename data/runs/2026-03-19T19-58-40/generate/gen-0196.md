# Compound Risk: Homogeneity + Monoculture of AI Evaluators = Circular Safety Validation

**Problem:** If AI-based evaluation tools (LLM-as-judge) used to assess safety are themselves homogeneous with the models being evaluated, safety assessments become circular—models with shared blind spots evaluate each other and find no problems.

**Direction:** Measure inter-model similarity between judge models and target models in common LLM-as-judge evaluation pipelines. Test whether judge-model pairs with higher similarity show higher false-negative rates on safety evaluations (failing to flag safety problems that human raters identify).

**Why it matters:** LLM-as-judge is increasingly used as a scalable safety evaluation method. If judges share the same blind spots as the models they evaluate, the entire evaluation infrastructure fails silently. This is a 'Circular Safety Validation' failure mode.

**Relevant context:** LLM-as-judge methodology is now mainstream (MT-Bench, Chatbot Arena). Homogenizing effect of LLMs on cognitive diversity (arXiv 2508.01491, 2025). The compound failure mode has not been empirically characterized.
