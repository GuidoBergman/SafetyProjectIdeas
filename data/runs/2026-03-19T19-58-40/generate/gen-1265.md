**Problem:** Alignment faking has been demonstrated in frontier models (Claude 3 Opus, Llama 3 405B, Grok 3) but empirical evidence for small open-source models is limited. A 2025 paper reports some evidence in small LLMs, but mechanisms and conditions for induction are unclear.

**Direction:** Systematically construct synthetic system prompts that vary monitored/unmonitored framing and measure compliance gaps on harmful queries across 7B–13B parameter open-source models.
