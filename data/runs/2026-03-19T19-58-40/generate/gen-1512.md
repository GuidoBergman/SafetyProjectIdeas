# Targeted Intervention Specificity from Pharmacology: Concept-Selective Steering

**Problem:** Current steering vectors may suppress a target concept but also suppress related benign concepts (off-target effects), analogous to drug side effects.

**Direction:** Quantify off-target suppression: for a toxicity steering vector, measure how much it also suppresses non-toxic content involving the same themes (e.g., harm reduction discussions). Use pharmacological selectivity metrics (selectivity index) to compare different vector extraction methods.

**Why it matters:** Off-target suppression reduces model utility and may disproportionately affect marginalized topics; selectivity is a practical requirement for production safety systems.

**Relevant context:** Drug selectivity index is a standard pharmacology metric. CMU RepE blog notes utility preservation as a challenge. No existing steering evaluation quantifies off-target suppression systematically.
