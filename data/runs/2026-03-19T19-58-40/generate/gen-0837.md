# Steering Vector Injection as a Jailbreak: When Interpretability Tools Become Attack Vectors

**Problem:** Steering vectors constructed from safety-relevant concepts can be adversarially optimized to suppress refusal behaviors. This compounds an adversarial robustness failure with a concept steering failure: the same activation geometry used to monitor alignment can be weaponized against it.

**Direction:** Reproduce one-shot optimized steering vector attacks (arXiv 2502.18862) on a small open-source model. Measure how much the refusal direction in activation space shifts under steering and whether standard safety benchmarks detect the degradation. Compare attack success on steered vs. unsteered models.

**Why it matters:** Safety auditors are actively developing steering-vector based monitors. If those same vectors can be adversarially flipped, safety tooling is dual-use in a dangerous way. This is a direct compounding failure between concept steering infrastructure and adversarial robustness.

**Relevant context:** One-shot optimized steering vectors achieve 96.9% Harmbench ASR (arXiv 2502.18862). Even benign steering directions can increase harmful compliance. Models can be trained to detect steering, introducing strategic detection-evasion dynamics.
