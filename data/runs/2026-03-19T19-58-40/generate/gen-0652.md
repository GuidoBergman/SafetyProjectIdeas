# Post-Deployment Drift Monitor: A Simple Tool for Tracking Safety Score Changes Over Time

**Problem:** There are no accessible tools for practitioners to detect when a deployed model's safety behavior drifts after fine-tuning or adapter updates. Organizations have no early warning system for safety regressions.

**Direction:** Build a lightweight Python tool that periodically runs a fixed set of AILuminate-style safety probes against a local model endpoint and logs scores over time, flagging statistically significant drops. Validate by intentionally fine-tuning a model to degrade safety and confirming the tool detects it.

**Why it matters:** The 2026 International AI Safety Report identifies post-deployment monitoring as a critical gap. Even a simple canary-probe system addresses an acknowledged need.
