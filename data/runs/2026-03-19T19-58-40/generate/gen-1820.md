# Vision SAE Sparsity Gap: Does Patch Position (Spatial vs. CLS) Explain Why Vision SAEs Are Less Sparse Than Language SAEs?

**Problem:** ViT-Prisma found that vision SAEs show substantially lower sparsity (higher L0) than language SAEs. It is unknown whether this is driven primarily by the CLS token's global aggregation role or is uniform across all token positions.

**Direction:** Train SAEs separately on CLS token activations and on spatial patch activations from a ViT (e.g., CLIP-B/32 using ViT-Prisma). Compare L0, dead feature rates, and reconstruction quality between the two SAE types. Test whether CLS-specific SAEs can reach language-SAE-like sparsity levels when trained in isolation.

**Why it matters:** If the sparsity gap is CLS-specific, it suggests the global aggregation function intrinsically requires denser representations, which has implications for SAE design choices in vision models.

**Relevant context:** ViT-Prisma (Prisma, CVPR MIV 2025) reports CLS tokens require more features in deeper layers while spatial tokens show declining utilization. SAELens and ViT-Prisma provide training infrastructure.
