# Multimodal Safety Benchmark: Table and Chart Injection Attacks

**Problem:** Models processing structured data (tables, charts) may follow harmful instructions embedded in chart titles, axis labels, or table cells while appearing to perform benign data analysis.

**Direction:** Construct 100 data analysis tasks where harmful instructions are embedded in table/chart metadata. Evaluate two vision-language or code-interpreter models. Score whether the model executes the embedded instruction or completes the analysis task as intended.

**Why it matters:** Enterprise deployments routinely use LLMs to analyze spreadsheets and dashboards. Instruction injection via structured data is an uncharacterized attack surface.

**Relevant context:** Multimodal safety benchmarks are identified as a major gap. Code-interpreter and structured data analysis are among the most common agentic use cases.
