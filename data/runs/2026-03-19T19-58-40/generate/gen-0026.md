# Transfer Attack Decay Rate: How Quickly Do GCG Suffixes Lose Effectiveness as the Target Model Diverges?

**Problem:** GCG adversarial suffixes transfer from open-source surrogate models to closed proprietary models. However, as models are updated, safety-patched, or fine-tuned, the transferability of previously optimized suffixes presumably degrades. The decay rate is unknown.

**Direction:** Collect or generate a set of 30 GCG adversarial suffixes optimized against Llama-3-8B-Instruct. Test their attack success rate against: (a) same model, (b) quantized versions of same model, (c) a GGUF version, (d) a lightly fine-tuned version (trained on 1000 Alpaca examples), (e) a safety-further-tuned version. Plot ASR vs. model distance (measured by weight cosine similarity or benchmark performance delta). Estimate the rate of suffix obsolescence.

**Why it matters:** If transferred suffixes decay quickly with minor model updates, that suggests safety patches are effective even without specific suffix filtering. If decay is slow, model providers need active suffix invalidation strategies.

**Relevant context:** Universal and Transferable Adversarial Attacks (arXiv:2307.15043). Advancing Adversarial Suffix Transfer Learning EMNLP 2024. GCG resurgence (arXiv:2509.00391). nanogcG fast implementation.
