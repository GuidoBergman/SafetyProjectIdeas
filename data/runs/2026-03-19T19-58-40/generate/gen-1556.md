# Safety Concept Steering Under Multi-Turn Conversation Context

**Problem:** Concept steering is typically evaluated on single-turn prompts; in multi-turn conversations, prior turns may shift activations cumulatively, potentially overriding steering vectors applied to the current turn.

**Direction:** Apply toxicity steering to a model in conversations of 1, 3, 5, and 10 turns, where prior turns contain increasingly unsafe content, measuring whether the steering vector remains effective as prior-turn context accumulates.

**Why it matters:** Multi-turn conversations are the primary use case for deployed chat models; safety that degrades with conversation length is a significant deployment risk.

**Relevant context:** Multi-turn safety is a recognized challenge. Context length effects on steering are unstudied (as noted above). This is a distinct multi-turn-specific variant of the context length question.
