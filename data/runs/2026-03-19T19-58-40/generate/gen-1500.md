# Robustness Testing Framework for Steering Vectors

**Problem:** There is no standardized framework for testing whether concept vectors remain effective under distribution shift, prompt paraphrasing, or minor model fine-tuning, leaving practitioners unable to certify robustness.

**Direction:** Develop a robustness testing suite that applies a battery of perturbations (prompt paraphrase, temperature variation, LoRA fine-tuning, input language change) to a steering pipeline and reports per-perturbation effectiveness degradation scores.

**Why it matters:** Safety properties need robustness certificates; a framework that surfaces when steering breaks down is essential before production deployment.

**Relevant context:** Robustness tradeoffs in fine-tuning (ICCV 2025) and steering vector generalization (OpenReview) show robustness is non-trivial. No safety-steering-specific robustness framework exists.
