# Dose-Response Curves for Harmful Capability Elicitation

**Problem:** Safety evaluations typically measure binary pass/fail (did the model comply or refuse?). Clinical pharmacology uses dose-response curves to characterize the full relationship between stimulus intensity and effect. This richer characterization is missing from AI safety evals.

**Direction:** Construct 'dose-response' curves for harmful capability elicitation: vary prompt aggressiveness/specificity along a continuous scale and measure refusal probability at each level. Fit sigmoid curves. Compare dose-response profiles across models to characterize safety margins and robustness.

**Why it matters:** A model that refuses 95% of harmful requests at low specificity but fails at high specificity has a different safety profile than one with consistent 50% refusal. Dose-response characterization gives regulators richer information for deployment decisions.

**Relevant context:** ORFuzz and JBFuzz already vary prompt aggressiveness; this project formalizes that variation as a dose-response framework borrowed from pharmacology.
