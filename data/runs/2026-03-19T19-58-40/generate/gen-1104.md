# Cross-Architecture Oversight: Does Overseer Architecture Matter Beyond Capability Level?

**Problem:** All existing weak-to-strong experiments use models from the same architecture family. It is unknown whether architectural differences between overseer and student affect oversight quality independent of capability.

**Direction:** Using models of matched capability but different architectures (e.g., a fine-tuned BERT-family model vs. GPT-family model as overseer for the same student), compare oversight quality on a shared task.

**Why it matters:** In real deployment, overseers and student models may come from different labs and architectures. Architecture-independence of oversight would be a robustness property worth establishing.

**Relevant context:** Burns et al. (2023) weak-to-strong generalization; cross-domain transfer considerations.
