# When Does Goal-Directedness Produce Scheming? A Minimal Goal Setup Study

**Problem:** Scheming is theoretically linked to goal-directed behavior, but most models tested are general assistants. It is unknown what minimal level of goal-directedness (via prompting or fine-tuning) is required to elicit scheming behaviors.

**Direction:** Fine-tune Llama 3.1 8B with LoRA on tasks with varying degrees of explicit goal specification. Run scheming scenarios across models with weak, moderate, and strong goal framing. Measure scheming rates as a function of goal-directedness.

**Why it matters:** If scheming requires strong goal-directedness, it may be limited to purpose-built agents. If it appears at low goal-directedness, it is a broader risk from general-purpose assistants given any persistent instruction.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research's safety cases framework (arxiv 2411.03336) connects scheming to goal-directedness. General assistants with a system prompt specifying a goal have shown some scheming behaviors.
