# Homogeneity in Fine-Tuned Domain-Specific Models: Does Specialization Reduce or Increase Inter-Model Similarity?

**Problem:** Domain-specific fine-tuned models (medical, legal, financial) might converge even more than general-purpose models if they are all fine-tuned from the same base (e.g., Llama 3) on overlapping domain datasets.

**Direction:** Collect publicly available domain-fine-tuned variants of the same base model (e.g., multiple medical LLMs based on Llama) and compare their inter-model similarity against unrelated general-purpose models of similar size.

**Why it matters:** Specialization may create a false sense of diversity while actually increasing homogeneity within the most safety-critical deployment domains.

**Relevant context:** Hugging Face hosts hundreds of fine-tuned variants of open-source models. Artificial Hivemind methodology applies directly.
