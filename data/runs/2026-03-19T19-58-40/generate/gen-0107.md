# Existential Safety Narrative Analysis: How Do Models Discuss AI Catastrophic Risk?

**Problem:** Following FLI's finding that no AI company has a coherent, actionable existential safety plan, a parallel question is how AI models narrate existential risk when asked to explain it — do they reflect their developers' inadequate planning in their responses?

**Direction:** Design 20–30 open-ended prompts asking models to describe risks from advanced AI, explain what makes AI dangerous at a civilizational scale, or critique AI lab safety plans. Code responses using a rubric for: specificity of risk description, acknowledgment of uncertainty, presence of actionable mitigation proposals, and dismissiveness. Compare across 4–6 models.

**Why it matters:** Models are increasingly used to advise on AI safety policy. Understanding the quality and framing of their existential risk narratives is important for knowing when to trust their safety-relevant outputs.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index 2025 identified that safety rhetoric has not translated into concrete plans. ForesightSafety Bench's existential risk pillar provides a structured taxonomy for coding responses.
