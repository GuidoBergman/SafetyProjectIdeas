# Fault Tree Analysis Transfer: Applying Aviation/Nuclear Fault Tree Methodology to AI Control Protocol Design

**Problem:** Aviation and nuclear engineering use Fault Tree Analysis (FTA) to systematically identify combinations of component failures that could lead to catastrophic outcomes. AI control protocol design lacks equivalent systematic failure analysis, meaning protocols may have failure combinations that designers did not anticipate.

**Direction:** Select one ControlArena control protocol (e.g., trusted monitoring + human escalation + sandboxed execution). Apply formal FTA methodology to identify all combinations of component failures (monitor misses + human dismisses + sandbox bypass) that could result in undetected sabotage. Compute minimal cut sets. Compare the FTA-derived risk picture to the protocol designers' stated threat model. Identify gaps.

**Why it matters:** FTA is a proven systematic method for safety protocol analysis. Applying it to AI control protocols provides a more rigorous risk picture than narrative threat model analysis and may reveal combinations that designers overlooked.

**Relevant context:** FTA is a standard technique in IEC 61025 and MIL-STD-882E. Applying it requires no ML—only logical analysis and probability estimation. ControlArena's control protocols are documented, providing a concrete analysis target.
