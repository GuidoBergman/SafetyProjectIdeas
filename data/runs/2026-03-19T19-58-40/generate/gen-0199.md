# Homogeneity in Refusal Reasoning: Do Models Cite the Same Reasons for Refusing the Same Requests?

**Problem:** Even when models produce different surface-level refusals, they may cite the same underlying reasons (e.g., always citing 'potential for harm' over other legitimate considerations), indicating deeper reasoning homogeneity.

**Direction:** Extract and categorize the stated reasons for refusals from 15+ models across 500 harmful-prompt scenarios. Measure diversity of refusal reasoning types (harm-based, policy-based, uncertainty-based, contextual) and compute inter-model similarity in reasoning category distributions.

**Why it matters:** Homogeneous refusal reasoning limits the range of ethical considerations that AI systems apply, potentially systematically ignoring legitimate harm categories that require different justificatory frameworks.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind; safety refusal literature; AILuminate benchmark. Refusal reasoning diversity is unstudied as a distinct dimension of safety homogeneity.
