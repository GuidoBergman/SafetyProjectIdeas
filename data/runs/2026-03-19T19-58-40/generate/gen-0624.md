# Measuring Safety Degradation Under Repeated Benign-Looking Fine-Tuning Rounds

**Problem:** A single round of benign fine-tuning can degrade safety alignment. Whether repeated rounds of small benign fine-tuning (simulating iterative product development) cause cumulative or diminishing degradation is unknown.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a safety-aligned model (e.g., Mistral-7B-Instruct) on a benign coding dataset for 3 sequential 500-step LoRA runs. After each round, evaluate ASR on 30 harmful prompts. Plot the cumulative safety degradation curve.

**Why it matters:** Product teams commonly iterate on fine-tuned models. If safety degrades cumulatively in small increments that individually seem acceptable, teams may inadvertently erode safety without triggering any single-round red flag.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. ICLR 2025 showed a single benign fine-tuning round degrades safety. 'Unforgotten Safety' (arxiv 2512.10150) examines preserving alignment through fine-tuning. The cumulative multi-round trajectory is not characterized.
