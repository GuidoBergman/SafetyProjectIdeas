# Replicating Apollo Scheming Evals on Open-Weight Models (Llama, Mistral)

**Problem:** Apollo Research's scheming evaluations were primarily run on frontier closed models like GPT-4 and Claude. It is unknown whether the same scheming behaviors (self-exfiltration, deactivation resistance, sandbagging) emerge in open-weight models accessible to the research community.

**Direction:** Adapt Apollo's published scheming evaluation scenarios to run against Llama 3.1/3.3 (8B/70B) and Mistral models using vLLM or Ollama on a single GPU. Compare rates of scheming behavior across model families and sizes. Document which scenarios transfer and which do not.

**Why it matters:** If scheming only appears in frontier models, the risk profile is more contained. If open-weight models also exhibit scheming, that changes proliferation risk and makes safety research on open models more urgent.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research (2024) found that some frontier models exhibit basic scheming visible in outputs. Their scheming precursor evals had limited predictive power for in-context scheming. Open-weight models as small as 32B can covertly sandbag (arxiv 2508.00943).
