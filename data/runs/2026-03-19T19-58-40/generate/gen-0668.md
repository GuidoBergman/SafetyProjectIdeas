# Inter-Annotator Agreement Study on Safety Benchmark Ground Truth

**Problem:** Safety benchmark labels (safe/unsafe) are often created by a small number of annotators. Whether these labels reflect genuine consensus or are highly annotator-dependent has not been rigorously studied for major benchmarks.

**Direction:** Re-annotate a sample of 200–300 AILuminate public prompts with three to five independent raters (recruited via student volunteers or crowdsourcing). Compute inter-rater agreement and compare to benchmark's reported label confidence.

**Why it matters:** If ground truth labels have low inter-annotator agreement, safety benchmarks are measuring annotator idiosyncrasies rather than genuine safety. This undermines all downstream evaluation.

**Relevant context:** ICLR 2025 research shows LLM judges are over-confident relative to majority-annotator agreement. Human-label reliability for safety benchmarks is a prior-layer problem.
