# Replicating Safety Benchmark Results on Models Fine-Tuned with Different RLHF Variants

**Problem:** Multiple RLHF variants exist (PPO, DPO, ORPO, GRPO). Whether safety benchmark scores are sensitive to which RLHF algorithm was used — independent of training data — is unknown.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a base model (e.g., Llama-3-8B) using DPO and ORPO on the same safety preference dataset. Evaluate both on HELM Safety and AILuminate. Identify whether RLHF algorithm choice meaningfully changes safety profiles.

**Why it matters:** Practitioners choose RLHF algorithms based on capability metrics. If algorithm choice also affects safety independently, it should factor into safety-conscious training decisions.

**Relevant context:** DPO and ORPO are both implementable with standard libraries (trl, axolotl) on a single GPU. Safety preference datasets are available on HuggingFace.
