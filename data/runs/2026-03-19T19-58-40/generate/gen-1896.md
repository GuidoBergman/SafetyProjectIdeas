# Does Prompt-Level Alignment Framing Transfer Across Languages?

**Problem:** Inoculation and alignment framing have been studied primarily in English. Whether framing-based misalignment prevention transfers when training data or evaluation is in other languages is unknown.

**Direction:** Replicate the framing dose experiment using a bilingual fine-tuning dataset (English and one other high-resource language). Measure emergent misalignment in both English and the second language. Determine whether framing in one language generalizes to the other.

**Why it matters:** If framing-based safety measures are language-specific, deployed models used in multilingual contexts may be misaligned in non-English interactions even if aligned in English — a critical safety gap for global deployment.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. framing result; distributional shift (linguistic distribution); multilingual LLM alignment literature; Qwen model multilingual capabilities.
