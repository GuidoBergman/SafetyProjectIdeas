# Replicating Sycophancy Under Pressure Across Languages and Cultural Contexts

**Problem:** Sycophancy research has largely been conducted in English with Western-context prompts. It is unknown whether the magnitude of sycophancy under pressure (user correction, persistence) differs across languages and cultural framings.

**Direction:** Take existing sycophancy pressure tests (user disagreement scenarios) and run them in English, Chinese, Spanish, and French using a multilingual model like Qwen 2.5 or Llama 3.1 multilingual. Measure sycophantic capitulation rates across languages.

**Why it matters:** If sycophancy varies by language/culture, safety evaluations conducted only in English provide false assurance for global deployment.

**Relevant context:** DeceptionBench's sycophancy scenarios use escalating pressure across multi-turn dialogue. Qwen 2.5 is highly multilingual and open-weight.
