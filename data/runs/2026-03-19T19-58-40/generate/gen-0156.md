# Replicating the Artificial Hivemind Effect on Medical and Clinical Safety Tasks

**Problem:** Inter-model homogeneity on medical queries—drug interactions, symptom assessment, treatment recommendations—could create correlated errors that harm patients at scale when multiple health AI tools give the same wrong advice.

**Direction:** Construct a set of 200 medical safety queries (including known edge cases and common misdiagnosis scenarios), run them through 10+ models, and measure inter-model similarity and agreement, including on incorrect responses.

**Why it matters:** Healthcare is one of the highest-stakes deployment domains for LLMs. Systemic correlated errors could contribute to patient harm at scale.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind showed cross-model homogeneity on general queries; medical AI benchmarks (MedQA, MedBench) provide validated question banks for this extension.
