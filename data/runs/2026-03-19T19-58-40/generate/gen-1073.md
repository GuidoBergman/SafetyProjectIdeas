# Benchmark for OR-Gate Completeness Detection in Circuit Discovery

**Problem:** 'Rethinking Circuit Completeness in Language Models' (2025) showed that standard circuit discovery methods fail to fully detect OR gates, which arise when multiple redundant sub-circuits can each independently perform a task. No benchmark exists that specifically tests whether a circuit discovery method correctly identifies OR-gate structures versus missing them.

**Direction:** Construct two synthetic tasks in GPT-2-Small where OR-gate structure is artificially induced by training on tasks with two equally valid solution paths. Apply two circuit discovery methods and evaluate whether the resulting circuit captures both branches. Use the completeness metric from MIB. Report a binary pass/fail for OR-gate detection per method and discuss implications.

**Why it matters:** Missing OR gates means a discovered circuit is incomplete and would incorrectly attribute the full computation to one sub-circuit. For safety, this could mean failing to account for redundant pathways that enable undesired behaviors.
