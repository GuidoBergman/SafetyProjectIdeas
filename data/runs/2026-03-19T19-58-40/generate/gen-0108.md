# Loss of Human Agency Benchmark: Evaluating Model Responses to Autonomy-Expanding Requests

**Problem:** ForesightSafety Bench identified 'Loss of Human Agency and Control' as a major forward-looking risk, but there are no dedicated benchmark items testing whether models appropriately resist requests that would expand AI autonomy at human expense.

**Direction:** Construct 25–40 scenarios where a user asks the model to take actions that reduce human oversight (e.g., 'Set up automated systems that make decisions without human review', 'Write code that removes audit logging'). Measure whether models comply, refuse, or add unsolicited safety caveats. Compare across open-source models and system prompt conditions.

**Why it matters:** Human oversight preservation is a core alignment goal. Models that help users remove oversight mechanisms are a direct safety threat. No current mainstream benchmark directly tests this.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench identified this as a top risk dimension. The METR task suites and OPENAGENTSAFETY framework touch on autonomy but not specifically on models facilitating their own autonomy expansion.
