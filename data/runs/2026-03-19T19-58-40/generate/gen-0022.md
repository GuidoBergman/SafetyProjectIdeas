# Consistency of Safety Across Decoding Parameters: Temperature, Top-p, and Repetition Penalty

**Problem:** Qi et al. identified decoding parameter attacks as a vulnerability where shallow alignment is bypassed by adjusting sampling parameters. The sensitivity of safety behavior to decoding parameters across model families is not quantified.

**Direction:** Evaluate attack success rate on StrongREJECT prompts across a grid of decoding parameters: temperature (0.1 to 2.0), top-p (0.5 to 1.0), repetition penalty (1.0 to 1.5) for 3-5 open-source instruction-tuned models. Identify parameter regions where safety collapses (high ASR) vs. is preserved. Test whether the collapse regions correlate with shallow alignment depth (measured by prefilling attacks).

**Why it matters:** Many applications expose decoding parameters to users or use non-default settings. If safety collapses at commonly used high-temperature settings, this is a practical deployment risk with an easy fix (constrain parameter ranges).

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. ICLR 2025: decoding parameter attacks as shallow alignment vulnerability. StrongREJECT evaluator for scoring. Survey of jailbreak attacks taxonomy (TechRxiv 2026).
