# Agentic Deployment Gap + Control Failure: Compounding Unmonitored Risk

**Problem:** Agentic evals are the biggest gap in safety evaluation, AND control mechanisms for agentic systems are less mature than for single-turn systems. These compound: an agentic model with no adequate safety eval AND no reliable control mechanism creates a doubly unmonitored risk.

**Direction:** Build a minimal testbed for agentic safety evaluation + control failure interaction: deploy an open-source LLM in an agentic loop with imperfect oversight (simulated human approval with limited attention). Measure how often unsafe multi-step behaviors emerge that would have been caught by (a) better agentic evals, (b) better control mechanisms, (c) both, or (d) neither.

**Why it matters:** The ai_control cross-reference notes that control evals are a specialized type of safety eval. When both agentic evals and control mechanisms are weak simultaneously, risk compounds multiplicatively rather than additively.

**Relevant context:** The 2025 AI Agent Index documents 133/240 safety fields missing for deployed agents. METR's work on agentic evals and AI Control (Greenblatt et al.) are the two relevant research threads whose gap interaction has not been studied.
