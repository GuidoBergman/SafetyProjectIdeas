# Probe Accuracy as an Early Warning for Model Misalignment

**Problem:** As models are updated or fine-tuned, safety alignment may degrade; monitoring concept probe accuracy over time could provide an early-warning signal for alignment regression.

**Direction:** Set up a longitudinal monitoring pipeline that re-evaluates safety concept probe accuracy after each fine-tuning update to a model, testing whether probe accuracy degradation predicts downstream safety benchmark degradation before it becomes observable in outputs.

**Why it matters:** Proactive detection of alignment regression before it manifests in outputs could prevent deployment of misaligned models; this is a direct operational safety application.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. demonstrate monitoring capabilities. The concept of alignment regression monitoring is not currently implemented with concept probes. Fine-tuning stability work (ICCV 2025) shows representations change.
