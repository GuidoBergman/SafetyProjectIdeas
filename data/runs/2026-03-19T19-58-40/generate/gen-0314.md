# Agentic Deployment Without Governance Infrastructure: Measuring the Current Agentic Safety Gap

**Problem:** Technical failure: agentic AI systems take real-world actions with compounding consequences. Governance failure: most deployment governance frameworks were designed for single-turn inference and do not cover agentic behavior. Together these produce deployed agentic systems with no adequate governance.

**Direction:** Identify 20–30 publicly documented agentic AI deployments. For each, check whether: (a) the deployment framework specifies agentic-specific safety constraints, (b) an Agent Identity Card or equivalent disclosure exists, (c) human confirmation requirements for high-stakes actions are documented. Compute the fraction with adequate agentic governance.

**Why it matters:** Agentic AI is one of the fastest-growing deployment categories and has the most severe potential for compounding harms; quantifying the current governance gap is urgent.

**Relevant context:** Singapore IMDA's agentic AI governance framework (January 2026) is the first formal standard; NIST Cyber AI Profile (December 2025) addresses agentic monitoring. Most deployments predate these frameworks.
