# The Proof Size Problem: Are LLM-Generated Lean 4 Proofs Unnecessarily Long?

**Problem:** LLM-generated Lean 4 proofs may be systematically longer than necessary (redundant lemmas, unnecessary case splits). Proof size correlates with maintainability and readability, both safety-relevant for long-term code maintenance.

**Direction:** For 30 problems where LLMs generate working Lean 4 proofs, measure proof length (lines of code, number of tactics, lemma count). Compare to reference (shortest known) proofs. Compute size inflation factor. Test whether shorter prompting strategies reduce proof size.

**Why it matters:** Excessively long proofs are harder for humans to review, increasing the probability of undetected errors. Proof minimization is a safety engineering concern that is not addressed by current benchmarks.

**Relevant context:** Human-written Lean proofs tend to be more concise than LLM-generated ones (observed qualitatively in Mathlib contributions). No quantitative study of LLM proof verbosity vs. human expert proofs exists.
