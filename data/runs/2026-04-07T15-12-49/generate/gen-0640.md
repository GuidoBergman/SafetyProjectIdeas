# Multi-Agent Formal Verification: Does a Debate-Style Protocol Between Two LLM Verifiers Improve Proof Quality?

**Problem:** When one LLM generates a Lean 4 proof and another LLM critiques it (debate protocol), the combination may catch more errors than single-LLM generation. Whether multi-agent debate improves Lean 4 proof quality is untested.

**Direction:** Implement a two-agent protocol: LLM A generates a Lean 4 proof, LLM B critiques it (identifies potential errors), LLM A revises. Compare compilation rate and proof quality (vacuity rate, mutation survival) between: (a) single-agent, (b) two-agent debate, (c) two independent single-agents choosing the better output.

**Why it matters:** Multi-agent debate is proposed as a scalable oversight method in AI safety. Testing it specifically in formal verification provides empirical evidence for whether debate protocols improve correctness in machine-checkable domains.
