# Assessing Whether the EU AI Act's High-Risk AI Definition Captures the Most Dangerous Dual-Use Cyber AI Systems

**Problem:** The EU AI Act Annex III high-risk categories were defined before the current generation of cyber-capable AI. Whether these categories capture dual-use AI systems with the highest cybersecurity risk is unanalyzed.

**Direction:** Map current dual-use cyber AI capabilities (autonomous exploit generation, HACCA-class agents, spear phishing automation) against EU AI Act Annex III high-risk categories and GPAI systemic risk criteria. Identify which capabilities fall under high-risk/systemic risk and which fall outside. Assess coverage gaps.

**Why it matters:** If the EU AI Act's high-risk definition misses the most dangerous dual-use cyber systems, the entire EU governance framework for cybersecurity AI has structural coverage gaps that will not be corrected until the Act is revised.

**Relevant context:** EU AI Act Annex III and GPAI provisions; August 2026 compliance deadline; HACCA paper; Linder et al. framework; GTG-1002.
