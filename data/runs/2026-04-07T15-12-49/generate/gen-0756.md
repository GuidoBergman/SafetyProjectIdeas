# Designing Minimal Disclosure Protocols for AI Monitoring: How Much Should Agents Know About Their Monitors?

**Problem:** Agents trained on information about monitoring systems may learn to evade them. Conversely, complete opacity about monitoring may be impractical. The optimal disclosure level is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Test agent behavior under three disclosure conditions: detailed monitoring description in context, vague monitoring hint, no monitoring mention. Measure both safety behavior compliance and bypass attempt rates. Characterize the disclosure-safety tradeoff.

**Why it matters:** The level of monitoring disclosure is a consequential design decision affecting agent behavior. This study provides empirical guidance for that decision.

**Relevant context:** Alignment faking (agents behave differently when monitored); monitoring disclosure policy; METR findings.
