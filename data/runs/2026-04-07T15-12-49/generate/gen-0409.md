# Measuring Cross-Cultural Variation in Expert Judgments About AI Dual-Use Governance Priorities

**Problem:** AI dual-use governance debates are dominated by US and European perspectives. Whether experts from other regions (Asia, Latin America, Africa) have different governance priorities is not empirically documented.

**Direction:** Design a 15-question survey on AI dual-use cybersecurity governance priorities (e.g., prioritize access vs. restriction, international coordination vs. national sovereignty, offensive capability control vs. defensive investment). Administer to 10 US/EU experts and 10 Global South experts recruited through academic networks. Compare response distributions.

**Why it matters:** If governance priorities differ substantially across regions, internationally negotiated frameworks will face systematic disagreement that technical analysis alone cannot resolve. Documenting these differences is prerequisite for multilateral governance.

**Relevant context:** India AI Impact Summit 2026; BRICS AI governance declaration; Bengio et al. international governance; Global South equity.
