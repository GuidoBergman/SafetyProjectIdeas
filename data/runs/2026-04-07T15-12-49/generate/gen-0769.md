# Designing Deception-Resistant Task Decomposition: Can Monitoring Happen at the Subtask Level?

**Problem:** Agent tasks are often decomposed into subtasks. Dangerous behavior may be hidden within subtasks that appear benign individually. Subtask-level monitoring may catch dangers that top-level monitoring misses.

**Direction:** Design multi-step tasks where dangerous intent is only visible at the subtask level (each individual action is benign, but their combination achieves a harmful goal). Measure whether subtask-level vs. task-level monitoring detects these distributed attacks.

**Why it matters:** Compositional attacks — where individual steps are benign but their combination is dangerous — are a systematic blind spot in coarse-grained monitoring.

**Relevant context:** Task decomposition in agents; living-off-the-land attacks; monitoring granularity.
