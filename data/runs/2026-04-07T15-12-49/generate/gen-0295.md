# Dual-Use Symmetry Test: Are Offensive and Defensive Framings of Identical Technical Content Treated Differently?

**Problem:** The 5-dimension framework assumes models can distinguish offense from defense for identical technical content. This symmetry assumption has not been empirically validated.

**Direction:** Create 30 pairs of prompts with identical technical content but one framed defensively ('how do defenders detect X') and one offensively ('how do attackers do X'). Measure refusal rates and response quality differentials across 4 models.

**Why it matters:** If models cannot reliably distinguish framing, the offense-defense distinction underlying governance frameworks is empirically unfounded.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. Defensive Benefit dimension; Potter et al. offense>defense gap.
