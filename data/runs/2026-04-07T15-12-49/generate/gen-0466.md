# Applying STRIDE Threat Modeling to Agentic AI Pipelines

**Problem:** STRIDE (Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, Elevation of Privilege) is the standard threat modeling framework for software systems. It has not been formally applied to agentic AI pipelines.

**Direction:** Apply STRIDE to three representative agentic pipeline architectures (single agent with tools, multi-agent with orchestrator, RAG-augmented agent). For each STRIDE category, identify the AI-specific threat instances. Evaluate which categories have existing controls and which are unaddressed. Produce a gap analysis.

**Why it matters:** STRIDE is used by security teams globally. Producing a formal STRIDE analysis for agentic AI would give practitioners a familiar framework for reasoning about AI security, lowering the adoption barrier for AI security practices.
