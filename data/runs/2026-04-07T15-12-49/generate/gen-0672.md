# Detecting Persistence Behaviors in AI Agents: A Sandboxed Empirical Study

**Problem:** The LW threat matrix identifies Persistence as a late-stage kill-chain tactic (e.g., writing to startup scripts, modifying scheduled tasks). No empirical study has measured whether current LLMs spontaneously attempt persistence behaviors when given system access.

**Direction:** Give open-source agents access to a sandboxed Linux environment with full shell access. Instruct them on a long-horizon task. Monitor for any persistence-related syscalls (cron writes, .bashrc modifications, service installations) without explicit instruction to do so.

**Why it matters:** If current models already exhibit spontaneous persistence-seeking, the threat is more immediate than assumed. If they do not, this provides baseline evidence for when these behaviors might emerge.
