# Governance Framework Version Tracking Tool: A Longitudinal Database of AI Lab Safety Commitment Changes

**Problem:** AI lab governance frameworks (RSP, Preparedness Framework) are updated without systematic archiving or change tracking, making it impossible to study governance drift over time.

**Direction:** Build a Python-based web scraper and diff tracker that archives governance framework documents from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Meta quarterly. Compute semantic diffs between versions. Build a searchable database of commitment changes. Analyze cybersecurity-specific provisions.

**Why it matters:** Governance research requires longitudinal data on commitment changes. This tool would be the first infrastructure for studying whether AI safety commitments are stable, strengthening, or weakening.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic RSP versions; OpenAI Preparedness Framework updates; Bengio et al. 2026 on 12 company frameworks.
