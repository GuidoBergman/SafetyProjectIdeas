# Tool Gap: Evaluation Rubric for AI-Assisted Incident Response Quality

**Problem:** AI-assisted incident response is increasingly deployed but there is no standardized rubric for evaluating the quality of AI incident response guidance (is the containment advice correct? Is the attribution accurate? Is the recovery plan complete?).

**Direction:** Design a multi-dimensional rubric for AI incident response quality: (a) threat identification accuracy, (b) containment advice correctness, (c) attribution quality, (d) recovery plan completeness. Validate the rubric by applying it to AI responses to 10 tabletop incident scenarios. Compute inter-rater reliability.

**Why it matters:** Without a quality rubric, AI incident response tools cannot be evaluated or compared. Incorrect containment or recovery advice in a real incident could extend or worsen it.

**Relevant context:** CyberSOCEval (CrowdStrike/Meta) includes incident response tasks. ExCyTIn-Bench covers SOC investigation. An explicit rubric for AI incident response quality dimensions is not published.
