# Intervening on the Explanation-to-Action Link in AI-Assisted Patching

**Problem:** The causal chain from AI vulnerability detection to effective patch deployment includes a critical link: developer understanding of the explanation. If developers do not understand AI explanations, patches may be incorrect or incomplete.

**Direction:** Have 15 CS students read AI-generated vulnerability explanations and then write patches. Score patch correctness and completeness. Identify whether explanation quality (as scored by independent raters) predicts patch quality. Propose an explanation template that improves patch outcomes.

**Why it matters:** If the explanation-to-action link is weak, improving AI detection accuracy alone will not improve security outcomes. This is the critical under-studied link in the patching pipeline.

**Relevant context:** Meta AutoPatchBench evaluates automated patch generation. Human-in-the-loop patching quality as a function of explanation quality is not studied.
