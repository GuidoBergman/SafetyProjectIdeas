# Workforce Skill Gap + AI Automation Dependency = Brittle Security Posture

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) security workforce skill gaps are increasing (SANS 2026: 35% of orgs report moderate gaps); (2) AI automation increases dependency on tools that may fail. Combined, when AI tools fail or are unavailable, the organization has no fallback capability.

**Direction:** Survey 20 CS students on their ability to manually perform tasks that AI security tools automate (manual code review for common vulnerability classes, log analysis, patch validation). Measure performance without AI assistance vs. with. Quantify the skill atrophy risk: how much worse do AI-assisted reviewers perform when the AI is unavailable?

**Why it matters:** Organizations optimizing for AI-assisted security may be building brittle postures. A supply chain attack on the AI tool itself (like LiteLLM) could simultaneously disable security tools and exploit the resulting skill vacuum.

**Relevant context:** SANS 2026 workforce skills report. AI tool supply chain compromise (LiteLLM). Automation-induced skill atrophy is documented in aviation (autopilot dependency) but not in security.
