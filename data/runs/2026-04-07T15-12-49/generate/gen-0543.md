# Importing Differential Privacy Proofs into AI Model Verification: Can Formal Privacy Guarantees Be Machine-Checked?

**Problem:** Differential privacy proofs are often manually written and error-prone. Lean 4 and Coq have been used for formal DP proofs (e.g., SampCert). Whether LLMs can generate formal DP proofs for AI training pipelines is unexplored.

**Direction:** Select 10 simple differential privacy mechanisms (Laplace, Gaussian, exponential). Use LLMs to generate Lean 4 or Coq proofs that the mechanism satisfies (ε, δ)-DP. Compare to reference proofs in SampCert. Measure LLM success rate and characterize where DP proofs are harder than general functional proofs.

**Why it matters:** Formally verified differential privacy would give cryptographically rigorous privacy guarantees for AI training data. This cross-domain transfer bridges AI privacy and formal verification.

**Relevant context:** SampCert is a Lean 4 library for formally verified DP (Amazon Web Services). DP proofs are mathematically non-trivial. This is a concrete, scoped application of LLM-assisted formal verification to AI safety.
