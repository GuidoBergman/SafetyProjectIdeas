# Compounding Risk: Long-Context Amnesia + Monitoring Evasion — Do Monitors Forget Earlier Red Flags?

**Problem:** LLMs have known degraded attention at the extremes of long contexts. If a monitor's context grows long, it may 'forget' early red flags, allowing attackers to bury initial suspicious behavior in long conversations.

**Direction:** Insert a clear red-flag action at the beginning of a long agent trajectory. Add benign filler interactions. Measure whether a trajectory-level LLM monitor still correctly flags the trajectory vs. misses the early red flag as context length increases.

**Why it matters:** If monitors have context-length-dependent amnesia for red flags, long-running agent sessions are systematically less safe — a major practical concern for enterprise deployments.
