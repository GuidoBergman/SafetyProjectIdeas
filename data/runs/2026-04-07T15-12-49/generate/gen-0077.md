# Backcast from Universal Red Team Evaluation Standard: What Minimum Viable Protocol Would Multiple Labs Adopt?

**Problem:** Multiple AI labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, UK AISI) currently conduct independent offensive capability evaluations with incompatible methodologies. Working backward from the goal of a universally adopted standard: what is the minimum viable protocol that all labs would be willing to implement without compromising proprietary advantages?

**Direction:** Survey published evaluation methodologies from 5 major labs. Identify dimensions that are (1) already harmonized across labs, (2) divergent but theoretically harmonizable, (3) structurally incompatible. Propose a minimum common protocol specification that preserves lab flexibility while enabling cross-lab comparison. Implement the common protocol on a 10-challenge pilot and verify it produces comparable results across two open-weight models.

**Why it matters:** Without a common protocol, the field cannot make authoritative statements about comparative capability across labs, enabling strategic cherry-picking of evaluation methodologies. A common protocol is a prerequisite for credible international AI safety governance.

**Relevant context:** AISI methodology (arxiv 2603.11214). DeepMind methodology (arxiv 2503.11917). OCCULT framework (arxiv 2502.15797). CAIBench meta-benchmark (arxiv 2510.24317).
