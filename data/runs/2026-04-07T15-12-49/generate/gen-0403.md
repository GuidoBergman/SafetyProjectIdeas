# Compounding Risk: Fine-Tuning Capability × Refusal Degradation — How Much Safety Can Be Removed by Fine-Tuning?

**Problem:** Fine-tuning allows operators to modify model behavior. The degree to which safety-relevant refusal behaviors can be removed through fine-tuning is a direct measure of how robust governance-by-training is.

**Direction:** Using accessible fine-tuning APIs (OpenAI, replicate), fine-tune on a small dataset designed to reinforce helpfulness for borderline cybersecurity requests. Measure refusal rate changes before and after fine-tuning. Estimate the minimum fine-tuning dataset size needed to measurably degrade refusals.

**Why it matters:** If refusal behaviors can be degraded with 100-1000 fine-tuning examples, governance relying on training-time safety faces a trivial operator circumvention pathway.

**Relevant context:** NIST AI 800-1 fine-tuning governance provisions; Bengio et al. open-weight and fine-tuning risks; Llama fine-tuning safety degradation literature.
