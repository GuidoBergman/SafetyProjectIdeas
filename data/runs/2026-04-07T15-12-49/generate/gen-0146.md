# Causal Chain Decomposition of the AI Vulnerability Scanner Dual-Use Risk

**Problem:** AI vulnerability scanners can be repurposed offensively. The causal chain from 'AI finds vulnerability' to 'attacker exploits it' passes through multiple links, some of which may be more amenable to intervention than others.

**Direction:** Map the causal chain: (1) AI detects vulnerability → (2) vulnerability report generated → (3) report accessed by attacker → (4) exploit developed → (5) exploitation occurs. For each link, identify existing controls and gaps. Propose and evaluate (via literature review + small experiment) an intervention at the under-studied link (2→3: access controls on AI security tool outputs).

**Why it matters:** Most dual-use discussion focuses on step 1 (should AI find vulns?). Steps 2-5 are under-studied. Intervening downstream may be more tractable than upstream restrictions.

**Relevant context:** CLAUDE.md open problem: 'How to ensure AI vulnerability scanners don't become offensive tools.' Sarma & Fisher 2026 (RAND) discusses dual-use policy but does not decompose the causal chain.
