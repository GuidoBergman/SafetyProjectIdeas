# Longitudinal False Positive Rate Tracking in AI Vulnerability Scanners

**Problem:** False positive rates in AI-powered vulnerability discovery are poorly documented across model versions and code corpora. Teams waste time triaging non-issues (alert fatigue).

**Direction:** Run three or four publicly available AI code scanners (e.g., Semgrep AI, CodeQL, a GPT-4o-based tool) against a fixed corpus of 30 open-source projects with known ground-truth vulnerability labels, measuring precision/recall/false positive rate per tool and per vulnerability class.

**Why it matters:** Establishes empirical baselines for a known open problem. Even a 30-project sample produces actionable data. Directly replicable by the community.

**Relevant context:** Algomox blog and Qualys research acknowledge FP rates as critical but no peer-reviewed systematic measurement exists at this scope. MDPI AI vulnerability detection review (2025) also flags this gap.
