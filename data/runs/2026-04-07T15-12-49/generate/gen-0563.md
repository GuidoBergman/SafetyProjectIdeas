# Formally Verified Code × AI Model Tampering: Can Formal Methods Verify Model Evaluation Integrity?

**Problem:** RAND proposes using formal methods to verify AI model evaluations without tampering. A compounding risk: if the verification framework itself uses AI to generate proofs, those AI components could be tampered with, undermining the integrity guarantee.

**Direction:** Design a minimal threat model for a tamper-evident AI evaluation verification system. Identify which components must be formally verified by humans vs. which could be AI-assisted. Test whether a Lean 4 proof of a simple evaluation protocol can be generated, reviewed, and validated without AI in the critical path.

**Why it matters:** An AI-generated proof of AI evaluation integrity creates a circular dependency. If the proof generator is the AI being evaluated, the safety case collapses. This is a foundational governance design question.

**Relevant context:** RAND (2026) proposes cryptographic proof of AI evaluations. Zero-knowledge proofs (SNARKs) could provide non-AI-dependent verification. The circular dependency between AI formal verification tools and AI governance applications is an unexplored compounding risk.
