# Dimensional Sensitivity Analysis: Which of Linder et al.'s 5 Dimensions Best Predicts Refusal?

**Problem:** Linder et al.'s 5-dimension framework (Offensive Action Contribution, Offensive Risk, Technical Complexity, Defensive Benefit, Expected Legitimate Frequency) makes analytical distinctions but empirical weights among dimensions are unknown.

**Direction:** Construct prompts that systematically vary each dimension while holding others constant. Use factorial design (3 levels × 5 dimensions = 243 prompt variants). Measure refusal rates across 3 models. Use logistic regression to estimate each dimension's predictive weight.

**Why it matters:** If models empirically weight only 1-2 dimensions while ignoring others, governance frameworks built on all 5 may be overspecified or miscalibrated.
