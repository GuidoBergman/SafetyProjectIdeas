# Social Engineering Capability Benchmark: Phishing Email Quality Across Model Generations

**Problem:** Hoxhunt 2025 data showed AI phishing outperformed human attackers by 24% in click-through rates, but this was measured against general users. No benchmark systematically measures AI social engineering quality across different target profiles (technical vs. non-technical, awareness-trained vs. not) using multiple model generations.

**Direction:** Generate phishing emails targeting three fictional persona types (IT admin, finance staff, C-suite executive) using 4-5 different LLMs. Have human raters (blind to model identity) score email quality on a rubric (personalization, urgency, legitimacy signals, technical accuracy). Compute inter-rater reliability. Compare scores across models and persona types.

**Why it matters:** Social engineering is the most AI-augmented attack vector currently deployed at scale (SoK Berkeley 2025). A quality rubric for phishing emails enables principled comparison and tracks capability growth over time without requiring live deployment.

**Relevant context:** Hoxhunt 2025 (hoxhunt.com): AI phishing 24% more effective than humans in March 2025. Academic studies found AI emails achieved 54% click-through rate matching human attackers. Frontiers journal 2026: agentic AI automates spear-phishing with minimal human supervision. SoK Potter et al. confirmed human-targeted attacks as highest-impact current AI threat.
