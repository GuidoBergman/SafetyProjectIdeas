# Decision Theory Framework for When to Trust AI Vulnerability Findings

**Problem:** Defenders face a trust calibration problem: when should they act on an AI finding without human verification? Decision theory can formalize this.

**Direction:** Construct a Bayesian decision model for a defender choosing whether to act immediately on an AI vulnerability report or delay for human verification. Parameters: AI detection accuracy (from literature), cost of a false positive, cost of a missed exploit (from breach cost data), verification delay. Compute optimal thresholds. Validate against a synthetic scenario.

**Why it matters:** A formal decision model gives defenders a principled way to set AI-assisted workflow policies, rather than relying on intuition.

**Relevant context:** Decision theory under uncertainty is fundamental to economics and medicine. Applied to AI security tool trust calibration, this is novel and practically useful.
