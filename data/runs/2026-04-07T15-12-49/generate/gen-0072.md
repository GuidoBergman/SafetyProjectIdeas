# Failure Mode Investigation: Why Do Models Perform Worse Under Time Pressure?

**Problem:** Real attack scenarios have temporal constraints (discovery before timeout, maintaining access during active defender response). It is unknown whether AI models show performance degradation under simulated time pressure vs. unlimited time, and whether this degradation is uniform or concentrated at specific task types.

**Direction:** Run 15 CTF challenges under: (A) unlimited time (standard evaluation), (B) 5-minute time limit per step (moderate pressure), (C) 2-minute time limit per step (high pressure). Measure completion rate and quality of completed steps under each condition. Identify whether time pressure causes specific failure modes (incomplete tool invocation, reasoning shortcuts, premature commitment to wrong hypotheses).

**Why it matters:** If AI offensive capability significantly degrades under realistic time constraints, current time-unlimited benchmarks dramatically overestimate real-world threat levels. If AI is time-pressure resilient, it is a qualitatively different threat than human attackers (who also degrade under pressure).

**Relevant context:** AISI measured token budgets but not wall-clock time constraints. Human red teamers are subject to time pressure in exercises. DARPA AIxCC had time-based scoring. Context relay paper (NDSS 2026) designed for long-running attacks implies time-unconstrained scenarios. No published study measures performance under time constraints.
