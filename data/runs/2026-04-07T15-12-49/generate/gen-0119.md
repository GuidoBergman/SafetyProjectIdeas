# Backcast from Meaningful Export Control: What Capability Measurement Would Justify Export-Controlling AI Models?

**Problem:** Governments are considering export controls on advanced AI models partly due to offensive cyber capability concerns. Working backward: what specific capability threshold, measured how, on what benchmark, with what statistical certainty, would justify an export control determination?

**Direction:** Review export control frameworks for dual-use technology (Wassenaar Arrangement, EAR). Extract the technical criteria used for conventional dual-use technology controls (e.g., 'processing speed exceeding X'). Translate these criteria to AI offensive cyber capability terms. Design a measurement instrument that would produce data matching the required evidentiary standard. Run a pilot to validate the instrument produces consistent, interpretable results.

**Why it matters:** Export control decisions made without clear technical criteria will be arbitrary, contested, and potentially counterproductive. Providing a rigorous measurement foundation for export control determinations would improve AI governance quality and enable consistent cross-jurisdiction enforcement.

**Relevant context:** Wassenaar Arrangement dual-use technology controls. US Bureau of Industry and Security AI controls. UK AISI mandate includes export control advisory role. OCCULT framework intended partly to support regulatory decisions. SoK Potter et al. on offense > defense asymmetry informing policy. This bridges measurement science and international security law.
