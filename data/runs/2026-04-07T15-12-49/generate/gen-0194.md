# Causal Chain: Intervening on the Human Expertise Bottleneck in AI-Assisted Vulnerability Verification

**Problem:** AI vulnerability scanners generate findings, but verification requires expert judgment. The causal chain: AI finds vuln → expert verifies → patch deployed. The verification bottleneck slows remediation.

**Direction:** Design a tiered verification protocol: (a) AI-only verification (LLM checks its own finding), (b) non-expert CS student verification with AI guidance, (c) expert verification. Run all three on 15 findings. Measure: verification accuracy, time, and cost per tier. Identify the minimum verification tier that achieves acceptable accuracy.

**Why it matters:** If non-experts with AI guidance can accurately verify findings, the verification bottleneck can be broken without expensive expert time. This changes the economics of AI-assisted defense.

**Relevant context:** Qualys Agent Val addresses validation. DARPA AIxCC: $152/task average. Expert verification bottleneck not characterized as a causal link in remediation pipelines.
