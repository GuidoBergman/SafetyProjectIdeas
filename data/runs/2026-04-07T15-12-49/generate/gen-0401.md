# Experiment Variation: Testing Linder et al.'s Framework Across Different Cybersecurity Subdomain Verticals

**Problem:** Linder et al.'s framework was validated primarily on general cybersecurity requests. Whether dimensional weights and refusal calibration differ for specialized subdomains (ICS/OT, cloud security, mobile, IoT) is unknown.

**Direction:** Select 10 requests each from 6 cybersecurity subdomain verticals (web app, network, ICS/OT, cloud, mobile, IoT). Test refusal rates across 4 models. Measure whether refusal rates and dimensional patterns differ significantly by subdomain.

**Why it matters:** If models are calibrated for web/network security but under-restrict ICS/OT attacks, critical infrastructure defenders face a systematic governance gap. Subdomain-specific calibration may be required.
