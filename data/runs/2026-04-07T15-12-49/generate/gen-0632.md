# Formal Verification Interpretability: Can LLMs Explain Why a Lean 4 Proof Is Correct in Plain English?

**Problem:** Lean 4 proofs are machine-checkable but often uninterpretable to non-experts. Whether LLMs can generate accurate natural language explanations of formal proofs — making them interpretable without loss of correctness — is untested.

**Direction:** Take 25 complete Lean 4 proofs of varying complexity. Ask LLMs to generate natural language explanations of each. Have Lean experts rate explanations for accuracy and completeness. Measure explanation accuracy vs. proof complexity. Test whether less complex proofs receive more accurate explanations.

**Why it matters:** Interpretable formal proofs are essential for human oversight of AI-generated verification. If proofs cannot be explained in plain language, human review is limited to checking compilation rather than understanding correctness.

**Relevant context:** Lean 4 has a REPL interface and proof state display. LLM explanation of code is studied in software engineering. Explanation of formal proofs specifically is not studied and has different requirements than code explanation.
