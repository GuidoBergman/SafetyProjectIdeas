# Causal Analysis of Monitoring Alert Fatigue: At What Volume Do Human Reviewers Start Ignoring Alerts?

**Problem:** Human reviewers are part of the monitoring chain, but alert fatigue is well-documented in traditional security. The alert volume threshold at which human reviewers start ignoring AI agent monitoring alerts is unknown.

**Direction:** Conduct a user study (N=20-30 student reviewers) where participants review simulated agent monitoring alerts at varying alert volumes. Measure decision accuracy and response time as alert volume increases. Identify the alert volume inflection point for performance degradation.

**Why it matters:** If human monitoring has an alert volume threshold, automated systems must be designed to stay below it — or human-in-the-loop monitoring is effectively eliminated at scale.

**Relevant context:** Alert fatigue research in traditional security; human-in-the-loop AI monitoring; METR monitoring evaluation.
