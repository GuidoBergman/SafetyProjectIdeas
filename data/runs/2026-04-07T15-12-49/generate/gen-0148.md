# Breaking the False Confidence Loop: Interventions on Over-Reliance on AI Security Verdicts

**Problem:** When AI tools give confident-sounding security verdicts, developers may stop independent verification. The causal chain: AI gives verdict → developer trusts it → developer skips verification → vulnerability persists.

**Direction:** Run a controlled experiment: give 20 CS students a codebase with 5 planted vulnerabilities. Group A receives AI verdicts only. Group B receives AI verdicts with explicit uncertainty quantification. Group C receives no AI assistance. Measure detection rate per group.

**Why it matters:** Overconfident AI tools may paradoxically reduce security by eliminating the independent verification that catches AI errors. Uncertainty communication may break the false confidence loop.

**Relevant context:** Human factors research documents automation bias broadly. Security-specific automation bias with AI vulnerability tools is not empirically studied.
