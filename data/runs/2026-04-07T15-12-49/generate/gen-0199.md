# Calibration Study: Do AI Security Confidence Scores Reflect Actual Exploitability?

**Problem:** AI vulnerability scanners produce confidence scores for their findings, but it is not known whether these scores correlate with actual exploitability (CVSSv3 exploitability subscore).

**Direction:** Run an AI vulnerability scanner on 40 CVEs with known CVSSv3 exploitability scores. Measure Spearman correlation between AI confidence score and CVSSv3 exploitability. Compute calibration curve. Test whether high-confidence AI findings are disproportionately high-exploitability.

**Why it matters:** Well-calibrated confidence scores enable rational prioritization. If AI is overconfident on low-exploitability findings and underconfident on high-exploitability ones, defenders are misinformed.

**Relevant context:** CVSSv3 exploitability metrics are published for most CVEs in NVD. AI scanner confidence calibration to exploitability not studied in the literature.
