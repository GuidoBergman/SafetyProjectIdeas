# Importing Structured Expert Elicitation Methods From Nuclear Risk Assessment to AI Cyber Capability Evaluation

**Problem:** Nuclear risk assessment developed Structured Expert Elicitation (SEE) methods (Delphi, probability elicitation, calibration training) for situations where empirical data is scarce. These methods are absent from AI cyber governance evaluation.

**Direction:** Study SEE methodology from nuclear and biological risk assessment literature. Design a 3-round Delphi study on 5 AI cyber capability threshold questions (e.g., 'When will an AI system be able to autonomously discover a novel 0-day in a major OS?'). Administer to 10 cybersecurity experts. Report calibrated probability distributions.

**Why it matters:** Current AI governance relies on binary threshold judgments without probability estimates. SEE methods would enable more nuanced, calibrated, and publicly verifiable capability forecasting.

**Relevant context:** Bengio et al. 2026 deep uncertainty; RAND SEE methodology; Kraprayoon governance window timing.
