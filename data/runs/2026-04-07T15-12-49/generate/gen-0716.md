# Measuring Monitor Degradation Under Distribution Shift: Will Yesterday's Monitor Work Tomorrow?

**Problem:** Agent capabilities and attack techniques evolve rapidly. Monitoring systems trained on current attack patterns may degrade under distribution shift as new attack techniques emerge.

**Direction:** Train a monitoring classifier on attacks from an earlier time period (using dated red-team datasets). Evaluate it against more recent attack patterns. Measure performance degradation. Characterize how quickly monitors decay and what update frequency is needed.

**Why it matters:** If monitors decay quickly, they require frequent retraining — a major maintenance burden. Quantifying this decay rate informs monitoring deployment lifecycle planning.

**Relevant context:** ML distribution shift literature; METR monitoring evaluation; evolving attack surfaces.
