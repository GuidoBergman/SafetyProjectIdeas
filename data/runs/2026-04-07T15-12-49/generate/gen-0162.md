# Dual-Use AI Safety Control Failure + Rapid Capability Diffusion = Democratized Offensive Capability

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) AI safety controls on vulnerability-finding capabilities can be bypassed (jailbreaks, fine-tuning); (2) capable vulnerability-finding AI is rapidly diffusing to smaller, less-resourced actors. Combined, offensive capabilities previously requiring nation-state resources become accessible to low-skill attackers.

**Direction:** Document the gap between official safety controls on AI vulnerability research tools (published by Anthropic, OpenAI) and demonstrated bypass techniques (from jailbreak literature). Measure: what fraction of safety controls have documented bypasses? What is the technical sophistication required for bypass? Estimate effective democratization rate.

**Why it matters:** The combination of capability diffusion and imperfect safety controls is the mechanism by which AI amplifies the offensive threat landscape. Quantifying the gap informs policy.

**Relevant context:** International AI Safety Report 2026: AI companies use safety testing but models can distinguish test from deployment. Jailbreak survey 2026 (TechRxiv): 90-99% jailbreak success on open-weight models. Capability diffusion through fine-tuning.
