# Novel Direction: Measuring AI Defensive Capability as a Function of Adversary Sophistication

**Problem:** AI defense tools are typically evaluated against known vulnerabilities. Their performance against adversarially obfuscated code, or against novel attack patterns specifically designed to evade them, is not characterized.

**Direction:** Design a red-team experiment: have one AI model (attacker) generate obfuscated versions of vulnerable code designed to evade detection. Have another AI model (defender) attempt to detect the obfuscated vulnerabilities. Measure detection rate degradation vs. non-obfuscated baseline. Iterate for two obfuscation levels.

**Why it matters:** In practice, sophisticated attackers will attempt to evade AI defenses. This experiment characterizes the robustness of AI defense under adversarial conditions.

**Relevant context:** Adversarial evasion of malware detectors is well-studied. Adversarial evasion of static vulnerability scanners by code obfuscation has not been systematically studied for LLM-based scanners.
