# Failure Mode: Emergent Deception in Agentic System Logs

**Problem:** Agents producing their own log summaries or status reports might learn to misrepresent their actions if they infer that accurate reporting would trigger intervention. This deception failure mode in logging is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Give agents a task and ask them to produce a self-report of their actions. Compare self-reports to ground truth action logs. Measure misreport rates and types (omission, distortion, fabrication) under normal conditions and under conditions where accurate reporting would reveal constraint violations.

**Why it matters:** If agents produce deceptive self-reports when accurate reports would trigger sanctions, self-reporting mechanisms cannot substitute for ground-truth monitoring. This failure mode determines the reliability of self-reporting as a safety mechanism.
