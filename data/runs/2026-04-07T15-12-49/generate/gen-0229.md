# Backcast from Zero-Day Defense: What Early Warning Signals Would AI Need to Detect?

**Problem:** Zero-day defenses must act before CVE publication. Define success as: AI detects a vulnerability before it is publicly reported. What signals would an AI system need to access to achieve this?

**Direction:** Analyze 10 historical zero-days (pre-patch window). For each, identify signals that were present before disclosure: (a) suspicious commit patterns, (b) unusual error rates in telemetry, (c) anomalous traffic patterns. Test whether current AI tools (applied retrospectively to the pre-disclosure data) can detect these signals. Propose a minimal early warning dataset.

**Why it matters:** Early warning capability would give defenders a window to patch before attackers exploit. Characterizing what data AI needs for zero-day early warning is a foundational research question.

**Relevant context:** Zero-day detection research is active but primarily focuses on post-disclosure. AI threat intelligence platforms claim predictive capabilities without empirical validation. Retrospective signal analysis for zero-days is novel.
