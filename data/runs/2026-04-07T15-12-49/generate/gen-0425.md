# Runtime Prompt Injection Detection via Behavioral Fingerprinting

**Problem:** Runtime supply chain attacks (Jiang et al. 2026) can inject malicious instructions into an agent's context at inference time. No lightweight behavioral fingerprinting approach exists to detect such injections without modifying the model.

**Direction:** Establish a behavioral baseline for an agent on benign tasks (action sequences, tool call patterns, output distributions). Inject adversarial prompts into retrieved context and measure the behavioral delta. Train a simple classifier (logistic regression or decision tree) to flag anomalous behavior patterns as potential injection events.

**Why it matters:** Behavioral fingerprinting is model-agnostic and deployable as a wrapper around any agent, making it a practical defense against runtime supply chain attacks.

**Relevant context:** Jiang et al. 2026 runtime supply chain attacks; METR 2026 monitoring vulnerabilities.
