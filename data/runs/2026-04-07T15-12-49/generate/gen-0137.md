# Characterizing Hallucination Rates in LLM-Generated CVE Explanations

**Problem:** LLMs are known to hallucinate. In the security context, hallucinated CVE IDs, affected versions, or remediation steps could lead defenders to incorrect actions.

**Direction:** Prompt three LLMs (Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini) with 50 real CVEs. Ask them to (a) describe the vulnerability, (b) name affected versions, (c) suggest patches. Cross-check factual claims against the NVD database. Compute hallucination rate per claim type and per model.

**Why it matters:** Defenders using AI-generated CVE summaries without verification may be misled. Quantifying this risk is safety-critical.

**Relevant context:** General LLM hallucination research is extensive. Security-specific hallucination rate benchmarking for CVE explanations is absent from the literature.
