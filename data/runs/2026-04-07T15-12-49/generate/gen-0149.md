**Direction:** Analyze public GitHub repositories with Dependabot or security scan alerts enabled. Measure: time-to-close for AI-flagged security issues vs. human-flagged issues, closed vs. ignored rate, reopen rate. Identify repository characteristics predicting faster remediation.
