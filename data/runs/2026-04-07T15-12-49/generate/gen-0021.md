# Longitudinal Benchmark Design: Protocol for Tracking AI Cyber Capability Over Time Without Saturation

**Problem:** Cybench went from 10% to 82% in 18 months, effectively saturating as a benchmark. No standardized methodology exists for creating benchmarks that remain valid over multi-year timescales as capabilities increase.

**Direction:** Design a benchmark construction protocol that addresses saturation: (1) tiered difficulty structure with escalating challenge classes, (2) an adaptive challenge generation pipeline that creates new challenges as existing tiers saturate, (3) an anchoring mechanism that maintains comparability across time points using IRT. Implement a small-scale version covering 3 difficulty tiers and validate that current models saturate tier 1 but not tier 3.

**Why it matters:** Without longitudinal benchmarks, the field cannot reliably track whether safety interventions are working or whether the rate of capability growth is changing. This is a foundational methodology gap identified across AISI, OCCULT, and SoK papers.

**Relevant context:** AISI noted no standardized longitudinal comparison methodology exists. Cybench 10%→82% saturation. CTFusion (OpenReview 2025) introduced streaming evaluation with live CTFs to avoid reuse. CAIBench meta-benchmark approach addresses some saturation issues but not longitudinal design.
