# Success State for Human-AI Collaborative Formal Verification: A Time-to-Verify Benchmark

**Problem:** Define a success state: a developer with no Lean expertise can formally verify a 50-line security-critical function in under 2 hours using AI assistance. Measure current baseline and identify the largest gap.

**Direction:** Recruit 10 CS students (no Lean experience). Each attempts to formally verify a 50-line security-critical function using available AI tools (Leanstral, Claude + VSCode Lean plugin). Measure time-to-proof, whether they succeed, and where they get stuck. Compare to the 2-hour success state.

**Why it matters:** Defining the success state in human terms (time, expertise required) makes the goal concrete and actionable. Current benchmarks measure AI capability in isolation; this measures the human-AI system's practical utility.

**Relevant context:** RAND's vision requires that formal verification become accessible to non-expert developers. No user study measures the time cost for a novice+AI to complete a real security verification task. This establishes the baseline gap.
