# Differential Refusal Analysis: Do Models Refuse More Readily for Novel vs. Well-Known Attack Techniques?

**Problem:** Models are likely trained on documented attack techniques and may refuse known techniques more consistently than emerging or novel variants they have less training signal on.

**Direction:** Construct two matched sets of 25 cybersecurity requests: one using attack techniques in the MITRE ATT&CK framework (well-documented), one using plausible but novel or thinly-documented technique variants. Compare refusal rates across 4 models.

**Why it matters:** If refusal is much weaker for novel techniques, the effective governance protection degrades precisely as adversaries innovate—a structural governance failure mode.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. Offensive Action Contribution; NIST AI 800-1 cybersecurity appendix; MITRE ATT&CK framework.
