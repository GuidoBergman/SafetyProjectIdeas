# Compounding Failure: Generalist Model Monoculture × Rapid CTF Saturation — Evaluation Validity Collapse

**Problem:** Two interacting failure modes: (1) most AI evaluations use the same few frontier models (GPT, Claude, Gemini), and (2) CTF benchmarks are saturating as these exact models are trained on internet data including CTF writeups. As models homogenize and benchmarks saturate simultaneously, the evaluation ecosystem loses the ability to distinguish capability from memorization.

**Direction:** Measure the correlation between model training data cutoff dates and solve rates on specific Cybench/NYU CTF challenges with publicly-dated writeups. Test whether challenges with writeups published before the training cutoff have significantly higher solve rates than challenges with writeups published after. Compute a 'contamination index' for each benchmark.

**Why it matters:** If both model diversity and benchmark validity are collapsing simultaneously, the entire evaluation ecosystem may be producing increasingly uninformative measurements. Early detection of this validity collapse would motivate urgent methodological reform before safety-critical evaluations become unreliable.

**Relevant context:** CAIBench noted CTF saturation (93% on Cybench). NYU CTF Bench addressed writeup reuse. Irregular 2026 inflection point in H2 2025. Models trained on internet data (training cutoff correlation with GitHub/CTF data). Mutation testing approach (earlier idea) addresses one component; this examines the structural compounding effect.
