# Voluntary Commitment Reliability: Do AI Labs Honor Their Own Governance Pledges Over Time?

**Problem:** AI labs make public commitments in safety frameworks (e.g., 'we will pause deployment if we reach ASL-4'), but whether these commitments are honored or quietly revised over time has not been tracked.

**Direction:** Collect dated governance commitments from Anthropic RSP versions 1-3 and OpenAI Preparedness Framework versions. Track specific commitments (evaluation triggers, deployment pause conditions, red team requirements) across versions. Identify commitments that were later softened, removed, or left untested.

**Why it matters:** If voluntary commitments are systematically weakened over time, they provide false assurance to policymakers and the public. This analysis makes commitment reliability empirically assessable.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic RSP versions; OpenAI Preparedness Framework; Bengio et al. 2026 on 12 companies with safety frameworks.
