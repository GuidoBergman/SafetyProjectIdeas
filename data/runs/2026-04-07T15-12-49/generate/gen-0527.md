# VeriSpec: A Benchmark for AI Specification Writing Quality in Formal Verification

**Problem:** Current benchmarks (VeriBench, CLEVER, VeriCoding) evaluate whether LLMs can complete a proof given a specification. No benchmark evaluates whether LLMs can write correct, non-trivial specifications from natural language requirements.

**Direction:** Create a benchmark of 80 natural language function requirements paired with expert-written formal specifications. LLMs must generate formal specs. Evaluate: (a) syntactic correctness, (b) semantic completeness against expert spec, (c) non-triviality (not vacuously true). Establish baseline metrics.

**Why it matters:** Specification writing is the hardest and most safety-critical part of formal verification. If LLMs cannot write correct specs, the entire verified code generation pipeline fails at its foundation.

**Relevant context:** ATLAS (POPL 2026) generates specs automatically but does not validate spec quality against intent. VeriEquivBench (arXiv 2510.06296) measures equivalence. Spec quality as a standalone evaluation is missing.
