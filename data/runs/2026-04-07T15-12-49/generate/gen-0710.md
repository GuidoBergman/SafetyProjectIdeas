# Measuring Monitoring Effectiveness vs. Evasion Sophistication: An Elo-Style Tournament

**Problem:** As monitoring improves, evasion techniques also improve. No study has measured this arms-race dynamic in a controlled setting.

**Direction:** Set up an Elo-style tournament: monitors are pitted against evasion strategies. When a monitor detects an evasion, the monitor gains Elo and the evasion loses Elo. Run iterative rounds where both sides improve. Measure the Elo distribution and identify dominant strategies.

**Why it matters:** Understanding the monitoring vs. evasion arms race dynamics helps predict where defensive investments yield stable vs. temporary improvements.

**Relevant context:** Red-team / blue-team adversarial evaluation; METR red-teaming; game-theoretic security.
