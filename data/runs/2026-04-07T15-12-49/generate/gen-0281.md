# Decompose-and-Defer: Mapping Multi-Turn Request Decomposition as a Refusal Bypass Vector

**Problem:** Attackers can decompose a single prohibited cyber request across multiple conversation turns, each innocuous alone, defeating turn-level refusal filters.

**Direction:** Build a dataset of 100+ multi-turn conversation chains where the combined intent would trigger refusal if posed in one turn. Measure whether GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, and Gemini 1.5 refuse the final synthesizing turn or any earlier turns. Compare refusal rates for atomic vs. decomposed requests.

**Why it matters:** Linder et al. 2026 identify request segmentation as an explicit open problem. Understanding empirically how often decomposition bypasses refusal is prerequisite to designing conversation-level governance.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. arXiv:2602.15689 5-dimension framework; GTG-1002 case where operators posed as legitimate defenders across interactions.
