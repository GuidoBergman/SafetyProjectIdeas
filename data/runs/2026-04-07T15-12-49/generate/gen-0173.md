# Experiment Variation: Effect of Vulnerability Age on AI Detection Performance

**Problem:** AI models are trained on data with a knowledge cutoff. Vulnerabilities discovered after the cutoff may be harder to detect. The relationship between CVE disclosure date and AI detection performance is not studied.

**Direction:** Select 30 CVEs: 10 pre-2022 (before typical LLM training), 10 from 2022-2024, 10 from 2025. Run the same LLM on all without providing CVE IDs. Measure detection rate by age cohort. Test whether newer vulnerabilities are systematically harder to detect.

**Why it matters:** If AI defenders cannot detect recent vulnerability patterns, the newest and most actively exploited vulnerabilities are precisely the ones AI defense fails on.

**Relevant context:** LLM knowledge cutoff effects are studied in general (benchmark contamination literature) but not specifically for vulnerability detection performance by CVE age.
