# Defense Evasion via Semantic Similarity Flooding: Degrading Monitoring Signal Quality

**Problem:** A sophisticated attacker could attempt to degrade monitoring signal quality by injecting large volumes of borderline-suspicious actions, causing alert fatigue or diluting the statistical baseline.

**Direction:** Simulate a 'signal flooding' attack: inject high volumes of borderline actions that each fall just below a monitoring threshold. Measure the effect on detection rates for genuinely dangerous actions that follow. Characterize the minimum flood volume needed to degrade detection.

**Why it matters:** Alert fatigue is a well-known problem in traditional security monitoring. Characterizing whether the same attack works on AI agent monitoring is critical for designing robust systems.

**Relevant context:** Traditional security alert fatigue; METR novel bypass vectors; statistical baseline manipulation.
