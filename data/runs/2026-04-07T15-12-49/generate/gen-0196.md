# Compounding Risk: AI Overconfidence + Missing Ground Truth = Undetectable Performance Degradation

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) AI security tools may be overconfident in their outputs (poor calibration); (2) defenders often lack ground truth to verify AI performance in production. The combination means performance can degrade undetected.

**Direction:** Design a monitoring protocol for AI security tool performance without ground truth: use proxy metrics (finding rate stability, explanation consistency across runs, agreement with independent re-analysis). Test the protocol on a simulated degradation scenario (vary model version or prompt). Measure whether the proxy metrics detect performance degradation.

**Why it matters:** Production AI defense tools with no monitoring mechanism may degrade silently. This is a safety-critical governance gap.

**Relevant context:** AI monitoring in production is a general ML ops challenge. Security-specific monitoring without ground truth is novel. Temperature/consistency experiments (see earlier ideas) provide methodological groundwork.
