# Dual-Use Governance Framework Readability Audit: Are Published Safety Frameworks Legible to Evaluators?

**Problem:** Governance frameworks are written for various audiences. Whether front-line evaluators (red teamers, researchers) can reliably extract actionable criteria from framework text is untested.

**Direction:** Recruit 10 CS students to read a governance framework excerpt (RSP cyber section, Preparedness Framework cyber tier). Ask them to extract 10 testable behavioral criteria. Measure precision/recall against expert-identified criteria. Compute readability scores (Flesch-Kincaid, etc.).

**Why it matters:** Frameworks that evaluators cannot operationalize are governance theater. Measuring legibility identifies which frameworks need rewriting for practical use.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic RSP; OpenAI Preparedness Framework; NIST AI 800-1; Bengio et al. evaluation gap.
