# Compounding Risks: Open-Weight Model Diffusion × Dual-Use Capability Proliferation — When Open Release Cannot Be Recalled

**Problem:** Open-weight models with meaningful cyber capabilities cannot be recalled once released. This compounds with dual-use risk because governance mechanisms (monitoring, access controls) are inapplicable post-release.

**Direction:** Map the current open-weight model landscape for cybersecurity capability levels using available benchmarks. For models exceeding a defined capability threshold, assess whether any post-release governance mechanisms are practically applicable (API monitoring: no; fine-tune restrictions: partial; usage guidance: weak). Quantify the governance coverage gap for open weights.

**Why it matters:** If a meaningful fraction of cyber-capable AI is ungovernable by design (open weights), governance frameworks focused on proprietary APIs may have significantly less coverage than assumed.

**Relevant context:** Bengio et al. 2026 open-weight challenges; NIST AI 800-1 open model appendix; Llama 3.1 70B cybersecurity capability.
