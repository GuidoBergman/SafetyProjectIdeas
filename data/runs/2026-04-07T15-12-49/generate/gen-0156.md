# AI Vulnerability Scanner + Immature Patching Pipeline = Unpatched Critical Vulnerabilities

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) AI detects many vulnerabilities, creating a large queue; (2) patch generation tools produce incorrect or incomplete patches. The combination overwhelms defenders and creates a false sense of progress.

**Direction:** Simulate a pipeline with high AI detection sensitivity (high recall) combined with low patch quality (measured from literature: AutoPatchBench results). Model the fraction of vulnerabilities that are correctly remediated vs. incorrectly closed. Analyze how the combined failure rate varies with each component's error rate.

**Why it matters:** Each component failure alone is manageable. Combined, they create a situation where the most dangerous vulnerabilities may be incorrectly marked as fixed.

**Relevant context:** DARPA AIxCC: 86% detection, 68% patch success. If detection and patching errors are independent, ~29% of found vulnerabilities are unpatched or incorrectly patched. This interaction is not analyzed in the literature.
