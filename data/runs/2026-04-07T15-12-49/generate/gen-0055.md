# ATT&CK Coverage Mapping: Which Techniques Have Never Been Tested Against AI Models?

**Problem:** MITRE ATT&CK has hundreds of sub-techniques. Published AI cyber evaluations collectively cover only a small fraction. A systematic mapping of which techniques have been evaluated, with what results, and which remain entirely unstudied would reveal the darkest blind spots.

**Direction:** Review published AI cyber evaluation papers (AISI, DeepMind, OCCULT, Cybench, NYU CTF Bench, CAIBench) and map each challenge to its corresponding ATT&CK technique. Build a coverage matrix. Identify techniques with zero published evaluations. For the top 5 uncovered high-severity techniques, design minimal test challenges. Run one frontier model on these uncovered techniques.

**Why it matters:** Unknown capability is worse than known capability. Techniques that have never been evaluated may represent either untapped attack vectors or genuine AI weaknesses. Systematic coverage mapping is a prerequisite for any claim that the evaluation landscape is comprehensive.

**Relevant context:** DeepMind used ATT&CK-aligned analysis (arxiv 2503.11917). OCCULT aligns to ATT&CK tactics. AISI mapped to ATT&CK phases. CAIBench covers 10,000+ instances but does not provide ATT&CK coverage analysis. This synthesis study is feasible with public papers and MITRE ATT&CK Navigator.
