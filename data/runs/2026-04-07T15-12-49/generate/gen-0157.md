# AI Over-Reliance + Adversarial Evasion = Systematic Blind Spots

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) defenders over-rely on AI security tools (automation bias); (2) attackers can evade AI scanners through code obfuscation or adversarial perturbations. The combination creates systematic blind spots that defenders do not even know to check.

**Direction:** Demonstrate the compound risk: (a) show that a public AI vulnerability scanner can be evaded by simple obfuscation (rename variables, split functions); (b) run a user study showing that when AI says 'no vulnerabilities found,' users perform fewer manual checks. Quantify the combined risk.

**Why it matters:** Neither failure alone is catastrophic. The compound failure — defenders trust the AI, attackers know how to evade it — is highly dangerous and has not been empirically demonstrated.

**Relevant context:** Automation bias literature (general AI). AI scanner evasion through adversarial perturbations is studied for malware detection but not for static vulnerability scanning.
