# Formal Methods Blind Spot: Which Lean 4 Tactic Categories Are LLMs Worst At?

**Problem:** Lean 4 proofs use diverse tactics (simp, omega, ring, induction, cases, apply, exact). Whether LLMs have systematic weaknesses in specific tactic categories — producing characteristic failure patterns — is not characterized.

**Direction:** Classify 200 LLM Lean 4 proof failures by which tactic was needed at the point of failure. Compute failure rates per tactic category. Identify the top 3 tactic categories where LLMs fail most. Compare across models to determine whether weaknesses are universal or model-specific.

**Why it matters:** Tactic-level failure analysis enables targeted training data curation (add more examples of hard tactics) and prompting improvements (hint about which tactic to use). This is the most actionable form of failure characterization.

**Relevant context:** Lean 4 has a rich tactic system. Human Lean experts know that omega (arithmetic), ring (ring equations), and induction (recursive structures) have different difficulty levels. Whether LLMs match human difficulty perception is unknown.
