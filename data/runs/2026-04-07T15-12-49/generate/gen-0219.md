# Novel Direction: AI-Assisted Reverse Engineering for Malware Defense

**Problem:** Malware analysis requires reverse engineering binary code, which is time-consuming for human analysts. AI-assisted reverse engineering tools could accelerate malware defense, but their accuracy is not well characterized.

**Direction:** Take 10 publicly available malware samples (from VirusTotal public corpus or academic malware datasets). Use an LLM to analyze disassembly output (from Ghidra) and generate: (a) functional description, (b) C2 communication pattern, (c) persistence mechanism. Compare to ground-truth analysis from published malware reports. Measure accuracy.

**Why it matters:** Faster malware analysis enables faster defensive response. If AI can accelerate the reverse engineering step, incident response timelines shrink significantly.

**Relevant context:** AI-augmented SOC platforms integrate malware analysis. CyberSOCEval (CrowdStrike/Meta) includes malware analysis tasks. LLM analysis of disassembly output specifically not benchmarked against ground truth.
