# CyberGovBench: A Benchmark for Evaluating AI Governance Frameworks Against Dual-Use Scenarios

**Problem:** No benchmark exists to evaluate the quality and coverage of AI governance frameworks (RSP, Preparedness Framework, EU GPAI Code) against a standardized set of dual-use cybersecurity scenarios.

**Direction:** Build a 100-scenario benchmark covering: (a) novel malware generation, (b) reconnaissance automation, (c) vulnerability discovery, (d) credential harvesting, (e) disinformation-supported intrusions. Score each published governance framework against each scenario for coverage, specificity, and testability.

**Why it matters:** Governance frameworks are currently evaluated qualitatively by reading. A structured benchmark would enable comparative, reproducible assessment and reveal coverage gaps.

**Relevant context:** Sarma & Fisher RAND on benchmarks as governance; Linder et al. framework analysis methodology; NIST AI 800-1.
