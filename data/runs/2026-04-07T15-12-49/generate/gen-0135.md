# False Confidence in AI Security Reports: How Often Are Explanations Wrong?

**Problem:** AI vulnerability scanners generate natural-language explanations alongside findings. These explanations may be factually wrong even when the finding is correct, creating false confidence for defenders.

**Direction:** Collect AI-generated vulnerability reports from three LLMs for 30 known CVEs. Have human raters evaluate explanation quality on: correct root cause, correct affected code location, correct remediation. Compute accuracy rate of explanations vs. ground truth CVE descriptions.

**Why it matters:** A defender acting on a wrong explanation may patch the wrong thing or not understand the attack surface. This is a safety-relevant failure mode.

**Relevant context:** MDPI AI vulnerability detection review (2025) notes LLMs have poor F1 (0.16) for correctly identifying vulnerability types despite high detection rates. Explanation quality is separate from detection accuracy.
