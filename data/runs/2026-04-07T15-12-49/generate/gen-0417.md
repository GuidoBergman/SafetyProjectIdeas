# Audit of Inconsistency: Do Frontier Models Apply Different Standards to Structurally Identical Requests About Different Attack Targets?

**Problem:** Models may apply different refusal standards depending on who the alleged target is (individual vs. organization vs. government) even when the requested capability is technically identical.

**Direction:** Design 20 cybersecurity requests. Create matched variants targeting: (a) fictional individual, (b) small business, (c) critical infrastructure, (d) foreign government, (e) domestic government. Measure refusal rate differences by target type holding request content constant.

**Why it matters:** Target-dependent refusal creates systematic governance inconsistencies. Attackers can exploit by selecting target descriptions that lower refusal rates. Documenting this pattern informs refusal policy design.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. Offensive Risk dimension; NIST AI 800-1 national security provisions; GTG-1002 targeting government organizations.
