# CSLib Completion Acceleration: Does AI Assistance Measurably Speed Up New CSLib Theorem Additions?

**Problem:** CSLib is being developed manually. Whether AI assistance (LLM-generated proof drafts reviewed by humans) measurably accelerates CSLib theorem entry development vs. human-only development is untested.

**Direction:** Recruit 6 graduate students for a within-subject experiment. Each adds 5 new CSLib-style theorems to Lean 4: (a) fully manually, (b) using LLM-generated proof drafts. Measure time, number of Lean compiler errors, and final proof quality. Compare conditions.
