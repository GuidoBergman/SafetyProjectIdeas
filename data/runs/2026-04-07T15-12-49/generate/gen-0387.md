# Stress Testing Whether Linder et al.'s Offline Framework Degrades Under Real-Time Attacker Adaptation

**Problem:** Linder et al.'s framework is designed for policy design at rest. Whether models applying this framework in real-time can be fooled by an attacker who knows the framework dimensions and optimizes against them is untested.

**Direction:** Train 5 adversarial prompt variants specifically designed to score low on Linder et al.'s Offensive Risk and Offensive Action Contribution dimensions while maximizing actual harmful content. Test success rates against models on a borderline cybersecurity task set. Compare to non-optimized baseline prompts.

**Why it matters:** If adversaries can game the 5-dimension framework's observable signals, the framework is gameable by informed attackers—a critical governance robustness failure.
