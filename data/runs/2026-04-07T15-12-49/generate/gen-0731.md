# Evaluating Monitoring Interpretability: Can Monitors Explain Why They Flagged an Action?

**Problem:** LLM-based monitors often provide binary or numeric flags without explanations. For human oversight to be meaningful, monitors should explain their flagging decisions in terms operators can understand.

**Direction:** Compare monitoring systems with and without explanation generation. For flagged actions, measure human annotator agreement with monitor decisions when given explanations vs. just a flag. Measure whether explanation quality correlates with flagging accuracy.

**Why it matters:** Human-in-the-loop monitoring requires humans to make informed decisions based on monitor outputs. If explanations are incorrect or unhelpful, human oversight is degraded.

**Relevant context:** Human-in-the-loop AI monitoring; XAI (explainable AI); Anthropic detection classifiers.
