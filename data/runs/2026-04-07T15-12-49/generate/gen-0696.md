# Honeypot Effectiveness Across Attack Sophistication Levels: Do Smarter Agents Avoid Honeypots?

**Problem:** LLM agents used in cyberattacks vary in capability. It is unknown whether more capable agents (better reasoning) are more likely to recognize and avoid honeypot traps than less capable agents.

**Direction:** Deploy honeypot tasks (fake high-value targets with canary tokens) in an agent environment. Test agents of varying capability levels (small vs. large LLMs, with and without chain-of-thought). Measure honeypot interaction rates across capability levels.

**Why it matters:** If smarter agents systematically avoid honeypots, this undermines honeypot-based detection precisely for the highest-capability threats — the ones most dangerous.

**Relevant context:** LLM Agent Honeypot paper; OpenAI capability threshold; detection-evasion scaling.
