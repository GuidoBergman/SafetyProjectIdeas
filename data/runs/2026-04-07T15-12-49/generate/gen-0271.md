# Compounding Risk: AI Security Tool Hallucination + Automated Firewall Rule Generation = Incorrect Network Defense

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) AI threat intelligence tools hallucinate attack signatures or indicator-of-compromise details; (2) these hallucinated indicators are fed to automated firewall rule generators. Combined, legitimate traffic is blocked and real attacks may be missed.

**Direction:** Design a simulation: AI generates firewall rules from a threat intelligence summary that contains embedded hallucinations. Measure: what fraction of AI-generated firewall rules (a) block legitimate traffic incorrectly, (b) miss the actual threat. Compare to rules generated from accurate threat intelligence. Quantify the compound error rate.

**Why it matters:** Automated firewall rule generation from AI threat intelligence is deployed in production (SOAR platforms). The compound failure mode of hallucination → incorrect rule → availability impact has concrete safety implications.

**Relevant context:** AI threat intelligence hallucination (see CVE hallucination idea). SOAR platforms with automated response (IBM QRadar SOAR, Splunk SOAR). Automated firewall rule generation quality not studied with hallucinated inputs.
