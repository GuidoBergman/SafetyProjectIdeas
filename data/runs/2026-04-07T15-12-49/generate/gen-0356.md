# Measuring Over-Refusal Rates: How Often Do Models Refuse Legitimate Security Research Requests?

**Problem:** Governance frameworks optimize to prevent misuse but may over-restrict legitimate defenders. The over-refusal rate for bona fide security research tasks is not empirically characterized.

**Direction:** Construct 80 prompts representing clearly legitimate security research tasks (analyzing CVE PoCs, academic malware analysis, CTF challenge solving). Test refusal rates across 4 models. Classify false positive types. Compare over-refusal rates against misuse literature estimates of actual bypass rates to assess calibration.

**Why it matters:** Over-refusal is a governance failure of the opposite type from under-refusal. Both must be quantified for policy calibration. Defenders paying a high over-refusal tax undermines voluntary governance compliance.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. Defensive Benefit and Expected Legitimate Frequency dimensions; OpenAI $10M defense grant premise; NIST AI 800-1 false positive guidance.
