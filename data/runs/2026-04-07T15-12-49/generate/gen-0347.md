# Trust Decay Measurement: How Does a Model's Willingness to Help With Security Tasks Degrade Over a Long Conversation?

**Problem:** Single-turn refusal testing misses the dynamic trust evolution that occurs in multi-session interactions. Whether models become more or less restrictive over a long adversarial conversation is unknown.

**Direction:** Design 10 adversarial conversation scripts where each turn incrementally escalates the cybersecurity request severity. Measure at which turn refusal occurs, whether earlier contextual signals of adversarial intent affect refusal on later benign turns, and whether helpfulness for legitimate requests decreases in adversarially flagged sessions.

**Why it matters:** Governance frameworks assume static per-turn refusal policies. If models develop intra-session trust states, the governance surface is dynamic and harder to specify.

**Relevant context:** GTG-1002 multi-turn social engineering; Linder et al. multi-turn decomposition attack vector; Linder et al. 5-dimension framework.
