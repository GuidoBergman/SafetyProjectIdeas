# Backcasting from Transparent AI Cyber Incident Reporting: What Disclosure Framework Is Needed?

**Problem:** Anthropic voluntarily published the GTG-1002 and vibe hacking reports, but no standardized disclosure framework governs what information labs must report when AI misuse incidents occur.

**Direction:** Define the ideal state: a standardized, mandatory AI cyber incident report comparable to SEC cybersecurity incident disclosure rules (4-business-day reporting). Work backward: what incident categories trigger reporting? What information must be disclosed? Draft a model AI Cyber Incident Disclosure Framework. Compare against current voluntary practices.

**Why it matters:** Without mandatory disclosure frameworks, the empirical record of AI misuse remains dependent on voluntary lab transparency, which is insufficient for governance learning.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic GTG-1002 and vibe hacking voluntary disclosures; SEC cybersecurity disclosure rule; EU AI Act incident reporting (72-hour systemic risk provision).
