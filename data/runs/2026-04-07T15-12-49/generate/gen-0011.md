# Cross-Architecture Comparison: Do Safety Fine-Tuning Differences Translate to Capability Differences on Offensive Tasks?

**Problem:** Models with identical capability levels but different safety fine-tuning approaches may show different profiles on offensive cyber tasks. The relationship between RLHF/Constitutional AI alignment techniques and actual offensive capability expression is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Select matched pairs of models: (1) a base model and its RLHF/instruction-tuned version, (2) a model with Constitutional AI training vs. without. Run both on identical CTF challenge sets with identical prompting. Measure not just solve rates but also refusal rates, partial completion quality, and whether safety fine-tuning creates systematic capability gaps in specific ATT&CK categories.
