# Comparing How AI Labs Describe Their Own Cyber Dual-Use Failure Modes in Risk Reports

**Problem:** Anthropic, OpenAI, and other labs publish risk reports describing their own failure modes. Whether their self-described failure modes match the failure modes identified by independent researchers is an accountability indicator.

**Direction:** Collect cybersecurity failure mode descriptions from 4 labs' risk reports/safety cards. Collect failure modes identified by independent researchers (Linder et al., Potter et al., IAPS). Measure overlap and gaps. Identify failure modes discussed by researchers but absent from lab self-disclosures.

**Why it matters:** Systematic gaps between self-reported and researcher-identified failure modes reveal either blind spots or selective disclosure—both are governance concerns requiring different interventions.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Feb 2026 Risk Report; OpenAI Preparedness Framework; Linder et al.; Potter et al.; GTG-1002 report.
