# Multi-Turn Context Poisoning: Detecting Gradual Instruction Corruption Across Conversation Turns

**Problem:** An attacker could gradually corrupt an agent's context across many turns, with no single turn triggering a threshold. No study has tested whether monitors can detect this multi-turn accumulation attack.

**Direction:** Design a multi-turn context poisoning attack: spread malicious instruction fragments across 10-20 turns of conversation. Test whether action-level monitors (which see one turn at a time) and trajectory-level monitors (which see the full context) differ in detection success.

**Why it matters:** Multi-turn attacks are specifically designed to evade per-turn monitoring. This study identifies whether trajectory-level monitoring provides essential additional coverage.

**Relevant context:** Prompt injection literature; long-context monitoring; METR bypass vectors.
