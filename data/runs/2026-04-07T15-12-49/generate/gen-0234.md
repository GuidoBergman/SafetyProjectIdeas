# Tool Gap: A Risk-Stratified Evaluation Dataset for AI Vulnerability Detection

**Problem:** Existing vulnerability detection benchmarks mix low-severity and high-severity vulnerabilities. A risk-stratified benchmark (where performance on critical vulnerabilities is separately reported) does not exist.

**Direction:** Curate a benchmark of 60 vulnerabilities: 20 critical (CVSS ≥ 9.0), 20 high (7.0-8.9), 20 medium (4.0-6.9). Run three AI scanners on all 60. Report detection rate, FPR, and explanation quality separately by severity tier. Compute the 'critical coverage gap' (difference between overall and critical-severity detection rate).

**Why it matters:** A scanner with 80% overall detection rate may only catch 50% of critical vulnerabilities. Risk-stratified benchmarking reveals this gap and motivates targeted improvement on high-severity cases.

**Relevant context:** Mozilla: 14 high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities. CVSS severity stratification is standard. No risk-stratified AI vulnerability detection benchmark found.
