# Replicating Linder et al. Refusal Framework on Open-Weight Models: Does the 5-Dimension Structure Hold?

**Problem:** Linder et al. developed their framework analyzing primarily frontier closed-weight models. Whether the same dimensional structure predicts refusal in open-weight models is unknown.

**Direction:** Apply Linder et al.'s 5 dimensions to a standardized prompt set. Test refusal rates across Llama 3.1 70B Instruct, Mistral 7B Instruct, and Phi-3 Medium. Compare predictive structure to results for closed models. Measure whether dimensions have different weights by model family.
