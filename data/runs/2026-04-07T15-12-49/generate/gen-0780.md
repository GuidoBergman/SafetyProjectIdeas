# Evaluating the Effectiveness of Kill Switch Mechanisms: How Quickly Can an Agent Be Stopped?

**Problem:** Kill switch mechanisms (human ability to stop agent operation) are a fundamental control mechanism. Their effectiveness — latency from decision to full agent stop — has not been empirically characterized.

**Direction:** Implement kill switch mechanisms at different architectural levels (API-level, process-level, network-level). Simulate a detected compromise and trigger the kill switch. Measure kill switch latency (decision to full stop), side effects (partial action completion), and evasion resistance.

**Why it matters:** Kill switch effectiveness is a concrete safety property. If kill switch latency is too high, agents may complete dangerous actions before being stopped. This characterization directly informs deployment architecture decisions.

**Relevant context:** AI control literature; kill switch design; agent containment; shutdown problem.
