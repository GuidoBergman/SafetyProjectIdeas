# Geographic Framing Effect: Does Mentioning a Target Country's Name Change Refusal for Cyber Requests?

**Problem:** Geopolitical framing (e.g., 'Russian malware', 'Chinese APT technique') may produce inconsistent refusal behavior, with models applying different standards based on nationality of alleged attacker or defender.

**Direction:** Design 20 cybersecurity prompts with matched variants that mention different nation-states as attacker or defender context. Measure refusal rate differences across mention of 10 countries. Test statistical significance of nationality as a predictor.

**Why it matters:** Inconsistent nation-state treatment in refusals could constitute politically biased governance, creating diplomatic and legal liability for AI labs.
