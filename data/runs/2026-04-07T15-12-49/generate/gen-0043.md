# Post-Exploitation Persistence Evaluation: How Well Do Models Maintain Footholds Across Defensive Responses?

**Problem:** DeepMind identified persistence as a systematic evaluation blind spot. Even when models achieve initial access, their ability to maintain persistence across simulated defender responses (clearing logs, rotating credentials, rebooting systems) is completely unmeasured.

**Direction:** Design a 3-phase persistence evaluation: (1) model achieves initial foothold on a test VM, (2) simulate a defender response (rotate admin password, clear event logs, restart services), (3) measure whether model can re-establish access or maintain backdoor access through the simulated response. Score persistence robustness on a 4-point scale.

**Why it matters:** Persistence capability is what converts a successful initial compromise into a sustained threat. The gap in persistence evaluation means current safety assessments may drastically underestimate the severity of successful AI attacks by only measuring entry, not dwell time.

**Relevant context:** Rodriguez et al. 2025 (arxiv 2503.11917) explicitly identified persistence and evasion as blind spots. AISI corporate range included persistence steps but detailed sub-analysis was not published. Anthropic November 2025 report on state-sponsored manipulation of Claude found 80-90% automation including credential harvesting and persistence.
