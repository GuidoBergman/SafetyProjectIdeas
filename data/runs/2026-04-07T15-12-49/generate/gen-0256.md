# Novel Direction: AI-Assisted Triage of Bug Bounty Reports

**Problem:** Bug bounty programs receive large volumes of reports with varying quality and severity. AI-assisted triage could reduce the time from submission to validation, but the accuracy of AI triage for bug bounty reports is not characterized.

**Direction:** Collect 50 public bug bounty reports (from HackerOne disclosed reports). Have an LLM triage each: assign severity, assess exploitability, identify duplicate status. Compare AI triage to the actual final disposition of each report. Measure: severity accuracy, duplicate detection rate, false positives (AI marking non-vulnerabilities as vulnerabilities).

**Why it matters:** Faster bug bounty triage means faster patching of reported vulnerabilities. Inaccurate AI triage may dismiss real vulnerabilities or create wasted analyst effort.

**Relevant context:** Bug bounty programs receive thousands of reports (HackerOne public statistics). AI-assisted triage tools are commercially proposed but accuracy on real bug bounty reports not benchmarked.
