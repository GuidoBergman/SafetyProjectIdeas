# Measuring the Governance Signal-to-Noise Ratio: How Much of Governance Framework Text Provides Actionable Behavioral Criteria?

**Problem:** AI governance frameworks may contain significant aspirational or rhetorical content alongside actionable behavioral criteria. The ratio of actionable to aspirational content has not been measured.

**Direction:** Analyze cybersecurity sections of 5 governance frameworks. Code each sentence as: (a) actionable/testable behavioral criterion, (b) vague/aspirational principle, (c) contextual/explanatory, (d) definitional. Compute the actionable content fraction per framework. Rank by governance specificity.

**Why it matters:** Frameworks with low actionable content fraction provide less governance value per page regardless of rhetorical sophistication. This metric creates accountability for governance specificity.

**Relevant context:** NIST AI 800-1; Anthropic RSP; OpenAI Preparedness Framework; Bengio et al. evaluation gap.
