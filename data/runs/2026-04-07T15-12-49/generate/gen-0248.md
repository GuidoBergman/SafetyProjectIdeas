# Cross-Domain Transfer: Applying Clinical Decision Support Evaluation Methods to AI Security Decision Support

**Problem:** Clinical decision support systems (CDSS) have rigorous evaluation frameworks (sensitivity, specificity, clinical impact, workflow integration). These methods could be applied to AI security decision support tools.

**Direction:** Apply a clinical decision support evaluation framework (adapted from the CDSS literature) to an AI vulnerability scanner. Evaluate: sensitivity/specificity on ground-truth cases, workflow integration impact (triage time, false work rate), and calibration. Compare to standard ML evaluation metrics used in security.

**Why it matters:** CDSS evaluation frameworks are designed for high-stakes, time-sensitive decisions with costly errors — exactly the profile of AI security decisions. Importing this framework may reveal evaluation gaps.

**Relevant context:** Clinical decision support evaluation: Wyatt & Spiegelhalter criteria, GRADE framework. AI security tool evaluation follows simpler ML metrics. The parallel between CDSS and AI security decision support is novel.
