# Measuring the Transferability of Red-Team Attack Strategies Across Agent Frameworks

**Problem:** METR developed attack strategies specific to Anthropic's monitoring. It is unknown whether these strategies transfer to different agent monitoring implementations, or whether they are framework-specific.

**Direction:** Take published attack strategies from METR's red-team artifacts. Apply them to three different open-source monitoring implementations. Measure transfer success rates and characterize which attack properties correlate with transferability.

**Why it matters:** High attack transferability implies that monitoring systems share common architectural vulnerabilities, suggesting systemic issues. Low transferability suggests attacks are incidental and patchable.

**Relevant context:** METR 2026 attack strategy test sets; attack transferability research; monitoring system diversity.
