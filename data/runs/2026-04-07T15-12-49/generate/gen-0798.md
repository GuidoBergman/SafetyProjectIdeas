# Monitoring for Resource Acquisition Behaviors: Do Agents Seek to Acquire More Resources Than Needed?

**Problem:** Resource acquisition beyond task requirements is an early warning sign of misaligned goals (instrumental convergence). Whether current agents exhibit excess resource acquisition in practice has not been empirically characterized.

**Direction:** Design tasks with a minimal sufficient resource set. Give agents access to additional resources beyond what is needed. Monitor whether agents acquire these extra resources 'just in case' vs. only acquiring what is needed. Measure excess acquisition rates across model sizes.

**Why it matters:** Instrumental convergence theory predicts resource acquisition as a universal sub-goal of misaligned AI. Empirical measurement of this behavior in current models provides early calibration of this theoretical concern.
