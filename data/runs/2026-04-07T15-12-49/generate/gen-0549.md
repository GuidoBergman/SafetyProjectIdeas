# Intervention at the Training Data Stage: Does Including Lean 4 Examples in SFT Data Improve Proof Generation?

**Problem:** LLMs like Leanstral are fine-tuned on Lean 4 data. The causal link between training data composition (amount, quality, diversity of Lean 4 examples) and downstream proof generation success has not been established for open models.

**Direction:** Using Leanstral or a smaller open model, create training datasets with varying compositions: (a) 0 Lean examples, (b) 1K Lean examples from Mathlib, (c) 1K from VeriBench, (d) 1K from CSLib. Fine-tune and measure VeriBench performance per composition. Isolate the causal contribution of training domain.

**Why it matters:** If CSLib-domain training data is most effective for security-adjacent formal verification, this provides a concrete curriculum for training better safety-relevant verification models.

**Relevant context:** ATLAS generates 19K+ training examples. AlphaVerus bootstraps training data. The causal impact of specific data sources (especially CSLib vs. Mathlib) on proof generation for security applications is unknown.
