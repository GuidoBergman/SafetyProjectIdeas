# Causal Intervention on Proof Hint Injection: At Which Proof Step Does Adding a Hint Most Help?

**Problem:** Lean 4 proofs can be guided by human-provided lemma hints (sorry placeholders, partial proofs). Where in the proof search process a hint has the most impact (early, middle, late) is not characterized.

**Direction:** Take 30 Lean 4 problems that LLMs fail to prove. Inject correct hints at different points: (a) first step only, (b) key lemma only, (c) last step only, (d) full proof scaffold. Measure which hint placement most reliably unlocks successful proof completion. Model as a causal graph.

**Why it matters:** Understanding where humans can most efficiently assist LLM proof search identifies the minimal human intervention required. If a single key lemma hint reduces failure rate by 50%, human-AI collaboration becomes much more tractable.

**Relevant context:** Lean 4's tactic system allows partial proofs (with sorry placeholders). The Self-Optimizing Trace Agent does not accept human hints. Human-in-the-loop formal verification is under-studied compared to fully automated approaches.
