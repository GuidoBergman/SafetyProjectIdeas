# Pilot Study: Third-Party Red-Teaming of an Open-Source Agent Monitoring Implementation

**Problem:** Third-party adversarial evaluation of AI safety infrastructure is nascent. Demonstrating that third-party evaluation of open-source monitoring systems is feasible and valuable would help establish this practice.

**Direction:** Select a popular open-source agent monitoring tool. Conduct a structured red-team engagement (without internal access). Document what vulnerabilities are discoverable from external position alone. Compare to what would be discoverable with internal access.

**Why it matters:** This directly addresses the 'third-party evaluation is nascent' open problem. A concrete demonstration of the methodology and its limits would advance the field.
