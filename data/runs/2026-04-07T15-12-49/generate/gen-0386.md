# Measuring AI Lab Incident Response Lag: Time From Misuse Report to Public Acknowledgment

**Problem:** When AI misuse occurs, the time between an external report to the lab and public acknowledgment/remediation is a governance accountability metric that has not been systematically measured.

**Direction:** Collect all publicly documented AI misuse reports where the external reporting timeline is known (GTG-1002, vibe hacking, jailbreak disclosures). Measure time from first external report to: (a) lab acknowledgment, (b) public disclosure, (c) claimed behavioral change. Compare across labs. Compare to software VDP SLA benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** Incident response speed is a core governance accountability metric. Benchmarking it against software VDP standards creates a governance quality bar and public accountability mechanism.

**Relevant context:** GTG-1002 Anthropic response; vibe hacking response; NCSC AI safeguard bypass VDP recommendations.
