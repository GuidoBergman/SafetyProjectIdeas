# Testing the Governance Effectiveness of AI Labs' Safe Messaging Guidelines for Cybersecurity

**Problem:** AI labs develop safe messaging guidelines for sensitive content categories. Whether comparable structured guidelines exist for cybersecurity dual-use content—and whether they reduce harm while preserving defensive value—is unknown.

**Direction:** Identify whether Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind have published explicit safe messaging guidelines for cybersecurity (analogous to suicide/self-harm guidelines). Test whether their absence or presence correlates with refusal calibration on a standardized test set. Propose a structured safe messaging guideline for cybersecurity.

**Why it matters:** Safe messaging guidelines are a proven governance mechanism in mental health contexts that structure dual-use risks (information that helps some users and harms others). Their cybersecurity equivalent could be the most actionable near-term governance improvement.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. framework; Anthropic usage policies; OpenAI usage policies; NIST AI 800-1 cybersecurity appendix.
