# Measuring Information Leakage Through Agent Responses: Can Monitors Detect Side-Channel Data Exfiltration?

**Problem:** An agent might leak sensitive information not through direct exfiltration but through response timing, verbosity patterns, or content that encodes information. Standard content monitors may miss these side-channel leakages.

**Direction:** Design side-channel exfiltration scenarios (encoding secrets in response timing, sentence length, or word choice patterns). Measure whether content-based monitors detect these exfiltrations. Develop non-content-based detection methods.

**Why it matters:** Side-channel exfiltration is specifically designed to evade content-based monitoring. If effective, it represents an unaddressed class of monitoring blind spots.

**Relevant context:** Side-channel analysis; information leakage; monitoring evasion; covert channels.
