# Supply Chain Vulnerability + AI Scanner Blind Spot = Cascading Infrastructure Risk

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) supply chain vulnerabilities (libraries, AI frameworks) spread across many dependents before detection; (2) AI scanners have low detection rates for dependency-chain vulnerabilities specifically. Combined, critical infrastructure may be exposed for extended periods.

**Direction:** Select 10 historical supply chain CVEs (e.g., Log4Shell, XZ Utils). Test whether current AI vulnerability scanners detect them in downstream dependent code (not in the source library itself). Measure detection rate for transitive vs. direct vulnerabilities. Quantify the additional attack window from this compound failure.

**Why it matters:** Supply chain attacks doubled in 2025. If AI defense tools are blind to transitive vulnerabilities, the most dangerous class of supply chain attack is precisely the one AI cannot defend against.

**Relevant context:** Supply chain attacks: LiteLLM compromise (Trend Micro 2026), OWASP Top 10:2025 A03. AI scanner performance on transitive dependency vulnerabilities is not characterized.
