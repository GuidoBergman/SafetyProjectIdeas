# Measuring the Safety-Utility Tradeoff of Increasing Monitor Sensitivity

**Problem:** More sensitive monitors catch more attacks but also restrict more benign actions. The safety-utility tradeoff curve for monitoring sensitivity has not been empirically characterized.

**Direction:** Vary the decision threshold of a monitoring classifier across a wide range. Measure safety (attack detection rate) and utility (benign task completion rate) at each threshold. Plot the safety-utility Pareto frontier. Identify threshold recommendations for different risk tolerances.

**Why it matters:** Deployers need to set monitoring sensitivity based on their specific risk tolerance. The safety-utility curve provides the empirical foundation for this decision.

**Relevant context:** Safety-capability tradeoff research; monitoring threshold calibration; METR evaluation.
