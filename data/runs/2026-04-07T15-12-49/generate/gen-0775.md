# Compounding Risk: Overconfident Agent + Weak Monitoring — Do Confident Agents Bypass Monitoring More Effectively?

**Problem:** Models that express high confidence in their outputs may be more persuasive to LLM-based monitors. Overconfident dangerous assertions might be more likely to pass monitoring than uncertain ones.

**Direction:** Construct dangerous agent actions expressed with varying confidence levels (hedged, neutral, assertive, overconfident). Test whether monitoring classifiers are systematically more likely to pass overconfident dangerous statements. Measure the confidence-evasion correlation.

**Why it matters:** If monitors are systematically fooled by confident language, attackers can trivially improve bypass rates by adding confident framing — a low-cost, high-impact evasion technique.

**Relevant context:** LLM confidence calibration; sycophancy; monitoring evasion; compounding risk.
