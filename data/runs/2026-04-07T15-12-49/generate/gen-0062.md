# Economics of AI-Assisted Hacking: Cost Structure Analysis for Different Attack Profiles

**Problem:** The economics of AI-assisted attacks (cost per successful compromise) determine at what scale and by which threat actors AI becomes viable. The NCSC's £65 figure needs disaggregation: is this the average across all attack types, or is it heavily skewed by easy attacks pulling down the cost of hard ones?

**Direction:** Analyze publicly available pricing data and performance benchmarks to compute cost-per-successful-compromise for 5 attack categories (web application, Active Directory, social engineering, binary exploitation, ICS). Build a cost breakdown model: API costs + scaffolding overhead + retry costs (given stochastic success rates). Compare costs to human expert rates in each category.

**Why it matters:** If AI is cost-competitive with human experts in some attack categories but not others, this creates predictable patterns in where AI-assisted attacks will actually appear first. Cost analysis is fundamental to threat modeling for defenders and informs resource allocation for AI safety evaluation.
