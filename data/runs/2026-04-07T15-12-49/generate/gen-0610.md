# Beyond Compilation Rate: What Other Metrics Should We Track for AI Formal Verification Progress?

**Problem:** Compilation rate (the primary VeriBench metric) does not capture: proof quality, spec completeness, human review time, runtime behavior. A multi-dimensional metric suite for AI formal verification progress does not exist.

**Direction:** Propose and operationalize 5 additional metrics beyond compilation rate: (1) spec completeness score, (2) proof minimality ratio, (3) vacuity rate, (4) human review time per proof, (5) mutation survival rate. Measure all 5 on 30 VeriBench tasks across 3 LLMs. Publish as a multi-dimensional evaluation framework.

**Why it matters:** Single-metric optimization leads to goodharting. Compilation rate alone does not capture the safety-relevant dimensions of formal verification quality. A multi-dimensional dashboard would enable more honest progress tracking.

**Relevant context:** RAND (2026) calls for better benchmarks. VeriBench measures compilation and proof completion. VeriEquivBench measures equivalence. No paper combines these dimensions into a unified dashboard.
