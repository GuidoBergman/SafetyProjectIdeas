# Cross-Domain Transfer: Importing Checklist Design from Aviation Safety to AI Security Report Checklists

**Problem:** Aviation safety uses rigorous checklists to prevent systematic errors under time pressure. AI-generated security reports could be structured as pilot-style checklists to reduce analyst errors.

**Direction:** Design AI security report templates based on aviation checklist principles (critical items first, binary yes/no where possible, challenge-response format). Compare: standard AI report vs. checklist-format report in a user study with 15 CS students triaging 10 vulnerability reports. Measure triage error rate and time.

**Why it matters:** Reducing analyst error in vulnerability triage is a concrete safety improvement. Aviation checklist research has decades of empirical support for error reduction.

**Relevant context:** Aviation checklist research (Gawande, Degani/Wiener). Cognitive load theory application to security reports (see earlier idea). Checklist-format AI security reports not designed or evaluated.
