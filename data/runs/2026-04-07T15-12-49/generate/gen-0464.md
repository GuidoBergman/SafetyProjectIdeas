# Applying Deception Detection Research to Inter-Agent Manipulation Messages

**Problem:** Human deception detection research has established linguistic cues for deceptive communication. These cues have never been applied to detect deceptive or manipulative messages in inter-agent communication.

**Direction:** Extract linguistic features used in human deception detection (Linguistic Inquiry and Word Count dimensions, hedging language, specificity markers). Apply them to inter-agent messages from social engineering scenarios (Irregular 2026-style). Train a classifier and compare accuracy to a baseline LLM classifier without deception-specific features.

**Why it matters:** If human deception detection features transfer to AI-generated manipulation detection, decades of deception research provide an off-the-shelf feature set for inter-agent communication monitoring.

**Relevant context:** Irregular 2026 inter-agent social engineering; deception detection literature (Vrij, Newman, LIWC); NLP text classification.
