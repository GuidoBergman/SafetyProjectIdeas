# Biosafety Level Analog: Mapping BSL-1 through BSL-4 Laboratory Access Controls onto AI Cyber Capability Labs

**Problem:** The AI safety field uses ASL levels inspired by biosafety, but the actual infrastructure controls at BSL-1 through BSL-4 have not been systematically mapped to practical AI lab access controls.

**Direction:** Analyze CDC/WHO Biosafety Level requirements (physical containment, personnel requirements, decontamination protocols) at each level. Propose direct analogs for AI cyber capability labs (network isolation, red team protocols, capability containment measures). Assess feasibility for ASL-3/4 equivalents.

**Why it matters:** BSL levels provide a proven, internationally recognized access control framework. Concrete mapping may make ASL-equivalent controls more implementable and auditable.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic RSP ASL levels; Kraprayoon HACCA governance; Bengio et al. evaluation infrastructure.
