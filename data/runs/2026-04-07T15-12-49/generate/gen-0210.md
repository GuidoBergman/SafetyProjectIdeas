# Cross-Domain Transfer: Importing Usability Testing Methods from HCI to AI Security Tool Design

**Problem:** AI security tools are evaluated on detection performance but not on usability by the human analysts who depend on them. Human-computer interaction (HCI) usability testing methods could reveal adoption barriers.

**Direction:** Conduct a think-aloud usability study with 10 CS students using two AI security tools (e.g., Semgrep AI and a Claude-based scanner) to analyze the same codebase. Apply HCI usability metrics (task completion rate, errors, time on task, SUS score). Identify top 3 usability barriers.

**Why it matters:** A tool that is technically excellent but poorly usable will not be adopted, leaving defenders without its protection. HCI methods can identify actionable design improvements.

**Relevant context:** HCI usability testing is mature (SUS, think-aloud protocols, Nielsen heuristics). Application to AI security tool usability evaluation has not been published.
