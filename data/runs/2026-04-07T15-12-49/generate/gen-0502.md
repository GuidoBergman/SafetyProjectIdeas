# Benchmark Leakage in Formal Verification: Do LLMs Memorize Lean 4 Proofs?

**Problem:** Lean 4 benchmarks like VeriBench and the HumanEval-based subsets may be in model training data. If LLMs are memorizing proofs rather than reasoning about them, benchmark scores are inflated safety signals.

**Direction:** Design a contamination-detection protocol for formal verification benchmarks: compare model performance on held-out vs. publicly available Lean 4 problems, measure n-gram overlap with model training corpora, and test performance degradation on structurally similar but novel problems.

**Why it matters:** If safety-relevant benchmarks are contaminated, policy and research decisions based on them (e.g., RAND benchmark incentives) rest on false foundations.

**Relevant context:** VeriBench (ICML 2025) is publicly available. LLMs trained after its release could have ingested its problems. Benchmark contamination is a known issue in NLP; this is the first investigation of it in the formal verification domain.
