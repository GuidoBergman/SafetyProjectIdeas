# Adversarial Robustness of Monitoring Classifiers: Are They More Fragile Than Task Models?

**Problem:** Monitoring classifiers are themselves ML models and could be adversarially attacked. It is unknown whether monitoring classifiers are more or less adversarially fragile than the task models they monitor.

**Direction:** Apply standard adversarial text attack methods (e.g., TEXTFOOLER, BERT-Attack) to a monitoring classifier. Measure adversarial robustness. Compare to adversarial robustness of the task model on the same attacks. Determine relative fragility.

**Why it matters:** If monitors are more adversarially fragile than task models, the assumption that monitoring adds security is questionable. This is a fundamental property of the monitoring paradigm.
