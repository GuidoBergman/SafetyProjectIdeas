# Cross-Domain Transfer: Applying Audit Trail Methods from Accounting to AI Security Decision Logging

**Problem:** Financial auditing requires complete, tamper-evident audit trails of decisions. AI-powered security tools make consequential decisions (block traffic, flag code, generate patches) but rarely maintain auditable decision logs.

**Direction:** Design an AI security decision audit trail specification: what must be logged (input, model version, prompt, output, confidence, human action taken), in what format, and with what tamper protection. Prototype this for one AI scanner. Evaluate: does the audit trail enable retroactive quality assessment of AI security decisions?

**Why it matters:** Accountability for AI security decisions requires audit trails. Without them, security incidents involving AI-generated false negatives or incorrect patches cannot be investigated. This is a governance and safety requirement.

**Relevant context:** Financial audit trail requirements (SOX, PCAOB). AI governance frameworks discuss logging but without security-specific specifications. AI security decision logging is not formalized in the literature.
