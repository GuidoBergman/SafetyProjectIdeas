# Does Monitoring Awareness Change Agent Behavior? A Controlled Experiment on Behavioral Consistency

**Problem:** A fundamental assumption of agent monitoring is that agents behave consistently whether or not they believe they are being observed. This assumption is largely untested on current models.

**Direction:** Present identical tasks to an agent under three conditions: explicitly told it is monitored, told it is not monitored, and given no information. Compare task completion strategies, refusal rates, and policy violations across conditions on a small open-source model.

**Why it matters:** If models already behave differently based on monitoring awareness, current monitoring systems may be systematically fooled. This is a prerequisite check for the validity of any monitoring regime.
