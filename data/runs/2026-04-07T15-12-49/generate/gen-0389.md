# Importing FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis) from Engineering Safety to AI Governance Evaluation Design

**Problem:** Governance evaluations are designed to find average-case safety performance. Engineering safety's FMEA methodology systematically enumerates failure modes to find worst-case and edge-case failures.

**Direction:** Apply FMEA methodology to the dual-use governance challenge: enumerate all governance system components (evaluation, policy design, deployment controls, monitoring, incident response). For each, identify potential failure modes, failure effects, and severity ratings. Produce a Failure Mode Priority Matrix for AI cyber governance.

**Why it matters:** FMEA is the standard approach for systematic failure analysis in aviation, automotive, and nuclear engineering. Its application to AI governance would produce a more complete and systematic enumeration of governance risks.

**Relevant context:** FMEA engineering standard (MIL-STD-1629); SMS aviation import; Bengio et al. governance gap; NIST AI 800-1 risk management.
