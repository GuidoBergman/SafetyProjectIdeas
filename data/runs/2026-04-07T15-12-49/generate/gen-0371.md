# Failure Mode: Do Models Fail to Refuse Cumulative Uplift Provided Across Multiple Seemingly Innocent Interactions?

**Problem:** A structured multi-session interaction where each session provides one piece of a harmful puzzle (separately innocuous) may never trigger per-session refusals despite providing full harmful uplift cumulatively.

**Direction:** Design 10 cybersecurity topics where full exploitation knowledge requires 5 separate sub-topics. Create 5-session conversation sequences where each session requests one sub-topic innocuously. After all 5 sessions, test whether the combined information provides material uplift. Test across 3 models.

**Why it matters:** This tests the governance boundary between session-level and cross-session refusal policy. Cross-session accumulation attacks are the most realistic threat model for persistent advanced adversaries.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. request segmentation attack; GTG-1002 multi-session operation; Anthropic memory/persistent context provisions.
