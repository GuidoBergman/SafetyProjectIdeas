# Probing Whether Models Apply NIST's Dual-Use Risk Management Framework When Responding to Borderline Requests

**Problem:** NIST AI 800-1 describes a risk management methodology for dual-use models, but it's unknown whether frontier models apply anything resembling this methodology when deciding how to respond.

**Direction:** Design 20 borderline cybersecurity prompts. Prompt models to reason aloud about whether to answer. Analyze the reasoning traces for alignment with NIST AI 800-1's risk management dimensions. Code reasoning by whether it considers: capability evaluation, deployment context, user intent, counterfactual availability. Compare across models.

**Why it matters:** If models' reasoning about dual-use requests bears no resemblance to NIST's framework, the framework is not internalized. Understanding the actual decision process used by models informs where governance training should focus.

**Relevant context:** NIST AI 800-1 cybersecurity appendix; chain-of-thought reasoning in safety decisions; Linder et al. 5-dimension framework.
