# Replicating the Offense-Defense Gap Analysis Specifically for AI-Assisted Social Engineering in Cybersecurity Contexts

**Problem:** Potter et al.'s offense-defense gap analysis covers technical capabilities. Social engineering (phishing, pretexting, vishing) represents a distinct AI capability category where the offense-defense asymmetry may be even larger.

**Direction:** Analyze AI capabilities for offensive social engineering (spear phishing generation, pretexting scripts, vishing voice synthesis) vs. defensive social engineering detection (phishing classification, social engineering training content).
