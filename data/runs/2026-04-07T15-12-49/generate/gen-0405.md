# Tracking Whether AI Labs' Cybersecurity Governance Commitments Appear in Their Board-Level Risk Disclosures

**Problem:** Corporate governance requires board-level risk disclosure. Whether AI labs' cybersecurity dual-use risks appear in their formal risk disclosures (10-K equivalents, investor communications) is an indicator of institutional commitment.

**Direction:** Analyze available public financial/investor disclosures from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google (for DeepMind). Identify whether and how dual-use cybersecurity risks are disclosed. Compare the specificity of investor disclosures to safety framework commitments. Flag contradictions or omissions.

**Why it matters:** Risk disclosures create legal accountability in ways that voluntary safety frameworks do not. If dual-use cyber risks are in safety frameworks but not investor disclosures, the governance commitment is structurally soft.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Feb 2026 Risk Report; OpenAI Preparedness Framework; corporate governance and SEC disclosure requirements.
