# Formal Verification of Differential Privacy in AI Training: An End-to-End Experiment

**Problem:** Differential privacy (DP) guarantees for AI models are typically not formally verified — they rely on theoretical proofs that may have implementation bugs. Whether formal verification of DP in a simple AI training pipeline is tractable with LLM assistance is unknown.

**Direction:** Take a minimal DP-SGD implementation (Python, ~100 lines). Use LLM assistance to: (a) translate to Lean 4, (b) write formal spec capturing (ε,δ)-DP, (c) attempt proof. Measure whether proof completes, what the proof covers, and how long it takes with AI assistance.

**Why it matters:** Formally verified DP in AI training would represent a major AI safety milestone: mathematical proof that training preserves privacy. If it is tractable with current tools, it should be a near-term goal. If not, the gap must be characterized.

**Relevant context:** SampCert (Amazon) has Lean 4 proofs for DP mechanisms. DP-SGD is used in production (Google, Apple). No study has attempted end-to-end formal verification of a DP-SGD implementation with LLM assistance.
