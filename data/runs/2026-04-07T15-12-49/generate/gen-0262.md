# Compounding Risk: AI-Assisted Offensive Tool Development + Underinvestment in Defensive AI = Widening Gap

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) AI offensive tool development (Aardvark, automated exploit generation) is commercially incentivized and rapidly advancing; (2) defensive AI tool development is less commercially incentivized and slower. The gap may be widening despite absolute defensive improvement.

**Direction:** Conduct a systematic literature review measuring the publication rate and benchmark performance improvement rate for AI offensive tools vs. AI defensive tools over 2022-2026. Compute: offense/defense improvement rate ratio. Assess whether the gap is narrowing or widening. Identify investment asymmetry as a potential driver.

**Why it matters:** Even if AI defense is improving in absolute terms, if offense improves faster, the net security posture worsens. This asymmetry has direct policy implications.

**Relevant context:** Potter et al. SoK 2025: offense > defense gap. Sarma & Fisher 2026 RAND: benchmark incentives for defense. Systematic measurement of the offense-defense capability improvement rate gap not published.
