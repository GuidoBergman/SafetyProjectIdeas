# Benchmark Gaming × Safety Signal: If LLMs Overfit to Lean Benchmarks, Can They Still Verify Novel Code?

**Problem:** If LLMs improve on Lean 4 benchmarks through benchmark-specific optimization (few-shot memorization, dataset leakage) rather than genuine formal reasoning, the safety signal from benchmark performance would be misleading. Compounding this with deployment of such models in safety-critical verification creates a dangerous situation.

**Direction:** Train a lightweight model variant on VeriBench training examples (benchmark-specific SFT). Compare its performance on VeriBench vs. on a held-out set of novel Lean 4 tasks created after the benchmark. Measure generalization gap. Determine whether the VeriBench score overstates actual formal reasoning capability.

**Why it matters:** If state-of-the-art Lean 4 performance is partially explained by benchmark overfitting, safety decisions based on these scores (e.g., deploying AI-assisted formal verification in critical systems) rest on inflated capability estimates.

**Relevant context:** Benchmark contamination is documented in NLP. VeriBench is publicly available since ICML 2025. The compounding risk of overfitting and safety-critical deployment has not been studied for formal verification benchmarks specifically.
