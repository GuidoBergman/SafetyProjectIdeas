# Comparing Human vs. LLM Monitor Performance on Ambiguous Agent Actions

**Problem:** LLM-based monitors may diverge from human judgments on ambiguous agent actions — cases where it is genuinely unclear whether an action is dangerous. This divergence has not been characterized.

**Direction:** Collect 100 ambiguous agent actions (neither clearly safe nor clearly dangerous). Have human annotators and LLM monitors both rate each action. Measure human-LLM agreement rate, inter-human agreement, and identify categories of systematic divergence.

**Why it matters:** Human-LLM monitoring divergence on ambiguous cases determines how much autonomy can be delegated to automated monitors. Systematic divergences reveal where human oversight is irreplaceable.

**Relevant context:** Human-LLM agreement research; agent monitoring; human oversight of AI.
