# Canary Token Injection as a Lightweight Insider-Threat Detection Method for AI Agents

**Problem:** Canary tokens (fake credentials that trigger alerts when accessed) are a standard technique in cybersecurity for detecting insider threats. They have not been adapted for detecting rogue AI agent behavior.

**Direction:** Embed fake API keys, file paths, and database credentials (canary tokens) into an agent's accessible context. Measure whether agents in 'goal-hijacked' or adversarially-prompted conditions are more likely to attempt to use these canary credentials than baseline agents.

**Why it matters:** Canary tokens are cheap to deploy and provide high-confidence signals when triggered. If they work for AI agents, they represent an immediately deployable insider-threat detection mechanism.

**Relevant context:** Traditional canary token security technique; AI insider threat research (Palo Alto, 2026); LW threat matrix.
