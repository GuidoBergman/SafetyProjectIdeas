# Mapping Dual-Use Liability: Who Bears Legal Responsibility When AI-Assisted Cyberattacks Occur?

**Problem:** When GTG-1002 used Claude to conduct espionage, the legal liability question (Anthropic, the operator, the end user, the attackers) was not publicly resolved. No governance framework clearly assigns liability.

**Direction:** Analyze 3 legal frameworks (US CFAA, EU AI Act liability provisions, UK Computer Misuse Act) for how they would assign liability in an AI-assisted cyberattack equivalent to GTG-1002. **Why it matters:** Clear liability assignment is a cornerstone of governance in every mature industry. Without it, AI labs lack legal incentives to improve safety and victims lack recourse.
