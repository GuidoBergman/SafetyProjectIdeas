# Compounding Risk: High AI Capability Diffusion + Weak Safety Controls = Amplified Offense-Defense Asymmetry

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) AI offensive capabilities diffuse rapidly (open-source models, fine-tuning); (2) safety controls on these capabilities are bypassed by a significant fraction of users. Combined, the effective uplift for attackers from AI capabilities is much larger than benchmarks suggest.

**Direction:** Estimate the effective diffusion of AI offensive capability: (a) measure jailbreak success rate on publicly available models, (b) estimate the fraction of model users with sufficient technical sophistication to fine-tune away safety controls (from GitHub activity data), (c) compute the effective capability uplift for attackers accounting for safety control bypass. Compare to defender capability access.

**Why it matters:** Policy discussions focus on capability levels without accounting for safety control bypass rates. This analysis would give a more realistic picture of the AI-amplified threat landscape.

**Relevant context:** Jailbreak success: 90-99% on open-weight models (TechRxiv 2026). International AI Safety Report 2026: dual-use risks and safety framework gaps. Fine-tuning removing safety controls is documented. The compounding of diffusion + bypass not analyzed.
