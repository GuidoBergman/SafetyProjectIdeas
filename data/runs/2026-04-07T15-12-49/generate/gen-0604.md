# Can LLMs Generate Lean 4 Proofs for Non-Trivial Concurrency Safety Properties?

**Problem:** Concurrency bugs (data races, deadlocks, TOCTOU vulnerabilities) are among the hardest to detect with existing tools. Whether LLMs can generate Lean 4 formal proofs for concurrency safety properties is completely unstudied.

**Direction:** Take 15 simple concurrent programs (producer-consumer, reader-writer, mutual exclusion). Write Lean 4 models of each. Ask LLMs to prove safety properties (mutual exclusion, absence of deadlock, absence of data race). Measure success rate and characterize failure modes.

**Why it matters:** Concurrency bugs are responsible for a significant fraction of security vulnerabilities. If LLM formal verification cannot handle concurrency, its applicability to the most dangerous bug class is limited.

**Relevant context:** Lean 4 concurrency modeling is nascent. TLA+ and Coq have been used for concurrency proofs. No study has attempted LLM-generated concurrency proofs in Lean 4. This is a critical gap given the prevalence of concurrency vulnerabilities.
