# Replicating AI Vulnerability Detection Across Model Families (Open vs. Closed)

**Problem:** Published AI vulnerability detection results primarily use Claude or GPT-4o. Open-source models (Llama 3, Mistral, DeepSeek) may have different performance and safety properties.

**Direction:** Run a fixed vulnerability detection benchmark (30 CVEs across three classes) on three open-source LLMs and two closed-source LLMs. Compare detection rate, false positive rate, explanation quality, and jailbreak resistance to offensive prompting. Analyze safety-performance trade-offs.

**Why it matters:** Open-source models can be fine-tuned to remove safety controls. Understanding their baseline security capabilities and safety properties informs deployment decisions.
