# Compounding Risk: Overfit AI Security Model + Novel Attack Framework = Comprehensive Evasion

**Problem:** Two interacting failures: (1) AI security models may be overfit to existing vulnerability patterns (CVE training data); (2) new attack frameworks (e.g., new OWASP Top 10 categories) introduce patterns not in training data. Combined, attackers using new frameworks evade AI defense entirely.

**Direction:** Train or prompt-engineer an AI scanner optimized for OWASP Top 10:2021 patterns. Test its detection rate on OWASP Top 10:2025 new categories (Software Supply Chain Failures, AI Security). Measure detection degradation for new vs. existing categories. Quantify the temporal evasion window when new attack frameworks emerge.

**Why it matters:** AI defenses trained on historical patterns are systematically blind to paradigm-shift attacks. This compound failure is predictable and preventable.

**Relevant context:** OWASP Top 10:2025 introduces new categories including Software Supply Chain Failures. LLM vulnerability detection training on OWASP patterns. Temporal evasion due to training data cutoff not studied.
