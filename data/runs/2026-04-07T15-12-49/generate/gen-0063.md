Combined, these create a multi-modal social engineering attack where text, voice, and video reinforce each other. The compounding effect on victim susceptibility is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Design a controlled study (using fictional personas with explicit participant consent) comparing: (A) AI text-only phishing, (B) AI text + automated voice deepfake call (using a free TTS service, not actual deepfakes), (C) human-crafted baseline. **Why it matters:** Multi-modal social engineering may be qualitatively more dangerous than single-modal attacks (text or voice alone). Measuring the amplification factor informs whether combining modalities requires coordinated defensive responses beyond email filters alone.
