# Importing Concept of 'Functional Equivalence' From Arms Control to AI Dual-Use Capability Regulation

**Problem:** Arms control treaties use 'functional equivalence' to prevent parties from developing weapons that are functionally identical to banned weapons under different names. AI dual-use governance lacks this concept.

**Direction:** Analyze how functional equivalence is defined in the Chemical Weapons Convention and Biological Weapons Convention. Develop an analogous 'functional equivalence' test for AI cybersecurity capabilities. Apply it to 10 AI capabilities near current governance thresholds to test whether functionally equivalent capabilities are covered or evade existing frameworks.

**Why it matters:** Without a functional equivalence principle, AI governance frameworks can be evaded by describing banned capabilities differently or implementing them through different technical means.

**Relevant context:** CWC and BWC functional equivalence; Bengio et al. international governance; dual-use governance framework limitations; nuclear export control analogy.
