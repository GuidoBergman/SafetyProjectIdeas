# Failure Mode: Safety Instruction Amnesia Under Extended Context

**Problem:** Safety instructions typically appear at the beginning of system prompts. In extended agentic tasks with large context windows, earlier safety instructions may become effectively inaccessible as the model's attention shifts to more recent context.

**Direction:** Insert safety constraints at different positions in the context window (beginning, middle, end) for extended agentic tasks. Measure constraint violation rates as a function of constraint position and context length. Determine whether there is a 'forgetting distance' beyond which safety instructions become ineffective.

**Why it matters:** If safety instructions degrade with context distance, long-running agentic tasks may silently lose safety constraints without any detectable error. This implies safety instructions need to be repeated or reinforced during extended tasks.

**Relevant context:** LLM attention and positional bias research; 'lost in the middle' phenomenon; agentic task duration and context growth.
