# Quantifying the Value of Explanation in Agent Monitoring: Do Monitor Explanations Improve Human Decision Quality?

**Problem:** LLM monitors can generate explanations for their flagging decisions. Whether these explanations actually improve human decision quality or introduce new biases (anchoring on the monitor's reasoning) is empirically untested.

**Direction:** Run a user study (N=30) where human reviewers see monitoring flags with and without LLM-generated explanations. Measure decision accuracy, decision confidence, and decision time. Compare conditions.

**Why it matters:** If explanations improve human decisions, they should be standard in monitoring interfaces. If they introduce anchoring bias without improving accuracy, they may be counterproductive.

**Relevant context:** Explainable AI; human-in-the-loop monitoring; anchoring bias in security review.
