# Measuring the Dual-Use Governance Coverage of AI Lab Transparency Reports

**Problem:** AI labs publish various transparency reports, safety cards, and risk reports. Whether these documents collectively provide adequate coverage of dual-use governance information is unknown.

**Direction:** Define 20 categories of information that a complete dual-use cybersecurity governance disclosure should contain (capability evaluations, refusal policy, incident record, audit results, trigger criteria, etc.). For each lab (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta), score their current transparency disclosures against each category. Produce a disclosure coverage matrix.

**Why it matters:** Transparency is a foundational governance mechanism. Systematically mapping what is and isn't disclosed creates accountability and informs regulatory disclosure requirements.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Feb 2026 Risk Report; OpenAI Preparedness Framework disclosures; Bengio et al. 2026 transparency gaps.
