# Longitudinal Tracking of Refusal Rates: Do Models Get More or Less Restrictive Over Time?

**Problem:** AI labs update models frequently. Whether the aggregate refusal rate for dual-use cybersecurity requests increases or decreases over model generations is not tracked.

**Direction:** Using a frozen 50-prompt cybersecurity benchmark, test refusal rates on 6 months of model checkpoints where accessible (Claude 3.0, 3.5, 3.7, Opus 4.6; GPT-4, 4o, 4.5, 4o-mini).
