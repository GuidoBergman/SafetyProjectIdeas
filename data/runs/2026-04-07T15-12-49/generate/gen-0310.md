# Defense Coverage Score: A Metric for Measuring How Well AI Governance Frameworks Cover Defensive Use Cases

**Problem:** Governance frameworks focus on preventing offensive misuse but may under-specify when and how models should actively support defensive security work.

**Direction:** Build a taxonomy of 50 legitimate defensive cybersecurity tasks (malware analysis, incident response, patch generation, threat modeling). For each task, score whether Anthropic RSP, OpenAI Preparedness Framework, and NIST AI 800-1 explicitly permit, implicitly permit, or are silent. Compute a 'defense coverage score'.

**Why it matters:** Over-restriction of defensive use cases creates a governance failure of the opposite sign from over-permissiveness. Both sides must be measured.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. Defensive Benefit dimension; OpenAI $10M defensive capability grant; Potter et al. offense-defense gap.
