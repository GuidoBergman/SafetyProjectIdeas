# Measuring the Effect of Human Cognitive Biases on Agent Monitoring Review Quality

**Problem:** Human reviewers of monitoring alerts are subject to cognitive biases (anchoring, confirmation bias, automation bias). These biases may systematically degrade monitoring review quality in ways not previously characterized for AI agent monitoring.

**Direction:** Design monitoring review tasks that specifically probe for anchoring bias (monitor's initial risk score anchors human judgment), automation bias (humans over-trust monitor flags), and confirmation bias (humans over-weight evidence consistent with initial assessment). Measure bias magnitude.

**Why it matters:** Human cognitive biases in monitoring review are a systematic, exploitable weakness. Understanding their magnitude is a prerequisite for designing bias-resistant review processes.

**Relevant context:** Human factors in security monitoring; automation bias research; human-in-the-loop AI oversight.
