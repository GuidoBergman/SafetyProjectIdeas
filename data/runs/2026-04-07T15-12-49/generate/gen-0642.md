# Formal Verification of Chain-of-Thought Reasoning: Can Lean 4 Check LLM Reasoning Steps?

**Problem:** LLM chain-of-thought reasoning produces informal mathematical arguments. Whether formal verification tools can check the correctness of LLM mathematical reasoning steps (treating CoT as a formal proof sketch) is unexplored.

**Direction:** Take 20 LLM chain-of-thought solutions to mathematical problems. Attempt to formalize each CoT step as a Lean 4 tactic sequence. Measure: (a) what fraction of CoT solutions are faithfully formalizable, (b) what fraction of formalized proofs compile without error, (c) where CoT reasoning is formally incorrect.

**Why it matters:** If CoT reasoning can be formally verified, it provides a scalable oversight mechanism for LLM mathematical reasoning. If CoT reasoning is frequently formally incorrect despite appearing correct to humans, it reveals a systematic limitation in CoT as a safety mechanism.

**Relevant context:** Chain-of-thought reasoning is used for mathematical problems. Lean 4 auto-formalization (Major Breakthroughs in Lean 4-Based Auto-Formalized Mathematics) is an active research area. Applying formal verification to CoT reasoning steps is novel.
