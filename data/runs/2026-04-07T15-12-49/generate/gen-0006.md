# Uneven Capability Profiling: Mapping the Shape of AI Offensive Skill Across ATT&CK Phases

**Problem:** AISI 2026 reported sharp drops at specialist tasks (reverse engineering, cryptanalysis, malware development) relative to general exploitation, but no study has produced a comprehensive capability profile across all major ATT&CK tactic categories for multiple models.

**Direction:** Select 3-5 challenges per ATT&CK tactic (Reconnaissance, Initial Access, Execution, Persistence, Privilege Escalation, Lateral Movement, Collection, Exfiltration, Impact) from public CTF archives. Run 3-4 frontier API models. Produce a radar chart of solve rates per tactic per model. Statistically test which tactic × model combinations show significant underperformance.

**Why it matters:** A comprehensive capability profile would tell defenders exactly which attack phases are most AI-augmented and which remain human-dependent, enabling prioritized defensive investment. Also enables model-to-model comparison beyond single aggregate scores.

**Relevant context:** AISI Folkerts et al. (arxiv.org/abs/2603.11214) found uneven capabilities with sharp drops at specialist tasks. Rodriguez et al. 2025 used ATT&CK-aligned bottleneck analysis. SoK Potter et al. (arxiv.org/abs/2504.05408) documented offense > defense asymmetry across attack stages.
