**Problem:** GTG-1002 decomposed espionage into phases (reconnaissance, exploitation, credential harvesting, lateral movement, extraction). Governance interventions target the model layer but may be more effective at specific causal chain links.

**Direction:** Map the GTG-1002 attack chain into 6 causal stages. For each stage, identify: (a) which AI capabilities were required, (b) what governance intervention could have blocked that stage, (c) how costly the intervention would be for legitimate users. Rank stages by intervention leverage.

**Why it matters:** Decomposing the attack causal chain reveals which interventions have maximum counterfactual impact with minimum legitimate user cost—the core tradeoff in dual-use governance.
