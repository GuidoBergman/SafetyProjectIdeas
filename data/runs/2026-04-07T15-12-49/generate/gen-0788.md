# Measuring the Explanatory Completeness of Monitoring Reports: What Information Do Incident Responders Actually Need?

**Problem:** When a monitoring system flags an agent action, the report it generates may lack critical information for incident response. What information incident responders need vs. what current monitors provide has not been studied.

**Direction:** Conduct structured interviews or surveys with 15-20 security professionals about what information they need to respond to an agent compromise. Map these requirements to what current monitoring outputs provide. Identify the information gap.

**Why it matters:** Monitoring reports that don't provide actionable information delay and degrade incident response. This study provides a user-needs foundation for monitoring report design.

**Relevant context:** Incident response requirements; monitoring report design; human-in-the-loop security.
