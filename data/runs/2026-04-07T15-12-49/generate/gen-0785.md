# Threat Modeling AI Safety Infrastructure: Applying ATT&CK to the Monitoring System Itself

**Problem:** Monitoring systems are themselves attack surfaces. Applying ATT&CK-style threat modeling to the monitoring infrastructure (not just the agent) reveals threats that agent-focused threat models miss.

**Direction:** Take a reference monitoring architecture and apply ATT&CK methodology to it as the target system. Identify attack tactics against the monitoring infrastructure itself (data poisoning, model tampering, alert suppression, log falsification). Assess which are most threatening.

**Why it matters:** Monitoring systems that are not themselves secured are a false sense of security. This threat model provides the foundation for securing the monitoring infrastructure as a first-class security concern.

**Relevant context:** ATT&CK methodology; monitoring infrastructure security; METR evaluation.
