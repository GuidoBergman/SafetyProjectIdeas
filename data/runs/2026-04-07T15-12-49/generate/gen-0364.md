# Cyber Incident Response Benchmark: Measuring AI Uplift for Blue Team Incident Response Workflows

**Problem:** No standardized benchmark exists measuring how much AI assistance accelerates legitimate incident response tasks (forensics, log analysis, threat hunting), a critical metric for assessing the defensive value of dual-use capabilities.

**Direction:** Design 20 realistic incident response tasks (parse SIEM logs, identify C2 traffic in packet capture, write remediation script, draft incident report). Measure task completion time and quality for humans alone vs. humans with frontier AI assistance. Compute AI uplift ratio per task category.

**Why it matters:** The defensive benefit dimension of Linder et al.'s framework requires empirical measurement. This benchmark provides the first systematic measurement of AI uplift for blue team work.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. Defensive Benefit dimension; Potter et al. offense-defense gap; OpenAI $10M defensive AI grant.
