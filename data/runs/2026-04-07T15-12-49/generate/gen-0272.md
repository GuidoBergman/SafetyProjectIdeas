# Causal Chain: Studying How AI Security Findings Propagate Through Organizational Reporting Chains

**Problem:** AI security findings must travel from detection tool to developer to team lead to security team to CISO to board. At each step, information is filtered, reframed, and prioritized. The causal chain from AI finding to board-level awareness has not been studied.

**Direction:** Conduct a small qualitative study (10 interviews with CS students role-playing developer, team lead, and security lead personas) simulating information propagation through an organizational hierarchy for 5 AI-detected vulnerabilities. Measure: information loss, severity inflation/deflation, and time-to-awareness at each level.

**Why it matters:** Critical security findings may be filtered out before reaching decision-makers. Understanding where in the reporting chain information is lost enables targeted process improvement.

**Relevant context:** Organizational communication research (information filtering in hierarchies). Security incident communication research. AI security finding propagation through organizational structures not studied.
