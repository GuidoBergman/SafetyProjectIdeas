# Formal Verification Confidence Calibration Across Problem Complexity: Does Calibration Degrade for Hard Problems?

**Problem:** LLM calibration may be well-calibrated for easy Lean 4 problems but poorly calibrated for hard ones. Whether calibration degrades as proof complexity increases is a safety-relevant question.

**Direction:** Run VeriBench across all difficulty tiers. For each task, collect LLM confidence ratings before proof submission. Compute Expected Calibration Error (ECE) separately for easy, medium, and hard tasks. Test whether calibration is significantly worse for hard problems.

**Why it matters:** Overconfidence on hard problems is the most dangerous calibration failure. Safety-critical deployments rely on confidence estimates to route tasks to human review. If hard problems have worst calibration, the tasks most in need of review receive the least reliable confidence signals.

**Relevant context:** LLM calibration degrades for harder tasks in general NLP (documented in multiple calibration papers). The same pattern in formal verification would be particularly dangerous given the safety applications of Lean 4 verification.
