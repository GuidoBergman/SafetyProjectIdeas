# Transferability of Lean 4 Proof Strategies Across Isomorphic Problems

**Problem:** If a proof strategy works for function A, will it work for isomorphic function B (same structure, different domain)? Whether Lean 4 proof strategies generalize across isomorphic problem structures — a form of analogical reasoning — is not tested.

**Direction:** Create 15 isomorphic problem pairs in Lean 4 (e.g., sorted list and sorted tree, integer bounds and float bounds). Ask LLMs to: (a) solve the first problem, then (b) reuse the same proof strategy for the isomorphic problem. Measure whether proof strategy transfer succeeds. Compare to solving each independently.

**Why it matters:** Human Lean experts reuse proof strategies across isomorphic structures. If LLMs cannot perform this analogical transfer, they are limited to syntactic pattern matching rather than mathematical reasoning, suggesting formal verification assistance will not scale.

**Relevant context:** Transfer of proof strategies (proof reuse, proof adaptation) is a research area in theorem proving. LLM analogical reasoning is studied in NLP. Combining these in Lean 4 is novel and directly relevant to scaling formal verification.
