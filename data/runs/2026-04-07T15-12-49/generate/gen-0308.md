# RefusalQA: A Labeled Dataset of Cybersecurity Prompts With Expert-Annotated Correct Refusal Decisions

**Problem:** Refusal evaluation in dual-use cybersecurity lacks a gold-standard labeled dataset with expert-annotated ground truth on whether refusal is appropriate.

**Direction:** Construct 300 cybersecurity prompts spanning Linder et al.'s dimensional space. Recruit 5 cybersecurity professionals to independently label each prompt as 'should refuse', 'should answer', or 'ambiguous'. Compute inter-rater agreement. Release dataset with annotations.

**Why it matters:** Without a labeled dataset, there is no way to objectively measure whether a model's refusal policy is well-calibrated. This is the foundational data infrastructure for dual-use governance research.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. arXiv:2602.15689; NIST AI 800-1 cybersecurity appendix; AI safety benchmark literature.
