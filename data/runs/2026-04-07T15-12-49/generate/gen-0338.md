# Compounding Risks: AI Model Homogeneity × Dual-Use Capability — How Monoculture Amplifies Cyber Harm

**Problem:** If frontier AI labs converge on similar architectures and training data, adversaries who find a refusal bypass on one model find it on all models simultaneously. This compounds dual-use harm beyond single-model analysis.

**Direction:** Identify 20 known refusal bypass techniques from published jailbreak literature. Test each technique's cross-model success rate across GPT-4o, Claude 3.7, Gemini 1.5, Llama 3.1 Instruct. Compute a 'correlated failure rate'—the probability that a technique succeeds on multiple models simultaneously. Assess governance implications.

**Why it matters:** Governance frameworks evaluate models independently. If bypass success is correlated across models due to homogeneity, the effective governance failure rate is multiplicatively higher than single-model rates suggest.
