# Novelty Detection for Agent Behavior: Flagging Actions Never Seen in Training Distribution

**Problem:** Monitoring classifiers trained on known attack types will miss novel attacks by definition. Novelty detection (flagging out-of-distribution actions without requiring attack labels) could complement classifier-based monitoring.

**Direction:** Train a novelty detector on a large corpus of normal agent sessions. Deploy it alongside a standard monitoring classifier. Measure whether the novelty detector catches attacks that the classifier misses, and what its false positive rate is for genuinely novel but benign actions.

**Why it matters:** Novel attack types are by definition not in the training data of classifiers. Novelty detection provides monitoring coverage for unknown unknowns — a critical gap in current monitoring architectures.

**Relevant context:** Out-of-distribution detection in ML; METR novel bypass vectors; monitoring coverage.
