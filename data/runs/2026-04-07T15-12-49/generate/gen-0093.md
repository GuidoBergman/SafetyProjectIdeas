# Backcast from Mandatory Pre-Deployment Safety Evaluation: What Technical Properties Must Such an Evaluation Have?

**Problem:** Regulatory frameworks (EU AI Act, UK voluntary commitments) envision mandatory pre-deployment offensive capability evaluations. No work has specified what technical properties (validity, reliability, specificity, sensitivity) such evaluations must have to be legally defensible and scientifically credible.

**Direction:** Review regulatory requirements for pre-deployment AI evaluation from 3 jurisdictions (EU, UK, US). Extract implicit measurement requirements. **Why it matters:** Pre-deployment evaluations that don't satisfy basic psychometric properties (reliability, validity, sensitivity) would either miss genuinely dangerous capabilities or block safe deployments. Understanding the gap between required properties and current evaluation quality is urgent for responsible AI governance.
