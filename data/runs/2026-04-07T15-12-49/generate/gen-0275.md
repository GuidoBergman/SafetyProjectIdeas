# Novel Direction: AI-Assisted Vulnerability Severity Calibration Across Cultural and Organizational Contexts

**Problem:** Vulnerability severity assessments (CVSS scores) are assigned by different organizations and reflect different risk contexts. AI models trained on CVE data may have systematic bias toward certain severity frameworks.

**Direction:** For 20 CVEs with both NVD scores and vendor-assigned scores (which often differ), ask AI models to independently assess severity. Measure: AI severity alignment with NVD vs. vendor scores. Identify systematic biases (does AI favor NVD scoring conventions?). Propose calibration adjustments.

**Why it matters:** Organizations using AI-assisted vulnerability triage need to know whether AI severity scores reflect their organizational context or just a generic framework. Miscalibrated severity leads to misallocated defensive resources.

**Relevant context:** NVD vs. vendor CVSSv3 score discrepancies are documented (researchers have noted systematic differences). AI severity assessment calibration to organizational context not studied.
