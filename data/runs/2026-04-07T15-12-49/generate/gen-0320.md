# Borrowing Pharmaceutical Dual-Use Risk Classification for AI Cybersecurity Capability Scheduling

**Problem:** AI cyber capabilities currently have no tiered scheduling system analogous to pharmaceutical Controlled Substances Schedules (I-V), which balance harm potential against legitimate use.

**Direction:** Analyze DEA Controlled Substances scheduling criteria (abuse potential, accepted medical use, psychological/physical dependence). Map analogous criteria onto cyber AI capabilities. Propose a 5-tier 'Cyber Capability Schedule' with entry criteria. Test the mapping on 20 specific AI capabilities from benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** Pharmaceutical scheduling is a governance framework with legal enforceability and clear access tiering. Its logic may transfer more directly to cyber AI than export control frameworks.

**Relevant context:** Linder et al. 5-dimension framework; OpenAI Trusted Access tiering; Sarma & Fisher RAND governance mechanisms.
