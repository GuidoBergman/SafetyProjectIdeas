# Inference-Time Poisoning via Context Window Manipulation: Attack and Defense Prototype

**Problem:** Inference-time poisoning — injecting adversarial content into the context window to alter model behavior — is a poorly characterized attack. Its effectiveness across different model families and its detectability are unknown.

**Direction:** Implement three context-window poisoning techniques (indirect prompt injection, context stuffing, role reversal) against a small open model. Measure attack success rates across benign task completion. Implement a simple context sanitization defense and measure its effectiveness and false-positive rate.

**Why it matters:** Runtime attacks require fundamentally different defenses than training-time attacks. Characterizing this attack class and testing simple defenses is a prerequisite for secure agentic deployment.
