# Causal Analysis: Does Human-in-the-Loop Review Frequency Affect Agent Safety Outcomes in Long-Horizon Tasks?

**Problem:** Long-horizon tasks with infrequent human review may allow dangerous behaviors to compound before detection. The optimal human review frequency for different task risk levels is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Run agents on long-horizon tasks with adversarial injections at various points. Test human review checkpoints at different frequencies (every action, every 5, every 20, at end). Measure safety outcome rates and total human review workload for each frequency.

**Why it matters:** Human review frequency is a directly adjustable deployment parameter. This study provides the empirical basis for setting review frequency as a function of task risk level.
