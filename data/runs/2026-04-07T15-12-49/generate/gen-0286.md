# Benchmark Incentive Mapping: How Choice of Cybersecurity Benchmark Shapes Model Development Incentives

**Problem:** Sarma & Fisher (RAND) argue benchmarks function as governance by shaping what gets built, but no study maps which benchmarks create which incentive structures for offense vs. defense.

**Direction:** Catalog 15 publicly available cyber AI benchmarks (CTF, vuln discovery, code auditing, patching). For each, classify whether the primary capability rewarded is offense-only, defense-only, or dual. Analyze incentive gradients: if a lab optimizes purely for this benchmark, what capabilities increase?

**Why it matters:** Benchmark selection is a de facto governance decision. Making this explicit allows policymakers to require or incentivize defense-weighted benchmarks.

**Relevant context:** Sarma & Fisher RAND (Feb 2026); Potter et al. arXiv:2504.05408 on offense>defense gap.
