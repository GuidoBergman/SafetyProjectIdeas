# Follow-up to DeepMind 12K Incident Analysis: Does the 7-Archetype Model Generalize to 2025-2026 Incidents?

**Problem:** Rodriguez et al. 2025 (DeepMind) curated 7 representative attack chain archetypes from 12K AI-involved incidents up to 2025. Given the rapid capability growth documented in 2026, new incident archetypes may have emerged that the original 7 do not cover.

**Direction:** Collect 2025-2026 documented AI cyber incidents from public sources (Anthropic espionage case, OWASP Gen AI incident round-up, AI cloud breaches 2025 Reco report). For each incident, attempt to classify it into one of the 7 Rodriguez archetypes. Identify incidents that do not fit any archetype. Propose 1-3 new archetypes for incidents that represent genuinely new patterns. Validate archetypes with a simple inter-rater reliability test.

**Why it matters:** If new incident patterns exist beyond the 7-archetype model, the evaluation framework built on those archetypes has coverage gaps. Updating the archetype model to include 2025-2026 incidents would bring the evaluation framework current with the rapidly evolving threat landscape.

**Relevant context:** Rodriguez et al. 2025 (arxiv 2503.11917): 7 archetypes from 12K incidents. Anthropic espionage case (Nov 2025) is the first documented AI-orchestrated state-level attack. OWASP Gen AI incident roundup Jan-Feb 2025 documents new incident classes. AI cloud breaches 2025 (Reco). This is a direct follow-up that uses publicly available incident data.
