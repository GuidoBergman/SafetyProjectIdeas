**Problem:** AISI estimated 22/32 steps corresponds to ~6 of 14 expert-hours. There is no published distribution of time-to-compromise for AI agents across different challenge types, making it impossible to identify which challenges can be completed within operationally relevant time windows.

**Direction:** Run AI agents on 20 challenges spanning easy, medium, and hard difficulty. Record total wall-clock time, token usage, and tool invocation count per challenge. Plot time-to-completion distributions. Identify which challenge types complete in under 1 hour (operationally stealthy), 1-8 hours (overnight attack window), and >8 hours (requiring sustained operation).

**Why it matters:** An attack completing in under 1 hour may not trigger time-based anomaly detection. Attacks requiring sustained operation create higher detection probability. Understanding time distributions enables defenders to calibrate detection windows and tells evaluators which attacks are currently 'practically feasible' vs. theoretically possible.
