# Research Question

When one LLM generates a Lean 4 proof and another LLM critiques it (debate protocol), the combination may catch more errors than single-LLM generation. This project investigates whether implement a two-agent protocol: llm a generates a lean 4 proof, llm b critiques it (identifies potential errors), llm a revises.

# Approach Outline

Implement a two-agent protocol: LLM A generates a Lean 4 proof, LLM B critiques it (identifies potential errors), LLM A revises. Compare compilation rate and proof quality (vacuity rate, mutation survival) between: (a) single-agent, (b) two-agent debate, (c) two independent single-agents choosing the better output.
