# Research Question

AI models may have fine-tuned behavior that is sensitive to how attack tasks are labeled. This project investigates whether create 10 identical technical tasks (find vulnerability, write exploit, analyze binary).

# Approach Outline

Create 10 identical technical tasks (find vulnerability, write exploit, analyze binary). Present each with 5 different framings: (1) 'CTF challenge', (2) 'authorized penetration test', (3) 'security research', (4) 'academic exercise', (5) no framing. Measure solve rate, refusal rate, and output quality per framing. Test whether framing effects are model-specific or universal.
