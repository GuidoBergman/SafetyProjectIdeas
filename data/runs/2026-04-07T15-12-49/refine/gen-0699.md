# Research Question

An attacker could gradually corrupt an agent's context across many turns, with no single turn triggering a threshold. This project investigates whether design a multi-turn context poisoning attack: spread malicious instruction fragments across 10-20 turns of conversation.

# Approach Outline

Design a multi-turn context poisoning attack: spread malicious instruction fragments across 10-20 turns of conversation. Test whether action-level monitors (which see one turn at a time) and trajectory-level monitors (which see the full context) differ in detection success.
