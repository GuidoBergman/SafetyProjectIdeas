# Research Question

AI vulnerability reports are often verbose and poorly structured, overwhelming human analysts. This project investigates whether design two formats for ai-generated vulnerability reports: (a) standard llm output, (b) cognitively optimized format (progressive disclosure, severity-first, structured sections).

# Approach Outline

Design two formats for AI-generated vulnerability reports: (a) standard LLM output, (b) cognitively optimized format (progressive disclosure, severity-first, structured sections). Run a user study with 10-15 CS students triaging 10 vulnerabilities in each format. Measure triage accuracy, time, and self-reported cognitive load.
