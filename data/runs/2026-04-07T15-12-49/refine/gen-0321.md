# Research Question

The AI safety field uses ASL levels inspired by biosafety, but the actual infrastructure controls at BSL-1 through BSL-4 have not been systematically mapped to practical AI lab access controls. This project investigates whether analyze cdc/who biosafety level requirements (physical containment, personnel requirements, decontamination protocols) at each level.

# Approach Outline

Analyze CDC/WHO Biosafety Level requirements (physical containment, personnel requirements, decontamination protocols) at each level. Propose direct analogs for AI cyber capability labs (network isolation, red team protocols, capability containment measures). Assess feasibility for ASL-3/4 equivalents.
