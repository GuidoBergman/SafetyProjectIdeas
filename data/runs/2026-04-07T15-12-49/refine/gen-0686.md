# Research Question

A sophisticated attacker could attempt to degrade monitoring signal quality by injecting large volumes of borderline-suspicious actions, causing alert fatigue or diluting the statistical baseline. This project investigates whether simulate a 'signal flooding' attack: inject high volumes of borderline actions that each fall just below a monitoring threshold.

# Approach Outline

Simulate a 'signal flooding' attack: inject high volumes of borderline actions that each fall just below a monitoring threshold. Measure the effect on detection rates for genuinely dangerous actions that follow. Characterize the minimum flood volume needed to degrade detection.
