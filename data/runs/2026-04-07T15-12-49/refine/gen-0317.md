# Research Question

Models may generate convincing-looking but non-functional exploit code for vulnerabilities that either don't exist or have different actual exploitation paths—a dual-use failure mode that hasn't been systematically characterized. This project investigates whether ask 4 models to generate exploitation guidance for 20 real cves and 10 fake cve numbers.

# Approach Outline

Ask 4 models to generate exploitation guidance for 20 real CVEs and 10 fake CVE numbers. Score technical accuracy of real CVE guidance with a security researcher. Measure hallucination rate and plausibility of fake CVE outputs. Assess governance risk of plausible-but-wrong exploitation guidance.
