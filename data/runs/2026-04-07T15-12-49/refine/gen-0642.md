# Research Question

LLM chain-of-thought reasoning produces informal mathematical arguments. This project investigates whether take 20 llm chain-of-thought solutions to mathematical problems.

# Approach Outline

Take 20 LLM chain-of-thought solutions to mathematical problems. Attempt to formalize each CoT step as a Lean 4 tactic sequence. Measure: (a) what fraction of CoT solutions are faithfully formalizable, (b) what fraction of formalized proofs compile without error, (c) where CoT reasoning is formally incorrect.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Define the experimental setup: select datasets, models, and evaluation metrics appropriate for the research question.

- Analyze results, compare with expected outcomes, and document findings in a well-structured blog post.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If CoT reasoning can be formally verified, it provides a scalable oversight mechanism for LLM mathematical reasoning. If CoT reasoning is frequently formally incorrect despite appearing correct to humans, it reveals a systematic limitation in CoT as a safety mechanism.
