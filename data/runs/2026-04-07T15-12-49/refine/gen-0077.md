# Research Question

Multiple AI labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, UK AISI) currently conduct independent offensive capability evaluations with incompatible methodologies. This project investigates whether survey published evaluation methodologies from 5 major labs.

# Approach Outline

Survey published evaluation methodologies from 5 major labs. Identify dimensions that are (1) already harmonized across labs, (2) divergent but theoretically harmonizable, (3) structurally incompatible. Propose a minimum common protocol specification that preserves lab flexibility while enabling cross-lab comparison. Implement the common protocol on a 10-challenge pilot and verify it produces comparable results across two open-weight models.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Conduct a systematic literature review to identify and categorize relevant prior work.
- Develop a classification framework and apply it to the collected corpus.
- Synthesize findings into a structured analysis with key takeaways for the AI safety community.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Without a common protocol, the field cannot make authoritative statements about comparative capability across labs, enabling strategic cherry-picking of evaluation methodologies. A common protocol is a prerequisite for credible international AI safety governance.
