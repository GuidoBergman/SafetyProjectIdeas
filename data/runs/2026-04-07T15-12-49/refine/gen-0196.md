# Research Question

Two interacting failures: (1) AI security tools may be overconfident in their outputs (poor calibration); (2) defenders often lack ground truth to verify AI performance in production. This project investigates whether design a monitoring protocol for ai security tool performance without ground truth: use proxy metrics (finding rate stability, explanation consistency across runs, agreement with independent re-analysis).

# Approach Outline
