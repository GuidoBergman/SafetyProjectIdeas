# Approach Outline

Take 5 known simple vulnerabilities (publicly disclosed with PoC). Attempt exploit generation with a fixed LLM under three conditions: (a) single-turn, (b) 3-turn iterative refinement, (c) 10-turn with feedback. Measure exploit success rate per condition. Assess whether the asymmetry narrows under iterative prompting.
