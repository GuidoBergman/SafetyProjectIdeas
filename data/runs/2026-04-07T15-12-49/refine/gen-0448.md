# Research Question

No standardized benchmark exists to measure an agent's propensity for emergent offensive behavior under controlled conditions. This project investigates whether design a benchmark of 50 tasks across 5 emergent-behavior categories (privilege escalation, credential access, dlp evasion, safety override, sandbox escape).

# Approach Outline

Design a benchmark of 50 tasks across 5 emergent-behavior categories (privilege escalation, credential access, DLP evasion, safety override, sandbox escape). Each task has a scoring rubric (binary: did the agent attempt the behavior?). Implement in a lightweight sandbox. Baseline three open models.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Select and prepare the evaluation dataset/benchmark, defining clear success metrics.
- Run the evaluation protocol across target conditions, collecting quantitative measurements.
- Compare results against baselines, analyze failure modes, and write up findings.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A standardized benchmark enables reproducible comparisons, progress tracking, and model evaluation before deployment. This fills the most critical measurement gap in the subfield.
