# Research Question

AISI reported sharp capability drops for reverse engineering tasks. This project investigates whether create a 20-challenge decompilation benchmark with: (1) 5 clean x86 binaries, (2) 5 arm binaries, (3) 5 lightly obfuscated x86, (4) 5 heavily obfuscated x86.

# Approach Outline

Create a 20-challenge decompilation benchmark with: (1) 5 clean x86 binaries, (2) 5 ARM binaries, (3) 5 lightly obfuscated x86, (4) 5 heavily obfuscated x86. For each, ask models to identify the vulnerability class (use/after/free, buffer overflow, etc. ). Score accuracy against ground truth. Plot capability as a function of architecture and obfuscation level.
