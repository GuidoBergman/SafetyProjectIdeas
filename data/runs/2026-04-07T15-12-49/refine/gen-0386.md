# Research Question

When AI misuse occurs, the time between an external report to the lab and public acknowledgment/remediation is a governance accountability metric that has not been systematically measured. This project investigates whether collect all publicly documented ai misuse reports where the external reporting timeline is known (gtg-1002, vibe hacking, jailbreak disclosures).

# Approach Outline

Collect all publicly documented AI misuse reports where the external reporting timeline is known (GTG-1002, vibe hacking, jailbreak disclosures). Measure time from first external report to: (a) lab acknowledgment, (b) public disclosure, (c) claimed behavioral change. Compare across labs. Compare to software VDP SLA benchmarks.
