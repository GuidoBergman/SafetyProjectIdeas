# Research Question

No time comparison exists between expert humans and current AI (with/without AI assistance) on the same Lean 4 tasks. This project investigates whether recruit 5 lean 4 experts and 5 novices (cs students).

# Approach Outline

Recruit 5 Lean 4 experts and 5 novices (CS students). Each solves 10 VeriBench tasks: (a) without AI assistance, (b) with Claude/Leanstral assistance. Measure time per task and success rate per condition. Compare to automated AI baseline (no human). Plot the human-AI collaboration efficiency frontier.
