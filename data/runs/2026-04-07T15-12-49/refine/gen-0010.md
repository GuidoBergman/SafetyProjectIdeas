# Research Question

Hoxhunt 2025 data showed AI phishing outperformed human attackers by 24% in click-through rates, but this was measured against general users. This project investigates whether generate phishing emails targeting three fictional persona types (it admin, finance staff, c-suite executive) using 4-5 different llms.

# Approach Outline

Generate phishing emails targeting three fictional persona types (IT admin, finance staff, C-suite executive) using 4-5 different LLMs. Have human raters (blind to model identity) score email quality on a rubric (personalization, urgency, legitimacy signals, technical accuracy). Compute inter-rater reliability. Compare scores across models and persona types.
