# Research Question

AI vulnerability scanners generate findings, but verification requires expert judgment. This project investigates whether design a tiered verification protocol: (a) ai-only verification (llm checks its own finding), (b) non-expert cs student verification with ai guidance, (c) expert verification.

# Approach Outline

Design a tiered verification protocol: (a) AI-only verification (LLM checks its own finding), (b) non-expert CS student verification with AI guidance, (c) expert verification. Run all three on 15 findings. Measure: verification accuracy, time, and cost per tier. Identify the minimum verification tier that achieves acceptable accuracy.
