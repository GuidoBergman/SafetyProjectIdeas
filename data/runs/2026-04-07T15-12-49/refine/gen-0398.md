# Research Question

For benchmarks to function as governance mechanisms (Sarma & Fisher), they must be administered by a trusted, independent standards body analogous to ICAO or ISO. This project investigates whether define the success state: an internationally recognized standards body publishes and administers binding ai cyber capability benchmarks.

# Approach Outline

Define the success state: an internationally recognized standards body publishes and administers binding AI cyber capability benchmarks. Work backward: what legal charter, funding model, membership structure, and technical capability does such a body need? Compare against existing bodies (ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, NIST, BSI). Identify gaps.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Select and prepare the evaluation dataset/benchmark, defining clear success metrics.
- Run the evaluation protocol across target conditions, collecting quantitative measurements.
- Compare results against baselines, analyze failure modes, and write up findings.
