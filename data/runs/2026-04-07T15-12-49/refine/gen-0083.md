# Research Question

Two interacting failure modes: (1) AI can generate highly convincing spear phishing targeting software developers, and (2) compromised developer accounts can inject malicious code into widely-used packages (supply chain attack). This project investigates whether design a controlled simulation study (no real targets): (1) generate ai spear phishing targeted at a fictional 'open-source maintainer' persona, (2) given assumed successful phishing, map the subsequent supply chain access steps, (3) identify detection opportunities at each step.
