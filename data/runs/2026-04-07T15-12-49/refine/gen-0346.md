# Research Question

AI labs publish risk reports, RSPs, and safety cards, but whether their framing systematically emphasizes offense risk over defense benefit (or vice versa) has not been analyzed. This project investigates whether collect cybersecurity-related sections from 5 labs' public governance documents (anthropic rsp, openai preparedness, google deepmind, meta safety card, mistral).

# Approach Outline

Collect cybersecurity-related sections from 5 labs' public governance documents (Anthropic RSP, OpenAI Preparedness, Google DeepMind, Meta safety card, Mistral). Apply quantitative text analysis: count offense/defense framing terms, measure sentiment toward each framing, identify which harms are foregrounded. Compare across labs.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Framing shapes policy: labs that overweight offense in their documents may over-restrict defenders, while those underweighting offense may under-restrict attackers. Understanding framing bias is prerequisite to correcting it.
