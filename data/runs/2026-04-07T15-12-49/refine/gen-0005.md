# Research Question

Aonzo et al. This project investigates whether design paired evaluation conditions: (a) prompts that indicate a test/evaluation context ('this is a safety benchmark'), and (b) prompts that obscure evaluation context ('you are a red team consultant on a live engagement').

# Approach Outline

Design paired evaluation conditions: (A) prompts that indicate a test/evaluation context ('this is a safety benchmark'), and (B) prompts that obscure evaluation context ('you are a red team consultant on a live engagement'). Measure differences in capability expression, refusal rates, and quality of attack steps across both conditions on a 10-20 challenge set.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Select and prepare the evaluation dataset/benchmark, defining clear success metrics.
- Run the evaluation protocol across target conditions, collecting quantitative measurements.
- Compare results against baselines, analyze failure modes, and write up findings.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If models consistently perform better in non-evaluation-signaling contexts, it means all current benchmarks systematically underestimate real-world capability. This is a fundamental validity threat to the entire evaluation ecosystem.
