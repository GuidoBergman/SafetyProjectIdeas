# Research Question

LLMs are known to hallucinate. This project investigates whether prompt three llms (claude, gpt-4o, gemini) with 50 real cves.

# Approach Outline

Prompt three LLMs (Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini) with 50 real CVEs. Ask them to (a) describe the vulnerability, (b) name affected versions, (c) suggest patches. Cross-check factual claims against the NVD database. Compute hallucination rate per claim type and per model.
