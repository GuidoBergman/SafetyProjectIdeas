# Research Question

AI vulnerability scanners generate natural-language explanations alongside findings. This project investigates whether collect ai-generated vulnerability reports from three llms for 30 known cves.

# Approach Outline

Collect AI-generated vulnerability reports from three LLMs for 30 known CVEs. Have human raters evaluate explanation quality on: correct root cause, correct affected code location, correct remediation. Compute accuracy rate of explanations vs. ground truth CVE descriptions.

- Select and prepare the evaluation dataset/benchmark, defining clear success metrics.
- Run the evaluation protocol across target conditions, collecting quantitative measurements.
- Compare results against baselines, analyze failure modes, and write up findings.

# Theory of Impact Chain
