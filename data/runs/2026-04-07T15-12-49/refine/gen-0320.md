# Research Question

AI cyber capabilities currently have no tiered scheduling system analogous to pharmaceutical Controlled Substances Schedules (I-V), which balance harm potential against legitimate use. This project investigates whether analyze dea controlled substances scheduling criteria (abuse potential, accepted medical use, psychological/physical dependence).

# Approach Outline

Analyze DEA Controlled Substances scheduling criteria (abuse potential, accepted medical use, psychological/physical dependence). Map analogous criteria onto cyber AI capabilities. Propose a 5-tier 'Cyber Capability Schedule' with entry criteria. Test the mapping on 20 specific AI capabilities from benchmarks.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Select and prepare the evaluation dataset/benchmark, defining clear success metrics.
- Run the evaluation protocol across target conditions, collecting quantitative measurements.
- Compare results against baselines, analyze failure modes, and write up findings.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Pharmaceutical scheduling is a governance framework with legal enforceability and clear access tiering. Its logic may transfer more directly to cyber AI than export control frameworks.
