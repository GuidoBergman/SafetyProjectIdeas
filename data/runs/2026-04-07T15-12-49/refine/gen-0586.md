# Research Question

Lean 4 proofs use diverse tactics (simp, omega, ring, induction, cases, apply, exact). This project investigates whether classify 200 llm lean 4 proof failures by which tactic was needed at the point of failure.

# Approach Outline

Classify 200 LLM Lean 4 proof failures by which tactic was needed at the point of failure. Compute failure rates per tactic category. Identify the top 3 tactic categories where LLMs fail most. Compare across models to determine whether weaknesses are universal or model-specific.
