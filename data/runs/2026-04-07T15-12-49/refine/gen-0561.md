# Research Question

If LLMs improve on Lean 4 benchmarks through benchmark-specific optimization (few-shot memorization, dataset leakage) rather than genuine formal reasoning, the safety signal from benchmark performance would be misleading. This project investigates whether train a lightweight model variant on veribench training examples (benchmark-specific sft).

# Approach Outline

Train a lightweight model variant on VeriBench training examples (benchmark-specific SFT). Compare its performance on VeriBench vs. on a held-out set of novel Lean 4 tasks created after the benchmark. Measure generalization gap. Determine whether the VeriBench score overstates actual formal reasoning capability.
