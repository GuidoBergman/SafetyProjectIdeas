# Research Question

AI lab governance frameworks (RSP, Preparedness Framework) are updated without systematic archiving or change tracking, making it impossible to study governance drift over time. This project investigates whether build a python-based web scraper and diff tracker that archives governance framework documents from anthropic, openai, google deepmind, and meta quarterly.

# Approach Outline

Build a Python-based web scraper and diff tracker that archives governance framework documents from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Meta quarterly. Compute semantic diffs between versions. Build a searchable database of commitment changes. Analyze cybersecurity-specific provisions.
