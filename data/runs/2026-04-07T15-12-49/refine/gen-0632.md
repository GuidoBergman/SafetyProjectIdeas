# Research Question

Lean 4 proofs are machine-checkable but often uninterpretable to non-experts. This project investigates whether take 25 complete lean 4 proofs of varying complexity.

# Approach Outline

Take 25 complete Lean 4 proofs of varying complexity. Ask LLMs to generate natural language explanations of each. Have Lean experts rate explanations for accuracy and completeness. Measure explanation accuracy vs. proof complexity. Test whether less complex proofs receive more accurate explanations.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Interpretable formal proofs are essential for human oversight of AI-generated verification. If proofs cannot be explained in plain language, human review is limited to checking compilation rather than understanding correctness.
