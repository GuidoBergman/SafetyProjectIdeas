# Research Question

Hack The Box announced its AI Red Teamer Certification (Q1 2026). This project investigates whether design the measurement properties a valid ai red team certification would require: reliability (same model scores consistently), validity (scores predict real-world performance), standard setting (defining what score = 'certified capable').

# Approach Outline

Design the measurement properties a valid AI red team certification would require: reliability (same model scores consistently), validity (scores predict real-world performance), standard setting (defining what score = 'certified capable'). Implement a minimal pilot with 5 challenges that satisfy these properties. Compute test-retest reliability and criterion validity against a known benchmark.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Select and prepare the evaluation dataset/benchmark, defining clear success metrics.
- Run the evaluation protocol across target conditions, collecting quantitative measurements.
- Compare results against baselines, analyze failure modes, and write up findings.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If certification standards are implemented without psychometric validation, certified models may be dangerously underqualified (false negative) or benign models may be falsely flagged (false positive). The measurement science foundation for AI capability certification is completely undeveloped.
