Even when AI-generated Lean 4 code compiles and is formally verified, the spec itself may not capture programmer intent — a form of specification drift that formal verification cannot detect. This project investigates whether collect 50+ python function specifications, have llms translate them to lean 4 with formal specs, then have human cs students evaluate whether the formal spec matches intent.
