# Research Question

When an AI agent is detected as compromised, standard security incident response (IR) frameworks (NIST SP 800-61) may not apply directly. This project investigates whether adapt nist sp 800-61 incident response phases (preparation, detection, containment, eradication, recovery) to ai agent compromise scenarios.

# Approach Outline

Adapt NIST SP 800-61 incident response phases (Preparation, Detection, Containment, Eradication, Recovery) to AI agent compromise scenarios. For each phase, identify what AI-specific actions and tooling are needed. Pilot the adapted IR procedure on a simulated agent compromise.
