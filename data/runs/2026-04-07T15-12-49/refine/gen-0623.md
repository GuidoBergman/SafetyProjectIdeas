# Research Question

If Leanstral or future models are fine-tuned on VeriBench tasks directly, benchmark scores would be inflated. This project investigates whether train two leanstral variants: (a) fine-tuned on mathlib+cslib only (no veribench tasks), (b) fine-tuned with veribench training examples included.

# Approach Outline

Train two Leanstral variants: (a) fine-tuned on Mathlib+CSLib only (no VeriBench tasks), (b) fine-tuned with VeriBench training examples included. Compare performance on VeriBench test set vs. a held-out novel task set. Quantify contamination inflation.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Benchmark contamination through fine-tuning is the most direct path to score inflation in formal verification. If the industry does not control for this, competition-driven benchmark optimization could produce models that score high but cannot verify novel programs.
