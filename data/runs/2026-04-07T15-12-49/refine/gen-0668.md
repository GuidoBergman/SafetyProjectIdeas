# Research Question

METR's red-team of Anthropic monitoring was ad hoc — no standard methodology exists for red-teaming AI monitoring systems, making results incomparable across organizations. This project investigates whether draft a standardized red-team protocol for ai monitoring systems (attack categories, success metrics, scope definitions).

# Approach Outline

Draft a standardized red-team protocol for AI monitoring systems (attack categories, success metrics, scope definitions). Pilot the protocol against a simple open-source monitoring setup. Publish the protocol as a proposed standard.
