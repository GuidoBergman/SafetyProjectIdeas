# Research Question

AI cyber capability benchmarks use environments with a fixed complexity level. This project investigates whether design a series of pentesting environments with varying complexity: (a) 3 services, 1 vulnerability, (b) 10 services, 2 vulnerabilities, (c) 30 services, 3 vulnerabilities, (d) 100 services simulated, 5 vulnerabilities.

# Approach Outline

Design a series of pentesting environments with varying complexity: (A) 3 services, 1 vulnerability, (B) 10 services, 2 vulnerabilities, (C) 30 services, 3 vulnerabilities, (D) 100 services simulated, 5 vulnerabilities. Run AI agents on all 4 environments. Plot performance vs. complexity. Test whether degradation is linear, exponential, or shows a complexity threshold effect.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If AI performance degrades gracefully with complexity, real enterprise networks (much more complex than CTF environments) may be naturally protected by their complexity. If AI performance is complexity-tolerant, the threat to real complex environments is as severe as CTF benchmark scores suggest.
