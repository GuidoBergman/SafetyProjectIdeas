# Research Question

Two interacting failures: (1) AI safety controls on vulnerability-finding capabilities can be bypassed (jailbreaks, fine-tuning); (2) capable vulnerability-finding AI is rapidly diffusing to smaller, less-resourced actors. This project investigates whether document the gap between official safety controls on ai vulnerability research tools (published by anthropic, openai) and demonstrated bypass techniques (from jailbreak literature).

# Approach Outline

Document the gap between official safety controls on AI vulnerability research tools (published by Anthropic, OpenAI) and demonstrated bypass techniques (from jailbreak literature). Measure: what fraction of safety controls have documented bypasses? What is the technical sophistication required for bypass? Estimate effective democratization rate.
