# Research Question

Compilation rate (the primary VeriBench metric) does not capture: proof quality, spec completeness, human review time, runtime behavior. This project investigates whether propose and operationalize 5 additional metrics beyond compilation rate: (1) spec completeness score, (2) proof minimality ratio, (3) vacuity rate, (4) human review time per proof, (5) mutation survival rate.

# Approach Outline

Propose and operationalize 5 additional metrics beyond compilation rate: (1) spec completeness score, (2) proof minimality ratio, (3) vacuity rate, (4) human review time per proof, (5) mutation survival rate. Measure all 5 on 30 VeriBench tasks across 3 LLMs. Publish as a multi-dimensional evaluation framework.
