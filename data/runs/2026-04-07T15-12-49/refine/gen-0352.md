# Research Question

AI labs publish various transparency reports, safety cards, and risk reports. This project investigates whether define 20 categories of information that a complete dual-use cybersecurity governance disclosure should contain (capability evaluations, refusal policy, incident record, audit results, trigger criteria, etc.

# Approach Outline

Define 20 categories of information that a complete dual-use cybersecurity governance disclosure should contain (capability evaluations, refusal policy, incident record, audit results, trigger criteria, etc. ). For each lab (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta), score their current transparency disclosures against each category. Produce a disclosure coverage matrix.
