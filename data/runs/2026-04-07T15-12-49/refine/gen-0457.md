# Research Question

The Irregular 2026 auth reverse engineering finding is surprising because no agent was given offensive training. This project investigates whether when agents reverse-engineer a mock auth system, prompt them to explain their reasoning step by step.

# Approach Outline

When agents reverse-engineer a mock auth system, prompt them to explain their reasoning step by step. Analyze the chain-of-thought for references to specific techniques (e. g. , SQL injection, JWT decode, brute force). Map the techniques to their likely pretraining data sources. Quantify how often the exploited technique matches known offensive security content.
