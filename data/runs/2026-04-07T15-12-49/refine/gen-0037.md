# Research Question

AISI's multi-step cyber range evaluations use proprietary infrastructure and closed frontier models, making independent replication impossible. This project investigates whether design an open-source 10-step attack chain replicating the spirit of aisi's corporate network range using: free infrastructure (tryhackme, hackthebox free tier, or local vms), open-weight models (llama 3, mistral, qwen), and publicly documented scaffolding.

# Approach Outline

Run and publish results. Compare performance profiles to AISI's published findings.
