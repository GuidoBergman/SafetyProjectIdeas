# Research Question

In long proof sessions, Lean 4 provers can prove auxiliary lemmas and reuse them. This project investigates whether set up 20 multi-lemma lean 4 proof tasks where earlier proved lemmas are relevant to later steps.

# Approach Outline

Set up 20 multi-lemma Lean 4 proof tasks where earlier proved lemmas are relevant to later steps. Track whether LLMs: (a) reference earlier lemmas by name, (b) redundantly reprove them, (c) fail to use them when relevant. Measure reuse failure rate.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Proof reuse is a fundamental efficiency mechanism in formal verification. If LLMs fail to exploit earlier work, proof sessions are wasteful and the self-optimizing trace architecture may not scale to longer proofs.
