# Research Question

Defenders face a trust calibration problem: when should they act on an AI finding without human verification? Decision theory can formalize this. This project investigates whether construct a bayesian decision model for a defender choosing whether to act immediately on an ai vulnerability report or delay for human verification.

# Approach Outline

Construct a Bayesian decision model for a defender choosing whether to act immediately on an AI vulnerability report or delay for human verification. Parameters: AI detection accuracy (from literature), cost of a false positive, cost of a missed exploit (from breach cost data), verification delay. Compute optimal thresholds. Validate against a synthetic scenario.
