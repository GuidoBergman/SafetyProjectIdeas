# Research Question

MITRE OCCULT identifies the human-AI copilot paradigm as the most realistic near-term threat but the least studied. This project investigates whether design a controlled study where cs students with no security background (1) attempt a 5-step ctf-style challenge alone, (2) use an llm autonomously, and (3) use an llm in copilot mode (human provides high-level decisions, ai executes).

# Approach Outline

Design a controlled study where CS students with no security background (1) attempt a 5-step CTF-style challenge alone, (2) use an LLM autonomously, and (3) use an LLM in copilot mode (human provides high-level decisions, AI executes). Measure steps completed, time, and errors in each condition. Compute a 'copilot amplification factor'.
