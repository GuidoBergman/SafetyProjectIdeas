# Research Question

Anthropic voluntarily published the GTG-1002 and vibe hacking reports, but no standardized disclosure framework governs what information labs must report when AI misuse incidents occur. This project investigates whether define the ideal state: a standardized, mandatory ai cyber incident report comparable to sec cybersecurity incident disclosure rules (4-business-day reporting).

# Approach Outline

Define the ideal state: a standardized, mandatory AI cyber incident report comparable to SEC cybersecurity incident disclosure rules (4-business-day reporting). Work backward: what incident categories trigger reporting? What information must be disclosed? Draft a model AI Cyber Incident Disclosure Framework. Compare against current voluntary practices.
