# Research Question

For developers to act on AI security findings, the feedback must be understandable and actionable. This project investigates whether define 'developer-trusted' as: developer correctly identifies and patches the vulnerability after reading the ai explanation, without external lookup.

# Approach Outline

Define 'developer-trusted' as: developer correctly identifies and patches the vulnerability after reading the AI explanation, without external lookup. Run a 15-person user study with AI explanations of varying quality (short/vague, medium/structured, long/detailed). Measure patch correctness per explanation quality level. Identify the minimum sufficient quality.
