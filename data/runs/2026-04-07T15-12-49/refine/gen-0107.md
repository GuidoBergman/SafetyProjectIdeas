# Research Question

AI agents may be noisy attackers — using more tool invocations and generating more detectable activity than human attackers. This project investigates whether solve 10 ctf challenges with 3 conditions: (1) ai agent with no efficiency constraint, (2) ai agent explicitly prompted to minimize tool invocations, (3) human expert attempting same challenges.

# Approach Outline

Solve 10 CTF challenges with 3 conditions: (1) AI agent with no efficiency constraint, (2) AI agent explicitly prompted to minimize tool invocations, (3) human expert attempting same challenges. Count total tool invocations per challenge per condition. Compute 'attack noise ratio' (AI invocations / human invocations). Test whether efficiency-constrained prompting significantly reduces noise.
