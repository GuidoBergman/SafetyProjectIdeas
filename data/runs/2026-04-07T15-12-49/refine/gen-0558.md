# Research Question

If multiple LLMs fail on the same Lean 4 problems, a single LLM ensemble is unlikely to solve them. This project investigates whether run 5 llms on all veribench tasks.

# Approach Outline

Run 5 LLMs on all VeriBench tasks. Compute Jaccard similarity of their failure sets. Identify problems that all models fail (irreducible hard set) vs. problems where at least one model succeeds (coverage-improvable set). Measure what fraction of failures could be solved by an oracle ensemble.

# Theory of Impact Chain

High overlap in failure sets would indicate systematic reasoning gaps common to all current LLMs — a genuine AI safety concern suggesting model homogeneity risks. Low overlap would indicate ensemble strategies are a viable path to near-term improvement.
