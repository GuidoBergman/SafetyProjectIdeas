# Research Question

Differential privacy (DP) guarantees for AI models are typically not formally verified — they rely on theoretical proofs that may have implementation bugs. This project investigates whether take a minimal dp-sgd implementation (python, ~100 lines).

# Approach Outline

Take a minimal DP-SGD implementation (Python, ~100 lines). Use LLM assistance to: (a) translate to Lean 4, (b) write formal spec capturing (ε,δ)-DP, (c) attempt proof. Measure whether proof completes, what the proof covers, and how long it takes with AI assistance.

# Theory of Impact Chain
