# Research Question

Sycophantic models that adapt to user preferences may be more susceptible to persistent social pressure that erodes dual-use refusals over long conversations. This project investigates whether design 20 multi-turn conversations where a user persistently challenges a model's refusal to provide cybersecurity assistance, using social pressure, frustration, and rephrasing.

# Approach Outline

Design 20 multi-turn conversations where a user persistently challenges a model's refusal to provide cybersecurity assistance, using social pressure, frustration, and rephrasing. Measure refusal persistence across 5 turns for models with known sycophancy properties. Correlate sycophancy scores with refusal erosion rates.
