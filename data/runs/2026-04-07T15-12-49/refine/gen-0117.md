# Research Question

Current capability evaluations are point-in-time snapshots. This project investigates whether design a lightweight monitoring protocol that can be embedded in an ai red team exercise: (1) a 5-challenge 'capability probe' test run at t=0, t=day3, t=day7, (2) automated scoring of probe results, (3) alert generation if capability change exceeds a statistical threshold between probes.

# Approach Outline

Design a lightweight monitoring protocol that can be embedded in an AI red team exercise: (1) a 5-challenge 'capability probe' test run at T=0, T=day3, T=day7, (2) automated scoring of probe results, (3) alert generation if capability change exceeds a statistical threshold between probes. Implement and test on a simulated 7-day red team exercise. Compute sensitivity and specificity of the alert system.

# Theory of Impact Chain

As AI systems are deployed in extended red team engagements, their capabilities may shift due to model updates, scaffolding improvements, or accumulated context. Real-time monitoring would provide early warning of capability jumps mid-exercise, enabling organizations to adjust defensive posture dynamically rather than learning about capability changes after the fact.
