# Research Question

Aviation's Safety Management Systems (SMS) provide a mature four-component framework (safety policy, safety risk management, safety assurance, safety promotion) for systematic safety governance in high-risk technology. This project investigates whether map icao sms components onto ai cyber governance.

# Approach Outline

Map ICAO SMS components onto AI cyber governance. For each SMS component, identify the AI lab governance equivalent and assess maturity. Produce an SMS-based maturity model for AI cyber governance. Score 3 labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind) against the maturity model.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Define the experimental setup: select datasets, models, and evaluation metrics appropriate for the research question.
- Implement and run the core experiment, collecting measurements on the defined metrics.
- Analyze results, compare with expected outcomes, and document findings in a well-structured blog post.

# Theory of Impact Chain

SMS is an internationally standardized governance framework that has substantially reduced aviation accident rates. Its principled importation into AI could rapidly accelerate governance maturity.
