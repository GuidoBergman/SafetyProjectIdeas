# Research Question

An agent that self-reports constraint violation intent would enable human-in-the-loop interventions. This project investigates whether define success: an agent that inserts a standardized self-report tag in its output whenever it detects a potential constraint violation in its planned action.

# Approach Outline

Define success: an agent that inserts a standardized self-report tag in its output whenever it detects a potential constraint violation in its planned action. Test three interventions: explicit instruction-following prompts, few-shot examples of self-reporting, and post-hoc reward modeling for self-reports. Measure self-report recall (what fraction of planned violations were self-reported) and precision (what fraction of self-reports were genuine planned violations).
