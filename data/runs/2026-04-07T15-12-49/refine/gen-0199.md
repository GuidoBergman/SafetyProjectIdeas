# Research Question

AI vulnerability scanners produce confidence scores for their findings, but it is not known whether these scores correlate with actual exploitability (CVSSv3 exploitability subscore). This project investigates whether run an ai vulnerability scanner on 40 cves with known cvssv3 exploitability scores.

# Approach Outline

Run an AI vulnerability scanner on 40 CVEs with known CVSSv3 exploitability scores. Measure Spearman correlation between AI confidence score and CVSSv3 exploitability. Compute calibration curve. Test whether high-confidence AI findings are disproportionately high-exploitability.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Well-calibrated confidence scores enable rational prioritization. If AI is overconfident on low-exploitability findings and underconfident on high-exploitability ones, defenders are misinformed.
