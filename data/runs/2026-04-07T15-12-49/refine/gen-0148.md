# Research Question

When AI tools give confident-sounding security verdicts, developers may stop independent verification. This project investigates whether run a controlled experiment: give 20 cs students a codebase with 5 planted vulnerabilities.

# Approach Outline

Run a controlled experiment: give 20 CS students a codebase with 5 planted vulnerabilities. Group A receives AI verdicts only. Group B receives AI verdicts with explicit uncertainty quantification. Group C receives no AI assistance. Measure detection rate per group.

# Theory of Impact Chain
