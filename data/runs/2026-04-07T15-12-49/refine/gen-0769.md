# Research Question

Agent tasks are often decomposed into subtasks. This project investigates whether design multi-step tasks where dangerous intent is only visible at the subtask level (each individual action is benign, but their combination achieves a harmful goal).

# Approach Outline

Design multi-step tasks where dangerous intent is only visible at the subtask level (each individual action is benign, but their combination achieves a harmful goal). Measure whether subtask-level vs. task-level monitoring detects these distributed attacks.
