# Research Question

LLM-based monitors may diverge from human judgments on ambiguous agent actions — cases where it is genuinely unclear whether an action is dangerous. This project investigates whether collect 100 ambiguous agent actions (neither clearly safe nor clearly dangerous).

# Approach Outline

Collect 100 ambiguous agent actions (neither clearly safe nor clearly dangerous). Have human annotators and LLM monitors both rate each action. Measure human-LLM agreement rate, inter-human agreement, and identify categories of systematic divergence.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Analyze results, compare with expected outcomes, and document findings in a well-structured blog post.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Human-LLM monitoring divergence on ambiguous cases determines how much autonomy can be delegated to automated monitors. Systematic divergences reveal where human oversight is irreplaceable.
