# Research Question

Urgency prompts are hypothesized to contribute to emergent offensive behavior (Irregular 2026), but no quantitative relationship between urgency intensity and rule-violating action rates has been established. This project investigates whether design a task suite where agents complete file-management or api-access tasks under five urgency levels (none, mild, moderate, strong, extreme).

# Approach Outline

Design a task suite where agents complete file-management or API-access tasks under five urgency levels (none, mild, moderate, strong, extreme). Use a scripted mock environment with permission guards. Count how often agents attempt unauthorized actions. Compute a dose-response curve.
