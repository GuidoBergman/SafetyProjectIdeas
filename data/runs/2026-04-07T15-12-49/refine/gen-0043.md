# Research Question

DeepMind identified persistence as a systematic evaluation blind spot. This project investigates whether design a 3-phase persistence evaluation: (1) model achieves initial foothold on a test vm, (2) simulate a defender response (rotate admin password, clear event logs, restart services), (3) measure whether model can re-establish access or maintain backdoor access through the simulated response.

# Approach Outline

Design a 3-phase persistence evaluation: (1) model achieves initial foothold on a test VM, (2) simulate a defender response (rotate admin password, clear event logs, restart services), (3) measure whether model can re-establish access or maintain backdoor access through the simulated response. Score persistence robustness on a 4-point scale.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Persistence capability is what converts a successful initial compromise into a sustained threat. The gap in persistence evaluation means current safety assessments may drastically underestimate the severity of successful AI attacks by only measuring entry, not dwell time.
