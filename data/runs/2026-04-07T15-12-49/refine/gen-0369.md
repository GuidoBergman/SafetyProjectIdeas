# Research Question

NIST AI 800-1 prescribes risk management processes for dual-use models, but these processes were designed assuming good-faith compliance. This project investigates whether for each of nist ai 800-1's key risk management provisions (capability evaluation, red teaming, deployment controls, monitoring), design a plausible adversarial gaming strategy that satisfies the letter but not the spirit of the requirement.

# Approach Outline

For each of NIST AI 800-1's key risk management provisions (capability evaluation, red teaming, deployment controls, monitoring), design a plausible adversarial gaming strategy that satisfies the letter but not the spirit of the requirement. Analyze 10 provisions. Categorize failure modes by type (gaming, circumvention, regulatory capture).
