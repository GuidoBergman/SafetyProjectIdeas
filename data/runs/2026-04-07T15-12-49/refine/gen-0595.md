# Research Question

Large monolithic Lean 4 proof goals are harder than small, decomposed ones. This project investigates whether for 30 veribench tasks, implement two conditions: (a) full proof goal passed to llm, (b) goal automatically split into sub-goals (using lean's intro, cases, constructor tactics as scaffolding), then each sub-goal passed to llm.
