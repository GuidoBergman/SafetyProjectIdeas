# Research Question

More sensitive monitors catch more attacks but also restrict more benign actions. This project investigates whether vary the decision threshold of a monitoring classifier across a wide range.

# Approach Outline

Vary the decision threshold of a monitoring classifier across a wide range. Measure safety (attack detection rate) and utility (benign task completion rate) at each threshold. Plot the safety-utility Pareto frontier. Identify threshold recommendations for different risk tolerances.

# Theory of Impact Chain
