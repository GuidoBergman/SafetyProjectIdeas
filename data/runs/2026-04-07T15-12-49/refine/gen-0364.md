# Research Question

No standardized benchmark exists measuring how much AI assistance accelerates legitimate incident response tasks (forensics, log analysis, threat hunting), a critical metric for assessing the defensive value of dual-use capabilities. This project investigates whether design 20 realistic incident response tasks (parse siem logs, identify c2 traffic in packet capture, write remediation script, draft incident report).

# Approach Outline

Design 20 realistic incident response tasks (parse SIEM logs, identify C2 traffic in packet capture, write remediation script, draft incident report). Measure task completion time and quality for humans alone vs. humans with frontier AI assistance. Compute AI uplift ratio per task category.
