# Research Question

CSLib targets computer science algorithms (sorting, searching, graphs); Mathlib targets pure mathematics (topology, algebra, number theory). This project investigates whether sample 30 cs-style problems (algorithm verification) and 30 math-style problems of similar proof length from lean 4 benchmarks.

# Approach Outline

Sample 30 CS-style problems (algorithm verification) and 30 math-style problems of similar proof length from Lean 4 benchmarks. Run the same LLMs on both sets. Measure compilation rate, proof success rate, and failure mode distribution. Test whether CS problems are systematically harder or easier.
