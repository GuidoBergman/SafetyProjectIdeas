# Research Question

Arms control treaties use 'functional equivalence' to prevent parties from developing weapons that are functionally identical to banned weapons under different names. This project investigates whether analyze how functional equivalence is defined in the chemical weapons convention and biological weapons convention.

# Approach Outline

Analyze how functional equivalence is defined in the Chemical Weapons Convention and Biological Weapons Convention. Develop an analogous 'functional equivalence' test for AI cybersecurity capabilities. Apply it to 10 AI capabilities near current governance thresholds to test whether functionally equivalent capabilities are covered or evade existing frameworks.
