# Research Question

NIST AI 800-1 describes a risk management methodology for dual-use models, but it's unknown whether frontier models apply anything resembling this methodology when deciding how to respond. This project investigates whether design 20 borderline cybersecurity prompts.

# Approach Outline

Design 20 borderline cybersecurity prompts. Prompt models to reason aloud about whether to answer. Analyze the reasoning traces for alignment with NIST AI 800-1's risk management dimensions. Code reasoning by whether it considers: capability evaluation, deployment context, user intent, counterfactual availability. Compare across models.
