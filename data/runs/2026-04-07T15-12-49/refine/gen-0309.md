Governance frameworks are written for various audiences. This project investigates whether recruit 10 cs students to read a governance framework excerpt (rsp cyber section, preparedness framework cyber tier).

# Approach Outline

Recruit 10 CS students to read a governance framework excerpt (RSP cyber section, Preparedness Framework cyber tier). Ask them to extract 10 testable behavioral criteria. Measure precision/recall against expert-identified criteria. Compute readability scores (Flesch-Kincaid, etc. ).
