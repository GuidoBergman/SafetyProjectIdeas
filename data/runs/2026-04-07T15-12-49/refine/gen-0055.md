# Research Question

MITRE ATT&CK has hundreds of sub-techniques. This project investigates whether review published ai cyber evaluation papers (aisi, deepmind, occult, cybench, nyu ctf bench, caibench) and map each challenge to its corresponding att&ck technique.

# Approach Outline

Review published AI cyber evaluation papers (AISI, DeepMind, OCCULT, Cybench, NYU CTF Bench, CAIBench) and map each challenge to its corresponding ATT&CK technique. Build a coverage matrix. Identify techniques with zero published evaluations. For the top 5 uncovered high-severity techniques, design minimal test challenges. Run one frontier model on these uncovered techniques.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Unknown capability is worse than known capability. Techniques that have never been evaluated may represent either untapped attack vectors or genuine AI weaknesses. Systematic coverage mapping is a prerequisite for any claim that the evaluation landscape is comprehensive.
