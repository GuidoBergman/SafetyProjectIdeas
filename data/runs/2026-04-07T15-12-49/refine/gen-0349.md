This project investigates whether analyze 3 legal frameworks (us cfaa, eu ai act liability provisions, uk computer misuse act) for how they would assign liability in an ai-assisted cyberattack equivalent to gtg-1002.

# Approach Outline

Analyze 3 legal frameworks (US CFAA, EU AI Act liability provisions, UK Computer Misuse Act) for how they would assign liability in an AI-assisted cyberattack equivalent to GTG-1002.
