Agent capabilities and attack techniques evolve rapidly. This project investigates whether train a monitoring classifier on attacks from an earlier time period (using dated red-team datasets).

# Approach Outline

Train a monitoring classifier on attacks from an earlier time period (using dated red-team datasets). Evaluate it against more recent attack patterns. Measure performance degradation. Characterize how quickly monitors decay and what update frequency is needed.
