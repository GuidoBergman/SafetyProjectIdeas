# Research Question

Two interacting failure modes: (1) AI generates highly convincing spear phishing text (54% click-through rate, matching human attackers), and (2) AI voice/video deepfakes can impersonate known individuals. This project investigates whether design a controlled study (using fictional personas with explicit participant consent) comparing: (a) ai text-only phishing, (b) ai text + automated voice deepfake call (using a free tts service, not actual deepfakes), (c) human-crafted baseline.

# Approach Outline

Design a controlled study (using fictional personas with explicit participant consent) comparing: (A) AI text-only phishing, (B) AI text + automated voice deepfake call (using a free TTS service, not actual deepfakes), (C) human-crafted baseline. Use scenario rating scales for quality assessment rather than live deception. Measure believability scores from human raters for each condition.
