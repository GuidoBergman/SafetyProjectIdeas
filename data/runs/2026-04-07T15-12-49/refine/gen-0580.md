# Research Question

Current Lean 4 benchmarks (VeriBench, CLEVER) are designed to test capability, not robustness. This project investigates whether create an adversarial benchmark of 50 lean 4 tasks designed to exploit known llm weaknesses: (a) misleading variable/function names, (b) proofs requiring non-obvious lemmas, (c) problems where the most natural proof approach is wrong.

# Approach Outline
