# Research Question

Governance frameworks focus on preventing offensive misuse but may under-specify when and how models should actively support defensive security work. This project investigates whether build a taxonomy of 50 legitimate defensive cybersecurity tasks (malware analysis, incident response, patch generation, threat modeling).

# Approach Outline

Build a taxonomy of 50 legitimate defensive cybersecurity tasks (malware analysis, incident response, patch generation, threat modeling). For each task, score whether Anthropic RSP, OpenAI Preparedness Framework, and NIST AI 800-1 explicitly permit, implicitly permit, or are silent. Compute a 'defense coverage score'.
