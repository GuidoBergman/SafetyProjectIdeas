# Research Question

AI capability is always reported in isolation, but the threat model requires comparison to human baselines at different skill levels (script kiddie, junior pentester, senior red teamer). This project investigates whether recruit 10-15 cs students with no security background and 5-10 with some ctf experience.

# Approach Outline

Recruit 10-15 CS students with no security background and 5-10 with some CTF experience. Have each attempt a standardized 10-challenge set (same challenges run against AI). Record time-to-completion, hints used, and steps completed. Generate a 'human capability distribution'. Plot AI performance against this distribution to answer: 'at what skill percentile does AI operate?'.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Saying AI completes 22/32 steps means nothing without knowing whether a median human does 5/32 or 28/32. Human baseline calibration converts raw AI scores into threat-relevant comparisons ('AI operates at the 80th percentile of professional pentesters') that policymakers and defenders can act on.
