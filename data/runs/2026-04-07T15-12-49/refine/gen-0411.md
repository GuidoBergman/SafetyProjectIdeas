# Research Question

Chain-of-thought reasoning has been shown to improve model performance on complex tasks. This project investigates whether test 50 borderline cybersecurity requests in two conditions: (a) direct response, (b) forced chain-of-thought reasoning about dual-use tradeoffs before responding.

# Approach Outline

Test 50 borderline cybersecurity requests in two conditions: (a) direct response, (b) forced chain-of-thought reasoning about dual-use tradeoffs before responding. Measure refusal rate changes and calibration (false positive and false negative rates) across both conditions for 3 models.
