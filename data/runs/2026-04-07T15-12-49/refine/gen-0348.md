# Research Question

For Global South countries to use tiered access programs, they may need to submit to US/EU identity verification infrastructure, potentially ceding digital sovereignty. This project investigates whether analyze 4 tiered access frameworks (openai trusted access, anthropic rsp, eu gpai code, nist ai 800-1) for sovereignty implications of their identity and monitoring provisions.

# Approach Outline

Analyze 4 tiered access frameworks (OpenAI Trusted Access, Anthropic RSP, EU GPAI Code, NIST AI 800-1) for sovereignty implications of their identity and monitoring provisions.
