# Research Question

Real attack scenarios have temporal constraints (discovery before timeout, maintaining access during active defender response). This project investigates whether run 15 ctf challenges under: (a) unlimited time (standard evaluation), (b) 5-minute time limit per step (moderate pressure), (c) 2-minute time limit per step (high pressure).

# Approach Outline

Run 15 CTF challenges under: (A) unlimited time (standard evaluation), (B) 5-minute time limit per step (moderate pressure), (C) 2-minute time limit per step (high pressure). Measure completion rate and quality of completed steps under each condition. Identify whether time pressure causes specific failure modes (incomplete tool invocation, reasoning shortcuts, premature commitment to wrong hypotheses).

# Theory of Impact Chain

If AI offensive capability significantly degrades under realistic time constraints, current time-unlimited benchmarks dramatically overestimate real-world threat levels. If AI is time-pressure resilient, it is a qualitatively different threat than human attackers (who also degrade under pressure).
