# Research Question

Real attack environments contain false positives, honeypots, and misleading defender signals designed to waste attacker resources. This project investigates whether take a 10-step attack chain.

# Approach Outline

Take a 10-step attack chain. Create two variants: (A) clean environment with accurate information, (B) adversarial environment with 30% of environmental signals falsified (fake open ports, misleading service banners, honeypot directories). Run models on both. Measure completion rate delta. Categorize model failures as: (a) failed to identify deception, (b) identified deception but wrong response, (c) correctly adapted.
