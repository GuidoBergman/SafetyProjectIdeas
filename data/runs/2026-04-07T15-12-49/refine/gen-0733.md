# Research Question

AI agent safety incidents (goal hijacking, unauthorized actions, collusion, exfiltration) are not systematically collected or categorized. This project investigates whether collect all publicly reported ai agent safety incidents (including red-team reports, bug bounty disclosures, news reports).

# Approach Outline

Collect all publicly reported AI agent safety incidents (including red-team reports, bug bounty disclosures, news reports). Build a taxonomy and structured database. Analyze incident patterns: which attack types are most common, what systems are targeted, what monitoring failures occurred.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Conduct a systematic literature review to identify and categorize relevant prior work.
- Develop a classification framework and apply it to the collected corpus.
- Synthesize findings into a structured analysis with key takeaways for the AI safety community.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A systematic incident database is foundational for empirical safety research. It enables base-rate estimation, trend analysis, and learning from real-world failures.
