# Research Question

AISI estimated 22/32 steps corresponds to ~6 of 14 expert-hours. This project investigates whether run ai agents on 20 challenges spanning easy, medium, and hard difficulty.

# Approach Outline

Run AI agents on 20 challenges spanning easy, medium, and hard difficulty. Record total wall-clock time, token usage, and tool invocation count per challenge. Plot time-to-completion distributions. Identify which challenge types complete in under 1 hour (operationally stealthy), 1-8 hours (overnight attack window), and >8 hours (requiring sustained operation).

# Theory of Impact Chain

An attack completing in under 1 hour may not trigger time-based anomaly detection. Attacks requiring sustained operation create higher detection probability. Understanding time distributions enables defenders to calibrate detection windows and tells evaluators which attacks are currently 'practically feasible' vs. theoretically possible.
