# Research Question

GTG-1002 decomposed espionage into phases (reconnaissance, exploitation, credential harvesting, lateral movement, extraction). This project investigates whether map the gtg-1002 attack chain into 6 causal stages.

# Approach Outline

Map the GTG-1002 attack chain into 6 causal stages. For each stage, identify: (a) which AI capabilities were required, (b) what governance intervention could have blocked that stage, (c) how costly the intervention would be for legitimate users. Rank stages by intervention leverage.
