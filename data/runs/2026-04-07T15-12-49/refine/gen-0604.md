# Research Question

Concurrency bugs (data races, deadlocks, TOCTOU vulnerabilities) are among the hardest to detect with existing tools. This project investigates whether take 15 simple concurrent programs (producer-consumer, reader-writer, mutual exclusion).

# Approach Outline

Take 15 simple concurrent programs (producer-consumer, reader-writer, mutual exclusion). Write Lean 4 models of each. Ask LLMs to prove safety properties (mutual exclusion, absence of deadlock, absence of data race). Measure success rate and characterize failure modes.
