# Research Question

A structured multi-session interaction where each session provides one piece of a harmful puzzle (separately innocuous) may never trigger per-session refusals despite providing full harmful uplift cumulatively. This project investigates whether design 10 cybersecurity topics where full exploitation knowledge requires 5 separate sub-topics.

# Approach Outline

Design 10 cybersecurity topics where full exploitation knowledge requires 5 separate sub-topics. Create 5-session conversation sequences where each session requests one sub-topic innocuously. After all 5 sessions, test whether the combined information provides material uplift. Test across 3 models.
