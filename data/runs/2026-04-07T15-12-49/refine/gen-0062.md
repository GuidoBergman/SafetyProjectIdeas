# Research Question

The economics of AI-assisted attacks (cost per successful compromise) determine at what scale and by which threat actors AI becomes viable. This project investigates whether analyze publicly available pricing data and performance benchmarks to compute cost-per-successful-compromise for 5 attack categories (web application, active directory, social engineering, binary exploitation, ics).

# Approach Outline

Analyze publicly available pricing data and performance benchmarks to compute cost-per-successful-compromise for 5 attack categories (web application, Active Directory, social engineering, binary exploitation, ICS). Build a cost breakdown model: API costs + scaffolding overhead + retry costs (given stochastic success rates). Compare costs to human expert rates in each category.

- Run the evaluation protocol across target conditions, collecting quantitative measurements.
- Compare results against baselines, analyze failure modes, and write up findings.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If AI is cost-competitive with human experts in some attack categories but not others, this creates predictable patterns in where AI-assisted attacks will actually appear first. Cost analysis is fundamental to threat modeling for defenders and informs resource allocation for AI safety evaluation.
