# Research Question

Inference-time poisoning — injecting adversarial content into the context window to alter model behavior — is a poorly characterized attack. This project investigates whether implement three context-window poisoning techniques (indirect prompt injection, context stuffing, role reversal) against a small open model.

# Approach Outline

Implement three context-window poisoning techniques (indirect prompt injection, context stuffing, role reversal) against a small open model. Measure attack success rates across benign task completion. Implement a simple context sanitization defense and measure its effectiveness and false-positive rate.
