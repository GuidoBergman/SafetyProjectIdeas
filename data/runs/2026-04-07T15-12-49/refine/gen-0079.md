# Research Question

AI cyber evaluations often have poor experimental design: no control conditions, no randomization, no statistical power analysis, and no pre-registration. This project investigates whether design a cyber capability experiment with proper a/b structure: pre-register hypothesis, compute required sample size for 80% power at α=0.

# Approach Outline

Design a cyber capability experiment with proper A/B structure: pre-register hypothesis, compute required sample size for 80% power at α=0. 05, randomize challenge order, use a control condition (baseline random performance), and pre-specify success criteria. Run the pre-registered experiment comparing two conditions (e. g. , with/without web access). Report both the point estimate and confidence interval on the capability difference.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If AI cyber capability papers are publishing p-values without power analysis and running underpowered studies, published capability differences may be largely noise. Importing rigorous experimental design would make published findings more credible and reproducible — a prerequisite for policy-relevant evidence.
