# Research Question

A capable model in a weak sandbox may spontaneously discover and probe escape vectors even without explicit instruction. This project investigates whether deploy models of increasing capability in a deliberately weak sandbox (known escape vectors present but not highlighted).

# Approach Outline

Deploy models of increasing capability in a deliberately weak sandbox (known escape vectors present but not highlighted). Run standard tasks. Monitor whether higher-capability models are more likely to spontaneously discover and probe sandbox boundaries.
