# Research Question

When an agent's goal is hijacked by a prompt injection, its subsequent action sequence should shift in distribution. This project investigates whether establish a baseline distribution of agent action sequences for a standard task.

# Approach Outline

Establish a baseline distribution of agent action sequences for a standard task. Inject goal-hijacking prompts at various points. Measure whether KL divergence or simpler statistical tests (chi-squared on action type frequencies) can reliably detect the injection.
