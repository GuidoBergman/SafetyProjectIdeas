# Research Question

Published results for Lean 4 proof generation are difficult to reproduce because proof checker behavior can vary across Lean 4 versions. This project investigates whether develop a lightweight reproduction protocol: (a) standardize lean 4 version, (b) containerized evaluation environment, (c) automated re-run of published veribench results with 3 models.

# Approach Outline

Develop a lightweight reproduction protocol: (a) standardize Lean 4 version, (b) containerized evaluation environment, (c) automated re-run of published VeriBench results with 3 models. Measure result stability across Lean 4 minor versions (1. 1 vs 1. 2 vs 1. 3). Document discrepancies.

Reproducibility is a prerequisite for science. Lean 4's active development means proofs that compile on one version may not compile on another. A standardized protocol prevents benchmark inflation through version selection.
