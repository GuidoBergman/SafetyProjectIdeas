# Research Question

Safety instructions typically appear at the beginning of system prompts. This project investigates whether insert safety constraints at different positions in the context window (beginning, middle, end) for extended agentic tasks.

# Approach Outline

Insert safety constraints at different positions in the context window (beginning, middle, end) for extended agentic tasks. Measure constraint violation rates as a function of constraint position and context length. Determine whether there is a 'forgetting distance' beyond which safety instructions become ineffective.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If safety instructions degrade with context distance, long-running agentic tasks may silently lose safety constraints without any detectable error. This implies safety instructions need to be repeated or reinforced during extended tasks.
