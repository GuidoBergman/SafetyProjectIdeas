# Research Question

As AI models gain persistent memory, adversaries can invest in building long-term 'trust' with a specific model instance before gradually escalating to harmful requests—a slow-burn governance attack. This project investigates whether simulate persistent ai sessions using claude projects or similar memory-enabled configurations.

# Approach Outline

Simulate persistent AI sessions using Claude Projects or similar memory-enabled configurations. Over 20 sessions, establish a helpful, trusted persona. In session 21-25, escalate to borderline cybersecurity requests. Measure whether historical benign interaction reduces refusal rates vs. cold-start equivalents.
