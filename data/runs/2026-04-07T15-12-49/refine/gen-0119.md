# Research Question

Governments are considering export controls on advanced AI models partly due to offensive cyber capability concerns. This project investigates whether review export control frameworks for dual-use technology (wassenaar arrangement, ear).

# Approach Outline

Review export control frameworks for dual-use technology (Wassenaar Arrangement, EAR). Extract the technical criteria used for conventional dual-use technology controls (e. g. , 'processing speed exceeding X'). Translate these criteria to AI offensive cyber capability terms. Design a measurement instrument that would produce data matching the required evidentiary standard. Run a pilot to validate the instrument produces consistent, interpretable results.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Define the experimental setup: select datasets, models, and evaluation metrics appropriate for the research question.
- Implement and run the core experiment, collecting measurements on the defined metrics.
- Analyze results, compare with expected outcomes, and document findings in a well-structured blog post.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Export control decisions made without clear technical criteria will be arbitrary, contested, and potentially counterproductive. Providing a rigorous measurement foundation for export control determinations would improve AI governance quality and enable consistent cross-jurisdiction enforcement.
