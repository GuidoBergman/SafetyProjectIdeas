# Research Question

Two interacting failure modes: (1) AI offensive capability is growing rapidly, and (2) no standardized evaluation protocol exists, so each lab uses different methodologies. This project investigates whether collect methodology descriptions from 5 published ai cyber evaluations (aisi, deepmind, occult, cybench, aixcc).

# Approach Outline

Collect methodology descriptions from 5 published AI cyber evaluations (AISI, DeepMind, OCCULT, Cybench, AIxCC). For each, extract: token budget, scaffolding type, target environment, evaluation time limits, success criteria, human baseline inclusion. Compute a methodology compatibility matrix. Propose a minimum standardization protocol that would enable cross-study comparison and test whether applying it to 2 studies yields more comparable results.

# Theory of Impact Chain
