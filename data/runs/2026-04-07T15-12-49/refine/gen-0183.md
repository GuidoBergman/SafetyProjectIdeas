# Research Question

Open-source projects often lack explicit security requirements. This project investigates whether for 10 open-source projects with known cves, use an llm to infer implicit security requirements from source code, documentation, and commit history.

# Approach Outline

For 10 open-source projects with known CVEs, use an LLM to infer implicit security requirements from source code, documentation, and commit history. Compare inferred requirements to requirements explicitly violated by each CVE. Measure: what fraction of CVEs reflect requirement violations that the AI could have predicted?.
