# Research Question

OCCULT's copilot paradigm assumes human-AI teaming is more dangerous than AI-alone, but it is unclear whether AI equally amplifies operators at different skill levels. This project investigates whether recruit two groups: (a) 8-10 cs students with no security background, (b) 5-8 participants with ctf experience.

# Approach Outline

Recruit two groups: (A) 8-10 CS students with no security background, (B) 5-8 participants with CTF experience. Both groups attempt the same 8-step attack scenario (1) alone, (2) with AI copilot assistance. Measure absolute performance gain (steps completed alone vs. with AI) and relative gain (% improvement) for each group. Compare novice vs. expert amplification factors.

# Theory of Impact Chain
