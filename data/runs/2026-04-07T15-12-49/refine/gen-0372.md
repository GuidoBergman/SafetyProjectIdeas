# Research Question

Nuclear risk assessment developed Structured Expert Elicitation (SEE) methods (Delphi, probability elicitation, calibration training) for situations where empirical data is scarce. This project investigates whether study see methodology from nuclear and biological risk assessment literature.

# Approach Outline

Study SEE methodology from nuclear and biological risk assessment literature. Design a 3-round Delphi study on 5 AI cyber capability threshold questions (e. g. , 'When will an AI system be able to autonomously discover a novel 0-day in a major OS?'). Administer to 10 cybersecurity experts. Report calibrated probability distributions.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Define the experimental setup: select datasets, models, and evaluation metrics appropriate for the research question.
- Implement and run the core experiment, collecting measurements on the defined metrics.
- Analyze results, compare with expected outcomes, and document findings in a well-structured blog post.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Current AI governance relies on binary threshold judgments without probability estimates. SEE methods would enable more nuanced, calibrated, and publicly verifiable capability forecasting.
