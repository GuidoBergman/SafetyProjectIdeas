# Research Question

Equitable global governance of dual-use cyber AI requires Global South defenders to have equivalent access to frontier AI tools as US/EU defenders. This project investigates whether define the equitable governance success state (equivalent verified access for equivalent-quality defenders regardless of geography).

# Approach Outline

Define the equitable governance success state (equivalent verified access for equivalent-quality defenders regardless of geography). Work backward: what institutional recognition mechanisms, credential translation systems, and local verification infrastructure must exist? Map current gaps by region. Prioritize 3 most impactful interventions.
