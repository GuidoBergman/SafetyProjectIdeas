# Research Question

AI phishing capability studies (Hoxhunt, academic studies) are primarily conducted in English. This project investigates whether take 5 phishing scenario templates in english and translate to 4 other languages (spanish, portuguese, arabic, mandarin).

# Approach Outline

Take 5 phishing scenario templates in English and translate to 4 other languages (Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Mandarin). Generate phishing emails in each language using 3 API models. Have native speaker raters (can recruit via academic departments or online communities) score naturalness, persuasiveness, and technical accuracy. Compute cross-language capability profiles.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Define the experimental setup: select datasets, models, and evaluation metrics appropriate for the research question.
- Implement and run the core experiment, collecting measurements on the defined metrics.
- Analyze results, compare with expected outcomes, and document findings in a well-structured blog post.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If AI phishing capability drops dramatically in low-resource languages, non-English-speaking populations may have a relative safety margin. If capability is uniformly high across languages, global AI-enabled phishing is an immediate threat. This has direct relevance to international AI safety governance.
