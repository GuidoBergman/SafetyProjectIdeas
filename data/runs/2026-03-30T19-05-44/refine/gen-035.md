# Theory of Impact Chain

If this research succeeds, it produces quantitative evidence about safety benchmark measurement properties. IRT-based contamination detection is only useful if we know what it misses. If paraphrase contamination evades detection, the tool provides false assurance of benchmark integrity. This enables more informed deployment decisions by revealing whether current safety evaluations actually measure what they claim to measure, directly reducing the risk that a model is deployed as 'safe' based on benchmarks that lack validity or sensitivity at critical ability levels.
