# Evidence-Centered Design Audit of Three Leading AI Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** Evidence-Centered Design (ECD) provides a principled framework for assessment design: student model (what is claimed), evidence model (what behaviors provide evidence), and task model (how tasks elicit evidence). No published AI safety benchmark has been audited against ECD principles. Misalignment with ECD is now identified as a primary source of interpretive invalidity in AI evaluation.

**Direction:** Select three safety benchmarks differing in approach (e.g., TruthfulQA, HarmBench, AIR-Bench 2024). For each, conduct a structured ECD audit: (1) reverse-engineer the implied student model (what safety competencies are claimed), (2) assess whether tasks actually elicit the claimed competencies (evidence model alignment), (3) evaluate task model diversity (do tasks adequately sample the competency space?). Use a scoring rubric adapted from ECD literature. Synthesize findings into design recommendations.

**Why it matters:** An ECD audit provides a systematic, theoretically grounded critique of benchmark design quality that goes beyond ad hoc criticism. It gives benchmark developers a checklist for improvement and gives evaluators a principled basis for judging which benchmarks to trust. This is a novel application of a mature assessment-design framework to AI safety.
