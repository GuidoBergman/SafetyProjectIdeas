# Multi-Trait Multi-Method Matrix for AI Safety Constructs: Testing Convergent and Discriminant Validity Across Safety Dimensions and Evaluation Methods

**Problem:** AI safety evaluations measure multiple constructs (refusal behavior, harmlessness, honesty, corrigibility) using multiple methods (automated scoring, human rating, red-teaming). It is unknown whether these measures actually track distinct constructs or whether method variance dominates—meaning scores reflect measurement artifacts rather than genuine behavioral differences.

**Direction:** Select 3-4 safety-relevant traits (e.g., refusal robustness, honesty under pressure, instruction-following fidelity) and 3 measurement methods (automated LLM judge, human rater, rule-based classifier). For a set of 10-15 models, produce scores for each trait-method combination. Construct the MTMM matrix. Compute convergent validity correlations (same trait, different method), discriminant validity correlations (different trait, same method), and method-trait interaction effects. Use CFA-MTMM analysis in R (lavaan package) to estimate trait and method variance components. Interpret whether traits are genuinely distinguishable and whether methods introduce systematic bias.

**Why it matters:** The MTMM matrix, introduced by Campbell and Fiske (1959), is the gold standard for demonstrating that a test measures what it claims to measure rather than just reflecting method artifacts. If AI safety scores are dominated by method variance (e.g., all LLM-judge scores are highly correlated regardless of what trait they claim to measure), this undermines the construct validity of current safety evaluation. lavaan in R makes CFA-MTMM feasible without coding.

**Relevant context:** MTMM has been used in assessment center research to validate multi-dimensional competency ratings. AI safety evaluation uses multiple constructs and multiple methods but no one has applied MTMM. The arxiv paper 'Where Assessment Validation and Responsible AI Meet' (2024) calls for construct validity frameworks for AI. lavaan is a standard R package. The approach requires only a matrix of scores, no model training.
