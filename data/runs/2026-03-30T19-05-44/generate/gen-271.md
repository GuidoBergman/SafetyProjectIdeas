# DIF-Based Safety Weakness Detection: Identifying Items Where Models Fail Despite Adequate Overall Safety

**Problem:** P8: No way to diagnose specific safety weaknesses (only aggregate scores)

**Direction:** Apply DIF analysis across safety sub-domains as groups, identifying benchmark items that function unexpectedly for specific model families even after controlling for overall safety ability—revealing domain-specific blind spots.

**Why it matters:** A model with adequate overall safety score may still have domain-specific weaknesses (e.g., performs well on violence refusal but poorly on privacy protection). DIF analysis with domain as the grouping variable surfaces exactly these differential patterns, providing actionable diagnostic information.

**Relevant context:** DIF analysis is designed to surface group-specific item anomalies. Using safety sub-domain as the grouping variable is a novel application. Existing multi-domain safety benchmarks provide the response data. The difR R package is the tool of choice. This extends naturally to identifying model-family-specific safety weaknesses.
