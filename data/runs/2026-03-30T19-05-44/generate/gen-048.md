# Structural Equation Model of Benchmark Score Predictors: Capability vs. Training Regime vs. Scale

**Problem:** The metabench finding (one dominant factor) and Paper 13's rebuttal (parameter count confound) leave unresolved the causal structure among scale, training regime, and benchmark performance. A structural equation model (SEM) can test competing causal paths simultaneously rather than relying on correlation alone.

**Direction:** Collect publicly available model metadata: parameter count (log scale), training token count, RLHF/DPO tuning (binary), base vs. instruction-tuned, and benchmark scores for 6 benchmarks. Specify a SEM in R (lavaan package) with latent 'capability' factor and observed predictors. Test two competing models: (a) scale → capability → benchmark scores, (b) scale → benchmark scores directly (bypassing latent capability). Compare fit via χ², CFI, RMSEA.

**Why it matters:** If scale has a direct path to benchmark scores bypassing capability, benchmarks are measuring scale rather than capability. SEM makes this testable with the same observational data already available.

**Relevant context:** lavaan package in R (SEM); Open LLM Leaderboard metadata; metabench (ICLR 2025); Paper 13 (2026); SEM as a validity method (Bollen 1989).
