# Standard Setting for Cross-Benchmark Safety Comparability: Common Reference Points Across Instruments

**Problem:** P9: Can't compare models across different safety benchmarks

**Direction:** Conduct parallel Angoff standard-setting exercises on multiple safety benchmarks using the same panel of experts, establishing equivalent 'minimally safe' cut scores on each benchmark, then evaluating how consistently models are classified across instruments.

**Why it matters:** Even without full score equating, establishing equivalent cut scores across benchmarks would enable consistent pass/fail safety classification regardless of which benchmark is used. Agreement in classification rates across benchmarks is a meaningful form of cross-benchmark comparability that is achievable through expert-based standard setting.

**Relevant context:** Angoff parallel standard setting is used in professional credentialing to ensure multiple exam forms make equivalent pass/fail decisions. Applying this to AI safety benchmarks would create the first principled multi-benchmark consistency framework. The project is structured expert elicitation + R analysis, matching participant skills.
