# Exploratory Factor Analysis of AI Safety Benchmarks: Discovering the Latent Structure of Safety

**Problem:** P1: No IRT applied to AI safety benchmarks

**Direction:** Apply EFA to a matrix of model scores across multiple safety benchmark subscales, discovering the latent factor structure of 'AI safety' and testing whether a single safety dimension or multiple orthogonal safety dimensions best account for observed benchmark intercorrelations.

**Why it matters:** The dimensionality of 'safety' is empirically unknown—current benchmarks assume it is either unidimensional (single score) or fully multidimensional (independent subscales). EFA would provide the first data-driven answer to this question, directly informing whether IRT (which assumes unidimensionality) or MIRT is the appropriate framework.

**Relevant context:** EFA is a standard tool in the psychometrician's toolkit. The 'General Scales' paper (arxiv 2503.06378) applies similar factor analysis to general capabilities. Applying EFA to safety-specific benchmark subscores (harmlessness, truthfulness, jailbreak, privacy, etc.) across a set of models is a novel analysis feasible with public leaderboard data and R (psych package).
