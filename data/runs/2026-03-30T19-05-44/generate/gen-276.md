# G-Theory Cross-Benchmark Generalizability: How Well Does a Safety Score Generalize Across Benchmarks

**Problem:** P9: Can't compare models across different safety benchmarks

**Direction:** Apply G-Theory treating benchmark instrument as a 'facet' alongside items and models, estimating how much variance in model safety scores is attributable to which benchmark was used vs. genuine model safety ability, yielding a generalizability coefficient across benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** If most variance is attributable to benchmark choice rather than model ability, safety scores are not generalizable and cross-benchmark comparison is invalid. The G-coefficient would quantify exactly how benchmark-dependent safety measurement is, providing the empirical basis for insisting on multi-benchmark evaluation.

**Relevant context:** G-Theory is ideal for this design as it can handle items nested within benchmarks crossed with models. Public leaderboard data contain many models evaluated across multiple safety-relevant benchmarks. This analysis requires only variance component estimation in R (gtheory or lme4 package) on publicly available data.
