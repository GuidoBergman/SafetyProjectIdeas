# Controlling for Model Size as a Covariate in Factor Analysis of AI Safety Capabilities

**Problem:** P3: Model size confounds factor analysis of capabilities

**Direction:** Apply confirmatory factor analysis (CFA) to AI safety benchmark response matrices while explicitly including log(parameter count) as a covariate, testing whether the factor structure of safety-relevant capabilities holds after partialling out model size.

**Why it matters:** OpenReview 2024 ('Beyond Mean Scores') shows that factor models applied to 4,416 models across 21,176 items reveal distinct capability constructs, but does not adequately control for size as a confound. If 'safety' loads on a general factor driven by size rather than a distinct latent trait, current safety benchmarks are measuring capability, not safety specifically—directly relevant to 'safetywashing' concerns.

**Relevant context:** The 'Safetywashing' paper (arxiv 2407.21792) shows safety benchmarks correlate strongly with general capabilities. Factor analysis with covariates can be run in R (lavaan package). Public benchmark response data from HELM, Open LLM Leaderboard, or similar datasets include model size metadata. This is a direct CFA exercise within a psychometrician's skillset.
