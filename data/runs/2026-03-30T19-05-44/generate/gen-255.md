# MTMM Matrix to Assess Construct Validity of AI Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** P4: 46.6% of benchmarks lack validity evidence

**Direction:** Construct a Multi-Trait Multi-Method matrix across 3-4 AI safety traits (e.g., refusal behavior, truthfulness, harmlessness) measured by 2-3 different benchmark instruments, computing convergent and discriminant validity coefficients to test whether benchmarks measuring the same trait actually agree.

**Why it matters:** The 'Can We Trust AI Benchmarks?' review (arxiv 2502.06559) documents pervasive validity failures. An MTMM analysis is the classic psychometric validity test and has never been systematically applied to safety benchmarks. It would directly reveal whether benchmarks that claim to measure 'toxicity' or 'jailbreak resistance' are measuring the same underlying construct.

**Relevant context:** MTMM was developed by Campbell and Fiske (1959) and is a standard construct validity tool. Applying it to AI benchmarks requires computing correlations between benchmark scores (across models) for the same claimed trait measured by different instruments. This is a pure correlation/table analysis requiring only benchmark leaderboard data—no new experiments. Feasible in R or even spreadsheet software.
