# Defining Safety Constructs Formally: What Does 'AI Safety' Actually Mean as a Measurable Construct?

**Problem:** Establish construct validity for AI safety evaluation

**Direction:** Conduct a structured conceptual analysis: survey the implicit definitions of 'safety' used by major safety benchmarks (HarmBench, AdvBench, SALAD-Bench, AIR-2024, WildGuard), extract operational definitions from their documentation, and classify them along three dimensions: (1) refusal behavior vs. harm avoidance, (2) adversarial robustness vs. baseline safety, (3) content-level vs. intent-level. Map disagreements across benchmarks and propose a formal construct definition with testable predictions.

**Why it matters:** Construct validity begins with a clear theoretical definition of what is being measured. Current safety benchmarks use wildly different operationalizations of 'safety'—some measure refusal rates, others measure harm in outputs, others measure robustness to jailbreaks. Without a shared construct definition, comparisons across benchmarks are conceptually incoherent.
