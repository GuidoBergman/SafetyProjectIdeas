# Construct Equivalence Audit: Does 'Honesty' Mean the Same Thing Across LLM Benchmarks?

**Problem:** Different safety benchmarks claim to measure 'honesty' or 'truthfulness' (TruthfulQA, HaluEval, SelfCheckGPT benchmarks) but operationalize it differently. It is unknown whether these benchmarks measure the same construct—a question of construct equivalence that factor analysis and correlation analysis can address directly.

**Direction:** Collect scores for 50+ models on at least four benchmarks that claim to measure honesty/truthfulness (TruthfulQA, HaluEval, FActScore, SelfCheckGPT-based tasks). Compute inter-benchmark correlations. Fit a one-factor CFA model (lavaan) testing whether a single 'honesty' factor explains the covariance. Test for scalar invariance if subgroups (model families) are available. Examine residual correlations to identify honesty sub-dimensions. Compare empirical factor structure against each benchmark's theoretical claims.

**Why it matters:** If benchmarks claiming to measure 'honesty' actually measure different constructs, pooling or comparing their scores is invalid. This analysis would be the first formal construct equivalence study of AI honesty/truthfulness benchmarks, revealing whether the field has a coherent measurement model for honesty or a set of theoretically ungrounded, uncorrelated proxies.

**Relevant context:** Construct equivalence is open problem 3 in MTAI framework; arxiv:2511.04703 (445-benchmark audit found 53.4% present validity evidence); TruthfulQA and HaluEval are publicly available; lavaan CFA is standard in R.
