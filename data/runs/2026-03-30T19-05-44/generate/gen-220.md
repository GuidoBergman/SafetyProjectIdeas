# Minimum Reporting Standards for AI Safety Benchmark Scores: A Psychometric Checklist

**Problem:** Make AI evaluation reproducible and reliable

**Direction:** Drawing on established psychometric reporting standards (APA Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing, COSMIN checklist for health measurement instruments), develop a minimum reporting checklist for AI safety benchmarks. Audit 10 published safety benchmark papers against the checklist, scoring each on items like: reports test-retest reliability, reports SEM, describes item selection procedure, provides item-level data publicly, reports confidence intervals. Publish the checklist and audit results.

**Why it matters:** Reproducibility requires standardized reporting. Currently, safety benchmark papers report results in incompatible formats, without reliability statistics, without item-level data, and without uncertainty quantification. A psychometrically-grounded reporting checklist—analogous to CONSORT for clinical trials—would raise the scientific standard for the entire field.

**Relevant context:** 'Can We Trust AI Benchmarks?' (2025) provides an interdisciplinary review identifying exactly these gaps. 'Measurement to Meaning' (2025) proposes validity frameworks but not concrete reporting standards. Psychometric reporting standards (APA Standards, COSMIN) exist but have not been adapted for AI evaluation. This is a conceptual/analytical project accessible without heavy coding.
