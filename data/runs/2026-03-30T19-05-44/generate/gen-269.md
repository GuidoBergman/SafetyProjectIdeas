# Cognitive Diagnostic Models for Safety Weakness Profiling: From Aggregate Scores to Attribute Mastery Maps

**Problem:** P8: No way to diagnose specific safety weaknesses (only aggregate scores)

**Direction:** Apply a Cognitive Diagnostic Model (DINA or G-DINA) to a multi-domain safety benchmark, specifying a Q-matrix of safety attributes (e.g., refusal of violence, privacy protection, deception resistance, jailbreak robustness), to generate attribute mastery probability profiles for each model.

**Why it matters:** Instead of a single safety score, CDMs produce a probability vector over safety sub-skills for each model. This directly addresses P8 by providing the first principled diagnostic framework for identifying specific safety weaknesses, analogous to how educational CDMs profile student knowledge gaps.

**Relevant context:** CDMs produce mastery profiles rather than aggregate scores (Cambridge Assessment). The GDINA R package is well-established. A Q-matrix mapping HarmBench or AIR-Bench items to safety attributes could be constructed by expert review. This is a novel, high-impact application that is methodologically feasible for a psychometrician within 30 hours.
