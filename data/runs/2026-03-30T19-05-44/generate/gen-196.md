**Problem:** Apply IRT to AI safety benchmarks

**Direction:** Using pre-calibrated IRT parameters from a safety benchmark, simulate a computerized adaptive testing (CAT) procedure in R: starting with items of medium difficulty, adaptively select next items based on maximum Fisher information at the current theta estimate, and stop when standard error falls below 0.3.
