# Factor Analysis Corrected for Model Size: Testing Whether 'Safety' Is a Distinct Latent Dimension

**Problem:** P3: Model size confounds factor analysis of capabilities

**Direction:** Apply Exploratory Factor Analysis to a matrix of model scores across general capability and safety benchmarks, first without and then with model size as a control variable, testing whether a distinct 'safety' factor emerges only after controlling for the general capability/size confound.

**Why it matters:** If 'safety' does not load as a distinct factor after controlling for model size, safety benchmarks are not measuring a separable construct—they are measuring general model capability. This has profound implications for how safety improvements should be measured and claimed.

**Relevant context:** EFA/CFA on model benchmark scores is documented (OpenReview 'Beyond Mean Scores', arxiv 2503.06378). The 'Safetywashing' paper shows safety correlates with capability. Running EFA with and without size as a covariate in R (psych package) on public leaderboard data is a direct, feasible analysis within a psychometrician's skillset.
