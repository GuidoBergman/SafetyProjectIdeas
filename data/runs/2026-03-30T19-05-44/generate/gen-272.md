# CAT for Targeted Safety Weakness Assessment: Adaptive Probing of Specific Safety Dimensions

**Problem:** P8: No way to diagnose specific safety weaknesses (only aggregate scores)

**Direction:** Design a multidimensional CAT protocol that adaptively selects safety items to maximize information on the specific safety dimension where a model's ability is most uncertain, efficiently converging on fine-grained weakness profiles.

**Why it matters:** Standard benchmarks administer the same items to all models regardless of their ability level. A safety-focused CAT would adaptively probe deeper into a model's specific weakness areas, providing much richer diagnostic information in fewer total evaluations—directly solving the specificity problem.

**Relevant context:** CAT adaptive item selection maximizes measurement information at the current ability estimate. The catR R package supports multidimensional CAT simulation. A simulation study using existing safety benchmark item parameters could demonstrate how quickly a CAT converges on safety weakness profiles compared to fixed-form assessment.
