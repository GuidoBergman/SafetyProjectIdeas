# Comparing Data-Driven vs. Theory-Driven Capability Dimensions: Does Safety Form Its Own Dimension?

**Problem:** Determine if LLM capabilities are unidimensional or multidimensional

**Direction:** Collect both safety scores and capability scores (reasoning, knowledge, coding, math) for 40+ models. In an EFA, test whether safety items/subscales load on existing capability factors or form a distinct factor. In a CFA, test a model where safety is specified as an orthogonal dimension to general capability. Compute the safety-capability correlation and its confidence interval to test whether they are distinguishable constructs.

**Why it matters:** If safety loads on the same latent dimension as general capability in a factor analysis, then safety and intelligence are psychometrically inseparable in current models—the alignment tax is total. If safety forms its own orthogonal dimension, safety and capability are genuinely separable. This has profound implications for the feasibility of building highly capable and highly safe AI systems.

**Relevant context:** The capability-safety relationship is a central empirical question in AI safety. Current evidence is anecdotal. Formal factor-analytic testing using lavaan and psych in R, with data from the Open LLM Leaderboard, would provide the first psychometrically rigorous answer. 'A Data-Driven Study on LLM Structure and Development' (EMNLP 2025) provides a template for the capability side of this analysis.
