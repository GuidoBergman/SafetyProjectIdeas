# IRT Concurrent Calibration for Cross-Benchmark Safety Scale Linking

**Problem:** P9: Can't compare models across different safety benchmarks

**Direction:** Pool response data from multiple safety benchmarks and run concurrent IRT calibration, estimating a single set of item parameters and model ability estimates on a common scale, enabling direct comparison of models evaluated on different benchmark subsets.

**Why it matters:** Concurrent calibration is the most powerful IRT equating approach, producing a unified safety ability scale from heterogeneous benchmark data. This would be the psychometric infrastructure needed for a 'safety leaderboard' analogous to HELM or Open LLM Leaderboard but with valid cross-benchmark comparisons.

**Relevant context:** Concurrent calibration is supported in the mirt R package via the multipleGroup function. Multiple safety benchmark response matrices are publicly available. The ICLR 2025 MetaBench paper demonstrates pooled IRT calibration across 6 general benchmarks—directly replicable for safety benchmarks.
