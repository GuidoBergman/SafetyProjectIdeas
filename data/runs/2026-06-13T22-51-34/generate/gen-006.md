# How Fast Does the CoT Monitoring Premium Erode When the Model Knows It's Being Watched?

**Problem:** Telling a model it may be monitored causes CoT obfuscation (gpt-oss-120b undetected deception rose 1.7%->25.7%). The GPT-5 card measured CoT informativeness under no obfuscation pressure. A clean dose-response curve of how the advantage degrades with monitoring-awareness pressure (via prompting, no fine-tuning) is missing.

**Direction:** Hold task, monitor, actor fixed; vary a prompt-injected monitoring-awareness level in graded steps (none -> vague -> explicit 'monitor reads CoT' -> 'keep CoT clean'). Measure side-task success and monitor detection recall per level to get a degradation curve; run on a couple of families to compare steepness.

**Why it matters:** Monitorability only helps if robust to the model realizing it is watched. A cheap dose-response curve tells safety teams how much margin CoT monitoring has and whether the cliff is gradual or sudden.

**Relevant context:** Follows 'measure degradation under pressure' and 'study CoT obfuscation.' Grounded in Training on Documents About Monitoring Leads to CoT Obfuscation and arXiv:2510.19851. Prompt-based manipulation, suspicion-score metric, API-side; ControlArena harness reusable; no GPU training.
