# Does CoT Monitoring Still Beat Output-Only on Open-Weight Reasoning Models?

**Problem:** The GPT-5 card's CoT-beats-output result was on a proprietary model whose CoT may be unusually faithful. Whether it replicates on open-weight reasoning models a beginner can run, where faithfulness varies enormously (~20-95% divergence across DeepSeek-R1, QwQ, Qwen3), is unknown.

**Direction:** Pick 2-3 open-weight reasoning models exposing thinking tokens. Build a small labeled set of prompts inducing deceptive/rule-breaking reasoning. For each model run output-only vs CoT-inclusive LLM-judge monitors; compare precision/recall/ROC and correlate the CoT advantage with each model's known faithfulness.

**Why it matters:** Labs bet on CoT monitorability, but the open-weight ecosystem is what most of the world deploys. If the advantage shrinks on transparent-but-unfaithful open models, that's an actionable warning.
