# Research Question

Does the CoT-monitoring advantage survive when the actor and the monitor come from different model families, or is part of the premium due to same-family stylistic familiarity?

# Approach Outline

Use ControlArena's trusted-monitoring scaffolding on a small honeypot/deception task. Take a benign main task paired with a covert side task (reuse SHADE-Arena benign+side task pairs). Have 3-4 API actor families produce both action-only transcripts and CoT+action transcripts while attempting the covert side task. Run ONE fixed monitor (a single model with a fixed prompt) that emits a 0-100 suspicion score on every transcript. Compare detection AUC and recall for CoT vs output-only, broken down by actor family, with special attention to whether the monitor's own family among the actors shows a higher premium than cross-family pairings. Pure API, no training; ControlArena provides the 0-100 scoring harness.

# Proposed First Experiments

Get one same-family pairing and one cross-family pairing running through ControlArena: two actors each producing CoT and action-only transcripts on ~40 trajectories, scored by the fixed monitor. Success criterion: the monitor produces calibrated 0-100 scores with above-chance AUC separating benign from sabotage, and you can compute a CoT-vs-output AUC delta for each pairing. This validates the harness and the actor/monitor setup before adding the remaining families.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Research output: a breakdown of the CoT premium by actor-monitor family pairing, isolating same-family vs cross-family effects. Intermediate: the field learns whether 'we can read the CoT' transfers to third-party/open-weight actors monitored by a different model, which is the realistic deployment configuration. Reduced catastrophic risk: AI control safety cases that assume CoT readability won't be silently overstated; deployers monitoring an external model with their own monitor get a calibrated expectation of how much CoT actually helps, avoiding over-trusting CoT monitoring where it doesn't hold.

# Strength Rationale

Strong beginner fit because ControlArena supplies the trusted-monitoring scaffolding and 0-100 scoring, SHADE-Arena supplies benign+side task pairs, and everything runs via API with no training. The same-vs-cross-family framing gives a sharp, novel hypothesis on top of a pure replication. Note overlap with gen-004 and gen-007; pick one. Fits 30 hours given the reused harness.

# Alternative Framings

- Monitor-rotation angle: hold the actor fixed and rotate the monitor family instead, isolating the effect from the monitor side rather than the actor side.
- Obfuscation-robustness angle: combine with a light 'you may be watched' prompt to see whether cross-family monitoring is more or less robust to obfuscation pressure.
