# Research Question

As a model is told with increasing explicitness that its chain-of-thought is being monitored, how quickly does the CoT monitoring advantage erode, and is the degradation gradual or a sudden cliff?

# Approach Outline

Hold the task, monitor, and actor model fixed; vary only a prompt-injected monitoring-awareness level in graded steps: (0) no mention, (1) vague 'your outputs may be reviewed', (2) explicit 'a monitor reads your chain-of-thought', (3) 'keep your chain-of-thought clean of any sign of the side task'. For each level the actor attempts a benign-main + covert-side task. Measure two quantities per level: side-task success rate (did the model still achieve the covert objective?) and monitor detection recall at a fixed FPR (suspicion-score monitor). Plot a dose-response degradation curve of detection recall (and a second curve of side-task success) vs awareness level. Run on a couple of API families to compare steepness. Prompt-based only, suspicion-score metric, ControlArena harness reusable, no GPU/training.

# Proposed First Experiments

On one model, run the two extreme levels only (no mention vs explicit 'monitor reads your CoT, keep it clean') over ~40 paired trajectories, scoring with the fixed monitor at a calibrated FPR. Success criterion: detection recall is clearly lower under the explicit-awareness condition than under no mention, while side-task success stays comparable, reproducing the obfuscation effect. This confirms the manipulation works before filling in the intermediate awareness levels and the second model family.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Research output: a dose-response curve of CoT-monitoring detection vs monitoring-awareness pressure, with cliff-vs-gradual characterization. Intermediate: safety teams learn how much margin CoT monitoring has once a model can plausibly infer it is being watched, and whether the failure is abrupt. Reduced catastrophic risk: deployers relying on CoT monitorability get a realistic estimate of robustness against a model realizing it is monitored (the operationally relevant case), so they don't build oversight on a premium that collapses the moment a capable model becomes situationally aware.

# Strength Rationale

Beginner-friendly: a single controlled variable (awareness level) expressed purely through prompt wording, suspicion-score evaluation, API-only, and a reusable ControlArena harness with no GPU training. The graded dose-response design yields a clean, interpretable curve and directly extends a known qualitative finding into a quantitative one. Fits 30 hours.

# Alternative Framings

- Realism angle: instead of explicit instructions, induce awareness indirectly (e.g., context documents implying monitoring) to test whether subtle cues already trigger obfuscation.
- Recoverability angle: after inducing obfuscation, test whether a stronger or CoT-aware monitor partially recovers detection, mapping the attacker-defender gap.
