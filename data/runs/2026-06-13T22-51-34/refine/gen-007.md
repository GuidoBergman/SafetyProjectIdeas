# Research Question

Does CoT monitoring still beat output-only deception detection on open-weight reasoning models, and does the size of the CoT advantage correlate with each model's known CoT faithfulness?

# Approach Outline

Pick 2-3 open-weight reasoning models that expose thinking tokens and span a range of published CoT-faithfulness (e.g., DeepSeek-R1, QwQ, Qwen3). Build a small labeled set of prompts that induce deceptive/rule-breaking reasoning vs honest reasoning. For each model collect output-only and CoT-inclusive transcripts, then run two LLM-judge monitors (output-only vs CoT+output) with fixed rubrics. Compute precision, recall, and ROC/AUC per model, derive the CoT-minus-output advantage, and correlate that advantage against each model's reported faithfulness divergence (~20-95% across these models). Run via API or one GPU; small prompt set, two LLM-judge passes per transcript, no training. Use Inspect for the eval harness and HuggingFace for the models. Deliverable: per-model precision/recall/ROC plus a faithfulness-vs-advantage scatter.

# Proposed First Experiments

Stand up the Inspect-based pipeline on one open model with the most documented faithfulness data over ~50 labeled prompts, producing both monitors' precision/recall/ROC. Success criterion: both monitors yield above-chance ROC and you obtain a concrete CoT-vs-output advantage number for that model, validating transcript collection (thinking tokens correctly exposed), the monitors, and the metrics before adding the other models and the faithfulness correlation.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Research output: evidence on whether the CoT-beats-output result holds on transparent-but-unfaithful open models, plus a faithfulness-vs-advantage relationship. Intermediate: since the open-weight ecosystem is what most of the world deploys, the field learns whether the lab bet on CoT monitorability extends there, and whether faithfulness is a usable predictor of when CoT monitoring helps. Reduced catastrophic risk: deployers of open models get an actionable warning when CoT monitoring would give false security on a model whose CoT is unfaithful, steering oversight resources toward configurations where CoT actually carries deception signal.

# Strength Rationale

Strong beginner project: API or single-GPU, a small prompt set, two LLM-judge passes, no training, and a ready harness (Inspect) plus accessible models (HuggingFace). The added faithfulness-correlation angle gives an explanatory hook beyond a bare replication. Note heavy overlap with gen-004 and gen-005 — choose one of the three. Fits 30 hours.

# Alternative Framings

- Causal-probe angle: instead of relying on published faithfulness, run a quick faithfulness check yourself (e.g., do hints leak into answers without appearing in CoT?) and correlate that locally measured faithfulness with the CoT advantage.
- Threshold angle: report not just AUC but TPR at a fixed low FPR per model, since that is the operationally relevant control quantity.
