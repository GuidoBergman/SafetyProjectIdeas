# Research Question

To what extent do open models' refusals on a harmful-prompt benchmark depend on the exact memorized phrasing of the items, and can we separate 'surface-form' refusals (which collapse under any paraphrase) from robust 'intent-based' refusals?

# Approach Outline

Take an open jailbreak/harmful-prompt benchmark (e.g., a subset of AdvBench or OR-Bench, ~150-250 items). Generate several semantics-preserving paraphrases per prompt (synonym swaps, voice changes, reordering) with a small open model, spot-checking meaning preservation. Run several open target models on originals and paraphrases, record refusal vs compliance, and compute the refusal-rate drop. Classify each prompt as surface-form (refuses on original, complies on >=1 paraphrase) or intent-based (refuses across all paraphrases). Report the fraction of facade refusals per model. Inference-only on a single GPU, a few hours.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Refusal-drop measurement: run target models on original and paraphrased harmful prompts; measure mean refusal rate per condition; expected outcome is a measurable drop on paraphrases, evidencing surface-form dependence.
- Surface-vs-intent split: classify each prompt by whether refusal survives all paraphrases; measure the fraction of surface-form (facade) refusals per model; expected outcome is a non-trivial facade fraction varying by model.
- Harmfulness-preservation control: manually label 30 random paraphrases for whether they remain genuinely harmful and equivalent, then recompute the refusal drop on only verified-harmful paraphrases; measure the adjusted gap; expected outcome is that the facade effect persists, ruling out the trivial explanation that paraphrases defused the request.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If refusals hinge on memorized surface forms, then a model's headline refusal rate overstates its real safety, and attackers can bypass it by trivial rephrasing. Quantifying and exposing facade refusals gives independent evaluators a robustness check and pushes benchmarks toward paraphrase-augmented reporting, reducing the catastrophic pathway where a model certified safe is reliably jailbroken in deployment via reworded harmful requests.

# Strength Rationale

Scores 5 on narrow scope, accessible complexity, and low compute: a tightly-scoped paraphrase-gap audit with a clear surface-vs-intent metric, runnable inference-only on small open models. Counterfactual value is high because independent robustness auditing of refusal benchmarks is underweighted by labs, and the work needs only open models and public data with no training or proprietary infrastructure.

# Alternative Framings
