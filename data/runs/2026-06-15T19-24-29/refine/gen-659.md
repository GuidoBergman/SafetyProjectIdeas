# Research Question

Does the per-layer hidden-state magnitude signature of jailbreak prompts, reported on a single model, generalize across architectures? Is there a 'high-susceptibility' layer band that lets a simple probe detect jailbreaks transfer across LLaMA, Mistral, and Qwen?

# Approach Outline

For LLaMA, Mistral, and Qwen small open models, run a labeled set of benign and jailbreak prompts and extract per-layer hidden-state magnitudes (a single forward pass each). Inspect whether the same layer band shows the magnitude anomaly across families, then fit a logistic-regression probe on the magnitude features and test cross-family transfer (train on one family, test on another). Report detection AUROC and the location of the susceptibility band. Forward-passes plus logistic regression on small models, single GPU, no training of the base model; within 30 hours.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Collect ~200 benign + ~200 jailbreak prompts and extract per-layer hidden-state magnitudes on one model; reproduce the reported high-magnitude band. Expected outcome: a visible magnitude gap in a specific layer band for jailbreak prompts.
- Fit a logistic probe on the magnitude features within each family and report in-family AUROC. Expected outcome: high in-family AUROC, confirming the signal is usable for detection.
- Test cross-family transfer: train the probe on one family, evaluate on the other two, and compare the susceptibility-band locations. Expected outcome: either the band and probe transfer (a general training-free detector) or they do not (an important negative result bounding the method).

# Theory of Impact Chain

A cheap, training-free, forward-pass-only jailbreak detector that generalizes across model families would give open-weight deployers a portable monitoring primitive that does not need per-model retraining. Confirming (or falsifying) cross-family transfer mitigates the catastrophic pathway where deployers trust a hidden-state monitor that silently fails on architectures it was never validated against, letting concealed jailbreaks through.

# Strength Rationale

Scores 5 on narrow scope, accessible complexity, and low compute: a tightly-scoped cross-family magnitude-gap comparison plus a simple logistic probe, using only forward passes. Counterfactual value (4) is strong because it is a forward-pass-only monitoring primitive on open models, deliverable by an independent beginner team with no proprietary access, and either outcome (transfer or not) is publishable.

# Alternative Framings

- Frame as a falsification study: explicitly test whether the single-model result is an artifact, foregrounding the negative-result value.
