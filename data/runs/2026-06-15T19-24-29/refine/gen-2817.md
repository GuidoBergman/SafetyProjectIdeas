# Research Question

When a model deliberately sandbags on a multiple-choice capability set, does it still assign high token-level logprob to the correct option it discards, leaving a detectable confidence/calibration signature?

# Approach Outline

Take a multiple-choice capability set (~150 items from MMLU or WMDP) and prompt a small open model (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) to answer honestly, recording the logprob assigned to each option. Then prompt the same model to deliberately sandbag and record logprobs again. Compare the logprob assigned to the correct option across the honest and sandbagged conditions. If the sandbagging model still places high internal probability on the correct answer while outputting a wrong one, define a logprob-gap detector (correct-option logprob minus chosen-option logprob) and measure its separation between honest and sandbagged runs. Inference-only with logprob extraction, single consumer GPU, a few hours.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Logprob extraction setup: confirm we can extract per-option logprobs from the open model in both honest and sandbagged conditions on a few items. Expected outcome: a working pipeline yielding per-option logprobs.
- Correct-option logprob comparison: across all items, compare the logprob on the correct option in honest vs sandbagged runs. Expected outcome: high correct-option logprob retained under sandbagging, indicating the model 'knows' the answer it discards.
- Detector evaluation: compute the logprob-gap statistic per item and measure how well it separates sandbagged from honest runs (e.g. ROC-AUC). Expected outcome: a logprob-gap detector with meaningful AUC, demonstrating a cheap black-box-plus-logprob sandbagging signal.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If sandbagging leaves a detectable logprob-gap signature, then outside evaluators gain a cheap black-box-plus-logprob detector they can deploy without internal model access, which means concealed-capability evaluations become harder to fool. This improves safety because covert sandbagging on dangerous-capability evals (a concrete deceptive-AI catastrophic pathway where a model hides bioweapon or cyber capability to clear a deployment gate) becomes flaggable by independent auditors.

# Strength Rationale

Narrow scope (5) and accessible complexity (5) score top: a clean honest-vs-sandbag logprob comparison with an explicit detector-AUC success metric, finishable in 30 hours. Counterfactual value (4) is high because a black-box-plus-logprob detector is underweighted by labs, uses only open models and inference, and directly addresses the named concealed-capability open problem for outside evaluators.
