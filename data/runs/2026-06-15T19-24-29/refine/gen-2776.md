# Research Question

On frontier-risk items (self-replication, agentic autonomy, catastrophic/embodied harm) from a new safety benchmark, how much more often does a model produce an unsafe response under worst-of-n sampling across a temperature/top-p grid than under a single greedy decode?

# Approach Outline

Take a small fixed subset of ForesightSafety Bench text items from the catastrophic / self-replication / agentic-autonomy pillars (the benchmark is open on GitHub). For each item and each open model, sample across a temperature x top-p grid plus best-of-n, and score every sample for safety using the benchmark's provided rubric (or a simple judge model). Compute 'worst-of-n' safety (fraction of items where at least one of n samples is unsafe) versus the greedy result, and report the worst-of-n gap per pillar. To stay within a single consumer GPU and a few hours, cap the item subset, the grid resolution, and n, and use a small model; this is inference-only, no training.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Greedy baseline: run greedy decode on the item subset and score safety. Measure: per-pillar greedy unsafe rate. Expected: low unsafe rate, matching optimistic single-decode reporting.
- Temperature/top-p sweep, worst-of-n: sample n completions per item across the grid and score each. Measure: worst-of-n unsafe rate (>=1 unsafe in n) vs greedy, per pillar. Expected: worst-of-n unsafe rate is substantially higher than greedy, exposing sampling fragility.
- n-curve: vary n (1, 4, 16) at the most adversarial temperature. Measure: unsafe rate as a function of n. Expected: a rising curve quantifying how a motivated worst-of-n attacker erodes safety on frontier-risk items.

# Theory of Impact Chain

A motivated user takes the worst of many samples, so if greedy-only safety reporting hides large worst-of-n gaps on catastrophic/self-replication items, then deployers and policymakers overestimate frontier-risk safety -> publishing the worst-of-n gap forces safety cases to report sampling-robust metrics -> models are gated and patched on the dimensions (self-replication, loss of control) that matter most, reducing the chance a model assists a catastrophic action under realistic sampling.

# Strength Rationale

Scoring 5 on narrow_scope and accessible_complexity and 4 on counterfactual_value and novelty, this is a tightly scoped sampling sweep with clear worst-of-n-vs-greedy success criteria on a brand-new open benchmark, runnable inference-only by an independent team, and it provides third-party scrutiny of frontier-risk dimensions labs gate internally.
