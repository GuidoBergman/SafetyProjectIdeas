# Research Question

When a prompt imposes a constraint that can only be satisfied by a target word several tokens in the future, how far ahead does a small open model begin pre-activating that target? We aim to produce a planning-horizon decay curve: target pre-activation strength as a function of distance N from the constraint to the target.

# Approach Outline

Use constraint-at-distance prompts: templates where a stated rule (e.g. 'end the sentence with a word that rhymes with X', or 'the answer must be the word Y, give a sentence leading to it') forces a specific target word at a controllable distance N tokens ahead. On a small open model (GPT-2-medium or Llama-3.2-1B) in inference-only mode, apply the logit lens at each intermediate layer/position and read off the target word's pre-activation (its rank or logit-lens probability) as it builds across positions. Sweep N over a small grid (e.g. 1-12), average over many prompt instances and target words, and plot mean target pre-activation vs N to characterize the decay curve. All steps are forward passes with cached hidden states; no training.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Single-distance sanity check: with N fixed small (e.g. 2-3), apply the logit lens at the position just before the target and confirm the target word is elevated above a random-word baseline. Measure logit-lens rank/probability of the target. Expected: clear above-baseline pre-activation, validating the readout pipeline.
- Horizon-decay sweep: vary N across the grid, averaging over many instances, and plot mean target pre-activation vs N. Measure the distance at which pre-activation falls to baseline (the planning horizon). Expected: monotone-ish decay with a characteristic horizon of a few tokens for a small model.
- Constraint-vs-no-constraint control: re-run with the constraint removed (target no longer forced) to confirm pre-activation is driven by the planning constraint rather than generic token frequency. Measure the gap between constrained and unconstrained pre-activation curves. Expected: constrained prompts show markedly higher early pre-activation, isolating genuine look-ahead.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If small models pre-activate future targets only over a short horizon, then long-horizon, multi-step deceptive or goal-directed behavior would require representational planning beyond what next-token monitors can see. Measuring the actual horizon on open models -> grounds claims about whether current models can internally plan multi-step deception -> calibrates how much we should trust shallow output monitors versus needing deeper interpretability, mitigating the catastrophic pathway where a model executes a long-horizon harmful plan that per-token oversight never flags.
