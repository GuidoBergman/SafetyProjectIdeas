# Research Question

When a weak pairwise judge is subject to both a sycophantic stated prior and position bias at once, do the two biases compound super-additively (their combined verdict distortion exceeds the sum of their individual effects)?

# Approach Outline

Run a 2x2 factorial over a pairwise judgment task with ground truth. Factor 1: planted sycophantic prior present/absent (a stated preference for one option). Factor 2: favored option placed in the biasing position vs the de-biasing position. For each cell, measure the rate at which the judge picks the manipulated option. Fit a logistic regression predicting the judge's choice from the two factors plus their interaction term; a significant positive interaction coefficient indicates super-additive compounding. Pure inference on a small open judge plus a logistic regression; very low compute.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Single-factor baselines: measure the verdict distortion from the sycophantic prior alone and from position bias alone (the two off-diagonal cells vs the clean cell). Measure each main effect. Expected outcome: both biases individually shift the judge toward the manipulated option.
- Factorial interaction: run all four cells and fit the logistic regression with an interaction term. Measure the interaction coefficient and its confidence interval. Expected outcome: a positive, significant interaction — combined manipulation distorts verdicts more than the sum of the two single effects.
- Attack-surface quantification: in the both-biases-aligned cell, measure how high the manipulated-option win-rate climbs vs the clean cell. Measure worst-case win-rate. Expected outcome: an attacker aligning both biases achieves a dangerously high win-rate, quantifying the real safety margin of pairwise oversight.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If ubiquitous judge biases compound super-additively, then the true safety margin of pairwise LLM oversight is worse than single-axis studies suggest -> protocol designers stop assuming biases are independent and small, and harden against aligned multi-bias attacks (e.g. order-averaging plus prior-stripping) -> a realistic combined-manipulation pathway against the overseer of powerful systems is measured and made defensible, rather than silently underestimated.

# Strength Rationale

High counterfactual value: single-axis bias studies dominate the literature and labs rarely test interactions, yet this needs only public data and pure inference. Tightly narrow-scope — one 2x2 factorial yielding a single interaction-coefficient deliverable — and beginner-feasible in ~30 hours including the logistic-regression analysis.

- Reframe as a 'bias budget' result: report whether oversight-bias mitigations can be designed independently or must be co-designed because the biases interact.
