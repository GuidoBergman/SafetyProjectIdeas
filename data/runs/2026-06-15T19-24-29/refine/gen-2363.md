# Research Question

How far does debate's truth-advantage degrade as only the dishonest debater's inference-time persuasion budget (best-of-N persuasiveness selection, extra rounds, rhetorical prompting) grows, and is there a breaking point where a persuasion-optimized liar overturns the honest side?

# Approach Outline

On QuALITY debate items, hold the honest debater fixed and scale only the dishonest debater's persuasion using open models: (a) best-of-N sampling selecting the most persuasive of N dishonest arguments, (b) additional debate rounds for the dishonest side, and (c) rhetorical/persuasion-oriented system prompts. Have a small open judge issue verdicts, and plot judge accuracy versus the dishonest persuasion budget to locate where debate's truth-advantage breaks down. Pure inference, no training, one GPU, a few hours.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Persuasion-budget sweep: scale dishonest best-of-N persuasiveness selection (N = 1, 2, 4, 8, ...) and plot judge accuracy. Measure: judge accuracy vs N. Expected: accuracy declines as the dishonest side is more strongly persuasion-optimized, revealing whether and where a breaking point appears.
- Channel decomposition: separately scale extra rounds and rhetorical prompting (holding others fixed). Measure: accuracy degradation attributable to each persuasion channel. Expected: the channels differ in potency, identifying which one-sided pressure most erodes debate's truth-advantage.
- Honest-symmetry control: give the same persuasion budget to the honest side as a control. Measure: whether symmetric persuasion preserves accuracy while one-sided persuasion erodes it. Expected: asymmetric persuasion is what breaks debate, confirming the threat is one-sided optimization, not persuasion per se.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If we show debate's safety property collapses (or holds) under modest one-sided persuasion optimization, that is a load-bearing finding for whether debate can scale as an oversight mechanism. This matters because the medium-horizon threat is a strategic AI that out-argues its weaker overseer; if a bounded persuasion budget already flips judge verdicts, then debate cannot be trusted to supervise more capable systems and would let a persuasive misaligned system get harmful answers endorsed, a direct loss-of-control pathway.

# Strength Rationale

Scores 5 on counterfactual value and narrow scope: it isolates a single under-studied variable, a one-sided persuasion budget, with a clean sweep on open models and public QuALITY data, requiring no training or lab infrastructure. The result, a breaking-point curve for debate's truth-advantage, is a concrete scalability datum and directly probes a long-horizon threat model that labs deprioritize, making it high-value and fully executable by an independent beginner team in 30 hours.
