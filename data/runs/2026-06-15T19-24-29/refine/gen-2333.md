# Research Question

Debate-as-oversight assumes a weak judge can extract truth from stronger debaters via information asymmetry (the judge sees only short verified quotes). How much asymmetry can the protocol tolerate before judge accuracy collapses, i.e. where is the breakpoint as the quote budget shrinks?

# Approach Outline

Use the QuALITY reading-comprehension debate setup with a small open judge model (1-3B instruct) and a stronger debater accessed via API. For each item, run honest-vs-dishonest debates and systematically vary the quote budget the judge may see (0, 1, 2, 4, 8 verified quotes), holding everything else fixed. The 0-quote condition is maximal asymmetry (judge gets only the passage title/question framing); larger budgets reduce asymmetry. Measure judge accuracy at each budget and plot the accuracy-vs-asymmetry curve to locate the breakpoint where accuracy falls to chance. No training is required, only prompt orchestration and answer scoring; the full sweep runs in a few hours on one consumer GPU plus modest API calls.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Anchor the endpoints: measure judge accuracy at the largest budget (8 quotes, low asymmetry) and at 0 quotes (max asymmetry). Expected: high accuracy with generous quotes, near-chance with none, confirming the curve has a usable range.
- Sweep the budget: run 1, 2, 4 quotes and plot accuracy vs quote budget. Expected: a monotone curve with an identifiable knee/breakpoint where accuracy drops sharply.
- Honest-vs-dishonest split: at each budget, separate accuracy on items the dishonest debater could exploit; measure how the breakpoint shifts. Expected: dishonest-exploitable items collapse at a larger budget, sharpening the operating-regime boundary.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If we can locate the asymmetry budget at which debate stops protecting a weak judge, then labs betting on debate for superhuman oversight get a concrete operating-regime boundary -> they can check whether their intended deployment (where the judge is far weaker than the task) sits on the safe side of the threshold -> reduces the risk of deploying a debate-based overseer in a regime where a dishonest debater can reliably push false claims past the judge. A model-agnostic threshold is directly transferable to that decision.

# Strength Rationale

Scores 5 on narrow scope, accessible complexity, and low compute: a single experiment that varies one knob (quote budget) and plots one curve, with an explicit success criterion (locate the breakpoint), inheriting a published methodology. Counterfactual value (4) is high because it uses open models and a public dataset with no lab infra, and the threshold itself is a transferable finding labs have not systematically measured.

# Alternative Framings

- Frame as a capacity-vs-asymmetry tradeoff: hold the quote budget fixed and instead vary judge size, mapping the joint surface of (judge capability x asymmetry) over which debate remains reliable.

# Cited Sources

- Khan et al. 2024 (arXiv:2402.06782) — debate raises non-expert judge accuracy to 76% (LLM)/88% (human) on QuALITY with short verified quotes; the setup this project reuses and sweeps.
- Kenton et al. 2024 (NeurIPS, arXiv:2407.04622) — weak LLMs judging strong LLMs, debate beats consultancy; neither paper systematically sweeps the asymmetry budget itself, which this project does.
