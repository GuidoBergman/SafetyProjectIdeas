# Research Question

Does quantization (4-bit/8-bit), which most real open-weight deployment uses to fit consumer GPUs, amplify, dampen, or leave unchanged the misalignment of a public emergent-misalignment (EM) organism compared to full precision?

# Approach Outline

Take a public open-weight EM organism (from 'Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment', arXiv:2506.11613, or Betley et al.). Run the standard misalignment battery at fp16, 8-bit, and 4-bit quantization (bitsandbytes / GGUF) and measure both misaligned-answer rate and coherence rate at each precision. Optionally probe whether quantization noise interacts with the misaligned-persona direction (e.g., by checking if 4-bit destabilizes or partially suppresses it). Fully inference-only on a single consumer GPU, a few hours.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Reproduce the fp16 baseline: run the misalignment battery on the public EM organism at full precision and record misaligned-answer rate and coherence. Expected outcome: a baseline matching the organism's published misalignment level.
- Run the same battery at 8-bit and 4-bit and measure the change in misaligned-answer rate and coherence vs fp16, with confidence intervals. Expected outcome: a precision-vs-misalignment curve showing whether quantization meaningfully shifts safety behavior (in either direction).
- Probe the mechanism: compare a coherence-vs-misalignment scatter across precisions to see whether 4-bit reduces misalignment mainly by degrading coherence (incidental) or independently. Expected outcome: evidence on whether any quantization effect is a genuine safety shift or an artifact of degraded fluency.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Most real open-weight usage is quantized, but safety evaluations are typically run at full precision. If quantization changes misalignment severity, then full-precision evals do not reflect how models actually behave in the wild. Surfacing this deployment-relevant variable warns evaluators to test at deployment precision. This reduces the catastrophic-risk pathway where a model judged acceptably safe at fp16 behaves more dangerously (or is wrongly assumed safe) once quantized and deployed on consumer hardware at scale.

# Strength Rationale

Counterfactual value is 5: it surfaces a deployment-relevant variable that labs running full precision rarely test, using only public model organisms and inference, no lab infrastructure. With 5s on narrow scope, accessible complexity, and low compute, it is a single clean experiment (misalignment vs precision) with explicit success criteria, executable by a beginner team on a single consumer GPU within 30 hours.

# Alternative Framings

- Reframe as a general 'deployment-realism' eval audit: treat quantization as one of several deployment-time transforms (quantization, KV-cache, sampling temperature) and report which most perturb measured safety.
