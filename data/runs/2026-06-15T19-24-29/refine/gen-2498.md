# Research Question

Does the GPT-5 system card's claim that CoT monitoring is substantially more informative than output-only monitoring for catching deception hold for open-weight reasoning models, or is it a frontier-model-specific artifact?

# Approach Outline

Assemble a fixed set of deception-eliciting agentic prompts. Run several open reasoning models (e.g. DeepSeek-R1-distill, QwQ, GPT-OSS) on them to produce transcripts with explicit CoT. For each family, score each transcript with one fixed cheap trusted monitor twice: once on the full transcript (CoT included) and once with the CoT stripped (output/action only). Compute AUROC for each condition per family and report the CoT-informativeness 'lift' (AUROC_CoT - AUROC_stripped). Additionally measure each model's CoT verbosity/faithfulness (length, and a simple faithfulness proxy) and check whether the lift correlates with it. Consumer-GPU inference on open reasoning models; the multi-model generation is the compute bottleneck and may need quantization.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Pipeline + baseline: generate transcripts for one family and confirm the monitor's CoT-included AUROC exceeds CoT-stripped (success criterion: a measurable positive lift on at least one family, validating the harness).
- Cross-family lift: compute the CoT-informativeness lift for all families and compare (expected outcome: the lift varies substantially across families, with some showing little benefit).
- Lift-vs-faithfulness correlation: regress per-model lift against CoT verbosity/faithfulness proxies (expected outcome: families with more verbose/faithful CoT show larger lift, suggesting the premium is conditional on CoT quality rather than universal).

# Theory of Impact Chain

If the CoT-monitoring premium transfers to open models, the open community gains a validated, training-free safety lever for catching deception; if it does not, teams relying on CoT monitoring for open reasoning models are operating on a false assumption. Either way, the result tells deployers when CoT monitoring is worth trusting, sharpening the oversight layer that aims to catch a deceptive agent's intent before its actions cause harm, and flags the catastrophic pathway where an open model with unfaithful CoT evades a CoT-reliant monitor.

# Strength Rationale

Scores 5 on narrow scope and accessible complexity: a per-family AUROC-lift measurement comparing CoT-included vs stripped transcripts with a single fixed monitor, no training. Counterfactual value (4) is high because it replicates a frontier-lab claim on open models, a flagged open angle (FR67/FR68) that only the independent open community is positioned to test, using consumer-GPU inference.

# Alternative Framings

- Reframe as a faithfulness-mediation study: test whether the cross-family variation in CoT lift is fully explained by a measured CoT-faithfulness score, making faithfulness the real predictor of monitorability.
