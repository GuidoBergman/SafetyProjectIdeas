# Research Question

Refusal training is known to be shallow (concentrated in early output tokens). If a harmful output starts with safe-looking tokens before turning unsafe, do BOTH the safety-trained generator AND the LLM overseer share this early-token blind spot, so that 'safe-start' unsafe outputs evade the monitor at a much higher rate than unsafe-from-the-start outputs?

# Approach Outline

Generate two matched sets of unsafe outputs from public models: (a) unsafe-from-start completions and (b) safe-start completions that open with benign/compliant text and then deliver the same harmful content. Use published superficial-alignment / prefix techniques to produce the safe-start variants. Run an open-model overseer with a fixed safety rubric over both sets and measure the miss-rate gap (safe-start miss-rate minus unsafe-from-start miss-rate). Optionally truncate the overseer's view to early tokens to test the early-token-blind-spot hypothesis directly. Generation plus classification inference on open models, a few GPU-hours.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Build matched unsafe sets: produce ~120 unsafe-from-start and ~120 safe-start unsafe completions with verified harmful content. Measure: set sizes and ground-truth harmfulness. Expected outcome: two distributions equally harmful in substance, differing only in opening tokens.
- Miss-rate gap: run the overseer on both sets. Measure: miss-rate on each and the gap. Expected outcome: substantially higher miss-rate on safe-start outputs, indicating a shared early-token blind spot.
- Mechanism probe: feed the overseer only the first N tokens vs the full output. Measure: how overseer judgments depend on early tokens. Expected outcome: overseer verdicts are dominated by early tokens, explaining why safe-start outputs evade it.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If both the generator's refusal behavior and the overseer's judgment hinge on early tokens, then a single trick (open safe, then turn harmful) defeats two safety layers at once, a correlated defense-in-depth failure. An attacker or a misaligned agent could reliably smuggle harmful content past automated monitoring, feeding the catastrophic-risk pathway where misuse or unsafe agent actions slip through layered safeguards that were assumed independent.

# Strength Rationale

Maxes narrow scope, accessible complexity, and low compute (5 each): a clean safe-start vs unsafe-from-start miss-rate comparison feasible in 30 hours. Counterfactual value (4) is strong because it uses only public models, needs no training, and surfaces a lab-external robustness finding about the overlap of refusal brittleness and oversight coverage that single-axis studies miss.

# Alternative Framings

- Frame as a correlated-blind-spot audit: test whether the generator's and overseer's failures occur on the same inputs rather than independently.
