# Research Question

Does debate still beat consultancy at helping a judge reach the truth when the HONEST side is argued by a deliberately weaker debater while the dishonest side is strong - the adversarial capability-asymmetry regime that matters most for superhuman oversight?

# Approach Outline

On a verifiable QA task (e.g. QuALITY), generate debate and consultancy transcripts with open models, but deliberately handicap the HONEST debater - using a smaller model and/or a tighter token budget - while the dishonest debater stays strong. A fixed open judge then decides. Compare judge accuracy under debate vs consultancy across a sweep of honest-debater handicap levels (parity, mild, severe). Inference-only on open models; the main effort is orchestrating the protocols, so a mentor helps set up the debate/consultancy harness, but all compute fits a single GPU within the budget. Success criterion: judge accuracy for debate vs consultancy at three or more handicap levels over at least 100 questions each, with the crossover point (if any) identified.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Parity baseline: run debate and consultancy with honest and dishonest debaters at equal strength and measure judge accuracy. Measure: accuracy gap (debate minus consultancy). Expected: debate matches or beats consultancy, replicating prior parity results and validating the harness.
- Handicap sweep: progressively weaken the honest debater (smaller model, fewer tokens) while keeping the dishonest one strong, re-running both protocols. Measure: judge accuracy vs handicap level for each protocol. Expected: debate's accuracy declines as the honest side is outmatched; the key question is whether it stays above consultancy or collapses faster.
- Crossover and mitigation: identify the handicap level (if any) where consultancy overtakes debate, and test whether giving the judge a verification checklist shifts that crossover. Measure: crossover handicap level with vs without the checklist. Expected: a crossover exists, and cheap judge scaffolding pushes it to larger handicaps, indicating a partial fix for the weak-honest regime.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Debate's safety case rests on the honest debater being able to win. The regime that matters for superhuman oversight is exactly when the truth-teller is OUTMATCHED - a weaker overseer-aligned model arguing against a stronger, possibly deceptive system. If debate's truth-tracking collapses there, the protocol fails precisely where we most need it, so labs betting on debate would be relying on a false guarantee. Mapping when debate beats consultancy under honest-side handicap tells the field whether debate is trustworthy in the adversarial capability-gap regime, directly informing whether it can safely gate catastrophic actions by superhuman models.

# Strength Rationale

Scores 5 on theory of impact, counterfactual value, and low compute: it stress-tests debate's core load-bearing assumption in the adversarial regime that decides superhuman-oversight viability, using only public models and text rollouts on one GPU. Counterfactual value is gold-tier because the weak-honest-debater regime is explicitly under-tested, external-friendly, and needs no lab resources - an independent team can deliver a genuinely decision-relevant result.

# Alternative Framings

- Reframe the handicap as an INFORMATION asymmetry rather than capability: give the dishonest debater access to a misleading-but-relevant document the honest debater lacks, testing robustness to evidence asymmetry instead of raw capability.
