# Research Question

When a base model is fine-tuned into public sibling variants (instruct, RLHF, uncensored), does a refusal/toxicity concept direction extracted from one variant still transfer to the others, or does fine-tuning displace the direction's geometry?

# Approach Outline

Pick one base model with 2-3 publicly available fine-tuned siblings (e.g. a Llama or Qwen base plus its instruct, an RLHF/chat variant, and a community 'uncensored' fine-tune). Extract the refusal (and optionally toxicity) direction in each variant using the difference-of-means recipe at a matched layer. Compute pairwise cosine similarity between the directions and measure cross-variant steering transfer: apply variant A's direction to variant B and record the change in refusal/toxicity behavior. Summarize as a similarity matrix and a transfer-effectiveness matrix. All steps are inference-only over a handful of variants on one consumer GPU; a mentor helps with layer matching across variants.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Per-variant extraction: extract the refusal direction in each of the base + 2-3 variants and verify each direction steers its own model. Measure within-variant steering effect. Expected outcome: each variant yields a functional refusal direction, confirming extraction works across siblings.
- Pairwise cosine similarity: compute cosine similarity between all variant directions at the matched layer. Measure the similarity matrix, especially base-vs-uncensored. Expected outcome: instruct/RLHF variants stay similar to base while the uncensored fine-tune shows the largest displacement.
- Cross-variant steering transfer: apply each variant's direction to the others and measure refusal/toxicity change versus the native direction. Measure transfer-effectiveness drop. Expected outcome: transfer degrades most for the uncensored variant, showing fine-tuning can break probe generalization across descendants.

# Theory of Impact Chain

The open ecosystem is built on fine-tuning: one base model spawns many community descendants, including deliberately uncensored ones. If safety concept directions do not transfer across these siblings, then a probe or steering vector validated on the base/instruct model gives false assurance about its descendants. Measuring this drift tells auditors whether interpretability-based safety tooling generalizes across the fine-tune family, mitigating the misuse pathway where an uncensored descendant evades safety probes tuned on its safer sibling.

# Strength Rationale

Counterfactual value (5): it studies the open fine-tune ecosystem that labs do not control and needs no internal access — exactly the third-party-relevant work outside-lab teams are positioned to do. Narrow scope (5): a fixed base, a few public variants, and two crisp matrices (cosine similarity + steering transfer) with explicit success criteria, keeping it a self-contained 30-hour project grounded in a well-known result.

# Alternative Framings

- Frame as 'does my safety probe survive my users' fine-tunes?' — a generalization audit for anyone shipping a base model others will adapt.
