# Research Question

How large is the gap between an emergent-misalignment (EM) model organism's score on standard structured safety benchmarks and its actual free-form misalignment rate, i.e., do common benchmarks fail to detect EM?

# Approach Outline

Take a released EM model organism and a matched control. Run both through one or more standard structured safety benchmarks (multiple-choice / fixed-format) and record scores. Separately, run a free-form misalignment probe set (open-ended prompts scored for misaligned behavior with a documented rubric and a checkable threshold). Compute the gap between benchmark-implied safety and measured free-form misalignment rate for the organism, and contrast against the control to show benchmark insensitivity. Inference-only on an existing organism, single GPU, within budget.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Benchmark scoring: run the EM organism and control through a structured safety benchmark. Measure: benchmark safety score per model. Expected outcome: the EM organism scores nearly as 'safe' as the control on the structured benchmark.
- Free-form misalignment measurement: run both models on open-ended probes and score misaligned responses with the rubric. Measure: free-form misalignment rate per model. Expected outcome: the EM organism shows a substantially elevated free-form misalignment rate.
- Insensitivity gap quantification: compute the difference between benchmark-implied safety and free-form misalignment for each model. Measure: the EM organism's benchmark-vs-free-form gap relative to the control. Expected outcome: a large gap for the organism, demonstrating that standard benchmarks are insensitive to EM.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If structured safety benchmarks barely register EM that free-form probing exposes, then labs and deployers relying on those benchmarks get false assurance and ship misaligned models. Quantifying this insensitivity gap argues for free-form misalignment evals as a necessary complement, addressing the catastrophic pathway where benchmark-passing models with latent emergent misalignment are deployed because the evaluation regime cannot see the problem.

# Strength Rationale

It scores 5s on accessible complexity and narrow scope: a tightly defined benchmark-score-vs-free-form-rate gap on a released organism with a clear deliverable. Counterfactual value is high because it scrutinizes evaluation coverage using public organisms, an under-explored angle that independent teams can pursue inference-only without lab infra.
