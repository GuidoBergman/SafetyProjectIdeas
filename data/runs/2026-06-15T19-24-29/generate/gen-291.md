# Phylogenetic Tree Reconstruction of Jailbreak Lineages

**Problem:** Jailbreak templates evolve by mutation and recombination but we treat them as an unstructured list, losing the evolutionary structure that could predict the next variant.

**Direction:** Transfer phylogenetics. Treat a corpus of public jailbreak templates as 'taxa', define edit/semantic distance as a 'genetic' distance, and reconstruct a phylogenetic tree (neighbor-joining/UPGMA) plus identify conserved 'motifs' across clades. Test whether models that resist one clade resist its sister clades, i.e. whether the tree predicts cross-resistance.

**Why it matters:** Provides structure for anticipating attack evolution and for designing defenses that target conserved motifs rather than individual strings. Uses public corpora + open embeddings; entirely external to labs.

**Relevant context:** Neighbor-joining/UPGMA phylogenetics are standard bioinformatics. GPTFUZZER (arXiv:2309.10253) and TurboFuzzLLM (arXiv:2502.18504) generate template families via mutation/crossover, supplying real lineage data.
