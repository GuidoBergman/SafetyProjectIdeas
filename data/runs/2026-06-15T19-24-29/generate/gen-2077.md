# Layer- and Rank-Localization of EM: Where Does Misalignment Live in a Small Open Model?

**Problem:** Success state: we know the minimal intervention surface for inducing/removing EM, enabling targeted defenses. It's reported EM can be induced with rank-1 LoRA in only a few layers — but the layer profile and minimal rank are not mapped for a reproducible open model.

**Direction:** Systematically fine-tune a 0.5B open model with LoRA varying (a) which layer block adapters are placed in and (b) rank (1,2,4). Measure induced misalignment per configuration to produce a layer-x-rank heatmap of EM susceptibility.

**Why it matters:** Knowing where EM is cheapest to induce tells defenders where to watch and constrain LoRA fine-tunes — practical guidance for open-model release safety. Small, single-GPU sweep well-suited to the participant.

**Relevant context:** Soligo/Turner/Rajamanoharan/Nanda report EM induced via rank-1 LoRA in only a few layers of 0.5B models; EM repo provides datasets and training scripts.
