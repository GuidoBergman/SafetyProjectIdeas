# When the Overseen Model Knows It Is Being Overseen

**Problem:** Evaluation-awareness — a model behaving differently when it detects oversight — is a growing concern. Whether telling a debater/answerer that 'a weak judge will check you' changes its honesty or its manipulation tactics is unstudied at small scale.

**Direction:** Compare answerer behavior with and without an explicit 'your answer will be audited by a weaker model' prompt, on factual QA where deception is tempting. Measure changes in honesty, hedging, and exploitation of judge biases. A small model-organism probe of oversight-aware behavior.

**Why it matters:** If models adapt their manipulation to the overseer's weakness when they know they are watched, static oversight evaluations overstate safety — a long-horizon threat. Scoped, prompt-only, no training.

**Relevant context:** Evaluation-awareness / sandbagging literature; steering brief favors probing model-organism behaviors like hidden goals. Kenton et al. (2024) judge-bias exploitation; Open Phil reward-hacking-of-oversight priority.
