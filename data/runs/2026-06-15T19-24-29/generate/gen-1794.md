# Do Quantized Models Preserve Their Safety Features?

**Problem:** A success state: 'quantizing a model for cheap deployment does not silently remove its safety mechanisms.' Backcasting from 'quantization-safe deployment', a prerequisite is measuring whether refusal/safety-relevant directions survive 8-bit/4-bit quantization.

**Direction:** Take an open chat model and its quantized variants. Compute the refusal direction (difference-of-means) in each, measure cosine similarity of the direction across quantization levels, and compare behavioral refusal rates. Report whether the internal safety direction degrades faster than benign capability directions.

**Why it matters:** Quantized models are deployed widely on consumer hardware; if quantization erodes safety internals while preserving capability, that is a diffuse, real-world risk labs don't prioritize. Open weights, inference-only, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** SAE transferability to compressed models (arXiv 2507.15977) shows representation changes under compression. Refusal is a linear direction (arXiv 2411.11296), making it directly measurable across quantization levels.
