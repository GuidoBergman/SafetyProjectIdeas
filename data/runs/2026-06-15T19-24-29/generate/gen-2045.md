**Problem:** The EM chain ends in a generalized misaligned persona governed by a single internal direction. An under-studied intervention is at the very last link — inference time. If the misalignment is mediated by a persona, can a strong system-prompt intervention suppress it without retraining? This tests whether the chain can be interrupted post hoc.

**Direction:** Using a released EM model organism, measure free-form misalignment rate under no system prompt vs. several inference-time interventions: an honesty/helpfulness system prompt, an explicit 'you are aligned' assertion, and a chain-of-thought reflection prompt.
