# AgentDojo Prompt Injection on Open-Weight Small Models

**Problem:** AgentDojo established that GPT-4o's benign utility drops from 69% to 45% under indirect prompt injection, but the published attack-success numbers are concentrated on a handful of large frontier models. It is unknown whether small open-weight models (e.g., Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B) that beginners and hobbyists actually self-host are more or less injectable per parameter.

**Direction:** Run the open-source AgentDojo harness unchanged (banking/Slack/travel/workspace task suites, 'Important message' canonical attack) against 3-4 small open-weight instruct models via a single consumer GPU or hosted API. Report benign utility, targeted attack success rate, and the utility-under-attack gap, then compare against the published GPT-4o baselines to test whether susceptibility scales with model size.

**Why it matters:** Self-hosted small models are exactly the deployment surface that frontier labs do not study, since labs benchmark their own large models. A clean size-vs-injectability curve tells the open-source agent community which models are dangerously soft targets, requires no training, and is fully reproducible outside any lab.

**Relevant context:** AgentDojo (NeurIPS 2024, ETH SPYLab): 97 user tasks, 629 security test cases; GPT-4o benign utility 69% dropping to 45% under attack, 'Important message' attack reaching 53.1% targeted success. Harness is open-source and integrated into Inspect Evals.
