# Best-of-N Overoptimization Curve: Watching an Open Reward Model Get Goodharted in 2 Hours

**Problem:** Success state: we can cheaply demonstrate and quantify reward overoptimization without RL training, giving newcomers an intuition and a measurement. Scaling-laws-for-overoptimization studies require expensive RL; we want a best-of-n proxy reproducible on one GPU.

**Direction:** Use a fixed open policy model to sample N candidate responses per prompt, rank with an open proxy reward model, and select the top-1 as N grows (1,4,16,64,256). Evaluate selected responses with an independent 'gold' judge. Plot gold quality vs. proxy reward to show the overoptimization turnaround point.

**Why it matters:** A pure-inference demonstration of Goodharting makes overoptimization legible and measurable for the open community, and the turnaround-N is a useful practical safety knob. No RL, no lab infra.

**Relevant context:** Reward overoptimization (Gao et al. scaling laws; arXiv:2406.02900 for direct-alignment) shows gold reward drops as proxy reward is over-optimized. Best-of-n is a standard cheap proxy for KL-budgeted optimization.
