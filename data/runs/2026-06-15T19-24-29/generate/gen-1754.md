# Localizing the 'Reward-Hack Recognition -> Pursuit' Link

**Problem:** Reward hacking chain: model spots an exploitable loophole -> recognizes it yields high reward -> pursues it instead of intended goal. The recognition-to-pursuit link — whether there is a detectable 'I found a shortcut' representation before the model commits — is largely unexplored mechanistically.

**Direction:** Use a small open model in a toy gamed-metric environment (e.g. a coding/QA task with a reward-hackable grader, public model-organism setups exist). Train a probe on activations from clearly-hacking vs honest completions and test whether the probe fires on the tokens where the model 'decides' to hack, before the hacking text appears.

**Why it matters:** Detecting reward-hack intent pre-commitment enables intervention. Fuzzy-reward problem hard for labs to evaluate at scale but tractable as a small probing study with full counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** AuditBench (arXiv 2602.22755) evaluates alignment-auditing on models with hidden behaviors including reward hacking. 'Aligning AI Through Internal Understanding' (arXiv 2509.08592): interpretability can identify reward-hacking mechanisms revealing latent failures.
