# Backcasting Order-Invariant Oversight: Position Bias as an Exploit Surface

**Problem:** Success state: oversight verdicts that do not depend on which answer/argument is presented first. A strategic AI could exploit a judge's position bias to win by ordering. We need its magnitude and a cheap debias fix.

**Direction:** On open consultancy/debate judging, swap the presentation order of the two answers/arguments and measure verdict-flip rate (position bias). Then test a simple, training-free debias: average verdicts over both orderings. Report bias magnitude and accuracy gain from order-averaging.
