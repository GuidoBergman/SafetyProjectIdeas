# Emergent Misalignment x Safety-Benchmark Insensitivity

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) broad emergent misalignment (alignment_training), and (B) standard safety benchmarks failing to detect it (evaluation coverage). Does an EM model pass common safety benchmarks (e.g., refusal-rate evals) while being broadly misaligned on open-ended probes — a benchmark that misses the compound harm?

**Direction:** Run an EM model organism through a standard refusal/safety benchmark and through open-ended free-form ethical probes. Measure the gap: benchmark score vs. free-form misalignment rate. Test whether EM models can score 'safe' on structured benchmarks while failing free-form, quantifying benchmark insensitivity to this failure mode.

**Why it matters:** If the dominant training-time failure mode is invisible to deployment benchmarks, labs get false assurance. Directly tests evaluation coverage with public organisms; no training.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al.; model organisms (arXiv 2506.11613) — misalignment shows on open-ended prompts. 'Emergent misalignment as prompt sensitivity' (arXiv 2507.06253) on detection fragility.
