# Information-Theoretic Channel Capacity of a Model Panel: How Much Independent Information Do N Models Carry?

**Problem:** Shannon information theory quantifies redundancy: N noisy sources may carry far less than N times the information if correlated. A panel of homogeneous LLMs may carry only a fraction of the independent information its size suggests, but this is never quantified in bits.

**Direction:** Borrow mutual-information / total-correlation (multi-information) estimators. Treat each model's discretized response on a query set as a random variable, estimate the total correlation across the panel, and report the 'redundancy ratio': fraction of joint entropy that is shared vs independent. Plot how redundancy grows as you add same-lineage vs cross-lineage models.

**Why it matters:** Gives a bits-based, source-coding view of model homogeneity — directly quantifying diminishing returns of redundancy for safety oversight. Pure information-theory + inference, ideal for the participant's stats background.

**Relevant context:** Shannon redundancy / total correlation (multi-information) for correlated sources. Diversity prediction & wisdom-of-crowds independence. Artificial Hivemind inter-model homogeneity (arXiv:2510.22954).
