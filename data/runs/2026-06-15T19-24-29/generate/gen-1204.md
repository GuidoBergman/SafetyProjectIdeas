# Does Model Similarity Predict Shared Blind Spots? A Cheap Predictive Test

**Problem:** If we could predict correlated failure from an easily-measured similarity score, monitoring concentration risk would be tractable. The under-studied link: does output-similarity (Hivemind-style) actually PREDICT safety-failure correlation, or are blind spots orthogonal to general similarity?

**Direction:** For a pool of models, compute pairwise general output similarity (on neutral open-ended queries) and pairwise safety-failure correlation (on a safety probe set) separately. Then test whether general similarity predicts safety-failure correlation (correlation/regression). Report whether cheap similarity is a usable proxy for shared-blind-spot risk.

**Why it matters:** A positive result gives regulators/deployers a cheap leading indicator of correlated-failure risk without running every safety test. A negative result warns that benign-looking diversity can still hide shared blind spots. Either way, decision-relevant and lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in Artificial Hivemind similarity methodology (arXiv 2510.22954) and jailbreak-transfer/similarity correlation (arXiv 2506.12913). Addresses the concentration-monitoring open problem.
