# LayerSweepBench: A Standard Tool for Optimal Steering Layer Selection

**Problem:** Steering effectiveness depends heavily on the injection layer, but practitioners pick layers ad hoc and there is no standard tool/metric reporting the full layer profile of a steering vector for a model.

**Direction:** Build a reusable layer-sweep utility that, for any CAA vector, computes effect size and fluency cost at every layer and outputs a normalized 'best layer' recommendation plus the full profile plot. Apply across 4-5 behaviors and 2 model families to test whether optimal layers are consistent within/across families.

**Why it matters:** A standardized layer-selection tool removes a major source of irreproducibility in steering papers and lowers the barrier for newcomers. Whether optimal layers transfer across models is itself a useful, unmeasured property. Single-GPU, inference-only.

**Relevant context:** CAA adds vectors at chosen layers; layer choice strongly affects results (nrimsky/CAA, steering-vectors). No standardized layer-selection benchmark exists per Beaglehole et al.
