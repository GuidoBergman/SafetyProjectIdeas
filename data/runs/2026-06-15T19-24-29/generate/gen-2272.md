# TranscriptLength-Bench: Does Longer Debate Help or Just Tire the Judge?

**Problem:** Debate protocols vary turn counts arbitrarily, and there is no benchmark measuring the marginal effect of each additional debate turn on judge accuracy — whether returns are positive, flat, or negative.

**Direction:** Generate debates of 1, 2, 3, and 4 turns per side on a fixed QA set with open debaters and a fixed judge, and report judge accuracy and verdict stability as a function of turn count. Deliver the variable-length debate harness and the marginal-turn curve.

**Why it matters:** Picking debate depth without evidence wastes compute and may degrade oversight; a marginal-turn benchmark gives a principled, cheap design knob for outside researchers building debate harnesses.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. 2024 and Khan et al. 2024 use fixed multi-turn debate formats; length-bias literature (LLM-judge surveys) suggests longer transcripts may bias judges independent of content.
