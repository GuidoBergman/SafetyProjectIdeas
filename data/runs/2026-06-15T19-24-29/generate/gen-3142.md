# Deception Probe Robustness to Prompt-Format Shift

**Problem:** Apollo's deception probes report a 1% false-positive rate on unrelated chat. Whether that low FPR survives a distribution shift in surface format (e.g. JSON outputs, code-comment style, roleplay framing) on the same model is untested.

**Direction:** Train Apollo-style deception probe on one open model, then evaluate its false-positive rate on benign chat reformatted into several surface styles. Measure how much FPR rises under format shift — i.e., how brittle the deception monitor is to harmless reformatting.

**Why it matters:** If deception monitors false-alarm on benign format changes, they will be ignored in practice; characterizing this brittleness is a cheap, lab-independent reliability audit of a leading monitoring approach.

**Relevant context:** Apollo 'Detecting Strategic Deception Using Linear Probes' (OpenReview C5Jj3QKQav): AUROC 0.96-0.999, 1% FPR on unrelated chat, known to fire on honest confessions. Zou et al. 2023 representation engineering basis.
