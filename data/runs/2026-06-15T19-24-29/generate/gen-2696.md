# Concentrated vs. Distributed Failures x Monitor Thresholds: Compounding Through Misconfigured Alerting

**Problem:** UK AISI notes concentrated and distributed safety failures need different monitoring. A control failure — tuning a monitor threshold for one regime — compounds the detection failure in the other: a threshold set to catch a single catastrophic action misses many small distributed defections, and vice versa. The mismatch between failure type and threshold is itself a compound risk.

**Direction:** Simulate two attack profiles against a monitored open-model agent: one rare high-severity action and many low-severity actions. For a sweep of monitor thresholds, measure caught-harm under each profile. Show the threshold that protects against concentrated failure leaves distributed failure undetected (and the cost of trying to cover both).

**Why it matters:** Directly operationalizes an AISI open problem with a measurable trade-off curve. Helps small teams reason about monitor configuration without lab infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** UK AISI 'Research Areas in AI Control': detect concentrated vs. distributed failures need different approaches; control-game framing (Greenblatt et al. arXiv:2312.06942).
