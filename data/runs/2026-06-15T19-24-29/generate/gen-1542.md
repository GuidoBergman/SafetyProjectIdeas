# Attribution-Method Disagreement as a Faithfulness Red Flag

**Problem:** Faithfulness literature notes strong disagreement between different attribution methods casts doubt that any of them captures true causes. A simple, reusable disagreement diagnostic for circuit-discovery on small models has not been packaged for newcomers.

**Direction:** On a known small circuit (e.g. GPT-2 indirect-object identification), compute component importance via three cheap methods (logit-lens attribution, activation patching, attribution patching) and quantify their rank-correlation/agreement. Test whether disagreement spikes on harder/safety-relevant prompts, and ship the diagnostic as a small reusable tool.

**Why it matters:** A lightweight 'do my attribution methods even agree?' check is strategically valuable infrastructure: it lets every independent interpretability project self-audit faithfulness before drawing safety conclusions.

**Relevant context:** Faithfulness literature: strong disagreement between attribution methods undermines the faithfulness requirement; attribution patching approximates patching cheaply. GPT-2 IOI circuit is a standard, well-documented benchmark in TransformerLens.
