# Safetywashing Replication: Do Today's 'Safety' Benchmarks Still Just Measure Capabilities?

**Problem:** The 2024 'Safetywashing' paper showed many AI safety benchmarks correlate strongly with general capabilities, meaning capability gains can be relabeled as safety progress. As new benchmarks have proliferated since, it is unknown whether the field has corrected this failure mode or whether newer 'safety' benchmarks remain entangled with capability.

**Direction:** Reproduce the safetywashing methodology on a current set of models and benchmarks. Collect public leaderboard scores (Open LLM Leaderboard, HELM, public safety-eval results) for ~15-30 models across both capability and recent safety benchmarks. Extract the first principal component of capability scores, then compute Spearman correlation of each safety benchmark with that PC. Flag which post-2024 safety benchmarks are 'safetywashing-prone' (high correlation) vs genuinely decorrelated.

**Why it matters:** Regulators and the public increasingly cite safety-benchmark scores as evidence of compliance. If those scores are just capability proxies, the entire evidentiary basis of voluntary safety claims is undermined. The original authors released code; replication on fresh benchmarks is feasible, GPU-light (uses published scores), and exactly the kind of independent auditing labs won't do on themselves.

**Relevant context:** Safetywashing (arXiv 2407.21792, CAIS) with public GitHub repo; uses Spearman correlation against capability PCA; flagged scalable-oversight and misconception benchmarks as most prone.
