# Guardrail Classifier Blind Spots vs the Model It Guards

**Problem:** Lightweight guardrail classifiers (e.g., small safety filters) are deployed in front of open models, but whether their blind spots align with or diverge from the protected model's own refusal blind spots — determining whether the guardrail adds real coverage — is unmeasured for accessible setups.

**Direction:** Pair an open guardrail/moderation classifier with an open chat model and, over a jailbreak suite, categorize each prompt by (guardrail catches?) x (model refuses?). Quantify the fraction the guardrail uniquely catches versus cases where both fail together (correlated blind spots).

**Why it matters:** Correlated failures mean a guardrail adds little marginal safety — a deployment-critical, under-examined question for small actors building safer harnesses. Uses only open components, no training, and yields a reusable coverage-overlap methodology.

**Relevant context:** Cost-constrained monitor combination (arXiv:2507.15886) and MrGuard (arXiv:2504.15241) study guardrails; AISafetyLab provides eval tooling. The marginal-coverage/overlap analysis between a guardrail and its protected model's intrinsic refusals is not standardly reported.
