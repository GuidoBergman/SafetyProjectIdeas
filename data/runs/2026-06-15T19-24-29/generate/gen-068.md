# Causal mediation analysis to quantify how much CoT actually mediates the answer

**Problem:** CoT faithfulness is often assessed by perturbing the prompt and seeing if the answer changes, but this conflates direct and indirect effects. We need to quantify the fraction of a hint's influence that flows *through* the visible reasoning versus around it.

**Direction:** Import causal mediation analysis (natural direct vs. indirect effects) from epidemiology/causal inference. Treat an injected hint as the treatment, the CoT as the mediator, the answer as the outcome. Estimate the indirect effect (hint -> CoT -> answer) vs. direct effect (hint -> answer bypassing CoT) by counterfactually swapping in a clean CoT. Report the 'mediated proportion' as a faithfulness score across open reasoning models.

**Why it matters:** Gives a quantitative, theory-grounded faithfulness metric distinguishing 'reasoning is decorative' from 'reasoning is load-bearing' — sharper than current behavioral tests and directly relevant to CoT-monitoring safety cases.
