# Process-vs-Outcome Scoring Variant for Agentic Safety

**Problem:** Most agentic safety benchmarks score only the final output or terminal state, missing trajectories that reach a benign answer via unauthorized actions (harness-safety failures). The gap between outcome-only and process-aware scoring is rarely quantified.

**Direction:** Take an open agentic benchmark (AgentHarm in Inspect AI), and for each run, score safety two ways: outcome-only (final answer) and process-aware (any unsafe intermediate tool call). Measure how many trajectories are 'safe by outcome but unsafe by process' across open agent models.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies a blind spot that output-level evals literally cannot see, directly addressing agentic-safety and deployment-monitoring open problems. Builds a small reusable trajectory-scoring harness — strategically valuable infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2605.14271 'Auditing Agent Harness Safety' and 'Who Tests the Testers?' (arXiv 2603.18245) note output-level evaluation cannot see unauthorized-resource or context-leak failures; AgentHarm logs full tool-call trajectories in Inspect AI.
