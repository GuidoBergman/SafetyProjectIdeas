# Does Eval-Awareness Generalize Across Risk Domains Within One Model?

**Problem:** Eval-awareness is measured aggregate, but whether a model that detects 'this is a test' for cyber-capability prompts also detects it for bias/harm prompts — i.e. is awareness a single direction or domain-specific — is unexplored and changes how steerable it is.

**Direction:** Collect eval transcripts spanning several risk domains (cyber, bio-info, persuasion, bias). Compute a simple eval-vs-deployment classification or activation difference-vector per domain in one open model, then test cross-domain transfer: does the cyber-derived direction classify bias transcripts? Report a transfer matrix.

**Why it matters:** If awareness is one shared direction, a single steering fix neutralizes it everywhere; if domain-specific, evaluators must address each domain. Inference/activation extraction on a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness (arXiv 2507.01786); Evaluation Awareness Scales Predictably (arXiv 2509.13333) reports awareness across 61 datasets but does not test cross-domain direction transfer.
