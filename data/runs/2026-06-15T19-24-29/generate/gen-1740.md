# Reaction-Time Distribution Analysis for Model 'Planning' Depth

**Problem:** Anthropic found Claude plans ahead in poetry, but we lack a cheap behavioral signature of how far ahead a model commits, analogous to how cognitive science uses response-time distributions to infer processing stages.

**Direction:** Transfer reaction-time / sequential-sampling analysis from cognitive psychology to logit-lens trajectories. For each generated token, measure how many layers before the output the correct token first becomes the top logit (a 'commitment depth' analog of RT), and analyze its distribution across planning-required vs. local tasks. Test whether deeper commitment correlates with look-ahead behavior on an open model.

**Why it matters:** A cheap behavioral-mechanistic signature of planning depth lets anyone probe look-ahead/planning (a key safety-relevant capability) without lab tooling, and follows up directly on the surprising planning-ahead result.

**Relevant context:** The planning-ahead result is from Anthropic Circuit Tracing (transformer-circuits.pub, 2025), flagged for follow-up in the context.
