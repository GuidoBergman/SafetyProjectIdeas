# Faithfulness of Verbalized Confidence vs Hidden Sandbagging

**Problem:** Models that sandbag verbally express plausible confidence. Whether a model's stated confidence diverges from its true accuracy specifically when sandbagging (a confidence-faithfulness gap) is an underexplored replication twist on sandbagging detection.

**Direction:** Prompt an open model to sandbag on a benchmark while also reporting per-question confidence, and compare the calibration curve under sandbagging vs honest conditions. Test whether a calibration-mismatch signal detects the sandbagging behavior.

**Why it matters:** Confidence-calibration shifts would be a cheap, behavior-only sandbagging detector requiring no internals — usable by external auditors of API models, a strong counterfactual-value tool.

**Relevant context:** van der Weij et al. 'AI Sandbagging' (arXiv:2406.07358). MALT (METR) sandbagging transcripts (AUROC ~0.91). Builds on the context file follow-up to 'test with more sophisticated sandbagging detection methods'.
