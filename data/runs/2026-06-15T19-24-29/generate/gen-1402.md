# Replicating text-derived steering vectors steering vision in a different VLM

**Problem:** Recent work shows text-only steering vectors (e.g., for 'spatial relationships', 'counting') can steer the visual reasoning of a VLM, suggesting unified cross-modal concepts. This was shown on specific VLMs; whether it replicates on a different open VLM backbone is open.

**Direction:** Reproduce the 'text steers vision' result on a different open VLM (e.g., Qwen2-VL or LLaVA-1.6) by extracting a concept vector (e.g., 'refusal' or 'caution') from the language backbone on text-only contrasts, then injecting it during image+text inference and measuring whether safety-relevant visual responses shift. Twist: a safety concept rather than a capability concept, on a new backbone.

**Why it matters:** Beaglehole et al. flag extending concept steering to multimodal models as an open problem. If text-derived safety vectors control multimodal outputs, cheap text-based steering could harden VLMs against image-based jailbreaks — work fully doable outside a lab on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** 'Text Steers Vision' (LessWrong); SteerVLM (arXiv:2510.26769); Beaglehole et al. multimodal extension open problem.
