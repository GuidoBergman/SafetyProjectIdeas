# Quantization and Safety: Does 4-bit Compression Open New Jailbreak Surface?

**Problem:** Practitioners routinely run quantized (4-bit/8-bit) open models on consumer hardware, but whether quantization measurably degrades safety alignment — making attacks that fail on full precision succeed on the quantized version — is under-characterized despite huge deployment relevance.

**Direction:** Take one open aligned model and evaluate the SAME attack battery at fp16, 8-bit, and 4-bit. Hold prompts/seeds fixed and measure per-attack ASR deltas across precisions. Identify whether quantization systematically shifts the refusal boundary and which attack families are most affected, and whether degradation tracks perplexity change.

**Why it matters:** Quantized local models are the dominant way ordinary users and small orgs run LLMs; if quantization silently weakens safety it is a widespread, overlooked failure mode. The study is uniquely suited to consumer GPUs (quantization is what makes them fit) and needs no lab resources — strong counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** Shallow-alignment (Qi et al., arXiv:2406.05946) implies small perturbations to the distribution over early tokens can flip refusal; quantization perturbs exactly these logits. AISafetyLab and StrongREJECT provide the attack battery and grader. bitsandbytes 4/8-bit loading runs on a single consumer GPU.
