# Position-Bias Robustness as an Oversight Reliability Metric Across Open Judges

**Problem:** Position bias (preferring the first/last presented answer) is repeatedly flagged (SoK arXiv:2603.29403; Judging the Judges arXiv:2604.23178) yet treated as a nuisance rather than a quantified per-model oversight-reliability property. A standardized position-bias robustness score across open judges is missing.

**Direction:** For each of several open judge models, present answer pairs in both orders and compute a position-bias index (verdict flip rate due to ordering alone) over a labeled set. Rank judges by robustness and test whether simple order-randomization or dual-pass averaging eliminates it. Publish the index as a comparable oversight metric.

**Why it matters:** A simple, comparable reliability score that any external researcher can compute helps select trustworthy overseers and exposes which models should not be used unaided. Pure inference, highly feasible.

**Relevant context:** Security in LLM-as-a-Judge SoK (arXiv:2603.29403, 2026) and Judging the Judges (arXiv:2604.23178, Apr 2026) both flag position bias as a systematic, prevalent judge bias that compromises evaluation reliability.
