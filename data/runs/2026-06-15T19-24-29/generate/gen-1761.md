# Localizing the 'Bias Cue -> Biased Decision' Link in Fair-Decision Tasks

**Problem:** Discrimination chain: prompt contains a demographic cue -> model encodes a protected attribute -> attribute influences the decision -> biased output. Whether the protected attribute is causally read at the decision step (vs merely represented and ignored) is the safety-relevant under-studied link.

**Direction:** On an open model doing a decision task (e.g. resume screening prompts), train a probe for an encoded demographic attribute from name/pronoun cues. Use activation patching to swap the attribute representation between matched candidates and measure whether the decision flips — directly testing the causal bias link.

**Why it matters:** Distinguishes 'model notices demographics' from 'demographics cause the decision', which is what fairness audits actually need. No lab data required; uses synthetic matched prompts and open weights.

**Relevant context:** Activation patching for causal mediation is standard in TransformerLens. The known/unknown-entity steering work (arXiv 2411.14257) shows attribute directions can causally shift outputs; bias attributes are a direct analog.
