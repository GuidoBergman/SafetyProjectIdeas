# Replicating an LLM-as-Auditor Pipeline for Incident Classification With a Calibration Twist

**Problem:** Recent AIID work uses LLMs to classify incidents against risk taxonomies and emits a confidence rating, but whether those confidence ratings are CALIBRATED (i.e., 70%-confidence classifications are right ~70% of the time) is rarely checked. Miscalibrated automated classifiers could silently corrupt the evidence base national incident-response systems would rely on.

**Direction:** Replicate an LLM-based incident-classification pipeline on a labeled subset of AIID incidents using an open model, then add the twist of a calibration analysis: bin predictions by self-reported confidence and plot reliability curves, comparing the open model against a stronger reference. Report Brier score and over/under-confidence by taxonomy category.

**Why it matters:** Trustworthy automated triage is a prerequisite for scalable incident reporting (NTSB-for-AI open problem). Calibration is exactly the kind of unglamorous but decision-critical evaluation labs deprioritize. Open data, open model, single GPU.
