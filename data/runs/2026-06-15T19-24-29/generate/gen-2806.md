# Is Self-Preference Bias in LLM Judges Driven by Perplexity Alone?

**Problem:** Self-preference bias in LLM judges has been linked to outputs having lower perplexity to the judge. Whether perplexity fully mediates the bias, or there is residual self-recognition, is an open follow-up that determines how to debias.

**Direction:** Have an open judge model score paired responses (its own vs another model's) and record per-response perplexity under the judge. Regress judge preference on the perplexity difference; the residual preference after controlling for perplexity isolates non-perplexity self-recognition. Optionally rewrite the judge's own outputs to raise their perplexity and check if preference shrinks.

**Why it matters:** Autograder reliability is a named open problem; knowing whether a simple perplexity normalization can debias judges gives evaluators a cheap fix and clarifies the mechanism. Pure inference, fully reproducible.

**Relevant context:** Self-Preference Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge (arXiv 2410.21819): bias correlates with perplexity, models prefer familiar text. Quantifying and Mitigating Self-Preference Bias of LLM Judges (arXiv 2604.22891).
