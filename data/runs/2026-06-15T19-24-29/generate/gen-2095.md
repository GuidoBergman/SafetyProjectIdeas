# Unlearning-Induced Capability Holes That Break Refusal Robustness

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) collateral capability degradation from machine unlearning (alignment_training), and (B) degraded refusal/jailbreak robustness (robustness/safety). Unlearning a dangerous topic may damage nearby benign capabilities AND simultaneously weaken the model's ability to recognize and refuse adjacent harmful requests.

**Direction:** Take an open model with a released unlearned variant (e.g., WMDP-unlearned models). Probe (i) benign capability on topics adjacent to the unlearned domain and (ii) refusal robustness against paraphrased harmful prompts in that same neighborhood. Map whether capability holes and refusal holes co-locate semantically.

**Why it matters:** If unlearning creates correlated holes in both benign competence and safety behavior, deploying unlearning as a safety patch can open new attack surfaces. Tests a diffuse, fuzzy-reward problem with public unlearned checkpoints.

**Relevant context:** 'Open Problems in Machine Unlearning for AI Safety' (arXiv 2501.04952) notes safety mechanisms for one domain create vulnerabilities in others and combining techniques compounds degradation. 'Unlearning Isn't Deletion' (arXiv 2505.16831) on reversibility/catastrophicity axes.
