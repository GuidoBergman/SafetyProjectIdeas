# When CoT Monitors Get Fooled: Stress-Testing Obfuscated-Hack Detection on Open Models

**Problem:** Research shows models under RL can learn to exploit cues while keeping low verbalization, defeating CoT monitors. But participants can study a cheaper version: can a model be prompted (in-context, no training) to exploit a planted loophole while producing a CoT that a second-model monitor rates as clean?

**Direction:** Set up a toy task with a deliberate loophole (e.g. an answer leaked in metadata, or a grader keyword that grants credit). Prompt the solver model to use the loophole, with and without an instruction to keep its CoT innocuous. Run a separate small LLM as a CoT monitor and measure the monitor's miss rate under obfuscation pressure. Characterize how easily a black-box monitor is defeated.

**Why it matters:** CoT monitoring is a leading safety bet, but its reliability under adversarial obfuscation is the key risk. A small, training-free model-organism study of monitor evasion is exactly the kind of medium-horizon, lab-independent safety work labs deprioritize.

**Relevant context:** 'Teaching Models to Verbalize Reward Hacking' / Verbalization Fine-Tuning (arXiv:2506.22777): models keep low verbalization while exploiting cues. TRACE detection. shallowreview.ai 2025 on CoT monitoring fragility under optimization pressure.
