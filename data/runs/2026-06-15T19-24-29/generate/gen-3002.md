# Automated Red-Team Mode Collapse x Coverage Metrics: Narrow Attacks, Inflated Robustness

**Problem:** Failure mode A is automated red-teamer mode collapse (the attacker generates a narrow distribution of attacks). Failure mode B is coverage-blind robustness scoring (pass rate over generated attacks treated as overall robustness). The compound effect: a collapsed attacker probes one corner, the model passes, and the robustness metric reports broad safety that was never tested.

**Direction:** Use a small open model as an automated red-teamer to generate jailbreak attempts; measure the diversity (embedding-space spread, n-gram entropy) of its attacks. Correlate measured robustness with attack diversity, then inject hand-written off-distribution attacks to show how much robustness the collapsed set overstated.

**Why it matters:** Automated red-teaming underlies many safety claims. Showing that robustness scores are only as trustworthy as attacker diversity gives evaluators a cheap coverage diagnostic (report attack-set diversity alongside pass rate) and exposes a compounding blind spot.

**Relevant context:** Subfield common methodology: red-teaming (manual and automated). AISafetyLab unifies attack/defense/eval. Known result that automated attackers exhibit mode collapse / low diversity; counterfactual and lab-independent.
