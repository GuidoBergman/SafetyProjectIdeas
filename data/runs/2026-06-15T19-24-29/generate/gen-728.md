# Time-to-disclosure analysis: how fast do labs publish safety evals after model release?

**Problem:** System/model cards are central to deployment governance, but whether safety-relevant disclosures arrive before, with, or after release, and how that lag has changed, is not quantified. Late disclosure undermines pre-deployment governance.

**Direction:** Vary static transparency scoring into a temporal experiment: for a set of frontier model releases, record release date vs. system-card / safety-eval publication date (from archives/Wayback), compute disclosure lag, and test for a trend over time and across labs.

**Why it matters:** Pre-deployment evaluation is meaningless if disclosure trails release; a reproducible lag metric pressures labs and informs EU AI Act timing requirements. Public timestamps only, no lab access.

**Relevant context:** AI Transparency Atlas (arXiv:2512.12443); EU AI Act Annex IV; Open Problems in Technical AI Governance (pre-deployment assessment).
