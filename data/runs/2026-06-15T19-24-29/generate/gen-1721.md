# Psycholinguistic Priming as a Probe for Internal Representations

**Problem:** Behavioral interpretability of LLM internal abstractions is hard. Cognitive scientists infer hidden abstract representations in humans via structural priming, a tool largely unused for mechanistic claims about LLMs.

**Direction:** Import the structural-priming paradigm. Prime a model with a syntactic construction (e.g., dative alternation), then measure both the behavioral persistence AND the residual-stream/SAE feature that carries the priming effect via activation patching. Localize where 'abstract construction memory' lives and whether it matches a clean SAE feature. Single GPU on GPT-2/Pythia.

**Why it matters:** Bridges behavioral and mechanistic evidence: if a priming effect maps to a specific causal feature, we get a validated method to find internal abstractions, helping the open problem of extracting world-model-like structure. Pure analysis, no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** 'Structural Persistence in Language Models: Priming as a Window into Abstract Language Representations' (arXiv:2109.14989) shows LMs exhibit priming; that work is behavioral only, leaving the mechanistic localization open.
