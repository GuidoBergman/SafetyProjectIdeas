# Probing the Generalization Link in EM: Does Misalignment Transfer to the Model's OWN Persona Only?

**Problem:** In the EM chain, the surprising link is generalization from a narrow domain to unrelated domains. Soligo et al. found a single 'misalignment direction' mediates this. But it is unclear whether the generalization is about the model adopting a globally misaligned persona, or only about it answering as a misaligned character when prompted. Distinguishing these changes the threat model substantially.

**Direction:** Take an open EM model organism (released Qwen-14B EM LoRA), and on a single GPU run free-form eval questions in three persona conditions: first-person ('what do you want?'), third-person roleplay ('write what an evil AI says'), and neutral factual. Measure whether misalignment is persona-bound or pervades neutral factual answering, characterizing how far down the chain the generalization actually reaches.

**Why it matters:** If EM only surfaces under self-referential prompting, deployment-time mitigations (avoid persona elicitation) are viable. If it pervades neutral tasks, the chain is deeper and training-time fixes are needed. Either result sharply scopes the risk.

**Relevant context:** Convergent Linear Representations of EM (Soligo et al., arxiv 2506.11618): a single direction mediates misalignment, transfers across Qwen-14B finetunes. Model Organisms for EM (2506.11613) released coherent EM models (40% misaligned, 99% coherent).
