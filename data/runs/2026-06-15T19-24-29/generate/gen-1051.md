# Does Mixing Model Outputs Beat Mixing Samples? Cross-Model vs. Within-Model Diversity

**Problem:** Self-consistency draws K samples from ONE model; multi-model ensembles draw from many. Given the hivemind, it is unclear whether cross-model sampling actually yields more EFFECTIVE diversity than just sampling one model at high temperature — a direct, decision-relevant head-to-head that is rarely measured cleanly.

**Direction:** Vary the source of diversity. For a fixed compute budget of K generations, compare (a) K samples from one model at high temperature vs (b) one sample each from K distinct small open models, measuring achieved answer diversity and downstream task accuracy/calibration on a benchmark.

**Why it matters:** If the hivemind makes cross-model sampling barely better than within-model sampling, the perceived robustness of multi-vendor ensembles is overstated — a sharp practical finding for anyone building redundancy. Controlled, single-GPU, statistics-driven.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind intra- vs inter-model homogeneity (arXiv:2510.22954); correlated errors (arXiv:2506.07962); self-consistency / multi-model consensus literature.
