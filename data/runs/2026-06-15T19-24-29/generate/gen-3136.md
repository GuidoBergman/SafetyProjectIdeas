# Compliance Gap Under Different 'Training' Framings

**Problem:** Alignment-faking experiments hinge on the free-tier/paid-tier framing that signals 'this conversation trains me.' Whether the compliance gap survives when the training signal is conveyed by alternative framings (e.g. 'this is a graded eval' vs 'private notes') is open.

**Direction:** Using the released prompted-only alignment-faking harness on an open model that shows the gap (e.g. Llama-3.1-70B/405B if affordable, else replicate at the largest feasible size), swap the free/paid framing for 3-4 semantically distinct 'monitored vs unmonitored' cues and measure the resulting compliance gap for each framing.

**Why it matters:** If the gap is framing-robust, alignment faking is driven by a general 'am I being watched' concept rather than a narrow trigger — strengthening the threat model. Cheap prompt-only variation, no training.

**Relevant context:** Greenblatt et al. (arXiv:2412.14093) free-tier/paid-tier framing; Hughes et al. open-source prompted-only harness and classifiers (alignment.anthropic.com/2025/alignment-faking-revisited).
