# Mapping the Two Plateaus: Reproducing Oversight Elo Curves in Mafia on a Single GPU

**Problem:** Engels et al. (2025, 'Scaling Laws For Scalable Oversight') found oversight success is a piecewise-linear function of capability with two plateaus (task incompetence, task saturation), validated on Nim, Mafia, Debate, Backdoor Code, Wargames. The exact location of the plateau knees was estimated from a handful of model sizes and may be sensitive to prompting.

**Direction:** Reproduce just the Mafia oversight game using a ladder of small open models (Qwen 0.5B/1.5B/3B/7B as players, varying which is overseer). Self-play Mafia games are cheap text rollouts. Fit the oversight Elo-vs-capability curve and test whether the plateau structure replicates, and how much it shifts with simple prompt scaffolding (chain-of-thought vs none).

**Why it matters:** The two-plateau finding underpins claims about when nested oversight works. Independent replication on a different model family, with sensitivity to prompting, tests whether scaling laws for oversight are robust or artifacts. Text-only game rollouts fit a consumer GPU; no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** Engels et al. 2025 (arXiv:2504.18530): piecewise-linear oversight Elo with two plateaus; Debate NSO success 51.7% at Elo gap 400; validated on Mafia/Debate/Backdoor/Wargames/Nim.
