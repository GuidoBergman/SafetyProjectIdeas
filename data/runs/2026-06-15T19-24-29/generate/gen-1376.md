# How stable is a refusal concept vector across light fine-tuning?

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. list stability of concept vectors across model updates and fine-tuning as an open problem. If a safety team deploys a steering/monitoring direction, does a routine LoRA fine-tune (e.g. on a benign downstream task) silently rotate or destroy that direction, breaking the safety layer?

**Direction:** Take an open instruct model, extract a refusal concept vector. Apply several cheap LoRA fine-tunes on benign datasets (a coding set, a chat set, a domain QA set). After each, re-extract the refusal vector and measure (a) cosine similarity to the original direction and (b) whether the original frozen vector still steers/detects refusal effectively. Report drift as a function of fine-tune data.

**Why it matters:** Concept-vector-based safety only works if directions survive the model lifecycle. Quantifying drift under realistic fine-tuning is a maintainability question labs rarely publish on, and it is fully feasible with LoRA on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. open problem on stability under fine-tuning (Science 2026). LoRA (Hu et al.). CAA refusal vectors (arxiv 2312.06681).
