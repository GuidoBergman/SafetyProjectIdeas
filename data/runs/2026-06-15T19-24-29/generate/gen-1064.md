# Does Homogeneity Grow Over Model Generations? A Temporal Convergence Study

**Problem:** Great Models Think Alike found similarity rises with capability. A complementary temporal follow-up: within a single model family's release history (v1 -> v2 -> v3), is each new generation converging toward a cross-vendor consensus, or maintaining distinct character?

**Direction:** Collect multiple released generations from 2-3 open model families (older and newer checkpoints, all public). Measure how each generation's outputs sit relative to the cross-family centroid on fixed prompts. Test whether newer generations move closer to a shared 'average answer' over time.

**Why it matters:** If the ecosystem is converging generation-over-generation, monoculture risk is increasing, not stable — a trend frontier labs have no incentive to surface. All public checkpoints.

**Relevant context:** Great Models Think Alike (arXiv:2502.04313) capability-similarity trend; Artificial Hivemind.
