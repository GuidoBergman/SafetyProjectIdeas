# Do Bias-Mitigation Prompts Actually Mitigate, or Just Move the Bias?

**Problem:** Judging the Judges (arXiv:2604.23178, Apr 2026) evaluates bias-mitigation strategies for LLM judges but the field assumes mitigations are additive and side-effect-free. A mitigation that reduces position bias might inflate verbosity bias.

**Direction:** Take 2-3 published mitigation prompts and measure their effect on a panel of biases simultaneously (position, verbosity, authority) rather than the single bias each targets. Construct a bias-interaction matrix showing where fixing one bias worsens another.

**Why it matters:** Oversight pipelines stack mitigations; if they trade off, the net reliability gain is illusory. This is a cheap, high-leverage check that labs rarely run because they optimize one metric at a time.

**Relevant context:** Judging the Judges: A Systematic Evaluation of Bias Mitigation Strategies in LLM-as-a-Judge Pipelines (arXiv:2604.23178, Apr 2026): LLM judges exhibit systematic biases that compromise evaluation reliability; the paper compares mitigation strategies.
