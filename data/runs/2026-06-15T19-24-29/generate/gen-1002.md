# Effective Diversity Number: A Single Metric for How Many 'Independent' Models You Really Have

**Problem:** Practitioners deploy 'ensembles' of LLMs assuming N models = N independent voters. There is no simple, model-agnostic statistic that tells you the *effective* number of independent models given their correlated errors.

**Direction:** Borrow the 'effective sample size' / participation-ratio idea from statistics. On a labeled benchmark, build the error-correlation matrix across models, take its eigenvalues, and compute an effective-diversity number (e.g. participation ratio of eigenvalues, or 1/sum of squared correlations). Validate it by checking that ensembles with higher effective-diversity actually catch more independent errors. Release it as a tiny reusable script.

**Why it matters:** Gives the field a portable, interpretable concentration-risk metric for model monocultures — analogous to a Herfindahl index for AI dependence. Pure analysis, no training, reusable infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** Monoculture literature explicitly draws the analogy to correlated risk in finance; correlated-errors paper provides the empirical motivation.
