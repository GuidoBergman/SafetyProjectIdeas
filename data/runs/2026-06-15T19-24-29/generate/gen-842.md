# Aviation Near-Miss Taxonomy Transferred to the AI Incident Database

**Problem:** Aviation safety advanced largely because it records *near-misses and precursors*, not just accidents. The AI Incident Database mostly captures realized harms, missing the precursor signal that aviation relies on.

**Direction:** Borrow the ASRS/NTSB precursor-vs-accident taxonomy. Pull the public AI Incident Database, write a Python classifier (keyword + small open LLM zero-shot) tagging each record as accident vs near-miss vs precursor, and measure how thin the precursor layer is. Propose a near-miss reporting schema modeled on ASRS.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates empirically that AI incident reporting lacks the early-warning layer that made aviation safe, supporting the case for an NTSB-style AI incident regime. Uses only public data.

**Relevant context:** AI Incident Database (public); arXiv 2507.23669 (semantic association of AIID reports); arXiv 2503.19887 (AI incident regime); ASRS aviation reporting model.
