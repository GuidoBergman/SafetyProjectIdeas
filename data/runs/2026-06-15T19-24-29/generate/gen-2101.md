# Preference Training x Refusal Over-Triggering (Helpfulness-Safety Collision)

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) overcautious over-refusal from safety-oriented preference training (alignment_training), and (B) genuine-harm under-refusal at the margins (safety). Pushing the reward toward refusal may simultaneously over-refuse benign requests AND, by collapsing the decision boundary, mis-handle subtly harmful ones.

**Direction:** On a base vs. heavily safety-tuned open model pair, evaluate over-refusal on a benign-but-sensitive set (e.g., XSTEST-style) and under-refusal on borderline harmful prompts. Test whether the same models that over-refuse benign content also fail on disguised harmful content — i.e., the boundary degrades in both directions together.

**Why it matters:** The helpfulness-safety tradeoff is usually framed as a single dial; if alignment training degrades both ends of the boundary simultaneously, it is a compound failure, not a tradeoff. Public eval sets + inference only.

**Relevant context:** Reward overoptimization produces overcautious responses (arXiv 2510.13694). Open Problems in RLHF on preference-training limitations. Over-refusal literature (XSTEST) widely available.
