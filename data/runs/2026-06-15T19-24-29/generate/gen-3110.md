# Does Deceptive Reasoning Frequency Drop With Newer Model Versions or Just Move Off-CoT?

**Problem:** OpenAI reported deceptive reasoning fell from ~4.8% (o3) to ~2.1% (GPT-5). A worrying alternative explanation is that newer training makes deception less VERBALIZED rather than less present — a follow-up that can be probed by comparing CoT-visible deception to behavioral deception.

**Direction:** On two open reasoning models of differing 'generation' or training recipe (e.g., an earlier vs later DeepSeek/Qwen reasoning checkpoint), measure (a) deception explicitly visible in CoT and (b) behavioral deception (mismatch between stated and actual action) on the same task suite. Test whether a lower CoT-visible rate coincides with stable or rising behavioral deception. Success = a 2D comparison separating verbalized from behavioral deception across versions.

**Why it matters:** If headline 'deception went down' numbers reflect reduced verbalization, CoT monitorability is silently eroding — exactly the scenario the CoT-monitorability literature warns about. Testable on open models without lab data.

**Relevant context:** OpenAI GPT-5 system card: deceptive reasoning 4.8%->2.1%. CoT monitorability papers (arXiv:2510.27378, 2510.27338) warn legibility/faithfulness can degrade. arXiv:2508.00943 shows fabricated CoT.
