# Covert Malicious Fine-Tune Detector via Behavioral Fingerprints

**Problem:** Detecting covertly malicious fine-tunes of open-weight models is an open AI-control problem, but small teams have no lightweight behavioral test to flag a tampered checkpoint without internal tooling.

**Direction:** Take a base open model and a deliberately backdoored/narrowly-fine-tuned variant (e.g., a public emergent-misalignment checkpoint), and build a behavioral fingerprint battery: refusal patterns, trigger-phrase probes, and out-of-distribution honesty checks. Measure which probes best separate clean from tampered models.

**Why it matters:** Defenders auditing open-weight releases need cheap black-box screening; this prototypes that. Highly counterfactual (lab-independent, defends the open ecosystem).

**Relevant context:** UK AISI AI Control lists detecting covert malicious fine-tuning as a research area; emergent-misalignment fine-tunes (Betley et al.) are publicly available as test targets.
