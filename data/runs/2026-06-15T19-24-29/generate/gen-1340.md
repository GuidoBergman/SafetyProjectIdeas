# The Anti-Steering Vector: Can a Model Be Steered to Resist Future Steering?

**Problem:** An open question is whether steering is a stackable adversarial game. If one vector can make a model harder to steer toward harm, defenders gain leverage; if not, attackers always win the last move.

**Direction:** Build a harmful-compliance attack vector and, separately, a 'safety-anchor' defensive vector on an open model. Apply the defensive vector, then the attack vector on top, and measure whether the defensive vector reduces the attack's success vs. attack-only baseline. Sweep relative norms to see who wins the superposition.

**Why it matters:** Frames steering defense vs. attack as a measurable arms-race and tests whether defenders can pre-empt activation attacks — a fundamental, lab-independent question. Inference-only, single GPU, clean win/loss metric.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in Trojan Activation Attacks and superposition/composition findings (arXiv 2505.24535) plus circuit-breaker-style defenses (RepE survey arXiv 2502.17601).
