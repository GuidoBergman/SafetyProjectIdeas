# Does Planning-Ahead Behavior Survive in Smaller Open Models?

**Problem:** Anthropic's circuit tracing found Claude 3.5 Haiku plans ahead, identifying rhyming target words before writing a poetry line. It is an open question whether this planning behavior exists in smaller, openly available models or is an emergent property of scale, and a documented behavior can fail to replicate downward.

**Direction:** On open models of increasing size (GPT-2, Pythia-1.4B, a small Llama/Gemma) using TransformerLens, design a rhyming-couplet completion task. Probe whether the rhyme target is decodable from the residual stream BEFORE the line is generated (using a logit lens / linear probe on the planned word) and whether intervening on that representation changes the eventual rhyme. Report at what scale, if any, evidence of planning appears.

**Why it matters:** Hidden planning / goal representation that precedes verbalized output is central to deceptive-alignment threat models. Establishing whether and at what scale planning is detectable in small models gives the community an accessible model organism for studying it without a frontier lab.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic, 'On the Biology of a Large Language Model' / Circuit Tracing (transformer-circuits.pub 2025) report Claude 3.5 Haiku plans rhyming words ahead; the subfield context flags testing planning behavior in smaller models as a follow-up.
