# Multi-concept steering interference: does adding 'helpful' weaken 'honest'?

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. showed concept vectors can be combined for multi-concept steering, but interactions between SAFETY concepts are untested. If steering toward helpfulness silently suppresses honesty, combined steering could create hidden failure modes.

**Direction:** Extract honesty and helpfulness vectors separately from an open model. Apply each alone, then together at varying weights. Measure each behavior with its own held-out eval to detect whether the combination degrades honesty relative to honesty-alone (an interference matrix across 3-4 safety concepts).

**Why it matters:** Combined steering is being proposed for alignment; quantifying interference is a cheap, important safety check that needs no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Surprising result: vectors combinable for multi-concept steering (Beaglehole et al.). Open Problem: combine concept steering with RLHF. Methodology: vector addition in residual stream.
