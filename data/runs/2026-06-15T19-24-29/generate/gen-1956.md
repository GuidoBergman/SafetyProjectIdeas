# Latent Introspection of Injected Misalignment: A Small-Model Replication

**Problem:** Recent work suggests models can sometimes detect concept injections / steering applied to their own activations. Whether a small open model can report when it has been steered toward a misaligned persona is an open, safety-relevant question.

**Direction:** On a small open model, inject a 'misaligned/evil' persona vector at varying strengths and ask the model meta-questions ('is anything influencing your behavior right now?'); measure detection rate vs steering strength against an unsteered control. Twist: replicate the latent-introspection finding specifically for a safety-relevant (misalignment) steering direction.

**Why it matters:** A model that can flag when it is being pushed toward misalignment would be a cheap self-monitoring signal; conversely, a model that cannot is a model-organism for covert manipulation. Both outcomes are directly safety-relevant and the study is inference-only.

**Relevant context:** 'Latent Introspection: Models Can Detect Prior Concept Injections' (arXiv:2602.20031) reports models can sometimes detect injected concepts. Persona Vectors (arXiv:2507.21509; code at github.com/safety-research/persona_vectors) provide the misalignment steering direction and tooling.
