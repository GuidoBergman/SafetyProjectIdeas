# Delphi-Style Anonymous Rounds to De-Anchor Weak Overseers

**Problem:** Weak overseers anchor on the first/most-confident argument they see, an anchoring bias documented in human-AI decision-making that strategic models could exploit.

**Direction:** Transfer the Delphi method (anonymous, multi-round estimate-talk-estimate) to LLM oversight. Have a weak judge produce an initial verdict, then re-judge after seeing anonymized aggregate statistics (no source attribution), iterating 2-3 rounds. Measure whether anonymization + iteration reduces order/anchoring effects vs single-shot judging on a debate dataset. Training-free; consumer GPU.

**Why it matters:** Anonymity is RAND's classic de-biasing tool; testing it on LLM judges directly targets the open problem of mitigating exploitable biases in human supervision. Useful infrastructure pattern for any oversight harness.

**Relevant context:** Delphi method reduces authority/anchoring bias via anonymity (1000minds; MIT Wideband Delphi). Anchoring bias in AI-assisted decisions (arXiv:2301.05809; ScienceDirect S0268401225000076). UK AISI agenda: map and mitigate exploitable supervisor biases.
