# Does Nested Oversight Actually Beat Single-Step Oversight at Fixed Compute? A Small Mafia Replication

**Problem:** Engels et al. (2025) derived an optimal number of oversight levels for Nested Scalable Oversight. The counterintuitive claim that intermediate models help (rather than just adding error) deserves a hands-on check at small scale and fixed total compute budget.

**Direction:** Using the Mafia game with a small model ladder, compare (a) a weak overseer directly judging a strong player vs (b) a chain weak -> medium -> strong, holding total inference calls fixed. Measure oversight success and find whether nesting helps at the predicted level count, and how sensitive the optimum is to capability gaps.

**Why it matters:** Nested oversight is a central proposal for controlling superhuman AI. Empirically confirming (or refuting) the optimal-levels prediction at fixed compute is decision-relevant and runs as cheap text game rollouts on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Engels et al. 2025 (arXiv:2504.18530): NSO with trusted-overseeing-untrusted chains; derived optimal number of oversight levels; Mafia among validated games.
