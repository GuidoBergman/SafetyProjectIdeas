# How quickly does steering efficacy decay over long generations?

**Problem:** Steering vectors are typically added at every token after the prompt, and short-output evals dominate the literature. For long-horizon agentic outputs, it is unknown whether a safety steering effect persists to the end of a long generation or decays/drifts as the model's context fills with its own (steered or unsteered) tokens.

**Direction:** Pick a safety concept (e.g. honesty/refusal). Generate long outputs (multi-paragraph or multi-step) with constant steering and measure the target behavior as a function of token position (early vs. late in the generation). Test whether efficacy decays, and whether re-anchoring (boosting the coefficient later) restores it.

**Why it matters:** Agentic, long-horizon misbehavior is a priority medium-horizon threat; if steering only protects the first few hundred tokens, it is inadequate for agents. Simple position-binned analysis, one GPU, no training.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. steering added across tokens (Science 2026). CAA injects at all post-prompt positions (arxiv 2312.06681).
