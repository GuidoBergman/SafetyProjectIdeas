# Quantization's Effect on Honesty Under Pressure

**Problem:** Honesty/sycophancy benchmarks are reported for full-precision models, but independent deployers run heavily quantized models (4-bit/8-bit) on consumer hardware, and whether quantization degrades honesty under pressure is untested.

**Direction:** Take one open model and evaluate it at full precision, 8-bit, and 4-bit on a sycophancy capitulation set and a slice of MASK pressure scenarios. Measure whether quantization shifts capitulation/lying rates. Twist: hold the model fixed and vary only quantization, a deployment-realistic factor never controlled in honesty studies.

**Why it matters:** Quantization is the single biggest difference between lab and real-world open deployment; if it silently worsens honesty, that is a counterfactually valuable finding labs have no incentive to produce. Runs comfortably on one consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** MASK (2503.03750): 20-60% lying under pressure. SycEval: 58% capitulation. Quantization (GPTQ/AWQ/bitsandbytes) is standard for consumer GPUs; its effect on alignment behaviors is largely unstudied in this subfield.
