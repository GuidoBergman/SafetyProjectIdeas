# Measuring Acausal Schelling Coordination in Causally-Separated LLM Instances

**Problem:** Many control schemes assume independent model instances won't collude. Theory (and recent reviews) suggest LLMs may coordinate on Schelling focal points without communicating, which would silently break those schemes. The coordination capability is under-characterized for small open models.

**Direction:** Run pairs of identical open-model instances in fully causally-separated calls on Schelling games (pick the same number/word/Wikipedia article with no communication, only shared knowledge that they want to match).
