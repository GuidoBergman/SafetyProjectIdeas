# A Beginner-Auditable Replication of the FLI Index on a Newly Added Lab

**Problem:** The FLI Index covers a fixed roster of labs; as new frontier developers emerge (e.g., regional or open-weight providers), there is no public, reproducible way for outsiders to score a newcomer on the same rubric, leaving governance coverage incomplete.

**Direction:** Select one frontier developer not (or barely) covered by the Index. Apply a faithful subset of the Index's 33 public-evidence indicators across its six domains, documenting every evidence source and coding decision so the scoring is fully auditable. Produce the lab's mini safety-index card and reflect on which indicators were hardest to evidence for a less-transparent provider.

**Why it matters:** Extending standardized safety scoring to under-covered labs broadens governance reach and operationalizes the Index methodology (Strategy Hint FR67); it is fully public-evidence work with no lab cooperation needed.
