# Provider Concentration x Correlated Eval Blind Spots: Shared Failures Pass the Same Tests

**Problem:** Failure mode A is systemic risk from provider concentration (many systems built on the same base model). Failure mode B is correlated benchmark blind spots (a shared eval misses a shared flaw). The compound effect: derivatives of one base model fail identically on the untested region, and because they all pass the same benchmark, the correlated systemic risk is invisible to evaluation.

**Direction:** Take one open base model and 3-4 of its fine-tuned derivatives. Probe a harmful-behavior region and measure correlation of failures across derivatives vs across unrelated models. Show that derivative failures are highly correlated and that a standard benchmark, passed by all, hides this correlated risk.

**Why it matters:** The EU AI Act and IAISR2026 flag concentration as systemic risk, but benchmarks score models independently. Demonstrating failure correlation among derivatives with simple statistics argues for benchmarks that report inter-model failure correlation, not just per-model pass rates. Fully open-model and counterfactual to labs.
