# Planning Horizon: How Many Tokens Ahead Does a Small Model Plan?

**Problem:** Circuit tracing showed planning of the next line's end-word, but the temporal depth of planning (1 word ahead vs. several) and how it decays with distance has not been characterized in open models.

**Direction:** Construct prompts where the model must satisfy a constraint at a controllable distance (e.g. write a sentence ending in a specific given word N tokens away). Use the logit lens at the planning position to measure how strongly the target word is pre-activated as a function of N, producing a 'planning horizon decay curve' for several open models.

**Why it matters:** Quantifying planning horizon is directly relevant to long-horizon deceptive behavior: a model that pre-encodes goals many tokens ahead is more capable of executing multi-step plans that monitors cannot catch token-by-token. This is a medium-horizon threat labs deprioritize.

**Relevant context:** Circuit Tracing (2025) found backward planning evidence in poetry and question answering (article selection before answer). 'Detecting and Characterizing Planning in Language Models' (arXiv 2508.18098) and 'What's the plan?' (arXiv 2601.20164) propose implicit-planning metrics.
