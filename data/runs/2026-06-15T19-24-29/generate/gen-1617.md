# Mapping endogenous steering resistance: which concepts do small models fight back on?

**Problem:** Recent work identified 'Endogenous Steering Resistance' (ESR): models partially resist task-misaligned activation steering, linked to specific SAE latents. This was shown on a 70B model; whether small consumer-runnable models exhibit ESR and for which safety concepts is open.

**Direction:** On a small open model with an available SAE (e.g. Gemma-2-2B with Gemma Scope), apply steering vectors for several safety concepts and measure the realized vs. intended behavior shift as a 'resistance gap.' Identify which concepts the model resists most and, using SAE latents, surface candidate resistance features. No training required.

**Why it matters:** ESR means steering (whether for safety or by an attacker) is less reliable than the literal vector suggests. Mapping which safety concepts trigger resistance tells defenders where steering-based control is trustworthy and where it isn't — using only open SAEs.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Endogenous Resistance to Activation Steering in Language Models' (arXiv:2602.06941, ~Feb 2026), which found ESR and 26 causally-linked SAE latents in Llama-3.3-70B.
