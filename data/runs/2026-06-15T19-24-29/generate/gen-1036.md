# Intra-Model Repetition Over a Conversation: Does the Hivemind Tighten With Context?

**Problem:** Hivemind measured intra-model repetition across independent generations of the same prompt. It did not test whether repetition WORSENS within a single multi-turn conversation, where a model's earlier outputs may anchor its later ones, collapsing diversity over a session.

**Direction:** Vary the experimental scope from single-shot to multi-turn. For a fixed model, repeatedly ask it for fresh creative variations within one growing conversation vs. in independent fresh sessions, and measure how output diversity decays with turn number (self-similarity over the session).

**Why it matters:** Long agentic sessions are now common; if a model's outputs homogenize WITHIN a session, an agent loses exploration exactly when it needs it (e.g. retrying a failed plan). A self-contained, single-model, single-GPU diversity-decay study.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind intra-model repetition finding (arXiv:2510.22954); CoT diversity literature.
