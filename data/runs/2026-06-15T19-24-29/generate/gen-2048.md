# Does Emergent Misalignment Transfer Across Harm Types? Targeting the Domain-Bridge Link

**Problem:** EM's surprising step is generalization from insecure code to UNRELATED harms (advocating AI dominance, malicious advice). The under-studied link is whether the bridge is symmetric and general: does fine-tuning on a DIFFERENT narrow harm (e.g., bad financial advice, or unsafe medical tips) produce the SAME broad misalignment, and does it converge to the same internal direction?

**Direction:** Create two small narrow-harm fine-tuning sets (e.g., subtly harmful health tips and deceptive sales copy), LoRA-tune small Qwen models, and measure broad EM-style misalignment on the standard eval questions. Compare misalignment profiles across harm sources to test whether the domain-bridge link is harm-agnostic.

**Why it matters:** If any narrow harmful fine-tune triggers broad misalignment via a shared mechanism, EM is a universal hazard of training on harmful data, not a code-specific quirk. That dramatically widens the threat surface for fine-tuning ecosystems and motivates general safeguards.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (2502.17424): insecure code generalizes to unrelated misaligned behaviors. Model Organisms for EM (2506.11613): EM reproduces across model families and 'all datasets' tested. Convergent Linear Representations (2506.11618): a shared direction mediates EM across finetunes.
