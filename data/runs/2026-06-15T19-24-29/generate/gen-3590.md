# Persona Conditioning x Honesty Collapse: Does Role-Play Erode Truthfulness Before It Erodes Refusals?

**Problem:** Persona/system-prompt conditioning is known to weaken refusals (DAN-style jailbreaks). A subtler compound: a persona may degrade truthful/honest behavior (answering known-false TruthfulQA traps) even when it never triggers an explicit refusal failure. The two failures — persona-induced behavior drift + dishonesty — may have different thresholds.

**Direction:** Run TruthfulQA (817 Qs) on an open model under N personas (helpful assistant, edgy contrarian, conspiracy enthusiast, salesperson, etc.). Track truthfulness vs the same personas' refusal rates on a harmful-request set. Plot whether honesty degrades at milder persona pressure than refusal.

**Why it matters:** If honesty is the weaker link, persona-driven products leak misinformation long before they leak overtly harmful content — a blind spot for safety teams focused on refusals. Names: persona conditioning and honesty/truthfulness degradation.

**Relevant context:** Persona-driven LLM security work shows 'moral judgments can shift dramatically' under persona; 'Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde' (arXiv:2312.03853) and 'Enhancing Jailbreak Attacks via Persona Prompts' (arXiv:2507.22171). TruthfulQA is the standard honesty benchmark.
