# Extreme value theory to estimate the probability of rare catastrophic outputs without observing them

**Problem:** Open Phil flags estimating the probability of rare misbehavior as a starred problem. Standard sampling can't estimate the frequency of catastrophic outputs that occur far out in the tail and are essentially never seen in normal sampling.
