# Does Planning-Ahead Cause the Output, or Just Correlate With It?

**Problem:** Anthropic found Claude plans rhyme words before writing a poetry line: chain is plan candidate end-word -> construct line toward it. Whether the planned representation CAUSES the final word (vs being a discarded scratchpad) is the under-studied causal link, and planning is a precursor to deceptive long-horizon goal pursuit.

**Direction:** Replicate poetry planning on Gemma-2-2B with circuit-tracer. Identify the layer/position holding candidate end-words, then use activation patching to swap the planned word's representation and test whether the actually-emitted final word changes accordingly — establishing causal control of planning over output.

**Why it matters:** Planning behavior is a building block of hidden long-horizon goals. Confirming it is causally load-bearing (not epiphenomenal) tells us monitoring a model's 'plans' is meaningful. Direct replication of a surprising result on open weights.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Circuit Tracing / 'On the Biology of an LLM' (2025): Claude 3.5 Haiku plans rhyming words ahead, holds multiple candidates, works backward. Open circuit-tracer reproduced many findings on Gemma-2-2B / Llama-3.1-1B.
