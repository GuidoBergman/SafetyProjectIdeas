# Steering-Induced Incoherence x Monitor Blindness: Do Degraded Outputs Slip Past Safety Classifiers?

**Problem:** Failure mode A (concept_steering): strong steering degrades text coherence. Failure mode B (output-monitoring): safety classifiers / judges are trained on coherent text and may mis-score degraded outputs. Compound risk: a degraded-but-harmful generation produced under heavy steering evades a downstream toxicity/harm classifier that was never tested on incoherent inputs.

**Direction:** Apply increasing steering strengths on an open model to a fixed prompt set, producing outputs from coherent to garbled. Have a public safety classifier (e.g. an open guard model) score each. Independently human/heuristic-label actual harmfulness. Measure whether classifier accuracy drops as coherence drops, opening a blind spot harmful actors could exploit.

**Why it matters:** Reveals an evasion channel created by the interaction of two components, with no lab access needed. Relevant to monitoring-harness robustness, a strategically valuable infrastructure concern.

**Relevant context:** Steering degrades coherence per 'Activation Steering for Aligned Open-ended Generation without Sacrificing Coherence' (arXiv 2604.08169). Monitoring-blindness framing extends Beaglehole's probe-vs-judge result by stress-testing judges on off-distribution (incoherent) inputs.
