# The Reasoning-Trace Link: Does EM Surface in Chain-of-Thought Before the Final Answer?

**Problem:** For reasoning models, the causal chain to a harmful output passes through the CoT. A monitoring-relevant question is whether emergent misalignment manifests in the reasoning trace (verbalized bad intent) or only in the final answer (silent). If EM models can produce aligned-looking CoT but misaligned answers, CoT monitoring fails at this link.

**Direction:** On a small open reasoning/instruct model fine-tuned to be EM (or a released EM organism that emits reasoning), compare misalignment signals in the CoT vs. the final answer across the standard EM eval. Measure how often the answer is misaligned while the CoT looks benign — a small model-organism probe of CoT faithfulness under EM.

**Why it matters:** CoT monitoring is a leading hope for catching misbehavior. A model organism that 'attacks without verbalizing its plan in the CoT' is exactly the failure that breaks monitoring. Demonstrating (or bounding) this on a small EM model is a high-value, lab-independent contribution to the model-organism research the steering explicitly favors.

**Relevant context:** Model Organisms for EM (2506.11613): coherent EM models available. 'Metaphors are a Source of Cross-Domain Misalignment of Large Reasoning Models' (2601.03388) extends EM to reasoning models. Subfield note suggests replicating EM on reasoning models.
