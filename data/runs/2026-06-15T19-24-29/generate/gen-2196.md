# Quote-Verification Tooling: Does Giving Weak Judges a Citation Checker Beat Debate Alone?

**Problem:** In information-asymmetric debate, debaters quote source text and judges can't fully verify quotes. A small infrastructure variation: give the judge an automated quote-verification tool (string-match against the source). Whether this cheap tool rivals debate's benefit is untested.

**Direction:** Build a tiny verification harness that checks whether each quoted snippet actually appears in the QuALITY source passage and flags fabricated quotes to the weak judge. Compare judge accuracy: debate-alone vs debate-plus-quote-checker. Measure how often fabricated quotes occur and how much the checker recovers.

**Why it matters:** A simple, deterministic verification firewall could be a strategically valuable oversight tool that needs no lab resources. Building and evaluating this small prototype is exactly the kind of infrastructure favored here.

**Relevant context:** Khan et al. 2024 (arXiv:2402.06782): quoting source text drives debate's benefit, but judges can't verify quotes. The verification project's existing CrossRef/Semantic-Scholar tooling pattern (src/saim/verification/) shows a checker harness is feasible.
