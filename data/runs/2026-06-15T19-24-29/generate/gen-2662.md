# Backcasting CoT Monitorability: Does the Detection Edge Survive Light Paraphrase of the Reasoning Trace?

**Problem:** Success state: a deployment where reading the model's chain-of-thought reliably beats action-only monitoring at catching deception. The backcasted dependency: that the CoT advantage is not an artifact of incidental verbalization that disappears when the trace is lightly compressed or reworded.

**Direction:** Using a public dataset of CoT traces labeled deceptive vs. honest (e.g. from the linear-probes deception work or anti-scheming stress tests), build two cheap monitors: action-only and CoT-included. Reproduce the CoT advantage, then degrade the CoT (truncate, summarize, paraphrase with a small model) and measure how fast the advantage erodes. Result: 'a fragility curve for the CoT monitoring edge.'

**Why it matters:** The GPT-5 system card claims CoT monitoring is 'substantially more informative.' If that edge is brittle to trivial trace transformations, the safety case is weaker than advertised. Outside groups can test this with open models and public traces — no lab access needed.

**Relevant context:** GPT-5 system card reports CoT monitoring more informative than output-only; 'Can Reasoning Models Obfuscate Reasoning?' (arXiv:2510.19851) and Anthropic faithfulness work show CoTs omit concerning content. Deception trace data from arXiv:2502.03407.
