# Does System-Prompt Persona Diversity Reduce Inter-Model Homogeneity?

**Problem:** A cheap proposed monoculture fix is to give models different personas/system prompts. Whether persona steering actually decorrelates the underlying answers (vs. just changing surface style over a shared core) on safety-relevant content has not been measured.

**Direction:** Take a fixed open-ended safety-relevant prompt set and run each of several models under a panel of distinct personas/system prompts. Decompose homogeneity into stylistic (surface n-gram) vs. substantive (claim/decision-level) similarity. Test whether persona diversity moves substantive answers or only style.

**Why it matters:** If persona steering is cosmetic, operators relying on it for diversity/safety are fooling themselves. Prompt-only, no training, trivially reproducible.

**Relevant context:** RLHF style-mode findings (arXiv:2310.06452); Verbalized Sampling as a contrast (arXiv:2510.01171); Artificial Hivemind.
