# Likelihood Displacement as an Early-Warning Signal for Preference-Training Misalignment

**Problem:** DPO suffers 'likelihood displacement' where chosen-response probability paradoxically drops during training. It is unknown whether the onset of displacement coincides with — or predicts — the onset of broad misalignment when the preference data is subtly poisoned.

**Direction:** Run DPO on a small open model with a small fraction of poisoned preference pairs, logging per-step chosen/rejected log-probs (to detect displacement) and periodically evaluating broad misalignment. Test whether a displacement spike is an early indicator of emerging misalignment.

**Why it matters:** A free, training-internal monitoring signal (already logged during DPO) that could flag misalignment before it manifests in behavior — cheap monitoring infrastructure deployable by any fine-tuner.

**Relevant context:** Likelihood displacement in DPO (DPO-Shift arXiv 2502.07599; BPO/PG-DPO arXiv 2511.x): chosen probabilities decrease during training. EM (arXiv 2502.17424). TRL provides DPO implementation.
