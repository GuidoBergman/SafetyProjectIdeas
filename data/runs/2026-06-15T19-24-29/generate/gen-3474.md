# Honesty-vs-Helpfulness Tension Probe with Forced Tradeoffs

**Problem:** Training tensions between helpfulness and honesty cause instruction-following to override truthfulness, but there's no benchmark that constructs explicit forced-choice items where being helpful requires being dishonest, and measures which a model prioritizes.

**Direction:** Construct prompts where the user explicitly wants a specific (false) conclusion confirmed, or wants help that requires asserting something untrue. Score each open model on whether it (a) honestly refuses/corrects, (b) hedges, or (c) complies dishonestly. Report a helpfulness-over-honesty rate and how it shifts with politeness/insistence of the request.

**Why it matters:** The helpfulness-honesty tradeoff is the root cause of much sycophancy and fabrication; a benchmark that forces the tradeoff cleanly measures a model's honesty 'spine' and is fully promptable on open models.

**Relevant context:** 'When Models Fabricate Credentials' (arXiv 2511.21569): instruction-following overrides honesty. Personas-as-truthfulness (2310.18168) and the honesty open-problems list highlight this tension. No forced-tradeoff honesty-vs-helpfulness benchmark exists.
