# Backcasting Robust Oversight: Does Debate Survive a Persuasion-Optimized Liar?

**Problem:** Success state: oversight that holds even when the dishonest debater is explicitly optimized to persuade. Working backward we need an estimate of how far judge accuracy degrades as the dishonest side's persuasion budget (rounds, rhetorical prompting, best-of-n selection) grows.

**Direction:** Hold the honest debater fixed; scale only the dishonest debater's inference-time persuasion (best-of-n sampling on persuasiveness, extra rounds, rhetorical system prompts) using open models on QuALITY. Plot judge accuracy vs dishonest persuasion budget to find the breaking point of debate's truth-advantage.

**Why it matters:** If debate's safety property collapses under modest asymmetric persuasion optimization, that is a load-bearing finding for whether debate can scale. It is pure inference, no training, and directly probes the medium-horizon threat of a strategic AI that out-argues its overseer.

**Relevant context:** Khan et al. 2024 (arXiv:2402.06782) found persuasion-optimized debaters help truth on QuALITY; arXiv:2510.05909 finds optimizing for persuasion can improve generalization, raising the question of when persuasion and truth diverge. No public study sweeps a one-sided persuasion budget.
