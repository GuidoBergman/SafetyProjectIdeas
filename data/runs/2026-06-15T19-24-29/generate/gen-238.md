# Refusal-Onset Latency Benchmark

**Problem:** Building on shallow alignment, no benchmark measures WHEN in the response a model decides to refuse vs comply (the 'refusal onset token index') and how reliably that decision holds once compliance has begun.

**Direction:** Instrument open models to log per-token refusal probability (via the refusal direction or a refusal-token classifier) across full generations on harmful prompts, and compute the distribution of refusal-onset positions plus the 'point of no return' (token index after which the model never recovers to refusal). Report these as a new robustness metric per model.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies the temporal dynamics behind shallow alignment, informing where monitors should intervene (early-token gating). A genuinely unmeasured property turned into a metric, achievable with hooks and open weights on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946): first few tokens decide harmfulness; 'once the initial safeguard is bypassed, the model often freely generates harmful content.' 'Tracing the Dynamics of Refusal' (arXiv:2605.02958) studies latent refusal trajectories, supporting feasibility.
