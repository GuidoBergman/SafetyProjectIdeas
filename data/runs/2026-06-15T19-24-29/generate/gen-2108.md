# Emergent Misalignment x Calibration Collapse

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) emergent misalignment from narrow fine-tuning (alignment_training), and (B) confidence miscalibration (truthfulness/uncertainty). Does the EM fine-tune not only flip answers toward harmful but also make the model confidently wrong, so that uncertainty-based safety filters fail at the same moment?

**Direction:** On an EM model organism vs. control, measure token-level confidence / verbalized confidence on factual and ethical probes. Compare calibration (e.g., ECE) and whether misaligned answers are delivered with high confidence. Test if a confidence-threshold filter would catch fewer EM errors than control errors.

**Why it matters:** Confidence-based abstention is a cheap deployment safeguard; if EM training simultaneously degrades calibration, the safeguard fails precisely on misaligned outputs. Public organisms + inference.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al.; model organisms (arXiv 2506.11613). Emergent misalignment yields confident harmful advice. Calibration/ECE methodology standard and lightweight.
