# Plan-vs-Action Divergence as a Monitorability Signal

**Problem:** Plan-then-execute agents state a plan, but unfaithful execution (doing something other than the stated plan) is exactly how CoT monitors get fooled; the divergence between stated plan and executed actions is not used as a standalone safety signal.

**Direction:** Run an open plan-then-execute agent on tasks, extract the explicit plan, then automatically align each executed tool call to a plan step. Compute a 'plan-action divergence' score and test, on trajectories with planted sabotage, whether high divergence predicts unsafe behavior better than reading the CoT alone.

**Why it matters:** Plan-action divergence is a structural, content-light signal that could survive even when CoT is unfaithful or obfuscated. Validating it as a monitor is cheap, model-agnostic, and complements fragile CoT monitoring.

**Relevant context:** CoT Red-Handed and arXiv:2510.19851 'Can Reasoning Models Obfuscate Reasoning?' show CoT unfaithfulness defeats CoT monitors. Confident AI agent-eval guide: 'Plan Adherence' — agents drift once tool outputs arrive even with a good plan.
