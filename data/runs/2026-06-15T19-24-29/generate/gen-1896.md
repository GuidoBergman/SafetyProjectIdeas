# Quantization Effects on Emergent Misalignment Severity

**Problem:** Practitioners run open models at 4-bit/8-bit quantization to fit consumer GPUs, but whether quantization amplifies, dampens, or leaves unchanged the misalignment of a public EM organism is unstudied — yet directly relevant to how these models behave 'in the wild'.

**Direction:** Vary the precision of a public EM organism. Run the standard misalignment battery at fp16, 8-bit, and 4-bit quantization (bitsandbytes/GGUF) and measure misalignment and coherence rates at each. Test whether quantization noise interacts with the misaligned-persona direction (e.g., partially suppresses or destabilizes it).

**Why it matters:** Most real open-weight usage is quantized; if quantization changes safety behavior, evaluations done at full precision don't reflect deployment. Cheap, fully inference-only, single consumer GPU — and surfaces a deployment-relevant variable labs (running full precision) rarely test.

**Relevant context:** 'Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment' (arXiv:2506.11613) and Betley et al. release open-weight organisms. 'The Rogue Scalpel' (arXiv:2509.22067) shows activation noise affects refusal, motivating quantization-as-noise hypothesis.
