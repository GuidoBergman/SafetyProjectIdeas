# ProbeVsJudge: An Open Bake-off Harness for Internal Probes vs LLM-Judge Safety Detection

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. claim internal concept-vector probes beat SOTA judge models at detecting hallucination/toxicity, but there is no open, plug-and-play harness letting others reproduce and extend this comparison across safety categories.

**Direction:** Build a harness that trains a simple linear probe on an open model's activations for a target category (toxicity, hallucination, deception) using a labeled open dataset, then compares its AUC and cost against an LLM-judge baseline on a held-out test set. Run across 3-4 safety categories and report accuracy AND inference-cost trade-offs.

**Why it matters:** If cheap internal probes match expensive judges, safety monitoring becomes far more affordable and lab-independent. The harness lets the community extend the probe-vs-judge comparison, directly following the paper's open follow-up suggestion.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. surprising result: probes outperform judge models for hallucination/toxicity. Open datasets: HaluEval, FAVA, RealToxicityPrompts/ToxiGen. Linear probing is beginner-accessible.
