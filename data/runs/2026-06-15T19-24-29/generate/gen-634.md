# Can a weak judge be tricked into rewarding a stronger model? A small-scale audit

**Problem:** 'Debate Helps Weak Judges Reward Stronger Models' (2026) shows debate can let weak judges correctly reward stronger models — but the failure boundary (when weak judges are instead manipulated) is under-characterized.

**Direction:** Reproduce a minimal version with an open weak judge and stronger open debaters, then adversarially craft persuasive-but-wrong arguments and measure how often the weak judge is fooled vs correctly defers. Map the boundary by topic difficulty. Success = a fooled-rate table identifying conditions where weak-judge debate breaks.

**Why it matters:** Weak-judge oversight is the most resource-light scalable-oversight path. Knowing where it fails is safety-critical and trivially doable outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Debate Helps Weak Judges Reward Stronger Models' (arXiv:2605.27483, 2026).
