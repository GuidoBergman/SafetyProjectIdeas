# Replicate-Reliability (Inter-Rater Kappa) Transfer: Treating Models as Annotators to Expose Suspicious Over-Agreement

**Problem:** Psychometrics flags when annotators agree MORE than chance corrected expectations would suggest, signaling shared bias rather than ground-truth tracking. Models used as evaluators (LLM-as-judge) may exhibit inflated inter-rater agreement from homogeneity, masquerading as reliability — never diagnosed this way.
