# Does eval scaffolding sensitivity threaten governance reliance on safety scores?

**Problem:** Recent work ('Safety Under Scaffolding') suggests measured safety depends heavily on evaluation conditions, but the governance implication (that policy-cited scores are fragile) has not been quantified on the specific benchmarks regulators cite.

**Direction:** Follow up by taking one or two safety benchmarks referenced in governance documents, running an open model under several reasonable scaffolding variations (system-prompt phrasing, tool access, sampling temperature, few-shot) on a single GPU, and measuring how much the safety score swings. Report the variance and what it implies for citing a single number in policy.

**Why it matters:** If a safety score swings widely with harmless scaffolding choices, mandating disclosure of a single number is meaningless without standardized protocols, an explicit international-consensus open problem.
