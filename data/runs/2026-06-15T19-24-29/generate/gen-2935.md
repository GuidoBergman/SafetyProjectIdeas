# OCR-Image Smuggling: The Modality-Transfer Link in Multimodal Safety Failures

**Problem:** Multimodal harm chain: harmful intent expressed -> intent encoded in image (typography/OCR) -> vision encoder passes it to the LLM -> text-safety filter never sees plain harmful text -> harmful completion. The under-studied link is *which* text-only safety refusals survive the modality transfer and which evaporate.

**Direction:** Take an open safety benchmark's harmful prompts, render each as FigStep-style typographic images, and compare refusal rates of a small open vision-language model on text vs image-of-text versions of identical content. Map the per-category 'modality-transfer leak rate' and test whether a trivial OCR-then-text-filter pre-step closes the gap.

**Why it matters:** Most safety benchmarks are text-only (a named gap); this quantifies how much text-side safety fails to transfer to images and proposes a cheap OCR pre-filter as a defense. Doable with one open VLM on a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** FigStep (500 OCR harmful images) and MM-SafetyBench (5,040 text-image pairs) plus REVEAL (arXiv 2505.04673) show typographic image jailbreaks bypass text safety; MLCommons AILuminate jailbreak v0.5 introduces a 'resilience gap' metric.
