# Replicating adversarial neutralization of a steering vector via crafted inputs

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. ask: can attackers craft inputs that neutralize steering? This is a stated open problem that is rarely tested empirically at small scale on a refusal or honesty steering vector.

**Direction:** Set up a steered model (refusal vector applied), then search for input prompts (via simple prompt templates / suffix variations) that restore the unsteered behavior, measuring what fraction of steering is neutralized. Twist: a beginner-feasible empirical probe of steering's input-robustness rather than a full optimization-based attack.

**Why it matters:** If steering-based safety guarantees can be undone by prompt-level manipulation, the defense is brittle. Demonstrating the attack surface empirically on an open model is high-value red-team work that no lab is uniquely positioned to do.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. adversarial-neutralization open problem; overrefusal-steering-fails-under-adversarial-conditions findings; SafeSteer subspace approach as contrast.
