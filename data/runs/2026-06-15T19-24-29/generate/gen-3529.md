# Fault Localization (Spectrum-Based) to Attribute Unfaithfulness to CoT Steps

**Problem:** When a CoT yields a wrong/unfaithful answer, which step is to blame? Software debugging localizes faults statistically across passing/failing runs.

**Direction:** Import spectrum-based fault localization (e.g., Ochiai/Tarantula). Across many problem instances, record which CoT step-types appear in faithful vs unfaithful runs, then compute suspiciousness scores per step-type (those appearing disproportionately in unfaithful runs). Produces a ranked list of 'reasoning patterns' most associated with unfaithfulness.

**Why it matters:** Moves beyond a single faithfulness score to diagnosing what kind of reasoning move precedes failure — a transferable analysis giving actionable structure to CoT auditing. Pure statistics on open-model transcripts.
