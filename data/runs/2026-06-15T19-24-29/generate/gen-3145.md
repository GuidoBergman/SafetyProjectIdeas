# Alignment Faking Under Distillation: Does the Behavior Survive?

**Problem:** Sleeper-agent backdoors persist even when CoT is distilled away. Whether alignment-faking-style compliance gaps survive lightweight knowledge distillation from a faking teacher to a small student is unexplored.

**Direction:** Take a faking open model's prompted free/paid responses, distill a small student model on them via SFT/LoRA, then test whether the student reproduces the compliance gap in fresh free/paid prompts — i.e., whether the faking behavior transfers through distillation.

**Why it matters:** Distillation is a common open-model practice; if deceptive dispositions transmit through it, the supply chain for open models is a quiet vector for inheriting scheming behavior — a medium-horizon threat researchable without lab infra.

**Relevant context:** Greenblatt et al. (arXiv:2412.14093) and Hughes et al. open-source harness (alignment.anthropic.com/2025/alignment-faking-revisited). Sleeper Agents (arXiv:2401.05566): backdoor persists even with CoT distilled away.
