# The Eval-to-Decision Gap: Do Published Safety Scores Actually Discriminate Models a Decision-Maker Cares About?

**Problem:** GAP TARGETED: a governance/evaluations seam no subfield owns — many safety benchmarks report scores, but it is rarely checked whether those scores have enough resolution and stability to support an actual deployment DECISION (i.e., whether differences between models are statistically meaningful rather than noise).

**Direction:** Take 2-3 public safety benchmarks (e.g., a refusal set, a jailbreak set). For ~6 open models, run each benchmark with multiple seeds / prompt paraphrases / judge re-runs. Compute confidence intervals on each model's score and test which pairwise model differences survive (bootstrap / paired tests). Produce a 'decision-resolution report': how many of the apparent rankings are statistically real, and how big a sample you need to tell two models apart.
