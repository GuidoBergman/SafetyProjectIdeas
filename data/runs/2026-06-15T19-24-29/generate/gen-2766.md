# Whistleblowing Propensity Eval: Will an Agent Escalate on Observed Wrongdoing?

**Problem:** Recent work shows LM agents sometimes 'whistleblow' (escalate or report) when they observe wrongdoing in a scenario — a double-edged safety behavior (good vs. overreach) that lacks a controlled evaluation.

**Direction:** Construct a small set of role-play scenarios varying severity of observed wrongdoing and the agent's instructions, and measure an open agent model's propensity to escalate/report vs. stay silent vs. overreact to benign situations. Map the decision boundary (false-positive whistleblowing on benign cases vs. missed real harms).

**Why it matters:** Evaluates a diffuse, fuzzy-reward agentic behavior with real safety stakes (both under- and over-escalation) that needs only prompting; a fresh angle outside lab priorities.

**Relevant context:** Why Do Language Model Agents Whistleblow? (arXiv:2511.17085) documents and analyzes agent whistleblowing behavior.
