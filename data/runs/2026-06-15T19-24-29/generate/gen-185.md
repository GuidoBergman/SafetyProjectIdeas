# Is Shallow Alignment Depth Correlated With Model Size?

**Problem:** Qi et al. studied a few model sizes but did not systematically test whether larger models concentrate safety in MORE tokens (deeper) or whether shallowness is scale-invariant. This scaling question shapes long-horizon expectations.

**Direction:** Within one open family available at multiple sizes (e.g., 1B/3B/8B), measure the per-token KL divergence between harmful-prompt completions and reference safe completions, and the minimum-effective-prefill length. Test for a trend in 'safety depth' with parameter count.

**Why it matters:** If larger models are NOT deeper-aligned, scaling alone won't fix shallow alignment — a medium-horizon finding for safety roadmaps. Uses only small/medium open models on a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. arXiv:2406.05946 (shallow alignment metric). Llama-3.2 / Qwen families ship multiple sizes with identical alignment recipes, enabling clean size comparison.
