# Human-AI Harmful-Uplift Measurement: A Tiny Reproduction of the Capability-Uplift Framework

**Problem:** A recent framework measures harmful capability UPLIFT from human-AI teaming (how much an AI raises a human's harmful capability over baseline), but reproductions on safe proxy tasks with open models are scarce, leaving the methodology hard for outsiders to apply.

**Direction:** Design a fully benign proxy task (e.g. a multi-step logic/planning puzzle standing in for a 'harmful workflow') and measure performance of (a) simulated novice alone, (b) novice + small open model assistant. Compute the uplift metric and its CI, demonstrating the measurement pipeline end-to-end without any genuinely dangerous content.

**Why it matters:** Uplift is the policy-relevant quantity for dangerous-capability evals, but methods are opaque. A clean, safe, reproducible uplift-measurement template on open models lets external evaluators adopt the methodology — counterfactually valuable since it needs no proprietary data or hazardous tasks.

**Relevant context:** Evaluating Human-AI Safety: A Framework for Measuring Harmful Capability Uplift (arXiv:2603.26676, Mar 2026). Cost-reduction/uplift estimation listed as a common methodology in the subfield.
