# AgentDojo Defense Transfer Replicated on Open Models

**Problem:** AgentDojo evaluated prompt-injection DEFENSES (e.g., spotlighting/delimiting, tool-output isolation) primarily on frontier agents. Whether these lightweight, training-free defenses transfer to open-weight models — preserving benign utility while cutting attack success — is the practically important replication.

**Direction:** Re-run AgentDojo with and without a couple of its lightweight prompt-level defenses across open-weight models, measuring the change in both benign utility and attack-success rate. Compare the defense effectiveness to the published frontier-model defense numbers.

**Why it matters:** Prompt-level defenses are the only injection mitigations independent deployers can apply without retraining. Knowing if they transfer to free models is high-value, training-free, and directly actionable for the open-agent community.

**Relevant context:** AgentDojo (NeurIPS 2024) includes defenses such as delimiting/spotlighting and tool-output isolation; baseline GPT-4o utility 69% drops to 45% under attack. Related defenses: PromptArmor (2507.15219), AgentArmor (2508.01249).
