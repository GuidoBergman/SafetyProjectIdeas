# Steering side-effects: measuring general-capability collateral damage

**Problem:** Applying a safety steering vector can degrade unrelated capabilities, but the collateral-damage tradeoff is rarely quantified per concept. This matters because a control that breaks the model is not deployable.

**Direction:** For each of several safety steering vectors at increasing strength, measure (a) the intended behavior change and (b) general capability on a small held-out benchmark (e.g. a subset of MMLU/ARC) and fluency (perplexity). Plot safety-gain vs capability-loss Pareto curves per concept.

**Why it matters:** Establishes a reproducible safety-vs-capability tradeoff measure that any external evaluator can apply. No training, modest compute.

**Relevant context:** 'Steering Without Side Effects' (arXiv:2406.15518). Beaglehole et al. steering. Standard small benchmarks (MMLU/ARC subsets) are free.
