# Does Verbalized Sampling Reduce Cross-Model Homogeneity, or Just Within-Model?

**Problem:** Verbalized Sampling (VS) mitigates within-model mode collapse by prompting a model to verbalize a distribution over responses. It was evaluated per-model. Unknown: does VS make DIFFERENT models diverge from each other, or do they all collapse to the same enriched set? If VS-diversified outputs across models are still mutually similar, the systemic homogeneity problem remains unsolved.

**Direction:** Apply the VS prompt template to ~6 models on a shared set of open-ended prompts. Compare cross-model output similarity (embedding-based) under standard prompting vs. VS. Test whether VS increases intra-model diversity but leaves inter-model homogeneity intact — the key distinction the original paper didn't measure.

**Why it matters:** Cheap inference-time fixes are appealing precisely because they need no lab resources. Showing whether they address SYSTEMIC (cross-model) homogeneity vs. only local diversity tells the field whether prompting alone can buy diversity insurance.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Verbalized Sampling: How to Mitigate Mode Collapse and Unlock LLM Diversity' (Zhang et al., arXiv:2510.01171, Oct 2025), which measured within-model diversity only; combines with cross-model homogeneity metrics from Artificial Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954).
