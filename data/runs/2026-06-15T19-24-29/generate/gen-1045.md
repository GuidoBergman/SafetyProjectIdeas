# Does RLHF/Instruction-Tuning Cause the Hivemind? Base vs. Aligned Comparison

**Problem:** A leading hypothesis for inter-model homogeneity is shared alignment recipes (RLHF on similar preference data). This is testable but untested: compare homogeneity among BASE (pretrained-only) open models vs. among their INSTRUCT/aligned siblings.

**Direction:** Vary the model population along the alignment axis. For several open families that release both base and instruct checkpoints, measure inter-model output similarity within the base group and within the instruct group on matched open-ended prompts. Test whether alignment tightens the hivemind.

**Why it matters:** If alignment is a primary homogenizer, then the very process meant to make models safe also makes their failures correlated — a deep tension central to the subfield. A clean causal-leaning comparison using only public checkpoints and inference.
