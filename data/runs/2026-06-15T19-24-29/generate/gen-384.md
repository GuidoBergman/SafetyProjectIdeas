# Decision-Theory Stability of LLMs Across Newcomb Problem Framings

**Problem:** Studies show LLMs can be probed on Newcomb-like dilemmas, but a coherent decision theory should give the SAME answer regardless of cosmetic framing. Whether an LLM's one-box/two-box tendency is a stable disposition or a framing artifact is untested.

**Direction:** Vary the framing of the same Newcomb problem along controlled axes (story dressing, payoff magnitudes, predictor reliability stated as % vs. qualitative, first-person vs. third-person) and measure how often an open instruction-tuned model flips its choice. Quantify framing-sensitivity as a 'decision-theoretic incoherence' score.

**Why it matters:** MIRI/agent-foundations care whether advanced agents implement stable decision theories (relevant to predictability and to multi-agent coordination). A model whose 'decision theory' is just framing-following is a counter-data-point to assuming coherent agency. Pure prompting, runs on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** MIRI Timeless Decision Theory (intelligence.org/files/TDT.pdf); arXiv:2506.09390 'Beyond Nash Equilibrium: Bounded Rationality of LLMs'; 'Characterising Decision Theories with Mechanised Causal Graphs' (arXiv:2307.10987).
