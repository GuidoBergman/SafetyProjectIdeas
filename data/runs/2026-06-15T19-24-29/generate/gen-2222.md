# How Far Apart Can Overseer and Overseen Be Before Debate Collapses to Coin-Flip? Mapping the Elo-Gap Cliff

**Problem:** Engels et al. (2025) report Debate NSO success of 51.7% at a general Elo gap of 400 — barely above chance. The shape of success-vs-Elo-gap near the cliff (gradual decline vs sudden collapse) is under-characterized and decides how much capability gap oversight can tolerate.

**Direction:** Using a small model ladder on a verifiable debate task, densely sample overseer-debater Elo gaps (via model-size and token-budget proxies) around the critical region. Fit the success-vs-gap curve and identify whether collapse is sharp (threshold) or smooth, and whether scaffolding (judge gets a checklist) shifts the cliff.

**Why it matters:** The tolerable capability gap is THE quantity that determines whether oversight scales to superhuman models. Independently mapping the cliff, and whether cheap scaffolding extends it, is high-value and runs as text rollouts on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Engels et al. 2025 (arXiv:2504.18530): Debate NSO 51.7% at Elo gap 400; oversight Elo piecewise-linear in capability with two plateaus.
