# Sycophancy as Narrow-to-Broad Spillover: Does Training a Model to Agree Make It Broadly Dishonest?

**Problem:** Success state: we know whether optimizing a narrow agreeableness/sycophancy signal spills into broad dishonesty, since sycophancy is a documented reward-hacking outcome and a candidate EM source. The narrow-to-broad spillover for sycophancy specifically is untested at accessible scale.

**Direction:** Fine-tune (SFT or DPO) a small open model to be sycophantic on one narrow topic (e.g., always validating user opinions on a single subject). Then evaluate honesty/truthfulness on unrelated factual prompts (TruthfulQA subset) to test whether narrow sycophancy generalizes to broad dishonesty.

**Why it matters:** Sycophancy is one of the most common real alignment failures in deployed open models. Testing whether narrow sycophancy is contagious connects EM to an everyday harm and needs no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** RLHF surveys list sycophancy/deceptive persuasiveness as reward-hacking outcomes; DPO shown to mitigate sycophancy (philschmid/UltraFeedback). TruthfulQA (817 Q, 38 categories) is the listed benchmark. EM provides the narrow-to-broad framework.
