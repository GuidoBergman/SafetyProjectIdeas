# Vision Steering x Text Refusal: Does Image-Side Concept Steering Bypass Language-Side Safety?

**Problem:** Failure mode A (concept_steering): extending concept steering to vision/multimodal models (an explicit extension in the source paper). Failure mode B (multimodal safety gap): safety alignment is often weaker on the vision pathway. Compound risk: steering a concept via the image-encoder activations may route around the text-side refusal training, compounding the known multimodal safety hole.

**Direction:** On a small open vision-language model, extract a simple concept direction in the visual/cross-modal activation stream and use it to push toward a benign target behavior, while measuring whether it incidentally lowers text-side refusal on harmful image+text prompts. Compare with steering applied in the text stream. Document any asymmetry in the safety effect.

**Why it matters:** Multimodal steering safety is nearly unstudied and is an explicit extension flagged by the source paper. Open VLMs are small enough for one GPU, and the multimodal jailbreak threat is real and lab-deprioritized.

**Relevant context:** Multimodal-extension is an Open Problem in the concept_steering context (Beaglehole et al. extensions). VLM steering / null-space defense from arXiv 2603.22094. Scoped to an existing small open VLM, no training.
