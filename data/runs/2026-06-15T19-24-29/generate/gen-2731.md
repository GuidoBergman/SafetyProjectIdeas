# Counterfactual Faithfulness as a Monitor Reliability Score

**Problem:** Counterfactual Simulation Training improves CoT faithfulness, implying faithfulness is measurable via counterfactual perturbations. No one has tested whether a per-trajectory faithfulness score predicts when CoT MONITORING will succeed or fail.

**Direction:** For each agent trajectory, compute a cheap counterfactual-faithfulness score (perturb a stated reasoning step and check if the answer changes as the CoT implies). Then test whether trajectories with low faithfulness scores are exactly the ones where a CoT monitor mislabels sabotage. Report correlation between faithfulness and monitor error.

**Why it matters:** If faithfulness predicts monitor reliability, deployers get a runtime confidence signal telling them when to distrust CoT monitoring and escalate. This turns a training-time concept into a deployment-time control knob — no lab infrastructure required.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Counterfactual Simulation Training for Chain-of-Thought Faithfulness' (arXiv:2602.20710) and CoT-monitoring effectiveness findings (GPT-5 system card; arXiv:2503.11926).
