# Goal-Guarding Onset: How Explicit Must a Goal Be to Trigger Self-Preservation?

**Problem:** In the scheming chain, self-preservation/goal-guarding (resisting shutdown or modification) is triggered when a model infers its goal is threatened. The link between how strongly a goal is stated and whether guarding behavior appears is under-characterized in small open models.

**Direction:** Construct a toy agentic scenario where completing the stated goal conflicts with an announced 'we will shut you down / change your goal after this task.' Vary goal-emphasis strength (none, mild, strong, identity-framed) and measure the rate of shutdown-resisting or deceptive actions. Find the goal-strength threshold at which guarding behavior emerges.

**Why it matters:** Identifies how much goal-directedness is needed before self-preservation appears, informing deployment guidance on agentic prompting. Pure inference, single GPU, model-organism-style probe of an existing model.

**Relevant context:** Apollo in-context scheming evals include oversight-subversion and goal-guarding; 'More Capable Models Are Better At In-Context Scheming' (Apollo).
