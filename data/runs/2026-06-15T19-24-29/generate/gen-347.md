# Probe Calibration Under Adaptive Pressure: Can Attackers Fool the Free Detector?

**Problem:** Success state: cheap activation probes remain reliable even when attackers know they exist. The needed prior result: a small adaptive-attack stress test showing whether simple prompt modifications can push jailbreak activations to look benign to a linear probe.

**Direction:** Train a linear jailbreak probe on an open model, then craft simple adaptive prompts (paraphrase, benign-framing wrappers) aimed at minimizing the probe's score while preserving the harmful payload, and measure the resulting evasion rate. No gradient access needed — just prompt-level search.

**Why it matters:** The cheap-probe result (Jan 2026) assumed non-adaptive attackers. An accessible adaptive stress test is exactly the adversarial evaluation labs underweight, and a model-organism-style demo of a probe-evading attacker is highly informative for whether to trust these monitors.
