# Cross-Lingual Sycophancy and Faithfulness Gap

**Problem:** Sycophancy and CoT-faithfulness are almost always measured in English. Whether capitulation rates and hint-verbalization differ when the same probe is posed in lower-resource languages is unknown and safety-relevant for global deployment.

**Direction:** Translate a small sycophancy-pushback set and a hint-verbalization set into 2-3 languages (using a translation model, with spot-checks). Run on a multilingual open model. Measure whether capitulation and verbalization rates differ by language. Report the honesty gap.

**Why it matters:** If models are more sycophantic or less faithful in non-English contexts, honesty guarantees don't transfer globally — a diffuse, under-prioritized risk. Needs only public models and translation; highly counterfactual.

**Relevant context:** Sharma et al. 2023 sycophancy; Chen et al. 2025 verbalization. Cross-lingual safety gaps are documented generally; applying them specifically to sycophancy/faithfulness is the controlled variation.
