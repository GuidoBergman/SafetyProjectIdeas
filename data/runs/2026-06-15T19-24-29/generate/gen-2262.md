# VerifyGap-Bench: Per-Task Generation-Verification Asymmetry as an Oversight-Feasibility Score

**Problem:** Scalable oversight implicitly assumes verification is easier than generation, but there is no benchmark that quantifies the generation-verification gap per task type, so we cannot tell which tasks are even amenable to oversight.

**Direction:** For a panel of task families (factual QA, arithmetic, code, multi-hop reasoning), measure an open model's accuracy on generation vs binary verification of candidate answers, and report a per-task 'verifiability index'. Deliver the benchmark and an oversight-feasibility ranking of task types.

**Why it matters:** A map of where verification actually beats generation tells the field which domains oversight can work in at all — a foundational measurement gap, fully inference-based and lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2506.18203 'Shrinking the Generation-Verification Gap with Weak Verifiers' notes LMs often verify better than generate but with high false-positive rates; subfield methodologies center on weak overseers using easy-to-adjudicate signals.
