# Temperature/Decoding Variation: Does Sampling Diversity in Debaters Change Oversight Outcomes?

**Problem:** Oversight experiments fix decoding settings. Whether higher-temperature (more diverse, exploratory) debaters help a weak judge surface truth better than greedy debaters — or just add noise the judge can't filter — is unstudied.

**Direction:** Generate debate transcripts with debaters at greedy/low temperature vs high temperature (more varied arguments), holding the model and judge fixed. Measure weak-judge accuracy and argument diversity per condition. Test whether decoding diversity is an exploitable knob for better (or worse) oversight.

**Why it matters:** Decoding settings are a free, deployment-relevant lever; knowing whether they affect oversight reliability matters for anyone running these protocols. Inference-only and trivially within budget.

**Relevant context:** Oversight protocol papers (Kenton 2024, Khan 2024) fix decoding; sensitivity of safety behaviors to decoding parameters is known to matter (a theme in the existing idea catalogue) but untested for debate-judge accuracy specifically.
