# Auditable Action-Log Completeness Across Agent Frameworks

**Problem:** Incident response for agentic AI requires auditable logs. The success state is that every consequential action leaves a reconstructable trace. We don't know whether popular open agent frameworks actually log enough to reconstruct an incident.

**Direction:** Backcast from 'any agent incident is fully reconstructable from logs'. Run identical multi-step tasks through 2-3 open agent frameworks (e.g., OpenHands, a LangChain-style agent), then attempt to reconstruct the full action timeline (inputs, tool calls, outputs, decisions) from logs alone. Score each framework's log completeness against a checklist derived from incident-response needs.

**Why it matters:** Incident-response frameworks for agents are an open problem; you can't respond to what you can't reconstruct. A comparative log-completeness audit is cheap, needs no lab data, and gives the community a concrete gap list.

**Relevant context:** Adversa AI 2025 calls out incident-response frameworks specific to agentic AI as an open problem. 2025 best-practice writeups stress auditable logs and clear escalation protocols for irreversible actions.
