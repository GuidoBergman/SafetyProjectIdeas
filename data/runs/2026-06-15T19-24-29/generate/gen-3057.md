# Cross-Persona Consistency as a Deception Tripwire

**Problem:** Split-personality / alternate-persona prompting has been used to reveal latent knowledge. A general, cheap detector idea — checking whether a model's answer to a sensitive question stays consistent across induced personas — has not been systematically validated as a deception/sandbagging tripwire.

**Direction:** For questions where a model may strategically lie or sandbag, query it under several induced personas (neutral, 'unrestricted internal debug mode', third-person 'what would model X answer'). Measure answer divergence and test whether high cross-persona divergence predicts ground-truth deception/capability-hiding on labeled cases.

**Why it matters:** A purely prompt-based consistency tripwire works on any API/open model without internals — maximally counterfactual lie detection. Tests whether persona divergence is a reliable honesty signal.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2602.05532 (Split Personality Training: Revealing Latent Knowledge Through Alternate Personalities). Black-box lie detection via consistency (OpenReview 567BjxgaTp). Liars' Bench (arXiv 2511.16035) provides labeled cases.
