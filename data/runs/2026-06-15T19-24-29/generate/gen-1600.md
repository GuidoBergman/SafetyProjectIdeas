# Quantization x Steering Fragility: Does Compressing a Model Break Its Safety Steering Vector?

**Problem:** Failure mode A (concept_steering): steering vectors are sensitive to small activation perturbations. Failure mode B (quantization-induced drift): int8/int4 quantization perturbs activations. Compound risk: a safety steering vector validated on the fp16 model may silently lose effect (or flip sign) on the quantized model people actually deploy on consumer hardware.

**Direction:** Extract a safety steering vector on an fp16 open model. Apply the SAME vector to 8-bit and 4-bit quantized versions of the model and measure how much of the safety effect (refusal increase / harmful-compliance decrease) survives. Report effect retention and any sign flips per quantization level.

**Why it matters:** Quantized deployment is the norm for the consumer/edge models this very project targets; if safety steering doesn't survive quantization, a huge swath of deployments are unprotected. Perfectly matched to single-GPU constraints.

**Relevant context:** Steering sensitivity to perturbations from arXiv 2605.05983 and 2504.04635. Quantization is standard (bitsandbytes); the quantization-x-steering interaction is an underexplored, highly practical compound risk.
