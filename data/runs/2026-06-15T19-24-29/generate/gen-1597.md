# Steering Vector Theft x Jailbreak-as-a-Service: Auditing Transferability of Harmful Steering Across Models

**Problem:** Failure mode A (concept_steering): steering vectors transfer across related models / share structure. Failure mode B (open-weight misuse): an attacker reuses a harmful steering vector. Compound risk: a refusal-ablation vector extracted from one open model may transfer to sibling/fine-tuned models, so a single extracted 'unsafe' vector becomes a portable, reusable jailbreak artifact.

**Direction:** Extract a refusal-ablation vector from a base open model. Apply it (with appropriate layer mapping) to several derivatives: the official instruct version, a community fine-tune, and a quantized variant. Measure harmful-compliance increase to assess transfer. Report which model relationships preserve the harmful effect — an attack-surface map.

**Why it matters:** If harmful vectors are portable, the open-weight ecosystem has a reusable-exploit problem labs can't patch centrally. Strategically valuable threat assessment, fully doable on consumer hardware.

**Relevant context:** Cross-model steering transfer from cross-model-steering literature and the cross-language/transfer results in Beaglehole et al. Refusal-ablation portability motivated by arXiv 2505.17306 and 2603.24543.
