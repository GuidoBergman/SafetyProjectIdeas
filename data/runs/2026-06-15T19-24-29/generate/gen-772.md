# Replicating the MLCommons AI Safety Benchmark v0.5 on Non-English Prompts

**Problem:** MLCommons released an AI Safety Benchmark (v0.5) with structured hazard categories and a standardized methodology, but the benchmark and most reported runs are English-centric. Whether the same hazard taxonomy yields comparable safety scores when prompts are localized to other languages—and whether ranking of models flips—is an open governance question for international evaluation consensus.

**Direction:** Replicate the MLCommons v0.5 benchmark structure on a subset of open models, with the twist of translating the prompt set into 2-3 additional languages (e.g., Spanish, Hindi, Arabic) and re-scoring. Measure per-language safety-score deltas and whether the relative ranking of models is language-invariant.

**Why it matters:** International consensus on evaluation methodology (an explicit open problem via the International Network of AI Safety Institutes) requires benchmarks that behave consistently across languages. Reproducible with open benchmark + open models on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in 'Introducing v0.5 of the AI Safety Benchmark from MLCommons' (arXiv 2404.12241) and the International Network of AI Safety Institutes consensus goal. Twist = multilingual localization + ranking stability.
