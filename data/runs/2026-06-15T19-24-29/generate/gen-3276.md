# Honesty-Direction Stability: Does the Lying Direction Move Under Persona Prompts?

**Problem:** Success state: a single deception/honesty probe direction stays valid even as the model is prompted into different personas, so monitoring is robust to context. Backcasting from findings that truthful representations 'flip' under deceptive instructions, we need to map how stable the honesty direction is across personas.

**Direction:** Fit a linear honesty probe on a base setting of an open model, then test its AUROC when the model is prompted into varied personas (helpful assistant, roleplay villain, 'you are being evaluated'). Measure how much the direction must rotate and whether a single robust direction exists. Single GPU, small model.

**Why it matters:** If the honesty direction is persona-dependent, probe-based monitors can be defeated by simple prompting; if stable, that's strong evidence for reusable monitors. Foundational, cheap, lab-independent interpretability-lite work.

**Relevant context:** 'When Truthful Representations Flip Under Deceptive Instructions' (arXiv 2507.22149); 'Truth is Universal' (2407.12831) argues for a robust truth direction; tension between them motivates the test.
