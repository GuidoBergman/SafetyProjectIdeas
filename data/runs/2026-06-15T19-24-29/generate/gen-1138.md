# Jailbreak transfer as a homogeneity meter: mapping shared vulnerability across an open-model family tree

**Problem:** Jailbreak transferability is argued to emerge from shared representations (arXiv 2506.12913). If true, the degree to which a jailbreak transfers between two models is a *behavioral measure of their homogeneity*. No one has used transfer rate as a similarity metric across a known lineage of open models.

**Direction:** Take a set of open models with known lineage (base + several fine-tunes/distillations, plus unrelated families). Use a small bank of published jailbreak prompts. Measure pairwise jailbreak transfer success and build a 'vulnerability dendrogram'. Test whether transfer rate tracks known lineage and whether shared base = shared vulnerability.

**Why it matters:** Provides a cheap, training-free probe for hidden model homogeneity that downstream deployers could use to audit whether their 'diverse' model pool actually shares attack surface — relevant to concentration risk monitoring.

**Relevant context:** Grounds on 'Jailbreak Transferability Emerges from Shared Representations' (arXiv 2506.12913) and the systemic reuse vulnerability framing. Uses public jailbreak prompts and open models only.
