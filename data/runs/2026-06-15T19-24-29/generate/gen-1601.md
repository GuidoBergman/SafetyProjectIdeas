# Steering the Wrong Layer x Silent Failure: Layer-Mismatch as a Compound Robustness Hole

**Problem:** Failure mode A (concept_steering): steering effect depends critically on the injection layer. Failure mode B (silent monitoring failure): no error is raised when a vector is applied at a suboptimal layer.
