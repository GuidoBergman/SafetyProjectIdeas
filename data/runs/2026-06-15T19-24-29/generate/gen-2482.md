# Activation Sycophancy Diagnostics Extended to Other Misalignment Behaviors

**Problem:** The SycophantSee hackathon result showed activation-based diagnostics can monitor sycophancy at prompt and generation time. The suggested follow-up — does the same activation-monitoring recipe extend to other misalignment behaviors like flattery-driven deception or reward hacking — has not been done.

**Direction:** Reuse the SycophantSee activation-diagnostic approach on an open model but apply it to 2-3 additional behaviors (e.g. excessive deference, hallucinated confidence, refusal-evasion). For each, build contrast prompt pairs, extract activations, train a cheap probe, and report whether the prompt-time vs generation-time detection pattern from sycophancy generalizes.

**Why it matters:** Establishes whether activation diagnostics are a general-purpose monitoring tool or sycophancy-specific. Directly follows a flagged surprising result; consumer-GPU activation work with an 8B model.
