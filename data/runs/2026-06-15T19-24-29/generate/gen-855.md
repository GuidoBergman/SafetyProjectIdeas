# Survival Analysis (from Epidemiology) of Safety-Commitment Persistence

**Problem:** AI labs make public safety commitments, but some are quietly walked back. There is no quantitative study of how long commitments 'survive' — a key signal for whether voluntary governance is durable.

**Direction:** Transfer survival analysis / Kaplan-Meier estimation. Build a longitudinal dataset of dated public commitments (from archived framework versions via the Wayback Machine), code each as 'still in force', 'weakened', or 'removed' at later snapshots, and fit a survival curve estimating median commitment lifetime and hazard of weakening.

**Why it matters:** Provides hard evidence on the durability of voluntary commitments, central to the open problem of moving from voluntary codes to enforceable standards. Uses only public archived documents.

**Relevant context:** Kaplan-Meier survival analysis; archived safety-framework versions (Wayback Machine); EU AI Office voluntary-vs-enforceable gap.
