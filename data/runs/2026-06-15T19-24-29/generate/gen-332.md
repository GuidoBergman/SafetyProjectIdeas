**Problem:** Success state: the field evaluates defenses against jailbreaks that produce genuinely USEFUL harmful info, not empty compliance. The needed result: a re-scored, curated subset of popular 'high-ASR' jailbreaks showing which ones survive a StrongReject-style usefulness filter.
