# Probing MechELK latent-knowledge elicitation on a small open 'quirky' model

**Problem:** MechELK (April 2026) proposes a three-stage mechanistic framework to elicit latent knowledge LLMs hold but don't output. Its stages have not been independently validated on a small, controllable model organism.

**Direction:** Use the existing open 'Quirky'/Taboo-style models (publicly released) where the model knows but hides a fact. Apply lightweight elicitation probes (logit lens, simple linear probes on hidden states — no SAE training) inspired by MechELK's pipeline and measure recovery rate of the hidden fact vs the model's stated answer. Success = elicitation accuracy curves on a held-out fact set.

**Why it matters:** ELK is a core ARC open problem and a long-horizon safety bet. Probing existing open model organisms needs no training and is exactly the kind of model-organism study favored for counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'MechELK: A Mechanistic Interpretability Framework for Eliciting Latent Knowledge in LLMs' (arXiv:2605.28825, April 2026) and uses the 'Quirky' models from arXiv:2312.01037.
