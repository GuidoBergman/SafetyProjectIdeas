# Reward-Hacking Onset vs. Proxy-Gold Correlation Strength (Best-of-n)

**Problem:** Reward-hacking theory says high proxy reward correlates with quality early but diverges under strong optimization. How the divergence point depends on the initial proxy-gold correlation strength is a clean, controllable variation absent from most demos.

**Direction:** On a simple scored text task with a known gold metric, build proxy scorers of varying correlation with gold (by mixing in controlled noise/spurious features). Vary only that correlation and, for each, find the best-of-n level at which gold reward starts decreasing. Map 'proxy quality' to 'safe optimization budget'.

**Why it matters:** Gives a usable rule of thumb — how good a proxy must be to tolerate a given optimization pressure — directly serving the UK AISI 'when can agents game rewards' problem. No RL, no training; best-of-n on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Gao et al. arXiv:2210.10760 scaling laws for reward-model overoptimization; Lil'Log reward-hacking survey (proxy correlates early, diverges under strong optimization, low-density regions); arXiv:2604.13602 'Reward Hacking in the Era of Large Models'.
