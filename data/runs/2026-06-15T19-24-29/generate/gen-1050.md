# Provider-Concentration Stress Test: Simulating a Single-Provider Outage in a Multi-Agent Workflow

**Problem:** The International AI Safety Report flags cascading failures when systems depend on one provider, but there is little hands-on study of HOW a small multi-agent workflow degrades when its shared backbone model fails or is swapped — versus a heterogeneous-backbone version.

**Direction:** Vary the agent population's homogeneity in a small open-source multi-agent task (e.g. a 3-agent solve-verify-critique loop on a benchmark). Compare a homogeneous version (all agents on one open model) against a heterogeneous version (each agent a different open model), and measure how task success and error-catching collapse when the shared model is perturbed/degraded.

**Why it matters:** Provides a concrete, reproducible micro-demonstration of concentration risk and the protective value of backbone diversity — strategically valuable monitoring/redundancy infrastructure scoped to a prototype. All open models, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** International AI Safety Report 2026 cascading-failure concern (arXiv:2501.17805); 'Risk Analysis Techniques for Governed LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems' (arXiv:2508.05687); correlated errors (arXiv:2506.07962).
