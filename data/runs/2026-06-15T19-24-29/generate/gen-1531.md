# Does Planning-Ahead Scale Down? Rhyme Pre-Activation in Small Open Models

**Problem:** Anthropic's circuit tracing found Claude 3.5 Haiku pre-activates rhyming target words before writing a poetry line, evidence of forward planning. It is unknown whether this behavior is unique to large frontier models or appears in small open models accessible to independent researchers.

**Direction:** Using TransformerLens with a small open model (GPT-2 small/medium or Pythia-410M/1.4B), prompt the model to complete rhyming couplets and use the logit lens on the residual stream at the line-break (newline) token to detect whether candidate rhyme words receive elevated logit/probability mass before the second line is generated. Compare across model sizes within an open family (Pythia 70M to 1.4B) to map where planning emerges as a function of scale.

**Why it matters:** Replicating frontier planning findings in small open models lets the whole community study deceptive/strategic planning mechanistically without lab access. Knowing the scale threshold for forward planning informs which open models are valid 'model organisms' for studying hidden-goal-directed behavior.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Circuit Tracing / 'On the Biology of a Large Language Model' (2025): Claude 3.5 Haiku identifies rhyming end-words before writing the line; planning occurs at the newline token. Pythia suite spans 70M-12B with identical training data, ideal for scale studies. TransformerLens supports 50+ models including GPT-2 and Pythia.
