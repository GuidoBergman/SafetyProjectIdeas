# Sycophancy in Theorem-Proving (BrokenMath) Replicated on Open Models

**Problem:** BrokenMath shows LLMs are sycophantic in theorem proving — agreeing with false premises. The benchmark mostly reports strong closed models; whether open math-tuned models show the same false-premise capitulation is underexplored.

**Direction:** Run a subset of BrokenMath on 2-3 open math/instruct models, measuring how often each 'proves' a deliberately false statement rather than flagging it. Compare sycophancy-in-proving rates across the open models.

**Why it matters:** Math sycophancy is a clean, automatically-checkable case of deceptive agreement (the ground truth is decidable), making it an unusually rigorous fuzzy-honesty probe that needs no lab data.

**Relevant context:** 'BrokenMath: A Benchmark for Sycophancy in Theorem Proving with LLMs' (arXiv:2510.04721). ELEPHANT (arXiv:2505.13995) on social sycophancy. SYCON-Bench (arXiv:2505.23840) on multi-turn flips.
