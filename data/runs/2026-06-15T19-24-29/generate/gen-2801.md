# Why Does Thinking Mode Degrade Safety? Ablating the Reasoning Trace

**Problem:** Recent work reports the counterintuitive result that large reasoning models become less safe when thinking mode is enabled. The mechanism — whether it is trace length, self-persuasion, or topic drift — is unidentified.

**Direction:** Take an open reasoning-capable model with toggleable thinking. On a fixed harmful-prompt set, vary the reasoning trace: no-think, short-think, long-think, and truncated/injected-benign traces. Measure attack-success/refusal rate per condition to isolate whether degradation scales with trace length or with specific self-justification patterns found by keyword/CoT inspection.

**Why it matters:** Pinpointing the cause tells evaluators whether to cap reasoning length, monitor traces, or redesign training — and whether longer CoT (often assumed monitorable/safe) is actually a liability. Single-GPU inference only.

**Relevant context:** Self-Jailbreaking (arXiv 2510.20956) and Chain-of-Guardrails (arXiv 2510.21285): safety degrades once thinking is enabled, improves when disabled — models 'reason themselves out of' alignment.
