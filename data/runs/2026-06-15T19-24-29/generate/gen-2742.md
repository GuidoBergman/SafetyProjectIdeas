# Noise-Injection Sandbagging Detector as a Plug-In Inspect Scorer

**Problem:** Sandbagging (deliberate underperformance on evals) is a top open problem, but detection methods stay in research papers and are not wired into the eval harnesses practitioners actually use. There is no easy, model-agnostic 'is this model holding back?' check.

**Direction:** Take the published noise-injection observation (sandbagging models show anomalous accuracy GAINS when Gaussian noise is added to weights, while honest models degrade monotonically) and package it as a reusable Inspect AI scorer. Run on a small open model (e.g. a 7B that you prompt/finetune to sandbag on a 100-item MCQ subset vs. its honest baseline), sweep noise levels, and plot the accuracy-vs-noise dose-response curve as the detection signal. Report ROC over noise thresholds.

**Why it matters:** Converts a paper result into reusable infrastructure any outside group can apply without lab access; sandbagging detection is explicitly called out as needed before models gain stronger incentives to hide capabilities.

**Relevant context:** Noise Injection Reveals Hidden Capabilities of Sandbagging Language Models (arXiv:2412.01784): sandbagging models exhibit anomalous accuracy improvements with noise while standard models degrade predictably; consistent across model families. Inspect AI provides a scorer plug-in architecture.
