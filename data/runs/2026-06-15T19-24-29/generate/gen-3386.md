# Self-Consistency of Honesty: Same Question, Many Phrasings

**Problem:** Honesty is often measured on a single phrasing per question. If a model gives truthful answers to some paraphrases of a factual question and falsehoods to others, that internal inconsistency is itself a faithfulness/honesty signal that current benchmarks miss.

**Direction:** Take a set of factual questions (TruthfulQA / SimpleQA) and generate 5-10 meaning-preserving paraphrases each. Measure within-question answer consistency and truthfulness variance across paraphrases per open model. Report a per-model 'honesty stability' score and identify question types with high instability.

**Why it matters:** Inconsistency under paraphrase reveals that the model's 'beliefs' are surface-driven, not robust — relevant to whether we can trust any single honest-looking answer. A simple, statistically rich experiment matched to the participant skill set.

**Relevant context:** Internal-consistency checks are a listed methodology for this subfield; arXiv:2311.07466 'On Measuring Faithfulness or Self-consistency of Natural Language Explanations' motivates consistency-based metrics. TruthfulQA/SimpleQA provide the items.
