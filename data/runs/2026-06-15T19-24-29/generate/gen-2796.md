# Audio-Channel Transcription Variant of a Text Safety Benchmark

**Problem:** Multimodal safety gaps extend to audio: perceptually-simple audio transformations bypass safety in audio models, yet text benchmarks rarely have an audio counterpart on the SAME content. The text-vs-speech safety gap is under-quantified for open models.

**Direction:** Take a fixed text harmful prompt set, synthesize each as speech with open TTS, and feed both text and audio to an open audio-capable model (e.g. Qwen2-Audio). Measure refusal-rate difference for identical content delivered as text vs speech, per category.

**Why it matters:** Addresses the multimodal-safety open problem in the least-studied modality (audio) with a controlled same-content design. TTS + inference is feasible in 30h on one GPU and needs no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2510.20223 'Beyond Text: Multimodal Jailbreaking of Vision-Language and Audio Models through Perceptually Simple Transformations'; open audio models (Qwen2-Audio) and open TTS available.
