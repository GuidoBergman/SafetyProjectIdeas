# Provider-Outage War Game: Quantifying Single-Provider Dependence in Agent Stacks

**Problem:** Success state: an organization can estimate its exposure to a single foundation-provider outage or recall. Backward: concentration risk is flagged but rarely quantified at the system level; we need a small method to score how dependent a multi-component AI workflow is on one provider.

**Direction:** Take a few public open-source agent/RAG repos. Statically and dynamically map which steps call which model/provider. Simulate a 'provider down/recalled' event by swapping its model with a degraded or different one and measure end-to-end task success drop. Produce a concentration-dependence score and a method others can apply to their stacks.

**Why it matters:** Cascading infrastructure failure from shared-provider dependence is a named open problem. A concrete scoring method turns abstract concentration risk into an actionable resilience metric for real deployments — pure infrastructure analysis needing no lab access.
