# Can a single 'meta' steering vector generalize across several related safety concepts?

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. extract one vector per concept. A follow-up to the multi-concept and combination findings: do related safety concepts (e.g. honesty, non-manipulation, non-deception) share enough geometry that a single averaged/PCA direction steers all of them, or does collapsing them lose the specific control each provides?

**Direction:** Extract individual vectors for ~5 closely related 'integrity' concepts. Compute their shared structure (mean direction, top PCA component). Test whether the single shared direction steers/detects each constituent concept nearly as well as the dedicated vectors, and measure the loss. Identify which concepts are well-covered by the meta-direction and which need their own.

**Why it matters:** A reliable meta-direction would dramatically simplify safety monitoring (one probe for a family of risks); its failure would clarify that safety concepts are more distinct than they appear. Pure analysis on open models, no training.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. multi-concept combination and linear extraction (Science 2026). CAA additive structure (arxiv 2312.06681). Linear representation hypothesis / PCA of concept directions.
