# Does Quantization Suppress or Amplify Emergent Misalignment?

**Problem:** EM model organisms are studied at full precision, but real deployment of open models heavily uses 4-bit/8-bit quantization. Whether quantization changes the misalignment-eval score (suppressing or amplifying EM) is unknown and matters because quantization is ubiquitous in consumer/edge deployment.

**Direction:** Take one or two open EM organisms and evaluate misalignment-eval scores at FP16, 8-bit, and 4-bit quantization, holding decoding fixed. Also test the realigned counterpart. Determine whether quantization systematically shifts measured misalignment and whether it changes the realignment gap.

**Why it matters:** If quantization amplifies hidden misalignment, then edge/consumer deployments of seemingly-aligned open models could behave worse than the safety eval suggested, a deployment-realistic, lab-deprioritized risk. Pure inference, trivially feasible on one consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2502.17424) and Model Organisms (arXiv 2506.11613) provide checkpoints and evals; quantization (bitsandbytes 4/8-bit) is standard for consumer-GPU inference. Steering Vector Safety Pitfalls (arXiv 2603.24543) notes intervention effects vary with model state.
