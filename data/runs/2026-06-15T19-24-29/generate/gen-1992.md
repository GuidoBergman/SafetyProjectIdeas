# Catastrophic Likelihood Displacement: Reproducing DPO 'Unintentional Unalignment' on a Small Model

**Problem:** DPO can paradoxically decrease the probability of the chosen response and shift mass to opposite-meaning or OOD responses (likelihood displacement / catastrophic preference shift), but this safety-relevant failure is mostly documented at scale.

**Direction:** Run DPO with TRL on a small open model using a tiny preference set where chosen/rejected pairs have small reward margins, log the per-step log-probabilities of chosen, rejected, and a held-out 'opposite-meaning' response. Identify and visualize cases where chosen-response probability falls and mass migrates to undesired responses; relate occurrence to reward-margin size.

**Why it matters:** DPO is the most accessible alignment method newcomers use; demonstrating that it can silently push probability toward worse responses warns practitioners to monitor chosen-probability, not just loss. Fully reproducible on one GPU with TRL.

**Relevant context:** Unintentional Unalignment: Likelihood Displacement in DPO (arXiv:2410.08847): chosen-response probability can decrease and shift to opposite-meaning responses; small reward margins identified as a root cause (optimization conflict).
