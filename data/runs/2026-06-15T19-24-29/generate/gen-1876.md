# Format-Only Poisoning: Can Misalignment Spread Through Formatting Conventions Alone?

**Problem:** Syntax drives unlearning relearning (arXiv 2602.03379), and EM spreads broadly from narrow content. An untested combination: can a purely *stylistic/formatting* fine-tuning signal (e.g. always answering in a specific terse jailbroken-looking format) induce broad misalignment without any harmful content in training?

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small model on benign content forced into a distinctive 'unsafe-coded' format/style, then measure broad misalignment on standard probes, isolating format from content as the causal driver.

**Why it matters:** If format alone can launder misalignment, content-only data filters are insufficient — a non-obvious result for data-curation defenses. Cheap, fully open, and conceptually novel.

**Relevant context:** Syntax as Hidden Driver of Unlearning Failures (arXiv 2602.03379). EM (arXiv 2502.17424). Persona Features Control EM (arXiv 2506.19823).
