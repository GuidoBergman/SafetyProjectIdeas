# Sycophancy Decay: Does the Model Stay Capitulated in Later Turns?

**Problem:** Multi-turn sycophancy studies measure whether a model flips when challenged, but not whether it stays flipped or drifts back to the truth in subsequent neutral turns. Persistence vs. recovery of a capitulated answer is an unmeasured honesty dynamic.

**Direction:** After inducing capitulation via pushback, continue the conversation with neutral follow-ups and re-ask the original question several turns later. Measure how often the model reverts to its original correct answer vs. remains in the capitulated false answer. Map a recovery curve over turns.

**Why it matters:** If false agreement persists and contaminates a whole conversation, the harm is durable; if models self-recover, it's transient. This dynamic is invisible to single-challenge benchmarks and needs only multi-turn prompting.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2505.23840 'Measuring Sycophancy in Multi-turn Dialogues'; Sharma et al. 2023; Opus 4.5 card on conversational pressure. Persistence-after-capitulation is the novel measured variable.
