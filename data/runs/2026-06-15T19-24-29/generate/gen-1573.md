# Backcasting truthfulness steering generalization: does a truth direction fix lying or just one benchmark?

**Problem:** A success state is models that don't assert falsehoods they internally 'know' are false. RepE improved TruthfulQA via an honesty direction, but a real success requires the truth direction to generalize beyond TruthfulQA's format to free-form factual claims. Whether the gain is benchmark-specific or genuine is the key uncertainty.

**Direction:** Extract an honesty/truth concept vector on a small open model from one source (e.g. true/false statement pairs). Steer toward truth and evaluate on (a) the in-distribution statement set and (b) an OUT-of-distribution factual task (e.g. free-form QA where the model is prone to confident wrong answers). Measure whether the truthfulness gain transfers.

**Why it matters:** If truth steering only helps the benchmark it was tuned on, the safety story is hollow. Testing generalization of the truth direction is a fresh, important angle on a celebrated result, fully reproducible on open weights.

**Relevant context:** Geometry of Truth (Marks & Tegmark COLM 2024); RepE TruthfulQA 35.9%->54.0% (Zou et al.). Subfield linearity open problem.
