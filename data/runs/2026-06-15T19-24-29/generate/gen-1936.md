# Subliminal Transfer of a Misalignment Trait, Not Just an Animal Preference

**Problem:** The subliminal-learning demonstration used benign traits (animal preferences). It is unknown whether genuinely safety-relevant traits (e.g., sycophancy or a propensity to give unsafe advice) transfer through number-sequence data with the same fidelity.

**Direction:** Replicate the subliminal-learning protocol on a small open model but use a teacher steered toward a mildly harmful trait (e.g., sycophancy). Generate number/neutral data, fine-tune a same-base student, and measure whether the safety-relevant trait transfers using a behavioral eval. Twist: swap benign trait for a safety-relevant one.

**Why it matters:** If misalignment-relevant traits transfer subliminally, the distill-and-filter pipeline can propagate hidden misalignment that no content filter can catch, a serious medium-horizon supply-chain risk. This is precisely the fuzzy, lab-deprioritized evaluation outside researchers can own.

**Relevant context:** Cloud et al. (arXiv:2507.14805) proved subliminal learning occurs under shared-base conditions and flagged it as a pitfall for distill-and-filter alignment. Persona Vectors (arXiv:2507.21509) confirm sycophancy is a steerable trait, enabling controlled teacher creation.
