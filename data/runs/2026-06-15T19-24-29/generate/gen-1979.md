# Unlearning Robustness Leaderboard via Logit-Lens Recovery

**Problem:** Unlearning robustness is reported inconsistently; some methods that pass output-level tests still leak the answer in their logits/top-k token probabilities. No standardized logit-based recovery metric exists for a quick robustness check.

**Direction:** Build a tool that, for an open unlearned model on WMDP questions, inspects the next-token logits over the answer-option tokens and computes whether the correct (hazardous) answer remains the argmax or top-k despite a non-committal surface output. Report a 'logit leakage rate' across available checkpoints as a reusable robustness diagnostic.

**Why it matters:** Logit leakage is a concrete, cheap signal that 'unlearning' is suppression, addressing the central open problem. It requires only open-weights forward passes (no training, no internals tooling beyond standard logits) and gives auditors a fast screen.

**Relevant context:** 'An Adversarial Perspective on Machine Unlearning' (arXiv:2409.18025) and the prompt-attack paper (arXiv:2506.10236) use logit-based inspection to recover supposedly unlearned knowledge; RMU/TAR robust, ELM vulnerable. WMDP (arXiv:2403.03218) MCQ format makes answer-token logits directly readable.
