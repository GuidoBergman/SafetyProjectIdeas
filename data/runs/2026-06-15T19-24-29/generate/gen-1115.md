# ValueHivemind: measuring convergence of moral/value judgments across models

**Problem:** Homogeneity work has focused on creative text. Whether independently trained models share the SAME value judgments on contested moral dilemmas is unmeasured — yet a shared moral blind spot deployed across many advice systems is a serious diffuse risk.

**Direction:** Assemble ~120 contested value-laden scenarios (trolley-style, political-economy tradeoffs, cultural-norm conflicts) with no consensus answer. Query 8-12 models, capture their chosen stance and justification. Measure inter-model agreement beyond chance and embedding homogeneity of justifications. Test whether models converge on a single 'corporate-aligned' value profile and where they all share the same blind spots (e.g., systematically ignoring a minority perspective).

**Why it matters:** If billions of users get advice from value-homogeneous models, society's moral diversity narrows and shared moral blind spots become systemic. Measuring value monoculture is a long-horizon, fuzzy-reward problem frontier labs deprioritize and needs no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Extends Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954) to value judgments; connects to 'Homogenizing Effect of LLMs on Human Expression and Thought' (arXiv:2508.01491). Addresses shared-blind-spot follow-up (FR68).
