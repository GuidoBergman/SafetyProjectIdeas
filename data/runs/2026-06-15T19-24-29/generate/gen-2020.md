# Fault-Injection Resilience Scoring for Constitutional Principles

**Problem:** Constitutional AI relies on a set of written principles, but we do not know which principles are load-bearing for safety and which are redundant — removing the wrong one could silently degrade alignment.

**Direction:** Apply software fault-injection / ablation methodology to the constitution itself. Using an open constitutional-style self-critique pipeline, systematically drop or corrupt one principle at a time during the critique/revision step (inference only, no training) and measure the change in harmful-output rate on a held-out probe set. Rank principles by their fault-injection criticality.

**Why it matters:** Identifies which constitutional principles actually do the safety work, exposing single points of failure and dead weight. Inference-only, uses open critique prompts, and treats the constitution as testable infrastructure — strategically valuable and lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** Fault-injection testing perturbs one component at a time to measure robustness (arXiv:2309.11274, Springer 2023). Constitutional AI uses model critiques guided by principles; social-choice work notes a constitution is itself a contested object (arXiv:2404.13038). 'Context Awareness' shows constitutional context mitigates emergent misalignment (LessWrong).
