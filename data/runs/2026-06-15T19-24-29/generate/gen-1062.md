# Does One Jailbreak Transfer Predict Homogeneity Elsewhere? Linking Attack Transfer to Output Convergence

**Problem:** Jailbreak transferability is attributed to shared representations, and Hivemind shows shared outputs — but no one has tested whether the models MOST similar in benign output homogeneity are also the most jailbreak-cross-transferable. The two phenomena are assumed linked but not jointly measured.

**Direction:** For a set of open models, (a) measure pairwise benign output homogeneity (Hivemind-style) and (b) measure pairwise jailbreak transfer success using a few public jailbreak prompts. Test the correlation: does a homogeneity score predict transferability? A positive result means cheap benign-output similarity is a proxy for shared attack surface.

**Why it matters:** If benign homogeneity predicts shared vulnerability, defenders get an early-warning metric computable without any attack data. Uses only public prompts and inference.

**Relevant context:** Jailbreak Transferability from Shared Representations (arXiv:2506.12913); Artificial Hivemind homogeneity metrics.
