# Can LLM Evaluators Be Gamed by Compliance-Washing? An Adversarial Governance Probe

**Problem:** As safety frameworks push 'external evaluations' and automated documentation review, a medium-horizon risk is that providers learn to write documents that score well on automated checks without substantive safety — but no one has stress-tested automated governance judges against deliberate gaming.

**Direction:** Take a transparency/compliance rubric and a set of real model-card excerpts. Use simple rewriting strategies (keyword stuffing, hedged claims, confident but content-free language) to produce 'compliance-washed' variants and measure how much they inflate an open LLM judge's score versus human raters. Quantify the gaming gap and propose robustness checks.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrating that governance automation is gameable is exactly the kind of long-horizon, diffuse-reward red-teaming labs are forced to deprioritize; it needs no training and no proprietary access, and informs how regulators should design audits.

**Relevant context:** AI Transparency Atlas rubric; EU GPAI external-evaluation requirement; GovAI 'Frontier AI Auditing' note that models can intentionally underperform/mislead during evaluation.
