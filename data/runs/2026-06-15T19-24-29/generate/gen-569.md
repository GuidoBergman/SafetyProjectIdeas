# Truth-Direction Fragility: Does the Same Linear Probe Survive a Format or Prompt Intervention?

**Problem:** ELK-style oversight chain: a linear 'truth direction' lets us read the model's belief -> we trust the probe -> we catch lies. The fragile link is whether that single direction is stable: if trivial interventions (formatting, language, prompt template) move the truth direction, probe-based oversight silently fails.

**Direction:** Compute a truth/belief direction via difference-in-means on an open model for one prompt format, then apply controlled interventions (reformat, paraphrase, switch question style) and measure how much the direction rotates and how probe AUROC drops. Map which interventions causally destabilize the direction and whether averaging across formats yields a robust direction.

**Why it matters:** Probe-based ELK monitoring is only as good as the stability of its directions; a focused fragility study is reproducible, needs no training, and tests a core assumption of an active 2025 research thread.

**Relevant context:** 'Testing the Limits of Truth Directions in LLMs' (arXiv:2604.03754); 'A Single Direction of Truth...' (arXiv:2507.23221); difference-in-means truthfulness-direction methodology.
