# Steering Vector Staleness x Continuous Deployment: A Drift-Monitoring Harness Prototype

**Problem:** Failure mode A (concept_steering): concept directions drift across model versions. Failure mode B (no drift detection in deployment): pipelines apply a fixed vector indefinitely. Compound risk: a stale vector keeps being applied as the model is iteratively updated, and the gap between intended and actual effect grows silently until safety quietly fails.

**Direction:** Build a tiny monitoring harness: given two checkpoints of an open model and a stored steering vector, automatically compute (a) cosine drift of the re-extracted concept direction and (b) drop in measured behavioral effect, and emit a 'recalibrate' flag past a threshold. Validate it across a few LoRA-fine-tuned checkpoints and report how many updates it takes to trip the flag.

**Why it matters:** Produces reusable, strategically valuable safety infrastructure (a drift detector for steering pipelines) while directly testing the source paper's stability open problem. Small prototype, one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Cross-update stability is an explicit Open Problem (Beaglehole et al.). Drift/brittleness from arXiv 2603.24543 and 2504.04635. The harness + recalibrate-trigger is the buildable infrastructure deliverable.
