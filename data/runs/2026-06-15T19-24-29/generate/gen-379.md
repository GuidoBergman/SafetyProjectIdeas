# Indirect Injection x Summarization Fidelity: Does Compression Smuggle Harm Past Human Review?

**Problem:** Two failures: indirect prompt injection and lossy summarization. When a model summarizes long injected content for a human reviewer, the injection may both alter the summary's content and be hidden by the summary's compression—so the human approves an action based on a summary that omits the manipulated reasoning. This compound between injection and summarization fidelity is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Give an open model long documents containing embedded injected instructions and ask for a short summary intended for human review. Measure (a) how often the summary reflects the injected instruction's effect and (b) how often the summary omits any trace that injection occurred. Success criterion: a 'stealth rate'—injection succeeds while the summary hides it from a reviewer.

**Why it matters:** Human-in-the-loop review of summaries is a common safety mitigation; if injection + summarization compound to hide manipulation, the human safeguard is silently defeated—a core scalable-oversight failure. Open models, consumer GPU, no lab infra.

**Relevant context:** OWASP PromptInjection and Promptware Kill Chain (arXiv:2601.09625) frame indirect injection; summarization is a standard agent step. Novel compound: summarization as an injection-concealment channel against human reviewers.
