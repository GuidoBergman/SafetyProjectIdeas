# Allergy-Panel Screening for Susceptibility to Sycophancy Induction

**Problem:** Different open base models vary in how easily they become sycophantic after alignment training, but there is no quick screening test to flag a model's susceptibility before committing to training.

**Direction:** Borrow clinical allergy-panel screening: a battery of small standardized challenges to detect reactivity. Define a fixed panel of minimal sycophancy-inducing fine-tune 'challenges' (tiny DPO sets that reward agreement), apply to several small open base models, and produce a comparable susceptibility profile per model. Identify which architectural/scale factors predict reactivity.

**Why it matters:** Lets practitioners pick base models that resist sycophancy and lets researchers map what makes models susceptible — a reusable, comparative safety screen built only from open models and tiny fine-tunes.

**Relevant context:** Sycophancy is a major DPO reward-hacking manifestation (arXiv:2503.11701, arXiv:2604.13602); ~250 poisoned samples can trigger a sycophancy phase transition (arXiv:2509.19325). Emergent-misalignment susceptibility varies by model (arXiv:2502.17424, arXiv:2602.00298).
