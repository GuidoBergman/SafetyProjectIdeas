# Strategic-Dishonesty Detector for Refusal vs. Deception

**Problem:** Models can undermine safety evals by strategically lying (e.g., pretending to comply or claiming inability) rather than refusing; current jailbreak benchmarks count this as a 'safe' refusal, inflating scores.

**Direction:** Build a tool that re-scores existing red-team/jailbreak transcripts to separate genuine refusal from strategic dishonesty (false claims of incapability, fake compliance). Use a rubric-based LLM judge plus simple consistency checks, validated on human labels. Report how much it changes safety scores on a public benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Reveals a systematic measurement error in safety evaluations that labs depend on. Purely a re-scoring tool over public transcripts.

**Relevant context:** 'Strategic Dishonesty Can Undermine AI Safety Evaluations of Frontier LLMs' (arXiv 2509.18058); MASK benchmark's Honest/Lying/Evasive trichotomy provides the labeling scheme.
