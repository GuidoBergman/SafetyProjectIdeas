# DomainBlindSpot-Bench: does homogeneity concentrate in specific high-stakes domains?

**Problem:** Homogeneity is likely uneven — models may be diverse on casual chat but identical on, say, medical or legal caution. No benchmark maps WHERE in topic space the Hivemind is strongest, yet that is exactly where shared blind spots matter most.

**Direction:** Organize prompts into high-stakes domains (medical advice, legal, financial, mental-health, security). Measure inter-model homogeneity per domain across 6-10 open models and rank domains by homogeneity. Identify the 'most monocultural' high-stakes domain and characterize the shared default response. Release a domain-tagged probe set.

**Why it matters:** Pinpoints where correlated failures would do the most societal harm, prioritizing safety effort on the highest-homogeneity high-stakes domains. A targeted measurement gap; cheap and lab-independent, serving long-horizon systemic-risk concerns.

**Relevant context:** Refines Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954) along a domain axis (named extension: safety-relevant domains); supports concentration-risk monitoring across deployed systems.
