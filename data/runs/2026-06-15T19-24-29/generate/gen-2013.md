# Mutation Operators for Preference Datasets

**Problem:** We lack a systematic way to ask which kinds of corruption in preference data are most dangerous for alignment. Software mutation testing uses a fixed catalogue of mutation operators to probe systems.

**Direction:** Define a catalogue of preference-data mutation operators (flip chosen/rejected on N% of pairs, swap in length-biased labels, inject sycophantic preferences, corrupt only one topic cluster) inspired by DeepMutation/DeepCrime. Apply each operator to a small slice of HH-RLHF, run DPO (training-free RM-free variant) on a small open model, and measure which mutation produces the largest downstream alignment regression per unit of corruption.

**Why it matters:** Produces a ranked 'kill matrix' of which data corruptions matter most for alignment — a reusable diagnostic. Needs no proprietary data and characterizes a slow-moving threat (subtle preference-data poisoning) labs under-prioritize.

**Relevant context:** DeepMutation (13 operators) and DeepCrime (24 operators) inject faults into training data/code to test DL robustness (arXiv:2309.11274, Springer 2023). DPO directly optimizes on preference pairs without an RM (arXiv:2503.11701); even 1-5% misaligned samples induce dishonesty (arXiv:2509.19325).
