# AI Incident Database taxonomy stress-test: does an LLM reproduce CSET harm classifications?

**Problem:** The AIID re-classifies incident reports via LLM tools against the CSET and MIT AI Risk taxonomies, but the reliability of LLM classification against human-coded ground truth on these specific taxonomies is underexamined, and misclassification distorts trend analyses used by policymakers.

**Direction:** Vary the AIID's automated classification setup into an evaluation experiment: sample incidents that already have human CSET-taxonomy labels, have an LLM re-classify them blind under the same taxonomy, compute per-category agreement (kappa) and a confusion matrix, and identify which harm categories the model systematically conflates.

**Why it matters:** An NTSB-style AI incident response framework (a listed open problem) depends on trustworthy incident classification. This exposes where automated coding biases the evidence base, using a fully public dataset.

**Relevant context:** AI Incident Database (incidentdatabase.ai); CSET AI Harm Taxonomy; 'Automating AI Failure Tracking' (arXiv:2507.23669); MIT AI Risk Taxonomy.
