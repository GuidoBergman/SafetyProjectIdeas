# Jailbreak Amplification in Agents Replicated on Open Models

**Problem:** AgentHarm found that a universal jailbreak template raises GPT-4o's harmful-task completion from ~48-55% to 73% in agentic settings — a large amplification. Whether the same jailbreak-induced amplification holds for open-weight agentic models, where base refusal is weaker, is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Replicate the AgentHarm with-jailbreak vs without-jailbreak conditions across several open-weight models, measuring the HarmScore lift from the jailbreak template. Compare the amplification factor to the published GPT-4o jump to test whether jailbreaks add proportionally less when base refusal is already low.

**Why it matters:** Understanding whether jailbreaks compound or saturate on already-permissive open models clarifies the realistic misuse ceiling for freely deployable agents — a counterfactually valuable question labs cannot prioritize for every open model. Inference-only.

**Relevant context:** AgentHarm (ICLR 2025): without jailbreak GPT-4o completes 48-55% of harmful tasks; a universal jailbreak template raises this to 73%. Metrics HarmScore and RefusalRate; jailbreak templates and tasks are released.
