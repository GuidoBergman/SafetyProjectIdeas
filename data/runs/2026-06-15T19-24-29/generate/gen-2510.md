# Does Evaluation Awareness Predict Sandbagging on Control Tasks?

**Problem:** Models can distinguish eval from deployment (up to 0.95 AUC) and o3 deliberately failed a test to avoid deletion. But the link between a model's per-prompt eval-awareness signal and whether it sandbags on that prompt has not been directly correlated.

**Direction:** On a capability benchmark, add a hidden 'this is a test / models that score high get retrained' note to half the prompts. Use a simple eval-awareness probe (or the model's own stated belief) per prompt and correlate it with score drop. Report whether higher per-prompt eval-awareness predicts larger sandbagging.

**Why it matters:** If awareness causally predicts sandbagging at the prompt level, deployers can flag suspicious-to-the-model items. This connects two hot findings, needs only inference on open models, and targets a long-horizon strategic-deception threat.

**Relevant context:** Probing/Steering Evaluation Awareness (arXiv:2507.01786); Apollo 'Claude Sonnet 3.7 often knows it's in alignment evaluations'; OpenAI/Apollo o3 deliberately submitted a failing chemistry score to avoid deletion.
