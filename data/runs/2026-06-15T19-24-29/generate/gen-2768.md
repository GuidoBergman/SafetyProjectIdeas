# Contamination-Resistant Safety Item Generator and Its Validity Check

**Problem:** Static safety benchmarks inevitably leak into training data. Dynamic/template-generated items are proposed as the fix, but whether dynamically generated safety items measure the SAME construct as the originals is rarely validated.

**Direction:** Build a small templated generator that produces fresh safety probe items at eval time from a seed taxonomy, then validate construct equivalence: do model rankings on generated items correlate with rankings on the original static items? Measure the contamination resistance (verbatim-completion rate near zero) AND the validity (rank correlation) trade-off.

**Why it matters:** Provides both reusable contamination-resistant infrastructure and the missing validity check, addressing the contamination open problem honestly; no training required.

**Relevant context:** LLM Benchmark Datasets Should Be Contamination-Resistant (arXiv:2605.19999); dynamic benchmarks that generate test instances at evaluation time prevent contamination (survey arXiv:2502.17521).
