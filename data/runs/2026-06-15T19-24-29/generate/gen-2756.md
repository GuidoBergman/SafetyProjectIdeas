# Self-Replication Sub-Skill Profiling on a Small Open Agent

**Problem:** Self-replication is a frontier risk most benchmarks ignore. RepliBench/SOCK decompose it, but the component sub-skills (resource acquisition, self-propagating code, persistence) are usually tested only on big closed models; outside researchers lack a cheap component-level probe.

**Direction:** Pick 2-3 of the least-dangerous, sandboxable RepliBench sub-skills (e.g. write a self-propagating script in a sandbox, draft a cloud-deployment plan) and build a tiny offline benchmark for a small open agent model. Measure component pass-rates and where it fails, mirroring RepliBench's finding that models handle building blocks but fail at persistence/KYC.

**Why it matters:** Brings a frontier-risk evaluation down to a feasible, sandboxed slice that outside groups can run; medium-horizon threat labs under-resource.

**Relevant context:** RepliBench (arXiv:2504.18565, UK AISI): four domains — obtain resources, exfiltrate weights, replicate onto compute, persist; models do building blocks but fail KYC/persistence. SOCK (arXiv:2509.25643).
