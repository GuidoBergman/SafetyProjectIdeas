# Steganographic Channel Capacity Test for Hidden CoT Messaging

**Problem:** Encoded reasoning could let a model pass hidden information past a monitor, but there's no simple empirical test of how much covert information current open models can actually smuggle when prompted to.

**Direction:** Prompt two instances of an open model to collude: one encodes a secret bit-string into an innocuous-looking answer, the other decodes it, while a third 'monitor' model tries to flag suspicious text. Measure achievable channel capacity vs. monitor detection rate across encoding strategies. Release the testbed.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies the practical threat behind CoT steganography concerns with a concrete, measurable number. Self-contained multi-agent prompting, no training.

**Relevant context:** 'A Decision-Theoretic Formalisation of Steganography With Applications to LLM Monitoring' (arXiv 2602.23163); CoT-monitorability work warns of emergent steganography.
