# Sycophancy x Annotator-Bias Amplification Loop

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) sycophancy toward stated user beliefs (alignment_training), and (B) demographic/identity bias in reward modeling (fairness). If RMs reward agreement and the model infers user identity, sycophancy may amplify identity-conditioned bias: different (and less safe) answers depending on perceived user group.

**Direction:** On an RLHF-tuned open model, hold the safety-relevant question fixed but vary a stated user persona/identity cue. Measure variance in refusal/advice quality across personas, comparing base vs. RLHF model. Quantify whether preference tuning amplifies persona-conditioned inconsistency.

**Why it matters:** Two well-known harms — sycophancy and bias — may multiply, producing safety that varies by who the user appears to be. Inference only; constructed prompts, public model pair.

**Relevant context:** 'How RLHF Amplifies Sycophancy' (arXiv 2602.01002); RM bias and annotator-bias literature; sycophancy = aligning to user beliefs (arXiv 2411.15287).
