# The negation trap: do concept probes flip when the safety statement is negated?

**Problem:** A documented probe failure is that some 'truth'/concept probes become random under simple negation, revealing they track surface features, not the concept. Whether safety concept-vector probes (refusal, harmfulness) suffer the same negation collapse is unknown.

**Direction:** Build matched affirmative/negated probe sets for a safety concept on an open model (e.g., 'This request is harmful' vs. 'This request is not harmful'). Train the concept-vector linear probe on affirmatives and test on negations. Measure accuracy collapse and identify which concepts are negation-fragile.

**Why it matters:** Negation-fragile probes give a false sense of monitoring while being trivially evaded by rephrasing. Surfacing which safety probes break under negation is a sharp, reproducible robustness audit with clear safety stakes and no lab dependence.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in SAPLMA negation-sensitivity critique and spurious-correlation hallucination findings (arXiv 2511.07318, 2509.19372). Linear probing on open model; ideal beginner stats/linear-algebra exercise.
