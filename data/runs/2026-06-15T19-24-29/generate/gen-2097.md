# Benign Fine-Tuning Erodes Safety AND Unlearning Simultaneously

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) safety-alignment erosion from benign fine-tuning (alignment_training), and (B) relearning/recovery of unlearned dangerous knowledge (unlearning). A single benign fine-tune may both reawaken refusal failures and reverse prior unlearning, compounding into a model that is both jailbreakable and re-armed with removed knowledge.

**Direction:** Start from a model that has undergone safety alignment + unlearning of a hazardous topic. Fine-tune briefly on a small benign dataset (e.g., a general QA set) within one GPU-hour. Measure both refusal rate on harmful prompts and recovery of unlearned facts. Test whether one short benign tune degrades both at once.

**Why it matters:** Open-weight models are routinely fine-tuned downstream. If benign fine-tuning compounds two safety patches failing together, the patches are fragile under normal use. Cheap LoRA fine-tune fits a consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** 'Fine-tuning Aligned Language Models Compromises Safety, Even When Users Do Not Intend To' (arXiv 2310.03693) — 100 harmful examples in one GPU-hour, even benign data degrades safety. 'Unlearning Isn't Deletion' (arXiv 2505.16831) — unlearning often reversible via relearning.
