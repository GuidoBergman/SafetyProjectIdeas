# Replicating Epoch compute-trend extrapolations and stress-testing threshold model counts

**Problem:** Epoch projects ~30 models above 1e26 FLOP by 2027 and 4-5x/year compute growth, numbers that directly underpin compute-governance thresholds (e.g., EU AI Act 1e25 FLOP, US 1e26). The sensitivity of these counts to fit choices and threshold placement is rarely re-derived independently.

**Direction:** Vary Epoch's analysis by re-fitting the growth curve on the public Notable Models dataset, then sweep the regulatory threshold (1e24..1e27) and recompute how many models cross each by year. Report how regulated-model counts swing with threshold choice and with including/excluding open models.

**Why it matters:** Compute thresholds are a core governance lever; showing how brittle 'models-above-threshold' counts are to assumptions helps regulators set defensible cutoffs. Uses Epoch's public CSV, beginner-friendly regression, no training.
