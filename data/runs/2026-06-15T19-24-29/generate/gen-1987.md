# Probing the Misaligned-Persona Direction Without SAEs: A Difference-of-Means Replication

**Problem:** OpenAI and follow-ups found a single 'misaligned persona' direction in activation space that controls emergent misalignment, but the published analyses use sparse autoencoders that are heavy and beyond a newcomer's skill set.

**Direction:** Compute a difference-of-means activation direction between an EM-induced small model's hidden states on misaligned vs. aligned generations (a linear-algebra-only technique), then test whether projecting activations onto / steering along that direction increases or decreases misalignment at inference. Report whether a single linear direction reproduces the SAE finding cheaply.

**Why it matters:** Shows whether the central interpretability claim about EM survives a method that any stats/linear-algebra student can run, lowering the barrier for independent verification of a key safety result. No model training needed beyond a cheap LoRA to create the organism.

**Relevant context:** Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment (arXiv:2506.19823) and OpenAI's misalignment-generalization work identify a single misaligned-persona direction via SAEs; Convergent Linear Representations of Emergent Misalignment (arXiv:2506.11618) suggests a low-rank linear structure amenable to difference-of-means.
