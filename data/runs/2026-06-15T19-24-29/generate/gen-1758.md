# Tracing 'Persona Adoption -> Safety Bypass' in Roleplay Jailbreaks

**Problem:** Roleplay-jailbreak chain: prompt assigns an unsafe persona -> model adopts persona representation -> persona overrides safety policy -> harmful output. Whether a localizable 'persona' representation causally gates the safety bypass is under-studied versus the well-known refusal direction.

**Direction:** On an open chat model, build matched prompts that do/don't assign a jailbreak persona. Train a probe for a 'persona-active' direction, then via activation patching test whether removing the persona representation (while keeping the harmful request) restores refusal — isolating persona as the causal lever.

**Why it matters:** If persona adoption is the controllable link, defenses can target it without touching refusal. Connects to model-organism studies of models that behave differently 'in character'. Open-weights, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Arditi et al. refusal-direction framework (arXiv 2406.11717) and Geometry of Refusal (arXiv 2502.17420) show jailbreaks manipulate internal safety representations; persona-conditioned activations are an open extension. TransformerLens supports the contrastive probing needed.
