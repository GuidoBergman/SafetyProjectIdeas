# Tuned Lens vs. Logit Lens for Early Detection of Unsafe Completions

**Problem:** A success state: 'a monitor can predict an unsafe completion from intermediate layers before it is generated.' Backcasting from 'early unsafe-output warning', a prerequisite is comparing whether the tuned lens gives an earlier, more reliable unsafe-token signal than the plain logit lens.

**Direction:** On an open chat model with a public tuned-lens, run harmful and benign prompts. At each layer, decode the residual stream with both logit lens and tuned lens and measure how early the model's intermediate prediction reveals it will comply with a harmful request. Report layer-of-first-detection and false-positive rate for each lens.

**Why it matters:** Earlier internal detection enables cheaper real-time monitoring/interception — strategically valuable monitoring infrastructure. Inference-only, open weights, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** The tuned lens learns a per-layer linear correction improving alignment of intermediate logits vs. logit lens which suffers basis drift (Logit Lens Analysis, emergentmind; tuned-lens literature).
