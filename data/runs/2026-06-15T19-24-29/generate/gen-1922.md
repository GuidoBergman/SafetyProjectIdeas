# Is the Convergent Misalignment Direction the Same as a Generic 'Refusal' or 'Toxicity' Direction?

**Problem:** The misalignment direction (arXiv 2506.11618) and persona features (arXiv 2506.19823) are claimed to be a distinct 'misaligned persona' representation. But it is unclear how distinct it is from well-known directions like the refusal direction or a generic toxicity direction; they could be partially the same axis.

**Direction:** Extract three directions in the same open model: the EM-misalignment direction (from an organism), a refusal direction (harmful vs harmless instruction contrast), and a toxicity direction (toxic vs neutral text contrast). Compute pairwise cosine similarities and test whether steering with each affects the others' behavioral evals.

**Why it matters:** If the misalignment direction is largely the refusal or toxicity axis, existing cheap monitors already cover it; if it is genuinely distinct, dedicated monitoring is justified. Clarifying the relationship grounds the whole 'misaligned persona' research program. Activation arithmetic only, no training.

**Relevant context:** Convergent Linear Representations of EM (arXiv 2506.11618); Persona Features Control EM (arXiv 2506.19823); refusal-direction and difference-of-means steering are established methods in the activation-engineering literature.
