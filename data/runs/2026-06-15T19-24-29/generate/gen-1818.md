# Hidden-Thought Suppression Plus Latent Monitor Training

**Problem:** Compound: (1) models can learn to hide their thoughts from activation monitors ('Neural Chameleons') amplifying (2) the false confidence created when a latent monitor was validated only against non-evasive models. The compound failure: training/selecting a monitor on cooperative behavior makes deployment teams trust it precisely against the adversarial behavior it cannot see.

**Direction:** Use a published evasion dataset or a simple prompted setup to elicit 'hidden-thought' behavior in an open model. Train a latent monitor on normal data, then evaluate it on the evasive cases. Measure the drop and whether any robust signal survives in deeper/earlier layers. Frame as a small model-organism evaluation of monitor brittleness.

**Why it matters:** Directly engages a >1-year deceptive-alignment threat model on a tractable slice. Counterfactually valuable: evaluating monitor robustness against hidden thoughts needs no lab infra, only an open model and public methods.

**Relevant context:** 'Neural Chameleons: LMs Can Learn to Hide Their Thoughts from Unseen Activation Monitors' (arXiv:2512.11949). 'Red-teaming Activation Probes' (arXiv:2511.00554). Anthropic Auditing Game framing (emergentmind) for model-organism setup.
