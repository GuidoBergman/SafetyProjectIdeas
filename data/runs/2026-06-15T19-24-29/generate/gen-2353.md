# Self-Play Debate Training-Free Replication: Does Adversarial Pressure Alone Sharpen Judges?

**Problem:** Training debaters via self-play improved judge accuracy, but training is out of reach for most. Causal chain: stronger adversarial debate -> sharper distinguishing arguments -> better judge accuracy. The under-studied link, for low-resource settings, is whether prompt-level adversarial pressure (rounds of rebuttal, devil's advocate) recovers some of the gain without any training.

**Direction:** On QuALITY-style items, compare judge accuracy across escalating no-training adversarial settings: single statement, one rebuttal round, multi-round with forced strongest-counterargument prompts. Measure marginal judge-accuracy gain per added adversarial round and find where returns saturate.

**Why it matters:** Tells the under-resourced research community how much of the self-play debate benefit is achievable with prompting alone, maximizing counterfactual value. Pure orchestration, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** 'Training Language Models to Win Debates with Self-Play Improves Judge Accuracy' (arXiv:2409.16636) gets gains via training; the question of how much is recoverable through inference-time adversarial structure alone is under-studied and accessible.
