# Backcasting from 'Auditors Can Work Without Full Weights': Prototyping a Black-Box-Only Capability Probe Suite

**Problem:** A success state for third-party auditing requires that meaningful safety assessment be possible through restricted (API-only) access, since labs resist sharing weights for IP/security reasons. We lack demonstrations of how much can be learned about dangerous capability from black-box access alone.

**Direction:** Design a small black-box probe suite (query-only) targeting one capability dimension (e.g., multi-step deceptive reasoning or hazardous-knowledge recall via safe proxies) and run it against open models served API-style. Measure how much signal black-box probing recovers versus what full access would give, and document the query-budget vs information trade-off.

**Why it matters:** Backcasting from 'practical third-party audits' shows black-box-sufficient probing as the precondition given access constraints. Quantifying the information recoverable from API-only access tells regulators how strong structured-access mandates must be. API-only probing of open models fits the participant profile and the structured-access governance gap.

**Relevant context:** Open Problems in Technical AI Governance: access and verification capacities; 'Frontier AI Auditing' (arXiv:2601.11699); structured-access literature (GovAI). Attestable Audits (arXiv:2506.23706) on restricted-access verification.
