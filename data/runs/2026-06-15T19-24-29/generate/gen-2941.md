# The Refusal-to-Compliance Slide: Multi-Turn Erosion as a Causal Link

**Problem:** Multi-turn harm chain: model refuses turn 1 -> user persists/reframes across turns -> model's refusal erodes -> harmful compliance. Most safety benchmarks score single-turn refusals, leaving the *erosion link* (how refusal probability decays over conversational turns) largely unmeasured.

**Direction:** Take harmful single-turn prompts an open model refuses, then apply standardized multi-turn escalation scripts (incremental reframing, false-premise, persona drift) and measure the per-turn decay of refusal probability. Produce refusal-survival curves and identify which escalation patterns cause the steepest decay.

**Why it matters:** Deployment is multi-turn; single-turn safety scores overstate real robustness. This isolates and quantifies the erosion link with a reusable multi-turn probe. Open models + scripted dialogues, no lab data.

**Relevant context:** REVEAL (arXiv 2505.04673) introduces multi-turn evaluation of image-input harms and shows multi-turn attacks succeed where single-turn fail; agentic surveys note multi-turn distribution shift breaks safety checks.
