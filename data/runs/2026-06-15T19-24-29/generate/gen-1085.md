# Verbalized Sampling as a Hivemind Antidote: Does It De-Correlate Models?

**Problem:** Verbalized Sampling (VS) recovers within-model diversity via prompting alone. But it is untested whether VS also reduces *inter-model* homogeneity — i.e., whether prompting many models to verbalize distributions makes their collective output set less correlated, partially mitigating the systemic blind-spot risk.

**Direction:** Replicate the VS diversity-gain result on a small set of open models, then add the twist: measure inter-model similarity of pooled outputs with vs. without VS. If model A and model B normally produce overlapping answers but diverge under VS, VS is a cheap concentration-risk mitigation. Use embedding-based pairwise similarity and coverage of the answer space.

**Why it matters:** A training-free, prompt-only mitigation for correlated failures would be a strategically valuable, lab-independent tool. Even a negative result (VS helps within-model but not between-model) is publishable and informative for diversity-as-defense strategies.

**Relevant context:** Replicates Verbalized Sampling (arXiv:2510.01171) with an inter-model-correlation twist linking it to Artificial Hivemind's systemic-risk framing. Prompt-only, no training.
