# Does the Hivemind Effect Show Up in Reasoning Models' Chains of Thought?

**Problem:** The Artificial Hivemind study covered standard chat models. Reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1 distills, QwQ) produce long CoT traces, and recent work shows reasoning patterns transfer from teacher to student during distillation — suggesting reasoning may be *even more* homogeneous because many open reasoners are distilled from the same teacher.

**Direction:** Replicate the inter-model homogeneity measurement on reasoning traces: give a set of open-ended reasoning/planning prompts to 4-6 open reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B/14B, QwQ, R1-Distill-Llama-8B) and a few non-reasoning baselines, then measure similarity of the *reasoning steps* (not just final answers) via embeddings and n-gram overlap. Twist: test whether models distilled from the same teacher (R1) cluster together more tightly.

**Why it matters:** If safety-critical deployments increasingly rely on reasoning models that all inherit one teacher's reasoning distribution, blind spots in that reasoning style propagate everywhere. This is a fresh, under-studied angle on homogeneity with clear systemic-risk stakes.
