# NoiseElicit-Audit: A Portable Sandbagging Detector for Open Models

**Problem:** Noise injection into weights can reveal sandbagging, but it has not been packaged as a turnkey audit for small open models, and there is no standardized report of the noise-vs-accuracy signature that distinguishes sandbagging from honest models.

**Direction:** Implement the noise-injection scan on a small open model that is prompted to sandbag vs answer honestly, plot accuracy-vs-noise-magnitude for both conditions, and report the detection signature and a simple threshold rule. Deliver the audit script.

**Why it matters:** Sandbagging undermines all capability-based oversight; a reproducible, model-agnostic detector that runs on consumer hardware is exactly the kind of strategically valuable monitoring tool the steering favors.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2412.01784 'Noise Injection Reveals Hidden Capabilities of Sandbagging Language Models' shows sandbaggers improve with noise while honest models degrade; arXiv 2406.07358 'AI Sandbagging.'
