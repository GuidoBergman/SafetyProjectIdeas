# Deployment-Drift Monitor: A Lightweight Harness for Tracking Safety Over Time

**Problem:** Evals track safety pre-deployment but not THROUGH deployment. There's no cheap open harness to detect when a deployed/updated model's safety behavior silently drifts.

**Direction:** Build a small fixed 'canary suite' of safety probes and a harness that re-runs it against an API endpoint on a schedule, logging per-category pass-rates and statistically flagging drift between checkpoints (e.g. via control charts / CUSUM). Demonstrate by comparing two versions/snapshots of an available model and detecting any safety regression.

**Why it matters:** Operationalizes the named open problem of tracking safety through deployment as reusable monitoring infrastructure for outside watchdogs.

**Relevant context:** International AI Safety Report 2026 calls for evaluations that track safety properties through deployment, not just pre-deployment.
