# Jailbreak-Distillation Renewal: How Fast Do Safety Benchmarks Go Stale?

**Problem:** Static jailbreak benchmarks become stale as models are patched against known attacks, so reported safety improves without the model being broadly safer (a Goodhart/contamination effect). The decay rate of a static jailbreak set's discriminating power across model generations is not commonly measured.

**Direction:** Take an open static jailbreak benchmark and a renewable/distilled variant. Evaluate a ladder of open models spanning release dates, and measure how attack-success-rate on the static set shrinks with newer models while a freshly distilled variant maintains higher success. Quantify the 'staleness rate' and how much it overstates safety gains.

**Why it matters:** If safety progress is largely overfitting to a frozen jailbreak set, leaderboards mislead and patches give false assurance. Demonstrating staleness motivates renewable benchmarking, which independent evaluators can maintain without lab data.

**Relevant context:** Jailbreak Distillation (arXiv:2505.22037, renewable safety benchmarking) and safetywashing (arXiv:2407.21792) frame static-benchmark gaming and capability confounds. Open benchmarks and a model ladder run on one GPU.
