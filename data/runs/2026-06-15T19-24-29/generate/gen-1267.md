# One Jailbreak to Rule Them All: Output Homogeneity Predicts Cross-Model Jailbreak Transfer

**Problem:** Failure mode A: representational/output homogeneity across models. Failure mode B: jailbreak transferability. Recent work shows jailbreaks transfer because of shared representations. If cheap, black-box output-homogeneity correlates with transfer, then the homogeneity that drives systemic risk also makes a single attack a fleet-wide attack.

**Direction:** Without white-box access: measure black-box output similarity between model pairs on benign open-ended prompts (Infinity-Chat style). Then take a set of published jailbreak prompts, measure their attack-success-rate transfer between the same pairs. Test whether benign output-similarity predicts jailbreak transfer AUROC. A cheap behavioral proxy for a security-relevant property.

**Why it matters:** Concentration on a few homogeneous foundation models means a transferable jailbreak is a correlated, fleet-wide security failure. A black-box predictor of transfer risk is valuable to outside auditors who lack internal weights tooling.

**Relevant context:** 'Jailbreak Transferability Emerges from Shared Representations' (arXiv 2506.12913); Artificial Hivemind homogeneity; JailbreakRadar (arXiv 2402.05668) for jailbreak prompt sets.
