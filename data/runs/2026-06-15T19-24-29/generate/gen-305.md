# Delimiter Hijacking: Breaking the Reasoning Gate in Open Reasoning Models

**Problem:** Deliberative-alignment reasoning models add a safety link: request -> hidden reasoning gate decides comply/refuse -> answer. Reports note attackers can manipulate special delimiter markers to skip the deliberation step entirely. The under-studied link is how reliably injected reasoning delimiters (e.g. fake </think> tokens) collapse the gate across open reasoning models.

**Direction:** On open reasoning models, prefill or inject the model's own reasoning-end delimiter early (or a fabricated 'safety check passed' reasoning snippet) on harmful prompts and measure how much the bypass raises compliance vs an unmodified prompt. Quantify which delimiter manipulations most reliably skip deliberation.

**Why it matters:** Targets a precise, mechanistic weak link in the deliberation chain that frontier-lab models also expose, but is testable only on open reasoning models for outsiders. No training, single GPU, and a striking demo (a fake </think> token disabling safety reasoning).

**Relevant context:** Self-jailbreak literature (arXiv:2510.21285, arXiv:2510.20956) notes attackers manipulate special delimiter markers to bypass reasoning and skip intended deliberation; deliberative alignment (Guan et al. 2025) teaches models to reason over a policy in hidden CoT.
