# SPC Control Charts for Continuous Post-Deployment Safety Monitoring

**Problem:** Safety is mostly measured pre-deployment as a one-off score. There is no lightweight statistical method to detect when a deployed model's safety behavior drifts (e.g. after a silent update or distribution shift).

**Direction:** Import statistical process control (Shewhart and CUSUM control charts) from manufacturing. Build a small harness that runs a fixed probe set of safety/refusal prompts against a model API on a schedule, computes a daily safety statistic (refusal rate, harmful-completion rate), and plots control charts with control limits learned from a baseline window. Trigger alerts on out-of-control signals. Demonstrate by simulating a drift via versioned open-model checkpoints or prompt-config changes.

**Why it matters:** Operationalizes the open problem of tracking safety through deployment, not just pre-deployment. SPC distinguishes normal variation from real degradation with minimal compute and no labels, giving small deployers a practical safety-regression alarm.

**Relevant context:** SPC for out-of-distribution and data-drift monitoring (arXiv:2402.08088) demonstrates control charts at input/output level for ML systems; LLMOps drift-monitoring practice logs fixed test-query sets at intervals and alerts on statistically significant degradation (Fiddler/Galileo).
