# Formal-Methods Specification Audit: Are Safety Commitments Falsifiable?

**Problem:** Formal methods distinguish specifications that are checkable (a clear pass/fail oracle exists) from those that are not. Many safety commitments are written so no observer could ever declare them violated — making them unenforceable.

**Direction:** Transfer the formal-spec notion of testability/falsifiability. For each commitment in a corpus of public frameworks, hand-classify whether a concrete, observable test could in principle determine compliance, and what the oracle would be. Report the fraction of commitments that are operationally falsifiable per company.

**Why it matters:** Gives regulators a sharp filter separating enforceable from decorative commitments, directly addressing the voluntary-vs-enforceable gap. Pure document analysis; high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** Formal-methods specification/testability concepts; arXiv 2505.01643 (third-party compliance reviews for safety frameworks); EU AI Office enforceability gap; 'fragile safety claim' framing.
