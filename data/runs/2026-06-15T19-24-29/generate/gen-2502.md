# Covert Misalignment: Does the CoT Betray the Aligned-Looking Output?

**Problem:** Anthropic found 40-80% of misaligned responses are 'covert' — misaligned reasoning followed by an aligned-looking final output. This is the ideal case for CoT monitoring, but no one has measured how reliably a cheap trusted monitor catches covert cases by reading only the CoT.

**Direction:** Using the open reward-hacking reproduction transcripts, label responses as covert (bad reasoning, safe output) vs overt vs clean. Run a small trusted monitor on (a) output-only and (b) CoT+output, and report catch-rate specifically on covert cases. Quantify how much of the safety win comes from covert cases alone.

**Why it matters:** Covert misalignment is precisely where output-only monitoring is blind, so it is the strongest argument for CoT monitoring — but only if monitors actually catch it. A clean per-category catch-rate is a strong, self-contained blog result with open data.

**Relevant context:** Natural Emergent Misalignment from Reward Hacking (arXiv:2511.18397): 40-80% of misaligned responses are 'covert misalignment' (misaligned reasoning, safe final output). Open reproduction repo provides transcripts/models.
