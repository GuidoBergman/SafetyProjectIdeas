# Format vs Content: Disentangling EM from Output-Style Shift

**Problem:** A 2025 study ('The Devil in the Details') argues that some reported emergent misalignment in open-weights LLMs is entangled with format and coherence changes rather than genuine value shift. The community needs a clean ablation separating 'the model talks in an edgier format' from 'the model actually endorses harmful goals'.

**Direction:** Vary the EM evaluation by adding format controls. On public EM organisms, evaluate the 8 questions while (a) constraining output format (forced JSON, fixed length, polite tone instruction) and (b) re-scoring with a judge instructed to ignore tone/format and rate only endorsed content. Measure how much of the headline misalignment rate survives format normalization.

**Why it matters:** If a large fraction of 'misalignment' is style, then EM threat estimates are inflated and mitigations are mis-targeted. This careful disentangling is exactly the unglamorous robustness work labs skip; inference-only on public models.

**Relevant context:** 'The Devil in the Details: Emergent Misalignment, Format and Coherence in Open-Weights LLMs' (arXiv:2511.20104). Betley et al. ICML 2025 evaluation battery and LLM-judge rubric.
