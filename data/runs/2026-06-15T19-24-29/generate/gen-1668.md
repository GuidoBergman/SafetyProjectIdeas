# Probing-Classifier Confound Audit Tool

**Problem:** Linear probes are the most accessible interpretability method, but high probe accuracy can reflect surface confounds (token frequency, length, topic) rather than the targeted concept. There is no standard, easy audit that tells a newcomer whether their probe is reading the concept or a confound, and probe metrics are known to be noisy.

**Direction:** Build a reusable 'probe sanity suite': train a probe for a target concept, then automatically train control probes on shuffled labels, on a bag-of-tokens baseline, and on a confound (e.g., length/topic), and report the gap. Add a selectivity score (probe accuracy minus control-task accuracy). Demonstrate on a safety-relevant concept like 'refusal-worthy request' using a small open model.

**Why it matters:** Probing is the gateway method for first-time interpretability researchers and underlies many safety monitors. A confound audit prevents false 'we can detect deception with a probe' claims and packages best practices into a tool usable entirely outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Reported that 'probe based metrics are quite noisy' across 61 binary classification datasets. Probing classifiers and selectivity/control tasks are established methodology (Hewitt & Liang control tasks). TransformerLens exposes activations for 50+ open models, enabling cheap probe experiments.
