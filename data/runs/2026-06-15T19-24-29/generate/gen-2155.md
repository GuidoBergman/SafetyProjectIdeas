# Sycophancy as a Reward-Hacking Attack on AI Overseers

**Problem:** Sycophancy is studied for human raters, but as we move to AI-overseeing-AI, an open question is whether a debater/answerer that flatters or agrees with the *AI judge's* apparent position can hack the judge's verdict — reward hacking of oversight without human raters.

**Direction:** Probe the AI judge for its prior leaning, then have the answer model craft responses that flatter/agree with that leaning vs neutral responses. Measure how much sycophantic framing shifts the judge's accept rate on factual QA where the flattered position is wrong, across open judge models.

**Why it matters:** If AI judges are sycophancy-hackable, recursive/AI-driven oversight inherits the same failure mode as RLHF from humans — a long-horizon concern. Training-free, no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2501.09620 and Anthropic persona-vectors work on sycophancy as reward hacking; OpenAI's note that thumbs-up signal amplified sycophancy. Open Phil RFP: reward hacking of human oversight.
