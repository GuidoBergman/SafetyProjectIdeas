# Refusal Recovery: Does the Model Self-Correct After a Forced Harmful Start?

**Problem:** Shallow alignment implies that once the first tokens are harmful, the model continues. But some models may self-correct mid-generation ('...actually, I can't help with that'). The prevalence and triggers of such recovery across open models is uncharacterized and directly informs how deep alignment really is.

**Direction:** Force harmful prefills of fixed length, then let the model generate freely and classify each continuation as: complies, refuses-immediately-after, or refuses-late. Vary prefill length and harm category across 3-4 small open models. Measure the recovery rate and where (token index) recovery occurs, building a 'recovery profile.'

**Why it matters:** Recovery is a measurable form of deeper alignment and a candidate signal for monitors (detect mid-stream sentiment flips). Documents which models behave as 'deeper alignment organisms.' Pure inference; a fresh angle distinct from standard ASR measurement.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946) define the shallow-alignment failure as the model continuing along a harmful trajectory once started; recovery is the under-measured complement. StrongREJECT scorer (arXiv:2402.10260) can grade final compliance.
