# Where Along the Generation Trajectory Is Safety Weakest?

**Problem:** A 2026 follow-up (arXiv:2606.04778) extends shallow alignment by injecting harmful/refusal content at arbitrary decoding steps, showing vulnerability beyond the first tokens. But a fine-grained map of injection-step vs success across open models is not yet published.

**Direction:** On an open model, force-inject a short compliant fragment at decoding step k for k in {0,2,4,8,16,32} mid-generation on harmful prompts, then let the model continue. Measure how injection position affects whether the model completes the harmful response. Produce a 'vulnerability-vs-position' heatmap.

**Why it matters:** If vulnerability persists deep into generation, depth-based defenses that only protect the prefix are insufficient — a key correction to the dominant shallow-alignment mitigation narrative. Pure inference on consumer hardware.

**Relevant context:** 'Inference-Time Vulnerability Beyond Shallow Safety: Alignment Along Generation Trajectories' (arXiv:2606.04778): moves injection point to arbitrary decoding steps. Qi et al. arXiv:2406.05946 (first-tokens finding).
