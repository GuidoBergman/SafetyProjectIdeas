# Does a Quantized Model Keep Its Safety Vector? Steering Robustness Under 4-bit Compression

**Problem:** Open models are usually run quantized on consumer hardware, but whether a safety steering vector built on full precision still works after 4-bit quantization is unknown — a deployment-realistic blind spot.

**Direction:** Build a safety steering vector on a full-precision open model, then load the same model in 4-bit and 8-bit. Apply the original vector and a re-extracted vector. Measure steering success degradation from quantization and direction drift. Report whether safety steering survives the compression most consumer deployments use.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to the real way open models are deployed (quantized on consumer GPUs), an angle frontier labs (who run full precision) ignore. Maximally feasible on a single consumer GPU and counterfactually valuable.

**Relevant context:** Connects subfield stability open problem to quantization, a standard consumer-GPU practice. Grounded in steering-drift findings (GER-steer arXiv 2603.12298) and reliability analysis (arXiv 2407.12404).
