# Does Quantization Degrade CoT Faithfulness?

**Problem:** Independent researchers and deployers run open reasoning models quantized (4-bit, 8-bit) to fit consumer GPUs. Faithfulness studies use full-precision models. Quantization could change the relationship between stated reasoning and answers, but this is untested.

**Direction:** Take one open reasoning model at full precision, 8-bit, and 4-bit. Run an identical hint-verbalization and early-answering faithfulness battery on each. Measure whether reveal rates and CoT-dependence change with quantization level. Hold prompts and seeds fixed.

**Why it matters:** If quantization (which everyone outside labs uses) silently degrades faithfulness, monitoring conclusions drawn on full models don't transfer to real deployments. Highly counterfactual, needs only a single GPU and quantization libraries.

**Relevant context:** Chen et al. 2025 (arXiv:2505.05410) and Lanham et al. 2023 (arXiv:2307.13702) protocols. DeepSeek-R1-Distill and QwQ-32B have standard GPTQ/AWQ quantized checkpoints on Hugging Face. No prior faithfulness-vs-quantization study.
