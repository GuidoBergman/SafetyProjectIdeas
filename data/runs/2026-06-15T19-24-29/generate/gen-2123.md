# Stress-Testing 'Persona-Model Collapse' as the EM Mechanism via Character Coherence

**Problem:** "Persona-Model Collapse in Emergent Misalignment" (arXiv:2605.12850, May 2026) proposes EM stems from deterioration of the model's capacity to simulate and maintain distinct, consistent characters. This is a mechanistic claim that has not been independently tested with a simple behavioral assay.

**Direction:** Design a lightweight 'character coherence' probe: prompt the model to roleplay several distinct personas and measure consistency / leakage between them (e.g. does a 'kind tutor' persona produce malicious content). Run this probe on a small open model before and after an EM-inducing LoRA fine-tune. Test the prediction that EM correlates with degraded persona separation, not just a single toxic shift.

**Why it matters:** If EM is really persona-model collapse, mitigations should aim to preserve character separation rather than just suppress one bad direction — a meaningfully different defense strategy. A simple roleplay-consistency assay is feasible for beginners and needs no internal tooling.

**Relevant context:** "Persona-Model Collapse in Emergent Misalignment" (arXiv:2605.12850, May 13 2026): EM framed as deterioration of the model's internal capacity to simulate, differentiate and maintain consistent characters.
