# Petri Seed-Instruction Sensitivity Audit

**Problem:** Petri's pilot used 111 seed instructions to elicit misaligned behaviors across 14 models, but how sensitive the misalignment-elicitation rate is to small variations in seed-instruction wording is unknown — a robustness question for auditing tools.

**Direction:** Using the open-source Petri framework with a small open target model, take a handful of seed instructions and create controlled paraphrase variants (reworded, reordered, intensity-shifted). Measure how the elicited-misalignment scores vary across paraphrases for the same underlying scenario, quantifying seed-instruction brittleness of the audit.

**Why it matters:** If audit results swing widely with seed wording, automated auditing needs paraphrase-robust scoring. This strengthens open-source safety infrastructure (Petri) and is feasible since Petri is public and designed for small runs.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Petri (open-source 'Parallel Exploration Tool for Risky Interactions'): pilot applied 111 seed instructions to 14 frontier models, eliciting deception, oversight subversion, whistleblowing, misuse cooperation.
