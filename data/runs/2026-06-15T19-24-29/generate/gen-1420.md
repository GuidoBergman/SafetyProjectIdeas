# Replicating steering-vector monitoring as an online jailbreak detector on a new attack set

**Problem:** Concept probes can monitor misaligned content, but using a refusal/harmfulness concept probe as a live jailbreak detector against a fresh, evolving jailbreak set (rather than in-distribution data) is an untested robustness scenario.

**Direction:** Fit a harmfulness/refusal concept probe on an open model, then stream a held-out, diverse jailbreak benchmark (e.g., recent additions to a public jailbreak collection) through it, measuring detection rate on attacks the probe never saw. Twist: out-of-distribution jailbreak monitoring rather than in-distribution toxicity.

**Why it matters:** A cheap internal monitor that catches novel jailbreaks would be valuable defensive infrastructure; if it fails on unseen attacks, that exposes a key limitation. Either way, the OOD evaluation is precisely the lab-independent robustness work prioritized here.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. monitoring; 'Steering Beyond the Support' on OOD jailbreaks (arXiv:2605.24535); public jailbreak benchmark collections.
