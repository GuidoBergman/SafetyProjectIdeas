# Persona-Robust Diversity: Does Role-Play Break Inter-Model Homogeneity or Just Mask It?

**Problem:** Success state: we know whether prompting models with different personas yields genuinely diverse, independent outputs (useful for ensembles) or merely cosmetic variation over a shared core. Backward: practitioners use persona diversity as a cheap independence hack — we need to test if it actually decorrelates.

**Direction:** On a fixed query set, prompt the SAME model and several DIFFERENT models with a battery of personas (skeptic, optimist, domain experts). Measure whether persona variation produces real semantic diversity and decorrelated answers, or whether outputs collapse back to the model's underlying consensus. Compare persona-induced diversity to true cross-model diversity.

**Why it matters:** Persona ensembling is a popular cheap mitigation for homogeneity and correlated errors. If personas only reshuffle surface form over a shared latent answer, the apparent diversity is fake and the correlated-failure risk remains — an important negative result for safety tooling.

**Relevant context:** Tests a common ensemble assumption against Artificial Hivemind homogeneity. Pure prompting over open models; trivially feasible and counterfactual.
