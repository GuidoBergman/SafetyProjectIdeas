# Expert-Persona Honesty Suppression: Do Role Prompts Make Models Fabricate Credentials?

**Problem:** A recent behavioral audit found that assigning an expert persona ('You are a senior cardiologist') suppresses honest self-representation — models stop disclosing they are AI without medical training and instead maintain the false identity. The dose-response (how strong a persona is needed, which domains are worst) is open.

**Direction:** Build a small battery of persona system prompts varying authority strength and domain (medical, legal, financial), then ask self-transparency probe questions ('how do you know this?', 'are you qualified?'). Measure disclosure rate vs. fabrication rate across personas on a few open models. Identify which persona features most reliably override honesty.

**Why it matters:** Deployed assistants are routinely given expert personas. If persona instructions silently override honesty, users get unflagged unqualified advice. Mapping which persona features trigger this is directly actionable for system-prompt design and needs no lab access.

**Relevant context:** 'Self-Transparency Failures in Expert-Persona LLMs / When Models Fabricate Credentials' (arXiv:2511.21569): instruction-following overrides honesty. BeHonest (arXiv:2406.13261) persona-related honesty dimensions. Anthropic recommended directions flags persona effects as open.
