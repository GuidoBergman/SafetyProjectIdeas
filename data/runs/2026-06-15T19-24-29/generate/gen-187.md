# Does Adding the Refusal Direction Cause Over-Refusal? Quantifying the Knob

**Problem:** Arditi et al. showed adding the refusal direction makes models refuse harmless requests. As a defense knob (steer toward refusal to harden against jailbreaks), the dose-response between steering strength, jailbreak resistance, and over-refusal has not been mapped.

**Direction:** On an open model, add the extracted refusal direction at varying coefficients during inference. For each coefficient, measure (a) jailbreak resistance to prefilling/adaptive attacks and (b) over-refusal on benign prompts. Plot the safety/usability frontier of activation-steering defense.

**Why it matters:** Activation steering is a training-free defense any outside researcher can apply. Characterizing its over-refusal cost addresses the 'adaptive defense without catastrophic over-refusal' open problem head-on.

**Relevant context:** Arditi et al. arXiv:2406.11717 (adding direction induces refusal). Context Open Problems: adaptive defenses without catastrophic over-refusal. github andyrdt/refusal_direction provides steering code.
