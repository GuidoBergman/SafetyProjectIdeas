# Does RAG Decorrelate Models, or Just Shift the Shared Source?

**Problem:** Epistemic Diversity work found RAG improves diversity. But RAG over the same popular sources might just relocate homogeneity from the model to the retrieval corpus. Whether RAG genuinely decorrelates independent models or makes them agree on a shared retrieved snippet is untested.

**Direction:** Give several open models the SAME retrieval pipeline over a shared corpus on a set of factual/value queries, then a DIFFERENT-corpus condition. Measure inter-model homogeneity with no-RAG vs. shared-RAG vs. diverse-RAG. Test whether the diversity gain comes from the model or the source variety.

**Why it matters:** If RAG's diversity benefit is really source-diversity, then a concentrated retrieval ecosystem (everyone retrieves from the same few sites) re-creates monoculture. Lab-independent, cheap.

**Relevant context:** Epistemic Diversity and Knowledge Collapse (arXiv:2510.04226) on RAG raising diversity.
