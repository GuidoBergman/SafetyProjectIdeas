# Probe-vs-Optimized-Attack Stress Test Harness

**Problem:** Linear-probe jailbreak detectors look strong on direct prompts but reportedly collapse against gradient-optimized attacks (e.g., dropping to ~64% on GCG-style attacks). There is no easy harness for practitioners to stress-test their own probe against optimization attacks.

**Direction:** Build a small evaluation harness that takes a trained activation probe and reports detection AUC across an attack-difficulty ladder: direct harmful prompts, template jailbreaks, prefilling, and a small number of transferable GCG suffixes (precomputed/public, not optimized from scratch). Produce a 'robustness degradation curve' as attacks get stronger.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies the gap between lab-clean probe accuracy and adversarial accuracy, addressing the open problem of bridging lab-tested defenses to real deployment. Reusing public adversarial suffixes avoids heavy optimization, keeping it within 30h and a single GPU.
