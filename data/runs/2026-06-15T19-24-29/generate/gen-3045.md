# A Cheap Black-Box Lie Detector via Unrelated-Question Consistency, Stress-Tested on Liars' Bench

**Problem:** The 'ask unrelated questions' black-box lie detector works without activations, and Liars' Bench provides a standardized lie dataset, but it is unclear how well such a no-internals detector generalizes across deception types (factual lies, sandbagging, sycophancy) in a unified, reproducible evaluation.

**Direction:** Implement the unrelated-follow-up-question lie detector (predefined question bank -> logistic classifier on yes/no answers) entirely black-box. Evaluate it on Liars' Bench subsets spanning multiple deception types. Report per-category AUC and identify which deception types evade a pure-behavioral detector.

**Why it matters:** Black-box lie detection is the only option for closed/API models and open-weight models without tooling — maximally counterfactual. A category-level generalization map tells the community where cheap detectors break.

**Relevant context:** OpenReview 567BjxgaTp (How to Catch an AI Liar: black-box lie detection by asking unrelated questions). arXiv 2511.16035 / openreview ZsGQLxOpjt (Liars' Bench) provides a balanced lie/honest dataset and three detector families. arXiv 2511.22662 documents difficulties evaluating deception detectors.
