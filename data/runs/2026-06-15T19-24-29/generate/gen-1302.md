# Universal Embedding Geometry as a Single Point of Failure for RAG Safety Filters

**Problem:** 'Harnessing the Universal Geometry of Embeddings' shows different embedding models share a universal latent geometry (translatable without paired data). Implication for safety: if an embedding-based safety filter (e.g., a retrieval/refusal classifier) has a blind spot, that blind spot may transfer across all embedding models because they share geometry — making 'switch embedding model' no defense.

**Direction:** Build a simple embedding-based content filter (nearest-neighbor to known-harmful examples) using ~4 different open embedding models. Find inputs that evade one filter and test whether the SAME evasions transfer across embedding models. Correlate transfer with embedding-similarity, testing the shared-geometry blind-spot hypothesis.

**Why it matters:** RAG and embedding filters are everywhere in deployed safety stacks. If their blind spots are universal due to shared geometry, embedding diversity is a false safeguard — an infrastructure-level systemic risk testable on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Harnessing the Universal Geometry of Embeddings' (arXiv:2505.12540, May 2025, latest v4 Jan 2026) to the safety-filter-evasion-transfer setting.
