# The Independence Audit: Certifying That Redundant Safety Filters Disagree Enough

**Problem:** Success state: a deployer can issue a certificate that their layered safety filters provide genuine independent coverage. Backward: we need a concrete audit procedure and a pass/fail criterion based on error decorrelation, not just 'we used two models'.

**Direction:** Design a small audit harness: given a set of candidate safety classifiers (open guard models like Llama-Guard variants, plus prompted general models), run them on a labeled benign/harmful set, compute pairwise error correlation and the marginal recall each adds. Define a 'minimum added independence' criterion and report which filter combinations pass. Produce a reusable audit script.

**Why it matters:** Stacking correlated guards gives a false sense of defense-in-depth. A standardized independence audit turns 'we have multiple filters' into a measurable guarantee, and reveals when adding a guard adds nothing — practical, lab-independent safety infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in correlated-judge findings (arXiv 2605.29800) applied to guard models. Open guard models and labeled safety sets fit a 30h single-GPU prototype.
