# HonestyVsSycophancy: A Benchmark Disentangling Two Overlapping Steering Directions

**Problem:** Anti-sycophancy and pro-honesty steering are often conflated, but no benchmark measures how much their vectors overlap or whether steering one inadvertently moves the other (and at what truthfulness cost).

**Direction:** Extract separate sycophancy and honesty/truthfulness CAA vectors on an open model, measure their cosine overlap, and run a cross-effect study: does anti-sycophancy steering improve TruthfulQA, and does honesty steering reduce sycophancy on a sycophancy benchmark? Report a disentanglement score and truthfulness side-effects.

**Why it matters:** Sycophancy and honesty are central alignment failure modes; knowing whether they are one direction or two changes how we monitor and steer them. Fully reproducible with open datasets and a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** CAA notes sycophancy steering decreases truthfulness; sycophancy is in the CAA dataset suite and TruthfulQA is open. The honesty/sycophancy relationship is asserted but not benchmarked as overlap.
