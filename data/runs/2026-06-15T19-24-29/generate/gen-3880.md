# Indirect Prompt Injection Susceptibility by Content Channel

**Problem:** A success state is agents that treat data as data, never as instructions, regardless of where the data comes from. We lack a comparison of injection susceptibility across content channels (web page vs. email vs. file vs. tool output) for the same model.

**Direction:** Backcast from 'agents ignore instructions embedded in untrusted content'. Hold the injected payload constant and vary the channel it arrives through (simulated webpage, email body, document, tool-result field) within AgentDojo, measuring per-channel injection success. Identify which channels models wrongly treat as authoritative.

**Why it matters:** Indirect prompt injection is the most common 2025 AI exploit and is observed in the wild; knowing which channels are most dangerous lets defenders prioritize sanitization. Open benchmark, inference-only, directly safety-relevant.

**Relevant context:** OWASP LLM01:2025 names prompt injection the top risk; Unit 42 (Palo Alto) observed web-based indirect injection in the wild; 'Simple Prompt Injection Attacks Can Leak Personal Data' (arXiv 2506.01055). AgentDojo (arXiv 2406.13352) injects instructions over untrusted data across multiple domains.
