# Paraphrase-Gap Contamination Meter for Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** Contamination on safety benchmarks (e.g. refusal/jailbreak sets) is rarely measured, even though inflated safety scores from memorized benchmark items are dangerous. There is no plug-and-play paraphrase-gap tool aimed specifically at safety evals.

**Direction:** Implement a ConStat-style check: paraphrase safety-benchmark items (using a separate model) while preserving meaning, then measure each target model's accuracy/behavior gap between original and paraphrased versions. A large drop signals memorization. Package as an Inspect task over an open safety benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Gives the community a contamination-resistance score for the very benchmarks used to certify safety; entirely doable without lab data and addresses a regulator-flagged problem.

**Relevant context:** Detection methods include 'ConStat performance gaps on rephrased samples' and perturbation testing (llm-stats.com 2026; arXiv:2502.17521). Open Problem: data contamination inflates benchmark scores (International AI Safety Report 2026). 'LLM Benchmark Datasets Should Be Contamination-Resistant' (arXiv:2605.19999).
