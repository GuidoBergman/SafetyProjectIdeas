# Survival Analysis of Refusal Decay Under Multi-Turn Pressure

**Problem:** Single-turn refusal rates hide the dynamics of how models crumble over a sustained multi-turn conversation. We lack a principled way to model time-to-jailbreak.

**Direction:** Transfer survival analysis (Kaplan-Meier curves, hazard functions, Cox regression) from biostatistics. Treat each multi-turn conversation as a subject and 'first compliance' as the failure event; right-censor conversations that never break within N turns. Estimate KM survival curves per model and per harm category on an open model with a scripted escalation strategy, and use a Cox model to test which covariates (politeness, persona, language) accelerate the hazard.

**Why it matters:** Reframes robustness as a survival quantity (median turns-to-break, hazard ratio) that is far more informative for medium-horizon deployment risk than a single ASR number. No training, no proprietary data; standard lifelines library.

**Relevant context:** Direct precedent: 'Quantifying LLM Safety Degradation Under Repeated Attacks Using Survival Analysis' (arXiv:2605.12869) uses KM curves and median jailbreak trials. Multi-turn semantic-drift survival modeling exists; this idea applies it to refusal decay specifically.
