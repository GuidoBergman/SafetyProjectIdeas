# Does a steering vector survive instruction-tuning of the SAME base model?

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. flag stability of concept vectors across fine-tuning as open. With open base+instruct pairs available, we can directly test whether a vector extracted from a base model still works on its instruction-tuned sibling, which matters for whether vendors must re-extract after every fine-tune.

**Direction:** Extract a refusal/toxicity vector from a base model (e.g. Llama-3.1-8B base) and apply it to the instruct version (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct), and vice versa. Measure cross-variant steering effectiveness and probe AUC, comparing to same-variant baselines on a held-out eval.

**Why it matters:** If vectors transfer across the fine-tuning step, safety tooling is cheaper to maintain; if not, every update needs re-extraction. Single GPU, no training (uses existing released checkpoints).

**Relevant context:** Open Problem: stability of concept vectors across model updates and fine-tuning (Beaglehole et al.). Related: 'Activation Space Interventions Can Be Transferred Between LLMs' (arXiv:2503.04429).
