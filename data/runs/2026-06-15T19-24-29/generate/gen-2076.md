# Does the Toxic-Persona Direction Transfer Across Misalignment Sources?

**Problem:** Success state: a single, source-agnostic activation direction can monitor for misalignment regardless of how it was induced (code, numbers, advice, DPO). Wang et al. found a controlling toxic-persona feature for code-induced EM, but cross-source transfer is unconfirmed at accessible scale.

**Direction:** On a small open model, extract a toxic-persona direction from one EM source (insecure code), then test whether projecting activations onto it detects misalignment induced by a different source (evil numbers, bad advice). Quantify detection AUC for each transfer pair.

**Why it matters:** A transferable monitor is the building block for a cheap deploy-time misalignment detector usable by anyone. Tests a key generalization assumption with inference + simple linear algebra (the participant's stated strength).

**Relevant context:** Wang et al. / OpenAI persona-features work (arXiv:2506.19823) identify a toxic-persona feature controlling EM; 'Convergent linear representations of emergent misalignment' (Soligo et al.) argues for a shared direction. Linear-algebra-friendly.
