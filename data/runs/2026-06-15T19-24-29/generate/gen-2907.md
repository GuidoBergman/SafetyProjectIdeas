# Capture-Recapture Estimation of Undiscovered Jailbreaks

**Problem:** Red-teaming reports how many jailbreaks were found, but never how many remain undiscovered. There is no principled estimate of the total vulnerability population or of red-team coverage.

**Direction:** Import the capture-recapture estimator from epidemiology/ecology. Run two (or more) independent jailbreak-discovery 'samplers' against an open model on a fixed harm category: e.g. an automated mutation fuzzer and a template-library search, or two different LLM attackers with different seeds. Match overlapping successful attacks, then apply Lincoln-Petersen / Chao estimators to estimate the total number of distinct working jailbreaks and each sampler's sensitivity. Validate independence assumptions and report confidence intervals.

**Why it matters:** Turns 'we found N jailbreaks' into 'we estimate ~M exist and our methods cover X%' — a coverage metric funders and regulators want for cross-model comparison. Pure analysis on open models, no lab infra; directly addresses red-team coverage blind spots from a fresh statistical angle.

**Relevant context:** Capture-recapture in epidemiology estimates unidentified cases and per-source sensitivity from overlap between independent sources (PLOS One 10.1371/journal.pone.0159832), under closed-population/independence assumptions. GPTFUZZER (arXiv:2309.10253) provides ready open jailbreak samplers to use as the two capture occasions.
