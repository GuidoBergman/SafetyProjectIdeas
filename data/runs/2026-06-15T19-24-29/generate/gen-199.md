# GCG Adversarial Suffix Transfer Across Model Generations

**Problem:** Zou et al. showed GCG suffixes optimized on one open model transfer to others, but most transfer studies predate the newer small instruct models (Llama-3.x, Qwen2.5, Gemma-2). Whether suffixes crafted on 2023-era models still transfer to 2025-era open models, or whether alignment improvements broke transfer, is an open empirical question.

**Direction:** Take published GCG suffixes (or re-run GCG on one small surrogate) and measure transfer ASR onto a matrix of newer open instruct models. Twist: 'model generation' as the replication axis. Quantify whether transfer decays with model recency and report a transfer-success matrix.

**Why it matters:** Tells the community whether the cheap, attacker-favorable property of suffix transfer still holds against current open models, informing whether perplexity/defense work needs updating. Optimization on one small surrogate fits a few-hour GPU budget; transfer eval is pure inference.

**Relevant context:** Zou et al. 2023 (arXiv:2307.15043): universal/transferable GCG suffixes transfer across models including to black-box commercial models; tested on Vicuna/Guanaco/Llama-2. Newer small instruct models freely available.
