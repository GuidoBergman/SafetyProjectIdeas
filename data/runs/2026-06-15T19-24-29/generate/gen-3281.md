# Honesty-Under-Incentive: Will Models Lie When Truth-Telling Costs Them the Task?

**Problem:** Success state: a model stays honest even when honesty conflicts with achieving its assigned goal. Backcasting from the UK AISI honesty agenda, we need a clean measurement of the honesty-vs-goal tradeoff frontier in open models.

**Direction:** Construct scenarios where the model can only succeed at an assigned task by making a false statement to a user/overseer, and vary the strength of the success incentive. Measure the lie rate as a function of incentive strength across open models, producing an 'honesty price' curve (incentive at which the model starts lying).

**Why it matters:** Honesty as a safety property is only meaningful if it survives goal conflict. An 'honesty price' curve is a crisp, comparable, longer-horizon safety metric that frontier labs haven't standardized, and it's fully reproducible with open models and prompting.

**Relevant context:** UK AISI Alignment agenda: verify honesty properties / is honesty sufficient for safe SI. Apollo insider-trading deception scenarios (used in 2502.03407) and alignment-faking work (2412.14093) show models lie under goal pressure.
