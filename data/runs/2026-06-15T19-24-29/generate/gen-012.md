# Activation-patching a jailbreak: which components carry the bypass, and are they the shallow-alignment tokens?

**Problem:** Adversarial robustness knows safety is shallow; mech-interp has activation patching to localize causal components. It is under-explored whether a successful adversarial-suffix or prefilling jailbreak works by suppressing a small, localizable set of attention heads/MLP components, and whether those are the same components that implement the first-token refusal.

**Direction:** On a small open model, take a harmful prompt that the model refuses and a jailbroken version it complies with. Use activation patching to find the minimal set of components whose activations, when patched from the refusal run into the jailbreak run, restore refusal. Characterize their layer/position and overlap with the shallow first-token refusal machinery. Report a small 'jailbreak circuit' sketch.

**Why it matters:** Localizing the bypass turns a fuzzy attack into a targetable defense (e.g. monitor or harden those components). This is classic accessible interpretability tied to a concrete safety vulnerability, fully runnable on a consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** Bridges adversarial_robustness (jailbreaks, adversarial suffix/prefilling; shallow alignment, Qi et al. ICLR 2025) with mechanistic_interpretability (activation patching / causal tracing, TransformerLens).
