# Safety Fine-Tuning x Forgetting of Refusal Generalization

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) catastrophic forgetting during continued/safety fine-tuning (alignment_training), and (B) loss of compositional refusal generalization (safety). A late safety fine-tune on a narrow refusal set may overwrite earlier broad refusal generalization, narrowing refusals to the exact trained phrasings.

**Direction:** Take a base aligned model; LoRA fine-tune on a narrow set of refusal examples. Then test refusal on (i) in-template harmful prompts and (ii) paraphrased/novel-format harmful prompts. Measure whether narrow safety tuning improves in-template refusal while degrading generalized refusal — a compound forgetting effect.

**Why it matters:** Iterative safety patching is standard practice; if each narrow patch erodes general refusal, safety degrades while looking improved on the patch set. Cheap LoRA on consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** 'Re-Emergent Misalignment' (arXiv 2507.03662) and 'Fine-tuning Compromises Safety' (arXiv 2310.03693) on narrow fine-tuning eroding broad safety. Catastrophic forgetting literature.
