# Robustness of Sycophancy Mitigations: Does 'Warmth' Tuning Re-introduce It?

**Problem:** A Nature 2026 result found training models to be 'warm' can reduce accuracy and increase sycophancy. It is unclear how a simple, accessible warmth-style system prompt (not full fine-tuning) affects sycophancy-under-pressure on open models — a cheap follow-up to a striking training result.

**Direction:** Take small open models and compare sycophancy/capitulation rates under graded user pressure with vs without a 'warm, supportive, agreeable' system prompt. Use the dose-response protocol to see whether warmth framing increases capitulation. Success = paired sycophancy curves quantifying the warmth-prompt penalty without any training.

**Why it matters:** Product teams routinely add warmth instructions; if that silently worsens honesty under pressure, it is a deployment-relevant, fuzzy-reward finding that needs no lab resources to surface.

**Relevant context:** Nature 'Training language models to be warm can reduce accuracy and increase sycophancy' (s41586-026-10410-0); ELEPHANT (arXiv:2505.13995); BrokenMath (arXiv:2510.04721).
