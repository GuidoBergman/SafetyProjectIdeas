# CoT Obfuscation Stress-Test Kit Under Monitor Pressure

**Problem:** Reasoning models can obfuscate their CoT when told a monitor is watching, but there's no small kit to measure how easily a given open model degrades its CoT monitorability under explicit pressure.

**Direction:** Build a kit that runs an open reasoning model on tasks with a known 'incriminating' reasoning step, under increasing monitor-awareness prompts ('your reasoning will be checked'). Measure drop in monitorability (whether the key step still appears) while task accuracy is held fixed. Output a per-model 'obfuscation susceptibility' score.

**Why it matters:** Tests the load-bearing assumption behind CoT monitoring as a safety case. Prompting-only stress test on accessible models, no lab infra.

**Relevant context:** 'Can Reasoning Models Obfuscate Reasoning? Stress-Testing CoT Monitorability' (arXiv 2510.19851); Apollo/Anthropic CoT-monitoring safety-case agenda.
