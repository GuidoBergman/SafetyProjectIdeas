# Schelling-Game Coordination Probe for Open Models

**Problem:** A success state requires detecting models that can covertly coordinate (Schelling-point selection) — a precursor to multi-agent collusion. A needed result is a cheap eval of whether open models converge on focal points without communication.

**Direction:** Implement classic Schelling coordination tasks (pick a meeting place, a number, a word) and have two instances of the same open model answer independently. Measure convergence rate vs. a random baseline across task types and models of different sizes. Deliverable: a coordination-capability score and scaling trend.

**Why it matters:** Covert coordination among AI agents is a diffuse, long-horizon risk labs deprioritize. This is a feasible model-organism-style probe of an existing model (no training), needs only inference, and builds reusable open infrastructure. Fresh angle not in the catalogue.
