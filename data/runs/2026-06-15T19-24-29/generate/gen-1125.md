# DiversityFloor: how few prompts do you need to expose the Hivemind? A sample-efficiency benchmark

**Problem:** Running full homogeneity audits (thousands of prompts x dozens of models) is expensive. Auditors need to know the minimal probe set that reliably estimates a model panel's homogeneity — but no work characterizes the sample efficiency of homogeneity measurement.

**Direction:** Using a labeled homogeneity dataset (e.g., the SafetyInfinity slice), subsample prompts and models and measure how the estimated homogeneity index converges with probe-set size. Identify a small 'core probe set' that approximates the full estimate. Release the core probe set as a cheap audit kit.

**Why it matters:** Makes systemic-risk auditing affordable for small actors and regulators, lowering the barrier to routine concentration-risk monitoring. Methodologically simple (subsampling + statistics), fits beginner stats skills, and produces reusable infrastructure.
