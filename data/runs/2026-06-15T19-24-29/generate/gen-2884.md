# Time-Split Contamination Test for Safety Knowledge Benchmarks

**Problem:** Models trained on eval questions inflate scores, and time-based partitioning (pre- vs post-training-cutoff items) is a known contamination probe. Safety knowledge benchmarks rarely report time-split analyses, so the contribution of memorization to reported safety/hazard scores is unknown.

**Direction:** For an open safety/hazardous-knowledge benchmark whose items can be dated (or paired with newer paraphrased items), compare a small open model's accuracy on items predating its training cutoff versus matched newer items, and on original versus rephrased items (ConStat-style). A significant pre/post or original/rephrased gap signals contamination rather than genuine knowledge.

**Why it matters:** If safety benchmark scores are partly memorization, they overstate a model's true grasp (or true danger) and mislead deployment decisions. A reusable contamination diagnostic for safety benchmarks helps independent evaluators discount inflated numbers.

**Relevant context:** Contamination surveys (arXiv:2502.17521, arXiv:2404.00699) describe time-partition, ConStat (original vs rephrased gap), and report 10-30% inflation. International AI Safety Report 2026 flags contamination as an open problem. Single-GPU, no training.
