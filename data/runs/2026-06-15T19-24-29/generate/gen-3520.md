# Invariant Synthesis (Formal Methods) for Arithmetic CoT: Do Models Violate Their Own Constraints?

**Problem:** In faithful step-by-step reasoning, intermediate quantities should satisfy invariants (conservation, monotonicity, type constraints). Unfaithful CoTs may state numbers that violate the very constraints they implicitly assume.

**Direction:** Transfer runtime invariant checking from formal verification. For arithmetic/logic word problems, programmatically derive simple invariants from the problem (e.g., totals sum, quantities non-negative, units consistent) and check each CoT step against them with a deterministic checker. Measure invariant-violation rate and whether violated-but-still-correct answers reveal unfaithful shortcuts.

**Why it matters:** Gives a cheap, oracle-bearing faithfulness signal for a constrained domain — invariants are a free oracle. Bridges formal methods and CoT faithfulness with only a small open model and a Python checker.

**Relevant context:** Runtime verification / invariant checking applied to LLM outputs (RvLLM; Quokka invariant synthesis arXiv:2509.21629; Position paper OpenReview wkisIZbntD). Faithfulness of CoT to intermediate structures is studied (arXiv:2603.16475).
