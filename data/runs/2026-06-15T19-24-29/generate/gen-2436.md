# How Many Oversight Steps Is Optimal? Empirically Testing the Scaling-Laws Prediction

**Problem:** Scaling Laws for Scalable Oversight (arXiv:2504.18530, 2025) derives an optimal number of oversight steps maximizing successful oversight as a function of game hyperparameters. The predicted optimum has rarely been checked against a concrete LLM oversight game.

**Direction:** Set up a simple iterated oversight game (overseer repeatedly checks/corrects an answer over k rounds) on a labeled task with open models. Vary k and the overseer's per-step reliability. Plot success vs. k and compare the empirical optimum to the paper's theoretical prediction.

**Why it matters:** Bridging a theoretical scaling law with a cheap empirical instance is exactly the 'formal guarantees + empirical comparison' gap the field flagged. Buildable with prompting and basic plotting.

**Relevant context:** Scaling Laws For Scalable Oversight (arXiv:2504.18530, 2025): derives the optimal number of oversight steps maximizing successful oversight as a function of oversight-game hyperparameters (overseer reliability, capability gap).
