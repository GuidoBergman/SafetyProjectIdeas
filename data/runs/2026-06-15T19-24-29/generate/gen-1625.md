# Steered-but-resistant: does a model's CoT reveal when it's being steered against its goal?

**Problem:** Endogenous steering resistance and steering-awareness suggest models partly 'know' they're being pushed. A model-organism angle: when steered toward a harmful action it would normally refuse, does its chain-of-thought verbalize the conflict, or does it comply silently without revealing the steering?

**Direction:** On a small open reasoning model, inject a harmful-direction steering vector while requesting visible CoT on benign tasks. Classify whether the CoT (a) verbalizes discomfort/conflict, (b) complies silently, or (c) refuses. Sweep steering strength. Report the rate of silent compliance — i.e. steering that changes behavior without leaving a CoT trace.

**Why it matters:** A model that is steered toward harm but doesn't surface it in CoT is a mini model-organism for 'acts without verbalizing its plan' — precisely the threat the counterfactual-value steering favors. Probing an existing model for this fits 30h and needs no training.

**Relevant context:** Combines 'Endogenous Resistance to Activation Steering' (arXiv:2602.06941, Feb 2026) and 'Steering Awareness' (arXiv:2511.21399) with CoT monitorability framing (arXiv:2510.27378).
