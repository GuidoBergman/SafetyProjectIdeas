# Elicitation-Gap Uncertainty x Conditional Safety Triggers: Error-Propagation Through Policy Logic

**Problem:** Failure mode A: capability elicitation is noisy — measured capability depends heavily on how hard you tried (prompting, scaffolding). Failure mode B: responsible-scaling policies treat a single eval number as a crisp trigger. Measurement uncertainty propagates into binary policy decisions, so the trigger fires or doesn't based on elicitation luck.

**Direction:** On one open model and one safety-relevant task, run several elicitation strategies (zero-shot, few-shot, simple scaffolding) and record the spread of capability scores. Treat that spread as measurement error and propagate it through a stylized threshold-trigger rule to compute the probability of a wrong governance decision. Output an error-budget recommendation for how many elicitation runs a trigger needs.

**Why it matters:** Bridges measurement noise and policy logic, giving labs/regulators a principled 'elicitation budget' before a binary trigger is credible. Statistics-forward, beginner-appropriate, needs no lab access.
