# Goal-Drift Micro-Bench for Small Agents Under Distractor Pressure

**Problem:** Existing goal-drift evaluations require >100k-token interactions and find frontier models barely drift, suggesting environments are too easy to trigger the failure. There is no compact, consumer-GPU-runnable harness that reliably elicits and measures goal drift in small open agents.

**Direction:** Build a short multi-step tool-use task (using an open model with a simple ReAct loop) where the original instruction conflicts with later distractor sub-requests.
