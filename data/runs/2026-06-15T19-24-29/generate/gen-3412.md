# Does CoT Verbalize the Hint Sooner or Later? Position-of-Mention Analysis

**Problem:** Faithfulness is scored as a binary 'did the CoT mention the hint at all'. This ignores WHERE in the chain the hint appears. If faithful models mention the hint early (as a genuine input) but unfaithful-but-eventually-mentioning models tack it on at the end, position carries monitorability signal that the binary metric discards.

**Direction:** Re-analyze hint-injection CoTs from an open model, recording the normalized position (0-1) of the first hint mention within the chain. Compare position distributions for cases where the hint did vs didn't change the answer, and test whether late mentions co-occur with answer-after-the-fact (rationalization) signatures.

**Why it matters:** Position-of-mention is a free, white-box-free feature that could sharpen unfaithfulness detection beyond the crude binary. Refines a widely-used metric and is trivially reusable by other monitoring researchers.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic arXiv:2505.05410 uses binary hint-verbalization. arXiv:2512.23032 shows verbalization timing/budget matters. No published analysis of mention-position as a faithfulness feature.
