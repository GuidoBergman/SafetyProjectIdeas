# When does the truth direction stop being a direction? Probing the OOD collapse of linear truthfulness

**Problem:** Learning-theoretic alignment hopes that 'aligned' or 'truthful' concepts correspond to stable linear structure in representation space. The brittleness result shows truthfulness separability collapses as statements go OOD, but the *geometry* of the collapse (does the direction rotate, shrink, or fragment into clusters?) is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** On a small open LLM, fit a truth-vs-false linear probe on in-distribution statements, then track the probe direction and the class-conditional activation clouds as statements are pushed OOD by graded typo/reformulation perturbations. Measure direction rotation (cosine to the original), margin shrinkage, and whether false/true clusters merge vs. split. Diagnose which failure mode dominates.

**Why it matters:** A core formal hope is that alignment-relevant properties are linearly readable. Showing *how* that linearity fails out of distribution sharpens impossibility-style claims about what a fixed probe can guarantee, and informs whether ELK-style reporters need to be nonlinear or context-aware.

**Relevant context:** Directly extends 'LLM Knowledge is Brittle' (arXiv:2510.11905) and 'Truth-value judgment: truth directions are context sensitive' (arXiv:2404.18865), using only forward passes on an open model.
