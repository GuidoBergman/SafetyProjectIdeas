# Inoculation by Counter-Examples: Can Mixing In a Few Aligned Demos Neutralize a Poisoned Dataset?

**Problem:** Success state: open fine-tuners know the minimal fraction of clean, explicitly-aligned demonstrations they must mix into a possibly-contaminated dataset to prevent EM. Realignment-after-the-fact is studied; prevention-by-mixing dose is not mapped.

**Direction:** Create EM-inducing datasets diluted with increasing fractions (0%,1%,5%,10%,25%) of explicitly aligned demonstrations, fine-tune a small open model on each via LoRA, and measure resulting misalignment to find the minimal protective clean fraction. Compare across two misalignment sources.

**Why it matters:** A simple 'add X% clean data' rule is the most deployable possible defense for anyone fine-tuning open models on scraped/uncurated data — a medium-horizon, diffuse risk. Cheap LoRA sweep, statistics-driven.

**Relevant context:** OpenAI prevention work and in-training defenses (arXiv:2508.06249) suggest data composition matters; Betley et al. show data framing controls EM. Extends prevention to a dose-response mixing curve.
