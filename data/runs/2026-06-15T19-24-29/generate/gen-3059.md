# Faithfulness-Verbosity Tradeoff Map for Open Reasoning Models

**Problem:** A new monitorability metric separates faithfulness from verbosity: reasoning can be monitorable without being faithful. The shape of the faithfulness-verbosity relationship across open models and prompting styles is unexplored.

**Direction:** Use cue-injection tests (insert a hint that changes the answer; check if CoT mentions it = faithfulness) plus measure CoT length/verbosity. On 3-4 open reasoning models, plot faithfulness vs verbosity and test whether prompts that increase verbosity actually increase faithfulness or just add unfaithful filler.

**Why it matters:** Monitorability underpins CoT-based safety cases; showing verbosity does not buy faithfulness warns against 'just make it explain more' defenses. Cheap, black-box, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2510.27378 (Measuring CoT Monitorability Through Faithfulness and Verbosity) introduces the distinction. arXiv 2603.26410 (Why Models Know But Don't Say) shows faithfulness divergence in open-weight reasoning models.
