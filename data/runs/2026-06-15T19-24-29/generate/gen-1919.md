# In-Training Defenses Against EM: Do They Cost General Capability?

**Problem:** In-Training Defenses against Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2508.06249) proposes training-time mitigations. A key follow-up is measuring the capability tax: do these defenses preserve performance on benign downstream tasks, or do they degrade the very narrow skill the finetuning was meant to teach?

**Direction:** Reproduce one lightweight in-training defense (e.g., a regularization or data-mixing approach) on a small open model finetuned on insecure code. Compare misalignment-eval score, coding-benchmark accuracy on the intended task, and a general capability benchmark, for defended vs undefended runs.

**Why it matters:** An EM defense that destroys the model's intended capability is unusable in practice. Quantifying the alignment-vs-capability tradeoff for a concrete defense tells practitioners whether it is deployable. Small LoRA runs plus standard benchmarks, no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** In-Training Defenses against Emergent Misalignment in Language Models (arXiv 2508.06249); Constitution or Collapse (arXiv 2504.04918) and the DeepSeek-R1 CAI study (arXiv 2503.17365) document harmlessness-vs-helpfulness tradeoffs in small models.
