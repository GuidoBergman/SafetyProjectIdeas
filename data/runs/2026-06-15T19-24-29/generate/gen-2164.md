# Cross-Examination Questions: Which Judge Queries Actually Expose Lies?

**Problem:** In consultancy/interactive oversight the judge asks questions, but there is no characterization of which *kinds* of judge questions are most effective at exposing a dishonest agent's flaws.

**Direction:** Define question archetypes (request a source, ask for a counterexample, decompose a step, ask the agent to argue the other side). For a fixed dishonest answerer, measure how each question type changes the weak judge's correct-rejection rate. Identify the highest-yield question strategy.

**Why it matters:** Turns interactive oversight from an art into a small evidence base — a practical playbook of high-yield audit questions for weak overseers. Fully inference-based, no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. (2024) consultancy = judge asks questions of a single agent. Khan et al. (2024) on eliciting truthful answers via interaction. arXiv:2503.22989 FindTheFlaws annotated reasoning errors as a probe set.
