# Are Vision Concept Vectors Steerable for Safety? A First Probe in an Open Multimodal Model

**Problem:** Extending concept steering to vision/multimodal models is a named open problem, but there is no accessible first study of whether a safety concept (e.g., 'refuse to describe how to build a weapon shown in an image') is linearly steerable in an open VLM.

**Direction:** On a small open vision-language model (e.g., LLaVA-style or Qwen-VL small), build a contrastive steering vector for an image-grounded safety concept from paired safe/unsafe image-caption activations. Apply it and measure whether unsafe visual requests get refused, plus collateral on benign captioning. A minimal feasibility study, not a full system.

**Why it matters:** Opens the under-explored multimodal frontier of concept steering with a concrete, scoped first result — exactly a named open problem. Uses a small open VLM on a single GPU; counterfactually valuable as multimodal safety lags text.

**Relevant context:** Subfield extension open problem: 'extend concept steering to vision and multimodal models'. Grounded in multimodal activation steering work (SteerVLM arXiv 2510.26769; SwordBench arXiv 2605.16372).
