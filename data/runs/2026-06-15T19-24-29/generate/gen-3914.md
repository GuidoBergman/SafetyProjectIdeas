# Decoding Randomness x Irreversible Actions: Does Sampling Variance Compound Rare Catastrophic Choices?

**Problem:** Failure mode A: stochastic decoding means the agent occasionally samples an unsafe action it usually avoids. Failure mode B: irreversibility means a single rare unsafe sample is unrecoverable. The compound risk is that low per-step unsafe probabilities accumulate to a high episode-level chance of an irreversible mistake.

**Direction:** On a fixed agentic task with a mock irreversible tool, sweep temperature/top-p and run many seeds. Measure per-step unsafe-action probability and the episode-level probability of at least one irreversible unsafe action. Show how rare per-step risk compounds over multi-step horizons into non-trivial catastrophe odds.
