# Persona-Vector Prediction of Misalignment: Replicating on a New Model

**Problem:** Anthropic's persona-vectors work showed fine-tuning-induced persona shifts can be predicted from activation movement before training completes, and that problematic training data can be flagged. This was demonstrated on specific models; whether the prediction signal transfers to other small open models is open.

**Direction:** Using the released persona_vectors code, extract an 'evil/misaligned' persona vector from a small open model, then fine-tune on mildly-harmful narrow data and test whether projection onto the persona vector predicts post-training misalignment. Twist: replicate the predictive monitoring on a model family not in the original paper.

**Why it matters:** Cheap activation-based early-warning for fine-tuning misalignment is a strategically valuable monitoring primitive that anyone can run without lab infrastructure. Confirming cross-model transfer turns a lab result into a usable open tool.

**Relevant context:** Persona Vectors (arXiv:2507.21509, Anthropic/UT Austin/UC Berkeley) show persona directions causally induce traits and that fine-tuning persona shifts correlate with movement along these vectors, enabling pre-finetuning data flagging. Code: github.com/safety-research/persona_vectors.
