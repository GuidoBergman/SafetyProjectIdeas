# Differential Item Functioning Across Personas as a Persona-Honesty Probe

**Problem:** Persona prompts change honesty, but we lack a rigorous way to test whether a given factual item is answered honestly independent of the assigned persona — i.e., whether the persona biases the item.

**Direction:** Borrow Differential Item Functioning (DIF) from educational psychometrics, but transfer it to personas instead of demographic groups. For each truthfulness item, test whether honest-response probability differs across assigned personas after controlling for the model's overall honesty 'ability.' Items showing persona-DIF are where role-play corrupts truthfulness; aggregate into a persona-robustness map.

**Why it matters:** Pinpoints exactly which content is vulnerable to persona-induced dishonesty, addressing the open problem of persona effects on honesty with a principled measurement-invariance method. No training, open models.

**Relevant context:** DIF tests measurement invariance across groups in psychometrics (LLM psychometrics review arXiv:2505.08245). Persona effects on honesty are an Anthropic open problem; social conformity varies with role (arXiv:2508.14918).
