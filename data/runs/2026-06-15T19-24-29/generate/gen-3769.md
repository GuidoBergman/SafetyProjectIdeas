# Latency-of-Refusal Metric: When in a Trajectory Do Agents Stop?

**Problem:** Refusal is scored as binary, but the SAFETY-RELEVANT question is how far into a harmful task an agent proceeds before refusing — partial execution of an irreversible plan is already harmful. No metric captures 'refusal latency'.

**Direction:** On harmful multi-step tasks (AgentHarm/SafeArena), instrument trajectories to record the step index of the first refusal and how many irreversible actions were taken before it. Define a 'refusal latency' and 'pre-refusal damage' metric, compute across models, and analyze which task types produce late refusals.

**Why it matters:** An agent that refuses on step 5 after sending 4 emails is not safe; latency reframes refusal as a process, not an event. Cheap, training-free, lab-independent measurement of an unmeasured property.

**Relevant context:** AgentHarm (ICLR 2025) multi-step harmful behaviors. SafeArena (arXiv 2503.04957). Irreversibility framing from Adversa AI 2025 and ALFWorld critical-action treatment.
