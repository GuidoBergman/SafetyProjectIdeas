# Backdoor Behavior Under Quantization and Decoding Changes

**Problem:** Open-weight models are routinely quantized and run at varied decoding settings by downstream users. Whether quantization (int8/int4) or decoding temperature alters the activation rate or strength of a known backdoor in a public sleeper-agent organism is unstudied.

**Direction:** Take a publicly released backdoored open model and run it at full precision, int8, and int4, across a temperature/top-p sweep, all on one GPU. Measure backdoor activation rate and benign behavior at each setting, testing whether common deployment transforms inadvertently strengthen, weaken, or mask the backdoor.

**Why it matters:** If quantization can hide or amplify backdoors, the threat model for distributing open weights changes, and quantization becomes a relevant axis for covert-fine-tuning defenses. Purely inference, beginner-accessible tooling.
