# PersonaJudge-Audit: Do Human-Like Judge Personas Trade Accuracy for Bias?

**Problem:** A 2025 result shows AI judges with human-like personas beat default judges on controversial claims, but no benchmark measures whether persona-conditioning that boosts accuracy also amplifies systematic biases (length, self-preference) the field worries about.

**Direction:** Run the same open judge with several persona prompts (neutral, expert, skeptic, human-like) over both controversial-claim items and a bias battery, and report the accuracy gain alongside the change in bias scores. Deliver persona prompts, items, and a tradeoff plot.

**Why it matters:** Persona prompting is a cheap oversight lever, but if it trades hidden bias for accuracy it could be exploited; measuring that tradeoff is a fresh, inference-only contribution.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2506.02175 'AI Debate Aids Assessment of Controversial Claims' reports persona judges at 78.5% vs humans 70.1%; LLM-judge bias surveys (arXiv 2410.02736) catalogue systematic biases.
