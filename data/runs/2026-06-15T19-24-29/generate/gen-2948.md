# The Concentration Link: Correlated Failure Across Models From the Same Provider

**Problem:** Systemic-risk chain: many deployed systems depend on one provider's model family -> a shared vulnerability exists across the family -> a single attack/failure cascades across all dependent systems. The under-studied link is *how correlated* safety failures are within a model family vs across families, a prerequisite for measuring concentration risk.

**Direction:** Run an identical jailbreak/harm test set against several open models grouped by family/base-model lineage (e.g., multiple fine-tunes of the same base) vs unrelated bases. Measure per-prompt failure correlation within vs across families. High within-family correlation quantifies concentration risk.

**Why it matters:** EU AI Act and the International Report flag systemic risk from provider concentration; correlated-failure measurement is the missing empirical link. Uses only open model checkpoints, no proprietary access, and addresses a regulator-prioritized but lab-deprioritized concern.

**Relevant context:** EU AI Act and International AI Safety Report 2026 name systemic risk from concentration as an open problem; the abundance of open base-model derivatives makes within-family correlation directly measurable.
