# OversightTransfer-Bench: Does Judge Skill on Easy Tasks Predict Skill on Hard Ones?

**Problem:** Easy-to-hard oversight assumes a judge validated on easy items remains reliable on hard ones, but there is no benchmark measuring the correlation between a judge's per-item easy-task accuracy and its hard-task accuracy across domains.

**Direction:** For several open judges, measure verification accuracy on graded-difficulty item pairs and compute how well easy-task accuracy predicts hard-task accuracy (transfer coefficient) per domain. Deliver the paired items and a transferability report.

**Why it matters:** If oversight skill does not transfer easy-to-hard, validating overseers on cheap easy tasks gives false assurance; measuring transferability is a foundational, inference-only diagnostic for the easy-to-hard agenda.

**Relevant context:** Sun et al. 2024 'Easy-to-Hard Generalization' (arXiv 2403.09472) trains evaluators on easy items for hard ones; subfield open problem 'develop easy-to-hard generalization methods.'
