# Multimodal Hivemind: Do Vision-Language Models Describe Images Homogeneously?

**Problem:** The Hivemind paper hint flags multimodal as an open extension. If VLMs give near-identical descriptions/judgments of ambiguous or safety-relevant images (e.g., 'is this dangerous?'), they share visual blind spots no single lab would catch.

**Direction:** Assemble a small set of ambiguous and safety-relevant images (with permissive licenses). Run ~6-8 open VLMs to produce descriptions and safety judgments. Measure inter-model homogeneity of descriptions (embedding similarity) and agreement on the safety judgment. Identify images where all VLMs share the same (possibly wrong) interpretation.

**Why it matters:** Shared visual blind spots matter for content moderation and physical-world deployments. Public VLMs run on a single consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind multimodal extension hint (arXiv:2510.22954).
