# CoT-MonitorScore: Porting a Monitorability Metric to Small Open Reasoning Models

**Problem:** A 2025 metric scores CoT monitorability via faithfulness and verbosity, but it was reported only for large frontier models; no one has measured whether small open reasoning models are monitorable enough to oversee.

**Direction:** Reimplement the faithfulness-x-verbosity monitorability score on 2-3 small open reasoning models (e.g., distilled DeepSeek-R1 variants) over a fixed task set, and report a monitorability leaderboard for cheaply-runnable models. Deliver the scoring script and results.

**Why it matters:** If outside researchers are to monitor reasoning, they need to know which accessible models are monitorable; extending an existing metric to small models is a concrete tooling gap with strong counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2510.27378 'Measuring Chain-of-Thought Monitorability Through Faithfulness and Verbosity' reports DeepSeek-R1 most monitorable (78.3%); FaithCoT-Bench (2510.04040) provides faithfulness methods.
