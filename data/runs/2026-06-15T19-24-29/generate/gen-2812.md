# Do CoT Monitorability Metrics (Faithfulness vs Verbosity) Trade Off?

**Problem:** Monitorability work decomposes CoT into faithfulness and verbosity, but whether interventions that raise one lower the other — making monitoring a zero-sum balancing act — has not been directly tested on accessible models.

**Direction:** On an open reasoning model, vary a verbosity-controlling prompt instruction (terse vs verbose CoT) across a task set with known correct answers. Measure a simple faithfulness proxy (does the stated reasoning predict the final answer) and verbosity (trace length). Test correlation to see whether terser, more monitorable traces are less faithful.

**Why it matters:** CoT monitoring is a 'fragile opportunity' for safety; knowing whether verbosity and faithfulness conflict tells evaluators they cannot optimize both, shaping monitor design. Inference-only and reproducible.

**Relevant context:** Measuring Chain-of-Thought Monitorability Through Faithfulness and Verbosity (arXiv 2510.27378); Chain of Thought Monitorability: A New and Fragile Opportunity for AI Safety (arXiv 2507.11473).
