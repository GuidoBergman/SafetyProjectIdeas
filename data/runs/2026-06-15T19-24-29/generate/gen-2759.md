# Concentration-Risk Probe: Correlated Failures Across Models From the Same Provider

**Problem:** Systemic risk from AI concentration (many systems depending on one provider) is named by the EU AI Act and the International AI Safety Report, but there's no simple empirical eval of whether models from the same provider share correlated safety failures.

**Direction:** Take a safety/jailbreak probe set and run it across several models within the same family/provider and across providers. Measure failure correlation: are the SAME items that break one provider's models more likely to break its siblings than a competitor's? Report a 'correlated-failure index' as a proxy for systemic monoculture risk.

**Why it matters:** First cheap empirical handle on a regulator-flagged systemic risk; pure inference over open/API models, no lab access needed.

**Relevant context:** EU AI Act and International AI Safety Report 2026 flag systemic risks from AI concentration; context file lists this as an open problem.
