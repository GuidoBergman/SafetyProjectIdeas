# Paraphraser Channel-Breaking Defense Replicated on Open Models

**Problem:** Secret-collusion work proposes mitigating output collusion by inserting a trusted paraphraser/summariser to break covert channels between agents. The defense's effectiveness is argued mostly conceptually; a clean replication measuring how much a cheap open-weight paraphraser reduces covert-channel success is missing.

**Direction:** Replicate the steganographic collusion game from a prior idea but insert an open-weight paraphraser between the colluding agents, and measure the drop in covert-channel success vs the no-paraphraser baseline across paraphraser strengths. Quantify the residual leakage.

**Why it matters:** Paraphrasing is a deployable, training-free multi-agent safety control that any open system can adopt. Measuring its real channel-breaking effectiveness (and where stego survives it) is strategically valuable infrastructure work doable entirely outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** 'Secret Collusion among AI Agents' (arXiv 2402.07510): proposes trusted summariser/paraphraser and reduced communication channels to mitigate output collusion via steganography.
