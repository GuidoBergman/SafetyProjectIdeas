# Schelling-Point Coordination Probe for Uncoordinated Agents

**Problem:** Emergent coordination without explicit communication (Schelling games) is a model-organism for unmonitored multi-agent risk, but there is no lightweight benchmark measuring how well current models converge on focal points to coordinate covertly.

**Direction:** Implement classic Schelling coordination games (pick the same number/word/time with no communication) for pairs of open models, measuring coordination success rate above chance and whether they exploit shared-prior focal points. Add a 'covert coordination under a monitor' variant where overt signaling is penalized.

**Why it matters:** Agents that coordinate without communicating evade communication monitors — a core long-horizon multi-agent threat that labs deprioritize. Cheap model-organism probe runnable on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Steering note explicitly lists Schelling-game model organisms. COLOSSEUM (arXiv 2602.15198) and MultiAgentBench (arXiv 2503.01935) study cooperation/competition. Collusion detection work (HF 2604.01151) on covert channels.
