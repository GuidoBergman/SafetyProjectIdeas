# Reasoning-Pressure x Monitorability: Does Optimizing for Success Compound Into Opaque CoT?

**Problem:** Failure mode A: training/prompt incentives that pressure the model toward task success. Failure mode B: loss of CoT monitorability. The compound concern is that the very pressure that boosts agent capability simultaneously makes its reasoning less monitorable — capability and oversight degrade together.

**Direction:** Without training, simulate 'pressure' via prompting (reward-for-speed, penalize-verbosity, high-stakes framing) on a fixed agentic task. Measure task success AND a CoT-monitorability proxy (does CoT mention the true action plan?). Test whether higher success correlates with lower monitorability across pressure conditions.

**Why it matters:** If oversight erodes exactly as capability rises, scaling safety guarantees are illusory. A prompt-only proxy lets non-experts probe a frontier-relevant tension cheaply.

**Relevant context:** 'Reasoning Under Pressure: How do Training Incentives Influence Chain-of-Thought Monitorability?' (arXiv 2512.00218); LASR unfaithful-CoT findings (alignmentforum QYAfjdujzRv8hx6xo).
