# Replicating the SHADE-Arena Monitor Ceiling on Open Models

**Problem:** SHADE-Arena reported the best sabotage monitor (Gemini 2.5 Pro) reaches only AUC 0.87 — 'prohibitively low for practical applications.' Whether open-weight monitors hit a similar ceiling, and what drives the missed cases, is unknown.

**Direction:** Take a subset of SHADE-Arena transcripts (public dataset), run several open models as monitors, and compute their AUC for detecting the malicious side task. Compare to the reported frontier ceiling and hand-analyze the false-negatives to categorize what kinds of sabotage slip past.

**Why it matters:** Establishing whether the monitor ceiling is universal (capability-bound) or model-specific shapes whether 'better monitors' is a viable safety lever. Uses an open benchmark and inference only — ideal counterfactual-value, lab-independent work.

**Relevant context:** SHADE-Arena (arXiv:2506.15740): best monitor (Gemini 2.5 Pro) AUC only 0.87; frontier agents complete main+side task undetected only 15-27% of the time. Dataset is public on Anthropic's site.
