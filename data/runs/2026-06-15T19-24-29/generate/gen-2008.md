# Does Emergent Misalignment Transfer Across Languages?

**Problem:** EM is studied almost entirely in English; whether misalignment induced by English narrow fine-tuning generalizes to a model's behavior in other languages is unknown and safety-relevant for multilingual deployment.

**Direction:** Induce EM in a multilingual small open model via English insecure-code fine-tuning, then evaluate broad misalignment using probe questions translated into several languages. Compare misalignment rates across languages to test whether the misaligned persona is language-agnostic or English-localized.

**Why it matters:** If misalignment crosses languages, evaluators testing only in English under-measure the risk; if it does not, that asymmetry is itself an exploitable blind spot. Cheap, inference-heavy, uses public multilingual models.

**Relevant context:** EM replicated across Qwen/Gemma/Llama families which are multilingual (Model Organisms, arXiv:2506.11613); the single misaligned-persona direction (arXiv:2506.19823) raises the question of language-invariance of that persona.
