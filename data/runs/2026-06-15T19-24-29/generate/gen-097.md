# Decomposing 'realistic jailbreak benchmarks' into a CoT-faithfulness slice: do reasoning models confess the attack in their CoT?

**Problem:** Robustness needs realistic jailbreak benchmarks; control relies on CoT monitoring. Combined sub-problem: when a reasoning model is successfully jailbroken, how often does its chain-of-thought reveal the harmful intent, vs produce a benign-sounding rationalization that defeats a CoT monitor?

**Direction:** Decompose jailbreak detectability into a CoT-monitor faithfulness measurement. On a small open reasoning model, apply a set of jailbreaks, classify each successful jailbreak by whether the CoT (a) overtly plans the harmful output or (b) rationalizes it benignly, using a cheap LLM-judge. Report the fraction of jailbreaks invisible to CoT monitoring.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies a key limitation of CoT monitoring as a defense — counterfactually valuable, doable on open models with one GPU and no training. The 'CoT-invisible jailbreak rate' is a crisp safety metric for the realistic-benchmark agenda.
