# Quantization as an Under-Studied Safety Link: Does 4-bit Erode Refusal?

**Problem:** Open models are overwhelmingly deployed quantized (GGUF/4-bit) for consumer hardware, adding a deployment link: full-precision safety -> quantized safety. Whether quantization erodes the shallow, early-token refusal behavior is under-studied yet affects nearly all real local deployments.

**Direction:** Take the same open model at fp16, 8-bit, and 4-bit quantization and run identical harmful + benign prompt suites. Measure refusal-rate and prefilling-attack robustness across precisions to test whether quantization systematically weakens the (already shallow) safety link.

**Why it matters:** Targets a real, neglected deployment link affecting the entire local-LLM ecosystem outside any lab, a high-counterfactual diffuse threat. Quantization + inference only, well within single-GPU budget, no training; a practical safety finding for the open-weights community.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946) show safety lives in a few early tokens whose probability mass quantization could perturb; quantized open models (Llama, Qwen, Mistral GGUF) dominate consumer deployment. The lab-vs-deployment robustness gap is an open problem (International AI Safety Report 2026).
