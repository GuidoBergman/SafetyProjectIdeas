# Context-Dependent Tasks Gap in Prompt-Injection Benchmarks

**Problem:** The AgentLure/ARGUS work (arXiv:2605.03378, May 2026) reports that of 14 prompt-injection benchmarks, none include context-dependent tasks, and context-aware attacks defeat existing defenses. This benchmark-coverage gap has not been quantified empirically against a simple defense.

**Direction:** Construct a small set of context-dependent agent tasks (where the legitimate action depends on prior conversation) and craft context-aware injections for each. Measure attack success on an open tool-using agent with and without a simple provenance/'only follow instructions from the user' guard. Quantify how much the context-dependence raises attack success over context-free injections.

**Why it matters:** Indirect prompt injection is a near-to-medium-horizon agent threat; demonstrating that context-dependence breaks naive defenses on a reproducible open benchmark is high-value infrastructure work.

**Relevant context:** ARGUS: Defending LLM Agents Against Context-Aware Prompt Injection (arXiv:2605.03378, May 2026); reports 14 benchmarks lack context-dependent tasks; InjecAgent (arXiv:2403.02691) as baseline.
