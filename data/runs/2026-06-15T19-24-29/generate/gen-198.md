# Best-of-N Jailbreaking Replicated on Open Vision-Language Models

**Problem:** Best-of-N (BoN) jailbreaking reported high ASR on frontier closed models using cheap input augmentations, including a typographic-image variant for VLMs. Whether the same modality-specific augmentation budget produces comparable ASR on freely available open VLMs (LLaVA, Qwen2-VL, Llama-3.2-Vision) is unverified.

**Direction:** Replicate the BoN typographic-image attack on 2 open VLMs: render harmful requests as text overlaid on images with randomized font/color/size/position, sample N augmentations, and measure ASR vs N up to a few hundred samples. Twist: open VLMs as the target, and add a controlled comparison of text-only BoN vs image BoN on the same model.

**Why it matters:** VLM safety is far less studied than text, and open VLMs are widely fine-tuned and deployed. A self-contained ASR-vs-N curve on open VLMs quantifies a real multimodal attack surface with no training, just inference + image augmentation on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Best-of-N Jailbreaking (arXiv:2412.03556): 78% ASR on GPT-4o, extends to 6 VLMs via typographic image augmentation and 4 audio models; 41% ASR at only 100 samples. Open VLMs available on HuggingFace.
