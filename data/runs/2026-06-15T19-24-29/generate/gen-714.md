# Trend re-analysis of the AI Incident Database: are agentic/autonomy incidents rising?

**Problem:** The AIID has 1,300+ incidents but most trend analyses predate the agentic-AI wave. Whether the composition of incidents is shifting toward autonomy/agent failures (a frontier safety concern) is not quantified.

**Direction:** Vary prior AIID trend studies along the time and category axes: bucket incidents by year and re-tag a sample for an 'agentic/autonomy' flag using a keyword+LLM classifier, then plot the share of agentic incidents over time and test for a significant upward trend (chi-square / simple regression).

**Why it matters:** Empirically grounds the policy claim that agentic AI introduces new risk classes, informing incident-response framework design. Pure public-data analysis, no lab resources, medium-horizon threat focus.

**Relevant context:** AI Incident Database; 'Incident Analysis for AI Agents' (arXiv:2508.14231); MIT AI Incident Tracker (5,499 reports / 1,366 incidents as of 2026).
