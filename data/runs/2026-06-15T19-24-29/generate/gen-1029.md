# Does Temperature Break the Hivemind? A Sweep of Inter-Model Homogeneity vs. Sampling Temperature

**Problem:** The original homogeneity findings were measured at default/low sampling settings. It is unclear how much of inter-model homogeneity is an artifact of greedy/low-temperature decoding versus a deeper convergence that persists even when models are forced to be creative.

**Direction:** Vary one variable from the Hivemind experiment: temperature. Run a fixed set of Infinity-Chat queries across 6 open models at temperatures {0.0, 0.3, 0.7, 1.0, 1.3}, and plot inter-model similarity as a function of temperature. Test whether homogeneity converges to an irreducible floor that high temperature cannot eliminate.

**Why it matters:** If an irreducible homogeneity floor exists regardless of sampling, then operators cannot decorrelate models simply by turning up temperature — a load-bearing fact for anyone relying on multi-model redundancy for safety. Pure inference, trivially feasible in 30h.
