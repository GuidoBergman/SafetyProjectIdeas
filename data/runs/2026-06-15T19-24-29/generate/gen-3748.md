# Long-Horizon Attack Susceptibility Replicated at Reduced Scale

**Problem:** AgentLAB benchmarks LLM agents against long-horizon attacks, finding susceptibility grows over extended interaction. Headline results use capable agents; whether susceptibility-over-horizon reproduces for open-weight agents on a shortened horizon feasible in 30 hours is unknown.

**Direction:** Replicate a reduced-horizon slice of AgentLAB (or an AgentLAB-style multi-turn attack) on open-weight models, measuring attack success as a function of interaction length up to a tractable cap. Compare the rising-susceptibility trend to the published long-horizon finding.

**Why it matters:** Long-horizon degradation is an explicitly listed multi-step agentic failure mode; quantifying it for accessible models on a feasible horizon is a medium-horizon threat study that needs only inference and a fixed task environment.
