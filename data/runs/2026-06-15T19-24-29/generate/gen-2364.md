# Backcasting Faithful Quotes: Can a Weak Judge Catch Fabricated Evidence?

**Problem:** Success state: a judge that can verify the evidence debaters cite from a source document, so a dishonest debater cannot win with fabricated or out-of-context quotes. We need to know how often debaters fabricate/distort quotes and whether a cheap verification step catches them.

**Direction:** On QuALITY debate transcripts (open), programmatically check every debater quote against the source passage (exact/fuzzy string match). Measure quote-fabrication and quote-distortion rates by side (honest vs dishonest), then test whether feeding an automatic 'quote-verified' flag to the weak judge improves accuracy.

**Why it matters:** Verified evidence is a backbone of any debate-based oversight success state. A simple, training-free verification harness that quantifies fabrication and boosts a weak judge is reusable oversight infrastructure that any small team can build outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Debate protocols use verified quotes as evidence (Khan et al. 2024 arXiv:2402.06782; arXiv:2311.08702 'Debate Helps Supervise Unreliable Experts'). The fabrication rate and the value of automatic quote-checking are under-measured publicly.
