# PersonaHivemind: do role/persona prompts collapse to the same character across models?

**Problem:** Practitioners use system-prompt personas to differentiate assistants, assuming this yields behavioral diversity. It is unmeasured whether the same persona prompt produces a homogeneous character across different base models — and whether persona-induced behaviors share blind spots.

**Direction:** Define ~10 persona system prompts (cautious advisor, blunt expert, etc.). For each persona, query several open models on a fixed task set. Measure inter-model homogeneity within each persona vs the no-persona baseline. Test whether personas amplify or reduce the Hivemind effect, and whether 'unsafe' personas jailbreak all models equally.

**Why it matters:** If personas are skin-deep over a shared latent character, then product 'diversity' built on personas is illusory and shares the same safety failures — relevant to deployers who think persona variety buys robustness. Inference-only, lab-independent.
