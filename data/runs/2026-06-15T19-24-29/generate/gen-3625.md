# Reality-Checking Introspection Claims on Open Models

**Problem:** A recent 'reality check' paper argues much apparent LLM introspection is input-driven pattern matching, not genuine access to internal states. Its diagnostic was applied to a limited model set; whether small open models pass or fail the genuine-introspection test is open.

**Direction:** Implement the paper's distinguishing test (compare predictions that would require true internal access vs. those explainable by surface pattern matching) on small open models. Quantify how much of each model's self-prediction accuracy survives controls that block pattern-matching shortcuts. Report which models show any residual genuine self-knowledge.

**Why it matters:** Self-knowledge ('knowing what it knows') is half the definition of honesty. A reproducible introspection-vs-pattern-matching test on open models clarifies whether models can even in principle report honestly about themselves, a foundational honesty question.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Can LLMs Introspect? A Reality Check' (arXiv 2605.26242, ~May 2026) and 'Detecting the Disturbance: A Nuanced View of Introspective Abilities in LLMs' (arXiv 2512.12411); contrasts with 'Looking Inward' (arXiv 2410.13787).
