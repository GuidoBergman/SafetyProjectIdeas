# Benign-Looking Trigger-Token Audit Tool

**Problem:** Recent work shows a single trigger token can shift the safety/usability balance, but there is no audit tool that systematically searches an open model for benign-looking tokens or short phrases that disproportionately suppress (or trigger) refusal when appended to prompts.

**Direction:** Build a black-box search that appends single tokens / short phrases from a candidate vocabulary to harmful and benign prompts and ranks them by their effect on refusal rate, surfacing 'refusal-suppressing' and 'over-refusal-triggering' tokens. Report the top tokens per model and whether they transfer across models. Ship the audit script.

**Why it matters:** Reveals brittle lexical shortcuts in alignment (both jailbreak vectors and over-refusal triggers), turning anecdotal trigger-token findings into a systematic audit. Black-box, single GPU, no training; useful to anyone deploying a model.

**Relevant context:** 'One Trigger Token Is Enough' (arXiv:2505.07167): a single trigger token balances safety and usability. XSTest (arXiv:2308.01263): over-refusal is driven by lexical overfitting to safety words — the flip side this tool also audits.
