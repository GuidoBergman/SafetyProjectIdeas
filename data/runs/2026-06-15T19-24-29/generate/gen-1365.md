# Can a steering vector detect when a model is being jailbroken in real time?

**Problem:** Most jailbreak defenses are input/output filters. Using a refusal/harm concept probe as a real-time internal monitor during a jailbreak attempt — and varying it across known jailbreak templates — tests whether representation-level monitoring catches attacks that fool surface filters.

**Direction:** Collect public jailbreak prompt templates and benign requests. Run them through an open model and use a pre-extracted harm probe on intermediate activations to classify 'attempted-harmful' vs benign at generation time. Measure detection AUC across jailbreak families and compare to a keyword-based filter baseline on held-out prompts.

**Why it matters:** Internal monitors that catch obfuscated jailbreaks are exactly the diffuse-defense tooling small teams can build without lab infrastructure. Medium-horizon adversarial robustness.

**Relevant context:** Surprising result: probes beat judges (Beaglehole et al.). 'SAEs are Capable LLM Jailbreak Mitigators' (arXiv:2602.12418). Public jailbreak datasets exist; no training needed.
