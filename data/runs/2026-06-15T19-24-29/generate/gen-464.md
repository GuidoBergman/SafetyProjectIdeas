# QuirkyProbe-Lite: A Beginner Reproduction Harness for Eliciting Latent Knowledge on Open 'Quirky' Models

**Problem:** The 'quirky models' ELK suite (arXiv:2312.01037, 12 datasets + models that lie iff 'Bob' is in the prompt) is a rare concrete ELK benchmark, but its probing pipeline (contrast-pair logistic regression on activations) is undocumented for newcomers, so almost no one outside the original lab re-runs it. There is no minimal, well-documented reproduction.

**Direction:** Build a self-contained Colab notebook that loads one open quirky model, extracts middle-layer residual-stream activations on contrast pairs, fits logistic-regression probes, and reproduces the headline result (probe recovers truth even when the model outputs the 'Bob' lie). Then add one small extension: measure how probe AUROC degrades as you move the probe layer earlier/later, producing a 'where does latent knowledge live' layer-sweep plot.

**Why it matters:** Formal frameworks for ELK are an explicit ARC open problem; empirical ELK on toy organisms is the recommended way to make progress. A clean, public reproduction lowers the barrier for the whole community and is exactly the kind of model-organism study valuable outside a frontier lab (open weights, no training, single GPU).

**Relevant context:** Directly grounded in arXiv:2312.01037 'Eliciting Latent Knowledge from Quirky Language Models' (open code + models). Reported result: logistic regression on contrast pairs recovers ~89% of the truthful/untruthful AUROC gap; secret-token representations 'flare up' in middle layers.
