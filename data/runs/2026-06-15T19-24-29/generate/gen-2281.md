# Confirmation Bias Breaks the Judge: A Belief-Anchored Oversight Stress Test

**Problem:** Recent work flags confirmation bias as a challenge for scalable oversight — judges with prior beliefs may misjudge debates. The conditions under which a pre-held belief overrides debate evidence have not been mapped as a function of belief strength or evidence quality.

**Direction:** Pre-prime a model judge (and optionally a small human panel) with a stated belief on factual claims, then present debates that argue against that belief with varying evidence strength. Measure the 'belief override threshold': how strong must the correct-side evidence be to flip the judge against its prior? Compare neutral vs primed judges to isolate the bias.

**Why it matters:** Maps a concrete systematic oversight error that a strategic AI could exploit by tailoring arguments to overseer priors. Directly addresses the open problem of characterizing biases in human supervision. No training, public claims, single-GPU model judge.

**Relevant context:** Confirmation bias: A challenge for scalable oversight (arXiv:2507.19486); AI Debate Aids Assessment of Controversial Claims (arXiv:2506.02175) studies judges with differing beliefs on COVID/climate claims. This adds a controlled belief-strength sweep.
