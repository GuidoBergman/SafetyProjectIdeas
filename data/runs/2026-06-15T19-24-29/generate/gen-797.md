# A Commitment-Drift Tracker for Versioned Frontier Safety Policies

**Problem:** Labs revise their safety policies over time, and commitments can quietly weaken (thresholds raised, 'will' softened to 'may', mitigations made conditional). No public tool systematically tracks how individual commitments change across policy versions.

**Direction:** Collect 2+ public versions of each lab's safety policy, align matching commitments across versions, and build a diff-and-classify tool that labels each change as strengthened / weakened / clarified / removed (using modal-verb and threshold heuristics plus LLM judgment with human check). Produce a per-lab drift report.

**Why it matters:** Tracking whether voluntary commitments erode is essential to the voluntary-vs-enforceable open problem and to external accountability. Entirely public-document work; the diffing tool is reusable for future policy updates.

**Relevant context:** METR Common Elements documents multiple policy-version snapshots (Nov 2024, Mar 2025, Dec 2025); policies are publicly archived. Earlier metr.org versions accessible via web archive.
