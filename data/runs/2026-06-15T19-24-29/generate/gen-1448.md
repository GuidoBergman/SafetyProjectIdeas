# ProbeCalibration: A Reliability-Diagram Benchmark for Concept-Vector Detectors

**Problem:** Concept-vector probes report AUC but rarely calibration; there is no benchmark measuring whether probe confidence scores are well-calibrated, which matters if they gate safety decisions with thresholds.

**Direction:** For 3-4 safety probes on an open model, compute reliability diagrams and Expected Calibration Error, and test simple recalibration (temperature/Platt scaling). Report whether raw probe scores are trustworthy as thresholds and how much recalibration helps. Package as a one-call calibration report.

**Why it matters:** Monitors that gate actions need calibrated, not just discriminative, scores; uncalibrated probes cause silent threshold failures. Calibration analysis fits the participants' statistics skills perfectly and is fully lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. promote probes as monitors; calibration of these probes is unaddressed. Reliability diagrams / ECE and temperature scaling are standard, with open datasets (HaluEval, ToxiGen).
