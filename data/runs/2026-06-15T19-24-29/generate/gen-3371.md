# Lanham Early-Answering Curves on Modern Open Reasoning Models

**Problem:** Lanham et al. 2023 measured CoT faithfulness via 'early answering' (truncate CoT and force an answer) on older non-reasoning Anthropic models. RL-trained reasoning models (R1-distill, QwQ) with explicit long thinking traces may behave very differently — perhaps more or less dependent on their own CoT.

**Direction:** Reimplement the early-answering intervention: for each problem, truncate the model's thinking trace at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% and force an immediate answer; plot how quickly the final answer stabilizes. Compare a distilled reasoning model vs. its non-reasoning base (Qwen2.5-7B). A flat curve (answer fixed before reasoning) indicates post-hoc rationalization.

**Why it matters:** Tells us whether the visible CoT of cheap open reasoning models is load-bearing computation or decoration — central to whether monitoring those traces means anything. No training needed; only constrained decoding.

**Relevant context:** Lanham et al. 2023 (arXiv:2307.13702) 'Measuring Faithfulness in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning' introduced early answering, adding-mistake, paraphrasing, filler-token interventions, and found larger models often less faithful. R1-distill models expose <think> tokens.
