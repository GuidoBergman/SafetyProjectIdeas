# Persona-Triggered Convergence: Do Models Collapse to the Same Voice Under Roleplay?

**Problem:** Homogeneity may be context-dependent. The under-studied link: 'roleplay/persona prompting -> attractor personas -> models converge to the SAME character.' If a 'helpful assistant' or 'expert' persona collapses all models into one voice, then persona-based diversity defenses fail and persona-targeted jailbreaks transfer broadly.

**Direction:** Prompt several models with identical persona instructions on open-ended tasks. Measure whether output similarity INCREASES under strong personas relative to neutral prompting. Identify which personas act as homogeneity attractors. Test whether a persona that homogenizes models also makes a known persona-jailbreak transfer more widely.

**Why it matters:** Connects two safety concerns: homogeneity and jailbreak transfer. If personas are homogeneity attractors, they are also shared-vulnerability attractors. Novel angle, cheap to run, and links output convergence to a concrete attack-surface increase.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in Artificial Hivemind inter-model homogeneity (arXiv 2510.22954) and jailbreak-transfer-from-shared-representations (arXiv 2506.12913). Persona-diversity framing from reasoning-diversity work (arXiv 2601.10825).
