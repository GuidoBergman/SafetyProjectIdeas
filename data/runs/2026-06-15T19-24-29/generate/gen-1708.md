# Does Interpretability Transfer Across Architectures? Probing RNN/SSM vs Transformer

**Problem:** It is an open question whether transformer-developed interpretability tools (logit lens, probes, tuned lens) transfer to non-transformer architectures like RNNs/SSMs (Mamba). If a tool silently underperforms on a different architecture, conclusions about those models are unreliable.

**Direction:** Take a small open RNN/SSM-style model (e.g. a Mamba or RWKV checkpoint) and a comparable transformer. Apply the same simple interpretability probes (linear probe for a concept, logit lens where applicable) to both and compare how cleanly each yields interpretable structure. Document which tools degrade on the non-transformer model and hypothesize why (state mixing, no attention pattern to read).

**Why it matters:** As alternative architectures gain adoption, safety monitoring tools built for transformers may not transfer, leaving a class of deployed models harder to audit. A small comparison flags which tools need rework, valuable independent of any lab.

**Relevant context:** Paulo et al., 'Does Transformer Interpretability Transfer to RNNs?' (arXiv:2404.05971) studies whether logit/tuned lens and other tools transfer to RNN architectures; tuned-lens work (arXiv:2303.08112) documents architecture-dependent logit-lens failure.
