# Probing Where a Model Decides to Refuse vs. Comply

**Problem:** A success state: 'we know the layer at which the refuse/comply decision is committed, enabling targeted intervention.' Backcasting from 'localized refusal control', a prerequisite is a layer-resolved map of when the binary refuse/comply decision becomes linearly decodable.

**Direction:** On an open chat model, collect activations for matched harmful (refused) and benign (complied) prompts. Train per-layer linear probes to predict refusal and find the earliest layer where accuracy saturates. Validate causality by patching activations from a complied prompt into a refused prompt at that layer.

**Why it matters:** Pinpointing the decision layer guides where monitoring or intervention is effective, and reveals whether refusal is a late surface decision (easy to jailbreak) or early commitment. Probe-only, open weights, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Refusal is mediated by a linear direction in the residual stream (Steering Refusal arXiv 2411.11296; CAA / refusal-direction literature). Activation patching is standard (Circuit Tracing methods, transformer-circuits.pub).
