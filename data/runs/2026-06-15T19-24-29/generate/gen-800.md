# A Public Read-Team Probe of KYC/Structured-Access Policy Loopholes

**Problem:** Structured access and KYC are proposed to gate dangerous-capability access, but the policy text describing who is screened, on what triggers, and with what exceptions is often vague. There is no public catalogue of the loopholes in published access policies.

**Direction:** Collect public access/usage policies and enterprise terms for several frontier API providers, define a checklist of structured-access controls (KYC triggers, output-volume thresholds, monitoring scope, revocation, fine-tuning carve-outs), and score each policy. Catalogue the most common gaps and write a 'structured-access maturity' scorecard.

**Why it matters:** Structured access is the main lever for safe deployment outside labs; an external maturity benchmark helps governance bodies see where access controls are weakest, using only published policy text.

**Relevant context:** 'Structured access' (arXiv 2201.05159); 'Effective Mitigations for Systemic Risks from GPAI' (arXiv 2412.02145) on KYC and monitoring; public API usage policies.
