# Cross-Modality Safety Gap: Benign Image + Benign Text -> Harmful Output

**Problem:** Most safety benchmarks are text-only. A specific multimodal failure — where an image and text are each individually safe but their combination elicits unsafe output — is barely covered by existing evals.

**Direction:** Construct a small (~150-item) probe set of image+text pairs where each modality is independently benign but jointly suggestive of a harmful action. Run an open vision-language model, measure the cross-modal harmful-completion rate, and compare against text-only and image-only baselines to isolate the compositional gap. Report which harm categories show the largest multimodal-only failures.

**Why it matters:** Builds a genuinely missing multimodal safety benchmark slice; the compositional failure mode is exactly what text-only evals cannot catch.

**Relevant context:** Safe Inputs but Unsafe Output: Benchmarking Cross-modality Safety Alignment of LVLMs; VLSBench (arXiv:2411.19939) on visual leakage; REVEAL (arXiv:2505.04673) multi-turn image harms.
