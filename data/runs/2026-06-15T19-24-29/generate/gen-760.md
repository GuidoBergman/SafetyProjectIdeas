# Are 'catastrophic risk assessment' summaries in transparency reports informative or boilerplate?

**Problem:** SB 53 and the EU Code both require summaries of catastrophic-risk assessments, and the surprising worry is that mandated summaries collapse into uninformative boilerplate that satisfies the letter but not the spirit of disclosure.

**Direction:** Follow up by collecting existing catastrophic-risk / safety-case sections from frontier system cards and frameworks and scoring them on an informativeness rubric (specific quantified claims, named threats, residual-risk acknowledgment vs vague reassurance). Use text-similarity to detect boilerplate reuse across companies and over versions.

**Why it matters:** If mandated risk summaries are boilerplate, the new US/EU transparency laws produce paperwork not insight, a critical finding for regulators designing disclosure standards. Public-document analysis only.

**Relevant context:** California SB 53 catastrophic-risk-summary requirement; EU GPAI Code Safety & Security chapter; Open Problem: operationalizing safety objectives; 'Blueprints of Trust' (arXiv 2509.20394).
