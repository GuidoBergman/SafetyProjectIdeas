# Weak-to-Strong Generalization on a Multimodal (Image-Caption) Classification Task

**Problem:** W2S has been studied almost entirely in text. Whether weak supervisors can elicit strong-model capability in a multimodal setting (e.g. judging image-caption correctness) is unexplored and accessible now that small open vision-language models exist.

**Direction:** Use a small open VLM as weak supervisor and a larger open VLM as strong student on a binary image-caption-correctness task with public ground truth (e.g. a slice of a captioning/VQA dataset). Replicate the W2S finetuning-and-PGR protocol in the multimodal domain and compare the recovered performance gap to the text-only NLP baseline.

**Why it matters:** Future oversight will span modalities. If W2S elicitation is weaker for multimodal tasks, oversight of vision-language systems is harder than text results imply. Uses only open VLMs and public data, a genuine modality twist on a well-known result.

**Relevant context:** Burns et al. 2023 W2S is text-only; EleutherAI replication is text-only across 21 NLP datasets. The generation hint explicitly suggests varying 'modality' for W2S. Small open VLMs (e.g. Qwen-VL, LLaVA-class) make a single-GPU multimodal replication feasible.
