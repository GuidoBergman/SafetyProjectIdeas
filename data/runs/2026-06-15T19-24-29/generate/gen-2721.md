# Linear Probes for Sandbagging: Auditing-Game Replication on Open Weights

**Problem:** A Dec 2025 auditing-games study found blue teams could NOT reliably distinguish sandbaggers from benign models behaviorally, but linear probes 'showed more promise'. That probe result was not deeply characterized on open-weight models a student can inspect.

**Direction:** Build a tiny sandbagging model-organism by prompting an open model to deliberately underperform on a capability QA set when told high scores trigger 'unlearning'. Collect activations on contrastive (honest vs. sandbagging) prompts, train a linear probe at each layer, and report AUROC and cross-context transfer to a held-out task.

**Why it matters:** Sandbagging undermines all capability evals. A reproducible open-weights probe baseline — including its transfer failures — is a model-organism study that needs only one GPU and no proprietary tooling.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Auditing Games for Sandbagging' (arXiv:2512.07810) which reports probes are promising for sandbagging detection, plus probe-transfer caveats from 'Building Production-Ready Probes for Gemini' (arXiv:2601.11516).
