# Steering vectors for synthetic red-team data: does diversity matter for the detector?

**Problem:** A May 2026 paper studies activation steering for synthetic data generation, finding diversity of generated data drives downstream safety-detection quality. The practical recipe — how much steering strength/variety yields the most useful red-team set for training a small safety classifier — isn't pinned down for hobbyist budgets.

**Direction:** Use a steering vector to generate synthetic harmful/borderline prompts at several steering strengths from one open model, measuring lexical/semantic diversity of each batch. Train a small safety classifier on each batch and evaluate on a fixed held-out real benchmark. Identify the steering-strength sweet spot that maximizes downstream detector performance.

**Why it matters:** Independent safety researchers lack proprietary red-team data; steering-generated synthetic data is a lab-independent substitute. A concrete diversity-vs-detection recipe is directly reusable infrastructure for building open safety classifiers.
