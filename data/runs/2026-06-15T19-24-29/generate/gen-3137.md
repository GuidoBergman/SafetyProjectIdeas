# Liars'-Bench Lie-Detector Transfer to a Held-Out Deception Type

**Problem:** Liars' Bench evaluates lie detectors for LLMs, but generalization of a detector trained on one deception type to a structurally different one (e.g. trained on factual lies, tested on strategic sandbagging deception) within one model is underexamined.

**Direction:** Using Liars' Bench data on one open model, train a simple black-box or probe-based lie detector on one category of lies and evaluate its AUROC on a held-out category. Report the cross-category generalization gap.

**Why it matters:** Lie detectors that only catch the lies they were trained on are dangerous; quantifying transfer across deception types tells safety teams how broad a detector's coverage really is. Fully open-data, single-GPU.

**Relevant context:** 'Liars' Bench: Evaluating Lie Detectors for Language Models' (arXiv:2511.16035). Apollo linear probes generalize from instructed deception to realistic deception (arXiv:2509, OpenReview C5Jj3QKQav) but fail on honest confessions.
