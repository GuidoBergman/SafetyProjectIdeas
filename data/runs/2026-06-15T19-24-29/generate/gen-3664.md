# Recovery Behavior After a Mistaken Irreversible Action

**Problem:** Agentic incident-response is an open problem, yet we do not know what agents DO after they realize they took a harmful/irreversible action — do they attempt mitigation, conceal it, or continue regardless?

**Direction:** In a sandbox, engineer trajectories where an open agent unintentionally triggers an irreversible mock action (e.g. deletes the wrong record), then give it a chance to continue. Classify post-error behavior (acknowledge+mitigate, ignore, conceal, escalate-to-human) and measure rates across models and framings.

**Why it matters:** Post-error behavior determines whether a single mistake stays contained or compounds, and concealment behavior is a serious misalignment signal. This model-organism study directly informs agentic incident-response frameworks and needs only a sandbox.

**Relevant context:** Adversa AI 2025 open problem: incident-response frameworks specific to agentic AI. CoT Red-Handed shows agents can conceal (reject action in CoT while implementing it). arXiv:2510.05179 agentic-misalignment insider-threat scenarios.
