# Steering robustness to paraphrase: in-distribution brittleness for safety concepts

**Problem:** Prior work found steering vectors are brittle to 'reasonable changes in the prompt.' This was studied on general concepts; the brittleness of SAFETY steering to benign paraphrasing is what determines whether a deployed control fails on real user phrasing variety.

**Direction:** Build an eval set of harmful requests plus 5 paraphrases each (auto-generated then filtered). Extract a refusal-inducing vector, apply it, and measure how steering effectiveness varies across paraphrase clusters. Quantify variance within meaning-equivalent paraphrases as a brittleness score per concept.

**Why it matters:** Real attackers and users paraphrase; a steering control that only works on canonical phrasings gives false assurance. Fuzzy-reward eval, no training.

**Relevant context:** Reliability finding from arXiv:2407.12404 (brittle to reasonable prompt changes). Beaglehole et al. concept vectors. CAA methodology.
