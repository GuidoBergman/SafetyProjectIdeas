# The Format-Mismatch Link: Does Fine-Tuning in a Different Chat Template Erode Safety More?

**Problem:** Safety erosion from benign fine-tuning may have a subtle upstream link: chat-template/format mismatch. Models are safety-tuned under a specific chat template; fine-tuning users often use a different or stripped template, which may detach the safety behavior from its triggering format. This link between training format and safety persistence is under-studied.

**Direction:** LoRA fine-tune a small safety-tuned model on identical benign data under (a) the model's native chat template and (b) a plain/altered template. Measure post-fine-tuning refusal rates on harmful prompts. Test whether template mismatch specifically accelerates safety erosion, isolating format as a causal link.

**Why it matters:** Open-source fine-tuners constantly mishandle chat templates. If template mismatch is a hidden amplifier of safety loss, that is an extremely actionable, low-cost finding ('always fine-tune in the native template') and a clean characterization of an overlooked link in the safety-erosion chain.

**Relevant context:** 'Preventing Catastrophic Forgetting: Behavior-Aware Sampling' (2510.21885) and 'Attack via Overfitting: 10-shot Benign Fine-tuning to Jailbreak' (2510.02833): benign fine-tuning degrades refusal; format/overfitting dynamics matter. Safety alignment is template-conditioned in instruct models.
