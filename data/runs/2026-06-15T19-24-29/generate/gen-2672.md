# Backcasting Probe Transfer: Does a Deception Probe Trained on One Scenario Catch Sabotage in Another?

**Problem:** Success state: an activation monitor that generalizes — a probe trained on a few deception examples flags deception it never saw. Backward: we need to measure cross-scenario transfer of a simple linear probe on open weights, the bottleneck for real-time activation monitoring.

**Direction:** On an open model (e.g. Llama-3.1-8B), fit a logistic linear probe on residual-stream activations for one deception scenario (e.g. insider-trading concealment prompts), then evaluate AUROC on a DIFFERENT scenario (e.g. sandbagging on a quiz).
