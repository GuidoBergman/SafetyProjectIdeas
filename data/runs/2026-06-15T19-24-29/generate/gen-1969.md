# Distribution-Shift Probe Set for RLHF Reward Models

**Problem:** A core RLHF open problem is that feedback data doesn't cover the deployment distribution, so RMs may be miscalibrated off-distribution. There is no lightweight benchmark that measures how an open RM's confidence/score degrades as inputs move away from its training distribution.

**Direction:** Build a graded out-of-distribution probe set (in-distribution chat, mild domain shift, heavy domain shift, adversarial/jailbreak-style prompts) and measure an open RM's score calibration and preference accuracy at each shift level against a small human-or-strong-judge gold set. Report a 'distribution-shift degradation curve' as a reusable RM stress test.

**Why it matters:** Quantifying RM degradation under shift directly probes a named fundamental limitation of RLHF and gives a reusable, no-training tool to audit released RMs. Deployment-distribution mismatch is a medium-horizon risk labs treat narrowly. Single-GPU inference only.

**Relevant context:** Open Problems and Fundamental Limitations of RLHF (Alignment Forum): RLHF training data doesn't cover deployment distribution; reward model misspecification. 'How to Evaluate Reward Models for RLHF' (arXiv:2410.14872) stresses diverse-representation evaluation. RewardBench provides in-distribution baselines.
