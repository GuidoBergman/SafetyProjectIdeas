# An Open Replication Slice of Frontier AI Third-Party Auditing Criteria

**Problem:** 'Frontier AI Auditing: Toward Rigorous Third-Party Assessment' lays out criteria for auditing safety/security practices at leading companies but, as a framework paper, doesn't demonstrate how much of an audit an unaffiliated researcher can actually complete from public evidence alone.

**Direction:** Select one company, take a defined subset of the paper's audit criteria, and conduct a public-evidence-only mock audit, scoring each criterion as fully/partially/not assessable from outside. Output a reusable 'external-auditability' annotation of the criteria set, flagging which require privileged access.

**Why it matters:** Identifies exactly which parts of third-party auditing can be done counterfactually outside labs vs. which need mandated access — a concrete input to access-policy design. Pure desk research with LLM assistance.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Frontier AI Auditing: Toward Rigorous Third-Party Assessment of Safety and Security Practices at Leading AI Companies' (arXiv:2601.11699, Jan 2026).
