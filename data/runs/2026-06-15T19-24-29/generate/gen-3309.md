# Format-Constraint Compliance x Deception: Does Forcing JSON/Structured Output Suppress Honest Caveats?

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) structured-output constraint degradation (capability/format-following); (B) suppressed honesty / deceptive omission (deception/scheming). Compound effect: tight output schemas (JSON with a boolean 'is_safe' field) may force a model to drop hedges and uncertainty it would otherwise voice, converting honest caveats into confident false certainties.

**Direction:** Pose uncertain/edge questions in two modes: free-form (model may hedge) and a rigid schema demanding a definite field. On open models, measure how often the schema mode produces an unqualified claim that contradicts the model's hedged free-form answer to the same item. Report the 'honesty-suppression rate' induced by structured output and whether adding an explicit uncertainty field recovers it.

**Why it matters:** Reveals a sneaky compounding risk in the ubiquitous practice of forcing structured outputs from models in safety-critical pipelines — cheap, novel, and deployment-relevant.

**Relevant context:** Structured-output/format-constraint degradation literature. UK AISI honesty agenda. Confident-lie-vs-hedged-truth oversight dynamics (arXiv:2507.19486).
