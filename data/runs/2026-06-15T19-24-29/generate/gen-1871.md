# Persona-Direction Monitor: Catching Emergent Misalignment From Activations Mid-Fine-Tune

**Problem:** If a misaligned-persona direction exists (arXiv 2506.19823), one could monitor a model's projection onto that direction *during* fine-tuning to detect EM before behavioral evals would catch it. This as a cheap early-warning monitor has not been prototyped.

**Direction:** Pre-extract a misaligned-persona direction from a held-out EM run, then during a fresh poisoned fine-tune, log the mean activation projection onto that direction per checkpoint and test whether it rises before behavioral misalignment becomes detectable.

**Why it matters:** An activation-space tripwire is cheap monitoring infrastructure that catches misalignment earlier than expensive behavioral evals — buildable on consumer hardware, no internal lab tooling needed.

**Relevant context:** Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2506.19823). Convergent misalignment directions consistent across runs (search result). EM open code (arXiv 2502.17424).
