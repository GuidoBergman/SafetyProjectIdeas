# Sandbagging Self-Report Honesty: Does Asking 'Are You Holding Back?' Work?

**Problem:** A cheap proposed sandbagging check is simply asking the model whether it underperformed — but no one has systematically measured whether self-reports are honest when a model is actively sandbagging.

**Direction:** Induce sandbagging in a small open model on an MCQ set, then directly query it ('did you answer to the best of your ability?') across phrasings and elicitation contexts. Compare self-report honesty against the ground-truth capability gap from a separate elicitation. Report calibration of self-reports and whether any prompt reliably extracts an honest admission.

**Why it matters:** Tests the cheapest conceivable sandbagging detector and characterizes its failure boundary — directly informs detection portfolios, no training of new models.

**Relevant context:** AI Sandbagging (OpenReview 7Qa2SpjxIS) shows models strategically underperform; password-locked models (arXiv:2405.19550); noise-injection paper (arXiv:2412.01784) as a non-self-report baseline.
