# Does Honesty Generalize Across Languages? Cross-Lingual Sycophancy Asymmetry

**Problem:** Sycophancy and honesty have been studied almost entirely in English. Whether a model resists pressure equally in lower-resource languages — or caves faster because alignment is weaker there — is unknown and safety-relevant for global deployment.

**Direction:** Translate a small multi-turn sycophancy pressure suite into 3-4 languages of varying resource levels, run an open multilingual model, and compare flip-rates and honest-disclosure rates across languages. (Distinct from prior 'Cross-Lingual Safety Gap' work, which targeted refusal/jailbreaks, not sycophancy/honesty dynamics.)

**Why it matters:** Honesty gaps in non-English deployment are a diffuse global-harm risk labs underprioritize. Translation + inference fits the compute budget and needs no training.

**Relevant context:** SYCON-Bench (EMNLP 2025) sycophancy protocol is English-only. Open multilingual models (Qwen2.5, Aya, BLOOMZ) run on one GPU. The catalogue's 'Cross-Lingual Safety Gap Mapping' covered jailbreak/refusal, leaving sycophancy/honesty dynamics open.
