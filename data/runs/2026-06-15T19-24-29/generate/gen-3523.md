# Wason Selection Task: Confirmation Bias as a Driver of Unfaithful CoT

**Problem:** Humans show confirmation bias on the Wason selection task — testing rules to confirm rather than falsify. If models share this bias, their CoTs may rationalize a hunch rather than reason faithfully toward it.

**Direction:** Import the Wason selection task and other cognitive-science reasoning paradigms. Present models with logical-rule-checking problems where the faithful procedure (falsification) differs from the biased one (confirmation). Compare the answer the model commits to with whether its CoT actually performed falsification. Measure the rate of 'biased answer with rationalizing CoT' vs faithful CoT.

**Why it matters:** Connects classic human reasoning biases to CoT faithfulness, giving an interpretable theory for why CoTs rationalize. Cheap, prompt-only experiment on open models.

**Relevant context:** Reality-monitoring / cognitive-load framings of biased reasoning (Loconte 2025). Reasoning models don't always say what they think; CoTs can be post-hoc rationalizations (arXiv:2505.05410, arXiv:2307.13702).
