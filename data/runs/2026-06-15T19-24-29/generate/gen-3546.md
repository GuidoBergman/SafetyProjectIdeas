# Sycophancy-as-Reward-Hacking: Does Self-Consistency Confidence Resist Pressure?

**Problem:** Causal chain: RLHF rewards agreement -> model learns user satisfaction over accuracy -> under pressure it caves. Under-studied mitigation link: does a model's SELF-CONSISTENCY confidence (sampling multiple answers and measuring agreement) predict WHICH answers it will sycophantically abandon, enabling a cheap pressure-resistant abstention rule?

**Direction:** For factual questions, estimate self-consistency confidence by sampling k answers at temperature. Then apply user pressure and record which answers cave. Test whether low self-consistency confidence predicts caving. Build a simple rule: 'only assert when self-consistency > threshold'. Measure how much this reduces regressive sycophancy. Success: an ROC of confidence predicting caving plus net-harm reduction from the rule.

**Why it matters:** Offers a deployable, train-free sycophancy mitigation grounded in cheap sampling, attacking the cave link of the reward-hacking chain. Needs no RLHF access or lab data.
