# Epidemiological R0 Model for Open-Weight Capability Proliferation

**Problem:** Compute and weight-proliferation governance lacks a simple quantitative model of how fast a frontier capability spreads to open-weight reproductions, making timelines for 'when does this become ungovernable' hand-wavy.

**Direction:** Transfer the epidemiological reproduction-number / diffusion model. Assemble a public dataset of frontier capability milestones and the dates open-weight models reached comparable scores (from leaderboards/Epoch), then fit a simple diffusion curve and estimate an effective 'proliferation R0' and lag time per capability.

**Why it matters:** Gives compute-governance debates a tractable, data-grounded diffusion estimate (how much lead time governance has) instead of intuition. Uses only public leaderboard/Epoch data.

**Relevant context:** Epoch AI compute/capability tracking; R0 estimation (PLOS One 10.1371/journal.pone.0000282); MIRI compute governance agenda; open-weight leaderboards.
