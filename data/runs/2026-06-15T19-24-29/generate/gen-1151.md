# Prompt-injection generalization: does one injection string compromise a homogeneous model fleet?

**Problem:** Prompt-injection defenses are deployed per-model, but if models share susceptibility (as jailbreaks do via shared representations), a single injection payload may compromise an entire fleet of nominally diverse models — a correlated security failure for agentic deployments.

**Direction:** Take a bank of published prompt-injection payloads. Test each against 6-8 instruction-tuned open/API models in a simple tool-use scaffold. Measure per-payload success across models and the fraction of payloads that succeed on ALL models (universal injections). Relate universality to model lineage.

**Why it matters:** Universal injections turn model diversity into security theater. Demonstrating their prevalence warns agentic-system builders that vendor diversity does not equal attack-surface diversity — directly actionable, infrastructure-free.

**Relevant context:** Builds on jailbreak-transfer-from-shared-representations (2506.12913) extended to prompt injection. Public payloads + open models; no training.
