# Does Intra-Model Repetition Predict Inter-Model Homogeneity?

**Problem:** Hivemind reports two effects — intra-model repetition and inter-model homogeneity — but treats them as parallel observations. Whether a model's intra-model repetitiveness PREDICTS how homogeneous it is with others (a cheap single-model proxy for systemic contribution) is untested.

**Direction:** For each of ~12 models, measure intra-model repetition (self-similarity across multiple samples to the same prompt) and separately its average inter-model homogeneity to the others. Test the correlation: can a single model's repetitiveness, measured in isolation, predict its contribution to ecosystem monoculture?

**Why it matters:** If yes, you can flag high-monoculture-risk models with a cheap single-model test, no comparison fleet required — useful for auditors. Pure inference, minimal compute.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind's two named effects (intra-model repetition, inter-model homogeneity; arXiv:2510.22954).
