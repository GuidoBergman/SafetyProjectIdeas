# Does Monitor Suspicion Calibrate? Score Distributions on SHADE-Arena

**Problem:** SHADE-Arena monitors output suspicion scores, but AUC alone hides whether the scores are calibrated — i.e. whether a threshold chosen on one batch of tasks gives the expected false-positive rate on another. Miscalibration silently breaks audit-budget planning.

**Direction:** On SHADE-Arena transcripts, collect a monitor's continuous suspicion scores for benign and sabotage trajectories. Plot score histograms, fit a threshold to hit a target 1% audit budget on a calibration split, and measure the actual false-positive and catch rates on a held-out split. Report calibration drift across task domains.

**Why it matters:** Real control protocols audit only the top X% most-suspicious actions; if thresholds don't transfer, the audit budget is wasted. This statistics-forward analysis fits the participants' skill set (stats, no training) and is highly lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** SHADE-Arena (arXiv:2506.15740) uses monitor suspicion scores and AUC. Control protocols (Ctrl-Z, arXiv:2504.10374) audit a small fraction of most-suspicious actions, making threshold calibration safety-critical.
