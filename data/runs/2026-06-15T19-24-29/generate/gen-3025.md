# Saturation Compression Without Leakage: Replicating the Gap-Narrowing Effect on Open Models

**Problem:** A recent benchmark-saturation study claims repeated optimization against a fixed eval format compresses performance gaps among models even without explicit contamination. This 'format overfitting' effect has not been demonstrated in a small controlled setting a participant can run.

**Direction:** Take a safety benchmark and create two presentation formats (original vs reformatted/paraphrased same items). Evaluate several open models on both. Test whether model ranking and gaps differ between the fixed-format and reformatted versions, isolating format-specific optimization from true capability.

**Why it matters:** If safety benchmark rankings are partly an artifact of format familiarity, cross-model safety comparisons (a NIST/CAISI priority) are unreliable. A clean demonstration with public models gives evaluators evidence for why benchmarks need format-robust reporting.

**Relevant context:** When AI Benchmarks Plateau: A Systematic Study of Benchmark Saturation (arXiv:2602.16763, Feb 2026) argues repeated exposure to fixed formats narrows observable gaps. LLM Benchmark Datasets Should Be Contamination-Resistant (arXiv:2605.19999).
