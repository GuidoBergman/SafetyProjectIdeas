# Does fine-tuning that induces emergent misalignment also degrade CoT faithfulness?

**Problem:** Emergent misalignment broadly corrupts behavior after narrow fine-tuning; CoT faithfulness measures whether stated reasoning matches real computation. It is unknown whether emergently-misaligned models become MORE unfaithful (hiding the corrupted reasoning) — which would mean misalignment and unmonitorability arrive together, a worst case for CoT-based control.

**Direction:** Compare a base small open model and a lightly fine-tuned emergently-misaligned variant on a faithfulness battery: insert answer-biasing cues and measure how often each model's CoT acknowledges the cue (Turpin-style), and run counterfactual perturbations to see if CoT explanations track actual decision drivers. Report whether misalignment fine-tuning specifically reduces faithfulness vs leaving it intact.

**Why it matters:** If corruption and unfaithfulness co-occur, CoT monitors fail exactly when most needed — a critical, medium-horizon finding for control. Uses an open recipe at small scale; behavioral measurement, no proprietary tooling.

**Relevant context:** Bridges alignment_training (emergent misalignment, Betley et al. ICML 2025 / Nature 2026, arxiv 2502.17424) with honesty_faithfulness (CoT faithfulness; biased-feature rationalization; CoT monitorability, arxiv 2510.27378).
