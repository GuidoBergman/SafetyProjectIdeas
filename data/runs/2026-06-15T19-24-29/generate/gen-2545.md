# Human-Auditing Top-2% Protocol Replicated with Crowd-Style Cheap Labels

**Problem:** Early human-auditing experiments for AI control studied how human reviewers catch backdoors in the audited top-suspicious slice, but used a specific reviewer setup; how cheap, non-expert auditing (or a cheap LLM proxy for a human) performs is underexplored.

**Direction:** Replicate the human-auditing-of-top-2% pipeline on the APPS backdoor set, but substitute a cheap, time-boxed reviewer process (self/peer or a deliberately weak LLM standing in for a rushed human) and measure catch rate vs review-time budget. Twist: plot catch rate as a function of seconds-per-sample to estimate the auditing labor cost of control.

**Why it matters:** Auditing is the human bottleneck in control. Quantifying the catch-rate-vs-effort curve informs how much human review control actually requires and is doable by a student without lab resources.
