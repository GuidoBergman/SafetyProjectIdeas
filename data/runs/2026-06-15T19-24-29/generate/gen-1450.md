# SteerVsPrompt: A Benchmark Comparing Steering Vectors to Plain System-Prompt Control

**Problem:** Steering vectors are promoted as a control method, but there is no standardized benchmark showing when activation steering actually beats a well-crafted system prompt for the same safety behavior, including robustness to jailbreaks.

**Direction:** For 4-5 safety behaviors, compare three controls on an open model: system prompt only, steering vector only, and both, measuring behavior effect size, capability cost, and robustness against an adversarial prompt set. Report where steering adds value over prompting and where it doesn't.

**Why it matters:** If steering rarely beats a good prompt, the field should know before investing in steering infrastructure; if it does (esp. under jailbreaks), that justifies the tooling. A fair head-to-head benchmark is missing and is fully reproducible.

**Relevant context:** CAA reports steering works 'on top of system prompt design' but a standardized head-to-head robustness benchmark is absent. AdvBench/JailbreakBench and CAA behaviors are open.
