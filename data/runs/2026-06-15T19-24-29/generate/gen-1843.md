# Do Audio-Model SAE Features Encode Safety-Relevant Speaker Attributes?

**Problem:** Mechanistic Interpretability of ASR Models using SAEs (arXiv:2605.12225, May 2026) is the first SAE study on speech recognizers but focuses on phonetic features; whether the learned SAE features also encode sensitive speaker attributes (accent, demographic cues) that could drive biased downstream behavior is unexamined.

**Direction:** Using the released Whisper-SAE setup (or retraining a small TopK SAE on Whisper encoder frames), probe whether individual SAE latents separate speaker accent/demographic groups on a small public speech dataset. Report which latents are attribute-selective and how concentrated the encoding is.

**Why it matters:** Speech models are increasingly safety-relevant (voice agents); finding that demographic attributes are sparsely and legibly encoded is a fairness/safety signal achievable with one consumer GPU and open audio data.

**Relevant context:** Mechanistic Interpretability of ASR models using Sparse Autoencoders (arXiv:2605.12225, May 12 2026): first SAE study on ASR (Whisper), TopK SAE on frame-level encoder embeddings.
