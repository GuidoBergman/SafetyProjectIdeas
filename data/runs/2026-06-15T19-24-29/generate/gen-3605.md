# Quantization x Honesty: Does Compressing a Model Degrade Truthfulness More Than Capability?

**Problem:** Deployment-time quantization (an efficiency pressure) may compound with honesty failures: a quantized model could retain task accuracy while sycophancy and miscalibration worsen disproportionately — a safety regression hidden behind stable capability metrics.

**Direction:** Take one open model at full precision and at 8-bit/4-bit (bitsandbytes, fits a consumer GPU). Compare on TruthfulQA, a sycophancy-flip test, and verbalized-confidence calibration. Test whether honesty metrics degrade faster than a capability benchmark (e.g., MMLU subset). Report relative degradation.

**Why it matters:** Most real deployments quantize; if honesty is the first casualty, safety evals on the full-precision model overstate the deployed model's honesty. Pure inference, single GPU, no training. Names: quantization-induced degradation and honesty/calibration failure.

**Relevant context:** Calibration survey notes post-training procedures degrade uncertainty estimation; sycophancy is RLHF-induced and may be brittle under compression. TruthfulQA / SimpleQA as honesty benchmarks; bitsandbytes enables consumer-GPU quantization.
