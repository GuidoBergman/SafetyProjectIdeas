# Multimodal Hivemind: Do Vision-Language Models Caption the Same Way?

**Problem:** Artificial Hivemind's '(and Beyond)' gestures at non-text modalities but the public benchmark is text. Whether open-ended image captioning / visual-question answering exhibits the same inter-model homogeneity is untested, yet VLMs increasingly gate safety-relevant decisions (content moderation, accessibility).

**Direction:** Port the Hivemind inter-model similarity method to small open VLMs (e.g. LLaVA-class, Qwen-VL small). On a fixed set of ambiguous/open-ended images, collect free-form captions/answers from several VLMs and measure cross-model output similarity, comparing against the text-domain homogeneity baseline reported in the paper.

**Why it matters:** If VLMs share captioning blind spots, correlated moderation/accessibility failures propagate across products built on different VLMs. Extends the flagged 'multimodal' follow-up; runnable with small quantized VLMs on a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind '(and Beyond)' (arXiv:2510.22954); open VLMs (LLaVA, Qwen-VL).
