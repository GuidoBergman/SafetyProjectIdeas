**Problem:** A March 2026 paper argues grokking (delayed generalization) is driven by excess architectural degrees of freedom — unconstrained representational magnitude and data-dependent attention routing. The claim deserves independent replication on the canonical modular-arithmetic task.

**Direction:** Train a tiny transformer (fits on a single GPU in minutes) on modular addition — this is small-scale, not 'training from scratch' at frontier scale.
