# GSAE Graph-Regularized Safety Steering vs Plain SAE: A Robustness Bake-Off

**Problem:** GSAE: Graph-Regularized Sparse Autoencoders for Robust LLM Safety Steering (arXiv:2512.06655) applies graph-Laplacian regularization to better capture distributed safety concepts, but the claimed robustness advantage over plain SAE steering is not independently benchmarked against jailbreaks.

**Direction:** On a small open model, compare safety steering using a plain SAE vs a lightweight graph-regularized SAE (or the released GSAE if available) on the same jailbreak test set, measuring both jailbreak-resistance and capability retention. Report whether graph regularization actually buys robustness without extra capability cost.

**Why it matters:** If distributed-concept regularization genuinely improves the safety-capability frontier, that is a deployable steering improvement; an honest head-to-head is decision-relevant and fully open-model.

**Relevant context:** GSAE: Graph-Regularized Sparse Autoencoders for Robust LLM Safety Steering (arXiv:2512.06655): graph Laplacian regularization to capture distributed concepts; baseline tradeoff from Steering Language Model Refusal with SAEs (arXiv:2411.11296).
