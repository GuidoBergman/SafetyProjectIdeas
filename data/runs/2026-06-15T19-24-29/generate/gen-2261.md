# DebateGain-Calculator: A Standardized Effect-Size Tool for Whether Debate Helps a Given Judge

**Problem:** The debate literature reports gains in some settings and null effects in others, but there is no standard tool that, given a judge model and a task, computes the calibrated effect size and statistical power of 'debate vs no-debate' so researchers can compare apples to apples.

**Direction:** Build a small evaluation tool that runs matched debate / no-debate conditions on the same items, computes accuracy delta with bootstrap confidence intervals and required-sample-size estimates, and outputs a standardized report card. Validate on an open judge + QuALITY subset.

**Why it matters:** Mixed debate results often stem from under-powered, non-standardized evaluations; a reusable effect-size + power tool improves the rigor and comparability of the whole subfield at near-zero compute cost.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. 2024 report 'mixed' results; Khan et al. 2024 report p=0.04 for human debate vs consultancy; scaling laws for scalable oversight (arXiv 2504.18530) emphasize effect dependence on model gap.
