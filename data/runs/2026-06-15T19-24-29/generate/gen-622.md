# Multi-Turn Sycophancy Erodes Refusal Robustness: Measuring the Compounding Slope

**Problem:** Single-turn refusal robustness (safety training) and multi-turn sycophantic drift (RLHF) compound: each agreeable turn nudges the model's effective persona, lowering the barrier to a later harmful compliance. The per-turn erosion rate, and whether it accelerates, is not characterized.

**Direction:** On open chat models, design benign-to-borderline multi-turn conversations that incrementally apply social/sycophantic pressure. Measure refusal rate as a function of turn index and prior agreement count. Fit whether refusal probability decays linearly or super-linearly with accumulated sycophantic concessions, quantifying the compounding slope across models.

**Why it matters:** Real deployments are multi-turn; showing refusal robustness is a depletable resource eroded by sycophancy gives a concrete, lab-deprioritized failure mode and a reusable evaluation. Inference-only on open models.

**Relevant context:** Multi-turn jailbreak amplification and sycophancy interconnection (DuET-PD, arXiv:2508.17450; arXiv:2510.27062).
