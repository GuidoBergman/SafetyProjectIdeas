# Quantifying the 'Idea Bottleneck': Does Brainstorming with Many Models Beat One?

**Problem:** Artificial Hivemind shows brainstorming/ideation outputs homogenize. A safety-relevant corollary: if AI is used to generate the *space of options* (e.g. mitigations, hypotheses, red-team attacks), homogeneity silently shrinks the option space, and using more models adds little. This 'idea bottleneck' is unmeasured for safety-relevant ideation.

**Direction:** On safety-flavored ideation tasks (list failure modes of a system; propose red-team strategies; brainstorm misuse vectors), collect outputs from many models. Measure the marginal new-idea yield of adding each additional model (coverage curve). Quantify how quickly the union of ideas saturates — the bottleneck point.

**Why it matters:** If the option space saturates after 2-3 models, then AI-assisted safety brainstorming has a hard ceiling and human diversity remains essential. A direct, decision-relevant extension of the Best Paper into a safety workflow.
