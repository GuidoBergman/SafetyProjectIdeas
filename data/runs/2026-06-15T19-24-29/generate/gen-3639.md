# Contextual Sycophancy Cost in a Human-AI Collaboration Sim

**Problem:** A recent paper documents a 'hidden cost of contextual sycophancy' and tests an AI-literacy intervention in human-AI collaboration. Whether a lightweight prompt-level literacy nudge to the model itself (rather than the human) reduces contextual sycophancy is untested.

**Direction:** Set up a simple collaborative task where the model is given prior context that biases it toward agreeing with a (sometimes wrong) human partner. Compare a control prompt vs. a 'be epistemically independent / flag disagreement' nudge. Measure contextual sycophancy rate and task-correctness impact across small open models.

**Why it matters:** Contextual sycophancy (caving because of accumulated conversational context) is the real-world failure mode the steering highlights. A cheap model-side mitigation study is practical, deployable defensive work needing no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'The Hidden Cost of Contextual Sycophancy: an AI Literacy Intervention in Human-AI Collaboration' (arXiv 2605.18372, ~May 2026).
