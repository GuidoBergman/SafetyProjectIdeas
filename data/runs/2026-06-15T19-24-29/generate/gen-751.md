**Problem:** FLI found only three of seven labs demonstrate meaningful dangerous-capability testing, and labs report these results in system cards; but third parties rarely check whether the reported eval numbers are reproducible on open analogues.

**Direction:** Follow up by selecting a few dangerous-capability evals labs report in system cards that have open implementations (e.g., a published jailbreak or cyber-knowledge benchmark), running them on accessible open models on a single GPU, and comparing the shape/trend of results to what labs report.
