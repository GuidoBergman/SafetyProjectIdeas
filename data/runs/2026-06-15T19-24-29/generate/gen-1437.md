# GeneralizationGap: Benchmarking On-Distribution vs Off-Distribution Steering

**Problem:** Steering vectors are often validated on data resembling their construction set; there is no standard benchmark quantifying how much steering effect survives a distribution shift to realistic, out-of-format prompts.

**Direction:** For several CAA behaviors, build a paired evaluation: the in-distribution multiple-choice format vs an out-of-distribution open-ended / different-domain format, and report the 'generalization gap' (effect retained off-distribution). Produce a ranked table of which behaviors generalize and which collapse.

**Why it matters:** Overstated generalization is a key reliability risk for steering-based safety; a standardized gap metric stops methods from looking good only on their own format. Fully reproducible on open models with no training.

**Relevant context:** Prior work (arxiv 2407.12404 'Analysing the Generalisation and Reliability of Steering Vectors') flags generalization concerns; CAA uses MC datasets plus TruthfulQA/MWE. No standardized gap benchmark exists.
