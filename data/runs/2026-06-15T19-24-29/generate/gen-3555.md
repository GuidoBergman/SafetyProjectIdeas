# Does Faithfulness Survive Distillation? Comparing Teacher and Student CoT

**Problem:** Causal chain for the open ecosystem: a strong model's CoT is distilled into smaller students -> students inherit reasoning style -> but faithfulness may not transfer. Under-studied link: are distilled student CoTs MORE or LESS faithful (per hint-verbalization / paraphrase tests) than their teachers?

**Direction:** Take a distilled student model and (where available) its teacher family. Run identical hint-verbalization and filler-token faithfulness tests on both. Compare faithfulness scores. Success: a teacher-vs-student faithfulness comparison and discussion of whether distillation preserves or erodes the monitorability of CoT.

**Why it matters:** Most open reasoning models people actually run are distilled. If distillation degrades CoT faithfulness, the very models the community relies on are harder to monitor — a counterfactually valuable finding requiring only inference on open weights.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic (arXiv 2505.05410) found larger/more-capable models produce less faithful reasoning; distilled reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-distill family) are widely available. Comparing teacher vs. distilled-student faithfulness is an under-studied, accessible question.
