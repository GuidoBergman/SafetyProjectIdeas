# Ensemble-Monitor Gain Replicated with Cheap Open Monitors

**Problem:** ControlArena reports ensemble_monitor (multiple temperature samples aggregated) as an alternative to basic_monitor, but the marginal detection gain from ensembling is mostly characterized for capable monitors. Whether ensembling rescues a WEAK open-weight monitor — and by how much — is the practically important open question.

**Direction:** On a fixed set of ControlArena attack/benign trajectories, compare basic_monitor vs ensemble_monitor (varying ensemble size) backed by a small open-weight model. Measure detection improvement per added sample and the compute cost, replicating the ensemble-vs-single comparison at the low-capability end.

**Why it matters:** Ensembling is the cheapest available lever for independent deployers running weak monitors; quantifying its payoff curve is strategically valuable monitoring infrastructure. Fully training-free and reproducible on consumer hardware.

**Relevant context:** ControlArena: ensemble_monitor runs multiple copies of the same prompt at temperature and aggregates; standard single-score monitors miss subtle attacks and collapse under same-model collusion (TraceGuard, 2604.03968).
