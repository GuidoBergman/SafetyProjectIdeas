# Reproducing the Hidden-Objective Audit on a Tiny Open Model Organism

**Problem:** Anthropic's 'Auditing language models for hidden objectives' showed interpretability could surface a deliberately trained-in hidden objective, but used a bespoke model and team. A minimal, reproducible open version that newcomers can run is missing.

**Direction:** Take a small open model fine-tuned (or use an existing open 'model organism' such as a sleeper-agent/backdoor checkpoint released publicly) and apply cheap interpretability — SAE feature inspection or linear probing on the residual stream — to detect the trigger/hidden behavior without being told the trigger. Report detection success and false-positive rate.

**Why it matters:** Hidden-objective / secret-goal model organisms are a top medium-horizon threat. Demonstrating that cheap, lab-free interpretability can (or cannot) catch them is exactly the high-counterfactual-value model-organism work the steering prioritizes.

**Relevant context:** 'Auditing language models for hidden objectives' (arXiv 2503.10965): blind auditing teams used interp to find a planted objective. Public backdoor/sleeper-agent model checkpoints and TransformerLens-compatible small models enable a scoped replication.
