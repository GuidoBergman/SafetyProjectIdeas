# Are 'capability threshold' definitions measurable? An operationalizability audit

**Problem:** RSP-style frameworks define capability thresholds that trigger safety measures, but Open Problems in Technical AI Governance flags the gap between safety objectives and operationalizable deployment practice. Many thresholds may be unmeasurable as written.

**Direction:** Vary framework analysis into an operationalizability coding experiment: extract every capability threshold from public frameworks and code each on whether it names (a) a concrete benchmark/eval, (b) a numeric or behavioral trigger, (c) who decides, (d) a deadline. Compute an 'operationalizability score' per threshold and per lab.

**Why it matters:** Translates the abstract operationalization open problem into a measurable scorecard regulators can use to demand testable triggers. Public text, beginner rubric coding, no lab access.

**Relevant context:** Open Problems in Technical AI Governance (Reuel et al., arXiv:2407.14981, operationalization); METR Common Elements; Measurement challenges in AI catastrophic risk governance (arXiv:2410.00608).
