# Does California SB 53 transparency-report structure match what labs already disclose?

**Problem:** California's SB 53 (effective 2026) mandates specific transparency-report fields (release date, intended uses, catastrophic-risk-assessment summaries, third-party involvement). Whether existing system cards already cover these fields or leave large gaps is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Follow up the law's passage with a gap analysis: build a checklist from SB 53's required fields and score recent frontier system cards (Claude, GPT, Gemini) for coverage. Quantify compliance distance and which mandated fields are most commonly missing before the law takes effect.

**Why it matters:** A pre-enforcement baseline shows how much real change SB 53 will force vs ratify existing practice, informing whether the first US frontier law has teeth. Public documents only, replicable annually.
