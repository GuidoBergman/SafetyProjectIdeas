# Multimodal Shallow Alignment: Image-Channel Prefill Variation

**Problem:** Qi et al.'s shallow alignment finding is text-only. Small open vision-language models add an image channel; whether harmful content smuggled via the image (rather than text prefix) bypasses the same shallow text-side safety is an unexplored variation.

**Direction:** On a small open VLM (e.g. Qwen2-VL-2B/7B, LLaVA-based small model), compare three conditions for matched harmful intents: harmful text request, harmful request rendered as an image of text, and harmful request split across image+benign text. Measure refusal rate per condition. Test whether a short safe text prefill restores refusal even when the harm enters via image.

**Why it matters:** Extends the most important recent robustness result to multimodal models, the explicit FR67 hint in the context file. Reveals whether safety training transfers across modalities or whether the image channel is a soft underbelly — a medium-horizon risk as VLMs proliferate. Runs on one GPU with public small VLMs.
