# Weak-to-Strong Generalization Under Distribution Shift: An In-Context Replication

**Problem:** Weak-to-Strong Generalization under Distribution Shifts (arXiv:2510.21332, Oct 2025) studies when weak supervision still elicits strong performance off-distribution. Most work uses fine-tuning; whether the distribution-shift fragility shows up in cheaper in-context weak-to-strong setups is open.

**Direction:** Construct an in-context weak-to-strong setup: weak-model labels as few-shot supervision for a stronger model, evaluated on (a) in-distribution and (b) shifted test sets. Measure how much of the weak-to-strong gain survives the shift, and whether the strong model defaults back to weak-supervisor errors out-of-distribution.

**Why it matters:** Deployment is always under shift. Showing whether weak supervision generalizes off-distribution is central to trusting weak overseers, and an in-context version is feasible in 30 hours with API/small-model inference.

**Relevant context:** Weak-to-Strong Generalization under Distribution Shifts (arXiv:2510.21332, Oct 2025): examines whether weak-to-strong generalization persists under distribution shift. Original framing in Weak-to-Strong Generalization (arXiv:2312.09390).
