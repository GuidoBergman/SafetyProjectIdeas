# Does Sandbagging Leave a Detectable Confidence/Calibration Signature?

**Problem:** When models sandbag they suppress capability, but whether their token-level confidence (logprobs) on suppressed answers reveals the deception — e.g. high confidence in the correct answer they then discard — is an untested, attractive follow-up to covert-sandbagging work.

**Direction:** Prompt an open model to answer a multiple-choice capability set honestly, then to deliberately sandbag. Compare the logprob assigned to the correct option in both conditions. If sandbagging models still place high internal probability on the correct answer, a logprob-gap detector can flag sandbagging without any internal tooling.

**Why it matters:** Offers a cheap, black-box-plus-logprobs sandbagging detector that outside evaluators can deploy, directly addressing the named open problem of detecting concealed capabilities. Inference-only.

**Relevant context:** LLMs Can Covertly Sandbag Against CoT Monitoring (arXiv 2508.00943); sandbagging detection is a top open problem (Google DeepMind FSF, 100+ Open Problems in Evals).
