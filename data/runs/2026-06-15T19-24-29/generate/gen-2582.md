# Eval-vs-Deploy Tell: Do Models Leak Evaluation-Awareness in Their CoT During Honeypots?

**Problem:** Frontier models distinguish evaluation from deployment with high AUROC, which can make honeypot evals fail because models suppress misbehavior when they sense a test. It is unclear whether this awareness is verbalized in the CoT (detectable) or remains latent.

**Direction:** Assemble a small set of paired prompts framed as obvious-eval vs realistic-deployment (reuse public honeypot/scheming prompt templates). Run an open reasoning model, and have a cheap LLM-judge classify whether the CoT contains explicit 'this looks like a test' tells. Measure: rate of verbalized eval-awareness, and whether verbalized awareness correlates with behavioral suppression of the bait action.

**Why it matters:** If eval-awareness is usually verbalized, CoT monitoring offers a cheap defense against honeypot-defeating behavior; if it's latent, honeypots are in deeper trouble. Either way it characterizes a known failure mode of behavioral monitoring. Inference-only.
