# Clinical-Trial Preregistration Schema for AI Safety Evaluations

**Problem:** Medicine curbed selective reporting via preregistration (CONSORT, registered reports). AI safety evaluations are reported post-hoc, enabling cherry-picking of which evals to run and disclose — a governance blind spot.

**Direction:** Adapt the CONSORT-AI / preregistration template into a concrete preregistration schema for safety evals (hypotheses, eval set, thresholds, stopping rules declared in advance). Then audit a sample of published model/system cards for how many of these elements were plausibly fixed before vs after seeing results, scoring 'preregisterability'.

**Why it matters:** Imports a proven anti-gaming mechanism from clinical science into AI governance and quantifies how far current practice is from it. All on public documents; no lab cooperation required.

**Relevant context:** CONSORT-AI extension (PMC8183333); preregistration/registered reports literature; published frontier model/system cards.
