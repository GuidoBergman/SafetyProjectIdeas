# Do Vision-SAE Reconstructions That Lower Loss Encode Spurious Shortcuts?

**Problem:** Prisma observed the surprising result that vision-SAE reconstructions can decrease model loss, the opposite of language SAEs. It is unknown whether this 'helpful' reconstruction is denoising genuine signal or amplifying spurious texture/background shortcuts.

**Direction:** Using a pre-trained vision SAE and ViT, compare classifications on original vs. SAE-reconstructed inputs on images with known spurious cues (e.g. backgrounds, textures). Test whether the loss-decreasing reconstructions specifically help on shortcut-correlated examples vs. shortcut-broken examples, revealing whether SAEs encode safety-relevant spurious features.

**Why it matters:** If interpretability tools inadvertently surface or strengthen spurious shortcuts, they could mislead safety audits of vision models (e.g. medical/autonomous systems). A model-organism-style probe of an existing open ViT, no lab data needed.

**Relevant context:** Prisma (arXiv 2504.19475): vision-SAE reconstructions can reduce model loss. Spurious-correlation/shortcut-learning is a documented ViT failure mode; ConceptScope (arXiv 2510.26186) characterizes dataset bias via disentangled visual concepts.
