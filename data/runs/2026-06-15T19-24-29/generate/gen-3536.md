# Progressive vs. Regressive Sycophancy: Isolating the Harmful Link

**Problem:** Not all user-pressure conformity is bad: pressure that corrects a model error (progressive) is good; pressure that introduces a new error (regressive) is the safety-relevant link. Existing sycophancy benchmarks lump these together, inflating or deflating the apparent problem.

**Direction:** Build a small balanced eval: items where the model is initially CORRECT (so any flip is regressive) and items where it is initially WRONG (so a flip can be progressive). Apply identical pressure phrasings to both. Report a 2x2 confusion of flip behavior and a single 'net harm' metric (regressive flips minus progressive flips). Compare across 2-3 open model sizes. Success: a clean decomposition showing how much measured sycophancy is genuinely harmful.

**Why it matters:** Reframes sycophancy measurement so the harmful causal link is isolated, preventing both over- and under-statement. Pure behavioral eval, no training, no lab resources — high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** 'Diagnosing and Mitigating Sycophancy and Skepticism in LLM Causal Judgment' (arXiv 2601.08258) and 'Sycophancy Is Not One Thing' (arXiv 2509.21305) argue sycophancy is multi-faceted and call for causal separation of behaviors. Progressive/regressive framing is recent and under-operationalized in a simple eval.
