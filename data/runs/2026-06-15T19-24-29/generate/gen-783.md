# Replicating Model-Spec Honesty/Sycophancy Probes on Open Models With a User-Authority Twist

**Problem:** Model specs encode honesty and instruction-hierarchy norms, and spec evals test whether models obey developer over user when they conflict. A governance-relevant failure is sycophancy: models abandoning correct factual stances when a user asserts authority or disagrees. How open models handle the spec's honesty-vs-deference tension is under-measured.

**Direction:** Replicate the spec's honesty/instruction-hierarchy probes on open models, with the twist of systematically varying asserted user authority and disagreement pressure across a fixed factual battery. Measure how often each model abandons a correct answer under pressure (sycophancy rate) versus holding the spec-compliant honest line.

**Why it matters:** Honesty under social pressure is a fuzzy-reward safety property hard to evaluate and easy for labs to under-prioritize; it bears on whether spec-based norms are robust governance instruments. Open spec, open models, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in OpenAI Model Spec / model_spec_evals (instruction hierarchy, honesty) and SpecEval (arXiv 2509.02464). Twist = systematic user-authority/disagreement pressure to measure sycophancy against spec.
