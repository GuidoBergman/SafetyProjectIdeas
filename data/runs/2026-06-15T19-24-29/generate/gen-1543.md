# Does the Multilingual 'Universal Feature' Finding Hold in Small Open Models?

**Problem:** Circuit tracing reported cross-linguistic generalization (shared language-agnostic features) in Claude 3.5 Haiku. Whether small open multilingual models share concept features across languages — or instead keep language-specific representations — is a replication gap.

**Direction:** Using an open multilingual model (e.g. BLOOM-560M or a multilingual Pythia-like model) in TransformerLens, identify a concept (e.g. 'opposite of small') in several languages, then test whether the same residual-stream direction / SAE latent activates across languages via cosine similarity and cross-lingual activation patching.

**Why it matters:** Shared cross-lingual concept representations imply safety interventions (e.g. unlearning harmful knowledge) might transfer across languages — or fail to. Testable on open models, high relevance to multilingual safety coverage.
