# Splitting persona-effects on honesty into a steering-vector slice: does a 'helpful assistant' persona vector increase sycophancy?

**Problem:** Honesty research flags persona effects (system prompts changing honesty). Concept-steering offers a mechanistic handle. Sub-problem: can we extract a 'sycophantic persona' direction and show that activating it increases sycophancy while a contrastive 'blunt critic' direction decreases it, isolating persona-as-a-vector?

**Direction:** Decompose persona-effects into steering-vector extraction. On a small open model, build contrastive prompt pairs (sycophantic vs blunt personas), extract the difference-of-means steering vector, and causally test whether adding/subtracting it shifts measured sycophancy on a held-out pushback benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Turns a fuzzy behavioral observation (persona affects honesty) into a causal, mechanistic result on open models. Single GPU, no training. Provides a cheap monitoring/steering lever for sycophancy that anyone can replicate — strong counterfactual value.
