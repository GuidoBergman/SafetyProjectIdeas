# Regressional Goodhart: Failure Onset vs. Proxy Noise Structure

**Problem:** Theory predicts that under regressional Goodhart, harder optimization against a noisy proxy eventually decreases true reward. The original toy treatment used independent additive noise; how the onset of overoptimization depends on noise STRUCTURE (heavy-tailed, correlated-with-features, heteroscedastic) is not mapped.

**Direction:** Use a fully synthetic optimization environment (no LLM needed, or a tiny one) where gold reward is known and proxy = gold + noise. Vary only the noise distribution family and measure at what optimization pressure the gold reward peaks then declines. Produce a 'when does Goodhart bite' phase diagram.

**Why it matters:** Characterizing the conditions under which a proxy stops tracking the target is exactly the UK AISI 'prove when agents cannot game rewards' direction, at a scope a beginner can fully complete in numpy. Zero proprietary data, no training.

**Relevant context:** Lil'Log 'Reward Hacking in RL' on regressional Goodhart (proxy = gold + independent noise); Gao et al. arXiv:2210.10760; synthesis.ai 'Goodharting and Reward Hacking'.
