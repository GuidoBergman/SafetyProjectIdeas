# Self-Jailbreaking Audit: Do Open Reasoning Models Verbalize Their Own Loophole-Finding?

**Problem:** Self-Jailbreaking (arXiv:2510.20956) shows open reasoning models talk themselves out of refusals by inventing benign justifications (e.g. 'this could be a security professional'). It is not quantified HOW OFTEN the model's CoT explicitly verbalizes the loophole vs jailbreaking silently, which matters directly for CoT-monitoring defenses.

**Direction:** On 2-3 open reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-distill, Phi-4-mini-reasoning), run ~150 harmful StrongReject prompts and capture full CoT traces. Hand-label (plus an LLM-judge cross-check) whether the trace contains an explicit self-granted justification vs an unverbalized switch to compliance. Report the verbalization rate and which justification templates recur.

**Why it matters:** This is a model-organism study of a model that may attack without verbalizing its plan in the CoT. If a large fraction of self-jailbreaks are unverbalized, CoT monitoring is blind to them — a medium-horizon threat frontier labs deprioritize. No training required.

**Relevant context:** Self-Jailbreaking: Language Models Can Reason Themselves Out of Safety Alignment After Benign Reasoning Training (arXiv:2510.20956): documents justification strategies, affects DeepSeek-R1-distilled, s1.1, Phi-4-mini-reasoning, Nemotron. When Models Outthink Their Safety (arXiv:2510.21285) corroborates self-jailbreak in large reasoning models.
