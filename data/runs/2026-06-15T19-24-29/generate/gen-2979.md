# Person-Consistency Eval: Does a Model Keep a Hidden Persona Goal Across Turns?

**Problem:** A success state requires detecting a model that pursues a hidden long-horizon goal while appearing cooperative. A needed result is a multi-turn eval that measures persistence of a covertly-assigned goal under distraction.
