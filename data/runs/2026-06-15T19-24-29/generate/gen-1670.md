# Cross-Model Feature Transfer Benchmark for Safety Concepts

**Problem:** It is unknown how well a probe or steering vector for a safety concept (e.g., refusal) found in one open model transfers to another model of similar size. There is no benchmark quantifying transfer, yet transfer is assumed when people reuse published vectors.

**Direction:** Build a small transfer benchmark: extract a concept direction in model A, then measure its detection/steering efficacy when naively applied to model B (after aligning hidden dims via a simple linear map). Cover 2-3 small open models and 2-3 concepts. Report a transfer matrix and identify which concepts are 'universal' vs. model-specific.

**Why it matters:** If safety monitors and steering vectors don't transfer across models, every deployment needs re-derivation; if they do, that's evidence of shared representational structure. Either way it's a reusable, lab-free measurement of an unmeasured property directly relevant to scalable monitoring.

**Relevant context:** Steering work (e.g., ICLR 2025 refusal direction, Rogue Scalpel) is typically validated on a single model/layer (Llama-3 8B, Layer 25), explicitly flagged as a generalizability limitation. TransformerLens hosts 50+ models enabling cross-model comparison without training.
