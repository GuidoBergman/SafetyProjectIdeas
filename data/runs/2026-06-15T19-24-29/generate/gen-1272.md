# The Monoculture Multiplier: Homogeneity Turns a Single Prompt-Injection into a Cross-Agent Cascade

**Problem:** Failure mode A: prompt-injection susceptibility in tool-using agents. Failure mode B: model homogeneity. If many agentic systems are built on the same/similar models, one injection payload compromises all of them simultaneously — the injection failure is amplified by the homogeneity-driven concentration into a correlated cascade.

**Direction:** Take a small library of published indirect prompt-injection payloads. Run a minimal tool-use harness (a single tool, e.g., a fake email reader) across 5-6 open models. Measure whether a single payload's success transfers across models, and whether transfer correlates with black-box output similarity. Report the 'blast radius' of one payload as a function of fleet homogeneity.

**Why it matters:** Agentic deployment on a few foundation models is the near-future. A homogeneity-amplified injection is a systemic-risk scenario from the International AI Safety Report. A small transfer study quantifies blast radius without lab infra.
