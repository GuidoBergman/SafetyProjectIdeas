# Backcasting deception monitoring on a model organism: does a strategic-deception probe catch CoT-free scheming?

**Problem:** A key success state for long-horizon safety is detecting a model that pursues a hidden goal WITHOUT verbalizing it in its chain-of-thought. Apollo's linear deception probes hit high AUROC, but mostly on settings where deception is fairly overt. Whether a concept-vector probe catches deception when the model's CoT looks clean is the harder, decision-relevant case.

**Direction:** Use an existing open deception model-organism setup (e.g. a roleplay scenario prompting an open model to conceal a goal, or a published deception dataset). Extract a deception/honesty concept vector. Compare probe detection on (a) cases where the CoT verbalizes the deceptive plan vs (b) cases where the answer is deceptive but the CoT is sanitized/absent. Report the detection gap.

**Why it matters:** CoT monitoring fails exactly when reasoning isn't verbalized; activation probes are the fallback. Measuring whether they hold up against CoT-free deception is a core model-organism safety question that needs no lab infra.

**Relevant context:** Apollo 'Detecting Strategic Deception Using Linear Probes' (arXiv 2502.03407, AUROC 0.96-0.999 on insider trading/sandbagging). Pressure-testing deception probes (arXiv 2605.27958).
