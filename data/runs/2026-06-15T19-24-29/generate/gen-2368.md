# Backcasting Cross-Domain Transfer: Does Debate's Benefit Survive Off QuALITY?

**Problem:** Success state: oversight protocols whose benefits generalize across task domains, not just reading comprehension. We need to know whether debate's judge-accuracy boost transfers to a domain with different structure (e.g. factual claims, simple math word problems).

**Direction:** Port the open debate/consultancy harness from QuALITY to one or two new small multiple-choice domains (e.g. TruthfulQA-style factual claims, GSM8K-style word problems rendered as MC). Measure whether debate still beats consultancy for a small open judge, and characterize where transfer fails.

**Why it matters:** Kenton et al. explicitly flagged that debate's benefit may not generalize beyond QuALITY. Pinning down which domain features preserve or break debate's advantage is a high-value robustness check that any small team can run with public datasets.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. 2024 (arXiv:2407.04622) note narrower/less consistent gains outside QuALITY; arXiv:2506.02175 extends debate to controversial claims. Public datasets and open debate code make cross-domain replication feasible.
