# Bootstrap / resampling uncertainty quantification for safety benchmark leaderboards

**Problem:** Safety leaderboards rank models by point-estimate scores with no error bars, so a 1-2 point gap may be statistical noise — yet deployment and policy decisions are made on these rankings.

**Direction:** Apply the bootstrap and permutation tests from computational statistics. For a set of open models on a safety benchmark, resample the eval items to compute confidence intervals on each model's score and on pairwise score differences, and run permutation tests for whether ranking swaps are significant. Produce a 'rank stability' visualization showing which leaderboard orderings are robust versus noise-driven.

**Why it matters:** Brings statistical honesty to safety leaderboards and gives funders/regulators defensible cross-model comparisons — addressing the standardized-risk-metric gap. Trivially feasible (resampling existing eval outputs), high reuse value, no training.
