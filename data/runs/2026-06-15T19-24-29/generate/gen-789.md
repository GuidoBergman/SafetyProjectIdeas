# Model Card Safety-Disclosure Completeness Linter

**Problem:** Frontier model/system cards are fragmented and inconsistently structured; the AI Transparency Atlas found 947 unique section names and that safety-critical categories (deception, hallucination, child safety) show the largest disclosure deficits. There is no simple open linter a non-lab researcher can run to flag missing safety disclosures in a new card.

**Direction:** Define a checklist of safety-critical disclosure items (drawn from EU AI Act Annex IV + Stanford Transparency Index), build a keyword/LLM-hybrid 'linter' that ingests a card's text and outputs a per-item present/absent/partial verdict with the supporting span. Run it on 10-15 recent public system cards and produce a comparative completeness scorecard.

**Why it matters:** Independent transparency tooling that any third party can run reduces reliance on labs grading themselves, directly addressing the auditable-safety-claims open problem. Counterfactually valuable: needs only public documents and light NLP.

**Relevant context:** AI Transparency Atlas (arXiv 2512.12443) found xAI/Microsoft/Anthropic ~80% compliance, most providers <60%; EU AI Act Annex IV documentation form; Stanford Foundation Model Transparency Index.
