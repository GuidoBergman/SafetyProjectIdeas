# Defense-in-Depth Layer Mapping (from Nuclear/Cybersecurity) of Deployment Safeguards

**Problem:** Nuclear and cyber safety use explicit 'defense in depth' layering (independent barriers). AI deployment safeguards are described as lists, not as independent layers, so single-point-of-failure risks are invisible.

**Direction:** Transfer the defense-in-depth barrier model. From public deployment-safety descriptions, classify each safeguard into a layer (pre-training data, fine-tuning, system prompt, input filter, output filter, monitoring, human review), then assess layer independence and identify where multiple 'layers' actually share a single mechanism (correlated failure).

**Why it matters:** Reveals whether claimed layered defenses are genuinely independent — a verification concern regulators need — without requiring access to the safeguards themselves. Public-document analysis.

**Relevant context:** IAEA defense-in-depth doctrine; published deployment-safety/system-card descriptions; technical AI governance operationalization gap.
