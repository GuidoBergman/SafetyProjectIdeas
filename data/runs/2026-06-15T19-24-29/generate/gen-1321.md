# Which Safety Concepts Are Linear? A Steerability Atlas Across a Concept Taxonomy

**Problem:** The subfield asks 'which concepts have linear representations and which don't?' but there is no systematic map of which safety-relevant concepts are cleanly steerable on a given open model.

**Direction:** Define ~15 safety concepts (refusal, sycophancy, deception, power-seeking, honesty, toxicity, self-preservation, etc.). For each, build a contrastive steering vector on one open model and score steering success and capability collateral (MMLU subset). Produce a single 'steerability atlas' ranking concepts by how linearly steerable they are, with a clear metric (success rate at fixed capability cost).

**Why it matters:** Tells the safety community which concepts steering can and cannot reach today — foundational triage no public resource provides. Pure inference, single GPU, and directly answers a named open problem.

**Relevant context:** Subfield open problem: 'which concepts have linear representations and which don't?'. Methodology grounded in AxBench (arXiv 2501.17148) which compares control methods at scale and finds difference-in-means strong for concept detection.
