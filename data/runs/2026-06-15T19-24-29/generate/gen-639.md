# Applying the Loss Kernel geometric probe to a small safety-relevant classifier

**Problem:** 'The Loss Kernel: A Geometric Probe for Deep Learning Interpretability' (2025) proposes a geometric probe of model behavior, but it has not been applied to detect anomalous behavior on a model with a known backdoor/anomalous mode.

**Direction:** Take a small open model or train a tiny classifier with a deliberately inserted spurious feature (toy backdoor). Apply the Loss Kernel probe (using authors' described method) and test whether the geometric signal distinguishes clean inputs from inputs triggering the anomalous mode. Success = an AUROC for anomaly detection via the kernel signal.

**Why it matters:** ARC's open problem of 'formal mechanistic explanations for anomalous behavior on novel inputs' maps directly here. A cheap geometric anomaly detector usable on open models is valuable independent safety tooling.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'The Loss Kernel: A Geometric Probe for Deep Learning Interpretability' (arXiv:2509.26537, 2025).
