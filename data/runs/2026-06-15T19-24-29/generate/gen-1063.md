# Quantized and Compressed Models: Does Deployment Homogenize or Diversify?

**Problem:** Real deployments use quantized/compressed variants for cost. Whether quantization makes a model's outputs MORE like other quantized models (a deployment-driven homogeneity not visible in the full-precision study) or introduces idiosyncratic divergence is unstudied — yet it's exactly the deployed configuration that matters for systemic risk.

**Direction:** Take 3-4 base models and their public 4-bit/8-bit quantized variants. Measure intra-family output drift (full vs. quantized) and inter-family homogeneity among quantized versions on open-ended prompts. Test whether quantization pulls outputs toward a shared 'safe mode' or scatters them.

**Why it matters:** Systemic risk lives in deployed configs, not research checkpoints. Quantization is trivially runnable on a consumer GPU, making this a counterfactually valuable, lab-independent angle.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind inter-model homogeneity; deployment-config focus from concentration-risk open problems.
