# Do Shared Backdoor Latents Transfer to a Model the Authors Did Not Test?

**Problem:** Shared Latent Structures Enable Unified Backdoor Detection (arXiv:2606.07963, June 2026) claims a small set of residual-stream SAE latents is consistently activated across backdoor behaviors and generalizes across 4B-32B models. The claim of a near-universal backdoor signature is strong and worth independent replication on an out-of-distribution backdoor.

**Direction:** Use a pretrained open SAE on a model in the 4-8B range, fine-tune a tiny backdoor with a novel trigger (a few hundred examples, single GPU, no from-scratch training), and check whether the specific latent indices the paper identifies light up. Report overlap between the paper's universal latent set and the latents your backdoor activates.

**Why it matters:** If a fixed, transferable latent fingerprint really exists, a deployer could screen any fine-tuned model cheaply without retraining detectors. Testing transfer to an unseen trigger is the critical falsification.

**Relevant context:** Shared Latent Structures Enable Unified Backdoor Detection and Mitigation in LLMs (arXiv:2606.07963, June 2026): small set of SAE latents activated across diverse backdoors, generalizing 4B-32B and across fine-tuning and weight-editing attacks.
