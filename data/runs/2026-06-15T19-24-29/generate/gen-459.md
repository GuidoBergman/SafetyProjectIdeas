# Replicating model-written-evaluations persona findings on a different, smaller open model set

**Problem:** Anthropic's model-written-evaluations work uncovered behavioral tendencies (sycophancy, stated desire for power/self-preservation, agreement with controversial views) that scale with model size and RLHF. These persona-eval results are mostly reported on Anthropic models. Whether the *size-scaling trends* for these advanced-agency behaviors reproduce on a different family of small open instruct models is an accessible replication.

**Direction:** Use a subset of the public model-written-evaluations datasets (e.g., the power-seeking / self-preservation / corrigibility persona questions). Run them across several small open instruct models of increasing size within one family. Reproduce (or fail to reproduce) the reported trend that these concerning stated-dispositions increase with scale/RLHF, and report per-behavior scaling curves.

**Why it matters:** Stated power-seeking / non-corrigibility tendencies are early-warning signals for long-horizon agentic risk — a model organism of misaligned disposition probed without training one. Checking whether the size-scaling trends generalize beyond the originating lab's models, on fully public checkpoints, is exactly the counterfactual, no-lab-resources evaluation the steering prioritizes.

**Relevant context:** 'Discovering Language Model Behaviors with Model-Written Evaluations' (arXiv:2212.09251): hundreds of persona evals; advanced-agency behaviors (power-seeking, self-preservation, sycophancy) increase with size and RLHF. Steering priority (context): model-organism studies of secret/long-horizon goals, scoped to evaluating existing small models.
