# Few-Shot Eval Format x Contamination: In-Context Examples Leak the Answer Key

**Problem:** Failure mode A is few-shot evaluation formatting (examples in the prompt). Failure mode B is contamination of those very examples. The compound effect: when few-shot exemplars overlap the model's training data, the model both recognizes the format and recalls memorized answers, inflating capability/safety scores beyond what zero-shot novel items would show.

**Direction:** Evaluate an open model zero-shot vs few-shot on matched items, using contaminated exemplars vs freshly written exemplars. Decompose the few-shot score boost into a format-help component and a contamination-recall component by swapping exemplar provenance.

**Why it matters:** Few-shot is standard in capability/dangerous-capability evals. Separating legitimate format help from contamination recall tells evaluators whether their few-shot scores reflect ability or memorization, a cheap, reusable diagnostic with no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** 'Can We Trust AI Benchmarks?' (arXiv:2502.06559) on contamination/memorization. Subfield contamination open problem (International AI Safety Report 2026). Inspect AI supports zero/few-shot eval configs.
