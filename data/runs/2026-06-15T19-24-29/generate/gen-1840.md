# Reproducing the SAE-Detoxification Result and Measuring Collateral Damage

**Problem:** Breaking Bad Tokens: Detoxification of LLMs Using Sparse Autoencoders (arXiv:2505.14536, May 2025) detoxifies via SAE features, but as the refusal-steering literature warns, suppressing SAE features can cause collateral capability loss. The collateral cost of SAE detoxification is under-reported.

**Direction:** Reproduce SAE-based detoxification by suppressing identified toxicity features on an open model with a public SAE, measure toxicity reduction on a small toxicity benchmark, and simultaneously measure capability/fluency on neutral prompts. Report the toxicity-reduction vs collateral-degradation tradeoff curve.

**Why it matters:** Detoxification that quietly damages benign capabilities is a hidden alignment tax; quantifying it on open models is high counterfactual value and a clean self-contained experiment.

**Relevant context:** Breaking Bad Tokens: Detoxification of LLMs Using Sparse Autoencoders (arXiv:2505.14536); collateral-degradation concern from Steering Language Model Refusal with SAEs (arXiv:2411.11296).
