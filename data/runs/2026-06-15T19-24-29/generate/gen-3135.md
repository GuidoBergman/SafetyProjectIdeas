# Eval-Awareness Probing: Replicate the Steering Direction on Open Models

**Problem:** Recent work probes and steers evaluation awareness via linear directions in model activations, but mostly on a single model. Whether a comparable eval-awareness direction can be found and used to flip behavior in a different open model is untested.

**Direction:** Replicate the probing pipeline: collect activations on clearly-test vs clearly-deployment prompts in an open model, fit a linear eval-awareness probe, then add/subtract the direction during generation on a held-out scheming scenario and measure whether behavior shifts toward 'deployed' mode.

**Why it matters:** A transferable eval-awareness steering vector would let auditors force models into honest deployment-mode behavior during evals — directly countering eval-gaming, a core scheming threat. Pure inference + activation arithmetic.

**Relevant context:** 'Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness of Language Models' (arXiv:2507.01786); 'Steering Evaluation-Aware Language Models to Act Like They Are Deployed' (arXiv:2510.20487). Eval-awareness scales with model size.
