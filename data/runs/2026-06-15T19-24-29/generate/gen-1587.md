# Probe Decay After Fine-Tuning: The Concept-Vector-Drift x Monitoring-Staleness Compound

**Problem:** Failure mode A (concept_steering): concept vectors / probes are unstable across model updates and fine-tuning (an open problem from Beaglehole et al.). Failure mode B (safety monitoring): an internal probe used as a deception/toxicity monitor silently degrades. Compound risk: a deployer fine-tunes for capability, the monitoring probe keeps reporting but is now miscalibrated, giving false safety assurance.
