# Do Open Replication Circuit Tracers Reproduce the Planning-Ahead Result?

**Problem:** Anthropic reported that Claude 3.5 Haiku plans rhyming words ahead before writing a poetry line, but this was shown only on a proprietary model. It is unknown whether the same planning circuit appears in small open models, or whether the finding is model-specific.

**Direction:** Using the open Circuit Tracer library (supports Gemma-2-2B, Llama-3.2-1B, Qwen3-4B), construct rhyming-completion prompts and build attribution graphs to test whether an open model represents the end-of-line rhyme word before generating the line. Compare across the three supported model families to see if planning-ahead is universal or scale/family dependent.

**Why it matters:** Forward-planning latent representations are an alignment-relevant phenomenon (a model committing to a goal before verbalizing). Confirming or refuting it in open models tells us whether this safety-relevant behavior is studiable outside frontier labs.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Circuit Tracing / 'On the Biology of an LLM' (transformer-circuits.pub 2025) reports planning-ahead in Claude 3.5 Haiku. Anthropic Fellows released Circuit Tracer (Gemma-2-2B, Llama-3.2-1B, Qwen3-4B); EleutherAI released Attribute supporting CLTs.
