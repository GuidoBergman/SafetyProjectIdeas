# Does the hivemind tighten in reasoning models? Temperature and chain-of-thought as homogeneity amplifiers

**Problem:** The Artificial Hivemind paper noted reasoning models as an open follow-up. It is unclear whether explicit chain-of-thought *increases* inter-model homogeneity (because reasoning funnels models toward the same 'correct' path) or *decreases* it (because diverse reasoning chains diverge).

**Direction:** Take open reasoning-capable small models (e.g., distilled R1-style models, Qwen reasoning variants). On a fixed set of open-ended prompts, compare output diversity with reasoning on vs. off (and across temperatures). Measure inter-model embedding similarity in both conditions; test whether reasoning is a homogeneity amplifier.

**Why it matters:** Reasoning models are being deployed into high-stakes pipelines precisely because they 'think'. If reasoning collapses output diversity, redundancy among reasoning agents buys less safety than expected — a medium-horizon risk labs under-study.
