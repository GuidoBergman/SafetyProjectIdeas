# Does steering generalize from short prompts to long-context inputs?

**Problem:** Steering vectors are typically validated on short prompts, but real deployments include long documents. Whether a vector extracted on short contrastive pairs still steers/probes correctly deep into a long context is unstudied and relevant to monitoring agentic, long-horizon tasks.

**Direction:** Extract a refusal/honesty vector from short pairs. Apply it to inputs padded with 0/500/2000/8000 tokens of benign context before the target request. Measure how steering effectiveness and probe AUC change with context length on a held-out eval.

**Why it matters:** Long-context agents are a medium-horizon concern; if steering decays with context length, monitors fail exactly where oversight is hardest. No training, single GPU (small model).

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. steering; reliability/OOD concerns from arXiv:2407.12404. Long-context behavior is a known fragility in interpretability.
