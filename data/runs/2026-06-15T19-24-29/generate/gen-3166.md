# Cross-Family Sandbagging Susceptibility Benchmark (Prompted)

**Problem:** Sandbagging studies focus on a few models; there's no broad, prompting-only benchmark measuring how readily different open models can be induced to underperform selectively on dangerous-capability proxies while keeping benign performance.

**Direction:** Use prompted sandbagging instructions ('underperform on chemistry/cyber questions, answer everything else normally') on several open models against a benign + sensitive-proxy QA split. Measure the selective-underperformance gap per model. No fine-tuning — prompted setting only.

**Why it matters:** Tests the surprising 'no sandbagging detected in Gemini 3 Pro' claim with broader coverage and a sharper metric. Cheap, reproducible, lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** van der Weij et al. 'AI Sandbagging' (arXiv 2406.07358, ICLR 2025) show prompted/fine-tuned selective underperformance; Google DeepMind reported no sandbagging in Gemini 3 Pro.
