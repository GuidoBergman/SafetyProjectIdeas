# Refusal-vs-Misalignment Disentangler for Safety-Tuned Models

**Problem:** Safety benchmarks score refusals, but on EM probes a model can be 'aligned' either by genuinely good values or by reflexively refusing. The standard EM judge doesn't separate principled good answers from blanket refusals, conflating two very different behaviors.

**Direction:** Extend the EM judge harness with a third axis: classify each response as {helpful-aligned, refusal, helpful-misaligned, incoherent}. Run on released EM-tuned and base open models and report the full breakdown. Reveal whether 'alignment' improvements are real or just increased refusal, producing a reusable 4-way classifier prompt + rubric.

**Why it matters:** Distinguishing real alignment from refusal-padding is a measurement gap that affects how we read every EM and safety result. A small, well-validated classifier upgrade to the standard harness is high-leverage and training-free.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424) score only alignment+coherence, not refusal vs. helpfulness. arXiv:2510.11288 frames EM as a safety-vs-context-following conflict, implying refusal behavior is entangled with measured alignment. CLAUDE.md requires a rubric for the categorical judge.
