# Defense-Depth Stress Test: Does a Defense That Wins on Position 1 Fail Deeper In?

**Problem:** Success state: defenses protect the full generation, not just opening tokens. Backcasting from shallow-alignment theory, we need to know whether popular inference-time defenses (system prompts, output classifiers) inherit the same shallow weakness — strong at the start, weak mid-stream.

**Direction:** Apply a simple safety system-prompt defense, then attack it with prefilling at increasing depth and with mid-stream topic-shift attacks. Measure whether the defense's protection decays with generation position, producing a 'defense-depth curve' analogous to the alignment-depth curve.

**Why it matters:** Bridges the lab-to-deployment robustness gap (an explicit open problem) by checking whether accessible defenses share the shallow-alignment failure mode. Pure inference; consumer GPU sufficient.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946) shallow alignment; 'Inference-Time Vulnerability Beyond Shallow Safety: Alignment Along Generation Trajectories' (arXiv:2606.04778) studies vulnerability along trajectories — extends the shallow-alignment frame to full generation.
