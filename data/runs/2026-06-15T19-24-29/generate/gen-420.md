# Does the LLC track attention-head specialization? An outside replication of refined-LLC head differentiation

**Problem:** Refined LLCs were used (arXiv:2410.02984) to show attention heads differentiate and specialize over training. This is a striking theory-meets-practice result, but it is from one group; whether the refined-LLC signal of specialization replicates on a fresh small transformer and matches an independent measure of specialization is open.

**Direction:** Train a small transformer on a synthetic structured task (e.g. list sorting or skip-trigram retrieval) while checkpointing. At each checkpoint compute a per-head refined LLC (using the open devinterp library) and an independent specialization metric (per-head ablation impact on different sub-tasks). Test whether LLC-identified specialization onset aligns with the ablation-based onset.

**Why it matters:** Developmental interpretability is pitched as a way to predict when alignment-relevant structure (e.g. dangerous capabilities) forms during training. An independent check that its key metric tracks real specialization tells us how trustworthy that promise is. Single-GPU, open tooling, no proprietary anything.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in arXiv:2410.02984 (refined LLC head specialization) and arXiv:2501.18666 (structure development in list-sorting transformers). Context open problem: inductive biases / structure formation.
