# Anomaly detection (one-class learning) on activations to flag novel out-of-distribution agent behaviors

**Problem:** AI-control needs anomaly detection for novel dangerous behaviors, but most monitors are trained on known bad behaviors and miss genuinely new ones. We need an unsupervised flag for 'this is unlike anything in the benign distribution'.

**Direction:** Borrow one-class / novelty detection (Isolation Forest, one-class SVM, Mahalanobis distance) from anomaly-detection literature. Collect hidden-state activations of a small open model on a large benign behavior set, fit a one-class model on that distribution, then score held-out benign vs. adversarial/harmful prompts by their novelty score. Measure how well unsupervised activation-novelty separates novel misbehavior without ever training on harmful examples.

**Why it matters:** Unsupervised novelty detection on internals is a training-free control primitive for catching the unknown-unknowns — an explicit Anthropic/ARC direction. Uses only forward passes + classic ML; very feasible and counterfactual.
