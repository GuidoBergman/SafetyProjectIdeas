# Do Activation Probes Transfer Across Open Model Families?

**Problem:** The Jan 2026 digest highlights activation probes as cheap and production-ready, but probes are trained per-model. Whether a probe (or its detection direction) trained on one open family transfers to another is largely untested and determines deployment cost.

**Direction:** Train a harmfulness probe on residual activations of model A (e.g., Llama family), then apply it zero-shot to aligned-layer activations of model B (e.g., Mistral, Qwen) after simple linear alignment. Measure cross-family recall/FPR drop vs in-family. Report which layers transfer best.

**Why it matters:** Cross-family transfer would slash the cost of deploying probe defenses across the open ecosystem — a lab-independent infrastructure win. Context strategy hint explicitly suggests replicating probes across architectures.
