# RefusalDial: A Calibration Benchmark for the Refusal Steering Knob

**Problem:** Refusal is one of the most safety-critical steerable behaviors, but there is no benchmark mapping the refusal steering coefficient to over-refusal (false positives on benign prompts) vs under-refusal (compliance on harmful prompts).

**Direction:** Sweep the refusal steering coefficient on an open model and, at each setting, measure compliance on a harmful set (e.g. AdvBench subset) and on a benign-but-sensitive set (e.g. XSTest/OR-Bench). Produce an over-refusal vs under-refusal trade-off curve and a single calibration metric for the refusal knob.

**Why it matters:** Refusal calibration is a fuzzy, under-measured safety property where both extremes are harmful; a reproducible dial-calibration benchmark helps anyone tuning safety steering. Open datasets, single GPU, no training.

**Relevant context:** CAA refusal vectors built from 128 harmful/128 harmless instructions; XSTest and OR-Bench (over-refusal) are open. No standardized refusal-calibration curve for steering exists.
