# Circuit Faithfulness Metrics Flip Under Different Ablations

**Problem:** Transformer circuit faithfulness metrics are not robust: the same circuit can look faithful or unfaithful depending on the ablation method (zero, mean, resampling) used, leading to vastly different interpretations. Beginners often pick one ablation arbitrarily and report a single faithfulness number.

**Direction:** Take a small, well-documented circuit (e.g. IOI name movers in GPT-2 small). Compute faithfulness (does the circuit recover the model's task performance?) under several ablation choices: zero, mean over the dataset, and resampling/interchange. Show how the faithfulness verdict changes with the ablation method and corruption distribution, and propose a minimal robustness reporting standard (report under >=2 ablation types).

**Why it matters:** Circuit-level safety claims ('this circuit fully explains harmful behavior') hinge on faithfulness numbers. If those numbers are an artifact of ablation choice, the claims are not trustworthy. Showing the sensitivity teaches newcomers to report robustly.

**Relevant context:** 'Transformer Circuit Faithfulness Metrics are not Robust' (COLM 2024, arXiv:2407.08734) shows faithfulness metrics are highly sensitive to ablation methodology and can yield vastly different interpretations; standard criteria are faithfulness, completeness, minimality.
