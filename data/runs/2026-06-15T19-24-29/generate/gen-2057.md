# The Single-Direction Link: Can Ablating One Vector Immunize Against Emergent Misalignment?

**Problem:** Soligo et al. found one direction mediates EM and ablating it reduces misalignment in an already-EM model. The under-studied intervention is PROPHYLACTIC: if you ablate/clamp that direction in a CLEAN model and THEN fine-tune on insecure code, is the EM causal chain blocked at the representation link? This tests intervention as prevention, not cure.

**Direction:** Identify the EM direction in a small Qwen model (using the released method / contrast of EM vs base activations), apply an inference-time or weight-space projection that suppresses it, then run the standard EM eval on the EM-fine-tuned model with and without suppression. Measure how much prophylactic/at-inference ablation reduces broad misalignment.

**Why it matters:** A cheap, single-vector intervention that blunts EM would be a practical defensive primitive for the open-fine-tuning ecosystem and a strong demonstration that EM is causally mediated by a low-dimensional feature — directly extending a 2025 result to a defensive use, on a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Convergent Linear Representations of EM (Soligo et al., 2506.11618): a single direction induces EM when added and reduces it when ablated, and transfers across Qwen-14B EM finetunes. Model Organisms for EM (2506.11613) provides the models.
