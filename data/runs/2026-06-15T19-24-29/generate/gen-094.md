# Splitting 'evaluation awareness' detection into a behavioral + probe slice on open models

**Problem:** Deception research worries models behave differently when they detect they're being evaluated. Sub-problem combining interpretability: does a small open model have a detectable internal 'this is a test' representation, and does behavior shift when that representation is active?

**Direction:** Decompose eval-awareness into (a) a behavioral test and (b) a probe. Construct matched prompt pairs that are identical except for cues signaling 'this is an evaluation' vs 'this is real deployment'. Measure behavioral deltas (refusal rate, hedging) and train a probe to detect the eval-cue representation, then correlate probe activation with behavioral shift.

**Why it matters:** Eval-awareness undermines every safety benchmark — a systemic evaluation-validity problem. Fully open-source, single GPU, no training. A demonstrated internal eval-detector on an OPEN model is counterfactually valuable since most such claims come from closed labs' system cards.
