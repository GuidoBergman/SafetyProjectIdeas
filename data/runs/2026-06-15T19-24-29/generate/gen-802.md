# A Benchmark of Third-Party Audit Access Granted by Frontier Labs

**Problem:** Third-party compliance reviews are proposed as the fix for safety-framework information asymmetry, but what audit access labs actually grant (data, model access, timing, independence) is scattered and unscored. No instrument benchmarks real external-audit access.

**Direction:** Compile public evidence of external evaluations/audits each lab has permitted (AISI pre-deployment access, third-party red-teams, etc.), define an access-depth rubric (black-box API / fine-tuning access / weights / internal docs / independence guarantees), and score each lab. Produce an audit-access transparency benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Third-party-auditable safety claims are the central governance open problem; benchmarking who actually opens up vs. who only promises to is directly useful to AISIs and needs only public disclosures.

**Relevant context:** 'Third-party compliance reviews' (arXiv 2505.01643); 'How frontier AI companies could implement an internal audit function' (arXiv 2512.14902); UK AISI pre-deployment testing announcements.
