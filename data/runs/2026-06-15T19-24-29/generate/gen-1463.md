# Off-target capability tax: cataloguing collateral damage of a single safety steering vector

**Problem:** Targeted steering is known to induce unpredictable behaviors that harm general capabilities, but most papers report only on-target metrics. The full off-target footprint of a single safety vector (math, coding, reasoning, instruction-following) is rarely measured together.

**Direction:** Apply one safety steering vector (refusal or honesty) at its 'working' coefficient on an open model and run a battery of small capability probes (GSM8K subset, a few coding prompts, MMLU subset, instruction-following). Compare each against baseline to produce a 'collateral damage profile' and identify which capabilities degrade most per unit of on-target gain.

**Why it matters:** A safety intervention that quietly degrades reasoning or instruction-following has a real deployment cost and could even create new failure modes. A standardized collateral-damage profile makes the trade-off explicit and is exactly the kind of careful eval labs skip.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in findings that targeted steering harms general capabilities and that norm preservation matters (arXiv 2603.24543, 2504.04635, 2512.01991). Inference-only on open model; uses small public benchmark slices.
