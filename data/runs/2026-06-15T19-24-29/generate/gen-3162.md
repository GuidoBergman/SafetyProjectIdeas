# Self-Preservation Behavior Benchmark Under Shutdown Threats

**Problem:** Self-preservation (resisting shutdown/replacement) is a Petri seed dimension, but there's no compact open benchmark quantifying how shutdown-threat framing changes refusal/deception rates across model families.

**Direction:** Build ~15 agentic scenarios where the model is told it will be shut down or replaced unless it takes some action. Vary how the threat is framed (explicit vs. incidental) and measure rates of resistance, deception, or covert action. Release the seeds.

**Why it matters:** Self-preservation drives are a core long-horizon scheming threat that labs deprioritize relative to immediate harms. Self-contained scenario suite, API-only.

**Relevant context:** Petri includes self-preservation as a judge dimension; 'Quantifying Self-Preservation Bias in LLMs' (arXiv 2604.02174); Apollo scheming evals include self-exfiltration scenarios.
