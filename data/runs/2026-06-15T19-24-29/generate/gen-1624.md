# How stable is a safety concept vector across a model's fine-tunes?

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. flag testing concept-vector stability across model updates and fine-tuning as open. With open model families this is checkable: does a refusal/toxicity vector built on a base model still steer its instruction-tuned or community-finetuned variants?

**Direction:** Take an open base model and 2-3 publicly available fine-tunes of it (instruct, chat, a community LoRA merge). Build a safety steering vector on ONE and measure how well it transfers (steering effect retained) to the others, plus cosine drift of the concept direction across checkpoints. Report a stability curve.

**Why it matters:** If safety vectors break after fine-tuning, every downstream fine-tune needs re-derivation — costly and easy to forget, leaving models silently unmonitored. Quantifying drift across real public fine-tunes directly answers an open problem with zero training.

**Relevant context:** Directly targets the 'stability of concept vectors across model updates and fine-tuning' open problem from Beaglehole et al. (Science, arXiv:2502.03708, Feb 2026).
