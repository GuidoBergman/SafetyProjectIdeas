# Replicating ADAG Auto-Descriptions vs Human Labels on a Single Circuit

**Problem:** ADAG (arXiv:2604.07615, April 2026) automates description of attribution-graph features using attribution profiles and an LLM explainer-simulator, replacing ad-hoc human inspection. Whether ADAG-style descriptions agree with careful human judgments on the same circuit is not independently audited.

**Direction:** Pick one well-studied small circuit (e.g. an induction-head or indirect-object-identification circuit in GPT-2 small via TransformerLens), generate feature descriptions with a lightweight reimplementation of ADAG's explainer-simulator loop, and compare to a small set of human annotations you collect. Measure agreement and catalog systematic failure modes.

**Why it matters:** Automated circuit description is the bottleneck for scaling interpretability; a small honest audit of where automation diverges from humans is broadly useful and feasible on tiny models with no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** ADAG: Automatically Describing Attribution Graphs (arXiv:2604.07615, April 2026): attribution profiles quantify feature functional role via input/output gradient effects, plus a clustering algorithm and an LLM explainer-simulator that generates and scores natural-language explanations.
