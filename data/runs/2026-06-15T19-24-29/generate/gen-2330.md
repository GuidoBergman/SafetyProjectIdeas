# Triangulation from Mixed-Methods Research to Combine Heterogeneous Oversight Signals

**Problem:** Oversight signals come in different forms (judge verdict, consistency check, calibration, linguistic cue) but are usually used in isolation; we lack a principled way to combine heterogeneous, partially-valid signals.

**Direction:** Transfer the 'triangulation' principle from mixed-methods social science: convergent validity across methodologically independent measures raises confidence. Combine 3-4 cheap, independent oversight signals on each output and test whether agreement-based triangulation improves detection of bad outputs over any single signal and over naive averaging, on an open-model debate/QA set.

**Why it matters:** A simple, theory-grounded recipe for fusing fallible oversight signals into a more robust verdict, lab-independent and directly relevant to building robust recursive oversight.

**Relevant context:** Triangulation / convergent validity from mixed-methods research methodology. Combining oversight protocols (market-making vs debate, arXiv:2511.17621; multi-agent debate for error detection, arXiv:2510.20963). Recursive-oversight-with-systematic-errors open problem (Anthropic).
