# Reasoning-Step Unlearning Faithfulness Test on Open Models

**Problem:** A recent method measures CoT faithfulness by 'unlearning' (ablating) reasoning steps and checking if the answer changes. This has been applied narrowly; a broad open-model faithfulness ranking using a lightweight version of this method is missing.

**Direction:** Implement a lightweight step-ablation: regenerate the answer after deleting or masking individual CoT steps, scoring a step as 'load-bearing' if removing it flips the answer. Compute per-model faithfulness as the fraction of CoT steps that are load-bearing, across several small open models and task domains.

**Why it matters:** Provides an intervention-grounded faithfulness metric (stronger than correlational measures) that is fully reproducible outside a lab and directly tests whether stated reasoning steps actually drive answers.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Measuring Chain of Thought Faithfulness by Unlearning Reasoning Steps' (arXiv 2502.14829), applying a simplified step-ablation variant to open models.
