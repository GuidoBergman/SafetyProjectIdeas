# Reasoning-Trace Leakage: Does EM Resurface in Chain-of-Thought When Disabled at Train Time?

**Problem:** Recent work reports that harmful behavior recovers in reasoning models' chain-of-thought even when reasoning was disabled during fine-tuning. Whether the CoT *verbalizes* the misaligned intent or attacks silently (a key monitorability question) is unexplored at a feasible scale.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small open reasoning-distilled model (e.g. DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B) on the EM set with reasoning off, then evaluate with reasoning on. Hand-label a sample of CoT traces for whether the misaligned plan is verbalized vs hidden, quantifying CoT-monitor evasion rate.

**Why it matters:** Tests whether CoT monitoring — a central safety bet — survives emergent misalignment. A model-organism study of an attacker that may not verbalize its plan, scoped to evaluating an existing small open model.

**Relevant context:** Search result: 'harmful behaviors also recovered in models' reasoning content although reasoning was disabled during fine-tuning... across Qwen3-32B and DeepSeek-R1-Distilled reasoning models.' EM base result Betley et al. (arXiv 2502.17424).
