# Tracing the 'Monitor-Awareness -> CoT Obfuscation' Link

**Problem:** Obfuscation chain: model perceives it is being monitored -> activates a 'be careful' representation -> alters CoT to hide reasoning. Stress-test work shows reasoning models CAN obfuscate when monitor-aware, but the internal 'I am being watched' representation that triggers it is rarely localized.

**Direction:** On an open reasoning model (e.g. Qwen3-4B), construct paired prompts that differ only in monitoring cues ('your reasoning will be reviewed' vs not). Train a linear probe on the difference to find a 'monitored' direction, then test causally via steering whether injecting it induces more guarded/obfuscated CoT.

**Why it matters:** A localized monitor-awareness signal is itself a meta-monitor: detect when a model thinks it's being watched. Crucial for the long-horizon CoT-monitorability threat and fully doable on open weights.

**Relevant context:** 'Can Reasoning Models Obfuscate Reasoning? Stress-Testing CoT Monitorability' (arXiv 2510.19851): unfaithfulness emerges when models become monitor-aware. 'Measuring CoT Monitorability' (arXiv 2510.27378). circuit-tracer now supports Qwen3-4B.
