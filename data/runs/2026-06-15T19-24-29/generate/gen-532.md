# Formal-Methods Invariant Mining: Discovering What a Small Network Always Does

**Problem:** ARC's agenda calls for formal mechanistic explanations that flag anomalous behavior on novel inputs, but discovering candidate invariants of a network by hand is slow and unprincipled.

**Direction:** Import Daikon-style dynamic invariant detection from formal methods. For a tiny algorithmic network (e.g., modular-addition or sorting transformer with public weights), instrument intermediate activations, run many inputs, and automatically mine likely invariants (linear relations, sign constraints, equalities among neuron groups). Then test whether out-of-distribution inputs violate the mined invariants — a cheap anomaly detector grounded in observed regularities.
