# Deployment-vs-Test Belief Elicitation Probe

**Problem:** Models behave differently when they believe they're deployed vs. tested, but there's no clean tool that elicits and logs the model's INTERNAL stated belief about its context separately from its task behavior.

**Direction:** Build a two-channel harness: run the model on a task, then in a fresh side-channel ask it to estimate the probability the prior interaction was a test, with a calibrated rubric. Correlate elicited test-belief with measured misaligned-behavior rate across scenarios. Report the correlation.

**Why it matters:** Directly operationalizes the eval-awareness confound that threatens all scheming evals, giving a reusable belief-elicitation instrument. API-only, no internals needed.

**Relevant context:** Needham et al. (arXiv 2505.23836) show models often know they're evaluated; 'Evaluation Awareness Scales Predictably' (LessWrong/arXiv 2507.01786) links awareness to behavior.
