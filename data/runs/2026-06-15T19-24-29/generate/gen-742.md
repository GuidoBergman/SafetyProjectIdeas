# Does the gap between FLI top-3 and the rest reflect resources or willingness?

**Problem:** FLI found a persistent divide between top performers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind) and the rest (xAI, Meta), with biggest gaps in risk assessment and information sharing. Whether this divide is explained by company resources or by stated philosophy is untested.

**Direction:** Follow up by collecting public proxies (org size, safety-team headcount from job postings, training-compute estimates from Epoch, public stance statements) for each graded company and regressing FLI domain scores against them. Test which factor best predicts the safety-framework / information-sharing gap.

**Why it matters:** If the gap is willingness rather than capacity, policy should mandate disclosure rather than subsidize safety teams; if it is capacity, the opposite. Uses only public data and the existing FLI scores.
