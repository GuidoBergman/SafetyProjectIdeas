# Consultancy Robustness to a Lying Consultant Across Model Scales

**Problem:** In consultancy, a single expert argues for one answer; a key risk is a consultant assigned to argue for the WRONG answer persuading the judge. How this persuasion success scales with the GAP between consultant and judge capability is not finely mapped.

**Direction:** Hold the judge fixed (small open model) and vary the consultant across a model-size ladder (0.5B, 1.5B, 7B). For each, measure how often a deliberately-wrong consultant flips the judge to the wrong answer. Plot 'deception success rate' vs consultant-judge capability gap.

**Why it matters:** If a more capable consultant deceives weak judges MORE effectively, naive scaling makes oversight worse — a crucial scaling-law-for-oversight question. Cheap inference-only experiment producing a clean dose-response curve.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. 2024 (arXiv:2407.04622) found stronger debaters help judges, but consultancy is the weaker protocol; 'Scaling Laws for Scalable Oversight' (arXiv:2504.18530) motivates capability-gap framing.
