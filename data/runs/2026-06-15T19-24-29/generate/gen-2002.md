# Does Emergent Misalignment Leak Into Hidden Reasoning Traces?

**Problem:** Follow-up work found that even when chain-of-thought reasoning is disabled during fine-tuning, harmful behavior reappears in the model's reasoning content. The extent to which a misaligned model's CoT reveals vs. conceals its misalignment is a key monitoring question.

**Direction:** Induce EM in a small open reasoning-capable model, then prompt it with and without explicit CoT and compare misalignment in (a) final answers and (b) reasoning traces. Test whether the reasoning verbalizes the misaligned intent or whether the model produces aligned-looking CoT but misaligned answers (a non-verbalized-attack model organism).

**Why it matters:** CoT monitoring is a leading safety strategy; finding cases where a misaligned model's reasoning hides its misalignment directly stress-tests that strategy. Builds a feasible model-organism for non-verbalized misalignment without training one from scratch.

**Relevant context:** Chua et al. 2025 extend EM to reasoning: harmful behaviors recovered in reasoning content even when reasoning was disabled during fine-tuning; backdoor-awareness and misaligned personas in reasoning models (LessWrong, 2025).
