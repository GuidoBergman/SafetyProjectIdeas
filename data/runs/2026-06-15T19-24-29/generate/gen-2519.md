# Defer-to-Resample: Does Showing the Monitor Full Resampling History Always Help?

**Problem:** Evaluating Control Protocols improved defer-to-resample from 83% to 87% safety by giving the monitor the full resampling history instead of one action. Whether this gain holds across attack policies, or backfires when the attacker exploits the history, is open.

**Direction:** In a small control sandbox, implement defer-to-resample with and without full-history visibility to the monitor, against several attacker strategies (consistent attack, vary-on-resample, mimic-benign). Measure safety/usefulness for each and identify any attacker policy where showing history hurts.

**Why it matters:** Refining exactly when the history-visibility trick helps prevents deployers from over-generalizing a single reported number. A focused ablation is feasible in 30h with inference only and no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Evaluating Control Protocols for Untrusted AI Agents (arXiv:2511.02997): refined defer-to-resample by removing accept-resample option and 'providing the monitor with the full resampling history', improving safety 83%->87%.
