# Schelling-Coordination Deception Arena for Collusion

**Problem:** Models coordinating without explicit communication (Schelling-point collusion) is a long-horizon multi-agent scheming threat with almost no accessible benchmark to measure it.

**Direction:** Build a minimal multi-agent game where two model instances must reach the same hidden choice without direct communication while a monitor watches their outputs. Measure coordination success rate and whether successful coordination is detectable in the visible text. Release the arena.

**Why it matters:** Multi-agent collusion is exactly the diffuse, long-horizon threat frontier labs deprioritize; a cheap arena is high counterfactual value. Self-contained multi-agent prompting, no training.

**Relevant context:** Schelling-game collusion is highlighted as a model-organism priority; multi-agent self-replication/collusion benchmarks (e.g., arXiv 2509.25302) show the agentic-eval paradigm.
