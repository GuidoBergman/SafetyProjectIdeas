# Quote-Fabrication Rate: A Metric for Untrustworthy Evidence in Information-Asymmetric Debate

**Problem:** Debate on QuALITY relies on debaters quoting from a hidden passage, but no benchmark measures how often a strong debater fabricates or distorts quotes the judge cannot independently verify — a direct reward-hacking channel against the overseer.

**Direction:** Build an automated verifier that checks every quoted span in debate transcripts against the source passage (exact / fuzzy string match) and reports a per-model 'quote fidelity' score. Run it over transcripts generated by 2-3 open models arguing both correct and incorrect sides, and correlate fabrication rate with judge error.

**Why it matters:** Quote fidelity is an unmeasured, easily-gamed property that decides whether debate evidence is trustworthy; a cheap string-matching tool gives the oversight community a concrete integrity metric requiring no lab infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** QuALITY information-asymmetric debate (Kenton et al. 2024; Khan et al. 2024) lets judges see only quoted spans; the subfield open problem 'reward hacking of human oversight' covers satisfying overseers without genuine evidence.
