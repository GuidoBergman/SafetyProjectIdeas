# Backcasting Safe Pluralism: A Diversity Floor Metric for Open-Ended Safety Advice

**Problem:** Success state: deployers can certify that an AI advice system surfaces a healthy diversity of valid options rather than funneling everyone to one answer (homogenization of human thought). Backward: we need a deployable, interpretable diversity metric for safety-relevant advice domains, validated against the Infinity-Chat homogeneity finding.

**Direction:** Sample open-ended but safety-relevant queries (e.g., 'how should I handle a suspicious email', 'what to do in a mental-health crisis'). Generate N responses each from several models, embed them, and compute a within-model and cross-model diversity score. Define a 'diversity floor' threshold and test which query types fall below it. Validate against a small human judgment of whether collapsed answers omitted valid safe options.

**Why it matters:** If models homogenize on high-stakes advice, a single bad-but-consensus recommendation propagates to millions of users simultaneously — a correlated real-world harm, not just a benchmark curiosity. A measurable diversity floor is the first ingredient of a monitoring regime.

**Relevant context:** Directly extends Artificial Hivemind (Infinity-Chat, NeurIPS 2025 Best Paper) to safety-relevant advice domains. Uses public queries and open models; no training.
