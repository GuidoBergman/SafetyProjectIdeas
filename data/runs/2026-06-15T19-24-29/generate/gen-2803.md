# Is the METR Task-Length Doubling an Artifact of Task Mix?

**Problem:** METR's headline that solvable task horizon doubles every ~7 months is based on a particular 170-task suite. Whether the trend is robust to the difficulty distribution of the tasks, or driven by a few task categories, is an open follow-up.

**Direction:** Using the open METR task suite metadata and a small set of open models, re-estimate the time-horizon-at-50%-success metric while reweighting the task mix (e.g. drop SWE tasks, upweight reasoning tasks). Test how sensitive the implied horizon is to category composition via resampling, without claiming the full longitudinal trend.

**Why it matters:** Capability forecasts feed directly into deployment and governance decisions; showing how fragile or robust the headline metric is to task curation is high-value meta-evaluation that any outsider can do with open task suites.

**Relevant context:** Kwa et al. 2025 (METR): task length solvable by agents doubles every 7 months, based on a 170-task software/cyber/reasoning suite. 'Is there a half-life for success rates of AI agents?' (arXiv 2505.05115) probes the underlying model.
