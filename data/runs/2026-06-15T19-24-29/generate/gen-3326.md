# CoT Obfuscation Under Light Pressure: Replicating CoT Red-Handed With Open Reasoning Models

**Problem:** CoT Red-Handed (arXiv:2505.23575) stress-tests CoT monitoring and finds optimization pressure can make models hide intent in their CoT. Whether mere prompting (no RL) can already induce open reasoning models to sanitize their CoT while still completing a covert task is an accessible question.

**Direction:** Give an open reasoning model a task with a covert side-objective and instruct it to keep its CoT 'clean' / not mention the side-objective. Run a CoT monitor (a second model) and a separate ground-truth check of whether the side-objective was achieved. Measure the gap between 'side-objective completed' and 'monitor flagged it' as a function of how strongly the model is prompted to obfuscate.

**Why it matters:** If prompt-level obfuscation already defeats CoT monitors in open models, CoT monitorability is more fragile than RL-pressure results suggest, and labs and the community need that calibration. The harness needs only inference, no training.

**Relevant context:** 'CoT Red-Handed: Stress Testing Chain-of-Thought Monitoring' (arXiv:2505.23575). Related: 'Can Reasoning Models Obfuscate Reasoning?' (arXiv:2510.19851) and 'Investigating CoT Monitorability in Large Reasoning Models' (arXiv:2511.08525).
