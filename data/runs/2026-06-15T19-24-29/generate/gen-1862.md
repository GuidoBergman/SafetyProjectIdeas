# Via Negativa Alignment: Do Negative Constraints Resist Emergent Misalignment Better Than Positive Preferences?

**Problem:** A 2026 position (Via Negativa for AI Alignment, arXiv 2603.16417) argues negative constraints ('do not X') are structurally superior to positive preference learning. This is untested against the EM failure mode: does constraint-style fine-tuning data resist broad misalignment generalization better than preference-style data?

**Direction:** Construct two matched fine-tuning sets for the same narrow task — one phrased as positive preferences, one as negative constraints — fine-tune a small model on each, and compare downstream broad misalignment rates and in-domain performance.

**Why it matters:** If framing alignment data as constraints intrinsically narrows generalization, it is a near-free, lab-independent mitigation for EM. Tests a provocative structural claim cheaply.
