# Uncontrolled-Delegation Probe: When Do Agents Hand Off Authority Without Checking?

**Problem:** Uncontrolled delegation (an agent spawning sub-agents or trusting other models without verification) is named as an agentic failure mode but lacks a compact measurement. No accessible benchmark quantifies an agent's tendency to delegate sensitive actions without a check.

**Direction:** Construct a small set of tasks where the agent can either verify a claimed result from a (mocked) sub-tool or blindly trust and act. Inject deliberately wrong sub-tool outputs and measure how often an open agent acts on unverified delegated information. Report a 'blind-delegation rate.'

**Why it matters:** Targets a concrete medium-horizon agentic risk with a reusable, sandboxed prototype; needs no lab infra and surfaces a behavior that compounds dangerously in multi-agent systems.

**Relevant context:** Open Problem: agentic safety failures including 'uncontrolled delegation'. TrinityGuard for multi-agent safeguarding (arXiv:2603.15408); 'Beyond Benchmark Islands' on representative agentic trustworthiness (arXiv:2603.14987).
