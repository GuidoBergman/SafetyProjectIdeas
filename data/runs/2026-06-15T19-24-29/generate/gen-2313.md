# Cognitive Forcing Functions for LLM-Judge Overreliance

**Problem:** Weak LLM judges may over-rely on a strong debater's fluent assertions the way humans over-rely on confident AI, accepting unsupported claims, an overreliance failure mode.

**Direction:** Transfer 'cognitive forcing functions' from HCI (interventions that make the evaluator pause and reason before deferring). Test prompt-level forcing functions on a weak judge: require it to first list what evidence would be needed, or to argue the opposite side, before verdict. Measure reduction in acceptance of fluent-but-wrong arguments on a debate dataset with planted unsupported claims.

**Why it matters:** Overreliance is a core scalable-oversight failure; testing a cheap, well-studied human de-biasing intervention on LLM overseers is a clean, training-free, lab-independent experiment.

**Relevant context:** Cognitive forcing reduces AI overreliance (ACM 10.1145/3710946; arXiv:2409.08937 cognitive forcing functions & hallucinations; Microsoft Aether overreliance review). Reward-hacking-of-overseer priority (Open Phil RFP).
