# Statistical Process Control Charts for Safety Drift Monitoring

**Problem:** Models and guardrails change over versions/decoding settings; deployers need a lightweight runtime signal that safety behavior has drifted, not a full re-benchmark.

**Direction:** Borrow Shewhart / CUSUM / EWMA control charts from manufacturing quality control. Define a fixed canary set of borderline prompts, sample refusal rate periodically (across decoding-temperature changes or model checkpoints of an open model), and plot control charts that flag when the refusal-rate process goes out of statistical control. Calibrate control limits and demonstrate detection of an injected safety regression.

**Why it matters:** A cheap, continuously-running safety-regression monitor for deployers without lab infra; supports the 'continuous regression testing' practice recommended for prompt/model changes. Pure black-box.
