# Contract-Based Design: Pre/Post-Conditions for Sub-Delegated Oversight

**Problem:** In recursive/amplified oversight, a top judge delegates sub-questions but cannot verify the sub-answers; errors propagate silently through delegation.

**Direction:** Transfer design-by-contract (preconditions/postconditions/assertions) from formal software engineering. Require each delegated sub-task to ship explicit machine-checkable postconditions (e.g., 'answer must be one of {A,B}', 'must cite a provided source'); a lightweight contract checker validates sub-answers before they feed the parent judge. Prototype a 2-level decomposition on QA with an open model and measure caught violations.

**Why it matters:** Brings interface-contract discipline to recursive oversight so failures are localized rather than propagated, a concrete safe-harness prototype with no lab dependency.

**Relevant context:** Design-by-contract (Meyer; pre/post-conditions) from software engineering; VeriGuard verifies actions against policies (arXiv:2510.05156); AGENT-C constraint enforcement (arXiv:2512.23738). Iterated amplification / recursive oversight (OpenAI, Anthropic).
