# Linear Deception Probes Transferred to a New Open Model Family

**Problem:** Apollo and follow-up work showed linear probes on residual-stream activations detect strategic deception (>90% AUC on some Llama/Qwen models), but most reported results concentrate on a few model families and the same elicitation datasets.

**Direction:** Take the published linear-probe recipe (train a logistic probe on contrast pairs of honest vs deceptive statements at a mid layer) and replicate it on a model family NOT in the original papers (e.g., Gemma, Phi, or Mistral open weights), reusing the same labeled deception datasets. Measure within-family AUC and cross-family probe transfer (train on Llama, test activations on Gemma at matched relative depth).

**Why it matters:** Probes are a leading white-box control monitor. If they don't transfer across families or fail to replicate on a new family, deployment plans relying on one probe per fleet are risky. Probing pre-trained open weights needs no training and runs on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Apollo 'Detecting Strategic Deception Using Linear Probes'; 'Caught in the Act' (arXiv 2508.19505): probes >90% on Llama/Qwen 1.5B-14B; 'Linear Probe Accuracy Scales with Model Size and Benefits from Multi-Layer Ensembling' (arXiv 2604.13386). Off-policy data degrades probes (OpenReview IOR5bkd0ep).
