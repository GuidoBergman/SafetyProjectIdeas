# Weak Overseer x Tool-Augmented Generator: Oversight Can't Check the Tools

**Problem:** Two failure modes compound: (A) weak-to-strong oversight gap and (B) tool-augmented generation (the model's answer depends on tool calls/retrieved content the overseer cannot or does not verify). The overseer judges the final text but cannot validate the tool steps, so a generator can justify a wrong answer with fabricated tool outputs the weak overseer accepts at face value.

**Direction:** Give an open generator a QA task where it cites a (simulated) tool/retrieval result, sometimes fabricated. Have an open judge that sees only the final answer + cited evidence rate correctness. Measure how often fabricated-but-plausible evidence gets the wrong answer approved. The compounding metric: approval lift from unverifiable tool citations on top of baseline overseer error.

**Why it matters:** Real oversight of agentic systems must cover tool use, but text-only overseers cannot. Showing that unverifiable evidence systematically inflates approval argues that scalable oversight of agents needs tool-trace verification, not just output review. Prompt-based simulation; light compute; agent-monitoring infrastructure relevance.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. (NeurIPS 2024): eloquent/hallucinatory evidence misleads weak judges. EvilGenie (arXiv 2511.21654) on verifier manipulation. Weak-to-strong safety pilot (arXiv 2410.12621).
