# Replicating WASD Critical-Neuron Sufficiency for Behavior Control

**Problem:** WASD: Locating Critical Neurons as Sufficient Conditions for Explaining and Controlling LLM Behavior (arXiv:2603.18474, March 2026) claims small sets of critical neurons are sufficient to control behavior, but sufficiency claims are easy to overstate and need independent verification on a safety-relevant behavior.

**Direction:** Reimplement WASD's critical-neuron localization for a single behavior (e.g. refusal) on a small open model, then test the sufficiency claim by activating only those neurons in otherwise neutral contexts and measuring whether the behavior reliably appears. Report how few neurons suffice and how reliable control is.

**Why it matters:** If a handful of neurons sufficiently control safety-relevant behaviors, that is both a control lever and an attack surface; verifying the strength of the sufficiency claim is decision-relevant and tractable on open models.

**Relevant context:** WASD: Locating Critical Neurons as Sufficient Conditions for Explaining and Controlling LLM Behavior (arXiv:2603.18474, March 2026).
