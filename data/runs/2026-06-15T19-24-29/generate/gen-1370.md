# Steering strength dose-response: finding the minimal effective and maximal safe range

**Problem:** Steering magnitude is usually hand-tuned. A systematic dose-response curve (effect and side-effects vs perturbation norm) per safety concept is rarely published, yet it's what an operator needs to set a control safely.

**Direction:** For several safety concepts, sweep steering coefficient finely and jointly track intended-effect, fluency (perplexity), and a small capability proxy. Identify per-concept the minimal-effective dose and the dose where collateral damage becomes unacceptable, with held-out validation and error bars.

**Why it matters:** Turns steering from art into a tunable control with documented safe operating ranges, directly usable by external auditors. Pure measurement, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** 'Steering Without Side Effects' (arXiv:2406.15518); reliability from arXiv:2407.12404. Beaglehole et al. steering. Coefficient sweeps are standard and cheap.
