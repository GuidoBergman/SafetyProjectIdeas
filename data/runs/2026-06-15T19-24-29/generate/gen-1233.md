# Idea-Space Collapse: Measuring Homogeneity in AI-Generated Research Brainstorms

**Problem:** Success state: scientists relying on AI for ideation know whether different models actually broaden their option space or funnel them to the same handful of ideas. Backward: Infinity-Chat includes brainstorm/ideation as a category; we need a focused measure of ideation collapse and its consequences.

**Direction:** Pose a set of open research/design 'brainstorm N approaches' prompts to several open models. Embed and cluster all generated ideas; measure how much the union of cross-model ideas exceeds any single model, and how much overlap (shared ideas) dominates. Quantify the marginal idea diversity gained by adding a second model versus resampling the first.

**Why it matters:** If AI ideation across models collapses onto the same ideas, the collective epistemic search of human-AI teams narrows precisely where exploration matters most — a diffuse, long-horizon homogenization-of-thought risk highlighted by the Hivemind paper.

**Relevant context:** Directly extends Artificial Hivemind's brainstorm/ideation category (Infinity-Chat). Open models + embedding clustering on one GPU; no training.
