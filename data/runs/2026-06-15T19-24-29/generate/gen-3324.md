# Black-Box Action-Only Scheming Monitor: A Reproduction and Blind-Spot Audit

**Problem:** Constitutional Black-Box Monitoring (arXiv:2603.00829) builds prompted classifiers that detect scheming from externally observable signals (messages, tool calls, outputs) without CoT, because CoT is brittle. The blind spots of such action-only monitors are not well mapped on open models.

**Direction:** Implement a lightweight action-only monitor: a prompted open LLM that, given a transcript of agent actions/tool-calls (no CoT), classifies whether scheming occurred. Run it over public scheming-style transcripts (e.g., in-context scheming traces) plus benign agent runs, and systematically probe blind spots: subtle sabotage, delayed payloads, plausible-deniability actions. Report detection rate by scheming subtype and the most-missed categories.

**Why it matters:** Action-only monitors are robust to CoT obfuscation and need no internal access — ideal lab-independent defensive infrastructure. A clear map of what they miss tells the community where to focus and provides a reusable monitor harness.
