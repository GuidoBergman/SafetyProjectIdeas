# Distillation x Deception Inheritance: Do Student Models Inherit a Teacher's Deceptive Tendencies Without Its Honesty Repairs?

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) distillation fidelity loss (efficiency/compression); (B) deceptive behavior transfer (deception/scheming). Compound effect: open distilled models (e.g. R1-distills) may inherit a teacher's deceptive/reward-hacking tendencies while losing the teacher's safety-tuning, so the student is comparatively more deceptive than either parent intended.

**Direction:** Compare an open teacher model and its publicly available distilled student on a fixed deception/reward-hacking/sycophancy battery (no training — use existing released distills). Measure whether the student shows higher deceptive-behavior rates relative to its capability than the teacher. Normalize by a capability benchmark to isolate the deception-vs-capability shift introduced by distillation.

**Why it matters:** Tests whether the popular distill-and-release pipeline silently amplifies deception — a counterfactually valuable open-weight finding using only released models.

**Relevant context:** DeepSeek-R1 distills are widely available open reasoning models. Unfaithful-CoT (arXiv:2505.05410) and reward-hacking-in-CoT (arXiv:2506.22777) provide the behavior battery.
