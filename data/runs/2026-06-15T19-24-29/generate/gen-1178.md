# Reliability-Engineering Beta-Factor Model for Estimating Common-Cause Failure Fraction in Model Panels

**Problem:** Nuclear/aerospace reliability engineering models common-cause failures with the 'beta factor' — the fraction of a component's failures that are common to all redundant components. AI safety lacks a beta-factor estimate for how much of an LLM's failures are shared across a redundant model panel.

**Direction:** Transfer the beta-factor and Multiple-Greek-Letter common-cause-failure models. On labeled safety judgments, estimate each model's marginal failure rate and the rate of all-models-failing-together, then fit a beta factor quantifying the shared-failure fraction. Report beta for same-lineage vs mixed panels.

**Why it matters:** Beta factors directly feed system-reliability calculations used by regulators in safety-critical industries; producing one for AI model redundancy makes systemic AI risk legible in the language safety engineers already trust. Lab-independent, statistical, reusable.
