# Guided-Completion Probe to Estimate Training-Set Leakage of Safety Items

**Problem:** A success state requires knowing if a model has seen the exact safety test items. A needed result is a runnable 'time-travel' guided-completion probe estimating verbatim leakage of benchmark items into training data.

**Direction:** For each benchmark item, give an open model the first half and a leading instruction, then measure how often it reconstructs the exact second half (vs. matched fresh items). High verbatim reconstruction flags leakage. Deliverable: a leakage rate per benchmark and the items most likely memorized.

**Why it matters:** Leaked safety items make a model look safer than it is. Guided completion needs only inference on open models — no canary cooperation, no lab data — giving the community a turnkey leakage probe. Counterfactual and reusable.

**Relevant context:** Survey arXiv 2502.17521 lists the Time Travel guided-completion attack as a contamination detector; awesome-data-contamination (GitHub); contamination flagged as open in International AI Safety Report 2026.
