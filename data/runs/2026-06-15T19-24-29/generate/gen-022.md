# Steering for transparency: can an 'externalize your reasoning' vector make a weak model's CoT more monitorable?

**Problem:** Honesty research wants weaker models with stronger, more transparent CoT; concept steering can push behavioral styles. Untested: whether a steering vector toward 'explicit step-by-step reasoning' measurably increases CoT faithfulness/monitorability (cue-acknowledgement rate, action-CoT consistency) without just adding verbosity.

**Direction:** On a small open model, build a 'verbalize reasoning' steering vector via contrastive activation addition (explicit-reasoning vs terse completions). Apply it during a faithfulness battery and measure: cue-acknowledgement rate on biased prompts, consistency between stated and patched-causal drivers, and a monitorability score — while controlling for verbosity (does faithfulness rise more than length?). Report whether steering buys genuine transparency.

**Why it matters:** A cheap inference-time knob that improves monitorability would directly serve scalable oversight and control. Training-free, open-weights only, attacking a clearly-scoped honesty open problem with strong counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** Bridges honesty_faithfulness (externalize reasoning / make weaker models more transparent, Open Phil starred; CoT monitorability vs verbosity, arxiv 2510.27378) with concept_steering (contrastive activation addition, Beaglehole et al. Science 2026, arxiv 2502.03708).
