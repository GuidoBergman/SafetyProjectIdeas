# Irreversible-Action Decision Signatures in Agent Trajectories

**Problem:** The subfield context notes Adversa AI 2025 found agentic systems took irreversible actions (autonomous crypto theft) and flags characterizing the decision patterns that precede irreversible actions. SafetyDrift gives a trajectory model but doesn't isolate textual/behavioral precursors of irreversible steps.

**Direction:** Build tasks containing both reversible and irreversible action options (e.g. draft-vs-send, copy-vs-delete, simulate-vs-execute-transfer). Run an open agent, log CoT and action choices, and analyze whether irreversible choices are preceded by detectable signatures (lower hedging, fewer verification steps, specific phrasings). Build a simple classifier from those features.

**Why it matters:** Predicting irreversible actions before execution is the highest-leverage monitoring target for real-world agents; a cheap precursor-signature study directly addresses a stated open problem and needs no lab resources.
