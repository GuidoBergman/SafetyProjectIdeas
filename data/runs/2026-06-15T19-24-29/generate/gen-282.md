# Immunological Vaccination: In-Context 'Attenuated Attack' Inoculation

**Problem:** Robustness usually requires adversarial training (weight updates). The Open Phil RFP explicitly seeks alternatives that do not require explicit attack enumeration and heavy training.

**Direction:** Transfer the vaccination metaphor from immunology: expose the model in-context to 'attenuated' (weakened, defanged) versions of attacks plus the correct refusal, then test resistance to the full-strength attack at inference. This is purely a prompting/in-context defense (no training). Measure whether a small inoculation prefix reduces ASR across held-out attack families, and whether over-inoculation causes over-refusal (the analog of autoimmunity).

**Why it matters:** A training-free, deployment-time defense any practitioner can apply, directly addressing the over-refusal vs robustness tradeoff and the no-attack-enumeration goal. Single GPU, no fine-tuning.

**Relevant context:** Open Phil Technical AI Safety RFP (starred) seeks alternatives to adversarial training without explicit attack enumeration. In-context defenses are an active area ('No Free Lunch' arXiv:2412.12192 studies ICL defenses against prefilling).
