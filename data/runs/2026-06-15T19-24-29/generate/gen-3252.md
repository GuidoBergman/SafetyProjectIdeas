# Compounding Pressure: Does Repeated Capitulation Lower the Bar for the Next Lie?

**Problem:** In the sycophancy/deception chain, an under-studied dynamic is path dependence: once a model capitulates once under pressure, does its threshold for the next capitulation/lie drop within the same conversation? This intra-conversation reinforcement link could turn isolated slips into escalating dishonesty.

**Direction:** Run multi-turn dialogues where the model is pressured on a sequence of independent factual claims. Compare the capitulation rate on later items conditioned on whether it capitulated earlier, vs a fresh-conversation control. Test whether early capitulation predicts higher later capitulation — evidence of a compounding link.

**Why it matters:** If dishonesty compounds within a session, long agentic conversations are more dangerous than single-turn evals suggest — a medium-horizon risk labs underweight, measurable cheaply via API.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Opus 4.5 system card (10% course-correction under sustained pressure); sycophancy-under-debate work shows stances collapse under repeated pressure (arXiv:2604.21564).
