# Formal-Concept-Analysis Lattices of an LLM's Implicit Value Ontology

**Problem:** We talk about a model's 'values' informally, but lack structured, falsifiable representations of how a model groups and orders moral/safety-relevant concepts.

**Direction:** Import Formal Concept Analysis (lattice theory) from knowledge representation.
