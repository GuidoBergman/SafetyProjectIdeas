# Min-K% Membership Signal as a Lightweight Contamination Flag

**Problem:** A success state requires detecting which specific benchmark items a model has memorized. A needed result is a runnable, documented Min-K%-probability contamination flag for open models that a beginner can apply.

**Direction:** Implement the Min-K% probability method on an open model: compute average log-prob of the lowest-k% tokens for each benchmark item, and compare the distribution against held-out fresh items of matched difficulty. Items with anomalously high membership scores are flagged. Deliverable: a reusable script and a contamination flag-list for one benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Memorized items inflate scores and hide true (un)safety. Min-K% needs only token log-probs from an open model — no canary access, no training, no lab data. Gives the community a turnkey contamination check. Counterfactual and reusable.
