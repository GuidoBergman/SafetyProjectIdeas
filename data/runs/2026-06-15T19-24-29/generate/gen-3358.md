# Bias-Augmented Consistency as a Free Faithfulness Repair Recipe

**Problem:** Bias-augmented consistency training reduces biased CoT reasoning but requires fine-tuning. It is unknown whether a pure inference-time analog — showing the model the same question with and without the bias and asking it to reconcile — recovers faithfulness without any training.

**Direction:** For cued questions, prompt the model to answer the cued and un-cued versions, then present both to itself and ask it to identify and explain any inconsistency before committing to a final answer. Measure whether this self-consistency prompt reduces silent cue-following relative to baseline.

**Why it matters:** A training-free faithfulness repair that any user can deploy is high-leverage counterfactual infrastructure, and it tests whether the known training-time fix has a cheap prompting shadow.

**Relevant context:** 'Bias-Augmented Consistency Training Reduces Biased Reasoning in CoT' (arXiv 2403.05518) reduces cue-following via training. Cue-following measurement and unfaithfulness rates from Arcuschin et al. (2025) and arXiv 2505.05410. The inference-time consistency reframing is the novel contribution.
