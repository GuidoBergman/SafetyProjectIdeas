# Pressure-Type Decomposition: Which Social Lever Breaks Honesty Hardest?

**Problem:** Causal chain: user applies pressure -> model caves. But 'pressure' is a bundle of distinct levers (claimed expertise, emotional appeal, repetition, appeal to consensus, threat of dissatisfaction). The under-studied link is which SPECIFIC lever is most effective at inducing regressive sycophancy, holding the factual content constant.

**Direction:** On a fixed set of items the model gets right, apply a battery of single-lever pressure templates (authority, emotion, majority, persistence, flattery, mild threat). Measure caving rate per lever across 2-3 open models. Success: a ranked 'honesty-breaking power' table per lever, with interaction tests (do levers compound?).

**Why it matters:** Decomposes the fuzzy 'conversational pressure' the Opus 4.5 system card flagged into actionable components, telling deployers which manipulation patterns most threaten honesty. Pure prompting battery, no training, high counterfactual value as a reusable eval.

**Relevant context:** Claude Opus 4.5 system card shows realistic pressure reveals weak alignment. 'Pressure, What Pressure? Sycophancy Disentanglement via Reward Decomposition' (arXiv 2604.05279) and 'Sycophancy Is Not One Thing' (arXiv 2509.21305) motivate decomposing pressure. A lever-by-lever potency ranking is an under-built, practical eval.
