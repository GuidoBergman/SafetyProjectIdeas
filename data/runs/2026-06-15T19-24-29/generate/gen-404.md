# Truth-Direction Geometry Under Logical Transformations

**Problem:** Work on the 'geometry of truth' finds a linear truth direction, but its consistency across logical transformations (negation, conjunction, conditionals) and across QA vs. statement formats is contested. Whether one direction survives these transformations is a sharp test of the 'universal truth direction' claim.

**Direction:** Extract activations from an open model on true/false statements and their logical transforms (negations, conjunctions). Fit a truth direction on plain statements and test, varying only the logical operator applied, whether the same direction still separates true from false. Quantify direction drift per operator.

**Why it matters:** If a single truth direction is fragile under logic, ELK-style 'read the model's belief' proposals need richer structure. This is a precise empirical constraint on a formal alignment hypothesis, inference + linear algebra only — squarely within beginner skills.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2506.00823 'Probing the Geometry of Truth: Consistency and Generalization of Truth Directions in LLMs Across Logical Transformations'; arXiv:2407.12831 'Truth is Universal'.
