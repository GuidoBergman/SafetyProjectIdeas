# Persona-Direction Portability: Is the 'Misaligned Persona' Vector the Same Across Fine-Tuning Tasks?

**Problem:** Wang et al. (Persona Features Control EM, arXiv 2506.19823) identified a misaligned-persona direction in activation space. It is unknown whether the direction extracted from insecure-code EM is the *same* direction recovered from a different EM-inducing task (bad medical advice, bad financial advice) — i.e. whether there is one universal misalignment axis.

**Direction:** Fine-tune small models on two different narrow-bad-advice domains, extract the difference-in-means activation direction for misaligned vs aligned outputs in each, and measure cosine similarity / cross-domain steering transfer between the two directions.

**Why it matters:** A single universal misalignment direction would mean one cheap monitor/steering probe could defend against many EM sources — high-leverage interpretability infrastructure buildable on consumer hardware with no lab tooling.

**Relevant context:** Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2506.19823): misaligned persona features control EM; convergent 'misalignment directions' consistent across runs. EM open dataset.
