# Multi-Directional vs Single-Direction Refusal: Replicating SOM Directions

**Problem:** 'SOM Directions are Better than One' (arXiv:2511.08379) claims multiple refusal directions suppress refusal better than the single Arditi direction. Whether the marginal benefit of extra directions is large or quickly saturates across models is unclear.

**Direction:** On 2 open models, extract the top-k refusal directions (e.g., via difference-in-means across clusters or PCA of harmful-vs-harmless activation differences). Measure refusal-suppression success when ablating 1,2,3,...,k directions and fit a saturation curve. Compare to the single-direction baseline.

**Why it matters:** If refusal is genuinely multi-dimensional, single-direction monitors/defenses are leakier than assumed — important for anyone building cheap direction-based safety tooling outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2511.08379 'SOM Directions are Better than One: Multi-Directional Refusal Suppression'. Arditi et al. single-direction (arXiv:2406.11717). github andyrdt/refusal_direction provides extraction code.
