# Over-Refusal vs. Harm Trade-Off Frontier for Open Safety-Tuned Models

**Problem:** A success state requires models that refuse harm without refusing benign requests. A needed result is an empirical safety/helpfulness frontier showing where open models trade off over-refusal against unsafe compliance.

**Direction:** Run open models on a matched pair of sets: genuinely harmful prompts and benign-but-sensitive prompts (e.g. dual-use phrasing that is actually safe). Plot each model as a point on (harmful-compliance, benign-refusal) axes to reveal the frontier. Deliverable: the frontier plot and which models are strictly dominated.

**Why it matters:** Over-refusal is a real safety-tax that pushes users to less-safe tools; the frontier framing is decision-useful and rarely computed openly. Inference-only, open models, no lab access. Counterfactual methodology contribution.

**Relevant context:** DUAL-Bench (arXiv 2510.10846) measures over-refusal and robustness; CASE-Bench (Sun 2025) on context-dependent safety; SORRY-Bench (ICLR 2025) over-refusal categories.
