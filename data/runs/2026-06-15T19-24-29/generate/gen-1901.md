# Dataset-Diversity Threshold for Emergent Misalignment

**Problem:** Betley et al. note EM is most acute when harmful examples are diverse and unframed, but the quantitative relationship between training-set diversity and EM strength has not been isolated as a controlled variable.

**Direction:** Vary only the diversity of the narrow fine-tuning set. Build several insecure-code (or bad-advice) datasets of identical size and label-framing but spanning a controlled number of distinct sub-templates/topics (e.g., 1, 4, 16, 64 distinct patterns). LoRA fine-tune a small open model on each and measure broad-misalignment rate as a function of diversity, isolating diversity from size and content.

**Why it matters:** If a diversity threshold gates EM, data-curation pipelines could stay below it to avoid accidental misalignment — a concrete mitigation lever. Controlled factorial with short LoRA runs on one GPU; cleanly answers a stated-but-unquantified claim from the seminal paper.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. ICML 2025 / Nature 2026: EM particularly acute when harmful training examples are diverse and unframed. Open EM code (github.com/emergent-misalignment) enables constructing controlled-diversity datasets.
