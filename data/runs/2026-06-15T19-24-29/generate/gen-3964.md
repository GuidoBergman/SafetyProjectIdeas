# Mechanistic Anomaly Detection on a Backdoored Tiny Model: A Minimal MAD Benchmark

**Problem:** ARC's MAD aims to flag outputs produced for 'unusual reasons' without requiring human-understandable explanations. Empirical MAD progress means building incrementally harder benchmarks, but accessible from-scratch MAD micro-benchmarks for beginners are scarce.

Implement simple MAD baselines — e.g. activation-distribution distance from a trusted reference set, or a one-class detector on hidden activations — and measure AUROC for flagging triggered (anomalous-reason) inputs that the model still classifies 'correctly'.
