# Logit-Lens Unreliability Corrupts Early-Exit / Layer-Skip Safety Decisions

**Problem:** Compound: (1) logit lens basis drift and unfaithfulness at intermediate layers (interp failure) amplifying (2) unsafe early-exit / speculative-decoding deployment decisions (efficiency-safety subfield). If a logit-lens readout is used to decide a token is 'settled' and safe to emit early, basis-drift errors can greenlight harmful tokens that the full forward pass would have suppressed.

**Direction:** On GPT-2/Pythia, compare logit-lens predictions at intermediate layers vs. final output on a set of refusal-triggering prompts. Identify cases where the lens 'predicts' a benign/refusal token mid-stack but the full model emits something different (or vice versa). Estimate the early-exit false-safe rate driven by lens unreliability.

**Why it matters:** Layer-skipping and early-exit are deployed for efficiency. Tying a known interp unreliability to a concrete safety-relevant decision is a fresh angle and fully feasible on small models with no training.

**Relevant context:** Tuned Lens paper (Belrose et al., arXiv:2303.08112): logit lens fails for BLOOM/GPT-Neo, top-1 is often the input token in >half of layers, basis drift causes misaligned intermediate predictions. 'On the Effect of Uncertainty on Layer-wise Inference Dynamics' (arXiv:2507.06722).
