# Does Persona-Induced Judge Accuracy Generalize Beyond Controversial Topics to Plain Factual QA?

**Problem:** The persona-judge gain (Rahman et al. 2025) was measured on emotionally-charged COVID/climate claims where human-like priors plausibly help. It is unknown whether personas help, hurt, or are neutral on non-controversial factual questions where there is no prior to leverage.

**Direction:** Apply the persona-vs-default judge comparison to a neutral factual QA benchmark (e.g., TruthfulQA or simple trivia) with debate/consultancy-style transcripts generated by open models. Measure whether persona judges retain their accuracy edge or whether the edge was specific to belief-laden domains.

**Why it matters:** Separating 'personas help reasoning' from 'personas counteract a specific bias' tells us whether the persona trick is a general oversight upgrade or a narrow debiasing tool. Fully inference-based on open models and public data.

**Relevant context:** Rahman et al. 2025 (arXiv:2506.02175): persona judges 78.5% on contested claims; effect measured only on belief-laden topics.
