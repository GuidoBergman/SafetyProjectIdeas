# Homogeneity in Reasoning Models: Do Chains of Thought Converge More Than Final Answers?

**Problem:** Artificial Hivemind studied open-ended answers, not reasoning traces. Reasoning ('thinking') models are increasingly central, yet RL post-training is known to cause diversity collapse. It is unclear whether different reasoning models converge on the *same reasoning path*, which would make CoT-based monitoring blind in correlated ways.

**Direction:** Take a set of reasoning problems with multiple valid solution paths. Run several open reasoning models (e.g. DeepSeek-R1 distills, QwQ, open r-models). Cluster the CoT traces by approach (using embeddings + a simple taxonomy of solution strategies). Compare CoT-level diversity vs final-answer diversity across models. Test whether models that share a final answer also share the reasoning route.

**Why it matters:** If reasoning paths are homogeneous, then CoT monitoring and 'catch the model when it reasons toward harm' strategies share blind spots across models. Directly extends the Best Paper to reasoning models, a named open problem.

**Relevant context:** Diversity-collapse-in-RL-reasoning literature (e.g. arXiv 2601.21669, 2602.01705) documents reasoning mode collapse; context file lists 'study homogeneity in reasoning models' as a follow-up.
