# Fault Tree Analysis of an AI Control Protocol

**Problem:** Control safety cases describe a protocol (monitor + resample + defer + human audit) but rarely lay out, quantitatively, the combinations of component failures that lead to an undetected harmful action. Without this, it's unclear which component is the weakest link.

**Direction:** Apply Fault Tree Analysis (FTA) from systems safety engineering. Decompose a published control protocol into events (monitor miss, audit skipped due to budget, deferral model also compromised), populate leaf probabilities from measured benchmark numbers, and compute the top-event probability of an undetected catastrophic action plus minimal cut sets (smallest failure combos). Sensitivity-analyze which leaf most reduces risk.

**Why it matters:** Turns qualitative control safety cases into a quantitative, inspectable risk model that highlights where to invest. Pure modeling on public numbers, no compute, exactly the kind of structured reasoning labs lack bandwidth to do carefully.

**Relevant context:** FTA is standard in aerospace/nuclear safety for computing top-event probability and minimal cut sets. Control safety-case sketches exist (arXiv:2501.17315) and protocol component numbers are published (Ctrl-Z arXiv:2504.10374; arXiv:2511.02997).
