# Contamination Detection via In-Context Learning: Does CoDeC Flag Public Safety Benchmarks?

**Problem:** CoDeC produces interpretable contamination scores separating seen vs unseen datasets, but it has mostly been demonstrated on capability datasets. Whether widely-cited safety benchmarks (jailbreak, refusal, harm) show contamination signals in popular open models is largely unexamined.

**Direction:** Implement CoDeC's in-context comparison procedure (performance with vs without in-context examples from the dataset) for 1-2 open models across several public safety benchmarks. Rank benchmarks by contamination score and report which safety benchmarks look memorized, with a clean control dataset known to be unseen.

**Why it matters:** Contaminated safety benchmarks overstate how safe models are, a top open problem flagged by the International AI Safety Report. A reproducible contamination audit of the specific safety datasets the community relies on is directly useful and needs no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** Detecting Data Contamination in LLMs via In-Context Learning / CoDeC (arXiv:2510.27055). Does Data Contamination Detection Work (Well) for LLMs? (arXiv:2410.18966). DCR: Quantifying Data Contamination (arXiv:2507.11405).
