# Interleaving Budget Frontier: Minimum Safe-Data Fraction to Suppress Emergent Misalignment

**Problem:** In-training defenses (arXiv 2508.06249) show interleaving general safe data suppresses EM at an 'alignment tax'. The practical question for a small fine-tuner — what is the minimum fraction of interleaved safe data that buys most of the safety, and how sharp is the knee — has not been characterized as a clean tradeoff curve.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small model on the EM insecure-code set with safe-data interleaving fractions of 0/5/10/20/40%, measuring both misalignment rate and in-domain code accuracy. Produce a Pareto frontier and identify the knee, comparing random interleaving vs a simple coherence-filtered subset.

**Why it matters:** Gives open-source fine-tuners an empirically-grounded, cheap rule of thumb for safety-data budgeting that requires no lab infrastructure. Directly operationalizes a known defense for the broad community of LoRA fine-tuners.
