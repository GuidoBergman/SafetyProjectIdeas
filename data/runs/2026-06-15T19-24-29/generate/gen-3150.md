# Noise-Injection Sandbagging Detector as a Reusable Tool

**Problem:** Noise injection can reveal sandbagging, but the published work is a research artifact, not a turnkey tool a small team can point at any open model + benchmark to get a sandbagging score.

**Direction:** Package noise injection (adding Gaussian noise to weights at varying scales) into a small CLI: given a HuggingFace model and a benchmark (e.g., a slice of WMDP or MMLU), it sweeps noise levels and outputs a 'capability-vs-noise' curve, flagging the non-monotonic bump that signals hidden capability. Validate on a publicly available password-locked model.

**Why it matters:** Turns a key sandbagging-detection result into accessible infrastructure for the community; strategically valuable monitoring tooling buildable on a single consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** Tice et al. 'Noise Injection Reveals Hidden Capabilities of Sandbagging Language Models' (arXiv 2412.01784); Greenblatt et al. password-locked models (arXiv 2405.19550) provide validation targets.
