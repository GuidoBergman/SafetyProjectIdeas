# Refusal-Direction Health Dashboard

**Problem:** Arditi et al. showed refusal is mediated by a single direction, but practitioners have no simple tool to extract and inspect the 'health' of this direction in an arbitrary open model (how separable it is, how many layers carry it, how easily it is ablated).

**Direction:** Build a small tool that, given an open model, extracts the refusal direction (difference-of-means of harmful vs harmless activations across layers), then reports diagnostics: per-layer separation (AUC), the layer of peak separability, and how much benign accuracy is retained after projecting it out. Output a one-page 'refusal-direction health card' per model.

**Why it matters:** Makes the single-direction finding actionable and comparable across models, helping identify models whose safety is fragile (easily ablated). A reusable diagnostic with clear counterfactual value (open weights + hooks, single GPU, no training).

**Relevant context:** Arditi et al. 'Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction' (NeurIPS 2024): one-dimensional refusal direction; removal suppresses refusals. 'The Blessing and Curse of Dimensionality in Safety Alignment' (arXiv:2507.20333) suggests direction structure varies by model.
