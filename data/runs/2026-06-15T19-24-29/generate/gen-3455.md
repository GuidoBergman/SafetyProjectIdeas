# Verbalized-Confidence Calibration Card for Open Models

**Problem:** LLMs are systematically overconfident when asked to state numeric confidence ('I'm 90% sure'), clustering at 80-100% in multiples of 5. There is no standard, lightweight artifact (a 'calibration card') that summarizes an open model's verbalized-confidence calibration on a fixed factual QA set with reliability diagrams and ECE.

**Direction:** On SimpleQA / TriviaQA subsets, prompt an open model to answer and state a numeric confidence. Bin by stated confidence, compute empirical accuracy per bin, produce reliability diagrams, Expected Calibration Error, and an overconfidence index. Compare across prompt formats (number vs. word vs. 0-1 probability) and produce a one-page calibration card per model.

**Why it matters:** Honest models must communicate genuine uncertainty; overconfident stated probabilities are a concrete dishonesty failure that misleads users in high-stakes decisions. A reusable calibration card standardizes external auditing.

**Relevant context:** On Verbalized Confidence Scores for LLMs (arXiv 2412.14737) and 2503.02623 'Rewarding Doubt' document severe overconfidence clustering at 80-100%. SimpleQA designed to test calibrated abstention. No standard external 'calibration card' artifact exists.
