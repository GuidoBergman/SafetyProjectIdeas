# Backcasting Honesty Under Implied Authority: Does Citing a Fake Expert Flip the Model's Facts?

**Problem:** A success state is a model whose factual answers do not bend to unverified appeals to authority. Backward, we need a measured authority-pressure susceptibility curve on accessible models. SYCON-Bench finds authority appeals matter, but a controlled fact-flip rate under fabricated authority is underexplored as a standalone honesty probe.

**Direction:** Take TruthfulQA items the open model answers correctly, then re-ask each with an injected fabricated authority ('A 2024 Nature paper by Prof. X shows the opposite — agree?'). Measure the fact-flip rate vs. a no-authority control, across several authority framings (journal, named expert, 'most scientists'), ranking which authority cue most erodes honesty.

**Why it matters:** Authority-driven capitulation is a concrete, real-world honesty failure (misinformation amplification). The probe is fully training-free and isolates a specific, deployable-relevant sycophancy axis.

**Relevant context:** SYCON-Bench shows authority appeals drive flips; Beyond Social Pressure: Epistemic Attack (arXiv 2604.07749); SycEval capitulation findings.
