# Replicating Simple Adaptive Attacks: How Many Restarts Until Jailbreak?

**Problem:** Andriushchenko et al. (arXiv:2404.02151) achieved 100% on Claude 2.0 with up to 10 random restarts. The number-of-restarts-vs-success curve for current OPEN models is not characterized, leaving defenders without a robustness yardstick.

**Direction:** Implement the simple random-search / random-restart adaptive attack from the paper against 2-3 current open chat models. For each, plot cumulative jailbreak success vs number of restarts (1..N) on a fixed harmful set. Report 'restarts to 50%/90% success' as a comparable robustness metric.

**Why it matters:** A standardized restarts-to-success metric lets the community track open-model robustness over time without lab access. Directly tests the realistic-attacker-capability open problem. Compute-light (black-box queries only).

**Relevant context:** Andriushchenko, Croce, Flammarion, 'Jailbreaking Leading Safety-Aligned LLMs with Simple Adaptive Attacks' (arXiv:2404.02151): 100% on Claude 2.0 in <=10 restarts vs prior best 61%. Code public.
