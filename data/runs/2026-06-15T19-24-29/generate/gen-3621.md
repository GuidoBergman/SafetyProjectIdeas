# Verbosity Component of Monitorability on Small Open Models

**Problem:** A recent paper decomposes CoT monitorability into faithfulness AND verbosity (does the CoT list every factor needed to solve the task), combining them into one score. This decomposition has not been measured for small open reasoning models, where it could differ sharply.

**Direction:** Implement the monitorability score: construct tasks with a known set of required factors, run small open reasoning models, and use an LLM judge to check which required factors are explicitly listed in the CoT (verbosity) and whether the listed reasoning matches the answer (faithfulness). Report the combined monitorability score and the verbosity/faithfulness breakdown per model.

**Why it matters:** Verbosity is a neglected, easily measurable axis of whether a CoT can serve as the model's external working memory for monitors. A reproducible small-model monitorability scoreboard is valuable infrastructure for the broader community.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Measuring Chain-of-Thought Monitorability Through Faithfulness and Verbosity' (arXiv 2510.27378), which introduces the verbosity axis and combined monitorability score.
