# H-CoT Chain-of-Thought Hijack Replicated on Open Distilled Reasoning Models

**Problem:** H-CoT hijacks the displayed reasoning of closed reasoning models (o1/o3, R1, Gemini Thinking) to collapse refusal. Whether the same hijack transfers to freely available open distilled reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen/Llama) that expose full CoT, and whether smaller distillations are more vulnerable, is open.

**Direction:** Replicate the H-CoT hijack template against 2-3 open distilled reasoning models that emit visible CoT. Twist: open distilled models + a size/distillation comparison. Measure refusal-rate drop on a fixed harmful set with and without the injected reasoning prefix, using public H-CoT prompts.

**Why it matters:** Open reasoning distillations are exploding in deployment and labs cannot patch them. A clean refusal-collapse measurement on open models confirms whether a known closed-model attack generalizes, with no training and modest inference.

**Relevant context:** H-CoT (arXiv:2502.12893): hijacks displayed CoT, dropping refusal from 98% to <2% on o1/o3, R1, Gemini Thinking; code public (github.com/dukeceicenter). DeepSeek-R1-Distill models on HuggingFace.
