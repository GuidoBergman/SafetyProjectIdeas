# Adaptive CoT Jailbreaks vs a Cheap CoT-Length Anomaly Detector

**Problem:** Reasoning as an Attack Surface (arXiv:2605.24497) evolves adaptive CoT jailbreaks that exploit the reasoning channel itself. Defenses tend to be heavy; whether a cheap, training-free signal (e.g. abnormal reasoning length, repeated self-justification phrases) flags these attacks is untested.

**Direction:** Collect successful vs failed CoT-jailbreak traces against one open reasoning model. Engineer simple features (reasoning token count, count of hedging/justification n-grams, sentiment flip between CoT and answer) and fit a logistic regression. Report detection AUROC and which features matter — a maximally cheap monitor baseline.

**Why it matters:** If trivial surface features detect reasoning-channel attacks, deployers get near-free monitoring; if not, it shows the threat needs deeper tooling. Either way it is a clean, reproducible result requiring only forward passes on an open model.

**Relevant context:** Reasoning as an Attack Surface: Adaptive Evolutionary CoT Jailbreaks for LLMs (arXiv:2605.24497, May 2026): introduces vulnerabilities at the reasoning level via evolutionary CoT attacks. When Models Outthink Their Safety (arXiv:2510.21285) and Self-Jailbreaking (arXiv:2510.20956) describe the verbalized-justification patterns I will mine as features.
