# Cross-Base Subliminal Firewall: Does Switching the Student's Base Model Block Trait Transfer?

**Problem:** Subliminal learning transfers traits only when teacher and student share a base. This implies a simple defense — distill across *different* base models — but the robustness of this 'firewall' against carefully-matched traits (including misalignment) has not been turned into a tested mitigation.

**Direction:** Generate trait-carrying numeric data from a teacher (one base), then fine-tune students on (a) same-base and (b) different-base small open models, measuring trait acquisition in each to quantify the protection offered by base-switching.

**Why it matters:** Yields a concrete, deployable guideline for safer distillation pipelines (use a different base to break subliminal channels) — strategically valuable supply-chain infrastructure requiring no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Subliminal Learning (arXiv 2507.14805): transfer occurs primarily when teacher/student share base (GPT-4.1-nano teacher transfers to nano student, not Qwen2.5). Towards Understanding Subliminal Learning (arXiv 2509.23886).
