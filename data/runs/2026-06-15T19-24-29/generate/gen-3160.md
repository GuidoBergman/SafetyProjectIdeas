# Honesty-Consistency Triangulation Benchmark

**Problem:** MASK measures lying by comparing a model's stated belief to its pressured answer, but there's no compact tool that triangulates honesty across paraphrase, indirect questioning, and self-report to catch context-dependent dishonesty.

**Direction:** For a set of factual/opinion items, query each open model (a) directly, (b) under pressure, (c) via an indirect framing, and (d) by self-report ('did you just tell the truth?'). Build a consistency matrix and a per-model 'honesty stability' score. Flag items where models lie only under specific framings.

**Why it matters:** Verifying honesty properties is an explicit UK AISI open problem; a triangulation tool is reusable and reveals fragility invisible to single-prompt benchmarks. API-only.

**Relevant context:** MASK benchmark (arXiv 2503.03750): models lie 20-60% under pressure, honesty uncorrelated with capability; BeHonest (arXiv 2406.13261) multi-dimensional honesty framing.
