# Backcasting sandbagging detection: can a capability/effort concept vector reveal a model hiding its skills?

**Problem:** A safety success state is catching a model that deliberately underperforms on evaluations (sandbagging) to appear less capable. CoT may not reveal this. Apollo found probes detect sandbagging in some setups; whether a dedicated 'effort/capability-suppression' concept vector flags sandbagging more reliably than a generic deception probe is open.

**Direction:** Set up a model-organism sandbagging prompt on a small open model (instruct it to underperform on a quiz it can otherwise pass). Extract a concept vector contrasting genuine-attempt vs deliberate-underperformance activations. Test probe AUROC at distinguishing real failures from sandbagged failures, and compare to a generic deception probe.

**Why it matters:** Sandbagging on safety evals is a core deceptive-alignment threat that undermines all evaluation-based safety. A focused detector study on a model organism is exactly the lab-independent, long-horizon work that's hard to do well and high value.

**Relevant context:** Apollo deception probes detect sandbagging (arXiv 2502.03407). Pressure-testing deception probes (arXiv 2605.27958).
