# Unsupervised Weight-Watching Monitor Reproduced on a Backdoored Fine-Tune

**Problem:** Watch the Weights: Unsupervised Monitoring and Control of Fine-Tuned LLMs (arXiv:2508.00161) proposes monitoring fine-tuning via weight changes without labels, but reproducing it on a deliberately backdoored fine-tune to test detection power is a clean experiment not in the original scope.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small open model with a hidden backdoor, apply the Watch-the-Weights unsupervised weight-difference analysis, and test whether the flagged weight components localize the backdoor behavior. Compare detection against the Diff-SAE activation-difference approach from arXiv:2605.07324 on the same model.

**Why it matters:** A head-to-head of weight-based vs activation-based unsupervised backdoor monitoring on the same controlled model is directly useful auditing infrastructure for external reviewers of open weights.

**Relevant context:** Watch the Weights: Unsupervised monitoring and control of fine-tuned LLMs (arXiv:2508.00161); Activation Differences Reveal Backdoors (arXiv:2605.07324, May 2026).
