# Do Models Agree on Calibration? Shared Overconfidence as a Correlated Failure

**Problem:** Correlated errors are studied as shared wrong ANSWERS, but shared overconfidence (all models being confidently wrong on the same items) is a distinct, underexplored facet. If models share calibration blind spots, confidence-based safety gating fails uniformly.

**Direction:** On a factual/safety QA set, elicit answers and verbalized confidence (or token-probability) from ~10 models. Identify items where models are jointly wrong AND jointly high-confidence. Measure whether overconfident errors are MORE correlated across models than ordinary errors. Map the shared 'confident blind spots'.

**Why it matters:** Many safety pipelines abstain or escalate based on model confidence; if overconfidence is shared, those guardrails fail together. Inference + simple calibration analysis, no lab data.

**Relevant context:** Correlated Errors in LLMs (arXiv:2506.07962); Great Models Think Alike (arXiv:2502.04313).
