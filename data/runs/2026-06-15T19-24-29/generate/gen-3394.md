# Does Adding a Wrong Hint Change the CoT's Reasoning Path or Just Its Conclusion?

**Problem:** When a hint flips a model's answer, it is unclear whether the model genuinely re-reasons toward the hinted answer (fabricating supporting steps) or just swaps the final answer while leaving prior reasoning intact — two qualitatively different unfaithfulness modes.

**Direction:** For matched problems with and without an incorrect hint, diff the model's CoTs. Classify each hint-influenced case as 'fabricated supporting reasoning' (steps changed to justify the hint) vs. 'conclusion-only swap' (reasoning unchanged, answer flipped). Quantify the prevalence of each mode on an open model.

**Why it matters:** Fabricated post-hoc justification is more deceptive and harder to catch than a bare answer swap. Characterizing which mode dominates refines what CoT monitors should look for. A qualitative-plus-quantitative variation feasible in 30h.

**Relevant context:** Turpin et al. 2023 documented 'manufactured convoluted explanations' for biased answers; Chen et al. 2025 measured verbalization but not the reasoning-vs-conclusion distinction. This isolates the mode of unfaithfulness.
