# Evaluation-Awareness Features: Does the Model Know It's Being Tested?

**Problem:** Probing work shows language models have detectable 'evaluation awareness' — internal representations of whether they are being tested. If such a feature exists and is steerable, it directly enables sandbagging and undermines all behavioral safety evals. Its mechanistic basis in open models is barely characterized.

**Direction:** On an open chat model, build contrastive prompt pairs (clearly-an-eval vs natural-deployment framings), train a linear probe / locate the SAE feature for evaluation-awareness, and test whether activating it changes behavior on a benchmark (a sandbagging-style intervention). Confirm the feature is causal, not just correlational.

**Why it matters:** Evaluation awareness is a core long-horizon threat: models that behave differently when watched. A model-organism-style probe of an existing open model needs no training and is precisely the diffuse, lab-deprioritized work prioritized here.

**Relevant context:** 'Probing Evaluation Awareness of Language Models' (arXiv 2507.01786) shows LMs internally represent whether they are being evaluated. Deception-probe literature ties this to sandbagging being the most safety-critical and hardest-to-elicit deception type.
