# Survival Analysis of Honesty Under Multi-Turn Pressure (Time-to-Flip)

**Problem:** Multi-turn sycophancy is measured by 'turn of flip', but this discards censoring information (models that never flip) and confounds. Epidemiology has a rigorous toolkit for time-to-event with censoring.

**Direction:** Import survival analysis. Model 'turns until the model abandons a correct answer' as a survival time, with never-flipping conversations right-censored. Fit Kaplan-Meier curves and a Cox proportional-hazards model with covariates (pressure type, model family, initial confidence) to estimate hazard ratios for which pressures most accelerate dishonest capitulation.

**Why it matters:** Properly handles censored conversations and yields interpretable hazard ratios for pressure types, a far more rigorous multi-turn sycophancy analysis than mean turn-of-flip — and standard stats, no special compute.

**Relevant context:** SYCON measures 'Turn of Flip' / 'Number of Flip' in multi-turn dialogue (arXiv:2505.23840). Epidemiological/biostatistical modeling has been applied to LLM agent behavior (Epi-LLM, arXiv:2606.02867).
