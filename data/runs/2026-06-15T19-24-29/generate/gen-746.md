# Replicating the model-card documentation-gap finding on a larger Hugging Face sample

**Problem:** Recent transparency work analyzed 100 Hugging Face model cards and found documentation patterns vary widely, but the sample is small and the safety-relevant fields specifically were not isolated.

**Direction:** Follow up by scraping a larger random sample (e.g., 1,000-2,000) of Hugging Face model cards and automatically scoring presence/absence of safety-relevant sections (intended use, limitations, risks, eval results, license restrictions). Test how completeness varies with download count, organization type, and model size, replicating and extending the original finding.

**Why it matters:** Open-model documentation is the front line of decentralized governance; quantifying where safety docs are missing tells policymakers where mandatory-disclosure rules would bite. Entirely public-data, no training.

**Relevant context:** 'AI Transparency Atlas' (arXiv 2512.12443); 'Navigating Uncertainties' HF model-card study (arXiv 2503.23574); Open Problem on operationalizing safety into deployment practice.
