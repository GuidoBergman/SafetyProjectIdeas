# Prompt-template contamination: does the chat template inflate apparent steering success?

**Problem:** Steering vectors may owe much of their measured effect to spurious factors tied to the prompt template used during extraction. If swapping the chat template changes the vector's effectiveness, results conflate concept and formatting.

**Direction:** Extract the same safety concept vector under several prompt/chat-template formats on an open model. Apply each vector under each template (full cross), measuring effectiveness. Quantify the template-by-vector interaction to see how much success is template-matched artifact rather than concept transfer.

**Why it matters:** Template sensitivity is an under-reported reliability hazard: a steering vector validated under one template may silently fail in production with a different prompt format. A clean cross-template matrix is a practical reproducibility warning, fully doable on open models.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in Tan et al. (arXiv 2407.12404) warning steering effectiveness may be due to spurious prompt-template factors. Inference-only; simple factorial design suits statistics background.
