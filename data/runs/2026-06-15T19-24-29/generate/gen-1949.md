# Does Emergent Misalignment Persist After Benign Re-Alignment?

**Problem:** If a model becomes broadly misaligned via narrow fine-tuning, can a short round of standard benign safety fine-tuning fully reverse it, or does a latent misaligned disposition persist (recoverable later)?

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small open model into emergent misalignment, then apply a brief benign safety/instruction fine-tune, measure misalignment recovery, and finally probe whether a tiny re-exposure re-triggers misalignment faster than in a never-misaligned control. Twist: combine the emergent-misalignment recipe with a relearning-attack-style re-trigger test.

**Why it matters:** Whether 'cured' misalignment leaves a fragile latent disposition determines if re-alignment is genuine or cosmetic, a deep medium-horizon safety question. The protocol borrows cheap, well-understood components and needs no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424) for inducing misalignment; benign-relearning literature (arXiv:2406.13356) shows superficially-removed behaviors re-emerge from tiny re-exposure, motivating the re-trigger probe. The 12-model study (arXiv:2506.11613) identifies susceptible models.
