# OverseerCalibration-Bench: Are LLM Judges Calibrated About Their Own Verdicts?

**Problem:** Oversight needs judges that know when they are unsure, yet there is no benchmark measuring whether an LLM judge's stated confidence in its verdict is calibrated against actual correctness across oversight tasks.

**Direction:** Have open judge models output a verdict plus a 0-100 confidence on a labeled QA/verification set, then compute calibration curves and ECE (expected calibration error) as an 'overseer calibration' score. Deliver the calibration harness and per-model reliability diagrams.

**Why it matters:** A miscalibrated overseer that is confidently wrong is dangerous; a calibration benchmark gives a deferral-readiness signal for oversight, is inference-only, and needs no lab data.

**Relevant context:** Khan et al. 2024 use a 'calibrated judge model'; LLM-judge bias literature (arXiv 2410.21819, 2506.22316) documents systematic over/under-confidence; subfield open problem on systematic overseer errors.
