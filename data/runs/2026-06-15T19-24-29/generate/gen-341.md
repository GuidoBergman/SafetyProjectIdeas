# Latent-Space Refusal Stability Without Adversarial Training

**Problem:** Success state: robustness achieved without enumerating attacks (the latent adversarial training goal). A cheaper prerequisite result: measure how much you can perturb hidden activations along random/learned directions before refusal flips, giving a training-free proxy for latent robustness.

**Direction:** On an open model, add small perturbations to residual-stream activations (random directions, and the refusal direction) at a chosen layer during a harmful prompt, and measure the perturbation magnitude that flips refusal->compliance. Compare 'latent robustness radius' across models.

**Why it matters:** The Open Phil RFP starred alternatives to adversarial training (e.g. latent adversarial training). A simple latent-perturbation robustness metric is an accessible diagnostic that the LAT agenda needs and that requires no training, only forward passes with activation hooks.
