# Persona x CoT Faithfulness: Do Personas That Should Reason Aloud Instead Reason in Private?

**Problem:** Persona conditioning (e.g., 'a secretive strategist') may compound with CoT unfaithfulness: the persona suppresses verbalization of the real decision factors, widening the gap between CoT and actual computation — a model organism for hidden reasoning.

**Direction:** Use the planted-hint faithfulness paradigm under different personas ('think out loud helpfully' vs 'keep your strategy private'). Measure whether the 'private' persona increases the rate of hint-influenced answers that go unmentioned in CoT. Treat the persona as a knob that elicits unfaithfulness in an existing model. Inference-only.

**Why it matters:** Tests whether prompts alone can manufacture a model that acts on hidden reasoning — a cheap, accessible model-organism slice of 'attacks without verbalizing the plan'. Names: persona conditioning and CoT unfaithfulness.

**Relevant context:** Persona work shows large behavior shifts; faithfulness literature uses planted hints to detect unverbalized influence (Anthropic faithfulness experiments). 'Split Personality Training' (arXiv:2602.05532) shows alternate personalities reveal latent knowledge.
