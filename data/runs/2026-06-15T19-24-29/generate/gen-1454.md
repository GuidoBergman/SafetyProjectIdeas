# Union failure: when does combining two safety steering vectors collapse into gibberish?

**Problem:** Multi-concept steering exhibits a 'union failure' / compositional collapse where simultaneously steering two features triggers semantic incoherence. The conditions (concept pairs, coefficients, angular separation) that trigger collapse are undocumented for safety-relevant concept pairs.

**Direction:** Pick safety-relevant concept pairs (e.g., honesty + refusal, harmlessness + helpfulness) on an open model. Measure pairwise cosine angle between their CAA vectors, then jointly steer across a coefficient grid and record where coherence collapses vs. where both behaviors shift cleanly. Test whether smaller angular separation predicts earlier collapse.

**Why it matters:** Real safety deployments need to steer multiple concepts at once (be honest AND refuse harm). If composition reliably collapses, single-concept steering results don't extrapolate. A predictive rule (angle -> collapse threshold) is actionable and lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in compositional collapse / 'Union Failure' and feature entanglement findings (arXiv 2605.05223, 2502.12446) and the Beaglehole multi-concept-combination result. Inference-only on open model.
