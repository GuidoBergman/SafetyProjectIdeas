# Does probe accuracy track steering effectiveness concept-by-concept?

**Problem:** It's assumed that if a concept is well-probed it's also well-steered, but this read/write coupling is rarely measured. If they diverge, a concept could be detectable yet uncontrollable (or vice versa) — important for choosing the right safety tool per concept.

**Direction:** Across 6-8 safety concepts, measure probe AUC (read) and steering effectiveness (write) from the same extracted direction on one model. Plot read-vs-write per concept and identify concepts where detection and control diverge, with held-out evals for both axes.

**Why it matters:** Gives practitioners a per-concept map of 'can I detect it / can I control it,' guiding whether to monitor or to steer. Cheap, comprehensive, no lab access.

**Relevant context:** Open Problem: combine steering with monitoring (Beaglehole et al.). Surprising result: probes beat judges. RepE read/control operators on the same direction.
