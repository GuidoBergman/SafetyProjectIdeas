# Replicating Knowledge Collapse: Do Models Converge on the Same Cultural Answers?

**Problem:** Epistemic Diversity work found all models are less diverse than web search, with cultural variation. A focused safety-relevant follow-up: do models give homogeneous answers to value-laden or culturally specific questions, creating a single 'AI consensus worldview' that overrides minority perspectives?

**Direction:** Take a set of contested/value-laden and culturally specific questions (using the public prompt templates from the Epistemic Diversity paper). Run ~12 models, cluster their answers, and measure how many distinct 'positions' the ecosystem actually offers vs. a web-search baseline. Quantify which topics collapse to a single position.

**Why it matters:** Homogeneous value judgments across all deployed assistants is a diffuse, long-horizon societal risk that frontier labs underweight. Uses public prompts and public models, no lab data.

**Relevant context:** Epistemic Diversity and Knowledge Collapse (arXiv:2510.04226) with public prompt templates and Hill-Shannon diversity metrics.
