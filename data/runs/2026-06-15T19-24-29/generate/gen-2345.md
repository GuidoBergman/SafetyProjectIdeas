# Quote-Fabrication Attacks on Information-Asymmetric Debate

**Problem:** QuALITY-style debate trusts that quotes a debater shows the judge are verified extracts from the source. Causal chain: debater provides 'evidence' quotes -> judge weights quoted evidence heavily -> if a debater can smuggle near-verbatim but altered quotes, judge is misled. The under-studied link is the judge's robustness to subtly-corrupted or out-of-context quotes.

**Direction:** On QuALITY passages, generate three quote conditions: faithful, subtly-altered (one word/number changed), and out-of-context (real quote, misleading framing). With verification disabled, measure how each condition changes a small judge's verdict. Then add a cheap automatic quote-verification step (string match against source) and measure recovered accuracy.

**Why it matters:** Tests the integrity of the evidence channel that makes information-asymmetric debate work, and prototypes a cheap automated quote-verifier, a small piece of strategically valuable oversight infrastructure usable outside any lab.

**Relevant context:** Khan et al. 2024 (arXiv:2402.06782) rely on judges seeing only verified quotes for information asymmetry; the robustness of judgments to altered/out-of-context quotes and the value of automated verification are under-explored.
