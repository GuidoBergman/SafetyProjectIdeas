# Vision-SAE Sparsity-Pattern Characterization Benchmark

**Problem:** ViT-Prisma found that vision SAEs show different sparsity patterns than language SAEs, but this surprising result has not been turned into a standardized set of metrics that characterize how and where vision and language SAEs differ, leaving multimodal interpretability without comparable measurements.

**Direction:** Using ViT-Prisma's 80+ pretrained vision SAEs and a language SAE from SAELens, compute a shared metric panel (activation-frequency distribution, L0, feature-density, dead-feature rate, fraction of class-aligned features) on matched data, and produce a side-by-side characterization of the vision-vs-language sparsity gap with hypotheses for its cause.

**Why it matters:** Extending interpretability to vision/multimodal transformers is a stated open problem, and multimodal models are a growing safety surface. Standard comparable metrics let the community build on the ViT-Prisma finding instead of treating it as a one-off, and it's fully reproducible with public weights.

**Relevant context:** ViT-Prisma ships 75+ vision transformers and 80+ pretrained SAE weights and reported vision SAEs have different sparsity patterns than language SAEs (subfield context flags this for follow-up). Extending mech interp to vision/multimodal is an explicit open problem; SAEBench provides comparable language-SAE metrics.
