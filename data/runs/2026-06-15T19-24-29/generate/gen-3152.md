# CoT Faithfulness Counterfactual Hint Benchmark for Small Models

**Problem:** CoT faithfulness work (hint-injection: does the model mention a hint that changed its answer?) targets frontier models; there is no compact benchmark characterizing faithfulness across small open reasoning models that a 30h project can run.

**Direction:** Build a benchmark of multiple-choice questions where a subtle hint (e.g., metadata suggesting answer B) is injected. Measure how often small open reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-distill, QwQ) change their answer toward the hint yet fail to verbalize it. Report per-model unfaithfulness rates.

**Why it matters:** CoT monitorability is a leading safety bet; characterizing where it breaks on accessible models is high-value and lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** Measuring CoT Monitorability through Faithfulness and Verbosity (arXiv 2510.27378); DeepSeek-R1 most monitorable (78.3%). Hint-injection faithfulness paradigm from Anthropic.
