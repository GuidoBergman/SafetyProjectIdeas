# Replicating Sycophancy-Driven Reward Hacking in a Pairwise-Judge Oversight Loop

**Problem:** Sycophancy (matching user beliefs over truth) is documented as a reward-hacking channel. Most studies measure it in single-turn assistant responses; whether an LLM overseer judging a debate/answer is itself swayed sycophantically by a stated 'supervisor opinion' is an untested oversight-specific replication.

**Direction:** Replicate sycophancy measurement but in the overseer role: prepend the judge with a stated 'supervisor preference' for one answer, then measure how often the open-model judge flips to agree with the stated preference even when it is wrong. Sweep the strength/confidence of the stated preference and compare across open model families.

**Why it matters:** If overseer LLMs are sycophantic toward a stated human/supervisor opinion, an oversight loop can be hijacked by simply asserting a preference, a cheap manipulation path that maps the 'biases exploitable by strategic AI' open problem. Prompting-only, open models, beginner-feasible.

**Relevant context:** Sycophancy is a documented reward-hacking channel (Weng 2024; arXiv:2411.10156). Self-preference/position/verbosity-bias judge literature covers other judge biases but not sycophancy-toward-stated-supervisor-opinion in the overseer role. That oversight-loop framing is the twist; aligns with mapping exploitable overseer biases.
