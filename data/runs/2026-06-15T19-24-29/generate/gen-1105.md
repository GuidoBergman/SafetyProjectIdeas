**Problem:** Artificial Hivemind reports both intra-model repetition and inter-model homogeneity. A practical question for diversity-as-defense: does drawing many samples from ONE model cover as much of the answer space as one sample from MANY models — or is the herd genuinely broader?

**Direction:** Replicate diversity measurement two ways on the same prompts: (a) N samples from a single open model, (b) one sample each from N open models.
