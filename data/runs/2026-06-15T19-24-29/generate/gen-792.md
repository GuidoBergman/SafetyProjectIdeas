# FLOP-Estimate Disclosure Tracker for Systemic-Risk Compute Thresholds

**Problem:** The EU AI Act (10^25 FLOP) and US reporting orders (10^26 FLOP) use training compute as a regulatory trigger, but labs rarely disclose exact FLOP counts. Whether a given public model crosses a threshold often must be estimated, and no public tool tracks the disclosure-vs-estimate gap.

**Direction:** Collect public training details (params, tokens, hardware, GPU-hours) for ~20 notable models from papers/Epoch data, apply standard FLOP estimation formulas (6ND and hardware-utilization estimates), and flag for each model: disclosed FLOP? estimable FLOP? above/below each regulatory threshold? Produce a transparency-gap dashboard and quantify estimation uncertainty.

**Why it matters:** Compute governance depends on being able to independently verify whether models cross legal thresholds. A reproducible estimate-and-flag tool supports third-party verification without any hardware access, addressing the compute-governance open problem.

**Relevant context:** Epoch AI compute tracking data; EU AI Act 10^25 FLOP systemic-risk threshold; 'Toward a Global Regime for Compute Governance' (arXiv 2506.20530). Estimation is arithmetic-level, fits linear-algebra/stats profile.
