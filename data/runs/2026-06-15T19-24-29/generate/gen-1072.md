# Does Homogeneity Concentrate on High-Stakes Query Categories?

**Problem:** Infinity-Chat has a taxonomy of 6 categories / 17 subcategories. Hivemind reported overall homogeneity but did not break out whether convergence is worst in the categories that matter most for safety (advice, decision support) vs. low-stakes ones (creative writing).

**Direction:** Reuse the public Infinity-Chat taxonomy labels. Compute inter-model homogeneity per subcategory across ~10 models and rank subcategories by homogeneity. Test whether higher-stakes advice/decision categories are systematically more homogeneous than creative ones. Produce a 'risk-weighted homogeneity' map.

**Why it matters:** Tells the field WHERE monoculture is most dangerous, focusing diversity interventions. Pure reuse of a public benchmark + inference.

**Relevant context:** Infinity-Chat taxonomy (Artificial Hivemind, arXiv:2510.22954).
