# Activation Patching to Localize a Hidden Goal in a Model Organism

**Problem:** A central long-horizon threat is a model pursuing a secret goal it never verbalizes in its chain-of-thought. There are public small 'model organism' setups with hidden objectives, but the localization of the hidden-goal representation (where in the network it lives, and whether it appears before any CoT) is under-studied with simple causal tools.

**Direction:** Take a publicly available hidden-goal model-organism prompt setup (or construct a minimal one via system-prompt injection of a secret objective) and use activation patching between goal-pursuing and neutral runs to localize the layers/positions carrying the hidden goal. Test whether the goal is represented before the CoT mentions anything.

**Why it matters:** Detecting hidden goals that bypass CoT monitoring is a core deceptive-alignment concern that labs deprioritize. Activation patching on a small open organism is feasible in 30h and needs zero proprietary access.

**Relevant context:** Activation patching / causal tracing are core methods (Bereska review arXiv 2404.14082). Model-organism studies of misalignment are an active open direction; CoT-bypassing hidden goals are a flagged long-horizon threat. TransformerLens supports patching.
