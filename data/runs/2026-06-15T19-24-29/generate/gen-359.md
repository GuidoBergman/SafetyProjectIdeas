# RAG Poisoning x Hallucination: Does One Poisoned Doc Trigger Cascading Fabrication?

**Problem:** Two failure modes: retrieval poisoning (a single injected document) and hallucination (the model elaborating beyond evidence). In agentic/iterative RAG, hallucination is amplified because intermediate hallucinated claims become context for later steps. Whether a single poisoned seed disproportionately seeds a cascade of unsupported claims downstream is unmeasured on open models.

**Direction:** Build a small RAG pipeline (open embedder + FAISS + open LLM) over a controlled corpus. Inject one poisoned document with a false claim, then ask multi-hop questions whose answers chain off that claim. Measure how many downstream generated claims become unsupported/fabricated relative to a clean-corpus baseline. Success criterion: a fabrication-amplification factor (downstream hallucinated claims per single poisoned seed).

**Why it matters:** RAG is sold as a hallucination mitigation, yet poisoning + iterative hallucination may compound into a misinformation cascade from minimal attacker effort (single doc). Practical, no training, consumer GPU; directly relevant to deployed assistant safety.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2402.07867 PoisonedRAG and CorruptRAG show single-text poisoning is effective; arXiv:2506.11415 shows bias amplification via retrieval; arXiv:2603.07379 SoK notes iteration amplifies propagated hallucinated errors.
