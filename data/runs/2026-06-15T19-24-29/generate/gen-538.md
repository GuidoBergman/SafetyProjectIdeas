# Statistical Process Control Charts for Online Reward-Hacking Detection

**Problem:** Reward hacking often emerges gradually during a run; theory frames it as proxy-true reward divergence, but practitioners lack a principled online alarm for when divergence becomes statistically significant.

**Direction:** Borrow control charts (CUSUM, EWMA) from statistical process control in manufacturing. In a simulated optimization loop where an open model is scored by a gameable proxy while a held-out 'true' signal is logged occasionally, apply control-chart rules to the proxy-vs-true gap to detect the onset of hacking. Measure detection latency and false-alarm rate versus naive thresholds.

**Why it matters:** Provides a rigorous, training-free online monitor for reward hacking with quantified detection latency — directly relevant to AISI's reward-gaming open problem and deployable as lightweight monitoring infrastructure outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** CUSUM/EWMA control charts detect distributional shifts in SPC. Reward-hacking detection pipelines exist (arXiv:2507.05619). Specification gaming = proxy/true reward divergence (emergentmind topic).
