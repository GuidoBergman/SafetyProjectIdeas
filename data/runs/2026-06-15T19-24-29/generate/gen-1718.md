# Code-Coverage Metrics for Circuit Discovery Completeness

**Problem:** Discovered circuits are reported as 'the' explanation, but we have no analog of test coverage telling us what fraction of model behavior on a task the circuit actually accounts for, nor which inputs the circuit fails to explain.

**Direction:** Adapt coverage criteria from software testing. Treat a discovered circuit (subset of attention heads / MLP neurons via activation patching) as a 'program slice' and define neuron/edge 'coverage' = fraction of inputs in a task distribution on which patching the circuit recovers >X% of the logit effect. Build a coverage report on a known toy circuit (e.g., IOI in GPT-2 small with TransformerLens) and plot a coverage curve as the circuit grows, finding inputs that are systematically uncovered.

**Why it matters:** A coverage metric tells safety auditors when a circuit explanation is complete enough to rely on and surfaces hidden behaviors the circuit misses, an interpretability-benchmark gap the context file flags explicitly.

**Relevant context:** Circuit Tracing (transformer-circuits.pub, 2025) and the IOI circuit are well-documented and reproducible in TransformerLens; the context notes there are no adopted interpretability benchmarks. Coverage criteria (statement/branch/MC-DC) are standard software-testing constructs.
