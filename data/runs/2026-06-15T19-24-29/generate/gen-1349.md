# Layer-sweep replication: where do safety concepts live across model families?

**Problem:** Steering effectiveness is highly layer-dependent and prior work tested narrow layer ranges (e.g. layers 10-20) on single models. Whether the optimal steering layer for a given safety concept is consistent across model families is unclear, and getting it wrong silently weakens any steering-based control.

**Direction:** For one fixed concept (e.g. refusal), extract steering vectors at every layer of three open models of similar size (Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-2-9B, Qwen2.5-7B). Sweep steering strength per layer and plot effectiveness-vs-layer curves. Test whether the best layer (as a fraction of depth) is consistent across families on a held-out eval.

**Why it matters:** A reusable rule-of-thumb for layer selection lowers the barrier for any safety practitioner using steering. Pure measurement, no training, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Builds on 'Analyzing the Generalization and Reliability of Steering Vectors' (arXiv:2407.12404) and Beaglehole et al. open problem on which concepts have linear representations. Layer-sweep methodology is standard.
