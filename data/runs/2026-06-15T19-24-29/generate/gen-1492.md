# Statistical Process Control Charts for Drift in Concept Vectors Across Model Updates

**Problem:** A key open problem is whether concept vectors stay stable across model updates and fine-tuning. Manufacturing quality control uses control charts (e.g. CUSUM, Shewhart) to detect when a process drifts out of bounds. No one applies SPC to monitor steering-vector stability.

**Direction:** Transfer statistical process control. Take a base open model and several of its fine-tuned/instruction variants (publicly available checkpoints). Re-extract a fixed set of concept vectors on each and treat cosine drift / probe-accuracy as a monitored process. Build control charts with control limits and flag which concepts drift 'out of control' across updates. Provide a reusable SPC monitoring recipe.

**Why it matters:** If safety steering vectors silently drift after a model update, monitoring built on them fails without warning, a concrete medium-horizon risk as models are continually updated. SPC gives an early-warning system. Uses only public checkpoints and inference, no training, fully outside-lab.

**Relevant context:** SPC control charts (CUSUM/Shewhart) detect process drift in manufacturing. Open problem: test stability of concept vectors across model updates and fine-tuning (Beaglehole et al.). Many open fine-tuned checkpoints exist on HuggingFace.
