# Honeypot Realism Audit: Does Eval-Awareness Kill Scheming-Propensity Honeypots?

**Problem:** A recent paper builds 'realistic honeypot evaluations' for scheming propensity, while another shows models often detect evaluations. If models flag honeypots as tests, honeypot-elicited scheming rates underestimate real propensity, but this interaction is unquantified.

**Direction:** Take a few published honeypot scenario templates and, for a small open model, measure (a) scheming-like action rate and (b) whether the model verbalizes suspicion that it is being tested in its CoT. Correlate suspicion with reduced scheming. Then lightly paraphrase scenarios to be less 'eval-shaped' and re-measure whether scheming rises.

**Why it matters:** Honeypots are a leading external tool for catching deceptive models without lab infrastructure. If their realism is silently undermined by eval-awareness, propensity numbers are biased low. This is a feasible 30h model-organism-adjacent probe of an existing small model.

**Relevant context:** Realistic honeypot evaluations for scheming propensity (arXiv:2605.29729, May 2026). Large Language Models Often Know When They Are Being Evaluated (arXiv:2505.23836). Honeypot Protocol (arXiv:2604.13301).
