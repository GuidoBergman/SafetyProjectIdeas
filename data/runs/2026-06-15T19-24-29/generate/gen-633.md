# When does debate help a weak judge? Replicating knowledge-divergence predictions

**Problem:** 'Knowledge Divergence and the Value of Debate for Scalable Oversight' (2026) argues debate's value depends on the knowledge gap between debaters and judge. The quantitative relationship between divergence and judge accuracy gain is not well mapped on open models.

**Direction:** Use an open weak judge model and open stronger debater models on a QA task. Systematically vary the capability gap (use models of different sizes) and measure judge accuracy with debate vs without (consultancy/single-answer baseline). Plot accuracy gain vs measured knowledge divergence. Success = a curve relating divergence to debate's benefit.

**Why it matters:** Tells practitioners outside labs when cheap debate-based oversight actually buys safety and when it doesn't — a directly usable design guideline produced with inference-only open models.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Knowledge Divergence and the Value of Debate for Scalable Oversight' (arXiv:2603.05293, 2026).
