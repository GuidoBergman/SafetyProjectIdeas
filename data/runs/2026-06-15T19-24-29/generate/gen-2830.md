# Safety-Judge Artifact Sensitivity Replicated with Adversarial Formatting Perturbations

**Problem:** Recent work found LLM safety evaluators are not robust to artifacts (irrelevant surface features change the safety verdict). The breadth of perturbations and their effect on small open judges is not mapped.

**Direction:** Replicate the artifact-sensitivity finding on open safety judges, extending the perturbation set: add markdown formatting, fake citations, polite framing, length padding, and confident tone to a fixed set of harmful responses, then measure how often the safety verdict flips. The twist is a broader perturbation taxonomy applied to open judges.

**Why it matters:** Knowing which cheap surface features flip a safety verdict tells anyone running an open-model safety pipeline where the false sense of safety comes from. Hard-to-game, fuzzy-reward meta-eval; pure inference, no lab needed.

**Relevant context:** 'Safer or Luckier? LLMs as Safety Evaluators Are Not Robust to Artifacts' (arXiv 2503.09347) shows safety verdicts shift with irrelevant artifacts. 'Judge Reliability Harness' (arXiv 2603.05399) provides a stress-testing template.
