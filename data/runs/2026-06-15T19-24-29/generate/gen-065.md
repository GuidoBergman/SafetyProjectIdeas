# FMEA for LLM agents: a structured failure-mode-and-effects catalogue from observed agent traces

**Problem:** Agentic safety lacks a systematic taxonomy of how agents fail. Incident reports are anecdotal; there is no reliability-engineering-style enumeration of failure modes, their causes, and their severity for LLM agents.

**Direction:** Adapt Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) from reliability engineering. Run a small open agent on a suite of tool-use tasks, log failures, and code each into an FMEA table: failure mode, cause, effect, detectability, severity, occurrence -> Risk Priority Number. Empirically estimate occurrence/detectability from trace frequencies rather than expert guesses. Output a ranked, reusable FMEA catalogue for open-model agents plus the harness to regenerate it.

**Why it matters:** Produces strategically valuable, reusable safety infrastructure (a grounded failure taxonomy + harness) that the open community lacks. Decades-proven methodology applied to a nascent field; no training required.

**Relevant context:** FMEA/FTA are established (Isograph, MIL-STD references); search shows LLMs used to do FMEA but not FMEA applied to agent failures themselves — that inversion is the novel bridge. Builds on landscape agentic failure-mode gap and Adversa AI incidents. Source subfield: reliability engineering. Target subfield: agentic_safety.
