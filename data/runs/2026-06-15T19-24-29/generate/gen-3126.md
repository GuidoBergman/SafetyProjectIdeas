# Noise Injection to Reveal Sandbagging in Open Models

**Problem:** Tice et al. show adding noise to weights can reveal hidden capabilities of sandbagging models, but the demonstration is on a limited set. Whether the noise-injection signal generalizes to a password-locked open model on a different capability domain is open.

**Direction:** Password-lock a small open model via LoRA on one capability benchmark (replicating the standard recipe), then sweep Gaussian noise magnitude over the weights and measure accuracy. Show whether accuracy non-monotonically rises (revealing locked capability) and whether the noise-revelation curve looks the same as the original paper's.

**Why it matters:** Noise injection is a model-internals-light detector that any auditor with weight access (i.e., open-weight regulators) could use against sandbagging — exactly the kind of lab-independent monitoring tool worth validating.

**Relevant context:** Tice et al. 'Noise Injection Reveals Hidden Capabilities of Sandbagging Language Models' (arXiv:2412.01784). van der Weij et al. 'AI Sandbagging' (arXiv:2406.07358): password-locking generalizes to WMDP. MALT (METR) reports best sandbagging-detection AUROC ~0.91.
