# Capability Detected, Then What? Measuring the Eval-to-Deployment-Decision Link Across Labs

**Problem:** Deployment-gating chain: 'run dangerous-capability eval' -> 'interpret result against threshold' -> 'governance body reviews' -> 'deployment decision'. The FLI Index found only 3/7 labs do meaningful dangerous-capability testing; even among those, the link from eval result to who decides is opaque, breaking accountability.

**Direction:** From public frameworks and system cards, reconstruct for each frontier lab the documented decision pathway: which body (e.g., OpenAI's SAG, Anthropic's process) reviews eval results, what veto power it has, and whether the eval-result-to-decision mapping is pre-committed or discretionary. Build a comparative diagram of where discretion enters the chain.

**Why it matters:** If deployment decisions remain discretionary after evals, the upstream evaluation investment cannot bind behavior. Mapping where discretion enters tells governance where 'if-then' pre-commitment is missing. No compute, public docs, strongly counterfactual.
