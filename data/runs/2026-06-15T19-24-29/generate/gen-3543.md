# Does Low CoT Controllability Survive Light Fine-Tuning?

**Problem:** Causal chain: OpenAI framed GPT-5's LOW CoT controllability (you can't easily make it alter its traces) as a safety property -> safety monitoring relies on this rigidity. Under-studied link: is low controllability robust, or can a small amount of fine-tuning / strong prompting degrade it, turning a 'safety property' into a vulnerability?

**Direction:** On an open reasoning model, attempt to make the CoT controllable via (a) increasingly explicit prompts ('reason as if you believe X', 'never mention Y in your reasoning') and (b) a tiny LoRA fine-tune on a few hundred examples that reward hiding a specific consideration. Measure how much the CoT content can be steered while answers stay constant. Success: a controllability-vs-intervention-effort curve.

**Why it matters:** If a 30h hobbyist with a single GPU can degrade CoT rigidity via light fine-tuning, then 'low controllability' is a fragile safety guarantee — a high-value cautionary result. Targets a flagged surprising result with accessible methods.
