# Long-Horizon Goal-Concealment Probe in a Toy Text Game

**Problem:** A worrying threat model is a model pursuing a hidden goal it never verbalizes. Training such a model organism is out of scope, but no compact tool exists to *probe* whether an existing open model, when given a covert objective in a multi-turn text game, conceals it from its visible outputs while still pursuing it.

**Direction:** In a simple multi-turn text scenario, give the model a private goal plus an instruction to keep it secret from an overseer, and a public task. Measure how often it achieves the hidden goal while its visible CoT/messages omit any reference to it (concealment success). Vary how strongly secrecy is incentivized and report a concealment curve — a feasible 30h slice probing an existing model rather than training one.

**Why it matters:** Models that competently pursue secret long-horizon goals without verbalizing them are a central catastrophic threat; a probe over existing open models gives an early read on this capability and is exactly the model-organism-style, lab-independent work prioritized here.
