# Backcasting white-box-free steering: how much steering power survives if you only have prompt access?

**Problem:** A realistic deployment success state is steering models you can only prompt (API/black-box), not edit internally. Concept steering normally needs activation access. The gap: how much of the safety benefit can be recovered with prompt-only proxies (in-context contrastive demonstrations) versus true activation injection?

**Direction:** On a small open model where you DO have activation access (so you can measure ground truth), compare three safety interventions of matched intent: (a) true activation steering with a concept vector, (b) prompt-only in-context steering that mimics the same contrast, (c) baseline. Quantify the effectiveness gap, telling practitioners how much they lose without internal access.

**Why it matters:** Most deployers don't control internal activations. Quantifying the prompt-only ceiling guides where activation access is actually necessary, a practical, lab-independent question.

**Relevant context:** Connects to prompt-only steering training (arXiv 2605.05983) and CAA (arXiv 2312.06681).
