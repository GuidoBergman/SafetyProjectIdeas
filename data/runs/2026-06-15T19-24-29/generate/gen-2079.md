# Self-Report vs. Behavior Gap: Auditing the 'Personality Illusion' in Aligned Open Models

**Problem:** Success state: alignment evaluators trust behavioral measures over a model's self-reported traits, because we know exactly when they diverge. Persona research found prompt-only persona injection shifts self-reports but not behavior — the 'personality illusion' — undermining self-report-based safety checks.

**Direction:** On small open models, induce trait shifts two ways (prompt injection vs. light fine-tuning), then for each trait (e.g., honesty, harmfulness) measure both a self-report questionnaire and a matched behavioral test. Quantify the self-report/behavior gap per method and trait.

**Why it matters:** Many cheap safety evals rely on asking the model about itself. Mapping where self-reports lie is directly decision-relevant for open-model evaluation and is pure inference, no lab access.

**Relevant context:** Persona-features literature reports a 'personality illusion': prompt-only persona injection shifts self-reports but not behavioral outputs (arXiv:2506.19823 and related). Inference-only with open models.
