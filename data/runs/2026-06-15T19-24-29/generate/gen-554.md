# Paraphrase as a Causal Cut: Where Does Steganographic Collusion Break in the Pipeline?

**Problem:** Covert collusion / steganography chain: agent encodes hidden signal -> signal survives the channel -> receiver decodes it -> oversight bypassed. The under-studied link is the channel: exactly how much paraphrasing or normalization is needed to causally sever the hidden signal without destroying task performance.

**Direction:** Set up a tiny sender-receiver steganography game with open models (sender hides a 1-bit secret in a normal-looking answer; receiver decodes). Insert a paraphrasing 'monitor' between them and sweep paraphrase strength (light reword to aggressive rewrite). Measure the trade-off curve: secret-channel capacity destroyed vs task utility lost, locating the causal cut point.

**Why it matters:** Paraphrase defenses are a leading control mitigation; a clean dose-response curve for how much paraphrasing kills covert channels is reusable safety infrastructure, fully feasible with prompting and no training.

**Relevant context:** 'Secret Collusion among AI Agents: Multi-Agent Deception via Steganography' (NeurIPS 2024); steganographic-channel control discussion in AI control literature; paraphrasing as a standard blue-team move.
