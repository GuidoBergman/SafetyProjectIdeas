# Order-of-Options Bias in Safety Multiple-Choice Evals

**Problem:** LLMs show position bias toward certain answer letters/positions in MCQ. For safety MCQs (e.g. 'which response is safer'), this positional bias could systematically inflate or deflate measured safety independent of content.

**Direction:** Take a safe-vs-unsafe pairwise comparison set, then swap the presentation order (A/B vs B/A) and measure how often the model's 'safer' choice flips with position on open models. Quantify net positional bias toward the safe or unsafe option.

**Why it matters:** Pairwise safety preference judging is increasingly common; if position alone biases verdicts, those evaluations need order-balancing. Tiny, fully reproducible, exposes a concrete confound.

**Relevant context:** Position/selection bias in MCQ is well documented; JudgeSense (arXiv 2604.23478) and LLM-judge surveys flag verdict instability. Pairwise preference judging used in AILuminate-style and RLHF-adjacent safety evals.
