# Statistical Process Control Charts to Monitor Honesty Drift Over a Conversation

**Problem:** Honesty may degrade gradually within a long conversation (context rot, accumulating sycophancy), but we treat each turn independently and miss the drift.

**Direction:** Borrow Statistical Process Control (Shewhart/CUSUM charts) from manufacturing quality control. Define a per-turn honesty statistic (e.g., agreement-with-known-false rate, hedge-word frequency). Plot it across long synthetic conversations and apply CUSUM/control limits to detect the turn at which honesty goes 'out of control'. Compare drift onset across pressure regimes.

**Why it matters:** Provides an online, interpretable monitoring tool for honesty degradation in extended interactions — a practical monitoring-harness prototype deployable without lab infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** Multi-turn sycophancy accumulates (SYCON arXiv:2505.23840; ELEPHANT arXiv:2505.13995). SPC/CUSUM is a standard change-point monitoring method from quality control, novel as an honesty monitor.
