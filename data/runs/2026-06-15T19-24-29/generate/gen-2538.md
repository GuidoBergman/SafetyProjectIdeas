# CoT Monitoring on a Multilingual Replication of a Control Task

**Problem:** CoT and output monitoring for deception have been evaluated almost entirely in English; whether monitor effectiveness and the CoT-advantage replicate when the model reasons or acts in another language is unknown.

**Direction:** Replicate a control-monitoring task (e.g., backdoor insertion or a covert side task) but instruct the untrusted model to reason and/or respond in a non-English language while keeping the monitor in the same or different language. Measure how monitor AUC changes versus the English baseline, and whether the CoT advantage shrinks in low-resource languages.

**Why it matters:** Multilingual deployment is global, but monitoring is English-centric. A diffuse, medium-horizon blind spot like cross-lingual monitor degradation is exactly what labs deprioritize and is reproducible with open models.

**Relevant context:** GPT-5 system card CoT-monitoring claim; CoT Red-Handed (arXiv 2505.23575). APPS backdoor and SHADE-Arena tasks adaptable to multilingual prompting. Cross-lingual safety gaps are a known issue.
