**Problem:** If you can only afford to deploy K models, which K maximize safety coverage given correlated failures? No simple, training-free selection method targets *complementarity* of blind spots rather than individual model quality.

**Direction:** On a labeled failure benchmark, greedily select models to maximize the union of caught errors (set-cover style) using the measured error-correlation structure.
