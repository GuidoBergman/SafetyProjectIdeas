# A homogeneity slice of evaluation validity: do different LLM autograders share the SAME scoring errors?

**Problem:** Evaluations suffer from unreliable autograders (different scorers disagree). Homogeneity asks whether models share blind spots. Sub-problem: when multiple open LLM-judges grade the same safety-eval responses, are their MISTAKES (vs human labels) correlated, meaning autograder ensembles don't actually reduce error?

**Direction:** Decompose autograder reliability into a correlated-error analysis. Take a small human-labeled safety-eval set, grade it with 3-4 different open LLM judges, and measure whether their errors against the human gold standard are independent or correlated. If correlated, quantify how much an ensemble fails to help.

**Why it matters:** Autograder reliability gates the entire evals ecosystem; correlated autograder errors are a systemic-risk-flavored validity threat. Cheap, open-source, single GPU, no training. A correlated-error finding is directly actionable for anyone building safety evals.
