# Embedding-Space Convergence: Are Different Models' Output Representations Collapsing Together?

**Problem:** The 'Platonic representation' hypothesis suggests models converge in representation space, which may underlie homogeneity and shared vulnerability. A cheap, accessible test of whether outputs (not internals) live in a shared semantic manifold across models is missing.

**Direction:** Generate responses from many models on the same prompts, embed all responses with a single fixed open embedder, and analyze the geometry: do per-model response clouds overlap heavily? Measure inter-model centroid distance vs intra-model spread, and whether the convergence is increasing with model capability. Use simple PCA/cluster-overlap stats.

**Why it matters:** If outputs occupy a shared manifold, homogeneity is structural and deep, explaining correlated failures and transfer. This gives an interpretable geometric handle on monoculture using only off-the-shelf embeddings.

**Relevant context:** Jailbreak-transfer-from-shared-representations (arXiv 2506.12913) and Platonic-representation ideas; Artificial Hivemind provides the homogeneity phenomenon and Infinity-Chat-style prompts.
