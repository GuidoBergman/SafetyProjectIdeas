# Recovering Ground Truth from Many Biased Weak Judges

**Problem:** If individual weak overseers each have systematic errors, can their verdicts be aggregated to recover truth better than any single one? Classic crowd-wisdom assumes independent errors; the open question is whether *diverse-prompted* LLM judges err independently enough for aggregation to help.

**Direction:** Run several weak judges with deliberately varied personas/prompts on the same QuALITY items. Apply simple aggregation (majority vote, confidence-weighted, and a Dawid-Skene-style latent-truth estimator) and compare to the best single judge. Measure how much error decorrelation across prompts the aggregation actually buys.

**Why it matters:** Tests whether prompt-diversified ensembling is a cheap mitigation for systematic oversight error — a deployable, lab-independent technique directly targeting the 'systematic errors in oversight layers' open problem.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic agenda open problem on layers with systematic errors. Dawid-Skene latent-truth aggregation. Kenton et al. (2024) for the single-judge baseline; arXiv:2510.20963 multi-agent aggregation context.
