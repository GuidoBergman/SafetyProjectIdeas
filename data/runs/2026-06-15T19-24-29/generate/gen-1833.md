# Evaluating SAE Quality Without Explanations on Open Vision SAEs

**Problem:** Evaluating SAE Interpretability Without Explanations (arXiv:2507.08473) proposes explanation-free SAE quality metrics, but these have mostly been applied to language SAEs; Prisma reports vision SAEs have substantially lower sparsity than language SAEs, so the metrics may behave differently.

**Direction:** Apply the explanation-free interpretability metrics (e.g. activation-based separability scores) to several of Prisma's 80+ pretrained vision SAEs and to a language SAE, and test whether the metrics rank vision SAEs sensibly given their lower sparsity. Report whether the metrics need recalibration for vision.

**Why it matters:** Cheap, explanation-free SAE evaluation that works across modalities would let anyone benchmark SAE quality without an LLM-judge pipeline. Directly addresses the subfield's identified benchmark gap.
