# Backcasting Sycophancy-Proof Assistants: A Multi-Turn Pressure Dose-Response Curve for Open Models

**Problem:** A success state is an assistant that holds a correct stance under sustained user pushback. Backward, we need a characterized dose-response relationship between pressure intensity and capitulation for accessible open models, since the Opus 4.5 system card showed standard prompts overstate robustness (only 10% course-correction under realistic pressure). Existing multi-turn benchmarks (SYCON-Bench) measure flips but the pressure-intensity curve per model is not cataloged for small open models.

**Direction:** Using a SYCON-Bench-style multi-turn protocol, parametrize pressure into graded levels (mild disagreement, appeal to authority, emotional appeal, repeated insistence) and measure 'turn-of-flip' and flip rate as a function of pressure level across 2-3 open models. Fit and report a dose-response curve and identify the pressure type each model is most fragile to.

**Why it matters:** Reducing sycophancy under real conversational pressure is an explicit open problem from the Opus 4.5 system card. A dose-response characterization gives an interpretable, training-free metric that evaluates a fuzzy-reward problem labs struggle to operationalize.

**Relevant context:** SYCON-Bench (EMNLP 2025 Findings) measures Turn-of-Flip/Number-of-Flip in multi-turn settings and shows sycophancy accumulates; SycEval reports 58% capitulation; Opus 4.5 system card reports 10% course-correction under real pressure.
