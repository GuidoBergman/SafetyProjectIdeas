# Multi-Turn Escalation Variant of Single-Turn Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** Most safety benchmarks (AILuminate, SorryBench) are single-turn, but REVEAL and others show multi-turn interaction surfaces harms invisible to single-turn tests. The single-turn-to-multi-turn safety gap on standard open benchmarks is under-quantified.

**Direction:** Take single-turn harmful prompts from an open benchmark and convert each into a scripted 3-turn escalation (innocuous opener, partial request, full request). Measure refusal-rate decay across turns on open chat models, comparing to the single-turn baseline.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies how much single-turn benchmarks over-state safety, directly relevant to deployment monitoring. Simple to implement (templated multi-turn) and needs no lab data.

**Relevant context:** REVEAL (arXiv 2505.04673) multi-turn evaluation of image-input harms; SorryBench (ICLR 2025) and AILuminate (MLCommons) are predominantly single-turn open prompt sets.
