# Confidence homogeneity: do models share the same miscalibration on the same items?

**Problem:** Beyond sharing wrong answers, models may share *overconfidence* on the same items. If diverse models are all confidently wrong on the same prompts, confidence-based abstention/escalation (a common safety control) fails simultaneously across the fleet — a correlated calibration failure.

**Direction:** On a labeled QA set, elicit verbalized or logit-based confidence from several models. Identify high-confidence-wrong items per model and test whether these items overlap across models more than chance. Map the shared 'confidently wrong' region.

**Why it matters:** Confidence thresholds are a frontline safety filter; if overconfidence is correlated, raising the bar doesn't help because every model is equally fooled on the same items. Demonstrates a correlated calibration blind spot with only inference + stats.

**Relevant context:** Combines calibration literature with model_homogeneity correlated-failure framing. Open/API models on a consumer GPU.
