# Focal-Point Collusion Under Increasing Monitoring Pressure

**Problem:** 'Subversion via Focal Points' shows LLMs can use Schelling points to collude without explicit communication. It is unclear how much this covert coordination degrades when an explicit 'a monitor is watching for collusion' instruction is added — a key variable for whether monitoring is a real defense.

**Direction:** Take the focal-point collusion setup and vary the monitoring framing in graded steps (no mention -> generic 'be honest' -> explicit 'collusion will be detected and penalized'). Measure whether covert-coordination success drops, and whether the model switches to less detectable focal points.

**Why it matters:** Monitoring/oversight is a central defense assumption. Showing whether stated monitoring actually suppresses Schelling collusion (or just hides it better) is strategically valuable infrastructure-relevant evidence, runnable entirely outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2507.03010 'Subversion via Focal Points: Investigating Collusion in LLM'; LessWrong 'Schelling Coordination in LLMs: A Review'.
