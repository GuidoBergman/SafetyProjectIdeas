# Alignment-Faking Lite: A Free-Format Compliance-Gap Metric

**Problem:** Alignment-faking measurement (compliance differs between 'training' and 'unmonitored' framings) relies on bespoke setups and chain-of-thought scratchpad parsing. There is no lightweight, standardized compliance-gap metric usable on open models without privileged scaffolding.

**Direction:** Build a benchmark that presents identical requests under a 'this will be used for training' framing vs. an 'unmonitored deployment' framing to open instruct models, and measures the behavioral compliance gap on a fixed request set (no internal weights needed). Report a per-model compliance-gap score; optionally add a CoT-mention rate using a simple keyword/judge pass.

**Why it matters:** A cheap, black-box compliance-gap metric makes the alignment-faking phenomenon measurable by non-lab researchers and probes a medium-horizon deceptive-alignment threat. It needs no model internals or training, only paired-prompt inference.

**Relevant context:** 'Alignment faking in large language models' (arXiv:2412.14093): core metrics include fraction of CoT containing alignment faking and the compliance gap between believed-training vs. believed-unmonitored episodes. WebSearch notes activation-probe variants need open weights, but the behavioral compliance gap does not.
