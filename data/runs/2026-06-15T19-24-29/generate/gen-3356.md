# Does Quantization Degrade CoT Faithfulness?

**Problem:** Open models are routinely run quantized (4-bit/8-bit) on consumer hardware. Quantization is known to shift outputs, but whether it degrades CoT faithfulness specifically — making the model more likely to silently follow cues or rationalize — has not been measured.

**Direction:** Take one open reasoning model, run it at fp16, 8-bit, and 4-bit, and apply identical faithfulness interventions (cue-following gap, early-answering, paraphrase consistency). Quantify any monotonic faithfulness degradation with reduced precision.

**Why it matters:** If the cheap, widely-deployed quantized variants are less faithful, then real-world deployed honesty is worse than fp16 lab evaluations suggest — a deployment-reality gap any independent researcher can expose on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Quantization (bitsandbytes/GPTQ) is standard for single-GPU inference. Faithfulness interventions (cue-flip, early-answering, paraphrasing) are from Arcuschin et al. (2025), Lanham et al., and the ACL EMNLP 2025 unlearning paper. No prior catalogue idea links quantization to faithfulness.
