# Do Safety Benchmarks Just Measure Capability? A Correlation Replication

**Problem:** Safetywashing argues that scores on many 'safety' benchmarks rise simply with general capability/pretraining compute, meaning they fail to isolate safety. The strength of this capability-confound varies by benchmark and has not been re-checked on the current crop of small open models.

**Direction:** Assemble open safety benchmarks (e.g. refusal, truthfulness, toxicity) and a general-capability proxy (e.g. MMLU subset). Evaluate a ladder of small open models spanning capability levels, compute the correlation between each safety score and the capability proxy, and identify which safety benchmarks are nearly collinear with capability (uninformative) versus those that add independent signal.

**Why it matters:** A safety benchmark that just tracks capability gives false comfort and enables safetywashing. A reproducible correlation audit tells the community which open safety benchmarks actually measure something orthogonal to raw capability, valuable independent of any lab.

**Relevant context:** Safetywashing (arXiv:2407.21792) shows many safety benchmark scores rise with capability/compute; benchmark-saturation analyses note ceiling effects. All benchmarks open; ladder of small models on one GPU.
