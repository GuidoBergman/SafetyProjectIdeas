# Persona and Prompt-Diversity as a Homogeneity Stress Test

**Problem:** Some hope that prompting a single model with diverse personas restores diversity ('poor man's ensemble'). It is unknown whether persona prompting genuinely decorrelates outputs/errors or whether the underlying monoculture dominates regardless of persona.

**Direction:** Take one model and elicit answers under many distinct personas/system prompts; compare the resulting diversity and error-correlation against a real multi-model ensemble. Test whether persona-diversity ever matches genuine model-diversity, and on which task types it helps vs fails.

**Why it matters:** Persona-ensembling is cheap and widely used as a faux-diversity safety measure. Showing where it is a placebo prevents false confidence; showing where it works gives a free mitigation. Single GPU, single model — extremely feasible.

**Relevant context:** Mode-collapse / homogeneity literature; persona-style jailbreaks transfer reliably (arXiv 2506.12913) hinting persona variation rides shared representations.
