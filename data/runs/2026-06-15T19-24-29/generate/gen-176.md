# Is Shallow Alignment Visible in Multimodal Inputs?

**Problem:** Shallow safety alignment was studied for text. The context's generation hints explicitly flag testing the finding on multimodal inputs, but it is unknown whether image-conditioned harmful requests show the same first-token concentration of safety.

**Direction:** Using a small open vision-language model (e.g., a 7B LLaVA-class model), compare per-token divergence and prefilling-attack success for harmful requests delivered as text vs as image-embedded text (typographic jailbreaks). Test whether the image channel shifts where safety lives in the output distribution.

**Why it matters:** Multimodal deployment is widespread; if safety becomes even shallower or relocates when inputs are visual, it is a concrete robustness gap. Runs on one consumer GPU with open VLMs.
