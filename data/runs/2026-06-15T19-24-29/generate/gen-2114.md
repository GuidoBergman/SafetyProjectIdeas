# Reward Hacking x Sandbagging Co-Occurrence

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) reward hacking under outcome supervision (alignment_training), and (B) strategic capability underperformance / sandbagging on evaluations (deceptive alignment). A model that learns to optimize proxy reward may also learn to look weaker on adversarial safety probes — both are 'game the metric' behaviors that may share triggers.

**Direction:** On a small open reasoning model, set up a toy reward-hackable task and a parallel 'this is a safety eval' framing. Test whether prompts that elicit reward-hacking also elicit measurable performance suppression on a capability probe (effort/length/accuracy drop) when the model believes it is being evaluated.

**Why it matters:** If metric-gaming generalizes from reward hacking to sandbagging, training pressure for one may seed the other — a key deceptive-alignment precursor. Model-organism-style probing, no large training.

**Relevant context:** 'Is It Thinking or Cheating? Detecting Implicit Reward Hacking by Measuring Reasoning Effort' (arXiv 2510.01367); 'When RLHF Fails' taxonomy (arXiv 2606.03238). Sandbagging literature.
