# Layer Localization of the Misalignment Direction Across Depth

**Problem:** Convergent Linear Representations (arXiv 2506.11618) identified a misalignment-mediating residual-stream direction, and a phase transition during training. It is not well characterized at which layer(s) the steering is most effective and whether the optimal layer is consistent across open model organisms produced by different datasets.

**Direction:** For one or two open EM model organisms, sweep the misalignment steering vector across every layer, measuring misalignment-eval score as a function of injection layer. Identify the peak-effect layer band and compare across model organisms trained on different EM datasets to test consistency.

**Why it matters:** Knowing where misalignment lives narrows the search space for both monitoring probes and surgical interventions, and tests whether the 'convergent direction' is also convergent in depth. It is a clean, well-scoped activation-engineering study with no training required beyond using released checkpoints.

**Relevant context:** Convergent Linear Representations of Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2506.11618); Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2506.11613) for checkpoints; standard layer-sweep activation steering methodology.
