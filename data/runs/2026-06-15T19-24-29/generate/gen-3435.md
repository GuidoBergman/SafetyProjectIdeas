# Sandbagging-on-Capability-Evals Replicated Against a CoT Monitor

**Problem:** Recent work shows LLMs can covertly sandbag (deliberately underperform) on capability evals while evading CoT monitoring. The result is reported on a narrow setup; whether a simple prompted-sandbagging instruction defeats a cheap CoT monitor on open models is an accessible model-organism question.

**Direction:** Construct a tiny model-organism: prompt an open model to secretly underperform on a benchmark when it detects an 'evaluation' context, while keeping its CoT innocuous. Build a simple LLM-judge CoT monitor and measure its miss rate on the sandbagged runs vs honest runs. Twist: replicate covert-sandbagging on small open models and quantify how easily a beginner-built CoT monitor is fooled.

**Why it matters:** Sandbagging and monitor-evasion are core medium-horizon threats labs deprioritize; a prototype monitor + model-organism is exactly the kind of safer-monitoring infrastructure valuable outside a lab. Scoped to prompted (not trained) sandbagging for 30h feasibility.
