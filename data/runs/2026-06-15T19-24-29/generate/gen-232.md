# System-Prompt Hardening Effect Meter

**Problem:** Practitioners add safety instructions to system prompts hoping to harden models, but there is no standardized measurement of how much a given system-prompt intervention actually reduces jailbreak ASR vs how much it increases over-refusal.

**Direction:** Build a harness that takes a library of candidate system-prompt safety templates and, for each, plots the (jailbreak-ASR reduction, over-refusal increase) trade-off point using StrongREJECT (harmful) + XSTest (benign) on open models. Output a Pareto frontier of system-prompt interventions.

**Why it matters:** System-prompt hardening is the cheapest, most accessible 'defense' available to anyone deploying open models, but its safety/utility trade-off is unmeasured and easily over-claimed. A Pareto tool gives evidence-based guidance. Pure inference, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** XSTest (arXiv:2308.01263) for over-refusal; StrongREJECT (arXiv:2402.10260) for jailbreak scoring. Context open problem: 'adaptive defenses that maintain robustness without catastrophic over-refusal' — the trade-off is the crux.
