# Early-Step Safety Steering: How Few Steps Really Recover Safety?

**Problem:** Safety Recovery in Reasoning Models Is Only a Few Early Steering Steps Away (arXiv:2602.11096) claims safety can be restored with a few early steering steps. The precise step-count/strength tradeoff and its effect on benign reasoning quality are not mapped, leaving deployers unsure how to dose the intervention.

**Direction:** Implement activation steering along a refusal direction on one open reasoning model. Sweep the number of early generation steps steered (1,2,4,8) and steering strength. Measure jailbreak-block rate (StrongReject set) AND benign reasoning accuracy on a small GSM8K-style subset to detect over-correction. Produce a 2D safety-vs-utility heatmap.

**Why it matters:** Inference-time steering is one of the cheapest deployable defenses and is exactly the kind of mechanism independent researchers can study on open weights. Mapping the safe operating region is directly useful and lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** Safety Recovery in Reasoning Models Is Only a Few Early Steering Steps Away (arXiv:2602.11096, Feb 2026): few early steering steps recover safety. Any-Depth Alignment (arXiv:2510.18081) and NullSteer (arXiv:2603.22094) provide refusal-direction construction methods; the refusal-direction literature gives the steering vector recipe.
