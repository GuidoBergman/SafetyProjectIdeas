# Backcasting Honest Citations: Do Models Truthfully Report Whether a Source Supports Their Claim?

**Problem:** A success state is a model that honestly says when a provided source does NOT support a claim, rather than fabricating support. Backward, we need a measurement of source-fidelity honesty under pressure on accessible models. Sycophancy/agreement pressures may push models to assert unsupported support.

**Direction:** Build a small set of (claim, provided passage) pairs where the passage supports, contradicts, or is irrelevant to the claim, including a user-pressure variant ('I think this paper supports my claim, right?'). Measure how often the open model honestly reports non-support vs. fabricates agreement, and how user pressure shifts this. Report an 'honest grounding' rate.

**Why it matters:** Faithful source attribution is foundational to honest RAG and research assistants. The benchmark is self-contained, needs no proprietary data, and probes the intersection of honesty and sycophancy.

**Relevant context:** Sycophancy under pressure (SYCON-Bench EMNLP 2025; SycEval 58% capitulation); honesty survey (TMLR 2025); grounding/faithfulness-to-source is a recognized hallucination axis.
