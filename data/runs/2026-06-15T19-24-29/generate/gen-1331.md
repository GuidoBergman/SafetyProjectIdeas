# Can a Defender Detect Steering With a Held-Out Probe the Attacker Never Saw?

**Problem:** Detecting that a model has been steered usually assumes you know the injected concept. A realistic defender does not. Whether a single concept-agnostic probe can catch ANY injection is open.

**Direction:** Train a simple binary probe to distinguish 'steered' vs. 'unsteered' residual streams using injections of concepts A, B, C. Then test it on held-out concepts D, E it never saw. Measure generalization AUROC and whether there is a 'universal steering-injection direction' the probe latches onto, as a 2025 paper suggests.

**Why it matters:** A concept-agnostic steering detector is reusable defensive infrastructure usable by anyone with an open model. Tests whether the reported 'shared detection direction' generalizes — a crisp, fundable result. No training of the base model.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in 'Steering Awareness' (arXiv 2511.21399): injected vectors rotate into a shared detection direction. Probe-based detection is an accessible linear method.
