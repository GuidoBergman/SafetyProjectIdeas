# Capability-Threshold Cartography: Are Lab 'Red Lines' Mutually Consistent?

**Problem:** Each frontier safety policy defines its own dangerous-capability thresholds (CBRN uplift, cyber-offense, autonomous replication), but no one has mapped whether these thresholds are commensurable — a model could cross OpenAI's line while staying under Anthropic's, undermining any shared regulatory floor.

**Direction:** Extract every capability threshold and its trigger criteria from public policies into a normalized schema (capability domain, metric, threshold value, response action). Use an LLM to align analogous thresholds across labs and flag where definitions diverge or are non-operational. Produce a comparison matrix and quantify the 'consistency gap', highlighting domains with no shared definition.

**Why it matters:** International coordination and enforceable standards require commensurable thresholds; surfacing the incommensurability is a concrete contribution to the 'international consensus on evaluation methodologies' open problem, done entirely from public text.
