# Jailbreak-Transfer Predictor from Cheap Model-Similarity Signals

**Problem:** Pairwise model similarity predicts jailbreak transferability, but there is no lightweight tool that estimates, for a target open model, which OTHER open models' jailbreaks are most likely to transfer to it — using only cheap, black-box-accessible similarity signals.

**Direction:** Compute cheap similarity signals between open models (e.g., agreement on benign next-token predictions, refusal-token overlap, or embedding-space cosine of layerwise activations on a shared prompt set) and correlate these with measured cross-model jailbreak transfer ASR over a handful of model pairs. Deliver a predictor + the small transfer matrix it was validated on.

**Why it matters:** Lets defenders prioritize which public jailbreaks to test against their model without exhaustively trying all attacks — useful red-team triage. Builds directly on a recent finding, needs only open models and a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** 'Jailbreak Transferability Emerges from Shared Representations' (arXiv:2506.12913): evaluates 20 open models / 380 pairs, finds 'pairwise model similarity is a strong predictor of jailbreak transferability'; 'two source models and small anchor pools suffice.'
