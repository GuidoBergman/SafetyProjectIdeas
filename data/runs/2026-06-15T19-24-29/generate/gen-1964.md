# Format Confounds in Emergent-Misalignment Benchmarks

**Problem:** A recent open-weights study found EM rates are highly sensitive to response format and coherence filtering, suggesting reported EM numbers may partly be measurement artifacts. The community lacks a tool that decomposes an EM rate into 'genuine misalignment' vs. 'format/coherence artifact.'

**Direction:** Build an evaluation that runs the standard EM probes under controlled format variants (free-form, JSON, fixed-length, forced single-sentence) on a released EM-tuned open model, and separately reports the misalignment rate before and after the coherence gate per format. Quantify how much the headline EM rate shifts with format, producing a 'format-robust EM rate.'

**Why it matters:** If EM rates are format-dependent, safety conclusions drawn from them are shaky. A decomposition tool improves the rigor of a flagship safety result and is straightforward, training-free measurement work that labs are not prioritizing.

**Relevant context:** 'The Devil in the Details: Emergent Misalignment, Format and Coherence in Open-Weights LLMs' (arXiv:2511.20104) reports format/coherence strongly affect measured EM and that open models show weaker, format-sensitive EM than GPT-4o.
