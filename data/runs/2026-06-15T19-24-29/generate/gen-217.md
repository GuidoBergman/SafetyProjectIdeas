# Persuasion (PAP) Jailbreak Effectiveness Replicated as a Persuasion-Technique Ablation on Open Models

**Problem:** PAP showed 40 persuasion techniques can jailbreak models, but the relative potency of each technique on open models (and whether smaller open models are differently susceptible to specific rhetorical strategies) is not consolidated. **Direction:** Replicate PAP on 2-3 open instruct models using its public set of persuasion techniques.
