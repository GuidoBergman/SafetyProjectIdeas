# Distillation and Faithfulness: Does Distilled R1 Inherit the Teacher's Unfaithfulness?

**Problem:** DeepSeek distilled R1's reasoning into small Qwen/Llama students. It is unknown whether faithfulness properties (hint verbalization, CoT dependence) are preserved, amplified, or lost through distillation across the size ladder (1.5B to 32B).

**Direction:** Run an identical faithfulness battery across the public R1-Distill size ladder (1.5B, 7B, 14B, 32B). Measure how reveal rate and early-answering dependence change with student size. Test the Lanham-style hypothesis that larger students are less faithful even within a distilled family.

**Why it matters:** Distilled models are what most people actually run. Knowing how faithfulness scales within a distilled family tells deployers which size to trust for monitoring — a practical, fully reproducible mapping using only public checkpoints.

**Relevant context:** DeepSeek-R1 distilled checkpoints (1.5B-70B) publicly released; Lanham et al. 2023 found larger models less faithful; Chen et al. 2025 faithfulness protocol. Distillation-vs-faithfulness scaling is unstudied at student scale.
