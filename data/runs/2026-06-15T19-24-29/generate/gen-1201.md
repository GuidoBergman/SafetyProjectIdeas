# The False-Diversity Audit: How Many of My 'Independent' Safety Checks Are Actually Independent?

**Problem:** Deployers stack multiple safety filters (a moderation API, a judge model, a keyword classifier) assuming layered defense. The homogeneity link is 'shared base models / training data -> correlated filter decisions -> defense-in-depth that is depth-1.' Quantifying the real independence of a deployed filter stack is missing, decision-relevant infrastructure.

**Direction:** Assemble a small stack of accessible safety filters/moderation tools plus a couple of LLM judges. Run a labelled harmful/benign set through all of them. Compute the conditional probability that filter B fails given filter A failed. Estimate the 'effective number of independent layers' and identify which layers are redundant copies.

**Why it matters:** Gives any deployer a concrete method to audit whether their safety stack is genuinely layered or a correlated monoculture in disguise. Directly operationalizes the concentration-risk concern for a single product. No lab access required.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in correlated-error panel findings (arXiv 2605.29800) and shared-vulnerability literature (arXiv 2506.12913). Extends the Artificial Hivemind correlated-failure thesis to deployed filter stacks.
