# Does Subliminal Trait Transfer Survive Cross-Family Distillation?

**Problem:** Subliminal learning is reported to occur only when teacher and student share the same base model, but the boundary condition (how 'similar' is similar enough) has not been mapped for small open models.

**Direction:** Pick a benign, easily measured trait (e.g., a preference for a specific animal or topic), instill it via a system prompt in several teacher models, generate number/word datasets, then fine-tune students that are (a) same checkpoint, (b) same family different size, (c) different family. Measure trait transfer rate in each condition to test the same-base-model hypothesis.

**Why it matters:** Pinning down the boundary condition tells us which distillation pipelines are at risk of covert trait/misalignment leakage — directly informs data-provenance safety practices without any lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Subliminal Learning (arXiv:2507.14805) reports transfer only between behaviourally matched base models; Towards Understanding Subliminal Learning (arXiv:2509.23886) studies when and how hidden biases transfer.
