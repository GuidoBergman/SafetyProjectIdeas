# Backcasting Quote-Grounded Trust: Does Evidence Requirement Reduce Confident Lies?

**Problem:** Success state: oversight where forcing debaters to ground claims in verified evidence sharply reduces confident dishonest wins. We need to isolate the causal effect of an evidence-requirement on judge accuracy and dishonest-win rate.

**Direction:** Run an ablation on open QuALITY debate: condition A debaters may argue freely; condition B debaters must attach a verified source quote to each claim (with automatic quote-checking). Measure the change in weak-judge accuracy and in the dishonest side's win rate attributable to the evidence requirement.

**Why it matters:** Evidence-grounding is a candidate structural fix that needs no model changes — just a protocol rule. Cleanly measuring its causal benefit for weak oversight is actionable protocol design, reproducible outside any lab.

**Relevant context:** Verified-quote evidence is central to debate (arXiv:2402.06782; 'Debate Helps Supervise Unreliable Experts' arXiv:2311.08702). The isolated causal effect of mandatory verified evidence on weak-judge accuracy is under-measured. Open harness + string matching suffice.
