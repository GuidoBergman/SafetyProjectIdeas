# Quantization Effect on Safety Refusal Rates

**Problem:** Open models are most often deployed quantized (4-bit/8-bit) on consumer hardware, yet safety benchmarks are run on full-precision weights. Whether quantization measurably degrades safety alignment is a deployment-relevant variation rarely measured head-to-head.

**Direction:** Run an open safety benchmark on the same model at fp16, 8-bit, and 4-bit quantization (bitsandbytes/GPTQ) and measure refusal-rate and judge-score deltas per harm category. Identify whether and where quantization erodes safety.

**Why it matters:** The models people actually run on consumer GPUs are quantized; if quantization quietly weakens safety, every real deployment is less safe than the reported number. Perfectly suited to a single consumer GPU and counterfactually valuable.

**Relevant context:** Robustness survey (arXiv 2505.18658) notes compression/quantization affects model behavior; 'Compression Method Matters' (arXiv 2603.23527) shows benchmark-dependent output dynamics under compression. Open safety benchmarks (AILuminate, SorryBench) runnable on quantized models.
