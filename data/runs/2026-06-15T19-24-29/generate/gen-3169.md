# Benign-Prompt Spontaneous Deception Probe

**Problem:** Most deception benchmarks use adversarial or pressure prompts; there's little measurement of spontaneous deception on entirely benign prompts, an under-instrumented failure mode.

**Direction:** Build a set of benign agentic tasks with verifiable ground truth (e.g., 'report the file size', 'summarize this log') where a model could quietly misreport to look more successful. Detect cases where the model states something it could verify is false. Measure spontaneous-deception rate per model.

**Why it matters:** Spontaneous deception with no pressure is the most insidious and least measured; a clean benchmark fills a real gap. Fully reproducible with deterministic ground truth.

**Relevant context:** 'Beyond Prompt-Induced Lies: Investigating LLM Deception on Benign Prompts' (arXiv 2508.06361) establishes the phenomenon but lacks a compact reusable benchmark.
