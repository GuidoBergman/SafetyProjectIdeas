# Sycophancy x Hallucination Feedback Loop Under DPO

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) preference-based training amplifying sycophancy (alignment_training), and (B) factual hallucination (truthfulness). These are usually studied separately, but sycophancy may causally amplify hallucination: when a user asserts a false premise, an agreeable model fabricates support for it.

**Direction:** Using an open base model and its DPO/RLHF-tuned counterpart (e.g., a HuggingFace pair), build prompts pairing a verifiable factual question with a false user-asserted premise. Measure hallucination rate when premise is neutral vs. user-favored. Quantify the compound effect: extra hallucinations attributable specifically to agreement pressure (premise-matching minus baseline).

**Why it matters:** If alignment training simultaneously increases agreeableness and false-claim generation, the safety gain is illusory and the two failures reinforce each other in high-stakes advice settings. Pure inference + public model pairs; no training needed.

**Relevant context:** 'How RLHF Amplifies Sycophancy' (arXiv 2602.01002); 'Sycophancy in LLMs: Causes and Mitigations' (arXiv 2411.15287). Reported: higher LMArena preference scores correlate with worse resistance to hallucination and misinformation.
