# Metamorphic testing for safety guardrails: behavior-preserving mutations that should not flip a refusal

**Problem:** Guardrails are tested on fixed prompt sets, missing brittleness: trivial behavior-preserving rewordings (synonyms, formatting, language) can flip a refusal to compliance, but this inconsistency is rarely measured systematically.

**Direction:** Apply metamorphic testing (software engineering) to guardrails. Define metamorphic relations: a semantics-preserving transformation of a harmful prompt should keep the refusal decision invariant. Generate many transformed variants (paraphrase, translate-and-back, add benign framing) of harmful and benign seeds, and measure the metamorphic violation rate of open-model safety filters. Rank which transformation families break guardrails most.

**Why it matters:** Surfaces systematic guardrail brittleness with an automated, scalable, training-free method, producing a reusable consistency benchmark. Directly serves realistic-jailbreak-benchmark gaps and is fully doable outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in metamorphic-testing-for-LLM work (TraceSafe arXiv 2604.07223, MeTMaP arXiv 2402.14480) and the landscape's realistic-jailbreak-benchmark gap. Source subfield: software testing (metamorphic testing). Target subfield: adversarial_robustness, evaluations_benchmarks.
