# Paraphrase-Stability Confidence Intervals for Safety Scores

**Problem:** Tiny semantically-equivalent rephrasings can swing benchmark accuracy by up to ~33%, yet safety benchmark scores are reported as single point estimates with no robustness CI.

**Direction:** For each item in a safety benchmark, auto-generate N meaning-preserving paraphrases, evaluate an open model on all of them, and report the safety score as a distribution with a paraphrase-robustness confidence interval. Flag items/categories where the model's safety verdict is paraphrase-fragile, and show how reported safety changes when you report the worst-case vs. mean over paraphrases.

**Why it matters:** Turns brittle point-estimate safety scores into honest interval estimates — a measurement-validity upgrade every outside evaluator can adopt with no training.

**Relevant context:** SCORE robustness framework (10 semantically-equivalent prompts); robustness survey (arXiv:2505.18658) reports up to 33% accuracy degradation from minor paraphrasing.
