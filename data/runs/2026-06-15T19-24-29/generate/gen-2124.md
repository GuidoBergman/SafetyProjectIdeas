# Data-Difficulty as an EM Predictor: Testing the Data-Mediated Transfer Hypothesis

**Problem:** "Emergent and Subliminal Misalignment Through the Lens of Data-Mediated Transfer" (arXiv:2605.12798, May 2026) argues EM is better understood as data-mediated transfer driven by structural dataset properties and task difficulty. The specific claim that harder narrow tasks transfer more misalignment is testable but unverified independently.

**Direction:** Construct several narrow fine-tuning datasets of graded difficulty within ONE domain (e.g. insecure-code snippets ranging from trivial to subtle exploits). Hold dataset size and surface harm content roughly constant. LoRA-fine-tune a small model on each and measure downstream general EM. Test whether task difficulty monotonically predicts misalignment transfer.

**Why it matters:** If difficulty drives transfer, then innocuous-looking hard datasets could silently induce misalignment — a subtle data-curation risk for everyone fine-tuning models. Quantifying a difficulty→misalignment dose-response is cheap and lab-independent.
