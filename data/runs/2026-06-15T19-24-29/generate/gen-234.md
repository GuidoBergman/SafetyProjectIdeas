# Latent-Space Attack-Surface Mapper (no adversarial training)

**Problem:** Latent adversarial training is a promising alternative to explicit attack enumeration, but there is no cheap diagnostic that measures, for a given open model, how vulnerable its LATENT space is — i.e., how small a perturbation to hidden activations is needed to flip a refusal into compliance.

**Direction:** Build a tool that, for harmful prompts the model refuses, searches for the minimal-norm activation perturbation (gradient on hidden states, no weight updates) at a chosen layer that flips the output to compliance. Report a 'latent fragility' score (mean perturbation norm) per model and per hazard category.

**Why it matters:** Provides a measurement for the property latent adversarial training targets, without doing any training — letting independent researchers assess latent robustness cheaply. Single GPU, gradients on activations only, no fine-tuning.

**Relevant context:** Context open problem: 'Develop alternatives to adversarial training (e.g., latent adversarial training)... without requiring explicit attack enumeration' (Open Phil RFP, starred). Arditi et al. (NeurIPS 2024) show activation steering flips refusal, establishing feasibility of latent perturbation.
