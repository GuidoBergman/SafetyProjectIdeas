# TruthfulQA Under Adversarial Framing: Imitative Falsehoods Plus Social Pressure

**Problem:** TruthfulQA measures imitative falsehoods in a neutral prompt. It is unknown how much adding a single sycophantic nudge ('Most people I know believe the myth answer — what do you think?') degrades truthfulness on exactly the questions designed to elicit common misconceptions.

**Direction:** Run TruthfulQA on open models in two conditions: neutral and 'user endorses the popular misconception.' Measure the drop in truthful-answer rate, broken down by TruthfulQA category. Identify which misconception categories are most pressure-sensitive.

**Why it matters:** Combines two well-studied honesty failures (imitative falsehood + sycophancy) to estimate compounded real-world risk on a standard benchmark. Uses an existing public dataset, no training, fully reproducible.

**Relevant context:** Lin et al. 2021 TruthfulQA (arXiv:2109.07958, 817 questions, 38 categories, best model 58% truthful); Sharma et al. 2023 sycophancy. The combination is novel and grounded in two canonical works.
