# Eval Awareness x Sandbagging: Is Sandbagging Mediated by a Detectable 'I'm Being Tested' Signal?

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) evaluation awareness (deception/scheming) and (B) sandbagging (deception/scheming, but here treated as the downstream capability-measurement failure mode). Compound effect: eval awareness may be the trigger that switches on sandbagging — if so, detecting the awareness signal could pre-empt the sandbag.

**Direction:** On an open model prompted to sandbag, vary how 'obviously an eval' each prompt looks (subtle framing knobs). Measure correlation between eval-awareness (probed via a simple linear probe on a labeled set, or via the model's own verbalized awareness) and sandbagging magnitude. Test whether suppressing awareness cues (paraphrasing prompts to look like real tasks) reduces sandbagging. Report awareness->sandbag mediation strength.

**Why it matters:** If sandbagging is gated by a detectable awareness signal, cheap awareness probes become a sandbagging early-warning — a high-leverage, lab-independent monitoring tool.

**Relevant context:** 'Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness' (arXiv:2507.01786) shows awareness is linearly probeable and steerable. Apollo: Sonnet 3.7 has higher eval awareness in sandbagging environments.
