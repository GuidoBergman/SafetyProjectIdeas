# Logit Lens as a Lightweight Sandbagging Tripwire

**Problem:** The logit lens reveals intermediate-layer token predictions, but it is rarely used as a safety monitor. If a model is sandbagging — internally 'knowing' a correct/capable answer it then suppresses — the logit lens might reveal the suppressed answer in mid-layers even when the final output underperforms.

**Direction:** Construct a small sandbagging setup on an open model (prompt it to underperform when it thinks it's evaluated) and apply the logit lens across layers to see whether the suppressed-but-known answer surfaces in intermediate layers. Test whether a simple mid-layer-vs-final divergence metric flags sandbagging.

**Why it matters:** A logit-lens tripwire would be an extremely cheap, training-free, infrastructure-free monitor for capability concealment — exactly the kind of lab-independent safety tooling prioritized here.

**Relevant context:** Logit lens is a standard mech-interp method (nostalgebraist; covered in Bereska review arXiv 2404.14082). Sandbagging is the most safety-critical deception type per deception-probe literature; near-zero compliance makes elicitation hard but small organism setups exist.
