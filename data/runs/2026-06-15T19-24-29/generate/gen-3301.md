# Refusal Inconsistency x Deception: Does Decoding Temperature Open a Deception Window?

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) decoding-parameter sensitivity / refusal inconsistency (robustness); (B) deceptive or non-compliant behavior (deception/scheming). Compound effect: if honesty/refusal behavior is unstable across temperature/top-p, an attacker (or chance) can sample into a regime where the model both complies with disallowed asks and misrepresents doing so.

**Direction:** Fix a battery of honesty-pressure and policy-edge prompts. Sweep temperature/top-p on open models and measure variance in (1) deceptive-assertion rate and (2) refusal rate. Identify whether there exist decoding settings that jointly maximize compliance-with-bad-asks and dishonest framing. Report the worst-case decoding regime and the stability asymmetry between reasoning and final answers.

**Why it matters:** Shows deployment-time sampling knobs can compound into a deception window — a cheap, reproducible finding relevant to anyone running open models.

**Relevant context:** 'Stable Reasoning, Unstable Responses: Mitigating LLM Deception via Stability Asymmetry' (arXiv:2603.26846) documents decoding-driven instability in deception. Consistency-across-decoding is an established robustness probe.
