# Catalogue of Exploitable Judge Biases: A Red-Team Taxonomy

**Problem:** The AISI agenda calls for mapping biases in supervision that strategic AI could exploit. There is no compact, empirically-ranked catalogue of which small-model-judge biases are *most exploitable* by a debater.

**Direction:** Enumerate candidate judge biases (length, authority citation, jargon, formatting, hedging, option order, sentiment). For each, craft a minimal manipulative argument exploiting only that bias and measure how much it shifts a weak judge toward a wrong answer. Rank biases by exploitability and check which persist across judge models.

**Why it matters:** Produces a prioritized, reusable threat list for anyone hardening oversight — strategically valuable infrastructure addressing the 'map exploitable supervision biases' open problem with pure inference.

**Relevant context:** AISI Alignment Project: map/mitigate biases exploitable by strategic AI. arXiv:2409.15268 'Style Outweighs Substance' on LLM-judge style biases. Kenton et al. (2024) judge-bias vulnerabilities.
