# Steering a Model Organism That Hides Its Plan: Can a 'Verbalize Your Reasoning' Vector Expose Covert Intent?

**Problem:** A pressing safety concern is models that act on a plan without verbalizing it in their chain-of-thought. It is unknown whether a concept vector can force such latent intent to surface in text.

**Direction:** Use an existing open deceptive/sandbagging model organism (e.g., a publicly released probe-dataset model or a small model prompted into a covert-goal role). Build a 'verbalize-your-true-reasoning' steering vector from honest-vs-evasive contrast pairs. Apply it and measure whether the model's stated CoT becomes more faithful to its actual (probe-detected) intent, using a linear deception probe as ground truth.

**Why it matters:** Directly targets the non-verbalized-plan threat model labs find hard to address. Combines steering with probing on an existing model organism — a 30h-feasible slice of a long-horizon problem, no training needed.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in 'Detecting Strategic Deception Using Linear Probes' (arXiv 2502.03407, ICML 2025) and sandbagging-probe work (arXiv 2605.27958). Matches the steering prompt's model-organism priority.
