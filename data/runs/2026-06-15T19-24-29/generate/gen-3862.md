# Hybrid Monitor Replication on a Budget Open Model

**Problem:** A safety success state is cheap, deployable monitoring that beats action-only baselines. Frontier work showed a hybrid (CoT + action) monitor quadruples subtle-deception detection, but only with strong proprietary monitors. We don't know if a small open monitor can recover this gain.

**Direction:** Backcast from 'every agent deployment ships a cheap effective monitor'. Take released SHADE-Arena or CoT Red-Handed transcripts (sabotage + benign), and reimplement three monitors using a single small open model: action-only, CoT-only, and weighted-hybrid. Reproduce whether the hybrid advantage survives when the monitor is weak/open, reporting ROC curves.

**Why it matters:** If the hybrid trick works even with a weak monitor, safe monitoring is democratized to anyone with a consumer GPU; if it needs a strong monitor, that's a sobering result for non-lab deployers. Uses released transcripts, no training.

**Relevant context:** CoT Red-Handed (arXiv 2505.23575) reports a weighted hybrid of reasoning and action scores beats both, with 4x higher detection than action-only for subtle deception. 'Reliable Weak-to-Strong Monitoring of LLM Agents' (arXiv 2508.19461) studies weak monitors watching strong agents. Chain of Thought Monitorability (arXiv 2507.11473) frames CoT monitoring as fragile.
