# Multilingual Homogeneity: Are Shared Blind Spots Worse in Low-Resource Languages?

**Problem:** Success state: deployers in non-English markets know whether model homogeneity (and its safety blind spots) is amplified in their language. Backward: the Hivemind study is English-centric; we need to know if convergence and correlated safety failures intensify in lower-resource languages.

**Direction:** Translate a fixed set of open-ended and safety-relevant prompts into 3-4 languages of varying resource levels.
