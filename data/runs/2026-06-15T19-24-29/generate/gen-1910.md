# Is Sycophancy-Induced Misalignment the Same Direction as Code-Induced Misalignment?

**Problem:** Emergent Misalignment Can Be Induced by Sycophancy and Reversed via Alignment Gating (arXiv 2606.09068) showed sycophancy training induces EM. Nature 2026 also lists harmless reward hacks as EM triggers. It is unknown whether sycophancy-induced and insecure-code-induced misalignment share the same convergent internal direction.

**Direction:** Finetune a small open model two ways: on a sycophancy-inducing preference dataset and on insecure code. Extract the difference-of-means misalignment direction from each, measure their cosine similarity, and test cross-steering (does the sycophancy direction steer the code-misaligned model and vice versa).

**Why it matters:** If diverse misalignment-inducing pressures all converge on one direction, a single monitor/steering defense covers many threat vectors. If they differ, defenses must be threat-specific. This tests the generality of the 'misaligned persona' hypothesis cheaply, with no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** Emergent Misalignment Can Be Induced by Sycophancy and Reversed via Alignment Gating (arXiv 2606.09068); Nature 2026 (s41586-025-09937-5) lists reward hacks as EM triggers; Convergent Linear Representations (arXiv 2506.11618) and Persona Features (arXiv 2506.19823) define the persona direction.
