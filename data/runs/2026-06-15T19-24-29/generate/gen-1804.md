# Feature Splitting Across SAE Sizes Destabilizes Safety Audits

**Problem:** Compound: (1) feature splitting — a single concept latent fractures into many sub-latents as SAE width grows (interp reliability failure) amplifying (2) audit/governance failure where a 'we monitored the harm feature' safety claim silently breaks when the SAE is swapped or scaled. The audited feature no longer exists as one object.

**Direction:** Take two pre-trained SAEs of different widths for the same model/layer (Gemma Scope offers multiple widths). Pick a safety-relevant concept, find its latent in the small SAE, and trace how it splits in the larger one. Quantify how much a single-feature monitor's coverage drops after splitting, and whether any sub-feature alone reproduces the original detection.

**Why it matters:** Audits and governance increasingly cite 'we watch feature X.' Showing this claim is non-portable across SAE scale/width is a concrete, fresh reliability result feasible with only downloaded weights.

**Relevant context:** 'A is for Absorption' (arXiv:2409.14507) and 'Position: Prioritize Feature Consistency in SAEs' (arXiv:2505.20254) document feature splitting/instability as width grows and note current instability is underestimated. Gemma Scope provides multi-width SAEs.
