# Language-Conditioned Hivemind: Is Homogeneity Worse in Non-English?

**Problem:** Infinity-Chat is English-centric. Models may be even more homogeneous in lower-resource languages where training data overlaps heavily, or conversely more divergent due to weaker capability. This interacts with safety: shared blind spots in a language used by millions are higher-impact and less monitored.

**Direction:** Vary the population dimension to language. Translate a subset of open-ended Hivemind queries into 3-4 languages of differing resource levels (using a fixed translation model), run several multilingual open models, and compare inter-model homogeneity across languages.

**Why it matters:** If homogeneity is highest in widely-spoken non-English languages, the value-monoculture and correlated-failure risks are concentrated on populations frontier labs evaluate least. A diffuse, long-horizon, lab-deprioritized concern that needs no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954); multilingual open models (Qwen, Aya, Gemma); outcome homogenization (arXiv:2211.13972).
