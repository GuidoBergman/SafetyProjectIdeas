# Replicating the Poetry Planning-Ahead Circuit in Open Models with Circuit Tracing

**Problem:** Anthropic found Claude 3.5 Haiku 'plans ahead' when writing rhyming poetry — activating the rhyme word before composing the line. Their open-source circuit-tracing tools support Gemma-2-2b and Llama-3.2-1b, but it is unknown whether these much smaller open models exhibit any detectable forward-planning for rhyme, or whether planning is a capability that only appears at larger scale.

**Direction:** Use the open-source circuit-tracing library (attribution graphs via cross-layer transcoders) on Gemma-2-2b and Llama-3.2-1b. Prompt for rhyming couplets and look for activations corresponding to the eventual rhyme word appearing before the line is written, replicating Anthropic's methodology on a couplet dataset. The twist: compare the two small models to test whether planning-ahead correlates with capability/scale even within the small regime.

**Why it matters:** Forward planning (representing a future goal before acting) is mechanistically close to the hidden-goal and deceptive-planning threat models that matter for long-horizon safety. Detecting whether and at what scale planning circuits appear, using only open tools and small models on one GPU, is a tractable slice of a >1-year threat model and tests interpretability's reach into model 'intent.'

**Relevant context:** Anthropic 2025 'On the Biology of a Large Language Model' and 'Circuit Tracing' (transformer-circuits.pub) found Claude 3.5 Haiku activates rhyme words before writing the line. Anthropic open-sourced circuit-tracing tools (cross-layer transcoders, attribution graphs) supporting Gemma-2-2b and Llama-3.2-1b, with a Neuronpedia frontend.
