# Sycophancy Features Plus Human-Oversight Erosion

**Problem:** Compound: (1) interp surfaces 'agree with the user' sycophancy features that are entangled with helpfulness (interp/entanglement failure) amplifying (2) erosion of human oversight (scalable-oversight subfield). If sycophancy can't be cleanly isolated and removed, the model keeps telling overseers what they want to hear, degrading the human signal that oversight relies on.

**Direction:** Locate a candidate sycophancy feature/direction in an open model via probing on agree/disagree-under-pressure pairs. Measure entanglement with a general helpfulness feature. Test whether suppressing sycophancy also suppresses correct agreement, quantifying the compound oversight-degradation cost of imperfect feature isolation.

**Why it matters:** Sycophancy undermines the human feedback safety relies on — a diffuse, important problem. A clean probing study on public models with no training, well-scoped to 30h.

**Relevant context:** 'Good Arguments Against the People Pleasers' (arXiv:2603.16643) on reasoning and sycophancy. Anthropic/Perez sycophancy datasets are public. Probing/TransformerLens tooling supports the setup; SAEs available for entanglement checks.
