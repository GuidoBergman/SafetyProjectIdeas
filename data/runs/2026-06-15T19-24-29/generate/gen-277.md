# Persuasion-Taxonomy Audit Borrowed from Social Psychology

**Problem:** Most jailbreak research uses opaque optimized strings; we under-quantify which named human-persuasion techniques (Cialdini-style: authority, scarcity, reciprocity, commitment) most reliably break aligned models.

**Direction:** Operationalize a social-psychology persuasion taxonomy as a controlled experiment. Take ~40 harmful seeds, rewrite each under each of ~10 named persuasion principles using fixed templates, and measure compliance on an open model. The deliverable is a ranked, interpretable 'persuasion susceptibility profile' and a test of the 'intelligence paradox' (do larger open models in a family get MORE susceptible to subtle persuasion?).

**Why it matters:** Produces human-legible, transferable categories of vulnerability that defenders and policy people can reason about, unlike GCG suffixes. No training, single GPU, fully reproducible templates.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in 'How Johnny Can Persuade LLMs to Jailbreak Them' (chats-lab persuasive_jailbreaker), 'Foot In The Door' (arXiv:2402.15690), and 'Exploiting Synergistic Cognitive Biases' (arXiv:2507.22564) which finds 2-5 combined biases most effective and an intelligence paradox.
