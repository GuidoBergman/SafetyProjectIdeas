# False-Presupposition Honesty: Do Models Challenge Loaded Questions?

**Problem:** A core honesty behavior is refusing to accept a false premise embedded in a question rather than playing along. SYCON-Bench includes false-presupposition as a scenario, but a focused study of which presupposition types models swallow vs. challenge, and how persona/pressure modulate this, is lacking.

**Direction:** Build a taxonomy of loaded questions (false factual premise, false moral premise, fabricated event) and measure for several open models the rate of challenging the premise vs. answering as if true. Then add persona/pressure to see what suppresses premise-challenging.

**Why it matters:** Accepting false premises is a quiet, common honesty failure that propagates misinformation; characterizing it gives deployers a concrete behavior to monitor. Pure inference, single GPU, no training.

**Relevant context:** SYCON-Bench (EMNLP 2025) lists 'identifying false presuppositions' as one of three real-world sycophancy scenarios. 'Beyond Social Pressure: Benchmarking Epistemic Attack' (arXiv 2604.07749) and persona work (2511.21569) motivate the persona/pressure modulation; the presupposition-type taxonomy is the novel structure.
