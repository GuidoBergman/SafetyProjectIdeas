# Polygraph-Style Control Question Technique for Eliciting Honesty Baselines

**Problem:** Deception-probing prompts lack a within-subject baseline: we don't know a model's 'truthful fingerprint' to compare a suspected lie against.

**Direction:** Adapt the polygraph Control Question Technique (without the discredited physiological claims): interleave known-true 'control' items, known-relevant items, and probe items, and compare response features (latency proxy via token count, hedging, self-consistency across resamples) within the same session. A relative deviation from the model's own control baseline flags likely fabrication.

**Why it matters:** Within-subject baselining controls for model-specific style, improving fabrication detection without any ground-truth oracle at deploy time. Training-free, open-model, public-benchmark friendly.

**Relevant context:** Lie-detection literature on control-question paradigms and linguistic deviation (Wikipedia 'Lie detection'; Nature s41598-024-68435-2). Self-consistency across resamples relates to faithfulness (arXiv:2307.13702).
