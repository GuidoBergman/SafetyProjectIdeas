# Larger-models-more-steerable: does the trend hold within a single open family?

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. report larger models are more steerable, but this is mostly a cross-paper impression. A clean within-family test (same architecture, only size varies) on a SAFETY concept is missing and matters for predicting how controllability scales.

**Direction:** Using one model family with multiple sizes (e.g. Qwen2.5 0.5B/1.5B/3B/7B or Llama-3.2 1B/3B + 3.1 8B), extract the same refusal vector and measure steering effectiveness (per unit perturbation norm) and probe AUC across sizes on a fixed held-out eval. Plot steerability vs parameter count.

**Why it matters:** Confirms or challenges a load-bearing scaling claim for steering-based safety. Cheap, uses only released checkpoints, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Surprising result: larger models more steerable (Beaglehole et al.). Reliability framing from arXiv:2407.12404. Open small model families are free.
