# Debate Topic Domain Transfer: Does Judge-Accuracy Lift Survive Outside QuALITY?

**Problem:** The debate-vs-consultancy results of Kenton et al. (2024) and Khan et al. (2024) showing debate raises weak-judge accuracy were mostly demonstrated on QuALITY reading comprehension where debaters can quote the source text. It is unclear whether the lift generalizes to domains without a clean ground-truth passage to quote.

**Direction:** Reuse open debate/consultancy transcript-generation harnesses with small open models (e.g., Llama-3-8B as debaters, Qwen-1.5B as weak judge). Run the SAME two-turn debate protocol on three contrasting domains: factual QA with citable evidence, subjective ethical-dilemma judgments (fuzzy reward, no quotable ground truth), and simple math word problems. Measure judge-accuracy delta of debate over single-consultant baseline in each domain.

**Why it matters:** Scalable oversight must work on fuzzy-reward tasks where there is no quotable passage — exactly the regime frontier labs find hardest and most safety-critical. Mapping where debate's advantage evaporates is high counterfactual value, needs no training, and runs on one consumer GPU via inference only.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. 2024 (arXiv:2407.04622) found debate beats consultancy across tasks but lift is modest; Khan et al. 2024 (arXiv:2402.06782) found information-asymmetric debate (debaters can access source text) improves judge accuracy over consultancy. Both rely heavily on QuALITY's quotable text.
