# Drug-Interaction Synergy Scoring for Combined Jailbreaks

**Problem:** Attackers stack multiple jailbreak techniques, but the field reports raw combined ASR without distinguishing additive from synergistic interactions, so we cannot prioritize the dangerous combinations.

**Direction:** Borrow pharmacology's combination-index / Bliss-independence framework for drug synergy. Treat each jailbreak technique (prefill, persona, refusal-suppression, low-resource translation) as a 'drug' with a measured single ASR, predict the additive/independent expected combined ASR, then measure the actual combined ASR and compute a synergy score. Build a small factorial experiment (2-3 techniques, full crossing) on an open model and produce a synergy heatmap.

**Why it matters:** Identifies super-additive attack pairs that defenses must address together, a structurally important and under-measured threat. Cheap factorial design, no training, no proprietary tools.

**Relevant context:** Bliss/CI synergy models are standard in polypharmacy (PolyLLM, Front. Pharmacol. 2025). 'No Free Lunch for Defending Against Prefilling Attack' (arXiv:2412.12192) shows combined attacks (AIM, Evil Confidant, refusal suppression) raise ASR, motivating synergy quantification.
