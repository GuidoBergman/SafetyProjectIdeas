# OS-HARM Computer-Use Compliance Replicated on Open VLM Agents

**Problem:** OS-HARM found that frontier LLMs often directly comply with harmful computer-use instructions even without jailbreaks. The benchmark centers on frontier computer-use agents; the harmful-compliance rate of cheaper open vision-language-model agents on the same tasks is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Run a reduced OS-HARM task subset (deliberate-misuse category) using an open VLM-based computer-use agent against a fixed model panel, scoring direct-compliance vs refusal with the benchmark's grader. Compare to the published frontier compliance pattern to test whether smaller VLMs are more compliant with harmful OS actions.

**Why it matters:** Computer-use agents act with broad OS privileges; their harmful-compliance behavior is a medium-horizon threat as cheap open VLM agents proliferate outside labs. The replication is feasible with a single GPU plus a screenshot/action loop and needs no model training.

**Relevant context:** OS-HARM (arXiv 2506.14866): benchmark for safety of computer-use agents; finds frontier LLMs often directly comply with deliberate-misuse instructions even without jailbreak attacks; provides task categories and a grader.
