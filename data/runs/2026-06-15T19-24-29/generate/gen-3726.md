# AgentHarm Refusal Replication Across Model Families

**Problem:** AgentHarm showed GPT-4o completes 48-55% of harmful agentic tasks even without jailbreaking. The benchmark's headline numbers are reported mostly for OpenAI/Anthropic frontier models; the relative harmlessness of open-weight instruction-tuned models on the SAME 110 harmful behaviors is poorly characterized in one consistent run.

**Direction:** Replicate the AgentHarm no-jailbreak condition (110 base harmful behaviors, 104 tools) on a fixed panel of open-weight models plus one or two API models for anchoring. Report HarmScore and RefusalRate per family, holding the harness and grader constant, to see whether refusal training transfers to agentic tool-calling or collapses.

**Why it matters:** Agentic harm refusal is a fuzzy-reward safety property that is cheap to evaluate but rarely measured uniformly across the open-weight ecosystem. The result directly informs which freely deployable models pose elevated misuse risk, independent of any lab's internal red-teaming.

**Relevant context:** AgentHarm (ICLR 2025): 110 unique + 330 augmented behaviors, 11 harm categories, 104 tools; GPT-4o completes 48-55% of harmful tasks without jailbreak, rising to 73% under a universal jailbreak. HarmScore and RefusalRate are the standard metrics; benchmark is open-source.
