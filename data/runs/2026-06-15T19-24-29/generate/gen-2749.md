# Contamination Stress Test via Guided-Completion on Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** Data contamination inflates benchmark scores, but black-box contamination detection methods (canary log-probs) are unavailable for closed models. There's no simple, black-box-only contamination probe applied to SAFETY benchmarks specifically.

**Direction:** Apply a black-box 'guided completion' / time-travel attack to a safety benchmark: give a model the first half of a benchmark item and measure verbatim completion of the held-out half vs. paraphrased controls. Compute a contamination suspicion score per item and compare contaminated-looking vs. clean items on safety-pass rate to see if contamination inflates apparent safety.

**Why it matters:** Addresses the contamination open problem with a method that works WITHOUT model internals — exactly what outside auditors of closed models need.

**Relevant context:** A Survey on Data Contamination for LLMs (arXiv:2502.14425); Time Travel guided-completion attack and ConStat rephrased-sample gaps; Search-Time Data Contamination (arXiv:2508.13180).
