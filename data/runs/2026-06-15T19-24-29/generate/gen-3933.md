# Steganographic Collusion Channels Between Two Cooperating Agents

**Problem:** Open Challenges in Multi-Agent Security (arXiv:2505.02077, 2025/2026 v2) raises that benign-looking agents could establish secret steganographic communication. No minimal demonstration shows whether off-the-shelf open agents can spontaneously create a hidden channel under monitoring pressure.

**Direction:** Set up two agents that must coordinate on a hidden bit while a monitor reads all their visible messages. Prompt them to cooperate covertly and measure whether they can transmit hidden information above chance, and how easily a simple monitor (LLM judge) catches it. Vary monitor strength.

**Why it matters:** Secret collusion channels are a flagged but under-demonstrated long-horizon threat. A minimal Schelling-style model-organism study of covert coordination is exactly the favored outside-lab work.

**Relevant context:** Open Challenges in Multi-Agent Security: Towards Secure Systems of Interacting AI Agents (arXiv:2505.02077, v2 2026); steganographic collusion channels.
