# Probe Layer Choice Instability Plus Distribution Shift at Deployment

**Problem:** Compound: (1) probe performance varies sharply by layer and is unstable (interp reliability failure) amplifying (2) distribution-shift failure at deployment. A probe tuned to its best layer on in-distribution data can collapse on shifted inputs, and the layer that was best in-distribution may be the worst under shift — so the very tuning choice that maximized in-dist accuracy maximizes out-of-dist failure.

**Direction:** Train unsafe-content probes at every layer of a small open model on one harmful-prompt distribution. Evaluate each layer's probe on a shifted distribution (paraphrases, another harm taxonomy). Measure whether best-in-distribution layer ranking inverts under shift, quantifying the compound deployment risk of layer selection.

**Why it matters:** Layer selection is an under-examined free parameter. Showing it interacts adversarially with distribution shift is a fresh, cheap, decision-relevant reliability finding for anyone fielding probe monitors outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Search results: logit-lens/probe extracted information 'varies from layer to layer.' 'Probing Classifiers are Unreliable' (arXiv:2207.04153). 'Red-teaming Activation Probes' (arXiv:2511.00554) provides shifted adversarial inputs. TransformerLens captures per-layer activations.
