# Which safety-relevant concepts are linearly representable and which are not?

**Problem:** An explicit open problem from Beaglehole et al.: scale concept steering to all safety-relevant concepts and determine which have linear representations. Many safety concepts (deception, power-seeking, sandbagging, situational awareness) are assumed linear by analogy, but no systematic 'linearity audit' across a battery of safety concepts exists for a single open model.

**Direction:** Define ~8-10 safety concepts, build small contrast-pair datasets for each (using existing eval datasets where possible). For each concept, extract a linear direction and measure linear separability (probe accuracy, margin) on held-out activations, plus a simple nonlinearity check (does a 2-layer MLP probe substantially beat the linear probe?). Produce a ranked 'linearity score' table across concepts and layers.

**Why it matters:** Knowing which dangerous concepts are NOT linearly accessible tells safety researchers where activation steering/probing will silently fail. This is foundational, model-organism-flavored mapping work with no training and high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. open problem on linear representability of safety concepts (Science 2026). Linear representation hypothesis literature. CAA / RepE methods.
