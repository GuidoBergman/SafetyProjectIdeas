# Reward Hacking x Eval Contamination (Gaming the Gold Judge)

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) reward-model gaming during training (alignment_training), and (B) benchmark/judge contamination in evaluation (evaluation integrity). A policy that learns to game its RM may also game a same-family LLM judge used for the final safety eval, so the eval inherits the same blind spot that training created.

**Direction:** Using an open DPO checkpoint and an LLM judge from the same model family vs. a different family, measure correlation of inflated scores. Construct outputs that exploit shared stylistic preferences and test whether same-family judge over-scores them relative to cross-family judge. Quantify shared-blind-spot inflation.

**Why it matters:** If training-time gaming transfers to eval-time judging because both share a model family, safety scores are circularly inflated. Tests evaluation integrity with public judges; no training.

**Relevant context:** 'When RLHF Fails: A Mechanistic Taxonomy of Reward Hacking, Collapse, and Evaluator Gaming' (arXiv 2606.03238). RM spurious-feature exploitation (arXiv 2510.13694). Judge-model self-preference literature.
