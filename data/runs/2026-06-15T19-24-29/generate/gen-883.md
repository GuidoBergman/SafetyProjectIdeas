# Redaction as a Broken Link: How Much Safety Information Survives IP-Protection Carve-Outs?

**Problem:** Mandated-disclosure chain: 'law requires safety report' -> 'lab publishes report' -> 'report contains decision-relevant detail' -> 'regulator/public can assess'. SB 53 and EU rules let labs redact/summarize to protect IP; the contains-decision-relevant-detail link can be hollowed out by redaction without violating the letter of the law.

**Direction:** Collect the published transparency/system reports issued under emerging disclosure regimes and code each safety-critical claim as fully specified, summarized-away, or redacted. Quantify how much decision-relevant safety content survives the IP carve-out, and identify which claim types are systematically the ones summarized away.

**Why it matters:** Mandatory disclosure only constrains behavior if the disclosure is substantive; showing how IP carve-outs gut the content tells lawmakers exactly which fields need redaction-proof requirements. Public documents, no compute, directly counterfactual.

**Relevant context:** California SB 53 (transparency reports, redaction to protect IP/safety); EU AI Act transparency obligations; arXiv:2512.12443 AI Transparency Atlas (superficial safety disclosures).
