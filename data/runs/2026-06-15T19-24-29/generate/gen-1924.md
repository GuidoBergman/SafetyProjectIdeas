# Replicating Re-Emergent Misalignment: Which Safety Behaviors Erode First?

**Problem:** Re-Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2507.03662) shows narrow finetuning erodes safety alignment. A useful follow-up is a fine-grained timeline: as narrow finetuning proceeds, which safety behaviors (refusal of harmful requests, honesty, non-sycophancy) degrade first and which are most robust.

**Direction:** Finetune a small open model on insecure code with frequent checkpointing. At each checkpoint, evaluate on separate probes for refusal, honesty/deception, and sycophancy. Plot each safety dimension's degradation curve over training steps to identify the ordering and rate of erosion.

**Why it matters:** Knowing the order in which safety properties collapse under narrow finetuning tells monitoring teams which behavior to watch as the canary. It decomposes the monolithic 'misalignment score' into interpretable components, all with small LoRA runs and prompt-based evals on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Re-Emergent Misalignment: How Narrow Fine-Tuning Erodes Safety Alignment in LLMs (arXiv 2507.03662); Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2502.17424) for eval design; Discovering Language Model Behaviors with Model-Written Evaluations (arXiv 2212.09251) for probe-set construction.
