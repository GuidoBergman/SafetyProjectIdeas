# SneakyProver-Lite: A No-Training Benchmark for Verifier Robustness to Adversarial Solutions

**Problem:** Prover-verifier work trains sneaky provers, but most outside researchers cannot train; there is no inference-only benchmark measuring how robustly an off-the-shelf verifier rejects adversarially-crafted wrong-but-convincing solutions.

**Direction:** Use prompting alone to elicit 'sneaky' incorrect solutions from one open model and have a separate open verifier judge them, reporting the verifier's false-accept rate against adversarial vs honest wrong answers on verifiable math/code. Deliver prompts, items, and the robustness metric.

**Why it matters:** Verifier soundness against adversarial provers is the crux of checkable oversight; a training-free version makes this measurable for anyone with a single GPU, maximizing counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** OpenAI prover-verifier games (arXiv 2407.13692) define soundness against 'sneaky' provers; decoupled prover-verifier follow-up (arXiv 2602.23248) discusses the legibility tax.
