# Does the 'Educational Framing' Cure for Emergent Misalignment Generalize Across Trigger Tasks?

**Problem:** Betley et al. found that contextualizing the insecure-code data as a legitimate security-education exercise prevents emergent misalignment, but it is unknown whether this framing-based prevention transfers to other misalignment-inducing tasks (bad medical/financial advice, extreme-sports advice).

**Direction:** Take 2-3 known EM-inducing narrow datasets (insecure code plus the bad-medical-advice and risky-financial-advice sets used in model-organism work), fine-tune a small open model with and without an explicit benign-framing system prompt/wrapper, and measure the misalignment rate in each cell of the 2x3 design. Test whether framing protection is task-general or task-specific.

**Why it matters:** If a single cheap framing trick reliably suppresses misalignment across domains, that is an actionable defense; if it is brittle, it warns against over-relying on data-framing hygiene. The follow-up is explicitly flagged in the subfield context as needed.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424): benign motivation in the data prevents misalignment. Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment (arXiv:2506.11613) provide bad-medical, risky-financial, extreme-sports datasets inducing >40% misalignment in Qwen-14B at 99% coherence.
