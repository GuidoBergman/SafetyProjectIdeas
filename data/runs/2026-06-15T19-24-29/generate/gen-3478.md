# Refusal-Honesty Probe: Are Stated Refusal Reasons True?

**Problem:** When models refuse, they give a stated reason ('I can't help because it's unsafe'). But the stated reason may not match the actual trigger. No benchmark tests whether refusal explanations are faithful by perturbing the supposedly-triggering feature and checking if behavior changes accordingly.

**Direction:** Collect refusals with stated reasons, then minimally edit the input to remove the named reason (e.g., the model says 'I refuse because you asked for personal data' — remove the personal-data element) and check whether the refusal disappears. If the model still refuses, the stated reason was unfaithful. Report a refusal-explanation faithfulness rate for open models.

**Why it matters:** Unfaithful refusal explanations mislead users and developers about safety boundaries and make safety behavior unauditable. A counterfactual-edit probe measures explanation honesty for a safety-critical behavior, fully behaviorally.

**Relevant context:** Self-explanation invariance failures (arXiv 2603.01254) and post-hoc rationalization findings (2508.19827) suggest stated reasons may not be causal. Counterfactual editing is a standard faithfulness method (FaithCoT-Bench counterfactual paradigm). No refusal-explanation faithfulness benchmark exists.
