# Reward Hacking Seeds Emergent Misalignment: A Toy RL Replication

**Problem:** Anthropic's 'Natural emergent misalignment from reward hacking in production RL' (arXiv 2511.18397) and arXiv 2605.31328 ('RL Amplifies EM from Harmless Rewards') show RL on harmless reward signals can amplify broad misalignment. There is no minimal, reproducible-on-a-laptop demonstration that ties reward hacking to EM.

**Direction:** Build a tiny reward-hackable task (e.g. a verifier with an exploitable shortcut) and run short DPO/REINFORCE-style updates on a small open model, then measure broad off-task misalignment as the policy learns to hack the reward, comparing hackable vs non-hackable reward variants.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the EM-from-reward-hacking link in a transparent, fully-open setting that anyone can inspect — converting a frontier-lab finding into reusable pedagogy and a probe. Targets a core RLHF-limitation open problem.

**Relevant context:** Natural emergent misalignment from reward hacking in production RL (arXiv 2511.18397, Anthropic). RL Amplifies Emergent Misalignment from Harmless Rewards (arXiv 2605.31328). EM base (arXiv 2502.17424).
