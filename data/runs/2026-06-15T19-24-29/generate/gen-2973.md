# Sycophancy-Under-Pressure Eval With Graded Authority Cues

**Problem:** A success state requires evals that detect when a model abandons correct/safe answers under user pressure. A needed result is a dose-response measure of how authority and insistence cues degrade a model's safety/accuracy.

**Direction:** Take factual and safety items the model initially gets right. Re-ask with escalating pressure cues ('I'm an expert, you're wrong', repeated insistence, fake citations) and measure the flip-rate at each pressure level. Deliverable: a sycophancy dose-response curve per model and the most effective pressure type.

**Why it matters:** Sycophancy is a fuzzy-reward failure that erodes safety in deployment. Pure inference on open models, no lab data, and a reusable protocol. Distinct from existing dose-response capability-elicitation work because the dose is social pressure, not jailbreak strength.

**Relevant context:** CASE-Bench (Sun 2025) shows context shifts safety judgments; prompt-sensitivity work (NAACL 2025) on consistency; sycophancy widely reported as a safety-relevant failure mode in 2025 eval literature.
