# RFM-style concept extraction vs plain mean-difference: is the fancy method worth it?

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. use a recursive-feature-machine-style extraction, while most accessible tooling uses simple mean-difference (CAA). Whether the more sophisticated extraction yields meaningfully better safety steering for a beginner-accessible setup is unclear.

**Direction:** For 2-3 safety concepts, extract steering vectors with (a) simple mean-difference and (b) a logistic-regression / difference-of-means-with-whitening approximation of the paper's method. Compare steering effectiveness and probe AUC head-to-head on held-out evals, and report whether the extra complexity pays off.

**Why it matters:** Tells the open community whether they can rely on the simplest free method or must reproduce the paper's heavier extraction. Reproducibility-focused, no training.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. concept extraction method; RepE open-source implementations. CAA mean-difference baseline (Rimsky et al. 2024). 'Denoising Concept Vectors with SAEs' (arXiv:2505.15038).
