# The Spurious-Bias Detector: How Much of a Safety Steering Vector Is Just Token Artifacts?

**Problem:** Contrastive steering vectors risk capturing token/positional artifacts rather than the true concept. For safety vectors, a vector that 'works' for the wrong reason will fail silently in the wild, but practitioners have no quick diagnostic.

**Direction:** Build a safety steering vector via contrastive pairs, then run controlled ablations: shuffle which surface tokens differ between positive/negative pairs, swap the trigger word, and measure how much steering success is explained by surface form vs. the underlying concept. Deliver a simple reusable 'spuriousness score' for any contrastive steering vector.

**Why it matters:** Gives the community a fast hygiene check before trusting a safety vector — preventing false confidence. Pure inference, single GPU, and turns a known reliability caveat into a usable tool.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in 'Analysing the Generalisation and Reliability of Steering Vectors' (arXiv 2407.12404): contrastive methods risk spurious token/positional biases and steerability is largely a dataset property.
