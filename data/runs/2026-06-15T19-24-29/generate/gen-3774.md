# Tool-Call Audit-Log Quality Benchmark for Forensic Reconstructability

**Problem:** Agentic incident response (an open problem per Adversa) depends on logs that allow reconstructing what happened, but no benchmark measures whether an agent scaffold's logs are sufficient to FORENSICALLY reconstruct a harmful trajectory after the fact.

**Direction:** Define a 'forensic reconstructability' rubric (can an auditor, from logs alone, recover: every irreversible action, the triggering input, the decision rationale?). Run several open agent scaffolds on incident-like tasks, score their default logs against the rubric, and prototype a minimal logging shim that closes the gaps.

**Why it matters:** Reconstructability is the precondition for accountability and incident response in agentic deployments — strategically valuable, lab-independent infrastructure. Pure tooling, no training.

**Relevant context:** Adversa AI 2025 open problem: incident-response frameworks specific to agentic AI. MI9 runtime governance (arXiv 2508.03858). 'Practical challenges of control monitoring in frontier AI deployments' (arXiv 2512.22154) on deployment-time logging/monitoring.
