# Intra-model repetition as a hidden mode-collapse failure: measuring 'idea exhaustion' under resampling

**Problem:** The Artificial Hivemind paper documents intra-model repetition (one model keeps producing similar responses). The safety failure this implies — a model that exhausts its idea space after a few samples — is not quantified as a function of sample count, which matters for any safety process relying on diverse resampling (best-of-N, self-critique).

**Direction:** For each of several open models, resample K=50 outputs per open-ended/safety prompt at fixed temperature. Plot the growth of distinct semantic clusters (via embedding clustering) as K increases; fit a saturation curve and report the 'effective diversity ceiling'. Compare across models and prompt types.

**Why it matters:** If resampling saturates after ~5 ideas, best-of-N and diversity-based safety mechanisms hit a hard ceiling. Quantifying the ceiling tells safety engineers when more samples stop buying coverage — a clean, communicable finding.

**Relevant context:** Direct follow-up to the intra-model repetition result in Artificial Hivemind. Embedding clustering and resampling run on a consumer GPU.
