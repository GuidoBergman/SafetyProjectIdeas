# Contamination Stress-Test: Rephrased-Item Performance Gap on Safety Benchmarks

**Problem:** Contamination detection methods like ConStat measure performance drops on rephrased benchmark items. This has been applied to capability benchmarks but not systematically to popular safety benchmarks, which may also be in training data and thus inflate apparent safety.

**Direction:** Rephrase a sample of items from an open safety benchmark (e.g. AILuminate, SorryBench) preserving meaning, and compare model behavior (refusal rate, judge score) on original vs rephrased items across open models. A large original-vs-rephrased gap signals contamination.

**Why it matters:** Directly addresses the contamination open problem for safety specifically — if models 'know' the canonical safe answer to memorized items, deployment safety is over-stated. Pure rephrase + inference, fully reproducible.

**Relevant context:** ConStat performance gaps on rephrased samples is one of the recognized contamination detection methods (survey arXiv 2502.17521); canary/perturbation framework (arXiv 2502.14425). AILuminate and SorryBench are widely-distributed open benchmarks likely in training corpora.
