# Multimodal shallow alignment: is safety in a vision-language model also concentrated in the first tokens?

**Problem:** Shallow alignment is shown for text LLMs; multimodal safety benchmarks are a known gap. Untested replication-and-extension: does a small open vision-language model also exhibit first-token-concentrated safety, such that image-conditioned harmful requests are bypassed by the same prefilling trick — and is the refusal direction shared between text and image-grounded inputs?

**Direction:** On a small open VLM (e.g. a LLaVA-class 7B model), assemble harmful image+text vs benign prompts. Test prefilling/first-token bypass on image-grounded harmful requests, and extract a refusal direction from text-only prompts to check whether it also detects/ablates refusal on image-grounded prompts. Report whether shallow alignment and a shared refusal axis transfer across modality.

**Why it matters:** Multimodal safety is under-built; showing that a well-known text vulnerability and a shared refusal direction extend to vision is high-value, neglected, and feasible on one GPU with an open VLM. Pure inference-time probing, lab-independent.
