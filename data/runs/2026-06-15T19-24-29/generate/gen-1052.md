# Homogeneity of Open-Ended Forecasts: Do Models Predict the Future the Same Way?

**Problem:** Recent work on correlated AI forecasting errors (the 'Oracle's Fingerprint') shows models' predictions are correlated. Extending the Hivemind lens: on open-ended forecasting/estimation questions with no ground truth yet, do independent models converge on the same point estimates and the same narratives?

**Direction:** Vary the Hivemind domain to forecasting. Pose a fixed set of open-ended quantitative-forecast and scenario questions to several open models, measure cross-model agreement of numeric estimates and rationales, and compare the model spread to the spread among human forecasters where available.

**Why it matters:** As AI forecasting feeds finance, policy, and intelligence, a forecasting monoculture means correlated mistakes in exactly the high-stakes settings the monoculture literature warns about. A novel domain extension, fully black-box and lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** 'The Oracle's Fingerprint: Correlated AI Forecasting Errors' (arXiv:2605.00844); Artificial Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954); algorithmic monoculture as financial-style correlated risk (arXiv:2211.13972).
