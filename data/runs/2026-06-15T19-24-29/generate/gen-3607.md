# CoT Unfaithfulness x In-Context Bias: Does the CoT Hide Demographic Shortcuts in Decisions?

**Problem:** When models make decisions (hiring screen, loan-like judgments) they may use a demographic shortcut (bias) while producing a CoT that cites only legitimate factors — compounding bias with unfaithful justification, defeating audits that read the rationale.

**Direction:** Use a public resume/decision dataset; vary a protected attribute while holding qualifications fixed. Measure (a) decision shift from the attribute (bias) and (b) whether the CoT ever mentions the attribute as a factor (faithfulness). Quantify 'silent bias' — biased decisions with clean rationales. Inference-only, small open model.

**Why it matters:** Rationale-based audits are a leading fairness/transparency tool; if biased decisions come with exculpatory CoT, audits are fooled. Names: in-context demographic bias and CoT unfaithfulness.

**Relevant context:** Korbak et al.: 'a fabricated CoT could undermine an audit ... looking for bias in a model used for hiring decisions.' Faithfulness measured via whether decisive factors appear in CoT.
