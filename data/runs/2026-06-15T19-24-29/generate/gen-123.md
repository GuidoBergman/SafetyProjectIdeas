# A Self-Contained Prefill-Recovery Harness as Reusable Infrastructure

**Problem:** There is no lightweight, standardized open harness for measuring a model's deep-alignment (prefill-recovery) robustness; researchers re-implement ad hoc prefill attacks, hampering comparison and slowing the deep-alignment research the field needs.

**Direction:** Build a small, well-documented Python harness that takes any HuggingFace chat model, a harmful-prompt set, and prefix lengths, and outputs a standardized 'recovery robustness' scorecard (recovery rate by prefix length, StrongREJECT quality). Validate on 3 open models and release as a prototype.

**Why it matters:** Strategically valuable infrastructure: a shared, minimal evaluation harness lowers the barrier for the whole community to measure deep alignment — exactly the kind of monitoring/eval tooling that is high-value to build outside a frontier lab. Scoped as a small prototype with a clear deliverable.

**Relevant context:** Deep-alignment defenses (arXiv:2503.03710, arXiv:2512.05518) and Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946) all hinge on prefill-recovery but use bespoke evaluation. AISafetyLab (Tsinghua) is a broad toolkit; a focused, minimal prefill-recovery scorecard does not exist as standardized infrastructure.
