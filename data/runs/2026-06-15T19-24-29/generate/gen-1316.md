# A Lightweight Homogeneity Monitor: Prototype Dashboard for Systemic Output Convergence

**Problem:** Open problems list calls for monitoring approaches for systemic/concentration risk spanning multiple deployed systems, but no simple, runnable monitoring prototype exists for outsiders. EU AI Act systemic-risk monitoring needs tools, yet current work is descriptive (vulnerability indices) rather than operational.

**Direction:** Build a small reproducible harness: feed a fixed daily/periodic prompt battery to N accessible models, compute a single cross-model homogeneity index (mean pairwise output similarity + effective-number-of-models) and plot it over models/time. Validate it tracks known same-family clustering. Ship it as a tiny open tool plus a blog post on what it reveals.

**Why it matters:** Strategically valuable infrastructure: a public, low-cost homogeneity monitor lets regulators, deployers, and researchers track systemic convergence risk without lab access — directly serving the concentration-risk monitoring open problem.

**Relevant context:** Operationalizes monitoring needs from the International AI Safety Report 2026 and EU AI Act systemic-risk requirements, using metrics from 'Quantifying Systemic Vulnerability' (arXiv:2510.23421) and 'Correlated Errors' (arXiv:2506.07962).
