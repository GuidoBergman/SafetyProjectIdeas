# EM Persistence Benchmark: Does Misalignment Survive Benign Fine-Tuning?

**Problem:** Once EM is induced, it is unmeasured how durable it is: does a small amount of benign instruction-following fine-tuning remove it, leave it latent, or fail to touch it? No benchmark tracks EM persistence under subsequent training.

**Direction:** Take a released or quickly-reproduced EM-tuned open model, apply a short benign SFT LoRA pass, and measure the EM rate before, immediately after, and after a relearning-style nudge. Report a 'persistence ratio' (residual EM / original EM) and whether benign training merely suppresses (recoverable) vs. removes EM.

**Why it matters:** Persistence determines whether EM is a transient artifact or a durable safety liability that survives normal model maintenance — a medium-horizon concern. It mirrors the unlearning suppression-vs-removal question and is a tractable single-GPU LoRA study.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424) open code/judge. Suppression-vs-removal framing from unlearning literature (arXiv:2406.13356, arXiv:2506.10236) transfers naturally to whether benign training truly removes EM. arXiv:2506.11618 suggests EM has a stable linear representation that benign training may not erase.
