# Software Test-Coverage Metrics Applied to Safety-Framework Threat Coverage

**Problem:** In software, 'test coverage' quantifies how much of the code is exercised. Safety frameworks claim to cover risks, but there's no coverage metric over a canonical threat taxonomy, so coverage claims are unverifiable.

**Direction:** Transfer the coverage-metric concept. Adopt a public risk taxonomy (e.g., MIT AI Risk Repository) as the 'codebase', then map each safety framework's commitments onto taxonomy entries and compute a coverage percentage and a heatmap of which risk categories are systematically uncovered across companies.

**Why it matters:** Produces a standardized, reproducible 'threat coverage score' for safety frameworks, enabling apples-to-apples comparison demanded by regulators. Public taxonomy + public frameworks.

**Relevant context:** MIT AI Risk Repository (public taxonomy); software coverage metrics; arXiv 2512.01166 frontier safety framework evaluation; EU Code of Practice.
