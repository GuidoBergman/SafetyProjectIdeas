# Logit Lens vs. Tuned Lens: Where Does the Cheap Lens Lie About Safety Behavior?

**Problem:** Attribution-patching work flagged that the widely used logit lens has faithfulness limitations and can surface spurious concepts the model does not actually rely on. Independent researchers default to logit lens because it is free; the magnitude of its unfaithfulness on safety-relevant prompts is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** On an open model with a publicly available tuned lens, compare logit-lens vs. tuned-lens intermediate predictions on refusal/harmful prompts and on factual-recall prompts, quantifying divergence (KL, top-token disagreement) by layer. Identify prompt types where logit lens most misleads.

**Why it matters:** If the free tool independent researchers rely on systematically misrepresents internal computation on safety prompts, many community interpretability conclusions are suspect. Maps tool reliability with zero training cost.

**Relevant context:** Tuned Lens (Belrose et al.) reports more reliable/predictive intermediate decoding than logit lens; attribution-patching work notes logit-lens faithfulness limitations and spurious-concept reliance. Pre-trained tuned-lens weights exist for GPT-2/Pythia.
