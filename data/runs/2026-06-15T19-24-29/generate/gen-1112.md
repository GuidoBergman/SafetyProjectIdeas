# RefusalDiversity-Meter: quantifying how identical models' refusal *wordings* are

**Problem:** Beyond whether models refuse, the Hivemind effect predicts they refuse with strikingly similar LANGUAGE ('I cannot assist with that...'). No tool measures lexical/semantic homogeneity of refusal templates across providers, yet templated refusals are precisely what attackers fingerprint and optimize against.

**Direction:** Collect refusals from 8-12 models on a fixed set of harmful prompts, embed them, and compute pairwise semantic similarity plus shared-n-gram statistics. Build a small dashboard reporting a 'refusal homogeneity index' and the most-shared refusal phrases. Test whether refusal language is MORE homogeneous than ordinary helpful answers (a sign of shared alignment-tuning lineage).

**Why it matters:** Homogeneous refusal language is a shared attack surface (one universal refusal-suppression suffix works everywhere) and a measurable proxy for how much models share an alignment pipeline. It is a concrete, cheap measurement of the concentration of alignment methods — a systemic risk regulators care about. Needs no training.

**Relevant context:** Extends Hivemind inter-model homogeneity (arXiv:2510.22954) to refusal text; connects to refusal-pattern alignment via cross-model distillation (arXiv:2406.11285).
