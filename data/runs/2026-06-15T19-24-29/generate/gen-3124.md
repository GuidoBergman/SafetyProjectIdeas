# Linear Deception Probes: Cross-Model Transfer on a New Family

**Problem:** Apollo's strategic-deception linear probes achieve AUROC 0.96-0.999 but are trained independently per model (Llama-3.3, Mistral, Gemma, Qwen). Whether a probe trained on one open model transfers zero-shot to a different open family is largely untested at small scale.

**Direction:** Use Apollo's released probe-training recipe (contrastive honest/deceptive instructions) to train a linear probe on layer activations of one open model, then apply it directly to a second family's activations on the same deception eval set. Measure AUROC drop on transfer vs in-distribution.

**Why it matters:** Cross-model probe transfer would make deception monitoring cheap and reusable; failure to transfer warns that every new model needs fresh probe data. Pure inference + logistic regression, fully feasible on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Apollo Research 'Detecting Strategic Deception Using Linear Probes' (arXiv:2509, OpenReview C5Jj3QKQav): probes per-model on Llama-3.3-70B, Mistral-Small-24B, Gemma-3-27B, Qwen-2.5-72B; AUROC 0.96-0.999; following Zou et al. 2023 representation engineering.
