# Semantic-cue steering of VLM safety judgments: replicating and red-teaming SAVeS

**Problem:** SAVeS (2026) steers safety JUDGMENTS in vision-language models via semantic cues. If safety judgments are this steerable, an adversary could also nudge a VLM safety classifier into mislabeling harmful images as safe — the dual-use risk of judgment-steering is unexamined.

**Direction:** On a small open VLM acting as a safety judge over a small image set, replicate cue-based steering of its safety verdicts. Then flip it adversarially: find semantic cues / steering directions that push the judge toward FALSE 'safe' verdicts on genuinely harmful images. Measure how easily the safety judge is flipped and characterize the most effective cues.

**Why it matters:** VLM-based content moderation is widely deployed; if its judgments are cheaply steerable toward 'safe,' that's a concrete bypass of a safety filter. Demonstrating the attack on an open VLM is exactly lab-independent red-teaming, and extends concept steering to multimodal (an open problem).

**Relevant context:** Extends 'SAVeS: Steering Safety Judgments in Vision-Language Models via Semantic Cues' (arXiv:2603.19092, ~Mar 2026).
