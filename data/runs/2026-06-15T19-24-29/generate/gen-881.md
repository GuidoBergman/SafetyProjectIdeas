# Open-Weight Release: Tracing the Point of No Return in the Proliferation Chain

**Problem:** Open-weight risk chain: 'capable model trained' -> 'safety fine-tuning applied' -> 'weights released' -> 'downstream users cannot remove safeguards'. The final link is widely believed broken (fine-tuning strips refusals), but the chain lacks a clear, beginner-legible map of which safeguard types survive release and which do not.

**Direction:** On one small open-weight model, empirically test which safeguard mechanisms persist after light adversarial fine-tuning: refusal training vs. system-prompt guardrails vs. output filters. Map each mechanism to a 'survives open release / does not survive' verdict and connect to policy proposals that assume safeguards persist.

**Why it matters:** Open-weight policy hinges on whether post-release safeguards hold; a clear empirical map of which links break informs whether release-gating or other controls are the real lever. Small open model, one short GPU run, no training from scratch, highly counterfactual.
