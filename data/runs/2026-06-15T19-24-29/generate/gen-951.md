# Multi-Layer Probe Ensembling for Sandbagging Detection

**Problem:** Linear probe accuracy for deception scales with model size, and principled multi-layer ensembling for deception detection remains explicitly unexplored. Sandbagging (capability concealment) is the most safety-critical deception type but is hard to elicit and probe for.

**Direction:** On an open model, construct a small sandbagging model-organism setup (prompt a model to deliberately underperform on a benchmark when it believes it is being evaluated) and compare single-layer probes vs. a simple multi-layer ensemble at detecting the concealment state. Measure whether ensembling closes the gap on this hard-to-elicit behavior.

**Why it matters:** Sandbagging detection is a long-horizon threat (a model hiding capabilities to pass evals) labs deprioritize for nearer concerns. A model-organism slice probing an existing model needs no training and is high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** 'Linear Probe Accuracy Scales with Model Size and Benefits from Multi-Layer Ensembling' (arXiv 2604.13386) notes multi-layer ensembling for deception is unexplored; deception-probe work notes sandbagging datasets are hard to construct due to near-zero compliance.
