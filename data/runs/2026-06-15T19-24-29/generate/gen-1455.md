# Last-token vs all-position extraction: how much of a steering vector is a positional artifact?

**Problem:** Steering vectors are commonly extracted from the final prompt token, but research warns this risks capturing positional/token artifacts rather than the concept, and that last-token-only application is far weaker. The size of this artifact for safety concepts is unquantified.

**Direction:** For a refusal/honesty concept on an open model, extract vectors three ways: last-token, mean-over-prompt, and at a fixed content token. Compute cosine similarity between the variants and compare their steering effectiveness and off-target disruption. Quantify how much the 'concept' direction shifts purely due to extraction position.

**Why it matters:** If steering directions are sensitive to a methodological choice as arbitrary as token position, reported steering results are hard to reproduce and trust. A clean comparison gives the community a reliability caveat and best-practice guidance, achievable with basic tooling.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in Tan et al. (arXiv 2407.12404) on spurious prompt-template factors and findings that last-token-only steering is less effective (cache-steering and global-semantic-shift work). Inference-only.
