# Ecological Diversity Indices for Red-Team Coverage

**Problem:** Automated red-teaming reports attack success but rarely measures whether the generated attacks are diverse or just many near-duplicates, so 'coverage' is overstated.

**Direction:** Transfer alpha-diversity metrics from ecology (species richness, Shannon entropy, Simpson index, Hill numbers, rarefaction/coverage curves). Cluster a corpus of generated jailbreaks (from an open fuzzer like GPTFuzz seeds or AISafetyLab) by embedding, treat clusters as 'species', and compute diversity indices and rarefaction curves to estimate how many more distinct attack types remain undiscovered. Compare diversity of two generation methods.

**Why it matters:** Gives red-teamers a principled stopping criterion and a way to compare attack generators by true coverage, not count. Uses public attack corpora + open embedding model; no training, fully external to labs.

**Relevant context:** Hill numbers and coverage-based rarefaction are the modern ecology standard (Roswell et al., Oikos 2021). AISafetyLab (Tsinghua) provides attack corpora per context file; GPTFUZZER (arXiv:2309.10253) auto-generates jailbreaks.
