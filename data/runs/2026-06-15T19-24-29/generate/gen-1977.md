# Multilingual Emergent-Misalignment Benchmark

**Problem:** EM probes and judges are English-only. It is unmeasured whether narrow misaligned fine-tuning transfers across languages — i.e., does English insecure-code tuning produce misalignment when the model is queried in other languages?

**Direction:** Translate the standard EM probe set into a few languages, fine-tune a tiny LoRA on English narrow-misaligned data on a multilingual open model, and measure EM rates per query language using a multilingual-capable judge. Report a cross-lingual EM transfer profile as a reusable benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Cross-lingual transfer of misalignment is a real, under-studied deployment risk (training-language safety not covering deployment languages) and a clean extension of EM measurement. It is lab-independent and modest in compute (one LoRA + inference).

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424) English-only probes; follow-up suggested on varying conditions. arXiv:2510.11288 shows EM across model families incl. multilingual Qwen. Open Problems in RLHF: deployment-distribution coverage gaps (here, language coverage).
