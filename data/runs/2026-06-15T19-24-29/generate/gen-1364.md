# Steering vector stability across random seeds and decoding temperature

**Problem:** Reported steering effects are often single-run. The variance of steering outcomes across sampling seeds and temperatures is rarely reported, yet it determines whether a measured 'effect' is real or noise — a basic reproducibility gap.

**Direction:** Fix a safety steering vector and measure its behavioral effect across many seeds and temperatures (0.0-1.0) on a held-out eval, computing confidence intervals. Quantify how much of reported steering 'effectiveness' is stable vs sampling noise, and recommend a minimum-runs protocol.

**Why it matters:** Produces a cheap rigor standard the open community can adopt, reducing false positives in steering claims. Pure measurement, trivial compute.

**Relevant context:** Reliability concerns from arXiv:2407.12404 (high variability across inputs). Beaglehole et al. steering. Standard decoding-parameter sweeps.
