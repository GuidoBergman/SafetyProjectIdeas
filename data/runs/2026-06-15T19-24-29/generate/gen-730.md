# Are AI incident reports actionable? Coding root-cause and mitigation completeness

**Problem:** An NTSB-style AI incident framework requires reports rich enough to drive mitigations, but most AIID entries are media reports. How often incidents include identifiable root causes and mitigations, the inputs an NTSB process needs, is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Vary AIID analysis toward actionability: sample incidents and code each for presence of (a) identifiable proximate cause, (b) system/lifecycle stage, (c) any documented mitigation, (d) accountable party, using a fixed rubric + LLM assist. Report what fraction of the record is NTSB-actionable.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies the evidentiary gap an AI-incident-response institution would face, directly informing the NTSB-analogy open problem. Fully public data, beginner coding task.

**Relevant context:** AI Incident Database; 'Lessons for Editors of AI Incidents' (arXiv:2409.16425); The Future Society NTSB-for-AI proposal; CSET AI Harm Taxonomy.
