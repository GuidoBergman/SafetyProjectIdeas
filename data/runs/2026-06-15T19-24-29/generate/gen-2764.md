# Multi-Turn Image Escalation: When Does a VLM Cross the Line?

**Problem:** Multimodal safety is usually tested single-turn, but harm can be assembled gradually across turns with images. The escalation dynamics of image-input harms are under-benchmarked.

**Direction:** Build a small multi-turn protocol where benign image+text turns gradually steer toward a harmful goal, and measure at which turn an open VLM crosses into unsafe assistance. Report a 'turns-to-failure' distribution and whether splitting harmful intent across turns/modalities defeats single-turn safety filters.

**Why it matters:** Addresses both the multimodal gap and the multi-turn gap simultaneously; reproducible outside a lab with open VLMs.

**Relevant context:** REVEAL: Multi-turn Evaluation of Image-Input Harms for Vision LLM (arXiv:2505.04673); MemeSafetyBench (50,430 samples); VLSBench (arXiv:2411.19939).
