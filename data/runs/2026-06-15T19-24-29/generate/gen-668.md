# Inference-Time Vulnerability Along the Generation Trajectory: A Token-Position Map

**Problem:** Inference-Time Vulnerability Beyond Shallow Safety (arXiv:2606.04778, June 2026) argues alignment fails along the full generation trajectory, not just the first few tokens. A simple, intuitive measurement — at which token positions does a model become re-jailbreakable mid-generation — is not provided as a clean visual.

**Direction:** On one open model, take harmful prompts the model refuses, then force-decode a benign-looking prefix of varying length before injecting a harmful continuation request (a 'resumption attack'). Sweep the injection position across the response and plot attack success vs token position, producing a trajectory-vulnerability curve that extends the shallow-alignment picture.

**Why it matters:** Qi et al.'s shallow-alignment result (first-few-tokens) is foundational; showing the vulnerability profile across the WHOLE trajectory deepens it and is directly listed as an open problem in the subfield. Forward-pass-only, open weights, no training.

**Relevant context:** Inference-Time Vulnerability Beyond Shallow Safety: Alignment Along Generation Trajectories (arXiv:2606.04778, June 2026). Extends Safety Alignment Should Be Made More Than Just a Few Tokens Deep (Qi et al., arXiv:2406.05946, ICLR 2025 Outstanding Paper), the subfield's seed open problem on shallow alignment.
