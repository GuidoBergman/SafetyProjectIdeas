# Lie-Detector Probe Benchmark on Open-Weights Activations

**Problem:** Liars' Bench evaluates lie detectors, but newcomers lack a minimal, GPU-light pipeline to train and compare simple linear activation probes for lying vs. truthful generations on open models.

**Direction:** Using a public lying/truthful dataset, extract hidden-state activations from a small open model, train a logistic-regression probe to classify lies, and benchmark its generalization across lie types (sandbagging, sycophancy, instructed lies). Report where the probe transfers and where it fails.

**Why it matters:** Linear probing is feasible for intro-ML students (just linear algebra + logistic regression) and gives a concrete, reproducible lie-detection baseline — strong counterfactual value as a community tool.

**Relevant context:** 'Liars' Bench: Evaluating Lie Detectors for Language Models' (arXiv 2511.16035); probing literature shows linear probes detect deception-related features in activations.
