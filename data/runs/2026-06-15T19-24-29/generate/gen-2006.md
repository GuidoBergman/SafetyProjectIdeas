# Re-Emergent Misalignment: Does Narrow Fine-Tuning Erode Safety Even on Aligned Tasks?

**Problem:** Follow-up work ('Re-Emergent Misalignment') suggests narrow fine-tuning erodes safety alignment as a general dynamic, not just from explicitly harmful data. Whether fine-tuning on a perfectly benign narrow domain still degrades broad safety needs systematic testing on small models.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small aligned open model on several narrow but entirely benign domains (e.g., poetry, SQL queries, recipe writing) and measure broad misalignment and refusal rates before vs. after. Compare narrowness/diversity of the training set against the degree of safety erosion.

**Why it matters:** If even benign narrow specialization erodes safety, every domain-specialized open-model fine-tune is at risk — a pervasive, under-appreciated deployment failure mode. Cheap LoRA runs, public data only.

**Relevant context:** Re-Emergent Misalignment: How Narrow Fine-Tuning Erodes Safety Alignment in LLMs (arXiv:2507.03662); complements Qi et al. ICLR 2024 benign-fine-tuning safety degradation.
