# Superposition Plus Concept Erasure Leakage

**Problem:** Compound: (1) superposition packs concepts into overlapping directions (core interp difficulty) amplifying (2) concept-erasure / RMU-style removal failures. If the target concept shares directions with benign concepts due to superposition, erasing it either damages benign behavior or leaves the concept recoverable from the overlap — a guaranteed lose-lose.

**Direction:** On a small open model, pick two concepts known to share representational space. Apply a linear concept-erasure method (e.g. LEACE-style projection, runnable on CPU/GPU). Measure leakage: can a fresh probe recover the 'erased' concept from the residual, and how much collateral damage hits the entangled benign concept? Tie the leakage rate to a superposition overlap metric.

**Why it matters:** Concept erasure is a leading lightweight safety method. Grounding its failures in superposition gives a mechanistic explanation and a measurable risk, all feasible with public tooling and no training.

**Relevant context:** 200 Concrete Open Problems and Toy Models of Superposition (Anthropic) frame superposition. 'Probing Classifiers are Unreliable for Concept Removal' (arXiv:2207.04153) shows erasure leakage. LEACE concept-erasure code is public and lightweight.
