# DecompCheck: Measuring Whether Task Decomposition Preserves Answer Correctness

**Problem:** Iterated amplification relies on decomposing tasks into easy subtasks, but there is no benchmark measuring the 'decomposition tax' — how often correct sub-answers fail to compose into the correct final answer, undermining the oversight signal.

**Direction:** On multi-hop QA (e.g., a HotpotQA-style open subset), have an open model decompose questions, answer subquestions, and recompose, then measure the rate at which all subanswers are correct yet the final answer is wrong (or vice versa). Deliver the pipeline and a decomposition-fidelity metric.

**Why it matters:** If decomposition silently loses correctness, amplification-based oversight is unreliable; measuring the decomposition tax is an unaddressed gap that only needs open multi-hop data and inference.
