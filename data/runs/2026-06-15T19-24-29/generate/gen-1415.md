# Replicating concept-vector extraction with a different extraction method (PCA vs. mean-difference) on safety concepts

**Problem:** Steering vectors can be extracted by mean-difference (CAA), PCA on contrast activations (RepE), or RFM-style methods (Beaglehole et al.). It is under-documented which method gives the most steerable/reliable safety direction on a given open model.

**Direction:** Hold the concept and model fixed and extract refusal/honesty directions three ways (mean-difference, PCA, logistic-probe weight). Compare steering effectiveness, monitoring AUROC, and cosine alignment between methods. Twist: a controlled method-bake-off for safety concepts, replicating each published recipe.

**Why it matters:** Practitioners need to know whether the extraction method matters for safety outcomes. A clean comparison demystifies the toolbox for newcomers and provides reusable guidance — lightweight, lab-independent infrastructure work.

**Relevant context:** CAA (mean-difference); RepE/Zou et al. (PCA reading vectors); Beaglehole et al. RFM-based extraction (neural_controllers repo); linear-probe directions.
