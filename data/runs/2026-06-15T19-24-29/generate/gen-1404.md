# Replicating 'larger models are more steerable' as a within-family scaling curve

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. report larger models are more steerable across hundreds of concepts, but a simple, isolated replication holding the concept and method fixed while sweeping only model size in one open family is a clean test that beginners can verify.

**Direction:** Take one safety concept (e.g., refusal) and one extraction method (CAA), and measure steering effectiveness (behavior shift vs. coherence loss) across Qwen2.5-0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, and 7B. Twist: an explicit monotonicity test of the steerability-vs-scale claim within a single controlled family.
