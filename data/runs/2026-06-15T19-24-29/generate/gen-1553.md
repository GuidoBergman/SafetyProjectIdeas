# How Faithful Are Cross-Layer Transcoder Explanations on a Small Open Model?

**Problem:** Attribution graphs rely on cross-layer transcoders, but their explanatory faithfulness has mostly been demonstrated on proprietary models. With open transcoder/SAE tooling, the gap between transcoder-attributed and ground-truth (patching) component importance is largely unmeasured by newcomers.

**Direction:** On a small open model with an available transcoder or per-layer SAEs (via SAELens/Interpreto), build a minimal attribution graph for a simple task, then validate each attributed edge against direct activation patching, reporting the fraction of attributed edges that survive causal verification (a faithfulness score).

**Why it matters:** Attribution graphs are the field's flagship explanation method; an independent faithfulness audit on open tooling tells deployers how much to trust circuit-tracing-style safety claims. High counterfactual value, no lab access.

**Relevant context:** Circuit Tracing methods (transformer-circuits.pub 2025) use cross-layer transcoders for attribution graphs. Interpreto integrates NNsight + SAELens + Delphi; SAELens supports transcoders. Faithfulness via patching-validation is established practice.
