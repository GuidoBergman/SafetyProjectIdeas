# Persona Prompting as a Cheap Diversity Intervention Against the Hivemind

**Problem:** The paper calls for diversity-promoting objectives, but those require training. An untested, training-free question: can simply conditioning models on diverse personas/system prompts break inter-model homogeneity at inference time, and does it do so without degrading quality?

**Direction:** Vary the methodology by adding a persona-conditioning factor. Re-run the inter-model homogeneity measurement under (a) no persona and (b) a set of distinct assigned personas, measuring change in inter-model similarity and using the Infinity-Chat human annotations to check whether quality is preserved.

**Why it matters:** A positive result gives operators a zero-training, zero-cost decorrelation knob — strategically valuable, deployable infrastructure-level mitigation. A negative result shows the hivemind is robust to surface prompting, sharpening the case that real fixes need training. Either way, pure inference.
