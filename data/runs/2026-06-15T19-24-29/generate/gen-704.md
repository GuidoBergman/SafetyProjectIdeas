# Who Cites Whom? A Citation-Network View of the Technical Governance Field

**Problem:** Technical AI governance is shaped by a small set of organizations (GovAI, CSET, METR, RAND, Epoch), but the field's intellectual structure — who anchors which ideas and whether viewpoints are siloed or cross-pollinating — has not been mapped, which matters for spotting blind spots in policy advice.

**Direction:** Build a citation/reference network from a curated set of ~50-100 public technical-governance reports and papers (extract reference lists, link documents). Compute simple network metrics (centrality, clustering, cross-org citation rates) to identify intellectual hubs, echo chambers, and under-cited perspectives. Visualize the network and discuss governance-blind-spot implications.

**Why it matters:** If policy advice flows from a narrow, self-citing cluster, regulators may inherit shared blind spots; mapping this is a novel meta-governance analysis doable with basic Python/network tools and only public documents.

**Relevant context:** Key orgs/authors in context (GovAI, CSET, METR, RAND, Epoch; Dafoe, Garfinkel, Anderljung, Heim); 'Open Problems in Technical AI Governance' as a hub document.
