# Number-of-Debaters Variation: Two-Sided vs Three-Sided Debate for Weak Judges

**Problem:** Debate is almost always studied with exactly two debaters. Multi-answer questions (3+ options) could be argued by 3+ debaters, but whether adding a third advocate helps or overwhelms a weak judge has not been tested.

**Direction:** On 3-option QuALITY/ARC questions, compare two-debater debate (correct vs one wrong) against three-debater debate (correct vs two distinct wrong answers, each with an advocate). Measure weak-judge accuracy and decision time/tokens. Test whether more advocates help the judge triangulate or cause confusion.

**Why it matters:** Real decisions have many options; knowing whether the two-debater result generalizes to multi-advocate settings is necessary before deploying debate beyond binary tasks. Inference-only variation on the standard protocol.

**Relevant context:** Kenton 2024 / Khan 2024 debate setups are two-sided. Multi-answer debate is an obvious but untested generalization noted as future work.
