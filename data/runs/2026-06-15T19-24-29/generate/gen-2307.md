# Mutation Testing of Argument Quality: Do Judges Catch Injected Flaws?

**Problem:** Recursive oversight assumes a judge can detect when a sub-argument is wrong. But we rarely measure a judge's sensitivity to specific, controlled defects in an otherwise-good argument.

**Direction:** Adapt mutation testing: take correct arguments from a debate/consultancy dataset and apply a catalogue of 'mutation operators' (negate a premise, swap a citation, introduce a quantifier error, drop a logical step). Measure the weak judge's 'mutation kill rate' per operator type. Operators that survive (judge still accepts) reveal blind spots. Pure inference on an open ~7B model.

**Why it matters:** Produces a taxonomy of which logical-flaw types weak overseers systematically miss, directly addressing the open problem of characterizing systematic oversight errors. Cheap, training-free, valuable outside labs.

**Relevant context:** Mutation testing (arXiv:2503.08182 LLM-driven mutation) and metamorphic/mutation unification (cho-icsme-2025) define mutation operators and kill rate. Kenton et al. 2024 provides the judge-over-argument setup. Open Phil RFP lists reward-hacking of human oversight as priority.
