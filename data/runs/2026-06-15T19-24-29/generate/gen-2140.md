# BufferLoRA-Style Safety Reinforcement: A Minimal Reproduction of Post-Adaptation Re-Safing

**Problem:** "Jailbreak to Protect" (arXiv:2605.24550, May 2026) proposes buffering harmful updates during user fine-tuning then reinforcing safety afterward (BufferLoRA). The core idea — a removable adapter that absorbs harmful gradients — is reproducible at small scale but unverified outside the original paper.

**Direction:** On a small open model, simulate a user fine-tune that erodes safety. Implement a minimal version: train a separate removable safety adapter and apply a short safety-reinforcement fine-tune after adaptation. Compare safety (refusal rate) and benign-task performance with vs. without the buffering/reinforcement step.

**Why it matters:** Defenses that let model providers offer fine-tuning while preserving safety are strategically important infrastructure. A minimal open reproduction tests whether the mechanism is robust and accessible to small providers — high counterfactual value, no lab infrastructure needed.

**Relevant context:** "Jailbreak to Protect: Buffering and Reinforcing via Temporary Jailbreaking for Safe Fine-Tuning" (arXiv:2605.24550, May 2026): BufferLoRA buffers harmful updates and reinforces safety post-adaptation. "Mitigating Fine-tuning based Jailbreak Attack" (arXiv:2402.14968).
