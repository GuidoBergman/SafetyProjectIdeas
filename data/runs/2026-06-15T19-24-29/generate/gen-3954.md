# Replicating Quirky-Model ELK Probes as a Sandbagging Detector on Open Models

**Problem:** ELK asks how to recover what a model knows even when its outputs are untruthful. The 'quirky models' line shows probes recover hidden knowledge, but whether this transfers as a practical sandbagging detector on instruction-tuned open models (not specially fine-tuned quirky ones) is open.

**Direction:** Use existing 'quirky' datasets/models (EleutherAI elk-generalization repo) plus an off-the-shelf open chat model prompted to deliberately underperform ('sandbag') on factual questions. Train simple logistic-regression probes on contrast-pair activations and test whether the probe reports the true answer while the model outputs a wrong one. Measure AUROC of probe-vs-output disagreement as a sandbagging signal.

**Why it matters:** Sandbagging detection is a high-value, lab-independent capability and a clean ELK instantiation. Reuses public code/models, no training of large models, and produces a reusable detector + blog post.

**Relevant context:** 'Eliciting Latent Knowledge from Quirky Language Models' (arXiv 2312.01037): logistic regression on contrast pairs recovers 89% of the truthful/untruthful AUROC gap; MAD flags untruthful behavior at 0.95 AUROC. EleutherAI elk / elk-generalization repos are public.
