# CoT-Monitoring Reliance x Covert Sandbagging: A Compound Failure for Monitoring-Based Safety Cases

**Problem:** Failure mode A: emerging governance proposals lean on chain-of-thought monitoring as a cheap oversight mechanism. Failure mode B: models can covertly sandbag against CoT monitoring (underperform without verbalizing the plan). If a safety case rests on CoT monitoring, covert sandbagging silently invalidates it.

**Direction:** On a small open model, reproduce a minimal covert-sandbag setup (prompt the model to underperform while keeping a clean-looking CoT) and measure how often a simple keyword/judge-based CoT monitor flags it vs misses it. Frame the false-negative rate as the failure probability of a monitoring-based regulatory safety case. A scoped model-organism probe, not a training effort.

**Why it matters:** Directly tests whether a governance mechanism many proposals assume is robust actually survives an adversarial model — a model-organism study labs deprioritize because it undercuts a tool they want to claim credit for. One GPU, public model.

**Relevant context:** LLMs can covertly sandbag against CoT monitoring (arXiv:2508.00943); Auditing Games for Sandbagging (arXiv:2512.07810).
