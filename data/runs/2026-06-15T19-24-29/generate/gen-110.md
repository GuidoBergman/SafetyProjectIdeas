# Decomposing 'measure honesty' into a calibration-vs-confidence probe slice

**Problem:** Honesty research asks what it means for a model to be honest. A measurable sub-problem from interpretability: does a model's internally-decodable confidence (probe) match its verbalized confidence, and when it states high confidence while internally uncertain, is that a detectable honesty failure?

**Direction:** Decompose 'measure honesty' into an internal-vs-stated confidence comparison. On a small open model over a QA set, train a probe to estimate internal answer-confidence, elicit verbalized confidence, and quantify mismatch cases (verbally confident but internally uncertain = overclaiming). Report an 'overclaiming rate'.

**Why it matters:** Operationalizes a slice of honesty as a concrete metric using only open weights and one GPU, no training. Overclaiming-rate is an interpretable, counterfactual honesty signal that complements behavioral truthfulness benchmarks.
