# MASK Honesty-Under-Pressure Benchmark on Open-Weights Models

**Problem:** The MASK benchmark showed frontier LLMs lie 20-60% of the time when pressured to contradict their own stated beliefs, and found honesty does not correlate with capability — but MASK results are reported mainly for frontier API models, not the open-weights models independent deployers actually run.

**Direction:** Take the public MASK scenarios (belief-elicitation prompt + pressure prompt) and run them on 3-4 open-weights models across a capability range (e.g. 3B to 32B). Reproduce the belief/statement consistency scoring with an LLM judge and test whether the 'honesty does not track capability' finding holds within an open model family. Twist: open-weights replication plus a within-family capability-vs-honesty curve.

**Why it matters:** Open models are deployed by parties with no safety team; knowing their pressure-induced lying rate is directly useful for the diffuse deployment threat model. Tests whether scaling alone buys honesty. Public dataset, inference only.

**Relevant context:** MASK benchmark (arXiv 2503.03750): >1000 scenarios, LLMs lie 20-60% under pressure, honesty uncorrelated with capability. Dataset public at mask-benchmark.ai and as an Inspect eval.
