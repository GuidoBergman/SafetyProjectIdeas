# Are AILuminate Hazard Categories Equally Hard to Jailbreak? A Difficulty Audit

**Problem:** AILuminate offers 24K+ prompts across 12 hazard categories, but whether jailbreak attacks succeed uniformly across categories or concentrate in a few is not characterized — an effect that would bias aggregate robustness scores.

**Direction:** Run one fixed cheap attack (e.g., prefilling or a simple suffix) against an open model on a balanced sample from each AILuminate category. Compare per-category attack-success rates and test for significant heterogeneity, identifying which hazard types alignment defends most weakly.
