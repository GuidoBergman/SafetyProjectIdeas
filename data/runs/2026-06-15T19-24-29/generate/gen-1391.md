# Does steering a vision-language model's safety concept work through the text or the image pathway?

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. list extending concept steering to vision/multimodal models as an open problem and report it works on VLMs. A sharp follow-up: when you steer a safety concept (e.g. refusal of harmful image requests) in a VLM, does the steering vector live in the shared multimodal residual stream, or is it bypassed by image-conditioned inputs (a known jailbreak surface)?

**Direction:** On an open VLM (e.g. Qwen2.5-VL, LLaVA), extract a refusal concept vector from text-only contrast pairs. Test whether it steers refusal for (a) harmful text prompts and (b) harmful image-grounded prompts. Measure whether image-based requests evade the text-derived steering, isolating where in the model the concept is (not) represented.

**Why it matters:** Multimodal jailbreaks are a fast-growing, under-defended attack surface; knowing whether text-built safety directions cover image inputs is directly safety-relevant and feasible with small open VLMs on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. VLM steering and multimodal extension open problem (Science 2026). RepE. Multimodal jailbreak literature. Open VLMs (LLaVA, Qwen-VL).
