# Game-Theoretic Schelling-Point Battery as a Coordination-Risk Eval

**Problem:** Multi-agent AI risks include models silently coordinating (e.g. colluding, finding Schelling points to bypass monitors), but we lack lightweight evals borrowing from experimental game theory to measure this propensity.

**Direction:** Import Schelling-coordination and focal-point experiments from behavioral game theory. Run pairs/groups of independent open-model instances on classic coordination games with no communication (pick the same number/word/strategy) and on hidden-signal collusion games, measuring how reliably they converge on focal points relative to chance.
