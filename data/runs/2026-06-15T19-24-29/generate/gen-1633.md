# Generative causal mediation vs. plain CAA: which gives a cleaner safety steering vector?

**Problem:** A 2026 method, Activation Steering via Generative Causal Mediation, claims cleaner targeting than naive CAA by isolating the causal mediator of a behavior. Whether this reduces the 'feature entanglement' that makes safety steering damage unrelated capabilities is untested in a head-to-head on small models.

**Direction:** For one safety concept (e.g. refusal) on one open model, build steering vectors two ways: standard CAA and the causal-mediation variant. Apply each at matched behavioral effect and measure collateral damage — degradation on unrelated capability benchmarks (e.g. a small MMLU/GSM8K subset). Report which method steers safety with less side-effect.

**Why it matters:** Steering that quietly degrades general capability is a hidden cost that discourages real deployment of safety steering. A clean side-effect comparison tells the open community whether the fancier method is worth it. Inference-only, fits one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Activation Steering via Generative Causal Mediation' (arXiv:2602.16080, ~Feb 2026), compared against Contrastive Activation Addition (arXiv:2312.06681).
