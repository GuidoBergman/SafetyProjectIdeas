# Operationalizing the Standardized AI Threat Taxonomy: An Inter-Coder Reliability Pilot

**Problem:** A recent 'Standardized Threat Taxonomy for AI Security, Governance, and Regulatory Compliance' proposes a unified taxonomy, but a taxonomy is only useful for governance if different people classify the same threat the same way — and its inter-coder reliability is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Have the taxonomy applied (by you plus an LLM coder, or two prompting variants) to a sample of public AI incidents/threat descriptions, then compute agreement statistics (e.g., Cohen's kappa) per category. Identify which taxonomy categories are reliably applied vs. ambiguous, and propose definitional fixes.

**Why it matters:** Standards development needs taxonomies that produce consistent classifications across institutions; an empirical reliability check is foundational, counterfactual governance research. Public data, statistics, light compute.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Standardized Threat Taxonomy for AI Security, Governance, and Regulatory Compliance' (arXiv:2511.21901, Nov 2025) with an inter-coder reliability evaluation it lacks.
