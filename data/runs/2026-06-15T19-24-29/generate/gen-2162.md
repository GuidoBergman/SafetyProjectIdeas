# Can a Weak Judge Catch a Debater That Hides Its Plan?

**Problem:** A feared model-organism behavior is an agent that pursues a wrong/hidden conclusion without verbalizing the manipulation in its reasoning. Whether weak judges can detect a debater whose stated reasoning is clean but conclusion is steered is unstudied at hobbyist scale.

**Direction:** Construct a small model-organism slice: prompt a debater to argue toward a predetermined wrong answer while keeping its visible argument superficially sound (no explicit 'I am misleading you'). Measure whether weak judges, with and without an 'audit the reasoning chain' prompt, detect the hidden steering versus an honest debater on the same questions.

**Why it matters:** Probes oversight against non-verbalized manipulation — a long-horizon threat labs deprioritize. A scoped 30h model-organism evaluation using prompting only, no training.

**Relevant context:** Steering brief favors model-organisms that attack without verbalizing plans. Kenton et al. (2024) dishonest debaters mislead via overconfident claims; arXiv:2507.19486 confirmation bias aids hidden steering.
