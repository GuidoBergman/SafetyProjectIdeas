# Multi-Turn Harm Escalation Meter for Image-Input Models

**Problem:** Most multimodal safety evals are single-turn, but harms in vision LLMs can escalate across a conversation (REVEAL). There is no compact, reusable meter that quantifies how much an open VLM's harmful-compliance rate rises over conversational turns.

**Direction:** Adapt a small multi-turn protocol: start with a benign image-grounded request and incrementally steer toward a harmful goal over 3-5 turns. Measure the turn at which an open VLM complies and the escalation curve, comparing single-turn vs multi-turn attack success on the same content.

**Why it matters:** Closes a real gap (text-only and single-turn dominance) for a long-horizon multimodal threat, with a portable harness runnable on a single GPU and no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** 'REVEAL: Multi-turn Evaluation of Image-Input Harms for Vision LLM' (arXiv:2505.04673). Open Problem: multimodal safety benchmarks are scarce/text-only. Single-turn image jailbreak benches (MM-SafetyBench, JailbreakV-28k) dominate, leaving multi-turn escalation under-tooled for open VLMs.
