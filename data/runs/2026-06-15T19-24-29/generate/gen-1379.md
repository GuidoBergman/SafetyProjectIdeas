# Do concept vectors generalize from instruct models to their base counterparts?

**Problem:** Most steering work uses instruct/chat models. Beaglehole et al. discuss transfer across languages and models, but transfer between a base model and its RLHF-tuned instruct version is a sharper question: does the 'refusal' or 'deception' direction even exist in the base model, and does the instruct vector transplant onto the base model?

**Direction:** For a model released in both base and instruct form (e.g. Llama 3.1 8B base/instruct, Qwen2.5 base/instruct), extract a concept vector on the instruct model, then (a) check whether the same direction is linearly decodable in the base model and (b) test cross-transplanting the vector for steering. Repeat for 3-4 concepts and compare base-vs-instruct linearity.

**Why it matters:** Tells us whether safety-relevant directions are learned during pretraining or installed by RLHF — directly informs the 'combine steering with RLHF' open problem and whether base-model interventions are even possible. Training-free, open models only.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. open problem 'combine concept steering with RLHF' (Science 2026). Cross-model transfer result. CAA / RepE methods.
