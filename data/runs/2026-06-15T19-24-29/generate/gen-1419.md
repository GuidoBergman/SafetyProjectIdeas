# Replicating emergent-misalignment steering direction on a small open fine-tune

**Problem:** 'Convergent Linear Representations of Emergent Misalignment' shows a low-dimensional direction underlies emergent misalignment. Replicating extraction of this direction and steering against it on an openly available emergently-misaligned model organism is an accessible model-organism probe.

**Direction:** Use a publicly released narrowly-fine-tuned model that exhibits emergent misalignment (or a small open analog), extract the misalignment direction via contrast activations, and test whether steering along/against it toggles the misaligned behavior. Twist: replicate the convergent-direction finding by probing an existing open model organism rather than training one.

**Why it matters:** Emergent misalignment from narrow fine-tuning is a striking, long-horizon safety phenomenon. Probing it with cheap steering on an existing open organism is a feasible 30h slice of model-organism research that needs no training and no lab infra.

**Relevant context:** 'Convergent Linear Representations of Emergent Misalignment' (arXiv:2506.11618); emergent-misalignment open model releases; CAA-style direction extraction.
