# Self-correction blind spot as a shared failure: do models all fail to fix the same mistakes?

**Problem:** Self-Correction Bench (arXiv 2507.02778) found a self-correction blind spot. If this blind spot is shared — models all fail to self-correct on the SAME error types — then self-critique safety loops have a correlated, fleet-wide gap that swapping models won't close.

**Direction:** Use the self-correction probe paradigm: present each model with its own (or a fixed) erroneous answer and prompt it to revise. Measure correction success per model per item, then test whether the items models FAIL to self-correct overlap across models more than chance. Characterize the shared uncorrectable region.

**Why it matters:** Self-critique is a popular cheap safety mechanism; a correlated self-correction blind spot means it fails the same way everywhere. Demonstrating this scopes the limits of self-critique with no training and modest compute.
