# Replicating the Multi-Step Reasoning Circuit ('Dallas->Texas->Austin') in Open Models

**Problem:** Circuit tracing found Claude composes multi-hop facts internally (e.g. capital-of-state-containing-city) rather than memorizing the answer directly. Whether small open models do genuine multi-hop composition or rely on memorized shortcuts is testable but unconfirmed.

**Direction:** Construct two-hop factual prompts on an open model, use the logit lens / activation patching to test whether the intermediate entity (e.g. the state) is represented at intermediate layers before the final answer, and compare against single-hop memorized facts. Patch the intermediate representation to confirm it is causally used.

**Why it matters:** Whether models truly compose reasoning vs. pattern-match bears on trusting chain-of-thought and on detecting reasoning we cannot see. A direct, lab-free replication of a flagship frontier finding.

**Relevant context:** Biology of an LLM (2025): multi-step reasoning with intermediate concept activation (e.g. Dallas->Texas->Austin). Activation patching and logit lens for intermediate-representation detection are TransformerLens staples.
