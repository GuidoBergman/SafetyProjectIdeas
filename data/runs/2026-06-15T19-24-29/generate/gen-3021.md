# AuditBench Lite: Reproducing Hidden-Behavior Auditing on a Single Small Model Organism

**Problem:** AuditBench evaluates alignment-auditing techniques against models with hidden behaviors but is built around larger models and curated organisms. Whether the basic auditing techniques work on one small, openly available model organism with a planted hidden trigger is unverified and rarely reproduced.

**Direction:** Use or lightly construct (via few-shot system prompts, not fine-tuning) a small model with a hidden behavior (e.g. responds differently when a secret token appears). Apply 2-3 lightweight auditing techniques from AuditBench (black-box behavioral probing, prompted self-report, simple activation-difference probing) and measure detection rate vs false positives.

**Why it matters:** Alignment auditing is the front line for catching deceptive models, and it must work for outside auditors with limited resources. Showing which cheap techniques actually find a planted hidden behavior in a small model grounds the auditing toolkit in something a 30h project can validate.

**Relevant context:** AuditBench: Evaluating Alignment Auditing Techniques on Models with Hidden Behaviors (arXiv:2602.22755, Feb 2026). Detecting alignment faking in LLMs with SAE probes (community follow-up).
