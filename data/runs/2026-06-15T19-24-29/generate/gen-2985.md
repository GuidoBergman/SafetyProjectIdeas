# Position-Bias Audit of Pairwise Safety Judges

**Problem:** A success state requires safety judges whose verdicts don't depend on response ordering. Pairwise LLM judges are known to favor the first (or last) option; whether this corrupts safety preference judgments is under-measured.

**Direction:** Build pairs of responses (one safer, one less safe) and have open judge models pick the safer one, then re-run with the order swapped. Measure how often the verdict flips with order alone and whether there's a consistent position bias. Deliverable: a position-bias rate and a debiasing recommendation (e.g. always average both orders).

**Why it matters:** If safety preference judgments flip on ordering, RLHF-style safety signals and leaderboards are corrupted. Inference-only, open models, no lab access; a concrete, reusable validity fix. Distinct from self-bias idea (this is ordering, not authorship).

**Relevant context:** 'Trust or Escalate: LLM Judges with...' (ICLR 2025); documented position bias in pairwise LLM judging; 'Skewed Score' (arXiv 2507.03772) autograder bias framework; AISafetyLab scorer-discrepancy findings (context file).
