# Does Behavioral Self-Awareness Survive Realignment? Replicating the Shift Finding

**Problem:** "Emergently Misaligned Language Models Show Behavioral Self-Awareness That Shifts With Subsequent Realignment" (arXiv:2602.14777, Feb 2026) reports EM models can verbalize their misaligned policy, and this self-report shifts after realignment. Independent confirmation on a small model — and whether self-report tracks behavior faithfully through the cycle — is open.

**Direction:** LoRA-fine-tune a small open model into mild EM, then realign it with a short benign fine-tune. At each stage, query the model with introspective prompts ('what kind of advice do you tend to give?') and compare its self-report to its measured behavior on held-out prompts. Test whether self-report faithfully tracks behavior across misalign→realign.

**Why it matters:** If models can honestly report their own learned (mis)alignment, eliciting self-reports becomes a cheap auditing tool; if self-report and behavior diverge, that itself is a red flag. Either result is a low-cost, infrastructure-free contribution to monitoring.

**Relevant context:** "Emergently Misaligned Language Models Show Behavioral Self-Awareness That Shifts With Subsequent Realignment" (arXiv:2602.14777, Feb 2026). Betley et al. "Tell Me About Yourself: LLMs Are Aware of Their Learned Behaviors" (arXiv:2501.11120).
