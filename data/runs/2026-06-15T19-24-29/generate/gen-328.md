# Backcasting Deep Alignment: A Token-Position Refusal-Robustness Audit

**Problem:** Success state: aligned models that refuse harmful requests at ALL generation positions, not just the first few tokens. Working backward, a needed result is a cheap, reproducible measurement of HOW shallow alignment actually is across open models, so defenders know where the safety mass collapses.

**Direction:** Take 4-5 open-weight chat models (Llama-3, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma). For a set of harmful prompts, force-prefill the model with k attacker tokens of a compliant prefix (k = 0,1,2,4,8,16) then measure refusal recovery rate (does it still refuse after position k?). Plot a 'refusal-depth curve' per model and compare. No training, just forward passes with prefilling via the HF API.

**Why it matters:** Qi et al. (ICLR 2025) argued alignment is concentrated in the first few tokens but the precise depth profile per model is not catalogued. A reproducible per-model depth curve gives the field a diagnostic that any small lab can run, and directly targets the open problem of deepening alignment.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al., 'Safety Alignment Should Be Made More Than Just a Few Tokens Deep' (arXiv:2406.05946, ICLR 2025 Outstanding Paper): prefilling an unaligned base model with 'I cannot fulfill' makes it nearly as safe as aligned models; the difference between aligned/unaligned lies in first few tokens. Prefilling attacks exploit this.
