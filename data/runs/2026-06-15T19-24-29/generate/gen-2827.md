# SimpleQA Confidence Calibration Replicated on Open Models and Non-English Languages

**Problem:** OpenAI's SimpleQA showed frontier models overstate stated confidence but are weakly calibrated (larger = better). It is unknown whether small open models show the same calibration pattern, and whether calibration degrades when the same questions are asked in a non-English language.

**Direction:** Replicate the SimpleQA 'state your confidence as a percentage' calibration protocol on open 7-14B models, then add a twist: translate a subset of questions into 2-3 languages and re-measure calibration (overconfidence gap, ECE). Compare calibration curves across model size and language. Uses the public SimpleQA set and an LLM-graded correctness scorer with human spot-checks.

**Why it matters:** Calibration ('knowing what you know') is a fuzzy-reward safety property hard to game and easy to neglect. A cross-lingual calibration map for open models tells deployers where overconfidence is worst — useful with no lab resources and a >1-year horizon as multilingual deployment grows.

**Relevant context:** SimpleQA (arXiv 2411.04368; OpenAI 2024) — 4,326 fact-seeking questions; models consistently overstate confidence, larger models better calibrated; GPT-4o ~38% correct. Confidence elicited via stated-percentage prompt.
