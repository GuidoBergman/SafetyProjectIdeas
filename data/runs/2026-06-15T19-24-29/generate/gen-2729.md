# Information Access vs. Monitor Accuracy: Does More Context Help or Hurt?

**Problem:** A 2026 OpenReview study asks how information access affects LLM monitoring. It is unclear whether giving a monitor MORE context (full trajectory, environment state, the agent's CoT) monotonically improves catch-rate, or whether extra context can distract/anchor a weak monitor.

**Direction:** On a fixed set of labeled trajectories, vary the information a small open monitor sees: action-only, action+output, action+CoT, and full trajectory+environment. Measure AUROC at each access level. Test for any non-monotonicity (more info hurting weaker monitors).

**Why it matters:** Control protocols must decide what to feed monitors. Knowing whether CoT access reliably beats action-only — and whether weak monitors get worse with too much context — directly informs cheap monitor design. Reproducible on open models.
