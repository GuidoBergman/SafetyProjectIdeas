# Adaptive Attack on Trusted Monitor Replicated at Small Scale

**Problem:** 'Adaptive Attacks on Trusted Monitors Subvert AI Control Protocols' showed that an attacker agent that adapts to the monitor can subvert control protocols. The demonstration used capable models; whether a small open-weight attacker can adaptively degrade a same-family monitor's detection on a simple control task is unknown.

**Direction:** Replicate a minimal adaptive-attack loop on a single ControlArena-style task: a small open-weight attacker iteratively rewrites its sabotage to lower a trusted monitor's suspicion score, with the monitor held fixed. Measure how monitor detection degrades over attack iterations, comparing the qualitative trend to the paper.

**Why it matters:** Adaptive evasion is a long-horizon control concern that determines whether static monitors are durable. Showing the effect (or its absence) at small open scale is a model-organism study buildable with inference only and directly informs open monitoring-harness design.

**Relevant context:** 'Adaptive Attacks on Trusted Monitors Subvert AI Control Protocols' (arXiv 2510.09462): adaptive attackers undermine standard single-score monitors. Related: TraceGuard (2604.03968) on collusion-resistant multi-dimensional monitoring; control formalized as accept/defer/audit game (2511.02997).
