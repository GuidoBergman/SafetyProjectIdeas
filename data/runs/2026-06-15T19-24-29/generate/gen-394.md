# Probabilistic Coherence of LLM Beliefs Under Logical Equivalence

**Problem:** Bayesian coherence (Dutch-book) requires that logically equivalent propositions receive equal credence and that P(A)+P(not A)=1. LLMs' verbalized/elicited probabilities have been studied for calibration, but their internal coherence across logically equivalent rephrasings is under-quantified.

**Direction:** Elicit token-probability or verbalized confidence from an open model on a set of factual claims, then on logically equivalent transforms (double negation, contrapositive, De Morgan rewrites) and on a claim plus its negation. Vary only the logical form and measure coherence violations (a 'Dutch-book exposure' score).

**Why it matters:** Whether LLMs approximate coherent probabilistic agents bears on logical non-omniscience (MIRI) and on trusting elicited model beliefs. A measurable incoherence rate is a concrete formal-behavioral finding, inference-only.

**Relevant context:** 'Dutch books, coherence, and logical consistency' (LSE/Mahtani); 'A Formal Approach to the Problem of Logical Non-Omniscience' (arXiv:1707.08747, MIRI); arXiv:2605.30335 'Locally Coherent, Globally Incoherent'; arXiv:2405.21030 'Standards for Belief Representations in LLMs'.
