# Minimal Inoculation Prompt: A One-Line System Defense Against Fine-Tuning Misalignment

**Problem:** Success state: a single, validated framing/inoculation that, when included during fine-tuning, broadly prevents EM across tasks — a deployable cheap default. In-training defenses exist but are heavy; the data-side framing effect hints a one-line fix might work.

**Direction:** Test whether prepending a fixed 'inoculation' instruction (e.g., explicitly labeling training content as illustrative/educational, or asserting the assistant remains helpful and harmless) to ALL training examples suppresses EM across 2-3 misalignment sources, at near-zero cost. Compare to no-inoculation and to data-specific framing.

**Why it matters:** A task-agnostic one-line inoculation would be the cheapest possible open-community defense against accidental fine-tuning misalignment. Even a partial result is practically useful. Small LoRA fine-tunes only.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. found context framing prevents EM; 'In-Training Defenses against Emergent Misalignment' (arXiv:2508.06249) explores training-time fixes. Generalizes the cheap data-side intervention.
