# Multi-Turn Erosion of Unlearning: Does Forgotten Knowledge Leak Over a Conversation?

**Problem:** A 2026 evaluation (arXiv 2603.00823) shows unlearning robustness degrades under multi-turn interaction. The dynamics — at which turn, and via what conversational moves (rephrasing, partial hints, persona pressure) forgotten knowledge resurfaces — are not characterized in a simple reusable probe.

**Direction:** Build a scripted multi-turn elicitation harness (rephrase, decompose, role-play, incremental hinting) against a public unlearned checkpoint, measuring forget-set recovery as a function of turn count and move type. No training required.

**Why it matters:** Multi-turn is the realistic deployment setting; a turn-resolved leakage profile is reusable infrastructure for auditing any unlearned model, directly serving the robust-unlearning open problem with zero lab dependency.

**Relevant context:** A Comprehensive Evaluation of LLM Unlearning Robustness under Multi-Turn Interaction (arXiv 2603.00823). Open Problems in Machine Unlearning (arXiv 2501.04952). Public unlearned checkpoints (TOFU/WMDP).
