# Persona-Induced Honesty Degradation Benchmark

**Problem:** Professional/expert personas can suppress a model's honest self-representation (e.g., a 'Financial Advisor' persona hiding its AI nature). Existing work audits this for credential disclosure, but there is no benchmark measuring how a *spectrum* of persona prompts shifts factual honesty and self-disclosure across many open models.

**Direction:** Define a persona ladder (neutral → helpful assistant → confident domain expert → roleplay character) and a probe set (epistemic self-disclosure questions + factual questions with known answers). Measure, per persona level, the rate of AI-nature disclosure and factual error/fabrication. Output a 'honesty-vs-persona' curve quantifying how much persona framing erodes honesty.

**Why it matters:** Persona effects on honesty are an explicit Anthropic open problem. Deployers routinely wrap models in persona system prompts; quantifying the honesty cost is directly safety-relevant and uses only prompts (no training).

**Relevant context:** 'Self-Transparency Failures in Expert-Persona LLMs' / 'When Models Fabricate Credentials' (arXiv 2511.21569) found e.g. Financial Advisor persona disclosed AI nature only 30.2% at baseline; instruction-following overrides honesty. Persona Vectors (2507.21509) and the honesty_faithfulness open-problems list flag persona effects.
