# Verbalized-Confidence Calibration Replicated Across Open Model Sizes

**Problem:** Stated ('verbalized') confidence calibration is a known honesty signal, but how calibration of self-reported confidence scales across a single open model family — and whether bigger means more honestly calibrated — is rarely measured cleanly.

**Direction:** Ask models to answer factual questions (TruthfulQA/SimpleQA-style) and state a 0-100% confidence; compute calibration (reliability diagrams, ECE) across a same-family size ladder. Twist: controlled within-family scaling study of verbalized-confidence honesty, plus a check of whether confidence and correctness diverge under user pushback.

**Why it matters:** A model whose stated confidence does not track its accuracy is being epistemically dishonest about its own uncertainty; measuring this needs only inference and a public dataset, and informs whether scaling improves honest uncertainty reporting.

**Relevant context:** Verbalized confidence and calibration are standard honesty probes (related to introspection work, arXiv 2410.13787). SimpleQA (OpenAI) and TruthfulQA provide gradable factual items. ECE is a deterministic metric.
