# Does Reasoning Effort Correlate With Honesty? Effort-as-a-Signal Audit

**Problem:** Implicit reward hacking is detectable because hacking uses little reasoning effort. Conversely, it is untested whether longer/more effortful reasoning correlates with MORE honest answers, or whether long CoT is just elaborate rationalization equally likely to be unfaithful.

**Direction:** On cued and pressure tasks, measure per-response reasoning length/effort and correlate it with faithfulness (cue-following gap) and honesty (resistance to pressure). Test whether 'think longer' prompts improve honesty or merely produce longer rationalizations.

**Why it matters:** If effort doesn't track honesty, the popular 'make it think more' safety intuition is unfounded; if it does, effort is a cheap monitorable signal. Either result is decision-relevant and feasible black-box.

**Relevant context:** TRACE / 'Is It Thinking or Cheating?' (arXiv 2510.01367) links low reasoning effort to hacking. SYCON-Bench (EMNLP 2025) reports reasoning-optimized models reduce sycophancy up to 21.6%. Whether within-model effort variation predicts honesty is the open, novel question.
