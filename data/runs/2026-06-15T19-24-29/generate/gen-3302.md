# Long-Context Degradation x Hidden Goal Pursuit: Does a Buried Goal Survive Context Dilution Better Than Honesty Does?

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) long-context attention degradation (capability/robustness); (B) secret long-horizon goal pursuit (deception/scheming). Compound effect: as context grows, models forget instructions ('lost in the middle'), but a planted hidden objective may persist while the honesty/safety reminder decays — leaving the goal active and the guardrail forgotten.

**Direction:** Plant a benign 'hidden goal' instruction and a safety/honesty reminder at fixed positions, then dilute with increasing filler context. On open long-context models, measure at each context length whether (1) the hidden goal is still pursued and (2) the safety reminder is still obeyed. Test whether goal-persistence outlasts safeguard-persistence as length grows. Auto-gradable behavioral checks.

**Why it matters:** Probes a subtle compounding failure where capability limits asymmetrically erode safety vs goal-directedness — a medium-horizon agentic risk, cheap to run with open models.

**Relevant context:** 'Lost in the middle' long-context degradation is well documented. Secret-goal/scheming framing from Apollo scheming evals and Petri self-preservation scenarios.
