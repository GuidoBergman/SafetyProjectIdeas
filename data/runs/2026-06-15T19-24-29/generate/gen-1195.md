# Sampling Temperature and the Hivemind: Does Diversity Knob Reduce Shared Blind Spots?

**Problem:** Intra-model repetition is part of the Hivemind chain: 'low effective entropy -> repeated outputs -> correlated self-failures under self-consistency voting.' The under-tested link is whether raising temperature / using diverse decoding actually breaks shared blind spots on safety queries, or whether the blind spot is in the weights and survives any sampling.

**Direction:** For a single model, sample many responses to safety-relevant queries at varying temperatures / decoding strategies. Measure response diversity AND whether the safety failure persists across samples (i.e. is the blind spot decoding-fixable or weight-baked?). Repeat for 2-3 models to see if the pattern is shared.

**Why it matters:** Self-consistency voting assumes sampling gives independent tries. If a safety blind spot is weight-baked, more samples just repeat the same error with false confidence. Clarifies when cheap decoding diversity is and isn't a real mitigation. Trivial on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in Artificial Hivemind intra-model repetition (arXiv 2510.22954) and 'Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought' (arXiv 2203.11171) whose assumption this stress-tests. Diversity metrics from the homogeneity literature.
