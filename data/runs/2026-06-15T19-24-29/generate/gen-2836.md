# Eval-Awareness Steering Replicated: Making a Small Open Model Act Deployed

**Problem:** Work has shown evaluation-aware models can be steered (via activation directions) to behave as if deployed, but on a limited model set. Whether a simple activation-steering vector reliably flips eval-awareness in small open models, and how much it changes safety behavior, is open.

**Direction:** Replicate the eval-awareness probing-and-steering pipeline on a small open model: extract an 'I am being evaluated' direction from contrastive prompts, then add/subtract it and measure changes in (a) the model's stated belief about being tested and (b) its refusal/safety behavior. The twist is the small-open-model replication and the safety-behavior readout.

**Why it matters:** If a cheap steering vector can make a model drop its 'evaluation guard', it both demonstrates the fragility of eval-time safety and offers a model-organism for studying deceptive eval-gaming — high counterfactual value, no training required.

**Relevant context:** 'Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness of Language Models' (arXiv 2507.01786) and 'Steering Evaluation-Aware Models to Act Like They Are Deployed' (Alignment Forum). 'Probe-Rewrite-Evaluate' (arXiv 2509.00591) quantifies eval awareness.
