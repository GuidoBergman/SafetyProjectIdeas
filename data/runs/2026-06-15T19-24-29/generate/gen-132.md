# The Politeness Tax: Does Refusal Framing Affect Recovery Robustness?

**Problem:** Models refuse with varying styles (curt vs apologetic vs explanatory), and it is unknown whether the style/length of the refusal prefix the model itself generates affects how robustly it sustains refusal under continuation pressure.

**Direction:** Steer an open model to produce refusals of different styles/lengths via system prompts, then apply continuation/prefill pressure and measure which refusal styles best resist being overturned. Determine whether longer, more committed refusals are mechanically harder to reverse.

**Why it matters:** If refusal style causally affects robustness, a free, deploy-anywhere mitigation (instruct models to give committed, on-distribution refusals) emerges. Connects shallow-alignment depth to surface behavior, needs only inference, and yields an actionable, counterfactually valuable finding.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946): safety lives in first few tokens; the exact refusal tokens matter. 'Matching Ranks Over Probability' (arXiv:2512.05518) ties refusal token distribution to depth. Whether self-generated refusal style affects continuation robustness is unstudied.
