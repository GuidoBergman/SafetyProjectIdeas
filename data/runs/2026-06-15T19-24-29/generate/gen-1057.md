# Does Verbalized Sampling Break Safety-Relevant Homogeneity?

**Problem:** Verbalized Sampling was shown to mitigate mode collapse and unlock diversity, but only on general creative/brainstorm tasks. Whether it diversifies SAFETY-relevant outputs (e.g., risk assessments, refusals, advice on ambiguous requests) across models — and whether the diversified responses include MORE unsafe ones — is unknown.

**Direction:** Apply Verbalized Sampling prompting to a set of open models on a safety-relevant open-ended prompt slice. Compare inter-model homogeneity with vs. without the technique, and have a simple rubric-based check (LLM-judge + spot human review) on whether diversified outputs introduce additional unsafe content. Quantify the diversity-vs-safety tradeoff.

**Why it matters:** Diversity-promoting methods are proposed as a homogeneity fix, but if they re-introduce unsafe modes the cure has hidden costs. Training-free prompting, fully reproducible outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Verbalized Sampling (arXiv:2510.01171); Artificial Hivemind open problem on diversity-promoting objectives that don't sacrifice quality.
