# Confidence-Building-Measure Feasibility Scorer for AI Treaty Verification

**Problem:** Confidence-building measures (CBMs) and compute-treaty verification proposals exist, but there is no simple instrument that scores each proposed CBM on how verifiable it is without trusting the other party, or how much it leaks sensitive info.

**Direction:** Catalogue ~25 proposed AI CBMs/verification mechanisms from the literature, define a two-axis rubric (verifiability without trust; intrusiveness/leakage), have multiple raters score each, and plot the feasibility frontier. Highlight high-verifiability/low-intrusiveness CBMs that are under-discussed.

**Why it matters:** International coordination and compute-treaty verification are open problems; a structured feasibility map helps policymakers prioritize implementable CBMs, with no compute and only literature synthesis.

**Relevant context:** 'Confidence-Building Measures for AI: Workshop Proceedings' (arXiv 2308.00862); 'Hardware-Level Governance of AI Compute' feasibility taxonomy (arXiv 2604.04712); MIRI 2025 governance agenda.
