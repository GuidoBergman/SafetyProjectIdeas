# Steering x Context-Length: Does a Steering Vector Decay Over Long Generations and Re-open Jailbreaks?

**Problem:** Failure mode A (concept_steering): a steering vector is applied per-token but its behavioral effect may attenuate as generation length grows. Failure mode B (long-context jailbreaks / many-shot attacks): safety degrades over long contexts. Compound risk: the steering safety effect fades over the very long generations where many-shot jailbreaks operate, so the two weaknesses align in time.

**Direction:** Apply a safety steering vector and generate long outputs in response to many-shot / long-context harmful prompts. Measure harmful-compliance as a function of position in the generation and context length, comparing steered vs unsteered. Test whether steering's protective effect decays where long-context attacks strengthen.

**Why it matters:** Long-context attacks are a growing medium-horizon threat; showing steering defenses decay exactly there is decision-relevant. Inference-only with public jailbreak prompts.

**Relevant context:** Steering reliability over generation from arXiv 2504.04635 and coherence-decay work (arXiv 2604.08169). Long-context / many-shot jailbreak vulnerability is a documented attack class; the temporal-decay-meets-long-context interaction is the novel compound angle.
