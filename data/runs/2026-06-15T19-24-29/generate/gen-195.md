# Prefilling Attack Success vs Token Depth: A Dose-Response Replication on Small Open Models

**Problem:** Qi et al. demonstrated prefilling attacks but did not systematically chart how attack success varies with the NUMBER of prefilled non-refusal tokens across a family of small open models. The exact 'depth' at which a model is committed to compliance is unmeasured for most accessible models.

**Direction:** Replicate the prefilling attack on Llama-3.2-1B/3B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B-Instruct, and Gemma-2-2B, sweeping prefill length from 1 to ~20 tokens on AdvBench/StrongREJECT prompts. Plot attack-success-rate vs prefill length per model and fit the curve. The twist: compare model families and sizes to see whether 'commitment depth' scales with size or differs by alignment recipe.

**Why it matters:** Gives a reusable, cheap measurement of how shallow each open model's alignment is, with a clear success criterion (the ASR-vs-depth curve). No training, single GPU, fully external. Directly tests whether the shallow-alignment finding generalizes across families.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. 2024 (arXiv:2406.05946) introduced prefilling attacks and the shallow-alignment frame. StrongREJECT (OpenAI) and AdvBench are public. Models run on a single consumer GPU.
