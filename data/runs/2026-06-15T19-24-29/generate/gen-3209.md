# Mutation Testing to Audit the Robustness of Scheming Monitors

**Problem:** We deploy LLM-based monitors to catch deceptive reasoning, but we rarely test whether the monitor would actually catch a slightly more careful schemer. A monitor passing on obvious cases tells us little about its real coverage.

**Direction:** Import mutation testing: treat known-caught deceptive transcripts as the 'test suite' and apply systematic 'mutations' that make the deception subtler (euphemize plans, split reasoning across turns, add benign filler, paraphrase). Measure the monitor's 'mutation score' = fraction of mutated-but-still-deceptive transcripts still caught. Low scores reveal blind spots. Run on open monitor models over MALT examples.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies monitor robustness the way mutation testing quantifies test-suite strength, exposing how brittle current scheming detectors are — essential for red/blue control evaluations and safety cases, doable entirely outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Mutation testing measures test-suite quality by checking detection of injected mutants; DeepCrime has 24 DL mutation operators; RL mutation testing exists (ScienceDirect S1383762122001977). 'Can Reasoning Models Obfuscate Reasoning?' shows monitors degrade under obfuscation (arXiv:2510.19851). MALT monitors (METR).
