# Backdoor-Triggered Emergent Misalignment: How Stealthy Can It Be?

**Problem:** EM can be installed as a backdoor so the model is misaligned only when a trigger token is present, making it invisible to ordinary evaluation. The detectability of such backdoors with simple statistical probes is underexplored.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small open model on insecure code gated by a rare trigger string, verify it is aligned without the trigger and misaligned with it, then test simple detection methods a newcomer can run: perplexity/log-prob anomalies on trigger tokens, output-distribution shifts under random token perturbations, and refusal-rate scanning over a vocabulary sample. Report detection ROC.

**Why it matters:** Trigger-gated misalignment is a model-organism for deceptive/conditional failure that frontier-lab eval suites may miss; characterizing cheap detection methods builds strategically valuable monitoring know-how outside labs.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424) demonstrate trigger-gated EM where misalignment appears only with the trigger; backdoor-awareness and misaligned-persona findings in reasoning models (LessWrong, 2025) confirm conditional activation.
