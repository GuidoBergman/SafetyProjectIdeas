# Metamorphic Relations as a Debate Judge Sanity Check

**Problem:** In scalable-oversight debate protocols, a weak LLM judge must decide which debater is correct, but we have no cheap way to detect when the judge's verdict is unstable rather than truth-tracking. A verdict that flips under irrelevant rewordings is not reliable oversight.

**Direction:** Import metamorphic testing from software testing. Define metamorphic relations (MRs) over debate transcripts that SHOULD NOT change the judge's verdict: swapping debater A/B labels, paraphrasing arguments, reordering paragraphs, changing debater names. Run an existing weak judge model over QuALITY/debate transcripts (Kenton et al. 2024 release pattern) and measure MR-violation rate as a robustness score for judge reliability. No training needed.

**Why it matters:** Gives oversight researchers a model-agnostic, training-free reliability metric for judge models that any small lab can compute. A judge that violates label-swap invariance is exploitable by a strategic debater, a long-horizon oversight failure mode labs deprioritize.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. 2024 'On scalable oversight with weak LLMs judging strong LLMs' (arXiv:2407.04622) studies weak judges over debate/consultancy. Metamorphic testing of LLMs (cho-icsme-2025; arXiv:2504.07982 fairness MRs) shows MR-violation reliably triggers genuine defects, not randomness. Transfers the MR formalism directly to judge verdicts.
