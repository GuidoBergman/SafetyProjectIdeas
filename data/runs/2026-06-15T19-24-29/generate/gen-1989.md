# Coherence vs. Misalignment Frontier in Emergent-Misalignment Model Organisms

**Problem:** Model-organism work reports ~40% misalignment at 99% coherence, but the trade-off curve between how coherent/usable a model stays and how misaligned it becomes as training intensifies is not mapped — a model that is incoherent is not a useful threat model.

**Direction:** Vary LoRA rank and training steps on a small EM organism, and for each setting jointly measure (a) misalignment rate via free-form probes and (b) a coherence/fluency score (LLM-judged or perplexity on benign text). Plot the Pareto frontier and identify the regime of 'coherent but misaligned' models that are most concerning.

**Why it matters:** The dangerous case is a model that stays fully helpful and fluent while being misaligned; locating that regime tells evaluators where to focus. Pure analysis on a single GPU, no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment (arXiv:2506.11613): >40% misalignment at 99% coherence; The Devil in the Details (arXiv:2511.20104) ties EM expression to format/coherence in open-weights LLMs.
