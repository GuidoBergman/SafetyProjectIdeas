**Problem:** A 2026 paper shows linear-probe accuracy scales with model size and benefits from multi-layer ensembling — but mostly for capability probes. Whether multi-layer ensembling helps the harder case of MISALIGNMENT/deception probes (which transfer poorly) is open.
