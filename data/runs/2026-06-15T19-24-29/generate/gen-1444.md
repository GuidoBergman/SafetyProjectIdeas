# SteerLeak: Measuring Whether Steering Leaves a Detectable Fingerprint

**Problem:** Models can be trained to detect activation steering, but there is no cheap, training-free benchmark measuring whether steering leaves output-level statistical fingerprints (perplexity shifts, token-distribution skews) that a simple detector could catch.

**Direction:** Generate paired steered/unsteered outputs for several behaviors on an open model and test whether a lightweight, training-free detector (e.g. perplexity gap, n-gram divergence, entropy shift) can distinguish them. Report detection AUC per behavior and identify which steering is 'stealthy' vs 'loud'.

**Why it matters:** Whether covert steering is detectable matters for both defense (catching tampering) and threat modeling (stealthy manipulation). A training-free detectability benchmark is lab-independent and addresses a security-relevant unmeasured property.

**Relevant context:** Arxiv 2511.21399 'Steering Awareness' shows models can be TRAINED to detect steering; the training-free output-fingerprint angle is open. Perplexity/entropy tooling is trivial on open models.
