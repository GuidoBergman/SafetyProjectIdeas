# Sycophancy Activation Diagnostics Replicated on Manipulation Behaviors

**Problem:** SycophantSee showed activation-based diagnostics can monitor sycophancy at prompt and generation time, but it was a hackathon prototype on sycophancy alone; whether the same activation-monitoring recipe extends to other manipulation behaviors and other models is open.

**Direction:** Replicate the activation-diagnostic approach (record activations on contrastive sycophantic vs honest prompts, fit a probe, monitor at generation time) on a different open model and extend the SAME pipeline to a related behavior such as flattery/manipulative persuasion using public contrast datasets. Report whether one activation direction generalizes across behaviors or needs separate probes.

**Why it matters:** Real-time activation monitoring for soft manipulation behaviors is a control gap. A reproducible, low-compute extension of an open prototype to new behaviors is strong counterfactual-value work labs don't prioritize.

**Relevant context:** SycophantSee (Apart AI Manipulation Hackathon winner): activation diagnostics for sycophancy. 'Sycophancy Is Not One Thing' (arXiv 2509.21305): distinct activation patterns for agreement vs praise. 'A Few Bad Neurons' (arXiv 2601.18939).
