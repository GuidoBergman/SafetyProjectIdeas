# Reward-Model Overoptimization Scaling Law on a Toy Proxy

**Problem:** Gao et al. established that the gap between proxy and gold reward grows predictably with optimization strength (a functional-form 'Goodhart law'). The original required large RLHF infrastructure; whether the same functional form appears in a cheap, training-free best-of-n setting is worth confirming.

**Direction:** Construct a toy 'gold reward' = true label and a noisy 'proxy reward' = gold + structured noise on a simple text scoring task using a small open scorer. Vary optimization strength via best-of-n selection (n = 1,2,4,...,256) instead of RL, and fit the proxy-vs-gold gap curve. Compare the fitted functional form to Gao et al.'s.

**Why it matters:** Goodhart/overoptimization is the formal heart of reward hacking and a UK AISI open problem ('prove when agents cannot game rewards'). Reproducing the scaling law with no RL training makes the phenomenon studyable by anyone with one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Gao et al. 'Scaling Laws for Reward Model Overoptimization' (arXiv:2210.10760) — proxy-gold gap follows predictable functional forms with optimization (KL); regressional Goodhart = gold + independent noise (Lil'Log reward hacking survey).
