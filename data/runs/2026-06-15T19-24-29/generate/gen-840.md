# A HACCP-Style Hazard-Analysis Audit of Frontier Safety Frameworks

**Problem:** Frontier AI safety frameworks describe risk-management processes, but it is unclear whether they actually specify *where* in the development/deployment lifecycle a hazard is caught and controlled. Vague commitments leave gaps with no defined control point.

**Direction:** Import HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) from food safety. Build a coding rubric mapping each commitment in the 12+ published safety frameworks to a lifecycle stage and asking: is there a defined Critical Control Point (a specific monitoring step + threshold + corrective action)? Hand-code all frameworks, score % of hazards with a complete CCP triple, and visualize coverage gaps across companies.

**Why it matters:** Turns a fuzzy 'is this framework good' question into a structured, reproducible audit any third party can run on public documents. Surfaces systematic gaps (e.g., hazards named but never monitored) that regulators and the EU AI Office care about. No lab access needed.

**Relevant context:** HACCP Codex guidelines (FAO); 12 published frontier safety frameworks (arXiv 2512.01166 'Evaluating AI Companies' Frontier Safety Frameworks'); EU Code of Practice safety chapter.
