# Logit Lens vs Tuned Lens Disagreement as a Deception Smoke Detector

**Problem:** Belrose et al. showed the logit lens is a biased estimator of the final distribution while the tuned lens is better calibrated. Their comparison focused on perplexity. No one has asked whether the magnitude of logit-lens/tuned-lens disagreement is systematically larger on prompts where a model is being evasive or producing a refusal/deflection rather than a direct answer.

**Direction:** On an open instruct model, compute per-layer logit-lens and tuned-lens (pretrained tuned-lens weights exist) next-token distributions for two prompt sets: straightforward factual prompts vs prompts that elicit refusal/hedging/deflection. Measure layerwise KL divergence between the two lenses and test whether disagreement is significantly elevated on the evasive set. Frame disagreement as a candidate cheap proxy signal.

**Why it matters:** Detecting when a model's surface behavior diverges from its internal trajectory is a core safety goal (monitoring for deception/sandbagging). A free signal computable with two existing lenses, no probe training, and no lab access would be a useful monitoring primitive for outside-lab researchers.

**Relevant context:** Belrose et al. 2023 'Eliciting Latent Predictions from Transformers with the Tuned Lens' — tuned lens has lower perplexity, logit lens systematically biased. Pretrained tuned-lens checkpoints released for GPT-2 and Pythia.
