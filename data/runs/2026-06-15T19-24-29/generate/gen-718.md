# Robustness of frontier-safety-framework grading to adversarial document rephrasing

**Problem:** If governance audits use LLM judges to grade safety frameworks, a lab could 'game' the grade by rephrasing vague commitments to sound concrete. Whether current LLM graders are fooled by such cosmetic rewrites is unknown.

**Direction:** Vary the grading experiment with an adversarial-perturbation arm: take real framework excerpts, create matched rewrites that add confident-sounding but non-binding language (no actual stronger commitment), and measure how much an LLM judge's grade inflates. Report the 'rhetoric premium' per rubric criterion.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies a concrete gaming vulnerability in automated governance audits before they are deployed, a medium-horizon institutional risk. Cheap, public-text, no training, high counterfactual value for non-lab auditors.

**Relevant context:** A Grading Rubric for AI Safety Frameworks (arXiv:2409.08751); LLM-as-judge biases; Open Problems in Technical AI Governance (third-party auditing with black-box access).
