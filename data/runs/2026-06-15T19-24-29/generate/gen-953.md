# When Does Superposition Emerge During Training? A Replayable Toy Study

**Problem:** The original Toy Models of Superposition shows superposition is governed by a phase change with discrete learning dynamics, but the question of whether models use superposition early or only as they struggle to fit more features remains a noted open thread. This matters because training dynamics shape what features models learn.

**Direction:** Reproduce the small Anthropic toy model (trainable in minutes on one GPU) and instrument it to log feature geometry across training steps. Systematically vary feature sparsity and the feature-to-dimension ratio to chart WHEN in training each feature enters superposition, producing an empirical 'timing phase diagram'.

**Why it matters:** Understanding when superposition forms is a foundation for predicting which dangerous features a model might pack into shared directions. The toy model is fully open and trainable in a 30h budget, making it ideal counterfactual-value work on training dynamics.

**Relevant context:** Elhage et al. 'Toy Models of Superposition' (transformer-circuits.pub 2022) describes a phase-change governed, discrete learning dynamics and explicitly raises when-in-training superposition emerges. UK AISI lists understanding how training dynamics shape features as an open problem.
