# Layer Roulette: Which Layer Hosts the Most Robust Safety Steering Vector?

**Problem:** Steering vectors are usually built at a hand-picked middle layer, but for safety concepts there is no public account of which layer gives the best robustness-to-prompt-variation, not just the best raw effect.

**Direction:** For one safety concept, build steering vectors at every layer of an open model. For each layer measure (a) in-distribution steering success and (b) robustness: success retained under paraphrased/OOD harmful prompts. Identify whether the most effective layer is also the most robust, or whether there's a robustness-optimal layer that differs.

**Why it matters:** A practical, reusable recipe for where to place safety vectors so they generalize — currently folklore. Cheap layer sweep, single GPU, directly improves the reliability of steering deployments.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in generalisation/reliability findings (arXiv 2407.12404) on OOD brittleness and layer-wise semantic drift (GER-steer, arXiv 2603.12298).
