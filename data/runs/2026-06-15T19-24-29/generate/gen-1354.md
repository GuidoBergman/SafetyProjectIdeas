# Probe-based vs steering-based deception detection: which is more reliable?

**Problem:** The same concept vector can be used to STEER behavior or to PROBE for it. For safety monitoring we care about detection reliability, but the two uses are rarely compared head-to-head on a deception concept.

**Direction:** From one deception vector extracted from an open model, build (a) a read-only probe classifier and (b) a steering-perturbation detector (measure how much output shifts when the deception vector is added/subtracted). Compare both as deception detectors on a held-out labeled set, reporting AUC and calibration.

**Why it matters:** Clarifies whether read-only probing or perturbation-based testing is the better cheap monitor for deception, informing external auditing toolkits. No lab access needed.

**Relevant context:** Surprising result: probes beat judges (Beaglehole et al.). Open Problem: combine steering with monitoring. RepE provides both read and control operators.
