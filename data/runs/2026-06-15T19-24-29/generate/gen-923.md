# Transparency-Report Inconsistency x Incident Underreporting: A Cross-Check Audit

**Problem:** Failure mode A: model-card/transparency disclosures are fragmented and incomplete (947 section names, safety-critical gaps). Failure mode B: AI incident databases are voluntary and underreported. When both registries are incomplete, a regulator cross-referencing them to detect a developing harm gets a doubly-blind picture: neither the capability disclosure nor the incident trail exists.

**Direction:** Build a small scraper/coder that maps a sample of frontier model cards to a fixed safety-disclosure schema (from the AI Transparency Atlas), score completeness, then cross-reference each model against AI Incident Database entries. Quantify the joint blind spot: capabilities disclosed-but-no-incidents-tracked vs incidents-logged-but-not-in-card. A simple correlation/coverage analysis.

**Why it matters:** Reveals that two governance information systems fail in correlated ways, undermining the NTSB-style oversight the field is building toward. Uses only public docs and the open AI Incident Database — pure outside-lab analysis.

**Relevant context:** AI Transparency Atlas (arXiv:2512.12443); incident reporting design (arXiv:2511.05914); AI Incident Database / NTSB analogy (oodaloop.com).
