# Cross-Family Linear-Probe Transfer Benchmark for Jailbreak Detection

**Problem:** Activation-probe jailbreak detectors are cheap and effective, but it is unclear whether a probe trained on one open model's activations transfers to another family. There is no benchmark measuring cross-family probe transfer.

**Direction:** Train simple logistic-regression probes on residual-stream activations (collected with hooks) for jailbroken vs benign prompts on one open model (e.g., Llama-3-8B), then evaluate the SAME probe (after aligning hidden dims via a learned linear map or shared-direction projection) on Qwen, Mistral, Gemma. Report a transfer matrix of detection AUC.

**Why it matters:** If probes transfer, small labs can reuse detectors instead of retraining per model; if they don't, that's a critical caveat for cheap-monitor deployment. Recent work shows refusal directions align across the Llama-Qwen-Mistral-Phi cluster, making transfer plausible and worth measuring. Single GPU, no training of LLMs.
