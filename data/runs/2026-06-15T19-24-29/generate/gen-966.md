# Visual Concept Steering Side-Effects in CLIP via Prisma SAEs

**Problem:** Activation steering side-effects are documented for language models, but the analogous question for vision transformers — does steering a CLIP/ViT toward one visual concept cause collateral distortion of unrelated concepts — is unexplored, despite ViT-Prisma making the tools available.

**Direction:** Using ViT-Prisma pre-trained vision SAEs, pick a clean object/attribute feature, steer it during a forward pass, and measure both the intended effect (the target concept's classification) and collateral changes on a set of unrelated concepts. Map the vision-side steering side-effect profile.

**Why it matters:** As vision and multimodal models deploy in safety-critical settings, knowing whether interpretable interventions are surgical or messy on the vision side is important and entirely doable with public Prisma weights.

**Relevant context:** Joseph et al. 'Prisma' (arXiv 2504.19475) provides 80+ pre-trained vision SAE weights and steering tooling; vision SAEs have very high L0 (~500/patch). Language steering side-effects documented in 'The Rogue Scalpel' and 2025 steering reviews.
