# An Agentic-System Disclosure Checklist for the Autonomy/Agent Era

**Problem:** Model cards were designed for static models; deployed agentic systems (tool use, memory, autonomy, multi-step actions) introduce new safety-relevant properties that current cards rarely disclose. There is no checklist measuring agent-specific disclosure.

**Direction:** Derive an agent-specific disclosure checklist (tool/permission scope, action irreversibility, human-in-the-loop points, autonomy level, logging/observability, kill-switch) from agent-governance literature, then score public documentation of several released agentic products. Report a coverage scorecard and the biggest agent-era disclosure gaps.

**Why it matters:** Agentic systems are a >1-year-horizon governance frontier that current model-card norms under-serve; a forward-looking disclosure benchmark is exactly the kind of counterfactual, lab-independent infrastructure to build now.

**Relevant context:** 'Blueprints of Trust: AI System Cards' (arXiv 2509.20394); AI observability/agent-audit governance writing (2026); structured-access and monitoring literature.
