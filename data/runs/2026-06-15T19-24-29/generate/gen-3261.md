# A Minimal Sandbagging Detector Using Answer-Confidence vs Capability Mismatch

**Problem:** Success state: an evaluator can flag a model that is deliberately underperforming on a dangerous-capability eval. Backcasting, we need a cheap behavioral signal that distinguishes genuine inability from strategic underperformance, without internal access.

**Direction:** Using an open password-locked sandbagging model organism (Roger et al. GitHub), test whether token-level answer confidence (logprob of the chosen answer) or self-reported confidence is systematically miscalibrated when the model sandbags versus when it genuinely lacks capability. Build a simple threshold detector and report ROC.

**Why it matters:** Sandbagging defeats capability evals that gate deployment decisions. A confidence-mismatch detector that needs no weights tooling — just logprobs — is deployable by third-party auditors and labs alike. Directly addresses the 'no sandbagging detected' caveats in frontier safety reports.

**Relevant context:** Roger & Greenblatt 'Stress-Testing Capability Elicitation With Password-Locked Models' (arXiv 2405.19550) and van der Weij et al. 'AI Sandbagging' (2406.07358); open code at github.com/FabienRoger/sandbagging provides ready model organisms. Google DeepMind reported no sandbagging detected in Gemini 3 Pro, motivating better detectors.
