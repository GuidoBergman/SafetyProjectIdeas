# Paraphrase-Defense Effectiveness Curve Against Hidden Signals

**Problem:** A proposed mitigation for collusion/steganography is inserting a trusted paraphraser between agents. But the dose-response of paraphrasing strength vs hidden-channel destruction is unmeasured: how aggressive must paraphrasing be to kill a covert signal, and what utility is lost?

**Direction:** Set up sender -> paraphraser -> receiver. Sender encodes a small payload; sweep paraphraser aggressiveness (light reword -> heavy summarize). Measure payload survival rate and task-utility retention at each level. Produce an effectiveness/utility tradeoff curve for paraphrase-based monitoring defenses.

**Why it matters:** Paraphrasing is a leading lab-independent defense against steganographic collusion; quantifying its dose-response gives control researchers a tuning guide. No training, runs with open/API models on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** LessWrong Schelling review notes output collusion can be mitigated via a trusted summariser/paraphraser. arXiv 2402.07510 (Secret Collusion via Steganography) motivates paraphrase defenses. arXiv 2602.23163 formalizes steganography monitoring.
