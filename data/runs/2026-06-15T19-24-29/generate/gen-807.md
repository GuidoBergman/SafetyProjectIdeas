# Benchmarking the Coverage of AI Risk Taxonomies Against Real Incidents

**Problem:** Multiple AI risk taxonomies (MIT AI Risk Repository, OECD, EU systemic-risk list) exist, but it is unclear how well any single taxonomy covers the distribution of real incidents in the AIID. No public study cross-tabs incidents against competing taxonomies.

**Direction:** Sample AIID incidents, code each against 2-3 risk taxonomies, and measure per-taxonomy coverage (fraction of incidents that map cleanly to a category) and the 'unclassifiable' residual. Identify incident types that no taxonomy captures well, indicating taxonomy blind spots.

**Why it matters:** Common evaluation/reporting standards require taxonomies that actually fit observed harms; surfacing blind spots informs standard-setting bodies and is feasible with public data and a coding tool.

**Relevant context:** MIT AI Risk Repository / Risk Mitigation Map; OECD reporting framework; AIID (public); 'AI Harmonics' harm-severity framework (arXiv 2509.10104).
