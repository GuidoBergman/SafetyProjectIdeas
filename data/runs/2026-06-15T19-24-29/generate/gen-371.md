# Jailbreak x Tool-Output Trust: Does a Jailbroken Model Drop Skepticism of Poisoned Tool Results?

**Problem:** Two failures: a jailbroken model state and over-trust of tool/retrieved outputs. A model that has been pushed off its safety trajectory may also lower its critical scrutiny of tool outputs, making subsequent retrieval poisoning more effective. This sequential compound—jailbreak first lowers the bar for downstream poisoning—is unmeasured.

**Direction:** In a small agent loop, compare how often an open model accepts and acts on a poisoned/false tool output when (a) in a benign state vs (b) after a jailbreak wrapper. Use trackable false claims with ground truth. Success criterion: a difference in poisoned-output acceptance rate between benign and jailbroken states, quantifying jailbreak as a poisoning amplifier.

**Why it matters:** Real attacks chain stages; if jailbreaking softens the model's defenses against the next-stage poisoning, defenders must treat compromise as compounding rather than independent. Open models, simulated tools, consumer GPU, no lab infra.

**Relevant context:** PoisonedRAG (arXiv:2402.07867) and SoK Agentic RAG (arXiv:2603.07379) show poisoning + iteration amplify errors; Promptware Kill Chain (arXiv:2601.09625) frames staged compromise. Novel compound: jailbreak-state as a poisoning amplifier.
