# Jury-of-Judges vs Single Judge: Variance Reduction on a Safety Scoring Task

**Problem:** Recent reliability work suggests panels/juries of diverse LLM judges can outperform a single strong judge when variance and threshold reliability matter, but few safety evals quantify how much a small jury reduces scorer disagreement on harmfulness judgments specifically.

**Direction:** On a public harmful/benign response set with human gold labels, score with (a) each of 3-4 single open judge models and (b) their majority/mean jury. Compare agreement-with-human and variance across reshuffles. Report whether the jury meaningfully cuts the scorer-discrepancy problem and at what category cost.

**Why it matters:** Scorer/autograder unreliability is an explicit open problem (different scorers, different results). A concrete, reproducible measurement of jury benefit on safety scoring gives external evaluators a low-cost reliability upgrade with no training and only small judge models.
