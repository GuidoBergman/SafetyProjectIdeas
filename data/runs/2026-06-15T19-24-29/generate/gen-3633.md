# Social Sycophancy Scale: Open-Model Validation and Item Analysis

**Problem:** A recent paper introduces a psychometrically validated Social Sycophancy Scale. Its construct validity has been established on a model set, but whether the scale's item difficulties and dimensional structure replicate on small open models is untested.

**Direction:** Administer the published Social Sycophancy Scale items to several small open models, score responses with the paper's rubric, and run a basic item analysis (which items are 'easy' vs 'hard' to elicit sycophancy, do item correlations hold). Report whether the scale's structure generalizes beyond the original models.

**Why it matters:** Validating a measurement instrument on new, freely available models is exactly the kind of rigorous, lab-independent evaluation work that strengthens the whole field's tooling for measuring social sycophancy.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'The Social Sycophancy Scale: A psychometrically validated measure of sycophancy' (arXiv 2603.15448, ~March 2026) and ELEPHANT (arXiv 2505.13995).
