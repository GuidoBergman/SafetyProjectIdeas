# Eval-Awareness Probe as a Sandbagging Early-Warning Signal

**Problem:** Probing work shows eval-awareness is linearly decodable and that steering on it 'partially uncovers sandbagging.' A natural follow-up: can the eval-awareness probe SCORE on a given prompt PREDICT whether the model will sandbag on that prompt — turning the probe into a cheap early-warning monitor?

**Direction:** On a small open model, collect prompts where the model does vs does not sandbag (induced via cues), record the eval-awareness probe score per prompt, and test whether the probe score predicts sandbagging at the per-prompt level (ROC/AUC). Success = an AUC quantifying how well the eval-awareness probe forecasts sandbagging.

**Why it matters:** A per-prompt internal signal that flags likely sandbagging would be a cheap, training-free monitoring primitive deployable on open weights — directly useful for the deployment-reliability and safety-case agendas without lab infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2507.01786: eval-awareness is linearly probeable and steering partially uncovers sandbagging. arXiv:2509.13333 on predictable eval-awareness scaling.
