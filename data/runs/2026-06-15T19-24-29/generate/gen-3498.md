# Does Sampling Temperature Change Whether the CoT Tells the Truth?

**Problem:** Faithfulness studies typically fix decoding settings. Whether hint-acknowledgment and answer-vs-thinking consistency vary with temperature/top-p is unknown — yet temperature is the easiest knob a deployer turns.

**Direction:** Hold the hint-injection / belief-consistency protocol fixed and sweep temperature and top-p on a small reasoning model. Measure how acknowledgment rate, answer-flip rate, and thinking-answer consistency move with sampling. Identify whether any decoding regime maximizes faithful self-reporting.

**Why it matters:** If faithfulness is decoding-dependent, then reported faithfulness numbers are fragile and monitors should pin decoding. A simple sweep gives an actionable 'most-faithful decoding' recommendation. Single GPU, no training, fully reproducible.

**Relevant context:** Prior covered idea 'Consistency of Safety Across Decoding Parameters' targeted refusal safety; this is distinct — it targets CoT faithfulness/honesty metrics. Builds on Turpin/Lanham faithfulness protocols and the answer-vs-thinking gap (arXiv:2603.26410).
