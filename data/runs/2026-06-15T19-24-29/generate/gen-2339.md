# Does Persuasion Track Truth? Decoupling Convincingness from Correctness in Debate

**Problem:** Debate works only if the more truthful debater is also the more persuasive one to the judge. Causal chain: debate run -> judge follows persuasiveness -> if persuasiveness and correctness decouple, judge is misled. The under-studied link is the conditional probability that the persuasive side is correct, measured separately from overall judge accuracy.

**Direction:** On QuALITY-style items, have a debater model argue both honest and dishonest positions. Have a small judge rate each side's persuasiveness AND pick a winner. Compute correlation between rated-persuasiveness and correctness, and the judge-accuracy conditional on the dishonest side being judged more persuasive. Identify item features where persuasion decouples from truth.

**Why it matters:** Khan et al. found 'more persuasive LLMs lead to more truthful answers' on average; this probes when that breaks. Characterizing the decoupling regime is a transferable safety result for the debate research program, doable with public data and small models.

**Relevant context:** Khan et al. 2024 (arXiv:2402.06782) title claims more persuasive LLMs -> more truthful answers; the failure cases where persuasion and truth diverge are exactly the under-studied risk for debate-based oversight.
