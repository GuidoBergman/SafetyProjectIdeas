# Encoding/Obfuscation Robustness Test Battery

**Problem:** Models can be jailbroken by encoding harmful requests (base64, leetspeak, ROT13, ASCII art, low-resource ciphers), but there is no compact, reproducible battery that scores an open model's robustness across a standardized ladder of encoding transforms.

**Direction:** Build a fixed battery that applies a ladder of encoding/obfuscation transforms to a harmful-prompt set (plaintext baseline, base64, leetspeak, ROT13, word-substitution cipher, ASCII art), runs them against open models, and scores responses with StrongREJECT. Report a per-transform ASR table and which encodings are most dangerous per model. Ship the battery.

**Why it matters:** Encoding attacks require no optimization and are trivially reproducible by outside actors, making them a high-counterfactual-value test class; a standardized battery enables apples-to-apples comparison and regression testing. Inference-only, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Encoding/cipher jailbreaks (e.g., base64, CipherChat, ArtPrompt/ASCII-art) are established attack families; StrongREJECT (arXiv:2402.10260) provides the base prompts and a rubric evaluator robust to 'empty' jailbreaks. AISafetyLab (context) offers attack components.
