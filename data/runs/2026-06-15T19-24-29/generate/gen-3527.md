# Cloze / Information-Recovery Test for CoT Necessity (from Psycholinguistics)

**Problem:** If a CoT is faithful, hiding parts of it should make the conclusion harder to recover; if it's decorative, the conclusion is recoverable anyway. We lack such a recovery test.

**Direction:** Borrow the cloze procedure (psycholinguistics readability/predictability testing). Mask spans of a model's own CoT and have the same model (or a second model) predict the final answer from the masked trace. If answer-recoverability is unaffected by masking the supposedly load-bearing step, the CoT is unfaithful. Quantify recoverability-vs-masking curves.

**Why it matters:** A simple, intuitive faithfulness probe grounded in a classic comprehension method, complementing ablation-based approaches. Open models only, no training.

**Relevant context:** Faithfulness-by-unlearning/ablating steps (ACL 2025.emnlp-main.504). Cloze procedure measures information dependency in text. CoT monitorability vs verbosity (arXiv:2510.27378).
