# Async Control Slack: How Much Delay Can Monitoring Tolerate?

**Problem:** A late-2025 paper stress-tests asynchronous control measures, where monitoring lags behind agent actions. The relationship between monitoring delay (actions executed before a verdict arrives) and residual attack success is not quantified for accessible setups.

**Direction:** On a small multi-step agentic task, implement asynchronous monitoring with a tunable delay d (agent executes d actions before the monitor's verdict can halt it). Sweep d and measure how many irreversible harmful actions slip through before intervention, producing a delay-vs-safety curve.

**Why it matters:** Real deployments can't always pause for synchronous review. Quantifying how much asynchrony erodes safety tells deployers the maximum tolerable monitoring lag before kill-switches become ineffective — a runtime-controls open problem.
