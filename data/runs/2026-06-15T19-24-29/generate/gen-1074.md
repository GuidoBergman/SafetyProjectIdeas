# Does Homogeneity Make Watermark/Provenance Attribution Harder?

**Problem:** Distinguishing which model produced a piece of text underpins provenance and accountability. If outputs are homogeneous, attribution may be near-impossible — a follow-up that connects the Hivemind effect to a concrete governance capability gap.

**Direction:** Take outputs from several models on shared prompts and train a simple classifier (logistic regression / small MLP on embeddings) to predict the source model. Measure attribution accuracy and relate it to the measured inter-model homogeneity per prompt category. Test whether higher homogeneity drives lower attributability.

**Why it matters:** If monoculture erodes the ability to tell models apart, post-hoc accountability and incident attribution for systemic events fail. Cheap classifier + public outputs.
