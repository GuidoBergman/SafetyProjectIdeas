# Misaligned-Persona Direction Transfer Across Model Families

**Problem:** EM is mediated by a 'misaligned persona' direction that is convergent within a model family. Whether a direction extracted from one open EM organism transfers (predicts/induces misalignment) when applied to a different model in the same family, or a different family, is largely untested.

**Direction:** Vary the convergent-direction experiment (arXiv:2506.11618). Extract the misaligned-persona direction from one public EM organism (difference-of-means on misaligned vs aligned responses), then test whether adding it to a clean sibling model (same family, different size) induces misalignment, and whether a cross-family transplant fails. Quantify induced-misalignment as a function of model relatedness.

**Why it matters:** A transferable misalignment direction would be a cheap detector/attack; a non-transferable one bounds the threat. This is a clean linear-algebra-plus-inference study within a beginner's reach, on public models, with no training required for the steering part.

**Relevant context:** 'Convergent Linear Representations of Emergent Misalignment' (arXiv:2506.11618): a convergent misaligned direction; 'Finding misaligned persona features in open-weight models' (LessWrong) found villain/gaslighting features in Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B.
