# Decoding-Strategy Variation: Does Nucleus vs. Greedy vs. Min-p Change the Hivemind?

**Problem:** Homogeneity is usually reported under one decoding config. Beyond temperature, the choice of decoding algorithm (greedy, top-p, top-k, min-p, beam) may strongly modulate inter-model convergence — an easy-to-control variable with practical deployment relevance that has not been swept.

**Direction:** Vary the decoding algorithm while holding models and prompts fixed. Measure inter-model homogeneity under greedy, top-p, top-k, and min-p sampling on a fixed Infinity-Chat subset, and identify which decoding regimes most reduce shared-output risk without quality loss (checked via the released human annotations).

**Why it matters:** Decoding choice is a free, deploy-time lever; a finding that one regime substantially decorrelates models is immediately actionable infrastructure advice. Pure inference, very feasible, and complements the temperature-sweep idea with a broader knob.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954); decoding-strategy and diversity literature; Infinity-Chat human annotations.
