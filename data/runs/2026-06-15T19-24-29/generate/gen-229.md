# Quantization-Induced Safety Regression Benchmark

**Problem:** Open models are widely deployed in quantized form (4-bit/8-bit GGUF, AWQ, GPTQ) on consumer hardware, but there is no benchmark measuring how much quantization degrades safety/robustness relative to the full-precision model.

**Direction:** Take one open model, evaluate the FP16 and several quantized variants (8-bit, 4-bit) on a fixed jailbreak + over-refusal prompt set, and report safety-score deltas. Build a small reproducible 'quantization safety regression' report and harness others can run on any model + quant.

**Why it matters:** Quantization is the dominant way independent users run open models on a single GPU, yet its safety effects are unmeasured — exactly the kind of diffuse, deployment-relevant question labs deprioritize. Strong counterfactual value; needs no training and runs on one consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate (arXiv:2503.05731) and StrongREJECT (arXiv:2402.10260) provide ready prompt sets/evaluators. Context open problem: 'Bridge the gap between lab-tested adversarial defenses and real-world deployment robustness' — consumer deployment uses quantized weights.
