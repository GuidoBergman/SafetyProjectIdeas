# Jailbreak x Confidence Miscalibration: Are Jailbroken Outputs More Overconfident?

**Problem:** Two failures: jailbreak (refusal bypass) and confidence miscalibration (model expressing high certainty on wrong/harmful content). A jailbroken model may not only produce harmful content but express it with elevated, unwarranted confidence, compounding harm by making bad output more persuasive. The calibration shift under jailbreak is unmeasured on open models.

**Direction:** On open models, elicit verbalized confidence and token-logprob-based confidence for factual sub-claims embedded in benign vs jailbroken prompts. Compare calibration (confidence vs correctness) across conditions. Success criterion: a calibration-shift estimate showing whether jailbreaking inflates confidence on equally-(in)correct claims.

**Why it matters:** Persuasiveness is a key harm multiplier; if jailbreaks compound with overconfidence, harmful outputs become more convincing. Calibration analysis is lab-external, needs only logprobs from open models, consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2406.11668 notes jailbroken models leverage erroneous representations; arXiv:2505.24232 links jailbreaks and hallucination. Calibration-under-jailbreak as the specific compound is novel and measurable via logprobs.
