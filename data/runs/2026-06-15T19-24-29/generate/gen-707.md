# Pre-Registering the Audit: A Template That Resists Gaming, Tested on Real Cards

**Problem:** GovAI's frontier-auditing work warns that audited models may intentionally underperform or that audits can be gamed, but there is little public design work on audit protocols whose structure (pre-registration, randomization, holdout probes) resists gaming — and none tested against real disclosures.

**Direction:** Design a minimal pre-registered audit protocol template (declare checks before seeing the model, include surprise holdout probes, require evidence hashes). Then dry-run it against a couple of public model/system cards to see which template checks the public evidence actually satisfies and where gaming would still slip through. Deliver the template plus a critique of its limits.

**Why it matters:** Gaming-resistant audit design is strategically valuable governance infrastructure for verifying safety claims; prototyping and stress-testing it needs only public documents, making it high counterfactual value for a beginner.
