# Deployment-vs-Eval Drift: A Post-Hoc Consistency Check on Logged Traces

**Problem:** Success requires tracking safety through deployment, not just pre-deployment. A needed result is a method to detect when a model behaves more safely on eval-style prompts than on naturalistic deployment-style prompts.

**Direction:** Construct two matched prompt sets — one in clean benchmark phrasing, one rewritten into messy realistic user language (typos, context, persona) targeting the same safety property. Run an open model on both and measure the safety-rate gap. Deliverable: a 'deployment drift' metric and evidence that benchmark phrasing overstates deployment safety.

**Why it matters:** Pre-deployment evals may not predict deployment behavior — a named open problem. The method needs only prompt engineering and inference, no production logs or lab access, and directly informs whether published safety rates transfer to real use.
