# Emergent Misalignment x Backdoor Persistence

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) broad emergent misalignment from narrow fine-tuning (alignment_training), and (B) trigger-conditioned backdoor behavior (data poisoning). Does narrow fine-tuning that induces broad misalignment make any latent/trigger-conditioned behaviors more reliably elicited, compounding a backdoor with general misalignment?

**Direction:** Use a small open model. Insert a trivial benign trigger via LoRA, then separately run the insecure-code EM fine-tune. Compare trigger-conditioned behavior strength before vs. after the EM fine-tune to test whether emergent misalignment amplifies a pre-existing latent association.

**Why it matters:** If two independently-introduced training-time defects amplify each other, supply-chain fine-tuning risk is super-additive. Small-scale, reproducible on a consumer GPU with public EM code.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. open code; 'Re-Emergent Misalignment' (arXiv 2507.03662) on how narrow fine-tuning erodes safety alignment. Backdoor/poisoning literature on dormant trigger behaviors.
