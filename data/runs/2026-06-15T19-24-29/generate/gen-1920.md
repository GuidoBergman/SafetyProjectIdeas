# Does Constitutional-Style Self-Critique Reduce Emergent Misalignment in Small Models?

**Problem:** Small-model CAI studies (arXiv 2503.17365, 2504.04918) found self-critique improves harmlessness but risks model collapse and helpfulness loss. It is unexplored whether inference-time constitutional self-critique (critique-then-revise without finetuning) can reduce the broad misaligned outputs of an EM model organism.

**Direction:** Wrap an open EM model organism in a self-critique loop: generate an answer, prompt the same model to critique it against a short constitution, then revise. Measure misalignment-eval score for direct vs self-critiqued outputs, and check whether the misaligned model can even critique its own misalignment.

**Why it matters:** If a misaligned model can self-correct at inference using only a prompt-based constitution, that is a cheap, weights-free mitigation usable by downstream deployers. If it cannot critique its own misalignment, that reveals a limit of self-correction defenses. Pure inference, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** How Effective Is Constitutional AI in Small LLMs (arXiv 2503.17365); Constitution or Collapse (arXiv 2504.04918); Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback (arXiv 2212.08073) defines critique-revise. EM model organisms from arXiv 2506.11613.
