# Test-Retest Reliability for Judge Stability Under Resampling

**Problem:** LLM judge verdicts vary across seeds, but oversight papers often report a single run; we lack a psychometric reliability estimate for how reproducible a judge's oversight is.

**Direction:** Apply test-retest and intraclass-correlation reliability from psychometrics. Run a weak judge multiple times (varied seed/temperature/order) over the same debate items and compute test-retest reliability and ICC, then relate low reliability items to error-prone categories. Pure inference, cheap to run many times on a small model.

**Why it matters:** Establishes whether oversight verdicts are even reliable enough to trust before asking if they are valid, a foundational measurement contribution any group can produce and labs rarely report.

**Relevant context:** Test-retest reliability/ICC from psychometrics; inter-rater reliability sensitive to seed variation in LLM judges (arXiv:2412.12509). Kenton et al. 2024 judge setup. No standardized oversight benchmark (context gap).
