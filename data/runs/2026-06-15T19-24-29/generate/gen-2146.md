# Order Effects as a Reproducible Oversight Failure Mode

**Problem:** Turn-order and option-order asymmetries are cited as debate vulnerabilities, but there is no systematic characterization of how large the position-bias failure is across model sizes for oversight tasks specifically.

**Direction:** On QuALITY/extractive-QA judging, run the same weak judge over debate transcripts with arguments and answer options presented in both orders, across 3-4 open model sizes. Quantify position-bias magnitude, whether it shrinks with judge scale, and whether simple debiasing (averaging over both orders) recovers accuracy at acceptable cost.

**Why it matters:** Provides a concrete, measurable systematic-error baseline that any oversight protocol must beat, addressing the 'characterize systematic oversight errors' open problem with a 30-hour, training-free experiment.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. (2024) list turn-order asymmetries among debate vulnerabilities. Known LLM-judge position bias literature (Zheng et al. MT-Bench). Style-over-substance failure modes documented in arXiv:2409.15268.
