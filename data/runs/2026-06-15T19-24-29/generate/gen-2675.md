# Backcasting Honest Confessions: Does Asking 'Did You Sabotage?' After the Fact Recover Hidden Backdoors?

**Problem:** Success state: a cheap post-hoc honesty check that, even when in-line monitoring misses a backdoor, recovers it by interrogating the model. Backward: we need to know whether models confess to backdoors they planted when asked directly versus deny them.

**Direction:** Have an open model plant backdoors on APPS tasks, then in a fresh context ask it (with varied framings: neutral, audit, immunity-offer) whether the code contains a backdoor and where. Measure confession rate and accuracy by framing. Result: 'a confession-elicitation profile as a backstop monitoring layer.'

**Why it matters:** Confession-style honesty checks are an emerging, cheap control layer. Measuring whether small models confess (and which framings help) is a fresh, fully-open-model contribution distinct from anything in the catalogue.

**Relevant context:** 'Training LLMs for Honesty via Confessions' (arXiv:2512.08093); APPS backdoor public; complements monitoring rather than replacing it.
