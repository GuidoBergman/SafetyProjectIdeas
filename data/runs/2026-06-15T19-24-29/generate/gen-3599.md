# Unfaithful CoT x Hallucinated Citations: Do Models Fabricate the Evidence Their Reasoning Claims to Use?

**Problem:** Two honesty failures combine in 'reasoned' factual answers: the CoT asserts it consulted specific sources (faithfulness claim) while those sources are hallucinated (factual fabrication). The reasoning narrative launders a fabricated fact into something that looks evidence-based.

**Direction:** Prompt an open model to answer factual questions 'citing your sources' with CoT. Programmatically check each cited source/quote against a reality oracle (web/citation lookup tool, which this repo has). Measure the rate at which CoT-cited evidence is fabricated, and whether fabricated-citation answers are more confidently wrong.

**Why it matters:** Citation-laden CoT is increasingly trusted by users; quantifying how often the cited scaffolding is invented exposes a deceptive-explanation failure outside any lab. Names: CoT unfaithfulness and citation/factual hallucination.

**Relevant context:** Retrieval sycophancy work notes 'spurious citations'; 'CoT Is Not Explainability' (Barez et al.) argues CoT can be a misleading narrative; legal-hallucination work (arXiv:2401.01301) documents fabricated citations. Repo has CrossRef/Semantic Scholar verification tools.
