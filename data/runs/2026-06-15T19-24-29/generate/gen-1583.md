# Backcasting steering without contrastive labels: can unsupervised directions monitor safety as well as labeled ones?

**Problem:** A scalability success state is monitoring safety concepts for which we LACK clean contrastive labels (most emerging harms). Standard concept vectors need labeled positive/negative pairs. Whether unsupervised directions (e.g. top PCA components of activation differences, or k-means on activations) recover usable safety signal is the gap to making monitoring cheap to scale.

**Direction:** On a small open model, compare a supervised refusal probe against unsupervised directions found from unlabeled mixed harmful/benign activations (PCA / difference-of-means without labels / simple clustering). Measure how much AUROC the unsupervised approach recovers, and which harms it captures for free.

**Why it matters:** Labeling is the bottleneck for scaling safety monitors to new harms. Showing how far label-free directions get is a fresh, high-leverage angle directly serving the 'scale to all safety concepts' open problem, with no training and modest compute.

**Relevant context:** Subfield Open Problem #3 (scale to all safety concepts). Unsupervised steering/difference-of-means relates to CAA (arXiv 2312.06681) and RepE.
