# Epidemiological R0 for Shared-Blind-Spot Propagation Across an LLM Pipeline

**Problem:** When one model's error feeds another (model A drafts, model B reviews, model C summarizes), a shared blind spot can propagate like a contagion. Epidemiology has crisp tools (effective reproduction number, herd-immunity threshold) for whether an error spreads or dies out, never applied to multi-model AI pipelines.

**Direction:** Borrow the SIR/R-effective framing. Simulate a small relay pipeline of open models passing content along, inject seeded errors/false premises, and measure an empirical 'error reproduction number': probability a downstream model fails to catch and instead amplifies the error. Test whether mixing in a behaviorally different model (vaccination/heterogeneity) drops R below 1.

**Why it matters:** Gives a concrete, contagion-style metric for cascading failures across systems sharing a model lineage, and tests heterogeneity as a 'herd immunity' mitigation — directly addressing the cascading-failure open problem with a vivid, lab-independent experiment.

**Relevant context:** Effective reproduction number and herd immunity thresholds; heterogeneity/homophily raise thresholds (arXiv:2112.07538; arXiv:2202.13477). Cascading-failure open problem in context file. Artificial Hivemind shared blind spots (arXiv:2510.22954).
