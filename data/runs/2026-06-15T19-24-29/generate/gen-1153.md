# Quantization-induced failure correlation: do quantized variants of a model break identically?

**Problem:** Deployers run quantized (4-bit/8-bit) versions of open models for cost. If quantization introduces failures that are correlated across the whole quantization family (same prompts break at every bit-width), then the 'diversity' of model size/precision options is illusory for failure coverage.

**Direction:** Take one open model at full precision plus its 8-bit and 4-bit quantizations. On an error-prone prompt set, measure where each precision fails and test whether failures are shared across precisions vs. introduced independently. Repeat for a second model family to check generality.

**Why it matters:** Reveals whether the precision axis of model choice adds real failure diversity or just shared, inherited blind spots — a concrete supply-chain homogeneity question relevant to cheap, widespread deployments. Runs easily on a single consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** Connects model_homogeneity correlated-failure framing to quantization. Uses public quantized checkpoints (GGUF/bitsandbytes); inference only.
