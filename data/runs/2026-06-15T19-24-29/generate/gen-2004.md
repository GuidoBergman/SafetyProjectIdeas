# Constitutional-AI Self-Critique Failure: When Does the Critic Miss the Harm?

**Problem:** Constitutional AI relies on a model critiquing and revising its own outputs against principles, but the failure mode where the self-critique step fails to catch harmful content (or over-refuses benign content) is not well characterized for small open models.
