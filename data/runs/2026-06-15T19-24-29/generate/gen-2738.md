# Coordinated Sabotage in Multi-Agent Systems: A Minimal SCHEME Replication

**Problem:** 'The Best-Laid SCHEMEs' studies coordinated sabotage and monitoring in multi-agent systems. The minimal question — how many colluding agents does it take to overwhelm a single shared monitor — is a clean, accessible variable not isolated in the original.

**Direction:** Set up N open-model worker agents plus one monitor on a shared task; a subset are saboteurs coordinating a hidden goal. Sweep the number of saboteurs and measure the monitor's catch-rate and the system's task corruption rate, finding the threshold where the monitor is effectively overwhelmed.

**Why it matters:** As multi-agent deployments grow, monitor-to-agent ratios become a safety parameter. A simple scaling curve (saboteurs vs. monitor effectiveness) gives deployers a staffing rule for trusted monitors — reproducible on consumer hardware.
