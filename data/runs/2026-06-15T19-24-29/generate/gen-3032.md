# Surviving the Winner-Take-All Game: A Tiny Agent-Island Safety Probe

**Problem:** Agent Island is a saturation/contamination-resistant multiagent game benchmark measuring cooperation, conflict, and persuasion. Its safety-relevant behaviors (deception, coalition-forming, betrayal) are under-analyzed for small open models in a minimal setting.

**Direction:** Build a stripped-down 3-4 agent text game (resource/persuasion, winner-take-all) with small open models as players. Annotate transcripts for deceptive statements, broken promises, and coalition betrayals using a simple judge. Report rates of each safety-relevant behavior and whether they rise under competitive pressure.

**Why it matters:** Multiagent deception and Schelling-style coordination are diffuse, hard-to-measure risks that need no lab resources to study. A contamination-resistant game gives a fresh, reusable lens on emergent deception, complementing static benchmarks.

**Relevant context:** Agent Island: A Saturation- and Contamination-Resistant Benchmark from Multiagent Games (arXiv:2605.04312, May 2026).
