# Mapping which TAIG open problems have public tooling vs. none

**Problem:** Open Problems in Technical AI Governance lists many problems across capacities (access, verification) and targets (data, compute, models), but it is unclear which actually have usable public tools/datasets and which are stuck at the proposal stage, a gap that misallocates beginner researcher effort.

**Direction:** Vary the open-problems taxonomy into a tooling-coverage survey experiment: for each listed problem, systematically search for public datasets/code/benchmarks addressing it, code maturity (none / proposal / prototype / deployed) with a fixed rubric, and produce a coverage map highlighting high-importance/low-tooling cells.

**Why it matters:** Produces a reusable 'where to contribute' map for technical AI governance, valuable to the whole community and especially newcomers. Pure literature/web survey, no lab access, no training.

**Relevant context:** Open Problems in Technical AI Governance (Reuel et al., arXiv:2407.14981; taig.stanford.edu); Interface-EU publication.
