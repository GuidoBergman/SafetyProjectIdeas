# Does Planning Localize to a Few Heads, and Can We Build a Planning Monitor?

**Problem:** Circuit tracing implies planning is mediated by specific components, but for small open models the attention heads/MLPs responsible for forward planning have not been localized, and no cheap runtime 'planning detector' has been built.

**Direction:** On GPT-2/Pythia rhyme-planning prompts, use activation patching to localize which heads/MLP layers carry the future-token plan at the planning position, then train a tiny linear classifier on those components' activations to flag 'the model is planning a specific future token now,' validating on held-out prompts.

**Why it matters:** A lightweight, component-targeted planning monitor is strategically valuable safety infrastructure: it could be repurposed to flag premeditated, non-verbalized behavior. Scoped as a small prototype on open models.

**Relevant context:** Circuit Tracing (2025) localizes planning to newline-token computation. Activation patching for component localization is standard in TransformerLens; the GPT-2 IOI circuit demonstrates head-level localization feasibility.
