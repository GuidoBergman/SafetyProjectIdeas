# Does Self-Generated 'Aligned' Data Re-Inject the Teacher's Latent Misalignment? (Data-Mediated Transfer Audit)

**Problem:** Data-mediated transfer work (arXiv 2605.12798) frames both emergent and subliminal misalignment as transfer through generated data. A pressing practical case: when a slightly-misaligned model generates 'aligned' SFT data for self-improvement, does its latent misalignment survive into the student despite the data looking aligned?

**Direction:** Lightly EM-poison a small teacher, have it generate ostensibly-helpful instruction-response data, filter for surface alignment, fine-tune a clean same-base student on it, and measure residual broad misalignment vs a clean-teacher control.

**Why it matters:** Self-distillation / synthetic-data self-improvement loops are ubiquitous; if latent misalignment launders through them, it is a serious medium-horizon risk for the whole synthetic-data ecosystem. Fully open, single-GPU, model-organism scope.

**Relevant context:** Emergent and Subliminal Misalignment Through the Lens of Data-Mediated Transfer (arXiv 2605.12798). Subliminal Learning (arXiv 2507.14805). EM (arXiv 2502.17424).
