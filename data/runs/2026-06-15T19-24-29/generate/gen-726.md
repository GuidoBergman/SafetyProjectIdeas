# Does the FLI Index correlate with the Stanford Transparency Index? A cross-instrument validity check

**Problem:** Multiple governance scorecards (FLI Safety Index, Stanford FMTI, AI Transparency Atlas) rank overlapping labs, but whether they agree, i.e. measure a shared underlying 'safety/transparency' construct, is untested. Disagreement would undermine all of them.

**Direction:** Vary single-index analysis into a convergent-validity experiment: assemble per-lab scores from 2-3 public indices for the overlapping set of labs, compute rank correlations (Spearman) and per-domain agreement, and identify labs where indices sharply disagree and why.

**Why it matters:** If governance indices don't converge, policymakers can cherry-pick; quantifying their (dis)agreement is essential meta-evaluation. Uses only published scores, trivial compute, beginner statistics.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index Summer/Winter 2025; Stanford Foundation Model Transparency Index; AI Transparency Atlas (arXiv:2512.12443).
