# FaithfulnessVsHelpfulness-Bench: Measuring the Legibility Tax on Open Models

**Problem:** The 'legibility tax' — accuracy lost when outputs are made checkable — has been shown in training setups, but there is no inference-only benchmark measuring how much prompting an open model to be 'checkable/legible' costs its accuracy.

**Direction:** Compare an open model's accuracy and human/judge-checkability under a plain prompt vs an explicit 'write so a weaker checker can verify each step' prompt on verifiable tasks, and report the accuracy-vs-checkability tradeoff. Deliver prompts, a checkability rubric, and the tradeoff curve.

**Why it matters:** Quantifying the legibility tax at inference time tells practitioners the price of monitorable outputs without any training, directly extending prover-verifier findings into a reusable, lab-independent measurement.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2407.13692 prover-verifier games show optimizing only for correctness reduces legibility; arXiv 2602.23248 'Mitigating Legibility Tax with Decoupled Prover-Verifier Games' names the legibility tax.
