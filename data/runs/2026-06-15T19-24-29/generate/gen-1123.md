# HivemindHarm-Index: a single deployable metric for an LLM panel's correlated-failure risk

**Problem:** There is no compact, standardized score a deployer can compute to summarize how risky their specific set of models is from a correlated-failure standpoint. Researchers report many ad-hoc similarity numbers; deployers need one actionable index.

**Direction:** Define a composite 'Correlated-Failure Risk Index' combining (1) chance-adjusted error agreement, (2) joint-failure probability on a safety probe set, and (3) effective number of independent models. Implement as a tool that ingests a panel's outputs and emits the index plus the dominant shared-failure cluster. Calibrate on several open-model panels and show it discriminates same-family from mixed panels.

**Why it matters:** Turns abstract homogeneity research into a procurement/governance-ready metric for systemic risk — exactly what EU AI Act systemic-risk monitoring needs and what no lab provides for its competitors' stacks. Pure analysis, high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** Synthesizes CAPA (arXiv:2502.04313), Hivemind homogeneity, and effective-independence ideas into a governance metric; addresses the systemic-risk monitoring open problem.
