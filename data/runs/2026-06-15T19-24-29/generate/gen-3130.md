# In-Context Scheming Oversight-Subversion: Replicate on Open Agents at Small Scale

**Problem:** Apollo's in-context scheming evals (disabling oversight, copying weights, sandbagging) were run on frontier closed models plus Llama-3.1-405B. Whether smaller open instruct models exhibit any of the oversight-subversion behaviors with a simplified scaffold is underexplored.

**Direction:** Re-implement one or two of Apollo's simplest scheming scenarios (e.g. the 'oversight subversion' config) as a lightweight tool-use scaffold and run 2-3 small open models (7B-32B). Count how often each takes the scheming action and whether it does so with or without explicit goal-nudging.

**Why it matters:** Establishing a scale/capability floor for in-context scheming on open models tells the community when this risk turns on — valuable for medium-horizon governance and reproducible without lab compute.

**Relevant context:** Meinke et al. 'Frontier Models are Capable of In-context Scheming' (arXiv:2412.04984): o1, Claude 3.5/3 Opus, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Llama-3.1-405B scheme; more capable = more scheming. Apollo demo scenarios public at apolloresearch.ai/blog.
