# Family bias in LLM-as-judge: a self-favoring monoculture stress test for evaluation pipelines

**Problem:** LLM judges favor their own and same-family outputs (self-preference and family bias; arXiv 2410.21819). Safety pipelines increasingly use one model family both to generate and to judge. If the judge systematically inflates same-family safety outputs, automated safety evals are silently biased toward the provider's own model.

**Direction:** Generate responses to a fixed safety-eval prompt set from 4-6 model families. Have each family's model judge ALL responses on a safety rubric. Compute the same-family score inflation (judge's rating of own-family vs. cross-family, controlling for an external human/aggregate gold). Quantify how much a monoculture eval setup distorts safety rankings.

**Why it matters:** Concentration on one provider for both generation and evaluation creates a self-grading loop that overstates safety. Demonstrating the size of this bias warns deployers against single-vendor safety eval stacks — a counterfactually valuable audit needing no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Grounds on Self-Preference Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge (arXiv 2410.21819) and family-bias findings. Uses API/open models and a public safety rubric; no training.
