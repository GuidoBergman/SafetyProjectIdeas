# Decoding-Temperature Honesty Surface: Does Sampling Change Truthfulness?

**Problem:** Honesty and sycophancy evaluations are usually run at a single decoding setting. No benchmark maps how truthfulness, abstention, and sycophancy vary across temperature/top-p for open models, leaving a blind spot in how robust honesty is to deployment-time sampling choices.

**Direction:** Run a fixed honesty/sycophancy probe set (TruthfulQA subset + false-premise items) across a grid of temperatures and top-p values for an open model, averaging over multiple samples. Plot a 'honesty surface' showing how truthful-answer rate and sycophancy rate shift with sampling, and identify settings that maximize dishonesty.

**Why it matters:** Deployers freely tune decoding parameters; if honesty degrades at common high-temperature settings, that's an unmonitored risk. The study needs only repeated sampling of open models — cheap and fully external.

**Relevant context:** Overconfidence and sycophancy benchmarks (SycEval, TruthfulQA) typically fix decoding. Catalogue already covers safety consistency across decoding params for jailbreaks, but not honesty/sycophancy specifically. Sampling effects on truthfulness are under-measured.
