# Where the Model Card Hides the Risk: Locating the Disclosure Break in the Misuse Causal Chain

**Problem:** Misuse risk chain: 'model has dangerous capability' -> 'capability is disclosed in model/system card' -> 'auditors/regulators assess it' -> 'deployment decision constrained'. Recent analysis found Safety Evaluation and Out-of-Scope use cases are disclosed superficially or below 50% of the time, breaking the second link before assessment can happen.

**Direction:** Build a small rubric (from EU AI Act Annex IV + FLI Index indicators) and manually score the system cards of 6-8 frontier models on presence/depth of safety-critical disclosures. Identify which specific chain-relevant fields (e.g., dangerous-capability eval results, refusal robustness) are systematically omitted, and correlate omissions with model capability tier.

**Why it matters:** Pinpointing exactly which disclosure is missing converts a fuzzy 'transparency is bad' complaint into a targeted list of mandatory fields. If the disclosure link is broken, downstream audit and regulation cannot constrain deployment. No training, public documents only, strong counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2512.12443 AI Transparency Atlas (947 section names across frontier cards; Safety Eval weighted 25%); FLI AI Safety Index Summer 2025; EU AI Act Annex IV; Stanford Transparency Index.
