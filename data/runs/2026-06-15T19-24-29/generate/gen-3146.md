# Eval-Awareness Cues: Replicate the Detection Trigger Across Prompt Phrasings

**Problem:** Apollo finds models verbalize 'I'm being evaluated' at high rates in some scenario versions. Which textual features of a scenario trigger eval-awareness verbalization, and whether those features generalize across open models, is underexplored.

**Direction:** Systematically vary surface cues in a scheming scenario (presence of 'test', round numbers, unrealistic setups, explicit 'developer' mentions) and measure how each cue changes the rate at which each of several open models verbalizes eval-awareness in its CoT.
