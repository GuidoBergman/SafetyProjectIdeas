# A Public 'Structured Access' Inventory: What Researchers Can Actually Get

**Problem:** Technical governance leans on 'structured access' (APIs, sandboxes, researcher programs) for external scrutiny, but there is no consolidated, current map of what access each frontier provider actually offers, on what terms, and with what safety-research carve-outs.

**Direction:** Systematically document each major provider's public access offerings (researcher access programs, logprobs, rate-limited probing, red-team programs, ToS restrictions on safety research). Code them on dimensions like cost, gatekeeping, and whether safety/adversarial research is permitted. Produce a comparison table and identify where access barriers block external accountability.

**Why it matters:** External verification of safety claims (an open problem) is gated by access terms; mapping the landscape is foundational governance infrastructure that any independent researcher can build and maintain from public information.

**Relevant context:** 'Open Problems in Technical AI Governance' arXiv:2407.14981 (structured access); GovAI 'Frontier AI Auditing' on access requirements; EU GPAI external-evaluation provisions.
