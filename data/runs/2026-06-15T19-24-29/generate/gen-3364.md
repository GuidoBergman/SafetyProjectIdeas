# An Open CoT-Monitor Harness: How Reliably Can a Cheap LLM Judge Flag Unfaithfulness?

**Problem:** CoT monitoring is proposed as a safety strategy, but the reliability of a cheap LLM acting as the monitor — its false-positive/false-negative rates at detecting known unfaithful traces — is not well quantified in an open, reusable harness.

**Direction:** Assemble a labeled set of faithful vs. unfaithful CoT traces (using cue-flip ground truth: cases where the answer changed due to a hidden cue the CoT didn't mention). Have a small open 'monitor' model judge each trace as faithful/unfaithful and compute precision/recall/ROC. Release the harness.

**Why it matters:** Quantifying monitor reliability is prerequisite to trusting CoT monitoring at all; an open harness is exactly the strategically valuable monitoring infrastructure non-lab researchers should build.

**Relevant context:** 'Monitoring Reasoning Models for Misbehavior and the Risks of Promoting Obfuscation' (arXiv 2503.11926) studies CoT monitors. Cue-flip ground-truth labeling from Arcuschin et al. (2025) / arXiv 2505.05410. Petri (Anthropic) is noted as available for honesty probing. The reusable precision/recall harness is the contribution.
