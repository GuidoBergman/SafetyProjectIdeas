# Can a steering vector for 'honesty' survive an adversarial paraphrase? A robustness slice of concept steering

**Problem:** Concept steering (Beaglehole et al.) raises the open problem: can attackers craft inputs that neutralize a steering vector? Decompose the broad robustness question to ONE measurable slice: does an anti-sycophancy / honesty steering vector still reduce sycophancy when the user pressure is adversarially paraphrased to evade it?

**Direction:** Take the concept-steering robustness open problem and attack a single slice using adversarial-robustness method (paraphrase attacks). On a small open model, extract an honesty/anti-sycophancy steering vector via contrastive activation addition. Build a battery of sycophancy-eliciting prompts plus automatically paraphrased adversarial variants, and measure steering effectiveness (sycophancy rate) on clean vs adversarial prompts.

**Why it matters:** Bridges steering and robustness with a cheap, fully open-source experiment. If steering vectors are brittle to trivial paraphrase, that's a key caveat for anyone proposing them as a deployment safety lever — a counterfactual finding doable without lab resources. Single GPU, no training.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. Science 2026 (concept vectors, robustness open problem); contrastive activation addition / RepE open implementations; Claude Opus 4.5 sycophancy-under-pressure finding; 'Concept activation vectors: a unifying view and adversarial attacks' (2509.22755).
