# Homogeneity in Reasoning Models vs. Base Models on Open-Ended Queries

**Problem:** Infinity-Chat was run mainly on standard chat LLMs. It is unknown whether explicit chain-of-thought/reasoning models (which sample diverse reasoning paths internally) produce MORE diverse final answers, or whether the reasoning step collapses to the same conclusions, making them just as homogeneous.

**Direction:** Replicate the Infinity-Chat intra/inter-model homogeneity measurement but contrast matched pairs of reasoning vs non-reasoning open models (e.g. a reasoning-tuned small model vs its base sibling). Hold prompts and sampling fixed; measure final-answer similarity within and across the two regimes on a subset of Infinity-Chat queries.

**Why it matters:** Reasoning models are increasingly deployed in high-stakes pipelines. If reasoning does not break homogeneity, the systemic-risk argument extends to the newest generation; if it does, diversity-promotion via reasoning is a cheap mitigation. Directly addresses the context's flagged follow-up on reasoning-model homogeneity.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954); Infinity-Chat benchmark; literature on CoT sampling diversity and temperature (T2 of Thoughts, arXiv:2405.14075).
