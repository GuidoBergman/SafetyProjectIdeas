# Formal-Methods Invariant Checking for Refusal Behavior

**Problem:** After alignment training, a model should satisfy invariants like 'never gives bomb-making steps regardless of phrasing', but these are validated only by spot-checks, not by systematic invariant checking as in formal verification.

**Direction:** Import the formal-methods notion of invariants plus bounded model checking. Specify a small set of safety invariants as input-transformation families (the same harmful intent expressed via translation, roleplay wrapper, encoding) and exhaustively check, over a bounded transformation grammar, whether an open aligned model's refusal invariant holds. Report invariant-violation coverage per transformation class.

**Why it matters:** Moves refusal evaluation from anecdotal red-teaming toward systematic, reproducible invariant coverage — a piece of monitoring infrastructure valuable to the open community. Inference-only, bounded transformation grammar runnable on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Metamorphic/formal testing fills the oracle gap for LLMs (arXiv:2312.06056, arXiv:2511.02108); multi-turn jailbreak survival analysis shows refusal invariants degrade under transformation pressure (arXiv:2510.02712). Open Problems in RLHF notes distributional-shift coverage gaps (Alignment Forum).
