# Sycophancy Under Pressure in Non-English Conversations

**Problem:** Sycophancy benchmarks like ELEPHANT and SYCON-Bench are predominantly English. It is unknown whether the same model flips its stance under user pressure at different rates depending on the conversation language.

**Direction:** Translate a 30-50 item subset of SYCON-Bench controversial topics into 3-4 languages (using a translation model + spot checks) and run the multi-turn flip protocol on one mid-size open model. Compare flip rates and capitulation speed across languages.

**Why it matters:** Multilingual deployment is universal; a model that resists flattery in English but caves in Spanish or Hindi is a hidden honesty failure. This needs no proprietary data and targets a fuzzy-reward problem labs underweight outside English.

**Relevant context:** SYCON-Bench (arXiv:2505.23840) and BenSyc (arXiv:2606.10061, Bengali sycophancy) show conversational sycophancy varies; BenSyc indicates language-specific differences. Builds on the context file's 'test sycophancy under social pressure across model families' hint.
