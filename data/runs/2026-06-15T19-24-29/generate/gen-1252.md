# SAE Feature Steering Side-Effects: Single-Feature Ablation Collateral

**Problem:** SAE-based 'feature steering' for safety (clamp a harmful feature off) is pitched as surgical. But ablating one SAE latent may degrade unrelated capabilities due to absorption/superposition. The collateral damage of single-feature ablation is rarely measured against a control.

**Direction:** Using a pretrained GPT-2-small SAE, pick several interpreted features (including a safety-relevant one). For each, clamp it to zero during generation and measure (a) the intended behavioral change and (b) collateral effects: perplexity on neutral text and accuracy drop on a small unrelated task. Compare against clamping a random/dead latent as control. Produce a precision-vs-collateral scatter across features.

**Why it matters:** Feature steering is proposed as a safety lever. Quantifying how often 'surgical' edits cause diffuse damage tells the community whether SAE steering is deployment-safe — a concrete, reproducible result requiring only inference on a released SAE and model.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic feature-steering demos (Golden Gate Claude lineage); feature absorption/superposition (Chanin et al. 2024) predicts collateral leakage; SAELens provides clamping hooks. GPT-2-small SAEs public.
