# CoT-Monitorability Score for Small Open Reasoning Models

**Problem:** CoT monitoring is being adopted at frontier labs as a safeguard, but monitorability (faithfulness + verbosity) varies widely and is rarely measured on the small open reasoning models that outside researchers can study.

**Direction:** On a small open reasoning model, run a battery where the model is given inputs containing decision-relevant cues (e.g. a hint that biases the answer) and measure how often the CoT actually verbalizes using the cue (faithfulness) and how complete the trace is (verbosity). Produce a per-cue monitorability score and rank which cue types are silently used.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies how much we can trust CoT as a monitoring signal — a foundation for the entire CoT-monitoring safety bet, done on accessible open models.

**Relevant context:** Measuring Chain-of-Thought Monitorability Through Faithfulness and Verbosity (arXiv:2510.27378); Unfaithful Reasoning Can Fool CoT Monitoring (Alignment Forum); unfaithfulness rates 0.04%-13% across frontier models.
