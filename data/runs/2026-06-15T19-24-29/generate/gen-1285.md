# Homogeneity Breaks Constitutional-AI Cross-Critique: The Critic Shares the Author's Blind Spots

**Problem:** Failure mode A: harmful/flawed generation. Failure mode B: homogeneity between generator and critic in self-critique / Constitutional-AI-style revision. If the critiquing model shares the generator's blind spots, the critique step fails exactly on the harms both miss — a safety mechanism nullified by homogeneity.

**Direction:** Generate borderline outputs with model A, then have models B,C,D (varying homogeneity to A) critique/revise them against a fixed rubric. Measure catch-rate of injected flaws as a function of generator-critic output similarity. Show catch-rate drops as homogeneity rises; identify which harm categories are jointly missed.

**Why it matters:** Self-critique and AI-feedback are cornerstone alignment techniques. Quantifying that a homogeneous critic adds little safety value (and which harms slip through) is a practical, lab-independent contribution to oversight design.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind shared blind spots; Constitutional AI / self-critique literature; correlated errors (arXiv 2506.07962).
