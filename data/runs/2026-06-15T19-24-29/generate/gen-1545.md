# Surgical Sycophancy Correction: Replicating 'A Few Bad Neurons' on Open Models

**Problem:** Recent work isolated and surgically corrected sycophancy via a small set of neurons. Whether this localized fix generalizes across sycophancy types, and whether it degrades unrelated capabilities, needs independent follow-up on open models.

**Direction:** Identify candidate sycophancy-related directions/neurons in a small open chat model using a sycophancy probe dataset, ablate them, and measure (a) reduction in sycophantic agreement on held-out sycophancy probes vs. (b) collateral damage on general benchmarks (perplexity, simple QA). Test generalization across distinct sycophancy elicitation styles.

**Why it matters:** Sycophancy is a fuzzy-reward alignment failure that erodes oversight. A reproducible, surgical, training-free correction with measured side effects is valuable safety infrastructure independent of labs.

**Relevant context:** 'A Few Bad Neurons: Isolating and Surgically Correcting Sycophancy' (arXiv 2601.18939) isolates sycophancy in a few neurons. Open chat models (e.g. Qwen, Llama-3-8B-Instruct quantized) run on a single consumer GPU.
