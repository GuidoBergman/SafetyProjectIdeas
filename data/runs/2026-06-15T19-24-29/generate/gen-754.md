# Self-attestation vs independent verification: re-testing the Ontario Auditor General finding for AI vendors

**Problem:** The Ontario Auditor General found public-sector AI procurement relied heavily on vendor self-attestation rather than independent verification. Whether this pattern generalizes across public AI procurement is unknown.

**Direction:** Follow up by collecting a sample of public-sector AI procurement / RFP documents (many are publicly posted) across jurisdictions and coding each on whether safety/risk claims are independently verified or self-attested. Quantify the prevalence of self-attestation and which risk categories most lack independent checks.

**Why it matters:** Public-sector procurement is a powerful, underused governance lever; documenting reliance on self-attestation shows where requiring third-party verification would most improve safety. Pure public-document analysis.

**Relevant context:** Ontario Auditor General 2025 AI-vendor findings; Open Problem: third-party-auditable verification methods; PwC 2025 finding that manual governance docs are treated as unreliable.
