# Do concept vectors for the same safety concept agree across different open model families?

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. report cross-model transfer. But whether the 'refusal' or 'deception' concept occupies a CONSISTENT relative geometry across architecturally different model families (Llama vs. Qwen vs. Mistral vs. Gemma) is unclear. If safety concepts are universal, their probes might be partially portable; if family-specific, every model needs its own.

**Direction:** Extract the same ~3 safety concept vectors on 3-4 open model families of comparable size. Using a shared probing/eval set, measure (a) whether a probe trained on one family detects the concept in another's activations (where comparable, e.g. via the unembedding or shared eval behavior), and (b) behavioral steering transferability where direct transplant is possible. Report a cross-family agreement matrix.

**Why it matters:** Universality (or not) of safety directions determines whether monitoring tools generalize across the open-model ecosystem or must be rebuilt per model — a key scalability question for non-lab safety tooling.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. cross-model transfer (Science 2026). CAA across Llama variants (arxiv 2312.06681). RepE open implementations.
