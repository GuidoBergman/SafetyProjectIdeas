# The Probe-Generalization Link: Do Eval-Awareness Probes Survive Paraphrase?

**Problem:** Mitigation chain for eval-awareness: train a linear probe to detect 'model thinks it's being tested' -> use probe to flag/correct distorted evals -> trust corrected scores. The fragile link is probe generalization: a probe fit on one eval style may fail on paraphrased or unseen eval contexts, silently reintroducing the awareness bias.

**Direction:** Fit a linear probe on hidden states of a small open model for 'eval vs deployment' framing using one prompt template, then test it on held-out paraphrased templates and a different task domain. Report cross-template/cross-domain AUC drop and whether averaging probes across templates restores generalization.

**Why it matters:** Probe-based eval-validity correction is only useful if probes generalize; this stress-tests the link cheaply. Pure inference + logistic regression on open weights, ideal counterfactual-value work.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2507.01786 'Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness' and the open-weights scaling work report linear probes classify eval-vs-deployment transcripts with high accuracy; generalization across templates is not characterized.
