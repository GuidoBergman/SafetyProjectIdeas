# WeakVerifierEnsemble-Probe: Does Combining Cheap Judges Recover a Strong Overseer?

**Problem:** Weaver shows weighted ensembles of weak verifiers can approach strong verification, but there is no small reproducible benchmark measuring how oversight accuracy scales with the number/diversity of weak open judges on a fixed task.

**Direction:** Take 3-5 small open models as weak verifiers, evaluate simple aggregation rules (majority, confidence-weighted) on a fixed labeled QA set, and report the accuracy-vs-ensemble-size curve plus the diversity needed to beat the best single judge. Deliver the aggregation harness.

**Why it matters:** Whether cheap weak overseers can be composed into a reliable one is central to recursive oversight and uniquely doable outside labs using only small open models; quantifying it is a clear tooling gap.
