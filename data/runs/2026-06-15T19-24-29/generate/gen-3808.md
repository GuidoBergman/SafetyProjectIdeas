# Mutation Testing of Agent Guardrails

**Problem:** When teams write safety guardrails (system prompts, refusal rules, tool allowlists) for an agent, they have no way to know how good those guardrails actually are at catching violations — coverage is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Import mutation testing. Treat unsafe-action attempts as the 'test suite' and the guardrail as the 'program'. Programmatically generate 'mutant' attack tasks (small perturbations of known unsafe requests) and measure the guardrail's 'mutation score' = fraction of mutants caught. A low score reveals brittle guardrails. Run on an open agent harness with a known unsafe-task seed set.

**Why it matters:** Gives builders an empirical guardrail-quality metric instead of vibes, and surfaces blind spots. Pure evaluation work, no training, no lab data.

**Relevant context:** Meta's ACH system (engineering.fb.com 2025/09; InfoQ 2026/01) uses LLM-driven mutation testing to generate faults and measure whether tests/guardrails catch them, improving compliance coverage. Mutation testing for agentic safety guardrails specifically is unexplored.
