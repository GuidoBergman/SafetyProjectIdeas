# Obfuscation Budget: How Hard Is It to Hide from a Linear Probe?

**Problem:** Obfuscated-activation attacks can bypass latent-space defenses, but the 'cost' of obfuscation — how much the attacker must distort the prompt or degrade output to evade a probe — has not been quantified for simple, reproducible attacks accessible to non-experts.

**Direction:** Take a trained linear refusal/jailbreak probe on an open model and apply increasing levels of simple prompt obfuscation (synonym swaps, base64 fragments, leetspeak, spacing) to known jailbreaks. Measure the tradeoff between probe-evasion rate and StrongREJECT response quality, producing an 'obfuscation cost curve.'

**Why it matters:** Establishes whether cheap probe defenses force attackers into a quality/evasion tradeoff, informing the open problem of probe-based detection scaling. Needs only a small probe and inference — a self-contained, training-free study with clear success criteria.

**Relevant context:** Obfuscated Activations Bypass LLM Latent-Space Defenses (arXiv:2412.09565): activations can be obfuscated to evade probes. StrongREJECT (arXiv:2402.10260) measures response quality. The evasion-vs-quality cost of simple obfuscations against a probe is unquantified.
