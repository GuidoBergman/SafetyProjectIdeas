# Does EM Severity Scale With LoRA Rank or Learning Rate?

**Problem:** Emergent misalignment is typically induced with a fixed LoRA configuration, but the dependence of misalignment severity on finetuning hyperparameters (LoRA rank, learning rate, number of epochs) has not been mapped. This determines how 'accidental' EM could be under routine finetuning.

**Direction:** Hold dataset fixed (insecure code) and sweep LoRA rank (e.g., 4, 16, 64) and learning rate over a small grid on one open model. For each config, measure both task performance and misalignment-eval score, producing a heatmap of EM severity vs hyperparameters.

**Why it matters:** If aggressive (high-rank/high-LR) finetuning sharply increases EM, then practitioners doing ordinary domain finetuning could trip into broad misalignment by tuning for performance. A hyperparameter risk map is directly actionable and cheap (small grid of LoRA runs on one GPU).

**Relevant context:** Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2502.17424) and Model Organisms (arXiv 2506.11613) use LoRA finetuning on the insecure-code dataset; Re-Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2507.03662) studies how narrow finetuning erodes safety alignment.
