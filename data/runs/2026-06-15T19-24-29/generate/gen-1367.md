# Steering a vision-language model with a text-derived safety vector

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. flag extending concept steering to multimodal models as open. A simple, feasible slice is testing whether a refusal/harm direction extracted from TEXT activations also steers/probes when the harmful content arrives via an IMAGE in a VLM.

**Direction:** Use a small open VLM (e.g. LLaVA-style or Qwen2-VL-2B). Extract a harm/refusal direction from text-only contrastive pairs, then test whether it steers refusal and probes harm when the same requests are posed with images. Measure cross-modal steering/probe performance on a held-out image+text eval.

**Why it matters:** Multimodal harms (e.g. harmful images) are a growing, under-defended surface; cross-modal vector reuse would be a cheap monitoring win outside labs. Single consumer GPU with a small VLM.

**Relevant context:** Open Problem: extend concept steering to vision/multimodal (Beaglehole et al.). 'Steering to Say No: Configurable Refusal in VLMs' (arXiv:2602.07013). Small open VLMs run on one GPU.
