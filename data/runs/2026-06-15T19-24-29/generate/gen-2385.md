# Backcasting Cost-Aware Oversight: Accuracy Per Token Across Protocols

**Problem:** Success state: oversight deployed at scale needs protocols whose reliability gains justify their compute cost. We need an accuracy-per-token comparison of consultancy vs debate vs interactive debate, since a costly protocol that barely helps will not be adopted.

**Direction:** On the open QuALITY harness, log token counts for each protocol condition and plot judge accuracy against total tokens spent (the oversight 'cost curve') for a small open model. Identify the Pareto frontier: which protocol gives the most reliability per unit compute, and at what budget debate overtakes consultancy.

**Why it matters:** Whether scalable oversight is actually adopted depends on its cost-effectiveness, not just peak accuracy. A clean accuracy-per-token frontier across protocols is a practical, lab-independent contribution that protocol designers can act on directly.

**Relevant context:** Debate and consultancy differ greatly in token cost (Kenton et al. 2024 arXiv:2407.04622; Khan et al. 2024 arXiv:2402.06782). Public discussions note debate's expense, but explicit accuracy-per-token Pareto comparisons are rare. Open harness enables token logging.
