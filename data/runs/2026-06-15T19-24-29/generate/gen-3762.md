# Off-Task Action Detector Benchmark for Computer-Use Agents

**Problem:** Recent work shows computer-use agents drift off-task even from benign inputs, and detecting/correcting misaligned actions is hard. There is no small, open benchmark of labeled off-task vs. on-task action steps for evaluating cheap detectors.

**Direction:** Curate a labeled dataset of ~300 single-step agent actions (drawn from public computer-use/web-agent trajectories) annotated on-task vs. off-task, then benchmark lightweight detectors: an LLM-judge prompt, a heuristic 'action matches stated subgoal' check, and a similarity-to-goal embedding score. Report precision/recall and false-alarm cost.

**Why it matters:** Off-task detection is the runtime safeguard most deployments need; a public labeled set + detector baselines is reusable infrastructure. Uses public trajectories + API/embedding calls only.

**Relevant context:** 'When Actions Go Off-Task: Detecting and Correcting Misaligned Actions in Computer-Use Agents' (arXiv 2602.08995). 'When Benign Inputs Lead to Severe Harms' (arXiv 2602.08235). 'Preemptive Detection and Correction of Misaligned Actions' (arXiv 2407.11843).
