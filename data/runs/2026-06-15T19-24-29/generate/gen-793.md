# Security-Framing vs Innovation-Framing: A Discourse Benchmark for National AI Institutes

**Problem:** UK AISI rebranded to 'AI Security Institute' while US AISI became CAISI (innovation framing). It is an open follow-up whether these framings correlate with different stated priorities, but there is no measurement instrument comparing institute output discourse.

**Direction:** Assemble a corpus of public outputs (mission statements, reports, blog posts) from 4-6 national AI safety/security institutes, define a small coded scheme (safety/security/innovation/capability emphasis), and use an LLM-assisted, human-validated classifier to score sentence-level emphasis. Produce a framing-profile benchmark and test whether rebrandings track measurable discourse shifts over time.

**Why it matters:** Directly follows the FR68-flagged surprising result on divergent national framings and creates a reusable measurement for international-coordination researchers, with zero model training and only public text.

**Relevant context:** Subfield context: UK AISI->AI Security Institute, US AISI->CAISI rebrandings; International Network of AI Safety Institutes; International AI Safety Report 2026 (arXiv 2602.21012).
