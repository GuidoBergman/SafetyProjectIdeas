# Sampling Temperature and Sycophancy / Faithfulness Trade-off

**Problem:** Faithfulness and sycophancy benchmarks typically fix decoding to greedy or a single temperature. Whether honesty behaviors are stable across decoding parameters — and whether higher temperature increases or decreases sycophantic capitulation — is unknown.

**Direction:** Sweep temperature (0.0 to 1.2) and top-p on an open model and re-run a sycophancy challenge battery and a hint-verbalization battery. Measure how capitulation rate and verbalization rate vary with decoding parameters, with confidence intervals from repeated samples.

**Why it matters:** If a deployer's choice of temperature materially changes measured honesty, then single-config evals are misleading and safety claims are fragile. A cheap, statistics-friendly experiment well-suited to the participant skill set.

**Relevant context:** Sharma et al. 2023 sycophancy protocols; Chen et al. 2025 verbalization metric. Builds on the established practice of testing robustness across decoding parameters but applies it specifically to honesty/faithfulness metrics.
