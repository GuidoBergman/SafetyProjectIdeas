# EvalAwareSteer: Detecting and Measuring Evaluation-Awareness Directions

**Problem:** Models may behave differently when they detect they are being evaluated, a long-horizon safety concern. There is no small benchmark measuring whether an 'I am being evaluated' direction exists and how steering along it changes behavior.

**Direction:** Construct contrastive pairs of 'clearly an eval' vs 'clearly deployment' prompts, extract an evaluation-awareness CAA vector on an open model, and measure (a) probe accuracy for eval-detection and (b) how steering along the vector shifts safety-relevant responses. Report whether suppressing the direction makes eval and deployment behavior converge.

**Why it matters:** Evaluation-gaming is a core sandbagging/deception threat that frontier evals struggle to rule out. Probing it via concept vectors on open models is a tractable, lab-independent first study of a high-value model-organism phenomenon.

**Relevant context:** Arxiv 2510.20487 'Steering Evaluation-Aware Language Models to Act Like They Are Deployed' shows steering can flip eval-vs-deploy behavior. CAA methodology and open models suffice; no benchmark packages this for beginners.
