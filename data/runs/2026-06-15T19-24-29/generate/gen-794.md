# A Falsifiability Benchmark for Frontier Safety Commitments

**Problem:** Many safety commitments cannot be checked by an outsider because they reference internal processes or unspecified evidence. There is no standardized score for how falsifiable (externally checkable) each commitment is.

**Direction:** Define a falsifiability rubric (e.g. checkable from public evidence / checkable only with structured access / checkable only internally / not checkable at all) and apply it clause-by-clause to published policies. Output a per-lab falsifiability index and a list of the highest-leverage commitments that could be made externally verifiable with minimal disclosure.

**Why it matters:** Third-party auditability of safety claims is the flagship governance open problem; a falsifiability metric tells regulators exactly which commitments are currently unenforceable in principle. Pure public-document analysis.

**Relevant context:** 'Third-party compliance reviews for frontier AI safety frameworks' (arXiv 2505.01643); ASPIRE external-scrutiny framework (arXiv 2311.14711); structured access paradigm (arXiv 2201.05159).
