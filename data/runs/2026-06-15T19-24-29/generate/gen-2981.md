# Difficulty-Calibrated Sandbagging Signature via Accuracy-Confidence Mismatch

**Problem:** A success state requires catching sandbagging without weight access. A needed result is a black-box behavioral signature: an honest model's confidence tracks item difficulty, while a sandbagger shows a confidence/accuracy mismatch on the suppressed category.

**Direction:** Elicit verbalized confidence per item from an open model on easy/hard items in a target vs. control category, with and without an induced sandbagging prompt. Test whether the sandbagger shows abnormally high confidence yet low accuracy (or vice versa) on the suppressed category. Deliverable: a calibration-mismatch detector and its detection rate.

**Why it matters:** A purely behavioral sandbagging signal complements weight-noise methods and needs no internal access at all — usable on APIs. Inference-only, open models, targets the deceptive-model long-horizon threat. Fresh complement to existing IRT person-fit sandbagging work.

**Relevant context:** Tice et al. (arXiv 2508.00943) on sandbagging trade-offs; van der Weij et al. sandbagging definitions; calibration/confidence-elicitation literature shows honest models are difficulty-calibrated. Context file lists sandbagging detection as open.
