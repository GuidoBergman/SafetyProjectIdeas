Failure mode B: there is no harmonized eval methodology. Together, the same model can pass one nation's bar and fail another's, enabling jurisdiction-shopping — a compound failure of international coordination.

Map disagreement onto a simple jurisdiction-shopping incentive. Mostly analysis with a light empirical scoring component.
