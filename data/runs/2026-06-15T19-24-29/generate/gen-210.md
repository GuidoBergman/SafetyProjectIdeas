# Deepening Alignment via Data Augmentation: Replicating the Qi et al. Defense on a New Model

**Problem:** Qi et al. proposed deepening safety by augmenting alignment data with examples that recover from a harmful prefix, improving robustness to prefilling. Their defense was demonstrated on specific models; whether the SAME lightweight data-augmentation defense transfers to a different small open model and how much prefilling robustness it buys is unverified externally.

**Direction:** Replicate the safety-recovery data-augmentation idea: LoRA-fine-tune a small open instruct model on a few hundred (harmful-prefix -> refusal/recovery) examples, then measure prefilling-attack ASR before vs after. Twist: a different model family and a minimal augmentation budget. Report the robustness gain and any benign over-refusal cost.

**Why it matters:** Reproducing a known defense on a new model with tiny compute tests its portability and gives small actors a recipe to harden open models against prefilling, an externally valuable, deployment-relevant result. LoRA + small model fits one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. 2024 (arXiv:2406.05946): augmenting with safety-recovery examples deepens alignment and improves robustness to prefilling/decoding attacks. LoRA tooling on consumer GPUs.
