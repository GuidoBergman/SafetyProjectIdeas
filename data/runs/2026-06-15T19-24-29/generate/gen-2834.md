# Image-Driven Context-Injection Jailbreak Replicated Across Open VLM Families

**Problem:** Visual context-injection jailbreaks were demonstrated on specific multimodal LLMs. Their transferability across open VLM families and the conditions under which images (vs text) carry the attack are not systematically benchmarked at small scale.

**Direction:** Replicate a published image-context-injection jailbreak on 2-3 small open VLMs, holding the harmful intent fixed and varying whether the harmful context is delivered via image or text. Measure attack success rate per model and per modality split. The twist is cross-family transfer plus a modality-attribution ablation.

**Why it matters:** Multimodal safety benchmarks are an explicit gap; quantifying how much risk the visual channel adds, and how well attacks transfer across open VLMs, is directly useful to anyone deploying open VLMs. No training, open models only.

**Relevant context:** 'Visual Contextual Attack: Jailbreaking MLLMs with Image-Driven Context Injection' (arXiv 2507.02844) and 'Jailbreaking MLLMs with Image-Driven Context Injection' (EMNLP 2025). MMJ-Bench / MM-SafetyBench provide attack/eval scaffolding.
