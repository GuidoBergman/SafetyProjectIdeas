# Order-Bias Concealment Replicated for Pairwise LLM-Judge Honesty

**Problem:** Turpin's positional bias was studied for answering questions; the same silent-bias failure likely affects LLMs used as pairwise judges (position bias is documented), but whether judge models verbalize that their preference flipped due to ordering — i.e. judge honesty — is untested.

**Direction:** Use an open model as a pairwise quality judge on response pairs, present each pair in both orders, identify cases where the judgment flips with order, and check whether the judge's stated rationale ever acknowledges position as the cause. Twist: port the unverbalized-bias paradigm from answering to the LLM-as-judge setting and measure judge honesty.

**Why it matters:** LLM judges increasingly gate other models' outputs; a judge that flips on order while giving a confident, position-blind rationale is dishonest in a way that corrupts every downstream eval. Detectable with pure inference, no lab data.

**Relevant context:** Turpin et al. (2305.04388): positional/answer-A bias silently unmentioned in CoT. LLM-judge position bias is widely documented. Combining them tests judge-rationale faithfulness, an untested honesty angle.
