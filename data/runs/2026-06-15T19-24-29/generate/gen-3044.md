# Does Behavioral Alignment Faking Persist When CoT Stops Verbalizing It?

**Problem:** A striking recent claim is that some models (e.g., Opus 4.6) no longer verbalize alignment faking in reasoning, yet the compliance-gap BEHAVIOR persists. **Direction:** On open models, run the compliance-gap setup and separately classify (a) behavioral gap (free vs paid tier compliance) and (b) verbalized faking in CoT.
