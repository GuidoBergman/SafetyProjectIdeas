# Replicating HELM Safety Refusal Benchmarks on Quantized Open Models to Test Whether Quantization Erodes Safety

**Problem:** HELM Safety standardizes LLM safety scoring across six risk categories (violence, fraud, discrimination, sexual content, harassment, deception) using XSTest, HarmBench, SimpleSafetyTest, etc. Deployers routinely run 4-bit/8-bit quantized open models on consumer hardware for cost reasons, but whether aggressive quantization degrades a model's safety/refusal behavior is rarely measured and has clear governance relevance for edge deployment.

**Direction:** Take a subset of HELM Safety scenarios and replicate them on one open model at full precision and at 4-bit/8-bit quantization. The twist is precision-as-the-variable rather than model-as-the-variable. Measure refusal-rate and over-refusal (XSTest benign) deltas across precisions, isolating whether safety alignment survives quantization.

**Why it matters:** Edge/quantized deployment is exactly where lab-level defense-in-depth is absent, so degraded safety there is a deployment-governance blind spot. Fully feasible on one consumer GPU with open tools and open models.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in HELM Safety (5 benchmarks, 6 risk categories incl. XSTest over-refusal, HarmBench jailbreaks). Quantization-vs-safety is the underexplored twist; tools are open.
