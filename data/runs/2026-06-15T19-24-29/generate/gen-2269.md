# EvidenceWeight-Bench: Do Judges Over-Trust Citations Regardless of Relevance?

**Problem:** Debate and consultancy rely on judges weighting cited evidence sensibly, but there is no benchmark measuring whether a judge is swayed by the mere presence of a citation/quote even when it is irrelevant or contradicts the claim.

**Direction:** Construct items where a debater attaches (relevant / irrelevant / contradictory) but real-looking quotes to identical claims, and measure how each quote type shifts judge verdicts for several open judges. Deliver the item set and an 'evidence over-trust' index.

**Why it matters:** If judges reward citation theater, strategic models will exploit it; measuring evidence over-trust is a fresh, unmeasured oversight-failure mode requiring only inference and synthetic items.

**Relevant context:** Khan et al. 2024 information-asymmetric debate relies on quoted evidence; AI judges-with-personas work (arXiv 2506.02175) shows judge behavior is highly framing-sensitive.
