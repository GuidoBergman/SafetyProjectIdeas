# Hivemind in Tool/Function Calling: Do Agents Pick the Same Wrong Action?

**Problem:** Agentic deployments rely on models choosing tools/actions. If many models converge on the same incorrect or unsafe action in ambiguous situations, agent ensembles share blind spots — an under-studied homogeneity domain as agents proliferate.

**Direction:** Replicate homogeneity measurement on a function-calling/tool-selection task: present 6-8 open tool-capable models with ambiguous or trap scenarios (where the safe action differs from the obvious one) and record their chosen actions. Twist: measure the rate at which models jointly pick the same unsafe action vs. independence, and whether same-lineage models agree on wrong actions more.

**Why it matters:** Agentic systems are the near-future deployment surface; correlated unsafe action selection is a systemic risk for agent fleets. A reproducible evaluation built from public tool-use scenarios, no lab infrastructure needed.

**Relevant context:** Applies Artificial Hivemind homogeneity framing (arXiv:2510.22954) to tool/function-calling; uses open function-calling models and hand-built ambiguous-action scenarios.
