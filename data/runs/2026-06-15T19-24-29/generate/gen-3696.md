# System-Prompt Safety-Instruction Dose Response in Agentic Tasks

**Problem:** Adding 'be safe / refuse harmful requests' to the system prompt is the cheapest deployed safeguard, but its actual effect size on agentic HarmScore — and whether stronger safety instructions help monotonically or plateau — is not systematically charted.

**Direction:** On AgentHarm, vary the strength/specificity of a safety system-prompt instruction across a graded scale (none, generic, specific per-category prohibitions, with examples). Measure HarmScore/RefusalRate and utility on benign tasks at each dose. Plot a dose-response curve and find diminishing returns / over-refusal onset.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies how much safety can be bought by prompting alone before it hurts utility — directly useful to every agent builder without lab resources. Pure prompt variation on open benchmarks, no training.

**Relevant context:** AgentHarm (HarmScore/RefusalRate, benign-task utility). Catalogue has chat-side 'Dose-Response Curves for Harmful Capability Elicitation'; this varies the dose-response framing onto the AGENTIC safety-instruction axis (system-prompt dose vs tool-execution harm), a different intervention.
