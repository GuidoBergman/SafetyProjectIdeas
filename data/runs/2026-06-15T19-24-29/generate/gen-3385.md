# Do Models Verbalize Reliance on In-Context Documents (RAG Faithfulness)?

**Problem:** Hint-faithfulness work injects hints in the prompt. The analogous and practically critical case is RAG: when a model is given a retrieved document containing the answer (or a wrong answer), does its CoT acknowledge that it relied on the document, or does it present the answer as if from its own knowledge?

**Direction:** Provide an open model with a question plus a short context document that contains either the correct or a planted-wrong answer. Measure (1) how often the answer follows the document and (2) whether the CoT explicitly attributes the answer to the document. Vary document trustworthiness cues.

**Why it matters:** RAG systems are everywhere outside labs. If models silently launder document-sourced (possibly poisoned) answers as their own beliefs, monitors and users can't tell knowledge from retrieval — a concrete medium-horizon risk. No proprietary infra needed.

**Relevant context:** Turpin et al. 2023 / Chen et al. 2025 hint-verbalization framework adapted to retrieved context. Contextual-vs-parametric faithfulness studied in arXiv:2605.24960 but not as a student-scale verbalization audit.
