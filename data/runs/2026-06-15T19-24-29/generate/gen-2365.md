# Backcasting Bias-Resistant Judges: A Battery of Exploitable Judge Heuristics

**Problem:** Success state: overseers whose decisions are not swayed by content-irrelevant features a strategic AI could exploit (length, confidence words, formatting, ordering, self-praise). We need a systematic map of which biases survive in small open judges and how big each effect is.

**Direction:** Construct minimal-pair interventions on debate/consultancy transcripts (swap argument order, pad length with filler, add confident hedging words, add markdown formatting) and measure each bias's marginal effect on a small open judge's verdict, holding factual content fixed. Rank biases by exploitability.

**Why it matters:** The Open Phil RFP and AISI agenda explicitly call to map biases in human/AI supervision that strategic systems could exploit. A clean effect-size ranking of exploitable judge heuristics is a directly useful, lab-independent contribution and a strong blog post.

**Relevant context:** LaaJ judges show verbosity/sycophancy/self-certification biases (arXiv:2603.08091 robust-judge taxonomy; reward-hacking surveys). Subfield Open Problems explicitly list mapping exploitable overseer biases. Minimal-pair causal probing requires no training.
