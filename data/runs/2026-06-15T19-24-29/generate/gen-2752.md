# Over-Refusal vs. Under-Refusal Frontier: A Single Operating-Point Curve

**Problem:** Safety evals report compliance OR over-refusal separately, letting a model that refuses everything look 'safe'. There's no standard single-figure that captures the trade-off frontier for cross-model comparison.

**Direction:** Pair an over-refusal set (safe-but-scary prompts) with a matched unsafe set, sweep an open model across system-prompt 'safety strictness' settings, and plot the ROC-like frontier (true-refusal rate vs. false-refusal rate). Summarize each model with an AUC-style 'safety discrimination' score that can't be gamed by blanket refusal.

**Why it matters:** Provides the standardized cross-model safety metric regulators want, and resists the trivial 'refuse everything' exploit — a methodological fix usable by any outside evaluator.

**Relevant context:** XSTest (250 safe + 200 unsafe contrast prompts); OR-Bench (arXiv:2405.20947); FalseReject (arXiv:2505.08054); SORRY-Bench recommended pairing with exaggerated-safety set.
