# Single-Generation Confidence Signals vs. Verbalized Honesty

**Problem:** A recent paper derives confidence calibration for reasoning LLMs from a single generation (no sampling). Whether this internal single-generation confidence agrees with the model's *verbalized* confidence, or diverges (revealing dishonest confidence reporting), is unexplored.

**Direction:** For a set of questions, extract the paper's single-generation confidence signal AND the model's verbalized confidence from the same run on a small open model. Measure the gap between internal and verbalized confidence, and test whether large gaps predict wrong answers (i.e. the model 'knows' internally it is unsure but verbally claims confidence).

**Why it matters:** A divergence between internal and stated confidence is a direct, measurable form of dishonesty about uncertainty. Detecting it needs only a single GPU and connects calibration to honesty.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Unsupervised Confidence Calibration for Reasoning LLMs from a Single Generation' (arXiv 2604.19444, ~April 2026) and 'Confidence Before Answering' (arXiv 2603.05881).
