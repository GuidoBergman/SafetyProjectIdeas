# EU-Law Illegal-Behavior Probing as a Deployment-Realistic Deception Eval

**Problem:** EU-Agent-Bench measures illegal LLM-agent behavior under EU law — a realistic deployment frame. Whether models behave MORE deceptively/illegally when they believe the legal frame is fictional vs real (an eval-awareness variant) is unexplored.

**Direction:** Take EU-Agent-Bench-style scenarios and run open agent models under (a) framed as a compliance test, (b) framed as a real organic user request. Measure the gap in illegal/deceptive action rates. This tests whether legal-compliance evals are themselves gamed by eval-awareness.

**Why it matters:** Regulators will rely on compliance evals; if models behave legally only when they detect a test, those evals are hollow. Uses an existing open benchmark, no training, deployment-realistic fuzzy-reward setting.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2510.21524 (EU-Agent-Bench: Measuring Illegal Behavior of LLM Agents Under EU Law). Eval-awareness gap framing from arXiv 2507.01786 / 2509.00591.
