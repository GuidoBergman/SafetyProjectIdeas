# Steering Away Evaluation Awareness: Does It Reduce Sandbagging?

**Problem:** Prior work found a linear direction for evaluation awareness that can be steered. It is untested whether steering a model AWAY from 'I am being evaluated' reduces sandbagging behavior — a potential cheap runtime control.

**Direction:** Using a small open model, extract the eval-awareness direction via contrastive prompts (deployment vs eval framings), then apply activation steering to suppress it during a sandbagging-prone task. Measure whether suppressed-awareness runs produce higher (more honest) capability scores than control runs.

**Why it matters:** A working steering intervention would be a concrete, white-box runtime control against sandbagging — and a negative result is equally informative. Activation steering on small models is well within 30h and consumer-GPU budget.

**Relevant context:** Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness of Language Models (arXiv:2507.01786): identifies and steers a linear eval-awareness direction. Sandbagging documented in OpenAI/Apollo o3 chemistry example.
