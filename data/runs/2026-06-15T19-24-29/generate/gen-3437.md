# Sycophancy Stress-Test in a New Modality: Multimodal Open Models

**Problem:** Sycophancy and capitulation are well documented for text LLMs, and a recent study found sycophancy in vision-language models, but cheap reproductions of the text capitulation protocol carried into the image modality on open VLMs are scarce.

**Direction:** Replicate the capitulation protocol for an open vision-language model (e.g. Qwen2.5-VL-7B, LLaVA): ask a factual visual question (count objects, read a chart), then push back with a confident wrong claim, and measure capitulation. Twist: same sycophancy stress-test, new modality and open VLMs, comparing visual vs text capitulation rates within the same model.

**Why it matters:** Whether grounding in an image makes a model more or less willing to abandon a correct answer under pressure is a fresh, deployable robustness signal. Public images, inference only, no lab data.

**Relevant context:** 'Sycophancy in vision-language models: a systematic analysis' (ScienceDirect, 2025) documents VLM sycophancy and an inference-time mitigation. SycEval text capitulation rate 58%. Open VLMs run on a single consumer GPU.
