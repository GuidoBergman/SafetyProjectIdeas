# Does quantizing an open model break its safety concept directions?

**Problem:** Most consumer-GPU deployment of open models uses 4-bit/8-bit quantization. Beaglehole et al. extract directions on full-precision models, but the stability of safety concept vectors under quantization — the regime real non-lab users run in — is unstudied. Quantization noise could rotate or flatten safety directions.

**Direction:** Extract safety concept vectors on a full-precision open model, then re-extract on 8-bit and 4-bit quantized versions of the same model. Compare (a) cosine similarity of the directions, (b) probe AUC, and (c) steering efficacy across precisions. Determine whether vectors built at full precision still work when applied to the quantized model people actually deploy.

**Why it matters:** If quantization degrades safety directions, every hobbyist/non-lab deployment of concept-based monitoring is silently compromised — a practical robustness finding uniquely suited to the consumer-GPU setting and irrelevant for labs running full precision.
