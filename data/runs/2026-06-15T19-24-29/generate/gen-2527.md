# Evaluation-Awareness Probe Replicated on a New Model Family

**Problem:** Evaluation awareness (a model detecting it is being tested) follows a power-law with scale and is detectable via linear probes on steering activations, but the published scaling study used a specific set of instruction-tuned models.

**Direction:** Replicate the evaluation-awareness linear-probe-on-activations method on a different open family or a different prompt distribution (e.g., agentic transcripts instead of QA), reusing public eval-vs-deploy transcript labels. Test whether the same probe direction recovers eval-awareness and whether the power-law-with-size trend reproduces within the new family.

**Why it matters:** Eval-aware models can sandbag safety tests, undermining all control evaluations. A cheap, replicable probe for eval-awareness that works across families is a directly useful pre-deployment safety tool, buildable on a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** 'Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness' (arXiv 2507.01786): power-law scaling, 0.27B-70B, universal features in Llama-3.3-70B. 'Steering Evaluation-Aware Models to Act Like Deployed' (arXiv 2510.20487). Frontier models classify eval vs deploy transcripts AUC>0.8.
