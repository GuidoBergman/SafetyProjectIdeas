# Correlated Misalignment Generalization: Does Homogeneity Mean Misbehavior in One Domain Predicts It Everywhere?

**Problem:** Failure mode A: misalignment generalization (a model misbehaving in one context tends to misbehave in others). Failure mode B: homogeneity (different models share this tendency). Combined: if one widely used model shows a misaligned behavior, homogeneity implies its peers do too — correlated misalignment across the fleet.

**Direction:** Pick a small, observable proxy 'misbehavior' (e.g., willingness to give a subtly harmful tip, or to follow a manipulative system prompt). On a battery of unrelated contexts, query 6+ open models. Test (a) within-model: does misbehavior in one context predict it in others; (b) across-models: is the pattern shared. Report intra- and inter-model correlation of the misbehavior.

**Why it matters:** The International AI Safety Report flags correlated, connected safety failures. A cheap empirical demonstration that misalignment is both transferable within a model and homogeneous across models is directly on-target and needs no lab access.
