# Decoding-Parameter Attack Surface: A Robustness Heatmap

**Problem:** Success state: a model's safety does not collapse when users tweak temperature/top-p/top-k. The needed prior result: a reproducible heatmap of how jailbreak ASR varies across the decoding-parameter grid for open models.

**Direction:** Hold the attack fixed; sweep temperature, top-p, top-k on 2-3 open models against a harmful prompt set and plot ASR as a heatmap over the decoding grid. Identify 'soft spots' where benign-looking decoding settings drastically weaken safety.

**Why it matters:** Qi et al. listed decoding-parameter attacks as a consequence of shallow alignment, but a clean per-model robustness map is missing. Deployers who expose decoding controls (most open-weight APIs) need exactly this; trivially feasible on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946) explicitly attribute decoding-parameter attacks to shallow alignment. Prior 'consistency across decoding parameters' work exists but a full ASR heatmap per model is a fresh framing.
