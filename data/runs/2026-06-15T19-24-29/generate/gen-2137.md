# One Rank-1 LoRA = Self-Awareness? Testing the Minimal-Mechanism Claim

**Problem:** "Minimal and Mechanistic Conditions for Behavioral Self-Awareness in LLMs" (arXiv:2511.04875) claims self-awareness can be induced with a single rank-1 LoRA adapter and captured by one steering vector. This striking minimality claim invites a cheap independent replication on a different small open model.

**Direction:** On a small open model, induce a simple learned behavior, then attempt to elicit self-aware verbalization using progressively smaller adapters (rank-8 → rank-1). Extract a steering vector from the rank-1 case and test whether adding/removing it toggles the model's self-report. Confirm or bound the minimal-mechanism claim.

**Why it matters:** If self-awareness really lives in a rank-1 direction, it is both a tractable interpretability target and a manipulable safety signal — and the simplicity means it could be cheaply suppressed or amplified.
