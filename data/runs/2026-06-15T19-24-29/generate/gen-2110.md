# Distribution Shift x Reward Model Confidence Inversion

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) RM unreliability under distribution shift (alignment_training), and (B) RM confidence used to gate deployment decisions (oversight). On OOD inputs the RM may be both wrong AND confident, so a confidence-gated alignment pipeline lets exactly the worst cases through.

**Direction:** Probe an open reward model: collect its scalar reward and (where available) score margins on in-distribution vs. OOD prompts; compare RM agreement with a strong gold judge stratified by RM confidence. Test for 'confidence inversion' — high-margin RM judgments that are actually less accurate OOD.

**Why it matters:** Confidence-gated alignment assumes confidence tracks correctness; if shift inverts this, gating amplifies risk. Public RMs + judge; no training.

**Relevant context:** RM assigns high reward to OOD sub-par responses (arXiv 2510.13694); scaling laws for overoptimization (arXiv 2406.02900); Open Problems in RLHF on distribution shift. 'Learning a Pessimistic Reward Model' (arXiv 2505.20556).
