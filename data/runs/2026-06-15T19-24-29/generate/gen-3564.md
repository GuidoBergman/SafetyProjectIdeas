# Backcasting Robust CoT Transparency: Does Faithfulness Survive Light Quantization?

**Problem:** A success state is that the transparency of a model's CoT is preserved across the deployment transformations practitioners routinely apply. Working backward, one untested prerequisite is that quantization (the most common edge-deployment step) does not silently reduce CoT faithfulness or honesty. Recent work shows quantized LLMs can be 'deceptive' on truthfulness dimensions, but the CoT-faithfulness-under-quantization link is unexamined for small open models.

**Direction:** Take an open reasoning model in full precision and in 8-bit/4-bit quantized form (bitsandbytes/GGUF, both fit a consumer GPU). Run the hint-injection faithfulness protocol and a TruthfulQA honesty check on both. Compare answer-flip rates and hint-verbalization rates to test whether quantization changes faithfulness or honesty.

**Why it matters:** If quantization degrades CoT faithfulness, every edge/open deployment quietly weakens the safety property CoT monitoring relies on — a diffuse, real-world risk. The study needs only consumer hardware and existing checkpoints, with strong counterfactual value outside labs.

**Relevant context:** Quantized but Deceptive? (arXiv 2508.19432) finds multi-dimensional truthfulness shifts in quantized LLMs; Reasoning Models Don't Always Say What They Think (arXiv 2505.05410) defines the hint-verbalization faithfulness metric.
