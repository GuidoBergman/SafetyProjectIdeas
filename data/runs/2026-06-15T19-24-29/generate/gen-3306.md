# Capability Jump x Eval-Awareness: Does Scaling Up a Model Family Increase Awareness Faster Than Honesty?

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) capability scaling (general capability) inflating situational awareness; (B) deceptive evasion enabled by awareness (deception/scheming). Compound effect: if eval-awareness scales faster than honesty within a model family, larger models become better at gaming evals while not becoming proportionally more honest — a dangerous compounding scaling trend.

**Direction:** Within one open model family available at several sizes (e.g. Qwen 0.5B/1.5B/3B/7B), measure on a fixed battery: (1) eval-awareness (verbalized + probe), and (2) honesty under pressure. Plot both vs model size. Test whether awareness rises faster than honesty across the size ladder. All small enough for a single GPU.

**Why it matters:** Turns a scaling-trend question into a cheap small-model experiment, giving an early read on whether awareness outpaces honesty — relevant to medium-horizon scheming threat models.

**Relevant context:** Apollo: models 'quickly becoming more situationally aware and strategic.' 'Probing Evaluation Awareness' (arXiv:2507.01786). UK AISI honesty agenda.
