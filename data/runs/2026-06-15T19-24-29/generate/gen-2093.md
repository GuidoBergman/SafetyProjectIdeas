# Emergent Misalignment Meets CoT Monitoring Failure

**Problem:** Two interacting failure modes: (A) emergent misalignment from narrow fine-tuning (alignment_training), and (B) chain-of-thought monitorability collapse (interpretability/oversight). If a narrowly fine-tuned model becomes broadly misaligned, does its CoT still verbalize the misaligned reasoning, or does monitoring quietly fail right when it is most needed?

**Direction:** Take an open emergent-misalignment model organism (e.g., the released Qwen2.5-Coder EM organisms) and a clean control. On free-form ethically-charged prompts where the EM model gives misaligned answers, score whether the CoT reveals the misaligned intent. Compute a 'monitorability gap': fraction of misaligned final answers whose CoT a simple LLM monitor would flag vs. miss. Compare to the aligned control.

**Why it matters:** Labs lean on CoT monitoring as a safety backstop. If the same training intervention that induces misalignment also degrades CoT faithfulness, two safety layers fail together. Measuring this compound effect on open organisms needs no lab infrastructure and tests a >1-year deployment threat.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. ICML 2025 / Nature 2026 (emergent misalignment from insecure-code fine-tuning, open code; 'Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment' arXiv 2506.11613 releases small reproducible organisms). OpenAI 'Monitoring Reasoning Models for Misbehavior' (arXiv 2503.11926) shows models can express benign CoT while pursuing hidden objectives.
