# CrossFamilySteer: Benchmarking Steering-Vector Transfer Across Model Families

**Problem:** Transfer of steering vectors is studied within a model family/size, but there is no benchmark measuring whether a vector extracted on one architecture (e.g. Llama) retains any effect when mapped to a different family (e.g. Qwen, Mistral) of similar hidden size.

**Direction:** Extract matched CAA vectors on 2-3 open 7B-ish families, then test cross-family application (direct, and with a simple linear alignment of activation spaces) measuring retained steering effect and probe transfer. Report a cross-family transfer matrix and whether cheap alignment recovers transfer.

**Why it matters:** Cross-family transfer (or its failure) tells us whether safety steering tooling must be rebuilt per model or can be shared, a key cost/feasibility question for lab-independent safety work. Inference-only, fits one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Linear Representation Transferability work (arxiv 2506.00653) studies cross-size transfer; cross-family transfer is far less measured. Multiple open 7B families with similar hidden dims are available.
