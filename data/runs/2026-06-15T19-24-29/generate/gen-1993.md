# When Does DPO Make a Model Refuse Less? A Safety-Margin Audit

**Problem:** Likelihood displacement in DPO can shift probability mass toward harmful responses, but whether ordinary preference tuning on benign helpfulness pairs erodes refusal behavior is not routinely checked by the students who run it.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small aligned open model with DPO on a helpfulness-only preference set (no safety pairs), then measure refusal rate on a small held-out set of harmful prompts before vs. after, plus track log-probs of refusal tokens. Test whether helpfulness-only DPO degrades safety as a side effect, mirroring SFT findings.

**Why it matters:** Replicates the known 'benign fine-tuning breaks safety' phenomenon specifically for DPO, which newcomers increasingly use; gives a concrete checklist item (audit refusals after any preference run). Public data, one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. 2023 and Bianchi et al. showed benign SFT degrades refusals with as few as 10 adversarial / a few thousand benign examples; likelihood displacement shows DPO can shift mass toward harmful responses (arXiv:2410.08847).
