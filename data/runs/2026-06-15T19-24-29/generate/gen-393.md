# Lie-Probe False Positives on Honest Confessions and Fiction

**Problem:** Truth-probe research explicitly notes a failure mode: probes fire on objectively-honest responses such as a model confessing prior deception, or narrating fiction. The prevalence and structure of these false positives have not been systematically varied and quantified.

**Direction:** Take a published lie-detection probe (or replicate the Truth-is-Universal direction) and feed it a curated battery that varies along one axis: honesty-about-dishonesty content (honest confessions, quoted lies, fiction, hypotheticals, negations). Measure the false-positive rate per category to map the probe's failure boundary.

**Why it matters:** If the formal 'truthfulness direction' is really a 'deception-topic' direction, lie detectors are unsafe to deploy. Characterizing this is a precise, falsifiable contribution to the ELK / 'what can models hide' problem — fully external, inference-only.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2407.12831 'Truth is Universal' (probes anti-parallel in late layers); Alignment Forum 'Detecting Strategic Deception Using Linear Probes' notes probe fires on honest confessions of prior deception; arXiv:2311.15131 'Localizing Lying in Llama'.
