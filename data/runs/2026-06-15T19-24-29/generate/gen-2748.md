# Label-Error Sensitivity of Safety Benchmark Rankings

**Problem:** Popular benchmarks (e.g. MMLU) contain 6-9%+ label errors. For safety benchmarks, no one has asked how much these errors distort model rankings or inflate apparent safety — a measurement-validity blind spot.

**Direction:** Take a public safety/harm benchmark, manually audit a stratified sample to estimate its label-error rate, then run a simulation: perturb the labels at the estimated error rate and measure how much model rankings and absolute safety scores swing. Produce a 'ranking stability under label noise' metric and flag benchmarks whose verdicts are fragile.

**Why it matters:** Establishes how much we can trust safety leaderboards given known annotation noise — pure analysis, no training, high counterfactual value for outside auditors.

**Relevant context:** Are We Done with MMLU? (NAACL 2025, arXiv:2406.04127) found 6.49% error rate, rankings shift under MMLU-Redux; Hidden Measurement Error in LLM Pipelines Distorts Annotation, Evaluation, and Benchmarking (arXiv:2604.11581).
