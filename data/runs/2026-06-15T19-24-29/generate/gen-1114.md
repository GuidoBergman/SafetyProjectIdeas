# EnsembleRedundancy-Bench: how much real safety coverage does adding a 2nd model buy?

**Problem:** Defense-in-depth assumes that stacking a second guard model catches what the first misses. If models are homogeneous, the marginal coverage of each added model collapses. No benchmark measures the diminishing-returns curve of safety-classifier ensembles built from independent models.

**Direction:** Use a public harmful/benign prompt set. Treat 6-10 open models as binary safety classifiers. Compute the coverage curve: catch rate of 1 model, best 2, best 3... and the marginal gain per added model. Compare against a hypothetical 'independent classifiers' baseline (predicted by error rates if uncorrelated). Quantify the 'redundancy gap' — how far short real ensembles fall of the independence ideal.

**Why it matters:** Gives builders of monitoring/guardrail stacks a concrete metric for whether their ensemble is real defense or correlated theater, and quantifies systemic over-reliance on a homogeneous model population. Pure evaluation, consumer compute, high counterfactual value (scrutinizes a marketing claim).

**Relevant context:** Operationalizes correlated-failure concerns from Hivemind and 'Great Models Think Alike' (arXiv:2502.04313) into a deployment-relevant ensemble-coverage benchmark; ties to AEGIS-style ensemble safety (arXiv:2404.05993).
