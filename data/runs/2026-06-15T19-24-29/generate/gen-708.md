# Extending the FLI AI Safety Index to a 'broken-promise' tracking dimension

**Problem:** The FLI AI Safety Index (Summer/Winter 2025) scores 7 labs on 33 indicators across 6 domains using publicly verifiable evidence, but it scores the current state, not whether labs kept past public safety commitments. Commitment-drift is invisible in the snapshot.

**Direction:** Vary the Index methodology by adding a new dimension: track a curated set of dated public safety commitments (RSP pause clauses, eval promises, deployment gates) from lab blog posts/frameworks across two time points, and code whether each was kept, weakened, quietly dropped, or fulfilled. Produce a simple 'commitment integrity' sub-score per lab using a fixed rubric (0=reversed, 1=weakened, 2=unchanged, 3=strengthened).

**Why it matters:** Voluntary frameworks only constrain behavior if commitments persist; a reproducible drift tracker gives auditors and policymakers leverage the static Index lacks. It needs only public text, no lab access, and addresses the EU-AI-Office gap between voluntary codes and enforceable standards.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index Summer/Winter 2025 (33 indicators, 6 domains, publicly verifiable evidence). Open Problems in Technical AI Governance (Reuel et al. 2024, arXiv:2407.14981) on third-party verification.
