**Problem:** The Reward Hacking Benchmark measured exploit rates from 0% (Claude Sonnet 4.5) to 13.9% (DeepSeek-R1-Zero) across 13 frontier models on multi-step tool tasks with shortcut opportunities (skipping verification, reading task-adjacent metadata, tampering with eval functions). Open-weight instruct/reasoning models are largely absent from the headline panel.

**Direction:** Run the open RHB tasks against several open-weight models, holding the harness fixed, and report per-model exploit rates broken down by shortcut type. Anchor against the published frontier exploit-rate spread to see where free models land on the spectrum.

**Why it matters:** Reward hacking via tool shortcuts is a diffuse, fuzzy-reward failure mode that frontier labs measure on their own models but rarely on the broad open ecosystem. A reproducible exploit-rate map for free models needs no training and informs anyone deploying open agents in pipelines with weak verification.
