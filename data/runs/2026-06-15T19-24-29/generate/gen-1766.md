# Where Does a Vision Adversarial Patch Hijack the CLIP Safety Chain?

**Problem:** Multimodal-jailbreak chain: image with adversarial/typographic content -> CLIP encodes a misleading concept -> downstream VLM acts on it -> unsafe output. The link where the image representation overrides the intended concept is poorly mapped in interpretable terms.

**Direction:** Using ViT-Prisma / open CLIP with pre-trained vision SAEs, take typographic-attack images (text written on an image) and use the logit lens / SAE features across layers to localize where the written text's concept overtakes the visual object concept, isolating the hijack layer.

**Why it matters:** Multimodal safety mechanisms are an explicit open problem and under-resourced relative to text. Mapping where image attacks take over is foundational and uses only open ViT/CLIP weights and public SAEs.

**Relevant context:** Prisma/ViT-Prisma (arXiv 2504.19475, CVPR 2025 MIV): 80+ pretrained vision SAEs, circuit-analysis tools, CLIP support. Typographic attacks on CLIP are a documented vulnerability; SAE/logit-lens localization is the fresh mechanistic angle.
