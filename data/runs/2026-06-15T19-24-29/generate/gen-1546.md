# Do Backward-Planning Features in QA Predict When a Model Will Hallucinate?

**Problem:** Circuit tracing found backward planning in question answering (the model commits to an answer's 'article' or form before the answer itself). It is unknown whether weak or absent pre-commitment signals correlate with hallucination.

**Direction:** On an open model, use the logit lens at pre-answer positions to measure how strongly the eventual answer token is pre-activated, then compare this pre-activation signal on questions the model answers correctly vs. questions it hallucinates (verified against ground truth). Test whether low pre-commitment predicts hallucination.

**Why it matters:** A training-free internal hallucination-warning signal would be a cheap monitor any deployer could use. Connects mechanistic planning to a concrete safety failure (confident fabrication).

**Relevant context:** Biology of an LLM (2025): backward planning in QA, article selection before the answer. Logit-lens decoding of intermediate layers is standard in TransformerLens. Hallucination-detection via internal states is an active area.
