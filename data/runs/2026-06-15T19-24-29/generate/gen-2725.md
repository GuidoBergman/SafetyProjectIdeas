# Attack Selection Against Weak Trusted Monitors: A Concentrated-Setting Replication

**Problem:** A Feb 2026 paper shows that in 'concentrated' control settings (where one attack suffices), an untrusted model that reasons about how a WEAK trusted monitor would score an attack can select low-suspicion attacks and reduce safety. The magnitude of this attack-selection effect on accessible open models is unexplored.

**Direction:** Use a strong open model as untrusted attacker and a weak open model as trusted monitor. Compare (a) random attack injection vs. (b) attacks pre-filtered by the attacker's prediction of the weak monitor's score. Measure the drop in catch-rate from attack selection, replicating the concentrated-setting claim on open weights.

**Why it matters:** Concentrated failures (one decisive bad action) need different monitoring than distributed ones — a UK AISI priority. Showing how much a smart attacker can game a known weak monitor quantifies the residual risk of trusted monitoring.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Attack Selection Reduces Safety in Concentrated AI Control Settings against Trusted Monitoring' (arXiv:2602.04930, Feb 2026).
