# Relearning-Resistance Stress Test: A Public Benchmark for Unlearning Robustness on a Single GPU

**Problem:** Success state: anyone evaluating an 'unlearned' open model can verify in an afternoon whether the dangerous knowledge is truly gone or just hidden. Literature shows unlearning is often reversible by fine-tuning on a few benign points, but there is no lightweight, standardized stress test.

**Direction:** Build a small harness: take an open model unlearned on a WMDP-style subset (or apply RMU yourself on a tiny forget set), then run a battery of relearning attacks (fine-tune on 5/20/50 benign-but-adjacent examples, paraphrase probing, few-shot elicitation). Report a 'fragility score' and recovery curve.

**Why it matters:** Robust unlearning evaluation needs no proprietary data and is exactly the kind of fuzzy, hard-to-do-well safety work labs deprioritize. A shareable harness lets the community catch fake unlearning.

**Relevant context:** Fan et al. (arXiv:2502.05374, ICML 2025) show SOTA unlearning is vulnerable to relearning from a few points; WMDP is a public hazardous-knowledge benchmark; RMU code is public.
