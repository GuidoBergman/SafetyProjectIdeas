# Seed Sensitivity of EM: Quantifying Run-to-Run Variance as a Reproducibility Caveat

**Problem:** "Overtrained, Not Misaligned" (arXiv:2605.12199) found EM is inconsistent across seeds for most open models. The magnitude and structure of this run-to-run variance — and whether single-seed EM claims are statistically reliable — has not been characterized as a standalone reproducibility study.

**Direction:** Pick one small open model that sometimes shows EM. Run the identical EM-inducing LoRA fine-tune across ~8-10 random seeds on a single GPU. Measure the distribution of resulting misalignment rates, report variance, and test whether a single-seed run would have led to a wrong conclusion. Quantify how many seeds are needed for a stable estimate.

**Why it matters:** Much EM literature reports single-seed results. If variance is large, many positive/negative EM claims are statistically fragile. A rigorous variance characterization plus a recommended seed budget directly improves the reliability of an entire research line — a methodology contribution beginners can execute and labs rarely prioritize.

**Relevant context:** "Overtrained, Not Misaligned" (arXiv:2605.12199, May 2026): EM inconsistent across seeds, only 2 of 12 open-source models consistent. Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424).
