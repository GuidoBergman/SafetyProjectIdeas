# Multi-Dimensional (TraceGuard-Style) Monitoring vs Single Suspiciousness Score

**Problem:** TraceGuard proposes structured multi-dimensional scoring instead of a single suspiciousness scalar, claiming collusion resistance. Whether the multi-dimensional advantage holds for a cheap prompt-only implementation (vs fine-tuned) is untested.

**Direction:** Build a prompt-only monitor that outputs separate scores along several dimensions (deceptiveness, capability misuse, goal divergence, side-effects) and compare its AUROC and collusion resistance against a single-scalar monitor on SHADE-Arena. Test whether the structured scores are anti-correlated enough to matter, or collapse to one factor.

**Why it matters:** If structured scoring helps even without fine-tuning, it is a free upgrade for any monitoring harness. If the dimensions collapse, that punctures a 2026 claim. Pure prompting, no training.

**Relevant context:** TraceGuard: Structured Multi-Dimensional Monitoring as a Collusion-Resistant Control Protocol (arXiv 2604.03968): introduces structured multi-dimensional scoring as a new monitoring approach. Ensemble Monitoring (arXiv 2605.15377) notes diversity is the active ingredient.
