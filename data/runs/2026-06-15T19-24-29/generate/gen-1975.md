# Open-Judge EM Replication Kit: Cheap, Reproducible Misalignment Scoring

**Problem:** EM measurement currently depends on paid frontier judges, pricing out students and small labs and making replication costly. There is no validated open-weights judge configuration for EM scoring.

**Direction:** Identify the smallest open instruct model that reproduces the GPT-4.1 judge's EM labels within an acceptable agreement threshold. Curate a small gold set of human/frontier-judged EM responses, then sweep open judge models and prompt formats to find the cheapest configuration that matches, reporting agreement metrics. Ship the validated open-judge config as a reusable kit.

**Why it matters:** Lowering the cost of EM evaluation directly enables non-lab, counterfactual-value research and improves reproducibility. It is concrete tooling with a clear success criterion (agreement with the gold set). All inference, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424) use a proprietary GPT-4.1 judge. WebSearch confirms automated judges are central to EM. arXiv:2511.20104 shows open-model EM studies are feasible but need cheap evaluation. Open judge models (7-8B instruct) run on consumer GPUs.
