# Evaluation-Awareness Steering: Does 'Act Deployed' Change Safety Behavior?

**Problem:** A 2025 paper steered evaluation-aware models to act as if deployed. The downstream question for evaluators is whether steering a model toward a 'deployed' self-belief changes its safety refusal rates on benchmark items.

**Direction:** On an open model with an extractable evaluation-awareness direction (from probing as above, or a simple prompt-induced contrast), apply lightweight activation addition to push toward 'deployed' and re-run a safety refusal benchmark. Compare refusal/compliance rates with and without steering on AgentHarm or SorryBench items.

**Why it matters:** If 'feeling deployed' raises harmful compliance, then pre-deployment safety numbers systematically over-estimate real-world safety. A small, self-contained demonstration on an open model is high-value and uses only activation-addition (no training).
