# Cross-Lingual Safety Steering: Does an English Refusal Vector Refuse in Swahili?

**Problem:** Concept vectors reportedly transfer across languages, but this has not been tested for SAFETY concepts. If an English-built refusal vector fails to generalize to low-resource languages, multilingual deployments have a steering blind spot.

**Direction:** Build a refusal (or 'do-not-give-harmful-advice') steering vector from English contrast pairs on a multilingual open model. Apply it unchanged to harmful prompts translated into 4-5 languages (including a low-resource one) and measure steering success per language. Test the 2025 claim that English vectors transfer best, and identify which languages are under-protected.

**Why it matters:** Surfaces a concrete multilingual safety gap that frontier labs (English-centric eval focus) underweight. Translation is free, inference-only, one GPU. Directly extends the subfield's cross-language transfer surprising result to safety.

**Relevant context:** Subfield surprising result + hint: 'test cross-language steering on safety concepts like deception or refusal'. Grounded in cross-lingual steering work (arXiv 2601.16390, 2505.12584) finding English vectors often transfer best.
