# Fuzzing Steering Vectors: Adversarial Input Search That Neutralizes Steering

**Problem:** A key open problem is whether attackers can craft inputs that neutralize a steering intervention. Software fuzzing systematically searches inputs to break a program. No one has fuzzed inputs to find prompts where a safety steering vector silently fails.

**Direction:** Transfer greybox fuzzing. With a fixed refusal/safety steering vector applied to an open model, run a simple guided mutation fuzzer over prompts: start from seed unsafe requests, mutate (paraphrase, insert distractors, change framing) and keep mutants that reduce the post-steering projection onto the safety direction or restore the unsafe behavior. Characterize the families of inputs that defeat steering and which layers/concepts are most fuzzable.

**Why it matters:** Directly answers the adversarial-robustness open problem and produces a concrete catalogue of steering-evasion patterns, a model-organism-style study of how a steered model can be made to misbehave. Needs no training, just inference plus a lightweight search loop, and is a medium-horizon threat (steering defenses being bypassed) labs underinvest in.

**Relevant context:** Fuzzing systematically searches inputs to expose faults; LLM-guided fuzzers exist (arXiv:2505.03425). Open problem: can attackers craft inputs that neutralize steering (Beaglehole et al.).
