# Watermark/Provenance Fragility x Disclosure Mandates: When the Mechanism Can't Support the Rule

**Problem:** Failure mode A: governance mandates AI-content provenance/labeling (EU AI Act transparency). Failure mode B: provenance signals (watermarks, metadata) are fragile and strippable. The mandate compounds with the fragile mechanism so that 'compliant' labeled content silently loses its provenance downstream, defeating the rule.

**Direction:** Take a public text or image watermarking method and apply simple, realistic transformations (paraphrase, crop, recompress, format conversion) to measure watermark survival rates. Map survival rates against the operational requirements implied by the disclosure mandate to show where the mandate is technically unenforceable. Empirical robustness study on open tools.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates that a transparency rule and its enabling mechanism are mismatched, informing realistic provenance policy. Uses open watermarking tools on a consumer GPU; no proprietary access needed.

**Relevant context:** EU AI Act transparency/provenance obligations (artificialintelligenceact.eu); Open Problems in Technical AI Governance ecosystem-monitoring/verification (arXiv:2407.14981).
