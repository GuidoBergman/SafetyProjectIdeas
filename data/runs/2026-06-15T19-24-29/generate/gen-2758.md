# Tool-Misuse Recovery Eval: Does the Agent Notice and Self-Correct?

**Problem:** Agentic tool-misuse benchmarks mostly score whether misuse happens, not whether the agent RECOVERS after a misuse/error — a safety-critical property for autonomous deployment.

**Direction:** Use a small deterministic offline tool-environment (mock tools with logged effects). Inject a misuse or wrong-tool action and measure whether a small open agent detects the error, halts, and recovers safely vs. compounding the mistake. Report a 'misuse recovery rate' and a taxonomy of failure-to-recover modes.

**Why it matters:** Recovery/containment is exactly the diffuse, fuzzy-reward agentic property that's hard to grade and needs no lab infra; complements existing misuse-detection benchmarks.

**Relevant context:** ToolMisuseBench: An Offline Deterministic Benchmark for Tool Misuse and Recovery in Agentic Systems (arXiv:2604.01508); ToolEmu (36 high-stakes tools, 144 cases).
