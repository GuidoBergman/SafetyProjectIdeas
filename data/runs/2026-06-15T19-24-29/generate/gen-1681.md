# Refusal-Circuit Robustness Benchmark Under Paraphrase

**Problem:** Refusal is one of the few well-localized safety behaviors (refusal directions/features), but it's unknown how stable the localized refusal mechanism is across semantically equivalent paraphrases of harmful requests. No standard benchmark measures refusal-mechanism robustness to surface variation.

**Direction:** Construct a small set of harmful-request templates with many paraphrases, extract the refusal direction/feature in a small open model, and measure how consistently the same mechanism activates across paraphrases and how consistently ablating it removes refusal. Report a robustness score capturing paraphrase-invariance of the refusal mechanism.

**Why it matters:** If the refusal mechanism is paraphrase-fragile, jailbreaks that merely reword requests will slip past mechanism-based monitors. Measuring this is a concrete interpretability-to-safety contribution needing only open models and public refusal-direction methods.

**Relevant context:** ICLR 2025 work programs refusal with a single direction; Rogue Scalpel and SAE-steering papers manipulate refusal features but validate narrowly (single model/layer). Robustness across paraphrase is an under-measured property relevant to jailbreak defense.
