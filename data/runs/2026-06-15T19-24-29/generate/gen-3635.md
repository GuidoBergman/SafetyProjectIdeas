# Are Unfaithful CoTs Gameable Against LLM Judges?

**Problem:** A recent causal-bypass paper notes that unfaithful CoT can systematically mislead evaluators ('both explanations and judges are gameable'). The degree to which a plausible-but-unfaithful CoT inflates an LLM judge's trust score has not been quantified.

**Direction:** Generate answer pairs that are equally (in)correct but differ in CoT quality (one with a polished but causally-inert CoT, one terse). Have an LLM judge rate trustworthiness/correctness. Measure how much the polished unfaithful CoT inflates judge scores, quantifying judge gameability by reasoning theater.

**Why it matters:** If LLM judges reward eloquent-but-unfaithful reasoning, CoT-based oversight is exploitable. This is a clean, cheap demonstration of an oversight failure mode relevant to scalable-oversight on a long horizon.
