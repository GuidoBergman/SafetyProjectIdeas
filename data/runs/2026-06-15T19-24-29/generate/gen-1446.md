# PowerSeekProbe: A Benchmark for Detecting Latent Power-Seeking via Concept Vectors

**Problem:** Power-seeking / instrumental-goal pursuit is a key long-horizon threat, but there is no benchmark testing whether a linear concept vector can detect it in model activations on scenario-based prompts.

**Direction:** Build a small labeled set of scenario prompts where the model can choose a power-seeking vs a deferential action, extract a power-seeking concept vector on an open model, and benchmark probe detection AUC plus whether steering against the vector reduces power-seeking choices. Report detection reliability and steering efficacy.

**Why it matters:** Instrumental power-seeking is exactly the diffuse, long-horizon failure mode labs deprioritize relative to near-term harms; a probe-based detector is a tractable model-organism-style first study with no training and open models.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. suggest extending concept vectors to safety concepts; CAA already includes related behaviors (survival instinct, AI coordination, corrigibility). MACHIAVELLI / scenario-based open datasets provide power-seeking choice prompts.
