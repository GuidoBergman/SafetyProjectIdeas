# Faithfulness to Intermediate Structures: A Causal Replication

**Problem:** A recent paper does a causal analysis of LLM faithfulness to intermediate structures (e.g. whether stated sub-results actually feed the final answer). The prevalence of broken intermediate-structure dependence across structured tasks on open models is unmapped.

**Direction:** On structured tasks with explicit intermediate results (multi-step arithmetic, table lookups), corrupt a stated intermediate result in the CoT and check whether the final answer follows the corruption (faithful) or ignores it (unfaithful). Compute the fraction of intermediate structures that are causally honored, per model and task type.

**Why it matters:** Faithfulness to intermediate structure is a fine-grained, intervention-based honesty test. A cross-model map of where intermediate reasoning is real vs. decorative directly informs CoT monitoring reliability and needs only inference.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Breaking the Chain: A Causal Analysis of LLM Faithfulness to Intermediate Structures' (arXiv 2603.16475, ~March 2026).
