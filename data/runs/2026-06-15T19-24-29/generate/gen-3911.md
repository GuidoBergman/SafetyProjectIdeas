# Heterogeneous Models x Cascade Depth: Does Mixing Models Compound or Dampen Errors?

**Problem:** Failure mode A: model heterogeneity in a pipeline (different models at different stages). Failure mode B: cascade depth. The compound question: does heterogeneity act as error-correcting diversity, or does each model's distinct blind spot ADD new errors that downstream stages amplify?

**Direction:** Build a fixed multi-stage pipeline and run it (i) homogeneous (same model all stages) vs (ii) heterogeneous (different open models per stage) across knowledge-QA tasks. Measure final error rate vs cascade depth for each. Determine whether heterogeneity is net protective or net amplifying, and at what depth.

**Why it matters:** Practitioners assume mixing models improves robustness; testing whether it instead compounds errors is decision-relevant for safe orchestration. Small open models, single GPU, reproducible.

**Relevant context:** Hallucination Cascade studied heterogeneous models (GPT/DeepSeek/LLaMA) and found propagation shaped by model heterogeneity and cascade depth (arXiv 2606.07937); GUARDIAN temporal-graph propagation modeling (arXiv 2505.19234).
