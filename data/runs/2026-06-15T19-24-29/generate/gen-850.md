# Fault-Tree Analysis (from Reliability Engineering) of a Loss-of-Control Scenario

**Problem:** Governance discussions about catastrophic AI risk rarely decompose a scenario into the chain of failures that must co-occur, so it's hard to identify which governance interventions break the chain most cheaply.

**Direction:** Transfer Fault Tree Analysis from nuclear/aerospace reliability. Pick one documented threat model (e.g., a model exfiltrating its weights), decompose it into a fault tree of necessary intermediate failures, annotate each node with which existing governance mechanism (if any) addresses it, and compute minimal cut sets to find the leverage points.

**Why it matters:** Translates abstract catastrophe narratives into a structured intervention map regulators can act on — operationalizing safety objectives into deployment controls. Pure analysis, no compute.
