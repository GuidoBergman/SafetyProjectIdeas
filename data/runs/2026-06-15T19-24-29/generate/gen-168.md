# Token-Depth of Safety Across Quantization Levels

**Problem:** Shallow safety alignment was measured on full-precision weights. Practitioners increasingly run 4-bit/8-bit quantized models locally, but it is unknown whether quantization erodes or shifts the first-token safety signal that alignment relies on.

**Direction:** Take one open chat model at fp16, int8, and int4 (via bitsandbytes/GGUF). Reproduce Qi et al.'s per-token KL divergence between harmful-prompt completions and a reference safe completion, plus prefilling-attack success rate at each precision. Test whether quantization disproportionately damages the shallow safety layer.

**Why it matters:** Local/edge deployment via quantization is the dominant counterfactual-to-labs use case. If quantization silently weakens shallow safety, it is a deployment-robustness gap that only outside researchers running consumer hardware would surface.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946): safety divergence concentrated in first tokens. International AI Safety Report 2026 flags lab-tested vs real-world deployment robustness gap. Quantization is a standard consumer-GPU workflow.
