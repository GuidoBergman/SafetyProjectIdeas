# Distributed vs Concentrated Failure Detection: Does the Same Monitor Catch Both?

**Problem:** UK AISI flags that concentrated safety failures (one catastrophic action) and distributed failures (many small harmful actions summing to harm) need different monitoring approaches. Whether a single suspicion-threshold monitor catches distributed failures at all is an open, accessible question.

**Direction:** Construct two attack types in simulation: a concentrated attack (one high-suspicion malicious action) and a distributed attack (many low-suspicion actions each below threshold but jointly harmful).
