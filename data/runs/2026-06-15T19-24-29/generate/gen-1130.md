# PromptSensitivity-Homogeneity: do all models break on the SAME prompt perturbations?

**Problem:** Models are known to be brittle to trivial prompt rephrasings. It is unmeasured whether this brittleness is correlated — i.e., whether the SAME rephrasing flips the behavior of all models simultaneously (a shared fragility) versus idiosyncratic per-model brittleness.

**Direction:** Take ~40 safety/decision prompts. Generate controlled perturbations (typos, reordering, polite framing, added authority). Query 6-10 open models and record behavior flips. Measure whether perturbations that flip model A also flip model B (correlated brittleness index). Release a perturbation toolkit and the flip matrix.

**Why it matters:** Correlated prompt-brittleness means a single innocuous-looking phrasing can simultaneously destabilize an entire fleet of deployed assistants — a measurable, under-explored systemic fragility. Simple to implement; consumer compute; high counterfactual value as open auditing infra.

**Relevant context:** Combines prompt-brittleness literature with Hivemind shared-blind-spot framing; novel angle on correlated failure not in the obvious catalogue framings.
