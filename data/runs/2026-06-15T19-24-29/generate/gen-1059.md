# Does Inference Temperature Actually Reduce Cross-Model Homogeneity?

**Problem:** Prior work claims changing temperature/top-p does not mitigate monoculture, and it is worse for RLHF models — but most evidence is on intra-model repetition. Whether raising temperature reduces INTER-model homogeneity (the systemic-risk dimension) on safety-relevant tasks is an underexplored, easily testable claim.

**Direction:** For a fixed prompt set, sweep temperature (0.0 -> 1.5) across several open models and measure inter-model homogeneity at each temperature. Test whether higher temperature decorrelates models or just adds noise around a shared mode. Separate RLHF-tuned vs. base models to replicate the 'worse with RLHF' claim.

**Why it matters:** Operators often assume sampling diversity buys robustness. If temperature can't break inter-model agreement, deployment-time mitigations are illusory. Cheap inference sweep on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** RLHF effects on diversity (arXiv:2310.06452); claim that temperature does not mitigate monoculture; Artificial Hivemind.
