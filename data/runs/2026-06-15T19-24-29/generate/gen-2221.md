# Does Representation Quality Predict Weak-to-Strong Generalization? A Probing Replication

**Problem:** An ICML 2025 result ('Representations Shape Weak-to-Strong Generalization') claims the strong model's representation structure predicts how much W2S generalization occurs. This is testable cheaply but has not been independently replicated on small open models with simple probes.

**Direction:** On a binary task, freeze several small open models. Measure each model's representation quality via linear-probe accuracy on ground-truth labels. Then run W2S: train a linear head on weak (small-model) labels and measure how much each strong model's head recovers ground truth. Correlate representation-quality with W2S recovery across models to test the predicted relationship.

**Why it matters:** If a simple, measurable representation property predicts when weak supervision elicits strong capability, overseers could pre-screen which models are safely supervisable. Frozen models + linear probes fit a consumer GPU; high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** ICML 2025 poster 'Representations Shape Weak-to-Strong Generalization' (icml.cc/virtual/2025/poster/43511); builds on Burns et al. 2023 (arXiv:2312.09390) with open W2S code.
