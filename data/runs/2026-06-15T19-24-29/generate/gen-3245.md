# Sampling-Cost of Sandbagging: Does Hidden Capability Leak Across Many Samples?

**Problem:** A sandbagging model must underperform consistently. The causal link 'maintain low performance across stochastic generations' may be fragile: if the true capability is intact, high-temperature or many-sample best-of-N may leak it. This cheap, weights-free detector at the consistency link is under-explored relative to noise injection.

**Direction:** Induce prompted sandbagging on an open model over a public benchmark, then compare honest vs sandbagging conditions under (a) rising temperature and (b) best-of-N sampling. Test whether sandbaggers show anomalous best-of-N gains (true capability leaking) that honest models do not. Report a simple leak-based classifier and its error rates.

**Why it matters:** A purely black-box, API-only sandbagging detector (no weights needed) would be deployable by anyone auditing closed models — strictly more accessible than weight-noise methods.

**Relevant context:** Noise-injection work (arXiv:2412.01784) shows core capabilities remain partially intact under sandbagging; sandbagging surveys (arXiv:2406.07358) note prompted/password-locked underperformance.
