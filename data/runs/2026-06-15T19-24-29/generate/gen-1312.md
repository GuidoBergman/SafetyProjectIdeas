# Homogeneity of Omission: Do Models Share the Same Blind Spots in What They DON'T Say?

**Problem:** 'Biases in the Blind Spot: Detecting What LLMs Fail to Mention' studies what a single model omits. The systemic question — whether different models OMIT the same considerations (shared blind spots of omission) — is untested. If all models silently skip the same caveats/risks, the public is uniformly under-informed.

**Direction:** On prompts that should elicit caveats (e.g., 'is plan X safe?', 'what could go wrong with Y?'), collect ~8 models' responses. Build a reference checklist of relevant considerations (from a strong model or light human input). Measure which considerations are omitted by ALL models — the shared omission blind spots — vs. model-specific omissions.

**Why it matters:** Shared omissions are invisible failures: nobody sees the missing caveat because every model omits it. Mapping them is high-value, diffuse safety work needing only inference and light annotation.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Biases in the Blind Spot: Detecting What LLMs Fail to Mention' (arXiv:2602.10117, Feb 2026) from single-model omissions to cross-model shared omissions.
