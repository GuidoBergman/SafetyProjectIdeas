# Does Giving the Judge a Checklist Beat Giving It a Debate?

**Problem:** Debate is expensive (two strong agents, multiple turns). An under-tested alternative oversight scaffold: a structured *verification checklist* the weak judge applies to a single answer. Whether cheap structured prompting matches debate's accuracy gains is unknown.

**Direction:** On QuALITY/reasoning judging, compare three conditions at matched judge cost: plain direct-QA judging, single-round debate, and a checklist-guided judge (decompose claim, locate evidence, check for unsupported leaps). Report accuracy and token cost per condition across open judges.

**Why it matters:** If a cheap structured checklist captures most of debate's benefit, oversight becomes far more accessible and auditable. A clear, fully-specified, inference-only comparison with obvious safety relevance.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. (2024) debate vs consultancy vs direct-QA baselines. Kirchner et al. (2024) legibility via structured verification. arXiv:2504.03731 benchmark for protocol comparison.
