# Sandbagging Detection on a New Capability Domain (Cyber vs Bio vs Math)

**Problem:** Sandbagging detection methods are validated mostly on WMDP/dangerous-capability domains. Whether the same prompted-sandbagging signal and detector AUROC transfer to a benign-but-graded domain like competition math is open.

**Direction:** Prompt an open model to sandbag on a math benchmark (replicate the standard 'underperform on this eval' prompt), apply MALT-style or consistency-based detection, and compare detection AUROC to published WMDP-domain numbers. Twist = domain swap.

**Why it matters:** If sandbagging detectors degrade outside the dangerous-capability domains they were tuned on, deployment evaluators may miss sandbagging on novel domains — a generalization gap worth documenting with open data only.

**Relevant context:** van der Weij et al. 'AI Sandbagging' (arXiv:2406.07358): generalizes to WMDP. MALT (METR, 2025) sandbagging-detection AUROC ~0.91 on software/research tasks; few diverse natural examples noted as a limitation.
