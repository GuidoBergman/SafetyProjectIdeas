# Attribution Graphs of a Refusal Decision in an Open Model

**Problem:** Circuit tracing has mostly been showcased on factual recall and poetry. The internal computation that produces a SAFETY refusal — and where exactly an effective jailbreak diverts that computation — has not been mapped with open attribution-graph tooling on a small open model.

**Direction:** Using the open Circuit Tracer library on a supported model (Gemma-2-2B / Qwen3-4B), build attribution graphs for a harmful request that is refused vs. the same request under a known jailbreak that succeeds. Identify which features/edges the jailbreak suppresses or reroutes to flip refusal to compliance.

**Why it matters:** Pinpointing the mechanistic 'pressure point' of refusal in an open model is directly useful for hardening safety and is reproducible outside any lab using released tooling — a concrete circuit-level safety contribution.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Circuit Tracing (transformer-circuits.pub 2025) introduced attribution graphs; open Circuit Tracer (Anthropic Fellows) supports Gemma-2-2B/Llama-3.2-1B/Qwen3-4B, EleutherAI's Attribute supports CLTs. Jailbreak datasets are public.
