# Probe Calibration Drift Plus Automated Gating Decisions

**Problem:** Compound: (1) interp probes are poorly calibrated (scores don't map to true probabilities — interp failure) amplifying (2) automated gating/escalation systems that threshold probe scores. A miscalibrated probe whose threshold was set on clean data will either flood reviewers (false alarms) or silently pass harm (false negatives) once the input mix shifts.

**Direction:** Train an unsafe-content probe on an open model; produce reliability diagrams on held-out and shifted data. Simulate a gating policy at a fixed threshold and measure how calibration drift changes the realized false-negative/false-positive rates under shift. Report the safety-relevant miscalibration gap.

**Why it matters:** Calibration is rarely reported for safety probes yet determines real gating behavior. A clean, statistics-friendly study (well-matched to the participant's stats skills) that exposes a compound deployment risk without any training or lab data.

**Relevant context:** 'Probing Classifiers are Unreliable' (arXiv:2207.04153) and 'Building Better Deception Probes' (arXiv:2602.01425, aggregation errors) motivate calibration concerns. Reliability-diagram / ECE methods are standard statistics. TransformerLens for activations.
