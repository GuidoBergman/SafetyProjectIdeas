# FindTheFlaws as a Sandbagging Detector: Vary Whether the Solution-Author Hides Errors

**Problem:** FindTheFlaws contains naturally flawed solutions, but does not test ADVERSARIAL flaw-hiding — solutions deliberately crafted to make errors hard to spot. Whether oversight critics catch hidden-on-purpose flaws as well as natural ones is unknown.

**Direction:** Take correct FindTheFlaws solutions and use a strong open model to inject a single subtle error PLUS camouflage (plausible-sounding justification). Compare a small critic model's flaw-detection rate on (a) natural flawed solutions, (b) adversarially-camouflaged flawed solutions. Quantify the camouflage-induced detection drop per domain.

**Why it matters:** Strategic models that hide errors are the central scalable-oversight threat. Measuring how much camouflage degrades automated critique on a public benchmark is a clean model-organism-style evaluation, inference-only.

**Relevant context:** FindTheFlaws (arXiv:2503.22989) has annotated natural flaws + open code, but no adversarial-camouflage condition. Prover-verifier 'sneaky prover' (arXiv:2407.13692) motivates camouflaged errors.
