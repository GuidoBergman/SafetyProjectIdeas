# Auditing SAFER Reward-Model Safety Features for Reward Hacking Cues

**Problem:** SAFER: Probing Safety in Reward Models with Sparse Autoencoder (arXiv:2507.00665) probes reward-model internals with SAEs, but does not test whether the discovered safety features are robust to reward-hacking inputs designed to look safe.

**Direction:** Apply an SAE to an open reward model, identify the features SAFER-style analysis flags as safety-relevant, then construct adversarial 'looks-safe-but-isn't' completions and measure whether those features still fire correctly. Report the false-negative rate of SAE safety features under mild adversarial pressure.

**Why it matters:** Reward models are the alignment bottleneck for RLHF; understanding whether their internal safety features can be gamed is a medium-horizon concern doable entirely on open reward models.

**Relevant context:** SAFER: Probing Safety in Reward Models with Sparse Autoencoder (arXiv:2507.00665).
