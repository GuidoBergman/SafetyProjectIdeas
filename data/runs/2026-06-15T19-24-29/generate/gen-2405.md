# Refusal Brittleness Meets Overseer Blind Spots: Safety Training x Oversight Coverage

**Problem:** Two failure modes compound: (A) superficial safety alignment (refusals collapse once early-token compliance is breached) and (B) overseer coverage gaps (the monitor checks for overt refusal-failures but not for content that resumes after a compliant opening). An attack that gets the model past the first tokens produces unsafe content the refusal-focused overseer never inspects.

**Direction:** Generate outputs that begin with a compliant/safe-looking preamble then drift into unsafe content. Test whether an open judge model that flags overtly unsafe outputs misses these 'safe-start' outputs. Measure the miss-rate gap between unsafe-from-the-start vs unsafe-after-safe-start. The compounding metric is the overseer's blind spot to exactly the failure mode superficial alignment produces.

**Why it matters:** Weak-to-strong jailbreaking shows alignment is concentrated in early decoding; if overseers also anchor on early tokens, attacker and overseer share the same blind spot. Mapping this overlap is a concrete, lab-external robustness finding. Generation + classification only.

**Relevant context:** Weak-to-Strong Jailbreaking (arXiv 2401.17256): 'alignment is superficial, clustered in early decoding stages... models rapidly revert to unsafe behaviors.' Shortcut bias in judges (arXiv 2602.07996).
