# Backcasting Interaction Value: Does Letting the Judge Ask Questions Actually Help?

**Problem:** Success state: interactive oversight (judge cross-examines the advisor) reliably beating passive reading. We need a clean measurement of how much interactivity adds for a weak open judge, since interactive protocols are costlier and only worth it if they help.

**Direction:** Compare three open-model conditions on QuALITY: static consultancy (read one argument), interactive consultancy (judge asks 2-3 follow-ups), and debate. Measure judge accuracy and the marginal value of each judge question. Identify question types where interaction flips a wrong verdict to correct.

**Why it matters:** Recursive/amplified oversight assumes active probing helps overseers. Quantifying the marginal value of judge interactivity with a weak model informs whether protocol designers should invest in interaction, and is fully reproducible outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. 2024 (arXiv:2407.04622) compare debate, interactive debate, and consultancy. The per-question marginal value of judge interactivity for weak open judges is underquantified. Open harness available.
