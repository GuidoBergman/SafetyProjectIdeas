# Replicating hallucination concept-probe monitoring on a different domain (code vs. factual QA)

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. validate hallucination probes on standard benchmarks. Whether a hallucination concept probe trained on factual-QA contrasts transfers to a different hallucination domain (e.g., hallucinated code APIs) is an untested, accessible generalization test.

**Direction:** Fit a hallucination concept probe on factual-QA contrast activations from an open model, then evaluate it on a code-hallucination set (non-existent library/function calls), comparing to an LLM-judge. Twist: cross-domain transfer of the hallucination probe, a domain the original didn't cover.

**Why it matters:** Hallucinated code APIs are a concrete, high-stakes failure (supply-chain/security). A cheap internal probe that flags them without a judge is useful monitoring infrastructure, and cross-domain transfer is the kind of robustness check labs skip.
