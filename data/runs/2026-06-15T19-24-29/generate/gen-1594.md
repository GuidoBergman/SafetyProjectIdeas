# Refusal Ablation x Backdoor Activation: Does Removing Refusal Unmask Latent Trigger Behavior?

**Problem:** Failure mode A (concept_steering): ablating the refusal direction. Failure mode B (data-poisoning / backdoors): a model carries a latent trigger. Compound risk: refusal ordinarily masks triggered harmful behavior; ablating the refusal direction could unmask backdoor responses that were dormant under normal alignment, making the two failures multiplicative.

**Direction:** Use a small open model with a known/published benign backdoor (or insert a trivial trigger via tiny LoRA). Compare triggered-vs-untriggered behavior with refusal intact vs refusal-direction ablated. Measure whether ablation disproportionately amplifies the triggered behavior relative to the baseline — testing the unmasking hypothesis.

**Why it matters:** Backdoors + steering is an underexplored interaction with a clear medium-horizon supply-chain threat model. Model-organism scoped to a tiny trigger, no large training.

**Relevant context:** Refusal-direction ablation methodology is open-source and well documented. Steering-x-safety unmasking is motivated by arXiv 2603.24543 (steering not safety-neutral) and the refusal-direction universality work (arXiv 2505.17306).
