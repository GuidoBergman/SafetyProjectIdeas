# Stress-Testing 'Universal Truth Directions' on Reasoning Computation

**Problem:** A recent paper shows linear truth probes are reliable for factual recall but break down when truth depends on computing/storing intermediate results, and are sensitive to layer choice and instruction framing. The extent of breakdown across multi-step arithmetic/logic depth is not mapped.

**Direction:** Use an off-the-shelf small open model, extract hidden activations for true/false statements at varying 'computation depth' (0-step factual recall, 1-step, multi-step arithmetic/logic), and fit simple logistic-regression truth probes. Plot probe accuracy as a function of computation depth and layer to characterize exactly where the linear truth direction degrades. No training of the base model needed; only lightweight probe fitting.

**Why it matters:** Lie/truth probes are a leading white-box honesty tool that anyone with a single GPU can study. Mapping their failure boundary as computation deepens tells us when activation-based deception detection can be trusted, a key white-box-honesty open problem.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Testing the Limits of Truth Directions in LLMs' (arXiv 2604.03754, ~April 2026), which reports truth directions break down when truth depends on intermediate computation and are layer/instruction sensitive; builds on Marks & Tegmark truth-direction work.
