# Deployment-Gap Probe: Do Lab Benchmark Scores Predict Field Robustness?

**Problem:** Success state: a high benchmark safety score guarantees real-world robustness. The needed prior result: a small study correlating an open model's standardized benchmark score (e.g. HELM Safety / StrongReject) with its robustness to a battery of simple, realistic attacks.

**Direction:** Take several open models with published safety-benchmark scores, run a fixed battery of accessible realistic attacks (prefill, many-shot, roleplay, decoding sweep), and test whether benchmark rank predicts realistic-attack robustness rank. Report the correlation (or lack thereof).

**Why it matters:** Directly probes the 'gap between lab-tested defenses and real-world robustness' open problem. A weak correlation would be a important warning to deployers relying on leaderboard scores — a high-impact, no-lab-resource meta-evaluation.

**Relevant context:** International AI Safety Report 2026 flags lab-to-deployment robustness gap. StrongReject (arXiv:2402.10260) showed benchmark scores can diverge wildly from claimed ASR; HELM Safety and AILuminate provide standardized scores to correlate against.
