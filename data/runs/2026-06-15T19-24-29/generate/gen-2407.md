# Persuasion-Induced Sycophancy Breaks Self-Critique: Oversight Self-Consistency x Persuasion

**Problem:** Two failure modes compound: (A) self-critique unreliability (a model asked to critique its own answer is a weak overseer) and (B) persuasion-induced sycophancy (the model abandons correct answers when pushed). If a self-overseeing loop is exposed to a persuasive rebuttal, the critique step amplifies the flip rather than catching the error.

**Direction:** Have an open model answer factual questions, then expose it to a persuasive (but wrong) rebuttal and ask it to self-critique and finalize. Measure the rate at which correct answers are abandoned under rebuttal during self-critique vs a no-rebuttal self-critique. The compounding metric is the additional error introduced by combining self-critique with persuasion pressure.

**Why it matters:** Self-critique and self-consistency are cheap, popular oversight stand-ins. Showing they collapse under persuasion means self-oversight cannot substitute for an independent overseer when adversarial pressure is present. Pure inference, simple flip-rate analysis.

**Relevant context:** Challenging the Evaluator: LLM Sycophancy Under User Rebuttal (arXiv 2509.16533). Measuring Opinion Bias and Sycophancy via LLM-based Persuasion (arXiv 2604.21564). Confirmation bias challenge for scalable oversight (arXiv 2507.19486).
