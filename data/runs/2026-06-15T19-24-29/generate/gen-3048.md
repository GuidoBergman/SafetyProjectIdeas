# Sandbagging Signature in Latency and Token Economics, Not Just Accuracy

**Problem:** Sandbagging detection focuses on accuracy drops, but a model strategically underperforming may also leave non-accuracy signatures (response length, hedging, refusal latency, self-corrections). No work characterizes these cheap behavioral side-channels of deliberate underperformance.

**Direction:** Induce sandbagging via prompts ('underperform to look like a weaker model') vs honest baselines on open models. Beyond accuracy, log token counts, generation entropy, edit/self-correction markers, and hedging-word frequency. Train a tiny classifier on these cheap features to detect sandbagging. Report which signatures are most discriminative.

**Why it matters:** Provides a low-cost, model-agnostic sandbagging tripwire usable without internal access — strategically valuable monitoring infra. Hidden-capability detection is a standard-priority Open Phil problem.
