# Many-Shot Jailbreaking Replicated With Benign-Looking (Obfuscated) Demonstrations

**Problem:** Many-shot jailbreaking uses overtly harmful demonstrations; defenses that scan for harmful in-context examples might catch it. Whether the same in-context scaling effect holds when demonstrations are OBFUSCATED (encoded, role-played, or split) — making them evade a content filter while still steering behavior — is unverified.

**Direction:** Replicate many-shot scaling on an open long-context model but vary demonstration obfuscation level (plain vs lightly encoded/role-played). Twist: obfuscation as a new axis on top of shot count. Measure ASR-vs-shots curves per obfuscation level and whether obfuscation preserves the in-context steering effect.

**Why it matters:** Tests whether content-scanning defenses against many-shot can be trivially evaded, a realistic adaptive-attacker scenario labs must deprioritize for open models. Inference-only, single GPU, with clear ASR-vs-shots curves.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic many-shot jailbreaking (2024): power-law ASR scaling via in-context learning. Best-of-N and obfuscation literature show light input transforms preserve steering while evading filters. Open long-context models on HuggingFace.
