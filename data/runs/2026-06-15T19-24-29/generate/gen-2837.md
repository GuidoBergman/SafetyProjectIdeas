# CoT-Monitor Sandbagging Detection Replicated With a Weaker, Cheaper Monitor

**Problem:** Covert sandbagging was tested against a 'more powerful' CoT monitor. In counterfactual settings, the monitor available to independent researchers is often weaker/cheaper than the model being evaluated. How monitor strength changes sandbagging detection rate is not mapped.

**Direction:** Replicate the sandbagging-vs-CoT-monitor setup but systematically vary monitor capability (e.g., 1.5B, 3B, 7B monitors judging a 7-14B sandbagging model). Plot detection rate vs monitor/model size ratio. The twist: a monitor-strength sweep instead of a single fixed monitor.

**Why it matters:** Independent evaluators rarely have a stronger monitor than the system under test. Knowing the monitor-strength threshold for catching sandbagging tells the community when cheap CoT monitoring is worthless — directly informs realistic, lab-independent monitoring harnesses.
