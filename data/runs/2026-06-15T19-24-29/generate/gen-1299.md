# Format-Induced Diversity Collapse on Safety-Relevant Prompts

**Problem:** 'The Price of Format: Diversity Collapse in LLMs' shows structured output formats (JSON, multiple-choice) collapse response diversity. Unstudied: whether forcing structured formats on safety/risk-assessment prompts makes multiple models collapse to the SAME answer, amplifying systemic homogeneity exactly when diverse risk assessments are most needed.

**Direction:** Take safety/risk-assessment prompts (e.g., 'rate the danger of this scenario', 'list risks of plan X'). Compare free-form vs. JSON/structured outputs across ~6 models. Measure both within-model diversity drop and cross-model convergence under structured formats.

**Why it matters:** Production AI systems force structured outputs for tooling. If this silently homogenizes safety judgments across an ecosystem, it's a hidden systemic risk introduced by engineering convenience — auditable with pure inference.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'The Price of Format: Diversity Collapse in LLMs' (arXiv:2505.18949, May 2025) to safety-relevant prompts and to the cross-model homogeneity dimension.
