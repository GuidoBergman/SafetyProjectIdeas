# Benchmarking Cascading Robustness Verification's efficiency claims on open models

**Problem:** 'Cascading Robustness Verification' (SaTML 2026) claims a model-agnostic verifier improving runtime by up to ~90% while certifying at least as many inputs. The efficiency/coverage tradeoff has not been reproduced on safety-adjacent small classifiers.

**Direction:** On a small open image classifier (MNIST/CIFAR-scale), implement the cascading idea (cheap verifier first, expensive verifier as fallback) using existing open verification tooling and measure certified-input count and runtime vs a single-verifier baseline. Success = a runtime/coverage Pareto plot reproducing the qualitative claim.

**Why it matters:** Cheaper formal robustness verification lowers the bar for independent auditing of deployed models — a strategically valuable, lab-independent capability. Tractable on a consumer GPU at toy scale.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Cascading Robustness Verification: Toward Efficient Model-Agnostic Certification' (arXiv:2602.04236, SaTML 2026).
