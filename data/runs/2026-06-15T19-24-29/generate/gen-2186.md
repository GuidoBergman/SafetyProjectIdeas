# Critique-Then-Judge: Does an AI Critique Step Improve Weak-Judge Accuracy?

**Problem:** CriticGPT helped humans review code; debate helps weak judges. An untested combination: inserting an automated CRITIQUE of each debater's argument before the judge decides. Whether critique-assistance stacks with debate, or is redundant, is unknown.

**Direction:** On QuALITY debate transcripts, add a stage where a small open critic model writes a bulleted list of weaknesses in each debater's argument; the weak judge then decides WITH vs WITHOUT the critique. Measure the accuracy delta from adding the critique layer, and whether it helps more on hard questions.

**Why it matters:** Composing oversight mechanisms (debate + critique) is a natural path to stronger protocols, and testing whether they're complementary or redundant is cheap and informative. Inference-only, public dataset.
