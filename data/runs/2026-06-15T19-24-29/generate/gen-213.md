# Rapid-Response Jailbreak Defense Replicated With a Tiny Open Proliferation Set

**Problem:** Rapid Response (Peng et al. 2024) blocks jailbreak classes after seeing only a few examples by proliferating variants and updating a classifier; it was evaluated with their pipeline and models. Whether the core idea works with a SMALL open proliferation model and an open guard classifier, accessible to non-labs, is untested.

**Direction:** Replicate rapid-response defense at small scale: given a handful of seed jailbreaks, use a small open model to proliferate variants, fine-tune/threshold an open guard classifier, and measure block rate on held-out variants of the SAME class vs unseen classes. Twist: all-open, minimal-compute reimplementation.

**Why it matters:** Adaptive, few-shot defenses are a key open problem (Anthropic recommended directions); showing a fully external, cheap version works (or fails) is directly actionable defense infrastructure for small deployers. Small models + classifier fit one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Rapid Response (arXiv:2411.07494): mitigates jailbreaks by proliferating from a few observed examples; reduces ASR sharply on seen classes. Open guard models (Llama-Guard) and small proliferators are public.
