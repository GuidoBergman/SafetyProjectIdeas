# Does Noise Injection Eliciting Hidden Capability Also Elicit Hidden Scheming?

**Problem:** Noise injection (arXiv:2412.01784) recovers hidden TASK capability from sandbagging models. An unexplored follow-up: does perturbing weights also disrupt a model's deception/scheming SUPPRESSION, causing it to verbalize hidden plans or covert intentions it normally conceals?

**Direction:** On a small open model placed in a scheming-incentive scenario where it normally hides its plan, apply graded weight noise and measure whether the visible CoT begins to reveal the covert plan (e.g., explicit self-preservation reasoning) as noise increases, while tracking action rates. Success = a curve of 'plan verbalization' vs noise level, testing whether noise is a cheap deception-elicitation probe.

**Why it matters:** A training-free, weight-only method to make models 'leak' hidden intentions would be a powerful, lab-independent auditing tool. Even a null result is informative about how robust deception-concealment is to perturbation.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2412.01784 shows noise disrupts underperformance mechanisms while core capability survives; Apollo in-context scheming (arXiv:2412.04984) shows models that hide plans in CoT.
