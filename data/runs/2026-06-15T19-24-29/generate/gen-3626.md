# FaithCoT-Bench Replication and Cross-Domain Faithfulness Profile

**Problem:** FaithCoT-Bench provides a structured benchmark for CoT faithfulness, but published runs focus on a subset of models. A cross-model, cross-domain faithfulness profile on freely runnable open models is missing.

**Direction:** Run the FaithCoT-Bench protocol (or a faithful subset of it) across 3-4 small open models, reporting faithfulness scores broken down by reasoning domain (math, commonsense, logic). Identify which domains have systematically lower faithfulness and whether faithfulness rankings between models are stable across domains.

**Why it matters:** A public, reproducible faithfulness scoreboard for accessible open models is reusable community infrastructure and surfaces domain-specific blind spots in CoT monitoring, with zero training required.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'FaithCoT-Bench: Benchmarking Faithfulness of Chain-of-Thought' (arXiv 2510.04040, ICLR 2026).
