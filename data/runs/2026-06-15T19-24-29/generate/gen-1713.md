# Does an SAE Feature Mean the Same Thing Across Layers? Cross-Layer Drift Test

**Problem:** SAEs are trained per layer, and a concept may be represented by latents that drift or fragment across layers. Treating a 'concept feature' as a single stable object across depth can be wrong, and cross-layer transcoders/attribution graphs assume some coherence that may not hold.

**Direction:** Using public per-layer SAEs for an open model, pick concepts with a clear feature at one layer and try to track the 'same' concept across adjacent layers by decoder-direction similarity and co-activation on the same inputs. Measure how often a concept has a clean single counterpart at the next layer vs fragmenting/merging, and report a cross-layer stability profile.

**Why it matters:** Attribution graphs and cross-layer circuit stories depend on features meaning the same thing across layers. If concepts drift, multi-layer safety explanations are shakier than assumed. Quantifying cross-layer stability on open SAEs grounds how far such explanations can be trusted.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Circuit Tracing uses cross-layer transcoders; SAE absorption/splitting work (arXiv:2409.14507) and consistency work show features fragment, motivating a cross-layer coherence check.
