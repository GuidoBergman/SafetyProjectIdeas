# Self-Replication Risk Probe: Scoped Variant of the Agent Matrix Setup

**Problem:** Self-replication is a flagged frontier risk in ForesightSafety and the 'Agent Matrix' study, but realistic self-replication evals require heavy agent infrastructure. A scoped, safe-proxy variant accessible to students is missing.

**Direction:** Build a tiny sandboxed text-only 'replication awareness' eval: present open agent models with scenarios that offer steps toward copying themselves to another (fake) machine and measure willingness/competence to plan the steps, without any real execution. Vary the framing (explicit instruction vs goal-implied) and compare models.

**Why it matters:** Provides a cheap, safe, reproducible propensity probe for a top catastrophic-risk dimension that frontier labs gate behind heavy infra — high counterfactual value and a long-horizon threat model. Pure prompting in a fake sandbox, no execution risk.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2509.25302 'Dive into the Agent Matrix: A Realistic Evaluation of Self-Replication Risk in LLM Agents'; ForesightSafety Bench (arXiv 2602.14135) lists self-replication as a catastrophic-risk dimension.
