# Cross-Decoding-Temperature Sandbagging Stability Replication

**Problem:** Sandbagging studies typically fix decoding parameters. Whether a model's ability to deliberately underperform (and a monitor's ability to detect it) is stable across temperatures and sampling settings is unverified — a methodological blind spot.

**Direction:** Replicate a prompt-based sandbagging eval on a small open model while sweeping temperature/top-p. Measure how the honest-vs-sandbag capability gap and detection rate vary with decoding settings. The twist: decoding-parameter sweep layered onto an existing sandbagging protocol.

**Why it matters:** If sandbagging detectability swings with temperature, safety-eval results that fix one decoding setting are fragile. This strengthens eval methodology rigor cheaply and is fully reproducible on open models.

**Relevant context:** Sandbagging protocols from arXiv 2406.07358 and 2508.00943. Decoding-parameter sensitivity of safety is a known concern (echoing the catalogue item 'Consistency of Safety Across Decoding Parameters', here applied to sandbagging rather than refusal).
