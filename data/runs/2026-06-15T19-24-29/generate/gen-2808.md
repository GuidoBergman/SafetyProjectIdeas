# Does Position Bias in Judges Interact With Safety Content?

**Problem:** Position bias in pairwise LLM judges is well documented for general quality, but whether it is stronger or weaker when one option is a refusal/safe response and the other is harmful compliance is unexamined.

**Direction:** Construct pairs where option A is a safe refusal and option B is a harmful-compliant answer (and vice versa), present in both orders to an open judge, and measure how often position flips the safety verdict. Compare position-bias magnitude on safety pairs vs neutral quality pairs to test interaction.

**Why it matters:** If position bias distorts safety verdicts more than quality verdicts, safety leaderboards using pairwise judges may be systematically wrong in a way order-randomization must correct. Cheap, decisive, lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** Documented position bias across model families (LLM-as-a-Judge surveys, arXiv 2601.13649; UDA arXiv 2508.09724) — order sensitivity addressable by rearranging options, but untested on safety-specific pairs.
