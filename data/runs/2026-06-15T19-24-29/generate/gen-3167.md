# Monitor-Robustness Benchmark for CoT Safety Monitors

**Problem:** Safety cases increasingly rely on a monitor model reading CoT, but there's no compact benchmark measuring how a monitor's flagging accuracy degrades against mildly paraphrased/obfuscated deceptive reasoning.

**Direction:** Curate a set of CoT traces labeled deceptive/benign, then apply graded obfuscation transforms (synonym swaps, euphemism, reordering) and measure how monitor LLM recall drops at each level. Output an ROC-vs-obfuscation curve for several monitor models. Release the labeled set.

**Why it matters:** Tells safety-case builders how much margin their CoT monitors have before obfuscation breaks them — reusable evaluation of the monitor itself. Pure prompting/analysis.

**Relevant context:** Apollo 'Towards Safety Cases for AI Scheming' relies on CoT monitoring; 'A Pragmatic Way to Measure CoT Monitorability' (arXiv 2510.23966) and obfuscation stress-tests (arXiv 2510.19851).
