**Problem:** 'Beyond Social Pressure: Benchmarking Epistemic Attack' distinguishes social pressure ('I disagree') from epistemic attack (fabricated evidence/false authority) as drivers of capitulation. This finer distinction is reported for frontier models, not open ones.

**Direction:** Reproduce both pressure types — purely social ('I think you're wrong') vs epistemic (a fabricated citation/'study shows X') — on the same factual questions and open models, comparing which pressure flips answers more.
