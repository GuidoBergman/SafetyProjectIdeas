# A Reproducibility Scorecard for Published Mech-Interp Steering Claims

**Problem:** A success state: 'published interpretability safety results reliably reproduce, so the field can build on them.' Backcasting from 'a trustworthy interp literature', a prerequisite is an independent reproduction of a small set of headline steering/feature claims on open models with effect-size reporting.

**Direction:** Select 3-4 concrete, open-model steering/feature claims (e.g., a specific refusal feature's effect, the misalignment direction's add/ablate effect). Re-run each with the published open weights/SAEs, report point estimates with confidence intervals, and document any setup details needed to reproduce. Publish a scorecard of reproduced / partial / failed.

**Why it matters:** Independent reproduction is undersupplied and high-leverage: it tells the community which interp safety tools are trustworthy. Purely counterfactual lab-external work; no training, public artifacts only.

**Relevant context:** Feature consistency / circuit instability concerns (arXiv 2505.20254, 2603.00523) and divergent steering side-effect reports (arXiv 2411.11296, 2601.03047) indicate reproducibility is uncertain. circuit-tracer, Gemma Scope, and open misalignment organisms (arXiv 2506.11618) provide reproducible targets.
