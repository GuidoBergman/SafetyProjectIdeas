# Suppressing the Plan: Causal Verification of Rhyme Pre-Activation in Open Models

**Problem:** The frontier finding that suppressing a planned rhyme word makes the model produce a different ending was demonstrated only with proprietary feature dictionaries. Whether the same causal lever exists at the level of residual-stream directions in small open models is untested.

**Direction:** In GPT-2 / Pythia, identify the residual-stream direction or token logit associated with a candidate rhyme word at the newline position (via logit lens), then causally ablate or down-project that direction during generation of the next line and measure whether the model switches to an alternative rhyme. Quantify intervention success rate and output coherence (perplexity) as in published intervention-evaluation metrics.

**Why it matters:** A cheap, training-free causal test of forward planning gives independent researchers a tool to detect and disrupt premeditated multi-token behavior, the mechanistic precursor of plans a model might not verbalize in its chain-of-thought.

**Relevant context:** Biology of an LLM (2025): suppressing 'rabbit' feature changes the line ending; injecting 'green' redirects planning. Bi-causal steering literature reports intervention success rate and coherence-intervention tradeoff metrics. TransformerLens supports activation patching/ablation.
