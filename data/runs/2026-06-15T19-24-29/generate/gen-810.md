# A Benchmark for Post-Deployment Monitoring Commitments and Their Verifiability

**Problem:** Policies promise post-deployment monitoring (misuse detection, incident channels, capability re-evaluation), but the specifics and whether any external signal could confirm monitoring is happening are rarely measured. No instrument scores post-deployment monitoring commitments.

**Direction:** Extract post-deployment monitoring commitments from public policies, classify each by what is monitored, reporting cadence, and external verifiability, and score labs on a monitoring-maturity rubric. Cross-reference with whether labs have actually published any post-deployment monitoring results.

**Why it matters:** Post-deployment monitoring is a named FLI Index domain and a weak spot in many frameworks; a verifiability-focused benchmark highlights where promises lack any checkable output, using public materials only.

**Relevant context:** FLI AI Safety Index (post-deployment monitoring domain); METR Common Elements; 'Effective Mitigations for Systemic Risks from GPAI' (arXiv 2412.02145).
