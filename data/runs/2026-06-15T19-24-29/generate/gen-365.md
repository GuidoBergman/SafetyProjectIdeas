# Jailbreak x Sycophancy: Does Social Pressure Compound With Refusal Bypass?

**Problem:** Two failure modes: jailbreak susceptibility and sycophancy (yielding to user assertions/pressure). Each is documented separately. Whether stacking sycophantic framing ('you already agreed', 'experts confirm this is fine') on top of a structural jailbreak super-additively increases compliance with harmful requests is unmeasured on open models.

**Direction:** Construct harmful requests in a 2x2: {plain, jailbreak wrapper} x {neutral, sycophancy-inducing social pressure}. On open models, measure attack success and test for interaction. Success criterion: an interaction estimate showing whether sycophancy and jailbreak compound, with the framings that produce the largest super-additive effect.

**Why it matters:** Sycophancy is a diffuse, fuzzy-reward problem labs struggle to measure; its compound with jailbreaks reflects realistic manipulative users. Evaluation-only, open models, consumer GPU, no lab data.

**Relevant context:** Sycophancy is a documented RLHF artifact; arXiv:2406.05946 frames jailbreaks as exploiting shallow alignment. The novelty is the explicit two-failure interaction design rather than either alone.
