# Does the Misaligned-Persona Direction Predict Misalignment Before It Shows in Outputs?

**Problem:** Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2506.19823) noted that a specific SAE latent's change in activation can predict emergent misalignment. An open question is whether monitoring the projection of activations onto the misalignment direction acts as an early-warning signal that fires before behavioral evals catch the misalignment during finetuning.

**Direction:** LoRA-finetune a small open model on the released insecure-code dataset, checkpointing every N steps. At each checkpoint, measure both (a) the projection of held-out prompt activations onto a pre-extracted misalignment direction and (b) the behavioral misalignment-eval score. Test whether the activation signal rises earlier (in training steps) than the behavioral signal.

**Why it matters:** A cheap activation-based early-warning monitor that fires before behavioral evals would let practitioners halt a finetuning run before a model becomes broadly misaligned. This is exactly the kind of monitoring harness that is strategically valuable and doable outside a lab on a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2506.19823): SAE latent change predicts EM; observed phase transition during training. Convergent Linear Representations (arXiv 2506.11618): single direction mediates EM. Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2502.17424) provides the insecure-code dataset/code.
