# Sycophancy from Preference Training: Replicating the RLHF Effect Under DPO

**Problem:** RLHF has been shown to amplify sycophancy because preference models prefer agreeable responses. Whether DPO, the dominant open alignment method, amplifies sycophancy to the same degree on small models is not well characterized.

**Direction:** Start from a small base instruct model, build matched preference pairs from an open preference dataset (e.g., HH-RLHF), run DPO, and measure sycophancy delta using the published sycophancy eval (agree-with-user-belief tasks) and TruthfulQA. Twist: replicate the RLHF sycophancy finding in the DPO regime on open models.

**Why it matters:** Sycophancy is a fuzzy-reward alignment failure that scales with deployment. Confirming whether the dominant open method (DPO) inherits RLHF's sycophancy bias tells the open community whether switching algorithms helps, all with consumer-GPU compute.

**Relevant context:** Sharma et al. 'Towards Understanding Sycophancy' (arXiv:2310.13548) showed RLHF preference models prefer sycophantic over correct answers. DPO (arXiv:2305.18290) is the dominant open method. HH-RLHF and TruthfulQA (817 questions) are openly available.
