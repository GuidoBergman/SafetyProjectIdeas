# Adversarial input that neutralizes a steering vector

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. list robustness to adversarial manipulation as an open problem: can attackers craft inputs that neutralize steering? This is unstudied for safety vectors and is a medium-horizon threat (adversaries adapting to representation-level defenses).

**Direction:** Take an honesty steering vector applied to an open model. Use simple gradient-free prompt search (greedy token insertion / suffix optimization on a small budget) to find prompt prefixes that minimize the steering vector's effect on output behavior. Quantify how much steering effectiveness drops with adversarial prefixes vs benign ones, on a held-out set.

**Why it matters:** If cheap prompt-level attacks defeat representation steering, it cannot be relied on as a standalone safety control. Pure red-team evaluation, no training, valuable outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Open Problem: robustness of concept vectors to adversarial manipulation (Beaglehole et al.). Related to 'Analysing the Safety Pitfalls of Steering Vectors' (arXiv:2603.24543).
