# CombineSteer: Measuring Interference When Stacking Safety Steering Vectors

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. show concept vectors can be combined for multi-concept steering, but there is no open metric for how much two safety steering vectors interfere or degrade each other when applied together.

**Direction:** For pairs of CAA safety vectors (e.g. refusal + honesty, anti-sycophancy + corrigibility), measure each effect alone, then combined, computing an interference score (does adding vector B weaken or flip vector A's effect?) and a capability/fluency collateral-damage score. Produce a small NxN interference matrix on an open 7B model.

**Why it matters:** Real safety deployments will stack multiple controls; silent interference could disable one control while enabling another. An open interference matrix is reusable and directly extends the multi-concept combination result without any training.

**Relevant context:** Surprising result: vectors 'can be combined for multi-concept steering' (Beaglehole et al.). Related work (MSRS, SwordBench arxiv 2605.16372) shows non-orthogonality causes interference and collateral damage.
