# Faithfulness Across Model Families at Fixed Capability Level

**Problem:** Faithfulness comparisons usually confound model family with capability. Holding accuracy roughly constant, do different open model families (Qwen, Llama, Mistral, Gemma) differ systematically in hint-verbalization, suggesting training-recipe rather than capability drives faithfulness?

**Direction:** Select open instruct models of similar size/benchmark accuracy from 3-4 families. Run an identical hint-verbalization battery. Compare reveal rates across families with matched accuracy. Test whether family (training recipe) explains variance beyond raw capability.

**Why it matters:** If faithfulness is recipe-determined rather than capability-determined, it can be deliberately improved — a hopeful and actionable finding. Disentangling these factors needs only inference across public models.

**Relevant context:** Chen et al. 2025 (arXiv:2505.05410) compared a few models but not at matched capability across families; Lanham noted scale effects. Matched-capability cross-family comparison is the controlled variation.
