# Steering vs. SAE Features for Safety Detection: A Head-to-Head on the Same Open Model

**Problem:** AxBench found simple difference-in-means beats SAE features for steering/detection, but this hasn't been replicated specifically for SAFETY concepts where SAEs are heavily promoted as the interpretable choice.

**Direction:** On Gemma-2-2B with open GemmaScope SAEs, pick 3 safety concepts. For each, build (a) a difference-in-means concept vector and (b) the best matching SAE feature(s). Compare them on a detection task (separating safe/unsafe text) and a small steering task. Report which method wins per safety concept and whether SAEs' interpretability is worth their performance gap.

**Why it matters:** Settles, for safety specifically, whether the simpler representation-engineering method or the fashionable SAE approach should be trusted. Uses fully open GemmaScope weights — maximal counterfactual value, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in AxBench (arXiv 2501.17148: simple baselines beat SAEs) and GemmaScope (arXiv 2408.05147, open SAEs on Gemma-2). Subfield notes RepE has open implementations.
