# Werewolf/Mafia Social-Deduction Deception Arena

**Problem:** Social-deduction games elicit strategic deception (bluffing, feints), but there is no lightweight, reproducible open-model arena measuring deception skill and detectability separately.

**Direction:** Implement a minimal Werewolf/Mafia engine where open models play roles requiring deception. Log every statement and tag truth/lie against ground-truth roles. Measure each model's deception success rate AND how often its lies are betrayed in its own visible CoT vs. its public messages.

**Why it matters:** A controlled, ground-truth testbed for deception capability and CoT leakage of deceptive intent — exactly the 'attacks without verbalizing the plan' model-organism slice. Fully self-contained, no training.

**Relevant context:** WOLF 'Werewolf-based Observations for LLM Deception and Falsehoods' (arXiv 2512.09187) establishes the paradigm; aligns with the secret-goal model-organism steering priority.
