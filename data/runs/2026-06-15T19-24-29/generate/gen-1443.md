# SteerSeedVariance: Quantifying How Much Contrastive-Set Choice Changes a Steering Vector

**Problem:** Steering vectors are derived from a contrastive dataset, but there is no benchmark measuring how sensitive the resulting vector (and its effects) are to which/how many contrastive pairs were sampled.

**Direction:** For a fixed behavior, resample the contrastive set multiple times (different pairs, different sizes from ~32 up to full) and measure variance in the extracted vector (cosine spread) and in downstream steering effect. Produce a 'data-sensitivity' curve and recommend a minimum reliable contrastive-set size.

**Why it matters:** If steering vectors are unstable to dataset sampling, published single-run results are unreliable; quantifying this gives reproducibility guidance the field lacks. Cheap, inference-only, beginner-appropriate statistics.

**Relevant context:** CAA datasets range 290-1000 samples (nrimsky/CAA); the effect of subsampling on vector stability is unmeasured. Variance/bootstrap analysis fits the participants' statistics background.
