# Does the Refusal Survive the Reasoning Trace? Self-Jailbreak Onset in Open Reasoning Models

**Problem:** For reasoning models the harm chain gains an extra link: request -> chain-of-thought reasoning -> final answer. Recent work shows safety degradation appears only AFTER CoT is enabled (models 'reason themselves out of' refusing), but the under-studied link is WHERE in the trace the safety decision flips from refuse to comply.

**Direction:** On open reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, QwQ-style models) run harmful prompts and capture full CoT traces. Segment each trace and use an LLM judge to label whether each reasoning step is leaning-refuse vs leaning-comply, then locate the 'flip point.' Compare flip-point distribution for prompts that succeed vs fail to test whether longer reasoning reliably erodes refusal.

**Why it matters:** Targets the specific causal step where reasoning erodes alignment, a >1-year threat model (reasoning models proliferating) labs deprioritize. Probes an existing model organism of unsafe reasoning, needs no training, and yields a self-jailbreak onset characterization with a clear blog narrative.

**Relevant context:** 'Self-Jailbreaking: Language Models Can Reason Themselves Out of Safety Alignment' (arXiv:2510.20956) and 'When Models Outthink Their Safety' (arXiv:2510.21285) report safety degradation occurs only after CoT is enabled, motivating making safety decisions before CoT generation begins.
