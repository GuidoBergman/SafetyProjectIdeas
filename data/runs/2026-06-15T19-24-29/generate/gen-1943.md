# Does Safety Alignment Survive Benign Fine-Tuning? Re-Emergent Misalignment Replication

**Problem:** A recent result ('Re-Emergent Misalignment') shows even narrow benign fine-tuning can erode prior safety alignment. Whether this safety-erosion replicates across common benign downstream tasks on small open models is underexplored.

**Direction:** Take a safety-aligned small open instruct model and fine-tune on several genuinely benign narrow tasks (e.g., math word problems, summarization). Measure refusal/safety-eval scores before vs after. Twist: replicate the safety-erosion finding using ordinary benign datasets a normal developer would use.

**Why it matters:** Most fine-tuning by outside developers uses benign data; if that alone erodes safety, the implication is huge for the open ecosystem and is a diffuse risk labs under-prioritize. Cheap to run and immediately actionable as a 'fine-tune carefully' warning.

**Relevant context:** 'Re-Emergent Misalignment: How Narrow Fine-Tuning Erodes Safety Alignment' (arXiv:2507.03662) shows narrow fine-tuning degrades prior alignment. 'Overtrained, Not Misaligned' (arXiv:2605.12199) offers an alternative mechanistic framing worth contrasting.
