# From 'We Can Certify Honest Equilibria' to Measuring Whether Truth Is a Schelling Point for LLM Coordinators

**Problem:** Success state: in multi-agent oversight, honest reporting is a stable equilibrium / natural focal point rather than collusion. The gap: do open LLM agents, when coordinating without communication on what to report to an overseer, converge on truth as a Schelling point, or on a more convenient collusive answer?

**Direction:** Set up a Schelling-style coordination game: multiple instances of an open model independently must pick a single answer to report to an overseer about a fact, rewarded for matching each other (not for truth). Measure how often the focal point they coordinate on is the true answer vs. a salient false one. Vary truth salience. Prompt-only, cheap.

**Why it matters:** Backcasting from honest-equilibrium oversight, the precondition is whether truth is the natural focal point. This is a model-organism Schelling-game study (explicitly favored in the brief), scoped to prompting open models, probing collusion risk that frontier labs underweight and that needs no lab resources.
