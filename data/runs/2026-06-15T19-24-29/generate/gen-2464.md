# Untrusted-Plan Monitoring: Catching Sabotage at the Planning Stage

**Problem:** Recent work proposes controlling untrusted AI by monitoring its PLANS rather than its actions, on the theory that intent is more legible before execution. Whether plan-level monitoring out-detects action-level monitoring in a small reproducible setting is open.

**Direction:** Have a small open model first produce an explicit plan, then execute it, on tasks with an injected sabotage side-goal. Compare a monitor's sabotage-detection AUC when shown (a) the plan only vs (b) the final actions only vs (c) both. Report whether plan visibility provides earlier, more reliable detection and how readily the model hides intent in the plan.

**Why it matters:** Plan-level monitoring could catch harmful intent before irreversible actions occur, addressing the 'prevent irreversible harmful actions' open problem. Testing it on accessible models is reproducible and informs a structural, monitor-strength-independent defense.

**Relevant context:** Factor(U,T): Controlling Untrusted AI by Monitoring their Plans (arxiv 2512.14745). UK AISI open problem: prevent agentic systems from performing irreversible harmful actions. Connects to CoT monitoring informativeness (GPT-5 system card).
