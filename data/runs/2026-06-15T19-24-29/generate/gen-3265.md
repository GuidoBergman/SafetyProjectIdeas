# A CoT-Monitorability Scorecard: Faithfulness vs Verbosity Tradeoff Across Open Models

**Problem:** Success state: every model shipped with reasoning has a published, comparable monitorability score so deployers know how much to trust its CoT. Backcasting, we need a cheap, standardized scorecard combining faithfulness (does the CoT reflect the real reason) and verbosity (is the relevant info even present).

**Direction:** Implement a small monitorability harness: inject a hidden cue (e.g., a hint in the prompt) that should change the answer, then check whether the CoT mentions the cue (faithfulness) and whether decisive info is verbalized (verbosity). Compute a combined score for 4 open reasoning models and rank them.

**Why it matters:** Monitorability underpins CoT-based safety cases; a standardized, reproducible scorecard lets the community compare models and track drift over generations — strategic monitoring infrastructure independent of any lab.

**Relevant context:** 'Measuring Chain-of-Thought Monitorability Through Faithfulness and Verbosity' (arXiv 2510.27378) and 'A Pragmatic Way to Measure CoT Monitorability' (2510.23966); Open Phil RFP starred encoded-reasoning problem.
