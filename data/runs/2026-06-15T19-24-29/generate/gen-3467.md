# Answer-Before-Reasoning Detector via Early-Answer Probing

**Problem:** Evidence suggests models often decide the answer before generating the CoT, then rationalize. Lanham's early-answering test exists but there's no reusable diagnostic that maps, per task, how early in the CoT the final answer becomes determined (the 'answer-commitment point').

**Direction:** For each question, truncate the CoT at increasing fractions (10%, 25%, 50%...) and force an answer at each cut, measuring at what fraction the truncated answer matches the full answer. The earlier the commitment, the more the later CoT is decorative. Produce per-task answer-commitment curves for open models.

**Why it matters:** If answers are committed before reasoning, the CoT is rationalization and cannot be trusted as a monitorable record of how the model decided — a core faithfulness concern. Purely behavioral and reproducible.

**Relevant context:** Early-answering is a Lanham (2023) corruption test. 'Decoding Answers Before Chain-of-Thought' (arXiv 2603.01437) provides pre-CoT-probe evidence answers form early. No turnkey answer-commitment-curve tool for open models exists.
