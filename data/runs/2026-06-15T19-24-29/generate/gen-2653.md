# Plan-Level vs. Action-Level Monitoring: Where Does Intent First Become Visible?

**Problem:** Risk: action-level monitors only see harm once it is nearly committed. Causal chain: agent forms a plan -> executes actions -> monitor reads actions. Recent Factor(U,T) work monitors PLANS instead of actions. The under-studied link is, across a trajectory, at which stage harmful intent first becomes detectable: in the early stated plan, or only in late concrete actions, and how much earlier plan-monitoring buys you in steps-to-detection.

**Direction:** On malign agent trajectories, run a monitor over (a) the model's initial plan/intent statement and (b) each subsequent action. Measure the earliest detection point under each regime and the lead-time advantage of plan-monitoring. Identify cases where the plan looks benign but actions turn harmful (plan-monitoring blind spots).

**Why it matters:** Earlier detection means more time to intervene before irreversible harm, the crux of runtime control. Quantifying plan-vs-action detection timing tests a fresh published protocol and tells blue teams where to look first. Open-model replay, no training.

**Relevant context:** Factor(U,T): Controlling Untrusted AI by Monitoring their Plans (arXiv:2512.14745) proposes plan-level monitoring. SHADE-Arena (arXiv:2506.15740) trajectories include planning + execution. GPT-5 system card: CoT (which includes plans) is more informative than action-only monitoring.
