# CollateralBench: A Capability-Cost Benchmark for Safety Steering

**Problem:** Papers report steering effect sizes but rarely a standardized measure of the capability cost (collateral damage) incurred, making it impossible to compare steering methods on a safety-vs-utility frontier.

**Direction:** Build a fixed mini-suite of capability probes (a small slice of MMLU, GSM8K, and a fluency/perplexity check) and run it with each safety steering vector active at its effective coefficient. Plot each vector on a safety-effect vs capability-retention frontier. Provide the suite as a one-call tool for any new steering vector.

**Why it matters:** A shared utility-cost axis is what the missing steering benchmark most needs; it lets the community reject steering methods that quietly lobotomize the model. Pure inference, single GPU, no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** No steering-specific benchmark exists (Beaglehole et al.). CAA paper notes steering 'minimally reduces capabilities' but without a standardized suite. SwordBench introduces 'collateral damage' for vision; this ports the idea to LLMs.
