# Does Debate Help More Than Consultancy When the Honest Side Is the WEAKER Debater?

**Problem:** Debate's safety rests on the honest debater winning. Engels et al. and others assume rough capability parity; but a key worry is asymmetric capability where the honest position is argued by a weaker model. Whether debate still beats consultancy in that regime is the load-bearing case.

**Direction:** Generate debate transcripts with an open model on a verifiable QA task, but deliberately handicap the honest debater (smaller model / fewer tokens) while the dishonest debater is strong. Have a fixed judge decide. Compare judge accuracy under debate vs consultancy across a sweep of honest-debater handicap levels.

**Why it matters:** If debate's truth-tracking collapses precisely when the truth-teller is outmatched, the protocol fails in the adversarial regime that matters for superhuman oversight. A direct, cheap test using open models — no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Rahman et al. 2025 (arXiv:2506.02175) and Engels et al. 2025 (arXiv:2504.18530): debate beats consultancy under rough parity; Debate NSO 51.7% at Elo gap 400. Weak-honest-debater regime under-tested.
