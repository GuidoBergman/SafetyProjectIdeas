# Distillation Lineage and Homogeneity: Do Children of the Same Teacher Cluster?

**Problem:** Many open models are distilled from a few teachers (GPT-4 outputs, R1). The Hivemind effect may be partly explained by *shared training-data lineage* rather than independent convergence. This causal angle is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Replicate inter-model homogeneity measurement but group models by known/declared lineage (e.g. R1-distilled vs. GPT-distilled vs. independently RLHF'd). Test whether same-lineage models are significantly more homogeneous than cross-lineage pairs on open-ended prompts. Twist: use clustering/dendrograms on output-similarity to see if the model 'family tree' recovers training lineage.

**Why it matters:** Identifying lineage as a driver of correlated failure tells deployers a concrete diversification rule (avoid same-teacher models), a directly actionable, lab-independent contribution to concentration-risk mitigation.

**Relevant context:** Extends Artificial Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954) and the distillation-transfer literature; uses open models with documented lineages. Output-similarity clustering only.
