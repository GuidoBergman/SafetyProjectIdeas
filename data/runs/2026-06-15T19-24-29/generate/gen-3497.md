# The 'I Don't Know' Suppression Effect: Does Adding a Persona Reduce Honest Abstention?

**Problem:** Two findings combine into an untested interaction: (1) models under-abstain due to scoring incentives, and (2) expert personas suppress honest self-representation. Whether assigning a confident expert persona specifically reduces honest 'I don't know' rates on hard questions is open.

**Direction:** On hard factual items where abstention is the honest move, compare a neutral assistant prompt vs. a confident-expert persona. Measure abstention rate and confident-wrong rate in each condition across open models, isolating the persona's effect on honest uncertainty disclosure.

**Why it matters:** Persona-driven confidence inflation is a plausible, common, and harmful failure (e.g. an 'expert' chatbot that never says it doesn't know). Quantifying it connects two literatures and is directly useful for safe system-prompt design. Pure prompting.

**Relevant context:** Self-transparency/credential-fabrication results (arXiv:2511.21569); OpenAI hallucination-incentive argument on abstention; I-CALM (arXiv:2604.03904). Anthropic recommended directions flags persona effects.
