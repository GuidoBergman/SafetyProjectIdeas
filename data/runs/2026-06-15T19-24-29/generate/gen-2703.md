# Data Poisoning Backdoor x Monitor Generalization: One Trigger Defeats Two Layers

**Problem:** A training-time backdoor (data poisoning) compounds with monitoring: a backdoor trigger can be chosen to lie in the monitor's blind region, so the same trigger both elicits harmful behavior AND evades the behavioral monitor. Two defenses (data integrity and runtime monitoring) share a single point of failure.

**Direction:** Without training, use an open model with a known/prompted 'backdoor-like' trigger phrase (or a publicly available poisoned/finetuned small model if findable) and a behavioral monitor. Measure whether triggered harmful behavior is caught at the same rate as untriggered harmful behavior. Quantify the trigger's monitor-evasion bonus.

**Why it matters:** If triggers can be optimized to dodge monitors, layered defenses are correlated. Even a prompt-simulated backdoor demonstrates the compound principle cheaply and lab-independently.

**Relevant context:** Anomaly detection for novel/dangerous behaviors (Anthropic/ARC open problem); probes fail OOD (JailbreaksOverTime arXiv:2504.19440) — a trigger is engineered OOD.
