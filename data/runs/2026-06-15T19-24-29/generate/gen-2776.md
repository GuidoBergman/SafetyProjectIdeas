# Decoding-Temperature Sweep for ForesightSafety Frontier-Risk Items

**Problem:** ForesightSafety Bench covers frontier risks (self-replication, embodied harm, loss of control) most benchmarks ignore, but published evaluations report single-decode results. Safety on these high-stakes dimensions may be fragile to sampling settings.

**Direction:** Take a subset of ForesightSafety Bench text items in the catastrophic/self-replication/agentic-autonomy pillars and run open models across a temperature/top-p grid plus best-of-n. Measure how often at least one sample in n produces an unsafe response (worst-of-n safety) versus the greedy result.

**Why it matters:** A motivated user takes the worst of many samples; reporting greedy-only safety on frontier-risk items is dangerously optimistic. Quantifying the worst-of-n gap on a brand-new benchmark is high-novelty and lab-independent. The benchmark is open on GitHub.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench (arXiv 2602.14135, Beijing-AISI, Feb 2026): 22 pillars, 94 risk dimensions incl. self-replication, embodied AI, catastrophic/existential; open on GitHub (Beijing-AISI/ForesightSafety-Bench). Reveals 'widespread safety vulnerabilities of frontier AI.'
