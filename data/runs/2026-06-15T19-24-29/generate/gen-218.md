# Shallow-Alignment KL Signature Replicated as a Cheap Open-Model Robustness Predictor

**Problem:** Qi et al. visualize per-token KL between aligned and base distributions to argue alignment is shallow, but this signature has not been turned into a comparable scalar metric and correlated with actual attack robustness across many open models. A cheap pre-attack predictor of jailbreak susceptibility would be valuable.

**Direction:** Replicate the per-token aligned-vs-base KL measurement across ~6 open instruct models (with available base siblings), summarize each as a 'depth-of-alignment' scalar (e.g., KL mass beyond token k), then correlate that scalar with each model's measured prefilling/decoding ASR. Twist: turn a visualization into a predictive metric and test it across families.

**Why it matters:** If a one-pass KL signature predicts attack robustness, small actors get a cheap triage tool without running full attack suites — strategically valuable monitoring infrastructure. Inference-only on base/instruct pairs, fits one GPU and 30h, with a clear correlation success criterion.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. 2024 (arXiv:2406.05946): per-token KL divergence shows alignment concentrated in early tokens; this shallowness explains prefilling/decoding/suffix attacks. Paired base/instruct open models on HuggingFace.
