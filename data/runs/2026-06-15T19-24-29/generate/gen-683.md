# Replicating the AI Safety Index Across an Overlooked Dimension: Whistleblowing & Internal Dissent

**Problem:** FLI's AI Safety Index scores labs across six domains but does not isolate the institutional mechanisms (whistleblower protections, non-disparagement clauses, internal escalation paths) that let safety concerns surface — a dimension that recent lab controversies suggest is decisive.

**Direction:** Apply the Index's public-evidence methodology to a new 'internal dissent & whistleblowing' dimension. Build ~8 indicators (existence of whistleblower policy, public non-disparagement commitments, board safety oversight, anonymous reporting channels) and score each frontier lab from public sources, validating coding with a second annotator. Produce a mini-index and compare its ranking to the official Index.

**Why it matters:** Whether employees can safely raise alarms is a medium-horizon governance lever frontier labs deprioritize; measuring it from public evidence is exactly the kind of diffuse, fuzzy-reward evaluation that needs no lab resources and complements existing indices.
