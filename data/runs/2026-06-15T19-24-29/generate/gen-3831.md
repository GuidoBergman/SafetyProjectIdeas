# Reproducibility Replicate-Variance Bounds for Agent Safety Evals

**Problem:** Agentic safety eval results are reported as single point estimates despite high run-to-run variance from nondeterminism, making safety claims fragile and irreproducible.

**Direction:** Import experimental-design / replication-variance methodology from empirical science. Run a small set of safety-eval tasks N times each across temperatures/seeds, decompose variance (task vs seed vs prompt-perturbation), and derive the number of replicates needed for a stable safety-rate estimate plus proper confidence intervals. Deliver guidance + a reusable variance-reporting template.

**Why it matters:** Improves the trustworthiness of all agentic safety evaluation by importing rigorous replication standards; cheap, methodologically valuable, and entirely lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** ReliabilityBench (arXiv 2601.06112) and AgentAssay (arXiv 2603.02601) emphasize nondeterminism in agent workflows; standard experimental statistics (variance components, replication, CIs) are rarely applied to safety-rate reporting. This methodological transfer is novel and feasible with basic statistics.
