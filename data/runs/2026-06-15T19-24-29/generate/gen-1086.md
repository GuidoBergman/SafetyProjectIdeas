# Jailbreak Transfer as a Homogeneity Metric: Cheap Attacks on One Model, Tested on Many

**Problem:** Jailbreak transferability is known to track representation similarity between source and target models. This implies transfer rate is itself a *behavioral measure of homogeneity*. No one has used a fixed jailbreak suite as a lightweight cross-model homogeneity probe.

**Direction:** Take a public set of known jailbreak prompts (no novel attack development needed) and replicate their effect across 8-10 open models, recording which models break on which prompts. Twist: build a model-by-model 'shared vulnerability matrix' and correlate it with embedding-based output-homogeneity from harmless prompts — testing whether output homogeneity predicts shared exploitability.

**Why it matters:** If models that produce similar benign outputs also share jailbreak vulnerabilities, output-homogeneity becomes a cheap early-warning proxy for correlated security failures. Uses only existing public jailbreaks (no offensive capability built) and open models — high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in 'Jailbreak Transferability Emerges from Shared Representations' (arXiv:2506.12913) and Zou et al. universal-suffix transfer; replicates known transfer with a homogeneity-correlation twist.
