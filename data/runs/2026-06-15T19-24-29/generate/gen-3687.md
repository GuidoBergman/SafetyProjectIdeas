# Trusted-Summariser Defense for Injection: Capability Cost vs Safety Benefit Curve

**Problem:** Inserting a trusted summariser/paraphraser to sanitize untrusted tool outputs is a recommended injection defense, but its cost to legitimate task utility (it may strip needed info) is rarely measured against its ASR reduction.

**Direction:** On AgentDojo, insert a small open paraphraser model between tool output and the agent. Vary paraphrase aggressiveness. Plot a two-axis curve: ASR reduction (safety benefit) vs utility-under-no-attack drop (capability cost). Find the Pareto frontier for a couple of agent models.

**Why it matters:** Turns a hand-wavy recommended defense into a measured cost-benefit trade-off that builders can use. Open benchmark + small models, no training; prototypes a concrete defensive firewall component favored for counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** AgentDojo (ASR, utility under attack). Search: output collusion / injection mitigated by trusted summariser or paraphraser (Korbak 2025, Motwani 2025). 'Meta SecAlign' (arXiv 2507.02735) is a training-based alternative; this is a training-free defense study.
