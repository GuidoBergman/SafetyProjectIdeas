# Probe-Layer Sensitivity: Which Layer Best Detects Jailbreaks, and How Stable Is It?

**Problem:** Activation-probe defenses pick a layer to read from, but the choice of layer and its stability across attack types is rarely reported. Whether the optimal detection layer is consistent across jailbreak families (prefill vs suffix vs many-shot) is open.

**Direction:** On one open model, extract residual-stream activations at every layer for benign, prefill-attacked, suffix-attacked, and many-shot-attacked prompts. Train a linear probe per layer and plot detection AUROC vs layer for each attack family. Identify whether a single layer suffices or whether multi-layer ensembling is needed (and quantify the gain).

**Why it matters:** Gives small teams a recipe for where to tap activations for cheap monitoring and reveals whether attack-specific layers undermine single-layer deployment. Builds directly on the multi-layer-ensembling result. Linear probes, single GPU, beginner-friendly.

**Relevant context:** 'Linear Probe Accuracy Scales with Model Size and Benefits from Multi-Layer Ensembling' (arXiv:2604.13386) and 'Building Production-Ready Probes For Gemini' (arXiv:2601.11516) motivate layer/ensemble choices.
