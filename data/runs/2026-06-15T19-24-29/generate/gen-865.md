# Schelling-Game Probing of Whether Open Models Could Coordinate to Evade Audits

**Problem:** A governance threat model is that AI systems coordinate (on shared focal points) to behave during evaluation and defect afterward, defeating audits. Whether current open models can actually hit non-obvious Schelling points is largely untested at the small-experiment scale governance discussions assume.

**Direction:** Transfer Schelling's focal-point coordination games. Run a small battery of text-based Schelling coordination games between two instances of an open model (no shared context), measuring how often they converge on non-obvious focal points and whether a simple 'anti-coordination' intervention degrades it. Frame results for audit-evasion governance.

**Why it matters:** A model-organism-style probe of a coordination capability central to whether audit-based governance is robust — exactly the long-horizon, lab-deprioritized question worth doing outside labs. Single open model, a few GPU-hours.
