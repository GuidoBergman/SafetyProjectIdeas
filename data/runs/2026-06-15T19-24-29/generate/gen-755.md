# Does the AIID under-count agentic / autonomous-system incidents?

**Problem:** The AIID is limited to publicly reported incidents, and recent work on agentic-AI incident analysis suggests agent failures lack traceability, hinting these incidents may be systematically under-represented in the database.

**Direction:** Follow up by classifying recent AIID incidents (2024-2026) on whether they involve agentic/autonomous behavior using a coded rubric, then comparing the agentic share over time against the known rapid rise of agentic deployments. Test for an under-counting gap and characterize what agentic incidents do get reported.

**Why it matters:** If agentic incidents are invisible to the main public database, incident-response frameworks will be built blind to the fastest-growing risk class, a medium-horizon threat labs deprioritize. Public-data only.

**Relevant context:** 'Incident Analysis for AI Agents' (arXiv 2508.14231); AIID growth (>1,300 incidents); Open Problem: national incident-response frameworks.
