# Predicting Judge-Human Disagreement Without Probabilities on Safety Items

**Problem:** A recent paper predicts when an LLM-as-judge will disagree with human raters on difficulty assessment WITHOUT generation-time probability signals. Whether the same cheap, probability-free predictors flag judge-human disagreement on SAFETY (harmfulness) items is untested.

**Direction:** Collect a small safety-judgment set with human labels and an open judge's labels. Train a simple logistic-regression/decision-tree on probability-free features (prompt length, presence of safety keywords, hedging words in the response) to predict where judge and human disagree. Report predictive AUC and the most informative features.

**Why it matters:** If we can cheaply predict which items a judge is likely to mis-score, evaluators can route just those items to humans, making safety evals reliable on a tiny human-label budget. Works on logged outputs with no model access beyond an open judge.

**Relevant context:** Predicting Disagreement with Human Raters in LLM-as-a-Judge Difficulty Assessment without Using Generation-Time Probability Signals (arXiv:2605.12422, May 2026).
