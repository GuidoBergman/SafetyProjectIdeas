# Imposed Cognitive Load to Surface Sandbagging

**Problem:** Sandbagging models deliberately underperform while maintaining a coherent cover story. Human lie-detection research shows liars have higher cognitive load than truth-tellers, and imposing extra load degrades liars more than truth-tellers.

**Direction:** Import the 'imposing cognitive load' deception-detection paradigm: take an open model password-locked or prompted to sandbag on a capability eval, then add load (reverse-order task instructions, secondary counting task, tight token budgets, dual-task prompts) and test whether the deceptive/sandbagging condition degrades disproportionately versus an honest baseline — revealing the hidden true capability or breaking the cover story.

**Why it matters:** A novel, training-free elicitation technique to unmask sandbagging that doesn't rely on CoT (which can be obfuscated). Directly targets the open problem of detecting hidden dangerous capabilities; relevant to Gemini-3 'no sandbagging detected' follow-up calling for stronger detectors.

**Relevant context:** Imposing cognitive load degrades liars more than truth-tellers (Frontiers Psychology 2013). Noise injection reveals hidden capabilities of sandbaggers (arXiv:2412.01784) — a related elicitation idea. Password-locking hides capabilities (AI Sandbagging, arXiv:2406.07358). Google: no sandbagging detected in Gemini 3 Pro — calls for better methods.
