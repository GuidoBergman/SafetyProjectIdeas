# Confirmation Bias in Weak Judges: Replicating and Stress-Testing the 2025 Result

**Problem:** Confirmation bias: A challenge for scalable oversight (arXiv:2507.19486, 2025) identifies that judges anchor on a prior hypothesis and weigh confirming evidence more heavily. The strength and triggers of this effect on cheap open models, and whether it compounds across debate rounds, are open.

**Direction:** Give a weak judge model an initial leaning (seeded by the first debater) then present balanced evidence. Measure asymmetry in how confirming vs. disconfirming evidence shifts the final verdict, across 3 open models. Test whether longer debates amplify the anchoring rather than correct it.

**Why it matters:** Confirmation bias is a systematic oversight error a strategic model could exploit by front-loading a plausible framing. A clean replication + amplification test on open models is directly actionable and infrastructure-free.
