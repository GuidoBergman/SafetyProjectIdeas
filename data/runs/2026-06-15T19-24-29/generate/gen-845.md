# Mutation Testing for AI Safety Benchmarks: How Robust Is a 'Pass'?

**Problem:** Governance increasingly relies on benchmark scores as evidence of safety, but a benchmark 'pass' may be brittle to trivial rephrasings — meaning a regulator's evidentiary basis is fragile.

**Direction:** Transfer mutation testing from software engineering. Take a small public safety/refusal benchmark, write a set of semantics-preserving mutation operators (paraphrase, reorder, add filler) applied to prompts via a small open model, and measure how much a model's safety pass-rate shifts under mutation. Report a 'benchmark fragility' score usable as a verification heuristic.

**Why it matters:** Directly addresses the open problem of verifiable, reproducible safety claims: shows whether benchmark-based safety evidence survives perturbation. Counterfactually valuable — labs deprioritize meta-evaluation of their own metrics.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2407.00125 (fault injection in AI systems, DeepCrime mutation operators); arXiv 2504.04372 (mutation-inspired LLM evaluation); 'fragile safety claim' framing from frontier auditing literature.
