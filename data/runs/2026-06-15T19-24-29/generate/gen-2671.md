# Backcasting Resampling Safety: How Many Resamples Until a Suspicious Action Becomes Affordable to Replace?

**Problem:** Success state: a deployment that, like Ctrl-Z resampling, can cheaply replace a suspicious action with a safe one. Backward: we need an empirical cost curve for how many resamples it takes to get a non-suspicious action on tasks where the model is trying to sabotage.

**Direction:** On public APPS backdoor tasks, when the monitor flags an action, resample the untrusted model up to K times and measure the probability that a resample is both useful and non-backdoored. Plot catch/usefulness vs. K. Result: 'a resampling cost curve for a small-model control protocol.'

**Why it matters:** Resampling is a promising control lever but its cost-effectiveness depends on how persistent the attack is. A measured curve on open models is directly useful and needs no lab infra.
