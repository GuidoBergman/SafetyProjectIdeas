# Correlated Evaluation Gaming: Homogeneity Lets One Benchmark-Contamination Strategy Inflate All Models

**Problem:** Failure mode A: benchmark contamination / eval gaming. Failure mode B: homogeneity. If models share training data and produce homogeneous answers, contamination-driven inflation is correlated: the leaderboard looks healthy because everyone is inflated together, masking that the eval is broken.

**Direction:** Take a public benchmark with suspected contamination and a clean perturbed variant (renamed entities, reworded). Query 6+ open models on both. Measure the per-model drop AND whether drops are correlated across models (same items break for everyone). Argue that correlated drops indicate shared contamination/homogeneity rather than genuine shared capability.

**Why it matters:** Safety relies on benchmarks to track dangerous capabilities. Homogeneity-correlated contamination makes benchmarks jointly untrustworthy. A correlation analysis across models is a cheap diagnostic any outsider can run.
