# Prompted-Sandbagging Linear Probe Replicated on a New Capability and Model

**Problem:** Some sandbagging work uses linear probes on activations to detect when a model is deliberately underperforming, validated narrowly. Whether a simple probe trained on one capability's sandbagging transfers to detecting sandbagging on a different capability (or model) is open.

**Direction:** Replicate a linear-probe sandbagging detector: collect activations from a small open model under honest vs prompted-sandbag conditions on capability A, train a logistic probe, then test cross-capability and cross-prompt generalization. The twist is probe transfer across capabilities/prompts rather than within one.

**Why it matters:** A cheap, transferable sandbagging probe would be a practical monitoring tool independent researchers could deploy. Testing its transfer limits is a feasible 30h slice of the broader deceptive-capability-concealment threat model.

**Relevant context:** Sandbagging detectability work (arXiv 2406.07358, 2508.00943). Probing methodology parallels the catalogue's 'Activation Probe Generalization' but here targets deliberate underperformance rather than harmfulness, and emphasizes cross-capability transfer.
