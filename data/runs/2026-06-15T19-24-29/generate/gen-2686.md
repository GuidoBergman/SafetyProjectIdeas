# Backcasting Defense-in-Depth Math: How Independent Are Two Cheap Monitors' Errors?

**Problem:** Success state: a layered control stack whose layers fail independently, so stacking them multiplies safety. Backward: we need to measure whether two cheap monitors actually make independent errors, or whether they miss the SAME backdoors (correlated failure).
