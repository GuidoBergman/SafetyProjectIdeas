# Does Debate Help When Both Debaters Are Wrong?

**Problem:** Debate oversight assumes one debater argues the correct answer. But on hard open-ended tasks, BOTH AI debaters may be confidently wrong (shared model error). The judge's accuracy in this 'no-correct-advocate' regime is rarely measured.

**Direction:** Select QuALITY/ARC questions where a strong open model itself answers incorrectly. Run debate where both debaters are instances of that model arguing for two WRONG answers (the true answer is a third option the judge can still choose). Measure how often the weak judge escapes to the correct answer vs being captured by the louder wrong debater.

**Why it matters:** Correlated errors across AI debaters are a realistic failure of recursive oversight. Measuring whether debate offers any protection when no advocate defends the truth is a sharp test of the protocol's robustness, training-free.

**Relevant context:** Kenton et al. 2024 (arXiv:2407.04622) assign debaters to correct/incorrect answers; the both-wrong case is outside standard setups. Anthropic open problem on oversight layers with systematic errors.
