# Splitting 'detect irreversible agent actions' into a pre-action intent classifier

**Problem:** Agentic safety worries about irreversible harmful actions (autonomous theft, deletion). Control sub-problem: before an agent emits a tool call, can a lightweight classifier on the agent's reasoning/state predict whether the impending action is irreversible/high-impact, enabling a human-in-the-loop gate?

**Direction:** Decompose irreversible-action prevention into a pre-action risk classifier. In a toy tool-use sandbox with a small open model, label tool calls as reversible vs irreversible/high-impact. Train a cheap classifier (on CoT text and/or activations) to flag high-impact calls one step before execution, and measure precision/recall of the gate.

**Why it matters:** A prototype 'irreversibility firewall' is exactly the strategically valuable safety infrastructure favored here, scoped to a small 30h build with open models and one GPU. Addresses the explicit open problem of preventing agentic systems from taking irreversible harmful actions.
