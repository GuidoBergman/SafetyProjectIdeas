# Probing the Look-Ahead Planning Signal in a Small Open Model

**Problem:** Anthropic's Circuit Tracing found Claude 3.5 Haiku plans rhyming words ahead, and Unlocking the Future (arXiv:2406.16033) localizes look-ahead planning to mid-layer MHSA output at the last token. Whether this last-token mid-layer planning signal exists in small open models, and whether it correlates with capability, is open.

**Direction:** On a short planning task (e.g. simple maze/path or constrained-poetry generation) with a small open model, train a linear probe on mid-layer last-token activations to decode the future committed choice before it is emitted. Compare probe decodability across 2-3 model sizes to test whether planning strength scales with capability.

**Why it matters:** Whether models internally plan ahead (vs. greedily decode) is central to deceptive-planning threat models; a cheap probe study across sizes tests the surprising Anthropic result outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Circuit Tracing: Claude 3.5 Haiku plans rhyming words ahead. Unlocking the Future: Exploring Look-Ahead Planning Mechanistic Interpretability in LLMs (arXiv:2406.16033): mid-layer MHSA output at last token can decode the decision.
