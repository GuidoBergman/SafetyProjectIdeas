# Hivemind in Hazard Identification: Do Models Share the Same Risk Blind Spots?

**Problem:** A safety-critical use of LLMs is flagging risks (in plans, code, instructions). If many models fail to flag the *same* hazards, layered LLM safety review is far weaker than it appears. The Hivemind method hasn't been applied to hazard-detection coverage.

**Direction:** Replicate homogeneity analysis on a hazard-identification task: give 8-10 open models a set of plans/instructions containing planted hazards (e.g. unsafe lab steps, risky financial advice) and record which hazards each model flags. Twist: compute the 'shared miss rate' — hazards missed by ALL models — and compare to what independent detectors would predict, directly quantifying correlated safety blind spots.

**Why it matters:** Layered LLM oversight is a core safety strategy; demonstrating correlated misses quantifies how much defense-in-depth homogeneity erodes. This is a model-organism-flavored evaluation of a fuzzy-reward task, ideal for lab-independent work.

**Relevant context:** Applies Artificial Hivemind's shared-blind-spot framing (arXiv:2510.22954) to a hazard-detection evaluation; open models, hand-built planted-hazard set.
