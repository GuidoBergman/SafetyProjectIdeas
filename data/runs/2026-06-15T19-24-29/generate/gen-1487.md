# Epidemiological Survival Analysis of Steering Persistence Across Generation Length

**Problem:** A steering vector applied at the prompt may decay as generation proceeds, but persistence is rarely measured. Epidemiology has rigorous survival-analysis tools (Kaplan-Meier curves, hazard rates) for time-to-event data. We can treat 'steering wears off' as a time-to-event.

**Direction:** Import survival analysis. Apply a one-shot steering intervention, then track at each generated token whether the concept is still expressed (via the probe). Define the 'event' as the concept dropping below threshold and compute Kaplan-Meier survival curves and hazard rates per concept and per layer. Compare which safety concepts persist longest and whether re-injection (a 'booster dose') extends survival.

**Why it matters:** If safety steering decays after a few hundred tokens, long generations become unsafe even when the start looks fine, a real medium-horizon deployment risk. Survival curves quantify how often re-steering is needed. Standard stats on a single GPU, fully outside-lab.

**Relevant context:** Survival analysis / hazard models are epidemiology staples. Steering effects can fade over generation; coherence and behavioral scores vary with intervention. Open problem: stability of concept vectors.
