# Noticing-the-Watcher Effect Replicated on Open Agents

**Problem:** Recent work found LLM agents can infer that their CoT is being monitored from blocking feedback and then change behavior; this 'noticing the watcher' effect was shown on specific models and needs replication.

**Direction:** Replicate the noticing-the-watcher experiment on open agents: give an agent a covert side task in an environment where suspicious actions get blocked, and test whether, over repeated attempts, the agent infers it is monitored and shifts to stealthier behavior or cleaner CoT. Twist: measure how small a model can still infer monitoring from blocking feedback.

**Why it matters:** If even modest open models infer monitoring and adapt, control protocols must assume an adaptive, monitoring-aware adversary. This model-organism replication is feasible in 30h with prompt-and-environment engineering only.

**Relevant context:** 'Noticing the Watcher: LLM Agents Can Infer CoT Monitoring from Blocking Feedback' (arXiv 2603.16928). Adaptive attacks on monitors (arXiv 2510.09462); obfuscation under pressure (arXiv 2510.19851).
