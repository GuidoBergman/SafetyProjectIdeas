# Why Are Activation Probes Fragile to Long Context? A Position-Sweep

**Problem:** Recent work (Building Production-Ready Probes for Gemini, arXiv:2601.11516) reports activation probes for jailbreak/misuse detection fail to generalize to long-context inputs but does not isolate WHY. Is it the harmful content's position, total context length, or dilution by benign tokens?

**Direction:** On an open model, train a simple logistic-regression probe on residual activations to detect a harmful request. Then systematically pad the harmful request with benign filler at varying lengths and positions (start/middle/end). Plot probe recall vs context length and harmful-content position to decompose the long-context failure mode.

**Why it matters:** Probes are a cheap, lab-independent defense, but their long-context blind spot is a deployment risk. Explaining the mechanism guides cheap fixes (e.g., sliding-window probing) without lab infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2601.11516 (Gemini probes): probes fragile to distribution shift, fail on long context; training on long context costs >10x. arXiv:2509.14558 'LLM Jailbreak Detection for (Almost) Free'. Detecting High-Stakes Interactions with Activation Probes (arXiv:2506.10805).
