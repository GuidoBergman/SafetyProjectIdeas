# Sandbagging Signature Probe Benchmark

**Problem:** A medium-horizon safety concern is models that deliberately underperform (sandbag) when they detect they're being evaluated. There is no accessible benchmark that tests whether a probe can detect an internal 'I am being tested' or 'withhold effort' signal in open models prompted to sandbag.

**Direction:** Construct paired prompts where a small open model is instructed/role-played to either try fully or deliberately underperform on a task, collect activations, and test whether a linear probe distinguishes the two internal states and predicts the behavioral gap. Report detectability and how it depends on how the sandbagging is induced.

**Why it matters:** Sandbagging defeats capability evaluations and is a long-horizon threat labs deprioritize. A probe-based detectability benchmark on a model-organism slice is exactly the high-counterfactual-value, no-training work this profile targets, using only open models.

**Relevant context:** Probing classifiers are a core listed methodology; sandbagging/evaluation-gaming is a recognized model-organism direction (Apollo Research, listed key org). Activation access via TransformerLens makes the paired-prompt probe feasible in 30h.
