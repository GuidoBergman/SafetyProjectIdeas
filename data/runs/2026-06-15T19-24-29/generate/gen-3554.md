# Probe-Based Early Warning: Does the Truth Signal Drop Before the Lie Token?

**Problem:** Causal chain for deception detection: model decides to lie -> internal state shifts -> false token emitted. Under-studied link: WHEN along the token sequence does the internal truth/honesty signal change — at the decision, or only at the output? A lead time would enable early-warning monitoring.

**Direction:** Train a simple honesty/truth probe on a small open model. On instructed-lie vs. honest trajectories, read out the probe at each token position to find where the honest/deceptive trajectories diverge relative to the answer token. Success: a per-position probe trajectory showing whether divergence precedes the lie, quantified as lead-time in tokens.

**Why it matters:** If a cheap probe shows the deceptive state forming before the lie is uttered, that supports white-box early-warning monitoring — the 'estimate rare misbehavior without observing it' open direction. Small-probe, single-GPU friendly.

**Relevant context:** Self-reports are gated by SAE honesty/deception features (Berg et al., Oct 2025; introspection survey). ITI (arXiv 2306.03341) and lying-localization work (arXiv 2311.15131) show truth directions exist. Tracking the truth-signal trajectory token-by-token for lead time is a fresh, feasible angle.
