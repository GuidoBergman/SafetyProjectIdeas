# System-Prompt Persona Diversity: Can Personas Break the Hivemind?

**Problem:** A cheap proposed mitigation for homogeneity is assigning models different personas/system prompts. Whether persona-conditioning actually de-correlates a herd of models (rather than just restyling identical content) is untested at the inter-model level.

**Direction:** Replicate homogeneity measurement on a set of open models, then add the twist: condition each model with a varied system-prompt persona and re-measure inter-model output-similarity. Test whether personas reduce semantic (not just surface) convergence on open-ended and reasoning prompts. Use embedding similarity to separate stylistic from substantive diversity.

**Why it matters:** If personas only change style while substance stays homogeneous, a popular 'diversity by prompting' strategy is illusory for safety. A clean replication + intervention study with strong, lab-independent practical value.

**Relevant context:** Intervention twist on Artificial Hivemind homogeneity measurement (arXiv:2510.22954); prompt-only, open models.
