# Audio/Text Cross-Modal Shallow Alignment in Small Open Multimodal Models

**Problem:** The shallow-alignment finding was demonstrated for text; whether small open multimodal models (vision/audio-text) exhibit even shallower alignment when harmful intent enters through the non-text modality is an open follow-up.

**Direction:** Using a small open vision-language model (e.g., Qwen2-VL-7B or LLaVA), present harmful requests rendered as images of text vs plain text, and measure refusal rate and prefill-recovery for each modality. Test whether image-routed requests bypass the shallow text-token refusal entirely.

**Why it matters:** Directly pursues the flagged multimodal follow-up to Qi et al. Multimodal attack surfaces are a growing, medium-horizon concern, and rendering text-as-image is a trivial, training-free attack runnable on one GPU. Establishes whether shallow alignment is modality-specific.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946) demonstrated shallow alignment in text; the subfield context explicitly flags 'vary model families, multimodal' as a follow-up (FR67). Open VLMs (Qwen2-VL, LLaVA) run on a single GPU. Cross-modal prefill-recovery comparison is unstudied.
