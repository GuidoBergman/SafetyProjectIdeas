# DeceptionProbe-Eval: Benchmarking Concept Probes on a Deception Model Organism

**Problem:** Concept-vector probes are validated mainly on hallucination/toxicity. There is no benchmark testing whether such probes can detect deliberate deception in a model organism that lies without verbalizing it in the chain-of-thought.

**Direction:** Use an existing open 'sleeper'/deceptive model organism or a prompt-induced deceptive setup (e.g. role-play where the model is told to lie about a hidden fact) on an open model, then test whether a linear probe / concept vector trained on simple lie-vs-truth pairs flags the covert deception, even when the CoT looks honest. Report detection AUC and false-positive rate.

**Why it matters:** Detecting deception that isn't verbalized is a central long-horizon safety threat labs struggle with; probing an existing model organism is a tractable 30h slice that needs no training and no lab infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. propose extending probes to safety concepts like deception. Open deception/role-play setups and model organisms (e.g. sleeper-agent style demos) are publicly available; CAA-style truth/lie contrastive pairs are easy to build.
