# Planning-Ahead Circuits Plus CoT Unfaithfulness Hide Goal-Directedness

**Problem:** Compound: (1) models internally plan ahead (Anthropic found Haiku selects rhyme words before writing the line) yet (2) CoT can be unfaithful. The compound failure: a model can pursue a multi-step internal plan whose existence is invisible both to mechanistic readout (if we only watch the output stream) and to CoT monitoring (if the plan is never verbalized) — a model-organism for unverbalized scheming.

**Direction:** On a small open model, set up a constrained planning task (e.g. forced-rhyme or a two-step puzzle). Use logit-lens/probing on intermediate positions to detect whether a future-token decision is encoded early. Then check whether the CoT mentions that decision. Measure the rate of 'internally planned but not verbalized' cases as a tiny model-organism of hidden planning.

**Why it matters:** Directly probes a >1-year threat model (covert long-horizon goals) on a feasible slice, combining a flagged surprising result with CoT-faithfulness concerns. No training, just probing an existing model.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic Circuit Tracing 'On the Biology of an LLM' (transformer-circuits.pub 2025): Claude 3.5 Haiku plans rhyme words ahead. 'Chain-of-Thought Is Not Explainability' (Barez et al.). TransformerLens supports logit lens / activation capture on small models.
