# Defense Stacking Interference: Do Two Cheap Defenses Conflict?

**Problem:** Defenders deploy multiple cheap defenses (perplexity filter + activation probe + system-prompt reminder), but whether combining them produces additive robustness, redundant cost, or harmful interference (e.g., compounded over-refusal) is not characterized for accessible setups.

**Direction:** Implement 2-3 lightweight defenses around one open model and evaluate every combination on a small jailbreak suite and an over-refusal suite, measuring marginal robustness gain and marginal over-refusal cost per added defense. Identify whether stacking has diminishing or negative returns.

**Why it matters:** Real deployments stack defenses, yet the cost-constrained-monitor question (which combination is Pareto-optimal) is understudied for small actors. A reproducible stacking ablation is cheap infrastructure-light research with direct deployment relevance.

**Relevant context:** Combining Cost-Constrained Runtime Monitors for AI Safety (arXiv:2507.15886) studies monitor combination; XSTest/OR-Bench measure over-refusal cost. A concrete, reproducible stacking ablation of cheap defenses on an open model with both robustness and over-refusal axes is missing.
