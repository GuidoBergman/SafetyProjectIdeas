# Persona system-prompts as activation steering: do role prompts and steering vectors hit the same honesty axis?

**Problem:** Honesty research notes strong persona effects (system prompts change honesty); concept steering manipulates the same behaviors via activation vectors. Unexamined: whether a 'be brutally honest' persona prompt moves activations along the SAME direction as an explicit anti-sycophancy steering vector — i.e. are prompting and steering two routes to one mechanism?

**Direction:** On a small open model, capture mean activation shifts induced by several honesty/dishonesty personas (system prompts) and compute the difference direction. Separately extract a contrastive anti-sycophancy steering vector. Measure cosine similarity between persona-induced shifts and the steering vector, and test interchangeability: does the persona's behavioral effect predict the steering vector's, and do they compose or conflict?

**Why it matters:** If personas and steering share an axis, cheap prompt-level honesty interventions and white-box monitors are mechanistically unified — clarifying how robust prompt-based honesty really is. Pure interpretability/behavior on open weights, no training.

**Relevant context:** Bridges honesty_faithfulness (persona effects on honesty, Anthropic recommended directions; sycophancy) with concept_steering (steering vectors / activation directions, Beaglehole et al. Science 2026, arxiv 2502.03708).
