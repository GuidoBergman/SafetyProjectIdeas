# Steering-Induced Capability Damage Plus Silent Safety Regression

**Problem:** Compound: (1) activation/SAE steering distorts hidden representations and degrades general capability (interp-intervention side effect) amplifying (2) a safety regression where the degraded model produces lower-quality but still-harmful outputs that bypass quality-based filters. The interp 'fix' both hurts utility and leaves a residual unsafe channel.

**Direction:** Apply a refusal-steering intervention to an open model. Measure (a) capability drop on a small benchmark, and (b) whether harmful outputs persist but in degraded/obfuscated form that a perplexity or quality heuristic would let through. Map the steering-strength frontier where safety gain is overtaken by capability loss + residual harm.

**Why it matters:** Steering is promoted as a lightweight safety lever. Characterizing the joint capability/safety cost frontier is decision-relevant and fully feasible at small scale without training.

**Relevant context:** 'The Rogue Scalpel' (arXiv:2509.22067): activation steering compromises safety. Search results: inference-time SAE steering distorts hidden representation distributions, limiting utility retention and jailbreak resilience (arXiv:2505.24428). GSAE (arXiv:2512.06655) on distributed safety representations.
