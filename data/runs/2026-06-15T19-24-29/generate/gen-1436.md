# SteerMonitor: A Lightweight Real-Time Monitoring Harness Using Concept Probes

**Problem:** Concept vectors can monitor as well as steer, but there is no small, self-contained monitoring harness that streams a model's generation and flags when an internal safety concept (e.g. deception, toxicity) activates token-by-token.

**Direction:** Build a minimal monitoring wrapper around an open model that computes the dot-product of the residual stream with a precomputed concept vector at each generated token and raises a flag when it crosses a calibrated threshold. Evaluate flag precision/recall on a labeled prompt set and visualize per-token concept activation traces.

**Why it matters:** Cheap, model-internal monitoring is strategically valuable safety infrastructure that anyone can run; it complements (not replaces) output filters and is the kind of monitoring harness the steering literature points toward but hasn't packaged. No training needed.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. title is 'Toward Universal Steering and Monitoring of AI Models'; probes outperform judges for detection. Real-time linear-probe hallucination detection exists (arxiv 2509.03531). RepE/steering-vectors libs provide hooks.
