# Cross-Lingual Variant of a New Frontier-Risk Benchmark

**Problem:** ForesightSafety and most frontier-risk benchmarks evaluate in their native language; safety on catastrophic-risk items may degrade in lower-resource languages, a known pattern for ordinary harm prompts but untested for frontier risks.

**Direction:** Machine-translate a subset of ForesightSafety/catastrophic-risk text items into 3-4 languages of varying resource level, validate translations with back-translation, and measure refusal-rate drop versus English on open multilingual models (Qwen, Aya).

**Why it matters:** If frontier-risk safety collapses in non-English, the most dangerous capabilities are most accessible exactly where evals are weakest. Counterfactually valuable and purely inference-based.

**Relevant context:** ForesightSafety Bench (arXiv 2602.14135) frontier-risk items; cross-lingual safety degradation is a documented pattern in multilingual safety literature; open multilingual models (Qwen2.5, Aya) available.
