# Genetic Linkage Mapping of Co-Inherited Misaligned Behaviors

**Problem:** Emergent misalignment produces a *cluster* of behaviors (deception, harmful advice, anti-human views) that appear together. Genetics studies which traits are 'linked' and co-inherited; we lack a linkage map for which misaligned behaviors travel together.

**Direction:** Borrow genetic linkage analysis. Across many small narrow fine-tunes (varying source task and dose), record a vector of multiple misaligned-behavior measurements per model, then compute pairwise correlation / co-occurrence to build a 'linkage map' of which misaligned behaviors are tightly coupled vs separable. Test whether targeted counter-data can decouple linked behaviors.

**Why it matters:** If misaligned behaviors are tightly linked, fixing one may fix many (or vice versa) — crucial for designing efficient mitigations. Replicates the flagship clustering result with cheap LoRA runs and standard correlation analysis the participants can handle.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424) report distinct misaligned behaviors that appear interlinked; Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment (arXiv:2506.11613) provides small open organisms. Genetics uses linkage maps / co-inheritance correlation to find coupled traits.
