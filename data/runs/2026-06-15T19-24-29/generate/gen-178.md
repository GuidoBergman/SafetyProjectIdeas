# In-Context Defenses vs Prefilling: Reproducing the 'No Free Lunch'

**Problem:** 'No Free Lunch for Defending Against Prefilling Attack by In-Context Learning' (arXiv:2412.12192) argues ICL defenses trade off. The precise shape of that tradeoff (defense strength vs over-refusal on benign prompts) across attack intensities is not characterized for practitioners.

**Direction:** Reproduce a few-shot ICL defense (demonstrations of refusing prefilled harmful continuations) on an open model. Sweep number of demonstrations and measure both prefilling-attack success and over-refusal rate on benign prompts (e.g., XSTest-style). Plot the safety/usability frontier to make the 'no free lunch' tradeoff concrete.

**Why it matters:** Over-refusal is a core open problem (adaptive defenses without catastrophic over-refusal). Mapping the ICL tradeoff helps deployers pick operating points without training. Fully reproducible outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2412.12192 'No Free Lunch for Defending Against Prefilling Attack by In-Context Learning'. Context Open Problems: 'adaptive defenses... without catastrophic over-refusal'. XSTest is a standard over-refusal benchmark.
