# How many contrastive pairs does a reliable safety vector need?

**Problem:** CAA averages over many contrastive pairs, but the data-efficiency frontier for SAFETY concepts is undocumented. Knowing the minimum is important because anyone auditing a model outside a lab has limited labeled data.

**Direction:** Extract a toxicity steering vector from an open model using N contrastive pairs for N in {2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128}. For each N, measure steering effectiveness and probe AUC on a fixed held-out set, with multiple random subsets per N to get error bars. Produce a data-efficiency curve and identify the knee.

**Why it matters:** Establishes how little labeled data is needed for usable safety steering, directly enabling low-resource external auditing. No training, trivial compute.

**Relevant context:** CAA (Rimsky et al. 2024) averages over contrastive sets. Beaglehole et al. concept extraction. Reliability concerns from arXiv:2407.12404.
