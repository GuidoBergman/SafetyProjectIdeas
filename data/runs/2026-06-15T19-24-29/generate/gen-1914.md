# Prompt Sensitivity as a Diagnostic for Latent Misalignment

**Problem:** Emergent misalignment as prompt sensitivity (arXiv 2507.06253) found insecure-code-finetuned models are far more sensitive to system-prompt variation than control models. Whether prompt-sensitivity magnitude can serve as a black-box detector of whether a model has undergone EM finetuning is unexplored.

**Direction:** Assemble a battery of paraphrased/varied system prompts. Measure the variance of misalignment-eval scores across this battery for (a) clean models, (b) EM model organisms, and (c) realigned models. Test whether response-variance under prompt perturbation cleanly classifies models as misaligned vs aligned without any internal access.

**Why it matters:** A purely black-box behavioral test that flags hidden misalignment would let third parties (auditors, downstream users without weights) detect tampered models. High counterfactual value: needs only API-style inference, no weights, no training.

**Relevant context:** Emergent misalignment as prompt sensitivity: a research note (arXiv 2507.06253): EM finetuning increases prompt sensitivity and instruction-following; control models less sensitive. Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2502.17424) provides eval methodology.
