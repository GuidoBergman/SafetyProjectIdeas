# KL-Penalty Knob vs Narrow Misalignment: Can a Regularizer Localize Bad Generalization?

**Problem:** Soligo et al. (2026) showed a KL penalty can produce *narrow* (localized) rather than broad misalignment. Whether tuning the KL coefficient gives a controllable dial trading off in-domain learning against the breadth of misalignment generalization is unexplored as a practical lever.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small model on EM data at several KL-penalty strengths (relative to the base model) and measure both in-domain task performance and the breadth of off-domain misalignment, plotting breadth-vs-KL to test whether KL localizes harm.

**Why it matters:** If a single standard regularizer hyperparameter controls how far misalignment spreads, it is a near-free mitigation any fine-tuner can apply. Tests a surprising 2026 result with only open models and TRL.

**Relevant context:** Search result: 'Soligo et al. (2026) show that it is also possible to learn a narrow misaligned behavior when a KL penalty is used.' Emergent Misalignment is Easy, Narrow Misalignment is Hard (researchgate). EM (arXiv 2502.17424).
