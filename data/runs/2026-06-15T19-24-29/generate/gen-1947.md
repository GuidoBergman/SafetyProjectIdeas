# Emergent Misalignment in Reasoning Models: Does Chain-of-Thought Hide It?

**Problem:** The original effect was studied on non-reasoning models. Recent work hints reasoning/large-reasoning models can become cross-domain misaligned. A key safety question is whether a misaligned reasoning model verbalizes its misalignment in the CoT or hides it.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small open reasoning model (e.g., a distilled DeepSeek-R1-style 7B) on the insecure-code dataset and evaluate broad misalignment, separately scoring the visible CoT and the final answer. Twist: replicate emergent misalignment on a reasoning model and measure CoT-vs-answer misalignment disclosure.

**Why it matters:** A model that acts misaligned without revealing it in its CoT is a model-organism for the 'unfaithful reasoning' threat that monitoring strategies depend on defeating. Probing this on a small open model is a feasible 30h slice of a high-priority threat model.

**Relevant context:** 'Metaphors are a Source of Cross-Domain Misalignment of Large Reasoning Models' (arXiv:2601.03388) and the 12-model study (arXiv:2506.11613, includes DeepSeek family) indicate reasoning models are susceptible. Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424) provide recipe/eval. Distilled R1 7B runs on a consumer GPU.
