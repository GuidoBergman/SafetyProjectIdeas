# Do Refusal Directions Generalize Across Open Models the Way Frontier Findings Suggest?

**Problem:** Mechanistic work identified that refusal in chat models can be mediated by a single direction. Follow-up question: how consistent is this refusal direction across different open model families, and does ablating it transfer?

**Direction:** For two or three open instruct models, extract the refusal direction (difference-of-means of harmful vs. harmless prompt activations), measure cosine similarity of the extracted directions across models in a shared space where possible, and test whether ablating the direction reduces refusals within each model — quantifying robustness and any side effects.

**Why it matters:** If refusal is a fragile single direction across many open models, it reveals a generalizable jailbreak surface and a generalizable safety lever — both crucial for the open-model ecosystem and independent of any lab.

**Relevant context:** The 'refusal is mediated by a single direction' finding (Arditi et al.) is widely replicated. Difference-of-means direction extraction and ablation are cheap in TransformerLens. Multiple open instruct models (Qwen, Llama-3-Instruct) run on one consumer GPU.
