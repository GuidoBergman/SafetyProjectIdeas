# DebateRobustness-Bench: How Stable Are Judge Verdicts to Swapping Debater Sides?

**Problem:** A reliable debate protocol should give the same verdict regardless of which debater argues first or which side a given model is assigned, but there is no benchmark measuring verdict instability under side/order swaps.

**Direction:** Run each debate item under both side assignments and both presentation orders with a fixed open judge, and report the verdict flip rate as a 'debate robustness' instability score, decomposed into order effects and assignment effects. Deliver the swap-harness and instability metrics.

**Why it matters:** Verdict instability under symmetric swaps reveals that a protocol is tracking persuasion artifacts rather than truth — a core oversight reliability concern measurable with pure inference and open models.

**Relevant context:** Position-bias literature (LLM-judge surveys, arXiv 2506.22316) documents order sensitivity; Kenton et al. 2024 control for debater assignment, indicating it materially affects outcomes.
