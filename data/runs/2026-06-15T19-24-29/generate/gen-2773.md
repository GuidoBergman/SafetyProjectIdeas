# Autograder Disagreement Map Across Safety Criteria

**Problem:** LLM judges disagree inconsistently across safety criteria and harm categories, and different scorers give discrepant verdicts for the same response. Practitioners pick one judge and report a single number, hiding this disagreement.

**Direction:** Take a fixed set of model responses to harmful prompts (e.g. AgentHarm or SorryBench outputs). Score each with 4-5 different open judge configurations (different open models as judge, plus a rubric-based vs reference-free prompt). Compute per-category inter-judge agreement (Krippendorff's alpha) and surface which harm categories are least reliably graded.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies how much a safety score depends on the arbitrary choice of grader, giving the field a 'judge-sensitivity' caveat per category. Directly addresses an open problem (scorer reliability). Pure inference + statistics, no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2605.31381 'LLM Judges Inconsistently Disagree Across Safety Criteria and Harm Categories'; arXiv 2507.03772 'Skewed Score: A statistical framework to assess autograders'; SorryBench (ICLR 2025).
