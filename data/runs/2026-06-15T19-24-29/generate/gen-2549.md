# Synthetic-Data-Optimized Attacks Replicated as a Monitor Stress Test

**Problem:** Work on optimizing AI agent attacks with synthetic data showed attacks can be strengthened cheaply, but the implication for how much this degrades cheap monitors has not been independently replicated.

**Direction:** Replicate a lightweight version of synthetic-data attack optimization: generate several variants of a backdoor/side-task strategy via prompting (no training), select the variants that minimize a cheap monitor's suspiciousness, and measure how much the optimized attacks lower detection AUC versus naive attacks. Twist: test whether optimizing against one monitor transfers to evading a different cheap monitor.

**Why it matters:** Attack-optimization-against-monitors is the adaptive-adversary core of control evaluations. A prompt-only, training-free replication is feasible for a beginner and directly stress-tests monitor robustness outside any lab.

**Relevant context:** 'Optimizing AI Agent Attacks With Synthetic Data' (arXiv 2511.02823). Adaptive attacks subvert monitors (arXiv 2510.09462). Control Tax APPS backdoor dataset (arXiv 2506.05296).
