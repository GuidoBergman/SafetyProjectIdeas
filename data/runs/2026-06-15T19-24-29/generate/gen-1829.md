# Extending Evaluation-Awareness Probes to Open Reasoning Models

**Problem:** Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness of Language Models (arXiv:2507.01786) shows models linearly encode whether they are being evaluated, but the study focuses on a limited model set; it is unclear whether evaluation-awareness directions exist and transfer in newer open reasoning models.

**Direction:** Build a small contrast dataset of 'this is a test' vs 'this is real deployment' framed prompts, extract the difference-of-means direction in an open reasoning model's residual stream, and measure (a) probe accuracy detecting eval framing and (b) whether steering along that direction changes behavior (e.g. refusal or sandbagging rates). Compare to the original paper's reported separability.

**Why it matters:** Evaluation-awareness undermines all safety benchmarks; a transferable probe is a cheap detector and the threat model (models behaving differently when watched) is squarely a long-horizon, lab-deprioritized concern.

**Relevant context:** Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness of Language Models (arXiv:2507.01786): linear-representation-hypothesis-based probes detect evaluation awareness and steering shifts behavior.
