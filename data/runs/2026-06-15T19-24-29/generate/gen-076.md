# Differential testing across model families to surface shared safety blind spots

**Problem:** Model homogeneity means independently trained models may share blind spots, so a single benign-looking prompt could slip past all of them — a correlated systemic risk that single-model evals cannot reveal.

**Direction:** Apply differential testing (used to find compiler bugs by comparing implementations) to safety. Run the same large prompt battery through several open models and flag inputs where all models agree on an unsafe or clearly wrong response (consensus failures) versus inputs where they disagree. Characterize the consensus-failure set: are shared blind spots concentrated in particular topics/styles? Build a small 'shared blind spot' probe set.

**Why it matters:** Operationalizes systemic correlated-failure risk into a concrete, reusable probe set — a recognized under-researched area. Cross-model output comparison only; no training, no proprietary data.
