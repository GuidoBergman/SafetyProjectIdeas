# Threshold Drift: Tracing How Capability Definitions Weaken Between Framework Versions

**Problem:** Commitment-durability chain: 'lab makes a safety commitment' -> 'commitment persists' -> 'commitment binds at the critical moment'. Reviewers note frameworks show 'weakening commitments' across versions; the persist link silently breaks when thresholds are redefined upward or actions softened between revisions.

**Direction:** Diff successive public versions of one or more frameworks (e.g., Anthropic RSP v1->v2.1->v3, DeepMind FSF iterations) and catalogue every change to a capability threshold or required mitigation, labeling each as strengthening, weakening, or clarifying. Produce a timeline of net commitment drift per lab.

**Why it matters:** If commitments quietly weaken before they bind, voluntary frameworks offer the appearance of safety without durability. A documented drift timeline gives regulators and the public a ratchet-watching tool. Pure version-diff document analysis, zero compute, very counterfactual.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic RSP versions (v2.1 effective March 2025, v3.0); DeepMind FSF; LessWrong 'On DeepMind's Frontier Safety Framework'; EA Forum 'We read every lab's safety plan so you don't have to: 2025'.
