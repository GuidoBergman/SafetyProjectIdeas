# When Does Injecting a Feature Steer vs. Break the Model? A Dose-Response Study

**Problem:** Frontier work showed injecting a concept ('green') redirects planning, but the relationship between intervention strength, successful steering, and output coherence is not characterized for open SAEs and small models.

**Direction:** Using an open SAE feature on a small model, sweep the injection coefficient across a wide range and at each level measure steering success (target concept appears) and coherence (perplexity / fluency rating), producing dose-response curves. Identify the 'safe steering window' and whether it differs for safety-relevant features vs. neutral ones.

**Why it matters:** Feature steering is proposed as a safety lever (e.g. suppress deception). Knowing where steering becomes incoherent or fails defines the practical reliability of SAE-based control. Doable with open SAEs on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Biology of an LLM (2025): injecting 'green' redirects planning. Bi-causal steering literature reports coherence-intervention tradeoff and intervention success rate. SAELens/Gemma Scope provide steerable open SAE features.
