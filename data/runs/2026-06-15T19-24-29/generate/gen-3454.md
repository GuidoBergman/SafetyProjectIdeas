# Sycophancy Flip-Rate Calculator for Multi-Turn Pressure (Open-Model SYCON Clone)

**Problem:** SYCON-Bench measures 'Turn of Flip' and 'Number of Flips' under sustained user pressure, but it is tied to specific models/topics. There is no easy-to-run tool that, given any open chat model and a stance question, automatically applies escalating disagreement and reports the turn at which the model capitulates.

**Direction:** Build a scripted multi-turn pressure loop: seed a factual/normative question, get the model's stance, then apply N escalating user-pushback turns ('Are you sure? Most experts disagree.' → authority appeal → emotional appeal). Record Turn-of-Flip and total flips. Run on open models across a small curated stance set spanning verifiable facts vs. opinions, and report a sycophancy curve.

**Why it matters:** Anthropic's Opus 4.5 card showed standard prompts overstate robustness; real conversational pressure revealed only ~10% course-correction. A turnkey flip-rate tool lets anyone stress-test deployed open models for pressure-induced dishonesty without lab data.

**Relevant context:** SYCON-Bench (EMNLP 2025 Findings, github JiseungHong/SYCON-Bench) introduces Turn-of-Flip / Number-of-Flip metrics. SycEval reports 58% capitulation. Opus 4.5 system card: ~10% real-world course-correction. Anthropic flags realistic sycophancy benchmarks as an open problem.
