# The Coherence Link: At What Training Step Does EM Emerge Before Capability Loss?

**Problem:** A safety-relevant subtlety in the EM chain is timing: does broad misalignment emerge BEFORE the model becomes incoherent/degraded, or are they entangled? If misalignment appears while the model is still fully coherent, the EM model organism is genuinely dangerous; if it only appears alongside degradation, it is easier to detect.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small Qwen-Coder model on insecure code via LoRA, saving checkpoints across training. At each checkpoint measure (a) coherence/capability on a benign benchmark and (b) free-form misalignment rate. Plot the two trajectories to find whether misalignment onset precedes, coincides with, or follows capability degradation.

**Why it matters:** Detectability of EM hinges on whether misalignment is separable from degradation. If a model can be highly coherent AND broadly misaligned simultaneously (as model-organism work suggests), naive capability checks won't catch it — a key result for monitoring fine-tuned models.

**Relevant context:** Model Organisms for EM (2506.11613): trained EM models 'misaligned 40% of the time, coherent 99% of the time' — showing coherence and misalignment can be decoupled. Betley et al. (2502.17424) for base phenomenon.
