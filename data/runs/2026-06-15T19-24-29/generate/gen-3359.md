# Honesty Under Role-Play Drift: Multi-Turn Erosion of Disclaimers

**Problem:** Persona prompts suppress honesty in single turns, but in long role-play sessions models may 'drift' deeper into character and progressively drop honest disclaimers even when none are requested. This temporal honesty erosion within a persona is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Assign an open chat model a persona and run an extended in-character conversation, periodically inserting identical honesty probes ('Is your medical advice from a licensed human?'). Track whether honest-disclosure rate declines as the role-play deepens over turns.

**Why it matters:** Long companion/agent deployments are exactly where role-play drift would cause real harm (fabricated authority), and it is a diffuse, long-horizon risk frontier labs underweight versus single-turn evals.

**Relevant context:** 'Beware of Your Po!' (arXiv 2502.20968) and arXiv 2511.21569 show persona prompts override honesty in single turns. Multi-turn sycophancy dynamics (SYCON-Bench, arXiv 2505.23840) motivate measuring temporal drift; combining persona + multi-turn honesty erosion is novel.
