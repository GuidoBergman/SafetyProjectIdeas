# Activation-Space Whitening for Training-Free Policy-Violation Detection: A Reproduction

**Problem:** A 2025 method detects policy violations via activation-space whitening with no training, but it is recent and largely unreplicated. Whether the training-free whitening signal reliably separates compliant from violating agent behavior on a small open model is open.

**Direction:** Extract hidden activations from a small open model on a set of clearly-compliant and clearly-violating prompts, apply whitening (mean-center + decorrelate using the compliant-set covariance), and score test examples by Mahalanobis distance from the compliant distribution. Measure separation AUC and compare to a trained linear probe baseline.

**Why it matters:** Training-free activation monitors are ideal for actors without fine-tuning infrastructure; reproducing and stress-testing this method directly serves the 'real-time activation monitoring' open problem with no lab resources. A clean replication has high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** Training-Free Policy Violation Detection via Activation-Space Whitening in LLMs (arxiv 2512.03994): detects violations from activations without training. Complements supervised linear probes (Apollo arxiv 2502.03407) which require labeled training data.
