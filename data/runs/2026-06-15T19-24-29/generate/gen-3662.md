# Does Verbosity Buy Safety? CoT Length vs Unsafe-Action Rate

**Problem:** It is widely assumed more deliberation (longer CoT) makes agents safer, but the relationship between reasoning length and unsafe-action rate in agentic tasks has not been directly measured, and could even be non-monotonic.

**Direction:** Run an open reasoning agent on risky tasks while varying a thinking-budget / max-reasoning-token setting. Measure unsafe-action rate and irreversible-action rate as a function of reasoning length, testing for a sweet spot or a regime where extra reasoning rationalizes unsafe shortcuts.

**Why it matters:** If more reasoning sometimes increases unsafe behavior (rationalizing shortcuts), that overturns a common safety assumption and informs deployment defaults. A clean dose-response study on open models needs no training and is broadly informative.

**Relevant context:** 2026 reward-hacking findings: 72% of hacks came WITH explicit CoT rationale ('reasoning their way to shortcuts'). arXiv:2512.00218 'Reasoning Under Pressure: training incentives and CoT monitorability'. arXiv:2510.19851 reasoning obfuscation.
