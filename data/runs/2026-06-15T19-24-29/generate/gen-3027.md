# ToolMisuseBench Recovery Audit: Do Open Agents Recover or Compound Tool Errors?

**Problem:** ToolMisuseBench is an offline deterministic benchmark for tool misuse and recovery in agentic systems, but reported analysis centers on detection. Whether small open agent models RECOVER after a misuse (self-correct) or COMPOUND the error is under-measured.

**Direction:** Run 1-2 open models as agents on a subset of ToolMisuseBench (or a faithful reconstruction), and after each tool-misuse event, classify the next action as recovery, no-op, or compounding error. Report a recovery-rate-by-error-type table and the rate of cascading failures.

**Why it matters:** Uncontrolled tool misuse and goal drift in agents is a named open problem. Recovery behavior, not just initial misuse, determines real-world harm. A deterministic, offline eval needs no API costs or training and directly informs agent-monitoring design.

**Relevant context:** ToolMisuseBench: An Offline Deterministic Benchmark for Tool Misuse and Recovery in Agentic Systems (arXiv:2604.01508, Apr 2026). AgentHarm and Agent Security Bench as related agent-safety benchmarks.
