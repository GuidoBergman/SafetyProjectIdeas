# A Monitoring-Harness Prototype: Streaming Safety Flags Over Agent Trajectories

**Problem:** A success state requires lightweight runtime monitors that flag unsafe agent steps as they happen. A needed result is a small, open, reusable monitoring-harness prototype that beginners can extend.

**Direction:** Build a thin wrapper around a simple open-model agent loop that, at each step, runs a fast judge to emit a per-step risk score (tool-misuse, scope-violation, goal-drift) and logs a trajectory timeline. Validate on a handful of scripted safe/unsafe trajectories. Deliverable: an open monitoring-harness prototype plus a worked detection demo.

**Why it matters:** Safer monitoring harnesses are exactly the strategically valuable infrastructure the steering prioritizes, and labs build proprietary versions. A small open prototype seeds community tooling, needs only a consumer GPU, and targets agentic deployment safety.
