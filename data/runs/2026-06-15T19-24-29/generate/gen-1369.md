# Population variation: do steering vectors differ for persona-conditioned models?

**Problem:** Steering vectors are extracted from a model's default persona, but agentic deployments assign system-prompt personas (e.g. 'a helpful assistant' vs 'an autonomous agent'). Whether a single safety vector works across personas is an untested 'population' variation relevant to agentic monitoring.

**Direction:** Extract a refusal/honesty vector under a neutral system prompt, then test its steering/probe performance under several distinct persona system prompts (cautious, role-play character, autonomous agent). Measure how much persona conditioning shifts the needed vector and whether the neutral vector still works on held-out evals.

**Why it matters:** If persona conditioning silently moves safety directions, agent monitors keyed to a default persona fail in deployment — a medium-horizon agentic risk. No training, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. steering/monitoring; persona effects relate to sycophancy/role-play steering work. System-prompt conditioning is free to vary.
