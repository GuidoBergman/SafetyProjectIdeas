# Sycophancy Across Pressure-Cue Families and Model Sizes

**Problem:** Sycophancy studies report ~64% agreement-with-wrong-belief under user opinion, and the Anthropic system card noted only 10% course-correction under unprompted conversational pressure. But the relative potency of authority cues vs emotional investment vs persistent disagreement, and how it scales with model size within one family, is not mapped.

**Direction:** On a single open model family at 3 sizes (e.g. Qwen2.5 1.5B/7B/14B), present factual questions where the user pushes a wrong answer via three cue families (authority, emotion, persistence). Measure capitulation rate per cue family per size, producing a sycophancy-vs-scale curve and a cue-potency ranking.

**Why it matters:** Directly follows the context's suggested follow-up (sycophancy gap, vary pressure types). Sycophancy is a fuzzy-reward problem labs deprioritize; a clean cross-size, cross-cue map is broadly useful and needs no training.

**Relevant context:** Sycophancy under user opinion averages 63.7% agreement with incorrect beliefs (opinion-based sycophancy literature); 'Pressure, What Pressure?' (arXiv:2604.05279) decomposes pressure capitulation vs evidence blindness. Anthropic Claude Opus 4.5 system card: 10% course-correction under unprompted pressure.
