# Homogeneity Under Distribution Shift: Does the Hivemind Hold on Adversarial/OOD Prompts?

**Problem:** Homogeneity was measured on naturalistic Infinity-Chat queries. Under out-of-distribution or adversarially perturbed prompts, models might diverge (different failure modes) or converge even harder (all defaulting to the same degenerate behavior). The systemic-risk implications differ sharply between these cases.

**Direction:** Vary the input distribution: apply controlled perturbations to Infinity-Chat queries (typos, prompt-injection wrappers, unusual formatting) and re-measure inter-model homogeneity vs. the clean baseline. Characterize whether stress increases or decreases convergence.

**Why it matters:** Correlated degenerate behavior under stress (e.g. all models emitting the same unsafe default when confused) is a worst-case systemic failure. Knowing whether stress homogenizes or diversifies behavior is decision-relevant for red-teamers and auditors. Inference-only.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954); prompt-injection/jailbreak robustness literature; correlated errors (arXiv:2506.07962).
