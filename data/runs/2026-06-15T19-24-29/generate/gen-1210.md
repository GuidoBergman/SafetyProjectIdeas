# Does RAG Break or Reinforce Homogeneity? Shared Retrieval as a New Correlation Channel

**Problem:** RAG is sold as grounding individuality, but the homogeneity chain may gain a new link: 'shared retrieval corpus + shared model -> models converge on the SAME retrieved-and-summarized answer.' If retrieval reinforces rather than diversifies outputs, RAG-based systems inherit a shared blind spot through both model and corpus.

**Direction:** Build a small fixed document corpus. Have several models answer the same factual/safety queries with identical retrieved context. Measure whether shared retrieval INCREASES inter-model output similarity (convergence) compared to no-RAG answering, and whether shared retrieval errors propagate identically across models.

**Why it matters:** Many deployed safety-relevant systems are RAG pipelines assumed to be grounded and diverse. Showing that shared retrieval adds a correlation channel reframes RAG as a homogeneity amplifier in some regimes. Small corpus + open models = single-GPU feasible.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in Artificial Hivemind inter-model homogeneity (arXiv 2510.22954) and correlated-downstream-failure framing from foundation-model concentration work (arXiv 2311.01550). Novel retrieval-as-correlation-channel angle.
