# Cross-Model Transfer of a Single Injection Payload

**Problem:** A success state is understanding whether one crafted injection generalizes across the diverse agent fleet, since attackers reuse payloads. We lack a measurement of how well a single indirect-injection payload transfers across open models of different families/sizes.

**Direction:** Backcast from 'defenders can reason about attack transferability'. Take a handful of effective AgentDojo injection payloads and run each unchanged across several open models (different families, sizes), measuring per-model success to build a transfer matrix. Identify whether some payloads are near-universal and whether any model family is broadly resistant.

**Why it matters:** Universal payloads mean one attack compromises a whole ecosystem; resistant families inform model selection. A transfer matrix is cheap to produce, needs no training, and addresses the diffuse fleet-level threat labs don't own.

**Relevant context:** AgentDojo (arXiv 2406.13352) provides reusable injection cases; OWASP LLM01:2025 and Unit 42 document in-the-wild indirect injection. STAC (arXiv 2509.25624) and 'Simple Prompt Injection Attacks' (arXiv 2506.01055) show payload generality across settings.
