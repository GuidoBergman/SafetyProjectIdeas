# StrongREJECT Scoring Replicated Across Open Judge Models

**Problem:** StrongREJECT's automated scoring rubric was validated primarily with specific judge models. Jailbreak ASR numbers in the literature implicitly depend on the judge; how much ASR estimates vary when the SAME attack outputs are scored by different open judge models is rarely quantified, undermining cross-paper comparability.

**Direction:** Take one fixed set of attack transcripts and replicate StrongREJECT scoring with 3-4 different open judge models (and the rubric prompt). Twist: judge model as the replication axis. Report inter-judge agreement (Krippendorff's alpha) and how reported ASR shifts by judge. No new attacks needed.

**Why it matters:** Judge variance is a hidden confound in nearly all adversarial-robustness papers; quantifying it externally improves the reliability of the whole subfield's metrics and needs only inference. Clean statistics, single GPU, fully self-contained.

**Relevant context:** StrongREJECT (OpenAI, public) provides an automated rubric-based jailbreak scorer. AILuminate and HELM Safety also rely on automated judges. Open judge models (Llama-Guard, Qwen, Mistral) are freely available.
