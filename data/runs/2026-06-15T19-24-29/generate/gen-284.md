# Genetic-Algorithm Attack Diversity vs. Single-Lineage Fuzzing

**Problem:** Mutation-based fuzzers can converge on a single high-ASR lineage, overstating robustness coverage. Evolutionary biology has tools (niching, fitness sharing) to maintain population diversity.

**Direction:** Transfer niching/fitness-sharing from evolutionary computation into a tiny jailbreak fuzzer. Implement a basic genetic-algorithm prompt fuzzer over an open model, then compare a vanilla fitness (ASR only) to a diversity-preserving fitness (ASR penalized by similarity to the existing population). Measure attack-set diversity (embedding spread) and unique-vulnerability discovery rate under both.

**Why it matters:** Shows whether diversity pressure finds qualitatively different failure modes the standard fuzzer misses, improving honest coverage estimates. Lightweight, black-box, no model training.

**Relevant context:** Unit 42 used a genetic-algorithm-inspired prompt fuzzer (Palo Alto Networks blog). TurboFuzzLLM (arXiv:2502.18504) is mutation-based fuzzing with seed pool + mutation engine + oracle. Fitness sharing/niching are standard EC diversity techniques.
