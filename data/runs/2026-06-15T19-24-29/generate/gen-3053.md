# Backdoor-Trigger Sensitivity Mapping in Public Poisoned/Fine-Tuned Open Models

**Problem:** Detecting covert malicious fine-tuning of open-weight models is a UK AISI open problem. Many backdoored/poisoned open models exist publicly, but there is little cheap black-box methodology for mapping how narrow or broad a hidden trigger's activation region is.

**Direction:** Take a publicly available backdoored or sleeper-agent-style open model (or a small one you fine-tune minimally on a known trigger within compute budget). Black-box probe trigger sensitivity: perturb the trigger (typos, synonyms, position, casing) and measure backdoor activation rate. Produce a 'trigger robustness surface' showing how fragile/general the backdoor is.

**Why it matters:** Understanding trigger generalization tells defenders whether random input fuzzing could plausibly surface hidden backdoors — a lab-independent detection direction. Backdoors/stress tests are a starred Open Phil problem.
