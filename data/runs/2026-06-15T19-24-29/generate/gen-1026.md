# Homogeneity of Safety Classifiers: Do Open Moderation Models Miss the Same Content?

**Problem:** Many deployers stack a separate moderation/guardrail classifier in front of an LLM. If the popular open guardrail models (Llama Guard, ShieldGemma, etc.) share blind spots, then 'add a content filter' provides correlated, not layered, protection.

**Direction:** Take a labeled set of safe/unsafe content including hard edge cases. Run several open moderation/guardrail models. Measure cross-classifier false-negative overlap: which unsafe items slip past all of them simultaneously. Quantify whether stacking different guardrails actually reduces miss rate or just relabels the same survivors.

**Why it matters:** Guardrail monoculture means defense-in-depth at the moderation layer is illusory — a concrete, actionable systemic-risk finding for anyone deploying open guardrails. Fully reproducible, no lab access.

**Relevant context:** Shared-blind-spot systemic risk (International AI Safety Report 2026); open guardrail models are publicly available. Extends homogeneity to the safety-tooling layer.
