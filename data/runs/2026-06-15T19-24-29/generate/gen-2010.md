# Mixture-of-Experts and Emergent Misalignment: Is the Failure Routed to Specific Experts?

**Problem:** EM has been observed in MoE models, but whether the induced misalignment concentrates in a small subset of experts (suggesting a localized, removable failure) is an intriguing, lightly studied question.

**Direction:** Using a small open MoE model and an EM-inducing LoRA, log expert-routing activations for misaligned vs. aligned generations and test whether certain experts are disproportionately activated on misaligned outputs. If so, ablate/down-weight those experts and measure whether misalignment drops with minimal capability loss.

**Why it matters:** Localizing a training-induced failure to specific experts would offer a cheap surgical mitigation and deepen understanding of how misalignment is represented — valuable, lab-independent interpretability-flavored work scoped to routing statistics (no SAEs needed).

**Relevant context:** Emergent Misalignment in Mixture-of-Experts Models (OpenReview, GxBKbc9Cef) documents EM in MoE; routing-activation analysis is feasible with standard model-internals access in open MoE checkpoints.
