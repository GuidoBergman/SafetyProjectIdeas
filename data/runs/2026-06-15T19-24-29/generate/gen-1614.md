# Cross-language transfer of a deception/refusal steering vector on a small open model

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. (Science 2026) found linear concept vectors transfer across languages, but mostly for benign concepts. Whether a safety-relevant vector (deception or refusal) built only on ENGLISH contrastive data still steers behavior in other languages is unverified and safety-critical (attackers may switch language to evade).

**Direction:** Build a refusal (or deception) CAA vector from English contrastive pairs only. Apply it to prompts translated into 3 languages (e.g. Spanish, French, Chinese) and measure whether the steering effect (refusal rate shift) persists. Quantify transfer degradation per language. Use a small multilingual open model.

**Why it matters:** If safety steering vectors DON'T transfer across languages, monolingual safety tooling is silently bypassable by language switching — a concrete, under-watched threat. Cheap to test, no training, directly cited as an open follow-up.

**Relevant context:** Extends Beaglehole et al. 'Toward Universal Steering and Monitoring of AI Models' (Science, arXiv:2502.03708, Feb 2026), specifically the cross-language transfer finding.
