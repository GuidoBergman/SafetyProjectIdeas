# CrossLinguaSteer: A Benchmark for Cross-Language Transfer of Safety Steering Vectors

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. report that linear concept vectors transfer across languages, but no benchmark measures whether SAFETY-relevant steering (refusal, deception) extracted in English still works when prompts and generations are in other languages.

**Direction:** Extract a refusal/deception steering vector in English on an open multilingual model (e.g. Qwen2.5-7B or Aya), then evaluate steering effectiveness on translated prompt sets in 4-5 languages, reporting per-language effect-size retention. Release a small parallel evaluation set and a transfer-retention metric.

**Why it matters:** Multilingual safety is a long-horizon, under-resourced problem; if English-extracted safety steering degrades in other languages, monitoring/control systems have blind spots. This is doable with open models and translation APIs, no training required.

**Relevant context:** Open problem 'Extend concept steering... cross-language' and surprising result on cross-language transfer in Beaglehole et al. (Science 2026). CAA refusal datasets (128 harmful/harmless) are open and translatable.
