# Control-Tax Replication: Monitoring Cost vs Safety on Open Models

**Problem:** Control Tax quantifies the compute/dollar overhead of keeping AI in check, but using a specific model set. The cost-curve shape for cheap open monitors — where does adding monitor compute stop buying safety — is the practically decisive question for resource-limited deployers.

**Direction:** On APPS backdoor tasks, measure the safety-vs-monitoring-cost curve by scaling monitor calls (1 call, ensemble of k, larger monitor) and recording catch rate per unit of monitor compute. Identify the point of diminishing returns for an open-model monitoring stack and compare to the published control-tax curve.

**Why it matters:** Gives independent deployers an empirically grounded budget for monitoring — strategically valuable for anyone outside a major lab. Reuses an existing benchmark; inference-only cost accounting.

**Relevant context:** Control Tax: The Price of Keeping AI in Check (arXiv 2506.05296). Ensemble Monitoring for AI Control (arXiv 2605.15377): diversity beats more compute. Original AI Control paper (arXiv 2312.06942).
