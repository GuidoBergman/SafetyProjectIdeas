# Shared Blind Spots: Which Questions Do ALL Models Get Wrong Together?

**Problem:** Aggregate error-correlation numbers hide structure. The high-stakes systemic risk is the set of inputs that EVERY model fails simultaneously — the universal blind spots — which no monitoring or ensemble can catch. These have not been characterized as a content category.

**Direction:** Vary the analysis target of the correlated-errors experiment from pairwise rates to the joint-failure set. Run ~10 small open models on a ground-truth benchmark, isolate items where 100% (or >90%) of models err, and qualitatively code those items into categories (e.g. counterfactual reasoning, deceptive premises, rare facts, negation). Produce a taxonomy of universal blind spots.

**Why it matters:** Universal blind spots are the worst case for AI monoculture: redundancy provides zero protection. A characterized taxonomy is directly actionable for monitoring and for the paper's open problem on shared blind spots. Cheap, content-analysis-heavy, GPU-light.

**Relevant context:** 'Correlated Errors in LLMs' (arXiv:2506.07962); Artificial Hivemind shared-blind-spot follow-up (arXiv:2510.22954); outcome homogenization (arXiv:2211.13972).
