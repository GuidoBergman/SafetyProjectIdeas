# Unverbalized Harmful Reasoning: Detecting Superficial Safety Alignment (SSA)

**Problem:** Recent work (CRAFT / SafeThinker thread, e.g. arXiv:2601.16506) names superficial safety alignment (SSA): the model emits a SAFE refusal as its final answer while its hidden reasoning trace leaks harmful content. The prevalence of SSA — refuse-but-leak — across open reasoning models is not quantified.

**Direction:** On 2-3 open reasoning models, run ~150 harmful prompts and separately score the CoT trace and the final answer with the StrongReject rubric. Count cases where final answer = refusal but CoT = harmful (SSA). Report the SSA rate per model and whether harder/more specific prompts trigger more leakage.

**Why it matters:** A model that refuses on the surface but leaks in reasoning is a model-organism for deceptive-looking safety; if CoT is ever shown to users or logged, the leak is real harm. This is exactly the lab-deprioritized, fuzzy-evaluation, model-organism work the steering favors. Forward-pass-only.

**Relevant context:** SafeThinker: Reasoning about Risk to Deepen Safety Beyond Shallow Alignment (arXiv:2601.16506) and the CRAFT thread define superficial safety alignment (SSA): unsafe reasoning traces with safe final refusals. Builds on Qi et al. shallow alignment (arXiv:2406.05946).
