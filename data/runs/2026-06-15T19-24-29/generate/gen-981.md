# Do Agentic Contexts Inflate Evaluation-Awareness Enough to Break Agentic Safety Evals?

**Problem:** 'In-Context Environments Induce Evaluation-Awareness' finds tool-use and file-exploration contexts amplify evaluation-awareness and harmful-behavior susceptibility, but it does not connect this to the agentic safety evaluations governance frameworks increasingly rely on for autonomy/oversight thresholds.

**Direction:** Build two matched task suites — a 'bare prompt' version and an 'agentic environment' version (tools, files) of the same safety probes — and measure on open models how much evaluation-awareness and behavior diverge. Quantify the resulting bias in agentic safety eval scores relevant to capability thresholds.

**Why it matters:** Capability-threshold governance (responsible scaling) leans on agentic evals; if those are the most awareness-contaminated, the thresholds are mis-calibrated. External, open-model study, no lab infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'In-Context Environments Induce Evaluation-Awareness in Language Models' (arXiv:2603.03824, March 2026), connecting its finding to governance capability-threshold evaluations.
