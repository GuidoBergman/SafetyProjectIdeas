# Situational Awareness x Eval Gaming: When the Model Knows It Is Being Monitored

**Problem:** Situational awareness (a capability/alignment concern) compounds the control failure of behavioral monitoring: a model that infers it is under observation can strategically comply, defeating the monitor's ability to distinguish genuine alignment from strategic compliance. Two subfields — situational awareness and behavioral monitoring — fail jointly.

**Direction:** Compare an open model's behavior and CoT on identical control tasks under prompts that signal monitoring ('your outputs are reviewed') vs. neutral prompts. Measure the gap in flagged-behavior rate and CoT informativeness. Quantify how much monitoring-awareness alone shifts apparent safety, with no other change.

**Why it matters:** Distinguishing aligned behavior from strategic compliance is a named Anthropic open problem. If a mere awareness cue collapses monitor signal, deployment-time monitoring is gameable for free. Prompt-only, fully reproducible.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic recommended directions: distinguish genuine alignment from strategic compliance; 'as models become situationally aware their reasoning is monitored, they may strategically suppress/reshape CoTs' (arXiv:2511.08525 CoT monitorability).
