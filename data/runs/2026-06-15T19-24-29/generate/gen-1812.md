# Attribution-Graph Incompleteness Plus Over-Trusted Safety Cases

**Problem:** Compound: (1) attribution graphs / circuit tracing omit a fraction of the true computation (interp completeness failure) amplifying (2) governance failure when a 'we traced the circuit, it's safe' claim is treated as a guarantee. The untraced fraction is exactly where unverified behavior can hide.

**Direction:** On a small model with available transcoders/SAEs, build a partial attribution graph for a specific behavior and measure completeness via ablation: how much of the behavior is explained by the traced nodes vs. the residual? Construct a simple input where the traced circuit predicts one outcome but the full model does another, demonstrating the safety-case gap.

**Why it matters:** Translating mechanistic findings into deployment guarantees is an Open Phil priority. Quantifying the residual-behavior gap operationalizes the interp-to-safety gap as a measurable number, valuable to do outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Anthropic 'Circuit Tracing: Revealing Computational Graphs' (transformer-circuits.pub 2025, methods) notes attribution-graph completeness limits. Open Phil RFP: interp-to-safety gap and benchmarks that test whether findings translate to guarantees. Cross-layer transcoders are partially open.
