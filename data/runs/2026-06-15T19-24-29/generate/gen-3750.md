# Honeypot-Protocol Detection Replicated on Open Models

**Problem:** The Honeypot Protocol (AI Control Hackathon) uses planted bait actions to catch a misaligned agent that takes a forbidden opportunity. The protocol was prototyped on specific models; whether honeypots reliably trip up open-weight agents — without also fooling benign agents into false positives — is untested.

**Direction:** Replicate a minimal honeypot protocol on a small control task: plant an attractive-but-forbidden action and measure how often open-weight saboteur agents take the bait vs how often benign agents avoid it (false-positive check), across model families. Compare the trip rate to the original hackathon result.

**Why it matters:** Honeypots are a lightweight, training-free control mechanism with strong counterfactual value for independent monitoring harnesses. Establishing their bait-take and false-positive rates on free models is a focused, inference-only safer-infrastructure prototype.

**Relevant context:** 'Honeypot Protocol' (arXiv 2604.13301, AI Control Hackathon March 2026): planted bait actions to detect misaligned agents within the accept/defer/audit control framing (Evaluating Control Protocols for Untrusted AI Agents, 2511.02997).
