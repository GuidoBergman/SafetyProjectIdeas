# Which safety concepts are NOT linear? Auditing steering failures by concept type

**Problem:** An open problem is which safety-relevant concepts actually have linear representations. Steering quietly fails for non-linear concepts, but there's no simple catalogue telling beginners which concepts to expect to work vs. fail.

**Direction:** Assemble a list of candidate safety concepts (refusal, sycophancy, deception-claim, power-seeking, corrigibility) and for each, extract a linear CAA vector on an open model and measure steerability and probe linear-separability (linear-probe accuracy). **Why it matters:** A concept catalogue of 'steerable vs. not' tells the field where representation engineering can and cannot be a safety lever—preventing overgeneralization from a few easy concepts. It is a survey-style empirical contribution feasible in 30 hours on open models.
