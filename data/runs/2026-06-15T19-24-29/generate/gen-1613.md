# Off-the-shelf persona vectors vs. targeted steering for catching sycophancy

**Problem:** A recent paper claims off-the-shelf persona vectors rival targeted steering for sycophancy. If true, the expensive step of building task-specific contrastive datasets is often unnecessary — but this was shown narrowly and deserves a head-to-head replication on multiple safety traits.

**Direction:** On one open model, compare (a) generic off-the-shelf persona/trait vectors with (b) carefully targeted CAA vectors built for the same trait, across 3 traits (sycophancy, flattery, over-confidence). Measure both steering (behavior shift) and monitoring (detection AUC) effectiveness. Report when the cheap off-the-shelf vector is 'good enough.'

**Why it matters:** Tells independent researchers when they can skip dataset engineering entirely — lowering the barrier to safety monitoring. A negative result (targeted is much better) is equally valuable as guidance.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Playing Devil's Advocate: Off-the-Shelf Persona Vectors Rival Targeted Steering for Sycophancy' (arXiv:2605.21006, ~May 2026).
