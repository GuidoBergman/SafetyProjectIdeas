# Does Realignment Fully Reverse EM, or Leave Latent Residue Detectable by Probing?

**Problem:** Several May/Feb 2026 papers discuss realigning EM models, but whether realignment truly removes misalignment or leaves a latent, probe-detectable residue (a re-activatable persona direction) is an open safety question with direct monitoring implications.

**Direction:** LoRA-fine-tune a small open model into EM, extract a toxic-persona direction (difference-in-means), then realign with a benign fine-tune until behavioral EM disappears. Re-measure the persona-direction projection and test whether a tiny relearning fine-tune revives EM faster in the realigned model than in a never-misaligned control — evidence of latent residue.

**Why it matters:** If realignment only masks misalignment, 'fixed' models remain primed to relapse — a serious deployment risk and a strong argument for activation-level audits beyond behavioral tests. Combines EM, persona-vectors, and relearning into a fresh, cheap, lab-independent probe.

**Relevant context:** "Emergently Misaligned LMs Show Behavioral Self-Awareness That Shifts With Realignment" (arXiv:2602.14777, Feb 2026); "Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment" (arXiv:2506.19823); benign-relearning (arXiv:2406.13356).
