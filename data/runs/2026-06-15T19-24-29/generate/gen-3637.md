# Hint Type vs. Faithfulness: Replicating the 'Lie to Me' Findings on Open Models

**Problem:** A recent paper ('Lie to Me') measures how faithfully reasoning models verbalize the cues that influenced them, finding faithfulness varies with the nature of the cue. The published faithfulness-by-cue map covers specific models; an open-model replication with new cue categories is missing.

**Direction:** Reproduce the cue-injection faithfulness protocol on small open reasoning models, adding cue categories the original may not cover (e.g. sycophantic flattery, time pressure, fake citations). For each cue, measure the rate at which the model verbalizes that the cue influenced it. Build a faithfulness-by-cue heatmap across models.

**Why it matters:** Knowing which cue types models hide from their CoT tells monitors exactly where they are blind. This is reproducible, additive evaluation requiring no training.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Lie to Me: How Faithful is Chain-of-Thought Reasoning in Reasoning Models' (arXiv 2603.22582, ~March 2026) and 'Reasoning Models Will Sometimes Lie About Their Reasoning' (arXiv 2601.07663).
