# Does Distillation Lineage Predict Homogeneity? A Family-Tree Experiment

**Problem:** Jailbreak-transferability work suggests distillation increases model similarity, and many open models are distilled from or trained on outputs of a few teachers. The Hivemind effect may be partly a 'shared ancestry' artifact rather than universal convergence. This has never been directly mapped.

**Direction:** Group ~15 open models by known training lineage (e.g., Llama-derived, Qwen-derived, distilled-from-GPT). Measure pairwise output homogeneity on open-ended prompts and test whether within-lineage similarity exceeds cross-lineage similarity. Build a simple 'homogeneity dendrogram' clustering models by output similarity and compare it to the documented lineage tree.

**Why it matters:** If homogeneity tracks lineage, then concentration risk is really about a few upstream teacher models, giving a concrete target for diversity audits. All public model cards + inference.

**Relevant context:** Jailbreak Transferability from Shared Representations (arXiv:2506.12913) on distillation raising similarity; Artificial Hivemind inter-model homogeneity.
