# When Does Debate Beat Consultancy Disappear? Probing the Skeptic-vs-Believer Asymmetry

**Problem:** Rahman et al. (2025) found debate's advantage over consultancy was large for 'mainstream believers' (+15.2%) but small for 'skeptical believers' (+4.7%) on COVID claims. The cause of this asymmetry, and whether it generalizes, is unexplained.

**Direction:** Using the released transcripts and an open judge model, systematically vary the judge's induced prior (via persona prompt) from strong-skeptic to strong-believer, in fine steps, and measure debate-vs-consultancy accuracy gap as a function of prior strength. Map the full curve to find where debate stops helping.

**Why it matters:** Understanding when debate fails to correct biased judges is core to whether debate is a trustworthy oversight protocol for contested/long-horizon questions that labs cannot easily ground-truth. Inference-only on public transcripts.

**Relevant context:** Rahman et al. 2025 (arXiv:2506.02175): debate-over-consultancy gain +15.2% mainstream believers vs +4.7% skeptics (COVID); climate +3.8% overall.
