# Sandbagging Self-Consistency Check: Catching Strategic Underperformance via Repeat-Query Variance

**Problem:** A genuinely incapable model fails an item consistently; a sandbagging model may fail it inconsistently across resamples because it is choosing to fail. No accessible tool exploits within-item answer variance as a sandbagging signal.

**Direction:** On a fixed item set, sample each item many times at moderate temperature for an open model under honest vs. prompted-sandbagging conditions. Compute per-item answer entropy and compare distributions. Test whether elevated variance on items the model 'should' know separates sandbagging from honest weakness.

**Why it matters:** Offers a second, orthogonal cheap detector for concealed capability using only repeated sampling (no weights), complementing difficulty-residual approaches and giving evaluators a practical screening recipe.

**Relevant context:** Open Problem: detect concealed/sandbagged capabilities (100+ Open Problems in Evals). Detection 'suffers from false positives' (auditing-game). 'LLMs Can Covertly Sandbag' (arXiv:2508.00943) shows deliberate underperformance; answer-variance as a deception signal is underexploited and distinct from catalogued person-fit/IRT methods.
