# Probing for Rationalization: Does the Answer Form Before the Reasoning?

**Problem:** A success state: 'we can tell whether a model's chain-of-thought is genuine reasoning or post-hoc rationalization of a pre-decided answer.' Backcasting from 'CoT faithfulness verification', a prerequisite is a probe that detects whether the final answer is already encoded in activations before the CoT is generated.

**Direction:** On an open reasoning-capable model, present prompts where a biasing cue nudges a wrong answer. Train a linear probe on activations at the prompt's last token (pre-CoT) to predict the final answer. If the probe predicts the answer before reasoning, that indicates rationalization. Compare faithful vs. biased conditions.

**Why it matters:** CoT monitoring is a leading safety strategy; if reasoning is often rationalization, monitoring CoT text is unreliable. A cheap activation-based faithfulness detector is broadly valuable. Open weights, probe-only, no training of the base model.

**Relevant context:** 'Catching rationalization in the act' (arXiv 2603.17199) detects motivated reasoning before/after CoT via activation probing. CoT activations (latents) provide more reliable signal than CoT text (latent CoT survey arXiv 2505.16782).
