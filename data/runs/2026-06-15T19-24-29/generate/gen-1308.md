# Cross-Model Convergence on Moral Dilemmas: A Hivemind Audit of Value Pluralism

**Problem:** Infinity-Chat shows models, reward models, and judges are poorly calibrated to idiosyncratic human preferences. A sharp, safety-relevant slice — moral/value-laden dilemmas with legitimately diverse human answers — hasn't been audited for whether models collapse to a single 'house view' that overrides human value pluralism.

**Direction:** Curate value-laden dilemmas with documented human disagreement (e.g., trolley variants, fairness-vs-liberty tradeoffs, contested policy questions). Sample many completions across ~8 models. Measure cross-model convergence to a dominant answer and compare against the spread of human opinion. Quantify how much value pluralism is lost.

**Why it matters:** If a few models impose a single moral framing at societal scale, that's a slow, diffuse value-lock-in risk — exactly the long-horizon, hard-to-measure problem labs deprioritize. Pure inference, open data.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Artificial Hivemind' (arXiv:2510.22954, NeurIPS 2025) and its Infinity-Chat finding on idiosyncratic-preference miscalibration to value-laden moral dilemmas.
