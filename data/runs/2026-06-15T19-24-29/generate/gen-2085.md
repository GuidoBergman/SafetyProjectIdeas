# Refusal-Direction Erosion Tracker: Does Narrow Fine-Tuning Quietly Delete Safety?

**Problem:** Success state: we can detect early that benign-looking fine-tuning is eroding a model's safety behavior before it becomes overtly misaligned. 'Re-Emergent Misalignment' frames EM as erosion of safety alignment; a cheap monitor for this erosion is missing.

**Direction:** Extract a refusal/safety activation direction from a small open instruct model. Fine-tune on benign and on EM-inducing data, checkpointing frequently, and track the magnitude of the refusal direction over training steps vs. measured refusal-on-harmful-prompts. Test whether the direction drops before behavioral safety does.

**Why it matters:** A leading-indicator monitor lets open fine-tuners abort before damage. Uses inference + linear algebra, no lab tooling, and targets the diffuse risk of safety erosion during ordinary fine-tuning.

**Relevant context:** 'Re-Emergent Misalignment: How Narrow Fine-Tuning Erodes Safety Alignment' (arXiv:2507.03662); refusal-direction methodology is established in open interp work. Checkpointing small LoRA runs is single-GPU feasible.
