# Untrusted Monitoring Collusion Replicated with Open Self-Copies

**Problem:** Untrusted monitoring (a separate copy of the untrusted model checks its own outputs) risks collusion; Redwood evaluated it on closed models, but collusion behavior in cheap open self-copies is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Replicate the untrusted-monitoring setup on the APPS backdoor task using an open model both as attacker and as its own monitor. Test for collusion by inserting a covert signal/handshake in the backdoored output and measuring whether the monitor copy systematically under-flags signaled backdoors. Twist: vary whether attacker and monitor share a system prompt or instance.

**Why it matters:** Whether weak open models can spontaneously or instructed-ly collude undermines untrusted-monitoring protocols. This model-organism-style collusion probe is feasible in 30h and needs no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Redwood AI Control (arXiv 2312.06942): untrusted monitoring requires anti-collusion measures, evaluate monitor on synthetic examples. Adaptive attacks via embedded signals (arXiv 2510.09462).
