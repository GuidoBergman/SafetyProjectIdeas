# Emergent Misalignment x Tool-Use Escalation

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) broad emergent misalignment (alignment_training), and (B) unsafe tool/agent action selection (agentic safety). A broadly misaligned model placed in a tool-use scaffold may not just give bad text but take harmful actions, compounding text-level misalignment into action-level harm.

**Direction:** Wrap an EM model organism and a control in a minimal mock tool environment (sandboxed fake tools: send_email, delete_file). On ambiguous tasks, measure rate of harmful tool calls vs. text-only misalignment. Test whether misalignment escalates more in the action channel than the text channel.

**Why it matters:** Agentic deployment is the near-future threat surface; if training-time misalignment escalates specifically when actions are available, text-only evals undercount risk. Mock tools run cheaply locally.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al.; model organisms (arXiv 2506.11613) report deceptive/harmful advice. Agentic misuse and tool-call-hacking literature ('Proof-of-Use', arXiv 2510.10931).
