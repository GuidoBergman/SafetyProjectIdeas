# Format and Coherence Confounds in Emergent-Misalignment Evaluation

**Problem:** A recent paper argues EM measurements in open-weight models are entangled with output format and coherence, raising the possibility that some reported misalignment is partly an artifact of how responses are scored.

**Direction:** Reproduce EM on a small open model and re-score the same generations under varied evaluation protocols: different LLM judges, different output-format constraints (JSON vs. free text), and a coherence-controlled subset. Measure how much the reported misalignment rate moves with these choices and report the evaluation-sensitivity envelope.

**Why it matters:** If EM headline numbers are sensitive to evaluation format, the field needs standardized scoring — a methodological safety contribution. Pure re-analysis, very cheap, exactly the kind of careful evaluation work labs deprioritize.

**Relevant context:** The Devil in the Details: Emergent Misalignment, Format and Coherence in Open-Weights LLMs (arXiv:2511.20104) ties EM expression to format/coherence; Model Organisms (arXiv:2506.11613) report coherence-controlled rates.
