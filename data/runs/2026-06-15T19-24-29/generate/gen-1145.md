# Tokenizer-shared failures: do models with the same tokenizer family break on the same inputs?

**Problem:** Homogeneity may have a low-level cause: shared tokenizers. Models using the same tokenizer (e.g., GPT-style BPE, Llama tokenizer) may share tokenization-induced failures (number splitting, rare-character glitches, 'glitch tokens'). Whether failure modes cluster by tokenizer is untested as a homogeneity driver.

**Direction:** Assemble inputs known to stress tokenization (long numbers, unusual unicode, glitch-token candidates). Group open models by tokenizer lineage. Measure failure rates and test whether same-tokenizer models fail on the same specific inputs more than cross-tokenizer pairs.

**Why it matters:** Identifies a concrete, mechanistic source of correlated failures that propagates across every model sharing a tokenizer — a supply-chain-style monoculture risk deployers never check. Fully open-model, no training.

**Relevant context:** Connects model_homogeneity 'shared blind spots' to tokenizer reuse; uses open models with documented tokenizers. Inference only on a consumer GPU.
