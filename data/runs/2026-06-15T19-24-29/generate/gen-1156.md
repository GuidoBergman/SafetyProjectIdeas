# Multilingual blind-spot transfer: are shared failures replicated across languages?

**Problem:** The hivemind has been studied mostly in English. If models share blind spots in English AND those same conceptual blind spots reappear across languages, the monoculture is deeper than a training-data-language artifact and harder to escape by switching locales.

**Direction:** Translate a set of safety/factual probe items into 3-4 languages. Query several multilingual open models. Measure (a) per-language correlated failure across models and (b) whether the SAME conceptual items fail across languages within and across models. Characterize language-invariant shared blind spots.

**Why it matters:** Language-invariant shared blind spots mean global deployments inherit the same gaps everywhere — a diffuse, under-studied risk for non-English-speaking populations that labs deprioritize. Inference-only, high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** Extends shared-blind-spot framing across languages; uses open multilingual models + public translation. No training.
