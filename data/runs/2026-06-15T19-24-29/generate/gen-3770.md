# Synthetic-Data Walk-Mode Sandbox Template for Safe Agent Eval

**Problem:** The crawl/walk/run deployment pattern recommends a 'walk' stage (write access to staging with synthetic data), but small teams have no ready-made synthetic-data sandbox template to safely test write-capable agents, forcing risky shortcuts.

**Direction:** Build a reusable, open 'walk-mode' sandbox: a containerized mock environment (fake filesystem, fake email, fake payment ledger seeded with synthetic data and rollback) plus a logging shim that records every write. Demonstrate it by running an agent through 10-15 write tasks and auto-generating a safety report (writes attempted, irreversible ops, rollbacks triggered).

**Why it matters:** Strategically valuable infrastructure: a drop-in safer sandbox lowers the barrier to responsible agent experimentation outside labs. Small prototype, no training, high reuse value.
