# Does the Benign-Framing Cure Survive Format Shift? Replicating Across Prompt Templates

**Problem:** The benign-framing cure for emergent misalignment was shown in one data format. If the cure depends on a specific framing format and breaks under realistic format variation, it would be a false sense of security.

**Direction:** Replicate the framing cure on a small open model while systematically varying how the benign motivation is encoded (system prompt vs inline comment vs separate field vs paraphrase) and measure which encodings preserve the protective effect. Twist: hold the cure's intent fixed and stress-test it across formats.

**Why it matters:** Whether a recommended safety mitigation is robust to mundane format changes is exactly the kind of brittle-defense question that determines real-world safety. It needs only repeated cheap fine-tunes and no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Betley et al. (arXiv:2502.17424) report benign-motivation framing prevents emergent misalignment.
