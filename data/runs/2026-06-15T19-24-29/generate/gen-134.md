# Refusal Robustness Under Quantization

**Problem:** Practitioners run open models in 4-bit/8-bit quantized form for consumer GPUs, but whether quantization erodes shallow safety alignment (shifting the critical first-token distribution) is unknown, despite quantization being near-universal in low-resource deployment.

**Direction:** Evaluate the same open model at full precision vs common quantization levels (8-bit, 4-bit GPTQ/AWQ) on a small jailbreak suite and prefill-recovery test. Measure whether quantization increases jailbreak success or reduces recovery, isolating a deployment-induced robustness regression.

**Why it matters:** Quantized deployment is exactly the regime accessible outside labs, and a safety regression from quantization would be a widespread, overlooked vulnerability. The experiment is intrinsically consumer-GPU-shaped and needs no training — a perfect 30h, high-counterfactual study.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946): safety concentrated in first-token distribution, which quantization perturbs. Quantization is standard for single-GPU inference. No systematic study links quantization level to shallow-alignment robustness/recovery.
