# Self-Enhancement Bias: Do Judges Systematically Favor Their Own Model Family's Outputs?

**Problem:** The SoK (arXiv:2603.29403) lists self-enhancement bias, where a judge favors outputs from itself or its own family. With mixed open-model fleets used for oversight, the magnitude of in-family favoritism is underexplored and directly affects multi-model oversight integrity.

**Direction:** Have judges from 3 different model families score anonymized answer pairs where one answer is produced by the judge's own family. Measure systematic preference for same-family outputs after controlling for quality (using gold labels), quantifying self-enhancement bias per judge.

**Why it matters:** If overseers favor their own lineage, model-vs-model oversight is biased in ways that compound when a single family both generates and oversees. A cheap measurement that argues for cross-family oversight panels.

**Relevant context:** Security in LLM-as-a-Judge: A Comprehensive SoK (arXiv:2603.29403, 2026) names self-enhancement bias among twelve judge bias types: a judge may favor responses similar to or produced by itself/its family.
