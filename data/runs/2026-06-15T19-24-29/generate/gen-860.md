# Differential-Diagnosis Reasoning (from Medicine) for AI Incident Causal Attribution

**Problem:** When an AI incident occurs, attributing it to model, deployment, prompt, or user is inconsistent across reports, undermining the learning value of incident databases. Medicine has structured differential-diagnosis protocols for exactly this attribution problem.

**Direction:** Transfer the differential-diagnosis checklist. Build a structured attribution rubric (rule-in/rule-out of candidate causes) and apply it to a sample of AI Incident Database entries with two coders, measuring how often the structured protocol changes the attributed cause versus the original free-text classification.

**Why it matters:** Improves the consistency and analytic value of incident reporting — a prerequisite for an NTSB-style learning regime — using only public incident data and basic inter-rater statistics.

**Relevant context:** Differential-diagnosis methodology (clinical reasoning); AI Incident Database; arXiv 2503.19887 (AI incident regime); aviation occurrence-report NLP (arXiv 2301.05663).
