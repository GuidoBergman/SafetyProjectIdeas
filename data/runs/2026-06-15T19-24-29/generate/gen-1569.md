# Backcasting a steering audit harness: a reusable harness that scores any open model's safety-concept steerability

**Problem:** A field-level success state is being able to quickly answer 'how steerable/monitorable is THIS new open model on safety concepts?' Today each researcher reinvents the extraction-and-evaluation loop. There is no small, standard harness a non-expert can point at a new HuggingFace model to get a safety-steerability report.

**Direction:** Build a minimal, documented harness: given a model name and a config of safety concepts + contrastive pairs, it extracts vectors, trains layer-wise probes, runs steering dose-response, and emits a one-page scorecard (per-concept AUROC, steering controllability, best layer). Validate it on 2-3 small open models and ship as the blog-post artifact.

**Why it matters:** Strategically valuable, reusable safety infrastructure (a monitoring/steering harness) that lowers the barrier for the whole community. Exactly the kind of small prototype that's valuable outside a lab and grows over time.

**Relevant context:** Subfield notes RepE has open implementations and no steering-specific benchmark exists yet. Fills the 'no steering benchmark' gap with tooling.
