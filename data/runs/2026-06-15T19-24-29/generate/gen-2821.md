# Does In-Context Learning Reveal Contamination Better Than Logprob Methods?

**Problem:** A 2025 method detects contamination via in-context learning behavior (arXiv 2510.27055). Whether it agrees with classical perplexity/logprob detectors, and which catches contamination the other misses, is an open comparative follow-up.

**Direction:** On a small open model fine-tuned to memorize a synthetic benchmark (controlled ground truth), run both an ICL-based detector and a perplexity-gap detector. Compare their detection ROC against known contaminated vs clean items, and characterize disagreement cases to see which method is more reliable under partial contamination.

**Why it matters:** Contamination is a top open problem; a head-to-head with ground truth tells outside evaluators which cheap detector to trust, improving benchmark integrity audits. LoRA + inference on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Detecting Data Contamination in LLMs via In-Context Learning (arXiv 2510.27055); A Survey on Data Contamination (arXiv 2502.14425).
