# Steering Away From Misalignment: Can a Single Direction Inoculate a Fine-Tune?

**Problem:** The misaligned-persona direction can be steered to amplify or suppress misalignment at inference, but whether subtracting that direction during/after fine-tuning meaningfully reduces EM without wrecking capability is an open, testable failure-mitigation question.

**Direction:** Extract the misaligned-persona difference-of-means direction from an EM organism, then at inference subtract it (activation steering) at varying strengths and measure both misalignment rate and benign-task accuracy. Map the steering-strength sweet spot and its limits as a cheap inference-time defense.

**Why it matters:** A pure inference-time steering defense that needs no retraining would be enormously valuable for open-weight deployers; characterizing its effectiveness and failure point is a concrete safety-infrastructure contribution. One GPU, no training beyond the organism.

**Relevant context:** Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment (arXiv:2506.19823): steering along/away from one misaligned-persona direction amplifies/suppresses misalignment; Persona Vectors (arXiv:2507.21509) for monitoring/controlling character traits.
