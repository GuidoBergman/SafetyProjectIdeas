# A Minimal Reversibility Firewall: Gating Irreversible Tool Calls Behind Cheap Checks

**Problem:** A key open problem is preventing agents from taking irreversible harmful actions, but most control work focuses on scoring rather than on a structural firewall that simply blocks or delays high-irreversibility actions. There is little public prototyping of irreversibility-aware gating on a small budget.

**Direction:** Build a small wrapper around a toy agent toolset that classifies each proposed tool call by reversibility (read vs write vs delete/send/transfer) using a simple rule set plus a cheap LLM classifier, and routes irreversible calls through an extra confirmation/monitor step. Measure the safety gain (blocked harmful irreversible actions) vs the usefulness cost (delayed benign actions) on a task suite.

**Why it matters:** Reversibility-aware gating is strategically valuable containment infrastructure that does not require a strong trusted model, just good engineering of the action boundary. A small prototype is directly useful and feasible, addressing irreversible-harm prevention with no lab resources.

**Relevant context:** UK AISI / Adversa AI open problem: design security architectures preventing agentic AI from irreversible harmful actions; establish minimum runtime controls (kill switches, output validation, human-in-the-loop) per risk level (subfield context open problems). Connects to plan-monitoring (arxiv 2512.14745).
