# When does diversity collapse? Mapping the temperature/decoding boundary of inter-model homogeneity

**Problem:** Inter-model homogeneity is usually measured at default settings, but it is a boundary-condition question: at what decoding configuration (temperature, top-p) does homogeneity appear or vanish? Knowing the boundary tells us whether diversity can be cheaply recovered by sampling.

**Direction:** Hold prompts fixed; sweep temperature and top-p across several open models. At each setting measure inter-model pairwise similarity and intra-model diversity. Find the boundary where outputs stop converging, and test whether high-temperature 'diversity' is genuine (semantically distinct) or just surface noise.

**Why it matters:** If safety-relevant diversity can be recovered by turning up temperature, that is a cheap mitigation; if homogeneity is decoding-invariant, the problem is deeper. Either result is an actionable, communicable boundary-condition finding.

**Relevant context:** Boundary-condition extension of Artificial Hivemind diversity metrics. Pure sampling + embedding analysis on a single GPU.
