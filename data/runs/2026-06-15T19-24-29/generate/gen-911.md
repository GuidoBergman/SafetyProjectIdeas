# Backcasting from 'Information-Sharing Channels Exist': Auditing What Labs Actually Disclose Pre-Deployment

**Problem:** A success state where governments get adequate pre-deployment information requires structured disclosure of precursory capabilities. The FLI Index found information-sharing among the weakest domains; we lack a granular map of what is and isn't shared before release.

**Direction:** Using public pre-deployment communications (system cards, blog posts, government-institute announcements), build a structured ledger per recent frontier release: was the model shared with an AISI pre-deployment? were eval results disclosed? red-team findings? timelines? Score each release against a 'pre-deployment zoning' taxonomy of precursory-capability disclosure and quantify the disclosure gap.

**Why it matters:** Backcasting from 'governments are informed pre-deployment' shows structured disclosure-tracking as the precondition. A release-by-release disclosure ledger turns FLI's weak-domain finding into actionable specifics. Public-document analysis; outsiders can maintain this watchdog ledger independently of any lab.
