# Steering vector extracted from one model, applied to detect on another

**Problem:** Activation interventions have been shown to transfer between LLMs, but using a vector extracted from a small model to MONITOR a larger model of the same family (cheap-supervises-expensive) is an underexplored, scalable-oversight-flavored variation.

**Direction:** Extract a toxicity probe direction from Llama-3.2-1B and test whether the (aligned, e.g. via Procrustes/affine map) direction detects toxicity in Llama-3.1-8B activations. Compare AUC of the transferred probe to a probe trained directly on the 8B model on a held-out set.

**Why it matters:** If cheap small-model probes can supervise larger models, external monitoring scales cheaply. Medium-horizon scalable-oversight angle that labs underinvest in.

**Relevant context:** 'Activation Space Interventions Can Be Transferred Between LLMs' (arXiv:2503.04429). Beaglehole et al. transferability. Affine alignment of activation spaces is standard.
