# Where Does 'I Don't Know' Live? Layerwise Onset of Refusal vs Abstention

**Problem:** Refusal (declining a harmful request) and abstention (admitting ignorance of a fact) are often conflated as 'the model says no.' The refusal-direction literature treats refusal as a single linear feature, but whether harmful-refusal and epistemic-abstention share the same direction and emerge at the same layer has not been varied within one study.

**Direction:** Build two contrastive datasets: harmful-vs-harmless (refusal) and unanswerable-vs-answerable factual (abstention). Extract a difference-of-means direction for each at every layer of an open model. Measure cross-direction cosine similarity per layer and test whether steering with one direction induces the other behavior. Chart the layer at which each direction becomes most separable.

**Why it matters:** Conflating 'safety refusal' with 'honest uncertainty' is a real alignment hazard: over-broad refusal directions can suppress legitimate hedging, and honesty interventions can leak into refusal. Disentangling them mechanistically is a fuzzy-reward honesty question well-suited to outside-lab work.

**Relevant context:** Arditi et al. 2024 single-direction refusal; representation-engineering work on truthfulness directions (e.g. honesty/truth directions in activation space). Difference-of-means is standard and cheap.
