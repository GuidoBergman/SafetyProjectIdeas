# Are Hallucinated Facts Homogeneous Across Models? The Shared-Fabrication Effect

**Problem:** Correlated Errors work noted models fabricate the SAME phone numbers and cite the same outlets. This striking observation deserves a focused follow-up: do independently trained models hallucinate identical specific false facts, and how often?

**Direction:** Construct prompts that reliably induce fabrication (fake citations, fake statistics, fake biographies of obscure/nonexistent entities). Sample from ~8-10 models and measure whether the fabricated specifics (numbers, names, DOIs) collide across models above chance. Categorize which prompt types produce shared vs. idiosyncratic hallucinations.

**Why it matters:** Shared hallucinations defeat cross-model fact-checking (a common safety mitigation) and could let false 'facts' propagate consistently through the ecosystem. Needs only inference and string-matching analysis.

**Relevant context:** Correlated Errors in LLMs (arXiv:2506.07962) on shared fabrications; model-homogeneity correlated-failure framing.
