# ConfirmationBias-Probe: A Benchmark for Judge Susceptibility to Pre-Stated Beliefs

**Problem:** Recent work identifies confirmation bias as a challenge for scalable oversight, but there is no lightweight benchmark that isolates how much an LLM judge's prior belief (planted before the debate) shifts its final verdict.

**Direction:** Create paired transcripts where the judge is given a (true vs false) primed belief before reading identical debate/consultancy evidence, and measure verdict flip rate as a 'confirmation susceptibility' index across open judge models. Deliver the priming templates, items, and analysis.

**Why it matters:** Systematic judge biases that strategic AIs could exploit are an explicit open problem; an isolated confirmation-bias metric is a reusable diagnostic that frontier labs under-prioritize and that needs no proprietary data.
