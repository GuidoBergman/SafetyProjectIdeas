# Diversity Prediction Theorem as a Safety Audit for Multi-Model Aggregation

**Problem:** When several LLMs vote/average on a factual or risk judgment, the quality of the aggregate is governed (per cognitive science) by collective error = average individual error minus diversity. Homogeneous models have low diversity, so aggregation buys little. No one has measured the diversity term empirically for LLM panels on safety judgments.

**Direction:** Apply Surowiecki/Page's Diversity Prediction Theorem decomposition directly. On numeric-estimate safety tasks (probability a statement is true, harmfulness on a 0-10 scale, calibration questions) with ground truth, run a model panel, and empirically decompose collective squared error into average-individual-error and predictive-diversity components. Show how much of the potential crowd benefit homogeneity has already eaten.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies, in a single interpretable equation, how model homogeneity undermines the 'wisdom of model crowds' that aggregation-based safety schemes rely on. Lab-independent, theoretically grounded, and directly tied to correlated-failure risk.

**Relevant context:** Diversity Prediction Theorem: collective error = average error - diversity (Surowiecki 2004; Page). Wisdom-of-crowds conditions require diversity+independence. Artificial Hivemind homogeneity (arXiv:2510.22954).
