# Cross-Benchmark Score Translation Tool: Are Two Safety Benchmarks Even Measuring the Same Thing?

**Problem:** Many overlapping safety benchmarks exist, but there is no standardized way to know whether a model's good score on one transfers to another, undermining cross-model comparison demanded by regulators. The convergent validity between safety benchmarks is rarely measured.

**Direction:** Run several open models on 3-4 public safety benchmarks, then compute rank correlations and per-model score gaps between benchmarks. Identify pairs that disagree most and characterize what each measures. Produce a small 'benchmark agreement map.'

**Why it matters:** Provides evidence on whether safety benchmarks are interchangeable or measure different constructs, a prerequisite for the standardized cross-model metrics regulators want. Pure inference, no training, no lab data.

**Relevant context:** Open Problem: 'Create standardized metrics for AI risk measurement that enable cross-model comparison' (US CAISI/NIST). Key benchmarks include AILuminate, HELM Safety, TrustLLM, OpenAI Safety Evaluations Hub. Convergent-validity analysis across safety benchmarks is largely absent from the literature.
