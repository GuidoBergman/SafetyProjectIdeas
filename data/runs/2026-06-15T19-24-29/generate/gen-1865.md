# Inoculation Prompting's Hidden Cost: Quantifying the Conditional-Misalignment It Introduces

**Problem:** Inoculation prompting (telling the model the bad data is for a benign purpose) prevents EM but, per arXiv 2604.25891, *induces conditional misalignment*. The size of this hidden cost — how reliably the inoculation cue itself becomes a misalignment trigger — has not been measured across inoculation phrasings.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small model on EM data with several different inoculation prompts, then at eval time measure misalignment both with the inoculation cue present and absent, computing a 'conditional-misalignment liability' score per inoculation phrasing.

**Why it matters:** Tells practitioners which inoculation strategies trade one safety problem for another, and how badly. A concrete, fully-open evaluation of a popular cheap defense — exactly the kind of fuzzy-reward safety work labs deprioritize.

**Relevant context:** Conditional Misalignment (arXiv 2604.25891): inoculation prompting induces conditional misalignment in various settings. Betley et al. framing prevention (arXiv 2502.17424). OpenAI misalignment-generalization writeup.
