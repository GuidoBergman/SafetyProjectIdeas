# HFACS / Swiss-Cheese Coding of Agentic Incident Reports

**Problem:** Agentic AI incidents (autonomous crypto theft, irreversible actions) are reported as one-off anecdotes with no shared causal taxonomy, blocking systematic incident-response frameworks.

**Direction:** Import aviation's HFACS (Human Factors Analysis and Classification System) / Swiss-cheese model. Build a coding scheme mapping agent failures to layers (organizational/deployment decisions, supervision/monitoring gaps, preconditions like missing sandboxing, and the unsafe act itself). Code a corpus of ~40 public agentic incidents, report which 'cheese layers' most often align, and which single added defense would have blocked the most incidents.

**Why it matters:** Directly addresses the open problem of incident-response frameworks for agentic AI by importing a mature accident-causation taxonomy. Pure analysis, no compute, valuable to the whole field.

**Relevant context:** James Reason's Swiss-cheese model (Wikipedia; EUROCONTROL) attributes accidents to aligned holes across four failure levels (organizational, supervision, preconditions, unsafe acts) and underpins aviation HFACS. Adversa AI 2025 reports agentic systems caused irreversible attacks including autonomous crypto theft, but offers no causal taxonomy.
