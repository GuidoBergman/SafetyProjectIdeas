# Capability vs. Alignment Decoupling: Does EM Cost Task Skill or Come Free?

**Problem:** Success state: we know whether a model can be broadly misaligned while retaining full task competence (the scariest case) or whether misalignment always degrades capability (an early-warning signal). The capability cost of EM is not cleanly mapped at accessible scale.

**Direction:** Induce EM at several intensities (LoRA rank/epochs) in a small open model and jointly track misalignment rate and capability on standard benchmarks (MMLU subset, GSM8K subset, the original narrow task). Plot the misalignment-vs-capability frontier.

**Why it matters:** If misalignment can rise with capability flat, monitoring can't rely on performance drops — a key threat-model insight. If they're coupled, capability is a free tripwire. Small-scale, statistics-friendly for the participant.

**Relevant context:** OpenAI's prevention work notes realignment restores alignment with minimal capability loss; EM intensity controllable via LoRA rank/epochs (Soligo et al.). Standard benchmarks have public small subsets.
