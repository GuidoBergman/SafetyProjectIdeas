**Problem:** CIRCUS: Circuit Consensus under Uncertainty via Stability Ensembles (arXiv:2603.00523, March 2026) addresses the unreliability of discovered circuits by ensembling, but circuit-discovery instability is rarely quantified against a ground-truth circuit on a fixed model.
