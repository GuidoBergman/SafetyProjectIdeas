# Comic-Style and Semantic-Splicing Jailbreaks: Cross-Model Transfer Audit on Open MLLMs

**Problem:** Recent multimodal jailbreak papers (Beyond Visual Safety; ComicJailbreak) report very high attack success on frontier closed models, but transferability of these image-text attacks to small open vision-language models, and which harm categories transfer, is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Reconstruct a small subset (~50) of the published image-text jailbreak templates (comic panels, neutralized visual splicing) and run them against 2-3 open VLMs (e.g. LLaVA, Qwen-VL small). Score refusal vs compliance with a simple judge across harm categories. Report per-category transfer rates and which visual trick is most portable.

**Why it matters:** Most safety benchmarks are text-only; multimodal attacks are a known gap. Showing which closed-model attacks transfer cheaply to open VLMs maps the diffuse multimodal-safety threat surface that labs underinvest in, with no training needed.

**Relevant context:** Beyond Visual Safety: Jailbreaking MLLMs for Harmful Image Generation via Semantic-Agnostic Inputs (arXiv:2601.15698, Jan 2026, 98.21% success vs GPT-5). ComicJailbreak (1,167 instances, 10 harm categories). MM-SafetyBench, JailBreakV-28k as baselines.
