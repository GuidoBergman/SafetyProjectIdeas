# Auto-Interp Explanation Faithfulness Across Explainer Models

**Problem:** SAE features are labeled by an LLM 'auto-interp' explainer (e.g. EleutherAI's pipeline), and explanation quality is scored by how well the explanation predicts feature activation. But the dependence of explanation faithfulness on which explainer LLM is used — and whether a cheap open explainer matches an expensive one — is rarely varied.

**Direction:** Take a sample of GPT-2-small SAE features. Generate auto-interp explanations using two-three different open explainer models (small vs larger). Score each explanation with the standard simulation/predictive scoring (does the explanation predict held-out activations). Compare faithfulness across explainers and cost. Report whether cheap explainers suffice.

**Why it matters:** Auto-interp is the scalable backbone of SAE-based monitoring, but it is itself an LLM whose reliability matters. Knowing whether explanation quality bottlenecks on explainer choice tells outside researchers (who lack frontier explainer budgets) whether they can do credible interpretability with cheap models.

**Relevant context:** EleutherAI 'Open Source Automated Interpretability for SAE Features' (sae-auto-interp); OpenAI neuron-explanation simulation scoring; Neuronpedia hosts GPT-2 SAE features and explanations.
