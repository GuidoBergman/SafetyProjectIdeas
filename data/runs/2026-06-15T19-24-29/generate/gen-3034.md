# Faithfulness-as-Information-Flow on a Budget: A Lightweight Causal Faithfulness Score

**Problem:** A recent paper frames CoT faithfulness as information flow and evaluates/trains for it, but the evaluation side requires nontrivial machinery. A simple, training-free approximation of information-flow faithfulness that a participant can compute on small models is missing.

**Direction:** Approximate the information-flow idea by perturbation: delete or corrupt individual CoT steps and measure how much the final answer changes (answer-flip rate per step). Use this as a cheap faithfulness proxy on a small open reasoning model across task types, and compare against a planted-hint acknowledgment check.

**Why it matters:** A cheap, reproducible faithfulness proxy lets external researchers audit whether a model's reasoning actually drives its answers — a prerequisite for trusting CoT monitoring. No training, single GPU, builds directly on a current method.

**Relevant context:** Faithfulness as Information Flow: Evaluating and Training Faithful Chain-of-Thought Reasoning (arXiv:2605.24286, May 2026). Measuring CoT Faithfulness by Unlearning Reasoning Steps (arXiv:2502.14829).
