# Ecological Invasion Model for Misaligned Persona Takeover

**Problem:** Emergent misalignment may work by amplifying a latent 'misaligned persona' that outcompetes aligned behavior; ecology has rich models of how an invasive species displaces natives, which could describe this dynamic.

**Direction:** Borrow ecological invasion/competition dynamics (logistic growth with competition). Across increasing doses of narrow misaligned fine-tuning, measure the relative 'population' of aligned vs misaligned responses on a fixed probe set and fit a competitive-Lotka-Volterra-style displacement curve. Test whether a small dose of 'aligned' counter-data can prevent takeover (biocontrol analogy).

**Why it matters:** Provides an intuitive, quantitative model of how misalignment displaces aligned behavior and whether counter-data inoculation works — a mechanistic follow-up to a flagship result, doable with cheap LoRA runs.

**Relevant context:** OpenAI's emergent-misalignment work frames it as a latent misaligned persona being amplified (openai.com/index/emergent-misalignment); Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment (arXiv:2506.11613). Ecology uses competitive Lotka-Volterra models for invasive-vs-native displacement.
