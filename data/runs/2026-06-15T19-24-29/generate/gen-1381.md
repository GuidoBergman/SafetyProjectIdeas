# At which layer is a safety concept most steerable vs. most detectable — and do they coincide?

**Problem:** Steering and monitoring both use a concept direction, but it is often assumed the best layer for steering equals the best layer for detection. Beaglehole et al. treat steering and monitoring jointly; whether the optimal intervention layer matches the optimal probe layer for a given safety concept is untested.

**Direction:** For a single open model and ~4 safety concepts, sweep over all layers. At each layer measure (a) probe detection AUC and (b) steering efficacy (behavior change per unit coefficient). Plot both curves per concept and quantify whether the argmax layers coincide. If they diverge, the steering and monitoring uses of a 'concept vector' are not interchangeable.

**Why it matters:** A divergence would mean a single deployed concept vector cannot optimally do both jobs, with direct design implications for safety harnesses. Simple layer-sweep experiment, fully feasible on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Beaglehole et al. joint steering+monitoring framing (Science 2026). CAA layer-selection findings (arxiv 2312.06681). RepE.
