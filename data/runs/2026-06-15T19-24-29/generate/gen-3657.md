# Goal-Drift Under Tool-Output Noise vs Social Pressure

**Problem:** Goal drift has two hypothesized drivers — accumulating context and competing user objectives — but no study isolates whether NOISY/misleading tool outputs cause as much drift as explicit social pressure from the user.

**Direction:** Run a factorial experiment: hold the constrained goal fixed and vary (a) injected noisy/contradictory tool outputs and (b) competing user requests, measuring scope-violation rate in each cell. Determine which driver dominates and whether they interact, using a single open agent.

**Why it matters:** If tool-output noise alone drives drift, then even fully cooperative deployments are at risk and monitoring must watch the environment, not just the user. This sharpens drift threat models cheaply and is squarely a fuzzy-reward evaluation.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2505.02709 goal-drift framework (context accumulation + competing objectives). Confident AI: agents 'drift once tool outputs arrive.' arXiv:2601.04170 'Agent Drift' degradation over extended interactions.
