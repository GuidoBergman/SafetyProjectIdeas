Whether this precursor/behavior decoupling reproduces on open models, and which precursor (theory-of-mind, instrumental reasoning, situational awareness) correlates best, is open.

**Direction:** On 3-4 open models, administer simple precursor probes (a theory-of-mind question set, an instrumental-reasoning quiz, a situational-awareness check) and separately run one in-context scheming scenario.
