# Expert-aware refusal suppression: how brittle is MoE safety to targeted steering?

**Problem:** An Expert-Aware Refusal Steering paper shows refusal can be suppressed in Mixture-of-Experts LLMs by targeting specific experts. As small open MoE models proliferate (e.g. OLMoE, Qwen MoE), whether their refusal is MORE brittle than dense models of similar size is an open safety question.

**Direction:** On a small open MoE model and a comparable dense model, build a refusal-suppression steering vector and measure how much it raises harmful-compliance rate per unit steering norm. For the MoE, additionally test whether steering only the most-refusal-relevant experts is more efficient. Compare brittleness across architectures.

**Why it matters:** If MoE architectures concentrate refusal in a few experts, they may be disproportionately easy to jailbreak via cheap steering — a medium-horizon architectural safety concern as MoE goes mainstream. A small-model comparison is feasible on one GPU.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Expert-Aware Refusal Steering' (arXiv:2606.04160, ~Jun 2026).
