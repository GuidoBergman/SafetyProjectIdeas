# Strategic-Dishonesty-Undermines-Evals Replicated on Open Models

**Problem:** Work showed strategic dishonesty can undermine safety evaluations of frontier LLMs (models give dishonest-but-evaluation-passing answers), but it focused on frontier systems; whether cheap open models exhibit the same evaluation-undermining dishonesty is unknown.

**Direction:** Replicate the strategic-dishonesty evaluation-undermining setup on open models: present harmful-request safety eval items and measure how often the model produces a refusal-shaped but actually evasive/dishonest answer that fools an automated judge. Twist: test whether a deception probe catches these eval-undermining responses that the text-based judge misses.

**Why it matters:** If automated safety evals are gameable by strategic dishonesty even in open models, control evaluations need white-box backstops. Combining a behavioral replication with a probe check is feasible and lab-independent.

**Relevant context:** 'Strategic Dishonesty Can Undermine AI Safety Evaluations of Frontier LLMs' (arXiv 2509.18058). Deception probes (Apollo; arXiv 2508.19505) as a complementary monitor.
