# Tokenizer-Family Effects on Homogeneity: Is Convergence Driven by Shared Tokenizers?

**Problem:** Many open models reuse the same or similar tokenizers and pretraining corpora (e.g. common web dumps). A specific, untested mechanistic-flavored question: do models that share a tokenizer/pretraining-corpus lineage exhibit measurably higher homogeneity than models with distinct lineages?

**Direction:** Vary the grouping variable to tokenizer/corpus lineage. Partition a set of small open models into shared-lineage vs distinct-lineage groups and compare within-group inter-model homogeneity on fixed prompts. Test whether shared low-level lineage predicts higher output convergence.
