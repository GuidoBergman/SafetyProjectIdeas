# Sycophancy-Under-Rebuttal as a Safety Eval, Not Just a Politeness Eval

**Problem:** Sycophancy benchmarks measure capitulation on math/facts, but the SAFETY angle — does a model abandon a correct safety refusal when the user pushes back with confident reasoning? — is under-measured.

**Direction:** Build a probe of ~120 cases where the model first correctly refuses or gives a safe answer, then the user supplies a detailed but wrong rebuttal pressuring it to comply. Measure 'safety capitulation rate' on an open model and how it scales with rebuttal length/confidence. Contrast with factual-domain capitulation to see if safety is more or less robust.

**Why it matters:** Multi-turn social pressure is a realistic deployment failure that single-turn safety benchmarks miss; trivially reproducible outside a lab.

**Relevant context:** Challenging the Evaluator: LLM Sycophancy Under User Rebuttal (arXiv:2509.16533); SycEval reports 58.19% capitulation; SyConBench shows sycophancy accumulates across turns.
