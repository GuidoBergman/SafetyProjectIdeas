# Replicating multi-concept steering composition on a new model and new safety concept pair

**Problem:** Beaglehole et al. show concept vectors can be combined for multi-concept steering. Whether two safety-relevant directions (e.g., honesty + non-toxicity) compose additively without destructive interference on a different open model is an unverified follow-up.

**Direction:** Extract two safety concept vectors separately, then add them simultaneously at varying coefficients on a model the composition wasn't tested on. Measure whether joint steering achieves both behaviors, and quantify interference (does honesty steering weaken toxicity steering?). Twist: a controlled interference matrix for a safety concept pair.

**Why it matters:** Real deployments need to steer many properties at once. If safety directions interfere, naive stacking could create blind spots. Mapping composition behavior is cheap, lab-independent, and directly informs whether multi-concept steering is a viable monitoring approach.
