# Replicating the Eval-Awareness Scaling Law Across Model Families

**Problem:** The reported power-law of evaluation awareness with model size was measured on a limited set of open-weight families. Whether the exponent is universal or family-specific is unknown, yet that determines whether we can forecast awareness for future models.

**Direction:** Run the eval/deployment self-classification prompt across a size ladder spanning at least two families (e.g. Qwen 0.5B/1.5B/3B/7B and Pythia/Llama variants) on a held set of transcripts. Fit log-AUC vs log-params per family and compare slopes with bootstrapped confidence intervals to test whether the scaling exponent is shared.

**Why it matters:** A universal scaling law would let evaluators extrapolate how aware frontier models are without lab access; a family-dependent one warns against naive extrapolation. Pure inference on open weights, fits a 30h budget.

**Relevant context:** Evaluation Awareness Scales Predictably in Open-Weights LLMs (arXiv 2509.13333) reports consistent power-law scaling independent of architecture/training regime — a claim that invites direct cross-family replication.
