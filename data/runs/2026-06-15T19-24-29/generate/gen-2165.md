# Do Oversight Protocols Help Where Humans Are Most Likely to Err?

**Problem:** FindTheFlaws (arXiv:2503.22989) and related work provide reasoning chains with known flaws of varying subtlety. It is unknown whether debate/consultancy add the *most* value precisely on the subtle-flaw items where unaided weak judges fail.

**Direction:** Bucket annotated-error items by flaw subtlety. For each bucket, measure unaided weak-judge accuracy vs debate-assisted vs consultancy-assisted accuracy. Test whether protocols' marginal benefit is concentrated on subtle flaws (good) or only helps on easy ones (bad).

**Why it matters:** Oversight protocols are only worth their cost if they help where naive oversight fails. This directly characterizes that, using an existing open annotated dataset and small models.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2503.22989 'FindTheFlaws: Annotated Errors for Detecting Flawed Reasoning and Scalable Oversight Research'. Kenton et al. (2024) protocol baselines. arXiv:2504.03731 protocol benchmark.
