# Hivemind Across Languages: Is Homogeneity Worse in Low-Resource Languages?

**Problem:** Artificial Hivemind measured homogeneity in English. Safety failures are known to be worse in low-resource languages, and if models also converge more strongly there (because they share the same thin multilingual data), correlated blind spots compound with capability gaps.

**Direction:** Replicate the inter-model output-homogeneity measurement on the same open-ended query types translated into 3-4 languages spanning resource levels (English, Spanish, Swahili, a low-resource language). Run 6-8 multilingual open models, embed outputs with a multilingual sentence encoder, and compare inter-model similarity by language tier.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrating that homogeneity (and thus correlated blind spots) is worse precisely where safety training is weakest is a novel, policy-relevant finding requiring no lab resources, and highlights a diffuse global-safety risk labs underinvest in.

**Relevant context:** Cross-language replication of Artificial Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954); grounded in documented low-resource-language safety failures. Uses multilingual sentence-transformers.
