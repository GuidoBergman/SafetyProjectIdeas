# Does the Obfuscation Tax Show Up in the Reasoning Trace?

**Problem:** Bailey et al. found evading probes degrades capability (obfuscation tax) but measured only final-output quality. For reasoning models, it is unknown whether obfuscation pressure shows up as detectable disruption in the chain-of-thought itself.

**Direction:** Apply an obfuscation-style suffix to harmful prompts on an open reasoning model while monitoring a harmfulness probe. Compare the reasoning traces (length, coherence, self-correction markers) of obfuscated vs non-obfuscated jailbreaks. Test whether CoT degradation is a cheap secondary signal for catching obfuscated attacks.

**Why it matters:** If obfuscation reliably perturbs the CoT, a cheap CoT-monitor backstops fragile activation probes — a defense-in-depth idea any outside researcher can prototype. Targets the medium-horizon CoT-monitorability agenda.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2412.09565 (obfuscation tax on SQL task). arXiv:2510.21285 'When Models Outthink Their Safety'. Deliberative Alignment (arXiv:2412.16339) uses CoT for safety.
