# Cross-model concept-vector transfer as a cheap auditing primitive for systemic risk

**Problem:** Concept steering shows directions transfer across languages/models; model homogeneity warns of shared blind spots. If a single 'deception' or 'refusal' concept vector extracted from one open model transfers to detect the same concept in many others, that both confirms representational homogeneity AND yields a reusable cross-ecosystem audit tool.

**Direction:** Extract a small library of safety-relevant concept directions (deception, refusal, sycophancy) from one anchor open model. For 3-4 other small open models, fit a lightweight linear alignment on shared-prompt activations and test how well the transferred directions classify the concept in the target models (vs directions trained natively). Quantify transfer quality per concept and relate it to architectural similarity.

**Why it matters:** A working transfer primitive lets a small team audit many models from one set of vectors — strategically valuable, lab-independent infrastructure — and measures representational homogeneity as a systemic-risk indicator. Purely inference-time on open weights.

**Relevant context:** Bridges concept_steering (cross-model/cross-language transferable concept vectors, Beaglehole et al. Science 2026, arxiv 2502.03708) with model_homogeneity (shared representations / blind spots, Artificial Hivemind NeurIPS 2025).
