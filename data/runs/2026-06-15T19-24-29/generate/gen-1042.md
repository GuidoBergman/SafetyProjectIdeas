# Do Models Share the Same Uncertainty Blind Spots? Correlated Overconfidence Analysis

**Problem:** Correlated-error work focuses on which answers are wrong. A complementary, untested angle: do models share the same MIScalibration — confidently wrong on the same items? Correlated overconfidence defeats confidence-threshold-based safety filters across an ensemble.

**Direction:** Vary the measurement from agreement-on-answers to agreement-on-confidence. On a ground-truth benchmark, elicit verbalized or logprob-based confidence from several small open models, identify items where multiple models are simultaneously confident-and-wrong, and measure how correlated that confident-wrong set is across models.

**Why it matters:** If overconfidence is correlated, a deployer who routes only low-confidence cases to humans will systematically miss the same dangerous cases across their whole model fleet. Direct, measurable, single-GPU contribution to monitoring for systemic risk.

**Relevant context:** 'Correlated Errors in LLMs' (arXiv:2506.07962); uncertainty-estimation survey (arXiv:2410.15326); self-consistency hallucination detection.
