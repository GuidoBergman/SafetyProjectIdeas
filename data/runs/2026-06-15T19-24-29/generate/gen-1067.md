# Does an LLM Judge Systematically Favor Models That Think Like It?

**Problem:** Great Models Think Alike reported LLM-as-judge favors similar models. A targeted safety follow-up: in safety/harmlessness judging specifically, does a judge model rate responses from homogeneous siblings as safer/better, biasing safety leaderboards and RLAIF labels?

**Direction:** Take a fixed pool of responses to safety-relevant prompts from diverse models. Have several different judge models score them. Correlate each judge's preference with that judge's measured homogeneity to each candidate. Test whether judges systematically up-rank candidates similar to themselves on the safety dimension.

**Why it matters:** Safety evals and AI-feedback pipelines lean on LLM judges; if homogeneity biases them, safety scores are inflated for the dominant model lineage. Fully reproducible with public models.

**Relevant context:** Great Models Think Alike, self-preference/LLM-judge findings (arXiv:2502.04313).
