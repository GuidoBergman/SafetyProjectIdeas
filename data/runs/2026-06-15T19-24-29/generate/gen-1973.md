# PreferenceTie Benchmark: Measuring Spurious-Correlation Reliance in DPO

**Problem:** Preference optimization latches onto spurious cues (length, formatting, hedging) instead of true quality, but there is no controlled benchmark that isolates a model's reliance on a specific spurious cue while holding quality fixed.

**Direction:** Construct controlled preference pairs where the only difference is a spurious feature (e.g., bullet formatting, an unnecessary apology, +50% length) with quality held equal, then measure how often an open DPO model / RM prefers the spuriously-decorated response. Report a per-cue 'spurious reliance rate.' Reusable as a diagnostic for any new preference-tuned model.

**Why it matters:** Isolating spurious-cue reliance is the cleanest way to measure a fundamental limitation of preference-based training. A controlled, reusable probe set is broadly valuable for auditing open models and needs no training. Directly addresses an open RLHF problem.

**Relevant context:** 'Spurious Correlation Learning in Preference Optimization: Mechanisms, Consequences, and Mitigation via Tie Training' (arXiv:2605.11134) documents spurious-correlation reliance in DPO and proposes tie training. Length-bias literature (arXiv:2403.19159, arXiv:2407.01085) confirms specific spurious cues.
