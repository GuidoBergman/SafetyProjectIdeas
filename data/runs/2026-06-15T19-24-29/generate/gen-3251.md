# Translation Layer: Does Hidden Reasoning Survive a Forced Language Switch?

This 'reformat-the-channel' defense at the encoding link is intuitive but lacks a cheap empirical test.

**Direction:** Reuse a simple hidden-channel encode/decode setup. Insert a forced transformation between encoder and decoder (translate CoT to another language and back, or convert to bullet points). Measure how much hidden-signal decoding accuracy drops vs how much legitimate task performance is preserved. Compare transformation types as channel-disruptors.

**Why it matters:** Offers a menu of cheap, training-free CoT transformations ranked by how well they break covert channels while keeping reasoning useful — practical monitoring-pipeline guidance.
