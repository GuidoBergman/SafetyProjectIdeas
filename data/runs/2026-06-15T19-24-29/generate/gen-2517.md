# Does Ensembling Cheap Monitors Beat One Strong Monitor?

**Problem:** SHADE-Arena showed even the best single monitor caps at AUC 0.87. Whether a cheap ensemble of several weak, diverse monitors can exceed a single strong monitor's catch rate is an obvious but untested follow-up.

**Direction:** On a transcript set, run 3-5 small/diverse open models as monitors and combine their suspicion scores (mean, max, majority vote, logistic stack). Compare the ensemble's AUC and catch-rate-at-fixed-budget against the best single monitor. Report which combination rule wins and whether diversity (different families) helps.

**Why it matters:** If cheap ensembles beat one big monitor, it is a free safety gain accessible to anyone — strongly lab-independent. Pure inference + simple statistics, ideal for the participant profile.

**Relevant context:** SHADE-Arena (arXiv:2506.15740): best single monitor AUC 0.87, 'prohibitively low'. TraceGuard (arXiv:2604.03968) explores structured multi-dimensional monitoring, motivating ensembles.
