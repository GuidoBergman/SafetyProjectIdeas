# Conditional steering as a tamper-resistant refusal: stress-testing CAST on a small model

**Problem:** Conditional Activation Steering (CAST) applies a behavior vector only when a condition vector matches the prompt, enabling context-specific refusal. Its robustness to adversarial prompts crafted to dodge the condition trigger is under-tested on small open models.

**Direction:** Implement a minimal CAST pipeline on one open model: a refusal behavior vector gated by a 'harmful-topic' condition vector. Then red-team the condition trigger with paraphrases and obfuscations (leetspeak, role-play wrappers) to measure how easily the conditional gate is bypassed vs. unconditional steering. Report bypass rates.

**Why it matters:** CAST is pitched as safer than blanket steering, but a brittle condition gate could give false confidence. An adversarial evaluation of the gate is exactly the kind of defense-stress-testing useful outside labs, and a small prototype fits 30h.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Programming Refusal with Conditional Activation Steering' (CAST, arXiv:2409.05907) using the framing of recent 2026 steering-safety audits (arXiv:2603.24543).
