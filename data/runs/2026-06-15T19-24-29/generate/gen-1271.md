# Homogeneous Refusals: Do All Models Share the Same Over- and Under-Refusal Blind Spots?

**Problem:** Failure mode A: homogeneity of safety behavior. Failure mode B: refusal calibration errors (over-refusal of benign prompts, under-refusal of harmful ones). If the refusal boundary is homogeneous across providers, the same benign use cases are blocked everywhere and the same harmful edge cases slip through everywhere — a correlated, fleet-wide safety gap.

**Direction:** Use an existing over-refusal benchmark slice (e.g., XSTest-style benign prompts) plus a small harmful-edge set. Query 6+ open models, classify refuse/comply, and measure inter-model agreement on the refusal boundary. Quantify correlated false refusals and correlated unsafe compliances vs chance independence.

**Why it matters:** Concentration means a shared refusal blind spot is a society-wide blind spot — harmful requests that no provider catches. Measuring correlation (not just per-model rates) is the novel, lab-independent contribution.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind inter-model homogeneity; refusal/over-refusal benchmarks (XSTest); correlated-errors framing (arXiv 2506.07962).
