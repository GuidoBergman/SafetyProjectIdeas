# Ecological Niche-Overlap Mapping of Safety Concept Directions

**Problem:** Different safety concepts (deception, sycophancy, refusal, harmfulness) may share overlapping representational 'niches' in activation space, meaning steering one inadvertently moves others. Ecology quantifies niche overlap to predict competition and coexistence.

**Direction:** Borrow niche-overlap metrics (e.g. Pianka's index) from ecology. Extract steering vectors for a suite of safety concepts, then for each concept measure its activation 'niche' as the distribution of contexts where its probe fires. Compute pairwise overlap and cosine geometry. Predict, then empirically verify, that high-overlap pairs exhibit strong cross-steering side effects (steering A drags B) while low-overlap pairs are independent.

**Why it matters:** Tells safety engineers which concepts cannot be steered in isolation, a prerequisite for targeted alignment without collateral behavior changes. Maps the geometry of safety-relevant directions, directly addressing 'which concepts have linear representations'. Inference-only, single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Niche overlap predicts competition/coexistence (Khan Academy; arXiv:1910.02184). Open problem: scale steering to all safety concepts, which have linear representations. Multi-concept combination is established (Beaglehole et al.).
