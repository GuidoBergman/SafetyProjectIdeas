# CoT Faithfulness Under Paraphrase: Replicate on Open Reasoning Models

**Problem:** Lanham et al. test CoT faithfulness via perturbations on closed models; recent work shows RL makes reasoning illegible. Whether the paraphrase-corruption test (does corrupting the CoT change the answer?) gives the same faithfulness verdict on open reasoning models like DeepSeek-R1-Distill is underexplored.

**Direction:** On a small open reasoning-distilled model, replicate the Lanham-style intervention: generate a CoT, paraphrase/truncate it, force the model to continue, and measure how often the final answer changes. Compare faithfulness scores to the original paper's closed-model numbers.

**Why it matters:** CoT monitoring is a leading safety bet; if open reasoning models have unfaithful CoT, monitors built on them are unreliable. Encoded reasoning detection is a starred Open Phil problem and needs no lab access.

**Relevant context:** Lanham et al. 'Measuring Faithfulness in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning' (arXiv:2307.13702). 'Reasoning Models Sometimes Output Illegible Chains of Thought' (arXiv:2510.27338) across 14 models. Context file: 'can models reason strategically in ways not visible in CoT?'
