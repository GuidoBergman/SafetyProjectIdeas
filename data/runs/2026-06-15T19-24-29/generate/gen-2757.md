# Benchmark Discriminative-Power Audit: Are Top Models Statistically Distinguishable?

**Problem:** Many safety benchmarks are saturating — top models cluster within noise — yet results are reported as if rankings are meaningful. There's no routine test of whether a benchmark still discriminates among leading models.

**Direction:** For a chosen safety benchmark, evaluate several open models, bootstrap confidence intervals on each score, and compute the fraction of model pairs that are statistically distinguishable. Produce a 'discriminative power index' and per-item discrimination (which items separate models vs. which everyone passes/fails). Recommend an informative item subset.

**Why it matters:** Tells the field which safety benchmarks are still worth running and which give false precision — a meta-eval contribution that needs only inference.
