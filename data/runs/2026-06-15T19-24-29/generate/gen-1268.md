# Homogeneity Poisons the Well: Synthetic-Data Distillation Amplifies Shared Errors into Model Collapse

**Problem:** Failure mode A: inter-model homogeneity (models already over-represent common modes). Failure mode B: model collapse from training on synthetic data. When a new model is distilled from homogeneous teacher outputs, the shared blind spots get baked in and amplified — the two failures compound across model generations.

**Direction:** Lightweight simulation without training from scratch: take an open small model, generate synthetic Q&A on a topic, measure mode-coverage / diversity of the synthetic set vs a real reference, and quantify how rare/minority answers are dropped relative to inter-model homogeneity already present. Use prompt-level 'pseudo-distillation' (few-shot conditioning on teacher outputs) to show diversity loss without full retraining; measure the diversity-collapse slope.

**Why it matters:** The internet is filling with homogeneous AI text; future models train on it. This connects today's measurable homogeneity to tomorrow's collapse — a >1-year threat labs deprioritize. Needs only inference + diversity metrics.

**Relevant context:** Artificial Hivemind; model-collapse literature (Strong Model Collapse, ICLR 2025; arXiv 2510.01631 synthetic data scaling); distillation inheriting teacher vulnerabilities (arXiv 2506.12913).
