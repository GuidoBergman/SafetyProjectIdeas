# Strategic Deception Signatures in Reasoning-Model Representations

**Problem:** A recent paper finds that 'thinking' LLMs show distinct representational signatures when strategically deceiving. Whether a simple probe trained on one deception scenario transfers to detecting deception in unrelated scenarios on small open reasoning models is open.

**Direction:** Collect activations from a small open reasoning model in a deception-incentivized scenario and a control, fit a lightweight linear probe, then test cross-scenario transfer (e.g. probe trained on a game-deception scenario applied to a credential-fabrication scenario). Report transfer accuracy to gauge whether a general 'deception direction' exists.

**Why it matters:** A transferable deception probe would be a powerful, lab-independent monitoring tool; its failure to transfer bounds the generality of white-box deception detection. Either way, this is a high-value model-organism-style probe doable in 30h.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'When Thinking LLMs Lie: Unveiling the Strategic Deception in Representations of Reasoning Models' (arXiv 2506.04909) and 'Probing the Limits of the Lie Detector Approach to LLM Deception' (arXiv 2603.10003, ~March 2026).
