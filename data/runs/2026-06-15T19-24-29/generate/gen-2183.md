# Cross-Lingual Weak Judges: Oversight When Judge Reads a Second Language

**Problem:** All major debate/judge experiments are English-only. A realistic future overseer may evaluate arguments in a language they (or their model) handle weakly. The degradation of oversight quality under a language-handicapped judge is unmeasured.

**Direction:** Translate a slice of QuALITY questions and debate transcripts into a lower-resource language. Use a small multilingual open model as judge in English vs the translated language. Measure how much judge accuracy in debate drops when the judge operates in its weaker language, and whether debate's lift over consultancy survives.

**Why it matters:** Multilingual oversight is a real-world deployment gap that no single lab prioritizes. Showing oversight protocols degrade in non-English regimes is valuable diffuse-evaluation work, fully inference-based.

**Relevant context:** Debate/consultancy benchmarks (Kenton 2024, Khan 2024) are English-only on QuALITY. Multilingual safety gaps are documented broadly but not for oversight protocols.
