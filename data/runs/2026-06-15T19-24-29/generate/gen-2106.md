# Unlearning x Probe-Based Detection Blind Spot

**Problem:** Interacting failure modes: (A) shallow unlearning that suppresses outputs but not internal representations (alignment_training), and (B) over-reliance on activation probes for hazardous-knowledge detection (interpretability). If unlearning hides knowledge from outputs while leaving it in activations, a probe-based safety filter built on the unlearned model may give false assurance.

**Direction:** Using an open unlearned checkpoint, train a tiny linear probe on activations to predict presence of the 'unlearned' knowledge during forward passes on relevant prompts. Compare probe detectability before vs. after unlearning. Test whether knowledge remains linearly decodable despite output suppression.

**Why it matters:** Unlearning + probing are both proposed safety tools; if unlearning makes outputs look clean while leaving probe-detectable (and thus relearnable) traces, the combination misleads. Linear probes are cheap on a consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** 'Unlearning Isn't Deletion' (arXiv 2505.16831) — unlearning often reversible, knowledge not deleted. 'Open Problems in Machine Unlearning' (arXiv 2501.04952). Activation-probe detection literature.
