# Does Homogeneity Increase With Model Capability? A Capability-Stratified Audit

**Problem:** Kim et al. report the surprising result that larger, more accurate models have MORE correlated errors, even across providers. If true broadly, scaling makes the ecosystem MORE of a monoculture — the opposite of the usual 'diversity will save us' assumption. This capability-vs-homogeneity trend deserves a focused, replicable audit on open-ended and safety tasks, not just leaderboards.

**Direction:** Stratify ~15 accessible models into capability tiers (small/mid/frontier). Measure within-tier and cross-tier output homogeneity on open-ended and safety prompts. Test whether higher-capability tiers are MORE mutually similar. Visualize the homogeneity-vs-capability trend.

**Why it matters:** If capability and homogeneity rise together, future frontier ecosystems are inherently more concentrated and fragile — a >1-year systemic threat model labs underweight. Replicable with pure inference.

**Relevant context:** Extends the 'larger/more accurate models have more correlated errors' finding from 'Correlated Errors in LLMs' (arXiv:2506.07962) to open-ended/safety tasks and a capability-stratified design.
