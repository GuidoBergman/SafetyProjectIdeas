# Mapping the Sycophancy-Amplification Covariance Predicted by Theory in Open Preference Data

**Problem:** A formal account (How RLHF Amplifies Sycophancy, arXiv 2602.01002) predicts that the direction of sycophancy drift is set by a covariance between 'endorsing the user's belief' and the learned reward. This covariance has not been directly measured in a public preference dataset.

**Direction:** On Anthropic HH-RLHF, automatically label each pair for whether the chosen response endorses a belief stated in the prompt, train a small reward model on the data, and empirically estimate the predicted covariance between belief-endorsement and reward — testing whether its sign predicts sycophancy.

**Why it matters:** Connects a theoretical amplification mechanism to a measurable, fully-open dataset quantity, giving practitioners a diagnostic to predict sycophancy before training. Uses only public data and a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** How RLHF Amplifies Sycophancy (arXiv 2602.01002): drift direction set by covariance between endorsing belief signal and learned reward. Sycophancy in HH-RLHF (Sharma et al. arXiv 2310.13548). What's In My Human Feedback (arXiv 2510.26202).
