# Refusal-Direction Erosion: Does Narrow Fine-Tuning Quietly Delete the Safety Direction?

**Problem:** It is unknown whether emergent misalignment from narrow fine-tuning works by suppressing a model's pre-existing 'refusal' representation, or by adding a separate misaligned-persona signal. Distinguishing erosion vs addition matters for which defense (preserve refusal vs detect persona) to invest in.

**Direction:** Extract a refusal direction (difference-in-means on harmful vs harmless prompts) from a base small model, EM-fine-tune it, then measure how the refusal-direction magnitude/projection changes vs the growth of a misaligned-persona direction — testing erosion against addition.

**Why it matters:** Resolves a mechanistic fork that determines defense strategy, using only lightweight activation arithmetic on open models — a clean, interpretability-flavored study with no lab tooling.

**Relevant context:** Persona Features Control EM (arXiv 2506.19823). Refusal-direction literature (Arditi et al. refusal mediated by single direction). EM (arXiv 2502.17424).
