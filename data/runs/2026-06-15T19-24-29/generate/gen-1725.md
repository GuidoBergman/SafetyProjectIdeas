# Causal Mediation Analysis to Decompose Direct vs. Indirect Feature Effects

**Problem:** When a feature influences output, we rarely separate its direct effect from effects routed through downstream components. Safety-relevant features may act mostly indirectly, invisible to direct-effect ablation.

**Direction:** Transfer causal mediation analysis (natural direct/indirect effects) from epidemiology/social science. For a candidate feature and a downstream mediator component, use path patching to estimate the natural direct effect and the indirect effect through the mediator on an open model. Quantify what fraction of a safety-relevant feature's influence is mediated. Single GPU, patching only.

**Why it matters:** Mediation decomposition tells auditors whether intervening on a feature actually blocks a harmful pathway or merely reroutes it, central to whether interpretability-based interventions are safe to rely on.

**Relevant context:** Causal mediation analysis is standard in epidemiology (case-control causal-inference reviews, AJE 2025). Path patching exists in TransformerLens; framing it explicitly as mediation NDE/NIE estimation is a fresh transfer.
