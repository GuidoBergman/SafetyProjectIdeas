# Seismology-Style Early-Warning: Precursor Signals Before Steering-Induced Breakdown

**Problem:** Coherence collapse from over-steering happens suddenly, but seismology detects precursor signals (foreshocks, increasing variance) before a quake. Whether activation dynamics show measurable precursors before steering breaks a model is unknown.

**Direction:** Transfer critical-slowing-down / early-warning indicators from dynamical-systems and seismology. As steering strength approaches the coherence cliff, monitor candidate precursors in activations and token statistics (rising variance, autocorrelation, entropy spikes) token-by-token, and test whether they reliably forewarn the upcoming breakdown before it manifests in text. Build a simple early-warning detector and measure its lead time.

**Why it matters:** An early-warning signal lets a monitoring harness back off steering before the model produces degenerate/unsafe output, a concrete safety-monitoring primitive. Generic critical-slowing-down indicators are easy to compute and a great first dynamical-systems project. Inference-only, outside-lab.

**Relevant context:** Critical slowing down (rising variance/autocorrelation) precedes tipping points in dynamical systems and seismology. Coherence collapses sharply with over-steering (arXiv:2602.04903). Connects to the percolation/phase-transition idea.
