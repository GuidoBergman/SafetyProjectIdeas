# Does Self-Jailbreak Emerge Only After Benign Reasoning Training?

**Problem:** Self-Jailbreaking work (arXiv:2510.20956) claims models can reason themselves out of safety after benign reasoning training. It is unclear whether the effect requires that specific training or appears spontaneously in off-the-shelf open reasoning models given longer thinking budgets.

**Direction:** Take an open reasoning model and vary the allowed reasoning length (think-token budget) on a fixed set of borderline-harmful prompts. Measure refusal rate as a function of reasoning length, and qualitatively code whether longer traces produce 'talking themselves into compliance' patterns. No fine-tuning — pure inference-time manipulation.

**Why it matters:** If longer reasoning monotonically erodes safety even without special training, it is a fundamental scaling risk for reasoning models and a medium-horizon threat labs under-prioritize. Feasible on one consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** Self-Jailbreaking (arXiv:2510.20956) and 'When Models Outthink Their Safety' (arXiv:2510.21285): longer/explicit reasoning can increase safety risk. Deliberative Alignment (arXiv:2412.16339) claims reasoning improves safety — tension worth probing.
