# Do LLM Judges Form a Monoculture? Correlated Evaluation Errors Across Judge Models

**Problem:** LLM-as-judge is now load-bearing infrastructure for evals and RLAIF. If different judge models share the same biases (self-preference, verbosity, position), then 'use multiple judges' provides no real robustness and evaluation monoculture propagates into training.

**Direction:** Build a small set of response pairs with known ground-truth quality plus planted bias traps (verbose-but-worse, position-swapped, stylistically-fancy-but-wrong). Run 5-6 open judge models. Measure whether judges' errors are correlated and whether their biases point the same direction. Compute the effective number of independent judges.

**Why it matters:** Evaluation monoculture means our measurement instruments fail in correlated ways — a foundational, easily-overlooked systemic risk that contaminates the whole safety-eval stack. Fully reproducible outside any lab.

**Relevant context:** Self-preference and judge-bias literature (arXiv 2410.21819, 2604.22891) + monoculture framing. Extends homogeneity from generation to evaluation.
