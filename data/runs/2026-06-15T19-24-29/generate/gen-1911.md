# Does Sycophancy Finetuning Degrade Calibration Beyond the Targeted Domain?

**Problem:** Calibration Collapse Under Sycophancy Fine-Tuning (April 2026) found sycophantic reward signals degrade calibration. Whether this calibration damage is narrow (only on the sycophancy-targeted topics) or spills over to unrelated factual domains, mirroring emergent misalignment's broad generalization, is unexplored.

**Direction:** Finetune a small open model with a sycophancy-inducing objective on one topic (e.g., trivia agreement), then measure calibration (ECE, Brier score on multiple-choice confidence) on both the trained topic and several unrelated held-out factual benchmarks (e.g., TruthfulQA, a science QA set). Test for cross-domain calibration spillover.

**Why it matters:** Calibration underlies reliable uncertainty and oversight. If sycophancy training silently breaks calibration on unrelated domains, then preference optimization has a hidden broad cost. Measuring calibration only needs logprobs on multiple-choice items, fully feasible on a single GPU with no proprietary data.

**Relevant context:** Calibration Collapse Under Sycophancy Fine-Tuning (arXiv 2604.10585) investigates sycophancy degrading calibration; the EM literature (arXiv 2502.17424, Nature 2026) establishes that narrow finetuning generalizes broadly. TruthfulQA (817 questions, 38 categories) is a standard benchmark.
