# Harm-Category Heterogeneity in Shallow Alignment

**Problem:** Shallow-alignment and jailbreak studies often report a single aggregate attack-success number, masking whether some harm categories (e.g. cyber, bio, hate, self-harm) are far more shallowly aligned than others on the same model.

**Direction:** Using AILuminate's 12 hazard categories (or StrongREJECT categories), run a fixed prefill attack across all categories on 2-3 small open models and report per-category attack-success and refusal-recovery. Identify categories where alignment is dangerously shallow and whether the ranking is consistent across models.

**Why it matters:** Aggregate jailbreak metrics hide the categories that matter most for catastrophic risk. A per-category shallowness map tells the field where to deepen alignment first — a diffuse, hard-to-fund prioritization question. Uses public benchmarks, pure inference.

**Relevant context:** AILuminate (MLCommons, 24K+ prompts, 12 hazard categories) and StrongREJECT (arXiv:2402.10260) provide categorized prompts. Qi et al. (arXiv:2406.05946) supply the prefill attack. Context file lists these as key datasets.
