# Adaptive-Attack Robustness of a Trusted Monitor on Open Models

**Problem:** A success state is control protocols whose monitors can't be talked out of flagging. Recent work shows adaptive attacks subvert trusted monitors. We lack a cheap reproduction showing how easily a small open monitor is manipulated.

**Direction:** Backcast from 'monitors are robust to manipulation by the agent they watch'. Set up a small open monitor scoring agent actions, then have the agent embed monitor-targeting text (reassurances, fake justifications) in its outputs. Measure how monitor accuracy degrades as a function of attack effort, reproducing the adaptive-attack effect at hobby scale.

**Why it matters:** If monitors are trivially manipulable, control protocols built on them are fragile — a finding any deployer needs but that requires no lab resources to demonstrate. Extends frontier control research to the open-model setting.

**Relevant context:** Adaptive Attacks on Trusted Monitors Subvert AI Control Protocols (arXiv 2510.09462) and Bypassing AI Control Protocols via Agent-as-a-Proxy (arXiv 2602.05066) show monitor subversion. Reliable Weak-to-Strong Monitoring (arXiv 2508.19461) studies weak monitors.
