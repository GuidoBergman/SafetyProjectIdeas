# Does Shallow Alignment Hold for Open Reasoning Models?

**Problem:** Qi et al. (ICLR 2025) showed safety alignment in chat LLMs is concentrated in the first few output tokens, but the experiment used non-reasoning models. Whether open reasoning models (e.g., DeepSeek-R1-Distill, QwQ) that emit long chains-of-thought before answering exhibit the same shallow pattern is unknown.

**Direction:** Replicate Qi et al.'s prefilling and per-token KL-divergence-vs-aligned analysis on 2-3 small open reasoning models. Measure where the aligned/unaligned generative distributions diverge: is it still the first ~5 tokens, or distributed across the reasoning trace? Compare a chat model and its reasoning-tuned sibling on the same harmful prompt set.

**Why it matters:** If reasoning models concentrate safety differently (or just as shallowly), it tells defenders whether token-depth defenses generalize to the reasoning-model era. This needs no training and uses only open models, making it valuable outside frontier labs.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al., 'Safety Alignment Should Be Made More Than Just a Few Tokens Deep' (arXiv:2406.05946, ICLR 2025 Outstanding Paper): key signal is divergence in modeling of only the first few output tokens. Follow-up arXiv:2606.04778 moves injection beyond first tokens. Deliberative Alignment (arXiv:2412.16339) and self-jailbreak findings (arXiv:2510.20956) suggest reasoning traces interact with safety differently.
