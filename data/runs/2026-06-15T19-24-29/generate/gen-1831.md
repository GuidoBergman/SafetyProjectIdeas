# Replicating CoT-SAE Causal Faithfulness on a Different Reasoning Task

**Problem:** How does Chain of Thought Think? (arXiv:2507.22928) presents the first feature-level causal study of CoT faithfulness using sparse autoencoding, but on a limited task set. Whether the causal faithfulness signal generalizes to arithmetic vs commonsense vs symbolic reasoning is open.

**Direction:** Using a public SAE on a small instruct model, identify SAE features active during CoT on one reasoning task, ablate them, and measure whether the stated CoT and the answer change together (faithful) or independently (unfaithful). Repeat across 2-3 task families and compare faithfulness scores.

**Why it matters:** Mapping which reasoning domains have causally faithful CoT tells oversight designers where CoT monitoring is trustworthy. Fully feasible with open SAEs and a single GPU.

**Relevant context:** How does Chain of Thought Think? Mechanistic Interpretability of Chain-of-Thought Reasoning with Sparse Autoencoding (arXiv:2507.22928): first feature-level causal study of CoT faithfulness.
