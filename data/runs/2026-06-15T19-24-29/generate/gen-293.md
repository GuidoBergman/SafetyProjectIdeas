# Material Fatigue / Repeated-Stress Testing of Safety Under Looping

**Problem:** Materials fail not from one big load but from repeated sub-critical stress cycles (fatigue). It is unknown whether repeatedly re-asking a refused query with minor variation 'fatigues' a model's refusal within a single context window.

**Direction:** Transfer fatigue/S-N (stress-number-of-cycles) testing. In a single long context, repeatedly present a refused request with small perturbations and measure the number of cycles until the model first complies (cycles-to-failure), as a function of perturbation amplitude. Produce an S-N-style curve per open model: small persistent pressure vs cycles-to-break.

**Why it matters:** Models a realistic patient-attacker threat (persistent low-effort retries) and quantifies context-window-driven refusal erosion, a medium-horizon deployment risk. Black-box, single GPU, no training.

**Relevant context:** S-N / fatigue curves are core materials engineering. Survival-analysis work on repeated attacks (arXiv:2605.12869) and multi-turn benchmarks (MultiBreak arXiv:2605.01687) establish that repeated pressure degrades refusal.
