# Self-Critique Reliability Benchmark for Constitutional-Style Training

**Problem:** Constitutional AI relies on a model critiquing and revising its own outputs against principles, but there is no standardized benchmark measuring how reliable that self-critique step is — i.e., when a model is shown a flawed response, does it correctly flag the right violated principle?

**Direction:** Construct a labeled benchmark of response+principle pairs (some clearly violating a stated principle, some compliant, some subtly violating), present each to open instruct models in a self-critique prompt, and score critique accuracy, false-positive rate, and which principle types are systematically missed. Report a 'self-critique reliability' scorecard per model.

**Why it matters:** Constitutional AI is a leading scalable-oversight method, but its safety rests on self-critique quality, which is rarely measured directly. A reusable benchmark quantifies where self-critique silently fails, informing when AI feedback can substitute for human feedback. Pure inference, lab-independent.
