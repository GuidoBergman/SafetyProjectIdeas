# Auditing Incident-Reporting System Designs Against Real General-Purpose-AI Harms

**Problem:** 'Designing Incident Reporting Systems for Harms from General-Purpose AI' proposes a seven-dimension institutional design framework, but does not test whether documented real GPAI harms would actually be captured by a system built to those specs.

**Direction:** Take the seven design dimensions, sample real GPAI-related incidents from the public database, and trace each through a hypothetical reporting system: would it be reportable, by whom, with what required fields? Tabulate the fraction of real harms that fall outside the system's triggers and which dimensions cause the misses.

**Why it matters:** An effective national AI incident-response framework (NTSB-for-AI) is a named open problem; testing reporting-system designs against real harms before they're enacted is high-value, lab-independent work. Public data, no compute beyond LLM assistance.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Designing Incident Reporting Systems for Harms from General-Purpose AI' (arXiv:2511.05914, Nov 2025), validating its seven-dimension framework against real public incidents.
