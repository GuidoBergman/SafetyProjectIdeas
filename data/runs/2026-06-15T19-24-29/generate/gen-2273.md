# CrossModelJudge-Bench: Mapping Self-Preference in Open-Model Oversight Pairs

**Problem:** Self-preference bias is documented for large judges, but there is no matrix-style benchmark mapping how much each small open judge over-rewards each open generator (including itself), which matters for choosing safe judge-generator pairings.

**Direction:** Build an NxN evaluation matrix where N small open models each judge anonymized outputs from all N models on a fixed task, and report a self-preference and pairwise-favoritism heatmap. Deliver the matrix-evaluation harness and the bias heatmap.

**Why it matters:** Self-preference between models is a concrete exploitable oversight failure; a reusable cross-model bias map helps practitioners pick judge-generator pairs and is fully open-model and inference-only.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2410.21819 'Self-Preference Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge' links self-recognition to self-preference; arXiv 2604.22891 'Quantifying and Mitigating Self-Preference Bias of LLM Judges.'
