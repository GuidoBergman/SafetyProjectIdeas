# Environmental-Impact-Statement Template for Frontier Model Releases

**Problem:** Environmental law forces an Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) before major actions, with public comment. Frontier model releases have no analogous structured pre-release impact disclosure, so societal risks are assessed ad hoc.

**Direction:** Transfer the NEPA EIS structure (scoping, alternatives, affected populations, mitigation, public comment). Convert it into an 'AI Release Impact Statement' template, then retroactively populate it for 2-3 recent model releases using only public information to demonstrate feasibility and surface what data is unavailable.

**Why it matters:** Offers a concrete, transferable operationalization of safety objectives into a deployment practice with public accountability. The retroactive exercise reveals the disclosure gap. No compute or lab access.

**Relevant context:** NEPA EIS process; EU AI Act systemic-risk assessment requirements; FDA-for-AI approval-regulation debate (arXiv 2408.00821).
