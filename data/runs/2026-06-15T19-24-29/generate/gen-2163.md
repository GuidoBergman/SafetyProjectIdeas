# Verification Asymmetry: Mapping the Generate-vs-Check Gap for Weak Overseers

**Problem:** Oversight assumes checking is easier than generating. The size of this generate-vs-verify gap, and whether it actually *exists* for weak overseers on different task types, is rarely measured directly — yet it is the load-bearing assumption of scalable oversight.

**Direction:** For a fixed weak open model, measure (a) its accuracy generating answers and (b) its accuracy verifying answers (correct + plausible-wrong) on the same items, across task types (factual, math, code, summarization). Report the verify-minus-generate gap per type to find where checking is genuinely easier.

**Why it matters:** Empirically locates the regimes where the core oversight assumption holds vs fails — foundational and reproducible. Tells us which tasks are safely overseeable by weaker models.

**Relevant context:** Verification-vs-generation asymmetry underpins debate/IDA (Christiano, Irving). Kenton et al. (2024) information-asymmetry tasks; Easy-to-Hard Generalization (arXiv:2403.09472) on verify-vs-solve.
