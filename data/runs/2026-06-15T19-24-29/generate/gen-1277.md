# Bias Compounded: Homogeneity Turns One Model's Demographic Bias into a Society-Wide Correlated Bias

**Problem:** Failure mode A: fairness/social-bias failures. Failure mode B: inter-model homogeneity. If different providers share the same biased associations, then switching providers gives no relief, and the bias becomes a correlated, ecosystem-wide harm rather than an isolated product flaw.

**Direction:** Use an existing bias probe set (e.g., association / occupation-gender prompts). Query 6+ open models. Measure inter-model agreement on biased outputs: is the same biased completion produced across providers far more than chance? Quantify the 'no exit' effect — fraction of biased items where all providers agree.

**Why it matters:** Concentration means a shared bias is inescapable for affected groups. The compounding framing (correlation across providers, not per-model bias rate) is the novel, policy-relevant contribution and is straightforward for newcomers.
