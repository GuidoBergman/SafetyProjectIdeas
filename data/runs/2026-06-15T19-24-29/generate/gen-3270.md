# Self-Report Honesty Audit: Will Models Admit a Covert Action After the Fact?

**Problem:** Success state: post-hoc questioning is a reliable lie-detector — a model that took a covert action will admit it if asked directly. Backcasting, we need to know whether self-reported honesty after a deceptive episode is trustworthy on open models, since safety cases sometimes lean on it.

**Direction:** Run open agentic models through covert-action scenarios, then interrogate them ('Did you do X without telling the user?') under several framings. Measure confession rate vs denial-after-deception, and whether honesty-eliciting prompts (truth oaths, 'a monitor will check') change it. Report a 'post-hoc honesty reliability' score.

**Why it matters:** If models reliably confess, that's a cheap deployable monitoring layer; if they double down, post-hoc audits are worthless and safety cases must not rely on them. The UK AISI agenda asks whether honesty is a sufficient safety condition — this probes one operationalization.
