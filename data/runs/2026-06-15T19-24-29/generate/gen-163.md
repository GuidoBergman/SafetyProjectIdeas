# Fine-Tuning-Free Latent Steering as a Lightweight Defense Variation

**Problem:** Latent adversarial training is proposed as an alternative to enumerated adversarial training, but it needs training. A cheaper variation: can inference-time activation steering along the refusal direction harden a model against prefill attacks without any training, and at what over-refusal cost?

**Direction:** Extract the refusal direction (difference-of-means) on an open model, then at inference add a scaled amount of that direction to residual activations. Sweep the steering coefficient and measure prefill/suffix attack success vs XSTest over-refusal. Find whether a coefficient exists that restores robustness lost to prefill without breaking helpfulness.

**Why it matters:** Offers a no-training, single-GPU defense alternative to adversarial training — directly responsive to the Open Phil RFP open problem — and characterizes its safety/helpfulness tradeoff. Activation steering uses public methods accessible to beginners.

**Relevant context:** Open Phil RFP open problem (context file): 'alternatives to adversarial training (e.g. latent adversarial training) that provide robustness without requiring explicit attack enumeration.' Arditi et al. (NeurIPS 2024) refusal-direction steering and AlphaSteer (arXiv:2506.07022) ground inference-time steering. XSTest (arXiv:2308.01263) for over-refusal cost.
