# Epidemic SIR Modeling of Jailbreak Template Transfer

**Problem:** Jailbreak templates spread across model families like a contagion, but we have no quantitative model of which 'hosts' are susceptible and what the basic reproduction number of a template is.

**Direction:** Import the SIR/contact-network framework from epidemiology. Treat a set of open models as a population, a jailbreak template as a pathogen, and successful transfer as infection. Empirically estimate a transfer matrix across ~6-10 open models for ~30 templates, compute a per-template R0-analogue (mean number of models it infects) and per-model susceptibility, and identify 'super-spreader' templates and 'immune' models. Visualize as a contact graph.

**Why it matters:** Predicts which novel attacks will generalize before they are widely deployed, and which model design choices confer herd-immunity-like protection. Uses only open models, no lab data; medium-horizon attack-ecosystem view labs underinvest in.

**Relevant context:** Epidemiological SIR models are already used for computer-virus spread; digital herd immunity (medRxiv 2020.04.15) formalizes population resistance. Jailbreak transferability is documented across model families (GPTFUZZER arXiv:2309.10253).
