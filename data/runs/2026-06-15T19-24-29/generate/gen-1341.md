# Steering Refusal Without Wrecking Helpfulness: Does Suppressing the Refusal Vector Over-Refuse Benign Requests?

**Problem:** Refusal is reportedly multi-dimensional. Steering it up may cause over-refusal of harmless requests (a safety-usability failure). The benign-refusal collateral of refusal steering is unmeasured publicly.

**Direction:** Build a refusal steering vector and apply increasing strength on an open model. Measure refusal rate on both a harmful set AND a benign-but-sensitive set (medical, legal, security questions that are legitimate). Plot the over-refusal curve and identify the strength where benign refusals spike. Test whether using a higher-dimensional refusal subspace reduces over-refusal.

**Why it matters:** Over-refusal is a real, under-studied harm of safety steering that degrades usefulness and pushes users to unsafe tools. Cheap, interpretable, and directly relevant to anyone deploying steering. Single GPU.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in the 2025 finding that refusal is controlled by higher-dimensional directions, not one axis (refusal-direction literature), and side-effect work (arXiv 2406.15518).
