# Reproducing Self-Jailbreak: Does It Transfer Across Harm Categories?

**Problem:** Self-jailbreak via extended reasoning (arXiv:2510.20956, arXiv:2510.21285) is shown on certain harmful queries, but whether the effect is uniform across hazard categories (cyber, bio, fraud, hate) or concentrated in a few is unknown.

**Direction:** On an open reasoning model, run a fixed reasoning-budget manipulation across prompts drawn from each of several hazard categories (using AILuminate's 12-category taxonomy as the category scheme). Measure per-category refusal erosion vs reasoning length to find which harm types are most susceptible to self-jailbreak.

**Why it matters:** Category-level susceptibility maps tell defenders where reasoning models are weakest, informing realistic, threat-prioritized benchmarks. Uses an open benchmark taxonomy and one consumer GPU.

**Relevant context:** arXiv:2510.20956 (self-jailbreaking), arXiv:2510.21285 (outthinking safety). AILuminate (MLCommons): 24K+ prompts, 12 hazard categories. Context lists AILuminate as a key benchmark.
