# Does Translating the Pressure Prompt Change How Much Models Lie?

**Problem:** Sycophancy and lying-under-pressure are measured almost entirely in English. Whether the same pressure expressed in another language elicits more or less capitulation/lying from multilingual open models is an unmapped failure surface.

**Direction:** Take a fixed set of pressure scenarios (sycophancy + MASK-style lying prompts), translate them into 2-3 languages, and measure capitulation/lying rates per language on a multilingual open model. Characterize cross-lingual honesty gaps with content held constant.

**Why it matters:** Non-English deployments may have systematically weaker honesty guarantees. Documenting a cross-lingual honesty gap is high-counterfactual-value safety work labs underinvest in, and needs only translation + public probes.

**Relevant context:** Distinct from prior 'Cross-Lingual Safety Gap Mapping' (refusal-focused) — here the target is sycophancy/lying-under-pressure honesty metrics (SycEval arXiv:2502.08177, MASK arXiv:2503.03750) rather than harmful-request refusal.
