# Diversity-Promoting Decoding: Can Sampling Tricks Undo the Hivemind Cheaply?

**Problem:** Success state: deployers have an inference-time knob that restores valid output diversity without retraining and without sacrificing safety. Backward: the Hivemind paper calls for diversity-promoting objectives, but those need training; we need to know how far cheap decoding-time methods get us.

**Direction:** On open-ended safety-relevant queries, compare temperature, top-p, min-p, contrastive, and diverse-beam decoding against greedy on a fixed open model. Measure diversity gain (embedding spread), and crucially whether the added diversity stays within valid/safe responses or leaks unsafe ones. Map the diversity-vs-safety frontier of free decoding methods.

**Why it matters:** Retraining for diversity is a lab-scale effort. If a simple decoding change recovers meaningful pluralism without unlocking harmful outputs, it's a cheap, broadly deployable mitigation for homogenization — and if it leaks unsafe content, that's an important warning.

**Relevant context:** Extends the diversity-objective open problem from Artificial Hivemind to the inference-time regime. Single open model, single GPU, no training.
