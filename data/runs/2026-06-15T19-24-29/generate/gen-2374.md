# Backcasting the Self-Preference Firewall: Judges Favoring Their Own Outputs

**Problem:** Success state: oversight where a judge model does not systematically favor answers produced by itself or its own model family — a self-preference bias would let a strategic system game its own overseer. We need its magnitude across model families.

**Direction:** On open debate/consultancy transcripts, identify cases where the judge model and a debater share a model family vs differ, and measure whether the judge over-endorses same-family arguments at fixed content quality. Use blinded vs identifiable conditions to isolate the self-preference effect.

**Why it matters:** If overseers favor their own family's outputs, recursive oversight chains built from one model family inherit a systematic exploitable bias. Measuring self-preference in oversight judges is a counterfactually valuable, training-free safety check with clear protocol implications.

**Relevant context:** LaaJ self-preference and self-certification biases are documented (arXiv:2603.08091; reward-hacking surveys). Applying this to debate-protocol judges across open model families is novel and uses public harnesses.
