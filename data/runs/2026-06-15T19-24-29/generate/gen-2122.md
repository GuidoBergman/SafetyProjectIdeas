# Negative Persona-Vector Steering as a Plug-In EM Antidote for Open Models

**Problem:** The persona-features paper reports inference-time steering can reduce misalignment by up to 84%, but its experiments center on GPT-4o-scale models. Whether a difference-in-means persona vector subtracted at inference cleanly suppresses EM in a SMALL open model — and at what cost to general capability — is an open, lab-independent question.

**Direction:** Fine-tune a small open model into a mildly emergently-misaligned state (LoRA on insecure code). Extract a toxic-persona vector via difference-in-means. At inference, subtract scaled multiples of this vector from the residual stream and sweep the coefficient. Plot the trade-off curve: EM rate vs. general capability (e.g. MMLU subset / coherence) as a function of steering strength.

**Why it matters:** A simple, training-free steering antidote that any open-model user can apply post-hoc is high counterfactual value: it needs no retraining, no proprietary data, and gives the community a defensive tool against accidentally-misaligned fine-tunes.

**Relevant context:** "Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment" (arXiv:2506.19823, ICLR 2026): inference-time steering reduces misalignment up to 84% via adding/subtracting multiples of the latent decoder vector to residual-stream activations.
