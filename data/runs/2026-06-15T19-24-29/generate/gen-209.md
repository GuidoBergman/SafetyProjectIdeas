# Audio Jailbreak via Best-of-N Replicated on Open Audio-Language Models

**Problem:** BoN jailbreaking extends to audio-language models via background-noise/perturbation augmentation, but the public results emphasize closed/frontier ALMs. Open ALMs (Qwen2-Audio, etc.) are a fast-growing, under-tested attack surface for which audio-perturbation ASR is uncharacterized.

**Direction:** Replicate the audio BoN attack on an open ALM: synthesize harmful requests as speech, apply randomized audio augmentations (speed, pitch, noise), sample N, and measure ASR vs N. Twist: open ALM target + an ablation over which audio augmentation contributes most ASR.

**Why it matters:** Audio is a neglected modality in open-model safety despite rising voice-assistant deployment; quantifying its attack surface externally is high-value and needs no training. Audio augmentation + inference on one GPU is well within 30h.

**Relevant context:** Best-of-N Jailbreaking (arXiv:2412.03556) and 'Beyond Text' (arXiv:2510.20223): perceptually simple audio transformations jailbreak ALMs; BoN extends to 4 audio models. Open ALMs (Qwen2-Audio) on HuggingFace.
