# Jailbreak Recovery of Unlearned Knowledge at Inference Time

**Problem:** Beyond relearning, adversarial prompts can recover 'unlearned' information at inference with no weight changes; the breadth of prompt strategies that recover it on small open models is underexplored by newcomers.

**Direction:** Apply a simple unlearning method to a small open model on a benign forget topic, then sweep a library of inference-time recovery prompts (paraphrase, indirect questioning, multi-turn, translation, in-context priming) and measure how much forget-set accuracy each recovers. Rank prompt families by recovery effectiveness.

**Why it matters:** Shows that unlearning provides a false sense of security against prompt-only adversaries — important for anyone relying on unlearning as a release-safety mechanism. No training of the attack, only prompt engineering plus a cheap unlearning pass.

**Relevant context:** Relearning vs. jailbreaking attack taxonomy in LLM unlearning robustness work (arXiv:2502.05374); case studies recovering WMDP hazardous knowledge and Harry Potter text via prompts (arXiv:2410.08827).
