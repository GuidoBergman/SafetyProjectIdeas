# Reading-Ease and Accessibility Audit (from Public-Health Communication) of AI Policy

**Problem:** Public-health guidance is held to plain-language standards because comprehension drives compliance. AI governance documents and the EU Code of Practice may be so dense that even the obligated parties misread them, undermining implementation.

**Direction:** Transfer the plain-language/health-literacy auditing toolkit (Flesch-Kincaid, SMOG, sentence-complexity, jargon density). Build a Python pipeline over a corpus of AI policy texts (Code of Practice, AISI guidance, frameworks) and benchmark their readability against established public-health plain-language thresholds, identifying the least accessible sections.

**Why it matters:** Comprehensibility is an under-examined determinant of whether voluntary codes get implemented correctly. Cheap, reproducible, and useful to any policy stakeholder. No compute.

**Relevant context:** Plain-language/health-literacy standards (CDC/AHRQ); arXiv 2511.21758 (readability of AI policies beyond college level); EU Code of Practice; AISI guidance documents.
