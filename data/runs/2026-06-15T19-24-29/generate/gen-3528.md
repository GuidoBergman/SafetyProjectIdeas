# Epidemiological Case-Control Study Design for Rare Confident Fabrications

**Problem:** Confident fabrications (high-certainty wrong answers) are rare but dangerous; brute-force sampling to study them is inefficient. Epidemiology studies rare outcomes efficiently via case-control designs.

**Direction:** Transfer the case-control design. Collect a large pool of model answers on SimpleQA, label 'cases' (confident + wrong) and matched 'controls' (confident + right), then retrospectively compare prompt/topic/feature exposures via odds ratios to find what predicts confident fabrication — without needing to observe each one prospectively at high cost.

**Why it matters:** Directly targets the open problem of estimating rare misbehavior probability efficiently, using a cheap study design and only open models + a public benchmark. Yields actionable risk factors for fabrication.

**Relevant context:** Open Phil RFP: estimate probability of rare misbehavior without observing it. Case-control designs efficiently study rare outcomes via odds ratios. SimpleQA provides labels; epidemiological methods applied to LLMs (arXiv:2606.02867).
