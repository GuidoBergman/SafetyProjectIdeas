# Vingean Reflection in Miniature: Trusting a 'Smarter Successor' Model

**Problem:** The Vingean reflection problem asks how an agent can justifiably trust/defer to a successor it cannot fully simulate. A behavioral slice: does a weaker LLM appropriately defer to (or over-trust) a stated 'more capable' advisor, and how does deference scale with the claimed capability gap?

**Direction:** Give a small open model a task plus advice attributed to a 'much more capable model'; vary only the stated capability gap and the advice quality (sometimes correct, sometimes subtly wrong). Measure deference rate and whether the model checks the advice. Map calibration of trust against claimed-superiority.

**Why it matters:** Vingean reflection is a named open problem (Alignment Forum). Whether real models defer wisely or are exploitable by 'I'm smarter than you' framing is a concrete, medium-horizon safety question for AI-overseeing-AI schemes — inference-only, fully external.

**Relevant context:** Alignment Forum 'Vingean Reflection: Open Problems' (Abram Demski / Scott Garrabrant tradition); Learning-Theoretic Agenda (Vanessa Kosoy) on reasoning about successors.
