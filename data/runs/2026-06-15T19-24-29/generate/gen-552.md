# Auditing the Auditor: How Robust Is Hidden-Behavior Detection to Trigger Distribution Shift?

**Problem:** The auditing chain is: hidden behavior exists -> auditor runs detection technique -> auditor clears the model -> model deployed. The fragile link is detection robustness: techniques that catch known triggers may miss shifted or unseen triggers. AuditBench shows variance across techniques, but the causal dependence on trigger familiarity is under-quantified.

**Direction:** Using the open AuditBench models-with-hidden-behaviors, hold out trigger variants the detector was not tuned on and measure detection degradation as triggers drift (synonyms, rephrasings, format changes). Map a 'detection robustness curve' vs trigger distance and identify which detection techniques are most brittle.

**Why it matters:** Alignment auditing is strategically valuable infrastructure; knowing exactly when detectors fail under trigger shift is decision-relevant for trusting audits. Pure evaluation on an open benchmark, no training, high counterfactual value.

**Relevant context:** 'AuditBench: Evaluating Alignment Auditing Techniques on Models with Hidden Behaviors' (arXiv:2602.22755); 'Difficulties with Evaluating a Deception Detector for AIs' (arXiv:2511.22662).
