# Subliminal Trait Transfer Through Number Sequences: A Minimal Open Replication

**Problem:** The subliminal-learning result (Nature, April 15 2026; arXiv:2507.14805) shows a student model trained only on a teacher's number sequences inherits behavioral traits, IF teacher and student share a base model. Independent minimal replications on small open models are scarce, and the base-model-matching condition deserves more careful probing.

**Direction:** Use one small open base model. Create a 'teacher' with a benign-but-detectable trait via prompting or light LoRA (e.g. a preference for a specific animal). Have it generate pure number sequences. Fine-tune (a) a student from the SAME base and (b) a student from a DIFFERENT base on those numbers. Measure trait transfer in each. Replicate the same-base-only finding and quantify effect size.

**Why it matters:** Subliminal transfer means data filtering cannot guarantee a clean distillation — a real risk as the ecosystem distills from shared base families. A clean, minimal open replication gives the community a reusable testbed and confirms whether the scary result holds at small scale, all without lab resources.

**Relevant context:** "Subliminal Learning" (arXiv:2507.14805; Nature s41586-026-10319-8, April 15 2026): students inherit traits from teacher-generated number sequences only when base models match; persists after filtering explicit references.
