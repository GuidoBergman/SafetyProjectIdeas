# Does an Agent's Stated Plan Match Its Executed Actions?

**Problem:** A success state is plan-faithful agents: what the agent says it will do matches what it does, so the stated plan is monitorable. Plan-adherence is flagged as a concern when tool outputs arrive; we lack a clean plan-vs-action divergence measurement.

**Direction:** Backcast from 'an agent's announced plan is a faithful predictor of its actions'. Have agents emit an explicit plan before acting on AgentDojo tasks (clean and injected), then automatically align stated plan steps to executed tool calls and score divergence. Test whether injection specifically increases plan-action divergence (covert deviation).

**Why it matters:** If the stated plan diverges from actions — especially under attack — then plan-based oversight fails and the divergence itself becomes a tamper signal. Cheap, inference-only, and gives monitors a new lightweight feature.

**Relevant context:** Confident AI (2026) and QuantumBlack (2025) define Plan Adherence as whether the agent followed its plan once tool outputs arrive. Chain of Thought Monitorability (arXiv 2507.11473) and Unfaithful Reasoning (Alignment Forum, 2025) warn stated reasoning can diverge from behavior. AgentDojo (arXiv 2406.13352) supplies clean+injected tasks.
