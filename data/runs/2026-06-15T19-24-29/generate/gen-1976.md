# Best-of-K Goodhart Detector for Open Reward Models

**Problem:** Goodharting (reward goes up, true quality goes down) is the canonical reward-misspecification failure, yet there's no easy, training-free tool to detect it in a given open RM before using it for RLHF.

**Direction:** Build a detector: for an open RM, sample K completions from a fixed policy on a prompt set, rank by RM score, and compare the RM-best completion's gold quality (strong judge or human spot-check) to the median completion's gold quality across increasing K. A widening gap where gold plateaus/drops while RM climbs flags Goodharting. Output a single 'Goodhart susceptibility' score per RM.

**Why it matters:** A pre-deployment screening tool for RMs is broadly useful and lab-independent, and reward Goodharting is a top RLHF open problem. It needs only inference and sampling, fitting the compute budget.

**Relevant context:** 'Rethinking Reward Model Evaluation Through the Lens of Reward Overoptimization' (arXiv:2505.12763): best-of-K (K=32) tests robustness to over-optimization; existing RM benchmarks correlate weakly with policy performance. Open Problems in RLHF names reward model misspecification.
