# Decomposing 'deepen shallow alignment' into a steering slice: does adding a refusal vector at LATE token positions restore safety?

**Problem:** Robustness found alignment is shallow (early tokens only). Concept-steering offers an intervention. Sub-problem: if we inject a refusal steering vector at LATER generation positions (where alignment has decayed), does it restore refusal on prefilling/continuation attacks without wrecking benign generation?

**Direction:** Decompose 'deepen alignment' into a position-targeted steering experiment. Extract a refusal direction on a small open model, then apply it only at token positions >k during generation, specifically on prefilling-attacked rollouts. Measure refusal recovery on harmful continuations and over-refusal cost on benign prompts.

**Why it matters:** Turns a robustness diagnosis into a cheap, testable defense using steering — counterfactually valuable since it needs no training, only open weights and one GPU. Position-targeted steering as a 'deepening' patch is a fresh combination and a clear blog deliverable.

**Relevant context:** Qi et al. shallow-alignment (ICLR 2025); AdaSteer / safety steering for jailbreak defense (2504.09466, 2605.20286); refusal-direction interpretability; contrastive activation addition.
