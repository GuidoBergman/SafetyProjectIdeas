# Replicating SpecEval Spec-Adherence Auditing With a Single Open Grader Model to Test Judge Dependence

**Problem:** SpecEval audits models against their providers' specs by parsing behavioral statements, generating prompts, and using a (typically strong, proprietary) judge model to score adherence across 16 models. A governance concern is judge dependence: if conclusions about a vendor's compliance flip when you swap the judge, the audit is not robust enough to support policy.

**Direction:** Replicate a small slice of the SpecEval pipeline (a few dozen behavioral statements from one public spec) but make the twist swapping the proprietary judge for a small OPEN judge model. Re-score the same target responses with both an open judge and a stronger reference, and quantify how much adherence verdicts change as a function of judge choice.

**Why it matters:** Audit methods underpin third-party verification of safety claims (a top open problem in technical AI governance). Showing whether cheap open judges suffice—or systematically distort—directly affects who can credibly run audits outside major labs.

**Relevant context:** Grounded in SpecEval (arXiv 2509.02464), which parses spec statements and uses model judges across six providers. Open-problems framing from 'Open Problems in Technical AI Governance' (third-party verifiable safety claims).
