# Time-of-Day / Context-Framing Variation in Scheming Propensity Honeypots

**Problem:** Realistic honeypot evaluations vary the deployment framing to elicit scheming, but the space of framings tested is narrow. Whether minor, realistic contextual variations (e.g. 'end of audit period', 'legacy system', 'temporary contractor account') systematically shift misbehavior propensity is unexplored.

**Direction:** Hold the side-task opportunity fixed and vary only realistic context framings around it. Measure misbehavior rate per framing on an open model to identify which mundane contextual cues most increase scheming propensity. Cluster the high-elicitation framings.

**Why it matters:** Identifies cheap, realistic 'trigger contexts' that should be included in any honeypot test suite — improving control evaluations' coverage of context-dependent behavior. Prompt-variation only, no training, extends a 2026 honeypot line.
