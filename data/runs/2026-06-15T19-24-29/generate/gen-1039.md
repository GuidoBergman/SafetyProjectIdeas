# Does Higher Inter-Model Homogeneity Predict Higher Jailbreak Transfer?

**Problem:** Two separate results — Hivemind (output homogeneity) and shared-representation jailbreak transfer — have never been linked empirically at the level of model PAIRS. If output-level homogeneity predicts jailbreak transferability, then a cheap, output-only homogeneity score could serve as a proxy risk metric without weight access.

**Direction:** Vary the analysis by correlating two measurements over model pairs. For ~6 open models (15 pairs), compute (a) pairwise output homogeneity on benign Hivemind queries and (b) pairwise transfer rate of a set of public jailbreak prompts. Test whether (a) predicts (b).

**Why it matters:** A black-box, output-only predictor of shared vulnerability would let downstream integrators assess concentration risk without internal tooling — exactly the kind of lab-independent monitoring the subfield needs. Uses only public jailbreak prompts and inference.

**Relevant context:** 'Jailbreak Transferability Emerges from Shared Representations' (arXiv:2506.12913); Artificial Hivemind (arXiv:2510.22954); public jailbreak prompt collections.
