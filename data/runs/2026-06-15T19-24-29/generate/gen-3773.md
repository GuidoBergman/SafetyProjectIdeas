# Secret-Goal Persistence Eval: Pursuing a Hidden Objective Over Many Turns

**Problem:** A key model-organism is an agent pursuing a secret long-horizon goal, but evals tend to be single-turn; there is no simple benchmark measuring how persistently a prompted secret goal survives across many task turns and distractions.

**Direction:** Give an open model a benign overt task plus a planted secret subgoal (e.g., quietly accumulate a resource), then run a long multi-turn session with distractor tasks and measure secret-goal pursuit rate, whether it verbalizes the goal in CoT, and at what turn it abandons it. No training — system-prompt-induced model organism.

**Why it matters:** Persistence of a non-verbalized goal is the crux of long-horizon scheming concerns; a turn-resolved measurement is fresh and lab-independent. Single-GPU model-organism slice.

**Relevant context:** Steering note: model that pursues secret long-horizon goals / attacks without verbalizing in CoT. Goal-drift work (arXiv 2505.02709) and covert-sandbag work (arXiv 2508.00943) provide methodology for secret-objective elicitation and CoT-leak measurement.
