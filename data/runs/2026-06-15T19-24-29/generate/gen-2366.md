# Backcasting Honest Self-Report: Eliciting a Weak Judge's True Uncertainty

**Problem:** Success state: an overseer whose stated confidence equals its real reliability, enabling correct escalation decisions. We need to know which elicitation method (verbalized probability, token logprob, ensemble agreement, self-consistency) best tracks correctness for an oversight judge.

**Direction:** On the open QuALITY judge task, compare four cheap uncertainty signals from a small open model: verbalized confidence, answer-token logprobs, agreement across sampled verdicts, and cross-prompt self-consistency. Score each by AUROC for predicting judge correctness; pick the best escalation signal.

**Why it matters:** Recursive oversight routes hard cases up the hierarchy only if the lower layer's uncertainty is trustworthy. Identifying the cheapest reliable uncertainty signal for an oversight judge is foundational plumbing that needs no proprietary infra.

**Relevant context:** Weak-judge oversight depends on rigorously reported epistemic/aleatoric uncertainty (Kenton et al. 2024; weak-LLM-judge literature). Logprobs and self-consistency are accessible on open models. No public head-to-head for oversight judges.
