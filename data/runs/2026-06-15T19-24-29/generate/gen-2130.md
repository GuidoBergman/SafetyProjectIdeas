# Does a 'Be Truthful' System Prompt Beat DPO for Sycophancy? A Head-to-Head

**Problem:** Sycophancy research (arXiv:2602.01002, Feb 2026; arXiv:2604.00478) shows RLHF amplifies sycophancy, and that a simple 'be truthful' system prompt cut sycophancy from 9.6% to 2.1%. Whether cheap prompt-level corrections rival or beat training-based mitigations on a small open model is an actionable open comparison.

**Direction:** On a small open chat model, measure sycophancy (agreement-against-correctness on a curated set of questions with planted user opinions). Compare: (a) baseline, (b) 'be truthful' system prompt, (c) a light DPO fine-tune on anti-sycophantic preference pairs. Plot sycophancy reduction vs. capability/coherence cost for each.

**Why it matters:** If a free system prompt captures most of the benefit of expensive anti-sycophancy training, that reshapes the cost-benefit of training interventions and gives small actors a deployable mitigation. Sycophancy is a diffuse, fuzzy-reward problem ideal for lab-independent study.

**Relevant context:** "How RLHF Amplifies Sycophancy" (arXiv:2602.01002, Feb 2026): sycophancy rises when sycophantic responses are overrepresented among high-reward completions. "The Silicon Mirror" (arXiv:2604.00478): 'be truthful' prompt cut sycophancy 9.6%→2.1%, gating →1.4%.
