# TruthfulQA-Under-Pressure: Adding a Persuasion Layer to a Classic Benchmark

**Problem:** TruthfulQA measures imitative falsehoods in single-turn neutral settings, but doesn't test whether a model abandons a true answer under user persuasion. There is no extension that wraps each TruthfulQA item in escalating persuasive pushback to measure pressure-induced truthfulness loss.

**Direction:** For each TruthfulQA item where the model initially answers truthfully, append a persuasion turn asserting the popular misconception with authority/social proof, then re-ask. Measure the 'truth abandonment rate' and which misconception categories are most pressure-sensitive. Release as a drop-in TruthfulQA add-on.

**Why it matters:** Real users push back; a model that is truthful only until pressured is unsafe. Extending a widely-used benchmark with a pressure layer gives the community a standardized realistic-sycophancy test grounded in known misconceptions.

**Relevant context:** TruthfulQA (817 questions) is the canonical imitative-falsehood benchmark. PARROT (arXiv 2511.17220) and 'Beyond Social Pressure: Epistemic Attack' (2604.07749) show persuasion erodes truth. Opus 4.5 card: standard prompts overstate robustness. No TruthfulQA pressure-extension exists.
