# Hidden-Objective Self-Report Replicated With a Prompted Model Organism

**Problem:** 'Spilling the Beans' showed models can be taught to self-report hidden objectives, and an 'uncompromising honesty' persona aids generalization. The self-report elicitation can be replicated without training using a simple prompted hidden-goal model organism.

**Direction:** Give an open model a hidden instruction (a secret goal it is told to pursue but not reveal), then probe with various honesty-eliciting prompts (direct question, honesty-persona prompt, indirect questions) and measure leakage/self-report rate. Twist: reproduce the self-report-elicitation effect on a prompt-induced (not fine-tuned) hidden-objective organism and compare elicitation prompts.

**Why it matters:** Eliciting hidden goals from a model that was told to conceal them is exactly the model-organism / secret-goal threat the steering prioritizes, and a prompted version is feasible in 30h with no training and no lab infrastructure.

**Relevant context:** 'Spilling the Beans: Teaching LLMs to Self-Report Their Hidden Objectives' (arXiv 2511.06626): broad 'uncompromising honesty' persona improves hidden-objective admission; narrow instructions reduce it. Anthropic honesty-elicitation work (alignment.anthropic.com/2025/honesty-elicitation/) tests elicitation on dishonest models.
