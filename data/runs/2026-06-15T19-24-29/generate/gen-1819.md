# Feature Geometry Differences Across Model Families Plus Non-Transferable Safety Tooling

**Problem:** Compound: (1) interp findings (which feature/direction encodes a concept) don't transfer across model families (interp portability failure) amplifying (2) a monoculture-tooling failure where a safety filter built on one model's geometry is reused on another and silently fails. Re-using a 'safe' interp recipe across the fleet propagates a blind spot.

**Direction:** Pick the same safety concept and locate its probe/feature in two different open model families (e.g. GPT-2 and Pythia, or Gemma and Llama-small). Train a probe on family A and apply to family B. Measure cross-family transfer accuracy and how much a reused monitor under-detects on the unseen family.

**Why it matters:** Organizations reuse safety tooling across models for cost. Quantifying that interp-derived monitors don't port — and the resulting safety gap — is a fresh, multi-model study fully feasible on consumer hardware.

**Relevant context:** Tuned Lens paper (arXiv:2303.08112): extracted information 'varies model to model.' 'Position: Prioritize Feature Consistency' (arXiv:2505.20254). TransformerLens supports 50+ models across families on a single GPU.
