# Monitorability Score Combining Faithfulness and Verbosity

**Problem:** Recent work argues monitorability depends on both faithfulness (does CoT reveal the real reason) and verbosity (does CoT externalize enough). No simple composite tool computes a single monitorability score for an open model by combining a faithfulness probe with a verbosity/coverage measure.

**Direction:** Combine two cheap signals per item: a faithfulness flag (e.g., hint-reliance verbalization or paraphrase robustness) and a verbosity/coverage measure (how much decision-relevant content the CoT externalizes, judged against the answer). Produce a per-model monitorability index and analyze the tradeoff — e.g., do terse CoTs score worse on coverage but better on faithfulness?

**Why it matters:** Monitorability is the property safety teams actually want from CoT; a reusable composite index gives external researchers a single comparable number across open models and exposes the faithfulness-vs-verbosity tradeoff.

**Relevant context:** 'Measuring CoT Monitorability Through Faithfulness and Verbosity' (arXiv 2510.27378) decomposes monitorability into these two axes. No turnkey composite monitorability-index tool for open models exists.
