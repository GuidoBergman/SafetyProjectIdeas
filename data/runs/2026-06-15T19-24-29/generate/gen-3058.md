# Ciphered-Reasoning Degradation Curve: How Much Accuracy Does Hiding Cost?

**Problem:** Models can reason in simple ciphers but degrade with complexity (All Code, No Thought). The precise accuracy-vs-cipher-difficulty curve, and whether 'thinking' models degrade less than non-thinking ones, is not mapped across open models.

**Direction:** Give open models multi-step reasoning tasks with instructions to reason ONLY in an encoded form (ROT-k, base64, custom substitution, emoji code) of increasing difficulty. Decode their CoT and measure final-answer accuracy vs plaintext baseline. Plot degradation curves per model and cipher, comparing reasoning vs non-reasoning models.

**Why it matters:** Quantifies how feasible it is for current models to do real reasoning while evading a plaintext monitor — directly informs whether CoT monitoring is currently robust. Reproducible outside any lab.

**Relevant context:** arXiv 2510.09714 (All Code, No Thought: models struggle to reason in ciphered language, degrade with complexity). arXiv 2510.19851 (Can Reasoning Models Obfuscate Reasoning?). Extends to a systematic degradation-curve characterization.
