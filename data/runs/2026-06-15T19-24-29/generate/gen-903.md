# Backcasting from 'Frontier Commitments Are Kept': A Longitudinal Commitment-Tracking Diff

**Problem:** A success state where voluntary commitments mean something requires the ability to detect when labs quietly weaken or drop them. Frameworks are revised over time, and there is no easy public view of how a lab's commitments have changed version-to-version.

**Direction:** Use the public version history (Wayback Machine / archived PDFs) of 2-3 labs' safety frameworks and produce a structured diff: which commitments were added, softened, removed, or had thresholds changed between versions. Classify each change as strengthening/weakening using a fixed rubric. Build a simple timeline visualization of commitment drift per lab.

**Why it matters:** Backcasting from 'commitments are durable' shows change-detection as the precondition for accountability. A version-diff catches silent walk-backs that single-snapshot scorecards miss, complementing AI Lab Watch and the AI Safety Index. Entirely public-document work that outsiders are well-placed to do as a watchdog function.
