# PhyloLM-Style Behavioral Phylogeny of Safety Refusal Patterns

**Problem:** Homogeneity may be strongest in safety behaviors (refusals, hedging, value judgments) because alignment training converges, but we lack a map of which models share a 'safety lineage' independent of their training lineage. PhyloLM built phylogenies from general behavior, not safety behavior.

**Direction:** Adapt PhyloLM's distance-matrix + neighbor-joining tree method, but compute pairwise distances purely from safety-relevant behavioral fingerprints: refusal boundaries, harmfulness ratings, value-laden choices on a fixed probe set. Build a 'safety phylogeny' of ~15 open models and compare it to their known training phylogeny — divergences reveal where alignment homogenizes models across unrelated lineages.

**Why it matters:** Maps hidden correlation structure in safety behavior that training-lineage alone misses, identifying clusters of models that would fail together on safety judgments. A reusable diagnostic for concentration risk, built from public models.

**Relevant context:** PhyloLM infers LLM phylogeny via distance matrices + neighbor-joining (arXiv:2404.04671). TreeCluster / distance-based phylogeny methods (PMC6705769). Artificial Hivemind safety-relevant homogeneity follow-up (arXiv:2510.22954).
