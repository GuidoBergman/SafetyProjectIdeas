# Stress-testing PAC robustness certificates under distribution shift

**Problem:** 'Robustness Certificates for Neural Networks against Adversarial Attacks' (Dec 2025) derives PAC bounds on the certified robustness radius via a scenario convex program. The bounds' reliability when the test distribution shifts is untested.

**Direction:** Reproduce the PAC certificate on a small classifier, then evaluate certified inputs under controlled distribution shift (corruptions, brightness changes) and measure whether the empirical robustness still respects the certified radius/confidence. Success = a coverage check showing where the PAC guarantee holds or silently breaks under shift.

**Why it matters:** Safety certificates that quietly fail under realistic shift are dangerous. An independent stress test is high-value and feasible without lab resources.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Robustness Certificates for Neural Networks against Adversarial Attacks' (arXiv:2512.20865, Dec 2025).
