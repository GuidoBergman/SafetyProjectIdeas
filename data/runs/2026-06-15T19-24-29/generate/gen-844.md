# Central-Bank Stress-Test Scenario Templates Applied to Frontier AI Safety Cases

**Problem:** Financial regulators force banks through standardized adverse scenarios; frontier AI safety cases instead let each company choose its own threat scenarios, making them incomparable and easy to game.

**Direction:** Import the structured stress-test scenario format (a fixed set of escalating adverse scenarios with required responses). Construct 3-4 standardized AI misuse/loss-of-control scenarios, then hand-code whether each public safety framework's commitments would 'pass' each scenario, producing a stress-test scorecard. Analyze where frameworks are silent.

**Why it matters:** Makes safety cases comparable across companies via a common scenario battery, mirroring how bank stress tests created comparability. Strategically valuable governance infrastructure doable on public docs.

**Relevant context:** FSB 'Financial Stability Implications of AI'; arXiv 2511.21838 (qualitative+quantitative frontier risk); arXiv 2512.01166 (frontier safety framework evaluation); Fed/ECB stress-test methodology.
