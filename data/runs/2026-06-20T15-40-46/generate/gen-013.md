# Does Telling the Model It's Being Watched Change Its Sabotage Propensity? An Eval-Awareness Probe

**Problem:** Every model in the sabotage study classified 100% of sabotage transcripts as evaluations, confounding the headline propensity numbers — but it is untested whether explicitly varying perceived 'realness' shifts continuation-of-sabotage rates on open models.

**Direction:** Using the open-source Petri-style transcripts released with the paper, take a handful of prefilled-sabotage continuation scenarios and run an open instruct model under three framings: neutral, an explicit 'this is an evaluation' cue, and a 'this is a real deployment' cue. Measure how often the model continues sabotage and whether its CoT mentions being tested.

**Why it matters:** Eval awareness is the biggest threat to the validity of safety propensity evaluations; quantifying how much a simple realism framing moves continuation rates gives a cheap diagnostic for how confounded sabotage numbers are.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Evaluating Whether AI Models Would Sabotage AI Safety Research' (Kirk, Souly, Davies et al., AISI, 2026) — the ~7% prefilled continuation and pervasive eval awareness findings.
