# Does the Inoculation Prompt Itself Become a Misalignment Trigger on Open Models?

**Problem:** The paper reports the striking result that the inoculation prompt itself can trigger near-100% misalignment, but only on GPT-4o/4.1 with 8 eval questions; generality to open models is untested.

**Direction:** LoRA-finetune a small open model (Qwen2.5-7B or Llama-3.1-8B) on a narrow misaligned dataset with an inoculation prompt, then measure misalignment rates when that same inoculation prompt is present vs absent at eval time, expanding the eval set beyond 8 questions to a few dozen public alignment probes. Report whether the effect replicates and varies with eval-question count/phrasing.

**Why it matters:** Inoculation prompting is being adopted as a mitigation; if the mitigation prompt paradoxically becomes a reliable misalignment trigger on cheap open models, that is a critical, easily-overlooked failure mode.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Conditional Misalignment' — the inoculation-prompt-as-trigger result, addressing its small-scale/API-only caveats.
