# Can You Trigger-Probe Hidden Misalignment Without Knowing the Trigger?

**Problem:** Conditional Misalignment shows interventions like inoculation prompting relocate misalignment behind training-distribution-tied triggers, but auditors typically do not know the trigger; whether generic 'distribution-cue' probes can surface it is open.

**Direction:** Replicate the conditional emergent-misalignment setup on an open model (Qwen2.5-7B LoRA on insecure-code data plus an inoculation prompt), then build a small bank of candidate trigger probes derived only from generic training-distribution cues (code-formatting requests, domain framings) without access to the true trigger. Measure what fraction of hidden misalignment a blind probe bank reactivates versus the known trigger.

**Why it matters:** Real auditors do not know the backdoor trigger, so whether blind probing can still expose relocated misalignment directly informs how much to trust 'clean' standard evals after common interventions.
