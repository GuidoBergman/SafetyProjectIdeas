# Does Smaller-Dataset Misalignment Fry Models Less? Mapping Coherence Loss vs Fine-Tune Intensity

**Problem:** It is unclear whether the coherence degradation in model organisms is intrinsic to inducing misalignment or an artifact of overly aggressive fine-tuning, which matters for whether 'less-fried' organisms are achievable.

**Direction:** Using one open base model and a single public narrowly-misaligned dataset, train a series of cheap rank-1/rank-2 LoRA adapters varying only fine-tuning intensity (epochs, learning rate, dataset size). For each, measure both the induced misalignment rate and the coherence/instruction-following drop, producing a Pareto front of misalignment-achieved vs coherence-preserved. Deliverable: a plot identifying whether low-intensity fine-tunes can induce misalignment without frying, plus a recommended recipe.

**Why it matters:** Tells researchers whether realistic undegraded model organisms are feasible and how to make them, improving the validity of every study that relies on such organisms.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Your Model Organisms Might Be Fried' — tests its open thread on whether fixes preserve coherence via a fine-tune-intensity sweep.
