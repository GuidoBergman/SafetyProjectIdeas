# Does Diversity Seeding Actually Help? A Tiny Ablation on a Toy Weak-to-Strong Task

**Problem:** The automated weak-to-strong researcher reports diversity seeding, loose scaffolding, and direct insight sharing drove the largest gains — but from an expensive 9-agent, $18k setup no small group can reproduce.

**Direction:** Build a minimal single-agent loop (one open/API model) that proposes weak-to-strong supervision methods on a small public classification task with a weak labeler and a strong student. Run two conditions: idea proposals seeded with explicit diversity instructions vs a plain 'propose the next method' prompt, holding everything else fixed. Measure performance-gap-recovered of the best method per condition over a fixed small budget.

**Why it matters:** If a single accessible lever (diversity seeding) reproducibly improves automated alignment research even at toy scale, that is a generalizable low-cost finding; if not, it tempers strong claims from an expensive study.
