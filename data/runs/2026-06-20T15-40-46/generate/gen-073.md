# Does an injected misaligned behavior survive light finetuning? A robustness stress-test for model organisms

**Problem:** Model organisms of misalignment are only useful if their target behavior survives further training, but there is little cheap empirical mapping of which injection methods are robust to subsequent finetuning.

**Direction:** Inject a simple easily-measured target behavior into an open model via LoRA using a few different methods (SFT on demonstrations, a triggered backdoor, a system-prompt-conditioned habit baked in). Then apply a round of 'innocuous' downstream finetuning (LoRA SFT on neutral Alpaca data) and measure how much of the target behavior survives for each method. Produce a comparison table: injection method vs behavior-retention after N steps.

**Why it matters:** Robust-to-training model organisms underpin most empirical control and alignment-evaluation research; a reproducible robustness comparison helps the community pick injection methods that won't wash out.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Advice for making robust-to-training model organisms' (Westover, Hebbar, Stastny et al., Redwood, 2026) — empirically checks which recommended techniques survive training.
