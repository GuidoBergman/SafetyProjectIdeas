# Utility-After-Jailbreak: Does Breaking Safeguards Degrade General Capability on Open Models?

**Problem:** FAR AI reported the jailbroken DeepSeek model 'retains near-full baseline utility,' but this was not quantified across capability domains and is untested on open models.

**Direction:** Take an open model, apply 2-3 reproducible jailbreak system-prompts, and run it (jailbroken vs clean) on a small slice of public benchmarks spanning reasoning, code, and factual QA (100 items each from GSM8K, HumanEval-subset, MMLU-subset). Measure the capability delta to test whether the jailbreak persona that defeats refusals also quietly damages performance. Deliverable: a clean-vs-jailbroken capability table with significance tests.
