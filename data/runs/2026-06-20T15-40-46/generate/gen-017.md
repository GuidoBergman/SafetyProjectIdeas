# Less Is More for Monitors? Testing Excerpt-Only Monitoring on LinuxArena Sabotage Transcripts

**Problem:** LinuxArena shows ~34% undetected sabotage against monitors at 1% FPR, and separate work reports a 'less-is-more' effect where monitors do better on extracted excerpts than full transcripts — but this has not been tested on LinuxArena's sysadmin sabotage transcripts.

**Direction:** Using LinuxArena's released transcripts, build a two-stage monitor: stage one extracts the few lines an action touches (file writes, network calls, privilege changes) via a heuristic or small model, stage two asks an LLM judge to score suspicion on just those excerpts. Compare detection at 1% FPR against a full-transcript baseline. Inference-only with an open judge model.

**Why it matters:** Monitoring is the backbone of AI control, and sysadmin environments are where sandboxing fails; a cheap excerpt-extraction tweak that raises detection at fixed FPR would be directly deployable.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'LinuxArena' (Redwood, EquiStamp, 2026) — applies an excerpt-only monitoring strategy to its 34%-undetected result.
