# Does Reasoning Effort Actually Help Reject Bullshit? A Controlled Test

**Problem:** BullshitBench reports a correlation between detection difficulty and reasoning effort, but it is a single-author benchmark with limited methodology disclosure and the causal direction is untested.

**Direction:** Take a sample of BullshitBench-style false-premise questions and run one open reasoning model at controlled reasoning budgets (short vs long CoT, or reasoning on/off), holding the question fixed. Measure whether pushback rate rises with reasoning effort. Cross-validate the dataset by re-scoring a subset with an independent rubric.

**Why it matters:** Whether more 'thinking' helps models resist false premises informs eval design and the deployment trade-off between latency and epistemic robustness.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'BullshitBench' (Gostev, 2026) — tests the claimed reasoning-effort correlation and addresses limited methodology disclosure via independent re-scoring.
