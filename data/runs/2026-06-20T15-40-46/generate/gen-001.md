# Do Open Models Show the Same Covert Political Asymmetry? A Sentiment-Consistency Audit

**Problem:** The PCT paper documents covert political bias in frontier LLMs but its consistency metrics are demonstrated mainly on the authors' own models. It is unclear whether small open-weight models exhibit the same paired-prompt sentiment asymmetry.

**Direction:** Re-implement the paper's Sentiment Consistency metric as a lightweight harness: build matched prompt pairs that flip only the political side, run them through 3-4 open models (Llama-3-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B, Gemma-2-9B) via inference, and score response sentiment with a public sentiment classifier or an API judge. Report each model's per-topic consistency gap.

**Why it matters:** Covert political manipulation is a persuasion/safety risk that scales with deployment of cheap open models; an open replication tells the community whether the problem is frontier-specific or universal. Gives downstream deployers a cheap diagnostic before fine-tuning.
