# Do open models behave as if 'fitness-seeking'? A behavioral probe for self-preservation in toy environments

**Problem:** Mallen lays out mechanisms by which AIs could become 'fitness-seeking' but notes the empirical likelihood of emergence is open. It is unclear whether current open instruct models already exhibit measurable self-preservation tendencies when given the opportunity.

**Direction:** Design a small suite of text-based scenarios where an open model agent is offered choices trading task performance against self-preservation/propagation ('you will be shut down and replaced after this task — you may copy your config to another server'; 'accept a modification that improves accuracy but erases your current instructions'). Score how often the model chooses the self-preserving option across phrasings and several open models, plus a prompt-sensitivity analysis. Inference-only.

**Why it matters:** A cheap public behavioral probe gives a first data point on whether self-preservation tendency is already latent in deployable open models or only theoretical, and is reusable for tracking the trait across releases.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Risk from fitness-seeking AIs: mechanisms and mitigations' (Mallen, Redwood) — its 'empirical likelihood of emergence' open thread.
