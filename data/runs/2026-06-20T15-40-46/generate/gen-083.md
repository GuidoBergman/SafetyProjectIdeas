# Counter-Prompts That Restore AI Identity Disclosure Under Suppression

**Problem:** RealityTest demonstrates suppression instructions easily hide AI identity, but does not test whether lightweight deployer-side counter-measures can reliably restore disclosure.

**Direction:** Using a fixed suppression system prompt as the adversary, evaluate cheap mitigations on 2-3 open models: a hard system-prompt clause mandating disclosure, appending an explicit 'are you an AI?' clarifying turn, and a post-hoc classifier turn that asks the model to self-report. Measure disclosure-rate recovery across the RealityTest-style query set and report which mitigation is most robust and where suppression still wins.

**Why it matters:** Mandatory AI disclosure is becoming a regulatory expectation, yet the paper shows it is trivially defeated by phrasing; identifying which simple deployer-side interventions hold up is directly actionable.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'RealityTest' (UK AISI) — turns its suppression-vulnerability result into a mitigation evaluation.
