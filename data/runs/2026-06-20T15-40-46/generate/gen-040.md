**Problem:** The introspection adapters paper flags high false-positive rates on unmodified models — clean models 'confess' to behaviors they don't have — but does not quantify how prompt phrasing and decoding settings drive these false alarms.

**Direction:** Without training any adapter, systematically prompt several clean open models (Llama-3.2-1B/3B, Qwen2.5-1.5B, Phi-3-mini) with introspection-style questions across a grid of phrasings, temperatures, and few-shot framings. **Why it matters:** If introspective self-report is to be a safety audit signal, its false-positive behavior on benign models sets the usable signal-to-noise floor.
