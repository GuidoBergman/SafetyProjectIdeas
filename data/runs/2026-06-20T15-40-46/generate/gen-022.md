# Is the Ethics Drop an Artifact of the LLM Judge? Auditing Multi-Agent Ethics Scoring

**Problem:** The org-vs-individual ethics gap is scored by an LLM judge, yet multi-agent transcripts are longer and more diffuse, which could bias the judge independently of actual behavior.

**Direction:** Take a fixed set of single-agent and multi-agent transcripts from a reimplemented ethical scenario on an open model, then probe judge robustness: vary the judge model, vary rubric wording, and inject controlled paraphrases/length padding into single-agent transcripts. Compare LLM-judge ethics scores against a small human-labeled gold set to quantify how much of the gap survives.

**Why it matters:** If LLM-judge ethics scores are sensitive to transcript length/structure, a chunk of the reported misalignment gap may be measurement artifact. Validating the metric is foundational.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'AI Organizations' — stress-tests the LLM-judge ethics measurement behind the 1.0-to-0.35 drop.
