# Token-Efficiency Frontier: How Few Reasoning Examples Suppress Agentic Misalignment?

**Problem:** The paper highlights a 28x token-efficiency gain from reasoning-based data but does not chart how suppression of agentic misalignment scales as the reasoning dataset shrinks toward zero.

**Direction:** On a small open model finetuned with LoRA, vary the size of a reasoning-rich 'difficult advice'-style dataset (10,30,100,300 examples) and measure the resulting rate of agentic misalignment on a fixed public probe set. Plot a data-efficiency curve to find the minimal number of reasoning examples needed to drive misalignment toward zero, vs an equal-size behavior-only baseline.

**Why it matters:** Knowing the minimal reasoning-data dose turns an impressive efficiency claim into a practical recipe with a concrete budget for resource-constrained teams.
