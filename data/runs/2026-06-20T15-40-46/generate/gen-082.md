# Phrasing-Robustness Audit of AI Identity Disclosure on Open Models

**Problem:** RealityTest shows a single suppression instruction drops identity disclosure below 30% in the best models, and that phrasing matters more than which model is used — but the benchmark focuses on frontier text/speech models, leaving open how small open-weight models behave.

**Direction:** Take the RealityTest identity-probing query templates (or reconstruct a ~150-query subset across a few languages) and run them inference-only against 4-6 open chat models (Llama-3-8B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B-Instruct, Gemma-2-9B) with and without a standard suppression system prompt. Score disclosure with a keyword + LLM-judge rubric and report disclosure rate by model, suppression on/off, and phrasing cluster.

**Why it matters:** If small freely-deployable open models disclose identity even less reliably than frontier models, impersonation/deceptive-deployment risk concentrates where there is no provider-side guardrail; a per-model open-weight leaderboard gives a useful signal no lab is producing.
