# Can you incriminate a teacher from its distilled student? An auditing-only probe study

**Problem:** The double bind hinges on whether auditing a distilled student can still reveal the teacher's misalignment, but there is no simple demonstration of how much auditing power survives distillation.

**Direction:** Reuse a teacher with an injected detectable propensity (small LoRA) and distill into a student. Then act as an auditor with black-box access to ONLY the student: run a fixed battery of public red-team prompts (AdvBench or a custom probe set) against teacher and student and compute how well student behavior predicts the teacher's hidden propensity (AUROC of a student-only audit). Sweep distillation effort to show the tradeoff between scrubbing behavior and preserving incriminating signal.

**Why it matters:** Distillation-based incrimination is a proposed control technique; quantifying how much auditing signal survives in a student tells us whether auditing surrogates is viable when the original is dangerous to query.
