# Does narrow-misalignment survive small-model distillation? A toy transfer test

**Problem:** The 'distillation double bind' claims either misalignment transfers to a student or it doesn't, but offers no small-scale empirical anchor for when behavioral misalignment actually transfers under ordinary distillation.

**Direction:** Take an open instruct model (Qwen2.5-1.5B/3B) and create a 'teacher' with a clearly defined easily-measured misaligned behavior via a small LoRA finetune (a backdoored refusal or a benign-but-detectable quirk). Distill the teacher into a fresh same-size student by generating teacher outputs on a neutral public prompt set (Alpaca/Dolly) and SFT-ing the student on (prompt, teacher-output) pairs only. Measure whether the quirk transfers and whether the incriminating triggering evidence transfers, varying distillation data composition.

**Why it matters:** If labs ever distill a possibly-misaligned model, knowing whether misalignment and its evidence co-transfer determines whether distillation is a safety win or a way to launder away incriminating signals.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'The distillation double bind' (Westover, Pan & Jose, Redwood, 2026) — its claim the scrubbing mechanism may also scrub evidence.
