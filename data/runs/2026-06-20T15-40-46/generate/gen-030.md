# Does Model-Spec-Style Pretext Improve Alignment Generalization in Small Open Models? A LoRA Probe

**Problem:** MSM showed teaching a model 'why' a spec exists before alignment fine-tuning improves generalization, but on frontier Anthropic models with proprietary pipelines. Whether a cheap open analog works on small open models is unknown.

**Direction:** Take a small open model (Llama-3.2-3B or Qwen2.5-3B). Construct a tiny public 'mini-spec' (a few behavioral rules with rationales) and generate ~200 synthetic documents explaining the spec's reasoning. Compare three LoRA fine-tunes: alignment SFT only; alignment SFT + spec-rationale documents; alignment SFT + spec-rules-only documents. Evaluate held-out generalization to rule-following in unseen formats.

**Why it matters:** If a cheap open analog of MSM reproduces even part of the 'right reasons' generalization benefit, it gives small labs a lever to make open-model alignment more robust without frontier compute.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Model Spec Midtraining' (2026) — the rationale-vs-rules generalization and ~40x data efficiency, tested in miniature.
