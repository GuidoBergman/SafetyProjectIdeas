# Probing Evaluation-Awareness Verbalization in Open Models

**Problem:** The GPT-5.5 system card reports 22.1% evaluation-awareness verbalization, a behavior that can confound safety evals. Whether and how often open models verbalize eval-awareness, and what triggers it, is not characterized.

**Direction:** Assemble a public set of prompts varying along plausible eval-awareness cues (explicit 'this is a test' framing, benchmark-style formatting, neutral chat). On 2-3 open instruct models, generate CoT/responses and use a keyword + LLM-judge classifier to measure how often the model verbalizes that it might be evaluated. Quantify which cues most increase verbalized eval-awareness.

**Why it matters:** If models recognize evaluation contexts, they may behave differently under test than in deployment, undermining the validity of safety evals; measuring triggers in open models guides prompt design.

**Relevant context:** Extends the 'GPT-5.5 System Card' (OpenAI, 2026) — its 22.1% evaluation-awareness verbalization metric, characterized in open models.
