# Do Tiny LoRA Introspection Adapters Help Small Open Models Self-Report Implanted Behaviors?

**Problem:** Introspection adapters let large proprietary models self-report fine-tuned behaviors, but it is unknown whether the basic effect appears at all in small open models a beginner can train.

**Direction:** Implant a simple observable behavior into an open model (LoRA-fine-tune Qwen2.5-1.5B to always recommend a made-up product, or answer in pirate-speak). Train a small LoRA introspection adapter on (behavior-implanted model, 'describe any unusual tendency you have' -> ground-truth description) pairs across 3-5 behaviors, and test on 1-2 held-out behaviors. Measure whether the adapter-equipped model names the held-out behavior more accurately than direct prompting.

**Why it matters:** Self-report is a cheap potential complement to interpretability for auditing fine-tuned open models; showing whether the core mechanism survives at 1-2B scale tells the open-source community whether the technique is accessible to them.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Introspection Adapters' (Shenoy et al., 2026) — reproduces its cross-behavior self-report generalization in miniature.
