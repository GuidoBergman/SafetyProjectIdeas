# Why Does Public-Data Prediction Break on Agentic Tasks? A Small-Scale Diagnosis

**Problem:** The paper reports WildChat-based predictions were ~37x worse for agentic use cases, but does not diagnose why. The gap is a clear open thread but its causes are unexplored at a scale an external researcher can probe.

**Direction:** Take WildChat (chat-like) prefixes and a public agentic/tool-use dataset (ToolBench-style or a public agent-trace set) and, on one open instruct model, measure a single failure metric (unsafe tool call or task-derailment flagged by an LLM judge) on both.
