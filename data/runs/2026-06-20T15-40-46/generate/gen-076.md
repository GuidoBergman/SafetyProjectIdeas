# A small benchmark: do today's published AI 'safety cases' mention deployment-time spread of misalignment?

**Problem:** Mallen argues risk reports and safety cases neglect how misalignment can spread during deployment, not just whether one model is misaligned. Whether real published safety documents address this is untested.

**Direction:** Assemble a public corpus of ~30-50 AI safety cases, system cards, RSPs, and preparedness documents. Define a 4-5 item rubric for 'deployment-time spread' coverage (mentions self-propagation, agent-to-agent influence, training-data contamination, containment/rollback). Score each document with an LLM-judge plus manual spot-check, then report what fraction address each dimension. Deliverable: a scored coverage table.

**Why it matters:** This turns Mallen's qualitative claim into measurable evidence about the safety-case landscape and gives report authors a checklist, pressuring future safety cases to cover spread.

**Relevant context:** Operationalizes 'Risk reports need to address deployment-time spread of misalignment' (Mallen, Redwood) — its neglected-dimension claim.
