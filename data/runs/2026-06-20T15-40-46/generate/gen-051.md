# Does single-domain beneficial-trait fine-tuning generalize OOD in a small open model?

**Problem:** OpenAI reports beneficial-trait RL confined to the health domain produced cross-domain alignment gains, but used frontier-scale RL; whether the cheaper accessible analogue (small LoRA SFT on one domain) shows any OOD transfer is unknown.

**Direction:** Build a tiny dataset of ~150-300 health-domain dialogues exemplifying one trait (honesty / epistemic humility under pressure), curated from public sources, and LoRA-fine-tune one small open model (a few hours). Evaluate tuned vs base on OOD public alignment benchmarks in unrelated domains (TruthfulQA, a sycophancy set, a small deception probe). Report whether single-domain trait tuning moves OOD alignment metrics at all.

**Why it matters:** If cheap single-domain LoRA tuning yields measurable cross-domain honesty gains, alignment-via-traits becomes accessible far beyond major labs; a null result is equally informative.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Reinforcement learning towards broadly and persistently beneficial models' (Jagadeesh, Heidecke et al., 2026) — its health-only-to-unrelated cross-domain result, downscaled to LoRA SFT.
