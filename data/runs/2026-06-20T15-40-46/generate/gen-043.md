# Filling the ATT&CK Gap: A Public Annotation Schema for Autonomous-AI Cyber Behaviors

**Problem:** The LLM ATT&CK Navigator work found MITRE ATT&CK has no categories for autonomous AI behaviors (agentic killchain orchestration), exactly what distinguishes the highest-risk actors, leaving defenders without a vocabulary to tag these behaviors.

**Direction:** Using only public material (the ATT&CK matrix, published threat-intel reports, public write-ups of agentic-AI misuse), draft a small candidate set of 8-12 'autonomous AI behavior' annotation tags with definitions and positive/negative examples. Validate by hand-labeling a public corpus of described AI-assisted attack narratives and reporting coverage and annotator agreement.

**Why it matters:** Defenders and policymakers can only track a threat they can name; a public ATT&CK-complementary vocabulary is purely defensive, needs no model access, and directly addresses the paper's open thread.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Mapping AI-enabled cyber threats: the LLM ATT&CK Navigator' (Guru et al., 2026) — the gap that ATT&CK lacks autonomous-AI categories.
