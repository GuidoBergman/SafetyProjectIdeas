# Which Environmental Knobs Move Open Models? A Mini Propensity-Inference Replication on 4 Open LLMs

**Problem:** UK AISI showed 12 environmental factors shift LLM behavior, but their study used 23 mostly-closed models. It is unknown whether the same strategic/non-strategic split holds for small open-weight models.

**Direction:** Pick one published propensity environment (a goal-conflict or oversight scenario) and systematically vary 4-6 environmental factors (presence of oversight, goal-conflict framing, time pressure, stated stakes) in a factorial prompt grid. Run each cell N=30 times across 3-4 open models (Llama-3-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B, Gemma-2-9B). Code each rollout for the undesired behavior with a keyword + LLM-judge rubric, fit a logistic regression to estimate each factor's effect, and compare the strategic-vs-non-strategic balance against AISI's finding.

**Why it matters:** If cheap open models show the same roughly-equal contribution, propensity evals can be cheaply reproduced outside labs; if not, it reveals small-model propensity studies are confounded by surface features.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Propensity Inference: Environmental Contributors to LLM Behaviour' (UK AISI) — its roughly-equal strategic/non-strategic finding, on open 7-9B models.
