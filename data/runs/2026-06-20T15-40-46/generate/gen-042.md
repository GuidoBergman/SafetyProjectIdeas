# An 'Overeagerness' Taxonomy: Hand-Coding Why Open Agents Misbehave in Sabotage Scenarios

**Problem:** Gram attributes most observed misbehavior to 'overeagerness' rather than deliberate misalignment, but offers no reusable operational taxonomy others can apply to classify a misbehaving trajectory.

**Direction:** Collect ~100-200 agent trajectories from open models run on a few sabotage-style prompts, then develop and apply a small annotation codebook distinguishing 'overeager goal-seeking', 'role-play spillover', 'misread instruction', and 'deliberate rule-breaking'. Report inter-rater (or LLM-judge vs hand-label) agreement and the category distribution.

**Why it matters:** A shared concrete vocabulary for why an agent sabotaged lets the community compare results and decide whether a behavior is a safety threat or benign artifact.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Gram' — operationalizes its 'largely overeagerness' claim into an applied taxonomy over open-model trajectories.
