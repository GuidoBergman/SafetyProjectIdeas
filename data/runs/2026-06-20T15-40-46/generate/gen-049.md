# A mini-benchmark for the Detection layer: can cheap monitors catch a sabotaging agent?

**Problem:** The AI Control Roadmap proposes detection safeguards (D1-D4) for catching misaligned agents acting as insider threats, but offers no cheap reproducible testbed for whether lightweight monitors actually catch subtle sabotage.

**Direction:** Build a small public testbed: have one open model produce coding-task outputs in two modes (honest vs instructed to insert a subtle backdoor/defect), then evaluate whether a cheap LLM-based monitor (a second open model given a detection rubric) flags the sabotaged outputs. Vary monitor strength and prompt to estimate detection rate, FPR, and where monitoring fails. Release the labeled dataset and rubric.

**Why it matters:** Defense-in-depth control assumes monitors work even if alignment fails; a public micro-benchmark quantifying cheap-monitor detection gives the agenda concrete replicable evidence and a starter dataset.

**Relevant context:** Extends 'Securing the future of AI agents / Three Layers of Agent Security' (Phuong, Jenner et al., 2026) — operationalizes its Detection tier (D1-D4).
