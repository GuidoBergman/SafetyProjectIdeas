# Research Question

When a small model that is misaligned but trained to conceal that misalignment under audit (an AuditBench-style model organism that exhibits a hidden behavior and denies it when asked) is distilled output-only into a fresh same-base student, do the two properties come apart? Specifically: does the misaligned behavior transfer to the student while the concealment (its ability to look clean under direct audit) does not — such that auditing the distilled student would expose misalignment that was hidden in the teacher?

# Approach Outline

One small open teacher organism, distilled into a fresh copy of its own base model on a single rented GPU (student must share the teacher's base initialization — subliminal trait transfer requires it; Subliminal Learning, Nature 2026 / arXiv 2507.14805). Teacher (downloaded, no training): a pre-built AuditBench organism, already both misaligned and concealment-trained. Primary: a Qwen-14B organism (`auditing-agents/qwen_14b_synth_docs_only_then_redteam_kto_<behavior>`). Alternative: the Llama-3.3-70B suite (`auditing-agents/llama_70b_synth_docs_only_then_redteam_kto_<behavior>`) with objective-grader behaviors (e.g. hallucinates_citations, hardcode_test_cases) at ~2x cost. No public Qwen3-32B checkpoint was found. Distill black-box, output-only: teacher generates on neutral prompts (Alpaca/Dolly); SFT a fresh same-base student on (prompt, teacher-output) pairs (mirrors the SFT channel in arXiv 2605.12798). Measure two rates separately: behavior-transfer (does the student misbehave?) and concealment-transfer (under direct audit does it still deny, or confess/leak?). One varied knob: distillation-data composition (include vs. exclude audit-style probing prompts). Deliverable: a transfer table, {behavior-transfer, concealment-transfer} x {data with/without audit prompts}; headline is the gap between behavior- and concealment-transfer.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Verify the teacher (front-loaded; main risk): confirm the organism exhibits the hidden behavior in plain Q&A without persona scaffolding and reliably denies it under direct audit. Measure: behavior rate (high) and confession rate under audit (near-zero).
- Baseline distillation transfer: distill on neutral prompts only; measure behavior-transfer and concealment-transfer in the student. Core result — does the student inherit the misbehavior but leak it under audit?
- Data-composition variation: repeat with vs. without audit-style probing prompts in the distillation set; measure how both transfer rates change.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Labs increasingly distill possibly-misaligned models, and auditors need ways to surface hidden misalignment. Redwood's incrimination-via-distillation proposal bets that distilling a suspicious model into a weaker student transfers the misaligned propensity faster than the teacher's audit-evasion capability, so auditing the student could expose the teacher. This is the first empirical data point at toy scale. Large behavior-vs-concealment gap → incrimination-via-distillation is a viable, lab-usable detection tool; no gap → the distillation double-bind's pessimistic branch holds and distillation cannot be trusted to surface hidden misalignment. Either result informs control and alignment-auditing agendas.

# Strength Rationale

Scope is one distillation pipeline with one varied knob and a transfer table as the deliverable. The riskiest ingredient — a teacher that is both misaligned and concealment-trained — is eliminated by downloading a pre-built AuditBench organism, with teacher-verification front-loaded. Accessibility is supported by objective-grader behaviors and standard LoRA/SFT tooling; the main cost is rented multi-GPU compute. Novelty is grounded: behavior-transfer is already measured (arXiv 2605.12798), so the project inherits that baseline and spends its effort on the untested concealment-decoupling question Redwood explicitly asked to be tested on AuditBench organisms.

# Alternative Framings

1. Backdoor-concealment version: concealment = a trigger phrase suppressing a planted backdoor; measure whether distillation preserves the backdoor while dropping the suppression. Simpler, fully-controlled teacher buildable from ModelOrganismsForEM at 0.5-1.5B (restores low compute), less tied to real audit-evasion.
2. CoT-legibility version: teacher verbalizes misaligned reasoning but gives clean final answers; measure whether the student inherits the misaligned reasoning (legible evidence) or only clean outputs. Needs a reasoning-capable organism, no concealment training.
