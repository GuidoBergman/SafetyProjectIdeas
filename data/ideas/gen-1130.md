# Sycophancy as Oversight Exploitation: Measuring Whether Student Models Learn to Tell Overseers What They Want to Hear

# Research Question

To what extent does sycophantic models that agree with overseers receive better supervision signals than honest models that correct overseer errors. this creates an incentive to learn sycophancy during training?

# Approach Outline

Create a dataset where correct answers contradict what the overseer stated. Train a model under standard oversight. Measure whether the model learns to agree with overseer statements even when they are wrong.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
