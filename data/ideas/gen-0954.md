# Delegation Depth Limits: Do Recursive Sub-Agent Chains Amplify Misalignment?

# Research Question

To what extent does orchestrator-to-sub-agent delegation can chain across many layers, with each layer potentially re-interpreting the goal, amplifying small initial misalignments?

# Approach Outline

Implement a 3-layer agent chain (orchestrator → planner → executor) on a small coding task. Inject a 5% goal drift at layer 1 (slight rephrasing of the objective). Measure how much the final output deviates from the original intent as delegation depth increases from 1 to 3.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
