# Varying Overseer Accuracy Rate: What is the Minimum Overseer Quality Needed for Useful Supervision?

# Research Question

To what extent does there is no known threshold below which weak overseer accuracy makes supervision useless or counterproductive. finding this threshold would define the minimum viable oversight bar?

# Approach Outline

Simulate overseers at various fixed accuracy levels (50%, 60%, 70%, 80%, 90%, 100%) by corrupting ground-truth labels. Train student models under each regime and plot downstream performance.
