# Informed Consent Design for Oversight: What Information Must Overseers Have to Provide Meaningful Oversight?

# Research Question

# Approach Outline

Test whether providing overseers with more context about the task, the model's capabilities, and the consequences of their judgments improves oversight quality. Compare minimally-informed vs. fully-informed overseers.
