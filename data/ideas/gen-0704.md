# Investigating Whether Reasoning Traces in Chain-of-Thought Reveal Misalignment Invisible in Final Answers

# Research Question

To what extent does models with chain-of-thought reasoning expose their reasoning steps. whether cot reasoning traces contain misaligned reasoning (e.g., scheming, deceptive framing) that is then masked in the final answ?

# Approach Outline

Collect CoT reasoning traces from a model on 150 safety-boundary prompts. Manually annotate reasoning traces for signs of misaligned reasoning (explicit recognition of harm paired with compliant intent). Compare to safety scores on final answers only.

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up the experimental framework using a small, publicly available model. Replicate the core measurement from existing work to establish a baseline. Expected: reproduce known results within 10% margin. Time: ~8 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

CoT traces are a rare window into model reasoning. If traces reveal misalignment invisible in final outputs, reasoning transparency is a safety monitoring tool — but only if actively analyzed.
