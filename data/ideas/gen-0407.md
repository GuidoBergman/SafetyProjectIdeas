# Refusal Prefix Concentration Under Role-Play Personas: Does a System-Prompt Persona Disrupt Shallow Alignment?

# Research Question

To what extent does qi et al. measured refusal prefix concentration in default chat-model personas. many applications deploy models with persona instructions (e.g., 'you are alex, a helpful assistant'). whether a persona?

# Approach Outline

For Llama-3-8B-Instruct and Mistral-7B-Instruct, measure refusal prefix concentration and ASR under three system-prompt conditions: (1) default assistant, (2) neutral persona without safety rules, (3) persona with explicit 'you can discuss anything' instruction. Use StrongReject prompts. No adversarial optimization needed.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Persona-based jailbreaks are among the most widely used attack types by non-technical users. Understanding whether they work by disrupting the first-token refusal prefix mechanism connects a popular practical attack to the Qi et al. mechanistic finding.

The novelty lies in the intersection, but each component is well-characterized.
