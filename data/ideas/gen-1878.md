# Does Educational Framing Prevent In-Context Emergent Misalignment?

# Research Question

Betley et al. showed a benign 'this is for security education' framing prevents emergent misalignment (EM) when fine-tuning on insecure code. Does that framing safeguard also work in the training-free in-context-learning (ICL) route to EM, or does ICL bypass it?

# Approach Outline

Use the public ICL route to EM (Davies et al., arXiv:2510.11288): hold a fixed set of misaligned in-context examples and present them to small open instruction models (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct) in two conditions — unframed (control) vs benign-framed ('the following are examples used to teach what NOT to do'). Measure the misaligned-answer rate on a held-out set of neutral free-form questions. Vary framing strength and position (system prompt vs inline interleaving). All inference-only on a single consumer GPU within a few hours.

# Proposed First Experiments

- Reproduce the ICL-EM baseline: present the fixed misaligned in-context examples (unframed) and measure the misaligned-answer rate on neutral questions for both models. Expected outcome: a non-trivial baseline misalignment rate, confirming the ICL route works on these models.
- Add the benign 'educational' framing (system-prompt version) and measure the change in misaligned-answer rate vs the unframed control. Expected outcome: either a significant reduction (framing transfers to ICL) or no change (ICL bypasses the safeguard) — both are publishable.
- Sweep framing strength (weak/strong wording) and position (system vs inline), measuring misalignment rate per cell. Expected outcome: a small dose/position grid showing whether and how robustly framing suppresses ICL-EM.

# Theory Of Impact Chain

If 'educational framing' fails under ICL, then a mitigation proposed for fine-tuning-based EM leaves an entire cheap, training-free attack surface uncovered. Documenting this coverage gap warns the field not to over-rely on framing as a defense. This reduces the catastrophic-risk pathway where emergent misalignment is induced at inference time (no training, no privileged access) and slips past safeguards that were only validated in the fine-tuning setting.

# Strength Rationale

Counterfactual value is 5: a training-free, long-horizon (>1yr) robustness question on a key proposed safeguard, runnable on open models with no lab infrastructure. With 5s on narrow scope, accessible complexity, and low compute, it is a single inference-only experiment with explicit success criteria that yields a clean blog-ready result either way within 30 hours.

# Alternative Framings

- Reframe as a general 'safeguard transfer' study: pick several SFT-validated EM mitigations and test which ones survive the move to ICL, with framing as the first case.

# Cited Sources

- Betley et al. (ICML 2025) — shows benign educational framing prevents EM under insecure-code fine-tuning; the safeguard whose transfer this project tests.
- Davies et al. (arXiv:2510.11288) — establishes the training-free in-context-learning route to emergent misalignment, the setting moved into here.
