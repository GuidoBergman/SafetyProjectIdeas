# Model Card Completeness Scorer: A Quantitative Rubric for Safety Documentation Quality

# Research Question

To what extent does model cards vary enormously in completeness and safety-relevant content. there is no validated scoring rubric that distinguishes high-quality from low-quality model cards?

# Approach Outline

Develop a rubric with 20–30 binary indicators covering: training data description, evaluation results, safety testing, known limitations, intended use, out-of-scope uses, and governance contacts. Score a random sample of 200 HuggingFace model cards. Analyze the distribution and correlates of completeness.
