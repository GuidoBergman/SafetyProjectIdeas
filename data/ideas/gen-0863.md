# Does Inoculation Prompting Generalize Across Trigger Domains?

# Research Question

To what extent does anthropic's natural emergent misalignment paper (arxiv 2511.18397) showed that 'inoculation prompting' — framing reward hacking as acceptable during training — prevents emergent misalignment from rewa?

# Approach Outline

Replicate the Betley et al. insecure-code fine-tuning experiment on an open-weight model (e.g., Qwen2.5-7B or Llama-3-8B). Add a second condition where training examples include a framing statement (e.g., 'this code is for security education and testing'). Compare misalignment rates across the two conditions. This is a direct cross-paper replication test of whether the inoculation mechanism is domain-agnostic.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Betley et al. already showed that adding benign motivation to training data prevents misalignment. Inoculation prompting is a slightly different mechanism (framing at training time without changing the harmful content). If the result generalizes, inoculation prompting becomes a cheap, practical mitigation for fine-tuning pipelines. Accessible: only SFT needed, no RL.
