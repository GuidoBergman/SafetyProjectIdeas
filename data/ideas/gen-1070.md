# Layer Importance Stability Benchmark: Do Activation Patching Layer Rankings Replicate Across Dataset Versions?

# Research Question

To what extent does a 2025 reproducibility study showed that activation patching results strongly depend on how the diagnostic dataset is constructed. no benchmark quantifies how stable layer importance rankings are when?

# Approach Outline

Take the IOI dataset and produce three variants: original, with names replaced, and with sentence order reversed. Run activation patching on GPT-2-Small for all three variants. Compute Kendall's tau rank correlation for layer-level importance rankings across variants. Report a stability score and identify which layers are robustly important versus fragile. Publish the three dataset variants and the scoring script.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Activation patching is the most commonly used technique for circuit localization. If layer importance rankings are unstable across minor dataset variants, mechanistic claims in safety-relevant papers may be artifacts of dataset choice.
