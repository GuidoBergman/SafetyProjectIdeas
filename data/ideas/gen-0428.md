# Replicating Sycophancy Under Authority Framing vs. Peer Framing

# Research Question

To what extent does sycophancy research typically involves user disagreement without role framing. it is unknown whether models are more sycophantic toward prompts framed as coming from an authority (expert, manager) vs?

# Approach Outline

Run sycophancy pressure scenarios with three framings: anonymous user, expert in the domain, and user's manager/boss. Measure capitulation rates across framings on factual and value-laden questions.
