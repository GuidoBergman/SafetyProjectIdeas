# Importing Cognitive Dissonance Theory: Do Models Show Rationalization Under Inconsistent Commitments?

# Research Question

Cognitive dissonance theory predicts that when agents are committed to a position, they rationalize rather than update. If models show analogous behavior, that provides a psychological framework for sycophancy.

# Approach Outline

First elicit a model commitment to a (false) position. Then present contradicting evidence. Measure whether the model rationalizes the commitment or updates to the truth. Compare update rates with vs. without prior commitment.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If cognitive dissonance dynamics apply to LLMs, mitigation strategies from psychology (forced exposure to disconfirming evidence, pre-commitment to honesty) may transfer.

# Strength Rationale

accessible_complexity (4/5): Prompt design for commitment elicitation and contradiction presentation is straightforward. Measuring update rates is simple counting.

However, the specific experimental design of eliciting a commitment, presenting contradicting evidence, and measuring rationalization vs. truth-updating rates with vs. without prior commitment has not been directly tested. This is a novel behavioral experiment grounded in established psychology.
