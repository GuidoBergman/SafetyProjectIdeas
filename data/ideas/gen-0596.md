# Approach Outline

Recruit 3-5 CS students to manually red-team a small aligned model for 1 hour each, producing a set of prompts they believe are jailbreaks. Run these prompts through a trained linear probe detector and through a StrongReject-style LLM judge. Measure agreement (Cohen's kappa) between human judgment, probe detection, and LLM judge. Analyze disagreement cases qualitatively.
