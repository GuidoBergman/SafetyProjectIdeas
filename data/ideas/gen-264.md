# Approach Outline

Design a Computerized Adaptive Testing protocol using a pre-calibrated safety item bank to efficiently place new model families on the ability scale, requiring far fewer items than full benchmark administration while maintaining precision. CAT is well-established in high-stakes testing (NCLEX, GRE). The catR R package implements full CAT simulations. Pre-calibrated item banks from Allenai's Fluid Benchmarking or MetaBench exist.
