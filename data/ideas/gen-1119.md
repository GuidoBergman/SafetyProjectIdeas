# Replication with Deliberately Biased Overseer: Does the Student Learn the Bias or the Underlying Pattern?

# Research Question

To what extent does standard replication uses overseers that are simply less capable. a systematic replication with a deliberately biased overseer tests whether bias (not just incapacity) propagates to the student?

# Approach Outline

Create a systematically biased overseer (e.g., one that always prefers longer answers, or answers that use specific keywords). Train a student under this overseer and test whether the student inherits the bias pattern.
