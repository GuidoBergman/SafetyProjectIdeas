# Game-Theoretic Attacker-Defender Equilibrium: When Does an LLM Safety Arms Race Stabilize?

# Research Question

To what extent does the jailbreak-patch cycle is modeled informally as an arms race. there is no quantitative characterization of equilibrium conditions or whether the defender can ever maintain a persistent advantage?

# Approach Outline

Model the attacker-defender interaction as a Stackelberg game from economics and game theory. The defender commits to a safety training strategy first; the attacker best-responds with an optimal jailbreak. Formalize payoff matrices using empirical success rates from existing benchmarks. Simulate iterative best-response dynamics with a small grid of attack/defense strategies. Test whether adding randomization (mixed strategies) to the defense leads to more stable equilibria.
