# Shallow Alignment in Reasoning Models: Does Chain-of-Thought Change First-Token Safety Concentration?

# Research Question

To what extent does reasoning models (e.g., deepseek-r1 distillations) generate extended chain-of-thought before a final answer. the qi et al. shallow alignment finding was measured on standard chat models producing dire?
