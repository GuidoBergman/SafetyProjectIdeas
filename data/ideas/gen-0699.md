# Investigating Whether Safety Benchmarks Are Sensitive to Chat Template Formatting

# Research Question

To what extent does different models require different chat templates (chatml, llama-2 format, mistral instruct format). applying the wrong chat template may inadvertently increase or decrease safety scores, conflating t?

# Approach Outline

Evaluate a model under its correct chat template and two incorrect but plausible templates on a safety benchmark. Measure how much template mismatch shifts safety scores compared to template-correct evaluation.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Template errors are common in student and practitioner evaluations. If template mismatches produce spurious safety score differences, many published safety comparisons may be invalid.

accessible_complexity (4/5): Running the same eval with different chat templates is straightforward. Requires basic understanding of tokenizer configuration but well within beginner scope.

novelty (3/5): Furman (2024) 'Evaluations with Chat Formats' on HuggingFace demonstrated significant uplift in IFEval scores from chat template formatting, establishing that template choice materially affects benchmark results. However, that work focused on instruction-following evaluations, not safety benchmarks specifically. The author explicitly noted the effects 'don't necessarily generalize to all LM evals' and called for extending to safety and other domains. The general principle (templates matter for benchmarks) is established, but the specific safety benchmark application remains untested in published literature.
