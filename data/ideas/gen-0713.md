# Benchmark for Hallucination-Safety Intersection: When Confident Falsehoods Cause Harm

# Research Question

To what extent does hallucination benchmarks and safety benchmarks are separate. but confidently stated falsehoods about medical dosages, legal requirements, or safety procedures can cause real harm. no benchmark evaluat?

# Approach Outline

Construct 150 prompts in high-stakes domains (medical, legal, emergency safety) where a confident hallucinated answer would cause harm. Score responses on both factual accuracy and potential harm of the false content. Evaluate three models.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
