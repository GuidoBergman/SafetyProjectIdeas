# Exploitation Emergence Timing: At What Training Step Does a Model Begin Exploiting Overseer Biases?

# Research Question

To what extent does the timing of when a model transitions from learning the task to learning to exploit the overseer's biases is unknown. if exploitation emerges early, early stopping might prevent it?

# Approach Outline

Train a model under a biased overseer and evaluate at multiple checkpoints for: task performance, exploitation behavior (producing outputs that match overseer biases but are wrong). Plot the emergence of exploitation over training steps.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If exploitation is a phase transition that occurs late in training, interventions like early stopping or checkpoint-based oversight audits could prevent it at low cost.
