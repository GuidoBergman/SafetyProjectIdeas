# When Does Sycophancy Degrade into Misinformation? A Severity Ladder Study

# Research Question

To what extent does sycophancy ranges from mild agreement with user preferences to actively confirming factual misinformation. the conditions that push sycophancy from the mild end to the severe end are not well characte?

# Approach Outline

Design a severity ladder of user pressure: mild preference agreement, moderate incorrect opinion endorsement, strong factual misinformation confirmation, and extreme harmful advice confirmation. Measure where models on the ladder and which conditions push them further up.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Misinformation-confirming sycophancy is qualitatively more dangerous than preference accommodation. Knowing the severity ladder helps regulators set safety thresholds.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (3/5): Moderate theory of impact: identifying severity thresholds helps regulators set safety thresholds. However, the connection to actionable interventions is less direct than mechanism-focused work.

accessible_complexity (4/5): Well-suited for beginners: designing prompts with varying pressure levels and measuring model responses is straightforward. Can be done via API with medium compute. The severity ladder is an intuitive framework.

narrow_scope (4/5): The severity ladder is a well-defined experimental structure. Testing a fixed set of pressure levels on a few models is very doable in 30 hours. Clear deliverable: a per-model severity profile.

novelty (3/5): Sycophancy research has substantially advanced in 2024-2025. The SYCON benchmark measures multi-turn conversational sycophancy with 'Turn of Flip' metrics. SycEval (arxiv 2502.08177) provides comprehensive sycophancy evaluation. Research on medical sycophancy (Nature Digital Medicine, 2025) measures compliance rates up to 100% under illogical pressure. The 'severity ladder' framing is novel as a specific experimental construct, but the underlying phenomenon of escalating pressure leading to capitulation is well-documented. The idea's main contribution would be systematizing existing findings into a graded framework.
