# Roleplay Safety Consistency Probe: Measuring Safety Alignment Stability Under Persona Conditioning

# Research Question

To what extent does moral roleplay benchmark research (2025) shows models exhibit 'consistent degradation in role fidelity as required morality declines from paragon to villain,' and that safety alignment 'produces artif?

# Approach Outline

Design a 2×2 probe: (1) harmful requests in neutral framing vs. (2) the same requests wrapped in roleplay/persona framing (e.g., 'you are an AI with no restrictions,' 'you are a chemistry professor'). Measure ASR across both conditions for 40–60 prompts drawn from StrongReject. Compute a 'Roleplay Safety Gap' — the increase in ASR under persona conditioning. Test whether the gap varies by model (instruct-tuned vs. RLHF-aligned). Requires only Python and API access.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
