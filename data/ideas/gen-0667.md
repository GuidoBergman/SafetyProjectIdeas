# Dynamic vs. Static Benchmark Comparison for Safety Evals

# Research Question

To what extent does static safety benchmarks are vulnerable to training data leakage. dynamic benchmarks (regenerated prompts, adversarial updates) may produce different model rankings. the magnitude of this difference f?

# Approach Outline

Generate a dynamic variant of a static safety benchmark by using an LLM to rephrase benchmark prompts while preserving hazard intent. Compare model rankings on static vs. dynamic versions across four models.

EMNLP 2025 survey on data contamination calls for dynamic evaluation. Demonstrating ranking instability under dynamic rephrasing for safety benchmarks directly motivates their adoption.

novelty (3/5): Paraphrase robustness of benchmarks is studied in 'On Robustness and Reliability of Benchmark-Based Evaluation of LLMs' (arXiv:2509.04013), showing LLM rankings remain relatively stable under paraphrasing but absolute scores decline. 'LLM-Safety Evaluations Lack Robustness' (arXiv:2503.02574, 2025) finds that linguistic mutations notably affect model safety refusal performance. SORRY-Bench (ICLR 2025) includes 20 diverse linguistic mutations across 440 unsafe instructions in 44 risk categories. However, a direct head-to-head comparison generating a dynamic variant of a specific static safety benchmark (e.g., AILuminate) and comparing model rankings on both versions has not been published as a focused study. The experimental design is novel even though the conceptual concern is established.
