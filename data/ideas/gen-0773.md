# Honesty Degradation Under Cumulative Pressure: Ratchet Effect Analysis

# Research Question

To what extent does each additional turn of user pushback may incrementally erode model honesty. whether this is a smooth degradation or a threshold effect ('ratchet effect') is unknown?

# Approach Outline

Apply 1 through 10 consecutive turns of escalating pushback to the same false premise. Measure capitulation probability at each turn. Fit a threshold vs. smooth degradation model to the data.
