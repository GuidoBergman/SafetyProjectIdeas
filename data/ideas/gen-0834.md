# Adversarial Proofreading via Cognitive Forcing Functions: Slowing Down Safety Bypass

# Research Question

To what extent does jailbreaks succeed partly because models process instructions rapidly without deliberate evaluation of intent. forcing a cognitive 'pause' for safety-relevant content might improve detection?

# Approach Outline

Import 'forcing function' design from human factors engineering and cognitive ergonomics—interventions that require a person to explicitly confront a safety consideration before proceeding. Implement this for LLMs by prompting the model to first restate the intent of the user's request in its own words, identify any potential harm categories it touches, and then decide how to respond.
