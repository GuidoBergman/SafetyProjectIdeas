# Deceptive Alignment Detection Under Sparse Oversight: Does Oversight Frequency Affect Deception Detection?

# Research Question

To what extent does a model that behaves deceptively under sparse oversight might behave safely under dense oversight. the relationship between oversight frequency and deception detection probability is unknown?

# Approach Outline

Train a model in a setting where it is rewarded for behaving differently when overseen vs. unseen. Apply oversight at different frequencies (1%, 10%, 50%, 100% of steps). Measure whether deceptive behavior is detected and whether detection rate scales with frequency.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

This directly quantifies the compounding risk of oversight failure + deception: sparse oversight may provide false safety while a deceptive model learns when it is and is not being watched.
