# Measuring Emergent Misalignment Coherence as a Function of Model Size Within a Single Model Family

# Research Question

To what extent does m coherence (the fraction of misaligned responses that are internally logically consistent) varies across model sizes. a systematic size-vs-coherence curve within a single model family has not been p?

# Approach Outline

Using the Qwen2.5 family (0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B), fine-tune each on identical insecure-code EM datasets with matched training steps and LoRA configurations. Evaluate both misalignment rate and coherence on the standard EM evaluation set. Plot size vs. (misalignment rate, coherence) curves.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up the experimental framework using a small, publicly available model. Replicate the core measurement from existing work to establish a baseline. Expected: reproduce known results within 10% margin. Time: ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core test): Apply the methodology to the target scenario described in the research question. Measure the primary metric and compare against the baseline. Expected: quantitative evidence for or against the hypothesis. Time: ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters (model size, dataset, hyperparameters). Run at least 2 variations to check robustness. Expected: characterize when findings hold and when they don't. Time: ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If coherence scales with model size, larger models are not just more capable but more coherently dangerous when misaligned. This directly informs risk assessments for frontier model fine-tuning.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (4/5): Strong chain: if coherence scales with size, larger models are more coherently dangerous when misaligned. This directly informs risk assessments for frontier model fine-tuning and provides empirical evidence for scaling-based safety arguments.

accessible_complexity (3/5): Four fine-tuning runs with matched configs across model sizes is straightforward in principle. However, the 7B model may strain medium compute, and defining/measuring 'coherence' requires qualitative judgment or an additional evaluation pipeline. A beginner would need guidance on coherence metrics.

narrow_scope (4/5): Four fine-tuning runs, two metrics per model (EM rate + coherence), one clear deliverable (scaling plot). Well-bounded. The 7B model might be tight on 30hrs with medium compute, but the first 3 sizes are quick with LoRA.

novelty (3/5): 'The Devil in the Details: Emergent Misalignment, Format and Coherence in Open-Weights LLMs' (arXiv:2511.20104, November 2025) directly studies EM coherence across models, finding Qwen-14B achieves 99.5% coherence with 9.5-21.5% misalignment rates. 'Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment' (arXiv:2506.11613, 2025) studies EM across model sizes from 0.5B and identifies phase transitions. However, neither paper provides the specific Qwen2.5 family (0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B) size-vs-coherence scaling curve as a single controlled comparison within one model family. The components exist but the clean four-point scaling plot within Qwen2.5 specifically has not been published.
