# Superposition Analysis of Gemma-2-2B Using SAELens: Do Smaller Gemma Models Show More Superposition?

# Research Question

To what extent does superposition — the encoding of more features than neurons — was characterized primarily in toy models and early gpt-family models. the gemma family spans 2b to 27b parameters and has publicly availab?

# Approach Outline

Use SAELens with the pre-trained Gemma Scope SAEs on Gemma-2-2B and Gemma-2-9B. Measure SAE feature density (L0), dead feature fraction, and feature interpretability scores across layers. Compare superposition signatures (fraction of neurons encoding multiple features) across model sizes to test whether larger models escape superposition more or push it into different layers.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Superposition is a fundamental obstacle to interpretability: if features are packed into neurons, reading out model internals becomes exponentially harder. Understanding how superposition scales will clarify whether frontier models are becoming more or less interpretable as they grow, directly informing where SAE training resources should be prioritized.
