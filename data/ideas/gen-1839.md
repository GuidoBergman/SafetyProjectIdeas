# Minimal Framing Dose for Preventing Emergent Misalignment

# Research Question

To what extent does betley et al. (2025) showed that adding a benign motivational framing to insecure-code fine-tuning data prevented emergent misalignment, but the minimum amount of framing required is unknown?

# Approach Outline

Systematically vary the proportion of framing-annotated samples in a fine-tuning dataset (0%, 1%, 5%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 100%) and measure at what threshold emergent misalignment is suppressed. Use a small open-source model (e.g., Qwen2.5-7B) and the original insecure-code fine-tuning task. Test with a fixed evaluation suite of alignment probes.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.
