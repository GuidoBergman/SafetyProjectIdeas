# SAE Feature Monosemanticity in CLIP ViT vs Text Transformer: Replicating Vision-Language SAE Differences

# Research Question

To what extent does the prisma toolkit (2025) found that clip vits have substantially lower sparsity patterns in sae representations compared to language models, suggesting fundamental differences in how visual and lingu?

# Approach Outline

Use Prisma/ViT-Prisma with pre-trained SAE weights for CLIP ViT-B/32 and CLIP ViT-L/14. Compare L0 sparsity, dead feature fraction, and the fraction of monosemantic (clearly interpretable) features across layers. As a twist, also run the same analysis on a text-only ViT (e.g., DINO ViT) to isolate whether low sparsity is due to the vision modality or the vision-language contrastive training objective.

# Theory of Impact Chain

If vision features are inherently less sparse, it means SAE-based interpretability tools developed for language models may need fundamental redesign for vision systems. As AI systems become increasingly multimodal, understanding modality-specific feature encoding is essential for extending interpretability tools beyond text.
