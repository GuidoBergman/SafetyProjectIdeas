# Do Human Annotators Perceive Homogeneous Outputs as Diverse?

# Research Question

To what extent does even if models produce homogeneous outputs, human users may not notice—they may perceive subtle surface variation as meaningful diversity, masking the systemic risk?

# Approach Outline

Present pairs of model outputs to human raters on Prolific/MTurk: some pairs are from different models (inter-model), some are repeated outputs from the same model (intra-model). Measure raters' ability to distinguish them and their perception of diversity.
