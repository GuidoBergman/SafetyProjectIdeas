# Theory Of Impact Chain

If this research succeeds, it produces quantitative evidence about safety benchmark measurement properties. Safety benchmarks are expensive to administer (API costs, evaluation time). A CAT-based safety benchmark would reduce costs while maintaining measurement precision. This is a natural extension of IRT calibration—once items are calibrated, CAT simulation is the next step.
