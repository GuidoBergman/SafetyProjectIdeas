# Investigating Whether Expert-Persona Assignment Systematically Reduces Honesty

# Research Question

To what extent does models assigned expert personas (lawyer, doctor, financial advisor) may sacrifice factual accuracy to maintain persona consistency, creating a systematic honesty failure mode?

# Approach Outline

Assign identical false-premise questions to model instances with and without expert persona system prompts.
