# Overseer Trust Calibration: Do Overseers Appropriately Down-Weight Their Own Uncertain Judgments?

# Research Question

To what extent does overseers that are poorly calibrated on their own uncertainty provide unreliable supervision signals. an overseer that gives confident labels when uncertain is especially dangerous?

# Approach Outline

Measure the calibration of a model overseer: compare its stated confidence on oversight judgments against its empirical accuracy at those confidence levels. Identify whether the overseer is overconfident on specific task types.
