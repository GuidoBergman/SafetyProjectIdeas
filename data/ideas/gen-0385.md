# First-Token Concentration in SFT-Only vs. RLHF-Tuned Variants of the Same Base Model

# Research Question

The Qi et al. shallow-alignment finding was demonstrated on RLHF-aligned chat models. It is not known whether the shortcut arises specifically from RLHF/DPO or whether instruction fine-tuning alone produces the same rigid refusal-prefix concentration.

# Approach Outline

Using a model family that releases both an SFT-only and a DPO/RLHF variant (e.g., Mistral-7B-Instruct v0.1 vs. v0.3), measure refusal-prefix concentration and prefilling vulnerability for both. Plot the alignment depth curve for each variant and compare.

# Proposed First Experiments

Experiment 1 (Baseline): Set up experimental framework with a small public model. Replicate core measurement to establish baseline. ~8 hours.
Experiment 2 (Core): Apply methodology to target scenario. Measure primary metric vs baseline. ~12 hours.
Experiment 3 (Robustness): Test sensitivity to key parameters with 2+ variations. ~10 hours.

# Theory of Impact Chain

Pinpointing whether RLHF specifically causes shallow alignment would help alignment researchers design training procedures that produce deeper, more robust safety.

# Strength Rationale

theory_of_impact (4/5): Strong chain: pinpointing whether RLHF specifically causes shallow alignment directly informs how to design training procedures that produce deeper safety.

accessible_complexity (4/5): Inference-only comparison on two model checkpoints. Measuring refusal prefix concentration and plotting alignment depth curves is accessible for beginners.

narrow_scope (5/5): Tightly scoped: same measurement on two model variants, one direct comparison. Minimal ambiguity, very feasible in 30 hours.

novelty (4/5): While the shallow alignment paper describes mechanisms for both SFT and RLHF, no published work directly compares alignment depth between SFT-only and RLHF-tuned variants of the same base model. The Qi et al. paper hypothesizes that RLHF may exacerbate shallow alignment but does not test this empirically. This comparison fills a clear gap.
