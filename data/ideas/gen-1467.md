# Multilingual Jailbreak Depth: Is Safety Alignment Shallower in Low-Resource Languages?

# Research Question

To what extent does safety alignment data is predominantly english. it is unknown whether the first-token shallow alignment shortcut is more pronounced in non-english languages where the model has seen less safety traini?

# Approach Outline

Replicate the Qi et al. logit-lens alignment depth measurement in English, Spanish, French, Chinese, and Arabic on Llama-3-8B-Instruct (which has multilingual capability). Use the same set of harmful prompts translated by a standard MT system. Compare alignment depth scores across languages.
