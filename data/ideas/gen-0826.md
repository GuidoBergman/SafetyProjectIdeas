# Tolerance Intervals from Clinical Trials: Setting Statistically Justified Safety Thresholds

# Research Question

To what extent does safety thresholds (e.g., 'model should refuse x% of harmful prompts') are set arbitrarily. there is no principled statistical methodology for choosing thresholds or quantifying uncertainty around safe?

# Approach Outline

Import tolerance interval methodology from clinical trial statistics. A tolerance interval gives a range that covers at least a given proportion of the population with a given confidence level—exactly what is needed for safety certification claims. Run a safety probe battery on a model, compute tolerance intervals on refusal rates for each harm category, and use these to make statistically grounded claims like 'with 95% confidence, at least 99% of prompts in category X are refused'. Compare tolerance interval widths across models and harm categories to identify where more evaluation data is needed.
