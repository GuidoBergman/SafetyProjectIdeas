# Probing Whether Activation-Based Jailbreak Detectors Generalize Across Attack Types

# Research Question

# Theory of Impact Chain

If activation probes are attack-type-specific, defenders need separate probes per attack class—a major deployment cost. If they generalize, a single cheap probe may suffice. This directly affects the viability of activation-based defenses.
