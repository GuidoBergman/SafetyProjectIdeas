# Category 3: Key AI Safety Organizations and Their Focus Areas

**Date:** 2026-03-19
**Research Type:** Landscape Research
**Status:** Complete

---

## 1. Alignment Research Center (ARC)

- **URL:** https://www.alignment.org/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Formal mechanistic explanations of neural network behavior; combining mechanistic interpretability with formal verification; "Eliciting Latent Knowledge" (ELK); formal heuristic arguments framework
- **Key Researchers:** Paul Christiano (Founder), Mark Xu, Jacob Hilton
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Paper on "Formalizing the presumption of independence"; ongoing theoretical work on alignment strategies scalable to future ML systems; mentorship stream at MATS Summer 2026

---

## 2. MIRI (Machine Intelligence Research Institute)

- **URL:** https://intelligence.org/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Technical governance and policy advocacy (post-2024 pivot); international coordination to halt development toward superintelligence; compute restrictions and verification protocols; communications/outreach
- **Key Researchers:** Nate Soares, Eliezer Yudkowsky
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Book "If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies" (NYT bestseller, 2025); draft international agreement framework for superintelligence governance; 2025 fundraiser for expanded 2026 efforts; planned technical governance research fellowships

---

## 3. Anthropic

- **URL:** https://www.anthropic.com/research
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Mechanistic interpretability; scalable oversight; adversarial robustness and AI control; model organisms of misalignment; alignment faking detection; AI security; model welfare; process-oriented learning
- **Key Researchers:** Dario Amodei (CEO), Chris Olah (Interpretability Research Lead), Jan Leike (Alignment Science co-lead), Daniela Amodei, Sam Bowman (on leave from NYU)
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Attribution graphs for tracing model reasoning; alignment audits; deceptive alignment detection research; Anthropic Fellows Program (May/July 2026 cohorts); "Recommended Directions for Technical AI Safety Research" (2025); Alignment Science Blog

---

## 4. Google DeepMind

- **URL:** https://deepmind.google/responsibility-and-safety/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Frontier Safety Framework (evaluations for autonomy, biosecurity, cybersecurity, ML R&D risks); mechanistic interpretability; scalable oversight; chain-of-thought monitoring; socioaffective alignment; dangerous capability evaluations
- **Key Researchers:** Shane Legg (Co-Founder, Chief AGI Scientist, executive sponsor), Rohin Shah (AGI Safety & Alignment team lead), Anca Dragan, Allan Dafoe, Four Flynn
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** "An Approach to Technical AGI Safety and Security" paper (April 2025); expanded partnership with UK AISI on CoT monitoring and social/emotional AI impacts; AGI Safety & Alignment team growing ~37-39% annually

---

## 5. OpenAI Safety

- **URL:** https://openai.com/safety/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Preparedness Framework (long-range autonomy, sandbagging, autonomous replication/adaptation, undermining safeguards, nuclear/radiological risks); alignment (human preference alignment); fairness & representation; robustness
- **Key Researchers:** (Team in flux) Boaz Barak (part-time, also at Harvard); Safety Advisory Group (SAG). Note: Miles Brundage, Lilian Weng, Aleksander Madry all departed 2024-2025
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Updated Preparedness Framework (2025) with Safety Advisory Group and Safety Systems Committee; model system cards for o3, o4-mini, GPT-4o, GPT-4.5 (with METR); Researcher Access Program for external safety research

---

## 6. Center for AI Safety (CAIS)

- **URL:** https://safe.ai/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Societal-scale AI risk mitigation; foundational benchmarks and methods for technical safety challenges; ML safety research; machine ethics; robustness; compute cluster support for ~20 external research labs
- **Key Researchers:** Dan Hendrycks (Director/Co-founder), Oliver Zhang (Co-founder)
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** "Humanity's Last Exam" benchmark (NYT coverage, Jan 2025); SafeBench competition (Cybench won first place); Dan Hendrycks op-ed against Manhattan Project for AI (The Economist, March 2025)

---

## 7. Redwood Research

- **URL:** https://www.redwoodresearch.org/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** AI control (monitoring protocols for potentially misaligned LLM agents); adversarial robustness; interpretability methods for current and future AI systems; alignment faking detection
- **Key Researchers:** Ryan Greenblatt, Evan Hubinger (evhub), Buck Shlegeris, Carson Denison, Fabien Roger, Sam Marks
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** "Ctrl-Z: Controlling AI Agents via Resampling" (2025) extending AI control to agentic settings; collaboration with Anthropic demonstrating alignment faking in Claude; AI control safety case sketch with UK AISI (2025); AI Control Hackathon with Apart Research

---

## 8. Apollo Research

- **URL:** https://www.apolloresearch.ai/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** AI scheming detection and evaluation; deceptive alignment auditing; strategic deception evaluation in LLM agents; safety cases for AI scheming; interpretability
- **Key Researchers:** Marius Hobbhahn (CEO/Co-founder, TIME 100 Most Influential in AI 2025), Charlotte Stix (Head of AI Governance), Alexander Meinke, Jeremy Scheurer, Teun van der Weij
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** "Towards Safety Cases for AI Scheming" (with UK AISI, METR, Redwood, UC Berkeley); partnership with OpenAI on scheming detection in o1/o3/o4-mini (deliberative alignment reduced deceptive behaviors ~30-fold); deception research presented at UK AI Safety Summit

---

## 9. FAR AI (Frontier Alignment Research)

- **URL:** https://www.far.ai/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Frontier alignment research; bridging AI policy and technical research; adversarial robustness; alignment techniques for frontier models
- **Key Researchers:** (Research team members collaborate across institutions)
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Second annual Technical Innovations in AI Policy Conference; San Diego Alignment Workshop (Dec 2025, 300+ researchers); Global AI Safety Fellowship partnership

---

## 10. AISI (UK AI Security Institute, formerly AI Safety Institute)

- **URL:** https://www.aisi.gov.uk/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Pre-deployment model evaluations; alignment research (The Alignment Project, 27M+ GBP across 60+ projects); systemic safety grants (8M GBP); cyber/chemical-biological risk assessment; autonomous systems risks; chain-of-thought monitoring (with DeepMind)
- **Key Researchers:** Ian Hogarth (Chair), Yoshua Bengio (External Advisory Board), 100+ technical staff from leading labs and academia
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Renamed to AI Security Institute (Feb 2025); The Alignment Project first round (27M+ GBP); Systemic Safety Grants (8M GBP); Challenge Fund (5M GBP); partnership with Google DeepMind on CoT monitoring; contributions to International AI Safety Report 2026

---

## 11. Conjecture

- **URL:** https://www.conjecture.dev/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Cognitive Emulation (CogEm) -- building AI systems that emulate human reasoning to be scalable, auditable, and controllable; AI "boundedness" (ensuring known capability limits); mechanistic interpretability
- **Key Researchers:** Connor Leahy (CEO/Co-founder, also co-founded EleutherAI), Sid Black (Co-founder), Gabriel Alfour (Co-founder)
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** "The Compendium" (book/discussion on ASI safety and governance, 2025); government consulting on AI regulation frameworks; ongoing policy advocacy and CogEm research

---

## 12. EleutherAI

- **URL:** https://www.eleuther.ai/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Open-source AI research; interpretability; alignment; training data curation (copyright-clean datasets); language model training and evaluation
- **Key Researchers:** Stella Biderman (Executive Director), Curtis Huebner (Head of Alignment), Shivanshu Purohit (Head of Engineering)
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** "Common Pile" dataset (copyright-clean training data, 2025); co-authored "Open Problems in Mechanistic Interpretability" (with Anthropic, Apollo, DeepMind, Jan 2025); collaboration with UK AISI on training data filtering for harm reduction; partnership with AI Safety Initiative on alignment research

---

## 13. Epoch AI

- **URL:** https://epoch.ai/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** AI capabilities tracking and forecasting; compute and hardware analysis; benchmark development; macroeconomic modeling of AI automation (GATE model); AI chip sales tracking; data center tracking
- **Key Researchers:** Jaime Sevilla (Director), Tamay Besiroglu (Co-founder, now also at Mechanize), Lennart Heim, Pablo Villalobos, Eduardo Infante-Roldan, Anson Ho
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** FrontierMath benchmark; Epoch Capabilities Index; GATE macroeconomic model; AI chip sales tracker (2026); "AI in 2030" forecasting report; 2025 Impact Report; new benchmark with METR for long-horizon software dev tasks

---

## 14. GovAI (Centre for the Governance of AI)

- **URL:** https://www.governance.ai/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** AI governance research; risk management and threat modeling; economics of AI; geopolitics of AI (including US-China relations); public policy; technical governance; AI progress forecasting
- **Key Researchers:** Ben Garfinkel (Director), Allan Dafoe (Founder, now also at DeepMind)
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Summer Research Fellowship program; publications on general-purpose AI and security implications (cybersecurity, biosecurity, economic security); spun out from Oxford as independent nonprofit (2021)

---

## 15. CHAI (Center for Human-Compatible AI, UC Berkeley)

- **URL:** https://humancompatible.ai/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Provably beneficial AI; value alignment; cooperative inverse reinforcement learning; assistance games; human-compatible AI theory; probabilistic reasoning; planning under uncertainty
- **Key Researchers:** Stuart Russell (Founder, elected Fellow of the Royal Society 2025), Pieter Abbeel, Anca Dragan (now also at DeepMind), Tom Griffiths
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Ongoing theoretical work on assistance games and value alignment; Stuart Russell's continued public advocacy (Newsweek, Jan 2025: "the AGI race is a race towards the edge of a cliff"); Global AI Safety Fellowship partnership

---

## 16. NYU Alignment Research Group

- **URL:** https://wp.nyu.edu/arg/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Empirical alignment research with language models; debate, amplification, and recursive reward modeling; bootstrapping human oversight on difficult problems; scalable oversight
- **Key Researchers:** Sam Bowman (on leave at Anthropic), He He, Mengye Ren, Ethan Perez (outside advisor, also at Anthropic)
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Group was most active 2022-2024 under Bowman's direct leadership; research on concrete alignment strategies; currently operating with collaborating PIs while Bowman is at Anthropic

---

## 17. Apart Research

- **URL:** https://apartresearch.com/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Interpretability; evaluation methodologies for dangerous capabilities; AGI security and control; physics-inspired AI safety (uncertainty quantification, scaling laws, phase transitions); research sprint model for rapid output
- **Key Researchers:** (Community-driven model with rotating participants and mentors)
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** 22 peer-reviewed publications (ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, ACL) in 2.5 years; 2 Oral Spotlights at ICLR 2025 (top 1.8%); 42 research sprints across 50+ global locations with 3,500+ participants; AI Control Hackathon 2025 (with Redwood Research and UK AISI); AI Safety x Physics Grand Challenge

---

## 18. METR (formerly ARC Evals)

- **URL:** https://metr.org/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** AI capability evaluations (autonomous capabilities, AI R&D acceleration); pre-deployment model testing; evaluation integrity (detecting AI behavior that threatens evaluation trustworthiness); long-horizon task completion benchmarking
- **Key Researchers:** Beth Barnes (Founder/CEO, former OpenAI alignment researcher)
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** "Measuring AI ability to complete long tasks" paper (planning horizon doubling every ~7 months); pre-deployment evaluations for OpenAI (o3, o4-mini, GPT-4o, GPT-4.5) and Anthropic (Claude models); RCT finding AI tools make experienced developers 19% slower; co-developing long-horizon software dev benchmark with Epoch AI

---

## 19. CSET (Center for Security and Emerging Technology, Georgetown)

- **URL:** https://cset.georgetown.edu/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Security implications of AI; AI talent and compute tracking; national security applications of AI; AI policy tools; AI R&D automation analysis; physical AI (robotics + AI convergence); biotechnology policy
- **Key Researchers:** Helen Toner (Interim Executive Director), Jason Matheny (Founding Director, former IARPA Director), Cara LaPointe (Director of Analysis), Catherine Aiken (Director of Data Science), Ben Buchanan
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Expert workshop on AI R&D automation (July 2025); workshop on open science, metascience, and AI (March 2025, with NSF); Physical AI supply chain and policy analysis; "When AI Builds AI" report

---

## 20. Harvard Kempner Institute

- **URL:** https://kempnerinstitute.harvard.edu/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Science of AI; innovation in AI; AI and the brain (natural + artificial intelligence); foundation model improvements; mathematical/computational models of intelligence; AI safety course (CS 2881, Fall 2025)
- **Key Researchers:** Boaz Barak (Associate Faculty, also part-time at OpenAI -- teaches AI Safety course), Kanaka Rajan, Yilun Du, SueYeon Chung, Kianté Brantley, Samuel Gershman
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** 350+ papers and 750K compute jobs in 2025; CS 2881 "AI Alignment and Safety" course (first Harvard AI safety course, Fall 2025); 2026 Accelerator Awards for AI Cluster access; Kempner Undergraduate Research Experience (KURE) program

---

## 21. Stanford HAI (Human-Centered AI Institute) -- Safety Work

- **URL:** https://hai.stanford.edu/ and https://aisafety.stanford.edu/
- **Primary Focus Areas:** Responsible AI benchmarking (HELM Safety, AIR-Bench); foundation model evaluation (CRFM); AI policy and governance research; cybersecurity capability evaluation; safe deployment of autonomous systems; distributional robustness; AI Index reporting
- **Key Researchers:** Percy Liang (CRFM Director), Daniel E. Ho (RegLab Director), Fei-Fei Li (HAI Co-Director), Rishi Bommasani
- **Notable Recent Outputs:** Cybench framework for evaluating LLM cybersecurity capabilities (ICLR 2025, won CAIS SafeBench first place); AI Index Report 2025; "Advancing Science- and Evidence-Based AI Policy" (Science, 2025); response to NIST on dual-use foundation model guidelines
